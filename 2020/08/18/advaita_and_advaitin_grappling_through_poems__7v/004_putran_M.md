What do I see? (June 2019)

  

What do I see?

  

Objects of the world

Objects of the mind

  

That I am them

Tied to them

Affected by them

  

Seeing thus

The objects of Ignorance,

Projects more of them -

The cycle continues.

  

The hidden Object

The Knowledge

Of this Distinction

  

It is the seen

I am the seer

  

Must be seen,

For this illusory chain

Of Causality and Fear

To Vanish.

  

-------

  

The saying goes

"He who sees the Self in all

And all in the Self

Knows"

  

Appearing

As if the changing seen

And chained seer,

I pervade now and ever

The All

  

Remove the Obstruction

  

I am the Self indivisible

Unchanging Eternal.

  

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
