This world revolves (December 2016)

  

This world revolves

  

In love ofself,

In hate ofself;

Fear of Knowledge

Of that Self.

  

What must happen, what must not;

What is wrong, what is right;

What must change, what must remain;

  

What did who think

Of me, of me.

  

What can youthink

But of Me. The Self

I am of you, of He;

  

When all is One,

These bodies all

To serve that One

Who is All;

  

Tear each other.

  

Call it love, Call it hate,

Ignorant,

Yet forMy sake

  

This world revolves

  

In love ofself,

In hate ofself;

Fear of Knowledge

Of that Self.
