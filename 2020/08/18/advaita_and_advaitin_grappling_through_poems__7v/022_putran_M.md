Thank you Mother

  

You see:

  

The Beauty

  

In every experience

  

the momentary joys

Prolonged sorrows

People and things

  

Dancing Her Dance

Playing Her Play

Displaying Her Knowledge.

  

Ha!

  

You do not scare me,

Mother. I dare You

Come. Show me

  

Your svarupas

Of every kind

In unending

Oscillating barrage.

  

I am not bothered a bit!

Certainly no longer by pain.

You have taught, are teaching

I have learned, am learning

  

I have no victory

To attain,

Nor can suffer defeat

From anything, anyone

  

When I see You.

For You see me.

  

Thank you Mother

For revealing Yourself

In these forms, thoughts,

Cognitions

  

The perfect kamaphala

Blessings that befit

My karma

  

Thank you Mother

For blessing me now

The Awareness ...

  

(Not perfect? So what!

I thank my Mother then

even more)

  

For entrusting me

The Intent the Determination

  

the Fight I will fight

  

O Mother

  

To see You in all

And all in You

  

My Self.
