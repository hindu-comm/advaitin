Namaskaram,

  

Below a bit of effort to construct into poem form; since I was thinking also a general audience in mind, where I tried to present the three Vedanta schools, tried limiting or avoiding explicit suggestion that one viewpoint is ignorance or less to other.

  

----------------------------------------------------

  

I.

  

That

  

is the heart

  

Of my existence

  

That is the true Me,

My Reality, my Self.

  

I am That.

  

Atma.

  

Like Gold in rings & bangles

  

Like Energy in tables & chairs

  

I am the Unchanging

Fearless Truth

In the midst of change

Appearance imagination

  

The Ground Adhishtanam

Existence-Consciousness

Sat-Chit(-Anandam)

I am.

  

II\.

  

(Advaita oriented)

  

In Chit the imaginative potential,

The knowledge of the three worlds

Inheres; in Chit,

Sat appears embodied

In a show of name and form;

In Chit, superimposition,

Imagination Cognition

Of Sat. In Chit, the Self Atma I

Appears as the knower-Self

The Existence-Consciousness

The Dreamer of the dream

Who projects, identifies, cognizes;

Knows, desires, determines, manifests.

  

III\.

  

The play of Chit:

  

Consciousness

The power to know

to desire to determine

the power to illumine

  

The knowledge (chittam, Mind)

Of me, within me

  

Thus manifest, cognize

A world that is

Superimposition on me (A),

Or a part of me (VA),

Or apart from me (D).

  

IV\.

  

Five standpoints there are

  

The standpoint of Chit

Based in the negation of Sat -  
No reality, no self,

Only impermanence, change:

Naastika.

  

The standpoint of Chit

Based in the affirmation,

A Duality of realities

(Ishvara, jiva, jagat)

And negation of non-duality:

Dvaita.

  

The standpoint of Chit

Based in affirmation,

Of Narayana the All, the Self in all,

And negation of Nirgunatvam.

Duality (real) in non-duality:

Visishtadvaita.

  

The standpoint of Chit

Based in the affirmation

Of Nirguna Sat.

Duality (mithya) in non-duality,

Sat-Chit Ishvara the One

*Appearing*as many: Advaita

(vyavaharika).

  

The standpoint of Sat

The adhishtanam Self,

Sans all duality

Including of Sat & Chit.

Ekam eva adviteeyam

Nirguna Brahman: Advaita

(paramarthika).

  

V.

  

I am real, the world is real, the Lord is real.

Him my Mother Father Child Master Friend

The One behind my all,

To know Him, to serve Him, to please Him,

My dharma and moksha.

  

I am real, the world is real,

In Him, as a part of Him.

My Source, my Support,

My Guide, my Refuge.

To know Him, my union with Him,

To surrender to Him,

To serve please rejoice in Him,

My dharma, my moksha.

  

I am real, the world is real,

*As*Him the Self of all,

Appearing as-if (distinctly/mithya)

i and world and God.

To serve Him (ishvara), my sadhana;

To know Him (Ishvara), my Dharma;

In the truth of Him (Brahman) Moksha.
