**Key Examples and Themes in Vedantic Poetry:**

- **Swami Vivekananda’s Poetry:**Known for works like "The Song of the
  Sannyasin" and "Kali the Mother," his poetry embodies intense
  renunciation, strength, and Advaitic wisdom.
- **Swami Sivananda’s Poems:**[Swami
  Sivananda’s](https://articles.sivananda.org/our-lineage-and-the-gurus-teaching/poems-by-swami-sivananda/)verses
  often focus on bhakti (devotion) combined with Jnana (knowledge),
  praising the divine in all forms and encouraging spiritual practice.
- **Traditional & Devotional Poems:**[Sri Chaitanya’s
  prayers](https://vedantaatlanta.org/prayers-poems-chants/)highlight
  emotional longing for the divine and the surrender of the ego,
  illustrating the path to merging with the "Isness" of the universe.
- **Nature as Divine Reflection:**Poems often depict the world as a
  manifestation of the Divine, as seen in["Four
  Poems"](https://americanvedantist.org/2018/poems/four-poems/)published
  in*American Vedantist*, which describes God as the seer, the seen,
  and the unseen.
- **Metaphorical Tales:**[L. Ramaswamy’s "Vedanta in English Poems"
  series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLWjpkY4mU2RDdHR0DLbSPYGcKW0uYZLjo)interprets
  Western and Eastern poems through a Vedantic lens, such as using "The
  Blind Men and the Elephant" to explain the limitations of partial
  truths (Maya).

These poems are tools for contemplation, designed to shift perspective from the mundane to the eternal.
