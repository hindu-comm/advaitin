Hi Sreenivasa,



I was asking ‘what sort of response would provide you with the evidence you seek?’ E.g. if I said ‘I know that it is so’, would you accept that? If your closest friend or relation were to say ‘I know that it is so’, would you accept that? According to Shankara, when shruti tells you that it is so, this is infinitely more reliable than your closest friend.



The ‘claims’ of Advaita can never be verified by science or by perception. The pramANa-s of pratyakSha, anumAna etc. are of no use here. As I said, the only pramANa is shabda. This is where the characteristic of shraddhA comes in. Until you realize the truth for yourself, you must have some element of faith, I’m afraid.
