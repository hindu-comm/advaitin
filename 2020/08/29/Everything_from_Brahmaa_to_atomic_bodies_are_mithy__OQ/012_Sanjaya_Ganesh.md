<div dir="auto">

  

</div>

<div dir="auto">

Advaitha Sathya is very easy to believe even if you want to start with faith. Why?

</div>

<div dir="auto">

  

</div>

<div dir="auto">

Let us examine the term truth.  

<div dir="auto">

  

</div>

<div dir="auto">

Truth by definition is something which has been independently verified and validated. This is the definition even by science. Sun rises in East is truth they say because it can be independently verified by people in East West North and South and across ages and times in history.

</div>

<div dir="auto">

  

</div>

<div dir="auto">

  

</div>

<div dir="auto">

When Sufi Saint Mansoor Al Hallah said "Anaaal Haq" ( I am that) or when Jesus said '"I am that I am" or when Janis across ages and times like Ramana and Shankara and Ramakrishna , who wanted to find truth found exactly the same advaithic truth it tells you that advaitha Sathya has all features of definition of truth.

</div>

<div dir="auto">

  

</div>

<div dir="auto">

So even if you have never woken up before 7 am in life you believe sun rises in East. So why is it hard for advaitha Sathya belief even if you want to start with faith donain ?

</div>

<div dir="auto">

  

</div>

<div dir="auto">
