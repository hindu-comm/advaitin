Joining in the middle of the discussions.

<div dir="ltr">

<div dir="ltr">

Swami Dayanadaji answered in this question in the form of a story.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

Once a person suddenly became blind. He went to one eye doctor to the other, some suggested some medicines and some suggested some operations. He tried all the suggested medications but that did not help. He went through a couple of operations but even those did not help. He was getting dejected and depressed. At last, one suggested a very famous eye-doctor and the person reluctantly approached the doctor who suggested that this is a very special problem that cannot be cured by normal means. He has devised a special operation and suggested the patient to try. Since the doctor is very well known and highly recommended, the patient agreed for one more operation. The operation was done and the doctor came back to the patient and said, the operation was successful and removed the bandage and said please try to open your eyes and see. The patient said, Sir, I have been disappointed with many operations in the past. Please guarantee that I will be able to see if I open my eyes - otherwise, I do not want to open my eyes. The doctor responded - The operation was successful from our point. you have to open your eyes and see and prove to your self and to us that the operation was indeed successful. But the patient refused open until the doctor gives a guarantee that he will be able to see.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

After all the procedures and methods that were required for the operation, the success of the operation can only be proved when the patient tries to open his eyes and SEE. There is no other means available other than his opening and seeing the truth!

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

Vedanta points out the truth and provides a direction for the mind to follow. It is now left for the patient to open his mind and SEE. That is the only litmus test.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">
