Namaste Sanjaya Ganesh ji. Not sure if we should be discussing Jesus and Sufism in a group catering to the teachings of Advaita as propounded by mahatmas of yore and belonging to an age old paramparA. As Jagadguru Adi Shankaracharya said clearly in the Mundaka bhashya- asampradayavit murkhavat upekshaniya- dismiss one one does not belong to the sampradaya as a dullard. Let us adhere to that tenet so as to not stray from the traditional vision. This is not a comment on other personalities, but rather an appeal that they are not pertinent to the discussion. We cannot retrofit and assume their words meant to convey our own ethos. It is easy to make post hoc comparisons between almost anything - including Star Wars, words of Jesus, Sufism etc. but not suggested. Let's talk Advaita Vedanta.
