Both the Shruti and Shankara say that as long as we live in the Vyavaharika (relative) space, in the beginning, we need to trust some.
This will pave the way for us to cross over the Vyavaharika space to the Paramarthika (real) space. Questions like what you raised also came from the intellect with help of experience gained and registered on the mind from the senses. At this stage, we need to bring the analogy of the Pole Vault jump, where we have to use the pole to jump over the pole. Just at the instant of crossing the pole, the pole that was used to cross needs to be discarded! Similarly, the intellect used like the pole vault to jump beyond the level of intellect and at that time all intellectual thinking and analysis also need to be discarded. We need the Pole to go beyond the pole, Intellect to go beyond the intellect and the Trust to go beyond the Trust. I hope this provides some clue to getting the answer to your question. Sruti also states that one has to discard even the Sruti to attain the Truth!!
