It is interesting that you brought to our attention the Goedel's incompleteness theory. I discussed this in my article 'Science and Vedanta' - now included in the book 'Transcending Science', published recently by Indic Academy.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

I am sure Prof. VK may elaborate on the Goedel's theorem. In essence, it says any theory based on an axiomatic system - will itself be incomplete - hence cannot describe the truth.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

The world we see is real - is the basic axiom in all objective theories.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

Whereas my own existence is beyond any axiom, This is also the essence of mantra 7 of Mandukya Up. - since I swallow the whole universe - prapanchopashamam-

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

maayaa was discussed before. Einstein was looking for a universal force but could not come up with one.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

Upanishad (chadogya) talks about one becoming many - tat aikshataa, buhushyaam, prajaayeyeti

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

From the Physics point, a change of state requires a driving force. When I move an object from position A to B, the force is required, even though I cannot see that force. Newton in fact defines force by the change of state.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

Therefore one becoming may involve a driving force - which is maayaa, and like all forces, it is also imperceptible. That forms the universal forces.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

Einstein says - space-time is relative to the observer. However, he could never come up with an observer-independent of reference. The absolute reference is also the essence of the mantra 7 of Mandukya - the mantra ends with the statement that - that reference is - sa aatmaa - sa vijneyaH - that self has to be known - But how - by shifting the mind that because of which even the mind functions.

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

  

</div>

<div dir="ltr">

Kena Up. says 'pratibodha vidtam matam - the truth is revealed in every thought - since every thought is soaked with the consciousness that I am for me to be conscious of the thought.

</div>
