This message is well laid out in the 13th Chapter, early shlokas, where the Kshetra is taught to be the object - consistingof the entire mind-body-organs-outside world of sound, etc. and the Kshetrajna, the knower of the above. This division of object-subject is the basis for the idea that the subject, Kshetrajna, is never the object of the Kshetra ensemble.
