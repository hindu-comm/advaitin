Ø This is very good observation prabhuji, yes as per this shloka krishna’s normal appearance (before vishwarUpa) has to be with four arms!! But if we see the picture of bhagavan with four arms, we say it is vishNu NOT krishna…Yes, according to us, most of the times, krishna is allowed with only two arms, always with flute and some times with sudarshana chakra and other hand is free for some other purpose 😊 If he has four arms shankha, chakra, gada, padma then it is vishNu, by the way when arjuna wants to see the normal rUpa after getting afraid of seeing the vishva rUpa ( though krishna given him divya chakshu to arjuna to see the vishva rUpa, perhaps forgotten to give him enough courage to see this big rUpa with Prasanna and bhakti chitta 😊 ) he wants to see only gada and chakra (shankha -pAnchajanya and padma missing !!)
