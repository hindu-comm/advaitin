In 11-51, Arjuna says "Drishtavedam Manusham Rupam" (seeing this human form). Clearly human form has only 2 hands, so we can also think that the reference in 11-46 may not mean Krishna typically has 2 arms but that Arjuna also knows his four armed form as calming and pleasing, and at that time he was seeking that vision rather than the vishwarupam.

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

11-50 also indicates that this is "his own" ('familiar' in Krishna Warrier translation) form.
