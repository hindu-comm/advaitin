Thereafter, He explains that - the five elements, the ego, the intellect, the Unmanifest (Primordial Matter), the ten organs (of perception and action), the mind, and the five objects of sense (sound, touch, colour, taste and smell); also desire, aversion, pleasure, pain, the physical body, consciousness, firmness, this is the Kshetra with its evolutes (XIII.5-6).[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kshetrajna#cite_note-3)

So the person reading and responding is the Kshetra. As Ramana would say, the light of the bulb shines forth through the intellect and personality. What of the nature of the electricity. It makes the bulb shine but itself does not exhibit heat or light.

Kshetrajna (Devnagari: क्षेत्रज्ञ) "The knower of the Kshetra." refers to the electricity.  

</div>

  

<div>

<div>

Krishna tells Arjuna that:-

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

क्षेत्रज्ञ चापि मां विद्धि सर्वक्षेत्रेषु भारत \|

</div>

<div>

क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्ज्ञानं यत्तज्ज्ञानं मतं मम \|\| (13.3)

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

"Know Myself to be the Kshetrajna (knower of field) in all the Kshetras, O descendant of Bharata. It is the knowledge of Kshetra and Kshetrajna which I consider as the ultimate knowledge."

</div>

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

So not this not that, negations of sense of self. Or in buddhist terms, nothing as in empty.

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

Nisargadatta has a nice analogy. Take a piece of paper ad consider the paper fiber texture and written text as self. Tare a hole and then define how that self gives contextual definition to the hole. It can do so by giving boundaries as in in not this, not that. 

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

What then is the hole composed of... an absence of self.

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

So that is logic taking you to a boundary. What is the nature of Krishna?

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

THe four formed ideal is an analogy, not a physical representation.
Just as the hole is an analogy not a representation. "My true from is beyond your capacity to understand."

</div>

<div>

  

</div>

<div>

But the boundaries are within your capacity and is recommended as a means of self determination. Look for the juxtapositions in your life. Desire, anger, fatigue, hopelessness and recognise them as Kshetra. Then recognise the, 'I am the austerity that transcends these limitations', as a outer nature of Krishna.

</div>
