Hi all Gurutulyas and friends, Pranams.  

  

A humble question, with earnest respect to our Ancient Scriptures and Gurus. I am studying them for a while now. This question has been bothering me for a while.  

  
**What is the purpose of thoroughly studying various such works and their references?  **

**  **

With reverence and studious efforts, one may firmly come to grasp the underlying essence of the shastra's. What then?  

  

Apart from behavioral maturity and a level of detachment that develops from such understanding, how else does one change after such studious efforts? Or is it only the intellectual curiosity that is behind these studies?

  

To put the same question in an alternate way, if I am illiterate and deaf, do i get Moksham? How?

  

Namaste!

Ekam.
