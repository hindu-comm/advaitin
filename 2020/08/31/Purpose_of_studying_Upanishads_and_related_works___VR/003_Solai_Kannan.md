[[Solai Kannan	2020-08-31, 22:35:53 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/VR_XV1kFswo)]]

We are all conditioned to perceive the reality in certain way. The scriptures will throw new perspectives. We are in the ground and perceiving as we witness the world. These holy scripture uplift us in helicopter and show us a new perspective. What is the use of these new perspectives? They open up our inner mind to think deeper. Depending on our determination and divine intervention we march further towards liberation.  
  
In other words. We sit in a well and think the well is the largest in earth and scriptures say the world is bigger than the well. We have to come out of well and see in real. Some takes steps to come out and many are happy to be in well and believe well is real.  
  
Will deaf and illiterate have moksha? There are born artists who doesn’t need any training in art and they excel. Similarly if divine intervention is there anyone at any level and any age can get moksha. In all scriptures there are references to this effect.
