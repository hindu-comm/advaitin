Dear friend,

  

You have answered your own question. "Behavioral maturity and a level of detachment that develops from such understanding", lead us to the goal. Please look at the life, lived among us the mortals, by Lord Ram and Lord Krishna, which was at the highest level of maturity and detachment. I read about a great guru, who was not liked by some people and they wanted to malign him. They made a woman blame that guru
publicly, saying that she was bearing the child of the guru. When she confronted the guru publicly, he just smiled. With passage of time it was clear to all that the woman was blaming the guru falsely.  

  

For us the ordinary folks, probably the best way is to study the scriptures and talk to out gurus, to help us understand the impermanency of the world and everything in it, so that we can develop the Vairagya and begin to develop the sattvic qualities and don't forget that the Lord is there to help us. Nirgna Sadhana is difficult and Saguna Brahman is always there to help us achieve that. "Bhaja Govindam" will hep us on our way.

  

Best,
