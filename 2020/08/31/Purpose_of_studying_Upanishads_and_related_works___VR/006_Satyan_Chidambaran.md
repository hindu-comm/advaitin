नमस्ते,

What is the purpose of thoroughly studying various such works and their references?

  

To deliberate on this, let’s start with what isमोक्ष:? It isआत्यन्तिकदुःखनिवृत्ति:परमानन्दप्राप्तिश्च।Complete cessation ofसंसार:, complete cessation ofदु:खम्and attainment of limitlessness, i.e freedom from all limitations.

  

मोक्ष: is to be understood asएकसाधनसाघ्यम्, a goal that is attainedonlyby one means. Theonlymeans forमोक्ष:isज्ञानम्.

  

Whatis thatज्ञानम्by which aloneमोक्ष: is attained? Thatआत्मज्ञानम्which shows me thatमोक्ष: that I am seeking is already myस्वरूपम्.

  

Thatज्ञानम्is alsoएकसाधनसाघ्यम्. It is attainedonlybyश्रवणम्.

  

Let us make no mistake with regards to this. The purpose of studying the Vedantic texts is for thisआत्मज्ञानम्alone and not for anything else. Study is primarily in the form ofगुरुमुखात्श्रवणम्, listening to a qualified teacher unfold these texts. By this, a qualified student,अधिकारिgainsआत्मज्ञानम्which shows that theमोक्षone was seeking is already one’s own nature. The resultantज्ञानम्is direct,अपरोक्षज्ञानम्.Ifश्रवणम् doesn’tproduce theअपरोक्षज्ञानम्due to some obstacles, nothing else will. The only solution is to remove the obstacles and repeatedly doश्रवणम्.

  

With reverence and studious efforts, one may firmly come to grasp the underlying essence of the shastra's. What then?

  

When one looks at a mirror, the purpose is not to see the mirror but to see one’s own face. Similarly, the study ofवेदान्तis not to study the books for mere academic scholarship but to understand oneself as revealed byवेदान्त.वेदान्तis aप्रमाणम्, an instrument that producesआत्मज्ञानम्in anअधिकारि.

  

What then? One who has gainedआत्मज्ञानम्then knows oneself as unconditionally and completely free and limitless. One who knows thus is aकृतकृत्य:. There is nothing more to be accomplished by such a person as theपरमपुरुषार्थ:मोक्ष: is already achieved.

  

how else does one change after such studious efforts?

  

Such a person may engage in life in any manner acting out ofपूर्णत्वम्and a total sense of freedom. Much like a cricket team that has won 3 matches of a 5 match series. They still play the other 2 matches knowing that they have already won the series. Aज्ञानीhas won the series called life but continues to play the game knowing that nothing can affect him.

  

To put the same question in an alternate way, if I am illiterate and deaf, do i get Moksham? How?

  

Becoming anअधिकारिwho hasमनुष्यत्वम्मुमुक्षुत्वम्andमहापुरुषसंश्रय:requires an abundance ofईश्वरकृपा. A combination of all three is seen to be very rare indeed as stated inविवेकचूडामणि:

  

दुर्लभम्त्रयमेवैतद्देवानुग्रहहेतुकम्

मनुष्यत्वम्मुमुक्षुत्वम्महापुरुषसंश्रय:

  

Majority ofजीवs don’t even get a human birth. Others may be born as human but spend their lives pursuing only sense pleasures, pursuingअर्थ:काम:, thereby wasting their lives completely engrossed in material pursuits. Such people are as good as being spiritually deaf and ignorant even if their senses are all fully intact and even if they are scholars in their own fields. Still fewer are born as human and also have someमुमुक्षुत्वम्buttheir seeking doesn’t get the right direction. They don’t turn intoजिज्ञासुs and hence do not pursue gaining this knowledge under aगुरु:. Their seeking forमोक्ष: continues somewhat aimlessly. Only some rare ones haveमनुष्यत्वम्मुमुक्षुत्वम्and also the guidance of a teacher who brings them to pursueज्ञानम्as the one and only means forमोक्ष:. Thus, if conducive conditions for this pursuit come together in one’s life, it can only be due to a shower ofईश्वरकृपाwhich again is not arbitrary but requires the fructification of some great pastपुण्यम्.

  

श्रीगुरुभ्योनमः
