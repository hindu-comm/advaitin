Hare Krishna





Thank you all for very diverse and insightful points of view here. It strengthens the resolve to continue the journey.



I am tempted to continue this discussion with a follow up question. How does one make sure that there is a definite progress in this journey of seeking and one is not going in circles forever?



Ø Normally in the tradition GURU will take care of this assessment and guide accordingly his pupil/s as he has the contact with his disciples on 1:1 basis. So, a sincere shishya would leave this assessment to his guru 😊



What is your own way of checking this ?



Ø We the ‘vedAntic materialists’ do not have that luck to live with GURU and will be under his surveillance. So, what is the way of checking it ourselves!!?? If we don’t get disturbed when wife shouts at us unnecessarily, if we stop our aversion towards boss, no problem / chaltaahai attitude when our colleagues get their dues well in advance than me though they are juniors to me in years and experience, serenity / calmness when going somewhere urgently but getting stuck in traffic jam, not violently getting worked up by just seeing the kids’ progress cards, a fully satisfied life within the limited sources without comparing ourselves and unconditional contentment in walks of life
without giving much importance to external (objective) achievements / failures in life. Last but not the least, being a gruhastha, sticking to our svadharma and doing it with shraddha and bhakti. Perhaps much required vairagya would follow to get the jnana if we do this.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
