Someone said that in such situations we have to ask the questions to ourselves. For example, it is very easy for the ordinary folks like us, who are devotees of, say Lord Shiva or Lord Vishnu, though essentially both are the same. When we are jealous of someone, we have to ask ourselves, would Lord Krishna have been jealous like I am.
