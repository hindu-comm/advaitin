Namaskaram Ekam-ji,



> How does one make sure that there is a definite progress in this
> journey of seeking and one is not going in circles forever?  
>
>   
>
> What is your own way of checking this ?  
>
>   

  

I always found my best success in this regard has come when I took to bhakti-mode. Ishvara/Ishta-devata at the center of and behind things big and small, and I drive the mind to Him and through Him. Waking up on time, straightening the back, not dispersing the mind in worldly temptations, whatever - contact Him and affirm a relationship with Him as the basis for our decisions. Think of the Ishta devata as the one who has graced us with the Human Body, so precious and not to be wasted. Don't become distraught about 'mistakes' or lapses or choices not strict; just keep going back to Him in the smallest moments possible and be dogged about it. Have shraddha that he will open the way if we are sincere.

  

He can (I know) make things complicated that one struggles to do even the above and as a consequence goes back in circles. But that is my most trusted/dependable way of checking the problem, filling the leaks, and firming the ground for higher effort.
