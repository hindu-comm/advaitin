Namaskaram Sunil-ji

  

I am not saying "that" is easy or that I have reached some grand level. I am still in circles, or slipping upon snakes after ladders and working back up.

  

But it is an if-then statement. If we (strive to) havecontact with Ishta-devata (and make that our goal in the smallest of things), then He will begin to replace and resolve the world of desire and fear. Spiritual life can become clearer in this case. We associate happiness and sorrow with various body-mind-world forms, most of them imaginations just frittering our energies, taking us on rides out of sense and making us go in circles. Figure out how to bring Him in there, or make "parameshvara preethyartham" the goal that we redirect to Him. Why should I abide svadharma? Parameshvara preethyartham. Should I be wondering what that person thinks? No, what is my Ishta thinking - that is what matters, think of Him. Etc. It is a process.

  

Anycase, BG 9.27-28 is pretty much the idea:

  

9.27: Whatever you do, eat, sacrifice, offer as gift, perform as austerity, O Arjuna! - do all this as a dedication to Me.

  

9.28: Thus will you be released from the bonds of works with their fruits, good and evil. Liberated, with the Self integrated by the Yoga of renunciation, you will come to Me.

  

The difficult part of this process is to develop a proper sense of the personal saguna Ishvara in our Ishta-devata, that the jiva finds itnaturalto seek (vision/knowledge of) Ishvara thus.
