Dear All,



The purpose of studying prasthAna traya is not about becoming a ‘better person’ (happier, less stressed, less angry etc.). Advaita is about realizing that you are NOT a person. All the other stuff comes under the heading of j~nAna phalam and may or may not occur, depending upon the extent of ‘mind preparation’ prior to gaining Self-knowledge and the extent of continued nididhyAsana afterwards. There is no ‘reaching’ or ‘becoming’ Brahman; you are already unlimited, perfect, and free.
