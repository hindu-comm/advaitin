> The difficult part of this process is to develop a proper sense of the
> personal saguna Ishvara in our Ishta-devata, that the jiva finds
> itnaturalto seek (vision/knowledge of) Ishvara thus.

I would clarify that the sense of Ishvara as the Identity should come to us no matter the form. For instance, whether we praise Vishnu or Shiva, we should have recognition that we are seeing the same Lord as Vishnu or Shiva. And this sense we can inculcate even as we deal with the manifestations in the world. But when we are overwhelmed by the "person" in me and world, the Person of our Ishta Devata is singularly powerful recourse.
