मूकं करोति वाचालं पंगुं लंघयते गिरिम् ।

यत्कृपा तमहं वन्दे परमानन्दमाधवम् ॥

By whose grace dumbs start talking, lame men climb mountains, I worship that Sri Krishna, the supreme bliss.
