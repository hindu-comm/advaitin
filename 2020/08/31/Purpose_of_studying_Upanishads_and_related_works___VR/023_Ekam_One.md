Thanks for the very enlightening discussions in this thread so far.
With all humbleness, and in the true Upanishadic tradition, i would like to raise follow up questions.  

  

1\. Many of the scriptures, including the Gita, inform us that, Karma, Jnana and Bhakti Yoga's are the different paths to realization. Many of the posts in this thread so far refer to almost a mandatory need for studying scriptures. How do we reconcile these two points of view?

  

2. Also, this heavy focus on Sastra's make it that only those who understand them get Moksha. Theoretically, this is very small portion of the world population and people born into specific traditions / cultures. In my understanding, this does not reconcile with the universality of Brahman, We also have instances such as Vyadha Gita in Mahabharatha.  

  

Is the study of Sastra's really a precondition for realization? Or is it's role in helping us prepare us and speeding up the realization?  

  

Any additional light on these questions would be very useful, to me.  



Namaskarams.
