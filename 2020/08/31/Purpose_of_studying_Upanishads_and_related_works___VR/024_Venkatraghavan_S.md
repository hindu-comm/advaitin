Namaste,

  

1\) There are not multiple paths for realisation - jnAna or knowledge (of the self) is the only path. This is because the cause of bondage is ignorance. The only remedy to ignorance is knowledge, not action.

  

karma, bhakti, yoga etx are all important preparatory steps for chitta shuddhi or purity of mind, but they themselves do not lead to self knowledge. Once the mind is purified, their work ceases and the only way to liberation is jnAna, by exposing it to shAstra pramANa.The reason there are multiple paths such as karma, bhakti, yoga etc prescribed in shAstra is to cater for different kinds of candidates - for some karma is needed, for some bhakti is needed, for some yoga is needed - for chitta shuddhi, not moksha.

  

2\) Yes, it is true that only a very few people attain liberation. The Shruti itself confirms this -श्रवणायापि बहुभिर्यो न लभ्यः शृण्वन्तोऽपि बहवो यं न विद्युः।

आश्चर्यो वक्ता कुशलोऽस्य लब्धा आश्चर्यो ज्ञाता कुशलानुशिष्टः ॥

That it in itself does not militate against the universality of Brahman. Even if a law is universally true, only some are able to understand it, that does not affect its universality. The law of gravitation applies equally whether one is aware of it or not.

  

3\) Exposure to the truth expounded in the shAstras by a liberated soul is mandatory. Depending on the maturity of the candidate, this could involve a long and deep study of shAstras, or when one is highly evolved spiritually, by understanding the self in the silence in the presence of a liberated one, as उपशान्तोऽयमात्मा.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
