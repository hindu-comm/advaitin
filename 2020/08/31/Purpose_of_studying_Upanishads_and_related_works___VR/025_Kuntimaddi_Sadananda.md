PraNAms

  

There is a sloka in the Vivekachudamani - that says in the first line - The study of the scriptures is useless if one has not realized. The second line says - The study of the scriptures is useless if you have realized.

The first line implies that if that study does not translate into a) preparing the mind for contemplation - through yoga under the direction of a competent guru, the mere study will make one only a scholar but not a Vedantin. When a teacher or guide is required even to do Ph.D. or Masters in a university and the University will not allow the student otherwise, then what to mention about the subtlest knowledge of the absolute truth. For any knowledge, pre-requisites are essential. If I sit in a quantum mechanics class without proper preparation all I hear is only some greek letters of alpha, beta, etc.

The second line says after studying one has to uplift the mind in the direction pointed out by Vedanta. If not that study will not give the intended fruits.

  

Swami Paramarthanandaji's FIR reduction (frequency of perturbance, Intensity of perturbance, and Recovery time from perturbance) goes down slowly as on spends time contemplating on the truth. The scripture calls this process as nidhidhyaasana - when involves continuous contemplation on the teachings (now the study of scriptures helps to keep the mind in that vision), writing about it, discussing about it, talking about it - or meditating about it - any and all of the process which is suitable for that mind.

FIR reduction is a slow process and it may take years or lives also since the lingering vaasanaas that perturb the mind has to be slowly washed out.

  

Hence one has to engage in the study of the scriptures first to learn but later to help the mind to keep contemplating on the truth expounded by the scriptures.

  

There is also a sloka in the Vivekachudamani - that says

na yogena na saakhyena karmana no na vidyayaa, brhmaatmaikatva bodhena mokshaH sidhyati na anyathaa\|

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
