Pranams.

  

Almost all the responses so far state the following.  

  

A\) Actual realization is different from the long preparation for it.  

B\) Preparation can be done in several ways and  

C\) Realization happens only with needed knowledge (Jnana) is acquired from (pre-listed ) scriptures.  

  

I can fully understand and appreciate A and B. But, to me it is hard to believe that C is always TRUE.  

  

In my last post i said Brahma is universal. I correct my hypothesis - "**Realization that we are all Brahman is a universal possibility, Vedic scriptures are one way to facilitate that possibility**". Theoretically, it should be possible to trigger Realization in a number of other ways .  

  

I believe many Rishis who authored the Upanishads, Bhashya's and other works such as Vivekachudamani were also humans like us. Their intense seeking and discipline lead to intuitions through which they were able to come up with such beautiful insights and scriptures. They even many times extended previous works with own insights.  

  

Now, if one has the same level of intensity and discipline, they should be able to go through the realization, even if they have not received the guidance form scriptures. Or they received guidance from "other scriptures". With that logic, the intensity of seeking and discipline should let anyone realize, without the need for extensive study of scriptures.  

  

Shouldn't we be giving higher importance to intensity of seeking and discipline in order to gain realization, and use the scriptures as an **optional means** **to accelerate** the preparation where needed?  

  

So, in that case following statements would be true.  

  

A\) Actual realization is different from the long preparation for it.  

B\) Preparation can be done in several ways*, *and scriptures is one way to help accelerate them**  

C\) Realization happens when there is enough intensity of seeking and discipline, which is most of the times made possible through (B).  

  

Can someone kindly help me resolve this dilemma ?
