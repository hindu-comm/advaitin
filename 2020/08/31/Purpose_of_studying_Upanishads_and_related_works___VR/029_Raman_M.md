Sir,

Didn’t quite understand what you imply by

A\) Actual realization is different from the long preparation for it.

There is only one thing to realise and the preparation for it is to understand or grasp that reality. The preparation are the means; just like travelling by a car to reach a destination. Both, car and destination are different. Is that what you mean?



Since the concept of Brahmam is not conceivable to any of the senses (अद्रेश्यम्, अग्राह्यम्, अगोत्रम्, …), we need to rely on the scriptures only to 'reveal' it. what the other ways exists to trigger realisation?



If you meant become a Rishi …

I think Rishis who are mantra drishtAs appear during the start of a kalpa only; they are not revealed to multiple people or mutiple times is my understanding. any later revelations as part of an avatara during other periods, come to us in form of commentaries,prakarana granthas etc., not as scriptures.



Since we have lost many of the Upanishads (and perhaps some contents of vedas), would it be appropriate to have another Rishi born as a mantra drishtA now or in the future, or should we wait for a pralayam? I don’t know!
