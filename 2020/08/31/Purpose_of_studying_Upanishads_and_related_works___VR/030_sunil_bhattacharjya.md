The mind also has to be controlled, not just "Chitta-vriti-nirodha", it has to be like "Chitta -vritti -vilopa". I understand that Lord Ram had advised Hanumanji to became "Manah Shunya and "Ahankara shunya". As that is difficult for people lesser than Hanumanji, the best alternative is practise 'Bhaja Govandam', i.e, go for the acquisition of Advata knowledge plus surrender to the Lord.
