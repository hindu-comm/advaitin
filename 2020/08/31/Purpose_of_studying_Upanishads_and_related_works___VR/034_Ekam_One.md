Thank you sir for your reply.  

May be it is now time for me to share my personal situation which is making me seek answers from others here.  

  

I have the highest respect for Vedic Scriptures and their derivatives. In spite of being born into a Dwaitha tradition, i have for the last 15 years studiously studied a number of Vedantic works with utmost dedication. I must confess, I have also studied the works from other traditions. My regard for Vedantic works has not diminished due to this. In stead, i start to see the same underlying messages everywhere.  I can humbly state also that my FIR measurements have shown definite change in the positive directions.

  

However, of late, i have begun to notice that, studying scriptures has become more of an **intellectual avocation and less of a Sadhana**. Once the underlying messages and truths were intellectually grasped, the value from further reading seems to be very little. Moreover, every time, I read a new work, the analytical mind starts to become hyperactive and finds its (egoic?) satisfaction in the analysis of the matter being read and making connections to other works.  

  

I feel that, I am at a stage where I need to now stop reading more works, **calm down the spinning wheel of my intellectual mind, surrender to the Lord and experience the deep silence**.  

  

And my reverence to the works or the people studying them is fully in-tact.  

  

And my apologies if i sound amateurish to the enlightened people here in the group. I look forward to hear from the experiences of others and their advices.  

  

Pranams.

Ekam.
