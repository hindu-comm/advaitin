Hare krishna



Well those who are the knowers of sampradaya would answer that they (ramaNa, rAmakrishna) have completed the shravaNAdi sAdhana in their previous janma and in the current janma they have got the results. This emphasizing is required since shAstra vAkya’s shravaNAdi sAdhana is MUST for the ultimate realization (paramArtha jnana). And shruti and bhAshya also say jnana is the only mArga and that jnana has to come through shravaNa, manana and nidhidhyAsana of vedAnta vAkya. There is no short cut in that hence there is consideration that pUrva janma shAstra sAdhana for these noble souls.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
