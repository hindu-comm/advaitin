Dear friends,

  

Yes. Only who realized "sarvam khalvidam brahma" can know that "he himself is brahman" and he can only give the "tatvam asi" upadesha to others". Uddalaka realized that and that is why Chamunda Devi was happy to add Uddalaka's skull in her garland of skulls; and also for this very realization of 'sarvam khalvidam brahma" , Uddalaka could tell his son "'tattvam asi". Only when such brahmopadesha is received by the disciple, it is only a question of time for the disciple to practice, and eventually to make the solid pathway in the brain, so that the mind becomes unaffected by the phenomenal happenings.

  

Secondly, we have to leave the non-believers alone. That is also the message given at the end of the Bhagavad Gita.  

  

Thirdly, for the Dvaitins, it is an uphill task to male up the mind to seek an advaitin guru. Shri Madhavacharya lost the golden opportuity in his life, inspite of his being lucky to have an Advaita guru. It is a pitythat he did not even know that his divine-brother Vayu-putra Hanumanji got the "tatvam asi" upadesha from Lord Ram himself". If only Shri Madhvacharya had trusted the wisdom of his divine brother Hanuam, in following the Advaita path.

  

Regards,
