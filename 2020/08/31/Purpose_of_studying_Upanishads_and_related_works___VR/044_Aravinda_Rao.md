Namaskars toall,

I may be pardoned for not having followed the whole discussion, but I saw a few postings. An interesting question is

'Advaita is beyond time, how?'

Advaitam is not merely the textual content which tells about Brahman, but it is Brahman, which is non-dual. It is not merely ekam, One, which may mean modern day monism, which is exclusivist, intolerantandimperialist. The upanishads carefully add the word advayam, not merely ekam. Advayam accepts all, respects all.

Ekam may or may not be advayam. 'My god is the only god', ekam may say. Advayam does not talk of god but of Reality, which is satyam,

jnanam and anantam.

Ekam may say, 'my god created the universe some six thousand years ago, but advayam goes beyond time. Advayam does not even

propose time but for practical purposes we say that the universe which is an appearance in Brahman (maya) is co-eval with

Brahman and hence beyond time. This is elaborated in secondary texts like puranas which describe cycles of time

which talk of billions of years.

Advayam asks theseeker to have sraddha, an earnest trust in the teacher so as to realize the goal. It has to be understood/realized/felt.

There is no compulsion if someone does not like it. Monism demands belief.

Aravinda Rao
