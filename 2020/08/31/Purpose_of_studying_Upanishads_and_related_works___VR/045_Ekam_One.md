Respected Gurutulya's

  

Thank you for all your replies. I would now pause my further questioning here. To be honest, I did not yet get the guidance i am looking for. Perhaps, this might be due to my shortcomings.  

  

I would however want to state couple of things,  

  

1\. Constructive questioning is not necessarily non-belief. Whole Upanishads are based on people questioning their Gurus. Arjuna Questioned Krishna. My initiation to Advaita was through Swami Vivekananda and he said thus.. *“**Do not believe a thing because you have read about it in a book. Do not believe a thing because another man has said it was true. Do not believe in words because they are hallowed by tradition. Find out the truth for yourself. Reason it out. That is realization.”***** So, please do not see my questions as non-believer's blabbering.  

  

2. I find it very hard to accept that only those very small percentage of people who are lucky enough to rigorously study multiple vedic scriptures (over multiple births) gain realization. The last reply stated, " *Ekam may or may not be advayam. 'My god is the only god', ekam may say. Advayam does not talk of god but of Reality, which is satyam, jnanam and anantam*." Isn't it strange that only those who study Vedic scriptures seem to progress to gain glimpses of that Reality which is anantam?  

  

Thank you all for your time. I have new insights now, if not answers. I have better resolve to inquire into my real nature. I would silently follow this list looking for more insights.

  

Finally, I close by humbly quoting Ramana Maharshi here  

*“You wake up in the morning and look into the mirror and the mirror shows you that you have a growth and that you have to get rid of it. You may go on looking into any number of mirrors; every mirror will tell you the same, but no mirror can ever shave you. You have to shave yourself. Instead of wasting time looking into mirror after mirror it is best to start shaving after having looked into the first mirror and known the truth.* *So also all the books will tell you the same truth, perhaps in slightly different ways. Instead of wasting time reading book after book why not realize for yourself what was obvious from the very first book.”*

Pranams
