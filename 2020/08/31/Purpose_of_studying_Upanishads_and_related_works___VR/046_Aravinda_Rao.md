Namaskars,

"Isn't it strange that only those who study Vedic scriptures seem to progress to gain glimpses of that Reality which is anantam?"

  

I do not know whether this charge is justified to this group which is guided by enlightened persons like Acharya Sadnanda, Dennis Waite, Sunil ji and others. If an impression is given that only those persons who are privileged to read the scriptures (by implication, belonging to a community) would attain enlightenment, it is certainly incorrect. An enlightened one goes beyond all distinctions; he becomes ati-varnasrami. It is applicable in the stage of sadhana too.

Aravinda Rao
