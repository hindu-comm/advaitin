Ekam,



The ‘bottom line’ is that reality is non-dual. So:



1.  ALL teaching must ultimately be mithyA. Non-duality cannot be
    described – end of story. 2.  ANY teaching that brings you to this realization must be valid. It
    just so happens that Advaita provides a comprehensive, systematic,
    proven methodology and I am not aware of anything that comes close
    to being as good as this.



It is unfortunate that your background is Vivekananda. His teaching is far from being traditional Advaita. Amongst other things, he denigrated scriptures and he advocated beliefs and practices that clearly belong to Yoga, which is a dualistic philosophy.



I am addressing all these ideas and problems in depth in the book that I am currently writing – ‘Dispelling Confusions – in Advaita Vedanta’. I have nearly completed Volume 1 ‘Knowledge, Experience and Enlightenment'. The book refers all of these problems back to traditional Advaita and supports the reasoning by quotations from prasthAna traya and Shankara. Unfortunately, it will not be available until the end of next year at the earliest (I haven’t even told my publisher about it yet!).



As regards your questions, the practices are ONLY for preparation. The scriptures are for providing Self-knowledge, which is the ONLY means for enlightenment. No amount of disciplines (including samAdhi!) will give enlightenment.



Best wishes,
