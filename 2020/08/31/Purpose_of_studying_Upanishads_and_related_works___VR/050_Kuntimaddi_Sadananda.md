Dennis - there is a story of Jadabhara who has paroksha jnaanam but did not realize the truth (aparokha jnaanam) due to his attachment to a deer that he had. He has to be born again but had his previous knowledge hence used to get over the lingering vaasanaas. There is also another example, I forgot his name, I think it is Vaamadeva, born but remained in the meditative state until he realized.

  

Shankara's disciple Hastalamalaka is another example. His parents thought he was born with mental problems and brought him to Shankara. Shankara realized that the boy was actually a jnaani. In response to Shankara's question of who are you, he pours out Vedanta. In essence, he had studied Vedanta under a teacher in his previous life.

  

For those who are discussing about Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi, we know that he was born in a family that was well versed in Shastras. He understood that the death of the body is not the death of the soul and tried to meditate on that. When he reached in Tiruvaanmayur, he knew what Sanyaasa means and he took himself by discarding his yagnopaveetam before he went inside the temple for extended meditation.  

  

It is better not to discuss individual experiences. If you think you are like Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi and think that you have learned the scriptures in the last life, these discussions have no meaning. It is better to go ahead and contemplate on the truth and see if you can realize the truth that you have learned in the last life. Our best wishes on your path.

  

These discussions are for preparing the mind to be doubt free ( manana)- as scriptures advise the seeker to do, Shravana and then manana and then nidhidhyaasana, contemplation on the truth.

  

Vedanta instructions are for normal seekers who recognized that the actions will not give eternal happiness, and advise them to approach a teacher who is well versed in Vedanta and also has realized - tat vijnaanaartham sa gurum eva abhigacchet samit paaNiH, Srotriyam brahma nishTam.

  

Notice 'gurum eva' - applied a competent teacher ONLY - in other words, do not go and study the scriptures on your own or sit down and start meditating 'just who am I'. Swami Paramarthanandaji defines a competent teacher as the one who himself studied under another competent teacher for a prolonged length or time under his mind is doubt free in his Upadeshasaar.

  

tat tvam asi - involves a clear understanding of 'tvam' who is that 'you' and also 'tht' - that - Brahman. Please studies Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi's texts Upadesha Saara or Sat Dashanam where he emphasized first preparation of the mind.

  

Krishna says the same thing too - tat viddhi praNipaatena pariprashneena sevayaa..

  

For the difference between jnaanam vs vijnaanam, Shankara states that the vRitti jnaanam will get dissolved in the recognition of the absolute. kRitvaa jnaanam swayam nasyet jalam kataka renuvat - The vRitti jnaanam gets dissolved in the recognition of the absolute just like KaTaka Nut powder used to clean the water. In the olden days, they sprinkle KaTaka Nut powder to purify water. It forms a sludge on the water and sinks to the bottom and in its dissent traps all the colloidal dust in the water, leaving the water free from the dust as well as KaTaka Nut powder.

  

Hari Om!

Sada
