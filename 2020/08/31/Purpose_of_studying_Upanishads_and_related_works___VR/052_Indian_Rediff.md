Hari Om and pranams to all,

  

From my understanding, in the case of people like Sri Ramana Maharishi or Nisargadatta Maharaj, it would seem that such examples have been through many lives before wherein they have progressed in the spiritual path to such an extent, that a mere (proverbial) push is sufficient to make them fully realised. This is the explanation that Sw Paramarthananda gives, while cautioning us to NOT take such exalted people as the rulem but more as exceptions. In any case, both of the examples (Sri Ramana Maharishi and Nisargadatta Maharaj) also refer to teachings from the scriptures while providing their own explanations.  

  

My understanding is that this search is a deeply personal one. No matter how many biographies one reads, and how many youtube lectures one listens to, no matter how many books of explanations one reads - unless one is directly connected with a guru, (a live, competent and traditional guru as Sw. Paramarthananda puts it) who can correct each individual in their individualistic mistakes, there is no way to directly comprehend Brahman and attain self-realization.

  

Sai
