Dear Sada,



Not sure what point you are making here. I was simply pointing out that the realization of Brahman entails no further rebirth, irrespective of whether any j~nAna phalam is gained in what remains of this life. This fact is stated in many places. E.g. Mundaka Upanishad 3.1.3: *“When the wise seer sees Brahman, which is ever effulgent like gold, which is the creator, which is the Lord, and which is the cause of hiraNyagarbha, then he wipes out totally his puNya-papa-s, and thus having become free from impurities attains total identity with Brahman”* and Shankara bhAShya on Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 4.4.6: *“Rather, this man of realization is Brahman in this very life, though he seems to have a body. Being but Brahman, he is merged in Brahman. Because he has no desires that cause the limitation of non-Brahmanhood, therefore ‘being but Brahman he is merged in Brahman’ in this very life, not after the body falls. A man of realization, after his death, has no change of condition – something different from what he was in life, but he is only not connected with another body. This is what is meant by becoming ‘merged in Brahman’; for if liberation was a change of condition, it would contradict the unity of the Self that all Upanishads seek to teach.”*

**

If someone is reborn (ignoring for the moment that no-one is ever born in the first place, of course), it can only mean that they did not fully gain Self-knowledge in the previous life, however ‘close’ they might have come to it.



Best wishes,

Dennis

These discussions are for preparing the mind to be doubt free ( manana)- as scriptures advise the seeker to do, Shravana and then manana and then nidhidhyaasana, contemplation on the truth.



Vedanta instructions are for normal seekers who recognized that the actions will not give eternal happiness, and advise them to approach a teacher who is well versed in Vedanta and also has realized - tat vijnaanaartham sa gurum eva abhigacchet samit paaNiH, Srotriyam brahma nishTam.



Notice 'gurum eva' - applied a competent teacher ONLY - in other words, do not go and study the scriptures on your own or sit down and start meditating 'just who am I'.
