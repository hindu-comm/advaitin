Here is my understanding.

  

The guru should be the one who can help in guiding the disciple. The implication is that he should be alive to do that.

  

One can get help from many sources. Scripture indicates that one has to approach a teacher and ask appropriate questions related to Vedanta. The Upanishads themselves are a reflection of such an approach.

  

You can ask the same question about a guide for doing a Ph.D. in a university. How does the University respond?
