I am only differentiating between jnaanam vs vijnanaam or paroksha jnaanam vs aparoksha jnaanam. Nidhidhyaasana is prescribed for those who have literally understood Vedanta but not abole to abide in that knowledge due to remnant vaasanaas. They are jnaani's but not vijnaanis.

  

The 7th Ch. of Geeta is jnaana-vijnaana yoga - to differentiate the two.

  

Many feel that they understand Vedanta but have not realized. It is with reference to that, FIR reduction was stated by Swami Paramarthanandaji. That is how I was addressing the problem. Vidyaranya differentiates different types of jnaanees - all on the basis of how much of FIR has been reduced - since from the absolute point there is no differentiation of any kind.

  

Hope I am clear.
