Dear Sri Ekam,

  

I thought you started a nice thread. And thought, as expected, the great Advaitin scholars in this group have provided excellent answers. I am one of many who learnt a lot from these scholars.

  

However, this last post of yours piqued my interest - actually puzzled me. I wonder why the answers were not clear to you.

  

I would like to say few words on your query. Not that I can add any great insights to you more than what is already stated and what is already known.

  

You asked about Upanishads and related works. Not sure what you meant by related works.

  

**Upanishads** are "*sravana*" granthas - Informative Scriptures. They just state what the truth is. For an Advaitin it is simply stating that substantially you are same as every thing else around you. It is just that - Information. Once you have that, there is no need to study Upanishads any further.

  

But the informative knowledge needs to become Intellectual knowledge. One needs to develop conviction about this seemingly impossible equation. For that one needs to study **Brahma Sutram** or any of many ***prakarana granthas*** that great scholars have written. There should not be any doubt. Thus these are called *manana granthas.*

  

This is still not very useful knowledge other than for our erudition.

  

Habits die hard. Who does not know lying is bad, but we still lie. There is no lack of knowledge about ill effects of lying. But it is the propelling habit that stops the intellectual knowledge to become intrinsic knowledge - to put it differently - experiential knowledge - though the word experience is not a proper word. This is because, though we have got doubtless knowledge after sravanam and mananam, we still have not become qualified - adhikaratvam - to make that our own knowledge. Here is where your lifestyle becomes important. To put in scriptural jargon - karma yoga lifestyle.

  

That is where**, Bhagavad Gita** comes into picture. Normally wise say **Bhagavad Gita** is *nidhidhaysa grantha.*Reflective scripture. It gives importance to proper action and proper lifestyle to qualify to make intellectual into intrinsic. I still feel that Bhagavad Gita is still a manana grantham. It gives a better appreciation of what is stated in Upanishad along with theory of Karma Yoga. But It does not force you to lead yogic life style. We are still depend on our will power to do the right thing.

  

Though it is not absolutely a must, **saguna iswara bhakthi** is only way for majority of people to develop a qualification for leading karma yoga life style which in turn will qualify you to convert what Upanishad informed to your own knowledge. Here is where scriptures like Bhagavatam, Ramayanam etc come to our rescue.

  

This step wise qualification may take any amount of time as Sri Sadananda ji has stated. Habits die hard. Habits formed over many births are even more difficult to rid off.

  

**So the purpose of Upanishad is to inform you that you are IT**. Depending on your level of qualification it will decide how much useful it is to you personally.

  

You quoted Vivekananda regarding "bookish" knowledge. By stating Upanishad no one means it as a bookish knowledge. What it means is learning from a qualified teacher. The process of Intellectualizing the information involves questioning the very teaching and information, albeit with respect. That is what you do during *mananam* phase.

  

If information is what you need, then you just have to know the statement ***tat tvam asi***. You do not need to read many upanishads. However each upanishad approaches this information from different angle and finally comes to the same place. Studying multiple upanishads give us a better appreciation of the information. But if you want to become a teacher (even if you do not become a j~Anani) then it is important to study many upanishads and other granthas as you will be forced to explain and answers you get and quote them so that the student knows that it is not your brain wave but proper Vedantic knowledge. But to recognize the infinite you do not need to read all these scriptures. You just need the truth and you just have to have proper qualifications to make it **your own**reality. How you get your qualification is up to you.

  

This is what I want to convey.

  

To all the teachers who contributed to this thread, I apologize for regurgitating again what I learnt from you all.

  

praNamams
