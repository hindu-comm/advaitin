What happens if the guru is a fool or charlatan? Sri Ramakrishna told his disciple Naren 'Put me through the acid test!'. Same with the Buddha - do not rely on beliefs or hearsay but put the teacher and teachings to test in the fire of discrimination and reason.  

  

I have high regards for Ramana Maharishi perhaps a unique, pure and unworldly specimen radiating peace but that does not necessarily make him a Gnani. He was more of a secluded yogi who was cajoled to enter an ashram riddled with quarrels and conflicts, gas lighted by his own brother at Ramanasram. In the final days his body suffered immensely.
