We hardly know about the life of Vyas, Janak, Yajnavalkya or Maitreyi. There are no records of their everyday life.

  

On the other hand, authentic records of the lives of Ramakrishna and Ramana are available. Ramana lived in isolation for a long period but he did mix with the seekers, mostly without speaking.

  

It is not right to expect the outward expression of the Gnanis same for all. Each plays a role according to his nature. I prefer to perceive a unity among all the Gnanis.
