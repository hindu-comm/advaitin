Namaskars,

  

I agree with Ekam on his/her point. Even the upanishads do not say that the vedic scriptures alone (in Sanskrit, with all the intonations etc.,) have to be read. The knowledge of the upanishads, in whatever language, but under a realized teacher, will guide the student to realization. All the intellectual exercise is redundant. The sages are always kind and they accept all human beings.

  

As Mr. Dennis Waite has pointed out, Advaita provides a good framework to a seeker for realization. Here, realization means going beyond

all delimiting identities of caste, religion etc., and seeing thedivine in all beings.

Aravinda Rao
