Hare Krishna



As a matter of fact though yoga shAstra is dualistic in its doctrine, yOga is an integral part of Advaita sAdhana, experiencing the samAdi and various methods adopted by different experiencers to come out of this samAdhi would decide whether he is vara, vareeya or varishTa brahma vida ( the knower of brahman rather experiencer of brahman). Some prakaraNa like VC, JMV, panchadashi etc. have given special emphasis on this aspect of yOga. And some traditional renowned Acharya-s stresses the practical experience of yOga samAdhi which is theoretically explained in shAstra / vedAnta / shruti.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!
