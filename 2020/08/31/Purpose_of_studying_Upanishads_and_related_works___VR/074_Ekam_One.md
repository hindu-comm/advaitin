1\. With all the regular disclaimers and my reverence for Veda's, its hard to accept the following statement.

  

**"If the Vedas were not there, Brahman cannot be known".**

  

its is of course merely academic discussion for this group, as everyone in this group, including me have taken the Vedic path for realization.  

  

2. I am not sure if Mind and Manas are the same. When i said "Mind", it is the intellectual and analytical aspects of me which find (egoic) satisfaction in gathering more information, reading more books and continuously connecting dots between different scriptures.

 May be they translate into the *buddhi and ahankara.* Whereas Manas and Chitta are the faculties that also drop off at the time of realization. (मनो बुद्ध्यहंकारचित्तानि नाहम्. *mano buddhi ahankara chittani naaham* )
