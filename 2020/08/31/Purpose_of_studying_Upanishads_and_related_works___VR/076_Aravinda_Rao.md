**"If the Vedas were not there, Brahman cannot be known".**

  

An interesting point.

  

If by Brahman, we mean that all we see is Brahman and that realization is to realize divinity in all beings; if by Brahman we mean

respect and acceptance of all; then we can surely assert -**"If the Vedas were not there, Brahman cannot be known".**

**  **

In the entire human knowledge we are not aware of any other texts which say that you are a divine being. The most powerful and

dominant texts are the religious texts which say that unless you follow that religion, there is no salvation, one cannot go to heaven etc.,

They do not stop there, they try to convert me by all vily means, some others put a sword on my neck. This is not done by the

Vedas/Upanishads.

As Ekam is in the Vedic path, he/she may not be offended by my factual reference to the other books and their teachings.

Aravinda Rao
