Here is my understanding.

  

Nidhidhyaasana involves uplifting the mind and contemplating on the truth that has established by studying Vedanta under a teacher. Hence there is no specific book for nidhidhyaasana. One can study and discuss all the Vedantic texts, with not for learning but for contemplating on the truth. Nidhidhyaasana can be in the form of study, in the form of discussions, in the form of writings, in the form of pravachans, in the form of sitting down and meditating on the truth; any or all of the above depending on the capacities of the BMI (body, mind, and intellect).

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
