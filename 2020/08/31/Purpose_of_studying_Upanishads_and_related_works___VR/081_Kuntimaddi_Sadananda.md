PraNAms

  

The word 'Science' is derived from the root 'Scier' meaning 'to know' and Science means 'knowledge'. In Sanskrit 'vid' means to know and Veda means knowledge. Hence Veda is Science. Vedanta is the ultimate Science since it includes analysis of both the subject 'I' and the object 'this', while all other objective sciences refer word, Science, only to objectifiable entities using objective tools. Hence it is incomplete, at best.

  

When our Scientists of the yore contemplated on the absolute truth in the seat of meditation, the knowledge - Veda - that is ever-present was revealed to them. Hence these scientists are called Rishis or Veda draShTaas.

  

In essence, there was never a time Vedas are not there. Revelations happen only when one is contemplating using the mind in that direction. Revelations take place in the mind of the contemplating scientists only. This is true even for an objective Scientist. He may claim that he made a breakthrough or discovered the truth. Gravitational force is there before Newton 'discovered' it - he removed the cover and expressed in the language that can be understood.

As per Vedanta -Knowledge is eternal - only ignorance appears to cover the knowledge (aavarana), and one has to dis-cover using vRitti jnaanam - hence a teacher is needed to help.

  

About the mano buddhi .. sloka - who says that - It is again the mind only. When Bhagavan Ramana says - maanasantu kim maaragane kRite, naiva maanasam, maara aarjavat' -What is the mind if one starts inquiring, then one finds there is no mind at all - The sloka also has to be understood correctly - How is one going to inquire about 'what is the mind and the mind disappears' - it is again the mind only. This is where some of the teachings of Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi have to be understood correctly.
