Dear friends ,

  

If Ekamji has found it difficult to understand what is the Advaita path, he need not worry. It takes time to understand things, just like it takes nine months for the mother to deliver the child. I don't know if he has any science background. With science background one can understand Advaita easily. The world came to be with the Big Bang and the world would disappear in due course and then a new cycle will come, but there is something that is continuous through the appearance and disappearance of the universe and that is what is the permanent entity and all else in impermanent. In the Uddhava Gita Lord Krishna has explained the Advaita in a similar fashion. Unfortunately the Ajivika verse was removed from the Bhagavad Gita by Bhaskara Bhatta about 1200 years ago. So in the commonly available Bhagavad gita one would not get the verse. But one can surely read the Uddhava Gita and understand Advaita, if one finds it difficult to understand by other means.  

  

With best wishes,
