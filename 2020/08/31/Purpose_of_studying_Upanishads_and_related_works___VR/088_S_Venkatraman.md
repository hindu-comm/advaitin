Namaste Ramanji,

  

That ’Sarva deva namaskarah Yeshum prati gachhati’ cannot be the original can very easily be established. The whole shloka

  

आकाशात् पतितं तोयं यथा गच्छति सागरम् ।  
सर्वदेवनमस्कारः केशवं प्रति गच्छति ॥

  

is in Anushtub metre with each of the 4padas having 8 akshras. If yousubstituted‘Keshavam’ with‘Yeshum’, the 4th Pada will have only 7 aksharas. So that cannot be the original. But your overall point is well taken because there can be other cases where the error cannot be so easily pointed out.

  

Regards,

Venkat
