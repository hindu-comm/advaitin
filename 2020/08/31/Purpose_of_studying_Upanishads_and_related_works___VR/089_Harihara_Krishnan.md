The answer to the query lies in the Sruti.

tametam vedaanuvachanenabraahmana vividishanti(Brhadaranyaka Upanishad 4.4.22). =\>\> The braahmanas realise Him through study of the scriptures.

svadhyayapravachanabhyam na pramaditavyam(Taittiriya Upanishad 1.11.1) =\>\> Do not be negligent in the study and recitation of the Veda.

A humble question, with earnest respect to our Ancient Scriptures and Gurus. I am studying them for a while now. This question has been bothering me for a while.  

  
**What is the purpose of thoroughly studying various such works and their references?  **

**  **

With reverence and studious efforts, one may firmly come to grasp the underlying essence of the shastra's. What then?
