Dear Venkataramanji,

  

Sorry, the chapter in the Uddhava Gita I was referring to is 22 and not 11. While the whole chapter is on Advaita, I would like to emphasize on the following seven verses in the 22nd chapter. These are the verse nos. 9**,**12**,**13**,**15**,**16**,**17 and 22.  

  

***The verse 22.21 of the Uddhava Gita is very similar to the Ajativada verse of the Original Bhagavad Gita,** but is missing from the commonly availabale version*. In this verse Lord Krishna is saying that ***what did not exist before creation and will not exist after annihilation, has no real existence during the manifestation, in between, but only a superficial manifestation.***
