**Advaitin List Progress Report and Administrative Details**

Dear fellow members:  

Originally, the list started its service during June 1998 at the [coollist.com](http://coollist.com). Then on 31 August1998, Advaitin List moved to [onelist.com](http://onelist.com) which became [egroups.com](http://egroups.com). Finally [egroups.com](http://egroups.com) was taken over by the [yahoogroups.com](http://yahoogroups.com). The membership grew from 20 when it started to over 1984 at present. As always, this list is open to all. Membership is voluntary and non members can also view all the postings available at the public archive [https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin)

Currently the list site at[https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin) contains the archived messages from now on. We plan to store the archived messages from the Yahoogroups to a Google Site which will hopefully allow members to download them. Google continuously updating their homepage and may require members to pay more careful attention to learn the new features to access files, messages and search routines.
We advise you to use their Help feature to learn and make use of all available features  

There are notable differences between how these two Giants (Google and Yahoo) manage the mailing list groups. At Yahoo, the membership was counted by subscription based on email address and not by individuals.
At Google the membership is based on by person and not by email ID.
When advaitin was under Yahoo, the number of members were reported as 2645 where as at Google the numbers are reported as 1944. At Google, if the person has more than one email ID then Google uses only the primary email ID and count the membership accordingly. This may explain the discrepancy between 2645 and 1944. Yahoo mailing system probably failed because their system failed to distribute the mails for those with multiple yahoo email ids. We are happy to be at Google and we received no complaints about failure to receive messages from here.

**More on List Moderation and Members Responsibility:**

During the past twenty two plus years moderators have exercised this extraordinary privilege only very few times under the following situations:(1) when a member diverts the discussions by focusing on the personalities instead of the subject matter;(2) when a member repeatedly violates list guidelines;(3) when a member makes insulting remarks on another poster or religious texts;(4) when discussions get diverted and dragged away from the subject matter and(5) when two members keep on repeatedly pounding their side of the argumentto such an extent that very soon it becomes a confrontation of two egos. Currently the members enjoy the privilege to post all messages that are relevant to the subject matter of Vedanta.   

It should be pointed out that the list guidelines have been updated by incorporating constructive suggestions from the members. In general members should polish their language and focus on the subject matter and exhibit scholarship with humility. One of the best ways to control anger, frustrations and arrogance is by restricting as much as possible to post a maximum of 2 posts (list guidelines stipulate only 2 posts per day!) and we expect members to follow this honor code.Words inspire. Words bind too. Words are like a boat which we must use to cross a river. We must leave the boat and walk to the other shore. Attachment to verbosity has held back many a spiritual seeker from higher possibilities. The Upanishad says in Mantra 15: "When covered by the power of words (sound) one does not know the Self (called the sky or space here) being enveloped in darkness. When the darkness (ignorance) is destroyed, one sees the oneness, being the One". We earnestly request the members to take more time to contemplate before exhibiting their reactions
