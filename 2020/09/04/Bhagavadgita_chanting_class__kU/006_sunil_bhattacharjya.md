This is further to my earlier mail on the subject. The Bhagavad Gita Jayanti is on the Guruvayur Ekadashi day on November 25, 2020, Wednesday. The Guruvayur temple, which claims to be five thousand (5.000) years old, has been observing this Ekadashi day as the most special Ekadashi day and this is also known as the Kartika Shukla Ekadashi or the Prabodhini Ekadashi or Awakening Ekadashi. This is the day when Lord Krishna gave the Bhagavad Gita Upadesha to awaken the confused Arjuna, so that the latter would not hesitate to fight the Mahabharata war. The mahabharata calls the Bhagavad Gita as "Sarvashastramayee".
