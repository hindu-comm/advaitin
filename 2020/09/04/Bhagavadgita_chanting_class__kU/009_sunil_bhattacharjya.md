Dear Sunderji,

  

Less said the better about the works of Nilesh Oak. Firstly, he does not even know the four yugas and their time-spans. The Mahabharata war took place 36 years and a few months, before the start of the Kali yuga in February, 3102 BCE. As regards the Arundhati-Vashishth episode, he was absolutely wrong as he did not even know the relative motion of the Saptarshis, as compared to the movement of Earth in the CNP orbit. You may not be aware that his claims have been fully blasted in this current year.  

  

As regards the views of the Shringeri math, the Swamiji leaves all these calendrical and historical matters to the administrative staff to decide, and i am very sorry to have to tell you that they are very incompetent. Advaita is the quest for the Eternal Truth, but that does not mean that one can ignore the Phenomenal truth. If the phenomenal truth can be ignored, Lord Krishna would not have appeared on the earth. and the Lord would not have asked Arjuna to fight. It is a pity that the Shringeri math does not know even when Adi Shankara was born. I pray to Lord Krishna that he would help the administrative staff of the Shringeri math to open their eye to see the truth that Adi Shankara was born in 509 BCE. The Dwarka math has the copper plate inscription of Sudhanva, but the the Archaeological Survey of India took it away in the fag end of the 19th century CE, through the Dewan of the Jamnagar, ostensibly to examine that, but very soon after that the Dewan suddenly died and the Math could not get the inscription back. The Dwarka math has the copy of the inscription, but the Shringeri math thinks that Dwarka math has been lying. All the maths, other than the Shringeri math, have their guru-parampara list, right from the time when Adi Shankaracharya esyablished the Maths in the 5th century BCE.

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
