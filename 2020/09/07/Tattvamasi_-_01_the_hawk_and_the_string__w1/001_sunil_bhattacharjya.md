Even in the Fifth Veda-text, the "Brahmanda Purana", Lord Ram gave the "tattvanasi" upadesha to Shri Hanuman, Lord Ram also gave the "tattvamasi' upadesha to Shri Hanuman i another upanishada. As Shri Madhavacharya was not so well-read, he made the simple mistake in interpreting the Chandogya verse as "atatattvanamasi". Unfortunately his advatin guru was also not so well-read to correct his disciple, Madhacharya. The so-called scholarly disciples of Madhvacharya were also utterly ignorant of this fact that Madhavacharya made a Himalayam Blunder.
