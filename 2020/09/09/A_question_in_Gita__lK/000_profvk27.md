A question on the meaning of two shlokas in the Gita

The relevant texts are मद्भावमागताः in IV-10 and मम साधर्म्यमागताः in XIV
-2.

There are usually four types of mukti talked about in the shastras: namely, sAlokyam, sAmIpyaM (or sAdRRishyaM), sArUpyaM and sAyujyaM. These four are obtained by saguNa upAsanA. By nirguNa jnAna one obtains moksha or parama nirvANaM. Now in the above two shlokas which of these is meant?
