स्फुरन्ति सीकरा यस्मादानन्दस्याम्बरेऽवनौ । सर्वेषां जीवनं तस्मै ब्रह्मानन्दात्मने नमः ॥\>

This is the third shloka of the three shlokasof Yogavashishtha Maharamayana recited at the very beginning of that work as a sort of benediction for its completion without any impediments .

The eternal Brahman though ''अनिर्वचनीय''has been praised and the word by word meaning will be clear from the Tatparya-Prakasha commentary of Ananda Bodha Yati as attachment.

Also attached is a Hindi translation of these three shlokas.I hope this will serve your purpose.

If you must have an English translation also, I shall try to provide the same.

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!
