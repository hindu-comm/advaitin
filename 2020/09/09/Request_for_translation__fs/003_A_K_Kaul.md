> स्फुरन्ति सीकरा यस्मादानन्दस्याम्बरेऽवनौ । सर्वेषां जीवनं तस्मै ब्रह्मानन्दात्मने नमः ॥
>
>   
>
> स्फुरन्ति flow सीकरा dews (drops) यस्माद् from whomआनन्दस्य of delight अम्बरे
> (both in) heaven (and) अवनौ earth। सर्वेषां of all जीवनं life तस्मै to Him
> ब्रह्मानन्द Supreme Bliss आत्मने personified नमः salutations॥  

Salutations to Him (who is) supreme bliss itself, from whom flow the dews of delight (as water springs from a fountain) both in heaven and earth, and who is the life of all. (Translation of Vihari-Lala Mitra)

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!
