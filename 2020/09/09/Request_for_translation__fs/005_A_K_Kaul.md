Shri Solai Kananji,

Jai Shri Ram!

\< 1. अम्बरे - does this mean sky or clouds; or does it mean heaven? I feel it would be sky or clouds rather than heaven.\>

You can interpret it any way you want to, though heaven is more appropriate here.

\<2. Whether the meaning is "both in heaven and earth" or "it pours from sky to earth"?\>

Both in heaven and earth.

\< 3. नमः we all know it means salutation or obeisance.\>

It is in the same sense that it has been used here.

\<Please, clarify whether my understanding is acceptable. Please forgive me as I have no knowledge in sanskrit.\>

I suggest you try to learn Samskrit for understaing the real sense of such shlokas.

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!

A K Kaul

After you have started learning Samskrit you can ponder on the commentary on these shlokas by Ananda Bodha Yati that I had attached with my earlier mail.
