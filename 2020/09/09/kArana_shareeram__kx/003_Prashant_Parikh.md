Namaste,  
  
Yes, vāsanās would manifest / sprout in the sūkśma śarīra in the form of vr̥ttis, but their source would be in bīja rūpa in the kāraṇa śarīra.  
  
Now, connected to the above, I have two interrelated question between the correlation of vāsanā with karma, and vāsanā and jñāna if someone may confirm either through śāstra pramaṇa / guru upadeśa.  
  

**Q1**: Premise: As I understand, every āgāmi karma performed sows the seed of both a karma phala, and leaves behind a vāsanā/saṁskāra.  
Could we relate the vāsanā generated through an action as an immediate karma phala that takes place at the moment of the action? My presumption would be 'yes', because karma generates four types of results- āpyam, utpādyam, vikāryam, saṁskāryam. My understanding is there is some change that takes place in one's sūkśma śarīra (specifically manaḥ), wherein a vāsanā created has carved a gulf of sorts down which vr̥ttis more easily flow, therefore a modification to the manaḥ, being the result of a karma, is what we give the name a vāsanā to.

  

**Q2**. Premise: The above takes me down an additional line of speculation which again requires pramāṇa confirmation  
When we attain jñāna of some viśaya, avidyā of that viśaya has been removed. We also understand that jñāna is 'actionless'.

What is the correlation between gaining the jñāna of some viśaya and the corresponding shifts in our actions through destruction of a vāsanā?

Let me cite a real life example. Living in the US about 15 years ago I used to consume 'yoplait' yogurt which I enjoyed. Upon discovering it contains gelatin, which is a meat product, I immediately had a distaste for it.... earlier we saw that performance of karma can generate new vāsanās, what is the mechanism between the gaining of jñāna and the elimination of the vāsanā?

  

Dhanyavādaḥ!  
Praśānt
