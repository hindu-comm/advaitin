Quote (full version of above)

  

When jnana is attained, the causal body is destroyed or burnt out. The jiva has attained mukti. However the gross and subtle bodies continue due to momentum of prarabdha; and the rest of us continue to identify the "jnani" as the jiva because we see the shell operating as before. In truth, the "person" is no more though it appears like he is the same; the particular consciousness that we perceive from the body-mind no longer is of the "person" - i.e. what constitutes a jiva prior to realization. That realization which has liberated the jiva however continues to reflect (till the final dissolution of the remaining bodies) in the body-mind as the Knowledge of Brahman alongside consciousness of nama-rupa.
