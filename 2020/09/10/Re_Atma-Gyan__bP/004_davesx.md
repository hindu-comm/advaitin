From the standpoint of Paramartha or radical Ajata Advaita, the Infinite is not divided and cannot ever be divided. Therefore the evanescent finite is actually the Infinite Self-Awareness expressing as if divisible through imagination alone.

  

As such the everchanging known is actually the everpresent incomprehensible Brahman in its temporal formulation continually being refreshed as apparent lifeforms without beginning or end. The appearance of birth and death are as such Aja - the Unborn, the uncreated, the uncaused.  

  

So yes it may seem like different perspectives but paradoxically there are no perspectives and no separate individual self to have a perspective.

  

Is the issue clearer or more confusing?
