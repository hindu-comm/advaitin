Thank you Mr Dennis for the quote of Swami Dayananda. Given that what he says is true ( it must be valid for a majority), how do we reconcile the verse from Dakshinamurthy Stotram - Gurostu Maunam Vyakhyanam ... ?

  

I think the position is as follows:

  

For a vast majority of the seekers, words are necessary for the communication from the Guru to the disciple. In the rarest case, an advanced seeker meets a Guru like Ramana and in the silence, the transmission takes place. Since such an occurrence is extremely rare, it is not much mentioned in the scriptures.

  

Can we discount the possibility of a spontaneous samadhi which results into the Jnana?

  

May be, we are missing some points because such cases are ignored in the shastras and also because we lay more emphasis on the shastras compared to the life of a Jnani.

  

My most humble views !
