Namaste,

  

I am with Dennis that silence is a tool perhaps to understand the nature of the Self but not a mode of teaching in itself. Without words, the Atman cannot be known. Shankara recounts a dialogue from the tradition between Baskali and Badhva (brahma sutra bhashya 3.2.17) . In this dialogue, silence is used as a tool to communicate the nature of Brahman through negation. Shankara’s position is definitely not that silence is the way to teach Brahman.

  

As with people who gained mukti in the presence a silent teacher, it could be force of knowledge that cannot be stopped due to sadhana-s from past lives (refer to Brahma Sutra Bhashya 1.3.38). It is a case of mistaking the light switch (silence) to be the cause of switching on the light tube, when it was actually electricity (jnana) that did the job. :)

  

Kathirasan K
