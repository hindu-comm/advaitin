When the Supreme is beyond all the laws, all the causation, how valid is it to exclude the possibility of silence working on the disciple for his awakening ? If you deny this possibility, it means you are creating a rule which must be followed by the Supreme. The rule is -“ Silence will not work.”

  

Granted the possibility is remote and yet when we accept it, by implication, we also accept the truth that the Supreme is beyond all the laws.
