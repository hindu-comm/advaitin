Well said....The of silence of Dakshinamurti is for mature student in the final stages. Aspirants with questions require discussion with the preceptor to clear up doubts and confusion. Only after inquiry, contemplation and reasoning (manana मनन) are they prepared for that which is wordless - Silence.

  

As such I maintain that Ramana Maharishi was more a seasoned yogi though he was able to skillfully blend in aspects of gyana yoga such as introducing vichara (who am I?) and moving away from Nirvikalpa samadhi (deep sleep) to open eye Sahaj.
