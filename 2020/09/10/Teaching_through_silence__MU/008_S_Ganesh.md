Who requires the teaching? Having assumed bondage, it is the false I which is seeking freedom! I can understand both sides point of view about Ramana.

Swamy Paramarthananda has himself said that one can come to know by himself by logical analysis that he is not the body mind complex! The question will be who am I? But having negated the body mind complex I can remain as mere Sakshi not wanting answers for a false mind! He remained as mere witness established in his swaroopa!

We say he had Poorvajanma qualifications etc hence an exception! But the beauty of the tradition is that it has an evolved methodology To produce gnanis provided they are fit!
