About this word “Silence”, this is what Guru Swami Paramarthananda Saraswati has mentioned in his discourse on Dakshinamurthy Sthothram in the first talk.

  

I am paraphrasing it here. “***Silence can’t be a method of teaching. No teacher can teach through Silence if you take the literal meaning of the word. Even if one assumes that it is taught, each student will receive it differently. Regarding Dakshinamurthy teaching through Mounam means that the Guru is silent about “Brahman” but using other inferential means talking about other things but not mentioning Brahman directly, the Brahman is taught and known. Similar to Neti Neti teaching.***”

  

Swami gives quite an elaborate explanation in his talk. One can listen to his discourses.

  

Regards.
