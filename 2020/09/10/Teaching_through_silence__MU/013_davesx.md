How I read this is that Dakshinamurti is pointing out that silence is the final hallmark, the threshold of the mind. That which is beyond, mind cannot reach or apprehend.  

  

So after admitting the mind cannot know the Infinite Brahman, in hopeless despair, in utter humility mind falls silent.  

  

Silence is therefore final portal, an open receptivity conducive for the strike of lightening of Self-Awareness to whom the Self chooses - to quote both the Kena and Katha Upanishads.  

  

By the same token Self-Awareness can be aware of silence/ stillness or sounds/movement.
