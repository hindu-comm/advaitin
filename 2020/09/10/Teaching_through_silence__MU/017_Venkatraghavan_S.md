Namaste,

I am not a scholar by any means, but can provide my understanding of this mantra. The direct translation of the mantra is:

  

"Its teaching - It is like the flash of lightning and the blinking of the eye -this is the adhidaiva, divine (upAsana) of Brahman.

  

shankarAchArya says in his pada bhAShyam that the comparison with lightning is to indicate that Brahman, like the flash of lightning and the blinking of the eye, appeared and disappeared in a flash in front of the devatAs in the story outlined in the kenopaniShad.

  

In the vAkya bhAShya, shankarAchArya says that the upAsana of Brahman must be performed thinking of Him like a flash of lightning, because like the flash of lightning illuminates everything, Brahman's nature is as the illuminator of all. When Indra tried to approach Brahman, He withdrew Himself like the closing of the eye.

  

In the subcommentary to the padabhAShya by Anandagiri AchArya, it is said that the example of the blink of an eye is to indicate how Brahman creates the universe instantly, without any impediment or effort. The example of lightning is to indicate that just like lightning illuminates everything simultaneously, Brahman whose nature is illumination, and who is endowed with the Highest aishvarya, overlordship, simultaneously creates everything in the universe.

  

In the subcommentary to the vAkyabhAShya, Anandagiri AchArya writes that the example of lightning is to indicate Brahman's nature as the illuminator of all, and the blinking of the eye is to indicate that Brahman is not easily understood, even by the gods.

  

In summary, while there are slightly varying interpretations in the various commentaries, their essence is that this is a section dealing with the upAsana of Brahman, where one meditates upon Brahman using certain symbols (lightning and the blinking of the eye) on account of their similarity with certain aspects of Brahman's nature (universal illumination, difficulty of being cognised, effortless, instant creation etc).

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
