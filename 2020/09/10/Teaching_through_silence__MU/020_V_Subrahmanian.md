The Lalita Sahasra nama - taDillataasamaruchih also means the same She is of the splendor of a lightning streak. Bhaskararaya cites /vidyullekheva bhaasvaraa/ of the Narayana suktam.

  

All this is fine since it is Upasana context. Brahman, though, is not physical splendor.
