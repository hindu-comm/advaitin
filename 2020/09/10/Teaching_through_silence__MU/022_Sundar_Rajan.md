\>\> hence the futility of Yogis to achieve it as Patanjali exhorts yogic practitioners to suppress thought in his famous aphorism 'yoga chitta vritti nirodha'.  

  

There is nothing futile about yogic efforts.

  

Calling it 'futile' is like a person used to riding a bicycle, sweating, panting and laboring generalizing that experience to all transport and calling drivingin a self driven, air-conditioned and superlative sound equipped Tesla as 'tiresome'. It is not.

  

Swami Vivekananda says inreal meditation you forget the body, you feel such pleasure in it (ananada). Doesn't sound like 'futile' to me.

  

From a Vedantic perspective, Sri Ramana Maharishi (see Talks with Sri Ramana) has referred to*sphurana*as the foretaste of Realizationand at a minimum provides the impetus to keep on the path.
