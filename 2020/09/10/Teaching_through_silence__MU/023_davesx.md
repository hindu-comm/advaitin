I don't believe the primary focus of this forum is on Patanjali yoga but rather on Gyana (Jnana)...or if it is I'd rather move on.

  

May I repeat neither Swami Vivekananda nor Ramana Maharishi are considered Jnanis in my book.

  

Meditation to forget the world and body is certainly not recommended for the students of Gyana.

  

When the world is an expression of Brahman, there is no need for seclusion and to reject the world. Such a practice is for sleeping yogis in caves, an avoidance strategy to circumvent investigation into the world.
