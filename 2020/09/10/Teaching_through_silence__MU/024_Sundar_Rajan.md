Patanjali Yoga and Jnana are not mutually exclusive. Read the archives on this subject several years ago from Vidyashankar-ji etc

  

Meditationis not to forget the world, rather a complementary practice conducive to Shravana, Manana etc. One aspect of Nidhidhyasanam is deep intense concentrationnot very differentfrom the higher meditations in Patanjali. Nobody is advocating seclusionor rejecting the world. I can do my meditation in my Tesla, get out and go to work :-)

  

How can experiencingAnanda and experiencing one is not the body be detrimental to any Shravana,Manana etc. The whole point of Vichara is to realize one is not the body, mind complex.

  

If anything a sessionof deep meditation will make it a lot easier to grasp and gain insight on Vedantic texts.

  

While one may be aware of the existence (Sat) aspect and somewhat of Chit (Consciousness), Meditation undoubtedly provides awareness (or possibility) of the Ananda aspect.

  

sukham ātyantikaḿ yat tad  
buddhi-grāhyam atīndriyam  
vetti yatra na caivāyaḿ  
sthitaś calati tattvataḥ

(Gita 6:20..)
