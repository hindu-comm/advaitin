\>\>Chapter 6 deals with meditation, it states "Yoga is for purification". This means it is not for truth but discipline.

\>\>There are three foundational texts recommended for studying Gyana (a) BrihadAranyaka Upanishad, (b) Panchadasi (c) Ashtavakra Gita.

  

``` Here is Sankara's commentary on BSBh 2.3.39(samAdhy abhAvAc ca) Atma-pratipatti-prayojanas samAdhir upadishTo vedAnteshu In the vedAnta texts (vedAnteshu), samAdhi is taught (upadishTaH) as an aid to realize (pratipatti prayojanaH) the Self (Atman). ```

``` Further Sankara quotes the  Upanishads sentences as teaching samAdhi are ```

``` AtmA vA are drashTavyaS Srotavyo mantavyo nididhyAsitavyaH - bRhadAraNyaka 2.4.5 ```

  

You can see Sankara connects Shravana, manana, Nidhidhyasana directly to the Brihad Upanishad passage related to Samadhi
