Sundarji - The only Samadhi of value in Gyana is Sahaj Samadhi; the natural state. The others are unnatural - mind control, mind manipulated, mind suppression states that mislead the aspirant into another trance, a hypnos, stupor, unconsciousness or deep sleep. As stated, at most they may be preliminary but yogis get stuck in this trance state believing it to be Brahman. How can it be equated as Brahman when the world is excluded in Nirvikalpa? The third part of the equation is Brahman = World.
