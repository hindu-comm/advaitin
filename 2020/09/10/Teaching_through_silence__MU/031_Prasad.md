My two cents.

  

Going around "judging" others, let alone sampradayavit sanyasis and otherinspiring renunciates, doesn't seem the correct thing to do. Forums such as these exist for the upliftment of oneself or clarifying doubts from learned ones here. Now of course if a popular teacher is downright abusive to his group of disciples, feel free to warn others who may throng to such a teacher. But this kind of 'jnani-busting' of respectedpersonalities who lived well within the bounds dictated by Shastra, seems absurd and uncalled for.

  

Moderators should really take note; otherwise this forum like many will go down in personality bashing.
