Advaitin List Policies and Posting Guidelines:

Members of the advaitin list are expected follow the Yahoogroup Guidelines which are available at: [http://groups.yahoo.com/local/guidelines.html](http://groups.yahoo.com/local/guidelines.html)
It is also the responsibility of each member to familiarize themselves with the advaitin list policies and the guidelines for posting.

Members are responsible for the content of their messages and the acceptance of any message posted should not be considered as an endorsement by the moderators. The purpose of the Advaitin list is to facilitate discussions on Advaita as established by Shankara and carried on by succeeding traditions of teachers, with principal reference to the Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita and the Brahmasutras. Questions or observations on any aspect of this, or on closely related topics, are allowed.

Posting to the group implies that the member has read, understood and accepted these guidelines. The list moderates the new members to observe closely whether they have understood and are following the list guidelines. The moderation of the new members will be for a temporary time period.

MEMBERS SHOULD PREFERABLY POST NO MORE THAN TWO MESSAGES PER DAY.
Members replying to a post should not change the subject heading when sending their replies. When a new topic is begun, a new thread and subject heading should be used so that it is clear that it is a new topic.

Replies to posts should clearly differentiate between the new material and the earlier. It is suggested that, for example, the words of the earlier post be enclosed thus: \< \>. Please retain the name of the poster to whom the reply is addressed, and the message number if and when possible. Please do not include the entire message of the previous poster while sending your reply to a post.

Posts shall not consist entirely of quoted material, even from canonical advaitin texts. Please write enough in your own words to tie the quote in with the topic you are addressing. Quotes should be directly relevant to the topic under discussion and be included as authoritative support for the post. Rather than extensively quoting material from a web site, the address of the material should be provided.

Members are required to follow the list rules and regulations specified below:  

1\. Members are prohibited from posting messages that are not directly related to the stated purpose of the group, unless consent has been provided by the Board of Moderators.

2\. Members are prohibited from posting messages that are threatening, deliberately inflammatory, inciting, abusive, harassing, vulgar, obscene, libelous, hateful, or defamatory in nature. This covers not only the content of the message but also the tone put forth in the message.

3\. Members are prohibited from posting messages that are objectionable based on comments made with regard to race, ethnicity, creed, gender, or religion.

4\. Members are prohibited from posting messages that include personal attacks of other group members or other individuals.

5\. Members must always provide sufficient identification of the post's author through either an e-mail signature or an e-mail address that clearly provides the first and last names of the sender. This identification shall identify the legal identity of the sender and any use of a false name will result in immediate termination of all accounts associated with the sender.  

Violation of any of the above rules will be determined by the Board of Moderators and may result in action such as written warnings, temporary moderation of member posts, temporary suspension and termination of group membership. The moderators may also, subject to majority decision, place any member under moderation if this is thought to be justifiable. Any administrative decision of the Board of Moderators shall not be challenged in the List. The objections should be sent to the Moderators only for resolution.

Thanks again for your cooperation and understanding,

Advaitin List Moderators

Note: Freedom to express your viewpoints always come with responsibility to keep everyone happy and peaceful.
