Hari:om, - Just my writing on a day couple of years back. I am posting it since I thought I saw as many posts on silence. I hope it means something to somebody somewhere.



For the question “who am I” there are no direct answers through the five sense perceptions which are limited in their capacity. Sastras can lead only up to a distance beyond which no proofs or logic will -work- they remain a set of mere words-a thought process that starts and ends. It is also mithya. One is convinced that the body up to the mind-buddhi level is jada and that is enlivened by a life source. What is this life source-an electric principle or an inexplainable power etc.- no words are available for describing it. Restricting it to myself, I am only aware of that this body functions because of me and I don’t know when I entered it and when I would leave which gives sense that I exist beyond time space boundaries. in dream I create a world of my own and I remain its witness as well- my self-identity is lost in sushupti so who am I- silence! I am consciousness, ever existing and everywhere-just consciousness? All that there is, is only god/brahman, even that remains as words- mithya. the final answer is I am silence blissfully.



c.s.baskaran.
