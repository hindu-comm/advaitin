Namaste:

Silence is a word subject to definition and it can mean different things at different times. In general, silence means absence of sound or any kind of noise. But during communication, it is considered as absence of speech. Absence of speech can’t be concludes as no communication but it could be non-verbal communication. Depending on the context, the meaning of silence will likely differ.

Silence is a powerful tool to employ during speech and a small pause will force the audience to be doubly attentive. Some speakers use this technique provides the audience to prepare to hear a news much more important.

In legal contractual situations, silence is also a form of acceptance unless the person categorically declares no to the contract. During debates silence could be taken as defeat! In summary, we can easily conclude that silence is more powerful than a bundle of meaningless words. It should be understood, in more situations we misunderstand words in comparison to just silence.

Now coming back to the answers provided by Dennisji, they need more clarity for correct understanding. His statements assume that “silence is no communication or the absence of all channels of communication.”
But what I have provided in my previous paragraphs, silence can’t interpreted to mean zero communication! His second quotation from Swami Dayananda confirms that a medium of communication is necessary for gaining language and Swamiji did not rule out that Silence can be an effective communication media!

In Vedanta, the knowledge of the Brahman (Jnanam) is beyond all senses or in other words that any knowledge gained through the sense organ is not considered True knowledge. It is quite possible for anyone to say that ‘Silence’ can help us to go beyond all our senses to discover the True Knowledge. 

Anyone can make claims but any claim about “True Knowledge” is unanswerable and hence I want to be SILENT!
