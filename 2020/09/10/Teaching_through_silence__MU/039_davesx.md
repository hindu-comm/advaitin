Sri Nisargadatta Maharaj:  

  

"That pure knowledge, 'I am', is the God of this universe, but it is not the Real; the Real I cannot tell you because words negate that. Whatever I am telling you is not the Truth. The Truth of Parabrahman is inexpressible, inconceivable and indescribable.

  

You can never have knowledge of That because Parabrahman cannot be witnessed.

  

Remain focused on the ‘I am’ till it goes into oblivion, then the eternal is, the Absolute is, Parabrahman is.  

  

Transcend the ‘I am’ and enter the concept-free Parabrahman state, where you do not even know you are!"
