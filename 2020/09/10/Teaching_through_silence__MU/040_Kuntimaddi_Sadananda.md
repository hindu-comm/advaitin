PraNAms

  

By the statement of Shree Nisargadatta Maharaj, theses very statements along with the one who is making these statements are also not real.

  

Vedanta recognizes that even the Self-realization is only at the vyaavaharika level. Hence all the definitions including the maahavaakyas are called indicatory for the one who is confused to uplift the mind in the direction pointed out - to recognize that everything other than Brahman is mithyaa.

  

At the paaramaarthika level there is only Brahman, one without a second, and Vedanta says - na vaak gacchati, na manaH - speech nor the mind can reach there. yato vaacho nivartante apraapya manasaa saH - the speech and the mind return back without ever 'understanding' the absolute.

Hence even the word 'Advaita' is only from the point of dvaita.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
