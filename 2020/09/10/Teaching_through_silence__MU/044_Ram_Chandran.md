In addition to the list guidelines for posting, members can follow the following Golden rules that come directly from Manusmrithi:

**Some Golden Rules for Communication**  

Our communication can be a virtue or liability depending on how we express ourselves. Ancient wisdom from the Upanishads (a moral treatise from Vedas) provide this simple rule:

Sathyam Bruyath (speak the Truth)

Priyam Bruyath (speak with a courteous and pleasant tone)

Na Bruyath Sathyamapriyam (never attempt to speak the truth using words to cause hurt or unpleasantness)

These quotations will be quite handy reminders:

"There are no facts, only interpretations!"

"The difference between a smart person and a wise person is that a smart person knows what to say and a wise person knows whether or not to say it."

"A smart person talks from experience, a smarter person from experience does not talk."

"When you talk you can only say something that you already know, but when you listen, you learn what someone else knows."

"A wise man can see more from the bottom of a well than a fool can see from a mountain top."

"There is this difference between happiness and wisdom that he that thinks himself the happiest man really is so; but he that thinks himself the wisest is generally the greatest fool."

"Before God we are equally wise -- and equally foolish."
