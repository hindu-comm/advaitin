Pranaams Sadaji,

  

We know that Sat came from Asat. In Padma Purana Lord Shiva said that he would appear in an avatara to bring in the Asat Shastra and I think Adi Shankara was an avatara of Lord Shiva. In my understanding the "Asat-shastra" is "Vedanta", which teaches the "Advaita", where the aim is to be one with the Nirguna Braman or the Param-Brahmana. If my memory serves me right, Lord Ram used the word "Asampraajna-Samadhi", and in Mahayana the word used is "PrajnaaParamita-Samadhi and to my knowledge both of the words mean same, as in both one endeavours to achieve the "Manah-Shunya and Ahamkara-Shunya" state.  

  

To sum up, in Vedanta, the "Samadhi in Asat + Prajnaa" is
"Asampraajna-Samadhi"**;** and in Mahayana, the "Samadhi in ParamPrajnaa" is "Prajnaaparamita-Samadhi", and Asat-Prajnaaa" and "Param-Prajnaa" are the same Nirguna Brahman. I used the word "Nirvikalpa Samadhi", as our group members may be more used to the word "Nirvikalpa Samadhi", than the word "Asampraajna samadhi".  

  

If you like to put it a better way, kindly do not hesitate.

  

Regards,
