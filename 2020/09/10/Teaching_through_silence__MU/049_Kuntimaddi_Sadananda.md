Sunilji – PraNAms

First, I have no knowledge of Buddhistic philosophy to comment on it.

‘As for as sat came from asat’ – It is not as per Vedanta if asat stands for non-existence. In the Chandogya Up. Sat vidya (6^(th) Ch.) Uddalaka teaching his son Swetaketu – he starts with the statement – sat eva Soumya idam agram aaseet – ekam eva adviteeyam. Existence alone was there before creation, and it is one without a second (Shankaraji interprets this as there are no sajaati, vijaati, swagata bhedas). Then Uddlaka says ‘ tat eka aahuH. Asat eva idam agra aseet. ekamevaadviteeyam. kutastu khalu Soumya eva syaaditi ho vaachaa’ Some say that non-existence alone was existing before creation, and it is one without a second. How can that be possible? How can non-existence exists as it is self-contradictory? Hence Uddalaka repeats his first statement again stating that sat alone was existing before creation.

Perhaps ‘asat’ that you were referring to refers to the apparent existence or mithyaa. Then it is OK since any creation can only mithyaa using mayaa shakti that makes the impossible possible - aghaTita ghaTanaa paTeeyasi - maayaa.

Hari Om!

Sadananda
