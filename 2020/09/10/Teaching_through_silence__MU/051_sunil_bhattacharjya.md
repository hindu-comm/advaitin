Pranaams Sadaji,

  

***"Asat"* means *Unmanifested* and not non-existence. *"Asat"* always exists. in the context of my previous mail. It is the state before the creation., as well as before the next creation and it goes on.  **

**  **

**As regards Buddhism, I believe that the Puranas are right in saying that Lord Buddha was the 9th avatara of Lord Vishnu. Lord Buddha himself clarified that he was not promulgating any new religion. In Dhamapada where he talked about the tenets of Dharma, he said "*esha dharma sanatanah*", meaning that what he taught were the tenets of the eternal religion, that we Indians have been following, from time immemorial. His Mahayana is based on the upanishads. It is clear that he too believed that the Vedas are traigunya-vishayaa, and he too wanted the brahmins of his time to be nistraigunya, but most of the brahmins got affronted.  **

**  **

**The brahmins of his time forgot that the priest class of brahmins was formed, when the sage Vashishtha requested some of them to take up priesthood, for the good of the people, so that the Vedic ceremonies / rituals could be performed. Initially those ancient brahmins hesitated to take up priesthood, they agreed to take up priesthood, as an act of sacrifice on their part. Later on the priest class of brahmins seems to have forgotten the original premise. Bhishma said that animal sacrifice was introduced later on by some clever people, who wanted a ruse to eat meat. Lord Buddha opposed the animal sacrifice in rituals and the priest class angry with Lord Buddha. They spread that Buddha was bringing in a new religion, which is based n Nihilism etc. etc.  **
