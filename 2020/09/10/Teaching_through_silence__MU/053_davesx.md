Namaste - Sure Brahman is everpresent and always already the case but the realization must manifest on the transactional level as Sadanandaji nicely stated.  

  

So what is your interpretation of verse 1.2.23 of Katha Upanishad according to your version of Vedanta?

  

नायमात्मा प्रवचनेन लभ्यो न मेधया न बहुना श्रुतेन ।  
  

यमेवैष वृणुते तेन लभ्यस्तस्यैष आत्मा विवृणुते तनू्ँस्वाम् ॥ २३ ॥

  

nāyamātmā pravacanena labhyo na medhayā na bahunā śrutena \|  

yamevaiṣa vṛṇute tena labhyastasyaiṣa ātmā vivṛṇute tanūm̐ svām \|\| 23 \|\|

  

Here is my summary translation: ParamAtman cannot be attained by study of the Vedas, nor by intelligence, nor by hearing but to him whom the Self chooses.
