Not my version, that is the advaita interpretation of the mantra. That is why the pramANa was shown - see the shAnkara bhAShya and Anandagiri TIkA below.

  

The translation of the second line based on the passages quoted -

  

He who seeks the self alone (ie he has rejected the desire for all anAtma), to him the self reveals itself.  

  

The word यम् refers to the Atma, एव means only.

एषः refers to the mumukshu.

वृणुते meansप्राप्तुमिच्छति, desires to attain. Thus the phraseयमेवैष वृणुते therefore means "he who wishes to attain the self alone"...

  

The dvaita interpretation of the mantra is what you have provided. They translateयम् as whom,एषः as this, referring to Ishvara / paramAtma, meaning - The self is revealed only to those that paramAtma chooses.
