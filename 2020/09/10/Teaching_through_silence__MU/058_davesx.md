Namaste- Similar to the Mādhyamika view,it appears to (delusively)exist as a defined formbut is actuallyempty of anintrinsic nature, as everything arises co-dependently. Seenin this light they are allEmpty-Forms...like.Mithya.

  

Nāgārjuna gavethe example ofa tree whichdepends on all the elements
-soil, air, rain, insects, and countless othernon tree elements. Ultimately all formsdepend on the whole Kosmos. SimilartoVedānta In whichBrahman is intuitively seen in all apparentobjects.

  

So Nāgārjuna devised the formidable Indian logic oftetralemma: neither does it exist nor does itnot exist nor both nor neither, so that the aspirant remains open,free from allviews ofultimate reality.
