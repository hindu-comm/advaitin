Venkatraghavanji - PraNAms

  

Here is my understanding based on the sloka.

  

1\. Self-being all-pervading is always self-revealing.

  

It is similar to what Sankara gives an example of the sun being covered by clouds in the Atmabodha text. When the clouds are removed by the winds, the Sun automatically gets revealed. Similarly, when the ignorance 'apparently covering' the Sefl is removed by the knowledge, the Self reveals itself.

  

paricchinna ivaajnaanam tannashe sati kevalaH\|

svayam prakaashatehyaatmaa meghopaayemsumaaniva. Atmabodha - 4

  

All this, of course, is in vyavahaarika only. Hence in principle Atma blesses or Parmaatma blesses etc in a way true but blessing happens only when the seeker contemplates in the direction pointed out after purifying the mind.

  

Knowledge will descend to those minds whose minds are tuned to that knowledge since it is ever-present. Efforts are only to remove the cover of ignorance, dis-covering, using an appropriate pramaana.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
