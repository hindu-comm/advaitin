Dear Raghav ji,

  

//I recollect a conversation with a Madhva friend who suggested that 'even if you do all the shravana, manana, nidhidhyAsanam, still, GYAnam will elude you unless there is the "Grace of Narayana". //

  

One can see that what Shankara has said in BSB 2.3.41 is in no way wanting in the need for the grace of Paramatma:

  

परस्मादात्मनः कर्माध्यक्षात्सर्वभूताधिवासात्साक्षिणश्चेतयितुरीश्वरात्तदनुज्ञया कर्तृत्वभोक्तृत्वलक्षणस्य संसारस्य सिद्धिः ;तदनुग्रहहेतुकेनैवच विज्ञानेन मोक्षसिद्धिर्भवितुमर्हति। कुतः ? तच्छ्रुतेः \|

  

For both samsara siddhi and moksha siddhi, the indispensable role of Brahman is emphasized by Shankara on the basis of the shruti.

  

In the BGB too Shankara has said a similar idea while introducing the 15 Chapter:

  

यस्मात् मदधीनंकर्मिणांकर्मफलं ज्ञानिनां च ज्ञानफलम्, अतः भक्तियोगेन मां ये सेवंते ते मम प्रसादात् ज्ञानप्राप्तिक्रमेण गुणातीताः मोक्षं गच्छन्ति।

  

Karma phala is abhyudaya or jnana praapti yogyataa and jnana phala is moksha.

  

Also, the Brahma sutra itself says: It is not a rule that the SMN done in a certain birth will give the result of (jnana and ) moksha in that birth itself. If there is a pratibandhaka, jnana/moksha will wait for that to be crossed over, and then only rise. The example of Vamadeva is given.

  

So, what the Dvaitin would say 'grace of Vishnu' is nothing other than these natural forces at work. They only give another name to it. There is the Gita word 'svabhaavastu pravartate' in the 5th chapter for which the Dvaitins have taken trouble to explain in their own way, not finding the Advaitin's commentary to those two three verses.

  

regards

subbu
