By all means if you are still believe disciplines like tripping into deep Nirvikalpa Samadhi can help renounce attachments, it will work like a corrective medicine to remove this disease of addiction. This is probably why Shankara endorsed it.  

  

Sri Ramakrishna also initiated monks but the mission was to disperse them into the length and breath of the planet to work for the benefit and welfare of suffering humanity, not to sit everlasting in a cave seeking moksha.  

  

A true Gnani can never renounce anything. He only renounces the idea of separateness. Such a one gives up the the idea that the world and himself are different. That is what is meant by "Renouncing", the true value of Sannyas. Renunciation must be the renunciation of the idea of separateness of difference. Similarly the body too is equally Brahman, so the Gyani renounces the idea that body is separate from Brahman.

  

Seekers who renounce the world, wealth, wife etc. in search of spiritual life do so because they mistake these things to be real when in fact they are mithya. Such a one was Siddharta Guatama who eventually returned to the world as Buddha, teacher of the middle way.  

  

So Mahabharata says -“He who is competent to support life by his energy, he who can support himself by his own exertions, does not live, but really falls away from his duty by the false externals of a life of renunciation. That man only is capable of leading renunciant life or happiness in the woods, who is unable to support sons, grandsons, and the deities and rishis, guests and pitris. If, O King, anybody could obtain siddi, perfection and salvation or moksha from sannyas, renunciation, mountains and trees would surely obtain it!"
