Hare Krishna





Interesting to see that returning after a hiatus of over a decade we are still *rehashing*the same subject(s). There was a long thread for several months, maybe a year on the subject of Yoga and Advaita by Sri Vidyasankar Sundaresan that addressed many of these 'understandings' and viewpoints. I will dig this up and post a PDF to the library.



Ø Yes, fortunately, I was one of the participants with my guruji Sri Vidyashankar prabhuji at that time with my limited knowledge about the role and importance of experience of NS or AS ( in pataNjala yOga) in jnana sAdhana and phala. Some top prakaraNa grantha-s like VC, JMV, books on Jagadguru-s etc. insisting the importance of experiencing adviteeyam state in NS. Hence some scholars are doubting the authorship of VC in the name of bhAshyakAra as it is giving undue importance to samAdhi experience as against shravaNAdi vAkya janita vedAnta vAkya jnana as expounded in prasthAna traya bhAshya.



So, let us look into the understanding that Nirvikala is vikalpa rahitam and in Vedanta this being referred to asunbroken contemplation of the mind in the direction pointed out by the scriptures.



Ø dhyAnaM as explained in ashtanga yOga is purusha taNtra (tattu samanvayAt sUtra bhAshya) it can be kartum, akartuM and anyathAkartum shakyaM and more importantly it is NOT vastu taNtra. But dhyAna in vedAnta jnana mArga unlike yOga mArga, it is Atma svarUpa chintanaM a sAdhana of nidhidhyAsanaM subsequent to sharavaNa ( vedAnta vAkya sharvaNa) and mananaM. dhyAna in vedAnta is apUrvavidhi bruhadAraNyaka bhAshya on AtmatyEvOpAseeta is more relevant here.



My view on such descriptions is, this dilutes or water down or explains away the significance of Asamprajnata Samadhi (AS) or Nirvaikalpa Samadhi (NS) quite a bit (either unintentionally or deliberately).



Ø AS is the result of chitta vrutti nirOdha (suppressing the thoughts) it is not the sAdhana for the Atma darshana or Atmaikatva jnana as per shankara in bruhadAraNyaka. Na hi vedAnteshu brahmAtmavijnAnAt anyat parama purushArtha sAdhanatvena avagamyate…Elsewhere I read ( most probably in Arsha vidyA works of Swamy dayaananda or his direct disciples) that the person who experiences AS as per the sAdhana of Patanjali would become “kevala” in a particular state and to the limited period of time but vedAnta darshana Atma jnana fetch the knowledge of :adviteeyatvaM which is sahaja sthiti neither time bound nor particular avasthA restricted. 





Why? Because the scriptures not only describe th 'state' but describe the impact NS or AS it has on the Sadhaka. I quoted BG 6.20*sukham ātyantikaḿ Atyantikam Sukham or* Supreme Bliss is the direct result of NS or AS. If someone objects to quoting from Gita because it is for 'mass consumption' and not a true Advaita text, that's fine.



Ø Well, for that matter bhAshyakAra says the bliss experienced in sushupti too is athyantikaM, paramaM, shuddhaM and adviteeyaM…can we say sushupti is the mOksha??





Let us take up Gaudapada's commentary on the Mandukya. He uses almost the same words in III.47 to describe *uttamam sukham*, the unsurpassable happiness and moreover states that *it is open to the vision of the Yogis*.



Ø Same as above, both sushupti and samAdhi are not free from avidyA is the clarification from bhAshyakAra because there adveteeya jnAnam is conspicuous by its absence.



So, if anyone claims*uttamam sukham*during contemplation of the scriptures relaxing in a lounge chair in the backyard, I would like to have a zoom call with them and discuss this in detail :-)



Ø If you think you can generalize all traditional shrOtreeya sAdhaka-s just an armchair philosophers then there would be a valid reason to assert that dhyAna yOgi-s are comfortable in their AC rooms with their followers and teach meditation after rigorous bhajans and dancing using some relaxation techniques 😊





Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
