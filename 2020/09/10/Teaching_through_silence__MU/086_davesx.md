If I may add, while deceptive Maya or adventitious ignorance can be removed by repeated reflection, contemplation and effort, Immaculate Maya (Maha Maya) imagined by Brahman cannot be removed by the fancies of any imagined characters as stated in Gaudapada Karika:  

  

****6.** First of all, He imagines the individual self and then He imagines various objects, external and internal.  **

**  **

******19.** Paramatman is imagined as countless objects like prana, mind etc... This is the wonderful illusion of the luminous One, Brahman, by which It itself seems to be deluded, as it were.****

  

Brahman alone chooses when to end His divine illusory drama as the Bhagavad Gita states:  

> *Daivi hy esa guna-mayi**mama maya duratyaya*
>
> *mam eva ye prapadyante**mayam etam taranti te (7.14****)  ***
>
> "This divine illusion of Mine called Maha Maya, made up of the three
> gunas of Nature is certainly very difficult to overcome; those who
> unreservedly surrender in Me, can overcome this amazing illusion."
>
>   
>
> Such a fully liberated One is no longer restricted by the five
> elements, neither can be destroyed by fire nor drowned by water nor
> restricted by earth or space...
