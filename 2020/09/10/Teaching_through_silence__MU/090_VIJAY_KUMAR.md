Hari om All

  

To all learned Vedantins, and aspiring Vedantins,

  

I thought of writing my few cents notes to you all after reading the commentaries.

  

Any assumption, any imagination, starting with "if" is not there in Vedanta. Any question Starting with the word "if" is not possible. It is an illusion. It can never happen

  

There is a story. There was a Software Engineer working in well known company. The story is about 30 year old. The boss one day came and told him that he has to go to LA immediately. There is a product show and you have to show our piece.

  

There was no time. He has to leave the next day. He said Yes, and tried all airlines for a ticket. Nothing was available. He felt bad. Finally one lady in one of the airlines, (I am not mentioning the airline here) said that we can do one thing for you, although we don't do this for everyone. He was so glad

  

She said, "you can show up at the airport, little earlier than usual, and go to the counter and tell your name. I will call them and arrange. But it is only if there is empty seat. Sometimes the flight is never full. Try your luck. Keep your credit card and ID handy. All the best"

  

He was little nervous, What if? Any way left little early, said Goodbye to his wife and kids and reached Airport. He went to the counter and produced the ID, told his name. They asked him to wait for sometime, until the confirmed passengers board the plane.

  

He was sitting in a seat not far and praying. Finally the boarding is about to finish, and the attendant called this gentleman. He was happy, and they said, there is one seat, the passenger has not shown up, so you can get the seat. He was overwhelmed with joy. He got the ticket, called his wife, his boss everyone. He went in and the door closed and the plane started taxying out from the gate.

  

Now the actual passenger got late called taxi, and he hurried. The Taxi driver said there is construction and because of that there is a re-route. Well, he was already angry and said, do whatever. Finally he reached the Airport, of course late, and went to the counter. The staff already left. After the train moves away from gate, there is no need, and they close and go away. He asked the nearby airlines. They said the plane already left. He sat there cursing his fate, the taxi driver, the construction company all.

  

The plane took off and within few minutes, it burst into flames and burned down, all on board died instantly.

  

Now the question in "If the actual person is not late, then our Software Engineer would be alive today. Is it not? Answer is 'yes'.

  

But the question is wrong, because it starts with "if". There is no 'if'. Things happen on its own accord. Everything what happens will happen. There is no change possible. All you can do is do your best.

  

There is a question Could 9-11 have been avoided? The answer is "No". You can take all the measures not to happen. But......

  

Thank you

  

Hari om

  

Vijay Kumar
