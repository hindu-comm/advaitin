I try to follow this group, but succeed only selectively, but appreciate many for their insights and wisdom backed by decades of Sadhana!

  

This is a topic that has huge impact not just on one's Sadhana, but every human interaction, including parenting.

  

Believing no change is possible and doing one's best do not go together. Study of scriptures, teachings of master, entire fields of academics-

all presuppose change is possible. Hard to tell kids to do their best and also tell no change is possible because whatever is to happen will

happen anyway, leaves them with hardly any motivation. Reading this, and I writing this, presupposes change is possible and desirable.

  

In last camp of Swami Chinmayanandaji in 1993, the first sentence he uttered during his talks on Gita Chapter 16 (and 17) somehow stuck  
in my memory: "No progress is possible without change. But every change is not progress. In a mere four years, Narendra transformed into

Vivekananda. Can you at least become a Viveki?" I don't remember the rest- but those words have been part of me since and continue to inspire.

  

Hari OM
