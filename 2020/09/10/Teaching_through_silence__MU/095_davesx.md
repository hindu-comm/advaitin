Sundar-ji lets take one at a time:  

  

- *Samadhiis also described assushuptiinjagrat\[sleep in the waking
  state\]; the blissful pure being which prevails in deep sleep is
  experienced in**jagrat**, when the mind and the senses are fully
  alert but inactive.(Guru Ramana, pp. 112-13)*

  

How I see this is 'Jagrat-Sushupti' or 'waking-sleep' is a metaphor to skillfully combine 'awareness' experienced in the waking state with 'silence' of deep sleep.

  

So Sahaj Samadhi which Ramana Maharshi promotes above all other Samadhis, in such works as Guru Vachaka Kovai, is similar to Dzogchen, the great perfection Himalayan teachings, where primordial self-existing Wakefullness or Empty-Awareness (Awareness of No-Thing) is pointed out as ones true nature.  

  

Gaudapada has gone into this in more details and pointed out the need to transcend the three *AvastAtraya* including deep sleep.
