Namaste Dennis-ji,

  

A similar verse appears in the Sanatsujatiyam of the Mahabaharata, as follows:

  

kālena pādam labhate tathāyam, tathaiva pādam guruyogataṣca ।,

utsāhayogena ca pādamccet, ṣāstreṇa pādam ca tato'bhiyāti ॥13

  

The 4 components are time, teacher, enthusiasm and scriptures.

  

The original verse that you has enquired is found in Haradatta’s commentary on the Apastambha Dharmasutra called Ujjvala.
