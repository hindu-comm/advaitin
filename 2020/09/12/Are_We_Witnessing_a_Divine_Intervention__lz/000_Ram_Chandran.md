**Are We Witnessing a Divine Intervention?**(Ram Chandran)

Whenever any natural calamity such as an Earthquake or Hurricane or Wild Fires or Floods occurs, our mind indulges with the thought - “Can it be because of a Divine Intervention?” The ongoing worldwide spread of COVID19 is also a catastrophe of colossal dimension and that brought the thought of God’s play in our mind. Slowly and steadily we are witnessing the deterioration of Nature, downgrading of our morality and the decline of environment. We have been overwhelmed with the attack of Flu, HIV and innumerable number of other viruses. The current outbreak of Covid-19 in China during last December has penetrated countries across the globe in an alarming scale injecting fear and confusion. At times of calamities such as this, we look for guidance from Science and Religion to handle the situation. Science plays the primary role to provide the relief and help to guide us to take the necessary safety measures. The force of virus affects both the physical and mental faculties and we look for care and support. To keep the mind calm we seek the guidance from the sources close to the Divine such as religious texts and spiritual leaders. Those with religious convictions practice prayers and meditation to get mental peace.



Science and Religion peek at the natural calamities and come up with distinctive reasons and conclusions. Science finds evidences for our negligence of the Law of Nature and asks us to change our attitude and habits with respect to the use of natural resources. Religious scholars think that Natural calamities are an expression of God’s disapproval of our detrimental actions towards nature and environment. They further point out our failures in our roles to live up to what we have been ordained to do with respect to our fundamental duties. They have come to the conclusion that a majority of us have slipped from the right path of Dharma (following natural law) to the wrong path of Adharma (performing against natural law). For practical purposes, Dharma may be described as that which elevates a human being to reach the ultimate goal of life confirming to Nature. Though Dharma can’t be directly understood but it gets revealed when we discard all detrimental actions to nature. This is just like the revelation of “Cleanliness” when all dirt gets removed! 



In the social media and Television news we have been seeing an increasing number of crimes such as killing, stealing, adultery, bribery, cruelty, etc. We are also in the mist of the arrival and spread of COVID19 across the Globe. They all added up to make us to worry about our future. The virus situation has taken us into a depressing mood and we seek meaningful solutions from all sources. Mahatma Gandhi in his book on the “Message of Gita” states,” I must confess to you that when doubts haunt me, when disappointments. stare me in the face, and when I see not one ray of light on the horizon, I turn to the Bhagavad Gita and find a verse to comfort me; and I immediately begin to smile in the midst of over-whelming sorrow.” He is one of the role models to follow for finding a meaningful solution. Following his example, we look for a verse or two in Gita for guidance. Luckily, two verses of Bhagavad Gita (verses 7 and 8 of Chapter 4) suggest that the Divine may intervene whenever the threshold level of Dharma reaches the lower point. Through these two verses Lord Krishna makes some profound assertions:

· Whenever there is a decline of Dharma and rise of Adharma, then I manifest with a suitable form to correct the situation;

· My incarnation with an appropriate form is to protect the good, to destroy the wicked, and to reestablish righteousness. I am born in every time whenever I am needed.



In spite of tall claims about cleanliness, our atmosphere is unclean and we steadily polluting the nature with activities harmful to the environment. The heights of waters in oceans are steadily increasing and the artic regions along with the entire earth steadily warming up. The ozone is steadily depleting resulted in the escalation of ultraviolet radiation of the surface of the earth. We also have more incidences of skin cancer, eye cataracts and damages to the genetic and immune systems. Instead of eliminating the root causes of these problems, we focused our attention more on finding new medicines, technologies and treatment methods. Directly or implicitly every one of us has been responsible for the prevailing conditions in many aspects of our daily life. These facts support the necessity of Divine Intervention to correct and save us from the current situation.



I am of the opinion that the Divine was forced to intervene to awaken the entire world to restructure our life path. Divine Intervention is understandable on the basis of what we have been observing with the spell of COVID19. One of the many supporting evidences for this contention is the penetrating and destructive power of this virus across the Globe within a short span of time. The scientists and doctors are yet to find a cure for those infected with this virus. This virus has pervaded the entire world infusing fear, confusion and uncertainty at all levels of governments, businesses, workers and people. We have taken all precautionary steps to avoid body contacts even with our close relatives and friends. We came to the conclusion that our life is most precious than all the illusionary outside attractions such as visiting shops, restaurants, entertainments, temples, churches, etc., This is the first time both Science and religion jointly came with the conclusion that detachment is most essential to achieve physical health and mental peace. Our acceptance to all social regulations came with the blessings of the Divine. It appears as though we have come out of a dream and we are willing to change and scrutinize our activities in order to avoid the dangers around us. We are ready to discard the false notion to indulge and expand our wants and agreed to pay attention to the atmosphere.



No one can ever able to prove or disprove whether the world is experiencing a Divine Intervention. Those who believe in God also agree that God only knows what God can or will do! Life is mystical and what we may or may not achieve also is unpredictable. Persons and circumstances beyond our control play more significant roles than what we play. Consequently we alone can’t be responsible for our success or failures. Though we are cut off physically from each other, we were able to connect with the people across the globe using modern visual and audio technologies. This present time practice of physical detachment will hopefully help us to achieve the long term goal of detachment from material objects and divert our attention to spiritual unification. This sudden arrival of COVID19 Virus is an eye opener for us to change our attitude towards the nature and environment. Whatever the Scientists predicted in the past regarding shrinking glaciers, rise of sea level and unusual winter and summer climates are happening now. If the Divine Intervention is true then it can certainly force us to reduce all activities that are detrimental to Nature and Environment.
