I have listened to this eloquent speaking Swamiji and must say he's quite a learned young man in the Upanishadic tradition and can be an excellent teacher.  

  

However in his video with Deepak Chopra, I'm not sure if he's pointing to the thought-free Self-existing Consciousness or to a witness 'I-thought' which is actually a thought-simulated state observing secondary thoughts and objects. Thought is unbelievably clever and can split itself masquerading in the background as in paralleling computer processing.  

  

That is the very reason I proceeded to comment on the Verse 29 of the Kenopanishad and 1.2.23 of Katha Upanishad.

  

Manana is prerequisite preparation for mind to eventually fall silent creating the open space for the grace of Self-Awareness to strike. This is beyond the self-identity, the individual's choice, effort and comprehension.  

  

This strike of lightening, blasts everything and vaporizes one's entire life story built on the fabric of thought that imagines separation from Brahman leaving no one to say he is liberated. Losing everything is not something you really want, there is no gain here only loss of separate identity.... even the gods Indra, Vayu, Agni runs away :-). Think clearly do you really want this blast from Vishnu's sudarshana chakra?

*He who thinks he does not know It, knows It. He who thinks he knows It, does not know It. The true knowers think they can never know It (because of Its infinitude), while the*  
*ignorant think they know It.*
