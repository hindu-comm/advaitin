'Kuntimaddi-ji: Nice hearing your views on this. I see where you're coming from so yes noticing objects imply that 'non-objective Awareness' is present. This can be proven simply by inquiring "In what do objects arise?" Certainly not another objects as a contrast is needed for recognition to take place.  

  

Again inquiring "In what do thoughts arise?" implies the contrast - **That** which is free of thoughts (or 'thought-free Consciousness') but **That** does not restrict thoughts from arising, roaming and disappearing, reappearing afresh.  

  

So there there is a difference between "thought-free" versus "thoughtless". Someone who is thoughtless is a retard, momentarily asleep or a yogi in Nirvikalpa Samadhi. In fact I doubt if its possible to be thoughtless while the body is awake and functioning.

  

When 'Thought-free' Self-Awareness is mentioned, it simply means the gap between thoughts has momentarily opened up and in this pause the limited 'I-thought' is absent and a holistic Self-Awareness or Intelligence, which is this Presence of being aware here and now, is allowed to take over and express itself as creative inspiration, valid insights, valid wisdom for the moment.

  

But there is no grasping to these insights no matter how alluring they may be, the pause is perpetually emptying itself with fresh revelations and wonder not conditioned by the past, free from the programming of the mind filled with past prescriptions and dead formulas.
