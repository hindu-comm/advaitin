Someone wrote to me in another forum that it seems abiding in the thought-free gap, not knowing, is impractical. My response was this is not something new.  

  

Cutting edge managers in industry have been using a scaled down version for decades in decision making.

  

In what is called 'critical thinking' or 'lateral thinking' all the available data and alternatives are analyzed based on pros and cons and tools such as benefit to cost ratio, other financial indices, decision trees, EV's, spider diagrams and risk probabilities.

  

But with many unknowns still in the equation, managers are asked to suspend all judgement or sleep on it. Lo and behold suddenly out of nowhere a brilliant novel answer intuitively bubbles up influencing the decision outcome.
