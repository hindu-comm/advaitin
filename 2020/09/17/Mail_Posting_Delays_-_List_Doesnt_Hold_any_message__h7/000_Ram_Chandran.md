Members have send me private emails with respect to mail delays of members' postings. The List Moderators want to assure the members that we do not hold any mails for moderation purposes. Currently all mails get delivered as they were sent. But any mail sent to any Google group goes through a thorough spam/malware testing processes and any suspected emails will get delayed. Anytime Google suspect a message as a potential threat, they leave it at the Pending Window and ask the moderators to further review before releasing. During the past week, at least 4 mails were seen by me at the pending window and I got informed through emails to take action.

  

The list administrators have no control over Google decisions regarding suspected spam messages. If any of you are using your commercial company emails, the chances get greater for such suspicions. Some members wisely want us to add their private emails (gmail, yahoo mail or hot-mail, etc.) to avoid this. This is a good option.

  

We also want to point out members stop debating personally to establish your superiority over the other. Such debates eventually ended up becoming more personal instead of being useful to the general membership. The purpose of this list is to propagate Sankara's Advaita Philosophy and motivate everyone to take the spiritual path. Please avoid unnecessary claims and we are all equal and agree to share what we know and want to learn what others know! We all know that pure intellectual debates will not take us to our spiritual goals of life.
Just a reminder of what you all already know.
