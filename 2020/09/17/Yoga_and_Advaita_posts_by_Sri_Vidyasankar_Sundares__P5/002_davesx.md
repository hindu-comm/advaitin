I see that one of the source text for his argument is the Brahma Sutras may I make a quick passing comment.

  

Brahma Sutra begins with the assumption of a dogmatic Brahman which the student has never seen. Hence it is more relevant from a religious context for beginners who have yet to unfold discrimination and who believe in creation stories (i.e. causality). The doctrine of causality taught in Brahma Sutra is not the same as Mandukya's non-causality. It is only a beginning towards that; it says that you do not find in the effect what is not already present in the cause.
