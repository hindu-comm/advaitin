Pranams,

  

  

  

Yes Patanjali is dvaitin, no arguments. But the practices mentioned therein are very much a core part of Advaitictraditions - Dharana, Dhyana and even the *dreadedSamadhi*. Patanjali has 'extra' syllabus Samadhi's (and other practices) not relevant to the *core* curriculum of Vedantins, true. I will get to the*Venn*diagram this weekend showing the overlaps.

  

As Sri-VSji discusses (in the attached word document) Shankara directly relates Nidhidhyasana to Samadhi as well. Is that the only approach to Nidhidhyasana? No. Yet, it is one of the valid approaches.

  

If looked carefully, Shankara also discusses Yoga in the commentary on Brahma Sutra Bhasyas, Katha, Mundaka,Sveteswara , Mandukyaand Gita.

  

And He brings up Yoga even when the core*vakyams*themselves do not.Take for example the commentary on the *dva suparna* *(Mundka III.i.3):That soul, while constantly undergoing degradation of being born among ghosts, beasts, men etc in multiple births, perchance shown the path of Yoga, by a very compassionate soul; And then becoming endowed with ahimsa.. etc and concentration of mind,* ***yada*** *while engaged in Meditation,* ***pasyati*** *sees. What does he see? I am this one who is the self of all and I am not the illusory limited self conjured up by ignorance.*
