**Main Question:**

Why should one aspire for work towards enlightenment?  

  

**Auxiliary questions**

a\) When all the Brahman, and when the individuality is not really real, whats the point of realizing?

There is no mischief involved in this question. Just to ***revise and remind*** the self about the ***basics*** and strengthen the pursuit.
