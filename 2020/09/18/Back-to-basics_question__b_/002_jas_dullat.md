Namaste/Pranams/Hello

  

It has been a privilege to be a part of this wonderful group with so may learned wise and kind teachers.

I myself am very much a novice at the bottom of the ladder and am grateful for being allowed the opportunity to read the thoughts of such elevated souls on this list/group.

  

I would like to add my personal and very simple thoughts to this question as follows -

  

Main question - To remove the personal suffering inherent with avidya/ignorance as jiva in Samsara.

  

Auxiliary questions -

A\) All is Brahman, however I do not directly experience this Truth hence my suffering. To remove this defect I must realise this Truth Directly, with the Grace of the Guru teachings.

B\) Desire for Moksha is pure when the Sadhana Chatustya (essential fourfold requirements of a sadhaka) are fulfilled.

Basically these practices are to cleanse and prepare the mind for the Grace of enlightenment.

Mere academic knowledge is not sufficient as this may trap the individual jiva into pride/ego and subsequent suffering in Samsarsa.

  

Please accept my simple answers to these profound questions.

  

Jas
