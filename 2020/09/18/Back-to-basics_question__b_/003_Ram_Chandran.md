Namaskar:

  

In order to keep healthy spirit of discussions without falling in the pitfalls of Ego and also to demonstrate "that there is no mischief involved in the question" it is always good to provide what your thoughts on those questions. The way you have structured the questions, I do believe that you have your own answers and in the spirit of discussions to get the full benefit, you are obligated to provide your thoughts with an open mind. I am not raising this issue not as a moderator of the list but as an ordinary member and absolutely there is no mischief intended!

  

We the Jivas are continually evolving, at some state of our spiritual progress, the Atman within us direct us to redirect our life on the higher ground. It can happen within this life time or it can happen after taking many more births and why and when this can happen is beyond our limitations. When we got the thought that we should redirect our life towards enlightenment is coming from inside and the questions when and why can not be answered.

The auxiliary questions express the level and nature of our ignorance.
Maya is fully playing its power on us and we get all sorts of questions wondering and showering our ignorance. By the way we are always NORMAL and we have our own inappropriate proportion of behavioral perception of ourselves and others and this is part of the Samsara Sagaram (ocean of ignorance with no knowledge to get out!

  

The primary source of our problem is our Ego created by our own corrupted mind and the only way to get out of this mess is to start mind purification. The entire Gita contains the source for all our problems and the solutions are provided in the most subtle form and enchripted.
This may explain why we need a proper Guru to decode the messages of Gita.

  

With warm regards,
