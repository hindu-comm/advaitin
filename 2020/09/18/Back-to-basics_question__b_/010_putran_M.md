Namaskaram Davex-ji,

  

I understandas follows:

  

The jiva acquires a sthula-sharira that is changing. The association of self with that continuum of gross nama-rupa aggregates ceases at some point when the self still identified in the sukshma and karana shariras leaves that gross body. That event in time is called the death of the person/body. The self however remains identified in the sukshma and karana shariras of that jiva; this embodied self transmigrates and becomes the locus for a new sthula sharira. It is now born from the body of its jiva-mother in that new body and begins life as if a new person.

  

I do not find reason to object to your other perspective, unless someone can point out its deviation from Shankara sampradaya or shastra pramana.
