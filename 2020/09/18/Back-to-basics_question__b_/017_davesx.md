Namaste - Additionally, please note the following regarding the Shariras or sheaths:  

  

a\) Sthula - waking state of gross body,  

b\) Sukshma - dreaming state of subtle mind  

c\) Karana - deep sleep of causal body.

  

Consciousness can be coupled to any of the above bodies or Consciousness can be free of these three bodies and remain aware of it own Self - its own nonobjectivity - which western psychology is yet to understand.  

  

To repeat the primary aim of Gyana is to be knowingly consciousness: Consciousness conscious of itself as the witnessing Atman - free from names, forms (nāma-rūpa), attributes - but yet free to express itself in all names and forms.  

  

This can only come about before the nāma-rūpa disintegrates or dies. Most sentient beings only know themselves as objects and do not know themselves as the ultimate Subject or Consciousness so very few after death dissolve in Consciousness which knows Itself. Consciousness which knows Itself as the freedom from all limitations is ever fulfilled, remains in ultimate sufficiency (Santosha), and does not look forward for further expression or incarnation.  

  

Such a One remains at the service of Brahman in the stream of Life for His own purpose and intent. For example, we have heard of conscious birth of the Avatars for restoring Dharma, implied in Ramji's essay.
