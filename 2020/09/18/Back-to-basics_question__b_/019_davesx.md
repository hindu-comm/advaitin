Ekamji -  

  

\>"I believe it is for many people a gradual process, taking its own pace"

  

As Kena Up. states it strikes out the blue as a sudden flash of lightening but these flashes must be repeated until Sthita or stability in NiddhyAsana.  

  

The trance of self-centeredness, one's private movie that is running on autopilot from time immemorial has to be broken many many times. Each time there is alertness to one's unconscious thought-pattern, the light flashes and everything is seen As-It-Is; instantly the trance is broken.  

  

What is usually sold in the western spiritual market for exorbitant fees under the brand name 'traditional oriental meditation' is actually shifting from one trance state to another rather than breaking the trance!  

  

Each time the trance is broken, it becomes more difficult for mind to create the same movie with the same intensity, until one develops the knock-out punch :-).  

  

In Shaolin Temples it is said one is not afraid of 10,000 kicks which were never practiced but that one kick practiced 10,000 times :-).
