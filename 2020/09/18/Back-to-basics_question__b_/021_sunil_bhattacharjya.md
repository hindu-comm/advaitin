Dear Sadanandaji,  

  

At the outset kindly excuse me for wrongly writing your name in my last mail.  

  

As regards Krishnaji's mail I can understand his anxiety as the understanding as well as explanation of the topic is very difficult. You are a scientist and so am I, though both of us have retired a few decades ago. I think most of the people today understand, with the elementary science taught in the schools, that the human bodies, which are aggregates of the different chemicals or call it biochemicals, which have their origin in the molecules, which are combinations of the atoms and the atoms, in their turn, are made of the subatomic particles like Proton, Neutron etc. I will not go deeper than this, except saying that finally even these subatomc particles have their equivalence in energy. This amounts to the modern day science telling that world originally came from the premordial energy.  

  

Can we not say that, the scientists generally understand that all these necessary transformations, right from the energy stage, through the different stages, starting from the subatomic particles to the making of the living beings, and then the birth and rebirth of the living beings, occur due to ***an Intelligence***. It is also known to most of the people, that transformation of matter to energy is possible and that is how the nuclear enrgy is produced. This ***intelligence*** is what we call ***the Brahman*** in Advaita. Ramakrishna Parahamsa said that with the advancement of science a day will come when one will understand Advaita in a day and I think the day has arrived. My above treatment is an attempt to see if that helps in understanding what advaita is.  

  

As regards the strictly advaitic view you have already referred to the fact that the gross elements are formed from subtle elements by the process of ***panceekaranam***,and Adi Shankara's wrote the necessary text on this, for the advaitins.

  

Kindly feel free to give your comments.

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
