Mixing scientific analysis with sastras and giving higher benefit of doubt to science may not quite take one to where advaita is supposed to lead. If at all, anything that corroborates with science, that sastras say, should be used as proof to validate science, not vice versa.



Even if the theory of rebirths are to be viewed as untrue, there is still compelling messages to take away in ones current life - a life of dharma, passion , humility etc,, that are supposed to carry the 'good' vasanas for a better next life can be emulated in this life itself. Does science have any explantion on why an innocent child is born in an ISIS land or lands in a terrorists family? or does it have any ways to influence these 'ugly' randomness?



Like in any new skills or knowledge, even here there is some unlearning to be done to make progress.

Depending on what is defined as progress, one can start from a science perspective and come up with 'darwian theory' that can stun a section of the world, but that has no evidence from a sastra perspective, or go with the flow of sastra and get contentment. Its finally what the individual want now and in later life.

Social distancing, personal hygiene, (re) emergence of brass, copper etc., are a blow to the modern scientifc inventions and it will not be a surprise if the vedic way of life re-emerges in the near future.
