Yes, you are right. I had written once an article on Science and Vedanta. It is now in the book 'Transcending Science' published recently by the Indic Academy, available both Kindle version and hard copies on Amazon, etc. Science actually means 'knowledge' derived from the root 'scier' meaning 'to know'. In Sanskrit 'vid' also means to know and 'Veda' means knowledge = Science. Vedanta is the end part of Vedas also means the ultimate science. Scientists normally think only 'objective sciences' are real. They cannot analyze the 'subject-I', the knower, using the objective tools since the subject 'I' cannot be objectified - that forms the definition of the subject. Similarly 'Brahman' meaning 'infinite' cannot also be an objectified or knowable entity. Vedanta equates these two in the famous statement 'you are that' tat tvam asi. This knowledge cannot be intellectually grasped as intellect can only grasp an objectifiable entity. Hence 'Self-realization' involves using the very intellect to transcend the intellect to Recognize the ever-self-existing 'SELF' that I am is Brahman, defined as Pure consciousness that exists by Vedanta. Hence Vedanta forms an absolute science. Hence Shankara puts this crisply -

brahma satyam, jagan mithyaa, jeevo brahma eva na aparaH - Brahman alone is the absolute truth or knowledge and the whole universe of chemicals are mithyaa (only relative existence) along with the notion that 'I am= these chemicals', and the truth is " I am is not different from Brahman'.

In the next line - He says, 'anena vedyam tat shaastrams, iti vedanta DiDimaH', That by this the above facts are known is the real Science and that is the declaration also by Vedanta.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
