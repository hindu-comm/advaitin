The way I look at it:

  

Questioning is an important tool for learning and assimilating. Also, a conclusion without experimentation is unscientific. A mind that has concluded is not amicable to an inquiry of the truth too. Everyone is on the path to find the truth one way or the other, and this is reflected by the very search for happiness. Some may take more lives in the evolutionary ladder; and that is OK - if one looks at it all this is only part of His Vibhuti.

As Swami Dayananda Saraswati puts it - everything is in order. Some are reveling in their own glory, some in the glory of the Lord - both are in order. Some think Advaita is the greatest while others call is as maaya vaada - that is part of the order. Some complain all the time and some cause others to complain - both are in order.

  

Life itself is incredible - a bunch of carbohydrates, minerals, fat, and water, but acting incredibly active - a marvel indeed. The body is a wonder - with coffee or without coffee. Mind and intellect functioning and thinking - incredible indeed. Billions of galaxies with an expanding universe one side and billions of neurons active in a small brain on the other - a wonder of all wonders.

  

Hence seekers questioning is part of growing up and others questing this questioning is also part of the order - with the moderators stepping in to ensure that the posters follow the decorum of this list is also part of the order.

  

Shankara says:

yogaratova bhogaratova sangara tova sangha viheenaH\|

yasya brahmani ramate chittam nandati nandati nadatyeva\|\|

  

A wise man can be just spending his time in doing yoga in some remote place (also applies to those who think that he should be a parivraajaka) or he may be enjoying the coffee or sambar as you put it as a man in downtown, he may he amidst of group or he may be alone - these are not immaterial - it is only where his mind is reveling - It is called aatma kreeda and aatma rati.

  

Just my 2c

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
