Namaste - My few cents and words of caution before dismissing scientific enquiry prematurely.

  

We have been taught since childhood to believe in religious fables and not look at facts. Science takes observable phenomenon and examine the truth of what is experienced. Modern science should be the starting point of all religion and philosophies, it's just that western science has not taken it to its logical conclusion as yet. By science I don't mean, in this instance applied science but the scientific methodology. Science demands reason, objectivity, caution, scrutiny and examination of all the facts free from sentiments before jumping to judgements and conclusion about the Truth.

  

How does one know there is a God for example? Has anyone seen God? That mystics from various religions saw visions may be so. But the real question remains “Was what they saw the Truth?" In insane asylums patients make similar claims. They no doubt had a vision but they never stopped to inquire if their visions are true. That God created the world or manifested himself is merely a supposition. How do you know that what the Veda, Bible, Koran are stating is true? To accept these scriptures by blind faith without reasoning is to possess the mentality of a slave :-)  

  

Accept nothing from secondhand authorities but only if it proves true after examination. Ancient Indian astronomy said Rahu swallowed the sun during an eclipse, but today science has rejected this nonsense.

  

Newton observed certain empirical facts, then he formulated a hypothesis and went ahead to test, prove and formulate the law of gravity. This is example of sound scientific methodology. But note that it's basis began with observable facts; mental imagination came later.  

  

Similarly in Vedanta philosophy one must start with facts--the facts of this world, inquiring into "What is this world? What is life and death?" and progress to "What Am I?" based on a scientific foundation.  

  

The advances of science both in influence and discovery, will continue unstoppable.  

Therefore any ancient philosophy which ignores or refuses to harmonize with it, will perish by dismissal of future generations. Conviction of the Truth comes only by adequate reasoning and by the counsel of the wise. As such merely reading, memorizing and parroting books on Vedanta is not enough.
