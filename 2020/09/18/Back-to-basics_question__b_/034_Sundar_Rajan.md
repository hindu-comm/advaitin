Namaskar

  

\>\>

  

Shankara says:

yogaratova bhogaratova sangara tova sangha viheenaH\|

yasya brahmani ramate chittam nandati nandati nadatyeva\|\|

  

he may he amidst of group or he may be alone - these are not immaterial - it is only where his mind is reveling - It is called aatma kreeda and aatma rati.

\>\>

  

  

True, these are the characteristics of a Wise Man aka Siddha Purusha
For want of a better term, let us call it the 'End State' or 'Desired State'.

  

Question: Can a seeker who is in the 'As Is' state also assume he is reveling in the Atma right now 'End state' - aatma kreeda and aatma rati?

  

or are we going to say things like there is no End state or As Is state. We can't achievethe already achievedetc?
