Actually in the first ashrama of life, the time goes in Gurukula, in the second Ashrama one has the obligation of Pitri-rina, because of which one has to have a family and to take care of the consequent responsibilities. It is in the last two ashramas, one gets more time for the spiritual sadhana leading to sthitapraajna state, and what can be more peaceful and secluded place for such sadhana, than an ashrana or a
bungalow in a secluded place or a cave, provided of course the carnivores are not around.
