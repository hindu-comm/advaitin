Dear Raghav Kumarji,

  

I agree.There was a misconception in the western world that the world was created in 4004 BCE, and even the King James edition of the Bible had it written on its front-page. It was only in 1884 the western world realized that it was not correct and the King james edition of the Bible deleted it from its front page. But damage was already done and the western scholars could not believe how Aryabhatta could find the planetary conjunction in Pisces on February 17/18 in 3102 BCE. They claimed that Aryabhatta must have back-calculated and claimed the planetary conjunction on 3102 BCE, and it is a pity that many ignorant Indian scholars fell for it. But anybody, who read the Mahabharata well, did know that it was given in the Mahabharata that there was such a conjunction in 3102 BCE, when Lord Krishna passed away and the Kali yuga set in. When I did some studies on the dating of the Mahabharata, the first thing I did was to verify what Aryabhatta did, albeit using modern software, and found that there indeed was the planetary conjunction of planets in 3102 BCE, claimed by Aryabhatta. The Mahabharata war took place 36 years and a few months before Lord Krishna passed away, and Vedavyasa had given so many astronomical details that one can even find the year, month, tithi, nakshatra and the vara or day, on whch the Mahabharata war started.  

  

Many Indian scholars think that the astronomical details of the date of the birth of Lord Ram, as given in the Valmiki Ramayana, was wrong. It is a pity that, claiming this, the Akhil Bharatiya Vidvat Parishad (AVP) has published a booklet, written by one Vinay Jha. However, it is verifiable that Valmiki was one hundred percent right in saying that when Lord Ram took birth, the "Rising Sun" or "Lagna" was in "Karkata" or "Cancer", and it was Shukla Navami tithi and the nakshatra of the day was Punarvasu.

  

I can go on and on, but I will stop here, after the above two examples. The Indian history needs to be thoroughly rewritten. Even the history of the Tunga Shringeri Math has to be rewritten, The math has published more than one version of its history in the 20th century. The astronomical and other details available data shows that Adi Shankara established the Shringeri math in the 5th century BCE in Kudali, near Shimoga, and in the 13th century CE, ***the math was split, for a very wise and essential reason***. I will not go into the details here. While the original math is still there at the confluence of Tuga and Bhadra rivers in Kudali near Shimoga**;** Swami Vidyatirtha and Swami Vidyaranya shifted the math to a new location on the bank of the river Tuga, about 100 KM upstream of Kudali.  

Hari Om
