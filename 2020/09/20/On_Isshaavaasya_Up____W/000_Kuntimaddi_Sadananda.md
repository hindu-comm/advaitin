# 'Iśā Upaniṣad: Comparing the Commentaries of Ādi Śaṅkara and Vedānta Deśika'

[https://www.indictoday.com/long-reads/isa-upani%e1%b9%a3ad-comparing-commentaries-adi-sa%e1%b9%85kara-vedanta-desika/](https://www.indictoday.com/long-reads/isa-upani%e1%b9%a3ad-comparing-commentaries-adi-sa%e1%b9%85kara-vedanta-desika/)  

  

Upanishad starts with Ishaa vaasyam idagam sarvam - It is normally translated as 'Lord pervades everywhere'. Vaasyam is interpreted by Shankara as 'See that Lord pervades everywhere'. Every religion says Lord is omnipresent. Hence whether I see or not He is going to pervade the whole universe. Hence instruction is 'SEE' or recognize that Lord is everywhere - and everything. yo maam pasyati sarvatra sarvanca mayi pasyati- one who sees Me everywhere and everything in Me - also in the next sloka Krishna says - 'sarva bhuutastam aatmaanam sarva bhuutani ca aatmani' - one who sees himself or his SELF everywhere and everything as His SELF - They are wise people.

  

Obviously that vision goes beyond the chemicals that we were discussing.
