Jnana, the Supreme Knowledge of Self,  

Is ultimately realized through devotion,  

Offer all your mistaken notions,  

Who you consider yourself to be.

This is complete surrender.

  

What is false needs to surrender to Truth.  
What is limited needs to surrender to the Whole.  
What is transient needs to surrender to the Unborn.  

The true object of devotion is within:  

*Ever-present Radiant Light Infinite,*

All outside gurus point only to the certainty of THAT.

  

~Gyanananda
