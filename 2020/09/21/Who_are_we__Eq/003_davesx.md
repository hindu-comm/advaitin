Yes indeed Sundarji - This is risky business. Some joker comes around claiming the position “I am God” or "I am the Supreme".  

  

As Meister Eckhart declares when God is, I am not. There is no place for two or duality, so who is there to make such a claim? But gullible followers allow themselves to be exploited.

  

And then the claimant wears his trickster hat saying ‘I will provide you this true process of realization through guru-chela relationship. I’m building this nice big Ashram where I will sit on the throne and you will be my devotees. We'll play a grand Divine Leela, indulge in Tantric workshops and you can buy me expensive jewellery and rolls royces."

  

India has been riddled with such fraudulent holy men some of whom have been apprehended and convicted while others have skipped the country.
