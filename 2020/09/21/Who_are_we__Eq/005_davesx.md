Though it may take a soul 1 second, 1 minute, 1 year or 1 hundred years to drop the restless turbulence of the inner monologue replaying repetitive knowledge, a ripe one rests quietly in the blink of an eye and every continuing blink.  

  

Such a One instantly sees and understands the mechanism by which recurrent programs installed in memory strings ideas together to formulate and maintain the construction of a mental envelope - the sense of a separate self.  

  

And suddenly the light of knowing flashes and the entire superstructure of self-identity burns down, collapses and alongside the delusive spell of maya is broken. Everything is seen momentarily clear, whole and integrated, free from prejudice and self interest.  

  

Awakening is the stark realization that who one inherently is - the one Aware Self that is "No Thing" - cannot be defined by names, forms or attributes. Neither can one know, conceive or express this realization. This is indeed a most humbling acceptance.  

  

-davex
