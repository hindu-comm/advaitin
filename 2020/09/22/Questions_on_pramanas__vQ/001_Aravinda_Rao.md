Namaskars,

A scientist friend, who had also read the Upanishads, had told me that when the sages defined Brahman as 'satyam-jnanam-anantam'or speculated on the originof universe as a manifestation in consciousness, they were doing the same 'thought experiment' which themodern scientists are doing.

Science has advanced several hypotheses, just like Vedanta, which is a rational enquiry into the nature

of reality. The sages did not have an agenda to establish a religion. It was a pure and honest enquiry.

Aravinda Rao
