Putranji - PraNAms

  

I think in Nyaaya, the vyaapti in the case of fire/smoke is vishama vyapti - where there is a smoke there is fire but not necessarily where there is fire there is smoke. If you draw a concentric circles, the smoke is included in fire but fire being a bigger circle will not necessarily covered by smoke - circle.

  

I do not know if Nyaaya differentiates the arthaapatti from anumaana.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
