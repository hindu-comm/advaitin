Putranji - I have written a series of articles on that which Dennis white edited them and are available on his website. I was planning to give talks but got postponed.

  

The mind can have many attachments - expressed as vaasanaas. 'Attachment' definition is you depend on something other than your self for your happiness. On the other hand 'Love' CAN BE defined as you let others depend on you for their happiness.

  

However, the mind thinks of one thought at a time. However, these thoughts occur so fast that one thinks that the mind is crowded. A disciplined mind thinks logically rather than emotions that are governed by attachments.
