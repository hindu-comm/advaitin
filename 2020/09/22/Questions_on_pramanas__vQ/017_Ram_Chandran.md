Namaskar dear Putranji:



Before my retirement, I was teaching statistics at the university level and regression analysis is one of my favorite topic to teach.
Regression analysis is to understand the relationship between two variables (for example, cigarette and cancer. To understand the relationship, data collected on cigarette smokers where some of them also had cancer. The question often asked is that – Does cigarette cause the cancer? If we choose the answer as yes, then it implies that everyone who smokes will have cancer. But in reality, that is not the case though a large percent of them may have cancer. In other words, to pin point that cigarette has caused cancer, we need lot more information for a precise conclusion. But the relationship between cancer and cigarette smoking can in fact be established using a simple linear regression analysis. As a matter of fact that has been well established. But the lawyers from cigarette company were able to prove in a law suit that cigarette ALONE is not responsible for causing the cancer! The lawyer used to bring a reputed statistician to explain that the relationship is not essentially a causal relationship but a case of correlation (associated relationship)! I don’t want to go into more statistical theories to describe the difference between causal and casual relationships!!

In other words, the point we need to make sure is that as Vedantins, we should try our level best to describe the implications of Fire-smoke example. Fire and Smoke are two events, they can sometime occur simultaneously and at other times independently. Just by seeing a fire or smoke, it is impossible to make any precise conclusions! There can be more than one factor responsible for Smoke and fire at the most is only of the many factors (others include, dust, chemical reactions, etc.). I am of the opinion that many of the Vedantic terminology used are mathematically precise since logic is quite fundamental and mathematics is the grandfather of logic!!  

I am bringing these facts so that you get the necessary insights for further describe what you perceive.
