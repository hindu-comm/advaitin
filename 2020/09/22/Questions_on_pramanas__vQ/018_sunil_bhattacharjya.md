Dear Ramchandranji,

  

Your example is very appropriate. Recently one 109 year old healthy man in USA, who still goes on driving his car, was asked as to how he can go on drinking and smoking merrily without any side-effect. He said that though he smokes a lot, he does not inhale the smoke. He just smokes and lets the smoke out from the mouth. I know another person, a heavy smoker, who died of heart attack and people say that he died because of his excessive smoking.  

  

Now I have a query, Do we find the need for any type of pramana necessary in the following example:

The sage Narada was praying to Lord Krishna for a long time that the Lord should make him understand what ***Maya*** is. Finally one day, the Lord agreed and He asked Narada to accompany Him for a walk. After walking for a while the Lord told Narada to get him some drinking water, as He was feeling thirsty. Nerada had to walk a lot in search of a source of water and finally saw a house with a water tank, where a beautiful girl was taking bath. Narada watched her for sometime and when she went back to her, the sage Narada followed her. There he met the girl's relatives and they treated him nicely. Eventually he married the girl and raised a family. Then one day there was a big flood and the members of his family were swept away. Somehow Narada could save himself, but he was very sad. Then he remembered that the Lord was waiting for him.. He went to the Lord with the drinking water, and thanked the lord that he had understood what Maya is. My query is : Can any type of pramana be attributed to what happened in this story. Of course, if you think that the story is irrelevant, you can ignore it.

  

Regards

Sunil KB
