Namaste - The delineation is clear from your argument:  

  

\> "The self that 'I am' being the subject, and/or Braham, being infinite are aprameyam are not objects of knowledge"

  

Aligns with Shankara's declarations:  

  

1) Jiva = Sakshi (I am)

  

2) This witnessing Sakshi = Brahman (Infinite).

  

Jiva merges in Sakshi which in turn dissolves as a salt doll submerged in the boundless Ocean of Brahman, to borrow a line from Sri Ramakrishna, but the moment there is a demand for a response in functional living the 'I am" automatically reconstitutes, similar to how the Jiva reconstitutes every morning upon waking from deep sleep.  

  

Thanks for the elucidation.
