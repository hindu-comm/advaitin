I found your question interesting. I just want to describe its relevance as I understand in case readers will try to answer it.

  

What happened in the story is that Narada "understood what Maya is" as a consequence of his experiences. He obtained knowledge of Maya. So the question is whether any pramana (means of knowledge) came into play to his gaining the knowledge. If not, is there some other subtlety to his understanding maya that would differ from other knowledge for which the pramanas apply?
