Namaste -  

  
\>What is the pramana for knowing maya?  
  

Apart from the pointing out instructions of the Shastras, I'd add the faculty of Buddhi to discriminate and interrogate the real from the unreal. Seeing through and relinquishment of the illusion of separation.  
  
Recognizing again and again that all objects are mithya dressed up in disguises, imputed by the conditioned mind-imagination while appearing as if separate, distinct and discrete.  
When actually there are no independent objects as all are one interdependent or interconnected whole, one supreme Brahman, one life, one humanity to be lived in the spirit of brotherhood and corporation.  
  
"Established in the blissful Self, never fooled by delusion, act playfully in the world, O Raghava!" says guru Vasishta to Lord Rama.
