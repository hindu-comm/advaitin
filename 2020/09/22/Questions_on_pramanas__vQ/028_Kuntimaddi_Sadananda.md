What is the pramana for knowing maya?  

  

PraNAms

  

Scripture defines maaya is the same as prakriti - maayantu prakritim vidyaat, maayinatu maheswaram - Sweataswatar Up.  

Know that maaya is same as prakriti and weilder of maaya is Iswara.

  

Krishna says - daiveem eshaa gunami mama maayaa duratyayaa\|

maamevaye prapadyante maayam etaam taranti te\|

This maaya of mine is of divine orgin and one cannot easily cross over - only be surrendering to Me one can transcend the maaya.

  

Maaya is defined by Shankara as - aghaTita ghaTanaa paTeeyasi - that which makes impossible possible. Krishna say - maayaa adhyakshena prakritiH suuyate sa caraa caram - under my lordship the prakriti projects these movable and immovables.

  

One becoming many involves a driving force from thermodyanamics - that force is maaya which is imperceptible but can be deduced based on the work done, like any other force.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
