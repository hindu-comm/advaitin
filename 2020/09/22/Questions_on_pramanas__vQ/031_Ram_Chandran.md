Namaskar:

Advaita does recognize that Brahman alone as true and eternal — that which exists at all times and without change. Our Scriptures confirm that His Maya is the cause of the universe and at the same time Brahman is kept hidden. A Jnani who has crossed this Maya alone is able to discriminate between the real and the unreal.

In Panchadasi, Vidyaranya explains how a Jivanmukta becomes aware of the oneness of the Brahma Swaroopa with the Jnanam to deal with the world.
Jivanmukta is also aware of the duality caused by Maya created by Ishwara the sole creator of the objects in the world. We the Jivas on the contrary recreates these objects by how we perceive.

The entire universe is Ishwara srishti. But as Jivas we became as a secondary creator by transforming the Ishwara srishti for our own enjoyment. Jiva srishti is thus tempered by likes and dislikes. A banana is a creation of Ishwara and it remains so for ever. But as human beings, our mind identify the same banana as small, big, good, bad, tasty and rotten banana. As Jiva we creates the feelings of happiness, disappointment or indifference with regard to the banana but the nature of the banana as created by Ishwara remains the same.

We only have control over the Maya introduced during our secondary creation which can be removed with efforts. Until we become a Jivanmukta, we have no means to control or understand the Ishwara Sristi (Ishwara Maya)!
