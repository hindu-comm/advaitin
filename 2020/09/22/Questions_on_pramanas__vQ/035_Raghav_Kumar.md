Namaste ji

The way to neutralize jIva sRShTi or one's privately constructed world of rAga and dveShas, fancies etc., is said to be karma yoga. The practice of japam and devotion to the Lord and such other kind of
'sitting meditation' upAsanA practices are also included in this larger sense of the stage of karma yoga alone - they are to do with intelligent \*efforts\*. Once this privately constructed subjectivity has been largely overcome by karma yoga (incl. upAsanA), a person is to have antaHkaraNa shuddhi. Such a person has already a great degree of objectivity w.r.t even his mind and emotions etc., - i.e., he has accomplished drG-dRShya-viveka.

  

Thereafter Ishvara sRShTi (or mAyA) still presents itself as a manifold world of dvaita where existential angst and suffering are still experienced by that person having antaHkaraNa shuddhi. For such a person, shravaNam, mananam and nidhidhyaasanam are the necessary method to transcend the daivI mAyA by self-knowledge. As Sri Krishna says-

तेषामेवानुकम्पार्थमहमज्ञानजं तमः ।

नाशयाम्यात्मभावस्थो ज्ञानदीपेन भास्वता ॥ ११ ॥

"Out of compassion for them, I, dwelling in their hearts, destroy with the shining \*lamp of knowledge\* the darkness born of ignorance."  

  

Here we notice that even to remove this sense of duality, which is presented for experience as this world of divine mAyA, self-knowledge is \*necessary and sufficient\*. That self-knowledge can take place only through a cognitive process (pramANa vyApAra) which again needs Advaita vAsanA, Guru and his upadesha of shAstra - all of these factors need the Lord's grace.

  

The need for grace is not an additional new factor mandated for transcending the divine mAyA. If that had been the case, the first stage of acquiring antaHkaraNa shuddhi through devotion to Bhagavan and karma yoga, would have been followed by Bhagavan directly 'conferring' his divine Grace to remove the veil of divine mAyA. But this is not so.

  

The 'method', so to speak, adopted by Bhagavan, to shower his divine grace, is to lead the sadhaka to an acharya or Guru who can initiate a cognitive transformation in the sAdhaka. That divine process of Shravanam, mananam and nidhidhyaasanam from the divine Guru who teaches the divine shAstra removes the veil of ignorance of the Self, leafs to transcendence of divine mAyA.

  

Om

Raghav
