Purtanji -  

  

\>"I engage in mananam of advaita sometimes and sometimes the mind whips me back to slave-mode."

  

It is perfectly normal to forget and get distracted by the enchantment of Mohini Maya; even Indra and the Devas were dazzled by the brilliance of Goddess Uma and the luminous Narada just couldn't resist :-)  

  

Once the distraction is recognized simply remember again that there are no objects separate from it's ultimate Subject or Source - Brahman - and continue on with non-dual manana.

  

As such rather than being a distraction (as nicely explained by Sadaji) objects become a reminder to Brahman!

  

Good Luck
