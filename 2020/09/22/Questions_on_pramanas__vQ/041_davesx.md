Good point sir. In MandukhyaKarika, Maya is described as visible to the senses, reified by the mind but absent of inherent substantiality.

  

Things are "not this", not what they seem to be neither are they different.  

  

**"Just as a dream, mirage, echo, rainbow, magic and a city seen in the sky are all unreal, so too, is this universe seen to be unreal by knowers of Vedanta". MandukhyaKarika (2.31)**
