Namaskar:

There is a clear distinction between Vedantic discussion and Scientific experiments to determine whether a given drug is effective under various scenario. Such clinical trials are conducted using a scientifically designed experiment. Statisticians specialized in the branch of statistics known as the Design of Experiments develop a suitable design for clinical trials. You apparently tried to simplify the problem but unfortunately due to the multiple factors are responsible for curing any sickness, the employed example is rather confusing. Though that is not your intention but by not taking account of the presence of factors such as age, medical history, diet, etc., etc. your explanations are incomplete.  

When I have a headache, my immediate need is to get rid of my headache with the help of an aspirin, Tylenol or Aleve, etc. Only when I get my headache everyday then I want to find out the cause for the headache. Honestly speaking, most drugs are only capable to get rid of sick condition and get rid of the cause requires so many other factors other than the drug. For example, the blood pressure prescriptions only can control the blood pressure and to find the cause, we need additional medical/physical therapies and diet control.  

Sometimes, we become overenthusiastic and forget our focus and get into a non-ending loop of discussions with more and more materials added during the process.  

I am suggesting these points not to find fault with your Vedantic scholarship but just to make you aware that we all need to be little more cautious when we try to explain Vedantic concepts using real world examples.
