Namaskar:

  

You have stated, "Anumana is a pramana, a means to knowledge."

Everyone agrees and I have also stated the same.

  

You have also stated, "It is not inherently speculative or guessing of some possibility that needs further confirmation with additional facts." I am of the opinion that what you stated is not necessarily true. Until we know the Brahman and becoming the Brahman, (Paramarthika state), everything what we say, discuss and write is ONLY speculative and our best guess. The statement Pramana do not always mean that no additional knowledge is necessary. The smoke on the hill may potentially could imply fire but not always. What we have is a limited knowledge of smoke and to confirm that there is really Fire, we need to know additional knowledge for confirmation (such as heat, flame, etc.).

  

Also all knowledge that we gained through sense organs and grasped by the mind and intellect is not good enough to know the Ultimate knowledge of Brahman. The Shastras warned us that at some stage that we should be willing to give up the Shastras also to gain the True Knowledge!

  

With warm regards,

Ram Chandran
