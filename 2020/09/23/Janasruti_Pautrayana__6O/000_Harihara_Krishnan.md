Pranams to All.

  

In Apasudradhikaranam, while referring to the Raikva episode, Sankaracharya states that Janasruti Pautrayana was mentioned as a kshatriya elsewhere, and hence he could not have been a born sudra. My question is - there are other instances in Sruti where individuals with the same name (and patronymic) are mentioned in different places. Like Satyakama Jabala, Budila Ashwataraswa, Ushast(a/i) Chakrayana, Devakiputra Krishna. But in none of these cases has Acharya deigned to correlate the references. True to his style, he has given the bare minimum treatment to the 'individuals' and focused instead on the philosophical content. It is only in the case of Janasruti Pautrayana, that he chooses to refer to the other occurrence of the name in Sruti and make a decision based partly on that. Isn't this unusual?

  

Regards
