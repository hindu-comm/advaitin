Namaste - There has been ongoing controversy among scholars examining the 'fingerprint' of Saundarya Lahari whether this text carries the 'flavor' and is an authentic work of Adi Shankara Bhagavatpada or is it from another author bearing the title Shankara?  

  

Subba *Rao* for example, who later became known as Satchidanandendra Saraswati,

has argued that all commentaries on Shankara's Advaita Vedanta after Sureshvaracharya should be viewed with skepticism.
