Namaskar putranji.

  

The Bhagavadgita bhashya was written for refuting the Bhagavadgita Bhasya of Bhatta bhaskara, of the 8th century CE (AD). Dr. B.N.K. Sharma, a dvaita scholar, had discussed this in a paper showing that Shankaracharya opposed the commentary of Bhatta Bhaskara.  

  

The Kanchi Kamakoti math has the Guru-parampara, like all the other Shankara mathas, going back to the 5th century BCE. In fact the proof that Adi Shankara was born on 509 BCE, was indirectly given by one of the past gurus of the Sringeri math, when he quoted a document which said that Adi Shankara was born on the 14th year of the rule of king Vikramaditya. It is known that the son of his own guru Govinda Bhagavadpada (Pre-Sanyasha name Shri Chandra Sharma) was king Vikramaditya. This is just for the group, but I don't wish to confront the Sringeri math, as the math does not really care as to when Adi Shankara was born.  

  

Regards,  

SunilK. Bhattacharjya
