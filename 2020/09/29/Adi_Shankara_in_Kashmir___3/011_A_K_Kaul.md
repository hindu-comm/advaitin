Shri M Putranji,

Jai ShriRam!

Bhaskara, also known as Bhatta-Bhaskara was a staunch follower of"*Bheda-abhedda*" and "*gyana-karma-samuchchaya*".

He had written his commentary on the Gita to "criticise" almost all the other "doctrines" including the Advaita of Aadi Shankara's Bhagvad-gita Bhashya.

He called "Advaitins" as "Mayavadins".

This proves that Bhatta-Bhaskara was around after the Aadi Shankara of 8th century CE.  

This will also be clear from the attachment "*Gita-Samiksha*" by T Gopalaswamy Iyengar.

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!

A K Kaul
