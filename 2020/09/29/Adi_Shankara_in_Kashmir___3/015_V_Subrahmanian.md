There is a story of Mahaperiyava commissioning a geologist (?) to conduct some test on the River Purna in Kalady, especially in that part of the 'turn' in its course that is said to have occurred when Adi Shankara prayed to it to flow close to his house so that his aged mother would not need to walk the usual distance to the river every day for her bath. And it is said that the specialist's report corroborated the BC timeline for Shankaracharya.
