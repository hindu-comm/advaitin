Dear Venkataramaji,

  

I trust the Kanchi Kamakoti math, the Kudali Shringeri math, the Puri Shankara math and Dwarka Shankara math and that their guru-parampara going upto 5th century BCE. I also trust the Shringeri guru's statement that Adi Shankara was born on the 14th year of the reign of Vikramaditya. I don't trust the guruparampara of the Tuga shringeri math as the same month earlier claimed the date of Adi Shankara as-44 BCE, assuming that the Vikramaditya was the Sakari Vikramaditya who lived from 102 BCE tp 15 CE. They did nor about the the Vikramaditya, who was the son of the guru of Adi Shankara.  

  

The great saint, Maha Periyava of Kanchi Mutt (Parama Guru of the Current Acharya, was fully convinced about the 509 BCE date fo Adi Shankara. He, in fact encouraged one geologist to confirm the date of Adi Shankaracharya by doing the dating of the stream going by Adi Shankara's parental home, when it changed course (or was it made to change course by digging a cala?), so that Adi Shankara's mother would not have had to walk long distance from home to the river. in Kaladi. I understand that the geologist did his job and Maha Periyava told the geologist to give all the details to the Swamiji in the Tunga Shringeri math. It seems there was no reaction from the Shringeri math, as expected.  

  

Regards,
