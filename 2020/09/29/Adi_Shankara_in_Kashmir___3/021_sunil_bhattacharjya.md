Namskar Putranji,

  

The record in the Shringeri math, as claimed by one of Swamiji of that math about a century ago, was that Adi Shankara was born on the 14th year of the reign of Vikramaditya. It is a pity that the great math could not find out the relevant Vikramaditya uptil now. Once the math came out with a guru paramparaand hey gave the date of Adi Shankara as the first mathadhipai from 44 BCE, but that guruparampara was withdrawn unceremoniously. If you are banking on any date of Adi Shankaracharya from the Sringeri math, no amount of knowledge ofTarkashatra may be able to save the situation. My simple common sense says that there is no reliability on the date(s) claimed by the Sringeri math, until and unless it succeeds in finding the Vikramaditya, on whose 14 th year of reign, Adi Shankaracharya was born. When horse has horse-sense why not we man have the man-sense (popularly called the common sense).  

  

Regards,
