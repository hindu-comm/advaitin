Namaste - Being a non scholar, I've been following this debate with interest.

  

Swam Vivekananda did mention somewhere that Indians were not good at recording historical dates of authors, focusing more on disseminating the wisdom of Sanatan Dharma.

  

From the scientific data presented so far, it appears the Adi Shankara did live in BCE??

  

If so the question boils down who was the 'Shankara' in the 8th century AD that synthesized Saundarya Lahari?

  

It is highly likely that it was influenced or written by Abhinavagupta, a renowned philosopher, mystic and tantric yogi from Kashmir born ~ 950AD since he produced similar works on the Devi such as Lalita Tripurasundari, including the yantra symbol.
