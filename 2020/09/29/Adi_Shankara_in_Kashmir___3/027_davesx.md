Dear Sunil-ji - Without exact historical dates the only way to validate information is through a solid reference point and correlation.

  

It is evident that Gaudapada the grand guru of Adi Shankara knew of Buddha and his teachings which he repudiated in his Karika. Most scholars set Buddha birth date at 563 BCE. He also commented on 'Yogachara' a Buddhist derivative synthesized by Vasubandhu whose birthdate is given as between 4th - 5th century BCE. So far so good.  

  

What doesn't jive is Gaudapada extracted almost wholesale and refuted some of Nagarjuna's Madhyamaka views. Stanford university states Nagarjuna was born between 150 and 250 CE, unless that is completely wrong.
