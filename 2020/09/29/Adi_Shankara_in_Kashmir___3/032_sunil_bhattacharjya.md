Ancient sages and scholars gave importance to History, and that why ITIHASA had to be included in the Fifth Veda (the Puranas) and that had to be updated from time to time. The Mahabharata had given so much information that one can find the day, date, nakshatra,month and year of many important events of that. Vedavyasa, considered as one of the 24 avataras of Lord Vishnu, must had good reasons for giving those details, such that in future one can find out when and what happened in the Mahabharata war.  

  

Similarly one can find out the exact date, when Adi Sankara was born, from the details available. Then there are the guru-paramparas of the mathas, other than the Tunga Sringeri math, which give us the idea as to the different mathadhipatis that managed the particular mathas in a particular time periods.
