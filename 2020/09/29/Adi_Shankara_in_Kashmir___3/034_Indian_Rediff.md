Hari Om,

  

Swami Parmarthananda has mentioned in his talks, that it is the very nature of how a Jiva is perceived (Jivatma as a reflection of the Paramatma) that causes the sages, rishis and others to forsake identifying the Vedas and other Bhashyas as having been written by them. Similarly, since in advaita there is really no birth or death, the concept of linking dates to the taking on of a new body or giving up of the current body was (probably) thought of as insignificant, compared to the seriousness and profoundness of the matters being discussed in the Bhashyas and Karikas.  

  

Maybe, we should take a hint from that and look at the lessons we learn from their teachings, instead of arguing over pittances like the date of birth, or how long it took to deliver the BG during the Mahabharata war etc?

  

Sai
