Dear Sundarji,

  

As regards the date of Adi Shankara so many details have been given that one who is interested in casting his horoscope can easily do that. For me the information that he was born in Kali Era 2593 is sufficient as that means the birth year was 509 BCE. For those who want know the name of the year it was "Nandana'. One can go on for the finer details such as the month, the ithi, the nakshatra, the vara (day) and the lagna etc. Though our recording system is different, one can always find the western equivalent of the information.
