Dear Saiji,

  

I cannot wholly agree, even if a great scholar like Paramarthananda might have said like that. Truth is truth and one should not be afraid of the truth. Truth never hurts the speaker, but one should tell the truth to hurt others, unless it is a must, such as in a debate. We have to know which is what type of truth. We should cultivate the habit of looking at phenomenal truth as temporary and should not be overly attached to that.  

  

For a person who has not achieved complete vairagya like Paramarthanandaji, needs to be aware of, though not necessarily attached to, the phenomenal things. If such a sanyashi says that Lord Buddha was born in 6th century BCE, I shall feel sad because what he is speaking is not the truth. But if the sanyashi is honest and he says that he does not know or he does not care to know the date of lord Buddha, that will be fine. .

  

Thanks for sharing you points.

skb
