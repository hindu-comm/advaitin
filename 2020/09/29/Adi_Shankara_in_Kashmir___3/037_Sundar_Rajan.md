Shankara and Guadapada are NOT, repeat **NOT** *Necessary* and *Mandatory* requirements for a Sadhaka's Advaita Jnana.

  

There is no such thing as 'inventor' of Advaita. Such a Jnana should have been made available to the earliest humans that were qualified whenever that may be, through the Sruti. No one invented or developed Advaita.

  

Otherwise if we peg ourselvesto a human founder, we fall into the same trap as "Christ is the sole pathway to Salvation (Eternal heaven)" basis of Christianity.

  

Which I can dismantle in *one minute* flat with a simple question:

  

Tell me what happenedto the guy who died before Christ. Did he go to heaven or hell. **Either** answer dismantles Christianity. If he went to heaven beforeChrist was born, Obviously Christ is not needed. If he went to hell, Christ's God is an arbitrary unjust God!
