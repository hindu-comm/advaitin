Shri Dave-ji,

Jai Shri Ram!

Now that we know that there are no scientific proofs, but just the hearsay that the Aadi Shankara was born anywhere between 8th century CE and 5th century BCE, it is better to continue to stick to around 8th century CE, since that is more plausible instead of having two Aadi Shankaras----one the real Aadi Shankara and the other his duplicate as Abhinava Shankara.

Several years back there was a similar discussion on this very forum about the date of Aadi Shankara and the moderators had to put a full stop to the same as it had become just a never ending loop since you cannot convince someone who does not want to be convinced.

I am sure you know what I mean.

Let us therefore relax and continue with the usual discussions about Brahma Satyam Jagan-Mithya, jeevo brahmaiva-naaparah!

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!

A K Kaul
