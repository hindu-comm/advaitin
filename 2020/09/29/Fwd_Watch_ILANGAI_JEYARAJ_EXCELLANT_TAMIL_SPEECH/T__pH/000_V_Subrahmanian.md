That there are so many ways through which one can attain the Supreme is told by Sri Sureshwaracharya: यया यया भवेत्पुरंसां व्युत्पत्तिः प्रयगात्मनि...\[By whichever way one gains the right knowledge of the Atman, that way alone is falwless for him and there is simply no limit for those ways.
