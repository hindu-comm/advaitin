Namaskar:

I considered Sri Sunder Hattangadi as my elder brother and I am sorry to hear on his unexpected liberation last Friday. He likes Ramana Maharishi, a very silent Vedantin and without his presence this list wouldn’t have reached this level of Internet presence. Just ten days before, he sent me the entire archive of the advaitin list materials from the Yahoo Site and they are all now available at the Google Drive. He used to constantly added new materials at the advaitin Yahoogroup site with Sanskrit quotes and materials most relevant to Advaita Vedanta. I truly miss him and pray for his daughters and other members of the family to have peace and strength to bear this great loss. Even though Sunderji is not physically present, his presence will be always felt by this List For EVER.

Sri Sunder Hattangadi, a retired psychiatrist and a serious student of Sanskrit, discusses the concept of Karma with references to the classical scriptures of Hinduism. This piece is written at a very high level and a short bibliography is provided at the end for those interested in more information. Even though many Sanskrit terms are used in the text, Sunder-Ji provides excellent English translations to the Sanskrit verses which makes the article very readable by people of all backgrounds. The original writing was posted by Sunder-Ji to the Advaitin list. The Advaitin list is one of the largest lists in the world to discuss the doctrine of Advaita Vedanta as taught by Adi Shankracharya. Sunder-Ji is a member and a moderator of the Advaitin list. His exceptional mastery of both English and Sanskrit make his works of great value.

With Prayers for Peace,
