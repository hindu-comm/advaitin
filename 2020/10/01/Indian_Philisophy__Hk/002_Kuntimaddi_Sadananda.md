Looks like my books are missing in that list! - just kidding.

  

My suggestion is first to listen to Geeta talks available online by many - study Geeta first as explained by a teacher. You can select the one after listening to a few talks you get glued to the talks - that means your intellect is able to appreciate the logic presented there. If you are not able to appreciate talks of anybody, then your mind must be prepared to listen. In that case I would concentrate on the pouranic stories first. Karma yoga and bhakti yoga helps to prepare the mind to listen to the more serious talks on Vedanta. Otherwise, it will be wondering all over.

  

Later as the questions arise in your mind you can refer to the texts available.

  

In essence, shravana is more important - that is listening to a competent teacher. A competent teacher is one who himself studied under a competent teacher. There is sampradaaya or methodology in teaching.

  

Do not worry, the fact that you want to study is enough motivation to proceed further. Keep your mind open and think deeply about what you have heard and if questions arise, write it down, but keep listening, and slowly all those questions get resolved.

  

  

Best Wishes

  

Hari Om!
