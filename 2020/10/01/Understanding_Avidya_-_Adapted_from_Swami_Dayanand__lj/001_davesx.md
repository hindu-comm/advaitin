Namaste Ji -  

  

\>"Brahman alone is the cause of the jagat".

  

Really? Cause and effect theory is a super-imposition of ideas, latent within our own mind and imagination, projected without as a display of diverse phenomena.  

  

Vivartavada's theory of world creation is for beginners and mediocre minds and not the highest Vedanta; Vivartavada theory says Ishvara is the cause of the world while remaining Itself unchanged.  

  

This is not the view of Ajatavada, where there is no causation at all. Ultimately, no world of multiplicity has been produced (except in imagination), because from the standpoint of Paramartha there is no cause and effect! Brahman is thus the cause of world from a Vivartavada perspective only.  

  

Most of Sankara's commentaries on Vedanta Sutras does not go beyond Vivartavada as he needed to make compromises for the layman's common sense perspective. Ajatavata flies in the face of common sense and logic. There can be no real breakthrough into Moksha if one believes in causation (p.249,251 Mandukya).  

  

The scientific laws of causation still hold water in the sphere of practical or relative truth. Both Max Planck and Heissinger had shown (or at least started to show) that causation is no longer tenable in the sphere of ultimate truth.
