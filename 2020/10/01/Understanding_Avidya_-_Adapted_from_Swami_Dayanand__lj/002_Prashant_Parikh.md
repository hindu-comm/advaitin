Harih Om Davex ji,  
  
Naturally an āchārya of the stature of Pūjya Swamī Dayānanda ji recognizes what you are saying. He has clarified the same in several of his teachings. The context matters. Brahman can stand for Akśara Brahman (nirguṇa nirākāra), Kāraṇa Brahman (Saguṇa nirākāra) or Kārya Brahman (Saguṇa Sākāra). In this context, Swāmiji is referring to Kāraṇa Brahman, that we can also equate to Īśvara...  
  
Hariḥ Om
