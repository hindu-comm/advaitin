Prashanti-ji -  

  

\<"If one takes a text such as Drk-drsya viveka, one may, using this criteria, even fault the author for assuming that there is a knower and a known, when even such categories are rendered non-existent."\>  

  

The inseparability of Drk-Drsyam is itself the one and only Brahman!  

  

The aim of Drk-Drsyam Viveka is to show that Drsyam is only a mental construct. If there is to be an absence of contradictions, absence of duality, there must be one inclusive Being.

  

Conceptual divisions into Drk and Drsyam are required for teaching purposes only; what I'm pointing out is there is a tendency for teacher and student to get stuck in pathological conceptual divisions of either Drk or Drsyam, Brahman or World, identifying with one or the other, when in fact they are mutually inclusive. Simply analyze for yourself and see whenever Drsyam is noticed as having passed, there must have been the constant Drk which was aware of it.

  

Hope you get the drift.
