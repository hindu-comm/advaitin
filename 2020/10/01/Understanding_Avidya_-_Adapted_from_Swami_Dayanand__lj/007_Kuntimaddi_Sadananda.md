Swami Dayanandaji - responding to the questions posed. Hence it is teaching required for the student. The truth that is pointed out is beyond druk and drisyam too. No words can reach - na vaak gacchati na manaH - yato vaacho nivartante apraapya manasaa saH. This is also understood. The teacher has to come down to the student level and uplifts his mind for it SEE beyond the seer-seen duality - the underlying adhishtaanam for both - Until then what is needed is 'akhandaakaar vRtti' mind constantly contemplating on the truth expounded by the shastras - for that mind has to be free from doubts about the nature of truth. Mantra 7 of Mandukya provides the essence - Goudapaada has to write three following chapters to explain the words indicated in that mantra - Also he discussed the obstacles to self-realization.

  

This listserve also meant for that only to clear out any doubts by mananam.
