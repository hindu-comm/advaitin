Namaste Prashant ji

Thank you for sharing that.  

It is indeed true that  

Sri Swami Dayananda ji was too clear about the mithyAtvam of kArya-kAraNa sambandha to warrant any correction.

  

If someone is a GYAnI, then the cause effect relation or postulating this entity called avidyA need not even be mentioned to him. True.

  

But If someone (the student) is still suffering and complains of any feeling of duhkha or limitation, then it goes without saying that, he is already laboring under the cause-effect delusion. For such a student, it's quite relevant on the part of the Guru to bring in kArya and kAraNa. And point out that avidyA is indeed the cause and adhyAsa is the effect which leads to duhkha. In this manner the student's illusions are first brought in to a certain shape which is then amenable to avidyA-nivRtti through operating the shabda pramANam. Like even (in earlier times) a cataract had to reach a certain maturity/opacity before surgery is possible.

  

The student is only asked to observe that \*he is already laboring under the absolute reality of cause and effect\*. In doing so, the Guru can hardly be accused of postulating anything new that the shishya already is not assuming. Such a way of \*teaching kAraNatvam of brahman is appropriate and sound pedagogy\*.

  

Unsound pedagogy commonly followed by modern asampradAyavit vedanta teachers is to jump to paramArtha level straightaway. It's therefore not correct to say that even to talk of jagat-kAraNam brahman amounts to error.

  

It's actually the other way round. Any method of teaching Advaita imparted to a student (who obviously does not know the paramArtha satyaM to start with) that seeks \*to bypass the idea of jagat-kAraNam brahman is unsound pedagogy.\*

  

  

Om
