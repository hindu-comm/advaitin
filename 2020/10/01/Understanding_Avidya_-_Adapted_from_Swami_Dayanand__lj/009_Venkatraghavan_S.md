True. That is why the sUtrakAra starts with the taTastha lakshaNam, the incidental definition of Brahman as जन्माद्यस्य यतः. It is the classic adhyAropa apavAda methodology of the upaniShad-s.

  

1\) Utilise the principle of cause and effect to postulate a creator (cause) of the universe (effect).

2\) Identify Brahman as that creator.

3\) Establish that the universe is mithyA.

4\) Deny the "creator"-hood of Brahman.

  

What remains is Brahman that is beyond cause and effect.

  

This has been stated by shankarAchArya in his gItA bhAShya also -तथा हि सम्प्रदायविदां वचनम् — ‘अध्यारोपापवादाभ्यांनिष्प्रपञ्चं प्रपञ्च्यते’ इति। Note the reference to "sampradAya vidAm".
