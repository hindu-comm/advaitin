As mentioned Adi Shankara made compromises and prescribed 1) superimposition and 2) refutation. This methodology continued down the lineage of Advaita Siddhanta, but it doesn't mean it is to be accepted absolutely.

When Drik knows Itself even in the midst of Drsyam, that is most perfect Self-Abidance.
