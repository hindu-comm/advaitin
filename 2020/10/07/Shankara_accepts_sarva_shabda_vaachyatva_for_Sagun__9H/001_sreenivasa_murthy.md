Dear Sri Subramanaian,

  

Please Permit me to draw your kind attention to

the following mantras from Upanishads:

\(1\) ahamEvEdagaM sarvam \|\| Chandogya 7-25-1  

\(2\) AtmaivEdagaM sravam \|\| Chandogya 7-25-2

\(3\) Atmata Eva idagaM sarvam \|\| Chandogya 7-26-1

\(4\) sarvagam hyEtadbrahma ayamAtmA brahma \|\| Mandukya Mantra 2

\(5\) prapaMcOpaSamaM advaitam

 sa AtmA sa vijnEyaH \|\| Mandukya Mantra 7

  

From the above mantras it is absolutely clear  

that my True svarUpa which is nothing but Atman

is brahman and sarvam.  

  

The journey is from "me" to "I" / THE ATMAN.,

from the false center to TRUE CENTER.  

  

yat sAkShAd aparOkShA brahma ya AtmA sarvAntaraH \|\|

 The answer is

ESha ta AtmA srvAntaraH \|\| BruhadaraNyaka 3-4-1 &3-5-1.  

  

So Upanishad Sages are advising all the  

mumukshus / students of Vedanta to

realize their own true nature and thereby put an end to all  

these conceptual categories / divisions of nirguna brahma ,  

saguna brahma, jivas, vishva, EkajIvavAda, nAnAjIvavAda etc. etc.

which in no way help a mumukshu. On the other hand

they become barriers for realization by the 21st century mumukshus.

  

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa Murthy
