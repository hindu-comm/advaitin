Dear Subbuji,

  

At the outset Kindly permit me to clarify that this is not to criticize the contents of the nice paper of Prof R.P. Gupta,"*Why Śrīdhara Svāmī? The Makings of a Successful Sanskrit Commentary*. Religions (2020)".

  

However, I felt uncomfortable because of the fact that the paper does not discuss the historical aspects of the compulsion, which led Sridhara Swami's guru to ask Shridhara Swami to write the commentary on the Bhagavata purana. Before that we also have to ask as to why at all, Maharshi Vedavyasa, one of the 24 avataras of Lord Vishnu, wrote Bhagavatam, after he wrote the all-encompassing Mahabharata, which Vedavyasa himself calls the Karshna-Veda (or the Veda of Krishna). I think we have to request the great scholar, Dr. R.P. Gupta himself, to enlighten us on these two historical aspects, rather that a lesser person like you or me, attempt to write on this.

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
