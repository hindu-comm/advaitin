Hari Om

Avidya is the name given at the individual level for Maya the inscrutable power of Brahman.

Our next query is how did a power become an ignorance. For that the following example may help.

  

Associated with the capability of rendering the 7 notes of music correctly , any one can claim to become a singer. Thus Singer is a name given to one who has the power/capability of singing. Each school of music is a knowledge stream which has perfected a particular pattern in the rendering of the 7 notes. One who has knowledge of all schools of music is of course the Lord of Music.

  

Now at the individual level we could perfect one school of music say 'Hindustani'. Since we do not know all other schools and also are not aware that capability of singling lies in a 'perfect grasp of the 7 notes' and not in perfecting 'specific school of music' , we erroneously assume that our own school of music alone is authentic music. This is our ignorance related to our power of singing and thus intrinsically to the 'person' in us . Our further course of actions/activities would be directed based on this error. This is how
we could understand Avidya and Brahman. Hope this helps

  

Shri Rammohan
