Namaste Sadaji,

  

"Anivacaneeyam" probably literally means, which cannot be properly verbally expressed. It is somewhat like "Achintya", which means what one cannot really figure out. Jagat-Vijayi Keshav Kashmiri Bhatta initiated Chaitanya Mahaprabhu to Bhedabheda doctrine,and gave him the 16-word Mahamantra. Later on, the Chaitanya school started calling that as "Achintya Bhedabheda" doctrine.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna
