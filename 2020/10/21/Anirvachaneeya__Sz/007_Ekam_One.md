Thank you sir, for lucid explanation. However, i would like to raise the following *for all the Advaitins here* and wait more insights.  

  

"Gold ring is Gold. Ring is not eternal but Gold is".  

  

That is true. But, a Gold ring is made up of three things.  
  
1. **Gold**. The material which the eternal and truth.  
2. **The workmanship** : Which is a result of Brahman expressing itself in the form of workmanship.  
3. **The connoisseur** : which is again a result of Brahman looking at the ring and appreciating its qualities.  
  
Workmanship and ability to appreciate the workmanship, are also manifestations of Brahman, but where do they end up when they "subside"? ...  

  

In other words, How do we explain these three aspects of a Gold Ring, from the points of view of "SAT-CHIT-ANANDA" and MAYA /"Anirvachaneeya".  

  

Ekam.
