Namaste Sri Sadananda-ji,

  

  

To say that everything is nAmkevAste is to say that dharma / sAdhana is also

nAmkevAste or mithya.

  

This contradicts the fact that dharma is also sanAtana or eternal.

  

So I think accepting Brahman as manifesting through all names/forms

or Vishwaroopa is the correct vedic way.

  

Sri Shankara in his BS bhAshya equates saguna brahman with Nirguna Brahman.  

  

Looking upon the entire universe as that supreme person/Brahman still supports

adviata without denying dvaita :)

  

  

Namaste,  
Suresh
