Suresh - PraNAms

  

Namkevaste - is only from the absolute point.

  

At transactional point - you are different, I am different the world is different - this reference level is called vyaavaharika statyam -Hence Dharmas, etc are valid. This world and creation at this level are anaadi hence Sanatana dharma is also Sanatana only at that reference.

  

At the absolute level, there is no dharma no adharma - it is one without a second - sajaati, vijaati, swagata bheda rahitam - says Shankara.

  

Oneness is from the point of paaramaarthika level.

  

Similarly, the world in the dream is also real for the dreamer who is in the dream. Only a weaker says the dream is not real.

  

Hence naamevaaste statement is a scriptural statement - vaachaarambhanam vikaaraH naamadheyam - starting from pure existence that was alone before creation.

  

Hope I am clear.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
