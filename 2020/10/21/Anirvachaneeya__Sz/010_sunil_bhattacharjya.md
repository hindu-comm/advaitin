Namaste Sadaji,

  

If you permit me I wish to add a couple of sentences here. When Lord Buddha saw the sufferings of an old man and a diseased man, and then he saw a corpse being taken for creation, he felt that life is full of miseries, and he thought that to end this all, one has to find a way to get out of the cycle of life and death. Once one has this sort of vairagya, then one does start thinking of liberation, but that does not mean that one can afford to disregard the facts of life, as long as one is not yet liberated. If one direagars the facts of this life, the sufferings will be still more than what it is.  

  

SO THIS LIFE IS REAL AS LONG AS WE ARE IN THIS LIFE,AND LIVE THIS LIFE AS THE PAST WISE SAGES HAD ADVISED US TO DO, BUT NEVER LOSE SIGHT OF OUR ULTIMATE GOAL, EVEN FOR A MOMENT.

  

Jai Shri Krishna

Sunilk B.
