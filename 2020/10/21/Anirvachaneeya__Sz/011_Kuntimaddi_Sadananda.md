Sunilji - PraNAms

  

You are right. At each reference point, the experiences appear to be real. Even the dream experiences are real for the dreamer while he is in the dream. Only when one is awake to the higher state one can see the unreality of the experienced-reality. In the case of a dream, one cannot develop the requisite Viveka to recognize that the dream is not real while one is dreaming.

  

On the other hand, in the waking state, the waker can develop the requisite Viveka - (nithya anitya vastu viveka) using shaastra, yukti and anubhava (in that order) to recognize or realize that the experienced duality is not reality but only apparent reality as we hear in the story - even this will pass away. Hence Veveka-vairaagya- samaadi shatka sampaati and mumukshutvam are needed to transcend the experiences and also dharma and adharma too while still paying the obligatory duties that one is born with. The Live drama continues whether one is realized or not - Yet the real actor does not forget that he is only playing the drama of Life - for fun. That is what the stories of Rama and Krishna teach us. I am watching Jaanaki Ramudu (Telugu version) series on you-tube and like it very much!

  

Remember Buddha did not eliminate the suffering only taught how to transcend the suffering in the visualization of the truth.

Goudapaada's analysis of the reality of the world in the Vaitatya Prakarana applies - as you have quoted before- adouantecha yannaasti vartamaanepi tattadaa.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
