Ekamji - PraNAms

  

With a name like that you should have the answer too.

  

In the creation of rings and bangles etc from the gold - we have upaadaana kaarana (material cause) is different from the nimitta kaaraana (intelligent cause - Goldsmith).

  

However for the creation of the world, - it is EKAM as your name suggests. The nimitta and upaadana kaarana locused on one - Ekam - hence it is adviteeyam. Scripture say He himself became many. karanam - kaaranam - kartaa - says vishnusahasranaamavali - all are Vishnu (as you can see I am a pakka vaishnavate).

  

So you created this universe from yourself and how can I explain how it came. The dream example is also provided by the scripture where both material and intelligent are one and the same - the waker's mind with chidaabhaasa.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
