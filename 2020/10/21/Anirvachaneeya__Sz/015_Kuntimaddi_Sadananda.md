Suresh - PraNAms

  

I come from the Vishishtadvaita philosophy. I may mention that my father who has written many books on Vishishtaadvaita was offered to head the Parakalam matham - but he declined it - As the story goes my mother did not agree for his taking sanyaasa. I have transcribed his commentary on the ADaal's Tiruppavai taking the fist paasuram. This is included in my recent book 'Self and the Supreme' recently published by the Indic Academy. We still have to publish his voluminous commentary on Shree Vedanta Deshika's Rahashyatraya saara.

  

Bhagavan Ramanuja accounts for nimitta upaadana kaaranaas - - accepts sajaati vijaati bheda rahitam but not swagata bheda rahitam - since he accounts for internal differences of jeeva-jagat-Iswara with the organic relationship with the Brahman, identified with Lord Vishnu.

  

Late Shreeman S.M.S. Chari maama (he published extensively in English on Vishishtaadvaita and also translated the Shatadhuushanii of Vedanta Desika) stayed with me when he came to the US and taught me Bhagavan Ramanuja's Sri Bhasya on Brahmasuutra. He used to discuss it with me a lot but could not convince me of the concept of Moksha in the Vishishtaadvaita tradition. I discussed the seven objections of Bhagavan Ramanuja on Avidya of Advaita in my series 'Journey Beyond: A Non-Dual Approach' in response to the questions posed by a Vishistaadvaitin on this listserve, many years ago.

  

Anyway, I do not wish to go into discussions of how Bhagavan Ramanuja interprets the Ch. Up mantras - Vaachaarambhanam vikaara and also tat tvam asi statements. He uses visheshana vesheshyaabhyaam samaanaadhikaranam to account for the tat tvam asi.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
