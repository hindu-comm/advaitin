Namaste Sri Sada-ji,

  

It is great to know you have Sri Vaishnava background and thanks for sharing the details.

I am humble student of the currrent parakAla matam AchArya. 🙂

  

The statement that Sri Ramanuja accepts svagata bhedam in Brahman is not correct.

Can you provide some reference? I am curious.

  

As Brahman is the Self (antaryami atma) indwelling and supporting the universe as body, it cannot have

any svagata bhedam within itself.

  

Yes, there is inseparable oneness between Brahman and the universe. That does not mean  

Brahman itself has svagata bheda. The relationship between independent and dependent

will always be in the form of soul-body.

  

The svaroopa of body is to serve the soul. So the algorithm of body-soul perfectly captures

the nature of both Brahman and the jiva/jada.

  

All identification of name/forms culminate in Brahman only as the self alone is identified

through the body. So that is the Adviteeyatva ofVishishtAdvaita Brahman.

  

Brahman as the Self always remains the upAdAna/material and nimitta/efficient cause

for the universe/body.

  

So my question still remains :



Why should we still consider the world as unreal? If it is so, even the statement that

theworld is unreal will end up as unreal right?

  

My main intention here is not to refute Advaita but to unite all Hindus belonging to all the

three darshanas. This I think is possible only when we identify the entire universe as that

Brahman or Supreme purusha. This alone includes and transcends all kind of views.

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
