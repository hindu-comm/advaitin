Dear Sureshji,

  

Sadaji will reply to you, but permit me to add my 2 cents. Adi Shankara believes in the Ajatavada, and howsoever transient the world is, he still calls the world as Vyaaharika Satya and not Vyavaharika Mithya, and the verse saying the "Jaganmithya" is from one Upanishada, and that is what the world appears to a Jivanmukta and not necessarily to you and me.

  

Secondly, according to the Mahabharata, Lord Krishna spoke 620 verses, but in the common version, it has only 575 verses, due to the grave blunder of Bhaskara Bhatta, a few centuries before Ramanujacharya. So Ramanujacharya did not know that the Bhagavad gita endorses Ajatavada.

  

Best wishes,
