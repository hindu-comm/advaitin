Glad to know that you are blessed with Parakala Matham and Parakala Swami. My salutations to him.

My father has written close to 14 volumes of commentary on Vedanta Deshika's Rahasyatraya Saara, in Telugu. Shreeman Chinna Narayana Jeera (he knew my father very well) said he would publish it. My brother took the originals and handed over to them - unfortunately, they lost the whole set. We are struggling hard to publish the work based on the rough copy we have. I am attaching the first few pages of the recent book 'Self and the Supreme' that starts with Matrudevo bhava and Pitrudev bhava - then to Acharya and Isvara. Those know Telugu can appreciate more.

Vishishtadvaita, as I understand, subscribes to jeeva-jeeva bhinnatam, jeeva-jagat bhinnatvam, Jeeva-Iswara Bhinnatvam and Jagat-Iswara bhinnatvam. Moksha involves a jeeva going to Vikunta and sevring the Lord. Jeevas are anupmaanaat. Iswara pervads the jeevas and jagat as antaryamin yet different from both. He is controller and supporter of both just as the individual soul pervades the entire body. Organs of the body differ but the purpose is to serve the individual jeeva – hence the relationship between jeeva-jagat and Iswara is like organs of the body related to the Jeeva (called organic relationship). From the antaryamin point, there are no divisions but from the point of existence – sat – the existence of jeevas and jagat are different even though the Lord supports the whole thing as in Visharuupa darshanam in the 11^(th) Ch. There are also nitya mukta jeevas – like Maha Lakshmi, Vishvaksena (instead of Ganesha), Garuda, etc. My book goes into the details of the Iswara Darshanam – analysis of the 11^(th) Chapter of Geeta and also on Adhyaasa Bhashya of Adi Shankara.

Anyway, this listserve is catered to Advaita Vedanta. There used to Vishistadvaita group – Maniji used to run it. Now it is not active.

Hari Om!

Sadananda
