Namaskaram Raman-ji,

  

Your question is interesting. I think it is related to the question: How does one regard a baby's consciousness of the world? Is it like a jnani? But is it sameas a jnani's? Perhaps Dennis-ji or Sada-ji can answer this better. It is also related to whether the one who sleeps has "experiential" knowledge of advaita?  

  

As far as animals, I understand that their behaviour impelled by their gunas is in conformance to their svadharma, so they do not accumulate punya or papa samskaras. The upadhis of a jiva in the animal body-mind do not allow the possibility to go spiritually up or down in that life; whereas the human body-mind allows seeking of moksha or animal-existence. Our choice actively impacts the direction in our spiritual journey, whereas for an animal it is more a station of existencethat they have to experience in this life-time (due to pastkarma done in a human body?).
