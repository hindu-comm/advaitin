Namaste Sunil-ji,

  

Actually vyAvahArika satya = mithya = nAmkevAste (as per Sada-ji)

  

So having two kinds/levels of reality is another kind of dvaita or a split in reality :)

If I am an advaitin should I do sandhyavandana / nitya karma or just give up everything?

Neo advaitin's recommend not to do anything. Traditional advaita has no valid reason to

justify performance of nitya karma.

  

\<

Secondly, according to the Mahabharata, Lord Krishna spoke 620 verses, but in the common version, it has only 575 verses, due to the grave blunder of Bhaskara Bhatta, a few centuries before Ramanujacharya. So Ramanujacharya did not know that the Bhagavad gita endorses Ajatavada.

\>

  

This is something new to me. Do you have any pramAna for the above? Sri rAmAnuja formally studied advaita

and it is hard to accept his advaita teacher did not teach ajAtavAda in B.Gita.

Sri Shankara also does not mention "ajAtavada" anywhere in his Gita bhAshya.

  

ajAtavAda is same as mithyatva. Whether it is true or false either way it cancels itself.

  

So, I think there is a better way to establish Advaita without opposing Dvaita/V.Advaita  

and that is to consider the entire universe as Saguna Brahman. This view perfectly

aligns with Purusha Suktam, Rudram, Chamakam, BG 11th Ch, Vishnu sahasra nAma.

Brahma Sutra is also called ShArIraka shAstra.

  

The absolute dependence of the all embodied jivas on the universe for existence

cannot be denied by anyone. The organic oneness and the existential oneness

everything gets satisfied. Service to universe culminates in the service to Ishwara/Brahman.

Performance of nitya karma and varnAsrama duties as a service to this virAt purusha

gains validity.

  

Whether one worships Shiva or Vishnu or any pratIka it cannot be anything other

than this universal person.

  

Just think about it 🙂

  

Namaste,  
Suresh
