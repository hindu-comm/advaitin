Namaste Sri Sada-ji,

  

Thanks a lot for sharing the doc and other details.

  

Just wanted to clarify that in V.Advaita every jiva is considered equal as they are all essentially

jnAnAnandamaya.But they all derive their jnAnAnanda from the indwelling antaryAmi Brahman.

This is just like an iron piece acquiring magnetic properties when in contact with a magnet.

Brahman is also individually and completely available / established in all jiva/jada based on many

prmAnAs like "Sarva bhootastham ekam nArAyaNam". This is another reasonfor the equality.

  

But there is no svaroopa aikya at the same time there is no separation also. This difference and inseparable

onenesswhich is similarto body-soul is called apritaksiddha-vishesha and this satisfies all aikya and bheda

vAkyAs in the veda. As acknowledged by you, there is no svagata-bheda in Brahman in V.Advaita.

  

Sri Rangapriya Swamiji who is from V.Advaita tradition, explains the name Vishnu as the

"All pervading and indwelling Self". This should be acceptable to Advaitins in my opinion.

  

Infact Swamiji had followers/students from all the three vedanta traditions. 🙂  

  

Whatever may be the world view, the fact that Brahman is established as the inner Self

of all is the uniting factor for all vedantic traditions.

  

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
