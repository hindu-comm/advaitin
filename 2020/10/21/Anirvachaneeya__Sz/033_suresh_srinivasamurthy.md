Namaste Sri Putran-ji,

  

Yes, Hindus can unite based on "Ekam sat viprAh bahuda vadanti" and this points to

Vishwaroopi sarvanAma vAchya Brahman.

  

Your explanation of Advaitic truth which is commonly present in all is another way of arriving

at the same all pervading and indwelling Brahman

  

Truth is that which never changes and it is revealed when our thoughts words and deeds are aligned.

So Truth and dharma always go hand in hand.

  

In the embodied state we absolutely depend on the entire universe for our existence. So looking

upon that universe as Brahman aligns with our experience also instead of outrightly saying that the

world is mithya. While still in the embodied state it is not correct to reject the world. Of course what

can be rejected is kamya karma but not dharma / duty.

  

In the form of the universe Brahman is teaching sanAtana dharma - to unconditionally engage

in parArtha karma. Every entity in nature is unconditionally sustaining us. So we humans have

to reciprocate the same way.

  

Advaitic truth of the oneness in existence is not lost in this view of accepting the reality of world.

  

The world is the tat portion and the body is the tvam portion and both needs to be united.

The sva-para bheda should be eliminated.Only then total selflessness or total Selfhood can arise.

  

Probably this is a new view still based Veda/Gita/Sutra. But I think this universal view encompasses

all kinds of views. There can be opposition when our views are particular. But when we are looking

at everything there cannot be any kind of objection.

  

Sorry for blabbering. I could not control my thoughts 🙂

  

Namaste,  
Suresh
