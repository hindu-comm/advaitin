The arguments that you have presented are typical that I find in Vishishtadvaita traditional criticism of Advaita.

From a logical point: At vyavaharika level also – the ontological status of Gold is different from the rings and bangles. Both are real but the degree of reality of the two is different. This is what scripture says too – vaachaarambhanam vikaaraH – taking this gold and ornament example. In science, we call it as transformation-less transformation (in Sanskrit – vivarta). Gold still remains as gold, yet there is apparent transformation into rings, bangles, bracelets, etc., each with their own attributes different from those of others. Ring may undergo destructive transformation into Bangle (in Sanskrit called parinaama). From the Gold point, there is no real transformation – paaramaarthika, and from the ring point there is transformation. Ring may cry like Arjuna crying, Krishna can say you are gold – no need to cry – avyakaani bhuutani vyakta madyhaani bhaarataa .

Hence Krishna says later – mayaa tatam idam sarvam jagat avyakta muurtina – mastaani sarva bhuutani. I pervade this entire universe in unmanifested form. All beings are in Me. And in the very next sloka, he says – na cha mastaani bhuutani – There are no beings in me. The first one from the vyaavahaarika drushti and the second from the paaramaarthika drushti. From Bhagavan Ramanuja’s point, there are only two – sat and asat, each mutually exclusive. Now, is Ring sat or asat – It exists since from the utility point it differs from the bangle. However, from the reality point, it does not really exists – it is only a name for a form – what exists is only gold – that is beyond the names and forms (in this relative case). Hence the scriptural declaration that rings and bangles are – vaachaarambhanam vikaaraH. What is the truth – loham ityeva satyam – Gold alone is real.

Hence that which never undergoes any change – trikaala abhaaditam – satyam. Now Krishna’s statement from two reference points is clear – all beings are in Me – and yet no beings are in Me. Gold also can declare all ornaments are in Me or I pervade all ornaments (referring to only gold ornaments in this example), and yet Gold can still declare – there are really no ornaments in Me – I was gold, I am gold and I shall be gold.

For jeeva vyaavahaarika satyam will remain until he understands the abheda statement – aham brahmaasmi. For a dreamer the dream world is real until he wakes up.

Brahman means infiniteness – bruhad dhatu. In infinity, there are no parts, mathematically. Scripture also defines Consciousness is infinite – prajnaanam brahma (it uses a converse statement – that is necessary and sufficient condition) Chandogya starts with statement – eka vijnaanena sarva vijnanam bhavati as the thesis and then discusses the sat aspect and declares that you are that sat – tat tvam asi – swetaketu.
