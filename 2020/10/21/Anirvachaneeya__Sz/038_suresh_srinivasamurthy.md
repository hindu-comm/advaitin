Namaste Sada-ji,

  

Can you tell who is presenting this argument in support of jagan mithyAtva.

I mean the entity denoted by Sada-ji 🙂

  

It cannot be Nirguna Brahman as it is nishkriya and it cannot be jada also.

  

I would like to re-iterate that I am not trying to refute Advaita here, but

trying to establish the same without denying reality of the world.

This is needed in order to reconcile all vedAntic traditions and also

to protect sanAtana dharma.

  

Interestingly there are many Advaitins that I know who believe that

Dvaita is another way presenting Advaita 🙂

  

BTW, Sri Krishna's statement "Na Cha maststhAni bhUtAni" implies "as if" they

are not there due to Sri Krishna's/Brahman'sasangatva - this is as per

Sri Shankara bhAshya only. Moreover the words Ishwaram, bhUta bhruth,

and bhUtabhAvanapoints to Ishwara / Saguna Brahmanonly.
