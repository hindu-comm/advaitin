Namaste Sri Sada-ji,

  

Gold can also say "All ornaments are in me (potentially) and it can also say 'I am all ornaments' right?

The point is the same Gold/Brahman is identified through every name and form.

  

The identification is through sAmAnAdhikaraNyam where Brahman as the inner Soul alone

is identified through all name/forms.

  

The entire jagat is a pratIka or body or idol in which Brahma drushti can be made

As per the pratIkAdhikaraNa Sutra BrahmadrushtirutkarshAt' of Brahma Sutra.

  

Brahman in its unmanifested form can be taken to be Nirguna Brahman as it can never

be known. But the same Brahman is making itself known in the form of the Jagat.

  

Sri Shankara also teaches the oneness of nirguna and saguna Brahman in many  

places in his BS bhAshya:

  

Here is an extract from the bhAshya for the sutra :तदनन्यत्वमारम्भणशब्दादिभ्यः ॥ १४ ॥ (2-1-14)

  

ननु कूटस्थब्रह्मात्मवादिन एकत्वैकान्त्यात् ईशित्रीशितव्याभावे ईश्वरकारणप्रतिज्ञाविरोध इति चेत् , न ; अविद्यात्मकनामरूपबीजव्याकरणापेक्षत्वात्सर्वज्ञत्वस्य।[‘ तस्माद्वा एतस्मादात्मन आकाशः सम्भूतः’ (तै. उ. २। १। १)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Taitiriya?page=2&id=T_C02_S01_V01&hl=%20%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%A8%20%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%83%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%83)इत्यादिवाक्येभ्यःनित्यशुद्धबुद्धमुक्तस्वरूपात्सर्वज्ञात्सर्वशक्तेरीश्वराज्जगज्जनिस्थितिप्रलयाः,चेतनात्प्रधानादन्यस्माद्वा — इत्येषोऽर्थः प्रतिज्ञातः —[‘ जन्माद्यस्य यतः’ (ब्र. सू. १। १। २)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/BS?page=1&id=BS_C01_S01_V02&hl=%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%20%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%83)इति ;  

  

Yes, jagat is mithya or mAyA / mohini roopa of Brahman. It is mithya if we go after it as an object of enjoyment.  

But if we look upon it as Ishwara / Brahman and engage in its service through pravrutti and nivrutti it will be turn

out to parama sathya.

  

The jagat as the universal purusha can be identified as Vishnu and Shiva/Rudra also.

There is a mantra in Sri Rudram - Om namo bhagavate rudrAya Vishnave mrutyurme pAhi"

  

Also in Bhagavad Gita Sri Krishna while showing Vishwaroopa says " kAlosmi lokaih kshaya kruth pravruttah".

  

So this view supports / unites Vishnu and Shiva devotees🙂

  

Hope this opens up some new way of thinking. Please let me know if this understanding is not correct.
