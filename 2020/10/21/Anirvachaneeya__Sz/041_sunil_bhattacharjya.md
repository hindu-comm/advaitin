Dear Sureshji,  

  

Here we cannot be guided by the false notion of protecting the vedanta traditions. The dvaita (I won't call it a Vedanta tradition at all) is based on the unupnishadic and imaginary view of "atattvan asi". I can call it a tattvavada, though their tattva is not supported by the Upanshads nor by the Original Bhagavad Gita, with its Ajativada verse,
which calls all the phenomenal world as anitya and unreal too. Less said the better of the Vishishtadvaita view, as they too seem to be confused. Swami Vivekananda searched the whole of India, from Kashmir to Kanyakumari, for the commentary of Bodhayana, but could not find any. It seems somebody had hoodwinked Ramanujacharya. Must be someone from the Bhedabhaeda school could have tricked him.

  

Good wishes,

skb
