'atatvamasi' is the explanation by Shree Madhvacharya - that is dvaita philosophy. They do not accept Brahman as the material cause for the universe. Bhagavan Ramanuja (vishishtaadvaita) accepts Brahman as the material cause and he uses visheshana visheshaabhyaam samaanaadhikaraNam to explain tat tvam asi. Bhagavat paada Shankara uses bhaadhaayam samaanaadhikaranam as in jahat ajahat lakshana. Goudeeya philosophy closely follows dvaita philosophy.

  

In explaining Brahmaarpanam sloka of Geeta, Ramanuja uses upaasanaayaam samaanaadhikaraNam while Shankara sticks to the Bhaadaayam samaanaadhikaraNam.
