Namaste Sadaji,

  

The world of arguments is strange, such that one can twist anything to prove one's view as correct. Sometimes "kutarka" appears more genuine that "Sutarka".  

  

Lord Ram talked about four types of Mukti and advised his greatest devotee Hanumanji to meditate on his avayaya state and attain the
Nirvikalpa samadhi, and it seems Hanumanji wanted to enjoy the Bhaktirasa for some more time. Thus the attachment to the Saguna Brahman can be very great, but that too can eventually lead to the Advaita Mukti, through the help of the Lord. Did not the Lord say in the Bhagavata that he gives the ultimate jnana (the Advaita Jna) to his bhaktas? We may not know but the Lord does not forget that the creation has an end. Advaita mukti is the ultimate mukti. That is why I sounded earlier, as to which type of Mukti, the Vishishtadvains want ? The sad part is that out of ignorance the Dvaitins abd Vishishtadvaitns have been hostile to the Advains. Shri Madhvacharya's unforgettable crime is that he called Adi Shankara as the demon reborn. So also the Vshishtadvains were not far behind in finding imaginary fault of the Advaita philosophy.  

  

Jai Shri Kishna

Sunilk B.
