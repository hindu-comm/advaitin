Namaste Sunil-ji,

  

ajAtavAda is true with respect to Brahman. VedAntins from all traditions accept that.

But it is also a fact that all names/forms arise, remain and return back to Brahman.

  

The moment you think and talk about ajAtavAda, the related thoughts and words are also born.

Remember that these jada entities are not born / created without any chetana acting/willing.  

  

That is why Sri Shankara teaches the oneness of the nityashuddhabuddha Brahman  

with SarvagyaSarvaShaktiyutaIshwara.

  

Now the same question arises as to who is presenting this ajAtavAda.  

it cannot be Nirguna Brahman as it is nishkriya. It is not Saguna Brahman as it isnot sarvgya.

It cannot be jada also. So obviously it is the jiva stuck in the body.

  

From that point of samsari jiva - atat + tvamasi of Dvaita is also valid.

  

Interestingly it is not negating the advaitatva of Brahman who is established as the inner

Self - antaryAmi paramAtma of the jiva also.

  

The universal Self is the origin/abode/destination of all the infinite little selves.

  

The fact that Brahman is there everywhere is accepted by all traditions.

  

The VishishtAdvaitic view of looking upon the entire universe as the body/pratIka of

that Brahman provides validity to the performance of dharma as a service to Brahman.

  

Every food that you feed others or put in your mouth is a yagya performed onto that

Brahman who is outside as well as inside.

  

Hope this settles and reconciles all differences.  

  

Namaste

Suresh
