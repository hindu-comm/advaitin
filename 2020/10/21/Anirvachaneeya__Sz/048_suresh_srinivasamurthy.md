Namaste Sri Sada-ji,

  

It is not my statement that all names arise, remain and return back to Brahman.

There are many scriptural statements in support of that.

  

I am reproducing Sri Shankara BS bhAshya vAkya in support of that:

Here is an extract from the bhAshya for the sutra :तदनन्यत्वमारम्भणशब्दादिभ्यः ॥ १४ ॥ (2-1-14)

  

ननु कूटस्थब्रह्मात्मवादिन एकत्वैकान्त्यात् ईशित्रीशितव्याभावे ईश्वरकारणप्रतिज्ञाविरोध इति चेत् , न ; अविद्यात्मकनामरूपबीजव्याकरणापेक्षत्वात्सर्वज्ञत्वस्य।[‘ तस्माद्वा एतस्मादात्मन आकाशः सम्भूतः’ (तै. उ. २। १। १)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Taitiriya?page=2&id=T_C02_S01_V01&hl=%20%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%A8%20%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%83%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%83)इत्यादिवाक्येभ्यःनित्यशुद्धबुद्धमुक्तस्वरूपात्सर्वज्ञात्सर्वशक्तेरीश्वराज्जगज्जनिस्थितिप्रलयाः,चेतनात्प्रधानादन्यस्माद्वा — इत्येषोऽर्थः प्रतिज्ञातः —[‘ जन्माद्यस्य यतः’ (ब्र. सू. १। १। २)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/BS?page=1&id=BS_C01_S01_V02&hl=%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%20%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%83)इति ;  

  

I know all such creation statements comes under vyahAra in Advaita which is ultimately mithya.

But my only concern is this mithyavAda does not support Hindu dharma at all.

Instead it leads to all kinds of confusion that all religions are true or no religion is true.  

  

Traditional Advaita sanyasins engage in trikAla Ishwara puja. What is the justification for that?  

Please do not look at me as a fanatic V.Advaitin trying to refute Advaita.  

  

I have very high regards for Advaitins and I am a sincere truth seeker with the main intention

to unify Hindus from all different traditions.

  

My question as to who is the person/entity that is trying to establish jagat mithyAtva still  

remains unanswered. But I will keep quiet for now.

  

Namaste

Suresh
