> Namaste Suresh ji
>
> Apologies for interjecting. The idea of finding common ground amongst
> sanatana Dharma traditions is quite noble.
>
>   
>
> The only point to note is that knowledgable advaitins never say
> "Everything goes; all 'religions' are the same. There is no need to
> protect Dharma etc."
>
>   
>
> Sri Vidyaranya, a staunch advaitin and facilitator of the Vijayanagara
> samrajya, did not flinch from protection of Dharma.
>
> Madhusudana Saraswati another great acharya organized the dashanami
> sannyasis to defend Dharma against islamic fanatics.
>
>   
>
>   
>
> Popular notions of half-baked Advaita born of not studying Advaita
> properly might indeed lead some to such conclusions that Dharma can be
> ignored since it is ultimately mithyA. But none of the Advaita
> Acharyas say anything misleading like that; as you know, one of the
> key texts commented on by advaitic Acharyas like Shankara, Madhusudana
> Saraswati, Ananda Giri etc., has an injunction to fight a battle for
> Dharma for the protaganist - even while the text itself has been
> clearly shown by Advaita Acharyas as being uncompromisingly unfolding
> Advaita.
>
>   
>
> Advaita helps free all followers of sanatana Dharma from sectarian
> theology and is the best medicine for dharmic unity. So there is
> possibly no need to reinvent the wheel or imagine that Advaita fosters
> what you refer to as mithyAvAda. Because Advaita only fosters
> brahmavAda.
