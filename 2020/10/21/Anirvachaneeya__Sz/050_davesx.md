\< *no need to reinvent the wheel or imagine that Advaita fosters what you refer to as mithyAvAda. Because Advaita only fosters brahmavAda*.\>  
  

Namaste Ragav-ji: If I may interject also, your last sentence fits nicely in the Advaita camp.  

  

Objects reified as standalone, independent and autonomous are considered mithya since they depend on and borrow their reality from the non-objective Self or Brahman.  

  

Modern science now supports the Vedantic perspective that objects are a derivative of Energy or Light, except the immensity of the light of Brahman is simultaneously conscious, shining peacefully by Itself, dependent on nothing.  

  

So all objects are a cinematographic display of Light forms and to see it as having inherent autonomy is deceptive. Advaitins remain ever cognizant that Brahman is the source of the evanescent display.  

  

-dave
