Namaste Sri Venkataraghavan-ji,

  

The question still remains who is teaching all these mithyAtva of jagat and who is the one currently engaging in sAdhana? It cannot be jada padArtha like mind, ego etc...it cannot be nirguna/saguna Brahman also. But it also cannot be anyone other than the atman. It cannot be superimposed also because the atman is willingly using the instruments of mind and body.

I agree that Atman is unaffected and asanga. But in the embodied state its association with the body/prakriti cannot be denied. The body/mind cannot act by themselves and so the example of flower/crystal is not convincing enough. Sri Shankara makes this clear in the following sutra.

कर्ता शास्त्रार्थवत्त्वात्॥ ३३ ॥

तद्गुणसारत्वाधिकारेणैवापरोऽपि जीवधर्मः प्रपञ्च्यते। कर्ता च अयं जीवः स्यात्। कस्मात् ? शास्त्रार्थवत्त्वात् — एवं च ‘ यजेत’ ‘ जुहुयात्’ ‘ दद्यात्’ इत्येवंविधं विधिशास्त्रमर्थवद्भवति ; अन्यथा तदनर्थकं स्यात् ; तद्धि कर्तुः सतः कर्तव्यविशेषमुपदिशति ; न च असति कर्तृत्वे तदुपपद्येत। तथेदमपि शास्त्रमर्थवद्भवति —[‘ एष हि द्रष्टा श्रोता मन्ता बोद्धा कर्ता विज्ञानात्मा पुरुषः’ (प्र. उ. ४। ९)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Prashna?page=4&id=PR_C04_V09&hl=%20%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%B7%20%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9F%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%AC%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%83)इति ॥ ३३ ॥

  

Sri Shankara also recommends doing BrahmopAsana as opposed AtmOpAsana in objects like Aditya in the BS PrateekAdhikarana:  

ब्रह्मदृष्टिरुत्कर्षात् ॥ ५ ॥

तस्मात्नब्रह्मदृष्टिरादित्यादिष्वित्येवंप्राप्तेब्रूमः— ब्रह्मदृष्टिरेवआदित्यादिषुस्यादिति।कस्मात्?उत्कर्षात्;एवम्उत्कर्षेणआदित्यादयोदृष्टाभवन्ति

वाक्यशेषोऽपिचद्वितीयानिर्देशेनआदित्यादीनेवउपास्तिक्रिययाव्याप्यमानान्दर्शयति—[‘सयएतदेवंविद्वानादित्यंब्रह्मेत्युपास्ते’ (छा. उ. ३। १९। ४)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Chandogya?page=3&id=Ch_C03_S19_V04&hl=%E0%A4%B8%20%E0%A4%AF%20%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%AC%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%87)[‘योवाचंब्रह्मेत्युपास्ते’ (छा. उ. ७। २। २)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Chandogya?page=7&id=Ch_C07_S02_V02&hl=%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%9A%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%AC%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%87)[‘यःसङ्कल्पंब्रह्मेत्युपास्ते’ (छा. उ. ७। ४। ३)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Chandogya?page=7&id=Ch_C07_S04_V03&hl=%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%83%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%99%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B2%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%AC%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%87)इतिचयत्तूक्तम्—ब्रह्मोपासनमेवात्रआदरणीयंफलवत्त्वायेति,तदयुक्तम्,उक्तेनन्यायेनआदित्यादीनामेवउपास्यत्वावगमात्;फलंतुअतिथ्याद्युपासनइवआदित्याद्युपासनेऽपिब्रह्मैवदास्यति,सर्वाध्यक्षत्वात्;वर्णितंचैतत्[‘फलमतउपपत्तेः’ (ब्र. सू. ३। २। ३८)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/BS?page=3&id=BS_C03_S02_V38&hl=%E0%A4%AB%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%A4%20%E0%A4%89%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%83)इत्यत्र।ईदृशंचअत्रब्रह्मणउपास्यत्वम्,यत्प्रतीकेषुतद्दृष्ट्यध्यारोपणम्—प्रतिमादिष्विवविष्ण्वादीनाम्॥ ५ ॥

  

So the realization of the oneness of the tat aspect in Aditya/Universe which kind of acts like the fulfilling counterpart of the current state of predicament of the tvam pada vAchi jiva becomes very important

  

That is why I say doing Brahma drishti in the entire universe instead of denying it provides more validity to all the dhArmic pravrutti and nivrutti activities.

The oneness of triputi is not lost as the meditator is not external to the meditated.

  

This view of looking upon the entire universe as Brahman actually reconciles all kinds of darshanas within the dhArmic traditions. It also goes very well with our experience also as it is natural for Hindus to see God in all entities in prakriti. It also refutes all kinds of avaidhika dharshanas that locate God somewhere far away in heaven. :)
