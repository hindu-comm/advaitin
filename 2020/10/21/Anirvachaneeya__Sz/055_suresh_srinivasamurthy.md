Namaste Sri Ram Chandran ji,

  

My intention to reconcile all views is to basically to convince myself as I believe all these systems are complementary and not opposed to each other.

All these traditions have produced great saints and so they all must be valid. I firmly believe that all the vedAntic traditions are like different snAna ghattas leading to the same ganga of Brahman. As you said, the way one relates to this Brahman which is everywhere could be different depending on one's upAsana.

  

But still I think there is a need to reconcile all of them in my own consciousness so that I continue to revere all of them and this kind of unification also provides a broader basis for Hindus to unite, especially in these times when there is so much threat from other A-dhArmic (A-brAhamic)views.

  

I think looking upon the entire universe as Brahman which has support from Vedas, Gita, Sutra provides a common viewpoint for all Hindus to unite. So far I have not seen any objection from anyone against this viewpoint.

  

When I tried to do this kind of re-conciliation in the "Science-Religion-Philosophy" group few years ago, many of my Dvaitin friends told me it is not possible and to leave the traditions as is 🙂. Many of my V.Adavaita friends also felt the same way. Actually, I know many scholars personally in this forum and so tried here as I believe advaitins are more broad / open minded, and I still believe they are🙂.

  

But anyway it was very educative experience to discuss with you all. My sincere pranAms.  

  

Namaste

Suresh

  

Regards,
