Namaste,

  

If some non-Hindu asks me why I believe in Veda, just saying that it is apourusheya is not convincing enough. But if I tell them that the Vedic God on whom I depend absolutely for my existence is everywhere around me in the form of the universe it becomes unquestionable. As the entities in nature are sustaining us unconditionally, it becomes our duty to return back in the form ofdharma which is nothing but parArtha karma. So selfless service rendered to the any being or nature would end up as a service to that Supreme Vedic Hindu God. This supreme person is described vividly in Shata rudreeya and purusha Sukta and also in B.Gita 11th Ch. This Supreme being with Sun, Moon and agni as his eyes, could be meditated as Shiva or Vishnu/Narasimha or even as Shakti/Mother and thus it is possible to unite all sub religions within Hindu fold.

  

I am preaching this to myself Sri Putran-ji. But if you see any problem in this view please let me know.

  

Namaste,  
Suresh
