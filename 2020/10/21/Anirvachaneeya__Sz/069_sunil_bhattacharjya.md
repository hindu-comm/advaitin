Dear Ramchandranji,

  

Yes, you certainly have a point that my dig at the Advaita teachers who were humiliated by the Shri Madhvacharya and Shri Ramanujacharya, could have been unwarranted. But I tried to look at the point of view ( or rather the logic) of Shri Madhvacharya and Shri Ramanujacharya, but could not find any. I find in the Vedavyasa's Adhyatmaramayana, that Lord Ram was teaching Vayuputra Hanumanji "tat tvam asi" and not 'atattvam asi". So also I found in another upanishad ( other than Chandogya), where Shri Ramji was talking about "tat tvam asi" . That is why I am unable to accept another person claiming to be a Vayu-putra, trying to prove Vishnu Avatara Lord Ram to be wrong and also Vayu-putra Hanumanji to be wrong in accepting Lord Ram's advice of "tat tvan asi". If Madhvacharya's guru could not reply to his disciple, at least (for the sake of the truth) that guru should have approached the Shringeri math or the Kanchi Kamakoti math. That guru's meekly surrendering to a disciple's wrong notion does really hurt me. So also the "100 faults of Advaita " from the Vishishtadvaita camp also, hurt me no ends. It appeared to me that the Advaitins have no right to complain against the views or criticisms from the Dvaita and Vishishtadvata camps.

  

Regards,

skb  

  

As I look back, it appears to me that probably I should have kept my views t o myself.
