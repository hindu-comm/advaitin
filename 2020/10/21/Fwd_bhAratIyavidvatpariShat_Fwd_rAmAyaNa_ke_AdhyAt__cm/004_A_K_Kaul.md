Jai Shri Ram!

\<The very word "AUM" has come from the name "RAM", i.e. there is verse, which says AUM is "Rama Naamnaa SamutpannaH".

  

It would be nice to see if there is any textual reference. I do not know of any now.\>

  

Mandukya Upanishat, one of the ten principal Upanishadas, on which Acharya Gaudapada has written Karikaas, starts with

ओमेत्येतदक्षरमिदम् सर्वं तस्योपव्याखायनं भूतम्भवद्भविष्यदिति सर्वमोङ्कारेव \|
यचान्यत्त्रिकालातीतम् तदप्योङ्कारेव\|\| and ends with अमात्रश्चतुर्थॉव्यव्हार्य: प्र्पन्चोपशमः शिवोअद्वैत एवमोङ्कार आत्मैव संविशत्यात्मनात्मानं य एवं वेद\|

Similarly, there are other innumerable references whetherin the Rigveda which starts with ऊं अग्निमीडे पुरोहितं or the Gita ॐ इत्येकक्षरं ब्रह्म व्याहरन् मां अनुस्मरन् or the Patanjali Yogadarshan a ईश्वर प्रनिधानाद् वा --तस्य वाचकः प्रणवः ----तज-जपस् तदर्थ भावनं and so on. In fact, there is hardly any Purana or Upanishada एत्च्. that has not extolled Aum!

But then it is impossible to convince some people who do not want to be convivinced!

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!

A K Kaul
