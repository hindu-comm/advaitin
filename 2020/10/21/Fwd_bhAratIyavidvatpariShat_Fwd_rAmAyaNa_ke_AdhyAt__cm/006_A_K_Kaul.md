Dear Shri Subramanian,

Jai Shri Ram!

The Rig-Veda is said to be the earliest Veda supposed to be Apaurusheya.

And Bhagwan Ram has Incarnated after the Rig-Veda as per the tradition and the scriptures.

And since the Rig-Veda starts with Aum, It could not be a derivative of Ram.

Now coming to your question whether there is any reference for "Aum" deriving from the name "Ram" you can rest assured there isn't any.

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!
