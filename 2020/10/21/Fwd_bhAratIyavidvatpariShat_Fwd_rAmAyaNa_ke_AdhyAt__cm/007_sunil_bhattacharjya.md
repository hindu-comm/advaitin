Dear Subbuji,

  

Many people don't know that there was only one Veda till the sage Vedavyasa divided the Vedas. Vedavyasa lived around the time of the Mahabharata war, and he, along with his four disciples, separated the four Vedas, i.e. the Rig, the Yajur, the Sama and Atharva vedas. The remaining portion, comprising the Puranas, the upapuranas and the Epics, became the Fifth Veda. The Ramayana was more than three thousand years older than the Mahabharata and it is pretty certain that the word ***AUM*** could have come from the name of *RAMA*, who told Hanumanji to meditate his ***(Rama's) avyaya state***, particularly when the Kalika purana claims it so. If you can get hold of the kalika Purana, you can check it yourself.  

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
