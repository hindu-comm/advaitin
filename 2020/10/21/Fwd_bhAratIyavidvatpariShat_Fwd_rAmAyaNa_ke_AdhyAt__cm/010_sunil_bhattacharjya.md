Dear Subbuji,

  

I had very bad experience with BVP in the past, particularly when I opposed Aryabhatta's name being written as Aryabhata, as his book is called Aryabhatiya. A number of scholars vehemently protested till a scholar from Bangalore stood by me proving that the Chanda-shastra requited Aryabhatta's book to be mentioned as Aryabhatiya and not aryabhattiya. Most of the members in BVP are just Academic Sanskritists, with the high sounding qualifications and positions, but with no time for lateral thinking, i.e., to think of the applications of what they read in the ancient Sanskrit texts, in the real life problems.  

  

Ask them about what is the Divya-varsha, they will say it is 360 years of man. Ask them when Lord Ram was born, they would say that Lord Ram was born million years ago. Ask them about the dates of Patanjalai, Katyayana and Panini,they would say 2nd century BCE, 3rd century BCE and 4th century BCE respectively. Ask them when Adi Shankara was born, they would say it was in 8th century CE, but ask them to prove it they would remain mum. Ask them the date of Varaha mihira, they would say (c. 505 - c. 587). Ask them the date of Mahabharata war, they would not be able to tell you. The scholars in BVP are not to be blamed, as their training has been such that they are now trapped by the way they were taught and what they were taught. So I don't think anybody in BVP would be able to help if we refer the problem to that forum. However there are a great lot of Sanskrit scholars in your own city, Bangalore, who may be able to help.  

  

Regards,
