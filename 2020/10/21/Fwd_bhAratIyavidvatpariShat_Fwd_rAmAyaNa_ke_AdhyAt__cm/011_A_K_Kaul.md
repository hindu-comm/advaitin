Jai Shri Ram!

If I make a claim that I have read "such and such a statement in a particular work" the burden of proof lies on me and not on you or the members of some other forum.

Thus it is ridiculous, to say the least, to ask you to get hold of xyz work and prove my statements to be correct especially when I am not giving any exact references either as to where in that work that xyz statement has been made.

It is called sending somebody on a fishing expedition.

There is also a very famous saying ,"A bad workman quarrels with his tools"!

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!
