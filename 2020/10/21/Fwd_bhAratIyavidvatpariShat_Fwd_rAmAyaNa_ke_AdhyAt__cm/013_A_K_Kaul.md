Dear moderators of Advaitin forum,

Jai Shri Ram!

Shri Sunil Bhattacharya has made some sweeping statementsabout the members of BVP group and presented them in a very bad light.

I was expecting some comments from you that no reflections should be cast on other forums especially since they are not here to defend themselves.

But there has been nothing but silence from you.

I am myself a member of that august forum and to the best of my knowledge I find almost all its members to be quite well informed and scholars of extraordinary calibre especially about Hindu culture, having scriptural knowledge right from the Vedic lore and correlating it with the latest scientific developments. They have an open mind and listen to others' views pateintly, even if those views are contrarian.

What is all the more pathetic is that Shri Bhattacharya has a lot of confused thinking himself and he wants everybody else to agree with the same as otherwise they have "no time for lateral thinking" according to him.

He has made following insinuations about the members of BVP. And I am refuting each one of them.

1."I had very bad experience with BVP in the past, particularly when I opposed Aryabhatta's name being written as Aryabhata,

Aryabhata is correct and Aryabhatta is wrong.

2. "Ask them about what is the Divya-varsha, they will say it is 360 years of man."

As per all the siddhantas and the scriptures, a divya varsha comprises 360 solar years. We may not agree with that statement, but that does not mean that we can misinterpretthe same.

3."Ask them when Lord Ram was born, they would say that Lord Ram was born million years ago"

Again, since all the siddhantas and Puranas tell us that Tretayuga ended at least 864000 years back, that certainly means that Bhagwan Ram was around 864000 years before today. It is immaterial whether we agree with the same or not, but again, we cannot misinterpret the scriptures.

4. "Ask them about the dates of Patanjalai, Katyayana and Panini,they would say 2nd century BCE, 3rd century BCE and 4th century BCE respectively"

4. If Shri SKB does not agree with those dates, the burden of proof lies on him and he has to tell us as to when Patanjali etc. were actually around and prove his points instead of expecting others to prove them for him!

4."Ask them when Adi Shankara was born, they would say it was in 8th century CE, but ask them to prove it they would remain mum."  

Shri Bhattacharya has not been able to prove that Aadi Shankara was around in 5th century BCE, whereas quite a few scholars have given incontrovertible proofs that Aadi Shankara was really around in eighth century CE. Shri SKB should not thrust his views on others.

5. "Ask them the date of Varaha mihira, they would say (c. 505 - c. 587)."

Varahamihira has referred to Aryabhata in his Panchasiddhantika XV/20, that means the former was around either after Aryabhata or was his contemporary. And since Aryabhata was 23 years old in 499 CE, that means Varahamhira was also around either after that date or at least at the same time.

6." Ask them the date of Mahabharata war, they would not be able to tell you."  
This is a surprising statement! There has been no final conclusion as on date as to when the Mahabharata war took place since different scholars---all of them of impeccablereputation---have arrived at different dates! The well known Prof. R N Iyengar is of the view that it took place in 1478 BCE whereas Nilesh Nielakanth Oak and late Dr. P V Vartak are of the view that it took place on 16th October 5561 BCE.
Prof. Narhari Achar is of the view that it took place in 3067 BCE and so on. So Mr. SKB is making very wrong statements!

7. " So I don't think anybody in BVP would be able to help if we refer the problem to that forum"  

Shri S KB claims that he has read in the Kalika Purana that Aum is Ramanama-samutpannah, so he should not ask others to prove his statement! He should attach the relevant page from the Kalika-Purana himself to vindicate his stand.

With regads and Jai Shri Ram!

A K Kaul
