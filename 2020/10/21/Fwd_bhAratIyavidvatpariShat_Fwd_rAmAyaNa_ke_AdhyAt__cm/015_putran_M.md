Namaskaram,

  

I will say this generally. People may have convictions and sources and methods of knowledge in some specific topics that some others don't regard as valid or coherent enough for a logical discussion. In that case, it may be best to agree to disagree, state our opposing positions, and move on; it may also not be a topic that the general forum is invested in.

  

In our forum, the accepted source of scholarship is the Shankara sampradaya, the orthodox mathas and other acharyas/gurus who follow the teachings of Adi Shankara. Outside opinions and scholarship do not have independent validity or equal status, lateral or otherwise, and are dismissed implicitly when they seek to contradict the positions of the tradition. This does not mean moderators will be jumping in each time with a hammer. Members have their eccentricities, convictions, personal understandings etc., based on their own study, insight, experience. If it is not deemed a seriousissue and may be seen as the airing of an individual rather than the confusion for the group, then we may just adjust to the person and move on; unless someone seeks to discuss with that person the standpoint of the shastra or sampradaya. In the latter case, discussion may proceed so long as there is utility more than futility and it does not turn into a war of acrimony.

  

Again, the purpose of our group is to learn and discuss the understanding ofthe "tradition" and the assumption is that even where we argue on the content of shastra, that we are only affirming the knowledge known to the tradition.This Shankara-sampradaya scholarship may not be the basis for another forum like BVP, so naturally they can have a different mix of members stating their arguments from otherstandpoints. That is the essential content of Sunil-ji's accusation based on his experience; he knows also that members here do not agree with some of his viewpoints. If we make it a bigger discussion of the nature of that group, then moderators will probably step in. But one post on his bad experience there is better responded to with one post of Kaul-ji in their defense. We can move on from them and keep to the main points.
