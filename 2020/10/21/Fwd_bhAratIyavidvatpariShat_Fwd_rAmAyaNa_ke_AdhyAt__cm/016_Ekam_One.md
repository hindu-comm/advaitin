Every time I come across discussions such as this one, the questions on the importance of scriptures in one's spiritual sadhana reignite in my mind, for two reasons.

  

1\. All scriptures seem to be subject to questioning of their authenticity and interpretation. How can we take them as indisputable truths? Isn't in stead better we find our own truth?  

  

2\. In spite of the deep knowledge of scriptures, arguments keep arising between different sides, indicating that the differences become more important than the humble realization that in the grand scheme of things, none of this really matters. And this has been happening over the centuries.  

  

Are we running behind some kind of "**Spiritual Manifestos"** and not the ultimate truth??  

  

Pardon me for writing this here, but, as a beginner, it is important for me to clarify this.
