Namaskaram Ekam-ji,

  

No misbehaviour. Your questions are fine for the forum, though members may not always answer them and not all may appreciatethem inthe same way. Just keep cool about that.

  

LikeI said, the forum has its general rule of thumb. We are beginners and our guidance here is from the teachings found in the Shankara sampradaya. We keep it that way in our heads because (again as per sampradaya teachings) the topics we discuss like experts and jnanis require a certain sadhana-correspondence in our lives, spiritual maturity if you will. Writing nice posts don't tell this and every such person's notion of ultimate truth cannot become a standard for independent consideration. So, we have to check ourselves to link back to the pramanas. Our pramanas here are shastra and the Shankara guru-parampara. That is the idea, although generally we are flexible about a wide range of discussion related to advaita thatat least formally intends to stay true to the pramanas. Beginners can be mistaken about thesubject-matter, but they cannot undermine the guru and pramanas.

  

Members can take what information fits for them and work out their own path and understanding.
