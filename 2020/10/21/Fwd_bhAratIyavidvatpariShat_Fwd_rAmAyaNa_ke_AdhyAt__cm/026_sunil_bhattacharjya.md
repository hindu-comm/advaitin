Dear Putranji,  

  

Now that you are from the Dvaita school, may I ask you about the Dvaita guru Madhvacharyaji. Is it true that he claimed himself to be an incarnation of Bhimsena and he also gave the time Bhimsena was alive. That date will be of interest to all who are interested in the date of the Mahabharata war.  

  

Personally I don't agree with some of Shri Madhvacharya's claims. One is his claim that nobody can understand the Bhagavatam without his (Madhvacharyaji's) commentary.  This is a direct challenge to Vedavyasa himself. Sage Vedavyasa was advised by sage Narada to write a text, which the ladies and the shudras, who may not always be educated, would be able to understand, and Vedavyasa wrote the Bhagavatam. Being unhappy with the Dvaita explanations, Sridhara's guru asked Shridhara to write a commentary and save the Bhagavatam from being hijacked. That is my understanding. You may enlighten me about your views.  

  

Regards,

skb
