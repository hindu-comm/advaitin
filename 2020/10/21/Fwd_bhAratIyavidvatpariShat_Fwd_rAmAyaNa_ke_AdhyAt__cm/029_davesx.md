Namaskar Sunilji - If I recall correctly from my study of Sri Ramanujacharya many moons ago, he was a proponent of qualified Advaita. He stated that jivas retain their separate identities even after liberation (moksha) and then live forever in communion and serving Paramatman.  

  

Sri Ramanuja have written volumes about the eternal jiva. Yet the fact is that the separate individual I-dentity, which he speaks about, itself comes and goes (as in sleep) and has no permanent existence, thus is merely an ideation (Dvaita).

  

Not truly an expert here so maybe Sadaji can enlighten us some more.
