[[V Subrahmanian	2020-10-22, 09:07:39 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/aGFA8PNEGng)]]

This is an announcement about an initiative from the Sringeri Peetham for recording discourses on a variety of topics such as the Madhaviya Shankara Vijaya, Shankara's stotra literature, Prakarana texts, Mahabharata Shanti Parva, moral stories in the Upanishads, etc.. and upload them every second and fourth Saturday of the month. These will be available on the Mutt website for all to access any time. HH Sri
Mahasannidhanam will deliver a discourse inaugurating the project and HH Sri Sannidhanam will declare open the dedicated website on 26th October 2020.
