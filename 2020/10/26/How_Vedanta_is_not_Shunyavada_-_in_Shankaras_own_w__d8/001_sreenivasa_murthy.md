Dear Sri Subramanian,

  

 Thank you for this excellent posting.

You have written :

 "This knowledge, of the unchanging Brahman  

 and the changing,non-inhereing a-brahman constitutes  

 the liberating knowledge."

It will be very beneficial to many readers like me if you graciously share

your understanding, based on your own anubhava, how this liberating knowledge could be acquired by mumukshu and cognized within himself by himself in a simple, nontechnical and direct way.

It will be a yeoman service to mumukshus.

I am eagerly waiting for your help.  

  

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa Murthy.
