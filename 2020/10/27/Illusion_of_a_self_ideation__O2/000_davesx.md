The human visual system can process 10 to 12 images per second and perceive them individually, while higher rates are perceived as motion. Early silent films started at frame rates anywhere from 16 to 24 frames per second.

  

The animation of a 3-D self is an amazing trick by memory and imagination as it rapidly links up past successive self images (or ideation of a self) projecting into a fictitious future for its own illusory survival, in the space of now.  

  

Once an idea has passed and another one has not yet arrived, in this infinitesimal stillness, the continuity of a self (the magical illusory self) is momentarily broken in this present moment.  

  

In the search for enlightenment it suddenly dawns that nothing can be known about Truth (or Brahman - the Infinite) in the *future* and such thoughts immediately comes to a standstill, ceases to link up and perpetuate the illusion of a self since the fuel, the energy behind the objective of the search (salvation or moksha) is permanently cut off.
