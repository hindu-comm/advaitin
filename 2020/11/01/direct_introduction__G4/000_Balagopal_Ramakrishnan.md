direct introduction

-----------------------

One can't get a better and direct explanation of Brahman than the one in Kena Upanishad - 'pratibodha viditam matam'.

The complex, paradoxical, ironical Vedanta which teaches without teaching, expecting the students to learn without learning and gain knowledge without gaining and be liberated without being really liberated, cannot have a more simpler way of explaining Brahman.

The words - 'pRati, bodha, viditam, matam' - say it all.

pRati = every, bodha = knowledge, experience, viditam = known, matam = opinion'

The opinion (is) every knowledge / experience (is Brahman). That means, reading this post is EXPERIENCING 'Brahman'. Period. If one was not doing anything except breathing before reading - he was experiencing 'brahman'. Whatever one is doing after reading this - IS 'brahman'.

In short, for 'brahman realisation / experience' one NEED NOT put any extra effort ! All efforts are possible ONLY because of 'brahman / bhagavAn' alone ! Thus the Upanishadic 'vaakyams' are NOT description of the ultimate but merely INTRODUCTION ! Like a person standing in front of us !

All of us in our wisdom CLEARLY accept the FACT that BhagavAn ALONE is, was and will be. That being so one shall have no doubt in 'knowing' the 'non dual brahman'. Still due to the impression carried from the beginning less time on 'saguNa brahman' (mAya sahita brahman=Isvara) we are unable to swallow the Guru's silent teaching using the 'chin mudra' - 'TATWAMASI' and we ceremoniously continue to be 'busy living' as ourselves forgetting once again the mahavAkyam' and mumble - 'aham AHAMasmi na brahmAsmi'.

The problem of continuing with this 'AHAM vAkyam' is that it fattens the 'aham' and 'mama' kArams fueling the fire of 'samsAra' in which we are already being roasted alive twice and thrice over !

The beauty of VedAnta is that it saves one from all these troubles.

But then, being human also means - FREEDOM TO CHOOSE !!
