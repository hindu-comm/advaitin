PraNams

  

As I understand:

  

A simple explanation for 'pratibodha viditm matam' - is the truth is revealed in every thought that arises in the mind. Any bodha involves a thought process. Knowledge takes place via a pramana. All the pramanas operate via thought process.

  

If I see a pot, on the floor, the perception of the pot translates to pot thought in the mind. One becomes conscious of the 'pot thought' when the consciousness that I am, joins with the existence of the pot now expressed as the existence of the thought in the mind - for me to be conscious of the thought - and hence conscious of the pot outside. This is called 'perceptuality condition' by Vedanta Paribhasha.

  

Meditation is shifting our mind from the contents of the thought to that which reveals the thought - that is the pure consciousness that I am. Hence 'the consciousness that I am' is revealed by every thought that arises in the mind - pratibodha viditam matam. It is like seeing the presence of sunlight in every object that I see in the sunlight.

I need the object to reflect the sunlight. Without the reflection, I cannot RECOGNIZE the presence of sunlight. Similarly, I need a thought for it to reflect the light of consciousness that I am for me to RECOGNIZE the light of consciousness.

The whole of Vedanta is packed in the sentence - pratibodha viditam matam.

In the case of a thoughtless mind, the mind itself becomes an object for reflection. In that case, the mind without the thoughts has to RECOGNIZE the light of consciousness that is getting reflected by the thoughtless mind (it is called chidaabhaasa).

  

In essence one cannot directly recognize the pure all-pervading consciousness - an object is required for it to illumine - thoughtfully mind or thoughtless mind. One can have a thoughtless mind in the deep sleep state too. Hence thoughtless mind itself is not sufficient. What is required is the ability to shift the mind's attention from the object of reflection to that which is getting reflected. - Hence pratibodha viditam matam - is the essence of the teaching of Kena Up.

  

Hari Om!
