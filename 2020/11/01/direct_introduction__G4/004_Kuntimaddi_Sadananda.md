You are right. Just 'tat tvam asi' teaching is sufficient for self-realization. No further discussion too. Since you are taking a step further, it is better to buy the suggested books.

  

To avoid stepping further only Bhagavatpaada Shankar insists on the pre-requisites involving saadhana chatushtayam - Viveka, Vairagya, Samaadi Shakta samptti and Mumukshutvam. For those who do not have these fully, nidhidhyaasana becomes important. For many of us, it is a life-long process- sometimes goes beyond one's lifetime too depending on the purity or impurity of the mind that likes to step further.
