Namaste,  

  

This reminds me of the anecdote, where one rishi was sent by his father to have his last last spiritual lesson from King Janaka. He was intrigued, as he already completed his education, and was there anything left, which he had to learn from king Janaka? But being unable to say no to his father he went to meet Janaka. Janaka was extremely busy for the next seven days but he sent the message to the rishi that he would have to wait for one week and all arrangements were made for him to spend the week as Janaka's guest. At the end of the week Janaka met him and asked Ashtavakra to excuse him for the delay. Then Janaka told him to have his meals with the king, before they sat for the discussion. Accordingly, they ate the food and sat for discussion, but before that Janaka asked him as to whether he enjoyed the meal and whether the food was to his taste? The rishi said that he somehow just ate the food, as he could not forget for a moment that the hanging sword was about to fall of his head. In fact king Janaka arranged for a sword hanging precariously above the place where the rishi was sitting.  

  

Then Janaka added that he too remained himself busy all the time, but he never forgot for a moment about the one without a second, in spite of his busy schedule. That was the final lesson the rishi got from Janaka and he also underrtood why his father sent him to Janaka,  

That is how Prati bodha needs the Prati-Ahara or Pratyahara, i.e not only just controlling the food intake, one has to completely control the senses

to be able to concentrate on the One without the Second.
