My answer is yes or no.

  

Yes, if one has fully qualified then there is no need for nidhidhyaasana also. Shrevana manana will be enough as Sureswaracharya says in his naiskamara siddhi. These students are like Nichiketas, etc.

  

Nidhidhyaasana is required for manda adhikaaries who have just sufficient qualifications to sit down, listen, and think but not sufficient enough for one to abide in that knowledge. Most of us fall into that category. In that case, the nidhidhyaasana will help to overcome that deficiency. How long one has to do etc depends on to what extent the mind needs to be purified for one to abide in that knowledge.

  

Hence gradations in the jnaanis are also mentioned by Vidyaranya, etc.
