Pranaam Sadaji,

  

You said it so correctly, and very few people really realize this.
Nididhyasana is for Manda adhikari (or call it mediocre aspirant). The Sage Yajnavalkya told that to his wife, who was certainly not the Uttama Adhikari. For Uttama Adhikari it is Nirvikalpa Samadhi (Lord Ram called it the Asampraajna Samadhi, when he told Rudravatara Hanumanji about it) In Mahayana Buddhism it is Prajnaaparamita Samadhi for the Bodhisattvas, whio are the Jivanmuktas.

  

Jai Shri Krishna
