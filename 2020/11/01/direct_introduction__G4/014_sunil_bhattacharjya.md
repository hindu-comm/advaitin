Daveji,

  

We know **"Prajnanam** **Brahma". "Sat-Prajna" is the "Saguna Brahaman" or "Manifested Brahman" and "Asat Prajna" is "Nirguna Brahman" or "Unmanifested Brahman". Dhyana on the Saguna Brahman leads to Savikalpa Samadhi or SapraajnaSamadhi and Dhyana of Nirguna Brahman leads to Asmapraajna Samadhi or Nirvikalpa Samadhi. Patanjali need not necessarily invented it. There are several upanishadas discussing the Yoga system, which preceded Patanjali. . To my knowledge, Lord Dattatreya was the originator of the Yoga system.  **
