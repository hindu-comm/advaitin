Namaste Govindaraj Pandithaji,

  

Shodashi is Mother Lalita or Kamakshya. hri hodashi sahasranama should be the same as Shri Laita Sahasranama. As regards the 15 syllables of the 16 syllables, you will get those in the Shri Lalita Trishati, for which Adi Shankara had written a bhashya. The 16th syllable is given by the guru and that is the tradition.  

  

BTW, Adi Shankara wanted to write a commentary on the Bhagavad Gita, which is Sarvashatramayee (the epitome of all the Shastras), but instead of the Bhagavad Gita, he was advised to write a bhashya on the Lalita Trishati. This was done, probably because Shri Gaudapadacharya had already borrowed the Ajatavada verse from the Bhagavad Gita for use in the Mandukya-Karika.  

  

Regards,

Sunil k Bhattacharjya
