Respected Gurutulyas,

  

I was recently listening to a talk by Swami Sarvapriyananda, where he said, *because* he is committed to Advaita and is a sannyasin under that order, he would prefer to interpret scriptures in advaitic context.

  

This raised a question in my mind. This question is independent of any philosophy and after recognizing the importance and significance of Sraddhha in one's pursuit.  

  

**My Question:** In the pursuit of truth, do we always try to reconcile our observations , thoughts, experiences and realizations into the context of the chosen tradition (Advaita, Dvaita, Visishtadvaita etc)?
*Or* do we seek the truth with an open mind, with of course reverence to chosen path?  

  

**In other words,** How do you find the the balance between "open inquiry" and "Belief" ?

  

Pranams

Ekam
