Truth doesn't change and is one. Different are the pathsto arrive at. Since you quotedSwami Sarvapriyananda ji , Rama Krishna Parahamsasays Dvaita, Visitadvaita and Advaita are like rungs of the ladder and advaita is the highest rung once you climb this rung you have reached your goal and one is enlightened After which there are no doubts or questions that needs to be answered..

  

One has to first define 'Mind' to have 'an open mind.' One can choose one's own path or tread on a timetested path. Choiceis to the sadhaka
Advaita rested on anubhava not on Enquiry or belief. Even if a thousand Shruti statements say fire is cold, fire is always hot. There Vyavaharika, Pratbhasikaand parmarthika Satta. There is denial of the world and the laws that govern the world at Vyavaharika, but at paramathika level there are no three levels, onlyone..
