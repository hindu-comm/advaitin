Pujya Swami Dayananda Saraswati ji of Arsha Vidya Gurukulam describes Shraddha as thus:  
**"Trust pending understanding"**  
  
One gives the benefit of the doubt to dev-guru-shastra at the start of one's inquiry, approaches the same with an open mind, and with pramana buddhi.  
What begins with shraddha culminates in jnana  
  
Harih Om
