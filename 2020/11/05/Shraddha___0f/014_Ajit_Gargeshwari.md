NamasteSri Ram chandran Ji

श्रद्धा is not faith it is गुरुवेदान्तवाक्यादिषु विश्वासः . As a saint remarked, many religionssay all is within our sacred book. but we say, experiment, find out and experience the truth yourself with help of anenlightened guru. Upanishads without training appear contradictorybut if one reads them withश्रद्धा with the aid of an enlightened guru, contradictions are cleared and one gets enlightened. When one is enlightened one can throw away the books one reads.  

Regards
