\> When one is enlightened one can throw away the books one reads.  

  

  

Hari OM!

  

When one is enlightened one does not throw away the books, but gives them away to others. Only thing enlightened ones throw away is their ego.

  

Ego thrown away so much they do not even claim they are enlightened. Every breath of the enlightened one thereafter is only to serve and adore the Lord alone-  
"yadyad karOti tattadakhilam Shambho tavaaraadhanam"

  

Till then not safe from the lower self. Not safe from even what appears to world like great worship/service, but may actually be to deck own ego- "ahamkaara aaraadhanam" !

  

Hari OM  
Srinivas
