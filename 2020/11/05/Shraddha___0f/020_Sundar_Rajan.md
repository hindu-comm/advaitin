On a somewhat related subject, There was a householder in the late 70's/early 80's that V Subbu-ji and I have both met in Bangalore.Subbarao managed a Tea estate in Nilgiris, he also was a Jnani. He was a devotee of Sringeri Acharyal and during one of his visits, Acharyal pointed him to a visiting North Indian Avadhuta (who looked like a mad man to others) and asked Subbarao to talk to this Avadhuta. It is said that only after this conversation Subbarao's transformation or stabilization occurred. Couple of interesting things - Acharyal could have initiated, he did not do for whatever reason. The Avadhuta looked like a mad man to others but Acharyal was able to see his real state.
