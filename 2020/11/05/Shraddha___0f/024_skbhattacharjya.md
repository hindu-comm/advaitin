Dear Ganeshji,

  

Yes, there can be different levels spiritual teachings, as appropriate to needs of the spiritual aspirant, but there should not be any quarrel about it. In the times of Lord Buddha, the towering reformer, in the 19th century BCE, he had only to remind the Sanatana Dharmis not to be overly ritualistic. He was against animal sacrifice and in the Mahabharata also, Bhishma was against animal sacrifice. Lord Buddha's teachngs, particularly the Mahayana teachings, were from the Upanishads and his Vajrayana teachings were Tantric teachings. After about 1,300 years of Lord Buddha, the situation slowly deteriorated and many sects appeared and in the 6th century BCE (i.e., about about 2,500 years ago), another towering reformer, Adi Shankara appeared and he brought all of the Sanatana dharmis together and started the Panchayatana puja on the Vedic lines.  

For example, ***  ***

***(i)*** ***We are all Shudras by birth and the** **Ganapati Puja for removing the obstacles***, ***is for all.***

***ii) For health, we all have to pray to Lord Surya***, ***  ***

***iii) The Brahmins,of the post-Grahasta stage, are** **to worship Lord Shiva as they did have to live an austere life***.

***(iv)*** ***The** **Kshatriyas have to worship Lord Vishnu for wealth and prosperity as they have to look after the welfare and security of the people,***

***and so also the householders, who need the grace of Mother Lakshmi.  ***

**

***(iv) The worship of the Mother Parvati for Mukti.  ***

  

Adi Shankara gave the highest Upanishadic teachings to us. ** He ** also wrote the Lalita Trishati, so that the Tantric form of worship is also open to the advanced spiritual seekers. About 1,300 years after Adi Shankara, i.e., around the 8th century AD (CE), again the situation degraded with the appearance of many sects and unfortunately there was no another towering stalwart like Lord Buddha or Adi Shankara to unify the Sanatana dharmis. However thanks to the Lord , that there have been some spiritual greats like Abhinava Shankara, Swami Vidyashankara, Ramakrishna Paramhamsa, Ramana Maharshi, the Periava of Kanchi-Kamakoti math appeared, to name a few, and we the Sanatani Hindus are able to hold our heads high. Let us hope that the political situation will also improve and we will have the golden age back. ***  ***
