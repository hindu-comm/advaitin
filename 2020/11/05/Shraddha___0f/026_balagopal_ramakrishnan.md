Very true Ram Ji.

  

The main point is to approach anything for that matter with a fervent hope of getting what one is looking for. Thus, our attention will get to focus on the right parts leading to enlightenment of our inquiry. Even as simple as searching a place with some landmarks. If we begin the search itself as considering that those landmarks itself as wrong (suspicion, lack of trust, negative mindset, doubts etc.), then in all probability one will miss the referred landmarks. Eventually we will only be going in circles.

  

In the case of 'VedAnta', it is all the more necessary, as it is the ONLY instrument of knowledge (pRamANam) for the subject matter of inquiry.
