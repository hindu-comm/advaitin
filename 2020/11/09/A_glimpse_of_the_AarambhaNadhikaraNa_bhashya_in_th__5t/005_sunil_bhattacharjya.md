Dear Shri Rammohan Subramaniamji,

  

Lord Krishna says as follows and that seems to me to be self-explanatory**:**

What was not there in the beginning (i.e., before the creation) and what will not be there at the end (i.e., when the creation ceases to exist),
is also like not existing (i.e., does not have the permanency).  

  

Jai Shri Krishna

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
