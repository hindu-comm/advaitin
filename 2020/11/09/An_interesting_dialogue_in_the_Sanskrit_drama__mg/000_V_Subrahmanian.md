In the 'Ekachakram' rUpakam of MM Sri Ranganatha Sharma,
[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KlNpiVQ9hPE&feature=youtu.be](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KlNpiVQ9hPE&feature=youtu.be) there is a dialogue that is interesting in that it reminds us of Shankara Bhagavatpada's words in the Taittiriya Bhashyam: After 32 minutes in the video, Bakasura says: इदमस्ति मे भागदेयम्.....for which Bhimasena replies: इदं मे स्वस्त्ययनम् - यन्मां.....     This reminds us of the Bhashya vakya - एतदेव मे स्वस्त्ययनम् यन्मामेकयोगिनमनेकयोगिबहुप्रतिपक्षमात्थ ।
