***3.*** **The Four Maths lead people to follow the path of Dharma.**  
-These Maths have remained free of Ethical or Moral controversies during their entire history.

In the Mahabharata, **Lord Krishna said that the highest dharma is (speaking) the Truth. From this it follows that one who speaks untruth is teaching adharma.  **

**  **

**On one group, the Shringeri math claimss that Adi shankara lived about ~1300 years ago and on the other group, the other maths including (i) Dwarka math, (ii) Puri math, (iii) Kanchi kamakoto math, and (iv) the Kudali Shringeri math, at the confluence of the Tunga and Bhadra rivers near Shimoga, claim that Adi Shankara lived about ~2500 years ago.  **

  

**The above shows that one of the above two groups is not telling the truth. Will it not be the right thing for both the groups to sit down and sort out the matter amicably , at the earlest, once for all? To my knowledge Swamiji Bharati Teertha shifted from Kudali Shringeri to Tunga Sringeri around 1330 AD or so, only.  **

  

**Please contact the Chaturamnaya Maths for establishing the Dharma by finding out who is not telling the truth.  **
