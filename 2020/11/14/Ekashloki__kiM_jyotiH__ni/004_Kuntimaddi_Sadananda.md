PraNAms

Even from a scientific point, one cannot see the light directly – (not referring to a source of light like a lamp or bulb or sun). One can only see the reflected light – just as I see reflected light from the moon even though what I am seeing is really the sunlight. The same thing applies to the light of consciousness, which is all-pervading. One can only SEE the reflected consciousness and cognitively (using Vedanta knowledge) recognize the original consciousness, which one cannot SEE. For SEE and Recognize using the mind, one needs a subtle mind with Viveka (nithya anitya vastu Viveka). There is a famous sloka – na tatra suuryobhaati ..

Hari Om!

Sadananda
