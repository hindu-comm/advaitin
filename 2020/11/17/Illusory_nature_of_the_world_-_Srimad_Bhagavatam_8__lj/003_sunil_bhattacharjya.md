Namaste Sureshji,

  

The Advaita is at the upansihadic level and that makes the difference, Nobody can say that Dvaita is wrong, as it is right at its own level. Problem arises when the Dvaitins try to find fault with the Advaiins. If anybody tries to claim that upanishad says Atattvam asi, I shall have to show that person that he or she is not right. That boldness has been taught to the Advaitins by none other than Adi Shankaracharya himself. Advaitins have been most peaceful through the centuries, but they have the right to defend against the enemies of the Advaitavada.  

  

The world is mithya is in the sense that the things that comprise the world are anitya. If a dvaitin cannot understand this, we have to pity that person's ability to understand. For example, one is a human being in the present birth, but he or she might not have been born as human being in all the past births. The Advaitins understand this but the Dvaitins don't appear to understand this.  

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
