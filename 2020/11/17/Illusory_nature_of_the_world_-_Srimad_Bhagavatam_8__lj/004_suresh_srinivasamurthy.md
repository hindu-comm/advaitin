Namaste Sunil-ji,

  

You seem to be missing the point. This Bhagavata slokam and Sridhara Swamin's commentary is explicitly teaching that the reality of jagat as pratibimba or chAya or shareera needs to be accepted as it is pointing to the reality of the Brahman indwelling as the sarvAtman - or the Self of all.

  

'Sakalam idam aham cha vAsudevah' - Everything including the atman is Vasudeva / Brahman - this universal view which is IMO true Advaitic view includes and transcends all kinds of views.

  

Brahman as the inner Self of all is not just yourself or myself or this self or that self. It is THE SELF with which every name/form is identified. This unites both idealistic and realistic views.

  

It is interesting that the reality of the world as pratibimba is established on the nityatva of atman only in Dvaita. The upAdhi is jiva svarupa/atman only.

As consciousness is real all that is seen in it must also be real. So the argument between advaita and dvaita will never end unless one accepts Brahman as the source/abode/destination of all name/forms. When name/forms dissolve in Atman/Brahman only then true peace can arise and to attain this there is a need to realize Brahman through every name/form.

  

So if I am an advaitin, I would take dvaitin's interpretation of atat tvamasi as the elimination of identification of Brahman associated with a particular name and form of Swetaketu. Also, tvam tadadheeno si (you are dependent on that) can be taken as the dependence of the lower self (identified with the body) on the higher Self (identified with jagat).

  

Actually in V.Advaita it is split as tat tvam asi only as in Advaita, where the word tat and tvam points to Brahman only residing as the inner Self of jagat and jiva. So the reality of jagat/jiva can continue to exist as the body which is also an amsha or pratibimba as it has the opposite characteristics of Brahman/Atman. But it also acts as a means to attain Brahman. Hence the need to accept the reality of jagat.

  

If reality of jagat as body or reflection is denied then it leads to denial of reality to one's own body/mind/speech/sadhana/dharma without which no attainment of Brahman is possible.

  

So to conclude, accepting reality of jagat as body/indicator/linga of Brahman reconciles all views and preserves the eternal nature of vaidhika dharma comprising of pravrutti and nivrutti.  

  

Regards,
