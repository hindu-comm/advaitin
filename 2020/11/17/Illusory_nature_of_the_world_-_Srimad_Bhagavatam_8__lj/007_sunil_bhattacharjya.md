Dear Sureshji,

  

You are like all the Dvaitins, since the time of Shri Madhvacharya, believing blindly in the anti-upanishadic interpretation of Shri Madhvacharya, ***There is no place of "Atatvam asi" in upanishad.*** It is a pity that none of his disciples had the guts to stand upto Shri Madhvacharya to challenge him. Read the 22nd chapter of the Uddhava Gita of the Bhagavatam, and there could be a chance that it may help you. Had Shri Madhvacharya been fortunate enough to have a good advaita teacher, his teacher would have told him that Vayu-putra Hanuman got the "Atatvam asi" upadesha from none other than the Vishnu-Avatara, Lord Ram. ***I am sure the claim of Shri Madhacharya to be a Vayu-putra was his wild imagination and nothing else.***

  

Once Lord Ram tested Hanumanji and asked him (Hanuman) : "Who are you?"
Hanumanji replied :

Oh my Lord, (i) identifying myself with my body, I am your servant, (ii) identifying myself as the soul I am a part of yours and (iii) spiritually I am one with you. My suggestion is that you can try to understand what difference is there in these three approaches. If you read the 22nd chapter of the Uaddhava Gita, as I said above, there is a chance that

you may understand that the Advaitins understand that we are no different from Brahman.  

Further argument will not help you. Pray to the real Vayuputra (i.e.,
Hanumanji) for a long time till Shri Hanumanji is convinced that you are sincere, then there is a chance that he may make you understand that "atatvam asi" was a false claim of Shri Madhvacharya.  

  

Sincerely,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
