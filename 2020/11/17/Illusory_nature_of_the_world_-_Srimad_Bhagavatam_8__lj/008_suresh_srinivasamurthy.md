Dear Sunil-ji,

  

You seem to have missed my point again.I am not opposed to splitting the sandhi as tat tvam asi at all.

What I have shown is that even atat tvam asi is indirectly teaching BrahmAtmaktva of everything.

  

Dvaita and V. Advaita is not opposed to Advaita at all!All these paths are pointing to the same Brahman present

in all as the inner Self.

  

Brahman is not just in you. It is in me and every blessed thing between you and me. Everything is BrahmAtmaka.

Seeing the same Brahman in all leads to equal vision and this is not possible if jagat is an illusion.

  

Brahman is the Soul of all - sarvAtman and all name/forms denote Him only. So service to the jagat is service to Brahman.

The IshAvAsya Up begins by saying Ishwara/Brahman is pervading the entire universe. If you reject universe you are rejecting

Ishwara. So Brahman is Sarva nAma vAchya and sarvaroopa vedya also!

  

Jagat is mithya in one way! But it is also sathya when looked upon as an embodiment of dharma/sacrifice!

Every entity in jagat / nature is teaching sanAtana dharma - Sun, moon, earth water fire are all sustaining all beings for free!

So you are supposed to return back in the form of svadharma / selfless service.

  

  

You wrote:

"Once Lord Ram tested Hanumanji and asked him (Hanuman) : "Who are you?" Hanumanji replied :

Oh my Lord, (i) identifying myself with my body, I am your servant, (ii) identifying myself as the soul I am a part of yours and (iii) spiritually I am one with you."

  

Do you think Lord Ram would say the same thing to Hanuman?

  

Every name/form denotes Brahman only and this is what is taught in the Bhagavata sloka explained in this thread!

I am not expecting you to agree.But I would be more interested to hear from traditional advaitins like Sri Subbu-ji

  

  

Regards,  
Suresh
