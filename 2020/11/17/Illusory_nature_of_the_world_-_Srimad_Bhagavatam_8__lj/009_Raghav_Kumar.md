Namaste Suresh ji

  

You wrote that  
  

"Adviata (as paramartha satyam) and Dvaita (in vyavahaara) are not opposed to each other at all."

(Words in brackets are my additions).

  

The above statement is accepted (with some context) \*only\* by advaitins. It is not acceptable to dvaita in any context whatsoever. Therefore you may need to communicate this more to those who self-identify as dvaitins, rather than for those who understand Advaita.

  

You also wrote -

  

***"Traditional Dvaitin's may not accept this, but this is a point that cannot be denied."***  

  

This means that the way you present dvaita is unacceptable to traditional dvaitins. It is your own idealized dvaita which does not exist in the world today.

  

Also you misrepresent Advaita as declaring the 'unreality' of the world without any context. You also mix up the ideas of mithyAtva of jagat with unreality. mithyAtva and unreality are not the same. Advaita in fact emphasises antaHkaraNa Shuddhi in vyavahaara which you don't seem to recognise.

  

Lastly, it is incorrect to place Advaita (A) as one more theological system like vishiShTAdvaita (V) and dvaita (D) and then claim that your own 'reconciliation' of advaita dvaita and vishiShTAdvaita is the best and that it subsumes Advaita. Lets call this viz., your reconciliation perspective - lets call it S.The problem with such an attempt is that before long, someone else will come along and say that his newly discovered perspective (N) is a new unified superior perspective which reconciles A, V, D and also your S. And that his N is superior to A,V,D and S.

  

The fact is that Advaita already reconciles the various schools of dvaita, vishiShTAdvaita etc by employing categories of adhikaritvaM, sattAtrayam, antaHkaraNa-shuddhi, importance of karma and ishvara-anugraha etc. It is superfluous to seek a 'reconciliation' of 'advaita' with dvaita and vishiShTAdvaita since advaita has already done that. Such attempts suffer from the 'fallacy of composition'.

  

One last point which is the elephant in the room which you are ignoring is that all dvaita/vishiShTAdvaita is afflicted by rAga and dveSha. (This is pointed out by Shankara in mANDUkya bAShya). This can be seen in the Vishnu-alone-is-supreme-theology of Sri Madhva and Sri Ramanuja. There exists no extant non-sectarian vishiShTAdvaita or dvaita today. Such schools (like Bhaskara's bhedAbheda and Kapika's sAmkhya etc.) have existed in the past and tended to slowly merge in to and get subsumed by Advaita as far as their adherents go.
