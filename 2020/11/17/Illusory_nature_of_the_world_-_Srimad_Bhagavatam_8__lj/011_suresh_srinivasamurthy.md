Namaste Sri Raghav-ji,

  

Your questions are great and let me clarify.

  

Problems in Advaita:

Acceptance of dvaita in vyavahAra is accepting for nAmkevAste only. So how can this be acceptable to Dvaitins?  

  

The main objection is with regard to mithyAtva of jagat which totally eliminates any reality to Ishwara and this

wipes of sanAtana dharma and reflects Traditional Advaita as nAstika vAda.

  

Also splitting the one truth into paramArtha and vyavahAra gives rise to a different kind of Dvaita that gives

rise to a split in consciousness in a sAdhaka. Engaging in any sAdhana and upAsana for chitta shuddhi comes

undervyAvahAra only which is nAmkevAste.If I am already Brahman and jagat is mithya why should I engage

in any sAdhana? There is no point in doing sandhyAvandana, pitru tarpana and follow svadharma.

This is what Neo-Advaitins are teaching today challenging traditional Advaitins who adhere to the path of  

Dharma.

  

Problems in Dvaita and V.Advaita

  

As explained by you, Dvaita and V.Advaita on the other hand, accept the reality of jagat and have formed

a good framework of bhAshyas to support their view which is based on a particular deity Vishnu.

This definitely creates a false sectarianism and leading to clashes with other sects that considers Shiva,

Shakti and other Devatas as the ultimate Brahman.

  

Sri Shankara tried to integrate the six major religions based on the panchAyatana puja. But as all of these

come under vyavahAra which is mithya there is no firm basis for any religion.

  

So in order to protect sanAtana dharma and also support all the different religions based on the Veda

and dharma, there is a need to provide a firm philosophical ground that does justice to all.

  

This can be done if the entire universe is looked upon as that Supreme Purusha!

  

The pramAna for this comes from the purusha sUktam, Rudram, Chamakam, Bhagavad Gita 11th Ch,

Vishnu sahasranAma dhyana sloka, and many more.

  

As there cannot be a second entity other than or external to this purusha and all existences are

within that only. When we are looking at everything, every particular entity gets includedand transcended.

  

The reality of infinite jivas is not denied. Every name/form arises, remains and returns back to Him only.

So the world view of Dvaitins and V.Advaitins are also covered.

  

This Supreme Purusha could be looked upon as Vishnu, Shiva or Shakti and whatever may be the name,

it denotes the same entity. The Shatarudreeya has a great mantra that identifies this universal purusha

as both Rudra and Vishnu - "Om namo bhagavate rudrAya VishNave mruthyur me pAhi".

  

Purusha sUkta says - tamevam vidwAn amrutha iha bhavati and nAnya pantha ayanAya vidyate.  

There is no other path other than knowing and serving this Supreme Purusha.

  

The only mahA vAkya needed is : Sarvam Khalvidam Brahma.

  

So this cannot be a sectarian view at all and it cannot be non-sectarian also as it is universally

applicable to All. This cannot have any objections also as there is no second object other than

that.

  

But let me know if you have any objections.  

  

Namaste

Suresh
