Namaste -Sir it is the nature of apparent phenomena to be ever changing, ever fresh, forevernew, a creative power of life Supremeand that’sits beauty, it’s perplexity, that cannot be knownasreal or unreal -Mithya.

  

To say the screen alone is real is a half truth. There can be no ever present screen without the ever changing, just as there can be no sereneocean without turbulentwaves appearing and disappearing.

  

To devalue appearances (drysam) and hold Drk aloneas realhas been one of thedownfall of oriental school of thoughts and affectedthe socioeconomic prosperity of these regions. Both Drysam and Drkare Brahman. All this is Brahman!

  

That’s why it appearsRamana wasleaning moreasa solitaryyogi than a Gyani, though he touched uponand interpreted some ofthe latter texts.
