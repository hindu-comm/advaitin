Namaste All,  
I have often pondered over the following question. We are familiar with our accepted pramanas (pratyaksha, anumana, arthapatti, upama, anupalabdhi and shada (shruti vakya)).  
  
In our modern context, where do things such as weather predictions, or Data Science related areas such as regression and forecasting, probability distributions, clustering etc, and conclusions derived thereof come under?

  

Let's take two examples. Suppose I read the news which says there's a 75% chance of rain this afternoon. Naturally it is high enough for me to carry an umbrella in my car when stepping out. However, because it is not 100%, does it still constitute 'knowledge'? What value can one assign to probabilities?  
  
Furthermore, let us suppose we analyze the buying pattern of a customer, and predict their next purchase based on clustering samples and such. Does that constitute knowledge? Would it fall under the anumaan-aadi pramanas list?  
  
Would greatly appreciate clarity on this front  
  
Harih Om
