> I have often pondered over the following question. We are familiar
> with our accepted pramanas (pratyaksha, anumana, arthapatti, upama,
> anupalabdhi and shada (shruti vakya)).  
>   
> In our modern context, where do things such as weather predictions, or
> Data Science related areas such as regression and forecasting,
> probability distributions, clustering etc, and conclusions derived
> thereof come under?

  

I am not sure if all of them or even one as a whole can be categorised easily into one pramANa, but parts might come close to a pramANa. Or any probability may not even really be a pramANa as it is an uncertain unit of knowledge. Yet, I think if the source of the news is an Apta, it is shabda-pramANa for you. If their conclusion is based on invariable concomitance, it is anumAna and if they can establish their presumptive conclusion because of there being no other way for a certain pratyakSha data set to be possible, then it is arthApatti.
