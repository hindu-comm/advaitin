This aspect is the essence of ‘Schrodinger’s cat problem’ in Science. I have discussed this aspect in more detail in my ‘Transcending Science’ book. In essence – the question was posed if a cat is radiated in a chamber and ask the question whether the cat is alive or dead, the answer will come only in terms of probabilities – It is going to be 50:50 probability that the cat is dead or alive. Only when one opens the chamber and examines the probabilistic answer switch to deterministic with cat 100% dead or 100% alive. In essence, a conscious entity has to step in to make it a deterministic problem. It is similar to when I am sitting in a dark room and you ask me if there is a chair in that room – the answer can only in terms of 50% probability that the existence (or non-existence) is 50%. It is anirvacaneeyam. It becomes deterministic only when I open the light falling on the chair reveals the existence or non-existence of it (assuming my perceptual pamaana is working i.e the eyes are open and I have good vision and the mind is active).

Light of consciousness also works like the outside light – it has to illumine the thought that arise in the mind for me to be conscious of the thought and, therefore, the conscious of the existence of the chair outside.

Hence Vedanta says – the existence of an object is established by the knowledge of its existence. Otherwise, it is anirvacaneeyam. The same analysis applies to the existence of the world when we are in the deep-sleep state—the same problem with the weather predictions. The probabilities become deterministic only when you become conscious of the environment, which has to be done in the present.

  

Hari Om!
