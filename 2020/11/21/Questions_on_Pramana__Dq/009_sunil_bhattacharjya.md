Dear Chittaranjanji,

  

You are a great scholar in the Tarka shastra, which I am not. In fact.
While I like tarkas in any mundane research, I have some hesitation in application of tarka shastra for interpreting the verses in the Vedic texts and more so in the Upanishadic texts. This hesitation is due to the caution suggested in the saying "the Vedas love paroksha interpretation". Would you like to say how you look at this dilemma?

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
