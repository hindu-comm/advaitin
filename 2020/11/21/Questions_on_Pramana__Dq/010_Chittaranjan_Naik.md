Dear Sri Bhattacharjya-ji,

  

I am not a great scholar but i shall provide my opinion since you ask for it. :-)

  

I agree with you that one needs to exercise caution in the application of tarka for interpreting Vedic verses. I see tarka as a double edged sword. For those who have attained mental purity and arrived at the Gates of the Temple, it is a means to enter the Inner Sacred Space. But for those who have not yet arrived at the Temple Gate, its use is prone to lead to the unending Desert of Dry Ratiocination.

  

Warm regards,
