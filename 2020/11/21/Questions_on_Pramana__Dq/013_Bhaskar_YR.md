Hare Krishna



You may expect more authoritative answer from Sri Chitta prabhuji. But here is my take with regard to jeeva srushti and Ishwara srushti.
Stree, pum, flower, hills, mountains, rivers etc. is Ishwara srushti, taking the Ishwara’s srushti in our own hands and labelling it as husband, wife (mortal relations) perfumed flower, not so good looking flower, big and small rivers etc. comparison with our prejudices resulting in likes, dislikes (rAga – dvesha) etc. jeeva srushti due to lack of knowledge of existing real nature of Ishwara srushti for which he is the material and efficient cause.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
