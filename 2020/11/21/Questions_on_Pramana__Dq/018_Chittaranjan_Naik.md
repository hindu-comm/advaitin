*"Regarding your idea that "any vyApti GYAnam (which is held to be pramA) is necessarily not sublatable for all time except by Brahma GYAnam" - it does not accord with traditional vedanta pramANa understanding of vedanta paribhAShA etc., as I understand it."*

  

I would like to raise just one question here (as i had said above).

  

Take the case of the invariable relation between smoke and fire. When we apprehend the relation between smoke and fire, do we grasp a relationship that exists intrinsically between smoke and fire or do we grasp a relationship that is merely bestowed upon smoke and fire by the mind?
