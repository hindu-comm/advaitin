*Can I ask if there is a real world apart from what is perceived from the brain-mind i.e the image or representation of a world reified by acquired knowledge, language, ideas, thoughts...my own imagination ?  *

*  *

*What would such a real world be like?*

  

  

If one starts with the assumption that perception occurs through a stimulus-response process in which the brain finally projects a representation of the object, then it would be impossible to say what the real world is like nor whether there is even a world apart from what is perceived. But if one goes by the Vedic theory of perception in which the senses actively make contact with the objects, then the view presented by Sri Bhaskar-ji - of there being Ishvara shrishti and jiva shrishti - may be accommodated.
