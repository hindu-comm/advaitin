Namaste Sri Chittaranjan-ji,

  

Sure there is sensory perception of an ever changing display but upon looking deeply objects and events disappears miraculously the moment the quantum physicist tries to grasp or define exactly what it is. One simply has to look at thoughts for example and notice that it self-liberates naturally into open space and returns (or reincarnates) from this creative space refreshed as other new thoughts. So what is the continuity here the space or thoughts? Probably both :-)  

  

Anyway, the jnanendriyas by themselves senses but don't recognize a separate, meaningful world of distinction until the coordinator - the mind and intellect - intervenes and imputes nama-rupa based on memory and past associations of thoughts and ideas.

  

As a child we all have this recollection until the conceptual (artificial or social) self was formulated later on and superimposed itself on our naked being - our pure awareness.  

  

Is this childlike non-conceptual awareness, that is ever-present and within us the Atman we have been searching for ages, reading all the sacred scriptures and visiting all the temples, masters and gurus?
'That' which cannot be understood by mind or intellect but only skillfully indicated by one versed in Vedanta?  

  

Sri Nisargadatta Maharaj seems to be alluding to this when he stated: ***"**My Guru made me see that birth and death are mere ideas and that* *the child, which is you even now, is your real Self or Swarupa. Go back and rest in your original estate of pure being before it got contaminated with 'I am this’ or ‘ I am that’ and allow all functioning to arise and cease from there.*   

  

Nice to know you delved into this in your text and will pursue when it comes across.  

  

Om Shanti

Dave
