Namaste Sri Raghav-ji,

  

I have no issues with the epistemic process described by you for obtaining knowledge of the vyapti (though i would not call it leap of faith). My question was however not about the epistemic process but about the principle or conviction on which the epistemic process was predicated, i.e., whether it was predicated on the conviction that the relation is intrinsic to the objects related or merely bestowed upon the objects by the mind. According to the texts of Vedanta, the relation is something that is intrinsic to the objects; otherwise the knowledge of vyapti would become purusha-tantra and not vastu-tantra.

  

I am not sure why you are characterizing the arising of vyapti-jnanam as a leap of faith. If it were to be merely a leap of faith, then the Charvaka's charge on the Vedantin would hold good, i.e., that the Vedantin's assertion amounts to little more than a dogmatic belief. No, it is not merely a leap of faith but is valid and apodictic knowledge and it depends on cognition of the universal (samanya or generic attribute). In the specific case of the relation between smoke and fire, it is obtained by the cognition of smokiness whereby smoke is cognized to be that kind of thing that cannot exist without fire (albeit on some conditions like presence of wet fuel, etc). Since, in Advaita Vedanta, there is non-difference between samanya and vishesha, the cognition of the universal confers the knowledge that the relation holds good universally, i.e., the universal smokiness is invariably bound to the universal fieriness. The cognition of the generic attribute smokiness is present in every cognition of smoke (for otherwise the smoke would not be recognized as smoke) but the cognition of smoke being universally bound to fire is a kind of special wakefulness in the cognition that allows the universality of the relation between smoke and fire to be apprehended.

  

In Nyaya, samanya and vishesha are two distinct things that are brought into conjunction by the samavaya relationship; hence the articulation of the cognition of vyapti takes the form of extra-ordinary perception wherein all individual cases of smoke and fire are said to be cognized with smokiness inhering in each case of smoke and fieriness inhering in each case of fire. Surely, such a cognition is untenable and that is the reason why Madhusudhana Saraswati rejects it in the Advaita Siddhi. But having said that, the Advaita explanation is not so much different than the Nyaya explanation except that it is insulated from taking on an untenable form due to the Advaita principle that there is non-difference between samanya and vishesha.

  

Well, this is my understanding. I was arguing along these lines with Sri Venkatraghavan-ji when we had to call off the discussion.
