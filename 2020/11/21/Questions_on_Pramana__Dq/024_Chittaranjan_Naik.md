Namaste Sri Putran-ji,

  

  

There is another question that is relevant to this discussion and the question is this: Are words and objects connected by objective relations or are they connected by mind-dependent relations? In my view, if we consider them as mind-dependent relations, we would be slipping into the philosophy of the Bauddhas.

  

Also, the topic of svatah-pramanya is a deep and subtle topic. Even when we consider that there may exist a distinction between Ishvara-shrishti and jiva-shrishti, each instance of an individual's cognition would still be a valid cognition (according to the principle of svataj-pramanya); it is only in relation to other instances of cognition as revealed subsequently that it may become aprama. Again, aprama is of two kinds: wrong-knowledge and doubt-ridden-knowledge. The latter is often ignored in these kinds of discussions and that I feel is the problem here too.
