Namaskaram,

  

This discussion can be related to my earlier post on correlation vs knowledge of causality.

  

1.

  

Suppose we flip the coin 100s and 1000s of times and get ~ 75% Heads for all but a handful of times. The mathematical chance of this happening is exceedingly small for an unbiased coin.

  

Could it make us think that the coin "must be" biased to fall Heads? Yes it could.

  

Would such a conviction be vyapti jnanam? If I feel the conviction from the perception gained from these experiments, would this jnanam satisfy the status of vyavaharika prama (unless and until later sublated as brahma)?

  

Or is this not possible because the pramana for that knowledge is not such experimentation, no matter the conviction that seems to arise in my mind?

  

In mathematics, there may be conjectures for which there are trillions of affirming examples and other expected consequences from the result, and 0 counter-examples. Yet the way to "prama" is only a valid Proof.

  

2\. An interesting problem (elaborated in Chitta-ji's book) in Science with regard to pratyaksha is that it is held to happen in the brain's 'neural-screen'. A movie is said to be seen "there" that represents the "outside world", and we are told that in that brain-movie, we are seeing how that outsideworld is sensed and seen eventually in that brain. In this case, is pratyaksha for science a pramana for that 'outside world'? If it is not a pramana for that world, then can the conviction that we obtain be called prama within the framework of science?

  

3\. An interesting counter-angle for the other standpoint: In advaita the Self is all-pervading and all Knowledge is already in the Self, only hidden by ajnana. So even what is not directly pramana for certain vyavaharika knowledge can indirectly open our consciousness to that knowledge by virtue of resembling a pramana. So though the experiment or correlation-evidence may not constitute pramana for causality or general vyapti, still the vyapti-jnanam can rise in our mind because the Self has made manifest-contact with the reality. And such vyapti-jnanam or conviction should be regarded as prama (unless and until contradicted later). This is like showing the moon by pointing to the tree-top; the child may still see the moon because it is there shining from behind.
