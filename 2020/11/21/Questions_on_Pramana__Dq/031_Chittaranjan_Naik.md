Namaste Sri Raghav-ji,

  

  
*So, as long as the objects denoted by the words 'smoke' and 'fire' remain constant, we may seek to know the relation that abides between the objects denoted by these two words; and once this knowledge is obtained and has been validated, it may be termed as prama and such knowledge does not get sublated by the rise of any subsequent knowledge.*

  

Please append the words *"until rise of brahma-jnana"*to the above sentence.
