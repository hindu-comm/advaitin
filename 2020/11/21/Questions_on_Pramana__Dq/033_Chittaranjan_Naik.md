*Well, that still begs the question what makes the self-cognition or anuvyavasaaya occur.*

  

And that is a question that deserves to be answered. :-)

  

  

*And by deeming it fundamental or irreducible, svataH-prAmANyavAda avoids this infinite regress unlike parataH.  *

  

Merely deeming it fundamental and irreducible without understanding why it is so would amount to a dogma.

  

And as regards the infinite regress argument, perhaps you may not be aware that the Nayyayika Gangesa Misra has turned the infinite-regress-argument against the Vedantin himself. For he says that for the Nayyayika, the regress ends when there is anuvyavasaya but for the Vedantic who claims that every cognition is self-cognized and yet doubt cannot be prevented from arising, it will lead to a situation wherein the doubt will never cease. In other words, the attempt to annihilate the doubt will never cease since self-cognition, the device that is said to annihilate the doubt, itself cannot prevent it from arising. So, you see, the infinite-regress argument has been turned around to lay charge against the Vedantin himself. :-)

  

  

*Again, what I said is that the word "approximate" does not mean saMshaya.*

*We can say without any doubt that "the distance between a particular point in Mumbai and Delhi on so-and-so date is 1420 km" - that is pramA.*

  

Again, i repeat that whether the knowledge is held to be prama from the Indian traditional standpoint is not the question here. For, i would ask you the question: "What is it that assures you that the distance was indeed 1420 km on that day?" If you reply that it is empirical evidence as given by pratyaksha , i shall reply that when seen from a vehicle traveling at a velocity close to that of the velocity of light, the distance on that date was seen to be 1000 km only. And you see, your frame of reference has no privileged position over mine, so it is not possible for you to claim that the pratyaksha that established 1420 km has any special privileged position over the pratyaksha that claimed 1000 km. :-)
