Namaste Chittaranjan ji

Thank you to Putram ji for the full online quote.

Bhashya quoted says-

"For it is a patent fact of experience, that when a logician asserts, "This indeed is the true knowledge", it is upset by somebody else. And what is established by the latter is disproved by still another. "

While the context is no doubt brahma jiGYAsA, the criticism that tarka advanced by one taarkika is prone to contradiction by another smarter taarkika, was a general observation which is mentioned as an \*inherent limitation of tarka\*. And that \*general limitation inherent in tarka\* was being referred to by Sri Shankara to argue that their (taarkika) conclusions about pradhAna being jagat kAraNam are therefore flawed. (And so tarka should be shruti-matas-tarka etc. ) But that does not alter the validity of the fact that what is established as true by one taarkika is liable to be contradicted (or in some cases, refined and made defect-free by attaching upAdhIs to the hetu) by someone more sharp.
