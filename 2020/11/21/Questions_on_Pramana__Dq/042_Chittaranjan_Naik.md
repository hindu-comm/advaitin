The full quote does not change the fact that the entire bhashya to that sutra is made in the context of a subject matter whose knowledge is to be obtained from the Vedas alone. The tarka mentioned in those parts of the bhashya that you present is tarka regarding topics such as pradhana, etc, which are about ateendriya objects and which cannot be known by pratyaksha or anumana. And as regards the expression 'inherent limitation of tarka', it is true that tarka is inherently limited when it comes to determining the truth regarding ateendriya objects. :-)
