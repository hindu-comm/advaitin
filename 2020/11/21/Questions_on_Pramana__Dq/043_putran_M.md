Namaskaram Chitta-ji,

  

There is also the interesting line in there:

  

"And that true enlightenment has no diversity, since its contentisthe thing- in-itself.Thatcontent of knowledge is said to be the most real sinceitever remains the same; and *in the world, the knowledge**ofthat kind is said to be right knowledge, as for instance, the knowledge about fire thatit**is hot. This being the case,* people should have no divergence when they have true knowledge*, whereas the difference among people whose knowledge is based on reasoning is well known from their mutual opposition*."

  

This may in fact support your position; it can be understood that having *true* knowledge via pramanas (like "fire is hot") is possible, is non-divergent and vyavaharika satya, and is not to be conflated with knowledge based on the type of reasoning/tarka that the sutra is targetting here.
