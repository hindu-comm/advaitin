Dear Sri Putran-ji,

  

Here is an extract from another part of the brahma-sutra-bhashya, i.e., bhashya to sutra 1.1.4, which is also in the same vein:

  

"Hence the knowledge of Brahman is not dependent on human action.

On what does it depend then?  

It is dependent on the thing itself, as in the case of the knowledge of a thing got through such valid means as direct perception."  

(BSB.1.1.4)

  

It is clear from Shankara's words that a pramana such as direct perception presents an object 'as it is'. Obviously, Shankara means a defect-free perception.
