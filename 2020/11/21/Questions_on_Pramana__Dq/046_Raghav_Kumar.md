Namaste Putram ji and Chittaranjan ji

The issue here is not whether pratyaxa and anumAna deliver pramA. Of course they do.

  

Rather it is, whether there is any way to guarantee a given pratyaxa or anumAna will not be contradicted \*for all time to come\* (even before brahma GYAnam) before labeling a given perception or inference as pramA. Such a condition, as I said before, was not possible to fulfill as an \*a priori condition\* for every perception or inference. We cannot insist on that as an a priori condition because a given human intellect has only limited knowledge and sAmarthya about any viShaya and a clearer sharper buddhi can very well show flaws in it some time in the future.

  

Regarding Sri Shankara's mention of the taarkikas being contradicted by other taarkikas -

We see the numerous taarkikas contending about various \*indriya viShayas too\* (not just atIndriya viShayas like AtmA, svarga, Dharma etc., which are the province of the Veda) from sAmkhya and nyAya darshanas, each claiming that they alone have arrived at a faultless pramA while asserting that the other side has an imperfect syllogism due to the other side's mistaken application of intellect even on the topic of analyzing various entities in the world.

  

Even the simple example of rope-snake is sufficient to see that what is \*taken to be a snake\*, i.e., the snake is asserted to be pramA initially, later gets sublate. Such indriya and manodoShas are there in the human being which afflict his use of pratyaxa and anumAna-s. Whatwas earlier asserted to be pramA and held to be pramA for a length of time, later turns out to be bhrama.

  

  

You also made a very important point that a given anumAna is (a "platonic entity", so to speak) based on the eternal relationship between words whose meanings have been well-ascertained.

  

The situation there is that the general knowledge of a given entity (like smoke, fire etc) based on the word and its meaning is no doubt present. However, the doShas can very well be there in reaching inferential conclusions about that entity such as about its material constituents or it's relationship to other entities in the world which are infinite in number. In those contexts, we have to provisionally assume our perceptions and inferences are pramA unless they are contradicted in the future.

  

Thanks again for sharing your views on detail.
