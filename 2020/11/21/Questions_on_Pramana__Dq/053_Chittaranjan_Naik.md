Namaste Sri Raghav-ji,

  

  

*Any anumAna can have doShas like ativyApti etc., in the hetu which may only come to light much later after much closer examination by sharper intellects.  *
  

This is clearly a defect in the application of the method as it suffers from one of the three defects - avyapti, ativyapti and asambhava - that a hetu is supposed to be free from.

  

  

*In employing the syllogism, the person due to his individual defect can misapply it due to a wrong sAdhya and misperception of hetu (mistaking mist for smoke for example).  *

  

This too is a case of defective employment of the syllogism as the hetu is a misperceived hetu.

  

  

*Either of the two possibilities can be there.*  

  

Both the possibilities are not due to shortcomings in the method but are due to incorrect or defective employment of the method. The implication is that if the method should be employed without there being these defects in the employment, the syllogism would lead to a true conclusion. Why should the knowledge obtained from such defectless employment of the syllogism be treated as fallible within the realm of vyavahara and why would they not hold until the rise of brahma-jnana?
