> Namaste Chittaranjan ji
>
> The fact is that the defects in many anumAna-s may only come to light
> after a long time. The defects are only seen in hindsight. One person
> posits the anumAna oblivious to its defects. Another person much
> sharper finds out the AbhAsa-s / defects years later. All this is
> often observed.
>
>   
>
> This implies that the knowledge of worldly objects derived from
> anumAna are prone to correction of defects over the years. Moreover,
> such need for correction often arises due to new data. There is no
> guarantee that such viparIta dRShTAnta-s will not emerge after several
> years rendering the syllogism in need of modification.
>
>   
>
> But I do get your viewpoint on the eternal infallibility of anumAna
> based on words and meanings.
