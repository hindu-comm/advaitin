Dear Sri Putran-ji,

  

*Likewise with perception, even if pratyaksha is held to inform directly of the "real world" as it is, there can always be defects in our sense-organs and other obstructions to the application of pramana, that we are not aware of now and will be discovered later; there is no way to negate all such possibility of doubt even if presently we assume that we have prama of A.  *

  
There is a way to negate all such possibilities of doubt and that is what is achieved by anuvyvasaya or self-cognition. In Nyaya, this anuvyasaya is the cognition in which there is apperception or self-cognition. In Mimamsa and Vedanta however, every cognition is self-cognized, so what is it that provides freedom from doubt? If we go by Mimamsa, it would be that self-cognition in which sabda-pramana is unobstructed. This topic needs further discussion; we will do it some day.
