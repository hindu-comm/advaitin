These conversations between the scholars are quite enjoyable illustrating our limitation in understanding the “worldly knowledge.” Our agreements and disagreements of what we know and what we don’t know are expressions of our limited understanding of the different schools of thought. Any notion/definition/

terminologies has its built in limitations due to various levels of understanding of the different schools of thoughts.
