Namaste,  
  
I am currently reading Vedanta Sangraha of Ramaraya Kavi with the Commentary of Shri R. Balasubramanian. Explaining the famous Rope-Snake example of Vedanta in his Introduction to the work, Shri Balasubramanian writes as follows, “In this case, while the rope remains a rope all the time without undergoing any change, there is, nevertheless, the appearance of snake therein due to the modification of avidya in the form of snake. Advaita says that, while the rope, the substratum in this case, is the transfigurative material cause (vivarta upAdAna kAraNa), avidya is the transformative material cause(pariNAmi upAdAna kAnaNa). The moment the person gets the right knowledge of the object in front as the rope, avidya disappears along with the snake of which it is the material cause.”  
  
I am unable to understand the above statement. Can a knowledgeable person explain to me what is meant by vivarta upAdAna karana and pariNAmi upAdAna karana? I can understand the rope being the material cause of the snake, but how can something as immaterial as Avidya be the material cause it?  
  
Many thanks and regards,  
Venkat
