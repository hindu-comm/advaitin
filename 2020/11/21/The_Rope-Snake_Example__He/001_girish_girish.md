My 2 cents.

  

There are 4 aspects in the rope-snake anology:

1\. Actual presence of rope

2\. Idea of rope

3\. idea of snake

4\. presence of snake.

  

The idea of snake and presence of snake is the drik-dirshya viveka.
This is the pariNAmi upAdAna kAnaNa.

The idea of rope is vivarta upAdAna kAraNa.

  

Further:

The Idea of snake is jIva bhAva (mithyAtma)

The presence of snake is jagat bhAva (gouNAtma)

The Idea of rope is samyakbhAva (pratyagAtma)

Thesubstratum rope is the sat-chit - 'That Is'.
