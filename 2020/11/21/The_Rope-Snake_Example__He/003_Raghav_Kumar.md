The dream analogy is the best one available for creation of a dream world of objects with the flimsy material viz., veiling (AvaraNa) of the waker-identity and projection (vixepa) of the waker's samskAras (impressions and memories) to form specific dream content.

  

In the context of the dream example, the vivarta upAdAna kAraNa of dream would be the waker with his waking mind and body. A loss of wealth or health in a dream does not affect the waker's wealth or health.

  

And the pariNamI upAdAnakAraNa would be the waker's samskAraswhich are used to create the dream story.
