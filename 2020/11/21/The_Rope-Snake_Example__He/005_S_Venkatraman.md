Namaste Praveenji and Raghavji,

  

Your posts went a long way in clearing my confusions; but did not entirely remove them. Perhaps I did not express myself adequately.  

  

The Rope and the Snake belong to two different levels of reality - Rope Vyavaharika and the Snake Prathibhasika. A change of something at one level to another at a different level, can only be an appearance and not a real change. I agree that the Rope is the vivarta upadana karana by which term I understand that the Rope provides the substratum on which the appearance of there Snake takes place.

  

In the Rope-Snake example, Avidya to me seems more a nimitta karana and not an upadana karana because the material for the Snake has already been provided by the Rope. Since the Snake is only an appearance, Avidya also can only be a vivarta nimitta Karana - a cause which brings about an appearance change and not any real change. How can Avidya be parinami upadana karana because the term parinami suggests real change which as we have seen is not possible in this case?

  

In the dream example, as per the logic (or the lack of it) above, the samskaras (which are in Karana sharira and which are also Avidya karya) are the vivarta upadana karana and Avidya itself the vivarta nimitta karana.

  

Please correct me if I am wrong.

  

Praveenji, I have written to you separately on your Vicharasagara classes.

  

Many thanks and regards,

Venkat
