The rope is not the upadana / material cause of the imagined snake. The rope is the vivarta upadana karana for the snake. It is the rope that is wrongly seen as the snake. According to the Vedanta Paribhasha, the illusory snake is 'created' at that moment of apprehending it in the locus of the rope.
