Dear Karthikji,  

  

Suppose the doctor has told a person that he has only 24 hours to live, and he realizes that many things would remain satya to him for 24 hours, but if he really dies in 24 hours, those things , which are going to be satya to him for 24 hours, will become mithya to him after 24 hours. So he should realize that what he thinks as satya for 24 hours, he may as well think that those things which are satya for Only 24 hours can be as good as mithya, for him.  

  

When Bhima killed Duryodhana by hitting the latter on his thigh, Lord Balarama became very angry with BHima, then Lord Krrishna told Lord Balaram to think that Kali has come (though in fact Kali was 36 years away).  

  

As all the deliberations in the group has still left some doubts in your mind about the mithyatva, this is my another attempt to elucidate the concept of Mithyatca. Hope this helps.

  

Sincerely

Sunil KB
