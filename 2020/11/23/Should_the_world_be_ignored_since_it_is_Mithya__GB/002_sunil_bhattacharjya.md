Dear Kartikji,  

  

In fact, there is sign of mithyatva in life in a human being, every now and then, as so many fond hopes are shattered in the lives of the people, whom we call unlucky. In fact, nobody is truly lucky, whose all hopes are fulfilled all the time. Once one realizes that all these things in life are anitya, it is best that one does not forget for a moment that one will have to part with all these anitya things. Developing vairagya towards the anitya things are good in the long run, and particularly when one is seeking the oneness with the divine consciousness, where there is no trace of unhappiness.  

This is another way, how one can think the world to be mithya, for one's own good.  

  

Sincerely,
