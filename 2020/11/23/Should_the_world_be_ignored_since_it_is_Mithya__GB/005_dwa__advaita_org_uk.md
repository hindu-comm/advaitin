Hi Karthik-ji,



The situation is actually worse than you imply, There are some Advaitins who argue that the world literally disappears on gaining enlightenment – and they provide many quotations from prasthAna traya and Shankara to purportedly support this contention! You may be interested to read the various posts and following discussions. I have been defending the view that this is NOT the case. (Although I have received very little support!) I maintain that, on enlightenment, the intellect of the person now realizes that the world is just name and form of Brahman. That person then continues to live until expiry of the prArabdha karma that brought about that embodiment. He or she may also become a jIvanmukta, depending upon the extent to which the mind has been ‘purified’. Death signifies the end of saMsAra. (Of course this is all a vyAvahArika perspective. We accept that, in reality, no one has ever been born etc.)
