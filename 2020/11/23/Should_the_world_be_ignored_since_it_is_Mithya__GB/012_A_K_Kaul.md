Your statement is actually a paraphrase of Aadi Shankara's Viveka Chudamani 484

क्वगतंकेन वा नीतं कुत्र लीनमिदं जगत् \| अधुनैव मया दृष्टं नास्ति किं महदद्भुतम् \|\|

Similarly, in the Vasistha Maharamayana, there is a beautiful incident!

Vasishtha Muni kept on repeating time and again, in factad nauseum and ad infinitum, in the Yogavasishthathat the world/universewas non-existent!

He has gone to the extent of claiming that since it was never "born" thus it does not exist at all and therefore there is no question of it having an end either!

Bhagwan Ram poses a very queer question: ''If the universe does not exist, then who is this Vasishtha Muni who is answering my questions and with whom I am having this discussion".

On hearing this argument, all of a sudden there was a deafening silence since Vasishta Muni ''had no answer"!

After a long interval of silence, Bhagwan Ram----in fact the entire "sabha" of listeners, including Dasharatha and Vyasa and Devala etc. Munis---- felt uncomfortable that Bhagwan Ram had put such an inconvenient question to His Guru for which even the latter had no answer.

Bhagwan Ram apologisedto Vasishtha Muni for having put such a question which even he could not answer for He had felt that there was hardly any such question which even the omniscient Vasishtha Muni could not answer!

Vasishtha Muni, on hearing this plea of Bhagwan Ram answered, "No! I am not unable to answer your question! But since you had asked as to if the universe itself is non-existent, how could the Vasishtha Muni who was answering my question, exist then!

'And because in reality there is no Vasishtha Muni either (because the universe itself is non-existent!), naturally, how could a non-existent Vasishtha Muni answer any question at all Hence silence was the only answer to your aquestion!"

Similarly, one of the most ''confusing and mutually conflicting" (in fact illuminating!) definitions of the Indefinable has been given in the very first viz. the Isha Upanishat 4-5 in the following words:

अनेजदेकं मनसो जवीयो नैनद् देवाप्नुवन् पूर्वमर्षत् \| तद्धावतो अन्यानत्येति तिष्ठत् तस्मिन्नपो मातरिश्वा ददाति\|  

तदेजति तन्नैजति तद्दूरे तद्द्वन्तिके \| तदन्तरस्य सर्वस्य तदुसर्वस्यास्य बाह्यतः \|

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!
