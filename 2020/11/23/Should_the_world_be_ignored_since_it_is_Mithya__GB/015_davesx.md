Namaste Karthik-ji

  

If you are following the discussion on Nyaya logic, it is questioning the appearance of an external world and whether it is separate from you.

  

Isn't everything arising and ceasing in one's mind each moment? Even when an object is considered to be external, isn't it only a mental ideation, a thought construct or informational representation? Actually no one has ever seen any object independent of one's mind. One may see a scenery outside the body but not outside your mind.  

  

Eminent philosophers of Dvaita talk erroneously of external object assuming it is  

outside one's mind without proper analysis and inquiry. Body is an idea in mind, and to talk of object being outside body is tantamount to saying it is outside mind--an impossibility! Hence the existence of things as external is unproven.
