[[Akilesh Ayyar	2020-11-28, 11:59:27 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/GBprgU2WP50)]]

Who said Sankara did all these things? Ajnanis say he did it. They attribute agency, name the world, name individuals, say they did x and y and z, ascribe time and space, and do all the rest. That may not have been Sankara's "viewpoint." And his true viewpoint cannot even be detected in his words, because, again, what do his words mean? Who is interpretingthem?

  

The world being mithya does not mean it should be ignored -- it means that it is not an object at all... and therefore is not available for either ignoring or not ignoring. And theones who would decide whether to ignore or not are mithya too, meaning that there is in fact no decision-maker who gets to decide to ignore or not.

  

These are not merely concepts. This is the actual Truth. The end of Self-inquiry is to liberate us fromthe illusion of decision-making about all kinds of topics, including these.
