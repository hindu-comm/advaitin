Namaste Putranji:

Here is a statement made by Lord Krishna in verse 66 of chapter 18 where Ishwara (Bhagwan Krishna) asks us to

सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज ।  
अहं त्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा शुचः ॥ १८.६६ ॥

sarva-dharmān-parityajya mām-ekam śaraṇam vraja \|  
aham tvā sarva-pāpebhyo mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śuca: \|\| 18.66 \|\|

Swami Bhoomananda Thirtha beautifully translates as “Abandoning all dual notions of dharma (and[**adharma**](https://www.narayanashramatapovanam.org/component/seoglossary/2-glossary/210-adharma)), take refuge in Me alone. I shall redeem you from all sins. Grieve not.”

He further explains this verse that answers your doubts and supports Sri Karthik’s assertion. The process of transcending Dharmas and dualities requires an understanding of the entire Gita and in particular verses 2.45, 4.22, 5.3, 7.28, 15.5 and others. Swamiji’s discussion below is profound: “Dharma has its relevance when contrasted with adharma. Both thus go together, making a pair of opposites (dvandvas). Krishna has been emphasizing throughout, the need for rising above dvandvas. To pursue dharma is verily to denounce adharma. But this division, by its very nature, is contrary to the supreme spiritual Truth of Oneness. Dharma is but a transitory means to help the seeker. As the dhārmic votary has to keep away from adharma, the spiritual seeker has inevitably to rise above dharma too. Only then he can reign in the domain of the Impersonal, Indivisible Soul. Thus, in asking Arjuna and other seekers to abandon all dharmas, Krishna is in fact lifting them from all delusional constraints, and installing them safely in the supreme Oneness that penetrates and surrounds them.”
