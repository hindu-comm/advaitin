Dear Ramchandranji,

  

You said it well. I just want to say that the word "dharma" has been used in more than one sense, and that means we have to be careful where one uses the word "dharma'. Dharma may mean property, such as the dharma of the fire is to burn. Dharma may also mean righteousness, and adharma may mean unrighteousness. Lord Krishna is telling us to rise above all the mental conflicts of the anitya samsara and surrender to the Lord.

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
