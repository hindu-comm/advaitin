Dear Mr./Mrs. Ekam,

There are many youtube videos on vedantic meditation by several swamijis and teachers. If you want a pure vedantic meditation

which does not identify with any tradition (like advaita, dwaitaetc.,) you may see the videos of Mooji, which are aplenty on youtube.

They capture the essence of the method of Vedanta. If you want to hear some traditional swami, you may look for the videos of

Swami Tattvavidananda or of Swami Dayananda saraswati of Arsha Vidya Gurukulam([avgsatsang.org](http://avgsatsang.org)). You may search for 'guided

meditations' of the above teachers.

  

If you have time to read, I strongly recommend that you read Srimad Bhagavatam, which is almost like a commentary on what all

the Upanishads and the Gita say. Several episodes are like meditation pieces. I find it to be a nididhyasana text.

Aravinda Rao
