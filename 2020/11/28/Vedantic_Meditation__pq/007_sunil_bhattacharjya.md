Dear Ekamji

Good you admitted that you are not aware of who is advaitin. In most of, if not in all the past discussions, I have tried to make you understand that one who read / heard (shravana) the Upanishadic Mahavakyas, like “tattvamasi”, did the manana (applied mind on that/made efforts to understand that) and then gone to the next step of meditation on that, is a follower of the teachings of the Advaita Vedanta. Shri Madhvacharya did not know about the validity of this great path, and he tried to find fault with the Upanishadic Mahavakyas and I call him and his followers non-advaitin, and specifically call them Dvaitins, and they seem to be happy at that. I hope the other scholars would also try to help you out with still simpler language.

Secondly, it is not proper to call the Dvaita path as Dvaita Vedanta, as it opposes Vedantic / Upanishadic teachings, which culminate in the Mahavakyas. Hope the great scholars like Sadanandaji and Aravindaji would be able to tell you all these in a simpler language.

Best wishes

SKB
