Dear Elam-ki,

  

I sense your earnestness in this endeavor butbeforeyou rush offadopting anymeditation prescription,a bit of critical thinkingwill clear up some misconceptions.

  

Most of these traditional meditationpackages are actually an enducement toshiftfrom one unconscioustrance to another, rather than awakeningfrom the sleeptrance.

  

Meditation is actuallynot the focus of Gyana and if mentioned itis merely a prerequisiteto calm the restlessmind. What Is emphasizedis listening toand repeatedlycontemplatinganyUpanishadic Mahavakyauntil It’s true import is revealed and becomesa settled realization.

  

When the Vedantic preceptorutters ‘Tattwamasi’for example, the student noticeshow oftenhis attention is distracted andcaptivated by hisunconscious thought pattern - the narrative, a seriesofrepetitiveideas(or rogueprogram)running in memory,formulatinga finite selfidentity- and upon thisrecognition or insightthe identity crisis, the trance,is immediatelybroken revealing the Infinity of one’sinnateSwarupa declared bythe Mahavakya.

  

So Self-Recognition is the emphasis in Advaitarather thanyogicmeditation.
