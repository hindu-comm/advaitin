Dear Subbuji:

I do not dispute what is said in Ramayana but we can expand our understanding the fundamentals of Vedanta. Dreams, Visions, Intuition and Realization are all often mentioned and discussed by Vedantins and my posting was to point out the interconnections.

Dreams act as night-time processors where the rational, waking mind finally goes to sleep, allowing hidden thoughts, fears, and memories to surface through symbols and images. Dream characters and environments are projections of our own internal consciousness and not necessarily caused by external forces.

Vision takes the raw, unreal sensible parts of dreams and turns them into a larger perspective or useful purpose for life.

Intuition is a direct inner knowing that bypasses step-by-step rational thought or external proof.

Realization is the moment of emergence of Truth by which our abstract dreams becomes undeniably clear and integrated into our waking reality.

In short, dreams, visions, intuition and realization are deeply interconnected inner experiences where the subconscious or spiritual mind communicates without the interference of waking logic.

We are all still trying our level best to understand the basics and hopefully with proper focus, our dreams can become a reality!
