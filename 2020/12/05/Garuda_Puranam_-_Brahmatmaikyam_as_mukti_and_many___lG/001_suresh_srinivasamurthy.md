Namaste Sri Subbu-ji,

  

BrahmAtmaikatvam is fine but Shareera-Atma bheda must be accepted by advaitins also.

  

If shareera is mithya then theteaching of BrahmAtmaikatvam also becomes mithya.

Jagat as a shareera cannot be a hindrance or limitation for Brahman who is nirupAdhika.

  

So, the elimination of dvaita is actually the elimination of "many paramAtmans"that is appearing

to be indwelling in all the many jivas/bodies. (VaishwAnara) Fire is always one even though there

are manyinstances of the same in all the many beings.

  

The following Sloka in the beginning of Garuda PurAna makes it very clear that Brahman  

that ispresent in all -**सर्वभूतस्थितं** is One.

  

ॐ अजमजरमनन्तं ज्ञानरूपं महान्तं शिवममलमनादिं भूतदेहादिहीनम् ।।  
सकलकरणहीनं **सर्वभूतस्थितं** तं हरिममलममायं सर्वगं वन्द एकम् ।। 1.1।।  

  

The following sloka quoted by you is also teaching Vishwarooptva - universal form and "avina bhAva"

inseparable oneness that obtainsbetween jagat / body and Brahman / Atman.

  

न विना परमात्मानं किञ्चिज्जगति विद्यते ॥ १,२३५.३७ ॥  
विश्वरूपं तमेवैकमिति ज्ञात्वा विमुञ्चति ।  

  

This is what I am trying to establish in all my earlier posts. From this,it follows that there is nothing

in the world that does not have Brahman as the Atma.

  

The following sloka is teaching that everything arises from that one paramAtmA / nArAyaNa.

  
एको नारायणो देवो देवानामीश्वरेश्वरः ।।  
परमात्मा परं ब्रह्म जन्माद्यस्य यतो भवेत् ।। 1.12 ।।  
  

The spider and its web are different and the web when it iswithdrawn, is inseparably one with the Spider.

So why can't it be said that all Ikya vAkyAs are actually teaching inseparable oneness ofjiva/jagat and Brahman?

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
