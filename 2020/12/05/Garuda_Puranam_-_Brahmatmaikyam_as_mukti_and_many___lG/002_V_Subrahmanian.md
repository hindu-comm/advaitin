The above makes Brahman dependent on a sharira, in the form of the jagat and jiva, for its own existence. This is not the way Vedanta teaches Brahman. Just as a jivatma exists without the sharira and therefore can exist as liberated Brahman, Brahman also must exist without having to depend on the jiva-jagat sharira.
