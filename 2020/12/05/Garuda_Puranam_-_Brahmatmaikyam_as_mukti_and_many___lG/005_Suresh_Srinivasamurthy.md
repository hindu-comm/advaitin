Your comments against Dvaitin's seems to be based on emotion. You need to realize that the same Brahman exists in all whether one is a advaitin or dvaitin or whatever his philosophical position is. The argument between a dvaitin and advaitin will never stop unless the same paramAtman/Brahman is SEEN /Realized in each other.

  

The SharIra / pancha kosha / prancha bhUtAmaka prakriti do not disappear to nowhere/ShUnya. It has its origin/abode/destination in Brahman. As of now the shareera is dependent on the Atman/Brahman and it is in His control. . All pramANa vAkyas including the mahAvAkyas are pointing to this oneness of paramAtman that is indwelling in all the many name/forms/bodies. The epithet "SarvabhUtstham Ekam" is very significant here.
