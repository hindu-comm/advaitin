Namaste Suresh ji

You wrote -

  
"If shareera is mithya then theteaching of BrahmAtmaikatvam also becomes mithya."

  

The word mithyA does not imply something has no causal efficacy. mithyA food satisfies mithyA hunger.

  

AvidyA which is mithyA is removed by the GYAna-vRtti born GYAnopadesha by Guru which is also mithyA.

  

GYAnam is satyaM but GYAna-vRtti which is instrumental in arriving at or awakening to GYAnam, can very well be mithyA.

  

I request you to correct the error in your understanding of the word "mithyA" which you have conflated with "unreal" in your past posts as well as this post . In the context of orders of realities in Advaita, the word mithyA is \*not\* unreal. The word for unreal in Advaita teaching is "asat".

  

You keep mixing up 'mithyA' with unreal ('asat') in your posts. They are different words in Advaita.

  

Most vishiShTAdvains and dvaitins are unable to wrap their minds around this word mithyA as presented by Advaita. Please note that the word mithyA can mean other things such as "untruth" etc., in \*other\* contexts. In Advaita, mithyA does not mean "unreal".

  

In a recent post, it was pointed out that Sri Shankara's examples of how ChAndogya upaniShad mentions an occurrence in dream (seeing a woman or person with dark teeth) which foretells/forebodes an event in the waking state. Another example is of the dream tiger which startles a person awake. Although these dream entities belong to a lower order of reality, \*they convey some knowledge which does not get negated upon waking\* just because the conveying mechanism was through dream entities. Similar is the case why GYAna-vRtti which is a subtle mano-vRtti which sublates everything \*including itself\*, still manages to permanently remove avidyA and awakens the jIva to his true nature as brahman.

  

Therefore it is correct to say that the sharIra is mithyA and brahmAtmaikyaGYAna-vRtti which arises in the mind which is itself mithyA when taught the mahAvAkya by the Guru (this teaching transaction is also of the mithyA order of reality), neverthless permanently removes avidyA which is also mithyA.

  

BrahmAtma alone is satyam. There are no transactions in satyam brahma.
