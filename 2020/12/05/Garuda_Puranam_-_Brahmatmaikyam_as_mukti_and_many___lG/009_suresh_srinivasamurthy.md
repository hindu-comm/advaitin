Dear Advaitins,

  

It is funny that Sri Sunil-ji calls me a Dvaitin and you call me a V.Advaitin and many V.Advaitins call me a covered Advaitin because I happily move around with Advaitins and go to Shiva temple and like to See Vishnu in Shiva and vice versa🙂. My SharaNagati is still undisturbed because I am always looking at "Sarbhootastha", "SarvanAma vAchya" Brahman.  

  

Yes, I am a student of the current ParakAlamatam Swamy. But I was fortunate to do vedAdhyayanam under VedaBrahma Shri Shankara ShAstry, who was a great traditional Advaitin! I have also studied one-on-one under many Dvaita scholars. As a result, I have turned out to be a friend of all! I can proudly call myself a Hindu with no conditions!

  

I was also fortunate to study under Sri Rangapriya SwAmiji whose spiritual Guru was the great Sri Ranga Mahaguru, who established the AshtAnga Yoga VijyAna mandiram.

  

The etymological meaning of Vishnu according to Sri Ranga Priya Swamiji is - vyApnOti Vishwam Vishati cha - which simply means "one who pervades the entire universe and indwells in it". This in other words means "the All-pervading and indwelling Self". No wonder,Sri Rangapriya Swamiji had followers from all the three traditions!  

  

If Brahman is accepted as the universal Self, the universe/jagat can still exist as the body/linga/manifestation of Brahman!

The entire universe is the apara Brahman which is essentially made of shabda/veda/akshara. It is the indicator as well as the indicated!

  

If you deny it's existence you deny your own existence because your existence is not apart from it. Instead of doing deep analysis there is a need to look at reality as a whole.

  

"Om ityetad aksharam idagm sarvam" - your self, my self, this self, that self every blessed self is not external to this universal Self. How you relate to this universal Self-paramAtma is upto you. But in the embodied state it cannot denied that we are all absolutely dependent on this universal Purusha. This unites both saguna and nirguna Brahman.

  

This is the basis of my philosophy which goes very well with Veda, B.Gita and B.Sutra.Now tell me how this is not establishing Advaita Brahman?

  

Namaste,  
Suresh
