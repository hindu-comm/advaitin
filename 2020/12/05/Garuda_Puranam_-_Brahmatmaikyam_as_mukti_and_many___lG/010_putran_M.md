Namaskaram Suresh-ji,

  

With regard to your question "how this is not establishing 'Advaita Brahman'?", my answer would be that you have superimposed divisions on Brahman, such as 'universal Self' and its linga sharira (body/'universe'). Your starting point is already the context of a jiva identifying Self with certain upadhis and identifying Self with other upadhis, calling it variously as body and self, individual and universal, seer and seen. Visishtadvaita says such qualifications account for real difference in Brahman; advaita says they are mithya or vyavaharika-satya. Now, your phrase "deny its existence" suggests that the advaitin is saying the jiva-Ishvara bheda is asat - others have pointed out that mithya is not the same as asat.

  

I had earlier given the window-suns vs Sun analogy. One can take the standpoint of windows to describe the suns variously; the child takes the differences as real. The adult knows that the same Sun is being seen variously; doesn't mean the adult sees something different from the child, because both are inside the house. The adult is not negating what the child is seeing nor seeing anything different, yet knows a higher Truth regarding the Sun outside their house of upadhis/adhyasa where the window-qualifications simply don't apply.
