Dear friends,  

  

Nice that Sureshji was exposed to the teachings of Advaita, Vishishtadvaita and Dvaita teachers, but he seems to be thoroughly confused, in the sense that it appears to him that all these three streams are Vedantic, which is not correct. Only the Advaitavada is Vedantic, as it values the Vedantic Mahavakyas.  Further, it is a pity that both the Dvaita school and the Vishishtadvaita school claim to believe Lord Krishna to be the Supreme Lord and believe that they have the highest regards for Him. But they have not read the Mahabharata properly, even if at all they claim to have seen the Mahabharata, which Vedavyasa called as the Karshna Veda (or Lord Krishna's Veda). They have not seen that in the Mahabharata, Vedavyasa clearly said that the Bhagavad Gita has 745 verses out of which Lord Krishna spoke 620 verses. In the presently available Bhagavad Gita only 575 verses of Lord Krishna are there and that means this is not the complete Bhagavad Gita, which Lord Krishna spoke. Moreover, they have even not read the Uddhava Gita. which has all the verses of Lord Krishna intact. That claeraly shows that they have not received the Ajatavada message from Lord Krishna and that is why they are thoroughly confused.   

  

Though we have empathy for them we can't do anything, as the Dvaitins and the Vishishtadvaitins are so full of ego that the don't care to listen to the Vedantic teachings of the Advaitavada, which culmnated in the Vedantic Mahavakyas. We the Sanatana dharmis are lucky that Adi Shankaracharya had trained his disciples well and the highest Advaitic philosophy is being taught even today in its pristine form.  

  

Regards,

skb
