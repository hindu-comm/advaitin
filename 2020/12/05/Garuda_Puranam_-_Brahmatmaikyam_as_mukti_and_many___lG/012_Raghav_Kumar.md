Namaste Sada ji

Thank you for sharing the background. That was helpful.

  

I have observed that resistance to the Advaita sampradaya stems from

  

1\. A misunderstanding of Advaita as something that invalidates bhagavad-bhakti and the world altogether as tuccham or asat and unreal etc.

  

2\. A strong commitment to some other acharya or teacher who has some significant reservations about Advaita.

  

Those who are in category 1, by further study under Advaita Acharyas, may be able to come to a clearer understanding and then they give up needless nitpicking of Advaita.

  

But those in category 2 will find the going quite tough because unless that particular non-advaitic acharya whom they regard and revere as prAmANika changes his view, they will find it difficult to clarify their misreading of advaita.
