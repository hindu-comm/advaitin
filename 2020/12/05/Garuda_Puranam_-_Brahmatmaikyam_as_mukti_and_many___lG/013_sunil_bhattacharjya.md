Dear Raghavji,

  

Until and unless they have respect for the Mahavakyas, nothing can be done.  

  

Secondly, if somebody conveniently ignores that Adi Shankara did include Lord Vishnu among the deities worshipped in the Panchayatana puja, what can you do ?

  

Thirdly, Is there any Dvaita or Vishishtadvaita composition, which is superior in devotion to the "Bhaja Govindam" or "Moha Mudgara"
composed by Adi Shankaracharya. ?

  

Fourthly, in one of his bhashyas, , **Anandagiri, around 8th century CE (AD), quoted a verse from Manu, which stated that Brahmins should worship Lord Shiva, Kashatriyas should worship Lord Vishnu, the Vaishyas should worship Lord Brahma and the Shudras should worship Ganapati.** Lord Krishna said in a purana (to my memory it is in the Agni Purana) that the Devi (Mother Bhavani or Gauri) should be worshipped for Mukti. Unfortunately the verse quoted by Anandagiri imight not be available in all versions of Manu Smrti, presently in circulation. It appears that some people, who did not like the verse, could have removed the verse from the printed copies of the Manu Smrti. ***In this respect the Dvaitins and the Vishishtadvaitns are violating what Manu wrote, and what Manu says is considered as Vedavakya, unless modified to the need of times by any of the subequent four complementary Smrtis.***  

  

Jai Shri Krishna

Sunil KB
