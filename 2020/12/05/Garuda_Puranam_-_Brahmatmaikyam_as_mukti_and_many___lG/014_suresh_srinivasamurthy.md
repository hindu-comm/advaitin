Namaste Sri Putran-ji,

  

Actually, what is upAdhi in Advaita is SharIra in V.Advaita and Pratibimba in Dvaita.

In all these views Brahman is accepted as the inner Self of all. This is the common factor in all the three traditions.  

  

As already explained, themeaning of Vishnu/Brahman as the all-pervading and indwelling Self perfectly supports Advaita.

But still there is a need to accept the reality of jagat as the body or indicator so that the all-pervading nature of Brahman

is realized. Samatva is attained only when we SEE the same Brahman in all jIva/jada entities. This we have seen clearly in

the Sridhara Swamy's Bhagavata bhAshya posted by Sri Subbu-ji in the other thread.

  

Just because your body is made of parts do you think you, as atman made of parts? The atman is always part-less even though

it is indwelling in a body made of parts. Similarly, Brahman as the Supreme Self (paramAtma) who is pervading, indwelling and

supporting the entire universe as body is part-less also. The teaching that every entity in jagath is the shareera of Brahman is

explicitly taught in antaryAmi brAhmaNa and in Gita and Sutra.So, it is incorrect understanding to say that Brahman is made of

parts in V.Advaita.

  

I know in advaita all these are upAdhi kruta and upon aikya jnAna it goes away.But untillthat pAramArthika state is attained,

it helps sAdhakas to look upon the entire universe as the manifestation of that supreme Ishwara/Brahman.

BTW, attainment of kaivalya mOksha is accepted as valid in V.Advaita.

  

  

\<

I had earlier given the window-suns vs Sun analogy. One can take the standpoint of windows to describe the suns variously; the child takes the differences as real. The adult knows that the same Sun is being seen variously; doesn't mean the adult sees something different from the child, because both are inside the house. The adult is not negating what the child is seeing nor seeing anything different, yet knows a higher Truth regarding the Sun outside their house of upadhis/adhyasa where the window-qualifications simply don't apply.

\>  

  

There is a similar mantra in the Aruna Prashna - "nAnA lingatvAt ritUnAm nAnA sUryatvam". The same sun gets to be called differently due to difference in the ritu / season. This analogy could also be used to prove the oneness of sarvAntaryAmi paramAtman manifesting in varied forms as explicitly taught in Sri Rudram.

  

IMHO this sarvanAma vAchya Brahman establishes Advaita without needing for any kind of upAdhi, because that alone IS.

Whatever you see and touch is Brahman only.

  

Let me make it clear that my only intention is to unite all views and Hindus. I have no fanatic agenda to push my own view on anyone. 

  

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
