Dear Putranji  
It is a pity that the Dvaitins and the Vishashtadvaitins haven’t understood Lord Krishna’s statement on Ajatavada. They have not even understood the first verse of the Yogasutra on what Yoga is. Lord Krishna was time and again asking Arjuna “Be yogi”. A yogi becomes able to control the mind and achieve Pratyahara and can realize the Ajatavada and thus can become a Sthitaprajna or Jivanmukta. This is beyond their understanding and they are also unable to admit that. Unfortunately this is a hopeless situation. Maybe a good Advaita teacher like Lord Buddha can help. In his time many brahmins had similar problem and Lord Buddha could convince them as to how they can become one with the Brahman. I should not go into Lord Buddha’s way of explanation here.  
Regards  
SKB
