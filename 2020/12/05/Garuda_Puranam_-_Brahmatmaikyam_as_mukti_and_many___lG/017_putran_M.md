Namaskaram,

  

> Brahman (as Dreamer) is the real; the dream of "king", "dharma",
> "worshipping", "God" etc. is mithya.
>
>   

  

Brahman is the Real; the Dreamer-dream duality is itself mithya. However this adhyaropa allows us to grasp the mithyatva of the dream-dualities while situating Brahman as its non-dual Dreamer (Ishvara) or substratum.
