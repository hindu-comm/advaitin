Dear Sri Putran-ji,

  

My view is always based on Brahman as the Universal / Cosmic person (Vishwaroopi, Vaishwanara Atman).

From this point every individual perception / act / vyavahAra becomes a universal action or yagya.

  

When you feed a brAhmaNa or sAdhu or sanyAsi, or jiva, don't you think you are feeding Brahman in the form of vaishwAnara?

When you do prANAgnihotra dont you think you are doing yagya onto the indwelling Brahman.Every vyavahAra can be spiritualized

or could be made pAramArthika if it is done in bramhArpaNa bhAva.

  

This is the basis on which all vedAntic schools can be united. I know all of this comes under vyAvahArika satya in Advaita.

But still, these vaidhika yagyas or actions are common among all traditional VedAntins and is a must for attainment of Brahman.

  

If this is trashed as nothing, Vedantins will end up as Buddhists.
