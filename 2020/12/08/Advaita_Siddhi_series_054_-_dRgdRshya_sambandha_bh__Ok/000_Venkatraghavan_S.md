This chapter provides the *anukUla tarka*, or supportive reasoning for the inference of the world's *mithyAtva*.

  

The*anukUla tarka*or supportive reasoning given by the*advaitin*in this chapter is that there is no real relationship possible between the seer and the seen (objects) - despite this, the world is cognised. Therefore, it follows that there is an*AdhyAsika sambandha*, an unreal relationship between the seer and the world. Therefore, the world too is unreal.
