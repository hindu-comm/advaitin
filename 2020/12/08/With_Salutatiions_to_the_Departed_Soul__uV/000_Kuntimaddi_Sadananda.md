I felt sad to hear the news that Jay Lakhani passed away. I knew him since 1997. I was living in Saudi Arabia when he sent an email asking questions about Hindu dharma. We began email exchanges.

I was one of the moderators of a group called Advaitin which promotes Adi Shankara's non-duel philosophy [](https://www.facebook.com/frank.maiello.5?__cft__%5B0%5D=AZXOvCE6FKdSxCZld9f6JJg1IV9NRZ0kzsdni2BLmf3mMJ8cnCInBeCSrEWBTcMmKFwf2JmNO4m58nQmaoHM3I8lmFm4iGBNNDV7S1jml1h6lg&__tn__=-%5DK-R)

Kuntimaddi Sadananda

ji were fellow moderators. Swami Vivekananda is one of the greatest modern-day proponents of this philosophy. Jay was a member of this group. Thus began my interaction with Jay ji.

Jay was one of the few persons I contacted after I arrived in the U.K to settle. He was always cordial and helpful. We had academic disagreements when it came to the ritualistic aspects of Hindu dharma. I prefer to maintain rituals in their pure form, whereas Jay wanted to modernise them. In Hindu dharma we say 'spardhayaa vardhate vidyaa' through debates we increase our wisdom. I considered my academic disagreements as a way to improve my knowledge. Jay Lakhani ji was a pleasant individual. He promoted Hindu Dharma in U K. Thousands of children are educated in Hindu dharma in UK schools using the guidebooks created by Jay. My heartfelt condolences to Jay Lakhani's family and thousands of his followers.

May his Atman attain svargati.
