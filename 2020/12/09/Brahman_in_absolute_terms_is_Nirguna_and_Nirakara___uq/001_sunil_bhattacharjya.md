Dear Subbuji,

  

The Puranas have gone to the extent to supplement the Ajativada verse, by giving the life-time of the creation, in a way, by giving the life-time of Lord Brahma, the Creator**,** of Lord Vishnu, the Maintainer**,** and of Lord Shiva, the Destroyer**;** so as to wind up one cycle of the process of creation. I do not exactly remember the reference verses, as I read it several decades ago.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna

Sunil KB
