> Could you please explain how Nirguna Brahman is able to take a form
> with guna in order to bless sAdhakas.

  

It is in association with Maya. Even this association is not a voluntary act of NB. It is the Shaastra that makes this 'marriage' possible. So, for advaitins, it is not NB that 'takes a form' but the shaastra that has presented the NB as saguna for the purpose of upasana.
