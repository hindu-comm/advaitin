Dear Subbuji,

  

Any idea as to why is it that the intelligent persons like Sureshji have not heard / read about ***"HE wanted to be** **many***"? Such enquiry may be helpful in understanding the non-advaitins like Sureshji, who seem to be feeling insecure and is pursuing the bogey of unity among Dvaita, Vishishtadvaita and Advaita, in spite of Shri Hanumanji showing the broad unity in principle by saying to Lord Ram as follows **:** ***In (i) "deha-buddhi" I am "your daasa", in (ii) "jeeva-bddhi" I am "your amsha" and (iii) "spiritually" I am "yourself".  ***

***  ***

***When will the Non-advaitins realize that there is nothing wrong with the non-advaitins and they can merrily follow the path they have chosen? If however, they are not happy with the teachings they are following at present, then, of course, they can make a clean statement about that and learn Advaita at the feet of an advaitin guru. Without shraddha, things have never become easy for anybody.***  

  

Regards,
