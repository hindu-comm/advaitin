Thanks for your response Raghav ji. Yes, Advaitins hold that the arrangement of divine forms is a part of Vedic creation. In the available/approved forms one is free to choose any as his Ishta devataa for particular bhakti/meditation. And the approved forms are also not rigid; they evolve as per the passage of time and infusion of devotional practices and above all, as Shankara places a very high value to, the shiShTaachaara. This last will be the pramana for the 'new' divine forms that would be inducted into the practice in the passage of time.
