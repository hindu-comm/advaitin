Dear Advaitins - In the group's new incarnation, to use Shri Chittaji's phrase, we need to lift the bar a bit.

  

Where are all these forms in deep sleep?

  

Someone once asked Ramana what is the criteria to truth; his reply was: "That which is not present in deep sleep is not real".

  

Consciousness alone is present in deep sleep (as well as other states), but the waking mind and intellect wants to dominate the show and judge reality by its own standards and conventions, how impetuous!

  

Creation stories and the likes are for children especially followers of the Dvaita school.
