Namaste Sri Dave-ji,

  

Here is my 2 cents:

  

That entity that returns back to waking is the jiva who is the embodied "self" that is currently the "controller" of the body-mind-speech apparatus. This entity jiva could also be looked upon as a spark that has its origin, abode and destination in the fire of Universal Consciousness which is everywhere. The fact that this jiva has that"limited freedom" to use this apparatus to either engage in selfish (kArmic) or selfless (dhArmic) activity cannot be denied. If the universal Consciousness could be taken as the center of the bigger circle universe/brahmAnda, the jiva can be taken as the center of the smaller circle (body-pindAnda). They both Coincide/coexist just like the spark exists in the Fire. Brahman manifesting as the entire universe of prakriti - elements is constantly and unconditionally offering itself and sustaining all life forms. Likewise, the jiva is supposed to be in-tune with that. Going against this order of dharma / selflessness produces a reaction in the form of karma that continues to bind this jiva in various bodies.

  

So, IMHO constantly locating oneself in this fire of Brahman / Consciousness by seeing the same in all and engaging in unconditional dharma-selfless service which is nothing but sacrifice / Yagya is the essence of Vedic teaching. Hence importance for control of mind / celibacy Or Dharmic householdership - yagya, dAna, tapasya and so on

  

Regards,  
Suresh
