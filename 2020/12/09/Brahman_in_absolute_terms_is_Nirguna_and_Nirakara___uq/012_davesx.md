DvaitIst believethe jiva is a permanent entity recycling eternally withinSamsara. This is in stark contrast to Advaita where the Jiva isa mental construct confined within thewaking state.

  

Based on experience rather than hearsay, the jiva does not exist in deep sleep or under anesthesiabut reconstitutes itself frommemory and theaggregates upon awakening. Thereforethejiva’s perspectiveis relative and not the absolutereference forreality.

  

All activitiesof the jiva are self-centered and actually a means ofself-reinforcement protecting its imaginary survival.Only inrecognition and relinquishment of such activity is the separateJiva released from its psychosis.
