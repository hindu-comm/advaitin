Who is it that is currently thinking, talking, acting, eating, drinking so on...Like yourself, myself typing emails and trying to analyze and understand. That entity cannot be fictitious. The body is currently alive and working due to the existence of the self. This body cannot exist without food, air, water and if the body, mind and intellect are not functioning properly, even Self realization becomes impossible. Hence the importance to consume sattvic and purified food and engage in satsanga. So this conscious self or jiva that is currently controlling the body which is a product or fruit of its own karma cannot be unreal. Brahman in the form of VaishwAnara fire that is digesting all the food with the help of prAnas in all the many bodies also cannot be denied as it can be experienced by all.
