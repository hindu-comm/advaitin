Namaste Sri Sada-ji,

  

As I have already shown Vishnu/Brahman as the all-pervading and indwelling Self is common for V.Advaita, Advaita and even Dvaita.

The difference is only with regard to the relation that obtains between Brahman and jagat/jiva.

  

Whatever may be the differences, they all accept Brahman as that which is everywhere. Some may believe thatVaikunTa is a place high above in a different dimension based on some shAstra pramANa. But one of the names of Vishnu in Vishnu sahasranAma is VaikunTa and as Vishnu is everywhere,VaikunTa is also everywhere!

  

Brahman is that which is common for all, no matter to what philosophy one subscribes to and it is the origin/abode/destination for all. The Veda mantra - Namaste vAyo tvamEva pratyaksham brahmAsi. tvAmeva pratyaksham brahma vadishyAmi" clearly identifies vAyu/prAna that is inside and outside and sustaining all of us as Brahman that can be experienced in pratyaksha. I know all of these are vyavahAra in Advaita but accepting the jagat as a pratIka or body preserves dharma and jagat as body/pratimba acts as a pointer to the all-pervading Brahman which is non-dual.

  

If we reconcile like this then within our own consciousness, we will achieve samAnatva or equanimity which leads to lasting peace 🙂.

  

Regards,  
Suresh
