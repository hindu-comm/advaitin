Namaste Sadanandaji,

  

**You said as follows:**

\<Both Vishishtaadvaita and Dvaita do subscribe Moksha that is beyond the cycle of births.

**Only the Advaitins believe in being one with the Nirguna Brahman and that means the Mukti is beyond the "Cycle of Birth and Death", as there is no separate entity called a jeeva left.**

**  **

**The Dvaitins and the Vishistadvaitins, on the other hand don't believe in that and what they believe in the other muktis like *(i) Saamipya, )ii) Saalokya and (iii) Saaroopya*, the last one being the highest among the three. These three muktis are NOT BEYOND THE CYCLE OF BIRTH AND DEATH.  **

**  **

**There was one jeeva, who was in the *Saaroopya*** ***state*** **and he was a dwara-pala (or the door-keeper) in Vaikuntha, but because of his indiscretion (or inappropriate manners), he had to take birth as Ravana and we all know the rest of what happened.  **
