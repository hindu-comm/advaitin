What you said is not true as per Vishishtadvaita and Dvaita doctrines.

\- yat gatvaana na nivartante tat dhaama paramam mama - is quoted to show that once one has gone to Vaikunta there is no return back to samsara. Hence from their point, there is no return back to samsaara after one has acquired Moksha.

  

We may not accept their doctrine and find fault with it on the basis of Vedanta, but we have to quote their doctrine correctly. Otherwise, we will be like Shree Sureshji quoting wrong things about Advaita.
