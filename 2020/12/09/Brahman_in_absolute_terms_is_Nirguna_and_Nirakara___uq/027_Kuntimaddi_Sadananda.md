Sureshji - PraNAms

  

Please study my original responses to your early mails about what Brahman really stands for and what the explanation of the vaachaarambhanam vikaaraH really implies in terms of the ontological status of the created universe in relation to Brahman and what mithyaa really implies. Gold and gold ornaments for example are ontologically not in par - loham iti eva satyam. Similarly Brahman or sat swarupam iti eva satyam - sa aatmaa - tat tvam asi - swetaketo. All your mails related to mithyaa aspect arise in not clearly understanding scriptural statement and why Advaita resort to vivarta for creation of one to become many.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
