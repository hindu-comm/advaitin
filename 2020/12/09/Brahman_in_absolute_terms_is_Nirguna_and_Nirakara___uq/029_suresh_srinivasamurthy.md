Dear Sri Sunil-ji,

  

\<The Dvaita and Vishishtadvaita have the "Dehabhimana" (i.e.,
identifying oneself with the body). This "dehabhimana" (or abhimana of having an individual body of five koshas) is the biggest obstacle in the Vedantic path. Vedanta knows only one entity Brahman and no other individual.\>

  

Personally, I would not say this to anyone. But if you are a true Advaitin you should not be seeing anything other than Brahman 🙂

  

Regards,  
Suresh
