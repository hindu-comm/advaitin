PraNam Acharya-ji  

  

*Both Vishishtaadvaita and Dvaita do subscribe Moksha that is beyond the cycle of births*

*  *

Yes this is the perspective from both of these Sampradayas; I should have probably added that but it is to be noted Jiva(s) remain eternal nevertheless, in this instance playing the role of dasya in association with Paramatman.

  

*About the Deep sleep states, one can say that the Lord has provided to the experience of the Adriatic state. A Jeeva experiences three things ......  *

*  *

Here it was intended not to delve into the 'deep sleep state', one of the tri-states of the mind which is the focus of Panchadasi, but rather the direct experience of 'deep sleep' as brilliantly pointed out in the treatises of Sri Atmananda.  

  

The 'deep sleep state' can be defined as an idea the waking mind imagines and superimposes upon 'deep sleep' including the ideas of ‘nothing’ or 'blank' but it has absolutely no knowledge of 'deep sleep' as it was not present there. So from the standpoint of the waking state, 'deep sleep' cannot be apprehended and any conception is mind attempting to assert its authority.

  

The waking mind is programmed to believe that things and objects have substantial lasting inherent reality but as soon as this misconception is seen through and cleared up, the ‘nothingness’ of 'deep sleep' vanishes and is instead experienced as Peace.  

  

In other words, it is 'deep sleep' itself, not the 'state of deep sleep' as conceived by the mind, that is the underlying and default reality of all experiences of waking and dream and by tracing back it can be verified that both these states emerge and return to It -- which is non other than the everpresent Consciousness knowing itself in 'deep sleep' and in the midst of thoughts, feelings, sensations, perceptions of the waking and dream states.

  

Ramana called Jagrat-Sushupti (awake in deep-sleep) the liberated state of Gyanis.  

  

Shanti Om!
