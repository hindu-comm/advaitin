Sureshji -PraNAms

These are the statements that reflect a clear misunderstanding of what Advaita stands for. Advaita, by definition, is the negation of the perceived dvaita as reality. It is not monotheism. Braham means infinite and therefore cannot have a seer-seen duality in Brahman – ekam eva adviteeyam – says the scripture.

Your statement should be if you realize that you (as a conscious-existent entity) realize that you are that Brahman then you should not take the world that involves jeeva-jeeva bhinnatvam, jeeva-jagat bhinnatvam, Jeeva-Iswara bhinnatvam and Jagat-Iswara bhinnatvam as really real. It is only apparently real in the drama of life – jagat natamam.

Hence an Advaitin sees the world as transactionally real but not absolutely real as long his BMI perceives the plurality.

Perception of plurality is not a problem, but the problem arises when the perceived plurality is absolutely real. Playing a drama on the stage of life is not a problem, but the actor should not take the role as really real but for real for the play – That is what mithyaa means. The world becomes a vibhuuti for a jnaani.

I stop here.

Hari Om

Sadananda
