Dear Sunil ji,

  

The crux of what is said there is: Even though we get an impression that only one Truth is admitted in the Ramanuja system, yet, by a deep study we understand that:

  

The world does not have the same level of truth as Brahman's reality.
This is because the world keeps changing, transforming. It becomes non-existent in pralaya and is created afresh in the next cycle. Brahman is not so. Also the jiva too has not the same level of truth as that of Brahman. Jiva has embodiment and therefore is endowed with that upadhi.
\[This explanation is required since we understand that the Ramanuja system holds the world to be real.\] 

  

He says: All the three - bheda, abheda and bheda-abheda - together alone make up the Ramanuja system.

  

He says Ramanuja, in the Sribhashyam, says 'सत्यपदेन जगद्व्यावृत्तिः' in the Sribhashyam. This means: by the word 'satyam' (in the Taittiriya Upanishad 'satyam jananam anantam brahma') the exclusion of the world is meant.'

  

Here is a correction in the name of the scholar that I had mentioned earlier: it is Kothimangalam and not Kothamangalam as earlier stated.
