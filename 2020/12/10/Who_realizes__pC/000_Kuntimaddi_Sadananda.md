A few minutes back a seeker asked me in an email. Others may have different answers. Here is mine.

-------------------------------

A simple answer is - it is the Jeeva that identifies with BMI and feels that he is a local entity separate from the other Jeeves and also from the Jagat.

The next question then is who is that Jeeva that does this.

It cannot be the pure all-pervading consciousness since it is one without a second.

It cannot be the BMI itself since they are, by nature, inert.

It is some fictitious entity that arises what Gurudev calls it as ‘product of the unholy marriage’ of pure Brahman and the local inert BMI.

In essence, the mind which is a subtle matter, by reflecting consciousness (chidaabhaasa) acts ‘as though’ an independent conscious entity, Jeeva, without realizing the consciousness that it exhibits is only a reflection of the original all-pervading consciousness.

In essence, it is the activated mind or the enlivened mind that feels that it is an independent conscious entity, Jeeva by identifying as I am = this, where ‘this’ refers to BMI.

It is like the moon acting like an independent luminous entity in the sky without realizing that the luminosity that it exhibits is only a reflection of the original all-pervading ‘sunlight’. By the by, the moon is the presiding deity of the mind.

It is to that confused mind only, the scriptures advise that it should go to a teacher who can teach properly what is the ‘I am’ part and what is the ‘this’ part in the equation I am = this, which is nothing but ego or ahankaara.

This superimposition of the ‘I am’ part on ‘this’ part is what is called adhyaasa – or superimposed error.

  

It is, however, needed for transactional or for vyavahaara purposes, but should not be taken as ‘Really Real’.

  

A realized person also uses ‘this’ part – just as an actor playing a role of King or a Begger in a drama – The costume of the King or the beggar is needed for the play to go on. However, the actor should not forget that he is only playing the role for fun and that he is really an actor and neither a king nor a beggar.

Hence the confusion is not in the pure all-pervading consciousness or in the inert mind but in the activated mind by the consciousness that activates the mind by the process of reflection or chidaabhaasa.

Now you can pose that question to yourself – who is that 'you' that is identifying with your BMI as 'I am so and so' the whole bio-data. You (obviously using your mind) have to shift your attention in the equation, ‘I am = this’ in the inseparable combination, from the ‘this’ part to the ‘I am’ part which comes from the pure existence-consciousness that is getting reflected.

The tricky part is you have to use the mind only to do this.

It is like the moon has to recognize that the luminosity that it exhibits comes from the all-pervading sunlight via the reflection process. This recognition has to be done while still being a luminous moon in the sky. The confusion is with the apparently luminous moon or in our case the activated mind.

Hence realization is also in the activated mind. By the by, confusion in the mind is beginningless and ends with the knowledge.

Hence this realization is also at the transactional or vyavahaarika level.

  

There is no real confusion at the Brahman level - it is pure consciousness, prajnaanam brahma says the Upanishad.

  

Hope this helps.

Hari Om!

Sadananda
