> Who or what identifies with the BMI?

  

It is the sentient entity that identifies with the BMI. It is the one who thinks he is a human, subject to misery, etc. that comes to engage in the sadhana to free himself from this situation. It is he who succeeds in gaining the knowledge: I, Consciousness, have never been a jiva. I am ever the free Brahman.

  

This is the essence of the lengthy Brihadaranyaka mantra where occurs the 'aham brahmasmi' 1.4.10 which has a very involving Bhashya. One can read the translation of this by Swami Gambhirananda and gain a lot.
