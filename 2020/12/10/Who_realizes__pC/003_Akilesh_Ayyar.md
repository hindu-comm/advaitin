[[Akilesh Ayyar	2020-12-10, 10:35:18 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/pC94NamOTwk)]]

No one realizes, because the idea that there is someone who is ignorant who then realizes *is*ignorance. Indeed, the idea that ignorance exists at all*is*ignorance, and the idea that *that*ignorance exists is also ignorance, and so on, allthe way down.

  

Ignorance is merely a provisional concept,which makes realization also provisional. When 'realization' occurs it eats up ignorance and ends up by never having existed to have eaten up ignorance.

  

Who unties a knotthat was never tied to begin with?
