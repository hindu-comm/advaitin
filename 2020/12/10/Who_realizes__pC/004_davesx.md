Namaste - Not sure who or where this thread started but if Consciousness (pure unadulterated 'I am') alone is ultimately real then it stands to reason that Consciousness is responsible for the predicament, imagining that it is identical with objectivity whether jiva, sentient being, BMI, mansion, wife, sport car, credentials, name, fame, status.

  

Chasing these perishable objects with the hope of attaining permanent happiness disillusionment (vairagya) eventually sets in followed by Viveka, the awakening of Buddhi, and by its own grace Consciousness snaps out from this hypnotic trance - the mistaken notion that the Seer can be identical with the seen (Drg with Drysam) - and rests in the peace of its own swarupa, freed from the bondage to objectivity.
