It is clear from the talk that Sringeri Peetham regards Shankaracharya as an avatara of Shiva and Kumarila Bhatta as an avatara of Kumara and also that Mandana Misra is the same person as Sureshwaracharya.

  

Also, in a Workshop held in Mysore in the year 2016, the topic of whether the debate between Mandana Misra and Shankaracharya should be called 'vada' or 'jalpa' was debated (amongst Nayyayikas / Vedantins) and it was decided that according to the rules of Tarka, it would fall under the category of jalpa (even though Shankara may have had no ego-based personal motivation to win the debate); I see that the Sringeri Swamiji classifies it in the same manner.
