Dear Chittaranjanji,

  

There is no doubt that Suershwaracharya was Mandana Mishra, before his becoming a disciple of Adi Shankara. I read long ago that the all other Maithili scholars of Mahadha, for many centuries, did not accept that change in Mandana Mishra and they continued to believe that Mandana Mishra, the avatara of Lord BrahmA, could not have been defeated by Adi Shankara. They continued to believe that Vacaspati Mishra took that challenge and showed the validity of the several differences between the views of Mandana Mishra and Adi Shankaracharya, as Vacaspati Mishra came out with his own commentary against the Vivarana of Padmapada. As regards Padmapada's work on Vivarana, I understand that it was actually lost , while he was away and later on Adi Shankara told him not to worry as he (Adi Shankara) remembered it all as he had read that earlier. It was Adi Shankara who, from his memory dictated that, and that surely means that Padmapada's Vivarana was fully approved by Adi Shankara.

  

As for me also, s small fry in front the giants of the Advaita, I met my Guru and with the later reflections on it as well as through my comparative study of the Vulgate Bhagavad Gita and the Original Bhagavad Gita of 745 verses, and then by my reading on the Mahayana teachings of Lord Buddha, I became convinced about the truth behind the Advaita vada, as taught by Adi Shankara (in line with the Upanishads) as well as by the Sarvashatramaya Original Bhagavad Gita. It was after that I found that Vacaspati Mishra could be labelled as a dissenter of the doctrine of Advaita in the sense that after the end of the creation, he seemed to think that the memory of the earlier creation cycle could still be retained in the Brahman. Science also seems to believe that information is never lost, even if the Physical entities like visible astronomcal bodies are drawn into the Black Holes. However, it has been established that even the black holes have their emissions. He seemed to think that the memory of the earlier creation cycle could still be retained in the Brahman. Science also seems to believe that information is never lost, even if the Physical entities like visible astronomcal bodies are drawn into the Black Holes.However, it has been established that even the black holes have their emissions.

  

However, this dissention of Vacaspati Mishra, seems to vanish if we consider the memory of the past creation could be a part of Para-Prakrti or call it the Mahamaya or Maya, which is the Will or the Energy of Creation of Brahman. This Para-prakrti is different from the Apara-prakrti or Avidya, which is the imagination of the Jeevas, which make them see the rope as the snake. These imaginations do not have any permanence and vanishes when the Advaita jnana is attained. But then what about the other differences between Vacaspati Mishra and Padmapada? My specific question is should we continue to believe Vacaspati Mishra to be a true advaitin?
