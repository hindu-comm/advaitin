Hari om Chittaranjana ji

  

My pranams to you. I have not written a comment for a long time. Your article prompted me to write

  

"After creating them, Brahman entered them. The words of Advaita are the words of Brahman directed by Brahman to Brahman when Brahman is ready to exit what Brahman has entered. He exits in the same manner as He enters"

  

These words are okay for Vyvaharic. But in true sense "He neither enters nor exits" He neither have any 'karana' (instrument) to exit or enter.

  

And your usage ( and also my usage) as "He" is also incorrect. Brahman is neither "He" nor She" or "Tansgender"

  

Hope Sadaji or Dave ji can correctme if I am wrong

  

Hari om
