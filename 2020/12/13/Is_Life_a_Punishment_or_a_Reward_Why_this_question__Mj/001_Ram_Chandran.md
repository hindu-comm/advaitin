Hopefully, this will allow silent members to post simple poems with deep philosophical truth inside them. Here is a film song which touched my soul since it contained deep meaning about life and uncertainty.

Kannadasan the Tamil poet and film song writer is known for soul touching philosophical songs. This song is from the movie, Nenjil Or Alayam (Dil Ek Mandir is the Hindi version). The ‘God’ in subtler form is mentioned in the song. Interestingly God is mentioned in very subtle termss in the entire philosophy of the Upanishads! The poet and the original singer are no more but the song remains in the hearts of those who heard it oncel!!  

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=85uXGUR-tUo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=85uXGUR-tUo)

Philosophical Meaning of the song as I perceive:

If all our thoughts become the reality,

We don’t need God and He will have no roles to play!

If our thoughts are only on the past, we lose our peace of mind

No concluded story will ever continue in God’s mind!

A continued story never ends in a human mind!

There are thousands of gates with thousands of thoughts

Thoughts come and go and no one knows when they come and go

When we focus our mind with just one thought, we will have no more sufferings

By allowing more and more thoughts to get in we will be left without peace of mind

We don’t know when life begins, and we can’t predict when it ends,

This is the journey of our path of life without any clue

The Path keeps on changing and suddenly the journey ends

When reach the right path, mind is restrained to realize the God!

Ram Chandran
