Dear Subbuji,

**"मायावादमसच्छास्त्रं प्रच्छन्नं बौद्ध उच्यते" means Mayavada is Asat shastra** (***where "Asat" means "unmanofested" or "Nirguna Brahman"***), **which has been spoken of by Pracchanna Buddha** (***i.e., "Concealed Buddha***), **i.e., Adi Shankara is saying what Lord Buddha also said earlier. *This follows from the Vedic dictum SAT (manifested Brahman or the Saguna Brahman) came from the ASAT (unmanifested Brahman or the Nirguna Brahman)***.  

**Kindly feel free to give your valuable comment. I will be the last to say a statement of the Fifth Veda to be wrong.**

Regards  

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
