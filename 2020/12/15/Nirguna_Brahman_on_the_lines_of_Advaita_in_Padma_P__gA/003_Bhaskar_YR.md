praNAms Sri Suresh prabhuji

Hare Krishna



You had confirmed in another thread that Nirguna Brahman is capable of taking divine form to bless the sAdhaka.



Ø Yes for sAdhaka-s who cannot elevate themselves to realize nirguNa, nirAkAra, nirvisheshatvaM of brahman.
maNda-madhyamAdhikAri-s would like to do the dhyAna upAsana etc. to sOphAdhika brahma (apara brahma) which is according to some advaitins inferior to para brahman.





Also, Sri Shankara in his BS bhAshya (1-1-1) provides the meaning of Brahman as both nitya shuddha buddha and

sarvagya sarvashakti samanvita.



Ø sarvajnatvaM, sarvashakti etc. pertains to ONLY Ishwara / apara brahman, again, as per some advaitins since shankara elsewhere clarifies Ishwara’s omnipotence, omniscience qualities hold good only in avidyA kshetra.



In that case, nirguna can only mean devoid of inauspicious attributes, right?



Ø I am afraid that is very restricted interpretation of absolute nirguNa brahman. It is anything and everything if that is not the case sarvaM khalvidaM brahma would have the restricted boundaries of ONLY auspicious attributes. I reckon v.advaita says brahma is only sakala kalyAna guNa saMpanna devoid of any dosha-s.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
