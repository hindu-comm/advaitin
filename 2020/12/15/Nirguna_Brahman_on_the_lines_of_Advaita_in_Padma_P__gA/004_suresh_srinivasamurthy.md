Namaste Sri Bhaskar-ji,

  

ØYes for sAdhaka-s who cannot elevate themselves to realize nirguNa, nirAkAra, nirvisheshatvaM of brahman. maNda-madhyamAdhikAri-s would like to do the dhyAna upAsana etc. to sOphAdhika brahma (apara brahma) which is according to some advaitins inferior to para brahman.  

  

In the quote provided earlier, Sri Shankara says Brahman is nitya shuddha and also sarva shakti samanvita based on the etymological meaning of the word Brahman. So, it is not dependent on the capability of sAdhaka.

  

Traditional Advaitin's perform nitya karmas, pancha mahA yagya and prANAgnihotra on a daily basis. Even traditional Sanyasis engage in trikAla Ishwara puja and take bhiksha only from traditional gruhastas who engage in nitya karma. All these are obviously performed as a service to antaryAmi Brahman equally established in all. Do you think attainment of Nirguna brahman is possible without performing these obligatory duties or dharma towards the antaryAmi saguna Brahman, manifesting as deva, pitru and tiryak jantus?

  

Also, should we not look upon one's own Guru as verily Brahman (who obviously is a Saguna Brahman with no inauspicious qualities). Do you think attainment of Nirguna Brahman (if at all it is there) would be possible without rendering service to such a Guru?

  

The name Shankara itself means "one who does good for all". If this SarvAntaryAmi Saguna Brahman and the principle of yagya as a service to this Brahman, is ignored, then vedAnta will not be different from Buddhism.

  

Namaste  

Suresh
