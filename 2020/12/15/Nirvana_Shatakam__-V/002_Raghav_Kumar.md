It's a beautiful hymn for contemplation.

  

For the record, it's correct name is nirvANa \*ShaTkam\* षट्कम् (nirvana shatkam) implying six verses.

  

A common mistake is to write it as shatakam शतकम् which wrongly indicates 100 verses. (as in bhartRhari's hundred verses called shringaara shatakam etc.)
