Namaste Sri Raghav-ji.

  

The terminology from extracts expounded by different teachers may have unique connotations leading to confusion among readers.

  

From the standpoint of *Jiva*, there can be no direct knowledge of *Atman*. However Atman can only be intuited or indirectly inferred by the intellect as the source of perceptual objects.   

  

For example space, time and experience are known. How could these concepts be known without the presence of its contrast which is timeless, spaceless and experienceless?  

  

Essentially these attributes of Atman are conceptually no-thing from the waking mind perspective, yet it is the background or screen for all functionality.

  

Please correct me if I'm wrong.  

  

Om Tat Sat
