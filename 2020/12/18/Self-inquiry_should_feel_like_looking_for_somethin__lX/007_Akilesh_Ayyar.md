Namaste Raghavji,

  

*The "act of introspection", as you put it is well within mAyA or the realm of ignorance . That does not invalidate it's role in removing the veil of avidyA. Similarly for the process of knowing (pramANa-vyApAra) - it is in time but sublates time.  *

  

It may be in maya, but that's not the main point I was making about intellectual knowing. The point I was making is that there is a merely conceptual understanding that people believe they have when they read and "ponder" the words and hypnotize themselves into believing they understand -- that merely conceptual understanding is inadequate.

  

*Knowing is most certainly a happening or even a process within time with a definite culmination. But again, that in no way militates against its efficacy in ending the illusion of time. A dream tiger is instrumental in someone waking up. One cannot say, "any process or entity within time, being of the realm of ignorance, cannot remove the ignorance of taking oneself to be an entity within time". That's not logically tenable.*  

  

Knowing is the understanding that there is no ignorance and never was,and therefore the real knowing can never be an act in time. The knowing that is in time must then be a merely provisional way of talking about what isn't what it seems to be. But frankly this is a semantic debate with very little importance. I'm all in favor of effort-in-time for seekers.
