Dear friendsi,

  

Even though Lord Krishna emphasized on the role of Abhysha (practice/habit) to help do the seemingly impossible things possible, generally people appear to forget that advice of the Lord. We should rather learn to cultivate the habit to overlook / reject the anitya things in the world, to the extent possible, at the various stages of life. We should acquire the Paramarthika Jnana and with the practice of living life appropriate to that Jnana is the sure way to lead us to our Advaita Goal.
