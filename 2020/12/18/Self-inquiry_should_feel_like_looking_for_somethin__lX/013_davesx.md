Yes we have gone through several iterations on the topic of Self-Knowledge. If I recall correctly it was summized that Self-Knowledge is actually '*Self-Knowing'* as knowledge (at least among western philosophers) point to *objective* knowledge of which Atman, being the ultimate *Subject,* certainly is not.  

  

Self *Knows* Itself by *Being* at *Peace* with Itself (Sat-Chit-Anand) so it is unfathomable how any derivative of the Jiva's intellect (being a servant or tool of Atman) can know directly That which is upstream to it.
