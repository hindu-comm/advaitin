Namaste Sri Dave ji

  

The jIva's \*intellect\* does not 'know' the self which is indeed upstream. In fact the intellect being inert never knows anything be it sense objects or even brahman. The jIva alone is the knower.

  

The mature intellect only assumes a special modification whereby the veil of ignorance of the Self is destroyed and the jIva abides in his true nature as the Self. This special modification of the intellect is termed brahmAkAra vRtti. This is different from just introspection which is necessary but \*without holistic teaching of the Guru\*, this introspection will stagnate with quietness of mind and simple discrimination that I am not the mind. But non-dual knowledge which alone puts an end to sorrow, cannot arise without

  

In the case of sense perceptions, the viShayAkara vRttis are of the type, " I know the chair before my eyes" or "I know the sound of thunder presented by my ears" etc. But the brahmAkAra vRtti is of the form "I AM brahman", rather than "I know brahman". The fact of using words, post facto, to explain the brahmAkAra vRtti does not imply any objectification of brahman happens when the vRtti obtains.

  

Also that does not alter the fact that this is also a modification of the buddhi. That is why the sages have said that 'through the mind alone is the Self known' (manasA eva idam Aptavyam).

  

  

Om

Raghav
