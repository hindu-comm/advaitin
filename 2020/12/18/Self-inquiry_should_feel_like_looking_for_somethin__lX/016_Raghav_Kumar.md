\*But non-dual knowledge which alone puts an end to sorrow, cannot arise without the mahAvAkya upadesha of the Guru (or put it in "secular" language, non-dual knowledge cannot arise without receiving with an open mind the cognitive input in the form of the words of a resource person).

  

Without such mahAvAkya upadesha (be it in any language), the mind can be lulled in to introsopective laya which is taken to be Self-enquiry or it may be mesmerized by mantras.

  

Bhagavan Ramana teaches the mahAvAkya at several places including in his "Essence of Instruction" - "When the difference between jIva and Ishvara is understood as merely notional and the true nature of both is understood as One, then Ishvara is realised as one's own Self. That alone constitutes abiding in one's Self".

  

If any practice, call it introspection, or anything else, is organically embedded in a larger body of teaching the oneness of jIva and Ishvara, then alone it's acceptable.

  

(Introspection/meditation/looking within) + \*the overall teaching of a guru, of the identity with Ishvara\* = NidhidhyAsanam

  

(Introspection/turning within/quietening the mind) devoid of the context of an \*overarching teachings of a guru of the non-dual identity with Ishvara\* = just a technique, and like all techniques, subject to the law of diminishing returns.
