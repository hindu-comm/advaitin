Namaste Dear Friends:

  

My few insights into this subject..

  

Sentient Jivas are programmed to chase after success, happiness, security, enlightenment, moksha by acquiring objects, knowledge and power.

It is only when it is discovered that the thirst is never quenched.

That the search is fruitless and running after objects was chasing empty forms, dreams and mirages leading only to disappointment, misery and frustration.

Only when it is realized that the outward search turns out to be a dismal failure does the restless energy stop in the pool of Stillness.

And in the sudden cessation (Nirodha), one is already turned within established as the silent aware Drg, which the preceptor points out as *tat tvam as*i that was always the case except overlooked and ignored.

If one is making any deliberate effort to turn inwards such as introspective meditation, one is not yet a ripe Vairagi and simply on a reverse trip, an extension of the search.

Peace

Dave
