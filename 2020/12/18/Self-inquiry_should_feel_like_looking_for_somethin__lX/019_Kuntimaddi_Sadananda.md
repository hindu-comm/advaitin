Looking at the mails, I must say that Shree Raghavji had provided the correct import of the scriptures and the Advaitic perspective.

Yes, wrong notions are in the mind and the mind that needs the right understanding - hence mana eva manushyaanaam kaaranam bandha moksha yoH - says scriptures.

  

People misunderstand also Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi' texts and teachings - when he said - maanasantukim maargane kRite naiva maanasam, maarga aarjavat in Upadesha saara. We have taken that text in our Memorial Day camp series and the talks were uploaded on you-tube by Indic Academy. What is not there there is only reality assumed for the subject-object duality. Raghavji correctly pointed out the akhandaakaara vRitti needed for self-realization - there is vRitti vyaapti but no phala vyaapti since the object of knowledge is ever-present - the very inquirer who is inquiring - that was the statement of Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi also - enquire who is the inquirer.

  

Ultimately it is the scriptures that are the pramaanas and not the individuals however noble they are. We respect all acharyas but we follow what is right based on the scriptural understanding.

  

We are not programmed by anybody but by our actions in the past due to the lack of correct understanding.

  

Hence correct knowledge alone solves the problem - the rest is preparatory to gain the knowledge and **to abide** in that knowledge, what Bhaghavan calls it as dRiDaiva nishTa.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
