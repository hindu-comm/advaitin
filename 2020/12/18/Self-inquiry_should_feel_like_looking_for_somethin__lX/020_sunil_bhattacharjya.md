Namaste Sadaji,

  

In the Bhagavad Gita, quite in the beginning itself, the Lord told Arjuna that he knew all his past births. So also Lord Buddha said that he became awakened only when he remembered all his past births and then only he started preaching. Only that person is fully enlightened when he realizes that he or she does not have this human body for ever. Unfortunately none of the secondary writtings emphasizes the need to understand this.

  

Secondly, people have misunderstood Lord Buddha and call him a nastic. Like Lord Krishna telling Arjuna to surrender to him and Arjuna’s surrendering to the Lord, the followers of Lord Buddha too says “Buddham Sharanam Gacchaami. Like Lord Krishna said that the Veda trraigunyavishaya Lord Buddha also discouraged his disciples against being overly ritualistic and strictly against animal sacrifice. I wonder when the Hindus will know the 9th avatar Lord Buddha properly.

  

My two cents

Jai Shri Krishna
