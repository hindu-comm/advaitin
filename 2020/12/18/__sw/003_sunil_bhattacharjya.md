**One thing I forgot to add one additional point in my last mail and that is about the importance of the month of Magha. No doubt that Uttarayana is important, as the path to the Devaloka said to open with the Uttarayana, but the importance of the holy month of Magha (Punya-Magha) is equally important.** ***While Bhishma thought that he should depart from this world when the uttarayana starts,*** ***Lord Krishna told his great devotee Bhishma that he would get his liberation in the Shuklashtami of the Magha Month***. ***As both these are important, the Sanatani Hindus observe the sidereal (nakshatra-based)
Makara Sankranti, which falls in the six month-period of the Uttarayana, thus meeting the statements of both Bhishma and Lord Krishna, the Pari-purna Brahman***. **Thus there is absolutely no need to discard the presently followed Sidereal Sakara Sankranti.** I assure you that our forefathers were absolutely right in observing the Makara Sankranti, as we doing these days.
