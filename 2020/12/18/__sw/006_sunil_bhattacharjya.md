Dear friends,

We should not forget that the Abhijit nakshatra fell/disappeared around 5,000 years ago and there is mention of that in the Mahabharata. Further, Vahia et al. of the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR) showed about a decade ago that Abhijit was a dying star and it disappeared. After that the 28 nakshatra system obviously was replaced by the 27 nakshatra system, and the these were divided equally among the 12 Rashis. If you are keen to confirm that you have to read the Vayu Purana. The old month system was abandoned and the months Kartika etc. came into vogue. Hope you have seen the use of the names of Magha and Kartika etc. in the ancient texts. There is no point in living in the past.

As Lord Krishna was interested in giving guidance to his devotee Bhishma, he told Bhishma that he would be liberated in Magha Shukla Ekadashi. So most people follow the Makar Sankranti, which do occur within the six months of Uttarayana.

  

Most importantly Bhishma did not die on the very day of start of the Uttarayana. That means there is no particular need to stick to the very day of the start of the Uttarayana. Makara Sankranti does occur within the Uttarayana.

  

If some people prefer to observe or celebrate Uttarayana starting day on December 21, he or she can very well do that. If some ignorant people want to follow the 28-Nakshatra system, he or she can very well do that, in these days of Democracy.

Regards

Sunil Kumar Bhattacharjya
