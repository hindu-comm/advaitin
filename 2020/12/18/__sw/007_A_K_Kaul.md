Respected members of Advaitin,

Best wishes for the Pongal-cum-Makar-Samkranti-cum-Uttarayana!

\< Most importantly Bhishma did not die on the very day of start of the Uttarayana. That means there is no particular need to stick to the very day of the start of the Uttarayana. Makara Sankranti does occur within the Uttarayana.\>

Makar Samkranti is actually nothing but a synonym of Uttarayana, as per all the Puranas and siddhantas (attachment Shabda-kalpadrumaUttarayana") which means both take place simultaneously!

However, "almighty" Lahiri Makar Samkranti, which is called as "sidereal" Samkranti by quite a few people today, can occur only within the "six months of Uttarayana" if it takes place after Uttarayana, i.e. Winter Solstice, as is happening these days!

"almighty" Lahiri Ayanamsha these days is -24 degrees. That is why "almighty" Lahiri Makar Samkanti is taking place after 24 days of the Uttarayana-cum-Winter Solstcie. Thus we have such a Samkranti these days around December 21 plus 24 days = January 15!

In 3139 BCE, when the Mahabharata stalwarts claim that Mahabharata war had taken place, the same "almighty" Lahiri Ayanamsha was+47 degrees.
That means "almighty" Makar-Samkranti-cum-Lahiri-Magha was before at least 47 days of the real Uttarayana, for which Bhishma Pitamaha was waiting! Thus as explained in the appended mail, in 3139 BCE, the real Uttarayana took place on January15, 1939 BCE (Julian), but Lahiri Makar Samkranti would have taken place 47 days earlier i.e. on about 7th of December 1940 BCE (Julian)! Thus Bhishma Pitamaha could never have been waiting for ''slmighty" Lahiri Makar Samkranti or Magha!

So whichever way we look at it, we have to discard, once for all, "almighty" Lahiri Rashichakra if we want really to follow our Dharma.
Otherwise,

धर्मो रक्ष्यति रक्षितः, धर्मो हन्ति हतः!

With best wishes once again for the real Uttarayana-cum-Pongal-cum-Makar-Samkranti,

A K Kaul
