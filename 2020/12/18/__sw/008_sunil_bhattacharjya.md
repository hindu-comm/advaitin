Dear friends,

Sidereal Makara Sankranti alone is observed, by the Hindus as that is when the Sun comes to 270 degree of the Sidereal Zodiac and stays in the Makara Rashi till the Sun reached 300 degree of the Zodiac, is., the Sun stays in the Makara Rashi of a span of 30 degrees.

  

As against this, in December 21 the Sun comes to the Tropic of Capricorn and not the Makara Rashi.

  

If anybody is interested in observing the 21st of December in the Gregorian calendar, he or she can very well go ahead, but that will not be In terms of the Indian Calendar. The enthusiastic people for observing the 21st December, must spell out the day in terms of Indian Calendar, and not in Gregorian calendar.
