Respected members of Advaitin,

Jai Shri Ram!

Shri Sunil Bhattacharya is replying/commenting about my mails without having read them!

It has been made very clear in my very first mail to this august forum----appended herewith--- that it is on the bases of "Panchama Veda" like Srimad Bhagvata, Vishnu Purana, Vamana Purana etc. that we must really celebrate our Makar etc. Sankrantis instead of as per the whims and fancies of nirayanawallas!

And the Pauranic Rashis are all seasonal---so called Sayana!

Here is a challenge to Shri Bhattacharya and all the other nirayana advocates:

Just quote even a single shloka from any Purana or shastra or even siddhanta that says:

1\. Makar Sankranti is any other day than the shortest day of the year i.e.Uttarayana.

2. Mesha Sankranti is any other day than Vishuva i.e. Vernal Equinox.

3\. Karata Sankranti is any other day than the longest day of the year viz. Dakshinayana.

4\. Tula Sankranti is any other day than Sharat Sampaat viz. Autumn Equinox.

So long as Shri Bhattacharya or any other Nirayana advocate does not answer this challenge by attaching proofs----instead of claiming that he had read it in his childhood and forgotten the source and so on----- from the Puranas or other scriptures, it is wastage of time to reply his further mails.

In fact, we must adhere in letter and spirit to the injunctions of shastras like Srimad Bhagavat or Vamana Purana or Vishnu Purana and so on really and celebrate all our Sankranti which are aligned to the seasons instead of just parroting the shastras.

And all the shastra prove that nirayana like Makar Sankranti of January 14 is really niraadhar!
