Dear friends,  

  

I have written that if Mr. Kaul wants to celebrate the starting day of the Uttarayana, he can very well do that Himself, but as a member of this august group I consider it my duty to give you the true definition of the Nirayana Makara Sankranti.

  

It is al pity that Mr.Kaul has never read about the Nirayana or Sidereal Makara Rashi, which starts at the 270 degree of the Zodiac and it extends upto 300 degree of the Zodiac. I mentioned about the Sidereal Rashis, given in the Fifth Veda text of the Vamana Mahapurana, but he conveniently evaded it.  

  

Even in the Mahabharata Lord Krishna was referring to the Nirayana Magha month, when he was saying that Bhishma would get liberated on the Shuklashtami tithi of the Magha month, when the Moon will be in the Rohini Nakshatra.  

  

I am sure the members of this group of Advaitins know very well the definition of the Nirayana or Sidereal or the Nakshatra-based Rashis. Lord Krishna gave the Bhagavad Gita Upadesha to Arjuna in the Shukla Ekadashi of the Sidereal Kartika Month, in 3139 BCE, and that day is called the Bhagavad Gita Prabodhini day.  

  

Regards  

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
