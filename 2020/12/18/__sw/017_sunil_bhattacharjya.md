Kindly permit me to add a couple of points, left out earlier. These are as follows:

**A)**

Most of the persons dabbling with the dates of the ancient Indian scholars are actually very far from the real dates of these scholars. Let us take the case of Varaha Mihira, whio was living in 1st / 2nd century BCE, but the ignorant people go on saying that Varaha Mihira was born in 505 AD(CE). Here is what we know from Bhattotpala on the date of Varaha Mihira:

***Bhattotpala*** ** ***says in his commentary on the Brihat Sa****m****hita,*** ***called Chintamani,*** ***that*** ***Varaha Mihira used the Saka-Nripa-Kala, which*** ***obviously*** ***was prevalent at his time.*** ***The concerned verse is*** ***as follows:***

सहस्रद्वयेन पञ्चभि शतै षड्विंशत्यधिकै २५२६ **शकनृपकालो** युक्त कार्यं \| १३.३ \|

The Saka-Nripa-Kala started in 551 BCE, when Cambyses I, the father of Cyrus II the Great, passed away and Cyrus II formally became the king of Persia at Pasargadae, and yet as a vassal of Astyages, the Median emperor. This means that in the times of Varaha Mihira the Saka Nripa kala was used. Varaha Mihira said that the Panchasiddhantika was written 427 Saka kala and t**hat means that Varaha Mihira wrote Panchasiddhantiks in 551-427 = 124 BCE.**

**B)**

Most of the religious festivals, we observe are based on the Sidereal
or Nirayana calendar, and anybody calling the Nirayana calendar as niradhara or baseless, should be condemned severely. For example, **(i ) Ganapati Puja, (ii) Durga Puja/ Navaratrri, (iii) Saraswati puja,
(iv) Annapurna Puja, (v) Skanda-Shashthi, (vi) Ayappa Puja, (vii) Shiva Ratri, (viii) Ram-Navami, (ix) Krishna-Janmashtami. (x) Buddha purnima, (xi) Shath Puja, (xii) Gita Prabodhini Ekadashi, ((xiii) Vaikuntha Ekadashi, (xiv) Shankara Jayanti etc.**  

The Uttarayana is a matter of Tropical or Sayana calendar and that too there is no specific puja or ceremony to be observed on that day.  This is an exception that cannot be accommodated in the Nirayana Calendar. Most of the people who are interested in the Uttarayana are mentionng it as occurring in the date of 21 Decembet of the Gregorian calendar. Even in the Mahabharata times only the Nirayana purnimanta months were used for all purpose. ***Uttarayana was an event, which people observed physically, by noting the movement of the Sun. When the Pandavas left Hastinapur to meet Bishma on hss his bed of arrows, they observed and said that the Sun started the Northward movement.**  *
