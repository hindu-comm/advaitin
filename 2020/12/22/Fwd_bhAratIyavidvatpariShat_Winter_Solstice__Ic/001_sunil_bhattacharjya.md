Dear friends,  

  

The definition of the Nirayana or Sidereal Makara Rashi, as given in the Fifth Veda text of the Vamana Mahapurana, is as follows:

  

उत्तरांशास्त्रयो ऋक्षं श्रवणं मकरो मुने ।  
धनिष्ठार्धं शनिक्षेत्रं जानुनी परमेष्ठिनः ॥५.४०॥

  

The translation of this verse is as follows:

O sage, the house of Sani (Saturn), the Makara (Capricorn) sign of the Zodiac, extending over the remaining three quarters of Uttarashadha, the whole of the Shravana and the two quarters of Dhanishtha make the knee of the parameshwara.  

  

Nobody can say that the makara rashi is not there in the Vedic texts.

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
