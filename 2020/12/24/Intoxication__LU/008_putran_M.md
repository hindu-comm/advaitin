Namaskaram Chitta-ji,

  

Question relevant there would be "Dharma?" From what I understand, Yudhishtira as a king was acting within the bounds of his svadharma. If he was not, then he can be called out on that. He embodied model character and qualities,not necessarily an acharya or a saint.

  

Better example may be Sri Rama. Did he not kill Vali like a coward and scorn Sita into the fire or kill that shudra? Then how can we praise Rama as if God without mentioning or confessing such "major faults"? Well, we are asked all that all the time. Our response may still correspond to Dharma; however the modern feminist or shudra may not accept the explanation. For them the mention of Rama's goodness will require mention of these episodes for "balance".

  

The Church is committed to interpretations of the bible that mission the predation of Hindus. So long as the "bad" quotes are explicitly manifest in fundamental-faces worn by practitioners of that religion, yes Hindus should present both sides together if possible - for balance.
