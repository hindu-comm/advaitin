Namaskaram Chitta-ji,



> There is no other way to take precaution than to abide by one's own
> dharma. Finding fault with others' scriptures is not part of it. This
> is my strongly held view. We can agree to disagree if you don't agree
> with it.
>
>   

  

If a buffalo describes glowingly the strength of a lion or motherly instincts of a lioness, then another may think it necessary to point out that the lion is still coded to be a killer of buffalo. For the latter, not doing so will be irresponsible - a failure to abide by dharma. Yes, if the buffalo were to stick together and be committed to eating right etc., then they take necessary caution. But given such is generally not the case, praising the lion needs to be tempered by the knowledge that it is still the predator of your group - in spite of whatever parallel we find otherwise.

  

We are not into blaming another's scripture to show ours is the right path. Problem is that the followers of such scripture use it to attack us; it's a different ball-game then. Expose them at every turn, unrelentingly. We can disagree here.

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
