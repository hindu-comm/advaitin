Dear Sri Putran-ji,

  

  

*Problem is that the followers of such scripture use it to attack us; it's a different ball-game then.  *

  

Our response to the situation ought not to be dictated by their actions but should be guided by what our dharma demands that we do in the situation.

  

  

*Expose them at every turn, unrelentingly.*

*  *

And they will respond in a like manner (by pointing to what they see as faults in our scripture) and the ultimate result will be a 'street fight'.

  

Regarding this matter, I have made my views known in the past on how I think this situation needs to be tackled and it is not possible for me to repeat all of it again here. To put it briefly, I am against any course of action that merely seeks to preserve the shell of our dharma while allowing the kernel to be lost.

  

  

*We can disagree here.*

  

Yes.

  

  

Warm regards,

Chittaranjan
