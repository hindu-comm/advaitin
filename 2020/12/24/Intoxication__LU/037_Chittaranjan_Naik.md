Namaste to All,

  

I had started this thread on Christmas eve by quoting a passage from the Bible with a theme that is also found in Vedanta, namely that we become so attached to the world and intoxicated by it that we forget the prime purpose for which we are born in this world as human beings - until it is too late. In Vedanta this kind of attachment to the world with its consequent forgetfulness of the atman is compared to committing brahma hatya.

  

I did not expect this message to meet with strong objection. But it did. And it seems to me that there is a possibility of the discussion turning more unsavory, so I would like to close the thread at this point. But before I do so, I would like to mention that the Advaitin list has a long history of conducting free and open discussion with respect and tolerance shown to all religions. And it has had participation from members of diverse religions and groups (though this has reduced of late). I do recall the discussions we have had on this group drawing parallels between the lives led by the Desert Fathers, the early Christian hermits, and the sadhana undertaken by Advaita mumukshus, comparing the notion of avidya of Advaita with St. Augustine’s privation of knowledge, between Vedanta and the irruptions of Sufism, and so on. All these discussions have contributed towards enriching the quality of discourse on this list. I do hope that the openness and tolerance that has characterized this list in the past will continue to prevail in the future too.

  

I shall be closing this thread now.

  

Warm regards,

Chittaranjan
