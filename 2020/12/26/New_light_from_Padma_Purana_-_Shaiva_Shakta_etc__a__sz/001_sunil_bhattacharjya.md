Dear Subbuji,

  

In the Agni purana also Lord Krishna advises us to perform the puja of the five deities and tells us why we should do that. For example, the Sun should be worshipped for our health. Lord Krishna's Son Shamba suffered from skin disease and the Lord asked him to worship the Sun and he got cured. I heard that one of the Mathadhipatis of the Shringeri Math sufferred from Leprosy and he got cured by worshipping the Sun.
Early morning he used to pray to the rising Sun and he got cured of that disease. May be, any member close to the Shringeri math, would be able to throw more lght on this.

  

Regards,
