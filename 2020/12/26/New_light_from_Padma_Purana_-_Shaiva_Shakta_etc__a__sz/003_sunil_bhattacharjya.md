Dear Subbuji,

  

In the Agni Purana the Lord tells us to worship the Devi for liberation. That is probably the reason why Adi Shankara wrote the Bhashya on the Lalita Trishati. Adi Shankara breathed his last in the Moksha-Puri of Kanchipuram, where there is the temple of Mother Lalita, who is Kamakshi or Shodashi, the third Mahavidya, and which is one of the forms of the mother Uma or Parvatai or Gauri or Bhavani.

  

Regards
