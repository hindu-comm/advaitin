Pranaam Shri Sadanandaji,

  

Your article is based on an article of the great scholar Shri Janamadi Hanumanta Rao,which in turn was based on the research work of another great scholar Professor K. Shrinivasa Raghavan and acclaimed by other great scholars like Radhashyaam Shaastri from Hariyana, and Vice chancellor of Sourashtra University, Shrii D.R. Mankad, etc. You have mentioned that ***according to Professor Raghavan, Shri Krishna passed away on 3031 BC, April 13th Friday**.*

There is so much self consistency in all the events and the descriptions that it makes one to wonder. Based on these exact information Mahabhaarat must be Five thousand years Old (from today) as per the historical accounting. The analysis is extremely precise and scientific, matching the

astrological position of the Indian and the Julian Calendars.

As against the above, it may look utterly foolish for me, an ordinry peron, to question the above great work of Professor Raghavan. However if you permit me, I shall dare to raise the question, though it may appear silly to the members of this august forum. The question is how to accept the veracity of the above claim, in view of the fact that the ***traditional year of the passing away of Shri Krishna is 3102 BCE, when there was a planetary conjunction in and around the Meena Rashi.*** Brahmagupta also supported that saying that their was indeed a spread of the planets within the conjuction, i.e., that it was not a one single point conjunction. This planetary conjunction has also been confirmed
by using modern astronomical software.  

Further, if the Bhagavad Gita was told by Shri Krishna on Margashirsha Shukla-Ekadashi and according to the same Shri Krishna, Bhishma got
liberated in Magha Shukla-Ashtamai, then the time-gap between these two events will be less than 60 days, which is absolutely incompatible with the ten days of war followed by the 58 nights of Bhishma's sleep on the bed of arrows. This also shows that Professor Raghavan could not have been right in his dating of the Mahabharata events.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna  

SunilK. Bhattacharjya
