Dear Koenraad,  

  

There have been so many scholars trying to determine the date of Mahabharata war, who published papers and books to earn quick glory, which was short-lived. Thus was the work of Shri Nilesh Oak and even of the other scholars like Dr. Iyengar and Shri Vedver Arya, and they all failed. The reason is very simple. All of them distrusted the work of the great astronomer Aryabharra, who on the basis of astronomical study, confirmed that there was a planetary conjunction on the traditional date of the start of the Kali yuga, on 17/18 February, 3102 BCE, when Lord Krishna passed away. One who has read the Mahabharata (which was written by Vedavyasa, after the passing away of Lord Krishna), knows it very well that the Mahabharata too clearly mentions that there was a planetary conjunction in and around Pisces (Meena Rashi), when Lord Krishna passed away. Even the modern astronomical software supports that there was indeed a planetary conjunction in and around Pisces in 3102 BCE.  

  

The worst thing about the ego-centric modern Indian scholarship, one can see, is that none of the scholars, who worked in the past, could realize that the date of passing away of Lord Krishna, held the vital clue to the date of the Mahabharata war, as Lord Krishna passed away 36 years and a couple months after the liberation of the grand old man Bhishma. This means the Mahabharata war took place in 3139 BCE. Now you can see that one can find the year of the Mahabharata war, only with a bit of common sense, and nothing more is needed. Someone has rightly said "Horse has horse sense, man has no common sense"  

.  

Best

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
