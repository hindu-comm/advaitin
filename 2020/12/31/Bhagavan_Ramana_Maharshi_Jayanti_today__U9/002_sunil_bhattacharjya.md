Dear Daveji,

  

Where did Adi Shankara say the Nididhyasana to be atma-vichara? Simply stated, the acquisition of the Advaita Jnana is through the Mahavakyas along with the Ajatavada verse of Lord Krishna and reaffirming these with Manana and Dhyana. Nididhyasana helps us to make a pathway in the brain for stabilizing the Advaita Jnana so that the other thoughts on the Anitya materials and subjects are weakened gradually and eventually disappear, so that the Pratayahara is achieved and the mind becomes non-reactive, i.e., the Chitta-Vriti nirodha is achieved. Please correct me if necessary.  

  

As regards the Nirguna Upasana, Lord Krishna tells us that it requires ending of the dehabhimana and is difficult and obviously it has to be the second step, i.e.,  a step beyond the Saguna-Upasana. That is my take. Please do not hesitate to correct me, if needed.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna
