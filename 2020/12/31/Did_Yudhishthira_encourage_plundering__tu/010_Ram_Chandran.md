*Namaste:*

*Advaitin list has been in existence since August of 1998 to propagate Sankara’s Advaita philosophy to the universal population. In addition, our goal is to make everyone to remember and cherish Sanatanadharma and implied values cherished by all. This responsibility can never be possible without the goodwill and cooperation from Vedantic scholars who have joined and participated in the discussions.*

*This forum strongly encouraged discussions and allowed the members to interact with mutual respect and with civility. In an open forum, it is quite understandable that not everything someone state is agreeable and most of the time, members express their disagreements politely, but sometimes emotions interfere with civility. This is not unusual and this happens even during family get together and at large gatherings.*

*For most of us, the important characters of Mahabharat and Ramayana are considered as Role Model for establishing our Dharmic values. Our deep attachment to characters such as Yudhishtra, Rama, etc., inject reactions when someone makes a misstatement. All expressions of disagreements and replies can be conducted with civility if we can control our emotion filled Ego. None of us can claim to be free from behavior propelled by a momentary lapse of sensitivity and rational behavior. Knowing these facts, let us cool it down and take a deep breath and relax for few minutes, hours, days, etc.*

*Personally, I am a big fan of the character of Yudhishtra of Mahabharat who wants to be Truthful all the time. This does not mean that he was free from all other human weaknesses and he exhibited them and both he and his close relatives suffered for that. There came a challenging time for Yudhishtra during the Mahabharat war when Dhronacharya started destroying the entire Pandava army with his bows.
Lord Krishna suggested to Yudhishtra to make the announcement, “Aswathaama Hathaha” but he refused to do so. Then an elephant with the name of Aswathaama was killed and Bhagwan asked Yudhishtra to say,
“Aswathaama Hatha Kunjaraha” and he accepted to say so. It was a technical lie that the once-honest Yudhishtra uttered. He did that to break the stalemate in a bloody war and enabled the Pandavas to kill Dhrona and ultimately win the war.*

*The most important point of this episode is to illustrate that Pandavas always maintained and followed Dharmic values and obeyed the advice of Bhagwan Krishna. These facts are provided to make the members aware that let us not provoke the emotions by writing with language with high potentials to kindle emotions.*

*In a philosophical discussion forum, we are better off not to bring statements from other religions that have potentials for misunderstanding. We have an ancient heritage and we have lot to learn from our epics and Puranic stories and there is no need to worry about what others religions have to say and whether we agree or disagree.*

*Thanks again for your continued cooperation and support to advaitin list and we can all improve our behavior and that will help everyone else!*

*With warm regards,*  

*Ram Chandran*
