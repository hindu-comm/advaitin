[[V Subrahmanian	2021-01-06, 11:14:59 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/bav6cJ7kUUY)]]

According to Vedanta, creation is for the purpose of giving the jiva-s who are anaadi, without beginning, a platform to experience the effect of their past karma and to perform fresh karma. A creation cycle consists of 14 loka-s, all inhabited with various types of jivas, for bhoga. So, the hypothetical situation of 'there is a created world without humans or any life form' is not admitted in Vedanta.
