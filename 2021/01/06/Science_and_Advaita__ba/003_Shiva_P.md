Dear Ekam,

  

There was never a 'time' you did not exist.

  

The apparent universe(s) arise and dissolve within you. There is no universe outside of you, apart from you, without you. You are the timeless, changeless reality.

  

Humanity is one of your expressions, as a time-limited experience within that projected appearance. And one of the present human forms you pervade, is looking to peel back the veil.

  

Science and Advaita are not at odds, but offer different vantage points. Science can only measure and discover what's revealed in the reflected awareness. Advaita reveals pure awareness by itself.  

  

May I suggest you are attempting to reconcile a higher perspective through the lens of a limited vantage point 🙂.

  

Namaskar.

  

Shiva
