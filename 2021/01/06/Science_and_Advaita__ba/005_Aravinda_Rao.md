Namaskars,

While I am not an admirer of Ekam, I see that the question can be asked by our own children or any young person who accepts the anthropological view.

One option is to evade the question or refute it by the version that the creator Brahma created prajapati-s, sages and others. The third option is to accept the anthropological view and concede that there was a gradual evolution in human thought and that the Vedas/Upanishads are a result of that.

Regarding moksha of those who were born earlier, we may say that they lived in a pristine society in which morality was evolving. In several stories in the Balakanda of the Ramayana, we see that the code relating to man-woman relationships was evolving. In the Mahabharata we see the story of a sage who forcibly takes thewife of anothersage, and it was perfectly okay at that time. In such a society where the concepts of punyam or papam were evolving, we may say that they attained whatever heaven theybelieved in.

I think we can make such concessions to science. as it does not in any way harm the non-dual thought.

Aravinda Rao
