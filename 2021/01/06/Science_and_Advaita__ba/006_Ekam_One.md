My thoughts on the 3 options.

  

1\. Option 1: Evading this question - Saying that there was no "time" when we did not exist is a good answer from the absolute point of view. but, that approach negates the vyvaharika reality, including the need for all Sadhana, Jnaana and Mukti. If we accept that Sadhana and Mukti are necessary concepts for Jivas, at vyavaharika level, then we need to find the advaitic explanations for the questions  

  

2\. Using the mythological approach of Brahma and Prajapati etc, can be easily refuted by taking the help "pramanas". Using the advaitic principles, we cannot "accept" something that cannot be established by accepted pramanas. Of course, even the Big Bang and age of Earth/Universe cannot be established with certainty using the current scientific pramanas. But, we cannot also establish that evolved humans with intellectual skills to express the Vedic truths, walked on this earth 20000 years ago. So, this option is not valid. (And some of us might argue, this is Dwaitic perspective)  

  

3\. Option 3 sounds more logical to me. However, the argument that Paapa and Punya being in evolutionary stages during the grand old eons has a flaw. We can argue that they are still evolving even today. There is no one time when this evolution stops. May be a better argument would be, that there was no "agency" of the "doing Karmas" by the life forms existed at that time. When human "mind" evolved and started accumulating material, money, established family etc "agency" came into play. The Karma cycle started running at the same time.  

  

As you rightly said, shutting out scientific inquiry (even though this is merely at vyavaharika level) does not help Advaita saadhana.  

  

We should not deny/evade the vyavaharika reality. In stead, we look at the vyavaharkia reality with advaitc understanding.  

  

I invite more comments and perspectives.
