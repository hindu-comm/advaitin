Are you saying here that the Rishi's and / or their "Spiritual Knowledge" existed, for example, in year *250,000 BC*, on this earth? Or did they exist somewhere else on another planet / another Universe / another Loka / another dimension? (I am aware that Time and Space are at Vyavaharika level. This discussion is also at vyvaharika level).  

  

**Objection 1:**  

"Passing on the Knowledge" needs a knower and a student. Who were these life-forms on this earth, in 250,000 BC?

**  **

**Objection 2;**  

Example of Gravity does not necessarily apply here. Because,  

a\) Purvapaksha is **not** disputing the **Sat-Chit-Ananda nature of Brahman**. Only inquiring, when in chronological time, did Moksha became a phenomenon for Jiva's, when did they first start *aspiring* for it.
Who were attaining Moksha before the fully developed humans started actively aspiring for it.  

  

b\) Purvapaksha also accepts that Moksha may not have been needed until "Agency" to perform Papa and Punya karmas became possible. The "agency" did not exist, for example, for dinosaurs which lived on the earth. (if you ignore the Disney movies:-) ) They just performed basic activities needed to be alive and procreate.  

**  **

**Objection 3:** (Not fully relevant to the main question, here)  

Is the desire for and attainment of Moksha conditional to the a) existence of knowledge from the Rishi's and b) access to this knowledge by the Jiva?  

  

Again, *to be extremely careful,* repeating my Disclaimer - Aim here is not to negate the concept of Moksha but, to understand it deeper.
