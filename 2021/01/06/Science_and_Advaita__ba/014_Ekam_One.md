BTW, I am reminded of a "funny story" Swami Sarvapriyananda ji told in one of his sessions.  

  

Brahma wanted to have some fun. He created perfect human beings in his first attempt. But, the game of creation ended immediately, because the "perfect" humans got Moksha, in no time.  

  

Brahma then realized his mistake, and created humans with other Gunas (Rajas and Tamas), in the next attempt. And the game of creation is running in autopilot mode until today and Brahma is sitting back and enjoying the game, till today :-)
