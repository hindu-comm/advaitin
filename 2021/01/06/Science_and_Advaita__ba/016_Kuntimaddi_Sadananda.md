In his brilliant introduction to his commentary on Maitreyee Brahmana, Shankara says all the objective sciences only deals with the objectifiable entities that are false. Hence Knowledge itself is false. The self-knowledge alone stands out as true knowledge. We have taken this text for our Memorial Day Camp series a few years ago.

  

Hari Om!
