Ekam-ji,

  

Here is what the teachings of Vedanta say about this:

  

Where is the dream when you are sleeping? It is there in potential form, waiting to appear at the right time.

  

Similarly, before srishti (i.e creation or manifestation), the entire srishti was there in potential form in mAya. There was only bramhan and mAya, or we could say there was just Iswara. Iswara is anAdi (beginingless), so are bramhan and mAya. The condition during pralaya (dissolution) is similar to our deep sleep, that is the closest example to pralayam. When the time is ripe based on fructifying karmas of all jIvas, srishti begins (really it is the projection of srishti that begins). Space, time and five elements, galaxies, sun moon etc appear (much like them appearing in our dream world). Before humans appeared, the yet to be born humans were all there in a potential form in a causal state (karaNa sharIra form) in mAya just like the lemon tree exists as a lemon seed in soil waiting to sprout when conditions are right. Similarly, the jIvas are there in kAraNa sharIra form. It is just that their sUkshma (subtle) and sthUla (gross) sharIrAs (bodies) have not yet been projected. During any particular srishti, before human forms appear, some kAraNa sharIras could have manifested in other primordial forms (such as dinosaurs) or unicellular creatures, other kAraNa sharIras may not have manifested at all waiting for conditions to be right for humans forms to appear. It all depends on what karmAs need to be exhausted for each jIva that determines the forms that its kAraNa sharIra manifests as.

  

In Vedanta, there is no first srishti. Also note that jIvas are anAdi as well much like mAya is anAdi. There was never a time when a jIva was created as it always existed in kAraNa form. Only forms associated with the kArana sharIra come and go. Along with the kAraNa sharIra, their "to be manifested" bondage also has always existed in potential form…as their “to be manifested” ignorance is very much there in potential form. Note the kAraNa sharIra transcends pralaya and continues in subsequent srishtis also until knowledge dawns.

  

Thus, there have always been bound jIvas in unmanifest or manifest form. When they manifest, some seek moksha, most don’t. Those who seek mokshA by attaining Atma jnAnam “attain" jeevan mukti and then videha mukti thereby ending their kAraNa sharIras. Those who don’t attain Atma jnAnam continue as their kAraNa sharIras and keep getting associated with different forms until they attain jnAnam in some human birth in some srishti.

  

Atma jnAnam (or any jnAnam for that matter) is also there in potential form in mAya during pralayam. So are the Vedas. When the time is right, Iswara appears in a certain form (DakshiNamUrthy or Adi Guru: the first Guru) and communicates the knowledge to the initial students to “seemingly initiate” the Guru paramparA in a particular srishti. Again, it is just a manifestation of the unmanifest knowledge that has always existed. There was no time when this knowledge was invented by anyone. Iswara has always been a jnAni!

  

Regards,

—Satyan
