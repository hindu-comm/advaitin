Sunil-ji,

  

Yes, jIvas are anAdi from kAraNa sharIra standpoint, but they have an end.

  

During death, the jIva sheds the sthUla sharIra only. sUkshma and kAraNa sharIra continue.

  

During pralaya, the jIva sheds the sUkshma sharIra also. kAraNa sharIra alone continues.

  

kAraNa sharIra is anAdi: it has no beginning. It has always existed in mAya. It survives pralaya after pralaya only to manifest again as sUkshma and sthUla sharIras in the next sRishti…and next sRishti…and so on...until knowledge dawns. kAraNa sharIra ends with (and only with) Atma jnAnam.

  

Thus, even though jIva is beginingless/anAdi, it has an eventual end, only with jnAnam.

  

Regards,

—Satyan
