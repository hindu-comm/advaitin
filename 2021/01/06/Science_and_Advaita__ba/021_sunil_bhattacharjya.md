Dear friends,

  

"Karana sarira is anaadi" is a non-vedantic concept, and this violates the Mahavakyas, and also challenges the Ajativada verse of Lord Krishna. One can go beyond the cycle of birth and death, only when all the five koshas disappear. Let us hear what the other advaitin friends among us, have to say.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna
