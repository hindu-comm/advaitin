Sunilji - PraNAms

  

Kaarana is a causal state - Since ignorance is anaadi - the kaarana is anaadi for shariira.

  

prakRitim purusham chaiva vidynaadi ubhaavapi.

Purusha stands for the subject 'I' and prakRiti stands for the object 'this'; 'I am = this' - ignorance caused identification is anaadi too. Karma to jnanma has to be a beginningless cycle.

  

Scriptures say - tat aikshataa - bahusyaam - prajaayeyeti - It (pure existence that was one without a second which is of the nature of consciousness) - saw - and decided to become many and it became many. - On what basis? - on the basis of the previous life cycle of karmas of jeevas.

  

Hari Om!  

Sadananda
