Sunilji - PraNAms.

  

The discussion is only at vyvahaara level.

  

Any ignorance and ignorance generated karmas are anaadi. When the ignorance end - that is when I realize aham brahamaasmi, there is also a recognition that I was never a doer - then the karmas do not belong to me.

akarthaaham abhoktaham - becomes part of the knowledge.

  

The realization is only at the vyaavahaarika state.

  

For a realized person the Body-mind-Intellect will still remain due to praarabda. When life ends the body - B- merges with the Virat, Mind merges with Hiranya garba and Intellect merges with Iswara. The person has recognized that I am the all-pervading reality - the Brahman one that pervades everything.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
