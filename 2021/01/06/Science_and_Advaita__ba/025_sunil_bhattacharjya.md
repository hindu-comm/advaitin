Pranaam Sadaji,  

  

I see your point. The Dvaitins and the Vishishtadvaitins always live in the Vyavaharika level. So let them be in that stage of evolution. For the Advaitin, on being Videhamukta, the deha of the five koshas does not remain anymore.  

  

That is why Lord Ram asked Hanumanji to mediatate on his (Lord Ram's) Avayaya srate, and not on Lord Ram with a body. I understand that the Kalika Mahapurana says that th word "AUM" has come from "AVYAYA RAM", and some Sanskrit scholars with good knowledge of the Panini sutras have reportedly shown that.
