Sunil ji,

  

You wrote:

“ It cannot be eternal with the Jeeva identity.”

  

We are saying the same thing. No one said that that the kAraNa sharIra is eternal. There appears to be some misunderstanding about the meaning of the word anAdi. It just means “that which doesn’t have a beginning”. It doesn’t mean eternal or without an end. There are anAdi entities without a beginning that can have an end and are therefore non eternal: ignorance, kAraNa sharIra are examples.

  

Regards,

—Satyan
