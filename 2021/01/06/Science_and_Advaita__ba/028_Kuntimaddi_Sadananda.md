Sunilji - PraNAms

  

Everybody lives only at vyavahaara level.

  

Vedas point out the paaramaarthika - and that pointing out is also at vyaavahaara level. The mahaavaakyas are indicatory statements - lakshyaarthaas - for tat tvam asi one has to use bhaagatyaga lakshaana or jahat ajahat lakshana.

From the paarmaarthika level - there is nothing to live - it is one without a second.

  

na vaak gacchati - na manaH

  

yatho vaacho nivartante apraapya manasaa saH

  

No words can reach there nor mind.

  

Even the description of Advaita is only the negation of the experiential dvaita, as not really real on the basis of the scriptural statements. That negation has to be done and is done at vyavahaara level only.

  

There is no realization at paarmaarthika level - since it is one without a second.

  

With this, I stop.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
