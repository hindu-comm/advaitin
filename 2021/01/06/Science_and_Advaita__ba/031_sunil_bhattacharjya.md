Dear friends,

  

Further elucidation. Maya or Prakrti is eternal and the Jeeva comes with the creation and then it has the five koshas. When the Jeeva attains the Jnana the Jeevatva ends with the end of the five koshas. So ***karana sarira*** of Jeeva has start (**ADI**) with the creation and end (**ANTA**) with the realization of Jnana.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna
