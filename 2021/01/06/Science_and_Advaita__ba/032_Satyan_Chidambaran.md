Sunil-ji,

  

There appears to be some communication gap still. Need a few corrections as follows:

  

You wrote:

“Jeeva comes with the creation”

  

jIva doesn’t come with the creation (sRshti). Only the 4 koshas of the jIva become manifest during sRshti i.e sthUla sharIra (or annamaya kosha) and sUkshma sharIra (or the collection of praNamaya, manomaya and vijnAnamaya) are projected during sRshti. The kAraNa sharIra (same as anandamaya kosha) was there even before sRshti in mAya. The kAraNa sharIra is the jIva in dormant/seed form. In fact, the kAraNa sharIra has always been there forever, across infinite past sRshtis and pralayas.

  

You wrote:

"When the Jeeva attains the Jnana the Jeevatva ends with the end of the five koshas.”

Yes. It brings an end to the kAraNa sharIra.

  

You wrote:

"So***karana sarira***of Jeeva has start (**ADI**) with the creation”

No, kAraNa sharIra doesn’t have start (ADI) during sRshti. It is anAdi…meaning no ADI (beginning) at all.

  

Regards,

—Satyan
