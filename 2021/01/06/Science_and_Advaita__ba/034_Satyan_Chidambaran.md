Sunil-ji.

  

No one has said that the jIva doesn’t have five koshas! The question is only about which koshas manifest during sRshti and which kosha existed before sRshti also in an unmanifest form.

  

kAraNa sharIra (=anandamaya kosha) being anAdi existed before sRshti (in fact existed forever). The other four appear with sRshti.

  

  

Regards,

—Satyan
