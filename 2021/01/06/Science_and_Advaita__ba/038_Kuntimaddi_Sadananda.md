I concur with all your statements. 'Jeeva attains jnaana' - that involves only a **clear understanding**that 'I am not this jeeva but I am Brahman (aham brahmaasmi), but currently playing role in this BMI as jeeva. That understanding involves understanding as a fact and not just as a thought - Sthita prajna.

  

What ends with knowledge of jnaana is therefore only the moola avidya or self-ignorance, and nothing more. Kaarana shareera is the causal body because of which the beginningless cycle of birth-death- birth cycles has been there. All shareeras end only when the BMI drops out - as is called videha, since BMI is the product of the kaarana (praarabda karma).



From the jnaani's understanding point - He has understood that I am not these shareeras; but now I am playing the role as this particular jeeva until BMI vesham or costume is there.

  

In essence, the shareeras end only when the jnaani drops this BMI,

and Jnaanam itself involves only cognitive understanding of 'aham brahmaasmi' and abiding in that knowledge while still playing the drama of life.

  

Just my 2c.
