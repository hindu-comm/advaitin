Sunilji - PraNAms

  

When the understanding is that I am not this body, then the body's problems belong to the body. Does the jnaanai has praarabda karma - no/yes. Praarabda belongs to the BMI and a jnaani is one who has **realized or re-cognized**that I am not this body or I am not this - neti, neti; since any 'this' is an object; and also recognized that I am pure sat chit ananda - i.e Brahman - hence no shareera or correctly to say that never I had any shareeras - hence the statement akartaaham abhoktaaham. Hence praarabda also belongs to the BMI only, but when the jnaani is operating the BMI for transactional purposes, then he may at the BMI level feel the pleasures/pains at that level.

  

We know the stories of Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi's problems with this hand, and Shree Ramakrishna Paramahamsa with his throat problems. A jnaani will be acting as an actor playing the drama of life - just as Krishna or Rama did.

  

The jnaani's body will be maintained not only by his praarabda but also by the praarabda of those who are seeking him as a guru - One can say Lord himself maintains that BMI for the benefit of seekers or for loka kalyaanam.

  

Vedaha comes only when His BMI is no more needed for the world or it can be maintained for the world. I know - Swami Chinmayanandaji's heart was functioning only at 20% effiency, and that too failed after his lost camp in Wahsington D.C. that I was involved.

  

Hence Lord himself will decide when to end that BMI of a jnaani.

  

Hence Jnaaani is one, by definition, the one who has understood that he has no BMI but yet using the God-given BMI for the loka kalyaanam.

  

Of course until one abides in that knowledge, a jnaani will be struggling hard to de-identify with the BMI as discussed by Goudapaada in his Kaarika in terms of obstacles for the spiritual path.

  

As Swami Paramaarthanandaji says it is the reduction of FIR - Frequency of perturbation, Intensity of Perturbation, and Recovery time from perturbation - goes down as one abides more and more in the knowledge that is gained from shravanam, mananam and nidhidhyaasanam. He slowly becomes a jnaana nishTa.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
