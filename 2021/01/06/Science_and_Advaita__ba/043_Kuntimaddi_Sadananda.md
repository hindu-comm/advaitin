I am not sure of the scriptural quotes. Shree Satyan Chdambaran may be able to provide. He is currently studying under the guidance of Swami Paramarthanandaji.

  

Krishna states clearly - naivakinchit karomeeti yukte manyeta tatvavit - with shrunvan, pasyan, etc.

  

Karmani akarmayaH pasyat - sah buddhiman.

  

Prakrityaivaca kriyamaanaani .. yaH pasyati tat aatmaanam akartaaham sa pasyati.. as all actions are done by Prakriti - hence a jnaani is one who knows that I am akartaa inspite of all actions that are being done at BMI level -akartaaham sa payati.

  

To be a kartaa, for Prakriti to do - the BMI has to operate with the world. Hence all nine yards will be there but he has recognized that when BMI acts he is not **really** acting but actions will go on as needed. Hence karma belongs to the BMI but not for a jnaani. Just as what an actor does on the stage when he is playing the roles of a king or a beggar does not belong to the actor who is playing the role but belongs only to the roles.

  

Hence Life and the world become- Jagannatakam.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
