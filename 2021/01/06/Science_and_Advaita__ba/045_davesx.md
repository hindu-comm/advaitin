Namaste Friends - In my periodicchecking I just stumbled on this thread.

  

My few cents....

  

Ved-anta can mean the end of Ved(knowledge) which is the end of the limitation of theknown (as our senses operate within a very narrow bandwidthand cannot know the truth).

  

In other words it is the end of thought, more particularly the end of the I-Thought....the end of you. You are finished!

  

The end of thought is usually referred to as Brahman. So how on earth can one know what is on the other side (or Brahman)when thought,thinking and you areabsent?

  

It is all idlespeculation by thought about enlightenment which is not an experienced state.

  

In fact neitheryou or anyone can know anything postenlightenment, but massive volumes have been written in the shrutis and by Vedanticcommentatorstrying to express thatwhich isinexpressible, out of sheercompassion for suffering sentient beings which is probably causing more confusion thatjeeps theaspirantforever reading more and more scriptures,never realizing the import.

  

To summarize -to the enlightened free of divisivementalconcepts,the world is perceived assimply what itis -- One undividedinfinite Mystery.
