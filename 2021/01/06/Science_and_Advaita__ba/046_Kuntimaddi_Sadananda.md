Sunilji - PraNAms

  

Jnnaani is the one who has a clear knowledge of himself or his-self.

  

A saadhaka may fall - but jnaani has clearly understood. Once one has understanding, the ignorance cannot come back - If so, it will be a never-ending process.

  

In the initial stages, he may not be able to abide in that knowledge - due to remnant vaasanas still influencing. Hence FIR reduction is discussed. We have the story of Jadabharata in the Upanishad.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
