Namaste Sadanandaji,

  

There can be time gap between the time (i) when one gets enlightened (as one has to live with BMI till the end of the effects of the Prarabdha Karma) and (ii) when one becomes Videhamukta. Now we have two views. In one you think that the Jnaani cannot fall and in the other Kapila-muni felt that the jnaani may fall before getting liberated. There is also a Pauranic story that even Devarshi Narada fell once, when he succumbed to the influence of Prakrti, when Lord Krishna asked him to fetch him some water, as the Lord wanted to teach Narada as to what is Maya. We also know that even Brahmarshi Vishwamitra was enamored by Menaka, and we had Shakuntala.  

  

Kindly permit me to leave at that

Jai Shri Krishna
