Sunilji - PraNAms

  

There is a difference between jnaani and vijnaani, if one wants to make some distinctions. In this classification, jnaani is only paroksha jnaani - one who has intellectually understand the scriptures.

  

Vijnaani is the one who has internalized that knowledge - aparoksha jnanam - he is the one who is called - sthitaprajna.

  

The difference between the two - is due to lingering vaasanas - in essence - the mind is not completely purified. He is called madhyama adhikaari - for him only, the Nidhidhyaasana is prescribed. Most of us fall into this category. For him, only FIR reduction applies.

  

For uttama adhikaari like Nachiketas, shravana and manana are sufficient.

  

Yes, let us leave with that.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
