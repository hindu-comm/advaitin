Fortunately, I have dealt with this in an earlier section:



Shankara alludes to this misapplication of the metaphor in his bhAShya on Chandogya Upanishad 6.2.3. An objector suggests: “*In that case whatever is perceived is unreal, like a rope perceived in the shape of a snake etc.*” And Shankara answers: “*No. Since it is Existence itself that is perceived otherwise through the duality of different forms, therefore, there is no non-existence of anything anywhere.*” (Ref. 23)
