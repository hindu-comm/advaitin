That is fine. Let us agree to disagree. I think most people don't know what Lord Krishna told in the Uddhava Gita "What was not there in the beginning and not there at end, is not there in the middle". This verse is also there in the Original Bhagavad Gita, but about 1,700 years after Adi Shankara, one Shri Bhatta Bhaskara of the Bheda bheda school, wrote a commentary on the Bhagavad gita, without 45 verses of the Original Bhagavad Gita. Unfortunately, Abhinava Shankara, who wrote a commentary , refuting Bhatta Bhaskara, did not know that there is an Original Bhagavad gita of 745 verses.
