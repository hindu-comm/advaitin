Namaste:

For Vedantins Horoscope can’t be considered as an indicator of what has to come. What has to come depend on what we are! Our character is our destiny and we need to spend more of our time in building our destiny! As Vedantins we are also aware that we only can control what we can do (act) and the outcome is not in our control. Any assumption of Horoscope as an indicator implies that someone has determined our destiny which is not true! Acharya’s subtle message is to help us to avoid the anxiety or excitement about our future and to undertake all precautionary measures to prevent surprises!!
