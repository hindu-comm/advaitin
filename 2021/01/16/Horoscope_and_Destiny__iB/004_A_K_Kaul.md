Vedic Astrology?! Eh!

Nothing "Vedic" about it!

Kindly let us know as to on what basis you call predictive astrology as Vedic astrology and how do you call it scientific?

I am sure you have read the Bhagavad-Gita thoroughly!

When Arjuna asked Bhagwaan Krishna in 2/6

न चैतद् विद्मः कतरन्नो गरीयो यद्वा जयेम यदि व नो जयेयुः

"We do not even know which is preferable for us--to fight or not to fight, nor do we know whether we shall win or whether they will conquer us..."

Bhagwaan Krishna did not advise Arjuna to consult his grandpa Maharshi parashara of "Brihat Parashari" fame as to who would win, but just admonished in 2/37

हतो वा प्राप्स्यसि स्वर्गं जित्वा वा भोक्ष्यसे महीम्  

तस्मादुत्तिष्ठ कौन्तेय युद्धाय कृत निश्चयः

"Die and you will win heaven, conquer, and you enjoy sovereignty of the earth; therefore, stand up, Arjuna, determined to fight"

As such, without bothering about the so called "Vedic astrology"---which is anything but Vedic---muc h less scientific! we must proceed on our spiritual journey!

With regards and Jai Shri Ram!
