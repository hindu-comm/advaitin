Dear Ram Chandranji,

  

We have to experience the consequences of our karma, and that is natural. However if we sincerely regret our genuine mistakes, committed due to our ignorance, and also have faith in the Lord, then He does help us to overcome the bad effect of our karma.  

  

For example, when Gandhari's curse was about to materialize and the curse of the rishis were also to materialize, Lord Krishna could have nullified the curses, so the sage Vdavyasa told us, but the Lord didn't nullify. However, the Lord did try to help by advising his kinsmen not to drink wine and told them to go to Prabhasa kshetra, to devote themselves to the religious rites to purify themselves. Those who did not listen to what the Lord told, perished with their families.  

  

This is probably a lesson to learn, that if we harm someone, physically or mentally, and we should express our sincere repentance and try to undo the past wrong-doings, in whatever way it is possible, and the Lord would certainly give us a chance to do that.  

  

My 2 cents

Jai Shri Krishna
