**The Universal Law of Cause and Effect**

**(Ram Chandran)**

Human life is governed by the Universal Law of Cause and Effect. No one can escape from the consequences of one’s own actions and our life is controlled by this law. The Law of. Karma also explains the same. Karma literally means "deed" or "act." Karma is a natural law of the mind, just as gravity is a law of matter. Karma is not fate, and we act with our own free will to create our own destiny. The Vedas tell us, if we sow goodness, we will reap goodness; if we sow evil, we will reap evil. Karma essentially is the totality of our actions and their concomitant reactions. We will suffer only if we have made the conditions ripe for our sufferings. Ignorance of this law will not relieve us from the consequences of our actions. The Universe is endowed with a set of natural laws to govern and sustain which we call as Dharma. Dharma is the Divine law prevailing on every level of existence, from the sustaining cosmic order to religious, social and moral laws which bind us in harmony with that order. Related to the soul, dharma is the mode of conduct most conducive to spiritual advancement, the right and righteous path. When we follow dharma, we are in conformity with the Truth and we naturally abide in closeness to Nature. Adharma is opposition to the divine law.

Here below a list of associated laws of nature is provided to help us to lead our life to be a better citizen and most importantly a better human being.  

**The Great Law of Cause and Effect:** "As you sow, so shall you reap,".is the "Law of Cause and Effect". Whatever we put out in the Universe is what comes back to us. If we want Happiness, Peace, Love, Friend-ship; then we should try our level best to engage in activities to keep us Happy, Peaceful, Loving and develop true friendship.

**The Law of Creation:** Life doesn't just Happen, it requires our active participation. We are one with the Universe, both inside and out. Whatever surrounds us will guide us to our inner state. Be surround ourself with what we want to have present in our Life. Shanakarahrya in the famous Bhajagovindham refers this as Satsanghatve Nissangatvam. Iin the field of spiritualism, satsang (good association) should lead to nissang (non-attachment). This is so significant and is worth pondering over

**The Law of Humility:** What we refuse to accept will continue for us. If what we see is an enemy, or someone with a character trait that we find to be negative, then we ourselves are not focused on a higher level of existence. “The I in you see the you in me!”

**The Law of Growth:** "Wherever we go, there we are". For us to Grow spiritually, only we must change and not the people, places or things around us. We only have control our lives and when we change our life follows suit and changes our behavior towards others..

**The Law of Responsibility:** Whenever there is something wrong in our life, there is something wrong in us. We mirror what surrounds us and what surrounds us mirrors us; this is a Universal Truth. We must take responsibility to all of our actions.

**The Law of Connection:** Even if something we do seems inconsequential, it is important that it gets done as everything in the Universe is connected. Each step leads to the next step, and so forth and so on. Someone must initiate the work to get a job done. Neither the first step nor the last are of greater significance, They were both needed to accomplish the task. Past, Present and Future they are all inter connected...

**The Law of Focus:** You can’t think of two things at the same time.
When our focus is on Spiritual Values, it is impossible for us to have lower thoughts such as greed or anger. Hence we should focus on attaining and retaining higher values to get rid of lower tendencies.

**The Law of Giving and Receiving:** This law is based on the fact that everything in the universe operates through dynamic exchange. Every relationship is one of give and take because **giving and receiving** are different aspects of the flow of energy. Swami Chinmayanandaji suggests “Give more than what we take.”

**The Law of Here and Now:** Old thoughts, old patterns of behavior, old dreams will prevent us from acting at the present. Plug our EAR (Prevent Excitement during the present, Stop Anxiety over the future and do not Regret over the past.)

**The Law of Change:** History repeats itself until we learn the lessons that we need to change our path. We need to select the shoe that fits our size and we should prepare to adjust our behavior according to the time and surrounding!

**The Law of Patience and Reward:** All Rewards require initial toil. Rewards of lasting value require patience and persistence.. True joy follows doing what we believe in doing, and accept the reward when it
comes in on its own time..

**The Law of Significance and Inspiration:** We get back from something whatever we have put into it. The true value of something is a direct result of the energy and intent that is put into it. Everyone’s personal contribution is also a contribution to the Whole. Lack luster contributions have no impact on the Whole, nor do they work to diminish it. Spontaneous contributions bring life to inspire and impact the Whole.

Awareness about the Laws of Nature will help us to conduct our Karma tuned to Dharma. The sages of the Upanishads have stated us a very simple rule – “Life is a Bridge, Enjoy while crossing, Don’t build any castle.”

regards,
