Dear Ram Chandranji,

Yes, you have point questioning the need to find out the historical facts, but the Indian tradition is that even the Veda recommends that history is to be written and kept updated. Vedavyasa classified the single Veda into the four Vedas and the remaining Puranas and the Epics became the Fifth Veda and the history was the essential part of the Fifth Veda and there was the injunction to keep historical records updated. Falsity was never tolerated in the Vedas. That’s why I am somewhat concerned about the true history of Indian culture including the history of the Advaita schools. IMHO if any discussion is started, it is better that it ends with the establishment of the truth.

Jai Shri Krishna
