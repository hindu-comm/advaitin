\<I recognize that Indian astrology has evolved from the primitive era when it was believed

that Rahu swallowed the sun when in fact it was an eclipse and science has taught us to reject such nonsense.\>

The fact of the matter is that there are no Mesha, Vrisha etc. Rashis----whether the so called sayana or the so called nirayana---in any of the Vedas or the Vedangas!

Similarly, we do not find any mention of Mangal, Shani etc. planets, much less Rahu-Kethu in any of the Vedas, nor the earliest available indigenous astronomical work viz. the Vedanga Jyotisha of 15th century BCE!

Thatalone is a testimony to the fact that "Vedic astrology" is absolutely a misnomer!

Till about the middle of the last century, Indian astrology was known as either Hindu astrology or just nirayana astrology. But some vested interests, including the "imported Vamadevas" rechristened it as "Vedic astrology" so that they could earn lots of dollars in overseas countriesby such gimmicks!

Similarly, we do not find any mention of Mangal, Shani etc. planets, much less Rahu-Kethu in any of the Vedas, nor the earliest available indigenous astronomical work viz. the Vedanga Jyotisha of 15th century BCE!

So it is a sin, if not a crime, to ascribe some attributes to the Vedas, which were never reflected in them.

***Rashis in the Puranas and siddhantas are Sayana!***

On the other hand, we find mention of Mesha, Vrisha etc. RAshis times without number in the Puranas like Srimad Bhagavata, Shri Vishnu Purana, Shri Narada Purana and so on. But all of them are talking about a tropical, i.e. seasonal Rashichakra, which means we can not call the nirayana----euphemistically known as sidereal!---astrology even as Pauranic!

***Nirayana astrology is not even siddhantic!***

Now coming to the siddhantas! There has been a discussion already on this topic in this august forum and I do not want to repeat the same!

All I can say is that none of the siddhantas has touched the so called niraayyana rashichakraseven with a barge pole!

*Thus the niraayana astronomy and therefore nirayana astrology is really niraadhaar!*

***None of the Vedic or even Pauranic seers have advised to consult soothsayers!***

Since the main concern of this august forum is the spiritual journey to attain self-realization, all I can say from house tops is that none of the Upanaishadas, leave alone the Vedas, or Puranas or any Acharya including the Aadi Shakara has touched predictive astrology even with a barge pole!

I have gone through almost all the works of Aadi Shankara, and I could not find even a single reference to any "Bhagya-vada" in them!

On the otehr hand, when the Katha Upanishada 1/3/14 says

उत्तिष्ठत जाग्रत प्राप्यवरान्निबोधत \| क्षुरस्य धारा निशिता दुरत्यया दुर्गं पथस्तत् कवयोवदन्ति
