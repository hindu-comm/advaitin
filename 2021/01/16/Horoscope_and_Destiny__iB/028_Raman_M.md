namaste Kaul-ji,

that is an english translation from a compilation of shri paramacharya of kanchi of Deivathin Kural (divine voice) that I rcvd on my WA.

Incidentally, I rcvd it the same day I saw the discussion on astrology ( which I think is a derivate or extension of jyothisha that forms part of vedas) in this thread.

It does mention one of its use as to detemine the right time/place for a ritual and seems connected with the vedas. Hence I shared.

As regards your quote about Arjuna not being asked to approach an astrologer and hence perhaps debunking astrology, I think Sahadev was considered as very knowledgeable in astrology.

When Duryodhana approached Sahadeva for determining an 'auspicious' time favourable to him (Duryodhana) to start the war, Sahadev did provide him one.

Sri Krishna having come to know of this, faked a day as if the determined day already appeared and induced Duryodhana to start the war. I find this convincing enough that

Sri Krishna did believe or respect astrology. It also seems convicing about the play of destiny.

Astrology may not reveal in details everything that unfolds, but I think it can flag critical events and dangers.
