The world is "Anitya", as indicated in the Ajatavada verse of the Lord.
The advaitins, who have passed through the first three ashramas and discharged their duties towards their family and the society and also fulfilled the obligations, such as to the ancestors, as laid down in the dharma shastras, are in the fourth ashrama, whether they are in a joint family or otherwise. Detachment towards the anitya things probably can best be realized by them, than the ones still in the earlier ashramas. Of course, as the Lord indicates in the beginning of the 6th chapter of the Bhagavad gita, that one can be a sanyashi at any stage in life, as it is more a mental attitude.
