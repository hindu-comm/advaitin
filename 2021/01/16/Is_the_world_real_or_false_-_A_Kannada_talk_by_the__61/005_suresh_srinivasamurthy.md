Dear Sri Dave-ji,

  

Actually, I am in agreement with your view that vyavahAra and paramArtha form one indivisible whole.

This view is idealistic and realistic at the same time and integrates jnAna (truth) and karma/dharma.

It is also fully compatible with the Vedic view ofBrahman/Ishwara asthe sahasra shIrshA purusha,

the universal person.

  

Probably SwAmiji is also teaching the same thing in different words.When he says vyavahAra is removed

with Brahma jnAna, probably, he is pointing to the inseparability of thesame with paramArtha.

  

Others may disagree but I think vyavahAra/jagat is paramArtha in action :)

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
