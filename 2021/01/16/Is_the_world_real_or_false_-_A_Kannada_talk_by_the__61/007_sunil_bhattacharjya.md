Thank you Sureshji for sending the gist of the talk of the Shringeri Swamiji. Yes, the world does not vanish for the Jnani. The Jnani analyses himself that his emotions are not him, His anger is not him and he should be able to get over that. We should attain the Jnana and practise that so that we should be able to develop udaashina nature towrads the anitya materials and pleasures of this world. The Jnani may not have physical or emotional problem himself, but there are many who have problems and the jnani feels for them, and tries to help others, and look for the ways to help.  

  

Lord Buddha was a prince and everything was golden for him, yet he could feel for the people suffering from illness, old age and death. Long meditation opened his eyes, and he shared his knowledge and experience through the different promulgations. Our own Adi Shankara and the other spiritual gurus following him, have been trying to educate and guide us, so that we also develop ourselves.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna

sunil k b
