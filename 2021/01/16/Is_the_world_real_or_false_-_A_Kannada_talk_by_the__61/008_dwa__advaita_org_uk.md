Hi Davex,



I agree with the essence of what you say (although would not put it quite like that and don’t like your use of the word ‘experience’ at the end!) I find it amazing and worrying that someone such as a Sringeri Jagadguru can say something to the effect that the world “gets destroyed upon attainment of brahma jnana”. As I noted some time ago, I have been researching and writing around this topic for the past 6 months and, although there are quite a few ‘ambiguous’ statements in shruti and Shankara, there is no doubt in my mind that this is NOT what either was saying. ‘All this is Brahman’ is unambiguous to begin with. When the world has never been created, how could it be destroyed when the puny intellect of a seeker happens to realize this? EVERY ’thing’ is name and form of Brahman, including the Jagadguru and including the world.
