\<

**‘All this is Brahman’ is unambiguous to begin with. When the world has never been created, how could it be destroyed when the puny intellect of a seeker happens to realize this? EVERY ’thing’ is name and form of Brahman, including the Jagadguru and including the world.**

\>

  

The world is not created but manifested just like the tree is manifested from the seed.Only difference is Brahman is the immutable seed from whom

the world is manifested/sustained/withdrawn.

  

Destruction in shAstra is samhAra which means to withdraw. According to Brahma Sutra, Brahman consumes the world of moving and non-moving

entities and finally that alone remains. As the digestive fire (VishwAnara) it is consuming even now and it is experiential also.

  

ajAta vAdins may disagree, but ajAta also means dharma-jAta (birth for the sake of dharma), similar to Ahimsa which also meansdharma himsa.

Veda explicitly declares - ajAyamAno bahudA vijAyate.

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
