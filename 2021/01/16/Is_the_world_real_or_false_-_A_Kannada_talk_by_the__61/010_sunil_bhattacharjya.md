Dear Surshji,

  

Ajatavada means no creation and only manifestation, when Brahman wants to be many and goes back to the unmanifested state. That is what the Lord means in the Ajatavada verse in the Uddhava Gita and The Original Bhagavad Gita. However it is alright if it is called creation, albeit only to be consumed back. ***Did not one upanishad say that like a spider the Brahman takes the world back into Himself* ?** As regards the simile, you will be surprised that there is indeed such a spider, which can take beck its web into itself.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna
