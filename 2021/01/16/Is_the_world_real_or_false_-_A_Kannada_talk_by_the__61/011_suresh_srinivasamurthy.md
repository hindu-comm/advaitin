Sri Sunil-ji,

  

\<Ajatavada means no creation and only manifestation, when Brahman wants to be many and goes back to the unmanifested state.\>

  

The above is actually true in V.Advaita and Dvaita also and such a Brahman cannot be nirguna/nishkriya 🙂

Yes, spider's can withdraw back their own web and the analogy is apt because spider has eight legs which stands

for the eight forms of prakrithi.

  

All of this act of Brahman/Ishwara comes under vyAvahArika satya (which is not false or illusion in Advaita)  

according to Sri Swamiji's explanation.

  

Regards,  
Suresh
