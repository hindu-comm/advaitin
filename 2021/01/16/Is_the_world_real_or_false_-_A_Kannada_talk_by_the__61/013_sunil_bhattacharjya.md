Dear Sureshji,

  

For the purpose of manifestation, the Brahman assumes a form with the three gunas and thus He is Lord Krishna with his blue coloured appearance (the Tantric equivalent of which is the Adi-Vidya Mother Kali , whi is also blue coloured). Thus many, including yours truly, believe that Lord Krishna is the male form of the Mother Kali. Vashishta Rishi mentioned that when the sage Udddalaka became Videhamukta, the Mother Chandika happily took his skull to adorn her necklace of skulls, as her dear son became one with her. Interesting anecdote - isn't it?

  

Jai Shri Krishna
