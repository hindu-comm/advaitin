Namaste Dennisji:

  

You have stated, " I find it amazing and worrying that someone such as a Sringeri Jagadguru can say something to the effect that the world “gets destroyed upon attainment of brahma jnana." This is one of those cases where someone translated from what Jagadguru uttered in Kannada or Sanskrit. Jagadgurus probably would have meant to say that the world manifested in the mind gets dissolved with the Brahman! You probably know well that how language becomes a big barrier in communicating what we really mean! Same thing is true with respect to the acceptance of manifestation over creation! Honestly all these words that we express are thoughts of the mind and what really happens is that the thoughts get evaporated from the mind!
