Hi Ram-ji,



Yes indeed. Whilst writing my current book on ‘Dispelling Confusions in Advaita Vedanta’, I am encountering quite a few examples where esteemed writers translate Sanskrit in such a way as to produce English that expresses their own understanding of a topic rather than Shankara’s! Needless to say, this may easily cause seekers to accumulate wrong understanding. The trouble is that, if one does not have good understanding of the source language, it is very difficult to get to the originally intended meaning. The way that I have to do it is by looking at many different translations and then endeavoring to determine the precise meaning of specific key words. In this particular case, the difference between ‘manifestation’ and ‘creation’ is clearly crucial.
