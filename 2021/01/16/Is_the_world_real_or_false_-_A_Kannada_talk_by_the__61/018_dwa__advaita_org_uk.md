As I indicated in my response to Ram-ji, if one already understands, one can take the well-expressed bits and ignore or even reject the poorly expressed. But a naïve seeker may be completely thrown by a poorly translated piece. This can arise, for example, when someone whose essential belief is biased towards Yoga philosophy translates something written by an Advaitin. A seeker can end up believing that he has to go into samAdhi and ‘experience’ Brahman before he can become enlightened!
