Yes indeed. Whilst writing my current book on ‘Dispelling Confusions in Advaita Vedanta’, I am encountering quite a few examples where esteemed writers translate Sanskrit in such a way as to produce English that expresses their own understanding of a topic rather than Shankara’s! Needless to say, this may easily cause seekers to accumulate wrong understanding. The trouble is that, if one does not have good understanding of the source language, it is very difficult to get to the originally intended meaning. The way that I have to do it is by looking at many different translations and then endeavoring to determine the precise meaning of specific key words. In this particular case, the difference between ‘manifestation’ and ‘creation’ is clearly crucial.



Ø You have made an important observation between manifestation and creation, if the manifestation here means mere appearance then jagat is kevala vyakta due to our ignorance about nirvishesha brahman. That more or less declaring that jagat is mere appearance to the bhrAnta (ajnAni). Srushti para vAkya, IshwarAstitva and his designation as karmaphaladAta, karmAdhyaksha in shruti would go for the toss if we deny the srushti which is part and parcel of Upanishads though not consistent in presenting the order uniformly and it is mere upAya to drive home the ultimate stand of Advaita i.e brahman is nishkriya😊 His HH in his Kannada discourse makes one more important observation that jagat is not asatya but it is mithya. If we see the meaning of asatya contextually in bhAshya ( example taitereeya) bhAshyakAra attributes asatyatvaM to jagat since it is NOT trikAla abhAdhita satya. But elsewhere he clarifies nAma and rUpa (in kArya rUpa) is satya only when we recognize ( realize ) its kAraNa. If we realize the kAraNa behind all kArya, there is no thing that can be called ‘asat’ whatsoever. We have had hell a lot of discussion in Advaita-L about the jaganmithya aspect while attributing the jeeva satyatvaM and calling jeeva as brahma.
Jaganmithya and jeevo brahmaiva na aparaH. And interestingly those who deny the existence of jagat to the jnAni passionately attributes the avidyA lesha and prArabdha karma to him and insist that it is not kevala avidyA vyavahAra show for the ajnAni-s but a real thing that paramArtha jnAni would face in his post realization period and till the death of his physical body. And this conclusion is ofcourse based on bhAshya vAkya where bhAshyakAra insists even for the samyak jnAni ( paramArtha jnAni) his senses would indeed behave normally and vrutti-s he would get in his post realization period is not so different from that of an ajnAni. For example, if the ajnAni sees sheep as sheep, jnAni too would see the sheep as sheep only since paramArtha jnAni’s sthiti ( position) is not vyavahAra abhAva sthiti. But his jnana would help him to sublate the mithyAdhishtAna of jagat. In short seeing the ring, bangles, bracelet differently does not bother him as he has the nishchita jnana that it is gold and nothing but gold.
