Dear Bhaskarji,

  

Permit me to clarify as to how the Jagat is Mithya and it vanishes to a Sthiatprajna. On being a Sthitaprajna, one realizes that Jagat is Mithya, in the sense that it is Anitya, and what vanishes to one is the earlier outlook of one as an individual with the Ahamkara, towards the Jagat. When the Ahamkara-shunya state is reached, albeit can be by a gradual transformation, one sees the world as the Brahman, more specifically as the Saguna Brahman, which includes one too, unconcerned about the outwardly changes in the world, as one is glued to the state, in which one remains unchanged, i.e., one does not react to the external changes in the world. The happenings in the world of Maya does not affect him at all. If you or other advaitin of this august group, see any lacuna in this clarification, kindly do not hesitate to point that out. I have not invited comments from the non-advaitins, if any, who are looking at the deliberations in this group, as this particular thread need not end in a dispute between the Avaitins and the Non-advaitins.

  

Jai Shri Krishna
