Dear Siureshji,

  

I fully agree with what you said. However, sometimes I do think, whether a particular writer is making an "atishayukti" as an alamkara, or is it coming from his unawareness. In the former case we may take an understanding stand, but it in the latter case we may make our stand clear as nicely as we can. Of course, i don't deny that in some very rare cases, some writers may have their own agenda(s) too. The world of communication is very strange, sometimes very fascinating and at other times very agonizing.
