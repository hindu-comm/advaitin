Namaste Dennis-ji,

  

Another important point as stated by Sri Davesx-ji, is that paramArtha and vyavahAra

form one single whole.

  

A jnAni's vyavahAra is "no vyavahAra" because the giver and takeror the triad of

enjoyer(subject)-enjoyed(object)-enabler(Ishwara) is Brahman only.

  

Again, there is catch here. The above is true only when the action is dhArmic or totally self-less.

where there no separate identification between the individual and the universe.

  

Suresh
