I must say, you keep misinterpreting Advaita all the time. Please try to understand first - what is mithyaa where all vyaavahaarika and praatibhaasika belong.

  

Mithyaa is defined clearly as Venkatraghavanji also pointed out - is sat asat vilakshanam.

  

Try to understand the sat and asat vilakshanam means.

For Vishishtaadvaita there is mithyaa.

  

Brahman being brahman (infiniteness) cannot exclude anything.

  

Dharma and adharma belong to vyavahaarika only which is not real also - part of the definition of mithyaa.

  

Paaramarthika transcends everything - including the space-time continuum.

  

Just as in the dream state one experience both dharma and adharma too - when one wakes up from the dream, the dream creations are transcended.

  

Goudapaada discusses elaborately the dream analogy in his kaarka.

The translatability, perceptibility, utility, etc., are not the criteria for reality - The only criteria is eternity which is essentially trikaala abhaditam.

  

Viswaruupam includes one side gods and the other side rakahasas - covering both dharma and adharma.

  

From Brahman reference, there is nothing other than Brahman - no sajaati, vijaati, swagata bhedas that includes dharma and adharma too.

  

Hence from the Advaita point, it includes everything at the same time it is untouched by anything. Hence - neha naanaasti kichana - Sarvam khalu idam brahma - are scriptural statements.

  

na vaak gachhati na manaH -

yato vaacho nivartante apraapya manasaa saH.

  

Dharma and adharma come with the active mind (that includes both local and global).

  

In the deep sleep state - what is dharma and what is adharma - that is the experience of everyone.

  

By the by, the Lord is there in the adharmic people too. However - he says he is untouched by the activities of the jeevas where He is residing - na chaaham teshu avasthitaH.

  

Advaita is self-consistent and follows the scriptures closely.

  

Please try to understand these.

  

Hari Om!
