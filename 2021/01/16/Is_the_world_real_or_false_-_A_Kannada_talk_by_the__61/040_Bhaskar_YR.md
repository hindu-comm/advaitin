Hare Krishna



For us, Sat is ekam eva adviteeaym as the scripture says and that is also the description of the tureeyam - advaitam chaturtam manyante sa aatmaa sa vijneyaH as the Mandukya defines - after describing the three states - waking, dream, and the deep-sleep state.



Ø If my observation is right, when it comes to the definition of Sat, there is no qualms here among participants no matter they might be from different school of thought. Problem comes only when interpreting the words like mithya, mAya, asat, sat asat vilakshaNam (anirvachaneeyataM) etc. The question is very simple and at the same time complicated when it comes to jnAni’s vision of jagat, whether he continues to see jagat as mithya like snake in place of rope (mithyA darshana) or would see jagat as nothing but Atman (samyak darshana or Atmaikatva drushti). Shankara & shruti at various places say jnAni’s drushti is samyak drushti / bhUma drushti and sees everything in him and in HIM everything. Elsewhere bhAshyakAra clarifies in the post realization jnAni would realize that srushti / sthiti / laya though time bound and asat it is through HIM only it is happening and HE is the material and efficient cause of it. And finally no need to mention here vedAnta maryAda is considering all these aspects and accepting the fact that Ishwara is srushti karta and jagat is NOT mithya like snake in the rope but it is gold in the ornament (kAryAkAraM kAraNa rUpameva satyaM) Hence bhAshyakAra says like kAraNa, kArya too ( in its vyaktAvyakta rUpaM) trikAla abhAdhitaM.
