Bhaskerji - PraNAms

  

Glad to know that you are writing a book on Vedanta.

  

Regarding mithyaa aspect of sRiShTi - as you know, Shree Madhusudhana Saraswati discusses this in his Advaita Siddhi providing five definitions of mithyaa. One can look up Shree Venkatraghavanji Advaita Siddhi series for more details.

  

As you know, the snake rope example comes under praatibhasika or subjective error and ring/gold example comes under vyaavaharika or objective error - just as the mirage waters. In Chandogya scriptures provides the vyaavahaarika examples of gold into ornaments, clay into mud pots, and iron into iron tools - as transactional reality since the theme is how 'eka vijaanena sarva vijnaanam bhavati' - knowing one thing how one can know everything - as Uddalaka poses to his son Swetaketu.

  

At the transactional level, knowing that it is all gold does not make us confuse with the utility values of rings vs bangles. They are, in scientific terminology, transformation-less transformations or vivarta.

What disappears with knowledge is only ignorance.

  

Ignorance can result in subjective errors. Once I know it is a rope, the snake vision is gone, whereas once I know that it is mirage waters, the mirage can still be seen but understood as part of the objective error or Iswara sRiShTi.

  

Similarly, there are two worlds - one is Iswara sRuShTi and the other is jeeva sRuShti that is projected on top of the Iswara sRiShTi based on one's likes and dislikes.

  

Vidyaranya discusses these in his Panchadasi - 4th Ch.

With the knowledge, the jeeva sRiShTi goes away but Iswara sRiShti remains as long as BMI is there. Hence a Jnaani sees the Iswara SRiShTi, the projected plurality with the understanding that it is not really real but only transactionally really. It is like knowing that there is no real water in the mirage waters. That is where samatva dRiShTi comes in - vidyaa vinaya samanne brahmane gavi hasthini.. sloka explains.

  

It is similar to knowing, as a scientist, that everything is nothing but electrons, protons, and neutrons, and thus fundamentally the same. That is the samatvam. Yet he does not fail to discriminate the garbage vs delicious food. In the same way, what is Dharma and what is Adharma on a transactional basis is not confused as some posts claim.

  

That is why every Hindu god is fully equipped with all missiles in one hand and a blessing hand with compassion on the other.

  

Hence Jnaani also plays in the world - looking it as Iswara vibhuti or from the understanding wise the vibhuti of the self.

  

Sarva bhutastam aatmaanam sarva bhuutani ca aatmani or yo maam pasyati sarvatra sarvan ca mayi pasyati - Interestingly both these slokas come one after the other in the 6th Ch.

  

Bhaskarji - I know you are aware of all these. Just putting things together for those who are interested.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
