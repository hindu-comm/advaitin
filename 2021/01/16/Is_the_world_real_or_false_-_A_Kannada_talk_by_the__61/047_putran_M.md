My view is that if acharyas and bhashyas use words such as illusion, appearance, vanish, etc., then the words cannot be regarded as tabooin the context of explaining the subject matter (like maya, jagat etc.). We have to figure out the intended meaning if it should not be taken literally. There can be debate about it in the process of ascertaining this meaning. However we do have to be careful about translations. For example, the word "unreal" may be used in a translation for either asat or mithya and the reader may find confusion with regard to the original.
