Namaste fellow Advaitins -



"*Things \[do not\] havean inherent independent orlasting natureseparatefrom Totality*".



While I have a few moments to spare, the statement by no means is intended to downplay the relativity of the world \[vyavahArika satya\]. In other words "Apparent objects \[forms\] are not separate from the nature of your own Self". So there are no others distinct or outside your original Subjectivity.



Your Self, this formlessness Infinity, also encompasses finite forms. Form doesn't magically evaporate into thin air upon realization of the unified Self , you can still share as in Satsangs, in e-groups like this or dance to the music of Hare Krishnas playing as if there are many. So that’s the Oneness, the encompassing intimacy.



And what is this Oneness about? It’s the gift of the nature of the Self to Itself. When one sees that all this is your own creativity, others seeming **remain but are inseparable from oneself. That’s the enjoyment of the Self through form, for sharing and celebration. The formless Self without attributes couldn't enjoy itself if there were no forms to jump into. That’s why the Self has magically conjured this amazing duality.



That’s the esoteric meaning behind the rasa-leela, the play of the Supreme that has projected this realm to entertain itself. He as Krishna disguised as the Gopis. And from the point of view of each of the Gopis, He seems to have become deluded to find Himself again; so He’s playing all the roles for fun, for the sheer entertainment of it. Through sorrow and longing of being apart and forgetting his Self, He eventually remembers Himself as the Subject, as the Star of the movie, as Krishna, but then He continues playing with the Gopis, knowing that they are all Him, not apart from Him and having fun -- playing the flute, playing the lover, dancing with them, giving teachings etc. knowing fully well that they don’t exist apart, but as forms of Himself.

  

The play doesn’t end as long as there’s body-mind. The sense of duality seems to remain but now it's integrated in the realization of non-duality, in the realization “this is all Me”.
