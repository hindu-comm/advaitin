Dear Daveji, 

When King Parikshit put the dead snake on the neck of the sage, the latter did not come to know about it, even though it really happened. Does this not show that if the sense is withdrawn one can’t sense the reality? That is how one knows or does not know depending on whether the mind is withdrawn or not registering any activity. Please think over this.

Jai Shri Krishna
