Namaskaram,

  

(In asking who is jnani and what does he see, how might a jnani, one in jnana-nishta, an ajnanirespond differently, and where am i? Some extreme scenarios to consider directly)  

  

Suppose a jnani'sbody is abused violently.

  

Suppose a jnani'sbody is abused 'perversely' (rape etc.).

  

Suppose a jnani is (say, made) to do the above to his own child or mother.

  

Suppose a jnani is to put himself in situations (physically, mentally) where above can likely happen.

  

Suppose a jnani is (asked) to shed his clothes right now and walk in the street naked. (or "dance naked amongst a thousand women")

  

Suppose (in principle, easier) a jnani is to give up at once his pant and shirt and wear the orthodox dresses and symbols to his corporate job. Or related things of thisnature.

  

Suppose (in principle, easier) a jnani is to run his thoughts and identifications in his mind, playing the roles, intensely through above scenarios.

  

Over and over again.

  

Instead of a jnani, suppose a scientist with conviction that all matter is energy has to go through above.

  

The consensus (of at least one group of advaitins) seems to be that the jnani (lagging in nishta) may cry, resist, fight (for dharma) etc. (before and) during but not so much after when he can restabilize in jnana (assuming BMI is not badly damaged during, in which case he may be fighting impact from vasanas later). If the jnani has higher levels of jnana-nishta (is sthitha-pragnya), then he is (relatively) unperturbed in all such experience for he stays disassociated from the body-mind-world activity. The nishta prevents the body-mind responses and confusions that arise in the ajnani; and he can freely go through any of the above without becoming subject to desire, fear and vasanas. The ajnanicries etc. before, during and after.
