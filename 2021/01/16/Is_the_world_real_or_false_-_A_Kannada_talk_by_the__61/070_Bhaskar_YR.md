We can have our individual opinion on this. But as a matter of fact, “original personal reflections” would not form the valid pramANa in the traditional Advaita. bhAshyakAra specifically insists this.
“individual experience” ( vaiyuktika anubhava) is not a valid pramANa, shAstra is the only pramANa and anubhava (experience) should be universal and not unique.
