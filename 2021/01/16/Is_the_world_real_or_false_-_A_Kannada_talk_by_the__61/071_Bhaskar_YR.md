Namaskaram,



(In asking who is jnani and what does he see, how might a jnani, one in jnana-nishta, an ajnanirespond differently, and where am i? Some extreme scenarios to consider directly)



Ø I always wonder is this an unpardonable sin if an ajnAni try to understand what would be the stature of the paramArtha jnAni ?? is this not arjuna did in geeta asking about jnAni (sthita prajna) how he sits, how he talks, move etc.?? There are plenty of references in shruti, smruti bhAshya about jnAni’s ‘socalled’ vyavahAra ( transactions) To whom it has been said?? Obviously for the mumukshu-s, would be jnAni-s or presently ajnAni-s is it not?? And if the person who we think and already crowned as jnAni, behaves abnormally and licentiously ( like the third one below : abusing violently and perversely with kids and women) don’t we patch up those misdeeds and attribute it to his leela and prArabdha just because we have already consider him as “ paramOtkrushta jnAni” ?? I do think so. It is just because we have already given him the certificate as jnAni. And again when that jnAni, engaged himself in questionable activities, we dare not question him and with all philosophical approach we submissively murmur : who are we to adjudicate the activities of the jnAni, it is all his leela, it is all his pastimes to test his disciples shradha and bhakti in him. All type of justification we pour-in to substantiate his behavior. But if someone else who is already in our bad books ( though he is having as much big circle of disciples and followers as our jnAni has !!) a small and negligible misconduct by him would force us to hasten our opinion on him. And we passionately argue : did not I tell you, he is fake and quack and now it is proven beyond doubt etc. 😊. See this is how we adjudge who is jnAni and who is imposter NOT based on scriptural dictums but purely on our individual / subjective opinion polished in prejudices.



- So, IMO, knowingly or unknowingly we the ajnAni-s already do have big
  list of who are jnAni-s and who are all frauds 😊



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!
