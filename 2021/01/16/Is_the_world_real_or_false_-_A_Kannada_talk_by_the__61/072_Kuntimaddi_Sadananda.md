PraNAms

  

There is never a time we do not experience Brahman or the Self that we are.

  

Experience is different from knowledge. Knowledge can contradict experience as in the experience of sunrise and sunset. Knowledge cannot be contradicted - only a brahmaa can be contracted by a pramaa (knowledge)

  

What is needed is the knowledge of that truth that what we are REALLY experiencing all the time.

  

The scriptures become a pramaana to direct our minds to see the underlying truth of all experiences. For the mind to look deeply within without getting carried away by the objectifiable entities that it does normally, it needs to be purified - and that is the purpose of Yoga for yoking the mind in the right direction as indicated by the scriptures.

  

For that only sharavana, manana and nidhishyaasana are essential.

  

Initially, the study of the scriptures is needed to gain knowledge.

  

Later the study of the scriptures helps to keep the mind in the goal.

  

Initially scriptures support the saadhak for his evolution.

  

Later the jnaanai supports the scriptures for loka kalyaanam.

  

Shraddha or faith is defined by Shankara - shaastrasya guruvaakyasya satya budhyaavadhaarana - Shaastra comes first with Guru's proper explanation of the shaastras.

  

I am reminded of simple Bhagovindam slokas -

  

Yogaratova bhogaratova sangharatova sangha viheenaH

yadat brahmani ramate chittam nandati nandati nadatyeva.

  

In essence, it is not what he does but where his mind is constantly reveling, in spite of his external activities or environment - is important.

  

Another sloka - ratyaa charpita virachita virachita kanthaH, punyaa punya vivarjita panthaH\|yogii yoga niyogita panthaH, ramate baalonmatthava deva.

  

At the same time, there is also

  

jatilo mundee lunchita keshaH

kaashaayambara bukRita veshaH\|

pasyannapi na pasyati muuDhaH

hRidaya nimittam bahukRita veshaH\|\|

  

A jnaani was a saadhaka before. In the purification process, he has developed how to control the mind. Hence whatever a jnaani does will always be for loka kalyanam.
