praNams Bhaskar-ji,

  

Prabhuji if you keep engaging me I may not be able to leave this forum and switch from the shadow self into the Jyoti Deha :-)

  

When the lamp is lit and the entire room is illumined, in which corner of the room does tamas, darkness, the shadow run to hide?  

  

The luminous One - Sri Krishna Prabhuji - after describing the three gunas in Chapter XIVfinally declares "transcend the Gunas in Verse 20. Meaning all these gunas are concepts, ideas i.e. shadows which are to be transcended. Therefore be beyond ideas Arjuna...  

  

In the final analysis the Gnani has no view or position of his own to hold. He does not even hold an Advaita position. He ceases to posit anything. As soon as you posit something, you posit duality and invite an argument from an opponent. If he says that the system of Vedanta is such and such, he takes a stand which can be opposed or contradicted by another view and therefore no longer non-dual.

  

This ends the unpopular, outrageous, radical Ajata Advaita.

  

Salud and Shanti
