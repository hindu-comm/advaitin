500TH JAYANTĪ CELEBRATIONS OF SRIMAD APPAYYA DIKSHITENDRA

  

With the blessings of Ubhaya Jagadgurus, Tattvāloka and Sringeri Gurubandhava are hosting a series of lectures by eminent scholars on the works of Srimad Appayya Dikshitendra. Dikshitendra was a doyen in multiple śāstras viz., vyākarana, pūrvamīmāmsa, dvaita, viśishthādvaita and advaita. A study of his commentary Vedantakalpataru Parimala is important for students of Advaita.

  

Mahāmahopadhyāya Śrī R. Krishnamurti Sastrigal will be delivering the first two pravachanams on the Ātmārpana Stuti in Tamil.

  

Dates: 23/1/2021 and 24/1/2021.

  

Venue: Tattvāloka Auditorium

  

Time: 6:00 p.m. IST

  

Chennaites are requested to make use of this golden opportunity to attend the pravachanam personally.

  

The event will also be live streamed on Sringeri Gurubandhava YouTube channel. A link to the talk will be sent closer to the date.
