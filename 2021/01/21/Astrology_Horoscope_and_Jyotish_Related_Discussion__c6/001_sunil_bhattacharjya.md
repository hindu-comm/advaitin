**Dear Ram Chandranji,**

**  **

**Thank you. You have done well by closing the thread "Astrology Horoscope and Jyotish Related Discussions", Mr. Kaul was trying to impress on us that we should give up the Nirayana (Sidereal) Calendar and go for the Sayana (Tropical) Calendar, but he is not aware that all the dates of Lord Ram, Lord Krishna and Lord Buddha as well as of theevents of the Mahabharata etc. can be arrived at by using the Sidereal calendar and not by the Tropical calendar. In one of my earlier mails I gave the names of some of the annual festivals, the dates of which can be determined only using the sidereal calendar.**  

**  **

**I hoped that Mr. Kaul will try to prove me wrong by giving the dates of those festivals, like the** ***(1) Lakshmi puja, (2) Saraswati Puja, (3) Jagaddhatri Puja, Durga Puja (both the (4) Saradiya and that (5) Vasanti Pujas) (6) Kali Puja, (7) Tulasi Puja, (8) Mahashiva Ratri, (9) Janmashtami, (10) Ram Navami, (11) Ayappa puja, (12) Kartika puja, (13) Ganesh Chaturthi puja,(14) Ganesh Jayanti Puja, (15 to 39) the 24 Ekadashis, (40) Buddha Purnima, (41) Datta Jayanti, (42) Shankarachaya jayanti) (43) Shri Ramakrishna jayanti, (44) Ratha Saptami (44) Mahavir Jayanti, (45) Hanuman jayanti, (46) Naga panchami, (47) Shitalaa Saptami, (48) Nariyali Purnima (49) Rakshaa Bandhan, (50 +) different purnimas and Amavashy, etc***, **by using his tropical calendar, but he could not.**  

  

**Jai Shri Krishna**
