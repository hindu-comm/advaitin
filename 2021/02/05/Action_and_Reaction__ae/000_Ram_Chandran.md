Action and Reaction

(Ram Chandran)

In Chapter 16 of Bhagavad Gita, Bhagwan Sri Krishna states that both the Devil and Divine reside in our minds. Our minds can be influenced by either one of them and it is possible for us to control their influence with determination, discipline and practice. We can keep the Devil buried inside and avoid influenced by it if we can avoid Kama (unwanted desires), Krodha (unnecessary anger) and Lobha (greed). How we live, treat others and what we read, write, speak and do depend on whether we are influenced by the Devil or by the Divine. If we allow the devil to sleep inside, the Divine get awakened and will guide our actions. When we fall victims of Kama, Krodha and Lobha we are certain to put the Divine into sleep and allow the Devil to act!  

Our understanding gets better with stories and we come from a heritage where Puranic stories were used to illustrate the morals and values.
Here is a story to make us understand what is devil! A donkey was tied to a tree and the Devil came and release the donkey to run. The donkey ran into the fields and started destroying the crop. The farmer's wife saw this and shot the donkey dead. The donkey's owner was upset so he shot the farmer's wife. The farmer came back to see his wife dead, he went and shot the donkey's owner. The wife of the owner of the donkey asked her sons to go and burn the house of the farmer. The boys went late evening and carried out their mother's orders happily, assuming that the farmer too would have been burnt with the house. Sadly for them it wasn't that, so the farmer came back and shot the wife and the two sons of the owner of the donkey. Remorseful, the farmer asked the Devil why did all this have to happen? The Devil said, "I did nothing, I only released the donkey, but, all of you reacted, overreacted and released the devil buried inside within you."

Next time before replying, responding, reporting, rebuking or avenging take some time to think before your response. Most of the times what the Devil does is that it just releases the donkey and the moral for all of us is to learn to act and stop to react!

It may be worthwhile for us to understand the distinction between Action versus Reaction. The purpose of any action is to bring happiness to all and on the other hand, a reaction has high potential to make us and all around us miserable. Whenever we act with the goal to bring happiness to all we perform an action based on conscious choice. But when you react, we follow somebody else's choice and we are responding to an external stimulus influenced by the Devil within! Reacting means quick action without any thoughts where as acting means that we take responsibility along with the determination not to allow the Devil to release the donkey

Note: Please do not react and if you do so, the Devil is ever ready to release the donkey!!
