Excellent mail Ram Chandranji,

You wrote:

***If we allow the devil to sleep inside, the Divine get awakened and will guide our actions. When we fall victims of Kama, Krodha and Lobha
we are certain to put the Divine into sleep and allow the Devil to act!***

Permit us to add something from the Bhagavad Gita itself. Everything is the Lord. If we think the Lord to be the epitome of goodness, he would be good to us and to others through us, because, as the Lord says we get the Lord as we see or understand him and follow Him in action. If we think Him to be Ghost, He will say "OK, so am I to you". People would find God as they want. All the incarnations of God are epitome of truth and they never endorsed any untruth. Lod Ram pulled up Jabali for telling an untruth. ***If one is a devotee of Lord Ram and wants the Lord Ram to endorse his lies, then the Lord Ram would have to be a liar to him or her too, in order to endorse his lies, but the Lord will not save him or her from the consequences of the lies.*** This logic may appear strange to you , but that's what it is.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna

Sunil KB
