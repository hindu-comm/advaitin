Namaste:

If we remove all the impurities of our mind, then the mind will reflect the True Divine all the time. But our problem is that our mind is polluted with negativities such as Kama, Krodha, and Lobha which will reflect the Devil instead of the Divine. Interestingly, sometimes we get the reflections of the Divine and at other times we get reflections of the Devil. Whether we are guided by the Divine or Devil, depends on how we cultivated our mind. This may explain why our parents and grandparents wants us to pray and develop good virtues and avoid Kama, Krodha and Lobha. If we strongly believe that by invoking the Divine we can keep positive mental attitude, we can slowly avoid the influence of Devil! Please understand that everything is symbolic and our goal is keep a Positive Mental Attitude and remove negative tendencies.  

Both the Gita chapter 16 and the story point out that we the human beings possess both positive and negative impulses. The positive impulses are parts of our divine nature. The negative impulses are released by the devil side of our nature. Whenever we exhibit Kama, Krodha and Lobha that is a reflection of the devil within. In the story, releasing the donkey by the devil refers to impulsive negative reactions. Judging others without knowing all facts, making unpleasant remarks on others, etc., are all can be avoided if we make the devil to sleep and awaken the Divine!!
