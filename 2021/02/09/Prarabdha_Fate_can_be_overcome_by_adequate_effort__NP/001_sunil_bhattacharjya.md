Dear Subbuji,

  

The following statement is a great positive approach to life.

  

***Prarabdha or fate cannot be more powerful than one's will because fateitself is a creation of one's  ***

***will.What one has brought about due to exercise of one's will in past births can certainly b overcome  ***

***by exercise of will power in the present birth provided one makes adequate effort.***

***  ***

Sometimes one may think that one is doing a right thing, but that may not be the right action, if an innocent person suffers because  

of that action. There is a story of a truthful saint, who saw a man, who was runnng by and that person told the saint that some persons  

are trying to kill him and that person requested the saint not to reveal the direction he was trying to escape. Later on some people arrived

and asked the saint if he had seen a running man, and the truthful saint revealed the truth by showing the direction the man escaped. The  

chasers could kill that man. Here the saint was wrong, as he had betrayed a person who sought his help to save his life, but the saint had  

failed him. The saint will have to suffer for his action, and the merit of speaking the truth may not save him.

  

Any comment?
