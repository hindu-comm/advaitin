Did you mean this verse 2.4.17 from yoga vAsiShTha about our present exercise of free will having the capacity to prevail over the prArabdha which is created by our past exercise of that very free will?

प्राक्तनोऽद्यतनेनाशु पुरुषार्थेन जीयते ॥ १७ ॥

*prāktanaṃ caihikaṃ ceti dvividhaṃ viddhi pauruṣam*\|  
*prāktano'dyatanenāśu puruṣārthena jīyate*\|\| 17 \|\|

> Know our actions to be of two kinds namely, those of former and
> present lives: and that acts of the present life generally supersede
> those of the past.
