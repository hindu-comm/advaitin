Dear Subbu-ji,



I don’t want to get drawn into discussing free-will as it is one of the areas in which I tend not to adhere to traditional teaching!



I do, of course, accept that the seeker ‘chooses’ to follow sAdhana chatuShTaya sampatti in order to purify the mind for effective shravaNa. But the traditional teaching about prArabdha follows such metaphors as ‘arrow loosed from the bow’. The body-mind of the j~nAnI continues until prArabdha is used up, at which time the body drops. I have addressed some of these aspects in Part 2 of my series on pratibandha-s - [https://www.advaita-vision.org/pratibandha-s-part-2-of-6/](https://www.advaita-vision.org/pratibandha-s-part-2-of-6/). I have not come across any traditional sources that speak of a j~nAnI deciding to terminate prArabdha voluntarily here and now. How would one stop the speeding arrow? There are quite a few references to this and it is also supported by Vidyaranya.



In any case, why would it matter? Self-knowledge entails the realization that I am not this body-mind; I am Brahman
