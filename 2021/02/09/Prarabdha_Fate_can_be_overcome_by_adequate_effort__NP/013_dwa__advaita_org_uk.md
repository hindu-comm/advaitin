Dear Sunil-ji,



I am not interested in apocryphal or subjective views, only objective comparisons. Once cannot deny, for example, the statements on manonAsha and samAdhi and the fact that they differ from Shankara. Either it IS the case that only Self-knowledge can give enlightenment, or it is not. Shankara is emphatic that it IS the case. It is not possible that both Shankara and YV can be the ‘epitome’ of Advaita.
