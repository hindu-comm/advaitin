Namaskar:

The quote by [Abhinava Vidyatirtha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abhinava_Vidyatirtha)Mahaswami of Sringeri Mutt is a conditional statement with appropriate caveats to make it appealing:  

“Prarabdha or fate cannot be more powerful than one's will because fate itself is a creation of one's

will. What one has brought about due to exercise of one's will in past births can certainly be overcome

by exercise of will power in the present birth ***provided one makes adequate effort.”***

In economics perfect competition will ***provided Stable Price provided we have perfect information!***

In stock markets, there will be no buyers or sellers ***provided both buyers and sellers know how the future prices of all stocks!***

It is possible for us to know the outcome of our action ***provided we and all actors of that action make adequate efforts to get that outcome!***

In all the above statements there is underlined ‘uncertainty’ is indicated and from Vedanta points of view that only the ***Brahman knows everything implying that He alone exists with no uncertainty!***

If we can use the logical reasoning behind Swamiji’s assertion, we will be able to conclude that this statement doesn’t require any support from Sruti!

As Jivas, ***our fundamental problem is not knowing the amount of that adequate effort!!***
