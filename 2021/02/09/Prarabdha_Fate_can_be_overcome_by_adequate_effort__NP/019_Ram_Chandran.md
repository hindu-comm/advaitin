Namaste Subbuji:

Here is another profound statement from Franklin D Roosevelt during his first inaguration speech. He stated: "So, first of all, let me assert my firm belief that the only thing we have to fear is...fear itself." One of the obstructions in our life progress is 'fear' and we can get rid of this, we will be able to make adequate efforts wherever they are needed! Gita also provides the clue for success - Action is always better than inaction!! The Serenity prayer adds an extension - "To accept the things I cannot change; Courage to change the things I can; And wisdom to know the difference. Forever and ever in the next." This explains that "adequate efforts" needs a clearer understanding of what we should do and what not to do!
