I appreciate everyone’s commitment to supporting the quotation but there is no escaping the clear teaching of Adi Shankara. The only part that action of any sort plays in bringing about enlightenment is in the mental preparation prior to shravaNa. It is knowledge alone that can eliminate; nothing else. You can find the teaching on karma-j~nAna samuchchaya in his brahmasUtra and taittirIya bhAShya-s.

e.g. *“Only knowledge can liberate. To talk of the synthesis of action and knowledge is senseless… Liberation is not a product, nor a modification, nor is it an improvement or refinement, nor something to be attained (Brahmasutra bhAShya 1.1.4).* **
