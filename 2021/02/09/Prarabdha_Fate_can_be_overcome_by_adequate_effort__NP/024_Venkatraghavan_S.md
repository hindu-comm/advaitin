Namaste Dennis ji,

I suspect you are conflating two different issues here. The original statement by the AchArya that started the thread is referring to the capacity of actions in the present to overcome the vagaries of fate brought about by the actions of the past. This is not in contradiction with the Shruti and the bhAShya. In fact, the vaiShamya nairghRNya adhikaraNa of the brahmasUtra assigns agency to the individual (not Ishvara) for the type of circumstances faced in the current birth.

  

Your comments below are referring to the self sufficiency of knowledge to remove samsAra, which is a result of ignorance. The AchArya is certainly not seeking to repudiate that and his statement must be seen in the context it was made. For instance, there is no evidence from the quote to infer that he considers samsAra to be a result of past actions, requiring actions in the present to remove it.
