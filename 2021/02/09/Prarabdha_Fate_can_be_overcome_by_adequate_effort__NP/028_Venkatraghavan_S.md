Dear Dennis ji,

  

I did not say that a jnAni cannot overcome prArabdha - I meant he does not need to / nor does he choose to.

  

In fact the quote from Apastambha dharma sUtra below proves the point made by the Sringeri AchAryas -

  

"Defects therefore arise (even in the case of a man liberated in life) through action performed (in previous births) for the sake of results, and they must be **extirpated with efforts proportionate to their gravity**. "  

There are two ways one can overcome the results of past karma - either by experiencing the fruit of the karma, or as the quote says, with counteracting efforts proportionate to the gravity of the original karma. This is pretty much what the original quote by the AchArya said.

  

If prArabdha can't be overcome and one must necessarily experience the results, then why is there the reference to **efforts** proportionate to their gravity? Also, the shAstra prescribing prAyashchitta karma / expiatory rituals would be leading people down the garden path.

  

Anyway, as I surmise your mind is made up about this one, will call it a day from my side.

  

Kind regards

Venkatraghavan
