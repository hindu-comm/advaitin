I just want to add to what Venkatraghavan wrote.

  

There are two contributions to praaradba - one from one's action and the other from those local jeevas that interact strongly with the particular jeeva or samashTi vaasanaas. It is similar to Chaotic systems in science - being non-linear, the contribution from the other sources also affect the outcome - an extreme example given in Choas-Science is that the fluttering of a butterfly in China can also have some effect on the weather in New York.

  

For example - where I was supposed to be born is not only determined by my vaasanas but also the vaasanas of my parents and to some extent those of my siblings, etc. Similarly, Jnaani's BMI is maintained not only by his praarabda but also by those who depend on him for their growth.

  

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
