Dennisji is one of very few trusted ambassadors of Traditional Shankara’s advaita philosophy. Also his major contributions in the form of books are well appreciated by all. He also serves as one of the Senior Moderators of this List. He believes in freedom of expressions and a man of great wisdom with humility. He has no quarrels with the quotation from Sringeri Sanckaracharya and he has every right to agree or disagree with other contentions which were put forward by several members originated from Yoga Sastra.

Quite a few times during list discussions on Advaita Vedanta topics, members ended up mixing the Vyavaharika and Paramarthika realties.
During the discussions, many times members do get into intellectual arguments instead of focusing on the spiritual aspects of our life.

Here is a list of few argument stoppers that we can all employ on a daily basis when facing potential conflicts during our discussions:

1\. You may be probably right.

2\. What you have said is certainly one way of looking at it.

3\. I am more than happy to take your point into consideration.

4\. I want to take little more time and I do plan to get back to you.

5\. Thanks for sharing your thoughts and I respect what you have said.

6\. Let’s postpone and talk about this when both of us are calm.

7\. I am able to see the subtlety of your thoughts.

8\. I have come to the conclusion that arguing just isn’t worth it.

9\. Let’s respect each other’s position and agree to disagree. 10. Our opinions may differ but we can gain more by listening.

11\. There is some validity to what you are saying but we need more information to make a decision.
