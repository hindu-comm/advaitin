Dear Ram Chandranji,

Arguments apart there are other remedies too. Knowledge learned and imbibed is like fire and this should burn the karmaphal, when one is interested only in leaving the cycle of birth and death, and not interested even in good karmaphal. At the time of death, whatever is the prevailing thought, that decides the future.

Depending on intensity of the detachment mukti can be sooner or later.

My two cents
