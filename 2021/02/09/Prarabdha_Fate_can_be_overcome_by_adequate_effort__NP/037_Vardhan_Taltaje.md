Dear Vedantins,  
  
There is shloka in Hitopadesha that states "पूर्वजन्मकृतं कर्म तद् दैवमिति कथ्यते । तस्मात् पुरुषकारेण यत्नं कुर्यादतन्द्रितः". Of course, Hitopadesha is not a vedantic text, but shows that the concept of पुरुषार्थ or free-will being used to overcome undesirable prArabdha karma is a well known idea.  
  
Vardhan

> I don’t want to get drawn into discussing free-will as it is one of
> the areas in which I tend not to adhere to traditional teaching!
>
> 
>
> I do, of course, accept that the seeker ‘chooses’ to follow sAdhana
> chatuShTaya sampatti in order to purify the mind for effective
> shravaNa. But the traditional teaching about prArabdha follows such
> metaphors as ‘arrow loosed from the bow’. The body-mind of the j~nAnI
> continues until prArabdha is used up, at which time the body drops. I
> have addressed some of these aspects in Part 2 of my series on
> pratibandha-s -
> [https://www.advaita-vision.org/pratibandha-s-part-2-of-6/](https://www.advaita-vision.org/pratibandha-s-part-2-of-6/).
> I have not come across any traditional sources that speak of a j~nAnI
> deciding to terminate prArabdha voluntarily here and now. How would
> one stop the speeding arrow? There are quite a few references to this
> and it is also supported by Vidyaranya.
>
> 
>
> In any case, why would it matter? Self-knowledge entails the
> realization that I am not this body-mind; I am Brahman
>
> 
>
> Best wishes,

> Dennis
