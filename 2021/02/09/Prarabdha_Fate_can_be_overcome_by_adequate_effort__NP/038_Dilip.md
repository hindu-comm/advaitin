Namaskar to all,

  

Once when the subject of free will arose in the presence of Sri Ramakrishna, he remarked that the questioner must have been reading a lot English books.

  

For him, the relevant question was Freedom- and not free will. The concept of free will is an import from the West. It’s somewhat like a
catch 22 situation. For a will to be free, the person who wills must be free, but if he is truly free he has no free will. Whatever will appears to the others is the Universal will. Since such an enlightened person is not a person, he is free from a person, there is no question of a personal free will.

  

Thus the debate is more because of the nature of the language rather than because of the substance.

  

Humbly,

  

Dilip.
