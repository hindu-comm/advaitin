Namaskar all,

  

I have a serious question. Serious for me. In this forum the references are almost always of the shastras of the yore. I have seen very few references to the lives of the enlightened persons in the modern period ( say those in the 19th, 20th, 21st centuries). This bias runs deep and there seems to be an assumption that only those awakened souls of the yore who wrote the scriptures were perfect. That there is little to learn from these saints of the modern period.

  

I wonder if there is a bias working subliminally that there is no worth while contribution/ corroboration to the shastras by the enlightened persons of the modern period.

  

Humbly
