Dear Dilip-ji,



There is an excellent book by Anantanand Rambachan, called “The Limits of Scripture: Vivekananda’s Reinterpretation of the Vedas” if you can find it anywhere. This ‘perspective’ is certainly not mine. It is why Vivekananda’s teaching is called ‘neo-Vedanta’ because of its deviation from traditional.



Nisargadatta’s ‘I am That’ is a very powerful book and had quite an effect on me when I read it probably 30 years ago. The later talks, translated by Jean Dunn are somewhat less intelligible (although I understand her translations are more accurate than Frydman’s).



But just compare the ‘teaching value’, in terms of consistency, logic and progression, of ‘I am That’ with something like Swami Dayananda’s 2-volume ‘Mundaka Upanishad’ commentary. You will easily see the difference!



Best wishes,

Dennis
