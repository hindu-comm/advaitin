Dear Shri Dilip-ji,

  

Agree that Swami Sarvapriyananda’s lectures where attendees are from throughout the world, with his q&a sessions being deep have made Vedanta a lot more widespread, understandable, repetitions of points being welcome.  The comments section and number of hits reflects his lectures’ reach.

  

My journey also started by reading Vivekanada’s works 30 years ago, and don’t quite understand the neo or otherwise labels attached. His works enabled me to enter Shankarcharya’s works which is a never ending struggle. And Swami Sarvapriyananda’s lectures makes it easier to absorb Shankarcharya’s Advaita too. Ramakrishna/Vivekananda Vedanta Society’s Swamis, including Respectable Swami Paramarthananda, Swami Sarvapriyanada, and many others have no conflict with Swami Vivekanada’s works at least in their audio teachings.

  

Perhaps scholars who have gone word-by-word combing of Vivekanada’s works have found deviations from Advaita, but there’s differences everywhere including in interpretations of Shankarcharya’s works, based on some of the discussions here amongst highly learned members.

  

Dhanyvaad,

Sanjivendra
