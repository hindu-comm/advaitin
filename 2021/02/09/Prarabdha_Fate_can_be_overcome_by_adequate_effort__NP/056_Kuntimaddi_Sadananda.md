The topic 'Freewill vs Destiny' has been discussed exhaustively in the books - Journey Beyond: A Non-Dual Approach' the Shree Ram Chandran referred to in the earlier mail.

  

I was searching for that series on my computer - but could not find it since I have this new computer and have not loaded all the previous files on to this, to save space.

  

Here is the essence - What I have is destiny and what I with what I have is my free-will - The future destiny is past destiny modified by the present action' - the essence of Swami Chinmayanandaji's Statement.

  

Also ' I have free-will until I am free from will' - that is when I realize that all actions are done by prakruti only - prakrutyaiva ca karmaani kriyamaanaani sarvashaH, yaH pastyati tat aatmaanam akartaaham sa pasyati,

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
