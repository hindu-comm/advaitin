Namaskaram,

  

One well-known example in our puranas is Markandeya. He was "pre-destined" to die at age 16, but because of his karma, tapas and bhakti, Ishvara changed the course of his destiny.  

  

In general however, we do not know details of our prarabdha until we experiencethem; so the acharya's words can be understood as saying that the effects of prarabdha upon us can be overcome/mitigated/channelized through proper exercise of our will-power.

  

The body gets a disease unexpectedly. This could be a necessary result of prarabdha.

  

However, we can exercise, eat right, take medicine, etc., all of which will alleviate/cure the impact of the disease on our well-being. Not taking medicine etc. can result in aggravation and stress. The karma-decision of the jiva is basis for Ishvara'sfuture manifestation.
