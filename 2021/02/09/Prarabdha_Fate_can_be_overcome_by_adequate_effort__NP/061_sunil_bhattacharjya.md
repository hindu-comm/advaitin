Dear Ram Chandranji,

  

Sorry, my last mail was posted not to contest any of the dicussons on the free will.

  

Free will does exist, but anything done arbitrarily may also attract unpleasant consequences. Vedavyasa said that the Lord could have prevented all that happened in Dwarka, but he did not. The meaning is that the consequences of our action will surely follow. May be instead of producing new theses we can look at the views of our past stalwarts. Many of the past accounts are probably lost due to our not taking adequate care to circulate those. Recently I came to know that Swami Vidyashankara Teertha, the Mathadhipati of the Kudali Shringeri math and Shri Madhvachrya had a bid intellectual dual in Kanyakumari, but no details of that is available now. I tried to look for an Enlish edition of the Madhva-Vijya, but could not get any.

  

Regards,
