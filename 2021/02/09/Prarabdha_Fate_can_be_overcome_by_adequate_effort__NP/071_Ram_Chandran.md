Namaste:

The thoughts and messages of Ramana Maharishi focus more on how to get out of the plurality of the world that we live. All dualities and pluralities will disappear only if we recognize the reason for such pluralities. It is understandable and there can be no dispute regarding the negation of fate or free-will since there is no place for any notion! The problem is that all jivas need to cope up with the world of plurality and they have to undergo this process of transformation. Even Ramana Maharishi who was a student in Madurai (that happened to be my birth place where I lived over 20 years of my young life). From the point of view of Ramana, everything was ordained by the Brahman but all others did not necessarily recognize as an act of God. We all are in the same situation fully influenced by Maya and everything that we do to get out of this Maya will be only known to us when we realize the Truth!! It should be understood that even the discourse on fate and free-will is also within the spell of Maya. All notions, theory, logical reasoning and discussion in this group by all members also fall within Maya. Does this mean that we are wasting our time and if so then that is also because they are all ordained by the Brahman.

Warm regards,

Ram Chandran

Note: Everybody knows that Pure Advaita can never be disputed but pure advaita will not provide us any direction or clue on how to understand IT!!
