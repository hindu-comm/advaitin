Namaste:

Here is an interesting story of an old man whose home was seriously threatened by rapidly rising flood waters. The old man, despite desperate entreaties by his friends, refused to leave his home. He trusted absolutely that his God would save him. He continued to sit on the porch watching the rising flood waters. His neighbor in a car came offered him a ride to a safe ground. The old man once more declined and said that God would rescue him safe. The flood rose higher on the street and a friend came in a boat and requested him to get on the boat quickly. The old man waved his hand indicating not coming. The flood rose higher and higher and the entire home filled with water. The old man was forced to climb over the roof to look for God to come and help. At that time, a rescue helicopter came to take him away. But the old man was stubborn and refused to get on.

Finally the flood swallowed the home and the old man was also drowned. When the old man reached the heaven, he rushed straight to God and complained that God failed to save him. God with great compassion told him that I made three different separate attempts to save you. First I sent the vehicle to pick you, then I sent a boat and finally I dispatched the helicopter. But you firmly declined my help and I was just helpless! 

Yes indeed that God has a plan for all including the old man. It is our (also the old man) responsibility to recognize the signals and be prepare and ready both for the expected and unexpected helps! Surely, God can come in mysterious ways to come and help provided we become aware of the arrival of God in many forms!
