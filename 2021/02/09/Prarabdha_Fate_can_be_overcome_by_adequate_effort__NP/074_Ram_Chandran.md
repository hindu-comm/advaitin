Namaste:

Most of direct discussions on “fate and free-will” are more common now.
In Hindu scriptures, the topics are discussed indirectly (on subtle terminology) or using terminologies which do not necessarily a reference to fate and free-will. For example, the terms Prarabhda and Purushartha captures the essence of the roles played Jivas in various circumstances. It illustrates in clearer terms who has more authority to do what and what can be changed and what can’t be changed and who controls what? Our understanding is that fate and free-will both are integral part of human life and they were never considered as two options for conducting any action! Also interestingly we don’t need to invoke God because God is always present and always plays his role! I want to restate the Gita chapter 4 verse 7 here which provides when God wants to come and act:

*Yadaa yadaa hi dharmasya glaanir bhavati bhaarata;*  
*Abhyutthaanam adharmasya tadaatmaanam srijaamyaham.*

Meaning: Whenever there is a decline of righteousness, O Arjuna, and rise of unrighteousness, then I manifest Myself!

Here Lord Krishna confirms that he doesn’t need any 911 calls from us and He decides when to come in what form. The story of the old man is illustration of what God could do and what he couldn’t! Here are more examples of God’s presence and actions:

1. When the husband wants to drink alcohol, and the bottle was thrown out by his wife – Here God came in the form of his wife!

2. The smoker went to the doctor with coughing and the doctor tells him to stop smoking – Here God came in the form of his doctor

It is possible to give hundred more examples from sports, politics, science, etc.
