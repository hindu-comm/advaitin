Hari Om

  

Here area few points that may bring about a little more clarity on the subject of free-will and destiny. and also conforming the Validity of the quote shared by V.Subramaniam ji.

  

1.  Jiva identity is conferred with two aspects a) karthrutva or
    doer-ship and bhogrutva or enjoyer-ship.
2.  It is in the doer-ship one maintains the notion of free-will .
3.  Prarabdha or destiny affects the enjoyer-ship and it has no effect
    on the current state of doer-ship.
4.  Since these two \[doer & enjoyer\] are tied together in the same
    I-notion (ahamkara) then there is an intellectual conclusion that
    they are connected. The relative strength of these two\[doer &
    enjoyer\] aspects decides whether one believes in free-will or
    succumbs to destiny as all powerful.

In a Jnani neither of these two aspect of the I-notion exist. Prarabdha in the form of external factors would still reach a Jnani - but there is no free-will that manifests since there is no karta bhava. There is no enjoyer-ship (experiencer notion) too. So Prarabda plays out without causing any internal effects. In fact even in the preparatory Karma Yoga one has dedicated his Karta bhava to become only an agent of the Lord and remains unfazed by results which are accepted as Lord's grace.

  

The closest analogy is the dream analogy. An ignorant dreams his dreams considering them as real while the Jnani sees them as mere thoughts with no substance.

  

Om and with Prem

In his Seva

Shri Rammohan
