I was not getting into these discussions.

  

What you have quoted is about the state of mind of a tatva vit - a jnaani who has relegalized that aham brahmaasmi - for him this discussion of freewill and destiny are of no importance too.

  

Hence was my statement - I have freewill until I am free from 'will'. that is when I understand that I am - akartaaham and abhoktaaham - neither I am a doer nor an enjoyer.

  

He sees - karmani akarma yaH pasyate - saH buddhiman.

  

The karmanyeva adhikaaraste sloka applies not to the jnaani but ajnaani. - for him - and the sloka -

na hi kaschit kshaNam api jaatu tuShTasya karam kRit - where one cannot remain even a moment without acting also applies.

  

What I have is destiny, and what I do with I have is my free will
-applies until I recognize or realize that I am akarthaa and abhokaa that is when I am understand CLEARLY that I am pure sat chit ananda swaruupa and hence cannot act even since infinite that I am cannot act. Hence this discussion is also meaningless from that reference.  

  

As long as we do not get confused from what reference theses topics are discussed, there should not be a problem.

  

I agree with your comments that what is needed is knowledge and samaadhi is not a means of knowledge or a pramaana, but one attains samatvam in the dhee when one understands that I am brahman - vidyaa vinaya sampanne brahmane gavihastinee - sloka applies for sama dRiShTi.

  

Just my 2c.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
