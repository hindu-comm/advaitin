Dear Sada,



You said: “Hence was my statement - I have freewill until I am free from 'will'.”



The way that I would put it is: “I THINK that I have free-will until I realize that I do not have free-will.”



The way that you are putting it is that someone who still has Self-ignorance actually has free will whereas a j~nAnI doesn’t. This does not seem to make any sense.



I appreciate that an aj~nAnI does think he has free-will and that this is clearly an aspect of the teaching of Advaita. And I clearly indicate this when I write about it. All I am saying is that I personally have never found this to be helpful. It seems far more reasonable to me that any decision I make is a consequence of prior reading/teaching/discussion/etc. and that, if a quantum computer could be programmed to access all of my prior experience, it would accurately predict that decision. Simple cause and effect.



Best wishes,

na hi kaschit kshaNam api jaatu tuShTasya karam kRit - where one cannot remain even a moment without acting also applies.



What I have is destiny, and what I do with I have is my free will
