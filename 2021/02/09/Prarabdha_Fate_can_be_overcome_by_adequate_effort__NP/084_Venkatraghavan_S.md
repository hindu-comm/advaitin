Dennis ji,

Re "The way that you are putting it is that someone who still has Self-ignorance actually has free will whereas a j~nAnI doesn’t. This does not seem to make any sense."

The right way to state it is that both jnAni and ajnAni have free will from a vyavahArika standpoint. Neither do from a pAramArthika standpoint.

  

If free will is not accepted from a vyAvahArika standpoint, the entire sanAtana dharma system of karma, phala, karma yoga, chittashuddhi, AtmajnAna (which are all vyAvahArika btw) would be negated.

  

That an experiencer experiences a result is because he was the doer before. If he did not perform an action with free will, there would be no justification for results to accrue to him, for he would be no different to animals. In sanAtana dharma. it is held that animals don't have any karma phala accruing during their life as animals - precisely because they have no free will in their actions.

  

Regards

Venkatraghavan
