>
> If free will is not accepted from a vyAvahArika standpoint, the entire
> sanAtana dharma system of karma, phala, karma yoga, chittashuddhi,
> AtmajnAna (which are all vyAvahArika btw) would be negated.
>
> 
>
> That an experiencer experiences a result is because he was the doer
> before. If he did not perform an action with free will, there would be
> no justification for results to accrue to him, for he would be no
> different to animals. In sanAtana dharma. it is held that animals
> don't have any karma phala accruing during their life as animals -
> precisely because they have no free will in their actions.
