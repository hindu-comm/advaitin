Dear Ram Chandranji,

  

Some simplistic but effective teachings of the Sanatana dharma may be meant for the ordinary folks like you and me and may not be for the intellectual giants among us and elsewhere. The sage Vedavyasa says ***"The four 'G's*** can help us in the maturing of our spiritual knowledge and thus lead us to beyond the cycle of the birth and death (which is also obviously the end of the prarabdha karma as well),  

  

The sage Vedavyasa starts with the ***greatness of the teachings of the*** ***Sarvashastramayee Original Bhagavad Gita (the first 'G'***)***, which has all the 620 verses of Govinda***,  

  

The next is ***Ganga (the second "G")***, implying bath in the holy Ganga (or in water after doing Ganga-Avahana), and with a **sankalpa (or resolve)**, to wash off the past wrong-doings, done wilfully or otherwise, and never to repeat the wrong-doings, and thereafter to get engaged in doing **Lokasangraha** ( Work for the Welfare of the world), as advised by the Bhagavad Gita.

  

The third is the silent recitation / manana of the ***Gayatri (the third "G"*)**, obviously including the last portion also, which is generally given to the dikshitas / sanyshis / vairaaagis).  

  

The Fourth is **Govinda (the fourth 'G')**, and Adi Shankara had given us the **Bhaja Govindam**, and we have to surrender to our **Supreme Self, the Paripurna Brahman, Shri Govinda.  **

**  **

Kindly do not hesitate to send your valuable comments on this.  

  

My 2 cents

Regards

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
