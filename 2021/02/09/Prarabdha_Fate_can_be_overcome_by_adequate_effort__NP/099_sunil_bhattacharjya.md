Dear Ramanji,

  

Permit me to say that I may appear to be a bit partial to the Original Bhagavad Gita. The following verse is from the Mahabharata :

**गीता गङ्गा च गायत्री गोविन्देति हृदि स्थिते ।**

**चतुर्गकार संयुक्ते पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ॥**  

With quite a bit of hard work and mainly due to the blessings of Lord Krishna, I could publish the Original Bhagavad Gita of 745 verses, in the year 2014. The newer edition should appear soon.  

But I am very sad that nobody in India, except one ancient-most Vishnu-temple in Kerala, observes the Gita-Jayanti on the proper day. The Bhagavad Gita was told by Lord Krishna to Arjuna on the Gita Prabodhini Ekadashi or the Kartika Shukla Ekadashi day.  

Numerous scholars have been trying to determine the date of the Mahabharata war, over a long period of time,  but have not been successful. Now I started work on the dating of the Mahabharata and hope to bring out the book with the right-date soon.  

Best  

skb
