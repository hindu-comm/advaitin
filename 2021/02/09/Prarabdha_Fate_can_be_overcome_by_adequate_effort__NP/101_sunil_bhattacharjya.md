Sorry Ramanji,

  

The Original Bhgavad Gita is in the hard form only, and it is hard-bound and with the best of printing-papers, so that books will last long. The price has been kept unexpectedly low (Rs. 350/- in India) and the publishers have not paid a single penny to me for the book. I will bring out the next edition of the book soon, and will include in it, the Bhagavad-Gita-Saaara" as told to Arjuna by Lord Krishna himself.  

  

Unfortunately Adi Shankara was not allowed by his guru(s) to write a commentary on the Original Bhagavad Gita, and as a consequence the fact that Gaudapadacharya had taken the excerpt of the Ajatavada verse
from the Original Bhagavad Gita onto his Mandukya,karika, remained unknown to the world.

  

Jai Shri Krishna
