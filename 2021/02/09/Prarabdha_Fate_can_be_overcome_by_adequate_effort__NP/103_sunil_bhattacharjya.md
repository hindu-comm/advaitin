Dear Ram Chandranji,

  

There is only a total of 745 verses (***no more no less***) in the Original Bhagavad Gita, out of which there are ***620 verses spoken by Lord Krishna***, and that alone is the true Original Bhagavad Gita, according to the Mahabharata.  

  

I am not forcing anybody to read the Original Bhagavad Gita. I thought it my duty to bring out the Original Bhagavad Gita and I did, as a contribution from my side. Lord Krishna said at the end of the Original Bhagavad Gita that we should not force anybody, who does not have respect for the Original Bhagavad Gita, to read it. The Mahabharata says that the Original Bhagavad Gita is Sarva Shastramayee, and with its help one can even cross the Cycle of Birth and Death, and I personally believe in it.  

  

Secondly, as regards the Bhagavad Gita Saara, that is not part of the 745 verses. If anybody is claiming the Bhagavad Gita Sara to be part of the 745 verses, he or she is plainly bluffing.

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
