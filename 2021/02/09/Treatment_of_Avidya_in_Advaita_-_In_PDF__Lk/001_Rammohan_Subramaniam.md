Hari Om Subbu ji

  

Your Blog was very well researched. I have two observations the response to which may bring about more clarity on the subject

1\. Bhava and Abhava are to be understood only with reference to the Satta. There cannot be absolute Abhava at all (except in Shunyavada) \[ since Brahman is Existence.\] If that is true then Maya is Bhavarupa only in Vyaharika satta and Abhava rupa in parmarthika satta.{i think you have indicated this in your Blog\] So could one then conclude without referring to satta, that it is Bhavarupa only by sruthi references? \[My own opinion:*I believe that Sruthi stands as the bridge between the two satta and hence all sruthi Vakhya have to be understood and interpreted only in reference to the context.\]*

*  *

2, "The Knowledge of the True Nature of the Self, Brahman (avidya absence)" and " The experiencing of freedom from the doer-enjoyer identification (adhyasa absence)" are not two things - if we postulate them as two things then 'which leads which' dosha will arise.

  

I believe that both will happen simultaneously. If Snake vision is destroyed rope is self-revealed and if rope is perceived through the snake vison then snake is not there \[for all purpose\] . The first part is Jagat Mithya and second is Atma sathyam. Snake and Rope are not two things - Seeing the Snake different from the Rope is Avidhya

  

I will treasure your inputs in response

  

Om and with Prem

Shri Rammohan
