PraNAms

  

Here is my understanding.

  

Desire comes from the Vaasanaas or impressions of the past will-full actions.

  

Human being endowed with intellect has choice to act on the desire - to do, not to do and to do another way- kartum shakyam, akartum shakyam and anyathaa karthum shakyam - are the choices he has. Not to do is also a choice of action and one has to be accountable for that if not do is a will-full action. For example, not paying taxes on time and one becomes responsible for that inaction.

  

For a realized person, the desire for action also comes from samashTi vaasanas or one can gracefully say - it is Iswara Iccha - or Lord's wish. The desires are for loka kalyaanam or for the benefit of the totality.

  

In essence, as long as intellect or buddhi is functioning, action to overcome desires cannot be avoided. A jnaani understand that both desires and the resulting actions are at the BMI level while he still remains as akarthaa. While for ajnaani even though the same thing applies he takes the role of doer and therefore an enjoyer and the resulting vaasanas etc follow.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
