Namaste,

  

Free will is not the same as will. Will is simply the decision-making function of the mind. Free will suggests an agent that could have actively chosen to do a different action than the one it in fact did.

  

I'm not sure what pertinence your questionshave to the question of free will. A desire for something isof course preciselyan urge to do things in furtherance of that desire, and the meaning of a lack of desire in terms of complacency or contentment depends on the context of the mind withinwhich it occurs. No simple generalizations can be made.
