"resembles Sankhya" does not mean equivalence.

  

Using the Mandukya terminology, Shiva represents Turiya and Shakti represents Viswa-Taijasa-Prajna. Sankhya deems Reality as if Turiya ANDV-T-P. Advaita says Turiya is the Reality that appears or is identifiedin the mithya vyavaharika-standpoint as V-T-P.
