Another fact from astrophysics which strongly indicates that intelligence of some type is immanent in the universe is nevertheless glossed over as an inconvenient truth by scientists.

  

This is the astonishing fact that the universe started off in a very "intelligently put together" condition and then on unraveled/devolved gradually. Mathematically, this is expressed by saying that the initial state of the universe was an extraordinarily low entropy state.

  

"The classic example is a dropped cup: it will smash into pieces, but those pieces will never spontaneously recombine back into a cup. Analogously, a hot cup of coffee will always cool down if left - it will never draw warmth from a room to heat back up."  

  

As an anecdote , if we were to , lets say clean our home, dust it, order all the items scattered on the ground and place them neatly ordered back in shelves etc. That neat well-ordered condition is mathematically called equivalent to a very low-entropy state. (Entropy = disorder, and Low Entropy = Intelligently well-ordered). If we leave the room unattended for a long time, the entropy (disorder) goes on increasing, dust accumulates, insects might proliferate etc. From order to disorder is the flow of time and a powerful law of thermodynamics.

  

If we use this analogy, the universe in the beginning was like a cup kept on a table. Then it falls down and breaks. Thats the universe fragmenting in to galaxies, planets and human beings!

  

So its a given that this universe began in a very well-ordered condition. Then from there the orderliness gradually started decaying but not before imparting that orderliness to other entities like luminous suns, and complex life forms. (As one may expect, life forms are low entropy entities compared to non-living blobs of matter of similar mass and energy. )

  

It is therefore an eminently defensible position that traditional statements like "so'kAmayata, bahusyAm prajAyeyeti" indicating an intelligent first cause , an immanent cause (Ishvara's abhinna nimitta upAdana kAraNatvam) cannot be dismissed as being in conflict with science. (a.k.a 19th century scientific materialist claims of a matter just randomly ordering itself in to complex structures.)
