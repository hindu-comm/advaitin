Here is my understanding.

  

In the dream, there is predominantly enjoyer ship. Doing is done instinctively just as the animals do in the waking state. The intellect, Buddhi is at its low level, and hence the actions are dictated at the mind level due to pressure of the vaasanaas - which get exhausted in the dream without getting new vaasanaas.

  

Animals do not accumulate new vaasanas but only exhaust the existing vaasanas in those forms.

Human being is endowed with intellect hence he has choice in action and doer ship notion as well within the vyavahaara reference.

  

Yes, one can have enjoyer ship without the doer-ship. In the past there was a doer-ship that resulted in the vaasanas that can only be exhausted now in the dream state.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
