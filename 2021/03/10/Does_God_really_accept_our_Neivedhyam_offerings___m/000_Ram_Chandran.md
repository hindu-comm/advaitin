Namaskar:

One of the Sishyas who doesn't believe in God, asks his Guru: Does God really accept our Neivedhyam (offerings)? If God eats the Prasadham then how come we are able to distribute the prepared Neivedhyam in the form of Prasadam to others? Does God really consume the 'Prasadham,' Guruji? If God consumed the Neivedhyam then how come we are left with the same amount without any reductions?

The Guru did not provide any immediate answer; instead, asked all the disciples to prepare for the next day class. That day, the Guru was teaching his class about the Upanishads. He wanted to teach them the Invocation Mantra ofIshavasya Upanishad.

**Om Poornamadah Poornamidam Poornaat Poornamudachyate**

**Poornasya Poornamaadaaya Poornamevaavashishṣyate**

**Om Shaantih Shaantih Shaantih**

(That is the totality, this is the totality. Everything comes from the totality and what remains is still the totality. Totality is always complete and will never remain incomplete.)

He requested the students to memorize this invocation Mantra so that they can recite it without looking at the book. Next day at the beginning of the class, theGuru asked that very student who had raised his doubt about Neivedyam to recite the Mantra without looking at the book, which he did it very enthusiastically.Now the Guru gave a big smile and asked him the question: “Did you really memorize everything as it is in the book? The Shishya said: "Yes Guruji, I've recited whatever is written as in the book. The Guru asked him: "If you have taken every word into your mind from the book then how come the words are still there in the book?” The Shishya was quite stunned and requested Guruji to explain why this is so?Guruji with great compassion and kindness then explained that the words in our mind are in the Sookshma Sthiti (unseen form). The words in the book are there in the Sthula Sthiti (seen). Now he uttered the punch line thatGod too is in the Sookshma Sthiti.The offering made to Him was in the Sthula Sthiti.God takes the Neivedhyam in Sookshmam in Sookshma Stithi. Hence the Neivedhyam in the Stula Sthiti doesn't become any less in quantity just like the words in the book!While GOD takes the Prasadam in the Sookshma Sthiti. we take it in Sthula Sthiti. After hearing this the Sishya felt ashamed for his disbelief in God due to ignorance and surrendered himself to his GURU with a Sastanga Namaskaram.

With my warm regards,

Ram Chandran
