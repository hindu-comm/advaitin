Dear Friends,

  

The statement appears in the book " 108 Facets of the Inimitable

Guru" :

"Sri Abhinava Vidyatheertha Mahaswamiji was firmly established in Brahman".

Who is not established in Brahman?

Every one, Every thing is established in Brahman

because he is brahman only and nothing else.  

Mantra 2 Of Mandukya upanishad declares :

sarvagam hyEtadbrahma ayamAtmA brahma \|\|

  

MAntras 7-25-1 and 7-25-2 of Chandogya Upanishad state :

ahamEvedagaM sarvam \|\|

AtmaivEdagM sarvam \|\|

  

Mantra 7-26-1 Of Chandogya Upanishad declares :

Atmata EvEdagM sarvam \|\|

  

What do the above mantras teach?

  

Mantra 2-1-3 of Kathopanishad, mantra 2-4 of Kena Upanishad

and mantra 2-1-4 of Kathopanishad reveal how every one is

Atman / Brahman Here and Now and is not an entity .  

Why not every one of us heed to the advice of Sruti

and cognize our true nature Viz, Atman/ Brahman by

looking within ourselves?  

  

With respectful pranams,

Sreenivasa Murthy.
