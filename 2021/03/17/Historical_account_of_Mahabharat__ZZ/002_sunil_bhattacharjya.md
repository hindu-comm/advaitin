Namaste Sadaji

Several stalwarts in the past , such as prof Raghavan and some of his eminent followers, tried their best to debunk the 3102 BCE date of the start of the Kali Yuga, but failed miserably. Mahabharata clearly gave the details of when Bhishma died, and the great Mahabharata scholar Nilakantha, who was originally from Kerala, clearly found that out, but sadly no so-called scholars of today have the ability to see the facts. Today’s scholars don’t love truth and that’s why the truth eludes them.  
The same thing is happening in case of dating of Adi Shankara. One Phatak gave the incomplete details of one Shankara born in Chidambaram, as that of Adi Shankara to the colonial historian, and that fitted well with the cooked up AIT-timeline, and that appears to be the truth to all and that is how the 8th century AD date of Adi Shankara has come to stay. Finally the truth has been demolished and all seem to be happy.

Jai Shri Krishna
