Dear Sri Bhattacharjya-ji,

  

According to the Vedantic theory of perception, the human observer does not see the sun when the light from the sun reaches the eye; he sees it instantly even though the light from the sun may not have reached the earth because it is the indriya, the subtle sense eye, which instantly reaches out to the sun and forms a vritti at the location of the sun.
