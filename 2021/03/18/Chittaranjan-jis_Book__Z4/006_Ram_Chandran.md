Namaskar:

We all agree that perception is most of the time different from the Truth. Human visual perception is basically an opinion based on what one sees. Any need not necessarily takes account of all factors that account for the object visualized. Human perception varies between individuals because each one applies factors of one’s preference.
Consequently the information processed is never complete and hence labelled as opinion or perception. The sight of an early morning sun ignored the distance (space), the speed of the light to travel the distance and also time. When the time, speed of light and distance are fully accounted, the truth what was seen by the eyes did not represent the current state of Sun! 

The question raised explains how little we know about perception and why the subject matter requires details through a book!
