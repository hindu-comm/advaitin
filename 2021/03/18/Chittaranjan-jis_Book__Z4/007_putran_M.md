Namaskaram Sunil-ji,

  

One way to explain this is to use the dream analogy. When you see the sun in the dream, do you see the sun as it was projected earlier? The character in the dream due to its identifications with the dream body-mind-world may imagine the seeing in terms of the dream-events; but the true seer is the one Dreamer. Whatever is seen by the Seer is the here and now as projected by the Seer. So it is the Seeing of the Self that manifests as events of body-mind-world and not viseversa; so if we look at how the body reacts to external stimuli, brain acts after that, etc. and finally land up in "seeing happens", we are doing things in reverse order: rather the Self sees/manifests itself as the Sun and manifests as the light and body-mind reactions.

  

Not claiming I fully understand; but this is how I try to fathom how Chitta-ji is presenting the Vedantic theory in his book.
