Namaskar:

Most of us know the Parable of the**blind men and an elephant** a story from India which nicely illustrates how different individuals perceive what they experience. Each blind man conceptualize what the elephant is on the basis of touching the different body parts of the elephant. They then describe the elephant on the basis what part of the body that they touched. Each one was suspecting that the other person is dishonest.
The moral of this story is that we human beings have a tendency to claim that the absolute truth is ONLY based on their limited knowledge using their limited experience. Perceptions based on limited experience by individuals will likely differ from the truth.
