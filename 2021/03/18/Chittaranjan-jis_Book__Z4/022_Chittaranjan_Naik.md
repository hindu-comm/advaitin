Namaste Sri Chidambaran-ji,

  

"According to contemporary philosophy (see Thomas Kuhn and Feyerabend, for example) theory-ladenness is unavoidable and there is no such thing as absolute truth regarding any matter; the theory that has a better explanatory force is what prevails. Contemporary philosophy is constrained to speak in this manner because it does not have a**culture of pramanas.** I believe that it is for us who have a commitment to the Indian tradition to strive to establish the culture of pramanas once again in the contemporary world. This is how I look at it."

*  *

If the results of the Simultaneity Experiment show that we perceive the flare before the camera (or measuring instrument) detects the light from the flare, then the impasse will be broken because the scientific theory will be left without any explanatory force to explain the phenomenon. And then, the story of the stars which we perceiving having died out, etc., will have to be re-written. Indeed, much of physics will have to be re-written. That is the reason why I had written the following words in the introduction chapter of my book:

  

"**Direct Realism, if true, would not be just another theory that finds its place in the libraries of the academia; it will be a precursor to a major change in our *weltanschauung*, the way we will look at the universe and our place in it in the years to come."**
