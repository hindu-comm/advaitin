The unnerving part for us Vedantins would be the contention that if Direct Realism is not true, then Vedanta itself is undermined if not (fundamentaltenets) shown to be false. People here may not realize this strong claim. I understand that you are not merely suggesting the Vedantic theory of perception as an alternate vyavaharika world view ("just another creation theory") that would be consistent with Vedanta (the parts for which shabda and not pratyaksha is pramana), but one that can just as well be done without. Instead you are proposing the possibility that shabda can be tested through this experiment in pratyaksha. I can understand that the scientific theory can be disproven through an experiment, but I find it unsettling and suspect that Vedanta can likewise be disproven through experiment.

  

Is this what you are implying and if so, how can pratyaksha-experiment be the basis for affirmation or negation of truths for which we hold only shabda (and/or self-realization) is pramana?
