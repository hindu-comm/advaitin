Namaskaram Chitta-ji,

  

Let me know if I am dragging into details we have likely discussed before and are already in the book. But this would be a common question:

  

The jiva does not know/see all that Ishvara knows/sees. So even if the world is like a dream, for the jiva only a part of that is available for its knowledge. So those not in favour of committingto direct realism can say that the Seer (as jiva) sees the image presently reflected (projected) in its upadhis and not the original sun as projected/seen by Ishvara alone. There is no contradiction if the Seer sees the time-lagging reflected image in the jiva body-mind even while the real Sun is "dreamt" separately and available in 'Ishvara'scognition' - and we know the vyavaharika satya of this Sun not through pratyaksha but through shabda, on which basis we can treat pratyaksha as pramana for the same Sun. (?)
