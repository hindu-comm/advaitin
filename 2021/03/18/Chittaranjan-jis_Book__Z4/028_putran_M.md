> and we know the vyavaharika satya of this Sun not through pratyaksha
> but through shabda, on which basis we can treat pratyaksha as pramana
> for the same Sun. (?)  
>
>   

  

or shabda affirms pratyaksha as pramana, not because DR is true but as an affirmation that the reflected sun is 'reflection' of a separate real Sun in Ishvara'sconsciousness; hence based on shabda, we can regard pratyaksha as pramana for that Sun even if DR is false. Of course, this may not conform with VP; but can otherwise stand on the sruthi pramana. (?)
