Saw the other reply: that is just our usual picture based on science. Idea is that just as a camera records the "second sun" as reflected/created "later" in its film, so also the jiva sees the "sun" as reflected/created 'later' in its jiva BMI apparatus. We require shabda to know that pratyaksha of the reflected sun is indeed providing knowledge for the separate sun in Ishvara's consciousness - in that sense pratyaksha is held to be pramana (whereas science does not base itself in shabda pramana and hence its theories don't have logical coherence).
