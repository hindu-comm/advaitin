Namaste Sri Dennis-ji,

  

In the case of the bell within a jar, the walls of the jar obstruct the sense organ from reaching out to the bell. So, we don't hear the bell. But there are three objects which are making sound here: the bell inside the jar, the air inside the jar and the walls of the jar. So, even though the walls of the jar are an obstruction to hearing the sound of the bell or the sound of the vibrating air inside the jar, the sound made by the walls of the jar will be heard.

  

When the air within the jar is removed, there is only one object that is producing sound, i.e., the bell. But the walls of the jar become an obstruction for the sense organ to reach the object (bell). Hence no sound is heard.
