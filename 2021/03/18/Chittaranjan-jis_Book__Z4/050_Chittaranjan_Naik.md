*The bell just can't stay within the Jar without touching the sides of the jar. Thus, some vibration of the bell will be transmitted to the Jar and thereby some sound may ger emitted from the jar, even if it can be of a lower frequency than expected.*  

  

Yes, you are right but this will be true for both contact theory of perception and scientific theory of perception. The way I remember the experiment was conducted in school, the bell was kept on a dampening surface inside the jar (like a block of sponge) and the air was removed gradually from the jar. As the air was removed, the volume of the sound kept reducing until it became almost inaudible. The same result will be obtained irrespective of which theory is true, so the result can't be used to demonstrate that either this theory or that theory is true.
