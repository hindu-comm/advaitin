Namaskaram Chitta-ji,

  

What about the fact that if we are inside the house and hear a sound from far away, we hear it "outside" and not as if generated on the walls of the house? Part of the experiential basis that supports the claim that the mind forms the vritti from direct contact at the location of the source is that we experience the depth and distance of the source when we hear it and not as if it is immediately present near our location. In the case of a bird singing outside the house, I hear it where it sings and not at the walls or in the air within.

  

How do you explain this phenomenon if as you say the ear-sense organ cannot go beyond the wall obstruction?
