Dear Sri Putran-ji,

  

  

*What about the fact that if we are inside the house and hear a sound from far away, we hear it "outside" and not as if generated on the walls of the house?*  

  

This case is not similar to that of the bell in a sealed jar because the house is not sealed but will have doors and windows through which the sound will be heard. That is, the sound propagating through the air will enter the house through the doors and windows at which point in time the sense organ will envelop the sounding air and the sound will be heard.

  

  

*Part of the experiential basis that supports the claim that the mind forms the vritti from direct contact at the location of the source is that we experience the depth and distance of the source when we hear it and not as if it is immediately present near our location. In the case of a bird singing outside the house, I hear it where it sings and not at the walls or in the air within.*  

  

Since there are multiple points at which the sound will be heard (openings of doors, windows, etc), the net effect will be that the sound will be located where the mind forms the vritti. If the vritti formed by the mind does not coincide with the location of the object, then there will be an error of judgment with regard to the exact location of the sound. If the bird is singing right outside the window where there is no obstruction to the sense organ from reaching out to it, then the location of the sound will be heard quite accurately.
