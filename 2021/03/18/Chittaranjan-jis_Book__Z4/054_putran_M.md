Namaskaram Chitta-ji,

  

It will not be difficultto set up the experiment so that a person is in a "jar" that is completely sealed (no gaps for air to pass in or out). So the subtle ear has no way of going beyond the limits of the jar. Then we can generate the sound at a distance and the mind perceives the distance in the location and not as if the sound is generated at the walls of the jar.

  

Now we can say that the sound at the distance gets dispersed through the air and only a certain "portion" gets to the jar. Then when our ear senses the partial version of the sound at the wall-source, the mind locates on that basis the original(or more proximate) location, makes contact with the source and creates its vritti there.

  

Or that when the ear makes contact with the wall and the mind makes its vritti there as well, because what is heard is a partial version of the original source, the vritti formed at the wall imitates the actual vritti of the full version at the source location if there were no obstruction. We cannot tell a big difference between the imitation at the wall vs the real sound at the source.

  

Does either of these versions support what you are saying correctly'?
