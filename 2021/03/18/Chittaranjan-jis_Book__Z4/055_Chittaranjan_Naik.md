Dear Sri Putran-ji,

  

When the person is sealed in a jar, i think the cognition of any sound that is created outside the jar will be, first, a perception of the sound in the wall followed by, second, an inference which cognizes an object outside as the source of the sound. Since the sense organ as well as the mind envelopes the sound in the wall (at the instant the wall vibrates with the sound), i think we can say that the hearing of the sound in the wall constitutes a perception. The knowledge that the sound has its source in something outside will then be due to an inference. Need to contemplate more on this.
