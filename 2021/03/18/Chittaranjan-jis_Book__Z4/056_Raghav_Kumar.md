Namaste Putran ji

  

I was thinking along the same lines viz., the object's pre-existence by Ishvara shakti is allowed in vedanta.

  

  

The vedanta paribhAShA also would support what you wrote, as I understand. The viShaya is already present and is an upadhi of chaitanyam. It is termed viShayAvacchinna chaitanyam (consciousness conditioned by the object). This is Ishvara sRShTi.

  

And then when the pratyaxa cognition actually happens (which can well be with time delays which can be indriya dharma - the chaxu indriya peering through some instrument develops the vRtti of a solar flare 8 minutes after its occurrence on the Sun), then the person's mind extends to envelop the viShaya (whose information is presented to his eyes ) and thus extends takes on the form of the object. This vRttyavacchinna-chaitanyam (consciousness conditioned by the mental vRtti) becomes one with the viShyayAvacchinna-chaitanyam (consciousness conditioned by the object) during cognition.

  

Om

Raghav
