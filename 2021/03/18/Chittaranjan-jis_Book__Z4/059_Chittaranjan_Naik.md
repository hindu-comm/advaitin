Namaste Sri Raghav-ji,

  

  

*And then when the pratyaxa cognition actually happens (which can well be with time delays which can be indriya dharma - the chaxu indriya peering through some instrument develops the vRtti of a solar flare 8 minutes after its occurrence on the Sun),*  

  

Let us apply the same logic to the pratyaksha cognition of a star that is 1000 light years away. We would then have to say that the chakshu indriya develops the vritti of the star after a lapse of a 1000 years.

  

  

*This vRttyavacchinna-chaitanyam (consciousness conditioned by the mental vRtti) becomes one with the viShyayAvacchinna-chaitanyam (consciousness conditioned by the object) during cognition.*

  

Now consider the case of that star which is 1000 light years away. Also, consider the case in which the star has exploded and become non-existent 500 years ago. Now, when the star is being perceived in the present time, how can the consciousness conditioned by the mental vritti be one with the consciousness conditioned by the object **when the object is absent**?
