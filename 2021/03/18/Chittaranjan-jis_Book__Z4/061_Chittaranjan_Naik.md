Namaste Sri Raghav-ji,,

  

  

*which can well be with time delays which can be indriya dharma*  

  

There is no scriptural basis to say that it is in the nature of indriya dharma to form a vritti after a time lapse. The form of the vritti is the form of the object taken by the indriya when it envelops the object and as such the object must be present for it to be enveloped. If the vritti should be formed after a time lapse, then there will no assurance that the vritti that is formed at time t will actually reflect the form of the object as it existed at an earlier time t1; and when there is no such assurance of them being the same, one cannot speak of the oneness of the consciousness condition by the mental vritti and consciousness conditioned by the object. In other words, there can never be a veridical perception. It is therefore not justifiable to assume that it is in indriya dharma to form a vritti after a time delay.
