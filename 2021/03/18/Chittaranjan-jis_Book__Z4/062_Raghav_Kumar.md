Namaste Chitta ji

At the outset, I appreciate your taking the time to respond to so many posts.

  

Regarding the vRtti not embodying veridical perception etc., the important point would be what do we mean by the word object/vishaya. It can refer to two things.

  

1\. Whatever is presented to the indriya golaka.

2\. The actual object/event like sound etc.

  

If we look at an object kept in wavy water, what is experienced by pratyaxa is a wavy object. The other examples of a distant star which has ceased to exist but is still seen by the naked idea as the crab nebula or the gravitational lens example etc., can be reconciled by asserting that the word viShaya refers to whatever is available for experience at the indriya golaka after possible modification by the mAdhyamam (intervening medium).

  

Veridical perception will still occur when there are no karana doShas or no mAdhyama-dharmas such as the refractive index of the intervening medium. Another example, if the medium of air is smoky, that will give a blurred perception. If we thus agree as a matter of principle that the intervening medium does indeed matter and it can influence the vRtti formation, then there is no reason to disallow possible delays in vRtti formation as well (as in the dying star example).

  

I would venture that 1. is a good candidate for the word viShaya. Given that the role and importance of the indriya golakas is acknowledged in mediating the arising of the vRtti.

  

Also the role of the bhautika indriya golakas is in receiving sthUla inputs at the point of reception and facilitating a sUxma vRtti arising. This interplay between sthUla and sUxma is accepted in the tradition. That would not amount to representationalism. It would be more like the idea that "every pratyaxa qualia of experience has a sthUla bhautika correlation". (As for example anne prANaH pratiShThitaH, prANe annam pratiShThitam - taittiriya ). Each kosha mirrors/shows changes corresponding/correlating with changes in other koshas but that still does not imply either representationalism or reductionism. The indriya golaka at the annamaya level triggers or mediates (transduces to) a vRtti involving projecting outwards to create extension in to space etc.

  

Also I was unable to follow why peering at an object in a telescope is epistemologically different from seeing that same object with the naked eyes. What would be the situation for those who wear glasses? Its interesting to contemplate that while all those lucky blokes without specs directly see the world, I am only seeing a pathetic
representation of it , never veridically perceiving it? :)

  

I may have missed some of these posts addressing such questions. If so, a pointer to that earlier post would be sufficient.
