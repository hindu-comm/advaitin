Namaste Putran ji🙏

  

As far as I know, when we talk of direct knowledge we are only referring to the first person experience of consciousness. I know that I am and that I exist and that whatever I am experiencing, I am only experiencing consciousness and that if I negate the phenomenal world, then I am only experiencing direct consciousness. The part about perception thru the senses is direct only in the phenomenal world and not in the noumenal. I know I am consciousness, but someone has to point it out to me. It is this pointing which is indirect knowledge. In other words, I might tell you that you are conscious, but that will never be a direct experience for me. Only I can experience my consciousness by myself, which would be direct. All other forms of knowledge of consciousness would be indirect. But to reach the direct realisation, either one has to be a Ramana Maharshi, or someone has to show the way, which would always be indirect as far as realisation is concerned.

  

This is my understanding, Sir.

  

Best regards
