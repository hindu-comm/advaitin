Here is oneway I think on this.

  

The Self is all-pervading. All knowledge is already in the Self. The process of "knowing" is the removal of Ignorance that brings to direct awareness the self-knowledge of Self. In the phenomenal dimension (vyavaharika), perception is the means for removal of layers of ignorance that reveals to the Self (as jiva) its manifest projection as Ishvara. Whatever is known is direct since the contact is always there : it is One manifesting as if many; only we remove the ignorance via the mode of pratyaksha.

  

In the case of Self-knowledge also, the direct knowledge is ever present; only hidden/obscured by ajnana. The "indirect" process to attaining this "direct knowledge" is with regard to the removal of ignorance; the Light of self-knowledge shines in awareness thereafter.
