Namaskaram Raghav-ji,

  

Yes those are good questions, some are addressed in Chitta-ji's explanations in his book (and I gave my way of resolving certain dichotomies in the dream-analogy mail) but we should explore them more specifically later.

  

First step for me is to ban the fear of the topic itself (and of failure) and to be able to speak on it from a position of inner shraddha. Perhaps we take up the idea initially (or establish) that the essential questions regarding pramanatvam of pratyaksha are indeed beyond the realm of science to affirm or negate and there must be some sort of reliance in shabda pramana; then we have an accepted methodology of building tarka arguments that are meant to support shabda rather than stand on their own. We need not feel guilty about it. Setting the attitude right for our investigation, we can approach the problems head on with an air of confidence. If we do end up failing to explain, so be it; but generally people are scared by science beforehand and submit meekly to a compromise.

  

When you read the book, do also look at that annexure 1 on Roemer's experiment carefully. I recall I had some trouble or concern there. I will also try to re-read that part and look back at conversations with Chitta-ji to see what that was about.
