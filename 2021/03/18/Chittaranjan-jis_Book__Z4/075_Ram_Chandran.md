Namaste:

**Vedanta fundamentally says the same message of this article with Vedantic flavor. Vedantic analysis of negation, ‘Neti neti’ implies,** "Not this, not this." This key step of starting a Vedic inquiry, the seeker negates identification with all things of this world which is not the Atman or Brahman or true reality or the Truth. When the seeker completes this enquiry and negates the Anatman (all that is not Brahman), he becomes a Jnani! All notions are just mind observations through the sense organs and one has to go beyond senses to realize the Truth!!
