Dear Putranji,

  

Yes, This is Ekatma-vada, call it Shankara's Advaitavada or Budhha's Advayavada. Either one learns it the hard way by oneself by passing through the different births, as Lord Buddha learnt it when he could see his past births and was awakened, or through the grace of a Sadguru followed by wn sadhana. Lord Krishna also told Arjuna that he knows his past births but not Arjuna. In the former case it came without outside assistance This shows that we are basically Ekama-vadi, and we have only to discover ourselves.  

  

My 2 cents.
