Namaste 🙏

  

I have an observation in this connection. When the temporary "I" gains the knowledge that he is a product of the mithya chidabhasa, he may have three aspects to consider:

  

either to consider the knowledge also as mithya which leads to the negation of the mind, the ahamkara and the "I", hence realising that the original pure consciousness is the only reality devoid of any self/I.

  

The second aspect is to treat the reflected consciousness as pure consciousness since the latter in essence is pure consciousness only.

  

The third aspect would be to negate the mithya mind but not to negate the self since I am experiencing all senses thru the consciousness and that is direct experience. In the last option however, I would also know that the I is there only as long as the jiva is alive and that after his death only pure consciousness remains.

  

In any case all three will lead to liberation.
