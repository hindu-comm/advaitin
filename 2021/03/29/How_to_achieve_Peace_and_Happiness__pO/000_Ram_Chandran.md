Namaskar:

  

Here is a suggestion for enjoying peace and happiness.This message was attributed to Swami Vivekananda. Once a sage was walking along the soccer field. A soccer ball came to him and said - ‘please tell me how can I prevent me from kicking by everyone! I want to be free. ‘ The sage replied telling the ball that remove all the ‘air’ then no one will like to kick you! The punch line is that only those with ‘ego’ will be kicked around! If we let go our ego, we will be free!  

  

Warm regards,
