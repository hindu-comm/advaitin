Namaste,

Ego is a projection of self esteem, self pride. A mind with a low self esteem or one that is perceived by others to be of little or no value is the one that gets kicked around. On the contrary, an egoist mind tries to kick others around and in turn when the self esteem gets low gets kicked around.

A soccer ball’s prarabda is to get kicked. Its dharma is to respond to the kick in a dharmic way without prejudice, bias or preference. By not doing so, its air gets freed and is ‘dumped’ and made to feel worthless.



Pranams

Raman
