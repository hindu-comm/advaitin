Namaste,



Most traditional text use the analogy of mud or gold to describe the form and names that are created from a ‘base’ form and to show that ultimately they go back to the ‘base’ form which remains unchanged.

I have not seen any traditional text that use wood as an analogy to describe something that is only name and form, decomposing which leads us to nothing (which is what I derived from the analogy explained).

This wood analogy perhaps attempts at showing nothing is real and keeps changing, but does not provide any insight to what it ultimately is.
