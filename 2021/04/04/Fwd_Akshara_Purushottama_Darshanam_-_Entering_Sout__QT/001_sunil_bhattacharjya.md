Thank you for the good mail. Only thing that surprises me is that nowhere there was any mention of the original name of the Swaminarayan Pantha. It was originally known as the Udhhava panha, after the same great Krisna-bhakta Uddhava, to whom Lord Krishna gave the upadesha of the Uddhava Gita, which ws included in the Shrimad Bhagavatam by the sage Vedavyasa.  

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
