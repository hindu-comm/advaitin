Today our group member Brahmachari sri Bharat Shastry of Tiruvannamalai received Sanyasa Diksha from Pujyasri Omkarananda Mahaswamiji Theni. The yogapattam is Sri Brahmathmananda Saraswathi Swamijइ.

  

He was known as Bharat Sastry, from a Kannada Brahmin family in Bangalore. He had his education here and worked with some MNC-s and left all that a few years ago and joined an Ashram at Tiruvannamalai. Deeply into spiritual studies and practices, he has now taken sannyasa. A very pleasing person. He takes Vedanta classes in English.h.
