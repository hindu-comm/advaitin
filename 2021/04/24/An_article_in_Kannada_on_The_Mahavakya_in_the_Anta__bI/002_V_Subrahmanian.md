Namaste Sri Suresh ji,

  

Yes, this mantra of the Mandukya Upanishad is admitted as in Vyavaharika satya by Advaitins:

  

एष सर्वेश्वर एष सर्वज्ञ एषोऽन्तर्याम्येष योनिः सर्वस्य प्रभवाप्ययौ हि भूतानाम् ॥ ६ ॥

  

Here Ishwara, Saguna Brahman, is stated to be sarvajna, sarveshvara, antaryami and the jagat kaaraNam. Only the next mantra 7 is a negation of this and the earlier states/entities/world, as per Advaita.
