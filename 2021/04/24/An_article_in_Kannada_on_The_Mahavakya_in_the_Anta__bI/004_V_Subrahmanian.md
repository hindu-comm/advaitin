Dear Suresh ji,

  

Your doubt is not misplaced. The 'Antaryami' there has been clarified by Shankara as 'that Sakshi-Consciousness, by the mere presence of which all the organs of the jiva become activated.' So, the svarupa of Antaryami is Shuddha chaitanya and that is the svarupa of the jiva too.


  

> This leads to "Ishwaroham Aham bhOgi" which is considered as Asuri
> jnAna right?  

  

This is incorrect and this is how Madhvacharya (mis)understood Advaita and projected it this way in his Tattvodyota and Mahabharata Tatparya Nirnaya. 

> 
>
>   
>
> So is it correct to say that there is bheda between jiva and
> Ishwara/AntaryAmi in vyavahAra and the realswaroopa of both is
> Nirguna NirAkAra Brahman?

  

Yes, this is correct.
