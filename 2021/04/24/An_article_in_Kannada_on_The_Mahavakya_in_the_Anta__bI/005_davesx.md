Namaste - Were is the confusion when its such a beauty and wonder to live in both dimensions simultaneously; call it Dvaita or Advaita, Paramartha or Vyavaharika, Nirguna or Saguna.... they are inseparable, indivisible.

  

The apparent world is already established in Brahman and once understood as a momentary immaculate expression not divorced from but dependent on Brahman - the Totality - it is not an unreal appearance to be devalued, refuted or denied like in the illusionist schools of Mayavadins.

  

Being stuck in any one of these dimensions IS the confusion leading to pathological suffering.

  

Various Acharyas have tried to cherry pick a particular view from the Vedas and Shastras creating the great chasm and unending quarrel between Advaita and Dvaita. If one reads the Upanishads collectively there are flavors of both.

  

Shanti
