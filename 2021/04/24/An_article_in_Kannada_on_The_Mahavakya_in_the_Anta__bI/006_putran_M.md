Namaskaram Dave-ji,

  

Our forum is committed toShankacharya'sexplication of the Vedas and allied shastras. His "view" - as contained in his bhashyas, prakarana granthas etc. - is the correct one here; this is not an academic forum but of advaitins who deem the acharya as their adi-guru. We seek to understand the import and ramifications of the knowledge of shastras as taught by this acharya. Members may have differing understandings of Shankaracharya's teachings; and we may discuss on that, with mutual respect rather than name-calling Mayavadins etc. But there cannot be a casual slighting or ambivalence about his stature as our Guru. (You may not be doing this either but your language appears to have that sense.)
