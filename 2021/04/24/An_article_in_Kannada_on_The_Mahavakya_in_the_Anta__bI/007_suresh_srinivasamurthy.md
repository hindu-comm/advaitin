Namaste Sri Subbu-ji,

  

Thanks very much for the clarification.I may be wrong but here is my understanding:

  

Sri ShankarAcharya in the adhyAsa bhAshya makes it very clear that all loka-vyavahAra  

is a mixture of truth and untruth. So based on this it can be said all AchAryas are not

entirely wrong!

  

But, whatever may be the difference, the teaching of Brahman as the antaryAmi or the

innerSelf, brings everything together as there is a need to realize Brahman within oneself

andeverywhere!

  

So, this is what I think is the gist of Vedanta:

  

**The Self that abides in all - is not jada, is not jiva, is not Ishwara, but Brahman**

**who iseverything!**

  

Again, I may be wrong and so open for correction !

  

Regards,  
Suresh
