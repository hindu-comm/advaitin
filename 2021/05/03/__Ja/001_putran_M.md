Namaskaram Sada-ji,

  

Nice effort by Sri Maddiah-ji. I will understand that, based on the tenets of Vedanta, he is postulating a Brahman or "Briggs" *field*, itscharacteristics, an adjunct Power ("Maya") anchored in the field that may be a "carrier of .. force particles" which enables multiple functions related to manifestation. Thus he hopes this Vedanta inspiredframework based on this universal field and its shakti will aid in unifying, resolving and substantiating many of the modern scientific theories at the most fundamental rootlevel, that perhaps can fit within the theorizing-limits of science and for which it may be possible to formulate experiments in order to find supporting evidence.

  

He seems to be suggesting something stronger. That the Brahman of the Vedas may be this type of "scalar field", having an adjunct power that is a carrier of force particles whichcause the manifest appearance. Etc. It could seem like he is trying to present Vedanta using the perspectives of modern science. It may be wiser and more acceptable on all sides not to mix Vedanta into the speculative theories of science as if it is the explanatory framework for their discoveries, models and predictions. Rather he can carefully describe Vedanta concepts and from there, how he is inspired to formulate parallel ideas and entities that naturallyfits, supports and explains better the realm of today's contemplations in science and the results of experimentalobservations.

  

That would be my thinking for now. I read only some parts of the text.
