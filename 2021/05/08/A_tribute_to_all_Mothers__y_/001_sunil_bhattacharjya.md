Yes. Respect to all mothers, on tis Mother's day  

  

In the Mahabharata Lord Krishna told Arjuna two very important things and these are (i) ***the Mother is the highest of the Gurus*** and (ii) (speaking) ***the Truth is the highest dharma***. We have always to remember the sacrifices the mother makes. Many mothers even lose their life while giving birth to their children and for the rest, their whole life is for the good of their children.  

  

Jai Shri Krishna

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
