Namaste;

  

"I am very sad to report the sudden departure of the soul of Pujyasri Swami Omkarananda and Here is a message posted by Dushyanth Sridhar in Facebook:

Swami Omkarananda, came across as one of the best speakers of Sanatana Dharma. His expositions were a class in itself. I have heard quite a few of his Gita-videos. Though I follow the Visistadvaita school of philosophy, his videos were a learning package for me to know Sri Sankara's view point.Feel saddened to know that his AtmA has departed from the material body. May his teachings reach a wider audience!"  

  

Swami Omkarananda is one of the best exponents of Advaita Vedanta philosophy and Sanatana dharma to ordinary peoples in India. His Ashram is located in Theni, a town near Madurai at the foot of the Western Ghats Mountains. I have visited and had the opportunity to listen and discuss with him. A great soul with greater depth of knowledge on Gita, Thirukkural, Thevaram, etc. and he was well versed in both Sanskrit and Tamil scriptures. Most importantly, Swamiji was serving the people in the rural area and has eastablished a morning get together of lots of rual people to attend the Puja at the Dakshinamurthy Temple, a beautiful divine structure.

He was tireless travelling and propagating Vedanta in both Tamil and in English.

  

Though we will be missing his physical presence, his works and speeches will ne available for ever!

  

Ram Chandran
