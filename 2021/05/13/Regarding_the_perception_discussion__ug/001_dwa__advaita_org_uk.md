Regarding your point 1, I do not really follow. I thought this WAS representationalism. Here is how the ‘Philosophy Basics’ website ([https://www.philosophybasics.com/branch_representationalism.html](https://www.philosophybasics.com/branch_representationalism.html)) defines it: “Unlike [Idealism](https://www.philosophybasics.com/branch_idealism.html), Representationalism holds that our ideas come from sense data (or images) of a **real, material, external world** ([Realism](https://www.philosophybasics.com/branch_realism.html)), but that the **immediate** (direct) object of perception is only sense data that **represents** the external object.” I.e. (as I understand it) the reflected light from the object enters the eyes and the resultant electronic signals go to the brain which constructs a representation.



Apart from that, I would agree with what you say. But I am sure Chitta-ji will correct us!
