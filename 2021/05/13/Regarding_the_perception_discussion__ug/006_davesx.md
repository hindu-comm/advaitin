Namaste learned Advaitins - How I see this is that whatever is seen is a projection of creation of ideas configured or rather programmed in the body-mind complex.

  

The brain, memory and nervous system automatically kick-in in a fraction of a second to impute meaning and interpret the seen, reifying momentary stimuli as if it is a continuous substantial reality.

  

I even question the theory of representation and that ideas can be an interposing filter or screen that distorts perception.

  

Westerners are appalled that ideas can appear as if it is an external solid object, but this is more obvious in the dream state every night.

  

Advaitins see no division between the seer and the seen.

  

Seeing clearly, the vision of the Totality of Brahman, is immediate apperception free from all your ideas, notions and division of imaginary mental concepts.

  

As the Kathaopanishad states not by learning, practices or umpteen years of listening to scriptural discourses nor by philosophical debate, but to whom the Self chooses is It revealed; may you be that lucky one :-)

  

Peace
