Namaste Sri Venkatraghavan-ji,

  

*We may be seeing a star today based on light emanating from it millions of years ago.  *

  

We don't perceive an object on account of light reaching our eyes, we perceive the object on account of the subtle sense organ eye along with the mind reaching out to it to form a vritti of the form of the object.
I am not sure why you are giving me the explanation of science as if it overrules the Vedic explanation.

  

  

*In the most extreme case, the star may no longer exist when we are seeing its light in the present."*  

  

Pratyaksha is a pramaana, so if we are seeing a star by means of pratyaksha then it must be existing at that moment when it is being seen. You are taking the scientific theory of perception as if it is the gospel truth and using it to discount the Vedic theory. That is neither acceptable nor reasonable.

  

Warm regards,

Chittaranjan
