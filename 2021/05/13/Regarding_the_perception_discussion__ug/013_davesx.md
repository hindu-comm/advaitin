Namaste - Not only that, experience reveals there is no location for any object to exist, it disappears the moment it is seen, instantaneously replaced by another, only to re-appear the next moment and so on.

  

There is no continuity of momentary objects in the present except framed by memory and imagination.

  

This has already been declared by both Gaudapada and Shankara.

  

"Just as the appearance of a ghost in an empty place, of a castle in the air, and of a second moon in the sky, so is the world of objects seen by the knowers of Vedanta -- as ashimmering superficial display within the silent depth of Brahman".
