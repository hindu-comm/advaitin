Namaste Putran ji,

  

You are absolutely right. There are so many views within advaita - and it is not just now, it ever has been thus. If we read works like siddhAntalesha sangraha of SrImad appayya dIkshita-r, we can see the variety of thoughts within advaita from times immemorial.

  

No advaita hAni is possible, because it is a fact. Talk - whether of Atma and anAtma - is in the realm of vyavahAra. The Atma cannot be talked of, the anAtma is not worth talking about. Now, if we decide to stay silent, that is vyavahAra too. Whether we talk or whether we stay silent, whether we meditate or frolic, makes not one iota of difference to That Truth. यत्र यत् अध्यासः तत्कृत दोषेण गुणेन वा अणुमात्रेण स न सम्बध्यते.

  

That being the case, all this is a dream, a game to be enjoyed.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
