Namaste Chittaranjan ji

Thank you for your views, but I don't agree.

  

Your statement "The real world would then be something that lies beyond the field of consciousness." is not something that I can agree with, because I do not concede there is a real world in the first place.

  

Nor can I bring myself to agree with "Such a world, which lies beyond the field of consciousness, would be independent of consciousness." - when I do not agree that there is a real world, I certainly cannot agree that it can exist independent of consciousness.

  

Nor do I agree that there exists an unreal world that is independent of consciousness.

  

The world irrespective of whether I see it or not, or how I see it, is an unreal projection on consciousness, caused due to the ignorance of that consciousness - that is the dependence that the world has on consciousness - a dependence underpinned by ignorance.

  

That Avidyaka sambandha, does not vary whatever -ism one prefers. The sublation of the world occurs when ignorance is destroyed, irrespective of what was the cause of the perception of the world in the first place.

  

Regards

Venkatraghavan
