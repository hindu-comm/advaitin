Namaste Sri Venkatraghavan-ji,

  

*Your statement "The real world would then be something that lies beyond the field of consciousness." is not something that I can agree with, because I do not concede there is a real world in the first place.*  

  

My statement is a logical consequence of the theory of perception proposed by you, so you can't hold the theory to be true and say at the same time that you don't agree with its consequences. :-)

  

And as regards the world being real, even I do not agree with it nor would the author of Vedanta Paribhasa agree with it, but still the theory of perception is presented as if the world is real because, as you would surely be aware, the scriptures do adhyaropa for explaining things in the realm of vyavaharika sathya. The theory of perception should be such that when the adhyaropa is rescinded (through apavada), the world should vanish; but in the case of the theory of perception that leads to Representationalism, there would be two worlds, the phenomenal world and the putative real world (or putative external world or whatever you would like to call it) and only the phenomenal world would vanish when you rescind the adhyaropa leaving the hypothetical / putative external world intact. Thus there would certainly be Advaita hani. :-)
