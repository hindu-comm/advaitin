Namasdte Sri Venkatraghavan-ji,

  

*The sublation of the world occurs when ignorance is destroyed, irrespective of what was the cause of the perception of the world in the first place.*

  

If the Charvaka philosophy should be true, would the sublation of the world occur? It would not because the Charvaka philosophy and the sublation of the world are two incoherent theses.

  

You are able to make the assertion that the sublation of the world would occur when ignorance is destroyed based on the Siddhanta of Advaita and the conviction that it is true. You can't make the same assertion based on the Charvaka philosophy because it would result in loss of coherence. So, it is necessary that the assertions you make should be coherent with the philosophy you hold to be true. If Representationalism would be true, that coherence between the kind of Advaita Siddhanta that would result from holding Representationalism to be true and your assertion that sublation would occur when ignorance would be destroyed would be lost. That is what I want to say.
