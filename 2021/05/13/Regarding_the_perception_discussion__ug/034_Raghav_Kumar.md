The formulation of the problem is simple enough.

To restate it -

"1. It is empirically observed that distant objects/events are seen after a time delay.

2.How would vedAnta paribhAShA explain the perception of distant objects given that it talks of the mind elongating to reach and assume the rUpa of that object in the present moment, when the object might have moved away or stopped existing at that place?"

  

However Chitta ji I understand is saying that 1. itself is disputed, being only a theory, rather than an empirically observed fact.

  

As i understand it (any corrections welcome), the idea being expressed as the contact model of perception includes among other things, assertions that

  

1\. If the role of sensory stimuli received at the sense organs are brought in to formulate a theory of perception by acceptance of ideas such as prabhAva-avacchinna-chaitanyam, then such a theory of pratyaxa amounts to a type of representationalism. Or to put it more generally, Something you suggested to explain perception of distant objects etc. amounts to representationalism.

  

2\. Holding on to any variety of representationalism at the vyavaharic level is incompatible with shruti-compatible adhyaropa. (To give an example from a different context, jadakAraNatvam of jagat as a vyavaharic theory/worldview is incompatible with cetanakAraNatvam which is a a necessary (albeit not sufficient) component of any vyavaharic worldview which remains resolvable through apavAda to arrive at advaita.)

  

3\. The mind going out to the space occupied by the object to form the viShayAkAra vRtti is non-negotiable. The formation of vRttis based on stimuli received from external objects at our physical sense organs (golakas) is irredeemably realism. Since the actual object is never 'directly contacted' implying it remains unresolvable in to aparoxa Consciousness in any such representational prakriyA.

  

On a different note -

One other point to consider based on IshvarasRShTi and jIvasRShTi, is that any object is created by Ishvara as pAnchabhautika, but we can never actually perceive any of the panchabhUtas directly. We jIvas only observe shabda, sparsha etc. Yet we do not run the risk of representationalism ( because of tAdAtmya sambandha).
