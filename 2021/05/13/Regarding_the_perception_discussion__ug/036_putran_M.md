Namaskaram Raghav-ji,

  

>   
> On a different note -  
>
> One other point to consider based on IshvarasRShTi and jIvasRShTi, is
> that any object is created by Ishvara as pAnchabhautika, but we can
> never actually perceive any of the panchabhUtas directly. We jIvas
> only observe shabda, sparsha etc. Yet we do not run the risk of
> representationalism ( because of tAdAtmya sambandha).

  

This is a significant point that may be useful to the purvapakshin.

  

It says the self in its identification as jiva is fundamentally obstructed by its body-mind from certain dimensions of knowledge of manifest creation. It refutes the knowability (for jivas) of all objects in every sense of their existence.

  

So, the PP can ask,

  

why would the unknowability of the perceived world, beyond the seen representation, lead to advaita hani? It exists in the knowledge of Ishvara and existence is known to jivas from shastra, yet otherwise the jiva cannot attain direct and complete knowledge of objects. Similar to your example.

  

I do not fully understand how tadatmya sambandha resolves this objection.

  

(There is still the charge that we are not being consistent with regard to accepting Shastra as pramana. For existence it is pramana but for contact theory it is not, though tradition has always upheld the latter as well.)
