Dear Sri Putran-ji,

  

*On a different note -  *

*One other point to consider based on IshvarasRShTi and jIvasRShTi, is that any object is created by Ishvara as pAnchabhautika, but we can never actually perceive any of the panchabhUtas directly. We jIvas only observe shabda, sparsha etc. Yet we do not run the risk of representationalism ( because of tAdAtmya sambandha).*

  

*This is a significant point that may be useful to the purvapakshin.*

  
No, it will not help the purvapakshin because a dravya in the Indian logical tradition (Nyaya), unlike the substance of Western philosophy, is not a bare individual but is composed of the gunas. So, the perception of the gunas amounts to perception of the object.

  

  

*I do not fully understand how tadatmya sambandha resolves this objection.  *

  

Tadatmya means non-difference, so the perception of the gunas amounts to perception of the dravya object on account of non-difference of the guna from the dravya-object.
