Namaste Raghav ji,

Thanks for the summary of the discussion so far. Will take it up as a basis in a few days' time, but a couple of thoughts / questions.

  

Are you or anyone else aware of any shruti pramANa given for this prakriyA of the cognition traveling to objects? It has certainly been used vivaraNam onwards, but I don't remember if any of our pUrvAchArya-s have quoted a shruti to evidence that it is an adhyAropa made by the shruti Herself. Thus, is setting aside some aspects of such a prakriyA, shruti viruddham?

  

Also, I don't think that we need to state that only objects of perception are within the sphere of consciousness. That would lead to avyApti in paroksha jnAna viShaya.

  

For, we admit that the objects of paroksha jnAna are within the sphere of consciousness too. In those cases, the prakriyA admits that the vRtti does not go out to the object itself. How are they mithyA then? Is it because they are objects of pratyaksha jnAna somewhere / sometime? Don't think so.

  

But even if we assume that is so for the sake of argument, what about nityaparoksha viShaya-s (objects eternally beyond perception) such as dharma / adharma etc? They are never the objects of perception, but they are mithyA too - why? dRshyatvAt - because they are objects of cognitions, not because they are objects of perception.

  

In such cases, there is a sambandha between the vRtti and the object, which makes the objects vRtti-viShaya, without requiring the vRtti to travel to the object.

  

I will have to think about how shAstra addresses these questions further, but this is what I am mulling over at the moment.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
