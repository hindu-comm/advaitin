Dear Putran ji,

  

In the Bhagavad gita 5.22 for example there is this explicit statement:

  

  

ये हि *संस्पर्शजा***भोगा दुःखयोनय एव ते। आद्यन्तवन्तः कौन्तेय न तेषु रमते बुधः।।5.22।।  The experience born of the contact of the senses with their respective sense objects...

  

There are other such statements in the Gita and perhaps the Upanishads.

  

A search in the Advaitasharadayields about 2 dozen statements from the bhashya of Shankara and others using the word 'samparaka, samprayoga, sambandha, samyoga...' between the sense organ and the object.
