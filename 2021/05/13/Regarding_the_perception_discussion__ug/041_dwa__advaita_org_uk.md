Just to add to this, Shankara (not our Shankara) comments on the relevant passage:  
  
“The sense organ of knowledge itself is the channel, doorway, in the form of modifications like sound etc. for the changeful mind. Thus the mind, through that sense organ IS CHANGED into the form of the outside object, both in its particular and general nature, and is colored by it.”



This is from the yogasUtrabhAShyavivaraNa of sha~Nkara by T. S. Rukamani. It clearly sounds in line with the modern scientific understanding rather than the post-Shankara (our Shankara!) theory of AdhvarIndra.
