Namaskaram Dennis-ji,

  

I don't know of the Swami's writings beyond the book and cannot make out "neo" in what he wrote there. He was taking care about how he presented Indian knowledge about pramanas; again, the point is that if vedanta prakriya (of perception) was so definitely known with regard to its variance from Sankhya, this swami would have elaborated that fact in his book. It would have been the "neo" thing to do, given the conformance with science.

  

With the SwamiMaheshanandagiri, he is not claiming to derive vedanta prakriya from Sureshwaracharya'scommentary; rather he is writing as if this was already standard among vedantic scholars, only not popularized in the general purana-based opinions which went with Sankhya. That appears very neo-twisting to me; where is the basis for this vedanta prakriya so obvious?

  

Anycase, all this (about Dakshinamurthy stotra) is not within Shankara prastana-traya and sruti that you are focused on. Subbu-ji, a few posts back, did mention of references in Gita and elsewhere.
