Namaskaram,

  

There are 3 questions:

  

1\. Does the subtle sense-organ (eye) "go out" and meet the object? (Direct contact type 1)

  

2\. Does the mind "go out" and meet the object? (Direct contact type 2)

  

3\. Are these events "instantaneous"?

  

We know that the mind takes the "form of the object" at the end of these steps.

  

Direct contact+ instantaneous -\> Knowledge of object as it is.

  

For 1., Subbu-ji had given reference from Bhashya. So, this was definitely part of Vedanta prakriya, at least some version that Shankara posited in his bhashya.

  

For 2., Some say the mind also goes to the object and the vritti is formed at the object. Others say perhaps (?) that the mind stays at the location of the jiva's body; hence the vritti does not form at the object.

  

For 3., I have not seen negations of this point. However perhaps thetwo events need not happen together. That is, the obstruction to the eye-sense organ is not the same as the obstruction to the mind; one can open the eyes and the mind can be elsewhere. Not sure how this is understood. But if the obstruction is removed, the events are said to be immediate.
