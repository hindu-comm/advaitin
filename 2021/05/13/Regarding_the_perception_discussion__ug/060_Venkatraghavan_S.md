"*This is where I understand this debate to be centered at. I am not the expert to defend VP but I think people who consider the topic as merely of vyavaharika/mithya interest may be missing this central issue that others think has direct bearing on the way the siddhanta is understood.*

*  *

*One place where this subtlety may be indicated is the following: Advaita does not merely say that there is a maayicshow of world and an unchanging reality that is entirely distinct; not just jagan mithya, Brahma satyam. It says further jivo brahmaiva naaparaha, that "aham brahmaasmi".* "

  

This is precisely the point why this discussion came up. Just because the world is mithyA, we don't say any explanation goes. We seek to explain it. The vedAnta paribhASha articulates one way of explaining perception.

  

However any theory needs to be tested in the face of facts and must be able to explain how those facts occur. Phenomena such as the Doppler effect, red shift, constancy of the speed of light are observable. One cannot dismiss all these.

  

If the light from galaxies did not reach the eyes why is there a shift in the spectrum of light towards the higher wavelength when those stars and galaxies move away from us? If the speed of light was not a universal constant why did the De Sitter experiment demonstrate it to be so? If sound waves don't reach the ears why is there an observable increase in the frequency of sound emanating from an object moving towards us and decrease when it moves away from us (the siren from an ambulance)? Why is there a gap in the experience of seeing lightning and hearing thunder?

  

All of these are facts that we experience, not opinions. They cannot be simply dismissed away and say they are wrong explanations without explaining how the current model of perception accounts for all this. The world is mithyA but the explanation has to be valid within the sphere of mithyA.
