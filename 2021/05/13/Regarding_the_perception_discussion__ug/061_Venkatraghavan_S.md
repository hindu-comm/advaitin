One more point - Phenomena such as the red shift are not something observable by the naked eye either - they are observed using spectroscopy. That is, they record the wavelengths of light that have reached the point of observation. Depending on the nature of the medium in between the source and destination, certain wavelengths are absorbed, certain passed through.

  

These are recordings of the wavelengths of lights that have been emitted / passed through by the medium, made without human intervention - ie 1) there is no mind at the observing end and 2) it is measuring the wavelengths of light at the observer's end, not an observation at the star.

  

If light from the star did not reach the observer how can the measurement be made? There was no mind at the observer's end for us to postulate the vRtti reaching the star. We have got to find a way of explaining all this in the current model.

  

Please bear in mind that I have the greatest of respect for tradition - without it, we would have no access to the spiritual genius of the past. However, any model purporting to explain the world should attempt to do just that.
