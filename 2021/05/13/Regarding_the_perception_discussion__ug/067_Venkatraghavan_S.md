Namaste Satyan ji,

Great email, really enjoyed reading it. Re your last point:

"*Clearly, NischaladAsa lays a lot of emphasis on Drishti Srishti vAda (DSV), in which the whole VP model of vrtti vyApti and phala vyApti would not be applicable for how can thought envelop an object that yet needs to be projected? In DSV, the triad of pramAta, pramANam and prameya is projected every moment as in a dream. That again seems to be point to the dispensability of the VP model as it is incompatible with DSV prakriyA, which seems to reinforce the idea that VP prakriyA is not indispensable/central in Vedanta in general.*"

Hope you don't mind me intruding on your conversation with Praveen ji, but this very question was raised and answered in the context of dRShTi sRShTi prakriyA in the advaita siddhi -

  

Opponent:अथैवं घटादेः स्वज्ञानात् पूर्वमसत्त्वेन प्रतिकर्मव्यवस्थानुपपत्तिः। अधिष्ठानस्यापि शुक्तीदमंशस्य रूप्यादिवत्\`इदं रजतम्' इति ज्ञानात् प्रागसत्त्वेन संप्रयोगादिहेतुत्रय जन्यत्वरूपाध्यासतटस्थलक्षणस्य सत्यस्य वस्तुनो मिथ्यावस्तु सम्भेदावभासइत्यस्य स्वरूपलक्षणस्य चायोग इति चेन्न।

If pots do not exist prior to their cognisance, then pratikarma vyavastha (the cognisance of a particular object by a particular individual) would be untenable.Despite being the substratum, if the "this shell" aspect, like the shell-silver, does not exist prior to the cognition of "**this**silver". Both the taTastha lakshaNa (हेतुत्रयजन्यत्वम् the three causes of adhyAsa being samprayoga, contact of the object with sense organs, samskAra and doSha) and svarUpa lakshaNa (अतस्मिन् तद्बुद्धिः) to are not possible  

  
The siddhikAra replies -

प्रतिकर्मव्यवस्थायाः संप्रयोगादिहेतुत्रय जन्यत्वरूपाध्यासतटस्थलक्षणस्य च मन्दाधिकारिविषयत्वात्।

The postulation of pratikarma vyavasthA and the taTastha lakshaNa of adhyAsa as being caused by samprayoga etc. is meant for the ordinary person (it is an explanation provided only for those who ask why a particular object is seen by a particular person - that is, one who is asking from the standpoint of duality already).

  

सत्यस्य वस्तुनो मिथ्यावस्तुसम्भेदावभास इति स्वरूपलक्षणं तु दृष्टिसृष्टिपक्षेप्यविरुद्धम्।

The svarUpa lakshaNa of adhyAsa of an unreal object appearing as identical with a real object, is not incongruous in the drishTi sriShTi position also.

  

न हि इदमंशावच्छिन्नं चैतन्यं न वस्तु, न वा मिथ्यारूप्यस्य तेन सह न सम्भेदावभासः।

Consciousness delimited by the "this" object cannot be said to be non-existent, nor can it be said that it does not appear to be identical with the unreal silver.

  

न च- \`इदं रूप्यम्' इति ज्ञानकाले शुक्तित्वादेरभावेनाध्यासस्य तदज्ञानकार्यत्वादिप्रक्रियाविरोध इति- वाच्यम्।

Nor it can be argued thus - "When the knowledge "This is silver" is present, as the shell-ness of the object is non-existent (*note: in DSV*), the methodology to explain the illusion, namely, that the ignorance of its shell-ness is the cause of the illusion, would be untenable".

  

\`इदं रूप्यम्' इति ज्ञानकाले शुक्तित्वस्याभावेऽपि तदज्ञानस्थित्यविरोधात्। While the shell-ness of the object can be absent when the knowledge "this is silver" is present, there is nothing incongruous in saying that ignorance is present.
