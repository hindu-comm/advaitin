If pots do not exist prior to their cognisance, then pratikarma vyavastha (the cognisance of a particular object by a particular individual) would be untenable.Despite being the substratum, if the "this shell" aspect, like the shell-silver, does not exist prior to the cognition of "**this**silver", both the taTastha lakshaNa (हेतुत्रयजन्यत्वम् the three causes of adhyAsa being samprayoga, contact of the object with sense organs, samskAra and doSha) and svarUpa lakshaNa (अतस्मिन् तद्बुद्धिः) are not possible (within the dRShTi sRShTi construct).
