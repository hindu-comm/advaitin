Namaste Putran ji,

I am not 100% sure where you are going on with this one, but I agree in general with your first paragraph, with the exception that I would say that the svarUpa of the Self is jnAnam - i.e. it does not have a shakti to know (for that would require avidyA / mAyA, which is in a vyAvahArika plane), rather, its nature is one of illumination.



I do not agree with the view that the process of cognition of a sentient entity is the same as the process of recording of information by an insentient machine. The point I was making was that given that an insentient machine is recording the spectral pattern of light from stars, it follows that light from the stars is traveling to reach it.

  

That being so, if we replace the insentient machine with a sentient being, one does not have to postulate that the mind needs to travel to the object for it to be seen, when it has already been established through the example of the machine capturing the light spectrum, that the light has reached the same place from the same source.

  

I really do not understand what you are saying in the third paragraph, so will not comment on it.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
