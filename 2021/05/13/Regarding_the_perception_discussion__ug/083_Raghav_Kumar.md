Namaste Satyan ji and Putran ji

  

Putran ji wrote-

  

> 

>   
> "What I in jiva see is what I in Ishvara manifest here and now. And
> **based on this seeing, the Self manifests vritti and other body-mind
> changes.**"
>
>   

> So by the kriya shakti of the self, the obstruction is cleared through
> "modification of upadhis", i.e. by opening the eyelids. The VP theory
> implies that once the obstruction is cleared (by effecting the needed
> changes to body-mind-world), the knowledge of the corresponding object
> is immediate and direct."

> 
>
>   
>
>   
>
>   
>
> My follow-up -
>
>   
>
> The clearing of obstructions to formation of a pratyaxa vRtti may well
> include not only the jiva exercising kriya shakti to open the eye
> lids, but also Ishvara's niyati / manifestation (physical Order)
> allowing the light to fall on the eyes as a precursor to the vRtti.
> Such an admission of the role of light as a pratibandhaka-remover does
> not amount to acceptance of the scientific materialistic theory of
> perception. (In other words, the possible need for other factors other
> than one's own kriya shakti may well hold. For example if someone has
> covered an object, he needs to uncover it before we see the object.)
> Such a role for Ishvara's shakti in the role of light as a
> \*precursor\* to the arising of the vRtti does not necessarily imply
> it (such a theory) is contrary to Advaita. Such is my understanding.
>
>   
>
> Also as Satyan-ji pointed out, if we are ready to accept on the
> authority of sacred texts that
>
>   
>
> 1\. "in a mysterious process, the subtle entity called antaHkaraNa
> reaches out \*instantaneously\* to the space occupied by the object a
> million light years \*away\* and takes on the form of the object" (VP)
>
>   
>
> Then we can as well (extend the above assertion) and hold that
>
>   
>
> 2\. "in a mysterious way, the subtle entity called antaHkaraNa reaches
> out to the space occupied by the object a million of light years
> \*away\* and presents it, as it \*was\* a million years ago."
>
>   
>
> Reaching out deep in to space (as is being accepted by VP) and
> reaching back deep back in to time by the antaHkaraNa are both equally
> mysterious.
>
>   
>
> As to why VP does not mention any kAlavyavadhAna, that can easily be
> accounted for on the ground that objects like a pot, cloth etc were
> being used for illustration and the kAlavyavadhAna is insignificant in
> such nearby objects. The inclusion of the upadhi (clause) of
> kAlavyavadhAna would \*generalize\* the VP model to perception of
> distant objects, with the VP model (with non-mention of kAla)
> remaining uncontradicted being a \*special\* case for nearby objects.
>
>   
>
> Such a samanvaya has the advantage of VP left untouched with the
> simple clause of introducing kala as a factor for distant objects to
> account for current observations from science.
>
>   
>
> (Incidentally, we also have several mentions from yoga vAshiShTha
> etc., indicating that asserting the relative nature of time, is not
> contrary to the spirit of advaita.)
>
