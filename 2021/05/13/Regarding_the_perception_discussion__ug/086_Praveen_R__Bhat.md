Well, you can read it that way as well. What I was saying is that to each individual, VP might serve vyavahAra also, but serve enough to convince others. The reason is that the prapancha is mithyA and no matter how much one tries with any prakriyA or any scientific approach, it can \*never\* be understood completely, let alone explained. In Pujya Swamiji's words, ignorance in any subject is just one question away! That is, the more one tries to explain the mithyA prapancha, moreunanswerable questions will pop up. What can be understood completely without any dilution is one's own svarUpa, which is what is the goal of every prakriyA. Unfortunately, science has no such goal, therefore, trying to please oneself or the scientists by making a samanvaya with it is at odds with the very idea of Vedanta.
