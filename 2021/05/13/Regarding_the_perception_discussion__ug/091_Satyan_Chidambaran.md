Raghav-ji,

  

Thanks for your insightful thoughts.

  

I have a few comments about this topic in general and also your suggestion:

  

1\) We are talking about an apOuruSheya viShaya. sUkShma sharIra itself is an apOurusheya vishaya. Given this,

any model should ideally have come from the shruti. But it looks like shruti, and to a large extent Shankara/Sureshwara

are not very explicit about this. This was then probably largely the work of post Shankara Acharyas, and it is indeed

very useful as a prakriyA, no doubt.

  

2\) We have identified some holes with regards to yukti and anubhava. We are trying to fill them and there are

possibly alternative approaches for doing so.

  

3\) Do you have info on what is the source that says that antaHkaraNa goes out instantaneously to the object ion VP model?

  

4\) What you have mentioned of going back in time is indeed a very out of the box/thoughtful attempt at reconciliation!

My questions are as follows:  

a\) Given the apOurusheya nature of the viShaya, what pramANA do we have for saying that the antaHkaraNa is

going back in time. Can there be a charge of speculation? If we say, we are seeing a star that is now gone,

and therefore we are going back in time, the PP can say what you are seeing is the light from the star that is now

gone, you are not going back in time. What would the response be?

  

b\) It might be possible that I am seeing a dead star and also conversing with my friend...would the intellect then be

in two times simultaneously (both present time and 1 million years ago)? Or perhaps I am seeing the moon and next to the moon is the dead star much further away. Would the intellect again be in two times simultaneously? Or I am looking at the night sky and I see hundreds of stars...does that mean my intellect is in hundreds of past times simultaneously?Could that be problematic?

  

c\) Does it address all the holes? Or only the dead star hole? What about the gravitational lensing/doppler effect/red shift etc?

  

d\) Could seeing of a dead star be somehow qualified as a bhrama --- another alternative?

  

e\) What about the alternative that Swami M had? That will also address the dead star hole, and perhaps other

holes also? Somehow my gut feel says that unless one comes to Swami M's method of light reaching the golaka,

we may be under constant assault by something or other that is hard to explain. It may be an overall

simpler method to explain more phenomenon perhaps? But yes, it would bee a bigger change to existing VP model.

But is that a problem if it is a new prakriyA?

  

5\) Finally, if we agree that it is only a prakriyA, each person can use what one feels comfortable with (as Sureshwara says

यया यया भवेत्....... )So we could have existing VP model, and other alternative choices co-existing, and each one could pick

what one feels comfortable with e.g like picking between abhAsavAda versus avachCheda vAda is left to the individual's choice.

  

Regards,

--Satyan
