Well, you can read it that way as well. What I was saying is that to each individual, VP might serve vyavahAra also, but serve enough to convince others. The reason is that the prapancha is mithyA and no matter how much one tries with any prakriyA or any scientific approach, it can \*never\* be understood completely, let alone explained. In Pujya Swamiji's words, ignorance in any subject is just one question away! That is, the more one tries to explain the mithyA prapancha, moreunanswerable questions will pop up. What can be understood completely without any dilution is one's own svarUpa, which is what is the goal of every prakriyA. Unfortunately, science has no such goal, therefore, trying to please oneself or the scientists by making a samanvaya with it is at odds with the very idea of Vedanta.

  

Completely agree that there is no finality in any vyavahAra theory. Clearly as you say, science has to constantly update itself because there is no finality. But I think that is also part of the reason why VP theory is also facing similar issues...because it is dealing with vyavahAra and has to respond to onslaught of anubhava/yukti vyAvAharic objections if one tries to establish its vyAvahAric validity for the theory. And there may need to be some constant response to such objections....unless of course one takes Position 1 and uses it strictly as a personal prakriyA!

I am tempted to jump in and ask with which pramANa as per Vedanta do we know all this that you point out, but I will avoid it as we are very far away from what I started as a response to Swami Mji's words. If not, then why justify it and/ or change DMS, and/ or VP!

  

Personally, a lot of VP theory seems to have been created post Shankara/Sureshwara. It is a great prakriyA and I respect it and also follow it. However, I don't consider it as indispensable...it is a prakriyA. One can use whatever prakriyA one feels comfortable with as Bhagavan Sureshwara has also permitted (यया यया भवेत्....). Clearly, prior to VP theory being formalized, there were GYanIs for the Guru parampara existed even then. If they could arrive at GYanam without VP, so could anyone else today. In that sense, I consider Swami M's prakriyA to be also respectable for it perhaps worked for him and might work for others who are not able to accept VP prakriyA. As there is choice between AbhAsavAda and avachChedavAda, and it is left to the individual to choose, perhaps there could be choice between VP prakriyA and some alternatives such as what Swami M has, and each individual can choose. As I mentioned, I did not think Swami M had to stretch the meanings of the words in the mUla to arrive at what he did. IMHO, the mUla looks amenable to both VP and Swami M's views (and I know that you would disagree :-)). There is no question of changing VP anway....who can change a text? It could just co-exist with alternative models.

> Lastly, if indeed there is gamanam of sUkshma sharIra vrtti to
> vishayAs outside, and vrtti vyApti and phala vyApti etc, they all

How is a model or hypothesis saying that electrons go around the nucleus or other scientific claims as per their own scientific method any different? Why would anyone consider it as a pramANa in Vedanta?

  

That is why I am distinguishing between scientific theory and observations (for which the source may or may not be science). Light from a dead flare at a distance that is still seen after the flare dies is an observation, not theory. A bent rod in water is a direct observation not a theory. Mercury seen next to the sun when it is actually behind the sun is an observation, not a theory. Doppler shift can be directly heard when a car approaches me and leaves me is an observation, it is not a theory. All such observations are under the purview of lOukika pramANas, and hence pratyaksha is sufficient to know about such phenomenon. When science tries to explain it, yes it becomes a theory. But I am just talking about the direct observation of such phenomenon and not the scientific explanation or theory of it, which of course would be subject to change...but I don't believe that the observation will change as it is a part of the physical order in the creation. And for holding position 2, reconciliation with such observed vyAvahArika phenomenon would be needed....not reconcilialtion with scientific theories.
