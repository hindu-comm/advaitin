We can refer to Atma as nirvikara satchitananda in the paramarthika standpoint. But when we also refer to Mind, body, world, jiva, etc., then we are automatically referring to "Atma" in what youregard"mixture" sense. Or rather, in the sense that the final reduction from this reference standpoint is Brahman+Maya, in that Maya is the now intrinsic shakti of Brahman to imagine/project and know/cognize as Ishvara-jiva-jagat. So Brahman or Self is the knower of its projection: there is no other Knower but He.

1\. Perception is the direct and immediate cognition/awareness/knowledge of the Self of its manifestation. For the jiva, the perception of world is neither nil nortotal because of the natureof its ajnana/avarana through identification with its upadhis.

  

2\. The issue is whether the jiva cognizes/knows the object, or something else that is representative of the object.

  

Science says the latter; VP aligns with those who hold the former. VP is describing the manifestations in body-mind corresponding to the jiva's perception, the light of the Self shining on the identification of mind directly with object and forming the vritti.
