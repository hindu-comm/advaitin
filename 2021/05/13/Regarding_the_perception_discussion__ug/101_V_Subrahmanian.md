Venkat ji,

  

What you say below is very interesting. I think it leads to the confirmation of the Vedantic doctrine of 'ajnAtasattA of objects is not admissible'. No object can be said to exist unless it is perceived by
someone or the other at some time or the other. And 'tasya bhAsA sarvam idam vibhAti' also is valid only on the condition of there being a jiva to mediate the connection between Chit and the object. And the connection is undoubtedly through the vritti. The vritti is crucial because it is the only thing in creation that is designed to capture the reflection of Chit.

  

Thanks.
