Venkatraghavanji, Subbuji,

  

Subbuji wrote:

I think it leads to the confirmation of the Vedantic doctrine of 'ajnAtasattA of objects is not admissible  

  

Subbuji, that is only in dRRiShti sRRiShti vAda...I see therefore it is. In sRRiShti dRRiShti vAda,ajnAtasattA is permitted....it is therefore I see. In the latter case, even when I don't see, it is said to haveajnAtasattA.

  

Venjatji wrote:

The sarvaprakAshakatva, all illuminating nature of consciousness would be lost - ie shruti statements such as तस्य सर्वमिदं विभाति (tasya bhAsA sarvamidam vibhAti, by its illumination is all this illuminated) would be called into question, because, arguably, what is illuminated by consciousness is the light reflecting off objects and not the objects themselves.

  

Venkatji, as Raghavji had pointed out some time back: we cannot perceive the pA~nchabhOutikam objects themselves anyway as what is illuminated is only the guNas: shabda, sparSha, rUpa, rasa, gandha.

  

Also, post mahAvAkya understanding, when Atma is Bramhan, which is sat, then there is nothing outside of it anyway. The jaDa jagat and pramAtA are both projected by that one sat chit Atma which is Bramhan. The jaDa object emits/reflects jaDA light that reaches the eyes of the pramAtA and forms a jaDA image. Then there could be vRRitti vyApti and phala vyApti of that image. The VRRitti shines with reflected consciousness and the pramAtA now "knows" the jaDA object's guNas (rUpam). I know that whatever object my vRRitti illumines is mithyA on the strength of the mahAvAkya because the existence that I am is also the same one existence underlying Iswara who projects the jagat. Therefore, mithyAtvam of jagat is still established.

  

Also, it is only by the light of consciousness of AtmA=Bramhan by which anything is known because that consciousness needs to be reflected in the vRRitti. Sotasya bhAsA sarvamidam vibhAti is still preserved. We could also state it negatively: without that chit of Atma being reflected in the vRRitti as chidAbhAsa, nothing can be known.

  

Regards,

--Satyan
