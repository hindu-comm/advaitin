We are not talking about the refraction phenomenon. Did I deny it? We are talking about the way the jiva's cognition takes place. One possibility I see is that the obstruction of glass or water does not allow further passage of the subtle sense-organ, that it has only direct contact with the glass only. The jiva has cognition of the object directly as it is once the obstruction to its cognition is removed (the removal will also be partial, so its cognition of any object will be partial). In this case, given that we have obstructions in between, one possibility is that the subtle eye reaches to the outer surface of the glass and no further.

  

Alright. Then the refracted reflected light makes its "image" or presence on the glass while passing through which the subtle eye grasps along with the glass. Vritti is formed whichis what the jiva sees etc.

  

Other possibility is that the subtle organ is able to envelope the composite of obstructions + pencil (since it can expand across the transparent glass and water) which leads to a layering of vrittis that includes appearance of a bent pencil. Again no bhrama. It is what is seen by direct contact with the objects. The bhramais if the jiva due to its ajnana thinks it is seeing a bent pencil.
