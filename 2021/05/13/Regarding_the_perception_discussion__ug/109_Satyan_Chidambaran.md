Venkartaghavanji,

  

Excellent points!

  

This could be responded to at different levels:

  

1\) Firstly, does the prakriyA bring us to the tAtparyam or not: "brahma satyam jagan mithya jivo brahmaiva nAparaH"?

If we agree that it does on all three points in the tAtparyam, it is sAdhvI. Each prakriya may have some shortcoming.

AbhAsavAda is not perfect, nor is avacChedavAda, but that doesn't make us reject it. This could be the ultimate fallback for any issues

that are encountered ***outside*** of the tAtparyam,

  

So the first question is that do you see this issue ofsarvaprakAshakatvafalling within the tAtparyam?

  

If you say, it is tAtparyam, my question would be that why do you care aboutsarvaprakAshakatva of jagat if iultimately

jagat is mithya?Let there be a mithya jagat which not illumined directly. Why should it affect the tAtparyam?

It is not listed as a criteria in the tAtparyam. As long as I know I am sat chit Ananda brahma, jagat is mithya,

who cares if the mithya jagat is illumined directly or indirectly in a process which is also mithya? Why do we want

to insist that the mithya jaga in a mithya process should be illumined via direct contact of the mithya vRRitti only?

  

Having said that, the following options illustrate some further possible responses to address some

of the concerns that you have raised. For convenience lets say we call this prakriya VP2 (VP2) as opposed to VP.

  

2\) In VP2, the vRRitti is formed not at the object but at the golaka. The experience of the object shining is the same as VP though.

In my experience, I don't say "I know the image of the object". In my experience, I still say "I know the object".

So why do you really care if contact is direct or indirect?  

  

So yes, contact with object is indirect, but experience is the same.

  

Yes, your 1(a) case raises a good point. We will still see the star that may not exist. But VP2 is no worse than VP in that scenario.

In fact, VP2 is consistent with anubhava/yukti that we can see objects that were there in the past (dead star)....but doesn't have the issue that VP has where it needs the mind to go out to an object (dead star) that doesn't exist any more.

  

3\) I see Raghavji's other email where he is uncomfortable with using light for what light is can change based on science.

That is a very good point. And also, if Venkatji would like a more direct contact with the object to resolve issue \#2 and also the

sarvaprakAshakatva concernhe raised, here is one thought:

  

One could say that the agni tattvam from the surface of object pervades the space around the object. Therefore the rUpa guNa of the object is also stretching out in space. That agni comes in contact with the chaKshurindriya, the image is formed. In this sense, you are still contacting the object directly but the boundary of where the object ends becomes blurred because one of its guNas stretches out into space around the object. Hence, in VP mind goes out to the object, but in VP2, one could say that the agni tattvam of the object could stretch out to the chakShu golaka. This would help establish more direct contact with the object if that pleases you more. And may also help address thesarvaprakAshakatva concernif you think it is tAtparyam (but I would like to understand the reasons for why one should insist that it be tAtparyam).

  

Regards,
