Why this analogy is not meant as eka-jiva-vada.

  

The Self’s cognition in the context of the dream is the jiva’s cognition; Maya-Shakti in the context of the dream is the jiva’s iccha-jnana-kriya shakti. The jiva’s cognition in the dream corresponds with the manifestation or projection of vritti etc. And within the dream, there is Ishvara as well, who is not the “eka-jiva”.

  

Here the point is that reference to Brahman’s projection has already the split of seer-seeing-seen, so reference to Brahman from this standpoint will be Brahman+Mayashakti the Seer/Dreamer who Sees/Projects this seer-seeing-seen dream.
