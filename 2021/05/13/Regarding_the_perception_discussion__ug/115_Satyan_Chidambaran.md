Namaste Venkatji,

  

You wrote:

am not sure whether the new prakriyA "brings us to" (I am not sure in what sense you mean it,  

  

I meant that the prakriyA should in some way help one see the tAtparyam, and of course, should not

contradict that understanding.

  

You wrote:

The new prakriyA however, is contradicted (in my view) by shruti statements such as "tasya bhAsA sarvamidamvibhAti".

  

I see your concern better now...you are concerned about Shruti.  

  

My question isतस्य भासा सर्वमिदं विभाति really contradicted? This was the point I was trying to make in an earlier email as well  

but perhaps it didn't come out as clearly.I think it may depend on how you understand the mantra versus how I do,

and also on what prakriyA you use to understand the mantra.Perhaps there is a difference there.

  

At a high level, it means "By its light all this shines". I don't see that contradicted in VP2. Whatever I see, the sun, moon, stars, lightning,

andother luminaries that the mantra mentions, when I see them (assuming VP2 prakriyA), by whose light do they shine?

They all still shine by "its" lightalone. They cannot make themselves seen/known until there is a vRRitti in the mind

and there is chidAbhAsa in the VRRitti andthe chidAbhAsa is just a reflection of chit which is Atma/Bramhan. So all of those

continue to shine by chit alone in VP2 as well.

  

So I don't see a contradiction!

  

I am of course assuming the AbhAsavAda prakriya here because I have used chidAbhAsa borrowed

from chit which is available as sAKshi chaitanyam and hence VRRitti doesn't need to go to the object and

object avacChina chaitanyam is not involved.

  

If you assumed avacChedavAda, then of course that will require the vRRitti avacChina chaitanyam to unify with the object avacChina chaitanyam. I see that you had mentioned that in your earlier email. If you used that model, then perhaps you may have a

dilemma. But with AbhAsavAda prakriyA, I don't really see a problem.

  

You wrote:

The prakriyA should explain why someone says "I know the object" when all they experience is the light of the object.  

  

I was thinking about that as well. And Raghavji had provided some indications about this in some earlier emails.

Just as VP can claim that by some \*mysterious\* method, the sUkShma
sharIra goes out to the object, VP2 can

claim that by some \*mysterious\* method, the image in the 2 eyes is translated into a holographic 3D view in the mind and

the seen object is placed in the right "location" in that space in the mind and the object shines exactly as it does in VP

at that location and the experience is "I know the object".

  

You wrote:
