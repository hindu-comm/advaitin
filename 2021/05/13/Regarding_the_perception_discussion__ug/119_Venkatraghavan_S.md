Correct. However, the contention is that VP2 is saying that the light is arriving at the eyes, and what is cognised is the light. A distinction is being made between the light and the object that reflects / emits that light. As the sAkshi never makes contact with the object in this theory, the object is never illuminated - only its image arriving at the eyes through the medium of light is seen.

  

The तस्य भासा सर्वमिदं विभाति mantra is not just saying that everything seen is seen by consciousness - it is saying that *everything* is cognisedby consciousness (येनेदंसर्वंविजानाति). The former would imply that things there are things uncognisedby consciousness,and hence remain unseen, which is not accepted within siddhAnta.

Even in the AbhAsavAda prakriyA, it is the viShaya adhiShThAnachaitanya that is admitted to be the illuminator of the object,and for the pramAtA to know the object,the antah karaNa pratibimbita chaitanya, vRtti pratibimbita chaitanya and viShaya adhiShThAna chaitanya becoming one is required, after the ignorance present in the viShaya adhiShThAnachaitanya is removed by the antahkaraNa vRtti, which leads to the latter illuminating the object.

  

Whether you call it viShaya adhiShThAna chaitanya or viShayAvacChinna chaitanya makes no difference in this context. Please refer the panchadashIverseघटैकाकार धीस्थाचित् घटमेवावभासयेत्\|घटस्य ज्ञातता ब्रह्मचैतन्येनावभासयेत्\|\|

  

The vArttika also says this only*परागर्थप्रमेयेषु या फलत्वेन सम्मता ।संवित् सैवेह मेयोऽर्थ: वेदान्तोक्ति प्रमाणत: \|\|*

vRtti pratibimbita chaitanya is called jnAna and viShaya abhivyakta chaitanya is phalam / samvit. This vArttika is saying that when ignorance is removed due to the rise of vRtti jnAna, the viShaya chaitanya becomes abhivyakta and is called phalam. This phalam or samvit is the one that reveals the object.

  

In response to a nyAyAmRtaquery whether it is the vRtti pratibimbita chaitanya or the viShaya adhiShThAna chaitanya that is the illuminator (घटप्रकाशकं चैतन्यमुपदेशसाहस्त्र्यनुसारेण घटाकारधीस्था चिद्वा ; परागर्थप्रमेयेष्वित्यादिवार्त्तिकोक्तरीत्या धीप्रतिबिम्बितचैतन्याभेदाभिव्यक्तविषयाधिष्ठानचैतन्यं वा), thesiddhikAra answers
-प्रकाशकं तावत् अधिष्ठानचैतन्यम् \| The substratum-consciousness of the object is the one that reveals the object.
