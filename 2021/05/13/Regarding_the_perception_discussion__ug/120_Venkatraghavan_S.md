[[Venkatraghavan S	2021-05-24, 00:28:43 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/ugam3gvCEMI)]]

"Even in the AbhAsavAda prakriyA, it is the viShaya adhiShThAnachaitanya that is admitted to be the illuminator of the object,and for the pramAtA to know the object,the antah karaNa pratibimbita chaitanya, vRtti pratibimbita chaitanya and viShaya adhiShThAna chaitanya becoming one is required, after the ignorance present in the viShaya adhiShThAnachaitanya is removed by the antahkaraNa vRtti, which leads to the latter illuminating the object."  

  

To clarify, the word "latter" in the last line of the above paragraph refers to viShaya adhiShThAna chaitanya. The presence of antahkaraNa vRtti just before the "latter", leads to an incorrect meaning that it is the vRtti illuminates. Not so.
