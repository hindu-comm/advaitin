Namaste Venkat ji

  

Thank you for drawing out the nuances of the various prakriyas. And showing clearly through references how acharyas taught it.

  

The siddhikAra asserts that the viShayaadhiSThAna chaitanyam alone (rather than vRtti pratimbimbita chaitanyaM) has to be regarded as the prakAshaka of the viShaya. In effect this implies that the viShaya has to be physically present/existent at the seen location at the instant of perception? Correct? And is that why you had written -

"I am not convinced that when two people see the same object, what they see is the same either."

  

To go along with this particular line of thinking given by you above (I had something similar in mind in saying that antaHkaraNa dharma or Ishvaraniyati is to unfold one vista or slice of space and time of a set of objects to one observer which need not necessarily be the same as the slice or frame of space and time of objects for another observer and this difference happens to become particularly obvious when they are far apart. Even current theories of perception would not disapprove of such observer-specific nature of perception. ) That would be one simple way of non-conflict of VP with empirical scientific observations. Did I get you correctly?

  

  

Om

Raghav

  

P.S. you wrote "whether it is the vRtti pratibimbita chaitanya or the viShaya adhiShThAna chaitanya that is the illuminator (घटप्रकाशकं चैतन्यमुपदेशसाहस्त्र्यनुसारेण घटाकारधीस्था चिद्वा ; परागर्थप्रमेयेष्वित्यादिवार्त्तिकोक्तरीत्या धीप्रतिबिम्बितचैतन्याभेदाभिव्यक्तविषयाधिष्ठानचैतन्यं वा), thesiddhikAra answers
-प्रकाशकं तावत् अधिष्ठानचैतन्यम् \| The substratum-consciousness of the object is the one that reveals the object."

  

So upadeshadAhasrI is taking a somewhat different position in positing ghatAkAradhIsthA cit (vRtti pratibimbita chaitanyam) as the prakAshaka?

  

Hypothetically, If we go along with upadesha sAshasrI rather than vArttika can we say that then VP2 would be acceptable since we could assert that even if the viShayAkAra-vRtti arises with a delay, it still is the determinant of authentic pratyaxa notwithstanding the possible absence of viShaya at the instant of perception.
