That the tatkAlAvacChinna viShaya is what is seen, is accepted in the VP also. That is why when we have a continuous cognition of a pot, there is a question raised there - every cognition only lasts a moment. If the cognition of the pot in the second second is objectifying the same pot, then that second cognition is no longer a pramA, because apUrvatA / anadhigatatva is not there for the second cognition. You are seeing the same pot, so that second cognition is not a valid cognition.

  

The answer provided is that the second pot cognition is not considered apramA because the jnAna continues to be apUrva - the pot may be the same in the first instance of the cognition and the second, but it is not the tatkAlAvacChinna viShaya in the second jnAna, so it is an apUrva jnAna, and hence it is also pramA.

No, the nyAyAmRtakArahas interpreted the panchadashIkAra/ upadeshasAhasrIkAra's views incorrectly. He wants to superimpose his intent on to them tothen level the charge that the dRshya cannot be superimposed on such a dRg (vRtti pratibimbita chaitanyam), because the dRshya exists even before the tadAkAravRtti arises, thus proving that the basis for dRshyatvAt being a sAdhaka of mithyAtva, the dRgdRshya AdhyAsika sambandha is invalid.

  

The abhiprAya of the two AchArya's is something different. They want to show how the tadAkAra vRtti pratibimbita chaitanya is necessary to remove ajnAna, because of which the adhiShThAna chaitanya illuminates. The adhiShThAna chaitanya by itself cannot illuminate the object so long as ajnAna is present in it. The tadAkAra vRttipratibimbita chaitanya is needed to remove that ajnAna.

  

If we read the acyutarAya TIkA to the panchadashI verse (8.4, quoted in my previous email घटैकाकारधीस्था चित्...) ([https://archive.org/details/PanchadashiModakaTika/page/n309/mode/2up](https://archive.org/details/PanchadashiModakaTika/page/n309/mode/2up)), it is said "एवं च 'अयं घट' इति ज्ञाने घटचिदाभासभास्य एव 'ज्ञातो घट' इति ज्ञाने त्वसौ घटारोपाधिष्ठानीभूतब्रह्मचैतन्यभास्य इति तयोर्विभागः स्फुट एवेति भावः".

  

panchadashIkAra himself clarifies in verse 8.16

घटोऽयमित्यसावुक्तिराभासस्य प्रसादतः ।

विज्ञातो घट इत्युक्तिर्ब्रह्मानुग्रहतो भवेत् ॥

  

The reason for that prakAshaka being the viShaya adhiShThAna chaitanya and not sAkshi chaitanya is provided by the siddhikAra -

  

तच्चाध्यासेन विषयै: सह साक्षात्संबद्धं प्रकाशस्य च स्वयं भासमानस्य स्वसंबद्धसर्वभासकत्वमपि क्लृप्तमेव ; एतदनभ्युपगमे कल्पनान्तरगौरवापत्ते: \|

That (substratum-consciousness) can illuminate the superimposed object only if it has a direct relationship with the object, ie of being the substratum of the superimposed object, like light, which (reveals itself and) illuminatesobjects that it is in direct contact with.  

  

The sAkshAt sambandha between the prakAshaka and prakAshya is expected for illumination to occur. That can occur only for the adhiShThAna chaitanya, and not the sAkshi, or the vRtti pratibimbita chaitanya.
