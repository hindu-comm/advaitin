In this latter VP2 prakriya too, there is only a minor modification (i should think); there is direct contact and abheda between viShayAvacchinnachaitanyam and pramAtRaavacvhinna chaitanyam - \*only the location\* where the abheda occurs is at the location of the pramAtA.  

  

There are two versions possible.

  

1) One where vRRitti pervades the rUpa/agni tattva avacChinna chaitanyam which alsoremoves the ignorance (rUpa ignorance) and

then some mysterious power of the mind projects the objectout at the exact location.

  

2) Alternatively, where abheda between vRRitti avacChinna and pramAtRRiavachChina chaitanyam as you have proposed....only the location changes.

  

I think that the former is a more pragmatic/simpler vishacChAya model.

The latter is also possible and might be more similar to VP.....but I wonder if it might inherit some of the VP problems

that we are trying to solve....but could be a fallback if there are unsurmountable problems with \#1.....was what I was thinking.

  

Do you see any issues with \#1? I have tried to explain my understanding of "tasya bhAsa..." in the other email

to Venkat-ji, and why it is not contradicted in my understanding....and why no direct contact may not be a fault etc...

What is your view? Perhaps Swamiji's view would also help here.

  

If we look into the fundamental reasons why VP wanted vRRitti to contact the object, it is probably two fold:

1) To remove ignorance of the object by pervading it

2) To spatially locate it

3) Do you see any other reasons?

  

If in \#1, ignorance can be removed by pervading the viShayacChAya (rUpa guNa in agni), and if \#2 can be addressed by the mysterious powerof the mind to project the object AkAra vRRitti exactly at the location of the object....then do you really need the mind to contactthe object?
