Namaste Satyan ji,

I think we are slightly going in circles. I have presented my understanding of panchadashI with supporting TIkA / textual references. If there is another interpretation that works better for you, that is totally fine.

  

The siddhikAra does not take any position, be it as an AbhAsavAdin or an avacChedavAdin - he sets out to prove that every prakriyA within advaita is correct and defensible. The context of the discussion here is simply one where pratikarmavyavasthA is being discussed.

  

Will only respond to the one point that you raised about mAyA, avidyA etc not being dRshya, which to me appears to be a new one. To clarify, dRshya does not mean being objects of a jIva's cognition. dRshyatvam simply means चिद्विषयत्वम्, they are objects of consciousness,having some relationship with consciousness - that applies to mAya, etc.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
