Namaste Venkat-ji,

  

Just a brief comment on what you wrote:

dRshyatvam simply means चिद्विषयत्वम्, they are objects of consciousness,having some relationship with consciousness - that applies to mAya, etc.  

  

"Relationship with consciousness" can be at two levels depending on whether you are talking about chit or chidAbhAsa.

Now, in the context of VP2:

  

1\) As for chit, everything is still pervaded by chit. Why?

Because we agree that all objects are mithyA. Therefore they are pervaded by sat. Thus, they are pervaded by chit also for sat=chit. This includes the entire jagat...without any exception (mAya, kAraNa sharIra, sukShma sharIra etc are not exceptions either).

  

2\) As for chidAbhAsa, everything may not be/need not be illumined by chidAbhAsa: Why?

It is already the case e.g mAya, kAraNa sharIra, sUkShma sharIra and the "underlying object" etc are never illumined by chidAbhAsa. In the last case, only the object's guNas are.

  

Thus, I see no difference in VP and VP2 in both these respects. Just wanted to clarify that part in case it wasn't clear enough (or perhaps it already was).

  

In any case, I understand your view/concern. Thanks for sharing your thoughts.

  

regards,
