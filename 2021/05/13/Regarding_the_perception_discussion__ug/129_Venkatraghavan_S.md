Namaste Satyan ji,

  

Ok, thank you for the clarification. As I said before, I don't particularly see a problem with mithyAtvam with VP2, precisely because everything is chidviShaya.

  

My question was primarily around sarvaprakAshakatva. I am still not convinced VP2 works for that.

  

The Sanskrit TIkAs to the PanchadaSI (such as the rAma tIrtha, acyuta rAya moDak etc) / advaita siddhi etc are quite clear that it is the vRttyabhivyakta adhiShThAna chaitanya that is the prakAshaka. The vichAra sAgara interprets the chidAbhAsa as the prakAshaka. However, doing so does open it to the charge from the nyAyAmRta that the dRshya cannot be postulated to be superimposed on such a dRg.

  

As to the comment that only the guNas are cognised and so you still don't have sarvaprakAshakatva in VP, that is a limitation of the senses - the eyes can only perceive form and colour, the skin can only perceive touch, etc. That does not mean that consciousness does not illuminate everything. As Shankaracharya saysअपि चाविद्यादिमतः संसारिणः शरीराद्यपेक्षा ज्ञानोत्पत्तिः स्यात् ; न ज्ञानप्रतिबन्धकारणरहितस्येश्वरस्य .

  

For these reasons I prefer an amalgamation of the the two theories of perception , a description of which I provided sometime back.

  

Anyway, with that I will wind down the discussion on any new points from my side. Feel free to respond to any of the above.

  

Thanks to you and everyone who participated for an illuminating conversation.

  

shrI gurubhyo namah.
