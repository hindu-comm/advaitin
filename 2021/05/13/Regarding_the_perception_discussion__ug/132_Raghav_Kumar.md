Namaste Satyan ji

Thanks again for a helpful discussion.  
You wrote

  
"I would add Lightning, mirror image and vision correcting glasses on the list (and why the latter doesn't cause representationalism)."  

Indeed I too had mentioned that in one mail. But before that....

An overarching observation first. My understanding is that any vedantic perception explanation (like VP or anything else) is only an anuvAda or elaboration of what is really non-falsifiable being at the manas+indriya level. When i open my eyes and I give my attention to what is presented in terms of what I experience as space-time-objects before my eyes, a vRtti is formed. I do not experience a distance between the objects I see and "my" consciousness - that is the only restricted way i would understand the mind going out. VV and PV are non-falsifiable (in the scientific sense), being experienced all the time (non*-*falsifiable as much as my experience of a vivid color like red can never be contradicted by any physical model or *theory of* colors). That is the direction I am leaning. - \*Any\* explanation at the manomaya level is inherently an " inside to outside" scheme. (From sAxI to pramAtA to indriyas to viShayas.) tasya bhAsA sarvamidam vibhAti, - and it remains scientifically non-falsifiable.

Current science tells me that electro-magnetic field theory and gravity
is not consistent with the empirically observed photoelectric effect, gravitational lensing etc., and diffraction patterns observed of electrons implies they are waves etc., and then finally all these theories of particles and waves have been found to be \*inconsistent\* with recent observations of non-locality and instantaneous-action-at-a-distance. So we are asked by science to look at them (\*words\* like light, electrons, gravity etc) as merely useful mathematical models/conceptual entities (like complex numbers, derivatives of a wave function, etc) to predict future pratyaxa experiences. No physical reality is being claimed for them by current science. It can be argued that this implies that it is in fact the mathematical models or mathematical objects popularly called light, gravity, electrons, quantum wave function etc., which are actually the viShayacchAya or a mental map or representation or shorthand for groups of irreducibly manomaya pratyaxa experiences. Quantum mechanics gives more space compared to earlier physics for asserting such primacy of pratyaxa and the merely mathematical or platonic reality of any scientific entity (light, electrons etc.).

We can therefore only fall back on what is the \*unchanging basic raw data\* viz., experiences of sensory inputs + mind, these being primary *data.*

You had written  
"How about the gravitational case (mercury behind the sun but seen next to it?)"

Whatever space-time-objects frame becomes available as per Ishvara, I experience it by the same pratyaxa vRtti process -VV and PV etc. As you can see, I am rather conveniently relegating the "why" and the details of "how" (mercury etc) of whatever is experienced, the specific content of pratyaxa, and the inferences and theories regarding these coordinating these perceptions to our \*inferential reading\* of the Ishvara sRShTi/order of space-time about which pratyaxa gives the irreducible data*.* Any such inferential theory cannot alter the \*a priori validity\* of (all) pratyaxa before any anumAna is brought in . This is svataHprAmANyavAda - every means of knowledge is self-validating, as Sri Swami Dayanandaji would often put it. Thus the detailed description of the process of experiencing pratyaxa is something non-falsifiable being only a detailed elaboration of what is self-evident. Any inferential theory that i try to formulate cannot come back to me , so to say, and negate or even dilute the prAmANyam of the entire corpus of my pratyaxa prama as a shadow of some unknown and unknowable original object prapancha - it may negate some of the pratyaxa pramA-s as bhrama/prAtibhAsika but not the whole corpus of *pratyaxa*.This is the basic objection to representationalism. I am.still looking at if there a workaround or maybe an error in the way I framed it.

To restate it in another way, the objection to representational models would be-  
*"*That my inferences (based on pratyaxa) make me conclude that \*all\* my pratyaxa experiences are of shadows of another real world of objects out there, is regarded as parataHprAmAnyam of pratyaxa and is not in consonance with vedanta"

Regarding other things, even the reflection seen in a true mirror is traditionally labelled prAtibhAsika but its still useful - a variety of samvAdI bhrama. The label prAtibhAsika need not mean anything delusional as the widespread use of mirrors shows. I might see two stars if there is an intervening large object gravitational lens - prAtibhAsika but gives useful knowledge.

*You wrote - "*The explanation of things such as lightning, doppler effect, partially immersed objects etc by CT proponents looked quite complicated and somewhat unintuitive (at least to me). "

I agree. Any VP-based CT overreaches if it even tries to get in to nitty gritty to explain such phenomena. I would prefer to look at VP (if necessary with some modifications and clauses) as a mind level overlay or counterpart of some useful but not necessarily complete theory/model like *the "*instantaneous wave function collapse by observation" etc - please note how \*agency is being restored to the pramAta\* in these recent theories - the properties of the 'object' are only "created' by or during the fact of observation and remain in limbo when not being *observed - a reversion to the primacy or irreducibility* pratyaxa.
The vedantic explanation of perception is at the qualia or manomaya level and remain the \*same\* for eternity but the physical level theories can change from time to time. Hence, VP is not the place to look for, for explaining the specific contents of pratyaxa prama. Examining the particular contents of pratyaxa to form explanations/theories of those contents (using simpler postulates and fewer entities to avoid gaurava dosha/Occam's razor) is not what I would expect from a qualia/sUxma level description of the moment to moment pratyaxa experience.

Another of the problems, as I understand, with a representational views of perception, we have to rely on shabda or anumAna to draw any conclusion about whether our pratyaxa prama is veridical or not (true to the nature of the original object or not). But we should not have to bring in anumana, upamAna etc., to say that the image we experience is \*exactly the same as\* the actual object. Pratyaxa prAmANyam has to stand on its own feet as giving veridical knowledge. Also the original objects whose chAya alone we are perceiving, are all nitya paroxa in VP2. This may not be a problem if only some objects are nitya paroxa (as is in fact the case with dharma etc.) but if we relegate the \*entire pratyaxa world of the original viShayas\* to a nitya paroxa realm whose shadow alone is perceptible, that seems to compromise the prAmAnyam of all pratyaxa pramA.

Like I said there may some workarounds which i am not clear *about,* to defend a representational looking framework of VP2- Can we assign*the* original viShayas, whose indriya-pratyaxa chAya is directly seen, as sAxI pratyaxa. I had mentioned the Swamiji spoke of a sort of \*abheda\* of the tejas in light with the surface of the object. Could that address the need for sarvaprakAshatvaM - conceding we experience only viShayacchAyA but original objects are sAxI pratyaxa? But need to substantiate/think through these.

I hope to re-read the references and overall context provided by you and Venkat ji regarding the chidAbhAsa-bhAsya and
adhiShThAna-chaitanya-prakAshya ideas.

*The Acharya* Swamiji is hard to get on the phone. But will keep it in mind to articulate more precisely all these ideas and issues next time I go to meet him.

To come back to correctional glasses etc., if atleast \*some\* of the pramA of any pramAtA are given direct or non-representational status, the rest can be adjusted one way or the other. The problem is only a matter of principle.

Regarding pragmatism - that is the bottom line. - *the* dream tiger example is indeed a sign that even an imperfect understanding of theories of perception etc., need not prevent the rise of the brahmAkAra vRtti.

Apologies if I have labored some of the ideas in my post making it long.
