Obviously there is no official "CT explanation" for these examples. There is a basic CT principle that has to be preserved by the supporting logic and explanations.

  

This is: When obstruction to the jiva's cognition/jnana of object is removed, there is cognition of the object and naught else.

  

1\. Removal of obstruction involves a change in manifestation of the jiva'supadhis relative to the object. For example, opening of eyes.

  

2\. When the obstruction is removedat the body-mind-world levels, the consciousness or awareness is automatically expanded to include the corresponding unobstructed range in Ishvara'smanifestation.

  

3\. The principle here is that you are like all-pervading Space. The pot in question is a certain obstruction due to upadhis, wherein there is conditioned awareness or ajnana. Modify this and your cognition of the object in the "outside space" is instantaneous and direct, because you have only become aware of the Knowledge of manifestation that is in the Self that you are and not something outside of the Self.

  

(It is still a question to me why the jiva's obstruction should be removable to include cognition of external objects in the first place. But in the traditional or CT position, pratyaksha is really pramana for knowledge of objects, so in that case yes this happens.)

  

So the issue is the type of obstruction that the objects in front of the pencil poses. If the glass for example is suitably close to being vision correcting, then it may not be an obstruction for the sense organ; it may act as a natural correction to the defectivegross organ (I am taking this idea from Chitta-ji's book.) and hence we may say that direct cognition is possible.

  

Or in the other perspective where there is a layering of vrittis due to direct contact of composite of objects, the vritti formed from the vision of the non-distorting glass may not superimpose in a distorting manner that skews the vritti formed of the pencil. Whereas in the distorting case, the layering gives the bent appearance.

  

On the other hand, if the glass is bent or distorting, our cognition may be limited to its surface and what we see simply the light come onto itssurface.

  

Note that the vritti formation is not necessarily causative of cognition. Jiva's cognition is of the Self's cognition conditioned in the context of the jiva's upadhis; it is automatic to the extent ajnana is removed. However this cognizing corresponds with the projection of vrittis in the jiva's body-mind. This is done by Ishvara. The fact that all this is regarded as being instantaneous means that one can describe the process in whichever logical sequence one is comfortable with. Like sense-organs going out to object, vritti forms, light of self shines on vritti, finally cognition takes place. OK fine; but the philosophical basis is simply: remove ajnana, jnana is already there, and here and now shines in awareness. You the Self as jiva are cognizing what You the Self are projecting/cognizing as Ishvara - only your awareness in the context of the jiva upadhis will be partial.
