Namaskaram Satyan-ji,

>   
>
> 1\. Removal of obstruction involves a change in manifestation of the
> jiva'supadhis relative to the object. For example, opening of
> eyes.  
>
>   
>
> 2\. When the obstruction is removedat the body-mind-world levels, the
> consciousness or awareness is automatically expanded to include the
> corresponding unobstructed range in Ishvara'smanifestation.

  

“removed at body-mind-**world** levels” is because the Ajnana that conditions or limits total knowledge for a jiva is also manifest in factors involving the world relative to jiva’s upadhis, like distance, darkness, etc.
