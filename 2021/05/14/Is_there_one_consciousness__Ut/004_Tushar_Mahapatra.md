How can we know?



My 2¢:

We mere mortals are all like different colors. But white color is not actually a color, but a combination of all colors. The common consciousness would be like white color. As non-white colors, to know what it is to be white color, we have to transform ourselves into white color. Maybe as we become lighter and lighter in color, we may increasingly be able to sense what it is to be white color.
