Namaste - If I may add, what is illusory or unreal is our imaginary ideas, dreams and fantasies projected by the mind on What-Is.  

  

One is projecting a fictional layer of delimiting ideations; childish notions of permanence, security and eternal happiness on that which is actually transitive and evanescent, eventually resulting only in frustration and misery.  

  

The mature Viragi endowed with Viveka understands and sees the futility of what Freud call's "man's discontent and the pursuit of objective happiness".  

  

In Nirodha, the stoppage of the quest, the spell that keeps one enchained to delusional thoughts, ideas and concepts is broken and one is re-established in Stillness.

  

A living self-aware Silence that is undisturbed and indivisible from \*this\* amazing transitional display of Mahamaya (Vyavaharika), As-it-Is.   

  

"Just like a magical display, a shimmering mirage, a sparkling city in the sky, is this universe seen for those versed in Vedanta"**. ~ Mandukya Karika  **
