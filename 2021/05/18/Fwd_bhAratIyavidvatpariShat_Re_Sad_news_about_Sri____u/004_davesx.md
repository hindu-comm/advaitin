Additionally, my friend Dr Surujrattan Rambachan, brother of Dr Anantanand Rambachan, Professor of Religion, Philosophy and Asian Studies, describes below his own direct experience with the infection, his trials and his simple mitigations.  

  

  

"*I do not know whether my fellow citizens understand what it means to be afflicted with Covid. In effect no one will be able to understand the danger to life unless you are afflicted. It is academic to discuss your experience with those who have not had the same experience. Some have had a mild infection. Mine was severe and I do believe that I was helped by the fact that I had taken the astra Zeneca first shot. This mitigated the severity of the affliction.  
  
I am still to understand how I contracted it although I had the first shot.  
  
My son also got it and has had to fight harder than me because his oxygen fell below 80. Thank God we had oxygen at home and an oxygen generator which was used in the fight.  
You can’t fight this alone. You need help and 24 hour monitoring. My daughter Ranjana is a hero. Throwing caution to the wind in her own case she never stopped working with us for the past eleven days. Medicines on time. Monitoring of oxygen blood pressure pulse heart rates and sharing it daily with the surveillance unit who were calling every day to see how we were progressing.  
  
She did it all supported by advice from Dr Tim Gopeesingh, Dr Rai Ragbir amongst other doctors who volunteered to monitor our condition as well as by her constant research on the internet. I recall her horror when our oxygen levels suggested to the surveillance unit that we needed the ambulance. She got the oxygen and fought with us. We survived thanks to her dedication  
  
My neighbors of 32 years Susan and Indra have kept bringing us nutritious food. They are not just neighbors. They are friends in the truest definition of the word.  
  
Covid saps your very will to continue. When I first contracted it I could not understand why I was struggling to walk twenty feet. I wanted to lie down wherever I was and on some days I just lay down on the bare concrete bench unable to move. The energy just did not exist. It is at that time that you had to remind yourself of what was happening to you.  
The challenge was so severe that even brushing your teeth or going to have a bath took an incredible extraordinary effort. Yes. All of this is true. And I am sure that this experience is not just mine but several who have been afflicted.  
  
The real danger to me was the level of oxygen and the short breath. In the case of the short breath I practiced deep breathing. Even then as you tried to deep breathe you were coughing. So you had to breathe in shorter time spans. What helped was inhalation of steamed eucalyptus oil etc, with a head covered by a towel to get the full impact of the steam and fumes.  
  
Neem, fever grass, turmeric and ginger all boiled into a tea and consumed twice a day cleared the breathing passages. It did not taste good but it was effective.  
  
Sleep was seriously affected. Time did not matter. You walked the house at night afraid to fall asleep lest you did not get up. You prayed and prayed and prayed. And God answered. He sent his angels with messages. Work no longer mattered. Money was seen for what it is, a means to an end not an end in itself. If you were dead what the hell would happen to the work. Survive survive survive. That is the mantra. Thank God that I am lucky to have enough to buy what I needed. Nothing mattered except survival. With people dying in increasing numbers while you were ill you became frightened listening to the news. I often turned off the news.  
  
I was reminded about the image in Bhaja Govindam of the water drop playing on the lotus leaf. It has an unstable existence and could fall off at any point in time. Covid places you in that zone. Covid tells you how unstable life is. Covid tells you how little control you have except your faith and will power. I promised myself that I will beat Covid. I have kept telling my son that we are going to survive and be better.  
  
I reminded myself that I have work to complete especially a shrine I am building to honour the memory and lives of our ancestors. I envisaged ten years into the future of what I want to see and do in that building. I am giving myself a purpose to live for and to survive and grow in the strength I need. I am physically weak after these three weeks. I envisage that it will take months to really recover. Long term Covid is a real possibility.  
  
After Covid no one absolutely no one except God will dictate my LIfe. I will live for me because I have understood once again the meaning of the gift of a human birth. I want to live to sing the glories of the Ramayana, to chant Kirtan and bhajans to inspire others by my singing to reach for a higher faith and a deeper devotion  
  
Yes I will beat Covid. I will not be defeated. I will define and redefine my purpose and out of that develop the passion to continue to achieve. And no one will deter me. No one. I will live for me.  
  
Accept it or not but when you survive Covid you have been given a second life. Use it purposefully. I thank God for his mercy his love his forgiveness and his compassion. Tomorrow is a new day. A new opportunity to look forward but not to forget what I have been fighting."*
