Thanks for sharing this incident. Pujya Swamiji had famous long-pause-beginnings where he would look around the room from left to right and back again and yet again, many a time. Most would wonder as to what is happening! He would then try and fit the talk to the audience as otherwise he saw nothing wrong in the way things were. This is also why each person who approached Pujya Swamiji would find comfort in him as he would immediately put the person in a comfort zone, very particular to the person!

  

Daya was the only name that Sw. Chinmayanandaji found to fit right while naming Pujya Swamiji, the latter living by his own words as to how compassion is the dynamic form of love and how it manifests more and more with jnAna.
