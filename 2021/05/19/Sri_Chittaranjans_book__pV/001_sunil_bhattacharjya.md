Dear Dennisji,

  

We are aware that the advanced rishis of the ancient times could sit in
his dhyana (with closed eyes) and could see things in the far off places with the spiritual eye, in the sense that the a rishi closes his eyes and his astral body (leaving the material body behind, but remaining in contact by means of an individual string) goes to the far off places to see/find the things for himself. This is obviously beyond the scientific explanation.  

  

As one who read Shri Naik's book, you may be able to compare this ancient technique in the light of Shri Naik's approach. Awaiting your comments.

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
