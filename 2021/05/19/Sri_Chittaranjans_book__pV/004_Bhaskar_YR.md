Hare Krishna



Through one of the siddhi-s only shankara caused the kanaka dhAra and did the parakAya pravesha (left his body temporarily and entered king’s body to gain practical knowledge of kAma shAstra – vide ref. shankara Vijaya) and in some of the atharvaNa Upanishads there is mention of chakra-s like mUlAdhAra, svAdhishtAna and nAdi-s like ida, pingala, sushumna etc. Shat chakra-s are fundamentals in rAja, hata yOga or kundalini yOga. And in our hindu tradition yOga is the shAstra.
Traditional Advaita Achaarya-s too talked a lot about chakra-s, aNimAdi siddhi-s etc. So these are not a push aside mere apocryphal stories atleast in hindu tradition though not directly related to advaita’s jnana mArga.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
