In the 'Vedanta Tattvaloka' of Janardana (who is identified with Anandagiri, the Teekaakaaraof the Bhashyakara, we find an interesting epithet being used to denote Advaita: मायामतम् (Maayaamatam). This is a synonym for Maayaavaada/i, used by Bhamati, a few times.

  

This is not a pejorative usage by an opponent but a factual denoting of Vedanta (by the Vedantin himself), which is opposed to all the other schools such as Sankhya, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, etc. which hold the world, bandha, bheda, etc. to be real.

  

The term in the Tattvaloka occurs in the first pariccheda while refuting the Bhaskara doctrine of bhedAbheda. He accepts a Shakti that brings about the Parinama (in Brahman?) and the Advaitin asks whether that Shakti is asatya or satya. If it is the former, then it falls into theमायामतम् .
