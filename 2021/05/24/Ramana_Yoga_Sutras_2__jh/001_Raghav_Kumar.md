> One controversial idea by today's yardsticks of love, compassion and
> political correctness., is the passage you quoted -

>   
>
> "The master teaches that we should strictly  
>
> abandon all activities, contacts and social functions of the  
>
> world, whether good or bad, whether for individual or for  
>
> social benefit. The aspirant should be a ‘nissanga’ (nonattached).  
>
> He should not allow himself to be carried away  
>
> by such propaganda suggesting that ‘living in society means  
>
> participation in it for the good of society’, or for that matter,  
>
> for any portion of society or even for individuals".
>
>   
>
>   
>
>   

> When someone objected to such a seemingly 'selfish' pursuit, Sri
> Ramana's reply was along the lines -" the greatest service you can do
> to the world is to strive for self-knowledge."
>
>   
>
> The idea being that much damage to the world is done by non-discerning
> do-gooders particularly when other do-gooders as seen to get in their
> way of doing good to the world!
