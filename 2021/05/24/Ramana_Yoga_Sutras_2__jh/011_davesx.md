Ø May I know where exactly ramaNa said realized man has to destroy his mind and world should vanish for him !!??

  

There are numerous instances where this ans similar questions about the world are recorded, granted Sri Ramana wrote no books. One such is talk 188 of 'Talks with Ramana M.' where he advised the questioner to destroy the mind to achieve liberation and the questioner asked how to do this.
