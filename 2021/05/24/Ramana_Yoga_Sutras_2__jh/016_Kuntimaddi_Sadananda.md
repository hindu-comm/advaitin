With salutations to Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi - I would like to point out that we have taken Bhagavan's text - Upadesha saara in one of our two-day spiritual camps.

maana santu kim maargane kRite

naiva maanasam maarga aarjavat

Sloka has to be properly understood in the general background of previous slokas of that book.

It is the destruction of the notional mind that looks at the objective world as real.

This sloka has to be understood in relation to the slokas

aajya dhaarayaa srotasaa samam

sarala chintam virala tatparam

Where the mind has to meditate on the truth like the flow of ghee or like the flow of the river - where it gets absorbed from intermittent to fully absorbed in the nature of reality. Ghee example indicates the sneha bhaavana - where the mind is devoted to the goal and river example is given to show that persistent committed flow like the river trying to reach the ocean to merge with it - smallobstacles it jumps over with gurgling joy and the big obstacles like mountains it gracefully goes around even taking few back steps but never forgetting its goal to reach the ocean where the individual notions that I am this is merged with like the salt doll immersed in the ocean as the upanishad says.

Evaluating whether someone has realized or not is unintelligent avigation. If it helps, follow otherwise, seek some other teacher.
