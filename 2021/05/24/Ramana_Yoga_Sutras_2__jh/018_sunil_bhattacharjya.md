Namaste Sadanandaj,  

  

Though Lord Ram taught the way of Manonasha and dhyana on the ultimate oneness in his avyaya state, it is not that simple to assimilate. Lord Krishna the Paripurna Brahman avatara taught the same thing in a much simpler way, considering the leanings of the people of the ending
-Dwapara and the Kali-yuga . The Lord taught us "What was not there in the beginning and will not be there at the end, is as good as not there (even) at present". Once one understands this, the pratyahara, with practice, becomes easier and the Pratyahara is the first step in becoming the Sthitaprajna, as the Lord shows.
