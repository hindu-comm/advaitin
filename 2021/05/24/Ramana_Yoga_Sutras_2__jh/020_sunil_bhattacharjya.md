Tracing the causes is interesting. King Janaka knew what was to come and he solemnised the marriage of Jaanaki on the most auspicious day to avert the inevitable, yet the couple had to separate, in the material sense. Appears that astrologically the Saturn’s position was responsible. These ups and downs of human life should be treated as if not happened and that helps in the spiritual journey.
