One more thing from Lord Ram’s life. Ravana had a boon, by that nobody other than man could kill him and it became a must for Lord Ram to be like a normal human being.

Lord Ram could immediately recognize goddess parvati and addresses her as mother. Then goddess Parvati appeared in her own form and said that she wanted to check whether he was aware of his divinity.
