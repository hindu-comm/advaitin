Dear friend,

  

Yes, Swamiji is right when he said the following:  

**Swami Sarvapriyananda, has repeatedly said that after realization, the world and mind does not end or disappear.**

What Swamiji did not say here, is that this is strictly for the Dvaitins and the Vushshtadvains, who never leave the world.

  

For the Adavaitin, the world and mind disappear on Mukti. Lord Ram had said about the four types of Muktis. The Bhagavatam also said that. May be, the next time you hear his talk, you can request the Swamiji, to explain the four types of Muktis, to you.  

  

Sincerely,
