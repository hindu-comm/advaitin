Suniji - PraNAms

  

Even for Advaitin the world does not disappear also - Hed understands that whatever seen is mithyaa and only the conscious entity, the seer alone real.

  

What is real is also defined clearly in the Mandukya kaarika. Perceptibility, transactability, utility etc., do not make some thing real - only the abhaaditatvam or eternity or nithyatvam. 'tri kaala abhaaditam satyam' -that which is present in the waking state, in the dream state and in the deep-sleep state - that which transcends the space and time - that alone is real and the rest is false - not unreal- meaning mitya. - which is sat asat vilaksanam - that means neither real not unreal. Hence it cannot disappear.

  

Dvaitins and Vishishtadvaitins do not subscribe to mithyaa catagory. For them what is not sat is asat and what is not asat is sat. Bhagavan Ramanuja's criticism of Advaita in his mahaapurvapaksha is primarily centered on the avidya aspect of Advaita. He makes seven objections and many of them involve mithyaa aspect of Advaita.

  

One has to deal with the world - just as Lord Krishna dealt with the world - ready to use chakram when needed.



Hence Advaitin does not become a doormat for others to step on saying that is unreal.

  

The world becomes a play - jagannaatakam. One has to live dharmic way even after realization that it is false. That living also is a drama.

  

Bhagavan Ramana maharshi lived like a sage. He felt hurtful when people were unnecessarily over cutting the mango leaves from the tree - for the sake of puja. He was helping in the kitchen when he could.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
