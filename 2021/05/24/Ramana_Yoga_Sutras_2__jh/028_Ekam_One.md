SKB Sir,  

  

yes. Scientists and many people "know for sure" that there is no real matter. But same people still buy lands, build homes, pay fortunes to acquire gold and diamonds. Vyavaharikam continues..  

  

Similarly, after realizing that Sky is not really blue, and sun doesn't really rise in the east, we still say the sky is blue and enjoy it. We still refer to sunrise and sunset for vyavaharika purposes.

  

In the same way, The mind and world doesn't disappear for enlightened people. if it did, we wouldn't have any Guru's nor Vedic scriptures. The wheel of Prarabda karma keeps running for a long "time" after enlightenment.
