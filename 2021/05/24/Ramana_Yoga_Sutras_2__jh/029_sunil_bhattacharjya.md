Dear Ekamji,

  

Unfortunately people forget that there are four ashramas in Hinduism, though there are exceptions, in some individual cases. Adi Shankara became a  Bala-Sanyashi. Otherwise, the last ashrama of Sanyasha is the time for Vairagya after one has discharged the duties of the previous Ashramas. So there should not be any confusion. One can start Pratyahara at any age, depending on one's family - situation, educational and environmental conditions**;** but it becomes easier to develop Vairagya and start Advaita - sadhana in advanced age.
