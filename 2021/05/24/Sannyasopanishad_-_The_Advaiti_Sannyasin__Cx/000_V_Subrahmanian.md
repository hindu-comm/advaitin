The Sannyasopanishad, listed in the Muktika 108, prescribes a practice that is in vogue among Advaitin Sannyasins.

  

No shikha, yajnopavita, one staff and bhasmadhaarana:   

  

परमहंसः शिखायज्ञोपवीतरहित: पञ्चगृहेषु करपात्री एककौपीनधारी। शाटीमेकामेकं वैणवं *दण्डमेकशाटीधरो वा भस्मोद्धूलनपर: सर्वत्यागी।॥**  *

  

This is also found in the Narada parivrajaka upanishad:
