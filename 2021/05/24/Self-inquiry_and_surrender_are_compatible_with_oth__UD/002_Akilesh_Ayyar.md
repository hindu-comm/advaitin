Namaste,

  

Self may be said to be opposed to forms and objects, not opposed to them, or beyond both opposition and non-opposition.

  

Similar answers could be given to your other questions, because the answer to all questions touching on ultimate truth, which is beyond words, depends upon the context of the questioner and the purpose for which he asks the questions.

  

Perhaps it would be more productive for you, if you desire a fruitful discussion, to state your questions clarifying those issues, or perhaps by stating your disagreement with what you believe my position to be.

  

Akilesh
