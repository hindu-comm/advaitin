Dear friends,

  

What is Bhakti?

  

According to my understanding the true Bakti is as follows :

 adhyAtmayOgAdhigamEna dEvaM

 matvA dhirOharShaSOkaU jahAti \|\|

 so says mantra 1-2-12 of KathOpanishad.



(1) Sri Shankara says :

 (a) paramAtmA hi sarveShAM naH AtmA

 (b) cEtasa Atmani samAdhAnam adhyAtmayOgaH \|\|



\(2\) mayyEva mana Adhatsva \|\| Bhagavadgita



\(3\) mayi buddhiM nivESaya \|\| Bhagavadgita



and finally

 svasvarUpAnusaMdhAnam BaktirityaBidhIyatE \|\|

Won't you think that a student of Vedanta who wants to be an Atmavit

should have this frame of mind during his journey from 'nAnu' to 'tAnu'?

Is my understanding correct?

I request the learned scholars to share their understanding.  

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa Murthy
