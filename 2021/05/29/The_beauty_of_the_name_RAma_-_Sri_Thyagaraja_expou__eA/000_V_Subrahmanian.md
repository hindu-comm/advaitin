The beauty of the name 'RAma' - Sri Thyagaraja expounds  

  

In his composition 'Evarani....' Sri Thyagaraja says the two letters in the name 'Ra-ma' denote the jiva akshara, seminal letters, in the Shiva mantra - NamaH shivAya and the Narayana Mantra - Namo NarayaNAya - 'ma' and 'ra'.
