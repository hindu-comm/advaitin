Namaskaram,

  

A Chair is not the same as a Table.

  

A Chair and a Table are distinct nama-rupa projections/cognitions/knowledge of Wood (+Maya).

  

The higher Knowledge is that both Chair and Table are in reality only Wood.

  

In the context of this knowledge, the Chair and Table are nama-rupa superimpositions of separate identities on Wood.

  

The Knower knows the non-dual Reality Wood amidst all the changing cognitions of Chair and Table; the ignorant see the changing nama-rupas as either belonging to a plurality of realities or as signifying the absence of reality.

  

From thestandpoint of seer-seen duality, it is Wood (+Maya) that appears/projects as Chair and Table.

  

What does "appearance" signify?

  

1\. The Chair is *not* unreal (asat) as it is a fact of cognition. It is real in the standpoint of seer-seen duality.

  

2\. The seer-seen duality however is sublated in the standpoint of the Substratum. From the standpoint of Wood, there is no Chair and Table duality which therefore is not absolutely real (sat) either.

  

3\. The Chair-Table existence is therefore mithya - neither real nor unreal, whereas the Wood is Satya.

  

We refer to this Reality, Brahman, with both positive word-pointers such as Satyam-Jnanam-Anantam or Sat-Chit-Ananda or pure Consciousness, and negative ones such as nirguna, nirvikara, nirvikalpa; advaitam.
