Putranji - PraNAms

  

As I understand

  

naama (name) involves knowing. Ruupa is attribute of the object. Jagat involves only these two - naaam ruupatmakam idam jagat.

  

knowing means a conscious entity involved.

  

1\. Pre-existence of a conscious entity involved before the existence of the object with its attribute(s) can be established.

2\. The conscious entity must have knowledge of the existence of the object before naming it.

3\. Existence of an object is established by the knowledge its existence - otherwise it is indeterminate - anirvachaneeyam - just as the existence of a chair in the dark room. From my point, unless I see it, its existence I cannot establish. It may be there, it may not be there.

4\. Others people can see it and establish it - Still I have to first establish the existence of the other people first and then I should have a faith in their words - that knowledge is shabda pramaana.

5\. It is similar to asking a question - if the tree falls down a distance forest - who would know it? The question becomes invalid until the question has ascertained that the tree indeed has fallen down - thus bringing back the conscious entity.

  

7\. **I am** the only conscious entity in this world.

  

8\. Hence there is world when I am in deep -sleep state.

  

9\. In science also the big bang occurred - where? wherever the fellow is sitting and extrapolating backwards the movement of all galaxies to the point where the started - that will be where the conscious entity doing that extrapolations.

10\. In essence - on name and form for jagat and there is substantive - Vedanta says the substantive is Brahman - tat aikshataa - bahu syaam - prajaayeyeti. It became many.

11\. Hence Shree Vidyaranya says in dRik dRisya viveka-

asti bhaati priyam ruupam naam chaityanca pancakam

adhyatrayam brahma ruupam, jagat ruupam thathaa dvayam.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
