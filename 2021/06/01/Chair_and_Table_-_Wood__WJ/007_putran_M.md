Namaskaram Sada-ji,

  

I am following srishti-drishti viewpoint which I understand as below:

  

Ishvara-jiva-jagat is anaadi. The chair exists not because a jiva sees it or not; it is the manifestation of the eternal Knowledge of Ishvara. The world does not vanish when the jiva enters deep-sleep state ("deep sleep" is not for nirguna Brahman, it is a state for the jiva only). So when we have a discussion based in seer-seen duality, we admit that Brahman in association with Maya manifests this nama-rupa universe and its Order. Brahman in association with ajnana or the body-mind upadhis (i.e. as jiva) has the power to desire, know, act in this universe. Etc. The point still is that Brahman is the nondual the substratum/Self/Reality of this seer-seen duality; it is Satya while the latter is mithya.
