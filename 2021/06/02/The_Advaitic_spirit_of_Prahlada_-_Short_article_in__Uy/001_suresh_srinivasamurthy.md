Namaste Sri Subbu-ji,

  

The slokas quoted by G.R.Patil may appear to be supporting svaroopa ikya.

But here some points that needs to be considered.

  

In the following sloka, Sri parAshara says, prahlAda's identificationwith Vishnu

is basedon tanmayatva (being absorbed in / inseparable)

  

Śrīparāśarauvāca:

Ēvaṁsan̄cintāyanviṣṇuṁabhedenātmanōdvija \|

**Tanmayatvam**avāpyagryammēnēcātmānamacyutam \|\| 1-20-1 \|\|  

  

Here is another sloka often quoted in the V.Advaita literature, where  

jiva / atma is explicitly identified as jnAnAnandamaya and shesha of

paramAtma:

  

nAyam dEvO na martyO vA na tiryak sthAvarOpi vA \|  
jnAnAnandamayastvAtmA SEshO hi paramAtmanah\|\|  

  

Also in the following sloka, Vishnu is identified with jagat, which is jada:

  

Namōstuviṣṇavētasmai**yasyābhinnamidaṁjagat**\|

Dhyēyaḥsajagatāmādyaḥsaprasīdatumē’vyayaḥ\|\| 1-19-82 \|\|  

  

There are many slokas like the one below where everything isidentified

as the body of Vishnu:

  

yAni mUrtAnyamUrtAni yAnyatrAnyatra va kvachit \|

santi vai vastujAtAni tAni sarvANi **tadvapuh** \|\| 1,22,86

  

As jagat is jada, there cannot be any svaroopa ikya but shareerAtma

ikya between Jagat and Vishnu, right?

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
