Jagat is jada, but it is Brahman expressing Itself as the chara-achara prapancha. So, in Advaita, by the method of 'bAdhAyAm sAmAnAdhikaraNyam', by negating the superimposition of names and forms, the jagat is Brahman alone, the Chaitanyam. This is also because the 'asti' and 'bhAti' that we experience with respect to the jaDa jagat, are borrowed from Brahman that is Sat and Chit.
