You are quite right, some put forward this (misguided) view even today. It comes from a misunderstanding of the meaning of a jnani, who is not a person. In fact, that is what jnana is: the recognition of non-personhood. Only people suffer from karma.



I suspect no number of debates on the topic will resolve it, because the essence of advaita cannot be arbitrated using ordinary logic alone. The true understanding of the scriptures cannot be had without the relevant inner viveka and drishti. The merely textualist mind can never grasp the mystery of ajata.



Ø prArabdha is just like an arrow which has already left the bow with force and the trajectory of which cannot be changed. So whether he is jnAni, or a mortal jeeva or an avatAra purusha irrespective of his status he has to experience the prArabdha karma phala. And due to which he will have the avidyA lesha as well (remnants of avidyA, it is not exactly the avidyA it is just like avidyA saMskAra etc. they justify).
Arguments goes like this, since shruti says tasya tAvadeva charaM yAvannimmokshyeta saMpatsya iti etc. and since current body of the jnAni is the phala of prArabdha karma of that particular jnAni,the karma which he has done when he was in avidyA vyavahAra, he has to shed his mortal coil (which is avidyAkruta) then only he will get complete liberation ( mukhya mukti) and when he is still living in his body and experiencing all the worldly activities even though he is samyak jnAni praarabdha karma and resultant avidyA lesha would act as a pratibandhaka to his mukhya mukti, hence an embodied jnAni can have only gaUNa mukti till his physical death. The very potential justification for this assertion they can find in bruhad bhAshya : shareeraarambhakasya karmaNo niyataphalatvaat “samyakjnAna prAptAvapi” avashyaMbhAvinI pravruttiH vAngmanaH kAyAnAM, labdavrutteH karmaNO baleeyastvAt mukteshvAdipravruttivat.
