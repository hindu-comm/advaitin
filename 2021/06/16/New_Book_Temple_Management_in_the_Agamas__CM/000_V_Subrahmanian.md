Temple Management in the Agama-s, by Dr. Deepa Duraiswamy gives a wonderful overview of the traditions of Saiva Agamic temple worship predominant in Tamil Nadu.

My book review - [https://swarajyamag.com/.../book-review-read-this-one-if...](https://swarajyamag.com/books/book-review-read-this-one-if-youre-looking-for-an-introduction-to-the-unique-cultures-and-traditions-around-hindu-temples?fbclid=IwAR39_8aHdmer7r0QU9hBiYCr5fNgTRsCzPyu1nUwd3FdZSBYFbzbbuHQxi4)

"The author is a management graduate from IIM Kolkata and hails from a traditional Adisaiva family. Such a background gives her a unique advantage to present the subject with the authenticity, intimacy and the rigour it requires...

It proposes the temple to be viewed as a service organisation and a non-profit, non-business entity in the modern management parlance, outlining that the temple, just like any other organisation, has its own unique requirements of the four types of resources.. The author has attempted to blend non-compromising traditional beliefs and a modern management-oriented approach without getting into any unnecessary conflict.."
