Hari Om Subbu Ji

Namaskaram

  

I too was captivated by the Thiruvachakam have started reading it now and then,. To your observation that பயின்று as you said does have a meaning "having learnt, practiced, got trained.". I was thinking going by that meaning and in the context of "நள் இருளில் நட்டம்" may refer to "under the influence of Maya's Tamas he has practiced very well how to navigate (dance) in this samsara. கள்ளப் புலக்குரம்பைக் கட்டு அழிக்க வல்லானே meaning he has capability to free the illusory bonds of this field ( Play field of Samsara)- also supports such a meaning.  
  

போற்றிப் புகழ்ந்திருந்து பொய்கெட்டு மெய் ஆனார்

மீட்டு இங்கு வந்து வினைப்பிறவி சாராமே

part of the same verse reinforces that understanding.

my humble prostrations to all great saints.
