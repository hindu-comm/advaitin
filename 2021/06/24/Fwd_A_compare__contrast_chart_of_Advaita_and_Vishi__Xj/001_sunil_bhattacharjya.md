Thank you. Sometimes I think we should call Advaita alone as the Vedanta, as the Vishistadvaita is yet to grasp the antim (final) teachings of the Vedas, given in the Upanishads. As regards the Dvaita, to my knowledge, Shri Madhvacharya called it tattva-vada but not as a school of Vedanta. Kindly correct me if you think I need.
