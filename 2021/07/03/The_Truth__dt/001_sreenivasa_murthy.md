Dear Sri Subramanian,

  

Simply to accept some statements as Truth

goes against the spirit of the Upanishads,

unless they have been established as facts  

which can be verified in one's own life by one and all.

and show/ teach how to establish them as facts of life

which can be verified by one and all.

Hence this posting.  

  

Thanking you in anticipation,

With respectful namaskars,
