Young scholar Shashikiran, in the course of his five-day discourse on Shatavadhani R.Ganesh's poems, is delving on the poem composed in Sringeri, ex tempore, on Shankaracharya. After explaining the purport of the poem, he joyfully expatiates on the beauty of Shankaracharya's bhashya literature and recites a verse of Gaudapada and another by Shankara.

The discourse, though in Kannada, is full of Sanskrit lines.
