It's an interesting article. It's attached to this email to anyone having trouble with the links. It makes a mostly but not entirely compelling case that Swami Vivekananda's teachings are in line with the Upanishads. It does, however, say that he believes that moksha comes directly from samadhi, which I'm not sure is a correct interpretation of his teachings, but I don't know enough about histeachings to dispute it.

  
Samadhi may be a precondition of knowledge, but it is not knowledge itself. That requires an act of discernment, of inward-lookingat what or who is experiencing that samadhi. The scriptural words -- or those ideas derived from whatever source or guru -- of course assist this process, by suggesting what to look for.
