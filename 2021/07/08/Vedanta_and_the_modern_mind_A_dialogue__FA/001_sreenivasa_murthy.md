Dear Sri Subramanian,

  

The following upanishad mantras reveal the same thought position

as told by the late pontiff of Shringeri . The mantras are as foloows :

  

ahamEvEdagaM sarvam \|\|Chandgya 7-25-1  

AtmaivEdagm sarvavam \|\| Chandgya 7-25-2  

Atmata EvEdagAm sarvam \|\|Chandgya 7-26-1

sarvagaM hyetad brahma \| ayamAtmA brahma\|\| Mandukya Mantra 2

  

The above mantras reveal facts of life  

which can be cognized by one and all.

So says Sri Shankara and declares that Sruti reveals existing facts only.

  

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa murthy
