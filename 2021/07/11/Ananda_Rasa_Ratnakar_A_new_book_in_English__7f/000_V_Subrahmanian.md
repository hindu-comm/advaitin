Brief about the book :  
  
‘ Ananda Rasa Ratnakar ’ is a collection of Hindi writings of Maharajshree Swami Akhandananda Saraswati (1911 – 1987), the great sage of Brindavan. Compiled by his disciple, Swami Govindanda Saraswati, the book is a collection of 366 articles, structured as a daily reader. On the persistent request of devotees, an English translation of the book was decided to be brought out, in four volumes. The current book is the first volume.
