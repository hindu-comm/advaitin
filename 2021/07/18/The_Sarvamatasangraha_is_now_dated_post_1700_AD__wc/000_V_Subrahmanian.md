There is a work'Sarvamatasangraha' whose author is unknown, which gives a somewhat detailed account of many schools of philosophy. In a paper by Marek Major, on this work, the author states on p.261:

  

This work is found to have quotations from the Manameyodaya, a work of Narayana Bhatta (the famed author of theNarayaneeyam) and the Tarkasangraha of Annambhatta. Both these works are of the 17th Century
As such the 'Sarvamatasangraha' is placed post 1700 AD.
