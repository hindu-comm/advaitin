Namaste Subbu-ji,

  

Actually, Dvaita is also based on the reality/irrefutability of the Atman/sAkshi.

  

They say sAkshi pratyaksha can never be refuted and jagat is real because it

is seen/dependent on the Atman which is real.

  

IMHO accepting jagat/body as "dependent reality" does not refute the non-duality

of Atman/Brahman. But it helps toestablish the reality of the unbroken line of

Guru-shishya parampara 🙂

  

Regards,
