> how do dvaitis interpret the maha vakyams?

  

For them there is no concept of Mahavakyas. The so-called abheda vakyas are variously interpreted by them: Tat tvam asi is atat tvam asi for Madhva. Later Dvaitins have given some 20 interpretations for this one, all carefully avoiding the advaitic meaning. Aham brahma asmiis is also given a totally diff meaning.
