Yes, all the 24 Ekadashis are great occasions. The Advains wish to go out of the cycle of birth and death, and to my understanding, the Kartika Shukladashi, or the Bhagavad Gita - Prabodhini Ekadashi is very special for that. On this day Lord Krishna gave the Bhagavad Gita upadesha to Arjuna.
