Do relative descriptors make the absolute also relative?



Not necessarily - ie when they belong to a class of identifiers in vedAnta termed as upalakshaNa-s, they identify the svarUpa of the denoted, without forming part of the svarUpa.  

\>

  

The above actually supports V.Advaita as name/form/body always denotes the atma/soul without forming a part of the svaroopa.

Even the identification of oneself as "chidAnanda rUpah Shivoham" makes the self, qualified with aprithak siddha (inseparable)

vishesha of shivatva/auspiciousness.

  

So, I think one needs to withdraw oneself completely and just remain absolutely silent in order to attain advaitic absolute atman/Brahman.
