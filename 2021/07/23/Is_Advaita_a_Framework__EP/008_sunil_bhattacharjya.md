Dear friends,

  

\<So, I think one needs to withdraw oneself completely and just remain absolutely silent in order to attain advaitic absolute atman/Brahman.\>

Not necessarily. ***Mahājano yena gataḥ sa panthāḥ***. One has to be like Lord Krishna, who being one with the Brahman, came with a mission, and we have to observe how he lived in this world for the good of the world. Adi Shankara too being a Jivanmukta did not leave immediately. He had works to do, particularly to establish the Mathas **:** (four for his four disciples to head, and and the fifth one for himself in the Kashi of the South, to look after). Of course the Shringeri became two parts, where the original one with the Sharada Devi in the original-like wooden statue in the original site at Kudali Shringeri, near Shimoga in Karnataka**;** and the newer one established in the new Shringeri, in a safer place after the attack from Khiljis, in the beginning of the 14th century BCE. Thus the five mathas established by Adi Shanaka later on became six mathas. However, one of them, the Joshi matha is almost defunct, to my knowledge.  

My 2 cents
