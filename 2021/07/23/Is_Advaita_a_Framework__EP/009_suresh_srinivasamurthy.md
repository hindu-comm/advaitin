Namaste Sunil-ji,

  

Sri Anandagiri's quotation of Manu may have some significance. Probably it is because brAhmaNAs have to predominantly follow

nivrutti and Kshatryas pravrutti. But dharma comprises of both and there is always nivrutti in pravrutti and viceversa.

  

Also, brahma and kshatra tattvas (infact all the four varnas) formthe body of the Vishwaroopi Ishwara who is the supreme purusha.

  

So as long as Ishwara is identified as sarvAntaryAmi and Vishwaroopi, IMHO it does not matter what name/form one does his upAsana.

According to nArAyaNa upanishad all devatas arise, remain and return back to nArAyaNa.

  

IMHO, Vishnu and Shiva stand for Truth and Dharma and they both form one single whole. Infact, they worship each other and exist

in each other. So as long as we Hindus see Ishwara/Brahman in each other, then there will be unity, harmony and happiness 🙂

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
