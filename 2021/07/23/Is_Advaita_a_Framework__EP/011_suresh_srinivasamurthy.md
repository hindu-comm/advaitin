Namaste Sri Venkatraghavan-ji

  

I agree that Advaita and V.Advaita are different ultimately, but as long as the self is embodied

they both seem to converge. What is upalakshana in Advaita is shareera in V.Advaita.

  

The body derives its existence/reality from the self and it is under the control of the self and this

cannot be denied as it is experiential. But body is continuously created, maintained and destroyed

and so, it can be taken as upalakshana also.

  

But still in order to give validity to guruparampara and avatAras of Ishwara / jnAnIs like Sri Shankara,

there is a need toaccept brahma shareeratva and Sri Shankara confirms that in theprashnopanishat 6-8 bhAshya.

  

ते तमर्चयन्तस्त्वं हि नः पिता योऽस्माकमविद्यायाः परं पारं तारयसीति। नमः परमऋषिभ्यो नमः परमऋषिभ्यः ॥ ८ ॥  

  

ततः ते शिष्या गुरुणानुशिष्टाः तं गुरुं कृतार्थाः सन्तो विद्यानिष्क्रयमन्यदपश्यन्तः किं कृतवन्त इत्युच्यते — अर्चयन्तः पूजयन्तः पादयोः

पुष्पाञ्जलिप्रकिरणेन प्रणिपातेन च शिरसा। किमूचुरित्याह — त्वं हि नः अस्माकं पिता**ब्रह्मशरीरस्य**विद्यया जनयितृत्वान्नित्यस्याजरामरणस्याभयस्य।  

  

But I have one question:

  

You mentioned that the identifiers like shivam and advaitam are also dropped.

How can the non-duality of the atman be dropped?

  

  

Regards,  
Suresh
