Namaste Suresh ji,

In vayavahAra there is no fundamental difference between vishiShTAdvaita and advaita. Of course, in advaita, all forms of Ishvara are accorded equal status. We accept Hari Hara abheda.

  

I think in your second paragraph you may be using the term upalakshaNa in a different meaning to what was intended. An upalakshaNa in general is a qualifier to some entity that is qualified. The difference with the visheShaNa, which is another type of qualifier, merely lies in whether it is a part of the svarUpa of the qualified when it serves as the qualifier.

  

dvaitAbhAvatva is an upalakshaNa for Brahman, because in denying dvaita, it helps identify Brahman, but at thesame time, dvaitAbhAvatva is not a feature of Brahman. It is not a feature of Brahman because in advaita, Brahman is nirguNa, featureless.

  

mithyAtva is a concept that vishiShTAdvaita and dvaita do not agree with, hence all these questions.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
