Svagata bheda simply means "self contradiction" or a split in consciousness or truth.As far as I know, there is nosvagata bheda

in V.Advaita, as Brahmanis accepted as the Self of all which is always homogeneous.

  

------------

Swagata bhedas means internal differences - in VishiShTaadvaita - Brahman is accepted as infinite- anantam. Yet in Brahman - there is jeeva-jeeva bhinnatvam. Jeeva - Jagat bhinnatvam and Jeeva - Jagat bhinnatvam - all becomes visheshanas to Brahman as in total body with internal parts - hence vishiShTa Advaita.

  

In advaita, as Shree Venkatraghavanji explained, all bhinnatvams comes under mithyaa - sat asat vilakshanam - they are real enough to experience but not really real at the absolute level - na iha naanaasti kinchana. That categoryis not accepted in VishiShTaadvaita and dvaita - See the discussions in Advaita Siddhi of Madhusudana on the five definitions of mithyaa - Shree Venkatraghavan has posted his series on Advaita Siddhi.  

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
