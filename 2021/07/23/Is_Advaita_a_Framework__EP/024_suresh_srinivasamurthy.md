Namaste Sada-ji,

  

\<

In advaita, as Shree Venkatraghavanji explained, all bhinnatvams comes under mithyaa - sat asat vilakshanam - they are real enough to experience but not really real at the absolute level - na iha naanaasti kinchana. That categoryis not accepted in VishiShTaadvaita and dvaita - See the discussions in Advaita Siddhi of Madhusudana on the five definitions of mithyaa - Shree Venkatraghavan has posted his series on Advaita Siddhi.  

\>

  

As all differences are cognized in consciousness which is real name/forms cannot be outrightly denied as mithya.

There is no nAnAtva in the antayAmi Brahman in both V.Advaita and Dvaita. This is true in vyavahAra for Advaitins also.

  

ParamArtha satya pervades and provides reality/existence to all vyavahAra.When all vyavahAra is looked

upon as an act of yagya in thefire of Consciousness, then everythingat once becomes absolutely spiritualized.

  

IMHO, whatever is achieved by denying everything can also be achieved by recognizing the same in everything.

  

As already said, there is a need to "SEE" the same Brahman in each other without labeling anyone as

advaitin, dvaitin or v.advaitin. Only can there can be peace and harmony 🙂

  

Namaste

Suresh
