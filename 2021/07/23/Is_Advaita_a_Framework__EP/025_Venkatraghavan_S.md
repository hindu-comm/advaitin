Namaste Sadaji,

It is a difficult idea to accept for anyone and these questions will be natural. If it is difficult to accept, better to fall back on one's own school.

  

One has taken birth in a particular set of circumstances for a reason - Ishvara considers that birth to be the right circumstances for that jIva in that birth. One can confidently follow one's own svadharma safe in that knowledge.

  

The problem is one mistakes one's circumstances to be the ultimate and tries to prove the superiority of the system that one has adopted as 'one's own'. Minds greater than ours have done that already. Better to focus on improving ourselves.

  

By the way, by no means is this a comment on anyone here, just a common failing I have observed in myself.

  

Regards

Venkatraghavan
