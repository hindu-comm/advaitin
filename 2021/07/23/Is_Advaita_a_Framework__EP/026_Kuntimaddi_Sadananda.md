In a way you are right. I did come from very strong VishiShTaadvaita tradition - We are still trying to publish our father's works on VishiShTaadvaita. I had written few Telugu poems expressing my 'Shraddhangali' on my father when he left this abode and posted them recently on Facebook. 

  

Yes it is better to follow one's tradition until one starts questioning. My training in Science forced me to think on the issues and ask questions based on the issues than on the preceptors. I follow this even now in my Science Research.

  

Respecting all Acharyas is our tradition but following the truth is the essential Goal.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
