Quite contrary to the above observation, we have Shankaracharya aver in the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad Bhashyam:

  

न, सलिलफेनदृष्टान्तेन परिहृतत्वात् मृदादिदृष्टान्तैश्च ; यदा तुपरमार्थदृष्ट्यापरमात्मतत्त्वात् श्रुत्यनुसारिभिः अन्यत्वेन निरूप्यमाणे नामरूपे मृदादिविकारवत् वस्त्वन्तरे तत्त्वतो न स्तः — सलिलफेनघटादिविकारवदेव, तदा तत् अपेक्ष्य[‘एकमेवाद्वितीयम्’ (छा. उ. ६। २। १)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Chandogya?page=6&id=Ch_C06_S02_V01&hl=%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D)[‘नेह नानास्ति किञ्चन’ (बृ. उ. ४। ४। १९)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=4&id=BR_C04_S04_V19&hl=%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B9%20%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9E%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9A%E0%A4%A8)इत्यादिपरमार्थदर्शनगोचरत्वं प्रतिपद्यते ;  

  

The analogies of wave, bubble, surf - non-difference from water, clay - clay products non-difference, etc, when a person, from the *Paramartha drishti,* from the *absolute viewpoint*,.....the name-form duality is known to be non-existent,...then one attains to the realization of *paramartha darshana*, the *absolute viewpoint.*...

  

In fact the attainment of the absolute viewpoint is what is transcending all views. Then, it is not any viewpoint really.
