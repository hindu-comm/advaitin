In the Bh.Gita Bhashyam 13.20, Shankara gives a succinct definition of 'samsara', bondage:  

  

कःपुनःअयंसंसारोनाम?*सुखदुःखसम्भोगःसंसारः।*

  

What indeed is this 'samsara'? It is the experiencing of joy and sorrow.

  

पुरुषस्यचसुखदुःखानांसम्भोक्तृत्वंसंसारित्वमिति॥ २० ॥  

  

And the self becomes bound by his experiencing joy and sorrow.  

  

Further, how does this come about?

  

यदापुनःकार्यकरणसुखदुःखस्वरूपेणहेतुफलात्मनापरिणतयाप्रकृत्याभोग्ययापुरुषस्यतद्विपरीतस्यभोक्तृत्वेनअविद्यारूपःसंयोगःस्यात्,तदासंसारःस्यात्इति।  

  

When the self, who is distinct from the not-self,objects of experience, out of ignorance,is identified with them as their experiencer, then he becomes bound.

  

The central message of the 13th chapter is to present to the aspirant the distinction between the self and the not-self and inform that the self is non-different from Brahman and also that the not-self does not have an absolute existence. This knowledge constitutes liberation.
