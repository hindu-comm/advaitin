Dear friend,  

  

You have to read and understand the Vedic dictum : "Sat" came from "Asat". In the beginning the "Asat" wanted to be many and that is how "Asat" created the "Sat". This is not a matter for argumentation.  

  

Advaitins have no quarrel with the view  

यत्सत्येन जगत्सत्यंयत्प्रकाशेन भाति तत् ।  
यदानन्देन नन्दन्ति तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः ॥

  

There was one Dvaita guru, who challenged his Advaita guru by interpreting the upanishadic verse teaching  "tattvamasi" wrongly as
teaching"atattvmasi". The Advaita guru did not want to correct his adamant disciple, as it appeared to him to be a hopeless case. Usually an Advaitin does not want to convert a Dvaitin to Advaita. It is only a question of time, when the Dvaitin himself will eventually realize the truth that the world is "Anitya'.  

  

Best
