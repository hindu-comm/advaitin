Dear Subbuji,

  

The sage Vedavyasa wrote the Mahabharata, using the background of the Mahabharata, with the express desire book encompassing everything that all the scriptures can give. But he felt very sad that it turned out to be a text difficult to understand by most of the people of the society. Then the age Narada asked him to write another text that would be understood by one and all of the society and the resulting output was the Bhagavatam.  

  

It appears that Adi Shankara had similar feeling after writing all the commentiries on the Upanishada and the Brahmasutra, and he wanted a book , which could give the essence of all his writings in the simplest possible language, so that it will be understood by all. Thus came out his book "Vivekachudamani".
