Sureshji - PraNAms

  

First, the statement - buhusyaam, prajaayeyeti - comes after the vaacharambhnam statment.

  

vachaarambhanam statement itself comes to show how - eka vijnaananena sarva vijnaanam bhavati - by gaining the knowledge of one thing - how one can have the knowledge of everything.

  

Uddaalaka proves that it is possible by giving three examples - which illustrates a transformation-less transformation - like gold becoming ornaments or mud becoming pots or like wrought iron becoming iron tools. In all these becoming is only apparent and not real - since gold still remains as gold while we have varieties of ornaments like rings, bangles, bracelets etc., each has date of birth and date of destruction and each have its own attributes that differ from those of others. Yet none of these attributes belong to gold - and gold can declare - all ornaments are in me, and their modifications etc do not affect me - but in really there are no ornaments - look at my glory. Hence forming varieties or namekevaste creation without gold undergoing any transformation. In essence from the gold point at its intrinsic level there is really no creation at all. It was gold before, gold now and gold it shall be. Scripture is providing an example of how creationless creation can appear - which has utility, transactability, etc. and yet no absolute reality - as Goudapaada explains the reality is that which remains the same in all there periods of time - as in - gold it was, gold it is and gold it will be. A profound relative example the scripture is providing for us to appreciate.

  

Only after explaining the creationless creation, the teacher-father Uddaalaka starts explaining when the student wants to know more.

  

All these sequences in the teaching are very important.

  

Then comes the statement about the essence of creation - introducing first from the point of Sat - Sat **eva** smomya idam agra aseet... etc. By stating - tat aikshataa - it saw - indicting what was there is also of the nature of consciousness as Brahmasuutra also uses this to establish this.

  

Being ekam eva adviteeyam - it is Brahman, meaning infinite - Shankara explains in terms of sajaati, vijaati, swagata bheda rahitam - which I had explained in one of my posts before.

  

Brahman being infinite cannot really become anything - since it is infinite - hence no words can reach there in trying to describe anything about it.

  

Since creation is there and what was there is only pure sat chit and ananda (being brahman or infinite or limitless), creation can only be - vaachaarambhanam vikaaraH - which Shankara calls as mithyaa to illustrate the vaacharambhanam statements of the scriptures made earlier. Hence rings, bangles, etc are all mithyaa vastus only. However are real enough for transactions - vyavahaarika satyam - since they have utility and transactability, etc.

  

Hence from the statement of creation - tat aikshataa - bahusyaam we are already in the transactional reality -where the chandogya teaching corresponds to.

  

Hence Krishna's statements also that I had coated before and echos - Vedic statements - neha naanaasti kincha - sarvam khalu idam brahma, etc. while pointing to the sarvam idam. Thus teaching involves a student-teacher who are in the transactional world - pointing to the absolute truth that cannot be described in words - na vaak gachhati na manaH. Hence realization is also at vyavhaara about the nature of paarmaarthika- how - that is what sravana manana and **nidhidhyaasana** all about. The last step is not needed if a person has already prepapred the mind like Nichiketas in Kathopanishat. For Swetaketu, Uddaalaka patiently explains taking many example to teach - tat tvam asi, Swetaketo.

  

This statement - eaka vijnaanena sarva vijnaanam bhavati also forms the basis for the Mundaka teaching where the student wants to know - knowing which one can know everything - kasminno bhagavo vijnaate sarva vijnaanam bhavati - and the teacher provides an exhaustive answer in terms of para and apara vidyaas.

  

Hence it is very important to study the scriptures under a proper teacher to have a clear understanding.

The above statement comes under mithya and so cannot be absolutely true right, as essentially the jiva is that underlying satya?

  

------------

Yes - not only the above statement, all the scriptures too. mithyaa means it is vyaavaharika satya not asatya.

  

jeeva-jagat-Iswara - this triad is also mithyaa as per Advaitaand along with so called Self-Realization or God realization, etc.

From the Brahman reference there is no creation what so ever since infinite cannot undergo any real modification.

  

Some of the discussion and confusions are due to switching the reference states - from the paaramaarthika to vyaavahaarika states.

  

Hari Om!
