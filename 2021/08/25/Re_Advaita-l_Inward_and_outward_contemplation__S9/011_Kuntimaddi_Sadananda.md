Sureshji PraNAms

  

One has to be careful - when it is said no words can reach there - that being infinite one cannot described as this and as not this also.

  

Mathematically also, infinite is only a negation of finiteness only.

  

Scriptures only point out (lakshyaartha) for one to recognize that which is ever present and the sat-chit- ananda swaruupam - as one's own self. To shift the mind to that understanding - it has to be purified and all the yogas are intended for that.

  

Even one cannot describe 'who am I?' - Neither Shiva or Vishnu there - yet it is Shivam (auspicious) and Vishnu - vyaapakatvaat that pervades everything. - Hence I quoted - antar bihischa tat sarvam vyaapya naarayana sthitaH .

  

Vishnusahara naamavali starts with viswam - The entire universe - since Brahman can exclude anything, by definition.

  

It is better to contemplate on that now - these questions will not end until one gets established in that understanding.

  

With this I stop.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
