Hare Krishna



- My observations on your very interesting queries. If anyone has
  already addressed your queries more authentically you can ignore my
  reply.



- In ‘The gold in the bangle, chain etc.,’, the gold is observable and
  perceivable, but the brahman in the jagat is not perceivable or
  identifiable. If something not perceivable appears in a form that is
  perceivable (as jagat), how do we compare these two and also say
  brahman the underlying material is changeless?

- Yes you are right, jagat is ever changing and brahman its adhishtAnaM
  is changeless and nirvishesham. Sri Subbu prabhuji has recently
  shared one swamiji’s talks about rUpa and svarUpa. If you know
  Kannada kindly listen to it. svarUpa means its own rUpa ( form or
  feature or roughly shape) that rUpa which never undergoes any change
  it is satyaM. ( shankara in taittereeya bhAshya). As you rightly
  observed the rUpa of the jagat is changing from one form to another
  each and every second. Ofcourse the very definition of jagat is
  that. The changing jagat is in our direct perception. (pratyaksha
  pramANa) so when we talk about changeless behind the change and to
  know (realize) that pratyaksha pramANa is not the means. For example
  to know the sthiratvaM of sUrya and paribhramaNa of pruthvi is the
  subject matter of science not our direct perception of sun rise and
  sun set. When we see the bangles, bracelets, rings etc. there must be
  something which must be retaining or maintaining its own intrinsic
  nature is it not?? Here it is gold which is unchanging in all these
  nAma and rUpa-s. In short, the sva-rUpa (cause) behind nAma rUpa
  (kArya) is ONE. However, unlike gold and ornament example (wherein
  gold is very much perceivable) to determine changeless (svarUpa)
  behind changing jagat, pratyaksha is NOT the pramANa, as I said above,
  to know or realize this changeless svarUpa we need some other pramANa
  otherthan pratyaksha pramANa. ShAstra comes to our help here. Hence
  shAstra is the only pramANa to determine the svarUpa of rUpa/s.

- How do we use the gold example in the ‘ I am not the body’ example and
  say body is not brahman, when we say bangle is gold?

- One is pratishedha vAkya pramANa neti neti, another one is samyak
  drushti or Atmaikatva drushti of the paramArtha jnAni, formet is to
  cultivate viveka and vairagya and later is a result through which you
  would realize there is nothing exists there apart from THAT.

- Is a jada vastu an appearance of brahman or not? If yes, at some stage
  brahman must be revealed in it right? If no, then something else other
  than brahman exists right?

- No question of existence of something other than brahman in Advaita at
  any stretch of imagination. HE is behind all this sakala charAchara
  vastu. And he is sakalam/sarvaM (sarvaM khalvidaM brahma). HE is
  upAdAna and HE is antaryAmi as well. It is because of the simple fact
  that as per Advaita brahman is both the nimitta and upAdAna kAraNa.
  tatsrushtvA tadevAnuprAvishat says shruti. It is just like gold
  entering the ornament. For the ornament upAdAna is gold there is no
  individual existence for the ornament apart from gold. Hence yadidam
  kiMcha tatsarvamabhavatu, in that sense, HE (brahman) is upAdAna and
  antaryAmi as well.
