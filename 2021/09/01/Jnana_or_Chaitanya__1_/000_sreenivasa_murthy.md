**Dear mumumkshus,  **

**  **

**The jnani (name and form) appears in chaitanya or Jnana.**

**The ajnAni (name and form) appears in chaitanya or Jnana.  **

**So there is neither jnani nor ajnani.**

**There is only jnana or**

**Pure Consciousness/Atman/Brahman**  

It is very very important to cognize this fact within oneself by oneself.

  

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa Murthy
