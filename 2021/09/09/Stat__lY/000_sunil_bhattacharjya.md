I guess you are aware that a majestic 12.5-foot statue of Adi Shankaracharya (shown below) has been made in Mysuru and sent for installation at the Shankaracharya study chair and museum at the Shankaracharya Samadhi, behind the Kedarnath temple.  

  

![image.png](https://groups.google.com/group/advaitin/attach/6160247d63310/image.png?part=0.1&view=1)  
  

  

Adi Shankara's images have always been very impressive, and he is adored by one and all. Adi Shankara lived upto 32 years of age. Does anybody know why he has not shown anywhere with his beards? The Sage of Kanchi or Mahaperiyava, who was believed to be an incarnation of Adi Shankaracharya, was seen always with his beards, like all the other mathadhipatis of the Shankara mathas.  

  

I am also wondering, as to how Adi Shankara's Samadhi can be in Kedarnath, when he established the Kanchi Kamakoti month in the year 482 BCE and he passed away there in 477 BCE. His samadhi is very much there in Kanchipuram and how is it possible to have two samadhi-places of Adi Shankara.
