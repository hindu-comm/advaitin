PraNAms to all

  

Sharing an online discussion with a seeker.

-------------------------

A Seeker asked:

In katha Upanishad it says “parAnchi khAni vyatranat svayamvhuH”. That Ishwara created our minds and senses to face outwards. That is why Jiva looks for happiness outside and not where he should be looking. tasmAt parAnpashyati nAntarAtmA. Therefore, Jiva is programmed such that he always looks for what he wants outside.

Then another place Bhagavan says- na me bhakta praNashyati. Won’t let him perish.

I don’t get it. Why was it made outward-looking in the first place if He did not want His devotees to perish?

------------------

Ans:

First, let us understand the role of Iswara. Since we see or experience the waking world, we ask the question of who created this.

Like a dry scientist we can say, it is caused by a big bang. However, that leaves more questions for a rational mind. Why and where did the bang occur, and how did the matter get collected at one place for the bang to occur? And where did the material come from for the bang, who created that material and why, etc.? The end-less questions - with no answers.

‘Before and where the bang occurred’- are questions of time and space. Space and time came into the picture with the bang only. Hence in Science, it is called a mathematical singularity, which translates to 'we do not know'.

From Vedanta’s point, creation - sustenance, and dissolution are beginning-less. Iswara does not create the world and Jeevas out of His fancy. If He does, we can confront Him demanding, why did He create such a mess, and even if He wants, why did He create me with my mind habituated only to look outside and not inside, as your question poses.

Vedanta says, Iswara creates not out of His fancies but to bless the Jeevas to exhaust their previous life's vaasanaas. Hence an appropriate environment - proper parents, siblings, place and time of birth, and the type of body the Jeeva needs to exhaust their vaasanaas are all provided. There are supposed to be 85,000 life forms in the creation - starting from Covid 19 virus – and 14 lokas or fields of experience for Jeevas with varying types of vaasanaas. The creation is mind-boggling to make sure everyone is rightly placed – Now wonder He is called Sarvajna – the source of all knowledge.

The question then how did the previous life was created. The answer is - it is based on previous to a previous life—the next question - How did the first creation start. The answer is there is no first creation - it is a beginning-less cycle.



The fundamental problem for the creation is - ignorance of one's own nature. One does not know that He is ‘Sat Chit Ananda’ swaruupam. Hence due to ignorance from the beginning, the Jeeva has been searching for happiness. Due to ignorance - he is searching ’outside’ of himself for his happiness - hence dependence on things other than himself for his happiness - leading to action - results with vaasanaas of likes and dislikes- more actions - more vaasanaas. In fact, there is no happiness outside at all. Hence all searches for it outside are futile – says Vedanta.

Even if one analyzes logically, the happiness that we get by enjoying sense objects does not come from the objects per sec. A fresh good-hot cup of Madras coffee in the morning may be a source of happiness for me. If it is a real source of happiness, it should give happiness to everybody who drinks. And more coffee means more happiness!
Unfortunately, the guy next door does not know this. He hates coffee, but he wants a good hot cup of Tea, and he claims there is where real happiness is. I have coffee-vaasanaas, which is getting reinforced by my daily indulgence while he has tea-vaasanaas. The kind Lord has created good coffee and Tea stalls for us to look for happiness in the morning. He knows that there is no happiness in coffee or tea but out of compassion creating these stalls, since, like children, we are running after them. 

Even though the cycle is beginning-less, it can end - only because the root cause is ignorance which is beginning-less. Ignorance cannot begin - like my Chemistry ignorance - but ignorance can end with the required knowledge. The ignorance about oneself that he is the very source of happiness, as he is (Ananda swaruupam) can end only with Self-knowledge.

For that, the mind has to look at one's own self, which we call inwards. The above sloka mention that the mind is habituated from the beginning-less creation to look outside for a solution to the problem. To remove any ignorance, the Jeeva (with his mind of course) has to approach the appropriate teacher to gain the knowledge - like myself going to Chemistry teacher to remove my Chemistry ignorance. Philosophy teacher will not do. The teaching of Vedanta and the appropriate yogas (karma, bhakti, and jnaana) help to purify the mind from longing to look outside and help to turn the mind to question the very seeking itself.

Now you can see the statements of the kathopanishat and how they make sense.

When He says - My bhakta - the one who wants to know Me (jnaani is the greatest bhakta), I will not let him perish. When I am longing to know the truth, the Lord will ensure I get a proper teacher who can guide me - provided I keep my eyes open! Otherwise, Lord cannot do much! Closing eyes and complaining and crying that Lord is not helping to see is a foolish pursuit.

Hope this helps.

Hari Om!

Sadananda
