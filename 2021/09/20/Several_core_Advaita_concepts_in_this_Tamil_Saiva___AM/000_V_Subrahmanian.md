Ilangai Jeyaraj is a very popular, extremely learned speaker. In this video of about one hour, I was pleasantly surprised to find many an Advaitic concept so natural to this Saiva school:

  

1\. The idea that Jnana, Bhakti, Dhyana, Karma Yogas are not standalone paths but need to be integrated for sadhana.

  

2\. Triputee dissolves at the end.

  

3\. Several methods/means are there within the system to reach the goal: yaya yaya bhavet pumsam .....of Sureshwaracharya.

  

4\. The more one tries to probe the world, the more questions and ignorance one faces: निरूपयितुमारब्धे निखिलैरपि पण्डितैः \| अज्ञानं पुरतस्तेषां भाति कक्षासु कासुचित् \|\| of the Panchadashi.
