Dear friends,

  

I look forward to reading Prof. VK's book. I think it would be nice if Prof. VK send a copy of the book is given to each of our great Mathadhipatis in Shringeri Math and Kanchipuram Math. When I published my book on the Original Bhagavad Gita, I sent my books to the Mathadhipatis of the Shringeri math, the Kanchi Kamakoti math and to the Chitrapur Saraswat Math.  

  

I am not happy at the shringeri math's not yet publishing their Guruparampara from Adi Shankaracharya's time, for the last 2,500 years ago, but I have great regards for all the Mathadhipatis of all the Shankara mathas. To my knowledge, the real guru-parampara of the Tunga Shringeri math is from the fifth century BCE.

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
