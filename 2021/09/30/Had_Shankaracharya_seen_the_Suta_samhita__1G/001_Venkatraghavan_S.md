Namaste Subbuji,

  

Indeed - when I read the verse cited by you, specifically the words सोऽश्नुते सकलान् कामान् अक्रमेण, the first thing that sprang to mind is the taittirIya shruti -सोऽश्नुते सर्वान् कामान् सह. The सर्वान् कामान् सह is essentially the same as सकलान् कामान् अक्रमेण. The wordसह in the upaniShad is translated as युगपत् एकक्षणोपारूढानेव by the bhAShyakAra, which mirrors the अक्रमेण in the sUtasamhitA.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
