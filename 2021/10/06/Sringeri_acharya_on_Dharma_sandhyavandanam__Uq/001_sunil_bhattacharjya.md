Dear Putranji,

  

I tried to hear the talk of Sringeri Swamiji**,** but ***Kannada gothilla*****.** Hope someone will dub the talk in English.

  

The ***Sringeri Math*** itself is in a very beautiful location, by the side of the river ***Tunga***, and I was lucky to be able to visit this Math many years ago. I was also lucky to be able to visit the ***Original Sringeri Math in*** ***Kudali Sringeri*** near Shimoga, at the confluence of the river ***Tunga-Bhadra***, where the tributary ***Tunga*** meets ***Bhadra***. Mother Sharada's original statue in the standing posture is there. Very few people know that the original name of the river was ***Bhadra-Tunga*** and eventually it got changed to ***Tunga-Bhadra,*** and you can very well guess the reason for that. The Mahabharata mentions that Lord Balaram went to Bhadra-Tunga during his pilgrimage, more than 5,000 years ago.  

  

**Jai Ma Sharada**,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
