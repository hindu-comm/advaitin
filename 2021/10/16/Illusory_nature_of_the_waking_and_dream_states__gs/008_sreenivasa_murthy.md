YESindeed. Even the Vedanta teaching also applies for the seeker who thinks he isbound by space and time.  
Hence Mandukya talks about deep-sleep state experiencetoo, where there is space and time - yet the seeker is there, sleeping well.Hence, even the realization is also in vyavahaara level only. From the paarmaarathika level- na vaak gachhati, no manaH - yato vaacho nivartante apraapya manasaa saH -

  
Hence from the absolute point - nothing can be said or need to be said too. Ig is beyond space and time too since all words are within the realm of space and time. There is no word or world in the deep-sleep state too.  
Even the visvaruupam is also in the vyavahaara level only. jeeva-jagat-Iswara - divisions are all at vyavahaara level only. At paaramaarthika leval - being anantam - no divisions of any kind also possible, since it is anantam.  
  

Hence, Tai. Up starts with Brahman definition that which cannot be defined butprovides a working definition for a contemplative mind to think and transcend the space-timelimitations. It starts as satyam-jnaanam and most importantly anantam.  
  
Withoutwarning it switches from Brahman to Aatma - since they are not two different -sa aatmaa - tat tvam asi.  
  
Hencethese are called lakshana vaakyaas - pointers for a prepared mind tocontemplate and in the process RECOGNIZE that which is the very substratum evenfor the contemplation. That is very discussion of Om in the Mandukya Up. -switching from micro to macro.

  
  
Hari Om!  
  
Sadananda
