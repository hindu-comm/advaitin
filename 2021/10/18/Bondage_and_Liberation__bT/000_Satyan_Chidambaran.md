The dog was indeed already liberated but amusingly in his own vision, he was bound! The owner “removed his bondage” by opening the door which didn’t really “remove” any bondage, but that opening somehow did the trick: it had now “liberated” the already liberated dog! This is more or less what is going on in the vision of Vedanta. The shAstra and the Guru “remove the bondage“ that was never really there to begin with, thereby “liberating” the one who was always ever liberated!
