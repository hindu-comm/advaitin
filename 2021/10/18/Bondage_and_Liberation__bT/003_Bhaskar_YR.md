The shAstra and the Guru “remove the bondage“ that was never really there to begin with, thereby “liberating” the one who was always ever liberated!



praNAms

Hare Krishna



Yes there was / is / will never ever be a thing that can be called bondage (bandhana), but thinking (avidyA) that he is in bandhana or entrapped in bandhana and suffering from it is very much there ( atleast transactionally). Hence shAstra, guru, upadesha, sAdhana everything required to remove this type of delusion. avidyAkrutadvAd bandhAvasthAyAscha so brahma vidyA would eradicate this wrong notion prayOjanaM chAsyA brahmavidyAyA avidyA nivruttiH therefore tasmAt avidyA nivrutti mAtre mOksha vyavahAraH says bhAshyakAra. jnApakaM hi shAstraM na tu kArakaM. But alas!! Even for this avidyA, which is not at all there in paramArtha, we attributed bhAva rUpa and said has the Ashraya (locus) in brahman itself and lesha (avidyA lesha) would remain even in paramArtha jnAni.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
