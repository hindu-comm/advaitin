[[V Subrahmanian	2021-10-20, 14:00:17 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/JCPMfBvOPbY)]]

This verse is found in the Narada Puranam:  

  

गणेशभक्ताः सौराश्च वैष्णवाः शाक्तशाम्भवाः।  
सर्वेऽधिकारिणो ह्यत्र सकामाश्चाप्यकामकाः॥ ८२-४७ ॥  
  
Devotees of these पञ्चायतनदेवताः Ganesha, Surya, Vishnu, Shakti and Shiva - all are fit to study/hear this Purana in this पुराणम् whether they've any desire to fulfill or not.
