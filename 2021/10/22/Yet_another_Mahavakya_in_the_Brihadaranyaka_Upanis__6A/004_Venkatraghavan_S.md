Because the mantra isस वा एष महानज आत्मा योऽयं विज्ञानमयःis essentially so'yam - ie conveying the identity of sah with ayam.sah (He), mahAn (the Great), ajah (Unborn one), which refers to Brahman, is the same as ayam vijnAnamayah, this conscious jIva.

No meditation is necessary, knowing the truth that jIva is Brahman is sufficient. A mahAvAkya does not convey a meditation, it simply reveals that the jIva is Brahman.
