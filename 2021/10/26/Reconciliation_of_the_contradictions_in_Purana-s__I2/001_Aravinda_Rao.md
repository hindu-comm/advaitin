Pujya swamiji means that sage Vyasa himself wrote all the purana-s but gave different versions indifferentpurana-s, he being a 'practical teacher'.

I think that the traditional acharyas do not wish to deviate from the orthodox position that all the purana-s were written by sage Vyasa. The purana-s appear to be the work of several well-meaning sages who wished to integrate different traditions and bring them under the umbrella of the Upanishadic philosophy. The Upanishadic

doctrine is the central thread on which I can put my own god/goddess as the Supreme Brahman and all others as his/her assistants or vibhuti-s. This has served the

purpose of satisfying people of different traditions and keeping them in the broad embrace of Hindu dharma.

A lot of research seems to be going on in the western universities on the purana-s in order to give anthropological and racial interpretations to them and

divide the society.

Aravinda Rao
