Sri Sri Jagadguru Shankaracharya Mahasamsthanam

Dakshinamnaya Sri Sharada Peetham

Sringeri 577139  

  

  

In this holy land Bharata many great men took birth and performed extraordinary ennobling tasks and attained unparallelled fame. Among such exalted men the name of Sri Adishankara Bhagavatpadacharyashines above all. When the Sanatana Dharma faced severe affliction and when the Vedic path had almost perished, He incarnated in Kalaty, Kerala, refuted the systems opposed to the Veda, taught the entire humanity the essence of Dharma and with a view to the continued propagation of Dharma, he established four Vedic 'Seats' in the holy centres of Puri, Sringeri, Dvaraka and Badari. He ascended the Sarvajna Peetha (Seat of Omniscience) in Kashmir and giving up the mortal coil in the holy Kedar returned to Kailasa.

  

Thus it is said -

  

अवतीर्णश्च कालट्यांकेदारेऽन्तर्हितश्च यः । चतुष्पीठप्रतिष्ठाता जयताच्छङ्करो गुरुः \|\|

  

\[Glory to Guru Shankara who having incarnated in Kalaty, founded the four Peethas. andended His earthly sojournin Kedar.\]

  

Hence all followers of the Sanatana Dharma ought to remember Him always and express their gratitude to Him. We are extremely pleased to know that keeping this in mind the Prime Minister of Bharata, Hon. Narendra Modi ji will be consecrating in holy Kedar the beautiful image of Sri Shankaracharya on Thursday, the first day of the shukla paksha of the month of Kartika (5.11.2021). On this occasion we extend our blessings to all the followers of Sanatana Dharma.

  

We pray thattheLord confer upon the Prime Minister who has taken up such a noble task, a long life, health, etc.
