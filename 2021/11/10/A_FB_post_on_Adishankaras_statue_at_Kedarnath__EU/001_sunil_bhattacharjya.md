Dear Subbuji,

  

We all Indian Sanatana dharmis are proud that Adi Shankara came and spread the Advaita philosophy, which is the essence of the Sarvashastramayee Bhagavad Gita also. We identify ourselves with our body (both physical and astral) and always try to satisfy the dictates of our body and mind. We have to go beyond the body. Adi Shankara revived the advaita philosophy in detail. At the request of some brahmins, Lord Buddha in the 19th century BCE, gave the upanishadic teachings to some after him, there will be an avatara in a brahmin family and he will teach the upanishadic knowledge. That avatara was none other than Adi Shankara.  

  

We are all proud that Adi Shankara's statue has been inaugurated in Kedarnath. It was Adi Shankara, who installed the Mukti-lingam in Kedarnath. However, let us not forget the Adi Shankara took samadhi in Kachipuram and not in Kedarnath. The Samadhi in Kedarnath was that of a later Shankaracharya, named Abhinava Shannkara.  

  

It is said that if nectar is mixed with poison it no more remains nectar. My wish is that the truth, to whomsoever can be bitter, we must come out with the full truths and not the half-truths. Adi Shankara was born in 509 BCE and admitting this truth will make the Adi Shankara happy and not sad.  

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
