One sentence got garbled. Please read the following garbled verse:

At the request of some brahmins, Lord Buddha in the 19th century BCE, gave the upanishadic teachings to some after him, there will be an avatara in a brahmin family and he will teach the upanishadic knowledge.

corrected as below:  

At the request of some brahmins, Lord Buddha, in the 19th century BCE, gave the upanishadic teachings. Lord Budha also said that there will be dilution of his teachings with passage of time, and after about one thousand years, an avatara will appear in a brahmin family and he will teach the upanishadic knowledge. That avatara was Adi Shankara.
