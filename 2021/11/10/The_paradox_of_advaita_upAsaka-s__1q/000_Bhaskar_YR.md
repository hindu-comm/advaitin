Hare Krishna



I don’t know whether other Advaita sAdhaka-s undergoing this dilemma when they are doing the upAsana, archana, dhyAna etc. to their ishta devata in particular, saguNa brahman in general but certainly I do sometimes!!



Though, at the time of pooja, any number of times you say to your mind that “for you saguNOpAsaNa, Ishvaraanugraha is must and you have to see him as bestower of mukti since you are yet to get rid of your attachment to your dehAtma buddhi” etc. an advaitically doused mind kept on pricking me like :



1.  after all what you are doing is still in avidyA kshetra, the deity
    you are worshipping, the deity to which you are offering naivedyaM,
    to the deity you are chanting stotra-s, praising his glories to
    pacify him/her etc. are all in avidyA vyavahAra only 2.  you are striving to get only the second best something which is
    inferior to the ultimate !!
3.  kArya/apara/sOpAdhika/saprapancha/amukhya/savishesha/saguNa brahma
    which you are worshiping daily is in reality a product in your
    avidyA kshetra only and IT IS NOT THAT YOU WANT TO REALIZE
    ULTIMATELY.
4.  Though upAsana lead you to krama mukti, though without Ishwara krupe
    you don’t get ultimate mukhya mukti why on the earth you are
    striving to achieve something inferior (amukhya) when the goal is
    quite clear!!? 5.  When your goal is to realize avikruta, nirvishesha, nirupAdhika,
    mukhya, nishprapancha, nirguNa brahma, in the name of vyavahAra you
    are missing the main goal and wasting your time on the thing which
    is good ONLY in vyavahAra / avidyA. Does it not mean that you are
    simply adding one more avidyA act of worshiping amidst other
    loukika avidyAtmaka vyavahAra?? 6.  When the ultimate mOksha explained as neither vikArya nor saMskArya
    nor utpAdya so any amount of saguNOpAsana and sAdhana would fetch
    you the mOksha so why !!!?? 7.  When the result of krama mukti through archirAdi upAsana mArga could
    fetch you only Ishwarya (same to what Ishwara has!!), sadgati (
    reaching hiraNya garbha lOka) etc. which are again impermanent when
    compared to paramArtha jnAna why on the earth you are still
    struggling for it??
8.  OK even if you are thinking that you are doing the saguNOpAsana for
    chitta shuddhi ultimately through Ishwara krupa you will get
    paramArtha jnana, mind you that the Chaitanya which you are
    approaching for this with upAdhi is completely vishesha rahita,
    tatastha, nishkriya and vishesha upAdhi which you are seeing in the
    deity is jada (inert) without the instructions of this Chaitanya
    cannot act on its own!!?? So again, what is that you are trying to
    achieve here??



Like this hell a lot of questions / doubts arise, keeping them aside and concentrating on pooja sometimes very difficult. I explained this to my guru, he said try to forget that you are an advaitin when you are doing pooja and try to don’t recall bhAshya vAkya-s which insist that Ishwara is in only avidyA kshetra etc.



Others kindly educate me how they can get rid of these feelings when they are doing daily pooja and devata kArya etc.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

bhaskar
