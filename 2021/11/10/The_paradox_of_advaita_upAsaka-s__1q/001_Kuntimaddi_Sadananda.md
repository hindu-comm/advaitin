Bhaskerji - PraNAms

  

Little surprised reading your mail.

  

I will present my understanding of the prayer and upasana.

.................

You wrote:  

1.  after all what you are doing is still in avidyA kshetra, the deity
    you are worshipping, the deity to which you are offering naivedyaM,
    to the deity you are chanting stotra-s, praising his glories to
    pacify him/her etc. are all in avidyA vyavahAra only.

........

I am sure you know - The so-called realization is also in vyavahaara only. Hence as long as BMI is there and one is transacting, recognition of the presence of the world external to the BMI and invocation of the creator of the wonderful creation is required. With the Advaitic understanding, the prayer and invocation and all the expressions of the BMI surrender (in terms of upasana, etc.,to that absolute with thousands of names becomes even more beautiful- as it is the visualizationof the Vibhuti of the Lord in all its glory.

  

Seeing His presence in all names and forms itself is full entertainment. In the insects crawling on the floor, in the little bees flying around in search of the food, the varieties of the flowers, Vegitables, species, you name it - all is His expressions. The upaasana is only a formal way of recognition of His glories and our BMI is surrendering to that vision - with the recognition that even this BMI - is His glory. We start with aachamanam - - with the recognition of praana, apaana, udanaana etc are His expressions - and pointing every part of the body as His with chanting His name.

  

With the understanding that the upaasana becomes a method of formal recognition or remainder that this BMI is part of His glory only.

  

The formal upaasana becomes a remainder that this understanding should remain with us even after we finish that ritual.

  

As Vivekachudamani sloka says - this body is nothing but - tvak maamsa rudhira snaayuH medo majaasti sankulaH, puurnam muutra pureeshabhyaam sthuula idam vapuH. .. just skin, flesh, boons, fat, execratorymatter, etc - yet acting so beautiful due to His Presence.

  

Upaasnaas help us to remind this understanding and to keep that understanding all the time. If one keep that understanding all the time then you are doing that mental informal upaasana all the time.

  

In essence Saguana Brahman is only a means for the mind to help in the recognition of the absolute truth that is beyond any gunas or attributes.

...............

2. you are striving to get only the second best something which is inferior to the ultimate !!

-----------------

Baskarji - as I see it - there is no second best or inferior here - It is only the expression of the ultimate and upaasana with background knowledge helps to focus the mind on that ultimate.

  

That is the glory of the human birth. Otherwise how do you experience the beauty of that ultimate. Observing the whole creation and drama of Life becomes a joyfulentertainment and upaasanas only help to remind the mind to dwell on His Vibhuties - na ca mastaani bhuutani, pasyam me yogamaiswaram.

  

My 2c

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
