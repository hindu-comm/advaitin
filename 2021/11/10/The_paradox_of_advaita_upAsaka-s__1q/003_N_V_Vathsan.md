***Swami Paramarthanandaji has clearly explained this.***

***Nishkama Sagunopasana/puja is a very important aspect of Karma Yoga to develop chitta suddhi and chitta naiscalya***

***for acquiring Sadhana chatushtaya sanpatti, toenter Jnana Yoga. Then you practice jnana yoga { sravana, manana, nidhidyasanam}***

***and enter jnana nishta to get guru prapti. With guru upadesa of Sastra your goal**will be attained.***

***  ***

***Even a gnani performs saguna puja for showing the path to others and for loka sangraha {welfare of all}. Example, Sankaracharyas performing pujas to thedeities establishes in the mutts***
