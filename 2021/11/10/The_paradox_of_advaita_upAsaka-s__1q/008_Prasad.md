Dear Bhaskar ji

  

When Guru has saidso one must try to implement it - no matter how many intrusive thoughts come. In that attempt itself is our shreyas, is it not?

  

While the matter reallyends there, here afewthoughts from my side:

\_\_\_\_\_\_

  

The whole puja is indicative of jnaanaalone. It is a beautiful poetic ritual which signifies giving up of the ownershipwith various things we claim to possess/own,in a sequence. Finally the process culminates in jnaana which is signified by the aarati. In the maaya prapancha here is a great opportunity to remember the Paramatman who has garbed Himself in the form of the upaasyadevata for our benefit. True that the jnaani/Vedanta saadhaka is not fooled by this garb and does not mistake it for reality, but the wonder of wonders is to see Him in a form. How beautifully strange indeed! What else would one be doing if not this? More of samsara or more of vichaara? Does vichaara come only during such occasions and not when it is supposed to, such as when the mind is swayed by attachments and aversions? Let such opportunities to remember the Paramaatman not be wasted! By all that we have been given we can worship Him. This is to our advantage only. If the mind is raised to such heights by vichaara that even eating food is automatic, that we remain completely aloof from the worldly concerns, then puja can be dropped - it will be dropped of its own.

  

Regards,

\- Prasad
