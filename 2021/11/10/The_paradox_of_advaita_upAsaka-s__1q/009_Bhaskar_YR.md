praNAms Sri Sada prabhuji

Hare Krishna



As always you are kind enough to spend your precious time in replying the trifle doubts. My praNAms to you. Yes, whatever you said is absolutely true and fortunately most of the time my feelings also would be same as this. But, all of a sudden some or the other thought about vast scope of avidyA tempt me to think otherwise. I know these samshaya-s need to be nipped in bud but sometimes feel helpless. Some additional thoughts though feeling guilty of encouraging it knowingly.




I am sure you know - The so-called realization is also in vyavahaara only. Hence as long as BMI is there and one is transacting, recognition of the presence of the world external to the BMI and invocation of the creator of the wonderful creation is required.



- Yes this I have already stated. But at the same time don’t we get the
  parallel powerful thoughts like Ishwara’s omnipotence, omniscience
  etc. are valid only in avidyA kshetra?? The creator / creation etc.
  mere upAya nothing is there in reality?? The brahman needs to be
  realized through the negation of these mundane vyAvahArik attributes
  and what I am worshipping is NOT THAT!!?? I am not able to
  ascertain these type of thoughts are negative or supporting the
  saguNOpAsana after all teaching of Advaita by means of these
  superimpositions should be negated to realize WHAT IT IS NOT!!



With the Advaitic understanding, the prayer and invocation and all the expressions of the BMI surrender (in terms of upasana, etc.,to that absolute with thousands of names becomes even more beautiful- as it is the visualizationof the Vibhuti of the Lord in all its glory.



Ø But that LORD himself has the existence in avidyA kshetra is it not?? His vibhUti-s (pastimes) are neither belongs to HIM ( as his Chaitanya is nishkriya and nirvishesha and that same Chaitanya within us also) and his vishesha upAdhi-s donot act on their own without the operating force i.e. Chaitanya but again that Chaitanya is NOT concerned with anything !!! The individuality of Ishwara itself doubted from this perspective. Is it not?? The specific features of that Chaitanya which we are adoring and propitiating is not mere assumption (upAya) ?? If not atheistic it is definitely agnostic thoughts one would get if an advaitin knows the position of saguNa brahman in Advaita sAdhana.



Seeing His presence in all names and forms itself is full entertainment. In the insects crawling on the floor, in the little bees flying around in search of the food, the varieties of the flowers, Vegitables, species, you name it - all is His expressions. The upaasana is only a formal way of recognition of His glories and our BMI is surrendering to that vision - with the recognition that even this BMI - is His glory. We start with aachamanam - - with the recognition of praana, apaana, udanaana etc are His expressions - and pointing every part of the body as His with chanting His name.



With the understanding that the upaasana becomes a method of formal recognition or remainder that this BMI is part of His glory only.



The formal upaasana becomes a remainder that this understanding should remain with us even after we finish that ritual.



Ø All these divine feelings are kevala bhAvanAtmaka, a bhakta who sees his ishta devata with all these glories would certainly enjoys upAsana vidhi like this. But an Advaita upAsaka whenever trying to channelize his thoughts remembering the glories of upAsya devata would get the opposite thoughts like no it is not!! no it is not!! since the teaching of brahman in shruti is through neti neti only and whatever popularly accepted as brahma lakshana that IT is satyaM, jnAnam etc. is also not the true lakshana of nirguNa / nirvishesha brahman. At this point of time, my mind feeds the bhAshya vAkya from taitireeya where bhAshyakAra said : satyaM jnAnam etc. not directly denoted by these terms because brahman is devoid of genus and all specific features.



Baskarji - as I see it - there is no second best or inferior here - It is only the expression of the ultimate and upaasana with background knowledge helps to focus the mind on that ultimate.



\>\> yes prabhuji that is the main problem, when we focus on the ultimate, what we are doing right now and trying to achieve through it is not final destination, at the best what you achieve with these upAsana is chitta shuddhi, that means you are not yet even started the sAdhana for realizing the ultimate. And upAsana can fetch you the ticket only to hiraNya garbha lOka or some celestial abode. But is that the final?? No, it is impermanent and anytime you may return!! Hence always feel it is secondary meant for manda and madhyama adhikAri-s and my egoistic mind does not want to accept I am a mandAdhikAri.



That is the glory of the human birth. Otherwise how do you experience the beauty of that ultimate. Observing the whole creation and drama of Life becomes a joyfulentertainment and upaasanas only help to remind the mind to dwell on His Vibhuties - na ca mastaani bhuutani, pasyam me yogamaiswaram.



Ø Ishwara’s sarvajnatvaM, sarvashaktitvaM etc. holds good only in the realm of avidyA says bhAshyakAra. So, what does it mean?? in avidyAkshetra, being an avidyAvanta jeeva you are trying to adore, admire and worship an avidyAkruta saguNa brahman or sOpAdhika brahman for the krama mukti or chitta shuddhi !! sarcastically counters my other part of the advaitic mind.
