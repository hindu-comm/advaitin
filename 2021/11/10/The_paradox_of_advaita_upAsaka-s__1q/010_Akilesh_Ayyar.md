Namaste,

  

A few responses:

  

1\. That nirguna brahman which you seek to realize is not *without*features, upadhis, etc. It is *beyond*the *duality of features and featurelessness*. That is what is meant by neti neti. It is so beyond these things that in a deep sense it includes them.

  

It is not actionless. It is beyond the conceptual mind which must see something as *either*action *or*non-action.

  

It is not blank and plain. It is so intensely creative that it is literally unimaginable; its movement so sustained that it is absolutely still.

  

That creativity is intelligence. That supreme intelligence is nothing other than -- saguna Brahman -- Ishwara.

  

Ishwara is only a product of avidya if we believe we understand Ishwara tobe a simplecombination of attributes. But Ishwara is actually undefined. Omnipotence, etc. are all saying "so much power that it cannot be called power" in the same way that infinity is not a number.

  

Ishwara is supreme intelligence, supreme sentience -- and thus can listen to your prayers & poojas and grant you help. This is not a contradiction of advaita. This is no more or less miraculous than the seeming appearance of the universe in limitless Brahman in the first place. This is no more contradictory than the pursuit of mukti when avidya is unreal.

  

2\. Alternatively why not simply drop all these practices and pursue the formless only then? That is equally acceptable. Why force yourself to do something you do not believe in?
