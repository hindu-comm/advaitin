Namaste Sunil ji,

  

I just want to offer a small comment on the issue of menstruation. The menstruation practices you have mentioned is not fully Lokachara, it has Vaidika basis as well. The essential aspects of Rajodharmaare based on Sruti & Smriti, though it has grown in diverse ways among different communities (which come under Lokachara).

  

I have written on this in some detail in my book Menstruation Across Cultures-[https://www.amazon.in/Sabarimala-Confusion-Menstruation-Historical-Perspective/dp/9386473461/](https://www.amazon.in/Sabarimala-Confusion-Menstruation-Historical-Perspective/dp/9386473461/)
