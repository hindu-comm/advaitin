That is why the Mahabharata says that all the Upanishads are the cows, the milk-man is Lord Krishna and the Milk is the Bhagavad Gita. Sansjaya says at the end of the Original Bhagavad Gita of 745 verses, that Lord Krishna had given the essence of both the Aganas and Nigamas in it. Unfortunately that verse was deleted from the Original Bhagavad Gita, along with the other 44 verses deleted by Nimba-Bhashkara and , Shri Abhinava Shankara, who wrote the Shankara Bhashya on the Bhagavad Gita, did not restore these 45 deleted verses.
