Continuing from my previous post, the lyabanta passage, I presume Subbuji is talking about is the following:

  

तथा च श्रुतौ ‘अनात्मवित् शोचामि’ (छ उप ७/१/३) इति उपन्यस्य अत्मज्ञाने सर्वग्रन्थिविप्रमोक्ष उक्तः।

  

Similarly in the Sruti too,*after citing*, ‘Being not a knower of the self I grieve’ (Ch. Up. 7/1/3), it has been said that after acquiring the knowledge of the self, all knots are cut asunder (Mr. Panoli’s translation).

  

Here the lyabanta pada isउपन्यस्य (उप + नि + अस् धातु + ल्यप् प्रत्यय) and it means after citing.
