Bhagavad Gita App in Eight scripts along with the Commentary of Shankaracharya  
‘Bhuma Labs’ is engaged in several projects with the view of propagating Sanskrit literature. The production of Apps in Sanskrit is one such initiative.  
Bhuma Labs has as of now brought out five Apps on the Prasthana Traya Bhashyas of Shankaracharya, the Vedanta Paribhasha and the Vedanta sara.  
The new Gita App has the unique feature of enabling reading the Gita verses in eight scripts of Devanagari, Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam, Telugu, Gujarati, Bengali and Oriya.  
Also, along with each shloka is appended the Bhashya of Shankaracharya so that the seekers of knowledge are equipped with the meaning of the verses then and there.  
The searching of any word of the Gita is easily and quickly possible in the App.  
It is our fervent appeal that all benefit from the App by using it extensively. The message may be conveyed widely in various forums. The App is a wonderful device to the students of Sanskrit in Pathashalas, Gurukulas, Schools and Universities.
