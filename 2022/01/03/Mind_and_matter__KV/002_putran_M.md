Namaskaram Dennis-ji,

  

Thanks for the input. Someone I talked with brought up this type of example. The argument is that there is no independent chair and rather we can only speak of chair+mind. The "brown chair" is hence relative knowledge; one cannot say it is unreal because this mind "sees" it as brown and one cannot say is real since another mind sees it as black. Hence the chair (or the colour) is anirvachaniya; it is all the play of maya. What is real is the substratum Brahman.

  

So he wanted to arrive at Advaita but wanted to use this type of argument and example to first establish Maya.
