Here is my understanding.

  

As per adviatic position, the mind goes out with the senses and grasp the object. The senses could be defective - color blindness, or less than 20-20 vision etc hence even though the objects has its characteristic attributes, what senses can gather depends on their capacities - the senses feed what they have gathered - the attributive content of the object - they feed the mind- hence when the mind grasps the object the senses feed the attributes of the objects as per their capacities the perceiver perceives the object with those attributes. Hence the perception of the object by different perceivers can be different. In this srushti-drushti - the drashtaa can see the drusyam or the object of perception differently.

  

Only when the senses are corrected for their defects one can see the objects as it is - Hence in all scientific observations, they make sure the observations are independent of the observer's defects.

  

In the case of druishti- srushti vaada there is only one drashtaa. Hence from his reference what he sees is what is there -based on his pratyaksha pramaana. End of the story.
