I have not received Sri Putran prabhuji’s mail but received the below from Sri Sada prabhuji. Anyway after reading Sri putran prabhuji’s below observation,



//quote //

2. Is all such knowledge of the world relative (*hence* maya?) *because* the knowledge is product of an interaction between a body-mind and matter? For me chair is brown; for another person it is black; for an animal it may show up differently. So (the argument goes) none of this is absolutely true; shrishti is maya - only truth is Brahman the substratum.

//unquote//



I am getting following thoughts : why don’t we extend this to our perception towards shAstra as well?? What is the guarantee that our approach is genuine and more authentic when compared to other schools of thought!! After all our perception of shAstra do involve our own body-mind and matter is it not?? ONLY truth is brahman perhaps our own conclusion based on our own subjective approach towards shAstra we might be wrong or might not be wrong. When none of this is absolutely true, that statement itself might not be absolutely true since this statement is also originated from socalled conditioned body-mind-intellect complex only is it not??



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
