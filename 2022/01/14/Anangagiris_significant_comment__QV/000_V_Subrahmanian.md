For the gloss on the Bhashya for the Bhagavadgita verse:

  

देवद्विजगुरुप्राज्ञपूजनंशौचमार्जवम्।  
ब्रह्मचर्यमहिंसा च शारीरं तप उच्यते ॥ १४ ॥

  

Anandagiri says, for 'deva' it is brahma vishnu shiva, etc.  

  

तत्र शारीरं तपः निर्दिशति -



देवेति।

देवाः -ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवादयः

  

It is significant that Anandagiri is admitted to be of the

13th Century BCE, pre-Vidyaranya.
