Namaskaram,

  

(Below was written in slightlyedited form as a note of advice/guidance to a couple of younger familymembers.)

**  **

**Two Perfections to remind oneself of**

  

1.

  

-\> I am 'Perfect' already

-\> I am 'Perfect' always  

  

-\> I am True, Constant, Full, Complete.

-\> I am Bliss, Consciousness.

  

-\> I am like Undivided non-dual Space, identifying self with a body-mind pot & *imagining* i am a pot-space that must become bigger and biggest to secure peace and happiness.

  

-\> i must realize that I, *the Perfection I already am*: that is the only Moksha for the self, the liberation from identifications based in ignorance & the consequent sufferings etc.

  

-\> No matter what happens at the pot-level, I do not change; not bigger or smaller, not more or less; constant, unchanging, unchangeable. Always full, always complete. Truth, Consciousness, Infinitude, Bliss.

  

2.

  

But before establishing the moksha-realization, while we are still identifying self as a pot-space among other pot-spaces, there is another perfection to be mindful of.

  

-\> The Order that constitutes Life, Experience, Events & Happenings is Perfect.

  

-\> This moment is correct & proper.

  

-\> Ishvara coordinates His manifestation optimally to obtain the jivas the karmaphala for their past karma (including of past lives) and to present/grace them the opportunity for progressing 'towards' Moksha.

  

-\> We experience therefore only what is befitting us as karmaphala, to clear obstructions and accumulations (at the 'subtle-body' level) that we ourselves have caused through our karma.

  

-\> You are the determining factor for what you experience, and what you experience is perfectly aligned to your choices.

  

-\> Do not regret Life therefore, past or present. It is as it should be. Things happen as they need to happen.

  

-\> Accept Ishvara's will and judgment with regard to the earned results of our actions (including what comes in the form of harassing recollections and thoughts), and be detached as a witness of His Order thereby.

  

-\> But learn from thepast to inform choices hereon. At this moment, we have the option and opportunity to positively impact & influence the future: by choosing good over bad, truth over untruth, dharma over adharma, 'God-consciousness' over 'pot-consciousness', etc.

  

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
