Namaskaram,



> 
>
> -\> Accept Ishvara's will and judgment with regard to the earned
> results of our actions (including what comes in the form of harassing
> recollections and thoughts), and be detached as a witness of His Order
> thereby.
>
>   
>
> -\> But learn from thepast to inform choices hereon. At this moment,
> we have the option and opportunity to positively impact & influence
> the future: by choosing good over bad, truth over untruth, dharma over
> adharma, 'God-consciousness' over 'pot-consciousness', etc.
>
>   

  

In practice there are two sides to this perspective. The first as I said is recognizing our experiences as karmaphala for past decisions and actions. The second is staying aware that all karma will eventually return to us as phala. No good or dharmic act goes to "waste"; it will add to our account and later fructify. This motivates us to stay the course, to keep putting in the effort to make 'right' choices even in the midst of hardships. Our focus shifts to the Now and what we can do now; rather than getting overwhelmed by the force of what we have to experience from our past. For we know that the minutest sacrifice of this moment, the effort to resolve correctly, to even think correctly (or the higher thought/knowledge), will bear fruit later on in our spiritual journey: Ishvara knows and will direct the whole universe that we get the due result. As a corollary, because we know that every 'action' begets appropriate result, we are not overly caught up (i.e. we develop detachment) with regard to what result*must*follow our present action: Ishvara knows and we accept His judgment thatis based on Total Knowledge.

  

Ref: Bhagavad Gita 6.41-45[https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/6/verse/41-42](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/6/verse/41-42)etc.

PS Aside, with regard to above, "every 'action' begets appropriate result":

  

We don't have to think of this as always in a one-to-one sense (like this phala has to be for that particular action), but again in a holistic way. We shape our "subtle body" through our actions and our experiences will correspond with this "shape": if we experience a certain "virus" phala, then it means our subtle-body is karma-built to act as its receptor and getting such a phala is natural and 'necessary' to resolve that karma-accretion.
