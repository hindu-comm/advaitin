Namaskaram Sri Putran

  

Your commentary on the two perfections is reminiscent of the following well-known mantra, where the purnatvam can be interpreted as perfection or completeness. 🙂

  

ॐपूर्णमदःपूर्णमिदंपूर्णात्पूर्णमुदच्यते।  
पूर्णस्यपूर्णमादायपूर्णमेवावशिष्यते॥  
ॐशान्तिःशान्तिःशान्तिः॥  
Om Puurnnam-Adah Puurnnam-Idam Puurnnaat-Puurnnam-Udacyate \|  
Puurnnasya Puurnnam-Aadaaya Puurnnam-Eva-Avashissyate \|\|  
Om Shaantih Shaantih Shaantih \|\|  
  
**Meaning:**  
1:Om,That(Outer World) isPurna(Full with Divine Consciousness);This(Inner World) is alsoPurna(Full with Divine Consciousness); FromPurna is manifested Purna(From the Fullness of Divine Consciousness the World is manifested),  
2:Taking PurnafromPurna,Purna indeed remains(Because Divine Consciousness is Non-Dual and Infinite),  
3:Om,Peace,Peace,Peace.
