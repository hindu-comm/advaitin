Namaskaram Vinodh-ji,

  

Yes that is a nice connection: the reference to "Two Perfections" is indeed reminiscent of this mantra. The Self is Purna; the Lord is Purna. The Lord (+jivajagat) is vivarta/adhyasa on Self. Take away the "Lord", the Self the Reality remains.

  

The Lord is purna in reality for He is non-different from the Self; but He is purna also in the context of His appearance. *The Self initially is "known" as the Lord.* Because it is still vivarta/adhyasa only, this identification as "Lord" (like identification of sarpa) even though purna at its level can be removed (taken away) through the knowledge of the Self. T*he Lord of manifold powers and manifestations is thus realized as the non-dual Self*. The purna that ever is remains.
