Dear Subbuji,

  

Yes, the scholars of the earlier times, particularly those before the British-raj, knew the ancient history of India well, and could understand the Ramayana and the Mahabharata very well. Mahabharata says that one has to read the Mahabharata well to understand the Vedas, as Vedas say that Vedas love Parokshta interpretation, and the Mahabharata does have a lot of paroksha uktis. Panini did his great work on Sanskrit grammar, setting standards for the future, but Panini never told anywhere to interpret the Vedic and Pauranic texts, with the Paninian knowledge. Today's Sanskrit scholars have become unable to understand what is in the Mahabharata and Ramayana, consequently, today's Sanskrit scholars have been unable to find the date of the Mahabharata war, and of the Ramayana times, as they have not been able to interpret what historical informations are given in these epics.  

  

On the dating of the Mahabharata war, all the attempts till today have failed, including the wrong dating of Prof. Raghavan etc. The INDIC Academy announced last year in Febriuary 2021, that it has formed a secret team of three Sanskrit scholars and three Astronomers, with another scholar to lead the secret team, but even after one year there is no news from the Indic Academy. INDIC Academy has very cleverly kept the names in the team secret so that nobody can pressurise them on the progress of that work.  

  

As regards the date of Ramayana, there are very clear statements saying that it took place in the early Dwapara yuga and that some grahas were Uscha (or exalted) and some are in Svagrha (or in their own house) in Lord Rama's birth-chart, but the schoars have failed to understand the value of these statements. and could not find the date of birth of Lord Ram, till today.  

  

Today's scholars don't even know the true date of Adi Shankaracharya, though he himself mentioned the great king Purnavarman or Haala, of the Satavahana dynasty, who met him personally. Our present day Indian historians don't care for the historical facts. The Shankara-mathas of Dwarka, Puri and Kanchipuram as well as the Kudali Shringeri hindeed have their guruparampara from 509 BCE, yet the ***Tunga Sringeri,*** which was established much later, in the 13th century AD (CE), with the help of the Vijayanagara kings Hukka and Bukka , had brain-washed our historians, claiming that Adi Shankara was born in the 8th century AD (CE). They don't have respect for the historical truths, though Lord Krishna told in the Mahabharata that (speaking of) the truth is the Highest Dharma.  

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
