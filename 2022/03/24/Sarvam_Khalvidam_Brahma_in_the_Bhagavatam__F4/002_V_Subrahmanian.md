Here, Brahman is stated to take the form of the world, owing to the karma of the jiva-s. However, the true nature of Brahman is not the world-form; it is 'prapanchopashamam' (totally free of any connection whatsoever with the world) as the Mandukya Upanishad says. So, that is the true nature of Brhaman.

  

Even in the 'sarvam khalvidam brahma' situation, the world that is seen, is a wrong perception of Brahman. In other words, we should be seeing/perceiving/understanding Brahman but we are wrongly seeing Brahman as the world. Sarvam khalvidam brahma is the statement that tells us 'this world is truly Brahman and not the world. This snake that I am seeing is actually rope, wrongly perceived.'
