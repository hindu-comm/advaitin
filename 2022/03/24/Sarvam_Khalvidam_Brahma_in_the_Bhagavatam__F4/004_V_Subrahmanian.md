Not really false. Murti is also devised by the Shaastra to enable seekers who are not able to visualize Brahman 'as expressing itself as the whole universe' consisting of the good, the bad and the ugly.' So, such sadhakas have to be helped by presenting to them Brahman, in the form of the 'good, beautiful, lovely, etc.only.' For them are the stories woven around a form of Brahman like Krishna, Rama, etc. They have to evolve from that to be able to eventually 'worship, appreciate' Brahman as everything in the universe. As Swami Parmarthananda so nicely put: 'From ekarupa Ishwara to vishvarupa Ishwara to arUpa Ishwara' is the journey. Trust this helps.

  

Swami Madhusudana Saraswati has said: It is tasyaivaham - I belong to Him alone'. Then it is mamaivaasau - He is mine alone. Then finally it is: sa evaaham - I am He alone. This is the path of evolution.
