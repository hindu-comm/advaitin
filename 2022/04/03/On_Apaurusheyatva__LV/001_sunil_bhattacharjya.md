Dear friends,

  

The word "***Apaurusheyatva***" may mean different things to different people, but should that be really so?. Probably the best equivalent word of "Apaurusheyatva" in English would be "Anonymity", or "invisibility" or it may even mean "namelessnes". At times certain actions or statements of a Rishi or Saint in the Vedas may be too difficult to understand and it is attributed to some magical ability of the rishi(s), without naming them as the original source, whereas the rishi(s) (himself or themselves) may not think so, nor may feel the need to explain everything to everybody. In the Indian context, these apaurusheyatva is acceptable, without giving credit to each and everyone who discovered each and every eternal truth. However, one who is not an Indian, may find it difficult to accept all the ancient statements without knowing who, how and when they made the statements.  

  

Similar situations are seen in the Abrahamic religions too. For example, Moses, a prophet, an Israelite by birth, was brought up as a prince by an Egyptian princess, but later on when he became an adult, he saw an Egyptian severely punishing an Israelite slave, and he killed that Egyptian and he had to leave Egypt, as his revengeful action would not be acceptable to the Egyptian royalty. He went to Midian, where he married and had his family. Later on, the Israelites were experiencing great difficulties in Egypt and Moses saved them by safely taking them out of Egypt. This is called the **Exodus** in the Bible.  

  

While Moses succeeded in taking out the Israelites to safety, by crossing the seas, the Egyptian King, pursued them with his army with 600 chariots, many thousands of Cavalty and more than hundred thousand foot soldiers, and they were all got killed in the sea, which they tried to cross. The Exodus took place more than three and a half thousand years ago, and how Moses could take out the Israelites to safety by crossing the sea has been a mystery all these thirty five centuries. It is only now that the things are being clear as to how the super-intelligent prophet Moses could take out the Israelites, by finding the way for him and his company, through the seas, and why the Egyptian king and his huge army failed and got killed in the sea  

  

My 2 cents

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
