Dr. Gokulakrishna Aiyer made a very significant observation: Even those who do not know Sanskrit and have not studied the shaastras, even they have to listen to such vakyartha sadas. This will give them punyam. What is this punyam? It will generate interest in them to study Sanskrit and thereupon study the shastras and finally get the supreme benefit of the shastras: atma jnanam. So even if one does not understand anything in these sessions will surely benefit potentially.
