Namase Sadaji,

  

You are right when you say:

**" it is a perfect ordered system and not a random creation."**

However, it appears that at the level of the individuals there is some temporary leeway or liberty to act, as if independently, thus giving scope for disorderliness. However the things are brought to order from time to time during the major and the minor avataras.  

  

For example, at the end of the Bhagavad Gita, the Lord did not ask Arjuna to act according to what the Lord told him, but enquired what Arjuna had decided to do. So also after the Lord talked to Karna, he let Karna decide what he wanted to do.  

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
