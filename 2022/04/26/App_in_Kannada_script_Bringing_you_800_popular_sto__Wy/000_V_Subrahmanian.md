This is an App in Kannada script: Bringing you \>800 popular stotras, ashtottaras, vedamantras, etc  
  
Features  
\* Search option, Option to save "favorite" stotra/mantra to the TOP  
\* zoom option with slider  
\* White text on Black background to save battery & less stress to read  
\* Black text on White background option available (Settings button)  
\* Readable in both landscape & portrait mode  
\* Offline usable, light, free & no popups  
\* Saves last opened stotra (if home button used to exit)
