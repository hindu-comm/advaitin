Good points, Putran-ji. Unfortunately, I did not have the inclination to dig deeper into Nader’s theory, but I agree with what you say.

  

On a related note, a Swami was giving a talk on Taittiriya bhashya recently and was narrating an anecdote of another swami. From time to time, the other swami, who was also delivering lectures on Vedanta, would feel like he’s having deep and interesting new thoughts on the topics he was lecturing about. Every single time, however, when he goes back after the lecture to check his notes from decades back when he was a student of Vedanta, he would find that all these ideas were already there before. This is the inherent nature of Vedanta. Every time one intuitively understands Vedanta in a spontaneous burst of thoughts, it feels so fresh and new, but the same ideas have been there since time immemorial. It is the freshest and the most ancient at the same time! 😊
