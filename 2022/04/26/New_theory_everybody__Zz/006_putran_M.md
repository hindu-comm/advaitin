This felt tragic and painful to read. I do not consider myself knowledgeable enough to give an apt reply, but have also not seen this video. I will give some general thoughts. Maybe it can be a topic for future discussion if others participate. The notion that we accept without scientific research makes it appear that such objective or external research (whatever that be) can replace or are somehow superior to the traditional methods. Like we are incompetents in our taking to Sravanam and mananam and must learn once again from Science, instead of the other way around. I think this is ridiculous. We can build nice logics, analogies or supposed experiments to support Advaita to our intuition - but all the same, they don’t constitute pramana and one support is not superior to another. Great to have more for the purpose *for some minds that are more impressed by some than others*, and the modern mind likes to align with “scientific research”; but we are standing on shraddha and Sravana till we remove obstructions (through pravritti marga , Mananam, nidhidhyasanam, etc.) and have realization - which is an internal thing to happen. Those who think they are *proving* Advaita any more through newer jugglery are fooling themselves and others.
