Namaskars, Putran ji,

  

I think you have totally mistaken my purport. The advaita philosophy is undoubtedly on a sound footing because of the rigorous examination of the self by our sages. The enquiry was entirely scientific, and also, as time passed by, it was further strengthened by the arguments of commentators such as Shankaracharya, Madhusudana Saraswati and such.

  

What I wished to compliment in my earlier mail is different. Modern science has also been investigating the issue of whether matter is primary or consciousness is primary. Till recently the opinion was in favor of matter but recently the multidisciplinary scientific research, which was not available to our sages, is also coming to similar conclusions to which the preceptors of non-dualism came. This is a sort of validation of the philosophy by the modern scientific tools. This sort of validation is important for any philosophy. For instance, a sectarian philosophy which talks of Shiva or Vishnu or Shakti or Ganesha or someone else as the Supreme Reality would not get validated by modern science.

  

Such corroborative efforts do not in any way diminish the greatness of the intuitive methods of our sages who dedicated their lives for tapas and shraddhaa andrealized such truth.

Regards,

Aravinda Rao
