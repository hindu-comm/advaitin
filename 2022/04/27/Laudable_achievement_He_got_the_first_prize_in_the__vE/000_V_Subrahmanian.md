On Chaitra Krishnasthami (April 24), the Akhilabharatiya Shalaka Pariksha was organised by Sanskrit Bharati (Kashi). I participated in the Vedanta subject (first three prakaranas of Vidyaranyamuni’s Panchadashi) and got the first prize. Prof Vashishtha Tripathi was the chief guest.

  
The Sanskrit commentary of Ramakrishna was very helpful in the preparation. In the entire Panchadashi, there is one cryptic verse (1.50) which is the most difficult one to understand. And interestingly, the examiners asked the same at the very beginning of the second round. For this, I had taken help from many people and teachers, especially from Dr Tulasi Kumar Joshi acharya ji. And thanks to the Internet, we have access to the recorded lectures of many scholars and acharyas. It helped a lot in clearing many doubts.
