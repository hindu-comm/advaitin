> > I wanted to ask about this from many days,since we know that vedam
> > is nityam and apaurusheya,why does bhagavan bhashyakara in 1.1.3.
> > shastrayonitvat say that Brahman is the karta of vedam?  

  

It is because the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad says: The Veda is as though the breath of Brahman.

  

Just like the world is created, sustained and withdrawn during pralayam, the Veda too, though not really authored, nitya, is 'manifested' at the beginning of every cycle of creation, remains and then withdrawn during pralaya. That way it can be said that 'Brahman is the karta of Veda.'
It 'originates' from Brahman, that way. So Brahman is the 'kartaa' of the Veda, not literally.

  

Trust this clarifies.
