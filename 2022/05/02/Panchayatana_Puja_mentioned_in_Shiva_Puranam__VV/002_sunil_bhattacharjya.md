Dear friends,

  

In another purana, Lord Krishna also says about the Panchayatana puja and He agives the details, such as deity is to be worshipped for what. Lord Sun is to be worshipped for health. For example, Lord Krishna told his own son Samba to worship the Sun to get rid of his Leprosy.  

  

Of course, the panchayatana puja is to done in the right way following all the dos and don'ts. All pratima puja are for meant for the commoners, till one begins to see God in everything and everywhere. Lord Krishna says "Pratime alpabuddhinam, sarvatra samadarshineam".
