It is well known that the name \*adhyasa bhashya\* has not been mentioned by Shankara. It is now learnt that this name has been first used in the \*Bhamati\* in about 6 places. In the Panchapadika this word does not exist. Nor does Sureshvaracharya mentions this in the Taittiriyaka bhashya vartika. We do not know if this name exist in the Brihadaranyaka bhashya vartika or in the Naishkarmya siddhi.
