Thank you for information about the book on Vidyashankara temple. Now I could get a good paper, and this paper has lot of information about the Vidyashankara Temple in Tunga Shringeri, The details of the paper is as follows:  

**ASPECTS OF OBSERVATIONAL ASTRONOMY IN INDIA: THE VIDYASANKARA TEMPLE AT  **

**SRINGERI**, by N. Kameswara Rao .& Priya Thakur, both from Indian Institute of Astrophysics,  

Bangalore, and Published in the Journal of Astronomical History and Heritage, 14(2), 136 -144 (2011).

Both the authors were from from the Indian Institute of Astrophysics, Bangalore.  

  

**Abstract:** The navaranga in the medieval stone temple of Vidyasankara at Sringeri, built around A.D.  

1350, has twelve zodiacal pillars arrange**d** in a square with the zodiacal signs carved on them. It has  

been claimed that the morning sunrise lights up the pillar that corresponds to the zodiacal constellation  

in which Sun is located at that time, so the temple can be used as an instrument to predict calendar  

days. We carried out observations to investigate this aspect by monitoring both sunrises and sunsets,  

and found that the correspondence between the illumination of specific pillars and the zodiacal sign of  

the Sun could only be maintained if the epoch for such an arrangement was around 2000 B.C. The  

implications of this finding are discussed in this paper.
