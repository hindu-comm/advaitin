Namaste Sunder Rajan-ji,

  

I am a far cry from Mark Twain. It is a rare experience to read one's own obituaries. I enjoyed it very much here and elsewhere.

  

As an Advaitin, I am at a stage now where I am almost totally convinced that death is a non-event as illusory as one's transit from the so-called dreaming to the so-called waking. So, what appears before one's eyes as the world is a matter of blissful excitement to me though the contents of which might be tolerable or intolerable in line with one's karma. This was the issue I wanted to elaborate to my recently-deceased eldest brother. But, I didn't get an opportunity.
He, an agnostic, outdid me in the deft manner in which he meticulously arranged details of his so-called final exit.

  

My brother's story is here which created the confusion that it was I who had gone. Hope the Group can tolerate this aside.
