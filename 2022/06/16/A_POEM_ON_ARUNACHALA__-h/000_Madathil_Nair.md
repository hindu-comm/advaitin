Namaste.

  

Please read my poem "Going Round Arunachala" here. It is posted here because I believe it has advaita import.

  

[https://www.boloji.com/poem/28123/going-round-arunachala](https://www.boloji.com/poem/28123/going-round-arunachala)  

  

I have gone round the mount four times. The last one was on 26th February 2021. The previous three were done about ten years before all together in one day (45 km total).

  

It is an exalting experience in spite of the din and sound at certain sections of the route.

  

Pranams.

  

Madathil Nair
