Wikipedia:On the day of his demise in the year 1980 “Magh Shukla Chaturdashi”, he asked his disciples to sing the "Ayodhya Tyaga" story of[Ramayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramayana "Ramayana")for him; he himself did the recitation of[Sri Sukta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Sukta "Sri Sukta")and at the end by keeping the idol of[Krishna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krishna "Krishna")on his chest, he passed away by reciting "Shiva Shiva Shiva" thrice.
