praNAms Sri Subbu prabhuji

Hare Krishna



nanna hrutpurvaka namanagaLu haagu Shubha haaraikegaLu. Bhagavati vAgdeviya krupaa kataakha nimma mele sadaa erali. shAradA, chandramouLishwara, sadgurugala anugrahadinda aayussu, aarogya, adhyAtmika sampattu saMpUrNavaagi vruddhiyaagali endu praarthisuttene.



Like many others, I too had the good fortune to meet Sri Subbu prabhuji in Chamrajpet here in Bengaluru, more than decade back (though I have ample opportunities to meet him personally whenever I want as I am also staying in Bengaluru, so far I have met him only once), during our brief interaction (definitely not about vedAnta 😊 I realized that he is not just an armchair philosopher he is literally living sanyasi samAna jeevana dedicating his full time in adhyayana, mananAdi adhyAtmika sAdhana. With my very little knowledge in shankarAdvaita I used to vehemently argue with him in lists but deepest in my heart I always have utmost respect to him.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
