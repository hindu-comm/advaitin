[[Ramakrishna Upadrasta	2022-07-06, 09:49:08 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/qU9JOVdP83w)]]

namaste to All,

  

This is indeed a highly joyful news for all the advata and shaastra lists followers (the Advaita-L list, the formeradvaitin-yahoo-groups, the bvparishad, and the current list).

  

In receiving the puraskaara from the Jagadgurus, Brahma Shri Subbu-ji is following the footsteps of His Pujya Guruji Brahma Shri Jivanmukta Shri D. S. SubbaramaiayaGaruin his adherence to the Sringeri Parampara.

  

I too have had the honor of darshan of Brahma Shri-Subbu-ji sometime back in sringeri itself. His brahmaniShTha, shaastra-niShTha, guru-parampara-niShTha, guru-bhakti are well known, along with his compassion, timely advice and blessings that have continuously been there for many many saadhakas at various stages.

  

saaShTaang praNAmaHto Brahma Shri Subbu-ji and His pUjya guruji and His guru-parampara.  

  

namaste

Ramakrishna
