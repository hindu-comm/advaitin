Romila Thapar wants to say that Mahmud wanted to look for the idol of one of the pagan goddesses that could not be destroyed by the Muslims. This excuse invented by Thapar will not fool any Indian. Mahmud attacked Somnath temple, as he wanted to loot the riches to fill his coffers. Additionally he wanted to give the justification that he was was against idol worshipping, which Islam does not permit.  

  

In the 10th century there was a sort of civil war even around the Muslim's holy place Kaba and the black stone taken away from there (Kaba) and broken into pieces. Later on some of the broken pieces from the blackstone was recovered and put back in Kaba.  

  

The Muslims did not know that for the Hindus if an idol is broken, it is replaced by a new one and activated. The activation of the idol is called the "Prana-pratistha", as once an idol is broken, it becomes inactive and it is promptly discarded and replaced by a new idol, where the prana-pratistha was done, before using it for worship.
