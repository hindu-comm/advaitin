Thank you Putranji. I appear to forget at times that the Advaitin group is not interested in the rectification of the distortions in Ancient Indian History, and related topics. Recently there were some mails on adoring a muslim yogi, and I wondered how such things could appear in this great group.  

  

You must be aware that the Hindus' fifth Veda is very concerned about the need of the correct ancient Indian History. It does pain me that the ancient Indian History is distorted, it pains me more so that even the date of our dearest Adi Shankaracharya has been distorted. There was no Shankaracharya born in 788 AD and this fake date of 788 AD is promoted by none other than the Tunga Sringeri Math. Even the great Abhinava Shankara of the Kanchi Kamakoti math was not born in 788 AD, though he became the Mathadhipati of the Kanchi Kamakoti math in 788 AD.  

  

I am sure Adi Shankaracharya must be greatly perturbed and he must be cursing all among us, who are not opposing the false year of 788 AD being promoted as Adi Shankaracharya's birth year. Please join me in my prayer to Adi Shankaracharya to show some miracle and compel the Tunga Sringeri Math to discard their fake date 788 AD immediately and adopt ***the correct year of 509 BCE as the birth year of Adi Shankaracharya***. Didn't Lord Krishna say in the Mahabharata that **Truth is Dharma**?  

  

Thanks,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
