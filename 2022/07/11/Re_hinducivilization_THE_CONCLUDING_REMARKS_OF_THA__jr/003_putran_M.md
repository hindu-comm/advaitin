Namaskaram Sunil-ji,

  

I did not interfere in your thoughts on the dating of Shankaracharya (except earlier with regard to the language you used and some strong assertions youmade without giving references); the topic in anycase is withinour list scope. If others have a different opinion, they can discuss with you. I don't know enough of these historical details to take a strong position on this; it would be interesting conversation if we had members who wantedto argue for the788 ad date.

  

I also don't know whether the later date compromises other important chronologies that tradition has. If that can be shown, then you have a serious case for everyone to consider. For example, I can imagine Western historians may use a later date of Shankara to align with a post Buddha/Christ dating of scriptures he refers to. But this is not a necessary implication. It is possible that Shankara was born in 788 AD and still the BG was much earlier. Another such dating is with regard to the Bhagavatham; the fact that Shankara does not refer to this scripture is taken as a strong cue that he predated it. Whereas Madhvacharya referred to it and hence post-dated it. However the tradition will not accept such an argument (which again is not necessary implication of whether Shankara mentions or not). So, basically if Shankara being born in 788 AD leads to a fundamental contradiction in the way Tradition understands its own histories, then you are right: we cannot play the ostrich and avoid the topic.

  

The Muslim yogi topic was in the context of him being a yogi, initiated by a Hindu/advaita guru and writing books on Hinduism, and himself teaching Hindu thought to others; then members commented on the fact that he still seems to retain Muslim identity, whether he can be trusted etc.

  

Your present topic however on Thapar and Islamic invaders is not directly related (or you have not shown a significant connection) to learning about advaita or the advaita sampradaya; it strays from our list objectives at the very start.
