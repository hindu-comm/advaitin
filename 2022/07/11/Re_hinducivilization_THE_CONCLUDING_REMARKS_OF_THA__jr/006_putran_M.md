Namaskaram Sunil-ji,

  

I did some google search and these two sites have the two opposing views:

  

[https://www.advaita-vedanta.org/avhp/dating-Sankara.html](https://www.advaita-vedanta.org/avhp/dating-Sankara.html) (788 AD)  

[https://janamejayan.wordpress.com/2010/01/31/sringeri-sharada-peetham/](https://janamejayan.wordpress.com/2010/01/31/sringeri-sharada-peetham/) (509 BCE)  

  

What is your opinion on the assertion that Shankara has quoted Dharmakirti verbatim in Upadesasahariand that he quoted him has also been confirmed by Sureswaracharya? Dharmakirti is being dated as belonging to 6th century ad.

  

I see Janamejayan responds to this in the comments, citing someone to date the Buddha at 19th century BC. Do you agree to this? He thinks Dharmakirti should be dated 6th century BCE.

  

As I said, I don't know enough to analyze these things. However, apart from "truth", is there a dire consequence to other traditional understanding of chronologies and times if one should adopt 788 AD? (Of course, the history forwarded by the individual mathas are based on their dating of Shankara; but what about the general Hindu's picture of India, scriptures, etc. It seems *traditionalists* of all the mathas are able to maintain a common sense of history regarding the Vedic tradition no matter thisparticular divergence of opinion regarding the date of Shankara. It would be interesting to know how much the proponents of 788 AD date are also questioning Veda apaurusheyatva (in some modern or non-traditional sense), also think BG came after Christ, Upanishads were written much later, and so on. The dating of Shankara is an easier topic to express affinity to western indological position without appearing to compromise our own fundamentally. There are however many such things in traditional viewpoints that get dismissed by modern academics.

  

But I must think that since the Sringeri matha acharyas subscribe to this latter date, it is an isolatedissue in general thatdoesnot force us into compromising othertraditionalpositions.
