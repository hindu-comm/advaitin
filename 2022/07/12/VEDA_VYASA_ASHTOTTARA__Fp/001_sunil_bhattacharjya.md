Dear Subbuji,

  

You have sent the **Veda Vyasa Ashtottara Shatanamavali**, iat the right time, just before the Vyasa Purnima day. The  

77^(th) verse says as follows:

  

ॐ কুধৰ্মপৰিহাৰকায় नमः ।

  

By reciting this, the advaitins of the Shankara mathas have promised that they would desist from telling lies, as lying is at  

the root of all kudharmas. Hope now onwards the advaitins will loudly and widely announce that Adi Shankaracharya was  

born in 509 BCE, and that was the 14^(th) year of reign of ShriHarsha Vikramaditya, and he is famous for his work  

“Khandana khanda khadya” The year 457 BCE was the start of the first Vikrama Samvat. and this was clearly mentioned  

in Albiruni’s account. To my understanding, in early 20th century, the Mahaswami of the Sringeri math did declare that  

Adi Shankara was born in the 14^(th) year of reign of Vikramaditya, but due to the British Raj and later on the Raj of the  

Marxist historians in the historical matters, the true dating of Adi Shankara was not really taken up.

  

The Madhaviya Shankara Digvijaya also clearly mentions that the Khandankar Vikramaditya was a contemporary of Adi  

Shankarachaya.

  

I will particularly request Dr. V. R. Gowrishankarji the CEO and Administrator of the Dakshinamnaya Sri Sringeri Sharada  

Peetham, for the last 36 years since 1986, to urgently declare the truth that Adi Shankaracharya was born in 509 BCE. **  **

**Henceforth the Ancient Indian History books will write the true date of Adi Shanjaracharya as 509 BCE.** I  

don’t wish the great Shringeri math to fail to be appear among the truth-speakers., as very soon, new books on Ancient  

Indian History wiil be get published. I wish the Lord will give the Moral courage to Dr. V.R.Gowrishankaraji to be be bold to  

accept the truth of Adi Shankara’s birth in 509 BCE.

  

I shall also request Subbuji to send a copy of this mail to Dr. Gowrishankarji and I am also sending a copy of this letter to  

the email-id of the Sringeri Math.

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
