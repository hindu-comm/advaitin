Dear Putran ji,

  

Is a very interesting scenario that you have presented. There is definitely Vedanta, Advaita in this. Vedanta has a solution to this: jeevtva, that is, identification with the body mind complex is Anadi, without any beginning at all. This provides for the type of sukshma sharira a person has. Karmik forces that act at every situation is also
enmeshed in this whole scheme. If B has to experience Joy or sorrow or delusion or whatever emotion like fear love revenge anger et cetera, it needs a catalyst. That can happen from an external source like A, or his own remembering of a past event.

  

Prakriti that is nature will provide all the infrastructure for this to happen. It will place a different individual against or in favour of B. A natural event like thunder or earthquake or hurricane can trigger anger or whatever emotion in a person and direct it to another person. For A to direct an emotion to B and for B to be affected by that, there is a karmik connection. A's karma makes him generate a certain emotion and direct it towards B or the society in general. The various individuals that get affected by that is also a karmik effect that they have to go through. This scenario is typically scene in a terrorist attack.

  

Fortunately the Shastra has put in place the various do's and don'ts that an individual has to mandatory practice in order to regulate this terrible karmik force coming into play. That is where free will comes into place.

Subbu.
