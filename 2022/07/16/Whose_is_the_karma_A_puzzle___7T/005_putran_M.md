In practical sense, suppose the terrorist is caught and the government authorities decide to hang him. This may be fully aligned to dharma, so by carrying out that duty, the authorities are not accruing (at least) negative karma. The terrorist's action is fully responsible for the phala of hanging; the authorities are merely acting as catalysts and they don't acquire negative karma in the process either because their action is aligned to dharma. On the other hand, suppose on account of the terrorist's actions, a mob rages and kills other innocent people. Then though the killed experience only phala for their own actions, still the mob is indulging in adharma and hence accruing negative karma. Their choice to kill is wholly their own responsibility and cannot be indirectly faulted on the original terrorist - he has his fault no doubt and will get his phala whether via the mob or otherwise, but the mob has been set up with the test by Ishvara on account of their karma and they will still have to own up to and will be held responsible for their choices.
