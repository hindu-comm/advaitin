Namaste Putran ji,

Based on the nyAya from the gItA Sloka, यद्यदाचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः ।

स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तदनुवर्तते ॥ २१ ॥

we can say that people in positions of trust, leadership, influence have a greater responsibility than those who are not so placed. So if A is such a person of influence, I think his failure to uphold dharma would be subject to greater punishment.

  

Even in the manusmRti we find evidence of this principle, e.g. It is said that a theft committed by brAhmaNa-s to be punishable to a greater extent (a hundred fold more punitive) than a similar act by someone else who was not in such a position of influence. If a commoner is fined one kArShApaNa, the king should be fined a 1,000 kArShApaNa etc.

  

The same principle would apply to the pApa phala arising from A's poor actions if A was in a position of influence.

  

Therefore, in my view it is not B's performance of bad actions seeing A that is the cause for A's greater demerit - it is A's being in a position of influence.

  

B still has his free will, so B has to suffer from the consequences of his actions. 'A' is responsible but the severity is proportional to his position of influence.

  

Kind regards,

Venkatraghavan
