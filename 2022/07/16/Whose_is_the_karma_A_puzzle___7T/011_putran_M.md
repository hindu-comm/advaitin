Namaskaram,



> In the case of brahmanas, yes those born in that varna in a
> traditional setting have knowledge that while in vyavaharika, there is
> a pre-programmed svadharma that corresponds to them and by which they
> should abide. The “influencers” (like family, acharya, tradition etc)
> that guide him thus are in alignment with Dharma, therefore they are
> not karmically responsible (in a negative sense) since the process and
> their roles is in the natural flow with Ishvara’s Order. Whereas the
> “influencers” who guide him to deviate from his svadharma, they are
> karmically responsible for their adharma-influencing-karma and the
> Brahmana who deviates in the end will still have to suffer for his
> deviation (under such wrong influence) for his karma of having
> deviated from his svadharma.
>
>   
>
>
