Hare Krishna



I am just wondering whether all these exercises really required to know who gets what and through what!!?? When karma is meant for chitta shuddhi and ultimately leading us to nivrutti mArga. For dharma jignAsa shAstra is the ultimate source of reference. When Krishna says svadharme nidhanaM shreyaH paradharmO bhayAvahaH, what is our svadharma that needs to be taught by Achaarya, who knows the tradition (saMpradAyavida) is it not?? ayaM dharmOyamadharmamiti shAstrameva vijnAne kAraNaM, ateendrayatvAt tayOH, see vedAnta sUtra bhAshya 3-1-25 for AchaaryOpadesha with regard to what is dharma and what is adharma.
Don’t we have to often remember that we have to do our karma by offering it to Ishwara, Ishwara sharaNAgati, IshwarArpita buddhi?? Let HIM decide what is dharma and what is adharma after all he is karmAdhyAksha and karmaphaladAta. Pardon me for philosophizing the general practical scenarios. But do we have any other alternative here to think it in some other way!!??



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
