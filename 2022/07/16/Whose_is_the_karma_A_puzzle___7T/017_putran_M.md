Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

If Ramu watches a cricket match on TV, is that for Chitta shuddhi or for following Acharya instructions? Is he going to say “Let Him decide if this is dharma or not”? Rather he already decided in his heart of hearts this is svadharma in his body-mind-life context and is following it!

  

But actually … No. The kids are to blame, it is the wife, it is Indra and Chandra, ahem, no I am a Vedantin - it is vasana, it is prarabdha; Ishvara only knows if this is dharma, let me watch and let Him decide.

  

This happens at the family level and societal level with significant consequences.

  

More generally,

  

The vedantin cannot put on a hat only for online discussions, and in other settings adopt/accede to all other purvapakshi logic as if there are dimensions of life in which he actively participates, takes decisions and makes conclusions, and sees others do the same that impact him and his people, culture etc., but where he is going to pretend his tradition, its logic, religion has no say. So imo if there is a philosophical question/argument posed by the world whose answer can be the basis for people’s worldview, then us as Vedantins/Hindus better have firm logical answers at that practical level as well, answers based on pramana logic on which the religion is founded. Otherwise purvapakshins espousing nonsense will rule the roost and push us in an ever shrinking corner.

  

My topic is not standardized in our discussions. It is preliminary exploration yet and therefore will appear a bit out of place. But I think we should give such tarka some space.

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
