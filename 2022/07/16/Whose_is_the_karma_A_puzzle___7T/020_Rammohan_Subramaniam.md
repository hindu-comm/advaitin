Hari Om

  

I was discussing this topic with my wife ( also an Advaitin) , she said that based purely on what is said in Bhagavad Gita and illustrated in Puranas  

1. If the action is sincerely dedicated to Bhagavan then no effect on performer \[Agents and sub agents\] (meaning even if results come he/she is) not going to be affected like an ordinary person. \[Corollary : Only good actions \[or other actions accidentally\] would be performed.  

2\. The intentions \[in the mind of agent or sub-agents\] decides whether the action is going to produce severe effect or not on each of them.

3. All actions will produce effects , its severity is a mental perception - this will change \[ 0 - 100\] depending on the intention behind the action by various agents and sub agents or Karma Yoga was being practiced.  

  

Om and with Prem

In His Seva

Shri Rammohan
