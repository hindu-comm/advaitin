Hare Krishna



- I am afraid I am unable to understand what you are trying to determine
  by giving these day to day examples. Anyway, let me share how I
  interpret these things in my perspective :



If Ramu watches a cricket match on TV, is that for Chitta shuddhi or for following Acharya instructions? Is he going to say “Let Him decide if this is dharma or not”? Rather he already decided in his heart of hearts this is svadharma in his body-mind-life context and is following it!



Ø As I said earlier the definition of dharma depends on space, time, circumstances and atmosphere. If rAmu is watching cricket match bunking his school or ignoring his studies avoiding the vigilance of his parents then he is indulging himself in a wrong act as it is not his svadharma at that particular point of time. Here he should listen to the AaptavAkya from his parents and teachers and mend his ways accordingly to follow his svadharma. OTOH, if the same rAmu watching cricket match on TV during his leisure time with the permission of his parents & cricket coach to sharpen his batting skills ( as he being a good cricketer and representing his school / college / university) by watching Tendulkar or Kohli then it is for his own betterment. Being an encouraging parent / teacher / mentor we should encourage him in all his pursuits to sharpen his cricketing skills. Cricket is the future for him destined and we, being the parents / teacher of that aspirant have to go by it. Here what would be the determining factor to conclude whether one is indulging in doing adharma or otherwise!!??



But actually … No. The kids are to blame, it is the wife, it is Indra and Chandra, ahem, no I am a Vedantin - it is vasana, it is prarabdha; Ishvara only knows if this is dharma, let me watch and let Him decide.



Ø IMHO, it should be the bottom line when we are wearing the mentor cap. Karma or dharma that we are doing in day to day business is our prArabdha karma phala and in that teaching the kid about the importance of studies and making him to realize the same and stopping him wasting his time is our svadharma being a responsible parent/teacher/mentor and again it is ( guiding towards studies or encouraging the kid to play professional sport) also IshwarAdheena. It is through Ishwarechhe ONLY we are wearing different caps at different times. And Ramu is not an exception to it being a student or cricketer. 



This happens at the family level and societal level with significant consequences.



Ø Both vyashti and samashti events happening as per svabhAva, na katrutvaM na karmANi lOkasya srujati prabhuH, as you reap as you sow ..



More generally,



The vedantin cannot put on a hat only for online discussions, and in other settings adopt/accede to all other purvapakshi logic as if there are dimensions of life in which he actively participates, takes decisions and makes conclusions, and sees others do the same that impact him and his people, culture etc., but where he is going to pretend his tradition, its logic, religion has no say. So imo if there is a philosophical question/argument posed by the world whose answer can be the basis for people’s worldview, then us as Vedantins/Hindus better have firm logical answers at that practical level as well, answers based on pramana logic on which the religion is founded. Otherwise purvapakshins espousing nonsense will rule the roost and push us in an ever shrinking corner.



My topic is not standardized in our discussions. It is preliminary exploration yet and therefore will appear a bit out of place. But I think we should give such tarka some space.



Ø I could see no other alternative other than philosophical approach (based on shAstra) to sort out these daily dilemmas both at vyashti and samashti levels. If we want to rebel the opposing groups/individuals the premise should be shAstra only and nothing but shAstra as we are dealing with some adrushta phala-s of karma for which shAstra eva pramANam and we should not violate the dictum of scriptures just to pacify our egocentric ideas. Whether we are going to hold sword or pen or white flag to defend ourselves is depends on how we ourselves established in svadharma. BTW, do you think other religions who are attacking us with regard to karma theory etc. have already satisfied the logical requirements of their assertions?? I really doubt.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

bhaskar
