Therefore, if an action is performed that is illegal according to man made laws, it is not necessary that it is adharma; equally, if an action is performed that is perfectly legal according to man made laws, it is not necessary that it is dharma either. In each case, the determination of dharma and adharma is whether such an action is:



a\) prescribed to me / prohibited for me by the shAstra-s.



b\) at that place.



c\) at that time.



d\) in that manner



praNAms Sri Venkatraghavan prabhuji
