Hare Krishna



After reading Sri Putran prabhuji’s mail on Ramu watching TV, I got another scenario where person is having “two birds with single stone”.
A & B were neighbors before partition and both were fighting daily for one or the other trifle reason and often thinking about killing each other. After partition both sighed relief since B left the country.
Later on both joined army and became soldiers and again met at the war field with rifles in their hands, A in an attempt of defending his country comfortably shoots B and killed him but when he is shooting at him in A’s mind personal grudge was equally dominant with his patriotism. But he ‘killed’ his enemy for the sake of his country and by doing so he got rid of his personal enemy also at the same time!!
Two birds with one stone. What type of karma phala he would accrue here?? Killing the enemy protecting his country is his svadharma that he did efficiently but there is an added advantage in this act at the same time. So, whether the karma phala is pApa or puNya??
