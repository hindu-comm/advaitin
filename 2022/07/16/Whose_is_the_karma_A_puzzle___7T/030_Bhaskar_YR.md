praNAms Sri Putran Prabhuji

Hare Krishna



- Since you need to have an answer from vedAnta perspective (as you said
  : if Vedanta has a say in the matter) and since the shAstra is the
  only pramANa ( as I have been insisting) to determine what constitute
  dharma and adharma, I would like to analyze this from vedAnta
  perspective. You can correct me if I erred anywhere from this view
  point. Now to the A and B transactions.



A does an Action. (Kriya)



- This kriya and kriya iccha both in A are the result of his prArabdha
  karma phala and his samskAra is it not?? His action (new or
  otherwise) is the result of saNchita samskAra which already started
  giving the fruit in prArabdha form.



B obtains knowledge of A's action. (Jnana), B desires a new action in response. (Iccha) & B does that new action. (Kriya)



- Again, as per vedAnta all the three (jnAnecchakriya) what B gets from
  A’s action depends on B’s samskAra for which he is only responsible.
  Here point to be noted is A’s kriya itself is not the cause of B’s
  jnana etc. and how B’s perceives this kriya and obtains jnana depends
  on B’s saMskAra (pUrvArjita saMskAra phala) or in general, depends on
  maturity of his mind in interpreting the action by A. And again,
  purity of his mind is the reflection of his saMskAra.



When/How/To what extent is B's kriya the responsibility of A (i.e. A is cause for B's action)? When/How is it not?



- A’s action here is kevala nimitta, is he deliberately doing some
  action (with certain purpose in his mind) to make changes in B’s
  jnAnecchakriya?? If not, if A is doing his action as destined by him
  by his own prArabdha, how can he be directly responsible for B’s
  action?? Let us take a movie example, if the hero as per the
  instruction of director acting like a dipsomaniac and also helping the
  needy by donating his organs etc. and if one of his fans after seeing
  this started drinking intoxicants by following only his hero’s
  drinking habits, is the hero directly responsible for his fan’s
  addictions or fan’s blind followings?? Or am I missing something
  here??



I want to understand this in terms of karma and dharma, and whatever else is relevant.



- In vedAnta, as Sri Venkatraghavan prabhuji pointed out, dharma & karma
  has been taken in broader terms by not giving much head to case to
  case or day to day regular events. Karma is general toward pravrutti
  which should ultimately lead us to nivrutti through chitta shuddhi.
  It is for jeeva’s abhyudaya pravrutti dharma prescribed within the
  boundaries of varNa and Ashrama. And when we are doing/following
  these pravrutti dharmas we have to do it with IshwarArpaNa buddhi and
  without any phalApeksha or accepting the phala as it comes the same
  pravrutti dharma leads us to sattva shuddhi which is the platform for
  the jnAnanishTe. So, both pravrutti and nivrutti dharma ultimately
  lead us to niHshreyas (see bhagavatpAda’s introduction to geeta
  bhAshya). It is not vedAnta’s duty to analyze each and every scenario
  of life events and passing the verdict who gets what through which
  means etc. After all we know guNa guNeshu vartante, svabhAvastu
  pravartante, prakruteH kriya mANAni guNaiH karmANi sarvashaH,
  ahamkara vimUdhAtmA kartAhaM iti manyate, all actioned being performed
  by prakruti the fool only whose mind is deluded by avidyA thinks that
  ahaM karta “I am the doer” (3-27 geeta).
