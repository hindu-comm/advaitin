Namaste Putran ji,

Glad we have agreement.

  

Regarding the "not so", I was responding to this sentence in your earlier email:

"Whether youhave guidance or knowledge of it is prarabdha karma phala."

  

The "it" refers to the dharma in the previous sentence "Dharma is prarabdha karma phala." I agree with you with this sentence.

  

dharma (in this context, puNya) can be karma phala which is prArabdha. I say "can be", because it also can be sanchita or AgAmi.

  

My negation with respect to the prior sentence was merely to the contention that the knowledge of dharma is prArabdha karma phala - the result of past actions that has automatically manifested in the current birth as dharma jnAna.

  

Sure, one can have samskAra-s of dharma jnAna from undertaking an enquiry into dharma in a previous life, but for most jIva-s, to have dharma jnAna in this life requires the study of shAstra / guidance from a knower of dharma. Even for those with prior samskAra-s some study is necessary to reactivate prior samskAra-s. It is only for highly exalted jIva-s that the automatic manifestation of the knowledge of dharma (is natural recollection) is possible, without any study.

  

I'm not sure if that was the intended meaning of the sentence in question, I may be merely responding to the words.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
