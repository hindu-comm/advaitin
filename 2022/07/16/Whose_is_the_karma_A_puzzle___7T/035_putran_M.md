Namaskaram Venkataraghavan-ji,

  

Yes, I took the word "knowledge" in a common academic sense. Like a Brahmin is born in a family that teaches him his svadharma (so he "knows" it) and another in a family that does not do the upanayana even. Such a birth and guidance (availability of knowledge regarding dharma) is also part of that jiva's prarabdha karmaphala. I am not an expert at differentiating prarabdha from sancita, agami; but essentially in this example, both thedharma of a brahmin and the right circumstances for knowing and being taught svadharma are karmaphala of pastkarma since the jiva did not do anything here and now to receive such. And when it does study shastra etc, then by abidance to dharma, it acquires deeper knowledge like you speak of.
