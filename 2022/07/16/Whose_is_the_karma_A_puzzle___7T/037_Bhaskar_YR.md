Yes, I agreed with Sri Venkatraghavan prabhuji with regard to his observation that dharma or adharma conclusion depends on the desha, kAla and nimitta and not merely going by action (kriya). And that is what shankara bhagavatpAda also said in sUtra bhAshya.



My understanding/thinking:



The jiva had to do the karma,even what is now what you call"prarabdha". Adharma does not naturally happen; it is deviation from dharma. Ishvara does not do it for you; He is not a whimsical maniac. You the jiva have to decide against svadharma and go that route, knowingly or unknowingly. Thereby you accrue more karma that will obtain phala in the future.



Simply put, the kriya you do now (and more so, the kriya iccha) is influenced by the past becauseyour decision is based on self-identifications of various kinds. But it is not a direct result of the past; you are still the king on the chariot and in vyavaharika, the director of the charioteer and chariot - albeit the road,the horses, the chariot, the charioteer will influence your choices and have a bearing on the end outcomes. All the other factors, the road you are presently traveling on, the charioteer and horses, all of them can be attributed to your past karma; but in spite of them all, you are still the constantself-determining Consciousness of the future status of all of them. Because of the ajnana factor, your decision will generallynot be perfectly aligned with dharma and you will often tend to take "This is best for me" decisions that are only best for the "wrong" "me" and contrary to dharma.



You may not be saying against this; I cannot tell your usage of the word "result".



Ø Your explanation, attributing me as director to my charioteer and chariot reminds me Sri DVG’s mankutimmana kagga in Kannada, there, there is a saying : baduku chataka bandi vidhi adara saheba etc. etc. (it is very famous in Kannada literature and author got the jnAnapeetha also for this work) It says life is a horse cart, fate is its driver and you are the horse, HE the driver decides the passengers to the marriage or funeral run whichever way you are asked to. You have no control over where you are going. The events in your life it could be things that are good, pleasant, enjoyable sometime or there might be events make you sad or depressed. (marriage & funeral have these inner meaning in the above kagga) It is not your choice the driver is directing you, you are not responsible for your actions, enjoy or simply bear, there is no other choice. Having said this, kindly don’t think I am insisting everything is pre-programmed no hand of free-will at any point of time..What I am trying to say is there is free will within the sphere of destiny. The destiny itself provide the freewill to the jeeva to some extent. Perhaps my language limitations may not be allowing me to express this clearly, Sri Sada prabhuji used to : what you already have through your action is your prArabdha and what you are going to do with that is your free will or something like that. Though kriya shakti is not the direct result of your past karma phala it is not exclusive force the jeeva will get all of a sudden, it is included in destiny (your past karma phala). Paramahamsa rAmakrishna gives an example of cow tied to the pole, the freedom of roaming restricted to only length of the rope and pole is your destiny. He is bound by it but at the same time free to move around as per the length of rope. Let us take example of a terrorist, let us say he has become terrorist due to his paapa karma phala and when he is indulging in terrorism he definitely has his own choice of terroristic activities, either he can plant a bomb, plan for the shooting them down or even plan for human-bomb, these are definitely choices of his free will but ordained by his pUrva saMskAra of bhayOtpAdana. His thinking pattern/stream will be within the realms of his saMskAra. In this sense I was saying jnAnechakriya are influenced by his pUrva saMskAra.
