Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji, Venkataraghavan-ji,

  

I am still interested in having some opinion or shastriac justification regarding this specific question below. Perhaps you can address it and share your thoughts. Thanks.

  

Quote

  

I have a question on this since it sometimes prompts a missionary argument against us. Correct me *if my premise itself is wrong.*

  

Western countries have a greater share of wealth (presently). India has also suffered from a millenium of invasion and foreign rule. And today all sorts of inimical forces seem to be undermining the country and the sanatana dharma. Varnashrama dharma is getting openly pummeled into non-existence and brahmins targeted. We are constantly fending off people and ideologies that seem to be committed to our destruction.

  

Should we think it is because of our bad karma that we seem to be suffering inordinately and the karma of jivas in other nations that they seem to be relatively better off? There seems to be collective difficulty, like the religion itself is under siege; not like I personally could have done wrong elsewhere (or in another religion in a past birth) and am born here - the karmic forces seem to have an eye on bringing down Dharma rather than aiding it.

  

We feel blessed to be born in our religion but should we think Hindus are suffering for their past karma? Or is it the case that the higher you are, the greater the test youwillbe putthrough?
