Hare Krishna



Perhaps it is a socio-political based problem you want to discuss thinking India is in a disastrous state and other western countries rich/wealthy etc. By the way our dharma is Vishwa dharma ( an universal religion) hence it is also called as manukula dharma It is equally applicable at micro & macro levels. so I don’t think shAstra is dealing with solving the problems of citizens of one particular country in which we are living.



Historically, we the hindu-s spiritually oriented people not so particular about protecting the already available material wealth or gaining fresh wealth at the cost of others livelihood and even at the time of their own wealth forcefully taken away they hardly cared for it. Since time immemorial we are peace loving (shanti Priya) people.
It might be one of the reasons (though I am not sure) for free invasions and looting our physical/material wealth by foreign forces.
We have read biographies of some mahatma-s (like bali, vibheeshana, shibi, Dhruva, buddha etc.) who left everything, including wife, kids, royal life and what not!!?? in pursuit of ultimate truth. And another reason that I could presume is vairagya, shama, dama indriya nigrama etc. which is for self-aggrandizement wrongly interpreted to become impotent when external forces looting us. Wrongly thinking that spirituality (adhyAtmika shakti) is real power and material wealth is purely temporary which allowed outsiders to loot us freely!!! 



And frankly I don’t see justification in conclusion that we are the one who are really suffering and wealthy countries are happily living. The happiness or sorrow are quite relative and purely subjective is it not?? If India is not the place of serenity and peaceful why some foreigners leaving their luxurious lives and coming here in search of peace and happiness?? So, I have my own reservation in concluding that India is the place to suffer and other wealthy countries are always full of happiness and people who are living there will always be free from suffering.



Just my few thoughts.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
