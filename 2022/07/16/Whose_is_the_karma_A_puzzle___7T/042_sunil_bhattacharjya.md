Dear Bhaskarji,

  

You made a very good statement  

I agreed with Sri Venkatraghavan prabhuji with regard to his observation that dharma or adharma conclusion depends on the desha, kAla and nimitta and not merely going by action (kriya). And that is what shankara bhagavatpAda also said in sUtra bhAshya.

Luckily for us, Lord Krishna made a quite broad statement in the Mahabarata, and this makes things simpler for us. At first sight, Lord Krishna's statement may appear uninteresting, but it becomes more and more interesting and useful, if you go on contemplating on its implications and applications. Eventually it becomes a source of joy, in this material journey as well as in the longer spiritual journey. Lord Krisna said ***"Truth (Satyam) is dharma***". More and more we look for the truth in different areas we come across, more and more new revelations appear before us.  

  

My 2 cents,

skb
