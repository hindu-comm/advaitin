Namaskaram Venkataraghavan-ji,

  

Ok. I understand from your response that my question is misplaced. The logic that corresponds naturally at the vyashti level should not be transferred to the happenings at the samashti level. Trying to find cause-effect reasons for the happenings for group or community is unlikelyto find satisfactory answers since we have notions of what should or should not happen for such identifications as if they are individuals themselves. Generally it is beyond our say and need not conform to our imaginations. Only thing, Ishvara will ensure that the avenue of dharma will survive in whatever form that He decides, and otherwise each jiva has the duty to abide by its svadharma however it comes to know it. Will leave it there for now. Thanks.
