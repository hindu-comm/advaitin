praNAms Sri Venkatraghavan prabhuji

Hare Krishna



A lot of what you describe is simply the nature of kaliyuga. That each of us is born in such a Yuga is our prArabdha. What the world is going through is a result of the samaShTi karma-s of jIva-s born in this age.



- I read somewhere dharma jignAsa is klishta and durgama and human logic
  or dry logic (shushka tarka) cannot explain it satisfactorily.
  bhagavatpAda in sUtra bhAshya says, quoting manu, those who want
  dharma shuddhi (leaving the adharma and adhering the dharma) need to
  have mastery over pratyaksha, anumAna and Agama pramANa-s. And that
  which, has been taught as dharma by our ancestors / rishi / muni to be
  understood in line with veda and shAstra through shAstra sammata tarka
  or yukti. Without doing this exercise we cannot know the nature of
  dharma.



There is also an objective reality - which is that in Kaliyuga there will be the suppression of dharma. However, the one thing we can be certain of is that despite the seeming difficulties to dharma, it will ultimately be protected by bhagavAn. There have been innumerable kali-yugas in the past and there will be innumerable ones in the future - however sanAtana dharma will be ever present.



- Yes, bhagavAn will always be there to do dharma saMrakshaNa, yadA yadA
  hi dharmasya ……paritrANAya sAdhUnAm…saMbhavAmi yuge yuge is the
  assurance of Lord.



That doesn't absolve us of responsibilitythough - each of us must strive to protect dharma, and the best way to do it is by the regulation of our thoughts, speech and actions in accordance with dharma - dharmAcharaNameva dharmarakshaNam.



- Yes when we want samashti karma phala should be good and pleasant each
  and every one of us should follow our svadharma with all earnestness
  and with Ishwara praNidAna IshwarArpaNa buddhi. Svadharme nidhanaM
  shreyaH paradharmO bhayAvahaH….but what we are today is sadly
  different, knowingly or unknowingly we have become karma bhrashta-s
  and doing somebody else’s karma. 

Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

bhaskar
