Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

There are two aspects to this. One is advaita jnana, that we are talking about a mithya thing, the Self dreaming it is a suffering jiva. We can only seek to wake up, because no matter how terrible it appears, the movie does not become real and the Self is immutable.

  

The second is that the movie is said to have karma-karmaphala Order. The show is actually being run by Ishvara in a sensible manner, so given that we are identified with the jiva movie character, no doubt we want to know of the sense in this movie - especially in matters of suffering and the survival of what we think is dharma.

  

But then we are told that Sense of His we cannot fully know in all situations. We know it to a limited extent and if we are fortunate, may have someguidanceneeded to find our way to waking up from the movie-sleep. But not to knowing all the ins and outs of how and why He does things.

  

At the samashti level I am also baffled as my questions show. But then why am I individualizing religions, countries, races and peoples and asking why this or that group-identity suffered? I don't know, I cannot resist doing this. It may be entirely wrong way to think of it. Ishvara may not think my pet group (including Hinduism) needs to survive though "dharma" will besanatana, manifest in some form or other.

  

As to individuals' suffering that you mention, I am not that impressed. Human beings are rogues. We kill a butterfly with disregard and the Lord may blow our heads with a boulder in return,ten lives later. I don't bother asking this question at the individual level. In any case, the suffering removes the karma accretion from our past, so ultimately it frees the jiva from its own karma cage, in its quest for moksha.

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
