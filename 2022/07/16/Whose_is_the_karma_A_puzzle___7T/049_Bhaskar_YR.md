There are two aspects to this. One is advaita jnana, that we are talking about a mithya thing, the Self dreaming it is a suffering jiva. We can only seek to wake up, because no matter how terrible it appears, the movie does not become real and the Self is immutable.



Ø All questions have been satisfactorily answered with this view point 😊 is it not?? When there is no srushti where is the question of vyashti or samashti karma phala?? When there is no mumukshu where is the question of realization / mOksha 😊 wake-up from this world dream, that’s it, all problem solved.





As to individuals' suffering that you mention, I am not that impressed. Human beings are rogues. We kill a butterfly with disregard and the Lord may blow our heads with a boulder in return,ten lives later. I don't bother asking this question at the individual level. In any case, the suffering removes the karma accretion from our past, so ultimately it frees the jiva from its own karma cage, in its quest for moksha.



Ø There is naraka to experience these paapa phala, getting fried in pan etc. And for that separate department is there with complete strength, yama dharma raja, Chitragupta, yama kiMkara fully equipped with all sorts of things to give punishment to jeeva. And after this first course poor jeeva again has to take birth on this earth to experience second course of punishment…And Ishwara must be the adjudicator what is to be suffered/enjoyed in naraka/swarga through which karma and which karma phala is to be experienced through prArabdha in bhUmi.
