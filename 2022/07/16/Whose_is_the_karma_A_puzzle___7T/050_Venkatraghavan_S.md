Namaste Putran ji,

I didn't wish to imply that your question was misplaced

  

samaShTi is nothing but the sum total of the vyaShTi. Directionally, the logic at the vyaShTi level can be applied at the samaShTi also. If a war occurs and a large population suffers from it, it is because there is a critical mass of individuals whose prArabdha requires them to suffer and the war is the efficient medium through which such a karma phala can be dispensed to many jIvas simultaneously.

  

The world is the way it is to serve as the locus of experience of the jIva-s in that kalpa.  

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
