Namaskaram Venkataraghava-ji,

  

I am bad at this, abusing words for my own purposes. Ignorance is ok but I believe I was simply too lazy to discern the meaning of samashti and superimposed a different meaning onto it. Not sure what is the case but will be careful henceforth.

  

Like we ask, “Why did the Jews suffer the holocaust?” We are talking actually about a collection of jivas experiencing karmaphala, but it appears as if the target is a jada entity or construct called Judaism. Why would Ishvara connect these jivas with that and then make them suffer, as if Judaism’s survival needs to be threatened?

  

For Native Americans, a certain culture, civilization, dharma was forever destroyed in the course of giving samashti karmaphala for a group of jivas. The jivas have to suffer the destruction of their group identity, but Ishvara decided that group dharma itself should a casualty of his samashti Phala distribution.

  

Its like if all Hindus in a place are converted, we feel afflicted because of our identification with Hindu identity and dharma that seems to be getting wiped out in the process. When trying to fit this experience logically, I am finding difficulties with the vyashti logic.
