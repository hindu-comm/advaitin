Namaste Putran ji

There is no karma "joint" account in the name of a collection of jiivas. In other words, a jiiva cannot have to suffer karma phala because some other jiiva belonging to his collective label (co-religionist or fellow country man) did some karma.

  

If a set of different jiivas in different countries do some karmas (separately) which entail a karma phala in a later janma of falling in to the water and suffering some anxious moments, let's say. We can then informally talk of those particular different jiivas finding themselves in the same boat in this life, literally speaking and accidentally falling in to the water for a while.

  

(my understanding - Hindu Dharma's low ebb today is traceable to many many dRShTa factors like the Indian crab mentality, kShatriyas not intelligently following their Dharma and acting naiively etc etc. We can see the residues of those traits even today in those who were in leadership positions. But that is a separate topic.)

  

The implication may be peculiar but has the force of logic - if Hindus in the past acted

Dharmically (but let's say they were kaamaatmaanaH, they may have been born today in some suitable Scandinavian countries. Similarly those who in the western or Islamic world historically oppressed the Hindus in the past, may have been born today in some Indian slum and be living under difficult conditions.

  

Only vyaShTi karma accounts will have causal coherence.

  

  

Om

Raghav
