Thanks for quoting this beautiful use of analogy to show that anyone may drink water but there is nothing to quench if there is no thirst. It reminds me of the statement by one of the earlier Mahaswamijis of Sringeri as to an anadhikArI doing shravaNa may at most get puNya!

  

W.r.t. karma also, I heard Kailashashrama Mahamandaleshvara say recently that just because one can do some karma or upAsana well doesn't mean one is eligible to do it. I recall using a similar example to tell someone that if one can sing well, one may pick up vedasvaras soon and chant well, but it doesn't give adhikAra in vedapaThana.



gurupAdukAbhyAm,
