Dear Sundar Rajan,

  

Even the humorousreply of the Mahatma does not deviate from the Shaastra. In Advaita there is a Krama Mukti. The Ishwara, Saguna, Upasaka goes to Brahma loka (Vaikuntha in this context) and is taught the Advaita Atma Vidya by Brahmaa (Vishnu in this context), the head of that loka. The one who gains that vidya there, at Maha pralaya, when that Loka perishes, will attain videha mukti along with all the others who have gained Jnana, never to return to samsara.
