Namaskar,

  

I am a keen and regular listener of his talks. I have been listening
to his talks everyday for the last two years.

  

I find that his talks for the close devotees are not structured ( unlike his public talks). There is a freedom to talk extensively about anecdotes, some humour, the contemporary status of research in science, his memories of persons/professors/ scientists he met. There is a great variety and that makes his talks fascinating.

  

His talks on the Vedantasara are fantastic and they help you to prepare the foundation. The book is otherwise dry, and to the point. Sarvapriyanandaji makes it lively.

  

Moreover his ability to explain the nuances is amazing. His talks were a game changer in my learning of the Advaita Vedanta.

  

Regards

  

Dilip.
