Humble Pranam Srinivasji,

  

The places to visit include the main temple and Ramanashram and if possible to climb the mountain and see Virupaksha Cave and Skandashram and the summit.  

  

I visited Tiruvannamalai in 2013 withmy wife and son in law. We stayed at Hotel Nala . Ourreceptionist, Shobha Bakre, was very helpful. We did Girivalam, starting at around 6 PM in the evening, and returned around midnight. Next morning I climbed the Arunachalam peak, visiting Virupaksha Cave and Skandashram on the way. I took a guide namedTirumalai. We started climbing at 4:00 AM and reached the top by 6:00 AM. After sunrise it gets too hot in this area to do any climbing.

  

Enjoy! It's a divine place and the experience will be unforgettable.

  

\- Ravindra Shivde
