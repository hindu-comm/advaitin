[[Rammohan Subramaniam	2022-07-20, 09:13:33 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/Udqci5BE0h8)]]

"Should we think it is because of our bad karma that we seem to be suffering  
inordinately and the karma of jivas in other nations that they seem to be  
relatively better off?

NO EVERY ONE IS EXACTLY WHERE THEY SHOULD BE BUT THE FEELIMG THAT THEY ARE BETTER IS CREATED BY OUR PRARABDA. IF THOS FEELING IS CONVERTED INTO JEALOUSY THEM WE ARE DEVELOPING AN ATTITUDE. WHAT EVER WE THINK OR DO WITH AN ATTITUDE LEADS TO APPROPRIATE PHALA.

There seems to be collective difficulty, like the religion itself is under siege;

YES WHEN .MANY PEOPLE DEVIATE FROM DHARMA THEN IT MANIFESTS AS A GLBAL CALAMITY. THE KURUSHETRA IS AN EXAMPLE.

not like I personally could have done wrong  
elsewhere (or in another religion in a past birth) and am born here - the  
karmic forces seem to have an eye on bringing down Dharma rather than  
aiding it."

THE KARMIC FORCE IS ENACTING THE SUN TOTAL OF WHAT WE ALL DID IN THE PAST. ON ITS OWN IT DOWS NOT HAVE ANY SPECIFIC STAKE.  

  

IF ONE WISHES TO BE PROTECTED THEM FOLLOW A SATVIC LIFE STYLE ( 20 QUALITIES OF GITA ) AND BECOME DEVOTED TO THE LORD. HE WILL PROTECT.
