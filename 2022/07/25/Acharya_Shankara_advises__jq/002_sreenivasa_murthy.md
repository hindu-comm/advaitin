Dear Sri Sudesh Pillutla,

  

Upanishads have revealed the methodology for cognizing one's true svarupa.

By using your own day to day common experiences discover within yourself by yourself what remains unchanged which is YOUR TRUE SVARUPA and what changes and comes & goes (AgamApAyi) is not your true svarUpa.

Bruhadaranyaka Upanishad says : AtmanyEvAtmAnam paSyati \|\| 4-4-23

Sri Shankara in his commentary to this mantra writes :

Atmani Eva svE kAryakarasaMGAtE AtmAnaM pratyakcEtayitAraM paSyati\|\|

  

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa Murthy.
