Namaste Putran ji,

I too had seen the translation of Sri Shankaranarayanan but did not follow that pattern. My approach was to make thetext student-friendly. Hence, I gave the word to word translation of the verse, the purport of the verse and notes in brief. I did not translate the whole commentary of Swamiji, but picked up a few points from it and added my own notes where I thought I was telling a new point. Mahasannidhanam was kind enough to allow it. 

Aravinda Rao
