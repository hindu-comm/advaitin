Hare Krishna



Perhaps it is because Vishnu predominantly stands for Vishwa (Very first name in SahasranAma) and Shiva stands for the Atman. When the unity between these two is realized every deity/name/form gets automatically included !??



Ø As a matter of fact at somany places both shiva & Vishnu (devi, sUrya, gaNapati as well) eulogized as srushti, sthiti and antyakAraka and all are sarvajna, sarvashakta and sarvAntaryAmi. Keeping the uniform tattva behind all these different names and forms. When we realize this sAmAnya tattva behind all these names and forms there is no duality or any hierarchical order. But we have to be very clear being a upAsaka, we are doing upAsana as per the shAstra vidhi, in that scenario shiva is shiva not vishNu this difference is due to upAdhi vishesha.
Hence upAsya devata too have different upAsaka-s. And upAsaka should not think that he is upAsya devata. ( see sUtra bhAshya in devatAdhikaraNa). The phala we get by doing upAsana to different
devata-s also will be different. Hence in vyAvahAra there is no need to see the similarity among devata-s, each and every name and form has its own importance and significance in the sAdhana called upAsana and ORDER of it has to be like that only. We cannot argue like since shiva & vishNu both are one and the same pArvati is vishNu patni and Lakshmi is gaNapati’s mother etc. Though tattvawise they are all one and the same, in the transactional reality they have their own protected family and have their own set of devotees / bhakta-s. 😊 we should not mess it up!!! I reckon the problem starts when these devotees are starting to think ‘MY DADDY STRONGEST’and trying to belittle other daddies😊 that is not allowed and acceptable, we have to use iron box for ironing the clothes and air cooler to cool our rooms though power behind it one and the same😊



The oneness of saguna and nirguna Brahman also gets established with the above and there are many bhAshya vakyas to support that.



Ø When the Oneness (pUrNatvaM) is realized in totality there is no diversity whatsoever. nEha nAnAStikiMchana.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
