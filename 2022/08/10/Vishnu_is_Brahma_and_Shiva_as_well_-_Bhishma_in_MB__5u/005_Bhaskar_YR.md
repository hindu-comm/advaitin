Yes prabhuji, that is what atleast I think. vyavahAra means dvaita where jnAtru, jneya and jnana hold sway. In that transactional world,
ArAdhaka is parichinna jeeva and would like to propitiate avicchinna sarvashakta through shAstra prescribed prateeka or Alambana. So, accordingly what he reaps is what he sow. No need for comparing oranges with apples here to strike the similarities by arguing both are “fruits”. Ofcourse, shiva is shiva not vishNu and vishNu is vishNu not devi, we should not or supposed to mess-up with these forms though shakti/tattva behind these nAma and rUpa is ONE. BTW worshiping shiva as shiva, vishNu as vishNu does not anyway going to disturb the advaita’s ekatvaM it is just like using the SAME POWER for heating the foods in oven and cooling it in refrigerator 😊 nAma rUpa upAdhi has its own utility here nothing is superior nor inferior to anything.
