Dear Bhaskarji,

  

Many people get confused by the word "Deha" in "Dehabhimana". "Deha" here includes both **(i)** the "***Sukshma sareera***", which includes
Chitta- Man- Budddhi -Ahankara, and this sukshma sareera, which goes from body to body in different births, as well as the **(ii)** ***Sthula sareera*** or the physical body, which is left behind at the end of each birth.  

  

When one becomes Jnani , one does not have the dehabhimana any more, and then one can become bidehamukta and that is what is the aim of the advaitins. I am sure you know all these, but there may be many in the group, who are beginners.  

  

Bande Krishna Jagadgurum.

  

Sunil KB
