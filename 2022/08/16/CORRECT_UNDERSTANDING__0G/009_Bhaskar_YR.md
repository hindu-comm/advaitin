> Many people get confused by the word "Deha" in "Dehabhimana". "Deha"
> here includes both **(i)** the "***Sukshma sareera***", which
> includes Chitta- Man- Budddhi -Ahankara, and this sukshma sareera,
> which goes from body to body in different births, as well as the
> **(ii)** ***Sthula sareera*** or the physical body, which is left
> behind at the end of each birth.
