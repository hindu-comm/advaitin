Dea Bhaskarji,

  

Apte's Sanskrit to English dictionary tells us that the word "अभिमानः abhimānaḥ" means pride. Thus "Dehabhimana" means a sense of pride on account of possessing the Deha, which is impermanent. Do you think it
really sensible to nourish "dehabhimana" or being proud of this impermanent "deha"**?** This is avidya and it will only hamper our spiritual progress. Hope you will agree.
