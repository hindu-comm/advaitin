Pranams,

  

śanaiḥ śanairuparamēd buddhyā dhṛtigṛhītayā.

ātmasaṅsthaṅ manaḥ kṛtvā na kiñcidapi cintayēt ৷৷**6.25**৷৷

  

We should carefully read Sankara's commentary on the second part of this verse. He explicitly calls those out as the***highest instruction about Yoga*.**

**  **

**ātmasaṅstham**ātmani saṅsthitam 'ātmaiva sarvaṅ na tatō.nyat kiñcidasti' ityēvamātmasaṅsthaṅ**manaḥ kṛtvā na kiñcidapi cintayēt.**ēṣa yōgasya paramō vidhiḥ৷৷tatra ēvamātmasaṅsthaṅ manaḥ kartuṅ pravṛttō yōgī

  

atma-samstham, fixed in the Self, with the idea, 'The Self alone is all; there is nothing apart from It'-**thus fixing the mind on the Self**; na cintayet, **one should not think of**; kincit api, a**nything whatsoever**. This is the highest instruction about Yoga.  

  

Couple of points:

- If you spend a couple of minutes pondering over this and review what
  is discussed as Nidhidhyasana in this forum, hard to miss there is a
  possible disconnect.
- Shouldn't the 'bliss watchers' who cry foul over Vivekachudamani point
  out a problem here too (Bhagavan uses the word twice in 2 verses!)
