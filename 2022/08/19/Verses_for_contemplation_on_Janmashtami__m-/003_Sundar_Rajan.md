Namaste Putran-ji,

  

Since you mentioned contemplation, I wanted to discuss a lit bit on just this "**fixing the mind".** **No, not discussing Atma (self) etc here , just the word "fix" ![Emoji](https://ci5.googleusercontent.com/proxy/ctzXZIKxsLeNxBUFlr-2qV-26aRCgp7y60e5Xnlihe07tMNLZE47-RfIEEhMfQ6uVI1N-cex1hrXOFVDiUyEIdICCANdkJDhKNa-DsgT3FtNjX4CfQ=s0-d-e1-ft#https://s.yimg.com/nq/yemoji_assets/latest/yemoji_assets/1f642.png "Emoji")**.

  

Since currently I am spending a lot of time managing a property rehab, I am hoping it is OK to be less formaland use an example from day to day life.

  

Here are the *before* and *after* images of the rehab to the kitchen. I don't how to even fix a broken door, so everything is done by a contractor. So when we negotiated *fixing* the kitchen, it implied a long list of skills and competencies. Here is a short list:

- Demolish the ceiling, walls and floors
- Build new drywalls, ceilings and paint  
- Install Cabinets, countertops, backsplash and appliances

![Inline image](https://groups.google.com/group/advaitin/attach/266f162bb2d8b7/1661029115573blob.jpg?part=0.1&view=1 "Inline image")  
  

**Fix** in this example,****implies the person asked to *fix* has already acquired those skills and competenciesand there is no need to train or even explain to them.

  

Reverting back to the term *fixing the mind* in verse 6.24, it is implied the instructionsare for someone:

- whose mind is already in their control
- whose mind can listen to and follow their commands
- who already knows how to direct the mind (stay put in a certain
  'place')

In other words, all the preceding disciplines such as pratyahara, dharana and dhyana (and maybe even Samadhi) have been fully mastered.

  

It also implies, these instructions are not for someone who complains of wandering mind or sleepiness or dullness or any number of other problems during meditation.
