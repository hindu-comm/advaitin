Namaskaram,

  

>   
> Where it is a call for action, it is to be understood as having the
> goal of eliminating ajnana:

  

This appears like a jnanakarmasamucchaya confusion (?). May require correction or modification. Better: Where it is a call for action, the goal of samadhi is Self-absorption wherein the "seeker becomes free of the vagaries of the mind". It is in that state that"the understanding of 'I am pure indivisible all-pervading Existence-Consciousness-Bliss' should take root for jnananishta to happen." \[quoting from article\] Samadhi would be the final step*following* SMN that allows for the jiva to realize irrevocably the truth obtained in the first step of sravana.

  

Some think it is needed, some think it is highly useful, some think it is not centrally relevant to jnana and misleading to emphasize.
