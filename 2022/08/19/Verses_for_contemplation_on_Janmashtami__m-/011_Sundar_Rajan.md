Namaskaram,

\>\>  

\[quoting from article\] Samadhi would be the final step*following*SMN that allows for the jiva to realize irrevocably the truth obtained in the first step of sravana.

\>\>

  

Without even referring to the article, just from the Gita text and Sankara's commentary, it appears the "N" in SMN, namely Nidhidhayasana leading to Atma darshana is what is presented in the 6th chapter.

  

Couple of reasons:

- Sankara's word's introducing this chapter: ৷৷5.26৷৷ atha idānīṅ
  dhyānayōgaṅ samyagdarśanasya antaraṅgaṅ vistarēṇa vakṣyāmiThereafter,
  now, with the idea, 'I shall speak elaborately of the yoga of
  meditation which is the proximate discipline for full realization'
- Then Lord Krishnain 6.29 describing the result of this
  Sadhana:*sarva-bhūta-stham ātmānaṁ sarva-bhūtāni chātmani**O*ne who
  has his mind Self-absorbed through Yoga, and who has the vision of
  sameness everywhere, sees his Selfexisting in everything, and
  everything in his Self.

Sankara doesn't see it as Patanjali Yoga or Dvaita Yoga if the implication is Bhagavad Gita is somehow a dvaita yoga shastra.

  

Again to quote Sri Vidyasankar Sundaresan:*The distinction between yogic dhyAna and vedAntic dhyAna is moreacademicthan one of practice.*
