Hare Krishna



I don't much think of dvaita yoga shastra when I say yoga, just like if I talk of bhakti, Ishvara or "Vedanta", it does not mean dvaita Vedanta, or that Shankara was a Vaishnavite because he refers to Narayana here or there. Yes, there are dvaitinsof various kinds who use the same words; but when we use the terms it has to be in a vyavaharika or sadhaka context that is consistent with the final philosophy of advaita.



- That is fine prabhuji. But deliberation starts once there is
  conclusion that PYS’s ashtanga yOga, its doctrine and ultimate goal is
  same and on par with advaita’s Atmaikatva jnana. It does not anyway
  mean that advaitins are belittling the yOga sAdhana and pushing aside
  PYS & related sAdhana as useless. The words like yOga & samAdhi
  themselves do not have any problem as they have been used at multiple
  places in Advaita literature but problem starts when we try to
  interpret these words within Advaita tradition and taking PYS or
  dvaita YS as source.



The same has to be true when we refer to Nirvikalpa samadhi.



- I hope I don’t have to insist here that NS has been interpreted as
  YS’s asamprajnAtha samAdhi in the later works of Advaita.



As I understand, itis total Self-absorption where there is no room for the "other", whether as thought or object. But it is not deep sleep; it is merging in nirvikalpa awareness of Self whereupon the yogi/jnani "sees his Selfin everything and everything in his Self."



- First of all when we are talking about advaita’s paramArtha we have to
  bear in mind jnAni’s samyak darshana or Atmaikatva darshana is time
  bound, restricted to one particular state to which we often travel
  (entering that state and coming back by external force) and come back
  to ‘normal state’. IMHO, jnAni’s sarvAtma bhAva or Atmaikatva jnana
  is not vyavahAra abhAva jnana in some trance state. It is quite
  natural to him through shravaNadi shAstra sAdhana, the mOksha is not
  going to happen or will be experienced in a particular state by
  suppressing the thoughts (chitta vrutti nirOdha) and there is no
  possibility of coming back from it as in NS, OTOH it is ‘vyavahAra
  bAdhita’ jnana as jnAni would not have even an iota of ‘dehAdhyAsa’.
  Since this dehAdhyAsa is not there in sushupti (deep sleep state) he
  is with brahman says bhAshyakAra. And elsewhere he also says both
  sushupti and samAdhi states are NOT free from avidyA.



- As per some Advaita Acharya-s those who are questioning the necessity
  of dragging PYS & samAdhi, say that with these backdrop we have to
  understand the words like yOga, samAdhi, atyantika abhAva of
  vyavahAra, maNOnAsha etc.
