praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji



Samadhi would be the final step*following* SMN that allows for the jiva to realize irrevocably the truth obtained in the first step of sravana.



- I am neither an Advaita jnAni nor experienced any samAdhi but whenever
  I see statements like this I started doubting my understanding of
  Advaita’s paramArtha jnana!! IMO, Advaita jnana and its (socalled)
  experience does not call for any action to suggest that there is a
  subsequent step after SMN in the form of experiencing samAdhi.
  UddAlaka after preaching shwetaketu you are that did not insist by
  saying see this is theory, now go and experience this truth i.e.
  tattvamasi in a practical laboratory called NS. Lord after his
  enlightening discourse on geeta heard that nashtO mOha smutir labhdhva
  from Arjuna and bhAshyakAra clarified that Arjuna became paramArtha
  jnAni without mentioned anything about mandatory further step of
  experiencing NS. And vAmadeva realized that he is manu and Aditya
  etc. and it was not an exclamation in a strange state called NS it is
  the realization in his mother’s womb. If the Advaita jnana calls
  for some experience at some place/state at some particular point of
  time even after shAstra vAkya SMN janita paramArtha jnAna then the
  10^(th) man story would have continued you are THE 10^(th) man and now
  you retire to forest to experience this in NS. And the word samAdhi
  has been variously interpreted by bhAshyakAra to suit the particular
  contexts in which it occurs. Sometimes the samAdhi denotes Atman and
  some times as the mind itself. And to the Arjuna’s query with regard
  to sthita prajna : sthita prajnasya kA bhAshA, samAdhisthasya
  Keshava?? Etc. in sAnkhya yOga, bhAshyakAra clarifies what exactly is
  the characteristics of samAdhisthasya. He has nowhere ( as far as I
  know) stated there are two types of samAdhi-s like savikalpa and
  nirvikalpa in his PTB. Ofcourse I am not considering his popular work
  VC since its authorship is still in doubt.



Some think it is needed, some think it is highly useful, some think it is not centrally relevant to jnana and misleading to emphasize.



- Some would argue that shabda pramANa is just like operating and
  instruction manual first study it, understand it and get the direct
  experience of this theory in practical Lab called NS. The science of
  Being, according to them, to be experienced ONLY in scientific
  laboratory called NS. Without this experience you are just a data
  holder without any practical knowledge/experience.
