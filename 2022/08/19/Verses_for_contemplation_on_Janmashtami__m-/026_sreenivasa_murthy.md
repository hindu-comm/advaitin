Dear Sri Sundar Rajan,

You write :"On the contrary, Sri Madhusudan Saraswati cites this *Yacched-van-manasi* mantra and explains in great detail in the context of Gita 6.25 *Making the mind fixed in the Self, one should not think of anything whatsoever*!"

Is Madhusudana Saraswati's statement correct?  

  

In the above statement There are three vastus. (1) mind , (2) Self and (3) one who should not think of anything whatsoever. Can the boundaries between these three vastus

be shown actually? There are three words, but are there three vastus?

Please permit me to draw your kind attention to the following Sruti mantras which reveal the actual state of affairs HERE and NOW.

      sa EvEdagM sarvam \|\| Chandogya 7-25-1  

 ahamEvedagaM sarvam \|\| Chandogya 7-25-1

 AtmaivEdagM sarvam \|\| Chandogya 7-25 -2

What is the lakshana of sa, aham , Atman?

 Yatra nAnyat paSyati, nAnyat Srunoti, nanyat vijAnati sa BUmA\|\|  


Chandogya 7-24-1

Mandukya mantra 7 says :  

 prapaMcOpaSamaM advaitam sa AtmA sa vijneyaH \|\|

  

aham is Here & Now. Atman is Here and Now.

Hence I am advaita and PrapaNcOpaSamaH .  

  

In the light of the above Sruti mantras where are those three vastus HERE and NOW?

  

Please Ponder over.

  

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa Murthy
