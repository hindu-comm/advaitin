DearSreenivasa Murthy-ji,

\>\>

How long one has to be a child?  

Won't you think that one should become a grown up man?

Even after years and years if one is thinking like a child

is he fit to enter the portals of Vedanta?

\>\>

  

Brihadaranyaka upanishad actually wants a grown up to reverse and become like a child - the *opposite* of what you are asking for!  

  

BU 5.1:tasmAdbrAhmaNaH pANDityaM nirvidya bAlyena tishhThAset

When the pride of learning goes, he becomes like a child..
