Dear Bhaskarji,

Call Him Rama, Krishna or Eeshvara and one can concentrate on his
Vyakta state and achieve Saamipya Mukti or nearness to the Lord or go further near the Lord by intense saadhanaa and achieve Saalokya Mukti or being able to stay in His Goloka. The highest saadhakas can achieve the saarupya mukti by being the look-alike of the Lord. But only by realizing that the world is impermanent and doing dhyana on the Lord’s avyaya state, one can be one with the Lord and cross the cycle of birth and death, and thus achieve the Videhamukti, which is the goal of the Advaitin. Lord Ram and Lord Krishna taught this principle in the simplest way, as I look at it.
