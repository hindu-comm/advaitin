Dear Sundarji,

  

Bhaskarji will respond to your lengthy reply to his mail.  

  

Meanwhile, I would like to point out, in brief, that the Indriyas are active only for the material world of the Vyavaharika Satya. But when the sadhaka has the advaitic realization on the impermanence of the
material world and during the time of his living in the material world the advaita sadhaka is engaged in selfless work, then gradually the activity of his mind decreases and eventually the sadhaka leaves the sthulasareera and his shukshma sareera including the chitta too disappears and he achieves the Videhamukti.  

  

May be someone will be able to write this in a still shorter way. Too many words and too much of verbosity, appears to me as self-defeating.  

  

My 2 cents,
