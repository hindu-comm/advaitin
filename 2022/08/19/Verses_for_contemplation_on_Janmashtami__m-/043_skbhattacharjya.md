Dear Venkatraghavanji and friends,

  

A Vajrayana Buddhist scholar once made a statement that the separation between the Vyavaharika state and the Paramarthika state is very thin, Our Vyavaharika state is due to the projection of our mind and the projection is due to ignorance. Great ***skill*** and ***Jnana*** are required to reverse the side of the path. This view is no different from the Vedantic view.

  

In one of the Upanishads Lord Ram advised Hanumanji to meditate on His (Lord Ram's) avyaya state, and we know that "Aum" is "ramnaamaat pratutpannah". So also in the nididhyana, the procedure (skill) is to meditate on our paramarthika state by meditating on a Mahavakya.

  

Any comment?
