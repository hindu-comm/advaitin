Pranams Bhaskar-ji and others,

\>\>Namaste,

Keeping aside the personal remarks and focusing on the topic at hand –..

Thus, it appears that both from the words of Shankaracharya and their application in the practice of Vedanta by traditional followers of his teachings, the aShTAnga yoga system in total is not problematic to traditional Vedanta, it is when the philosophy deviates from the teaching of the Upanishads where there is a disagreement.

\>\>

I agree what was supposed to be a gentle jibe got rather personal and I apologize for that. This topic has grown long in the tooth (some of it since 2006), and one might say it’s getting old.

Venkatraghavan-ji now and many others in the past have established that PYS as a practical system is useful to traditional Vedanta even if its philosophy is dualistic.

To explain this further, something posted by Sri Vidyasankar caught my attention and is a good starting point for a discussion on 'Practical System':

*What happens in the advaita paramparA circles is that the brahmasUtra derived line of thinking is about organizing scripture in order tostudy it, while the yogasUtra and other texts pertain to the personal requirements that lead to fruition. Ultimately, all said and done, as far as someone searching for liberation is concerned, the jnAna conveyed by a text, even as exalted as the veda, needs to become one's own personal, non-mediated jnAna.*

*Note: This is not presenting VedAnta texts as theory and yoga texts as practical manuals*  

After all (as Swami Sarvapriyananda puts it) Nidhidhyasana is answering the question : **Is it real for me?**

In this context, a few months ago I had posted a message about a Jewish Professor of History’s interview on Meditation (wrote a world famous book). He never knew about Patanjali or Yoga . *Yet I could take his description of meditation and map it back to a number of Patanjali Yoga Sutras.*

[https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/bFD60iPJTLA/m/3745BHXHAAAJ](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/bFD60iPJTLA/m/3745BHXHAAAJ)

Another example: I have a few friends that are sincere Sadhakas practicing meditation. They have good exposure to Advaita Vedanta but not Patanjali Yoga. When they describe their meditative process, their description maps to PYS concepts such as eka tattva abhyasa, tatra sthithau, Chitta Bhoomi, Dharana etc etc. Have they achieved Dhyana and higher states, No?.

But by extrapolation, it can be understood when they progress to the higher states, those also will also map to PYS.  

  

Point is - not everyone reads the ‘theory’ of PYS and starts meditating. *Rather it is the opposite.* When someone progresses in meditation, after being taught by a Advaitic Guru, they find their experience maps to what is described in the Yoga sutras.

  

Are they following PYS? No, but it so happens theirmeditative process relates to various concepts in PYS.
