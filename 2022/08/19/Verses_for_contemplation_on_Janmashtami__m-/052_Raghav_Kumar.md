योगशास्त्रेऽपि — ‘ अथ तत्त्वदर्शनोपायो योगः’ इति सम्यग्दर्शनाभ्युपायत्वेनैव योगोऽङ्गीक्रियते ; अतः सम्प्रतिपन्नार्थैकदेशत्वादष्टकादिस्मृतिवद्योगस्मृतिरप्यनपवदनीया भविष्यतीति



  

  

I was reminded of the following.

  

One respected acharya of Arsha Vidya tradition suggested that this may even be a lost sutra/text of the yoga tradition in saying, योगशास्त्रेऽपि and referencing/quoting 'अथ तत्त्वदर्शनोपायो योगः’ with इति, this idea of yoga based dhyaanam being regarded as helpful as an upAya for GYAnam was acceptable within atleast some followers of yoga shAstra itself. In other words there could be some who, in practice/lifestyle, emphasized (as a matter of preference) yoga-based dhyaanam more than SMN but accepted/conceded that it (yoga based dhyaanam) was only an upAya and such followers of the sutra अथ तत्त्वदर्शनोपायो योगः did not try to contradict Advaita. (since they use the word upAya). The word tattva here being taken not in the sAMkhya sense in the referenced sutra but brahmAtmA).

  

This does not seem a contrived explanation. Because even today anyone who studies yoga shAstra and also has clarity about traditional SMN with shruti as pramANa would easily see the enormous benefits of the dhyaana and other practices mentioned in the yoga shaastra and the ease of showing non-conflict between much of the vyAvahArika postulates of the yoga tradition and Advaita pedagogy, a la Sri VidyaraNya in jIvanmuktiviveka. He and Sri Madhusudana in Gita 6th chapter bhAShya significantly validates yoga jargon such as samadhi etc., but it's clear they place/map/reinterpret these ideas in a Vedantic context.

  

In that sense, a generalized critique of yoga and NS would be out of place. The approach of Sri Vidyaranya in mapping much of the yogic jargon on to vedAnta without any compromise of the centrality of shravaNam etc., is insightful. Thus, the practice and endorsement of NS need not be a red rag for advaitins as demonstrated by the two stalwarts Sri Vidyaranya and Sri Madhusudana, not to speak of earlier texts like VC.
