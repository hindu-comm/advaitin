Dear friends,  

  

Yoga is a process for getting connected or united (with what is contemplated upon), depending in what is the aim of the meditator. Dvaita (say the Madhvite) meditator's aim is to get connected to Lord Krishna, and they very well know that Samadhi is the last stage of the Yogic process. As regards the aim of the Advaita meditators, all of you know your advaitic aim and the yogic process of Nididhyasana. There are 20 Yoga Upanishads and nobody should call Yoga as unvedic.
