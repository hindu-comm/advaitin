[[V Subrahmanian	2022-08-29, 18:08:03 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/ym8Bu2BvCtA)]]

The Centre for Brahmavidya is pleased to announce that the audio recordings of the Prasthana-traya Bhashya classes (in Tamil) of the reputed and revered scholar, Dr Mani Dravid Sastri, have been fully uploaded to the website www.centreforbrahmavidya and that those interested can click here (and go to 'Audio') to listen to the recordings made available therein.  
  
Earlier, Dr Mani Dravid Sastri’s bhashya classes on a few Upanishads and the Brahmasutras had been uploaded to the site. Now, the bhashya classes on the remaining Upanishads (forming part of the Ten major Upanishads) and the Bhagavadgita have also been uploaded, thus covering the complete Prasthana-traya.  
  
The classes have been nicely indexed and are easy to navigate.  
  
Please share this announcement with relevant groups/forums.
