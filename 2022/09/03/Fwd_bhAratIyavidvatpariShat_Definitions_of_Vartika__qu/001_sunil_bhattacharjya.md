**वार्त्तिक**, It may have been derived from ****वार्त्ता**** (vārttā) or वार्ता (vārttā), which means news, information,.  

Thus ** ***वार्त्तिक may possibly mean a comprehensive/inormative treatise on the subject.***  

**कारिका**, Apte's dictionary gives the following meaning

**कारिका** kārikā 1 A female dancer. -2 A business, or trade -3 ***A memorial verse, or a collection of such verses, on grammatical, philosophical, or scientific subjects, e. g. Bhattojī Dīkṣita's Kārikās on grammar; called वैयाकरणसिद्धान्तकारिका; सांख्यकारिका.***

**दीपिका,** Apte says as follows:

**दीपिका** dīpikā 1 A light, torch; आसन्नोषधयो नेतुर्नक्तमस्नेहदीपिका, R.4.75;9.70. -2 ***(At the end of comp.) Illustrator. elucidator as in तर्कदीपिका***. -3 Moonlight. -4 N. of a Rāgiṇī. -5 N. of some plants (Mar. वेखंड,  

**टीका**, Apte says as follows:

**टीका** ṭīkā \[टीक्यते गम्यते ग्रन्थार्थोऽनया\] ***A commentary, gloss; काव्यप्रकाशस्य कृता गृहे गृहे टीका तथाप्येष तथैव दुर्गमः.***
