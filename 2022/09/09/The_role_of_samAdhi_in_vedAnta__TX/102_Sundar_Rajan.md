![Inline image](https://groups.google.com/group/advaitin/attach/3840401a4f2ae/1663712411801blob.jpg?part=0.1&view=1 "Inline image")  
  

As an example is in Kahola-brahmana 3.5.1 of Brihad Up, where Nidhidhyasana is presented as meditation.

Brahmanah pandityam nirvidya balyena tisthset. Balyam ca pandityam…

बाल्यं च पाण्डित्यं च निर्विद्य निःशेषं कृत्वा अथ मननान्मुनिः योगी भवति ;

Having known all about this strength and scholarship, he becomes meditative, in other words, a Yogin.

एतावद्धि ब्राह्मणेन कर्तव्यम् , यदुत सर्वानात्मप्रत्ययतिरस्करणम् ;

एतत्कृत्वा कृतकृत्यो योगी भवति ।

This is all that a Brahmana (seeker of Brahman) should do, namely, to eliminate all ideas of the non-Self; By doing this, he accomplishes his task and becomes a Yogin...

कृतकृत्यो भवति — ब्रह्मैव सर्वमिति प्रत्यय उपजायते ।

He becomes accomplished — he attains the conviction that all is Brahman.

Obviously, in this section above Sankara is considering Nidhidhyasana as Yoga (Meditation) and the person practicing it as Yogin. He also brings up an important point “to eliminate all ideas of the non-Self” echoing “*na kinchdapi cintayet*” of the Gita 6^(th) chapter on the practice of Yoga.

\>\> Do I have to everytime wake you up that what has been said as yOga in geeta & Upanishads are adhyAtma yOga which is purely vastu tantra in nature and yOga what has been taught in PY and which you want to smell in PTB is purusha taNtra which bhAshyakAra categorically refuted by saying that people are under the delution that popular sAnkhya and yOga are mOksha sAdhana hence we are taking these schools for refutation??
If you are still not sure what bhAshyakAra said about sAnkhya and yOga refer 1-4-28 sUtra where he says that with the lengthy refutation of the sAnkhya all other systems including the yOga of Patanjali may be deemed to have been refuted. And 2-1-3 also don’t forget.\>\>

This is as I said before is arguing for argument sake, vidhanta vaada.

Don’t the Advaita Acharyas know that? The author of Advaita Siddhi and Jivanmukti Viveka surely knows what is dvaita shastra and what is not? They also know what aspects and practices Sankara accepted from PY – this is what Sri Vidya-ji repeatedly explained: *The distinction between yogic dhyAna and vedAntic dhyAna is more Academic than one of practice.*

In any case, how can a sit down Meditation, holding the body steady and focusing the mind, the Adhaytma Yoga taught in Gita be Vastu Tantra? That is absurd. The Sutras say “*samAdhy abhAvAc ca*” that if the jIva were not a doer of action, then attainment of samAdhi would be impossible. So if Jiva is the one accomplishing Samadhi, how can the Yoga Abhaysa practice (as described in Gita for example) that results in that Samadhi be anything but Purusha Tantra ?

Sankara glosses Nidhidhyasana as*Nischayena Dhyatvya* Steadfast meditation.

The result of Nididhyasana, Jnana is Vastu Tantra but how can how can*Steadfast meditation* be vastu tantra?

For arguments case, even if PY is Purusha Tantra and different from Adhyatma Yoga, the practice in PY will result in ekagratha and purification of the antahkarana and such a person is more fit to receive knowledge than otherwise.
