Members can carefully read the bhashya of BSB 2-1-3 "Refutation of Yoga".



Ø I am sorry if at all I am coming in the way of your passionate reading of this adhikaraNa/sUtra in favor of PYS. OK since you are quoting the gist of shankara’s message based on swami gambhiraananda’s translation. I would like to quote my paramaguru Sri SSS’s observation on this particular sUtra i.e. 2-1-3. Under the heading YOGA MATADA KHANDANE : He says in Kannada (for Kannada readers) :

Ø //quote// sAnkhyadante yogavU padhAnAdigalanne oppiruvudarinda sAnkhya khandaneyindale adannu (yOga shAstra) khandisidantaagiddaru tattva darshanakke upAyavendu yOgavannu shrutiyalliyu upadeshisiruvudarinda yOgAnushTaanavannu vedAntigalU oppabeku emba shankeyannu visheshavaagi elli tiraskariside.



- sAnkhyayOga smrutigalannu kelavu amshagalalli oppabahudendu Achaaryaru
  heluvudannu keli anekaru paatanjala yogada abhyaasavu vedaanta
  jnaanakki atyavashyavendu nambiruttaare. E nambikeyu sariyalla.
  Ekendare, shrutiyalli sAnkhyayogagalinda jagatkAraNanaada devana
  praaptiyaaguttade endu heliddaru alli ( shruti) vaidika jnaanavannu
  sAnkhyaventalu vaidika dhyAnavannu yOgaventalu helide endu (2-1-3)
  spashtapadisiruttaare. Dvaitigalaada sAnkhyara jnAnavu yOgigala
  anushTAnavu advaitakke upayOgavillavendu kanTaravarinda heliruttaare.
  Elli tilisiruva vaidika dhyaanavu yaavadembudannu eshtaralliye jnana
  sadhana vichaaradalli vishadagolisuvevu.//unquote//



- In short Sri SSS clarifying here : like sAnkhya, yOga too accept
  doctrine like pradhAna etc. and by refuting sAnkhya, the yOga shAstra
  also deemed to be refuted. The doubt like, since in shruti it has
  been said that yOga is means for tattvadarshana hence practice of yOga
  (yOgAnushTAna) has to be agreed / adopted by vedAntins notions like
  this also exclusively refuted here in sUtra (2-1-3). Going by
  Acharya’s observation that some points in sAnkhya-yOga smruti are
  acceptable, some would insist for pAtanjala yOga practice is
  indispensable for the vedAnta jnana. But this belief is NOT right.
  Because in shruti though it has been said through sAnkhya-yoga there
  is deva praapti what has been said there in shruti is vaidika jnana as
  sAnkhya and vaidika dhyAna as yOga. And this has been clearly
  clarified by bhAshyakAra in 2-1-3 itself. The dvaitins’ sAnkhya jnana
  and yOgins’ yOgAnushTAna has no role to play in Advaita sAdhana. That
  which has been mentioned here as ‘vaidika dhyAna” the nature of which
  will be explained in other section called ‘jnAna sAdhana’.



- The above is what Sri SSS’s observation on 2-1-3, which is obviously
  an ‘aapta vaakya’ pramANa for me to approach shankara vedAnta in
  general, yOgapratyuktvAdhikaraNa in particular. And IMHO Sri SSS
  conclusion is not completely out of context since following points
  have been categorically expressed by bhAshyakAra himself at various
  places in his PTB :



- (a) both sAnkhya & yOga are dvaitins and they are NOT Atmaikatva
  darshins. Hence whatever means they suggest to their followers
  obviously not aimed towards vedAnta paramArtha darshana i.e.
  Atmaikatva darshana.



- (b) In a nitpicking style shankara explicitly warns his followers that
  what has been said in shruti as sAnkhya and yOga is not popularly
  known as sAnkhya and yOga but it is vaidika jnana and dhyAna.



- ( c) chittavrutti nirOdha which forms the basic requirement for PYS’s
  ultimate state asaMprajnAta samAdhi is not mOksha sAdhana as clarified
  by bhAshyakAra himself.



- ( d) deep sleep and samAdhi both are not free from avidyA clarifies
  shankara hence this time bound state is NOT advaita’s paramArtha state
  / jnana.



- ( e) advaita’s paramArtha jnana does not have any time restrictions
  and not restricted to any particular avastha and it is not a vyavahAra
  abhaava sthiti, what is stated in PYS’s samAdhi is NOT jnAni’s sahaja
  avastha (natural state).



- (f) PYS’s yOga practice is not vastu tantra based practice by it is
  deliberate suppression and concentration on objects (like shatchakra
  or any other external objects) it is purusha/kartru/chOdana taNtra
  only. Whereas Advaita jnana is vastu tantra the realization is ‘as it
  is’ NOT ‘as we see’.



- With this backdrop I would like to see what has been stated in 2-1-3.
