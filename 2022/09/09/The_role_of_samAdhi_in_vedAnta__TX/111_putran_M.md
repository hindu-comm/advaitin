The Astaka ceremony is interesting. Yes, you need to stop convoluting as you just did above. Read the bhashya here without bias.

  

The bhashyakara specifically points to and **accepts** that the manonigraha state spoken of in the Ka.U. is also found in the yoga smriti. It is indicating "process of Yoga" as opposed to "knowledge of Brahman", and as process, he accepts it is a portion of yoga srmiti that is acceptable to the Vedic tradition. If it is a part of yoga smriti acceptable to Vedic tradition, then that has to be devoid of inherent philosophical bias that go against the Veda; it can be detached from superimposed or infused dualism and naturally fit within advaita sadhana.

  

The acharya **rejects** that this process is independently capable of giving liberation; only knowledge of Brahman can do so.
