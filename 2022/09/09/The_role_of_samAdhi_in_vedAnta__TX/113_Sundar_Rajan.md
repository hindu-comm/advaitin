I am guessing you are stating Sri SSS's position that Adhyatma Yoga is Vastu Tantra unlike PYS. Here is a link to a post:[https://tomdas.com/2021/05/19/how-swami-satchitanandendra-saraswati-sss-defines-nididhyasana-vedantic-meditation/](https://tomdas.com/2021/05/19/how-swami-satchitanandendra-saraswati-sss-defines-nididhyasana-vedantic-meditation/)

> \>\>This is further clarified in the introduction to the text
> Adhyatma Yoga. In this context the term ‘Vastu Tantra’ means
> Nididhysana is a means to Self-Realisation or Truth-Realisation.
> (‘Vastu Tantra’ means ‘a path to the truth’, which is independent of
> the person who is looking..\>\>

I am curious how much agreement is there on the claim that Adhyatma Yoga (Nidhidhyasana) is Vastu Tantra?

  

For example, SriVenkatraghavan-ji does not considernididhyAsana to be Vastu Tantra - See below:

[https://groups.google.com/d/msgid/advaitin/CAL34aEkZa4vgT1YmBFdS6Qx6NGcZT%3D%2B6UELxFou0ZYqW7z-wHg%40mail.gmail.com](https://groups.google.com/d/msgid/advaitin/CAL34aEkZa4vgT1YmBFdS6Qx6NGcZT%3D%2B6UELxFou0ZYqW7z-wHg%40mail.gmail.com?utm_medium=email&utm_source=footer)

  

"*My contention is that both manana and nididhyAsana are puruShatantra only - in the sense that both require puruSha prayatna. The former is the deliberate operation of reasoning for the purpose of removing prameya asambhAvana and the latter is the deliberate entertainment of brahmAkAra vRtti.So they are very much subject to kartum-akartum-anyathAkartum.*"

  

I had the same doubt. Just as with PYS, you perform Adhyatma Yoga by sitting down, keeping your head and back straight etc as explained in the Gita. It is definitely purusha prayatna. I am not sure how it can be classified as Vastu Tantra.

  

It is interesting that in both JVM and Sri Madhusudana Saraswathi, explain the so called Adhaytma Yoga mantra "Yachhed van manasi.." (Katha Up) as regular Yoga and cite the connections to Patanjali Yoga Sutras. In their opinion, Adhyatma Yoga and PY are equivalent. They are also Apta Vakyas.

  

Why would we consider their Apta Vakya (JVM ..) to be any less valid than the other one. Especially as it looks some of the assumptions regarding Vastu Tantra may be incorrect.
