Let me add anotherpoint of agree-disagree.

  

The statement is that we should debate this topic strictly based on PT+B or perhaps on bhashya alone.

  

But the fact is that PTB has given rise to multiple streams of interpretation. Those who vouch for a stream invariably point out Acharyas who are not Shankara (or who are thought by others to be not Shankara). Whether it is SSS or Swami Vidyaranya, or later scholars/acharyas who interpret them in turn.

  

This means that the debate cannot be conducted strictly based on PTB, but based on logical deductions done by my apta or your apta or myself or yourself. If the methodology is to leave out all such "aptas" and only accept Shankara, then we simply have to admit that PTB does not answer these questions in an air-tight this and only this manner - which again is why we have Swami Vidyaranya saying there are two paths (yoga and discrimination) and the yoga one can culminate in NS (or call it VS if you wish) at the time of realization (Panchadasi 126-128) and we have SSS who disagrees with such a position. The logic deductions based on textual analysis simply do not suffice to conclude unilaterally for all people, and they choose a direction that fits their mind or their apta's.
