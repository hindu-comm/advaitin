Dear Bhaskarji and Putranji and other friends.  

  

I have a query and shall be thankful if you can enlighten me as to what do you think about the following views:

  

Personally, I have great regards for Maharshi Patanjali and his "Patanjali Yoga Sutra" (PYS), though the general feeling among the Indian scholars seems to be that PYS is not for the Advaitic Sadhana. I think PYS does not ignore the Advaitic Sadhana, because in the Vyasa Bhshya on PYS, there is clear mention of the Samprajna / Savikalpa Samadhi in the verse: 1.17 and about Asamprajna / Nirvikalpa Samadhi in the verse: 1.18. Moreover Adi Shankara's Guru Govindamuni or Govindapada was known as an incarnation of Maharshi Patanjali Himself. Thirdly, Patanjali does approve concentration on Purna Brahman, who is both Manifested and Unmanifested.Katha Upanishad also says in 2.1.13  

  

अङ्गुष्ठमात्रः पुरुषो ज्योतिरिवाधूमकः ।  
ईशानो भूतभव्यस्य स एवाद्य स उ श्वः । एतद्वै तत् ॥ १३॥  

  

aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo jyotirivādhūmakaḥ .  
īśāno bhūtabhavyasya sa evādya sa u śvaḥ . etadvai tat .. 13..  

  

13 The Purusha, of the size of a thumb, is like a flame without smoke. The Lord of the past and the future, He is the same today and tomorrow. This, verily, is That.
