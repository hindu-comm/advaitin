# Inthe Sanat sujatiya (Fourth Chapter in Shankaracharya's Bhashya edition) here is a verse: 

# महाभारतम्-05-उद्योगपर्व-046

[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/2ab](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/2ab)

  

  

सदा सदासत्कृतः स्यान्न मृत्युरमृतं कुतः \|  
सत्यानृते सत्यसमानबन्धने; सतश्च योनिरसतश्चैक एव ॥१९॥  
योगिनस्तं प्रपश्यन्ति भगवन्तं सनातनम् ॥१९॥  

  

# Bhashya Shankaracharya comments: Both therelatively e real and unrealhave for their bases the supreme reality. That is, the vyavaharika and the pratibhasika reality have their support in the substratum that is the Paramarthika Reality.

# Yogins realize that Supreme reality.

  

We are reminded of the the Taittiriya Upanishad 2.6.1 statement:सत्यंचानृतंच सत्यमभवत् ।the Supreme Reality, Paramarthika Satya, Bramhan, itself became both the the relatively real (vyavaharika satya) and the seemingly real (pratibhasika satya).  

  

See a detailed article on the Taittiriya passage, citing the bhashya, etc:[https://adbhutam.wordpress.com/2010/02/17/paramarthika-vyavaharika-satyam/](https://adbhutam.wordpress.com/2010/02/17/paramarthika-vyavaharika-satyam/)
