Namaskaram Santosh-ji,

  

I read up to "What Mahabharata is not!". The straight-forward language including important quotes from Batman and Superman will definitely make for a compelling read. Will read the rest of at least the free portion. I am very interested in seeing how you carry out the 'refutational commentary'. Traditionalists used to such refutationin the bhashyas, I think, will warm up to your taking that style into the modern battlefields.

  

Speaking of batman and superman, the thought occurred thatwhen I read you chidingpeople for fighting over Karna and Arjuna, that these days they lose their minds caught in the world of sports personalities and teams. As if there is a hero and a villain and how unfair or not right if their team does not win. Etc. Its comical how entangled we are. I also recall seeing in youtube great war of words and evidence-videos being waged over whether lion or tiger is superior should they fight, between the Lion-fans and the Tiger-fans!

  

At the same time, even though I don't get caught in the Karna or Arjuna debate, some of the things you stated were difficult to digest: for instance, that the rivalry was more an excuse for Vyasa to teach dharma etc., or that the ithihasa is only a pre-destined play enacted by Vishnu. That would mean the actions of those jivas were entirely that of robots devoid of their own choices, that we should truly just think of the itihasa as only a movie (Vishnu's play rather than some human creation) unlike our lives in which we have to make active choice between dharma and adharma.

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
