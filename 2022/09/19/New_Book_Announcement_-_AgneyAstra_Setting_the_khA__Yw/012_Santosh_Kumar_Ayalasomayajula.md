Namaste Dear Sri Putran ji,

  

Thanks for your glance and the feedback. Yes, people these days have filled YouTube with only one versus the other game 😁. Mahabharata teaches dharma but no one takes interest in those chapters.

  

To answer the below quoted point from your email. Our lives are driven by Karma but devatas' are driven by leelas. Creation is cyclic so are the events (sUryachandramasau dhAtA yathA pUrvamakalpayat, says Rig Veda).

In the Mahabharata itself there are evidences to say that Mahabharata had happened hundreds of times in thepast kalpas too (that piece is not covered in this book though but you can see that in Janamejeya's sarpasatra concluding chapters and also in Sabha parva). Everytime the outcome would be the same. In fact the popularly understood thme as MBH was a Kaurava Pandava rivalry which led to mass annihilation is not true because as per Harivamsa as well as the Adi Parva the earth was **already** burdened by sinful beings. Yama was conducting a grand sacrifice for several yugas and hence he wasn't at his office seeing this opportunity rakshasas took birth in human wombs as princes and also through animals and tormented the earth for yugas without getting killed living for long lives. It was their burden that Vishnu wanted to relieve the earth from. So the devatas has a meeting and they all plan to descend on earth along with the rakshasa portions in them for being in opposite camp. Indra incarnated as Pandavas, kalipurusha as Duryodhana, Surya as Karna, and several celestial beings likewise as many warriors. They all fought on the excuse of family feud but amassed armies from all over the earth as their allies and that large scale destruction cleansed the earth of the sinful rakshasas. Kauravas and Pandavas were just the means to bring them to the battle ground for the grand slaughter. So in that sense yes it was Vishnu's plan. Even Krishna himself hints this in various places for example we consider Drona cut the finger of ekalavya but in Drona Parva Krishna hints it was he who did the same I mean inspiring Drona and achieving the task via him. Another interesting fact is Mahabharata script was composed much before the Kuru elders (Dhritarashtra et al) were even born. The actions happened later. Also in MBH itself there are instances where we see people having known the events that were destined to happen. The Panchama Veda wasn't created for the first time. Drona was learned in Mahabharata already so he knew the events of it. Bhisma is said to know all events be it past or future too. Arjuna in Vana Parva itself tells to Krishna how Krishna would submerge the Dwaraka at the end into ocean. There are many evidences to support that they were enacting a script and not performing karma like us without knowledge of the destined events.

We are bound by our karmas and our future course of destiny depends upon the choices we make but the Itihasas and puranas and the acts of the devatas have a purpose -- the purpose to teach lessons of morality, about vidhi and nisheda, to the mankind therefore their deeds are never random they are always planned as per the script. Devatas are the teachers of the mankind. Every Ramayana will have Rama alone winning in no instance Ravana ever becomes a pious guy and Rama a sinful being. In every Mahabharata Pandavas remain on Dharma and win. Kauravas get defeated. These all follow a template and events repeat in every kalpa. So yes true that our deeds and choices will shape our destiny but in a scriptural events the basic template remains the same only some changes happen due to kalpa bheda for example in our kalpa five Pandavas were five Indras and Draupadi was Shachi but in earlier kalpas Five Pandavas were five portions of Shiva's five faces and Draupadi was Parvati. In another kalpa Krishna was goddess Kali's manifestation and Arjuna was Vishnu's incarnation and so on. Puranas have summarily presented Mahabharata several times and we can see the variations over there and Skanda Purana specifically mentions the reason as Kalpa bheda butwho are the heroes who are the villains and how it begins and ends remains same always.

  

Within the limits of my little awareness there are no evidences observed this far which proves that script followed the actions. It was always that the actions followed the script.

  

In the Canto-2 of this book you will see exactly this topic being discussed at length how Itihasas follow a template and all. Unfortunately that portion isn't visible in the book preview.
