Hare Krishna



As I said earlier, it is better for me to express my thoughts (purely based on Sri SSS’s works & shankara’s PTB) on some typical issues without heeding much to what others say / think on these issues. First of all the similarity that can be seen between vedic no-mind state ( vide reference geeta 6^(th) chapter, kArika -3, katha shruti etc.) and PYS’s description on asamprajnAtha samAdhi or NS in Advaita Vedanta prakaraNa-s like VC. Whether the no-mind state achieved in jnana mArga is the fine-tuned or more sophisticated version of PY or purely vedic in its nature is not the matter of contention here. It is simply an attempt to explain what is NO-MIND state when Advaita Vedanta says so.



There are statements like atra veda avedaaH, atra deva adeva etc. in shruti. In sushupti when jeeva becomes one with brahman veda is no veda, deva is no deva etc. and also it says Atman is astUlaManaNu etc. All these words are indication that Atman alone is then cognized by the mind and this Atman svarUpa is beyond the reach of language, mind and intellect. (yatOvAchO nivartante aprApya manasa saha), As we all know mind too often prompts us to be always extrovert ( basic tendency of looking outward) but when the vedic words such as above signifies that Atman alone naama and naami (both name and named object). Just like wood that burnt away in the fire but still looking alike (firewood) and become fire likewise the vedas/devas too become one with Atman alone.
So, in the state of adviteeya whatever is there (like pramAtru, prameya, pramAna triputi) become one with the Atman alone. Let us take one more example where there is a strong room for assumption that jnAni meets immediate physical death after attaining jnana in br.up. 4-4-7 it has been said: when all the desires residing in one’s heart have been got rid of, then the mortal being becomes immortal and attains brahman here, in this life. Just as the cast off slough of a snake ***would lie lifeless*** in an ant-hill, ***so also the body of the enlightened person lie there***; and he is now (really) ***BODILESS***, the prANa, brahman alone, the light of pure consciousness alone. This obviously does not mean the physical death of the jnAni but here it has been taught the effacement of the idean on one’s identity with body. The intrinsic nature of bodilessness is revealed as soon as enlightenment dawns. The jnana sublates the very knowership (jnAtrutvaM) doership (kartrutvaM) of jnAni. In sUtra bhAshya shankara explains this in 4-1-13 : For as opposed to the previously entertained idea of being an agent and experiencer of the fruit of action, the knower of brahman will have to come to this conclusion that I am brahman devoid of all agentship and experiencing fruits of actions for all the three periods of time. I was never an agent or experiencer even before this, nor am I such now nor shall I be such in future. Since these functions i.e. katrutvaM, bhOktrutvaM, jnAtrutvaM, pramAtrutvaM etc. is the function of mind in avidyA which has been completely annihilated /sublated after the dawn of jnana ‘as if’ mind is completely dead like body of the jnAni lies there like slough of the snake. The functions of the mind with parichinnatvaM would be completely stopped since main wings of mind (katrutvaM, bhOktrutvaM etc.) completely clipped by the adviteeya paramArtha jnana it has been said mind has completely merged in its svarUpa i.e. Atman. Point to be noted here it is not literally mind-dead state or complete stoppage of mind function where intellect become numb it is the state where mind (karaNa) which has become sUkshma through the process of vedic sAdhana realizes Atman alone is real and atman alone all and in its svarUpa it is Atman alone nothing else.
Atmanyeva atmani pashyati. It realizes that Atman is shrOtasya shrOtraM, manOsoa manO, yadvAchova ha vAchaM sa U prANasya prAnaH (kena up). In kannada we often say when we touch the very hot water : abbaa!! benki haage sudutA ide as if here hot water itself the fire. In the same manner when the mind of the Advaita sAdhaka hears this statement Atman is the mind of even the psychic mind, the mind realizes the reality which is the substance of all including its own functions.



Thus no-mind state explained as mind itself gets merged or dissolved ‘as it were’ in Atman like salt doll while trying to measure the depth of the ocean it gets itself dissolved in the ocean waters. The same method has been described by Sri gaudapAda in his kArika (3-32) : Atmasatya anubhOdena na sankalpayate yadA amanastAm tadA yAti grAhyAbhAve tadagrahaM. The mind will give up its functions (through shAstra janita viveka/jnana) like saMkalpa vikalpa and its very nature of going outward etc. as a result of the viveka that there exists no anAtma vastu at all. Hence the state of no-mind is mind loses all its contents which are in the form of subtle desires attachments, shOka-mOha, rAga dvesha etc.). This is what is implied in kArika’s amanaska yOga and it is not patanjala praNeeta asaMprajnAtha samAdhi as a result of deliberate suppression of chitta vrutti. No mind state is nothing but no veda, no deva state of adviteeya Atman which mind itself realized within itself through vedic SMN and on the basis of sArvatrika pUrNAnubhava (universal experience of avasthA traya and its examination based on shrutyukta tarka). It is not special darshana of Atma or getting the AtmAkAra pratyaya or Atma sAkshAtkAra or brahmAkAra vrutti or akhandAkAra vrutti in a special state called NS. It is viveka janita jnana purely in a vedic way.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
