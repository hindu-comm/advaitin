Dear Bhaskarji,

  

Lord Krishna talks about the Ashta-prakrtis in the Bhagavad Gita. On death one leaves his or her sthula sareera or the physical body and in the next birth the Ashtaprakrtis create his or her fresh new body, depending on the past karmas. However, the successful Advaita sadhaka, the Sthitaprajna, who acquired and realized well the Advaita Jnana that everything in the Vyavaharika world is transient or fleeting or impermanent, can escape the cycle birth and death, which means that in his daath he or she loses both the Sthoola sarrera or the physical body as well as the Sukshma sarrera. This means the successful sadhaka, on death becomes sareera sunya, i.e.,, Sthula Sarrera shunya as well as the Sukshma sareera-shunya. Here the Sukshma sareera shunya means the same as the  Manah Shunya.  

  

This I think is one of the simplest ways of explaining the Advaita Mukti.
