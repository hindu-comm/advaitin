Namaste,

  

\>\>

the word "USELESS" has been used. It means it is useless only and nothing else.

Please do not twist the meaning of that word.

\>\>

  

This reminds me of a verse I read many years ago in Vivekachudmani :avijñāte pare tattve śāstrādhītistu niṣphalāThe study of the Scriptures is useless so long as the highest Truth is not known

  

If we take this literally then all scriptural study should be abandoned, obviously that is not the case asVivekachudmani itself has 400+ other verses discussing scriptures only!

  

So the context has to be understood before jumping into conclusions as we saw with the discussion about Sushupti being equivalentto Samadhi and some members thumping the tables declaring it to be the final verdict. And I responded with verses from Gaudapada Karika and Shankara Bhasya forcefully proving the opposite, that Sushupti **is NOT** Samadhi!.

  

Likewise here we have to understandwhy the same Maharishi is sayingYogas chitta vritti nirodhah is acceptable to all and dig deeper into his other sayings to determine the tatparyam.
