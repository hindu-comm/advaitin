The Pramana for knowing Dharma is the Veda; hence the Valid inner forms of the sanatana dharma are to be known by recourse to the Veda.***Hinduism in a restricted sense refers to the family of Religions that explicitly use the Veda for Pramana. In a more general sense, Hinduism refers to the Sanatana Dharma that is manifest in the valid Inner forms/elements of all Religions.***

  

So, a tribal may not use the Veda as Pramana; but the tribal may still be a Hindu and a follower of Hinduism in so far that her Religion can be validated through recourse to the Veda, as having a valid inner form of the sanatana dharma and perhaps invalid outer elements that are more or less superfluous.
