namaste,

I am not a great orator nor a convincing articulator, although I do have opinions and concerns similar to what is raised. I do not hope to influence anyone. but just feel like sharing some thoughts.

the disintegration of the Hindu as a culture, although is being argued as impossible, is happening in a manner that can affect lives or families in an individual (vysasti) manner, rather than in a collective (samasti) manner. while the total sum of the practicing Hindu people may well remain more or less same, or would be argued to be growing, the opportunity for a individual or family to associate or affiliate with such gathering is narrowing. Dilution of traditional practices and culture is happening in every family in varied degree and each of us are largely responsible. besides the political setup and governance is also contributing a lot and given the changing methods of occupation and lure, tradition has always being the easy one to be sacrificed or left behind.

Of course, the rate in which the decline or tampering is happening seems to be at an alarming pace, but, when nothing can be practically done to stall or counter them, a better way would be to shift focus and attention to happenings that are aligned to the culture and tradition and make attempts to pick and stay with those that are closer to your needs or desire.
