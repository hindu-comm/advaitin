[[Sundar Rajan	2022-11-02, 06:27:15 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/tqnZaG99Pag)]]

The late Director of Archaeology in Tamil NaduDr Nagaswamy had written a article about a Tamil translation/commentary ofSoundarya Lahiri. The book according to the references of Kings cited belongs to the 12th Century AD, the Chola period, This would it one of the earliest translations of Soundarya Lahiri in a different language.

  

In that bookDr Nagaswamy points outthe author makes a reference to Dravida Sisu in verse 74and describes the child who received the blessings as a Kauntinya from Sirkali (a small town) and argues that is a direct reference to Thirujnana Sambandar, the first of the Saiva Thevaram saints from Tamil Nadu.
