Dear Subbuji,

  

What you said is correct. About the Bhagavad Gita, Maharshi Vedavyasa says it is Sarva shastramayee. The Mahabharata is a complete text in all the four aspects, but Vedavyasa became sad after writing it, when he realized that the common people can't understand it. Then he wrote the Bhagavatam to tell the same thing in such a way that even the common people could understand everything.
