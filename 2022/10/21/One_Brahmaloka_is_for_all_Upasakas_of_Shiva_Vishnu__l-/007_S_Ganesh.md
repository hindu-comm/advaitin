[[S Ganesh	2023-11-28, 07:48:25 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/l-XtfDq_9I8)]]

The Paladyaya, 4th adyayaor last chapter of Brhmasutradiscusses mukthi or palam. The 2nd and 4th pada there, discuss about upasaka's mukthi.

Jaimini who comes as poorvapakshaquotes chandogya 4.15.6 and kata sruti 2.3.16 amrtatvamyeti etc to say upasakas attain parambrhmanonly.

Vyasa reconciles these 2 srutis by bringing in the concept of krama mukthi, where he says upasakas go to karya brahma or hiranyagarbathrough shukla gati

then attain parambrhmawithout travel after getting nirguna gnanam. by this gnanat eva kaivalyam is also not contradicted. He argues that chandogya 4.15.5

is only brhmalokaas it is not only logical 'asya gati upapateh' but also clarified in brahadaranyakasruti6.2.15 in 'visheshitatvat'sutra.
