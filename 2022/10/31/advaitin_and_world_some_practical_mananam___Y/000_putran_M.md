Namaskaram,

  

  

The world is my rupam/appearance. No matter the form I*appear*as, I am the same. Forms are not inherently good or bad, right or wrong, for all denote the unchanging Self - except from the practical standpoint of what may aid in the realization of Self.

  

(By world, of course is included the mental-world-content of our thoughts.

  

“What may aid in the realization of Self” is the path of ‘Dharma’. The three purusharthas: dharma, artha and kama accommodate for the jiva in the context of its Samsaric body-mind-world identifications but have the higher purpose and potential to allow the jiva to seek and realize the ultimate purushartha of moksha.)

  

*See the world*as rupam or appearance of Self (or in bhakti/dvaiticterms: of God), thereby shifting from world to Self.

  

*See the Self/I*pervading/appearing as world

  

See (focus on) the Self/I as unchanging and constant even as the world-appearance changes.
