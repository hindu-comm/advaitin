Some translated verses from the Sri Vasishtasangraha, sent by my father to some family members. I share below my comments (particularly after reading 56-57).

  

Quote (The Story of Bhusunda, Section 5: The cause of long life)

  

56-57. I neither think of the past nor the future at any time. Resting upon (or resorting to) the view (or perception) of the present with the mind, I remain here.

  

57-58. My mind is not delighted on the arrival of good fortune; nor does it suffer misery in misfortunes. It is ever equal (unaffected by circumstances). Due to that, I live free from disease.

  

58-59. "This one is a kinsman and this one is a stranger. This one is mine; this one is otherwise." Sage! I do not apprehend (or feel) thus. Hence, I am long-lived.

  

59-60. I do not allow (or permit) within, the contact of any mental disposition (or thought) prompted (or impelled) by the snare of hope, (remaining) composed. Hence, I live without disease..."

This is very practical advice for maintaining peace of mind, and also natural to practice as we correspond with Vedantic knowledge.  

  

1\. Whatever be the past, present or future manifestation, the Consciousness (Brahman/Ishvara) that appears thus is ever the Same.

  

One Being, Existence, Consciousness appearing Manifold; the Universe (ishvara-jiva-jagat) is denotation of this nondual Reality/Brahman.

  

Change in appearance does not in any way whatsoever fundamentally change the underlying Reality/Entity.

  

Practical: By maintaining awareness of the unchanging Reality/Being behind all our perceptions, desist becoming unduly desirous for or perturbed by particular changes at the level of appearance.

  

  

2\. Thoughts (including of past and future) are formsin the mind, projected \[illumined\] by the Self.

  

Practical: a. Separate the mind-world and the sense-world, and desist superimposing what is only mental projection onto the world. For this purpose, you can ask the question "What am I seeing?" Answer this through abidance in what the senses are directly revealing now, peeling off the addedthought & image interjections happening in the mind.

  

b\. Look upon the thoughts as the Self's (your own) forms of the mind (whose changes do not change You); and Look upon the Present manifest sense-world as the Self's (Ishvara's) projected form (whose changes do not change Him). (the same Mother whether She appears as Lakshmi or Kali.)  

  

3\. \[tagging from a different conversation to a worried person\] Stay positive.. Don't let the mind harass you in a rollercoaster of possibilities. Have faith that Ishvara (and/or the Divine Mother) is running this universe according to a Dharmic Order which best facilitates our spiritual journeys. Things happening, that seem beyond our control, are His decisions, and they are in our best interest ultimately and aligned with our karma. Our business is to focus on the right karma according to dharma, and He will take care of results. I am sure you have offered your prayers to the Lord; now just focus on helping ..in best way possible to alleviate \[the difficult situation\]. Again, don't preemptively allow the mind to become lost in fears beyond our knowledge or control.
