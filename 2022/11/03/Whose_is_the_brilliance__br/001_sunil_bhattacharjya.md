Putranji,

  

In the Indian context, a brilliant person is he or she, who values introspection and cultivates love for dharma. Adi Shankara was one of the most brilliant persons in the history of the Hindus. He promised his mother that he would be near her at her last moment and truthful that he was, he did keep the promise. He cared for truth and no social custom could prevent him from taking care of his mother at her last moment. He surely knew Lord Krishna's two statements that (i) the Mother is the greatest of the gurus and also (ii) that truth is dharma. It is pity that in this Kali yuga, most people don't have the love for the truth.  

  

My 2 cents.
