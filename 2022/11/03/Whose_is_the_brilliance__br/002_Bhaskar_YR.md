praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji

Hare Krishna



A relative mentioned a youngster in the family: ~ "He is a brilliant kid."



I interjected rather un-brilliantly, "Why (make a big deal of) brilliant?!"



The comment was not well received, and I was looked upon as being negative etc.



It may be true but there is an alternate way of looking at the situation.



Ø Yes, you could have handled the situation without hurting the sentiments of your relative 😊 Your exclamation is the result of ‘mixing’ the vyavahAra with semi-pAramArthika or shAstra drushti (karma, pUrva janma, saMskAra etc.) which is not palatable to that relative at that point of time as he was affectionately admiring his boy’s brilliance.



The starting point for the jiva is the phala for the karma it had done in its past lives. So the brilliance we see in our Ramu may be the fruit of the intensive labour done in that jiva's past life as some Mr. Ryan. Of course, there is nothing particularly wrong in appreciating the fact that Ramu has such and such unique qualities, for the purpose of encouragement that he may fulfill his potential in this life;



- Yes it should be so!! As we cannot assume somany other things on our
  own when one is finding the brilliance in his/her kith and kin.





but they are not necessarily achievements of "our Ramu" but of the unknown "Mr. Ryan" whom we care not for.



- And he is ‘seeing’ the brilliance in rAmu without knowing anything
  about his previous janma saMskAra which he achieved in the body of
  Ryan / Raheem / Krishna so it is quite natural for him to appreciate
  what he is ‘seeing’ at present.



Our joy over such bestowed characteristics in "our child" has to be thus tempered by the knowledge of the larger karma-karmaphala journey of that jiva in its quest for moksha.



Ø I don’t see any problem even from shAstra drushti with regard to sincere appreciation of some elevating ‘jeeva’ in a particular BMI.



In a different context, suppose a Brahmana is born with a higher degree of intellectual acumen. Even then, this birth and its varna-associated qualities that the jiva has are only the phala for the jiva's karma in the past lives (wherein varna, gender, etc. can differ). It puts the jiva in the best position possible, given its karma, in order that it may work its way forward (spiritually). So, the real credit to the individual in this life is earned in this life by using such bestowed abilities (and life circumstances) corresponding to one's svadharma for the "higher purpose" of human life. That is not about the starting point but what we do with it.



Ø The expression of those accrued karma phala (saMskAra) happening vividly in the current janma in the particular compartment of BMI and there should absolutely no qualms in acknowledging it appreciably. This is how I look at the things.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

bhaskar
