Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

Yes, there is no serious problem where one is appreciative of the manifestation of such qualities. One is reminded of Sri Ramakrishna’s response to seeing Narendranath. We just need to stay grounded in Shastra drishti or aastika buddhi, when we engage with our world and not become blissfully lost in a limited samsara perspective. When we see the latter situation prevail, sometimes there is an urge to object and look through the lens of the aastika understanding.
