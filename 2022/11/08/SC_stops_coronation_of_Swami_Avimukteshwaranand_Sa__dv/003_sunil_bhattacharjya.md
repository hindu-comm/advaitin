Yes Bhaskarji,  

  

It pains us to hear that that there had neen a lot of court cases in the past, between the Sringeri Math in the Chikkamagaluru district in Karnataka, and the Sringeri math in the Shimoga district of Karnatak as well as with the Kanchi Kamakoti [math.in](http://math.in) the past. It is better to sort out the differences mutually and peacefully as far as possible, keeping the egos aside, and giving due recognition to the facts / truths. After all the truth is Dharma, as Lord Krishna had told us.  

  

My 2 cents
