Hare Krishna



varom porom (we come, we go)



Ø Yes that is the cycle of birth and death ( punarapi jananaM punarapi maraNaM punarapi Janani jatare shayanaM) – karma phala



edhukku varom? (why do we come?)



Ø Karma phala bhOga and with good saMskAra strive to get rid of this punarAvrutti chakra by realizing we are ‘something’ beyond this. And to get this saMskAra / vAsana we require IshwarAnugraha.
