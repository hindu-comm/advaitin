A physicist in the comments: "realism does not mean nonexistence of definite values of the properties before observation (that's measurement problem). A system possesses reality if the system has an underlying variable (called hidden variable) which can be be used to predict the values of the property without performing an observation. I will give an example, consider coin toss, you cannot predict the result of the toss in each run accurately. However, if you have complete information about the dynamics of the toss then you can predict the result in each run successfully. The dynamics of the toss is the hidden variable. Nonlocality tells us, under certain condition (independence of sources) there exists no local hidden variable theory consistent with quantum theory. The test was proposed by John Bell and tested by Aspect, Shimony, zellinger, clausher and many more. This was the first example in the history ofmankind where we could test validity of theory from the observational statistics itself without assuming any structures of the theory. Aspect called it "experimental metaphysics". Although, you can interpret it as similar to vedantic philosophy **but nonlocality goes way beyond that**. In fact, it turns out although, nature is nonlocal it is not maximally nonlocal. Why there is a restriction on the nonlocality or whats the need for nature to be nonlocal we still dont know."
