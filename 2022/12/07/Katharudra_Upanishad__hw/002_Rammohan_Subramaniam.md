Hari Om Jaishankar ji.  

Namaste.

  

A brilliant concluding statement of the Upanishad

  

स्वयं मृत्वा स्वयं भूत्वा स्वयमेवावशिष्यते ॥  

The first स्वयं refers to अहंकार or जीवभाव it has to die \[naturally without any resistance\] so the upanishad does not use the term हत्वा.

The Second स्वयं refers to आत्मन् the साक्षि चैतन्यं - which is what we become after intense meditation.

The third स्वयं refers to ब्रह्मन् – which is what is realized as what remains in the end. signifying जिवब्रह्मऐक्यम्.

  

Many thanks for sharing this wonderful section of the Upanishad.

  

ॐ and with Prem

In his Seva

Shri Rammohan
