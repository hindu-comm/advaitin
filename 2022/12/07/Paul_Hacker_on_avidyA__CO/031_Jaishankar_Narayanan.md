Jai: No. mithyA ajnAnam is required to cover the adhisthAnam and project the snake etc. False knowledge is perceiving the projection as reality. It is not the cause. False Ignorance is the cause, as bhasyAkAra points out in numerous places, which covers the reality of the locus (वस्तुस्वरूपावरणात्मिका हि साBr Up 1.4.16 BhAshya)and projects the 'not that' (नामरूपकर्मत्रयं यन्मिथ्याज्ञानविजृम्भितं Katha Up Bh 1.3.14).
