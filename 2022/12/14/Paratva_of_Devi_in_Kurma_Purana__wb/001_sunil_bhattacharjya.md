Dear Subbuji,

  

Lord Krishna also said in the Purana, that ***in the panchayatana worship,** **the Devi is worshipped for Mukti or Liberation.*** Lord Krishna, according to the Ttantra shastra, is the male form of Mother Kali, one of the forms of Devi herself. Mother Lalita is also another form of the Devi, who gives Mukti. ***That is why Gaudapadacharya asked Adi Shankara to write his 16th or the last bhashya, i.e., the Lalita Trishati Bhashya*** and he (Adi Shankara) himself spent his last days
the feet of Mother Lalita in Kanchipuram.  

  

My 2 cents

Sunil K B.
