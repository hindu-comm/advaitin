Hare Krishna



Wanted to write this long back when Sri Jaishankar prabhuji wrote his write-up to prove avidyA is bhAvarUpa, But due to some time constraints not able to focus on this entirely. As we all know, Sri SSS has already dealt with this subject in detail in his work sugama, mulAvidyAnirAsa, shankara hrudaya etc. and cited various reasons why we should not consider avidyA as explained by Sri Shankara in his PTB and AB as bhAvarUpa / material cause for adhyAsa / brahmAshrita etc. First I would like to quote Sri SSS’s take on avidyA as per shankara from his work :



// quote //



avidyA is an innate beginningless misconception due to a mental superimposition of the real and the un-real, the self and the non-self as well as their properties on each other. This avidyA is known throught intuition. This superimposition is the primus of all notions of the distinctions of pramaatru, pramaana and prameya (knower, means of knowledge and the object of knowledge). VidyA or true knowledge consists in discriminating and determining the true nature of the self and the not-self with the aid of the shAstra.



It is evident that those who proceed to prove avidyA by means of reason or pramaana (valid sources of knowledge) or the authority of the shAstra are resorting to an obviously erroneous procedure, since the very notion of knower is due to avidyA. Avidya common life ignorance, doubt or misconception of objects and vidyA (true knowledge) that dispels it, are both in the sphere of avidyA par excellence. Strictly speaking even the distinction of avidyA and vidyA relative to Atman is a hypothetical notion allowed by vedAntins as a concession to the popular mind, just a device for explaining the highest truth. The final position is that the Reality or Atman transcends both vidyA and avidyA



Failure to realize this truth has given rise to the impermissible demand for the cause of avidyA. The topsy-turvy process of offering perceptions, inference, presumption or the shruti as pramANa in evidence of avidyA, has been in vogue just because of this failure. Curious problems have cropped up regarding the locus of avidyA as well as the number of avidya-s. Does it reside in the individual soul or brahman?? Is there a single avidyA or are there as many avidyA-s as there are individual souls?? Would universal liberation ensue when the avidyA of a single jeeva is destroyed?? And so on.



//unquote//



It is quite evident from the above that Sri SSS mainly based his take on avidyA from shankara’s introduction to BS i.e. adhyAsa bhAshya and says that adhyAsa itself is the main concern and this adhyAsa is called as avidyA in all through the PTB most of the time. And he is also reiterated that there is no need to prove this avidyA through pramANa.
And elsewhere he observes that why later vyAkhyAnakAra-s have taken all the troubles to prove avidyA through pramANa based on the sUtra bhAshya statement : possibility and impossibility of a fact are to be concluded according as there is or is not some pramANa to prove it. ([su.bh](http://su.bh). 2-2-28). And Sri SSS observes in this effort of proving avidyA through pramANa later vyAkhyAnakAra-s ignored shankara’s appeal to universal intuition in the adhyAsa bhAshya and showing how the convention of the distinction of pramaNa-s and prameya-s itself presupposes avidyA or adhyAsa!! They (post shankara writers) have failed to appreciate the dictum of bhAshyakAra that all attempts to prove or disprove by means of transactional pramaNa-s draws its very breath from adhyAsa or avidyA and as such can neither prove avidyA nor disprove the existence of Atman without any specific features.
Therefore it has to be concluded that since avidyA is Anubhavajanya lOka prasiddha it need not be proved using pramANa-s like arthApatti, anupalabdhi etc. When you prove something as existing through valid pramANa it would become prama ( really existing thing) and that which is reality existing as a solid positive entity it cannot be removed / effaced by any amount of jnana.



Now avidyA as jnAnAbhAva is not different in each and every individual it is ekarUpa. Lack of knowledge is quite same in one and all. Though this jnAnAbhAva avidyA is the cause of misconception, the misconception (adhyAsa) is the main culprit here not just jnAnAbhAva. For example, there is no danger for an innocent man who is ignorant of a horse race at Race course. But if some desire like making instant money prompts him to approach that side, there is the danger of losing his money on slow moving horses 😊 “Is it not the ignorant man who is extrovert that is motivated to act outwardly?” The ignorance is not at all the motivator. It is only of the nature of concealing the svarUpa of things!! Of course, just like his innocence may result in falling into that trap, his innocence may indirectly be the motivator. If mere innocence is not the direct cause what exactly is the direct motivator for racing? The answer is given in the shAstra. That is kAma, eshaNa etc.
[br.bh](http://br.bh). 1.4.17 relevant here eshaNA kAmaH saha. Similarly, avidyA which is agrahaNa (jnAnAbhAva) is not the direct cause of danger, instead, its effect, adhyAsa, is the direct cause.
Due to this misconception one thinks that he is the body, then sees himself as poor, longing for money prompts him to do gambling. Accruing pApa-puNya, thinking himself as kartru, bhOktru engaging himself in
mundane life and move from one birth to another. That is why bhAshyakAra says avidyA is not responsible for differentiation in jeeva-s, because it is uniform ekarUpa in all the jeeva-s. It is only when coupled with desire, etc. leading to Karma, it would become responsible for differentiation na cha kevala avidyA vaishamyasya kAraNaM ekarUpatvAt (su.bh.2.1.36).



The above statement in bhAshya allow us to know the nature of avidyA (jnAnAbhAva) is ekarUpa, uniform in all jeeva-s, so it cannot be responsible for the differences in jeeva-s. And now the question is Why and how is it uniform? It is because, it is the absence of the knowledge (jnAnAbhAva) which cannot be different in different jeeva-s. lack of knowledge is one and the same for all. This abhAva is what the cause of adhyAsa. (cause here means not in the sense of cause and effect sequence to argue that there was a time only cause was there in the form of mUlAvidyA and subsequently from this adhyAsa is materialized hence for adhyAsa, mUlAvidyA is the upAdAna kAraNa etc.) The detailed discussion about this one can find in Sri SSS’s mUlAvidyA nirAsa and method of vedAnta and author proves how talking about time and sequence itself is within the sphere of adhyAsa and adhyArOpita on brahman. And it is not svAbhAvika dharma of the even cognizer. Geeta bhashya 13-2: pUrva paxa : in that case avidyA becomes the dharma ( an attribute) of the cognizer. siddhAnti : not so, for just as whether it is wrongly seeing etc. or its cause of cataract etc. after saMskAra is given it will go it will not be seen in the cognizer (cognizer would got rid of this dOsha)and hence it is not his dharma. Similarly avidyA like agrahaNa, anyathaagrahaNa, saMshaya also should invariably become the dharma (attribute) of a particular karaNa (upAdhi) and not the dharma of the kshetrajna. Here it is evident that there is no approval whatsoever of bhAshyakaara to float the theory like : adhyAsa or mUlAvidyA rested in Atman alone. But on the contrary the mUlAvidyAvAdins floating this theory by saying mUlAvidyA has the resting place in chinmAtra (absolute pure consciousness) and it has objectified that parama Chaitanya itself.



Getting very lengthy, next mail we will see why this avidyA which is the cause of adhyAsa is NOT bhAva rUpa and it is only abhAva of the absolute jnana.
