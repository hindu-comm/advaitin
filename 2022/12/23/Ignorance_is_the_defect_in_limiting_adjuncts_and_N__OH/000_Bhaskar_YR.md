Hare Krishna



In the 13^(th) chapter 26^(th) verse : yAvatsanjaayate kinchitsatvaM sthAvara jangamum, kshetrakshetrajna saMyOgAH tadviddhi bharatarshabha, bhAshyakAra observes : kshetra-kshtrajnayOh vishayiNOH bhinna svabhAvayOH etaretarataddharmAdhyAsalakshaNaH saMyOgaH kshetra kshetrajna svarUpa viveka abhAva nibandhanaH. Absence of discriminative knowledge (jnAnAbhAva) is the cause for not knowing the svarUpa of kshetra and kshetrajna hence saMyOga (mixing the satyAnruta). And where all these gymnastics taking place it is not in kshetrajna itself but it is activated in the instrument he is using clarifies bhashyakAra elsewhere (13^(th) chapter 2^(nd) verse) : atrAha evaM tarhi jnAtrudharmO avidyA…..na, vipareetaadi grahaNam tannimittaM vA taimiritkatvAdidOshO graheetuchakshushuH saMskAreNa timirepaneete ……..and he continues after clarifying cataract how it is not the problem of seer but defect with the instrument that is used for ‘seeing’…sarvatrivAgrahaNavipareetasaMshaya pratyayAH sanimittAH KARANASYAIVA kasyachitbhavitumarhati, NA JNAATUHU KSHETRAJNASYA.
(emphasis is mine to drive home the point according to bhAshyakAra avidyA is karaNa dOsha not the problem with jeevaatma or vijnAnAtma itself).



Yesterday in a separate group I was talking about this bhAshyabhAga and further shared my thoughts as below based on Sri SSS’s view points since there was a wrong impression that this bhAshya reference is a valid source to prove avidyA is bhAvarUpa :



// quote //



First of all it should be noted Sri SSS himself quotes this bhAshya vAkya to prove that avidyA is mere antaHkaraNa dOsha not clinging to kshtrajna / Atman and it is not there to prove adhyAsa which is termed with rAga -dvesha is the product of avidyA beeja shakti as propagated by MVV-s. In this very 13^(th) chapter bhAshya bhAshyakAra says the problem for kshtra & kshetrajna saMyOga is due to NOT KNOWING the real nature of kshetrajna (kshetrakshetrajna svarUpa viveka abhAvanibandhanaH 13-26). And avidyA as jnAnAbhAva is the cause for this adhyAsa has been said N’no. of times based on bhAshya reference. The most important point here to be noted is for this adhyAsa as per MVV there is another mUlAvidyA WHICH IS NOT JNAANAABHAAVA but an existing entity clinging to brahman itself. To refute this view only Sri SSS taken this bhAshya as reference to prove that avidyA is not the svAbhAvika dharma of brahman / kshetrajna but it is only antaHkaraNa dOsha which is svAbhAvika and lOka prasiddha in day to day transaction. I am really surprised to see MVV taking this bhAshya reference as valid when they vehemently arguing avidyA is not upAdhi dOsha but has the locus in brahman!! Ofcourse avidyA here as adhyAsa rUpa has been objectified because we have to transactionally accept that there is avidyA and we have to eradicate this avidyA through vidyA. If we don’t accept the avidyA then whole shAstra, jignAsa, sAdhana, upadesha all would become absolute unnecessary !! Yes there is adhyAsa for which avidyA (jnAnAbhAva) is the kAraNa. We don’t know our svarUpa hence we are taking ourselves as some thing else which we are not (atasmin tadbuddhiH due to ‘abhAva’ of svarUpa jnana). Is it really a rocket science to understand this simple fact in adhyAsa bhAshya!!?? What on the earth prompting us to erect one more mithyA ajnAna which is brahmAshrita to create subsequent mithyA pratyaya!!?? Sri SSS somewhere says in Kannada : rAga dveshAdigalige tattvavannu tappaagi tiLiyuvude kAraNavAdaru, aa tappu tiluvaLikeyu tanna svarUpa jnAnada abhAvadindale untaagiruttade, tappu tiluvalikeke mattondu tappu tiluvaLikeya shakti athavA avidyA beeja shakti khanditavAgiyu agatyavilla. (rough translation : pleasure and pain etc. due to adhyAsa (misconception) and for this misconception absence of real nature of Atman which is quite natural and it is absolutely unnecessary to postulate one more avidyA mUla shakti in seed form for the subsequent misconception). And now the question how can this jnana abhAva can be the cause for the adhyAsa which we are feeling and experiencing in our day to day transaction ?? This question is quite mute since bhAshyakAra himself clarified this adhyAsa is naisargika and lOka prasiddha, svAbhAvika and anAdirananta. (evamayamanAdiranantO naisargikOdhyAsO mithyApratyayarUpaH ref. vide AB). When this basic thing not understood it will be very difficult to discuss anything meaningful with MVV-s.
