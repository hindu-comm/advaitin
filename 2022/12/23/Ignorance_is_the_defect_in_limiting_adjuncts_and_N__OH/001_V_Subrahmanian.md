Has anyone said that Avidya is an inherent dharma of the Atman/Brahman? If SSS has such an impression then one ought to know which vakya of any author SSS has cited for such a claim?

  

// I am really surprised to see MVV taking this bhAshya reference as valid when they vehemently argue that avidyA is not upAdhi dOsha but has the locus in brahman!! //  

  

Which MVV has said that avidya is not upadhi dosha but is located in Brahman?

  

It is pertinent to note that Shankara in the Brihadaranyaka 1.4.10 Aham Brahmasmi Bhashya says:

  

भवत्वेवं नाविद्याकर्तृ भ्रान्तं च ब्रह्म। *किन्तु नैव अब्रह्म अविद्यकर्ता चेतनो भ्रान्तोऽन्य इष्यते* —[‘नान्योऽतोऽस्ति विज्ञाता’ (बृ. उ. ३। ७। २३)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=3&id=BR_C03_S07_V23&hl=%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE)[‘नान्यदतोऽस्ति विज्ञातृ’ (बृ. उ. ३। ८। ११)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=3&id=BR_C03_S08_V11&hl=%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%83)[‘तत्त्वमसि’ (छा. उ. ६। ८। ७)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Chandogya?page=6&id=Ch_C06_S08_V07&hl=%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%BF)  

  

Translation:

  

Let it be that Brahman is not the author of ignorance nor subject to error. But it is not admitted that there is any other conscious entity but Brahman which is the author of ignorance or subject to error. Witness such shruti texts as: 'There is no other knower but him (Antaryami) in the body' (Br.Up.3.7.23.

  

This is the clinching evidence for Shankara admitting avidya for Brahman. This is titled as 'Brahmaashritaa avidya iti siddhaanta pakshah' by the editor.

  

So, any amount of mudslinging on the MVV that 'they attribute avidya to Brahman' is patently against the Bhashya that is cited above. We have to bear in mind this: Avidya deludes the Consciousness, that Shankara calls 'Chetana' (Brahman) by creating the body mind complex. The avidya, by default, will be in the antahkaranam only and not in the Atman. No Advaitin will ever say that Avidya is 'in' located, aashrita, in Atman, the Chaitanyam. This is because Chaitanyam is asanga and cannot 'support' avidya in it. So, by default, avidya is in the antahkarana alone, as a dosha, like raga, duhkha and devesha, 

  

So, there is no justification for SSS to fault the MVV people. In fact the MVV people has explicitly said that mulavidya will be eradicated, sublated, by Brahmavidya. I have cited this passage from Vedanta Paribhasha last week. So, if SSS thinks Mulavidya will/can never be eradicated by Brahmavidya then he has not understood the prakriya of the MVV people.
