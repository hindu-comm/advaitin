Hare Krishna



That is the reason why avidyA said to be abhAva rUpa. Not that avidyA itself abhAva, avidyA/adhyAsa is because of jnAnAbhAva. Lalita sahasranAma says devi is vidyAvidyA svarUpiNi shruti says brahman is vraNa (wound) to convey the omnipresence (all pervading) nature of tattva. It is in this scope only jagat also said to be originated from brahman, stays in brahman and goes back again into brahman. Since avidyA is abhAva it is not a vastu/thing to say it is also made up of brahman and avidyA also has the adhishtAnaM (substratum) like jagat.



But MVV-s say in brahman itself there is some positive entity called anirvachaneeya avidyA it is the root cause for nAma rUpAtmaka jagat and it has the power to even conceal the svarUpa of brahman. It is just like saying brahman getting the avidyA and became jeeva rather than saying Jeevan getting rid of avidyA and realized that he is brahman.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
