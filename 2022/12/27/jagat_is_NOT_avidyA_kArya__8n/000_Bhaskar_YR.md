Hare Krishna



We often see avidyA kalpita jagat, avidyAkruta nAma rUpa etc. It gives us the impression that this jagat is avidyA kArya, a product of avidyA!! Na!! that is not the case!! When we accept everything is just superimposed (adhyArOpita) on brahman no need to assume process of kAraNa-kArya etc. If this jagat what we are having/experiencing is the product of jada avidyA then immediate question that needs to be addressed is which Chaitanya using this avidyA to create jagat ?? Is it brahman’s avidyA?? Or is it jeeva’s avidyA?? Brahman’s avidyA is out of question here since being the jeeva we have to start our journey from jeeva level and we should stop talking on behalf of brahman and have to stop assuming and attributing avidyA in brahman 😊 It has been reiterating that when it comes to jagat, jagat creation and jagat kAraNatva it has to be invariably directed to parabrahman only nothing else. AND It is said in no ambiguous terms that parabrahman is the jagat kartru and IT IS THE VEDANTA MARYAADA. In the very second br.sU. janmAdhyadhikaraNa bhAshyakAra clarifies this. Despite this we the pAmara-s dare enough to say this jagat is kevala avidyA kalpita / bhrAnti, manOvikAra etc. when jnana itself does not have the power of creating anything new or destroy anything existing, how can avidyA has the capacity to create jagat!!?? Anyway enough said on this.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
