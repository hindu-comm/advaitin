Namaskaram,

  

In practical terms, if I think of Shiva&Shaktias manifesting the mind-world and sense-world I experience, it is easier for me to disentangle from duality and merge in awareness of a "non-dual" underlying Consciousness. This preliminary step based on personalizing Brahman (or appealing to our dvaitic personalization of Ishvara) is highly useful for the general sadhaka caught in the gimmicks of samsara and seeking freedom from their hold. Anycase, sampradaya says bhakti to Ishta devata is important and right method for stabilizing in chitta ekagratha.
