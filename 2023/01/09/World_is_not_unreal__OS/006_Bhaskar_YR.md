Hare Krishna



IMHO, as per some traditional understanding advaitic concept of NS is nothing but patanjali’s asaMprajnAta samAdhi, it is an individual experience in a particular state of mind or no mind state and there is ‘time count’ (kAla gaNane) for this experience. prakaraNa grantha-s like viveka chUdAmaNi, jeevanmukti viveka, panchadashi etc. talk a lot about absolute necessary of this time bound experience to get the advaita’s paramArtha jnana i.e. AtmaikatvaM. If that is not the case then NS is sahaja sthiti of the paramArtha jnAni without any restrictions of desha kAla but I don’t think NS is meant in that way in some of the popular works of Advaita.
