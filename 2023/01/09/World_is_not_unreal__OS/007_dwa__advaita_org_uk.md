Hi Bhaskar-ji,



As I noted, I have an entire section on this topic in ‘Confusions 1’ and cannot reasonably repeat all the arguments here. It is post-Shankara (especially from Ramakrishna onwards) that the idea that NS is ‘necessary’ became prevalent. Shankara himself does not even mention NS in any Upanishad bhAShya and only really in connection with Yoga elsewhere. In BSB 2.1.9, he says: “As in natural slumber and samādhi (absorption in divine consciousness), though there is a natural eradication of differences, still owing to the persistence of the unreal nescience, differences occur over again when one wakes up.” This probably triggered Swami Paramarthananda’s comment that the only difference between samAdhi and deep-sleep was that the former was sitting up and the latter lying down.
