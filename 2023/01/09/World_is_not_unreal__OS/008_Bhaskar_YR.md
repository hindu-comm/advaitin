- I don’t want to trouble you on those points either since Sri SSS and
  some ardent followers of Sri Arsha VidyA too are of the same opinion.
  But fact remains that even today there is some strong belief that NS
  induced and aided by Vedanta SMN is ideal to have the literal
  experience of Atma ekatvaM which is practically not possible in other
  states like waking and dreaming.



It is post-Shankara (especially from Ramakrishna onwards) that the idea that NS is ‘necessary’ became prevalent.



- As you know some later vyAkhyAnakAra-s from the school of vivaraNa and
  bhAmati too insisted on the mandatory experience of NS. Viveka
  chUdAmaNi, a prakaraNa in the name of Sri Shankara endorses this
  experience categorically. And other Advaita works like JMV and PD
  etc. giving boost to these claims.



Shankara himself does not even mention NS in any Upanishad bhAShya and only really in connection with Yoga elsewhere.



- However, samAdhi experience based on vedAnta vAkya shravaNa, manana
  and nidhidhyAsana is a perfect aid and acceptable in Advaita prakriya,
  so is the opinion of some. According to them, yOga samAdhi with
  vedAnta jnana is OK, perfect and good to have that experience in this
  way but yOga samAdhi achieved through ashtanga yOga without any
  shAstra jnana is not in line with advaita’s jnana sAdhana. Have you
  discussed the yOga experience from this view point in your work??
  kindly let me know.



In BSB 2.1.9, he says: “As in natural slumber and samādhi (absorption in divine consciousness), though there is a natural eradication of differences, still owing to the persistence of the unreal nescience, differences occur over again when one wakes up.” This probably triggered Swami Paramarthananda’s comment that the only difference between samAdhi and deep-sleep was that the former was sitting up and the latter lying down.
