Namaste.

I think a distinction needs to be made between lapsing into NS by one who has already attained Realization on the one hand, and the state one attains on Realization on the other. 

While the former is at the discretion of the jnAni and is not essential, the latter is automatic. On Realization, whether it is termed NS or not, the state of the sAdhaka is one of NS only at Realization.
