I hope this thread is not getting wings once again to fly high 😊 As it has been clarified umpteen times problem is not with the mere word ‘samAdhi’ since this word has been used by bhAshyakAra at somany places. Depending on context the word would give us the meanings like : samAdhAna, AtmasvarUpa, viveka prajna, AtmAnubhavanishte etc. Here what is to be noticed is; the word samAdhi is NOT used by shankara in the light of pAtanjali’s ashtanga yOga – samAdhi and there is no explicit mention of the word nirvikalpa samAdhi or asaMprajnAtha samAdhi in the light of dvaita yOga shAstra. That is the reason why Sri SSS in his foot note clarified this is not yOga-samAdhi but jnana samAdhi which is enshrined in vedAnta shAstra. In the pAribhAshika shabda kOsha Sri SSS lists the occurrences of the word ‘samAdhi’ in PTB at various contexts.
I think what Sri Dennis prabhuji pointing out is the word NS is not used by bhAshyakAra in connection with dvaita yOga shAstra.
