Concerning Upanishads, please refer MandUkya Up, Advaita PrakaraNa, Verse 37, Bhashya

\<\< समाधिःसमाधिनिमित्तप्रज्ञावगम्यत्वात्; समाधीयते अस्मिन्निति वासमाधिः। \>\>

\<\< samAdhiH samAdhinimittapraj~nAvagamyatvAt ; samAdhIyate asminniti vA samAdhiH \| \>\>

Translation (Swami Gambhirananda) \<\< samAdhih, divine absorption, being realizable through the insight arising out of the deepest Concentration (samAdhi). Or It is called samAdhi, because It is the object of concentration \>\>.

Sri SSS, while translating to kannada conveying practically the same idea, has this to say in a Foot Note

\<\< Mind coming to rest (samAdhAna) leads to AtmajnAna is the meaning of the first alternative. Unless it rests in the Atman, mind will not come to rest is the meaning of the second alternative. Cross refer BGB 2-53 \>\>.

Note Sri SSS gives cross reference to BGB 2-53 which is exactly what I had cited in my earlier post. So there is reference to NS in the Bhashya on Upanishads as well. The Samadhi referred to is NS only, as is clear from the BGB 2-53 portion copied here for ready reference.

\<\< अचलातत्रापिविकल्पवर्जिताइत्येतत्। \>\> ( \<\< achalA tatrApi vikalpavarjitA ityetat \| ).
