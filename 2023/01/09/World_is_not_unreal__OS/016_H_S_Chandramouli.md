Reg \<\< Shankara may not be very forthcoming on the topic of samAdhi but one thing he is very clear about is that mokSha comes only from j~nAna. And samAdhi is not a pramANa… \>\>,

Realization. I don’t know of any commentator who has made such a statement as mentioned by you. You may please give a couple of references at least if it is not too troublesome.

The issue is about your contention that

\<\<Shankara himself does not even mention NS in any Upanishad bhAShya and only really in connection with Yoga elsewhere\>\>.

In my understanding, that is contrary to the Bhashya, for which I have given references.
