Dear Chandramouli-ji,



You are correct in your understanding of what I said and so your response is in order.



However, without spending lots of time on this (I said I was not going to be drawn into discussion), I do not believe that the use of the word in BGB 2.53 means ‘samAdhi’ in the way that we are talking about it. Swami Dayananda says:



“Shankara defines samAdhi in his commentary on this verse as ‘samya AdhIyate chittam asmin iti samAdhiH, that into which everything resolves, AtmA. He thus makes it very clear that the word samAdhi means AtmA in this context.”
