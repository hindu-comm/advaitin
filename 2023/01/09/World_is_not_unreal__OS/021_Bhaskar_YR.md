I do not believe that the use of the word in BGB 2.53 means ‘samAdhi’ in the way that we are talking about it. Swami Dayananda says:



“Shankara defines samAdhi in his commentary on this verse as ‘samya AdhIyate chittam asmin iti samAdhiH, that into which everything resolves, AtmA. He thus makes it very clear that the word samAdhi means AtmA in this context.”



praNAms Sri Dennis Waite prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Likewise in kArika bhAshya (3-37) the use of the samAdhi in sentence : samAdhiH samAdhi nimitta prajnAvagamyatvAt, samAdheeyate asmin iti vA samAdhiH does not ring bell in favour of pAtanjali’s asaMprajnAta samAdhi. The samAdhi here is viveka prajna in which everything resolves IOW the pure consciousness in which everything resolves called samAdhi.
And in geeta 2-54 samAdhisthasya interpreted as samAdhau sthitasya ( the characteristic of sthita prajna) and this clarification does not even remotely matching with patanjalis asaMprajnAta samAdhi experience. Why we have to be meticulous when interpreting these terms is, before venturing to explaining these terms in bhAshya in favor of patanjali’s yOga shAstra and antima phala, we have to keep in mind Advaita jnana which bhAshyakAra insisting is not vyavahAra abhAva, lOkAntara, avasthAteeta jnana. OTOH, it is vyavahAra bAdhita jnana. The paramArtha jnana thou art that (tattvamasi) is not aligned within the frame of some avasta (state like nirvikalpa samAdhi in a mind inert state) and within certain time period. In sUtra bhAshya (2-1-14) bhagavatpAda without mincing any words categorically declares : na cha ayaM vyavahArAbhAvOvasthA vishesha nibaddhO abhidhiyate iti yuktaM vaktuM, “tattvamasi iti brahmAtmabhAvasya avasthA vishesha nibandhanatvAt”. It is because of this reason shankara very cautiously warns us in vedAnta sAnkhya is vaidika jnana and yOga is vaidika dhyAna and don’t get confused with classical system of sAnkhya and yOga.
Despite these clarification from bhAshyakAra himself even today we are seeing the efforts to push these systems into pure vedAntic method of jnana mArga.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
