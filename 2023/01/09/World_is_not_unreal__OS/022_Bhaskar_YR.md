Hare Krishna



The Upanishadic Asamprajna samadhi, as taught by Lord Ram to Shri Hanuman, or taught by Lord Buddha to his Advayavada disciples or mentioned by the sage Patanjali in his Yoga sutra is not any different from say the Upanishadic Nididhyasana or the Nirvikalpa samadhi. It is where and when the Jeeva gets the enightement and finally leaves the cycle of birth and death.



Ø With all due respects to great Patanjali and his school of thought, I would like to clarify that this classical yOga system and its ultimate result like asaMprajnAta samAdhi is not recommended and required for realizing Advaita paramArtha jnana though to discipline the wandering mind some initial steps of ashtanga yOga prescribed (like yama, niyama, Asana, pranayama, pratyahara). But practices like dhAraNa & dhyAna need to be done in vedAntic way that is what bhAshyakAra suggests to his followers. It is because of this reason only in PTB there is no mention of yOga-s shatchakra ( mUlAdhAra to sahasrAra), kundalini shakti (which travels through mUlAdhAra to sahasrAra), some hata yOgic exercises, tAntrika rituals, occult powers, siddhi-s etc.
What bhAshyakAra recommends for jnana prApti is purely vedAntic and not yOgic method. yOga is kartru taNtra / purusha taNtra whereas jnana is vastu tantra. There is difference between the yOgic dhyAna and samAdhi and vedic jnana and mOksha. And having said all these, I must confess here that I am neither yOgi nor jnAni to talk anything authoritatively on these practices and results, just sharing my thoughts purely based on academic studies and understanding. My humble prostrations to all mahAn yOgi-s in yOga system and all Atmaikatva jnAni-s in our Advaita paraMpara.
