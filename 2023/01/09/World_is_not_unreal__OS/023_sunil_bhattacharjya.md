Dear Bhaskarji,  

  

Did Patanjali demean Jnana anywhere? Nowhere. not at all.  

  

Nobody does Yoga for fun. The Yogis are serious people. Kindly don't belittle them. The Indian yogis do yoga for Mukti. I am sure you are aware of the four types of Mukti, which Lord Ram told shri Hanuman. Patanjali did not tell one to do saviklpa samadhi yoga alone. He had also told that if the yogi wants he or she can take help of Ishvara. Patanjali indicates that one can do Asamprajna or Nirvikalpa samadhi-yoga too.

  

My 2 cents

Sunil KB
