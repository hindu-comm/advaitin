Patanjali did not tell one to do saviklpa samadhi yoga alone. He had also told that if the yogi wants he or she can take help of Ishvara. Patanjali indicates that one can do Asamprajna or Nirvikalpa samadhi-yoga too.



Ø Tadeva arthamAtra nirbhAsaM svarUpa shUnyamiva samAdhiH says yOga sUtra. This is achieved through uninterrupted dhyAna on some particular object after desha bandhaH chittasya dhAraNaM ( concentrating on either shat chakra-s or any external objects like star / lotus etc.) This practice is purely based on purusha taNtra and result is obviously is under the control of sAdhaka, getting the freedom from prakruti and merging one with purusha. To get this saMyama ( nirbeeja asamprajnAtha samAdhi) the yOga sAdhaka has to do this purusha taNtra / kartru taNtra sAdhana like dhAraNa, dhyAna and samAdhi which kartum, akartum or anyatAkartum shakyaM clarifies bhAshyakAra. Whereas Advaita jnana sAdhana is in realizing the bhUta vastu as_it_is, vastu taNtra jnana, realizing the svarUpa as it is through shravaNAdi shrutyukta sAdhana.
