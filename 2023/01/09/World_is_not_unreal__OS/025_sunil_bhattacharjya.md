Dear Bhaskarji,

  

For your kind information, Advaita Mukti is also included as the last of the four types of Mukti, as taught by Lord Ram himself. Lord Ram guided Shri Hanumanji towards that.  

  

Lord Krishna told Arjuna that he was yoga-yukta, when he was telling the Bhagavad Gita to Arjuna. The manifested and the unmanifested world are nothing but the consciousness of the Lord. Lord included both the Agama and Nigama teachings in the Original Bhagavad Gita.  

  

Krishnam Vande Jagadgurum
