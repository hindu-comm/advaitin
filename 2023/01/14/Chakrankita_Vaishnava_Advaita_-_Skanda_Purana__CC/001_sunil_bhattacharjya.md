Dear Subbuji,

  

Lord Shiva or Lord Vishnu or his Purna-Avatara Lord Krishna or Mother Durga or Mother Lalita or Mother Kali are all no different from the Brahman. People like Raghavaendra Tirtha were thick-witted and that is why they could not assimilate the concept of Unity in Lord Vishnu and Lord Shiva. The saying: "Shivasya hrdayam Vishnu, Vishnoccha hrdayama Shiva", was beyond Raghavendra Tirtha's comprehension.  

  

Regards,
