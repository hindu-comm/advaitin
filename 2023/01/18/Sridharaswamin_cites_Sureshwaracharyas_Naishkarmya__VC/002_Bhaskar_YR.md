- And perhaps, this is the reason why Sri SSS insists that Sri
  Sureshwara as vArtikakAra is the only true follower of Sri
  bhAshyakAra. Unlike bhAmati and vivaraNakAra who are the proponents
  of brahmAshrita avidyA (mUlAvidyA which has the locus in brahman
  itself) Sri Sureshwara says both avidyA (kAraNa) and adhyAsa (kArya)
  were never ever there because of the simple fact that Atman is
  eternally jnana svarUpa any taints (mala like avidyA) in it is not
  admissible anytime. Not knowing this (jnAnAbhAva) is the kAraNa for
  the kArya (adhyAsa) i..e ahamkara (or jeeva bhAva/parichinnatva) and
  mamakAra.
