Dear Suresh ji,

  

Yes, the mind, upadhi, is essential to reveal the Upanishadic truth:
मनसैवेदमाप्तव्यम्... the mind is the only instrument through which the truth can be realized. The manas is neither real nor unreal (like hare's horn), so it is anirvachaniya. It has a purpose. When the purpose is over, it too ceases to be.
