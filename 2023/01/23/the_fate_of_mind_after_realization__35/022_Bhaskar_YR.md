Hare Krishna



svarUpa shUnya ultimate state as samAdhi is bhAmati stand, as per Sri SSS’s observation with regard to this school they say : atra nidhidhyAsitavyaH eti dhyAnOpadeshaH drashtavyaH eti samAdheH tathAhuH tadeva dhyAnaM arthamAtra nirbhAsaM ‘svarUpa shUnyamiva’ samAdhiH eti.
(bhAmati vyAkhyAna on br.man. bhAshya). No need to highlight here vyAkhyAnakAra directly imports here yOga sUtra of Patanjali to interpret vedAntic dhyAna and nidhidhyAsana.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
