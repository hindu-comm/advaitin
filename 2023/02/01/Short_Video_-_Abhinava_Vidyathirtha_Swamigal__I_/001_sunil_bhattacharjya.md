Dear friends,

  

It is nice to know more about the great shankaracharyas of the past .

  

*His Holiness Sachchidananda Sivabhinava Narasimha Bharati Mahaswamiji, considered to be a reincarnation  *

*of Adi Sankara, was the 33rd Jagadguru in the Dakshinamnaya Sharada Peetham. **He** ******was probably one of  ***

***the greatest Shankaracharyas, after Adi Shankaracharya.** He adorned the Vyakhyana Simhasana, “the  *

*Throne Of Transcendental Wisdom” **from 1879 to 1912.*** ***Within this crucial period,*** ***in 1884 AD to be precise**, the  *

*British scholars came out with the fake “Aryan Invasion Theory, which led to distortion of ancient Indian history.  *

*The Indian scholars have not yet been able to undo this mischief until now.  *

*  *

***He** **clarified,** **from** **the Matha record, that Adi Shankara was born during the reign of Vikramaditya** **  ***

***(****who wrotethe “Khandana khanda Khadya”****).** He was responsible for starting the celebration of Shankara  *

*Jayanthi festival in India. Because of his efforts the collected works of Adi Sankara were published under the  *

*title,Shankara Granthavali. His efforts led to the setting up of Pathasalas (Schools) for Vedas and Sastras in   *

*places like Bangalore.*

*Regards,*

*Sunil K. Bhattacharjya  *
