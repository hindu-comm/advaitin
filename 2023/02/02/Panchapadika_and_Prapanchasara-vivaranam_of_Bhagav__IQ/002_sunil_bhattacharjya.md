Adi Shankara did not write bhashya on the Bhagavad Gita. It was Abhinava shankara of the Kanch Kamakoti math, who wrote the Bhagavad Gita Bhashya, for contesting the Bhaskara's (of 8th century AD) book of the Bhagavad Gita. After Abhinava Shankara contested Bhashkara's work, the second half of Bhaskara's book (containing the chapter 10 to chapter 18), vanished or rather was withdrawn unceremoniously*.*
