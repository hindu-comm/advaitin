**यं शैवाः समुपासते शिव इति ब्रह्मेति वेदान्तिनो**

**बौद्धा बुद्ध इति प्रमाणपटवः कर्तेति नैयायिकाः।**

**अर्हन्नित्यथ जैनशासनरताः कर्मेति मीमांसकाः**

**सोऽयं नो विदधातु वाञ्छितफलं त्रैलोक्यनाथो हरिः॥**

**  **

May the lord of the three worlds, Shri Hari - who is worshipped as Shiva by the Shaiva, as the Brahman by the Vedantin, as the Buddha by the Buddhists as the Karta by the Naiyaayika, as the Arhat by the Jains, and as Karma by the Mimamsaka - bestow us with the fruits we desire.
