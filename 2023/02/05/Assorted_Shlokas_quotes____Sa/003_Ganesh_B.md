**अनेकशास्त्रं बहुवेदितव्यम्**

**अल्पश्च कालो बहवश्च विघ्ना:।**

**यत्सारभूतं तदुपासितव्यं**

**हंसो यथा क्षीरमिवाम्भुमध्यात्॥**

  

There are so many scriptures, so much to learn but time is very limited and there are so many obstacles as well! It is thus prudent to pay attention towards just the *'essence' - (the most important)*as a swan which drinks only milk even when it is mixed with water.  

  

~ Subhashita
