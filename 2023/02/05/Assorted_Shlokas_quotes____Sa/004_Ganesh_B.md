**बहूनां कलहो नित्यं द्वयोः संकथनं ध्रुवम्।**

**एकाकी विचरिष्यानि कुमारीशङ्खको यथा॥**

  

When many people get together, friction is inevitable. Even when just two people get together, there is conversation between them. Therefore, like the lone bangle on the maiden’s hand, solitude is the best recourse.  

  

Mahabharata \| Shanti Parva \| 176.68
