A wonderful incident connected to the essence of the previous Subhashita.  
  
Once, Sri. Krishnaswamy Iyer entered poojya Gurudev's room when He was alone. Swamiji signaled him to sit down and lapsed into silence. He was silent for more than twenty minutes. The atmosphere in the room was very peaceful. Iyer was enjoying the tranquility and the divine presence of poojya Gurudev. After a short while a gentle smile appeared on Gurudev's lips. Addressing Iyer He asked, "shall I tell you?" Iyer replied - "I would consider blessed if it can be shared."  
  
Poojya Gurudev said, "my mind was calm when you came in. After you came in, a thought shook my mind. It occurred to me that you were sitting nearby and expected me to speak. If I had to satisfy you, I had to speak. The chain of thoughts continued. To speak, there has to be a subject. If there was no subject, there would be nothing to talk about. I felt that all objects and subjects in this universe this universe could be classified into two broad categories. They are the permanent (satya) and the temporary (mithya). Only Brahman is permanent and all else is temporary. We can talk about one of these. Vedanta declares that Brahman is beyond the reach of the mind and speech. Thus it is not possible to speak about Brahman. What remains is the world, But, Vedanta has declared that the world is also beyond words - cannot be fully described by words. Thus it is not possible to speak about the world either. There is no third topic at all, to talk about. It is not possible to speak without a subject. When I reached this conclusion, I realized I had reached where I started from, and felt it was unnecessary to allow the mind to wander in such a manner. The train of thoughts I had, was similar toa *vehicle which leaves the main road and takes the beaten track to avoid the tax official, only to arrive at the check post*.You saw me smiling at this unproductive flood of thoughts."  
  
Poojya Gurudev again became silent. (Krishnaswamy) Iyer continued to sit in the presence of HH for a while. He thought "It should have oceurred to me that, poojya Gurudev was not speaking to me inspite of my being present here. This thought must have disturbed poojya Gurudev's Contemplation." He silently stood up, prostrated before HH and quietly left the room.
