Sri Ramana Maharahi also used to often narrate the same anecdote "*Checkpost*" (from previous post). He had this pithy adage -  
  
Bhagavan had this pithy adage -  
**  
சுங்கச்சாவடி தப்பாது. The toll both cannot be escaped**.  
  
Bhagavan used to narrate the following story:  
  
It was customary for tradespeople to transport their wares on bullock carts from one city to another, in the southern states of India. When crossing the state borders on the highway, they were required to pay taxes for their goods.  
  
To avoid this, sometimes they would take a long winding route, bypassing the toll booths. But if they slept off for even a brief stretch of time (as they invariably would, since most of this travel happened at night) they would wake up to find the bullock dutifully standing at the toll booth - சுங்கச்சாவடி தப்பாது - The toll both cannot be escaped. ! Since this was the default route, the bullock would have veered the cart back to the original course!  
  
Like how the toll booth was inevitable for a tradesman, no matter how much he tried to avoid it, the path of Self Enquiry / Atma vichara is inescapable for the spiritual aspirant.
