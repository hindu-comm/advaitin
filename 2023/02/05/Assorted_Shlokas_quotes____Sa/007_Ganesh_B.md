The Swamiji (Sri Chandrashekhara Bharati Sw) honestly believed and declared quite often that vedanta that was not practised in real life and, Sastradhyayana which was not translated into reality, were practically useless. It is much better, he affirmed, to observe Vaidika Karmanushtana than indulge in sushka Tarka. The former would ensure Chitta Shuddhi while the latter would provoke Raga and Dwesha.
