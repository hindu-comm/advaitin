Sri Natraj Maneshinde's post

=============================

The form of Sharadamba is dear to all Advaitians. She is worshipped with the deepest reverence in Shringeri Sharada peetham by the presiding Acharyas. We firmly believe that Bhagavati Sharada was established in Shringeri by Bhagavatpada Adi Shankaracharya himself and this form of Amba was quite dear to him. But what textual evidence do we have to substantiate this popular belief? This doubt is rendered insignificant by Bhagavatpada himself in the very first verse of Prapanchasara tantra where he invokes Bhagavati Sharadamba as the Jagat-karana-brahman. To our great fortune, this Mangalacharana shloka has a commentary by Bhagavan Padmapadacharya ji, the direct disciple of Bhagavatpada. In the tika, Padmapadacharya ji refutes the views of other darshanas and establishes that Bhagavati Sharadamba invoked here is verily the Brahman as understood in Advaita Vedanta.

Given that our Acharyas beheld Sharadamba as the Shuddha Chaitanya which is the adhishthana of Jagat, it is no wonder that we, their followers, are so deeply inclined towards worshiping this particular swaroopa of Amba!
