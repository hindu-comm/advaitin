NOTES FROM A TALK BY SWAMI PRAKASHANANDENDRAJI

If you simply answer, 'sruti', it's a matter of faith. Alternatively, if you say, I know nonduality because it's my guru's jnana, it's still faith. Who are we to believe, your teacher or mine? And how are we to know the teacher's own private experience?

The real evidence is sushupti - the experience of deep sleep/DS. It is svastha siddhi sushupti - Self-evident, universal, unnegatable experience of nonduality. BUT, this is not the sushupti you imagine it to be.

Sushupti can be understood from 2 viewpoints. Sankara himself comments from both positions. First, DS from the waking viewpoint: deep sleep is the avyakta/absence of waking - the notion we go to and return from sleep. This is adhyasa/superimposition.

Then there's sushupti, per se, as Experience, per se, that is beyond adhyasa. DS as Experience independent of the name and notion of DS from waking language and adhyasa. The very thought/the word, 'sushupti,' is adhyasa. This is a viewpoint not commonly recognized in Sankara Advaita.

This is understanding sushupti as free of even the idea of three states, waking, dream and DS, as free of all waking thoughts. Sruti and bhasya point out that sushupti stands un-negatable as Yourself alone. We don't wake from sushupti but we are in sushupti even with waking appearing. Appearance does not prove existence. Consider the sun travelling from horizon to horizon or the nightmare that seemed real at the time - mere appearance not reality

Consciousness does not change. Change appears as waking but like dream it is only name and form imagined IN Consciousness. Formless is there in spite of form appearing but form cannot replace formless. Dream appears IN Consciousness, waking also appears IN Consciousness. DS as experience (not a state) is direct experience that cannot be replaced. You, the Self/sAkshi are present whether the world is experienced or whether the world is not experienced - undeniably presence. The pure experience of sushupti cannot be lost by experiencing waking. Waking depends on our consciousness as objects depend upon space. As states, each state replaces the other state but as experience, You are always present. Not the waking you, it does not exist in sushupti, but the You that is pure existence.

If you try to prove advaita from waking viewpoint alone, you have no standpoint. Waking state is falsified by merging in deep sleep. Since you exist in DS and waking does not (anvaya/vyatireka) that proves YOU exist without adhyasa and that avidya does not actually exist thus waking is mithya.
