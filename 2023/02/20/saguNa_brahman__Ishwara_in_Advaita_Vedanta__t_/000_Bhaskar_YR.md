Hare Krishna



After reading plenty of stuff with regard to parabrahman / tureeya brahman / trimurthi-s etc. I am really puzzled to find out what exactly the status of Ishwara in Advaita vedAnta. Yes for the maNda and madhyama adhikAri-s there is Ishwara, lord, karma phala dAta, karmAdhyaksha and through upAsana mArga he can be reached and can be there with him but with limited powers (without getting the power of creation like Ishwara) till the mahApraLaya and after pralaya Ishwara would grant this upAsaka paramArtha jnana etc. All these are acceptable based on shruti/smruti/purANa and through shankara bhAshya also. But what is the siddhAnta drushti after athAto brahma jignAsa immediate sUtra is about brahma who is kAraNa for the jagat which is nothing but Ishwara or the brahman associated with mAya !!?? Is this brahma (inferior/kArya/sOpAdhika) that is going to be taught by sUtra which is in the sphere of avidyA kshetra!!?? And whose cognition / realization can give only secondary mukti and elevate us to some other celestial abode and allow us to live with some supernatural hero?? After reading all these in advaita literature which is supposed to be exclusively teaching the upanishat parama siddhAnta of nirguNa/nirAkAra/nirvishesha brahman appears giving undue importance to some existing dominating Chaitanya-s and its super vishesha upAdhi-s (like tureeya brahma), some originated vishesha upAdhi-s/chaitanya-s in the form of trimurthis and still secondary Chaitanya-s with upAdhi-s obtained through puNya phala and yet other tiny Chaitanya-s with karma janita upAdhi-s. So, in short Advaita is not about only jeevAtma ikyata something more than that and it is there with some definite purpose to accommodate the different adhikAri-s.



I read somewhere Advaita Acharya Sri appayya deekshitar in his siddhAnta lesha saMgraha talks about multiple jeeva-s and their realization in the sense of krama mukti i..e brahman is with qualities is the jagat kAraNa, the realized souls (jeeva-s) attain union with this saguNa brahman or Ishwara ONLY until all the jeeva-s ultimately released. And in shruti-s / bhAshya-s also we see the talk about para / apara, kAraNa / kArya ,
sOpAdhika / nirupAdhika, superior / inferior brahman!! So the concept of Ishwara in advaita is really puzzling especially when we talk about parabrahman / tureeya brahman / trimurtyAtmaka brahman / Ishwara in devata / Ishwara in jeeva, hierarchical gradations of the SAME and ONE and ONLY Chaitanya based on adjuncts etc. and it is very difficult to decide what exactly the role of Ishwara in this eka Chaitanya and avidyOpAdi vAda in advaita.



Are we not allowed to make it simple instead of complicating these upAdhi based Chaitanya-s?? There is one and only brahman and there is absolutely nothing ‘apart’ from it this is parama siddhAnta of shAstra
but somehow (anAdiranantO naisargikOdhyAsaH) when we think and perceive the world as real and independent entity and when we think ourselves as tiny jeeva prisoned in BMI the same akhanda Chaitanya is spoken as sarvajna, sarvashakta he is srushti / stithi/laya karta etc. And Ishwara is definitely the ruler of this jeeva and jagat and he is the bestower of fruits of karma (karma phala data) and the lord of karma (karmAdhyaksha). But when we realized we are not the tiny Chaitanya we are not mere pot space but mahadAkAsha and this ‘AkAsha’ is what is really existing in all these charAchara jagat where is the role of Ishwara fits in!!?? It is in the scenario of duality ONLY Ishwara comes into picture and in that kshetra the ONE and ONLY transcendental brahman appears as the cause of the universe and known as sOpAdhika / kArya / conditioned brahman or brahman with attributes or lord, upAsya devata, ishta devata, kula devata/ ArAdhya devata etc. etc. And as per Acharya this upAsya devata should be in accordance with shAstra.



But what exactly the position of this Ishwara?? bhAshyakAra answers this in sUtra bhAshya where he gives the variants of both the terms of para and apara brahman. Su. Bh. 4-3-14 refer this dialogue : here shruti teaching movement in the context of apara brahman have been wrongly applied to para brahman, merely because of failure to discriminate between the higher and lower brahman.



Question : Are there two brahman then, the higher and the lower??

Answer : Yes, there are two (this is borne out) by the shruti : O satyakAma, verily this OMkaara is both the higher and the lower brahman.

Question : which is the higher and which is the lower ??

Answer : brahman asthUlaM, negating specific feature such as name and form created by avidyA that is para where on the other hand THAT SAME BRAHMAN is taught as qualified by some specific features for the purpose of meditation as for instance by means of such words as manOmayaH, prANashareerO, bhArUpaH etc. that is the lower brahman.

Objection : the shruti teaching non-duality would be violated in this case, then!!

SiddhAnti’s reply : No, for this has been obviated by stating that the form with attributes is due to the conditioning adjunct of name and form created by avidyA.



// END OF THE STORY OF ISHWARA IN ADVAITA SIDDHANTA//



But having said this we have to keep in mind that bhAshyakAra never ever shy away persisting on the utmost importance of IshwarAnugraha and AchaAryOpadesha in advaita’s jnana mArga sAdhana.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

Bhaskar
