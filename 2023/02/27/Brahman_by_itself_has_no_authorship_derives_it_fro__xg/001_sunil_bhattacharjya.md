Dear friends,

  

There is an interesting story, which is generally told to the newly married couples of the Brahmin community in Assam. It seems Mother Bhavani/Gauri/Parvati/Uma (i.e., Prakrti) told Lord Shiva (Brahman), that he also has to do some work, and not just bless the world.  

  

Lord Shiva agreed to grow rice, taking the help of Nandi to prepare the land, for cultivation of Paddy. Paddy grew very nicely and some people became jealous seeing the field covered with luxuriant growth of paddy.
Due to the fire of their jealous eyes, the paddy-field caught fire, and seeing that Lord Shiva extinguised the fire, but by that time some portion of the field was scorched. This resulted in production of two types of rice, Laahi (the longer variety) and Saali (the shorter variety),

  

This is probably a lesson to the males, that when the need is felt even Brahman is prepared to work, and the males should not just study and teach alone, they should be prepared to do other work too, as needed.  

  

My 2 cents

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
