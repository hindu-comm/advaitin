Very sad to convey that our dear Dr. M.L.N.Murthy passed away suddenly following a massive heart attack this evening at Sringeri. RIP.

Dr. M L Narasimha Murthy served at the Rashtriya Sanskrit Vidyapeetha(National Sanskrit University, Tirupati) as Vice Chancellor & Senior Professor of Advaita Vedanta. He holds a Doctorate in Adwaita Philosophy (Mahavakyartha Vicara). He is well versed in Sanskrit, Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam and English languages. He has authored several books in Sanskrit & English besides gracing many Vakyartha Vidwat Sabhas across the country.
