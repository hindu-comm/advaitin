Dear all

  

I was reading Sri Ramanuja's Gitabhashya and found some criticisms of advaita, which I summarize below (any misrepresentations are mine).

  

1\. Perception of difference is a product of avidya. How is Lord Krishna, who is completely free from avidya, able to perceive difference and teach the Gita to Arjuna?

  

2\. Once you realize that there is no water in a mirage, you make no efforts to fetch water from it. Why then would Lord Krishna, who knows that the universe does not exist in reality, even bother to teach the Gita to Arjuna?

  

(This is a very brief summary and a lot more is said in the bhAshya.)

  

Kindly present the advaitic response to this.

  

\- Sri Krishnarpanam
