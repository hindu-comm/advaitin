Hare Krishna



I was reading Sri Ramanuja's Gitabhashya and found some criticisms of advaita, which I summarize below (any misrepresentations are mine).



Ø Some scholars are of the opinion that Sri rAmAnujaacharya framed his objections based on mere vyAkhyAna-s of post shankara advaitins and never bothered to refer mUla bhAshya 😊



1.  Perception of difference is a product of avidya. How is Lord
    Krishna, who is completely free from avidya, able to perceive
    difference and teach the Gita to Arjuna?

- How shankara taught his school of vedAnta to his shishya, how he
  engaged himself in vAda with maNdana Mishra when everything is avidyA
  and bhrama?? What is shAstra how it can remove avidyA when shAstra
  itself is in the realm of avidyA ?? plenty of questions can be framed
  like this when we don’t know the fundamental approach of Advaita. BTW
  mere perception of difference is NOT a product of avidyA, taking the
  difference as reality and attributing independent existence to it is
  avidyA. In vyAvahArika world or in transactional reality these
  differences can be accommodated for the pedagogical purpose.



2.  Once you realize that there is no water in a mirage, you make no
    efforts to fetch water from it. Why then would Lord Krishna, who
    knows that the universe does not exist in reality, even bother to
    teach the Gita to Arjuna?

- Again all these triad allowed as long as there is jnAtru, jneya and
  jnana. Here arjuna did not know what is the reality behind this grand
  show of Kurukshetra and the lord knows what exactly is going on
  there. So, he did the upadesha about this reality to the person who
  was suffering with shOka and mOha. And in this scenario we have to
  accept the duality i.e. Krishna as the jnAni and arjuna is the ajnAni
  seeking the upadesha of jnana from the lord 😊

Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
