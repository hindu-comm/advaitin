A friend sent this note:

  

Brahmashri.VijayaraghavaSastrigal, who served in the faculty of the Madras Sanskrit College for many years, passed away on Feb 9, 2023. Sastrigal was well known in Madras as both - a Ghanapati of the Shukla Yajur Veda and a scholar well versed in vedanta shastra. During the course of his life, he trained several students including his two sons. Sastrigal was closely associated with the Kanchi and SakatapuramMathas. He was also honored by the Sringeri Matha for his contributions to the larger sastraic community.

  

He was a very unassuming man who led a life of simplicity. I had the fortune of learning Sanskrit from him over two summers, going to his house for daily classes. Not many were aware of Sastrigal'sdeeplove for Tamil literature. He once surprised me by asking me the meaning of a Tamil film song that was hugely popular in the 60's. When I could not give a satisfactory answer, he smiled and explained it to me at length.

  

Sastrigal passed away on Feb9, while listening to his students doing Upanishad parayana. His passing is a great loss for the community of vaidikas and scholars alike.
