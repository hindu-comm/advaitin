It is not mentioned in PTB. It is explained in Vedantasara verses 171-173.

  

It is also mentioned in Sarva Vedanta Siddhanta Sara Samgrahah of Sri Bhagavatpada verses 798-799. There is a translation/commentary on this work by Sri SSS in kannada. He mentions that his commentary follows Vedanta Sara.
