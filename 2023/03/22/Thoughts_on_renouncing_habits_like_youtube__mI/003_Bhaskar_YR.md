Personally speaking, to be frank, the list of A is very big in my life…right from watching /checking whatsapp messages every other minute to sitting in front of TV, watching cinemas, tennis, cricket, badminton matches hours together. Yesterday morning, after morning saNdhya and pooja, I planned chanting Vishnu sahasranaama and udaka shanti pArAyaNa in the evening on the occasion of new year ugaadi but ended up in watching the cricket match from afternoon to till night (except swiftly completing sAyaM saNdhyA during inning break 😊). This is how dominating these habits / addictions in our day to day life. Infact, we are so engrossed in it, we hardly entertain the thoughts of getting rid of these habits. We know, we have to chose a fruitful / beneficial substitute to engage our mind, we know these passions should be nipped in bud, we know we have to be in the company of people who are leading their lives meaningfully without wasting any time on these type of useless and unwanted habits but we somehow always at the losing side due to one or the other reason or justification. The comprehensive dissection of these habits in a rational way as you pointed out below and finding the valid reason for eradication hardly finds a place in our busy minds 😊 But really worth giving a try if we are really serious about our weaknesses but I think we desperately need Ishwaraanugraha since will-power not so strong in these habituated minds.
