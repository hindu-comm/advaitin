YouTube has some of the greatest resources for Advaita learning. A lot of discourses/classes on Advaitic texts in a host of languages is present there. Swami Sarvapriyananda'sextremely valuable talks are there. Many Advaita Acharyas have used that tool to broadcast their classes that have benefited and are benefiting a very large seeking community. Not just Advaita but a lot more like Bhagavatam and other sublime discourses by great Masters like Brahmasri Sundar Kumar is there. Hence the renunciation of thisin toto will be fraught with the danger of throwing the baby along with the bathwater. The same with any other modern tool. So a judicious use of these is what is wise. The Sanskrit maxim अति सर्वत्र वर्जयेत् - Too much of anything is bad - needs to be our guiding light.
