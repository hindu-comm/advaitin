Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

The list doesn't have to be long, just one of them suffices to have us going in circles for days, months, years. That is samsara in general, but the modern gimmicks accelerate and exaggerate theimpact. You have candidly and eloquently expressed the extent to which these modern tech-gadgets are (apparently) dragging us. At the same time, most of the trouble is in our minds only and hence directly rectifiable by ourselves. youtube or tv is not literally chaining or caging us; it is an illusion of bondage that we are caught up in. We can just as immediately break that illusion through right choices and exercise of our shakti. (However, it becomes more complex when this type of medium and its content acquire a "necessary" status in the midst of other commitments in our world, like family, job, etc.)

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
