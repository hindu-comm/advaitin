1\. Their "bhakti" in B is prompted more by their desire for freedom from A. They may not have independent shraddha in B, and it may become a waning false show, losing the impetus to "know" B further, once they find some relief from A.



2\. Their desire for freedom from A (bad habits) may be more intertwined with their desire for success in some C (studies, job, etc.), so they subconsciously only want to use B ("Ishta devata") as a quick-acting lever for stopping A so that they can stabilize in C.



praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji
