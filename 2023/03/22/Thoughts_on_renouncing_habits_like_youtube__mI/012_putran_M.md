> Then use this discriminating knowledge to sublate Category 1 and the
> paradharma elements of Category 2 as nothing but mental
> projections/forms of self, and thereby stop the process of further
> imagination and pursuit of karma on their basis.
>

Details will vary with each person as we try and bring to regular practice, but I decided to outline a line of cogitation that I use. Below is a q&a session to run through your mind when you seek to check your mind and bring in above mentioned discrimination.

  

————-

  

Where is this thought/object presently occurring?

  

In my mind (not in the world).

  

“Feel” the separation of the mind-object from the world outside, so that it is now clear to your awareness that the object*of your cognition*is distinctly an object ’living’ in your mind and not an object of the world.

  

Who is projecting this object in the mind?

  

I am.

  

Now “feel” (visualize) your self as Consciousness taking the svarupa/form of that thought/object, thus instead of being linked to an object in the world, the mind-object’s link to you, as your manifestation, comes to the front of your awareness. The object loses its potency to appear as an independent reality, and gets withdrawn in the shift of attention from object back to the self.
