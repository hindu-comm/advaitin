(A practical tip mentioned below)

  

  

The vratha has to be connected to actual sadhana of training the mind to align with religious truths.

  

For example, one thing we can work on is to look at the thoughts arising in the mind like one sitting at the beach will look at the waves in the ocean. We appreciate the ocean/water for the various shapes it produces but don't get caught up over which wave is "my wave" or which one should have what shape. No matter what happens we are content that the ocean/water is constant and unchanging. That keeps us detached and sitting as witness rather than getting active, jumping up and down or throwing stones into the water.

  

Whichever wave does whatever and to whatever other wave, we know it all as the Ocean's/Water's play. We appreciate a certain wave-form the Ocean displays at the moment it is displayed but know fully well the wave is simply the Ocean/Water's appearance and in no way independent of It. This awareness keeps us detached and aloof from the momentary name-form projections, their past and future. The unitary Identity of the Ocean/Water stays at the center of our consciousness, as the reality.

  

Likewise if we can look at the thoughts in our mind and the world in our vision as waves \[appearance\] that do not change the Self, then we minimize getting entangled over what should happen and what should not - unless it is particularly necessary to do so given our state of identification.

  

Most of the thoughts of things in the world that make us want to go into internet for entertainment are not necessary for us; we should be able to look at them as simply waves in the ocean and not follow up on them with action (such as going into internet to find out what happened to it and so on). This is a sort of open-eyed meditation. The same person who happily spends ("wastes") 3 hours on youtube or tv ought to practice such meditation sitting at a place with no "outside activity". (Not that I am successful at it, but part of the purpose of taking such an internet vratha should be to train ourselves in such meditation that replaces youtube etc.)
