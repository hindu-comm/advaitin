I haven’t seen any subsequent reply / observation to this You tube vrata (YTV) that you are suggesting, everyone seems to be comfortable with this suggestion / sAdhana. But let me share my observation since I myself tried this long before but in vain. Though it was in a different version not like open eyed meditation with sAkshi attitude etc. but I wanted to restrict my TV watching time and spend more time on svAdhyAya.



The vratha has to be connected to actual sadhana of training the mind to align with religious truths.



For example, one thing we can work on is to look at the thoughts arising in the mind like one sitting at the beach will look at the waves in the ocean. We appreciate the ocean/water for the various shapes it produces but don't get caught up over which wave is "my wave" or which one should have what shape. No matter what happens we are content that the ocean/water is constant and unchanging. That keeps us detached and sitting as witness rather than getting active, jumping up and down or throwing stones into the water.Whichever wave does whatever and to whatever other wave, we know it all as the Ocean's/Water's play. We appreciate a certain wave-form the Ocean displays at the moment it is displayed but know fully well the wave is simply the Ocean/Water's appearance and in no way independent of It. This awareness keeps us detached and aloof from the momentary name-form projections, their past and future. The unitary Identity of the Ocean/Water stays at the center of our consciousness, as the reality.



Ø Good one, geeta explains this with the characteristics of ‘sthita prajna’. The one who see the things ‘as it is’ without adding any ‘masaala’ prepared in his/her own mental factory 😊 If this tranquility is achieved then ‘sthira buddhi’ is the result. The jnAnayogi who knows the reality of things even though seeing the things (hearing, touching, smelling, eating or drinking, walking, sleeping, breathing, speaking, even when answering the calls of nature, opening and closing the eyes, he does absolutely nothing. ( BG verses 5-8 & 9 more relevant here).
Seeing the kAryAkAra (waves) ‘as it is’ keeping the ‘kAraNatvaM’(ocean/water) in mind.



Likewise if we can look at the thoughts in our mind and the world in our vision as waves \[appearance\] that do not change the Self, then we minimize getting entangled over what should happen and what should not - unless it is particularly necessary to do so given our state of identification.



Ø Yes this is very important, looking at the moving thoughts in our mind and the world ‘in our vision’ as sAkshi would help us to add ‘extra’ petals to the ‘actual’ rose. Seeing the ‘rose’ as rose is not a problem but problem starts when we start seeing the rose with all sorts of designations and retributions (the ‘masAla’) like this rose is small, big, bright, dull, less petals, more petals not so good when compared to jasmine etc. etc.



Most of the thoughts of things in the world that make us want to go into internet for entertainment are not necessary for us; we should be able to look at them as simply waves in the ocean and not follow up on them with action (such as going into internet to find out what happened to it and so on). This is a sort of open-eyed meditation. The same person who happily spends ("wastes") 3 hours on youtube or tv ought to practice such meditation sitting at a place with no "outside activity".



- Though suggestion looks very good and attractive, I reckon it is not
  workable in our day to day affair. First and foremost thing is when
  we have absolute control / will power to do this why we should opt for
  this YTV?? Don’t we have any other better option to do this open eyed
  dhyAna?? Like watching the waves, birds flying, stagnant giri-kAnana,
  the Jyoti etc. which have less masala when compared to YTV. Can it be
  practically possible to switch off the TV / YT when there is ‘climax’
  which was about to happen within few minutes but beyond the few
  minutes of scheduled YTV?? I don’t think so. Let us take a practical
  example of T-20 cricket match. We have decided this is only cricket
  match, the result/outcome hardly affects me I ‘just’ watch it couple
  of hours as a part of my YTV sAdhana and switch off the set after 2
  hours and leave the place. But, mind is such a cheating character
  during our watching of two hours knowingly or unknowingly we ourselves
  entrapped in the match and if couple of overs left and match is at the
  interesting stage at the end of scheduled sAdhana, mind started giving
  all types of justifications to ‘stretch’ that ‘sAdhana’ timing just to
  ‘know’ the result. On the face of it, it appears we are doing
  nothing wrong here but it is the attachment without our wish. This
  could happen to any show in YTV like cinema, match, event, episode or
  for that matter a beautiful discourse by our revered speaker on our
  favorite subject. The attachment and clinging ‘chApalya’ is one and
  the same. So my question is why on the earth we have to risk
  ourselves by choosing these masala-s when plenty of other less
  dangerous options available to do open eyed dhyAna!!?? The YTV is
  nothing but vishayAsakti and the conditioned mind which is craving for
  vishaya sukha throw at us plenty of dangerous options like this just
  to gradually trap us in vishaya sukha.
