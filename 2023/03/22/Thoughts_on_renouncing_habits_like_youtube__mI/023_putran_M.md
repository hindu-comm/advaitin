Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

I may elaborate later but I think there is a misunderstanding. I did not suggest we should think Advaita while also indulging our desires. I meant we should practice this type of meditation when not watching and in general, especially since we will be confronted by thoughts prompted by our vasanas that make us want to go see YTV (outside our vratha time at least). It is something a sadhaka should anyway try to do, but
maintaining a vratha of this sort presents a challenge and opportunity to wrestle with the mind through systematic advaitic contemplation. That was the idea.
