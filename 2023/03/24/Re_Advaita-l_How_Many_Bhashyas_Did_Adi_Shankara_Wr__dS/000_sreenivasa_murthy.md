Dear Sri Sunil Bhattacharriya,

  

How many bhashyas written by Sri Shankara on Upanishads

have you read?

How many bhashyas have you understood?

How much of tattva as revealed by Sri Shankara you have  

cognized within yourself by yourself?

questions on Sri Shankara and his commentaries.

This is for your own good.

Your wellwisher,

Sreenivasa Murthy.
