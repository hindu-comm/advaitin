Dear Sri Sreenivasa Murthy,

  

Nice to see your frank and outspoken view, that it is not necessary to ascertain which are the bhashyas written by Adi Shankaracharya. Your statement may also mean that you are not sure about which are the authentic Adi Shankara-bhashyas.  

  

However, everybody may not endorse your view. Swami Chinmayanandaji, whom I admire, thought it necessary to name the bhshyas attributed to Adi Shankaracharya, but unfortunately he had not given any reference to support his view.  

  

Best wishes

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
