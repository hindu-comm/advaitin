Dear Shri Sreenivasa Murthy and friends,

  

Further to my last mail, I wish to draw the kind attention of the scholarly group members to the fact that the respected Swami Chinmayanandaji is no more, as he passed away 30 years ago, and now what we can try is to find out any other living scholar(s), who could have gone through the various AdiShankara-Digvijaya texts, to see if any of
of them can tell us at the earliest possible, if any of the texts had mentioned the number of Bhashyas authored by Adi Shankaracharya.

  

For this type of work we have to keep our personal egos aside, ***Of course, the Advaitins know the best that ego is the greatest hindrance in the path of the advaitic liberation.***.
