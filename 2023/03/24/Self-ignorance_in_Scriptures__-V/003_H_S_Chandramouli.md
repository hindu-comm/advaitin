Reg // Initially, we do not know who-we-really are. We do not have knowledge about our ‘Self’. We do not have ‘Self-knowledge’ //,

Sri Bhagavatpada states in AdhyAsa BhAshya

// ‘अहमिदम्’ ‘ममेदम्’इतिनैसर्गिकोऽयंलोकव्यवहारः॥ //

// ‘ahamidam’ ‘mamedam’ iti naisargiko.ayaM lokavyavahAraH \|\| //.

Sri Bhagavatpada does not state what you have said above. Please give reference to where he has stated so. What indeed is implied in his above statement is that lokavyavahAra is based on two convictions. Firstly neither any other \*\*I\*\* (Atman) exists (astitva) nor is that other \*\*I\*\* (Atman) perceived (bhAnatva). Why the two are separately stated is because they are negated in two different stages. By parOksha jnAna, the first one is removed while the second one is removed by aparOksha jnAna.
