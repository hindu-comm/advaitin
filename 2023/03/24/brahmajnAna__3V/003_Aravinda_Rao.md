Dear Dennis ji,

  

"Surely we understand that ‘I, the jīva, am Brahman’; it is not that ‘Brahman gets the knowledge that Brahman is Brahman’??"  

  

My understanding is that the Brahman, who has all the while been thinking that he was X,Y,Z has now realized that he is Brahman.

It can be seen in mantra-s likeब्रह्मैव सन् ब्रह्माप्येति य एवम् वेद ' while being Brahman, he realizes Brahman'. It is one of the mantras in the Sandhya-vandanam. I cannot readily quote from bhashyam but I remember having read lines to that effect.

Regards,

Aravinda Rao
