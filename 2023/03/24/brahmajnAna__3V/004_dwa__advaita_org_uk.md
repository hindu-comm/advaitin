I don’t mind if you are garrulous, Bhaskar-ji, as long as you say something useful! 😉



I need references to anything relevant said by Shankara. Regarding what you say here: I would not take ‘Self-knowledge’ literally – i.e. ‘I know myself’. This could be deemed contradictory. But ‘I know that I am Brahman’ or that ‘reality is non-dual’ or that ‘all this is Brahman’ are not contradictory. This is new knowledge that I did not have before. These do not boil down to Brahman ‘realizing’ that Brahman is Brahman because we are talking about the intellectual realization of the jIva.



(And here, there is another ‘warning’ that I will not get drawn into an argument that the j~nAnI now IS Brahman, and that the world disappears, and all of that. (I judiciously deleted the word ‘nonsense’… 😉)
