- There is saying in Advaita circle : brahman becomes Ishwara in
  relation to mAya (mAyOpAdhi etc. currently being discussed in
  Advaita-L). But here the question / doubt is : can brahman become
  jeeva in relation to avidyA? Is it not obviously wrong to say that
  brahman has created avidyA for Itself or that brahman itself is
  confused?” The following clarification by bhAshyakAra would throw more
  light on ‘eka chaitanya and socalled mAyA in Ishwara and avidyA in
  jeeva: “Yes it is true that the adhyArOpa of the dharma not existing
  in brahman is made in brahman. But can I not say that brahman is not
  responsible for this imposition and It is not the cause of avidyA? Let
  brahman not be the cause of avidyA and not Itself confused. But, the
  jeeva who has created avidyA for himself and confused is not different
  from brahman. Here jeeva who is having the avidyA gained vidyA and
  realized that he is brahman and it is not otherway round that
  ‘brahman’ got the avidyA and became jeeva!!?? More on this on Monday.
