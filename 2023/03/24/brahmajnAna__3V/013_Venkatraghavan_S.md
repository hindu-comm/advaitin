It is karmaNi shaShThi - brahmaNah jnAnam brahmajnAnam. The sixth case ending is used in the second case ending / objective sense, meaning brahmaviShayakajnAnam - knowledge of Brahman, not Brahman's knowledge.

  

It is similar usage to brahmajijnAsa as Chandramouliji said, on the same rationale mentioned in the sUtrabhAShya of that word.
