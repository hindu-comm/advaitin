Dear Sri Dennisji,

Please Permit me to share a few thoughts of mine with you.  

  

Kindly study in depth mantra 1-4-9 and 1-4-10 of Bruhadaranyaka Upanishad along with

Sri Shankara's commentary to them. That will help you to see where you have erred in understanding Advaita as taught by the upanishad sages.

  

With out a thorough knowledge of Upanishads and without cognizing the truth within in oneself by oneself by applying the methodologies as revealed by the sages in the upanishad writing books on advaita is like trying to read Shakespeare without a knowledge of the alphabets of English language.

  

With warm regards,

Sreenivasa Murthy
