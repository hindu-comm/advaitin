Not all 6th case meanings are genitive. In fact, all the English translations of cases such as accusative, instrumental, etc, are seen to be clearly unjust when one studies vyAkaraNa. In any case, 6th case of brahman here means the object of the dhAtu/ verbal root jnA in jnAna vide sUtra:

  

२.३.६५ कर्तृकर्मणोः कृति \[अनभिहिते षष्ठी शेषे\]

2.3.65 The 6th-Case-affix is used after a word, in denoting the agent and the object, when used along with a word ending with a kRt-suffix.
