Dear Aravinda-ji,



I posted a 2-part article on the subject a few years ago (but I don’t think my views have changed). [https://www.advaita-vision.org/the-devils-teaching-part-1/](https://www.advaita-vision.org/the-devils-teaching-part-1/)



Your statement “My understanding is that the Brahman, who has all the while been thinking that he was X,Y,Z has now realized that he is Brahman.” must be mixing levels, mustn’t it? If you are saying ‘there is only Brahman’, you are trying to speak from a pAramArthika viewpoint. (And we can never do this, of course!) And Brahman does not function in the realm of ignorance and knowledge. (In fact, does not function at all!) If, on the other hand, you are knowingly talking about the vyAvahArika appearance, then you cannot deny that there are many jIva-s.



The only way to play this is that we have to acknowledge the continuing appearance, even for the j~nAnI, whilst acknowledging that, in reality, everything is just name and form of Brahman. There will always be the appearance of many jIva-s. In reality, there are never any jIva-s. And there is never only one jIva!
