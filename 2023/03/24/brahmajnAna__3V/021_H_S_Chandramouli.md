Sri Bhagavatpada here itself, in BSB 1-1-1, has split the word brahmajnAna and explained the terms.

// ज्ञानेनहिप्रमाणेनावगन्तुमिष्टंब्रह्म।ब्रह्मावगतिर्हिपुरुषार्थः //

// The desire being that Brahman be realized (ie. uncovered) by that knowledge which is a valid means of apprehension. And the realization of Brahman is the highest human objective //

Interestigly, the word ज्ञान (derived from ज्ञ dhatu) is, in this context in the Bhashya, stated as a pramANa (means of knowledge) and not as a phala. The word अवगति is used for phala.

MahAvAkya is normally presented as pramANa for Realization. Then how is ज्ञान to be understood as the pramANa for the same? It is to be understood as the vritti (वृत्तिरूपज्ञान vRRittirUpaj~nAna) , called by later Commentators as अखण्डाकारवृत्ति (akhaNDAkAravRRitti) which , by way of आवरणभङ्ग (AvaraNabha~Nga /destruction of the AvaraNa) leads to Realization (अवगति avagati) . However mahAvAkya is termed as the pramANa because that is the external means which leads to the generation of अखण्डाकारवृत्ति (akhaNDAkAravRRitti) , which in turn becomes the direct means to Realization (अवगति avagati).

This process is explained beautifully in the following two talks, in tamil, by Sri Mani Dravid Shastri Ji on the relevant part of BSB 1-1-1

I think recently Michael JI wanted to know where in PSB the subject of
akhaNDAkAravRRitti is covered. It is covered in this portion of PTB—the term brahmajnAna used here in the Bhashya is by later Commentators termed akhaNDAkAravRRitti.

Needless to say, this is in terms of the mUlAvidyA concept.

Sri SSS also , in his kannada translation and commentary, interprets the term brahmajnAna as a sAdhana/pramANa. But I am not aware how he has elaborated on the process leading upto अवगति avagati.

I still have one doubt left though, and that is in respect of Sanskrit grammar. As per above understanding, is the term \*\* brahmajnAna\*\* a compound word or a single word. If it is a compound word, what is its derived expanded form? Perhaps Venkat Ji or Praveen Ji could clarify.
