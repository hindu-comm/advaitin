Namaste Chandramouliji,

  

I had mentioned the samAsa vigraha (the splitting of the compound word brahmajnAnam) in my original response to Dennis ji. It is brahmaNah jnAnam brahmajnAnam.

  

The sentence अवगतिपर्यन्तं ज्ञानं सन्वाच्याया इच्छायाः कर्म, फलविषयत्वादिच्छायाः in the bhAShya just before the sentences you quote is a very far reaching sentence and lays the foundation for the akhaNDAkAravRtti concept that is more elaborately brought out by later commentators.

  

The object of the desire (icchA) indicated through the san suffix in the word jijnAsA (jnAtum icChA = jijnAsA) is both 1) the vRttijnAna (indicated by the word jnAnam in the bhAshya sentence) that removes ignorance, and 2) svarUpajnAna (indicated by the word avagati).

  

The vRttijnAna (akhaNDAkAra vRtti) known through the mahAvAkya must culminate in the phala, which is avagati / svarUpajnAna (hence avagati paryantam jnAnam) for the jijnAsA to be satisfied.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
