[[V Subrahmanian	2023-03-28, 13:39:22 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/nqcXQEJ5esU)]]

  

A verse that praises Ganga as having emanated from the feet of Vishnu and that has touched Shiva's head and flown to the earth from there. Let that Ganga which has become exalted by the contact of Hari and Hara purify me.  

  

  

'गाङ्गं वारि मनोहारि मुरारिचरणच्युतम् ।  
त्रिपुरारिशिरश्चारि पापहारि पुनातु माम्' ।।

  

A sweet rhyme with the word ' vaari' in the neuter throughout.

  

There is also a verse which connects Ganga to Chaturmukha Brahma too, along with Hari and Hara, thereby being thrice blessed.

  

Om
