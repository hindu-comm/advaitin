**बुद्धिर्बलम्यशोधैर्यम् निर्भयत्वम्अरोगताम्  
अजाड्यम्वाक्पटुत्वम्च हनुमत्स्मरणात्भवेत्॥  
Buddhir Balam Yasho Dhairyam Nirbhayatvam Arogataam  
Ajaaddyam Vaak Pattutvam Ca Hanumat Smarannaat Bhavet \|\|**  
  
**This Sloka suggests that every Human Being should acquire the Virtues that Hanuman represents. On this Hanuma Jayanthi day, let us all acquire all the Virtues of Hanumanji that include Buddhi, Balam, Yashas, Dhairyam. Nirbhayatvam. Arogataam, Ajaaddyam and Vak Pattutvam and keep them with us throughout our life.**

**Meaning:**

**Buddhi – Intelligence to discriminate between right and wrong; permanent and temporary and do activities that are right and permanent.**

**Balam – Gain the strength of Hanumanji to execute all our works**

**Yashas – Represent the Graceful appearance of Hanumanji**

**Dhairyam – Attain the Courage of Hanumanji at appropriate moment**

**Nirbhayatvam – Free from Fear by getting rid of all mental blocks**

**Arogataam – Maintain Good Health and free from Diseases**

**Ajadyam – Free from Laziness and Keep Serving**

**Vak Patutvam – Skillfullness in Speech to achieve success in communicate**

**Yesterday was Hanuman Jayanthi day and let us all pray Him to get His Virtues!!**

**Ram Chandran**
