Namaste Putran ji,

I am sorry if I have offended you. I am in fact a devotee of Hanuman and so when a good note was sent by Ramchandran ji, I recalled how exalted the character of Hanuman was. I too quote the verse in my classes on Vedanta. I gave talks on the Ramayanam with great devotion. The classes on the Sundarakandam can be seen from talk no 138 in the playlist. The link is given below.

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=STCR8xWDFyQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=STCR8xWDFyQ)

However I feel bad to see our overenthusiastic crowds who, instead of picking up the qualities of Hanuman, try to use him for street brawls and make him an object of hate for other communities. You are aware of the Hanumad mantra -

बुद्धिर्बलं यशो धैर्यं निर्भयत्वमरोगता।  
अजाड्यं वाक्पटुत्वं च हनूमत्स्मरणाद्भवेत्॥  

We are expected to imbibe such qualities instead of making our gods the objects of hate for others.

Of course, this approach is again debatable, I know.

Pranams,

Aravinda Rao
