Namaskaram,

  

Please see this book “Sanatana Dharma” published in 1916 by the Board of Trustees of the Central Hindu College, Benares.

  

[https://www.vhp-america.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Book_Sanatan_Dharma_elementry.pdf](https://www.vhp-america.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Book_Sanatan_Dharma_elementry.pdf)  

  

Though published by the College, the foreword says “This elementary Text-Book … is intended for the use of Hindu boys in the middle and upper sections of the High Schools of India, and is designed to give them a general but correct idea of their national religion…” There is also mention later of “Advanced text” and of this book being part of a “series”. I wish to know if such books were indeed incorporated into schools at that time and made part of standard curriculum. At least, the CHC institutions followed these principles, according to the foreword.

  

I read Part 1 (first six chapters) till now. Chapter 1 on “One Existence” seemed very relatable to my general knowledge based on advaita. I am not sure of some parts of Chapter 2 onwards - regarding evolution of jiva etc. It is possible that since the College was founded by Annie Besant, there is a theosophical angle to some of their explanations. (Others may be able to comment on this.) Chapter 5 on Sacrifice reads like a beautiful insightful essay. Overall, the work and the intent constitute a genuine and erudite effort to provide Hindu youth a foundational knowledge of their own religion.

  

Please read the foreword to appreciate the vision of these Hindu educational leaders back then. I felt humbled and thankful to read it. Do “Compare and Contrast” with what our evolved post-independence selves have on the webpage today:
