I was clicking randomly at different times in this vaishnava conference and landed on this interesting observation/claim by this speaker.

  

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1PRk_f2BC40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1PRk_f2BC40)  

  

See from 44:30 to 45:15 where she says Vaishnavism accepts worship of **any** deity “path is not hard and fast”. I am not going to take it at face value, will have to check with others; however it was surprising to hear. Right after however she says that all attributes (creation etc) belong to Sri Hari only in Vaishnavism - but this does not contradict the prior comment. Perhaps their main assertion is that Hari is the underlying Deity behind all other manifestations, so even if we worship other deities thinking of them as different from Hari, it can suffice for liberation because such worship is also worship of Vishnu ultimately.
