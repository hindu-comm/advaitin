Shri Natraj Maneshinde:   

===========================  
“World follows dharma and is happy due the grace of Bhagavan Sureshvaracharya”  
===========================  
  
यत्प्रसादादयं लोको धर्ममार्गस्थितः सुखी ।  
भवभूतिसुरेशाख्यं विश्वरूपं प्रणम्य तम् ॥  
  
~ Vibhavana, tika on Balakrida of Shri Vishvarupacharya  
  
\[I bow down to Shri Vishvarupacharya, who is also known as Sureshvara and who is the devotee of Bhava, on account of whose blessings the world treads on the path of dharma and is happy\]  
  
Together with being a Vedantin of the highest order, Shri Sureshvaracharya, also known as Vishvarupacharya, has composed an authoritative commentary on Yagnavalkya Smriti called Balakrida. This is probably the earliest commentary on the Smriti. Given that Sureshvaracharya was a Shukla Yajur Vedi of Kanva Shakha, it isn’t surprising that he chose to comment on Bhagavan Yagnavalkya’s Smriti and also on Brihadaranyaka bhashya of Bhagavatpada.
