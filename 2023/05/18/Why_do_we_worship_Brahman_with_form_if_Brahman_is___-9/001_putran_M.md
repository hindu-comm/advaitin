Namaskaram Krishnakapoor-ji,

  

Do you ask a physicist who says “all matter reduces to energy”, why he eats carrots and not stones? There is the absolute “energy” or knowledge standpoint in which carrot and stone are both simply manifest appearances of energy and no matter which does what in the plane of appearance, nothing whatsoever changes about the substratum energy. But there is the appearance standpoint in which you ask about “eating” and it makes perfect sense then to say “eat carrot and not stone”.

  

When we come to Vedanta, Brahman or pure Consciousness is the unchanging substratum to the nama-rupa world of appearance. When we are ajnanis, it matters that we worship Saguna Brahman because such worship aids in our attaining jnana - or (in the appearance standpoint) the deity we worshipblesses us accordingly. When we have jnana, we realize Brahman alone in all appearance - so who is worshipper and who is worshipped when all is Self? Even then, Brahman in the form of jnani can participate as if “individual” in the manifest play enacted by Brahman in the form of Ishvara (Brahman+Maya). Like you and I enjoy watching a movieand root for the 'hero' towin over the 'villain'. So the jnani may choose to worship Saguna Brahman to set an example for ajnanis, even though such worship is not necessary for him directly.
