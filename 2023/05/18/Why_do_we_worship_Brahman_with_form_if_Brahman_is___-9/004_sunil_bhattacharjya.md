Dear friends,

  

Lord Krishna advised all those who desire Mukti to worship Mother Goddess Durga. Adi Shankara wrote his last Bhashya on the "Lalita Trishati". Mother Goddess Lalita, also called as Shadoshi or Kamakshi, is one of the Dasha-Mahavidyas of Mother Goddess Durga. Adi Shankara's samadhi is also in the Kanchi Kamakoti math, quite near the Devi mandir.
