Namaste.

The purpose of rope-snake illustration with reference to jagat is explained in VivaraNa Prameya Samgraha of Swami Vidyaranya as follows

// रज्जुसर्पदृष्टन्तेन ब्रह्मव्यतिरिक्तप्रपञ्चस्य स्वातन्त्र्याभावः//

// rajjusarpadRRiShTantena brahmavyatiriktaprapa~nchasya svAtantryAbhAvaH //

Translation by Prof Suryanarayana Shastri // By the example of the rope-snake, the non-existence of independence for the world as different from Brahman (is made possible) //.
