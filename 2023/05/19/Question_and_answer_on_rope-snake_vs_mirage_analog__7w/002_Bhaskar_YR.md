Translation by Prof Suryanarayana Shastri // By the example of the rope-snake, the non-existence of independence for the world as different from Brahman (is made possible) //



praNAms

Hare Krishna



I don’t know other way of reading the above statement is : the snake is ***existing*** depending on the rope. But ***snake never ever existed*** that is the knowledge what the jnAni gets when the rope knowledge is revealed. But world is not like snake on the rope (bhAshyakAra exquisitely said this in chAdOgya / sUtra bhAshya. He answers to the question : whether the world is like snake on the rope?? With a ‘No’. I reckon clay-pot is more appropriate with regard to existence of the world aka kArya-kAraNa ananyatvaM.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
