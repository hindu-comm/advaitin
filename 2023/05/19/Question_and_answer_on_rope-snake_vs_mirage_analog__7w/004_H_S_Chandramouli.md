No no. Illustrations are meant for a specific and limited purpose. They
are the causes of sambhAvana (possibility) of knowing Brahman from verbal testimony. The stated illustration it is not meant to mean that the snake is existing depending on the rope. It is meant to illustrate the possibility of appearance of the jagat even though it really does not exist. But the appearance requires Brahman . Hence appearance of jagat can serve as a pointer to Brahman. That it is so is as declared by the Shruti.
