praNAms

Hare Krishna



bahu syaam prajaayeya yatha mrudghataakaareNa yathaa vaa rajvaadi sarpaadyAkAreNa buddhiparikalpitena



asadeva tarhi sarvaM yadgrAhyate rajjuriva sarpAdyAkAreNa?? Na, sat eva dvaita bhedena anyathAgruhyamANatvAt nAsatvaM kasyachit kvachit iti bhrUmaH in shankara bhAshya chAndOgya 6^(th) chapter.



Here question asking after hearing both mrudghata and rajjusarpa examples is : jagat is also not an existing thing it is just an imaginary thing like the imagined snake on the rope?? For this question bhAshyakAra answers NO (na) it is only the brahman appearing as in special forms in a different way. Nothing here non existent at any time. As we know, we should not conclude by this and say snake is also an existent thing on rope like anirvachaneeya khyAti vAda.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!
