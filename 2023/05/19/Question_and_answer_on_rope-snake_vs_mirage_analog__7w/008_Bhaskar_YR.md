My few additional thoughts :



Here in rope-snake example, snake is merely avidyakalpita of the conditioned jeeva, according to saMskAra vishesha some jeeva may see the rope as snake someone else may see this as garland yet another look at it as a crack on the floor or mUtra of an ox…And it would also serve the purpose that brahman (rope) is nirvishesha even during the sight of jagat (sarpa). there is no bheda whatsoever in brahman. But here again in this example, like sarpa, jagat too as such is not just buddhi parikalpita of some conditioned jeeva because for the jagat brahman is the upAdAna and nimitta kAraNa ( but for the snake rope is not the upAdAna kAraNa, sarpa here mere buddhi parikalpita due to avidyA). Thus Sri SSS somewhere in his work emphasizes, the universe is not an illusion (or imaginary) to bAdarAyaNa as explained by Shankara. It is the Buddhists who deny reality to external objects. The vijnAnavAdin says that the object is identical with the idea, since both of them experienced together. But bhAshyakAra draws out different principle which is in accordance with shruti pramANa. In sUtra bhAshya he clarifies : neti neti ityasya kOrthaH?? Na hi etasmAd brahmaNO vyatiriktamasti ityarthaH. Following this statement Sri SSS in Kannada
observes : bestaru brahmave, garbhadaasaru brahmave, joojaaduva keelu janaru brahmave – endu heluttiruva a saarvatrikavaada tattvavannu sunischita reetiyinda bOdhisida shrutiya mahimeyannu ellarigu nenapige tandukotta yashassu Achaaryaradu. E tattvavannu aritukolluvude janarige janma sAphalya, adaralliyu brahmaNanige visheshavaagi janma sArthakyavannu kodatakkaddu.
