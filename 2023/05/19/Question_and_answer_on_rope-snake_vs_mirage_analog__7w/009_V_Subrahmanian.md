The above is directly contradicted by the Bhashya in these extremely unambiguous terms:

  

#### छान्दोग्योपनिषद्भाष्यम्षष्ठोऽध्यायःद्वितीयः खण्डःमन्त्र २- भाष्यम्

………; निरवयवस्य सतः कथं विकारसंस्थानमुपपद्यते ? नैष दोषः, रज्ज्वाद्यवयवेभ्यः सर्पादिसंस्थानवत्*बुद्धिपरिकल्पितेभ्यः सदवयवेभ्यः* विकारसंस्थानोपपत्तेः । ‘वाचारम्भणं विकारो नामधेयं मृत्तिकेत्येव सत्यम्’ (छा.………

  

Shankara says: How can the world-vikara be accounted for in the case of Brahman that is niravayava, without parts? He replies: that is not a defect, for just like the rope, etc. are sourced from the 'parts' of the rope, etc. (adhishthana), so too by one's buddhi parikalpana, the Sat, Brahman-parts the world-vikara is definitely possible. The Shruti pramana is: vAcArambhaNam vikAro nAmadheyam, mruttiketyeva satyam.

  

So Shankara holds that the world is a buddhiparikalpana on Brahman. The niravayava Brahman is wrongly seen to be sAvayava world.

  

Again says Shankara:

#### छान्दोग्योपनिषद्भाष्यम्षष्ठोऽध्यायःद्वितीयः खण्डःमन्त्र ३- भाष्यम्

………स्यां भवेयं प्रजायेय प्रकर्षेणोत्पद्येय, यथा मृद्घटाद्याकारेण यथा वा रज्ज्वादि सर्पाद्याकारेणबुद्धिपरिकल्पितेन । असदेव तर्हि सर्वम् , यद्गृह्यते रज्जुरिव सर्पाद्याकारेण ।…

  

Brahman resolved: I shall be born as many, just as clay becomes many pots, etc. and just as rope, etc. take the form of snake, etc. - all due to buddhiparikalpana.

Thus Shankara is very clear that the world is a buddhikalpana on the same lines as the rope-snake is a buddhikalpana.

  

The clinching term here is: buddhiparikalpitena, with applies equally to both the snake and the world.

Here are more bhashya passages that hold the world-perception to be a mithyadrishti: 

  

#### ब्रह्मसूत्रभाष्यम्प्रथमोऽध्यायःचतुर्थः पादःसूत्रम् १९- भाष्यम्

………इत्यादिना ; यो हि ब्रह्मक्षत्रादिकं जगदात्मनोऽन्यत्र स्वातन्त्र्येण लब्धसद्भावं पश्यति, तं*मिथ्यादर्शिनं तदेवमिथ्यादृष्टं ब्रह्मक्षत्रादिकं जगत्पराकरोतीति* भेददृष्टिमपोद्य, ‘इदꣳ सर्वं यदयमात्मा’ इति सर्वस्य वस्तुजातस्यात्माव्यतिरेकमवतारयति………

Here Shankara says: the world is taught to be Atma by the Shruti. When someone sees the world instead of Brahman, that world itself will kick out that person for his mithyadrishti.

  

#### श्रीमद्भगवद्गीताभाष्यम्द्वितीयोऽध्यायःश्लोक १६- भाष्यम्

………तत्त्वदर्शिनां दृष्टिमाश्रित्य शोकं मोहं च हित्वा शीतोष्णादीनि नियतानियतरूपाणि द्वन्द्वानि ‘विकारोऽयमसन्नेव मरीचिजलवन्मिथ्यावभासते’ इति मनसि निश्चित्य तितिक्षस्व इत्यभिप्रायः ॥ १६ ॥ ………

Here Shankara says: the world-vikara is asat only, but appears to be existing like the non-existent water appearing to be existing in mirage. If Shankara had held the world to be satyam he should not have given the rope-snake and mirage examples for the world. We must remember that in all these examples, it is the beholder's dosha, ajnana, that is responsible for the error. The same is the case with the world perception too. Brahman alone due to ajnana imagines the world and imagines itself as a bound jiva and all the others, the whole variety seen in the world.

  

The above are just a few examples from the many that are in the Bhashya.
