Ø Yes these are extremely unambiguous terms but lacking the contextual understating on the part of the reader and conveniently ignoring the fact that how these bhAshya vAkyas needs to be understood as per shruti pramANa by contrasting the theory of buddhi parikalpita jagat theory of vijnAnavAdins. Ofcourse, being an Advaitin nobody wants to wear this sticker😊 So let us understand these bhAshya vAkya-s as per shruti, yukti and anubhava.



Shankara says: How can the world-vikara be accounted for in the case of Brahman that is niravayava, without parts? He replies: that is not a defect, for just like the rope, etc. are sourced from the 'parts' of the rope, etc. (adhishthana), so too by one's buddhi parikalpana, the Sat, Brahman-parts the world-vikara is definitely possible. The Shruti pramana is: vAcArambhaNam vikAro nAmadheyam, mruttiketyeva satyam.



So Shankara holds that the world is a buddhiparikalpana on Brahman. The niravayava Brahman is wrongly seen to be sAvayava world.



Ø There are two different things here (a) nirvishesha / niravayava brahman and other is (b) sAvayava / savishesha jagat. Both needs to be understood as presented by shruti. There is talk of creation and also there is a talk of nirvikAri/niravayava/nishkriya brahman in shruti. And with regard to jagat shruti itself says jagat which is savishesha has indeed come from ‘brahman’ only who is partless!!. Ofcourse brahma hetuka srushti is ‘vadAnta maryAda’ as per bhAshyakAra himself. So we cannot so easily deny this part and keep only nirvikAri brahman. We cannot keep half hen for laying eggs and half for eating (ardha kukkuti nyAya). This scenario, that origination of sAvayava/savishesha jagat from nirvishesha/nishkriya brahman is very difficult to understand with limited intelligence and as a result we tempted to resort to only
partial / subjective holding of some shruti statements and ignoring major portions of shruti verdict. To reconcile this bhAshyakAra in the above statement says: just as the imagined parts of a rope give room to see a snake, the imagined parts of brahman could be the cause to see the diversified Jagat. This (a) i.e. seeing the parts in partless brahman is mere buddhi parikalpana which is only done by us (the jeeva) due to naisargika / svAbhAvika avidyA. But the situation with regard (b) that is sAvayava / savishesha jagat is different. The main intention here is to convey the message of the shruti / sUtra that though the world has emanated from brahman, the brahman is still transactionless (nishkriya /sarva vyavahArAteeta). So, brahman is wrongly seen to be sAvayava world or imagining the parts in partless is avidyA drushti of the jeeva BUT sAvayava jagat eminates from niravayava brahman is shruti siddhAnta. As said above both are different. And as a result we have to say : world as such is buddhiparikalpana OF brahman (so kAmayata, sa Ekshata, brahma mAnasa pratyaya), and the world IS NOT buddhiparikalpana ON brahman by the tiny soul jeeva. If the bhAshya vakya interpreted in such a way that world for which brahman is the ONLY abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa is mere buddhiparikalpita on brahman or just an imagination by the conditioned jeeva, then it is obviously shruti, yukti and anubhava viruddha.





Brahman resolved: I shall be born as many, just as clay becomes many pots, etc. and just as rope, etc. take the form of snake, etc. - all due to buddhiparikalpana.Thus Shankara is very clear that the world is a buddhikalpana on the same lines as the rope-snake is a buddhikalpana.



Ø Again it is brahma parikalpana that he shall become many ( dhAta yathA pUrvamakalpayatu) that which is already there in brahman in unmanifest (avyAkruta) form make it as manifest ( vyAkruta) it is, the jagat which is available for all practical purposes equally to jnAni and ajnAni is not the fancy imagination of the jeeva who sees the snake/crack/garland/urine etc in place of really existing thing. It is decided by brahman that he should born as many like the rope born as parts of the imaginary snake it is the statement attributed to Brahman by shruti and not of jeeva. Therefore, it means that it is the mental thought of the name forms in hiraNyagarbha /aparabrahman. the latter mantra-s in that upanishad substantiate this. In order to create the nAma rUpa he recalled in His mind the jeeva-s who lived in the previous cycle of srushti. The Ishwara hetuka srushti is for jeeva-s to experience their karma phala and for which Ishwara is nimitta upAdAna kAraNa not the jeeva’s imagination of serpent in place of rope.

The clinching term here is: buddhiparikalpitena, with applies equally to both the snake and the world.



Ø Yes but the world is brahma parikalpana and snake on the rope is due to avidyA of the jeeva. Both cannot be compared illogically. It is because of this reason for the kArya also bhAshyakAra granted the same status of kAraNa and termed both as trikAlAbhAdhita. If the world ( both in its vyAkruta / avyAkruta rUpa) mere imagination of the jeeva he would not have said that. In su.bh.2-1-16 : Just as brahman the cause never deviates from existence in all the three periods of time, so also the effect, the world never deviates from existence in all the three periods ( srushti, sthiti and vinAsha). And existence again is ONLY one. So for this reason also the EFFECT IS NONE OTHER THAN THE CAUSE. If we place the imaginary serpent in place of world one can see the repercussions, serpent will be there always and it is as real as rope !!
