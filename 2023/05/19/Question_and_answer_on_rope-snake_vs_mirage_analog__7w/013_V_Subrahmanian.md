The bhashya word 'buddhi parikalpitena' cannot be interpreted as above.
The reason is: Brahman has no buddhi. The Mundaka says: aprANo hyamanaaH shubhrah....where the body-mind-organs complex is denied for Brahman.
These are there for the jiva only. So, the bhashya word 'buddhi parikalpitena' is definitely jiva-buddhi-parikalpitena.

This is not the correct understanding. The sad-buddhi and asad-buddhi have been very clearly delineated by Shankara in Bh.gita bhashya 2.16. That buddhi which is avyabhicharita is called sat in that shloka and that which is vyabhicharita is called asat. For this Shankara gives examples: san ghatah, san patah, san hasti..So the statement made in BSB 2.1.16यथाचकारणंब्रह्मत्रिषुकालेषुसत्त्वंनव्यभिचरति,एवंकार्यमपिजगत्त्रिषुकालेषुसत्त्वंनव्यभिचरति।
means: just as the Karanam Sat, Brahman, does not go out of existence in all the three periods of time, so too the jagat does not give up sattvam. That is, in all periods, the jagat is experienced as 'the jagat IS' and not as 'jagat is not'. This only means that the jagat depends upon Brahman for its sattA.

  

Shankara concludes the 2.16 bhashyam thus:

#### श्रीमद्भगवद्गीताभाष्यम्द्वितीयोऽध्यायःश्लोक १६- भाष्यम्

………तत्त्वदर्शिनां दृष्टिमाश्रित्य शोकं मोहं च हित्वा शीतोष्णादीनि नियतानियतरूपाणि द्वन्द्वानि ‘विकारोऽयमसन्नेव मरीचिजलवन्मिथ्यावभासते’ इति मनसि निश्चित्य तितिक्षस्व इत्यभिप्रायः ॥ १६ ॥ ………

  

He gives the mirage-water example to the world of duality and says: just as the non-existent water appears to exist in a mirage, so too the world of dualities only appears to exist: asanneva mithya avabhAsate.

  

Shankara has given the clarification for the 'sattvam' of jagat in the Chandogya 6th chapter, cited already: Just as the agni, etc. are not real as themselves but only real as their cause, so too the jagat is not real as itself, but real as its cause. He draws this conclusion based on the shruti statement: Chandogya 6.4.4: apAgAt jagato jagattvam.
This statement very clearly says that the world has lost is world-ness, and the Cause Brahman, Sat, alone is real:  

  

यदि सर्वं जगत् त्रिवृत्कृतमिति अग्न्यादिवत् त्रीणि रूपाणीत्येव सत्यम् , *अ**ग्नेरग्नित्ववत्**अपागाज्जगतो जगत्त्वम्।* तथा अन्नस्याप्यप्शुङ्गत्वात् आप इत्येव सत्यं वाचारम्भणमात्रमन्नम्। तथा अपामपि तेजःशुङ्गत्वात् वाचारम्भणत्वं तेज इत्येव सत्यम्। तेजसोऽपिसच्छुङ्गत्वात् वाचारम्भणत्वं सदित्येव सत्यम् इत्येषोऽर्थो विवक्षितः।  

  

So, this settles beyond doubt that the B.S.B.2.1.16 statement that jagat does not lose is sattvam really means: jagat has no jagattvam but only Sat alone is real. This is the vivakshitArtha says Shankara.
