As far as I understand, yes there is validity for rope-snake analogy where snake represents the jagat seen in ignorance (of having existence different or independent of Brahman) and which goes away in knowledge.



Ø Yes absolutely right, the snake seen only in ignorance, the jagat seen in ignorance as having the independent existence (svatantra Astitva) or different from brahman ( seeing the jagat is abrahma / asarva). This is called avidyA drushti, parichinna drushti or asarvAtma bhAva of the conditioned soul.



The duality that remains in the cognition of a jnani but which he knows to be nothing independent (only appearance/denotation) of Brahman, that is mithya.



- jnAni’s drushti here is samyaK drushti or sarvAtma bhAva. When one
  realizes that there was / is / never be snake on the rope his drushti
  is Atmaikatva drushti he is not going see snake anymore. And in
  clay-pot or suvarNAbharaNa analogy, when he comes to know
  mruttiketyeva satyaM ( only mrut sAmAnya is really real) he realizes
  this truth even though he is using the clay pot/jug in vyavahAra for
  all practical purposes since Advaita jnana is NOT vyavahAra abhAva
  jnana but vyavahAra bhAdita jnana.



It is there in our relative standpoint but not there in the absolute standpoint of Brahman.



- Yes, it is available for the relative standpoint hence it is called
  vyAvahArika satya and bhAshyakAra says this temporary satya of the
  transactions also derived its reality from pAramArthika satya. Like
  loukika sukha / vishaya sukha is part and parcel of AtmAnanda /
  anantAnanda.



For an analogy, there is appearance of water vs chair in our relative standpoint - even if we know (as per physics) that both are fundamentally only manifestation of energy; but from the standpoint of energy itself, such duality is non-existent since there is only itself - beyond words and descriptions.



Ø Yes prabhuji completely agreed. From the stand point of nAma rUpa (kArya) all ornaments are gold (kAraNa)only but in gold ( or from gold stand point of view) there is no such difference as it is always nirvikAri gold only.



1.  Ignorance: There is water, there is chair. Both are
    \[fundamentally\] different.



\> If I can add to this, thinking that both are fundamentally different and independently existing (apart from its substratum / adhishtAnaM) is the ignorance or avidyA drushti.



2.  Knowledge: There is only Energy that appears in different nama-rupa,
    call it water or chair.



Ø Yes, it is gold only which is used as ring, bangle, bracelet etc. but its substratum is gold only even when we are wearing it at different parts of our body.



3.  Energy alone is - beyond words and thought. Absolute non-duality.

- Energy alone is (3) is the knowledge (2).
