Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

A counter-understanding is this.

  

The rope-in-dull-light, the thought of snake and the thought that the mind-snake seen in the mind is the rope-object seen outside are All 'equally real' objects of cognition.

  

Adhyasa is the light of consciousness illumining the last thought (whose roots are anaadi) whose meaning content is false, alongside illumining the cognized rope-in-dull-light. This thought therefore stands as the obstruction to right knowledge of the rope. It gets removed from cognition when the rope is seen for what it is, with the aid of guru, shastra, sadhana and Ishvara anugraha. After knowledge of rope, if at all the snake thought arises in the mind, the Adhyasa thought does not arise (except at worst superficially, some argue), and we know the snake only as a mind projection

  

When we replace rope with Brahman, we have to say the world-appearance as well as the thoughts in the mind are all projections of Brahman+Maya. Therefore He is the upadana karana even for the adhyasa experienced by jivas. Once He is known as the non-dual reality, the adhyasa thought is forever de-rooted from the mind and is no longer projected as part of that mind (except again superficially at worst, some argue) because the perfect clarity and illumination of the Knowledge of non-dual Self leaves no space for adhyasa to take root again.

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
