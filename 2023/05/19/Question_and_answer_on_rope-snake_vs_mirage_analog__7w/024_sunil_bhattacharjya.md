Dear Subbuji,

  

**This verse is available in the Original Bhagavad Gita of 745 verses**, Unfortunately, Bhaskara Bhatta, a scholar from Kashmir dropped 45 verses from the Bhagavad Gita in the 8th century AD and wrote a commentary of the **Vulgate version (i.e., the commonly circulated version of 700 verses from that time**)  to promote his Jnana-Karma-Samucchaya view. Shri Abhinava Shankaracharya, who was born in 788 AD, and became the Mathadhipati of the Kanchi Kamakoti math, wrote the Shankara bhashya on the same vulgate version and refuted the Jnana-Karma Samucchaya views of Jnana-Karma Samucchaya, to show that even the vulgate version also does not support the Jana-Karma Smucchaya. For that task, Shri Abhinava Shankara also referred to the Anugita etc, from the Mahabharata for additional support.  

  

Those scholars, who have no immediate access to the Original Bhagavad Gita of 745 verses, can read Lord Krishna's Uddhava Gita, which also a verse, with the same content, saying that the Vyavaharika world is illusory.

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
