Hare Krishna



When we replace rope with Brahman, we have to say the world-appearance as well as the thoughts in the mind are all projections of Brahman+Maya.



- I would like to say for our understanding it is better to
  differentiate between Ishwara srushti and jeeva srushti. World’s
  existence / appearance (first in unmanifest form (before srushti) and
  then in manifest form (after srushti)) is in the mind of
  hiraNyagarbha based on jeevas’ past deeds. There is an exclusive
  discussions with regard to this when the purpose of creation is
  discussed in sUtra bhAshya. For the appearing jagat in vyAkruta rUpa,
  brahman is the abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa. Vivarta for which
  adhishtAnaM is brahman and only brahman. Where as, thoughts in the
  mind of the jeeva for which upAdAna (if at all we can say that) is
  mAyAvikAra. Hence it has been mentioned that jagat (rope) is
  brahmamAnasa pratyaya (mAya-nAma/rUpa) and for the snake ( jeeva
  buddhiparikalpita/jeeva mAnasa pratyaya) the rope (Ishwara srushti)
  would be the shelter. From this it has been differentiated mAya from
  avidyA/adhyAsa. Sri SSS somewhere says for misconception adhyAsa
  (cognition of snake) the cause is existing rope (vAkruta jagat) not
  directly the nirvikAri brahman. He further explains, if the whole
  thing is mere buddhi parikalpita of the conditioned jeeva without any
  ‘sAdrushyaM’ then one would have imagined rajata in place of rope,
  snake in place of shukti or for that matter any damn thing at any time
  without depending on already ‘existing’ thing !! But, as you know,
  that is not lOkAnubhava.





Therefore He is the upadana karana even for the adhyasa experienced by jivas.



- IMHO, not so!! adhyAsa is the karaNa dOsha for which locus has not
  been discussed as it is anAdiranantO naisargikOdhyAsaH. And for the
  teaching purpose, using the analogy rope-snake, it has been explained
  existing rope gives room to wrong cognition of rope. OTOH, for the
  vyAkruta jagat (rope) shruti says brahman is the adhishtAna and he is
  antaryAmi, nimitta as well as upAdAna and the snake in place of rope
  is kevala avidya / adhyArOpita.



Once He is known as the non-dual reality, the adhyasa thought is forever de-rooted from the mind and is no longer projected as part of that mind (except again superficially at worst, some argue) because the perfect clarity and illumination of the Knowledge of non-dual Self leaves no space for adhyasa to take root again.



- Just curious to know in the above where would you like to accommodate
  traditionalists ‘avidyAlesha’ (traces of ignorance).
