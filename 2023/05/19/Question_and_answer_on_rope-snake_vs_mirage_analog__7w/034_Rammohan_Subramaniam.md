Dear Vedantins

Hari Om

  

Between the Rope-snake and Dessert Mirage examples a peculiar observation stands out which is worth exploring.  

The Dessert projects a mirage due to proximity of water vapor on its surface. The Mirage is perceived by almost all people no exceptions. This example is closer to Jagat being projected on Brahman/Iswara.  

  

The Rope does not project a snake but snake is a super imposition on the rope *by the individual.* This is specific to only the individual.
This example is closer to Jiva Bhava and Jiva srushti on the Jagat projected by Brahman.

  

If we keep these points in mind, most of the discordance noticed in applying these examples will be minimized. One should of course apply them appropriately else we will see challenges in their application.  

  

In his Seva

Om and with Prem

Shri Rammohan
