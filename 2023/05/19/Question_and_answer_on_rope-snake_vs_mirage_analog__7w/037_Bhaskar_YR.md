From the standpoint of vyavaharika, prathibasika is mithya. From the standpoint of knowledge of paramarthika, both vyavaharika (~ seen is jagat) and prathibasika (~ seen is jiva kalpana) are known as mithya - because all such duality based on the seer-seen divide are negated/non-existent (like the snake) in the absolute non-dual standpoint of Brahman. The seer-seen divide is not a real viseshanaof Brahman (pure Consciousness); such a dualistic division in Consciousness can best be known to the ‘seer’ as the projection or imagination of that Consciousness itself, due to or in conjunction with Maya-shakti. The viseshana that characterizes Brahman as jagat (both vyavaharika and prathibasika), the viseshana that exists only in the context of the seer-seen divide *imagined* on/by Brahman, is not intrinsic reality of Brahman nor is it absolutely unreal (for it is imagined “by the Lord”). It is therefore mithya.
