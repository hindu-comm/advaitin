Hare Krishna



With all due respects to post shankara Acharya-s and their contribution to the tradition, I would like to say that MS’s AS is purely a dialectical work and it is an attempt to refute the dvaitins’ (Sri Jayateertha’s nyAyasudha) objections on Advaita Vedanta. In this attempt Sri MS forced to take deviation from shankara’s mUla bhAshya at some point of discussion with dvaitins. Sri Vidyashankara prabhuji ( moderator of Advaita-L) too observed this in his web page. Sri SSS and Prof. SKR too of the same opinion when they are calling ‘go back to shankara’ to understand shankara’s Advaita vedAnta. Just for information and I am not discouraging the study of these works by any means. 



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

bhaskar
