Dear Venkat ji,

  

Upon a search in the Advaitasharadafor any such words Bhaskar ji suggested, I got this result, though not exactly what he suggested but about the charama sakshatkara, a vritti required at the time of death of the Aparoksha jnani, which would make him videha mukta:

  

न च वृत्त्युपलक्षित आत्माजीवन्मुक्तावप्यस्तीतितदापि मोक्षापत्तिः; मुक्तिमात्रापादनस्येष्टत्वात् परममुक्तेश्चरमसाक्षात्कारोपलक्षितात्मस्वरूपत्वेन तदापादकाभावात्। न च चरमसाक्षात्कारनिवृत्तेरात्मत्वेऽसाध्यत्वापत्तिः; अविद्यानिवृत्तेरसाध्यत्वेऽप्रवृत्त्यापत्तिवत् अत्र तदभावात्। न चजीवन्मुक्तिप्रयोजकवृत्त्यपेक्षयापरममुक्तिप्रयोजकवृत्तौ आनन्दाभिव्यक्तिगतविशेषाभावे चरमक्षणेन चरमश्वासेन वा उपलक्षित आत्मा मुक्तिरिति किं न स्यादिति वाच्यम् ; प्रारब्धकर्मप्रयुक्तविक्षेपविक्षेपाभ्यामभिव्यक्तिविशेषस्याङ्गीकारात्।  

  

It looks like there is a requirement of another akhandakaravrittiat the time of death in order to destroy the avidya lesha that sustained the jnani-body prarabdha anubhava vikshepa (though the avarana has been destroyed by the earlier sakshatkara the vikshepa aspect of avidya continues till the fall of the body. Hence in order to mark the destruction of this aspect of avidya, the charama kshana vritti is required). This idea, as far as I know, is not advocated by the prasthana traya bhashya. The Siddhi commentary, at the beginning of the Siddhi makes this proposition.

  

warm regards

subbu
