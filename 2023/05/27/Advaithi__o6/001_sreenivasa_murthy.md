Slight modification of your statement " Only Brahman exists,all other is Maya" :

"Only aham exists, all other is aham only"

Mantra 2 of MAndukya upanishad says :" sarvagM hi Etad brahma ayaMAtmA brahma".

Then where is the place for a second thing mAyA?

Will you kindly clarify?

  

With respectful pranams,
