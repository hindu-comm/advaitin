Dear Sir,

  

A few years ago, I too had such strong fixed ideas ( a better word is : beliefs) of Neo Vedanta, traditional Vedanta etc. But now as I hear Sarvapriyanandaji’s talks I find that such beliefs lose their grip on your mind. All such labelling is actually an impediment to the progress. In fact the teaching of the Advaita is to dissolve all the beliefs.

  

Vivekananda does not deviate from the core doctrine of the Advaita. He just emphasises a new way which is appropriate to the contemporary milieu ie the dominant scientific view. From a practical standpoint he recommends Karmayoga as a valid spiritual practice. It is only the shifting of the emphasis that the traditionalists find somewhat contradictory.

  

Dilip
