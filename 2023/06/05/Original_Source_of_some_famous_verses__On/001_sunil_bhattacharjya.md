Dear friends,  

  

There is one variant of the fourth verse as follows:

  

karAgre vasate lakSmIH karamadhye sarasvatI \|  
karamUle vasate Gauri prabhAte karadarzanama \|\|

  

**Note:** In Tantra, Lord Krishna is the male roopa of Mother Kaali, who is the another roopa of Mother Gauri, which she assumed for creating the world.  

  

Yours

Sunil K.Bhattacharjya
