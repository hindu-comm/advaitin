Hare Krishna



As I have already said innumerable times as per Advaita, if at all there is srushti the cause is ONLY Ishwara NOT jeeva. And as per bhAshyakAra treating Ishwara as the cause for the creation is ‘vedAnta maryAda’.
Some would argue, if each and everything comes from brahman ( since it has been there as ‘secondless’ before the creation) the jeeva, jagat, jeeva’s parikalpana on Ishwara srushti, jeeva’s avidyA/adhyAsa everything should come from brahman only since there is no second entity through which we can think of jeeva’s avidyA and his / her avidyAkalpita jagat. To understand this we have to go back to bhAshya clarification only. bhAshyakAra clarifies avidyA is anAdi and Ananta and it is svAbhAvika no need to find any cause for it!! If someone says 2x2=8, it means he does not know the tables, no sane person would ask why and how he does not know tables because everyone knows due to ‘abhAva’ of the jnana of tables he is saying ‘8’ (adhyAsa / misconception / anyathAgrahana). So asking for the reason for the avidyA, asking for the srushti of avidyA, asking for the locus of avidyA etc. are quite mute in advaita. However, bhAshyakAra when explaining the jeeva, jeeva’s avidyA / adhyAsa, his karma phala etc. postulates avidyA to jeeva only and srushti of jagat to Ishwara only and explains he (Ishwara) is avidyA vinirmukta and he is nitya shuddha buddha mukta svarupa always. Because of this bifurcation some of the traditionalists would not treat avidyA as synonym to mAya. mAya is Ishwarashakti and avidyA is karaNa dOsha of the jeeva. So, in the srushti scenario, at the time of mahA pralaya ( final dissolution ) what happens to the jeeva-s who are yet to get the jnana whether they go with their avidyA and merge in Ishwara ?? To avoid the akruta abhyAgama and kruta praNAsha dosha bhAshyakAra says avidyAvanta jeeva-s too move into the Ishwara and re-appear next kalpArambha, Ishwara does this srushti (prepares the stage called jagat) for the sake of jeeva-s to experience their ‘karma phala’ and to get knowledge also. Here is the bhAshya reference with regard to this from anumAnAdhikaraNaM sUtra bhAshya ( English translation from Sri SSS’s work ) :



// quote //

What is admitted by us is, however, only a previous state of the world dependent on the supreme Lord, not independent of Him. And it has to be necessarily admitted for it serves a purpose. To explain : without it the supreme lord cannot be a creator, since without this potentiality, he cannot be active. And on this supposition ony, THE RELEASED SOULS CANNOT BE REBORN, for in this case the causal potentiality is burnt up by vidyA. The causal potentiality is called avyakta ( the unmanifest) having its locus in the supreme lord is the illusory universal sleep, IN WHICH ALL THE TRANSMIGRATORTY SOULS ARE SLEEPING DEPRIVED OF THE KNOWLEDGE OF THEIR TRUE NATURE.

//unquote//



Please note this quote given just to say the point in mahA sushupti jeeva with their avidya will be there. And it is not the joyous point to prove avyakta is avidyA and avidyA has the Ashraya in brahman 😊 since avyakta is effect / causal potentiality WITH IT JEEVA’S AVIDYA ALSO THERE. So, no need for any confusion here to say that vidyA will burn the causal potentiality so jagat after the dawn of jnana would turn into ashes 😊 Why we should not draw this conclusion ( i.e. avyakta is avidyA/mAya) is because elsewhere bhAshyakAra says mAya is prakruti and mAyin in Ishwara and Ishwara is not avidyAvanta and mAya is not avidyA 😊 And therefore it must be concluded this manifold world of the experienced and experiences is not other than brahman in essence. In the jagadaadikaraNa shankara clarifies this how jeeva is not even imagine this grand universe and how the cause is brahman who is sarvajna and sarvashakta. Sri SSS’s translation :



//quote//



The omniscient and omnipotent cause from which proceeds the origin, sustentation and dissolution of this universe, the universe which is differentiated by name and form, comprehends many agents and experiencers (of the fruit of action) and is the abode of the fruits of actions regulated by particular places times and causes (the universe) whose creation IS NOT EVEN CONCEIVABLE BY THE MIND (OF THE JEEVA) THAT CAUSE IS BRAHMAN.

//unquote//
