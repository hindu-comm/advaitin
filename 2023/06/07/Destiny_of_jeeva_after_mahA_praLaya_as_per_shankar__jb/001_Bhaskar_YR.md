An addendum to this mail :



The process and method of creation is not the prime purpose of shruti but shruti by explaining all these events to drive home the point that brahman is ekam eva adviteeyam and there exists nothing but brahman.
The creation which is taught in various ways by means of illustrations like that of clay, metal and sparks is only a device for the purpose of leading the mind to the secondless truth there is no diversity on any account. bhAshyakAra observes in 2-1-27 : Nor does this shruti teaching evolution really purport to teach evolution as such, for no fruit is known to accrue by that knowledge. It rather aims at teaching the Atman or brahmic Nature, for, its knowledge is known to yield a good result.
Here only shankara clarifies neti neti should end in brahman and not in shUnya. But if any one dared to say this whole universe is kevala buddhi parikalpita of the jeeva like Buddhists do, then shruti / shankara vehemently objects this view and uphold the Ishwara hetuka srushti only.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
