Dear friends ,  

Mumukshus who want to be Atmavits are requested to ponder over

the upadesha and become kRutArthAs / Atmavits.

Quote:

When a person assumes this point of view from which alone he can survey all the regions of life, Viz., waking, dreaming and sleep, he has risen to a a position in which he is no longer the empirical ego.

Unquote.

  

It is so simple to become a Jnani or Atmavit  

if one applies these teaching to himself within himself.

BruhadaraNyaka Sruti Says : AtmannEvAtmAnaM paSyati \|\|

  

This posting is from one who has found vast benefits  

from such upadeshas.

  

With respectful namaskars,

Sreenivasa Murthy
