Yes, this is not denied. In the Taittiriya Upanishad there is the passage: satyam cha anrutam cha satyam abhavat. For this Shankara explains as: One Paramarthika (satyam) Brahman appears as both vyavaharika (satyam) and the pratibhasika (anrtam). This Taittiriya passage is the pramana for Advaitins for the three types of reality.
Sureshwaracharya too in the Taittiriya Bhashya Vartika said this.

  

What Sri SSS clarifies is also what I have stated in the post: the difference between vyavaharika and pratibhasika is obviated by Shankara while summing up his bhashya to the verse 2.16.
