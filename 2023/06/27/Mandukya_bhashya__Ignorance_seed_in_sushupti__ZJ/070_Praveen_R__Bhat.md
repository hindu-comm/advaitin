Very well said. I see bhAShya is vishvatomukha and has scope of more and more possibilities of explanation like sUtras. To some, these are confusing, while to some they are clarifying. I have always seen the focus of vyAkhyAs and TIkAs as the latter, meaning to clarify the genuine doubts of the seekers. The side-effect is vidvatpradarshana or vidvatmanoranjana. I find beauty in vivaraNa as well as bhAmati pakShas without compromising mUlabhAShya and the end advaita goal. The issue is only when there is focus on petty namecalling or when they are pitted against each other. No seekerreally needs to focus on it and lose heart!
