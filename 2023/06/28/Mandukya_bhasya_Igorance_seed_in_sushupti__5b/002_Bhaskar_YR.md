Thanks for collecting all the relevant quotes and presenting it here.
But do you know something!!?? Traditionalists would assert this this is mere partial reading of the bhAshya and reality is something else. To differentiate prAjna from tureeya they hold the mAdukya mantra 7, kArika and bhAshya relevant to this. And some other places in bhAshya itself bhAshyakAra implies that there exists avidyA, for example in [su.bh](http://su.bh). 3.2.9, iha tu vidyate viveka kAraNam karma cha avidyA cha. And here in this bhAshya itself bhashyakAra observes that if avidyA is not present there it would mean that they have attained mukti just by going into the deep sleep!! In that case nobody wants to do sAdhana if mukti / moksha / ikyata available just by getting the sleep. And there is always difference between anandamaya (prajna) and anandaswarupa (tureeya). Anyway all these objections have been dealt in detail in Sri SSS gaudapAda hruday, rahasya vivruttiH etc.
