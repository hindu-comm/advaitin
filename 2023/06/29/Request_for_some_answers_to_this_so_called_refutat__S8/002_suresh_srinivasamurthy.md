Namaste Subbu-ji,

  

I think the author is trying to justify "atat tvam asi" (or bheda) as opposed to "tat tvam asi" (or abheda).

  

But personally, I think "atat tvam asi" is an apasiddhanta and indirectly leads to identification with nirguna brahman only 🙂

It is only teaching non-identification with saguna brahman or Ishwara which is vyAvahArika.

The interpretation of tvam tadadheenosi (you are dependent on that) also supports Advaitic Self which can be said to be

dependent on it's own mahima! There is BhAsya vAkya in support of that.

  

Also, the examples of Sea-rivers and Honey-juices does not fully support dvaita because the rivers and juices have lost  

their name/form/individual identifications. When the sea is identified, all rivers are identified. Same way with honey.

Likewise when the Supreme Self / ParamAtma is identified, everything (the entire universe/sarva) including the jiva is identified.

  

In V.Advaita it is split as tat tvam asi only - But here, tat points to Brahman as antaryami of prakriti and tvam points Brahman

as antaryAmi of jiva. But ultimately it is accepted as the supreme Self only.

  

When the sarvottama Vishnu is identified as the "All pervading and indwelling Self" - Dvaita aligns with Advaita.

IMHO, the Advaitic Self continues to prevail either way!

  

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
