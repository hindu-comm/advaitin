Dear friends,

  

Please correct me if I am wrong, I have the impression that most of the Advaitin teachers today imagine that Adi Shankara had written the book "Saundaryalahari", but they had not been able to say as to how they arrived at that conclusion.  

  

In fact,to my understanding, the first portion consisting of 41 verses of that book is called "Anandalahari", was composed by a brahmin king. The second portion of that book, consisting of 59 verses of that book, is called "Saundaryalahari", and that was composed by the great Navavatara, Shri Abhinava Shankaracharya, who was born in the 8th century AD.  

  

I am sure, Adi Shankaracharya, a great lover of truth, is surely most unhappy with the people trying to give false credit to him (i.e., to Adi Shankara) for the book "Anandalahari".  

  

Regards,

Sunil K. Bhattacharjya
