Some thoughts from the perspectiveof a Vedantin:

  

Intelligence may be understood as the inherent power (Maya/shakti) of the Self to *know* its manifest state of identification; to *determine* good and bad, right and wrong, possible and impossible, in the context of that state of identification; to *desire* the good, right, possible; and to *direct* its manifest state through action towards that desired future.

  

Thus it is the Intelligence of the Self that ‘creates’ this body-mind-world (or, by exercising its Intelligence that the Self manifests). The BMW is the actualization of Intelligence. The activities of Intelligence, in terms of knowledge of objects, determination, desire and direction of action, are also ‘photographed’, so to speak, within the BMW. For instance, when one attains knowledge of a chair, it gets ‘registered’ in the body-mind through corresponding modifications.

  

The causal arrow is from Self(+Intelligence) to BMW manifestation. “If the Self decides thus, then the BMW change will be this. If the BMW is this, then the Self had decided thus.” The BMW is only the jada expression (appearance, effect) of the self-effulgent Self by recourse to its Intelligence.

  

The Purvapakshin, the charvaka, believes the causal arrow goes in the other direction. He places his bets on the reality of what we deem Ignorance. That the jiva-self that is identified by Ignorance with the BMW can be controlled by modifying the B-W. Thus, the charvaka seeks to predict the Self’s future decisions by mapping and decoding the B-W as it is presently manifest. In particular, he wants the power to control the jiva’s thought and action by impacting its B-W in a corresponding manner. What must he do to it to obtain what reaction? What must he do to its B-W to afflict and change its mind, its intelligence, its sense of self, its svadharma? How can he play on its fears and desires and turn it his slave? For to the charvaka, the foundation of self is the body-world and if he can force his will on the latter, he believes he can control (define) the jiva that is identified with it.

  

Is the Self a reactionary Robot whose intelligence is ‘Artificial’? Can we control the Intelligence of the Self (artificially) by forcing according to our wishes the form of the B-W? (Of course, even to thus modify the B-W as experienced by a jiva, the cause is the Intelligence of the Self as expressed through other jivas or Ishvara.) Is the jiva’s svadharma contingent on the B-W (via the causal sequence, BW -\> Mind
-\> Dharma -\> Intelligence -\> Self)? To what extent does the Jnani become free from the bondage of the BMW that he is beyond the purview and reach of AI? To what extent is he also like the man of ignorance who is identified with BMW and whose thoughts and actions can be controlled through the ‘external’ control of the B-W?

  

As we move into a world in which the charvaka develops, refines and focuses science and technology explicitly for entrapping the jiva by its bondage and ignorance, the Vedantin (the Hindu, the man of God) has to discern and reject the underlying principles and philosophy of the charvaka that are rooted in Ignorance. He has to stay rooted in the eternal truth of the immutable Self and as well the causal priority of the Intelligence of the Self over the manifest BMW.
