*As we move into a world in which the charvaka develops, refines and focuses science and technology explicitly for entrapping the jiva by its bondage and ignorance, the Vedantin (the Hindu, the man of God) has to discern and reject the underlying principles and philosophy of the charvaka that are rooted in Ignorance. He has to stay rooted in the eternal truth of the immutable Self and as well the causal priority of the Intelligence of the Self over the manifest BMW.*  

  

The first step to remain rooted in the causal priority of the Self is to be convinced that perception is not occasioned by a stimulus-response process but that it is by the luminosity of the Self shining on objects that we perceive them (objects).
