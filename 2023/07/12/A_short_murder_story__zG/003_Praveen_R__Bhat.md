> B: I suppose you could look at all this as a metaphor for ignorance

The death of the body is really abhAva of life, the kAraNa for life being intake of food, etc, whereas saMsAra is bhAvarUpa. This is the vaiShamyadoSha. And the example of kAraNa food, etc, produces kArya of life of the body has merely a corollary that without kAraNa food, there will not a kArya of life, otherwise called death. There is no kArya of death per se due to a abhAvarUpa non-existent kAraNa food!
