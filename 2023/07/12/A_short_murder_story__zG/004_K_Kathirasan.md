Namaste Chandramouli ji,

  

I agree that it is a valuable reference for both but an indispensable guide for the teacher more than anything else. To support this position, I lean on the evidence for the method of teaching unfolded in the Upadeshasahari, in which Shankara suggests nowhere that one should study the Bhashya-s to know the Self. Other similar, but less dense, texts such as the Vakyavrtti and Tattvopadesha also support the view, though implicitly, that the Bhashya-s are not necessary to know the Self.

  

Warmest Regards,

**Kathirasan K**
