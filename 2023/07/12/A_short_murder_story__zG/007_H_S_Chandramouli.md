Namaste Kathirasan Ji,

According to many teachers there is no need for any Bhashyas at all. Nor for that matter, even recourse to any Shrutis at all for Realization.

According to Sri Bhagavatpada, the need for vichAra as the means for Realization cannot be over-emphasized. Such vichAra is enabled only through the study of the Bhashyas because we understand the Shrutis themselves only through the Bhashyas. As such, the Bhashyas have as much validity/authority as the Shrutis themselves in conveying their purport. Even as the sAdhaka learns from the teacher who surely conveys the ideas as enshrined in the Bhashyas, he needs to absorb the same through constant recollection and analysis of what is taught. For this the sAdhaka should constantly be in touch with the Bhashya. I am not suggesting that ALL the Bhashyas must be studied and absorbed as part of the sAdhanA. Depending upon the maturity of the sAdhaka the need may vary.

This is my understanding.

Best Regards
