Point to note....

No buddhijeevi can go back in time to aadikaala .

It is either

Shruti pramaana

Or

अनादित्वेन कल्पितः।

For purpose of prakriyaa...only.

Shruti also does not have विवक्षा =\> तात्पर्य in detailing out सृष्टिः।

Jagat, Avidya, maayaa .., is, was, and never will be there in paramarthika satta

Om

LAKSHMEESHA
