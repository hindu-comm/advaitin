Hare Krishna



anAdiranantO naisargikOdhyAsaH. Yes, avidyA has an untraceable beginning!! And we have to accept it as we are suffering from avidyA 😊 OTOH, if we say that the avidyA has a beginning at some particular point of time, it would give an impression that HE who was Brahman previously, who was one without second at the very beginning, somehow forgot about his svarUPa and got the anishta called avidyA and become jeeva!\!😊 If brahman itselfs should lose the knowledge of its own svarUpa, It cannot be brahman at all. Therefore, avidyA is beginningless and abhAva rUpa as well but it meets its end with the dawn of vidyA.
And we should not forget the fact that in brahman there is no vyavahAra called vidyAvidya, it is only at the jeeva level who has the avidyA that lack of his svarUpa jnana (inAnAbhAva) and as a result adhyAsa.



And how this adhyAsa is also Ananta!!?? Well, that is a subject matter of another discussion 😊



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
