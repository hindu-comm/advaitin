Srshti, presupposes maayaa or Avidya.

If there is no pre existing maaya...there is no NIMITTA for srshti.

If Avidya is taken to have a beginning,

All jeevas who got janma were muktas before Avidya got created.

So

अनिर्मोक्षप्रसङ्गः।

मुक्ता ऽ will become बद्धाः।

There is no logical way of concluding that maayaa, avidyaa has a beginning.

In fact... 😀 ईश्वरः 's ईश्वरत्व IS ALSO अनादिः, सान्तः।
