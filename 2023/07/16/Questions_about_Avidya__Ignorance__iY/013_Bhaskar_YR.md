praNAms Sri Vishwanath prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Is this Avidya/Ignorance aroused at any time or is it Beginningless?



Ø When we know time, space, causation etc. themselves are just superimposed (adhyasta) on brahman, we would realize brahman is nirlipta, nirmala and nirvishesha. So, if any one questions the time of avidyA, please note that adhyAsa is already swaying this question.



If Ignorance is Beginningless how could it be there in Beginningless Atman/Brahman (Sat-Chit-Ananda Satyam-Jnanam-Anantham)?



Ø As I have already mentioned there is no transactions like vidyA and avidyA in brahman. So any question and related or suitable answer can be entertained ONLY in vyAvahArika drushti and in vyavahAra there is a jnAtru/pramAtru ( the knower), jneya/prameya ( to be known as) and jnana/pramANa (the knowledge) this triputi is again adhyAsa drushti or within the sphere of avidyA. Shankara bhagavatpAda explains this in sUtra bhAshya to the question who has avidyA?? It is YOU who is asking this question, but questioner counters but am I not brahman (jeevo brahmaiva na apara) ?? for this siddhAnti answers : if you know that you are brahman then there is no avidyA / ajnAna to whomsoever. So in Advaita avidyA does not have any existence from the transcendental view point ( as we know existence has been attributed to ONLY brahman) it is abhAva of that jnana (what is really existing) we have to deal with the concept of avidyA in vyavahAra where jeeva bhAva holds sway. 



Because of this confusion, I wonder if one can really say Ignorance is Beginningless or nothing can be known about it whether aroused or Beginningless.



Ø If we are asking this question that itself means there is an apriori acceptance of existence of avidyA. So, better to accept it that we have avidyA and do sAdhana to get rid of it 😊 



Is it useless to question about Ignorance on how/why/when/what it is, but attain knowledge of Atman which is everywhere in every beings/form here and even beyond?



Ø Yes, when brahman is realized or after the dawn of svarUpa jnana, the jnAni would realize that there exists nothing, nothing apart from brahman there is no anAtma that can be identified aloof from Atman.
There was / is / ever be Atman / brahman only and I was/is/never will be under the influence of avidyA and I always brahman only.
