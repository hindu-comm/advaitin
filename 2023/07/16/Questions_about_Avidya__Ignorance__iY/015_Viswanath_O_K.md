Thank you for sharing this deep understanding Bhaskar sir. My point is exactly the same. My question is about the concept imposed upon this Ignorance who feels there is Ignorance.

  

Would you say that, this concept of "Ignorance" calling it "Beginningless/Anadi" is really useful for those who feel Ignorance remains? I feel it really not useful but only makes to remain more Ignorant.

  

Like say, even though Snake does not exist in Rope, those who perceive Snake have to examine its reality/existence and scriptures have to pave the way for that inquiry. If the Enlightened one says to that ignorant "See, Snake does not arouse but exists Beginningless and comes to end as one attains Moksha", then what would the ignorant inquirer feel is, "Oh. then snake exists and my experience is true?", and makes a concept that "Snake exists until I inquire and attain Moksha" and take that not as just a concept but as Truth itself and fail to inquire upon this Ignorance.

  

So, do you feel, creating a concept about this Ignorance (even though it really not exists to the Brahma-jnani) really helps the inquirer, or does the concept about Ignorance deceives/stops inquiry?
