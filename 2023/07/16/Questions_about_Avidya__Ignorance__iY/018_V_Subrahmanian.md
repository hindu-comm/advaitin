Dear Praveen ji,

  

What you say here is so nicely summarized by Swami Vidyaranya in the Panchadashi:

  

**चोद्यं वा परिहारो वा क्रियतां द्वैतभाषया ।**  
**अद्वैतभाषया चोद्यं नास्ति नापि तदुत्तरम् ॥ ३९ ॥**

**  **

**Question and answer can happen only in the language of duality, vyavaharika. In the Paramarthika, Advaitam, there can be no question nor any reply.**

**  **

**The Avidya of the Vedanta is also belongs to the realm of vyavahara, dvaitam. It is only not knowing this that objection to the Avidya is levelled on the grounds that 'Such an Avidya will result in Advaita haani.'**

**  **

**warm regards**

**subbu**
