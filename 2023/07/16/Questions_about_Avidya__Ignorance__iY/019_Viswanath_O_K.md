Sir, do you know what happens practically?

  

When one says "Avidya is Anadi in Vyavaharika and leads to Inquiry", one really not inquires but remain stuck with this statement.

Because, one believes "Vyavaharika" as a "Reality", it is not a fact in Advaita where it clearly says this as "Dream". If this is Dream, and even if one is Chaturpuja Vishnu in this Dream, why to make any statements about this Dream? If one keeps one give endless statements/words about this Dream, it only intensifies or create very strong belief that this Dream is Real and be bound to this belief by stoppage of inquiry.

  

Say, if one strongly believes, "I am Human Being" can one inquire about Atman?

No. The perfect example is Jiddu Krishnamurti, his belief of "I am Human Being" blocked him to inquire many deep scriptures,Advaita,Upanishads thinking "I am Human Being, and as a Human knowing Atman is useless to me/for my living".

  

These beliefs (I keep on say the Vyavaharika statements as Beliefs because these are not Real but just Dream) one fail to question in Advaita because of Bhaskara's Philosophy integrated to Advaita.

  

In Ramana Maharshi, Shankara's, and Gaudapada's, you can see many times these are pointed as Dream,Mithya,etc. It's useless to give any explanation about this Mithya, but just never care to think about any in Vyavaharika, and root out such beliefs in Heart by negation, and realize Brahman.

  

"These are Dream, Find out who you are". "Atman is not body, not mind", etc., is repeated, not for passing time, but to really take that to Heart to cut off beliefs of "I am this, that,Jiva,etc." and realize Truth.

  

And yes sir, I have no doubt about this, but I have doubt "why those in Advaita give much importance to speaking Vyavaharika when Shankara,etc. are very clear about these - For Ego? For selling books and earn respect,money,etc.? For any other stuffs?" That's what I'm doubtful about.
