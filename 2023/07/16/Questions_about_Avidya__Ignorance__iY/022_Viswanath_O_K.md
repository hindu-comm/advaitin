Sir, when it comes to Advaita, is it necessary to know what causes Dream intellectually?

  

This Ignorance, actually is a very deep question, and inquiry into it ends up in "Knowledge of Atman".

Just an intellectual answer, and skipping to probe deep into this question, only makes one to dwell in more intellectual stuffs. Instead, if one don't comes up with an answer, but keep on inquiring about that question without explanation, then Knowledge be Realized.

  

And about Dream, it's only Ajati-Vada speaks about it, but not Dhristi-Shristi Vada, because Shristi means real creation and that happens out of perception, whereas what Advaita says is "there is no real creation, only Dream out of Ignorance",

And if one asks about Ignorance, any explanation to it only blocks deep inquiry, instead if one finds the Atman to whom this Ignorance felt by negating, then such Ignorance feeling disappears when ends up in Knowledge.

  

But if explanation are given, then feeling of Ignorance only deepens within one and does not disappear and does not let one to inquire deeply about Atman, it only creates intellectual hope "answers,answers,answers", and Bliss and Advaita knowledge remains with a feel of hidden always, and only Intellectual stuffs - Ego - etc. remains.

  

Practices or Methods or Bhajan are not path of Jnana, and anyone who is in pursuit of knowledge - when adds up Methods/Practices/Yoga/Bhakthi/Penances/etc. - only gets distracted from deep inquiry and fail to attain Kaivalya Moksha by attaining knowledge but only attains other Mokshas.
