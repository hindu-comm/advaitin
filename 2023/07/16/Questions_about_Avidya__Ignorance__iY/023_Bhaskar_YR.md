Would you say that, this concept of "Ignorance" calling it "Beginningless/Anadi" is really useful for those who feel Ignorance remains? I feel it really not useful but only makes to remain more Ignorant.



Ø Frankly, I am not able to understand this conclusion!! No need to mention any talk about avidyA is within the realm of avidyA / vyavahAra kshetra only. And on that stage, somany related queries would come up, like what I said above, what is the nature of avidyA?? Who is having avidyA and since when ?? how jnana would eradicate this avidyA?? How jeeva is suffering from this avidyA in saMsAra?? How brahman whom we sought is avidyA vinirmukta etc. All these are there in the brahma jignAsa as a mananaM part. And for that we have to first admit ourselves that we have some ignorance about ourselves and we are not what we are thinking!! It is just like accepting that we are down with fever and taking some paracetamol 😊



Like say, even though Snake does not exist in Rope, those who perceive Snake have to examine its reality/existence and scriptures have to pave the way for that inquiry.



- Yes, but pointed to be noted here ‘even though’ snake does not exist
  in rope, is the knowledge that we get in post realization period but
  when we are in avidyA /adhyAsa first we see snake only without knowing
  that there is rope. So effort is required to know the rope.



If the Enlightened one says to that ignorant "See, Snake does not arouse but exists Beginningless and comes to end as one attains Moksha", then what would the ignorant inquirer feel is, "Oh. then snake exists and my experience is true?", and makes a concept that "Snake exists until I inquire and attain Moksha" and take that not as just a concept but as Truth itself and fail to inquire upon this Ignorance.



Ø I am afraid very purpose of rajju-sarpa ( rope-snake) analogy is diluted here!! What is the purpose of this analogy?? In short, this analogy is not there to prove the existence of beginningless existence of snake but to show what really exists there. The enlightened one after showing the rope in the sufficient light would say to ignorant : see what you wrongly perceiving as snake is really rope only and there was / is / never existing snake there. The snake what you were perceiving in dim light what your mithyA jnana of the existing rope. No sane person would say there exists beginningless snake which has been killed after you realized it is only rope. An enlightened person or shAstra has this limited scope only, it only helps us to show what is actually there, neither it creates anything new nor it destroys anything already existing. Here eradication with regard to mithyAjnAna/ayataartha jnAna (misconception) and the dawn of yathArtha jnana is automatically happens on its own without any effort.



So, do you feel, creating a concept about this Ignorance (even though it really not exists to the Brahma-jnani) really helps the inquirer, or does the concept about Ignorance deceives/stops inquiry?
