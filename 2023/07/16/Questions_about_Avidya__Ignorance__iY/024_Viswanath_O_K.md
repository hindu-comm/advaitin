Sir, I will repeat the point, just watch slowly and carefully.

  

Regarding the question about Ignorance, there is only one Answer. The Answer is to find out the Atman, that's all it is, because the question of Ignorance ends up in "Knowledge of Atman" itself.

  

When one says any answer like "Ignorance is Beginning-less", it not only gives an explanation but such explanation/answer stops one to inquire deeply. You may say that it is the problem of inquirer to inquire deeply, but actually it is not, the answer about Ignorance only blocks one and binds one to remain in Ignorance as if the Ignorance exists

  

"We are in Ignorance in Vyavaharika" is only a feeling out of Belief but not Truth. We/I is Atman, nd Atman is never ignorant. When we say "We/I" keep on attaching to subtle body, and remain bondage to it and such belief of "subtle body as Oneself" becomes more intensified and blocks one to know the true nature of I/Atman.

  

Actually, We/I not ignorant and only the subtle body/Mind makes one feel that One is ignorant, and Mind makes one keep on binding to Vyavaharika as a "Reality" but not realizing this as a Dream/etc.

  

One has to come out of such belief abiding to whatever subtle body/Mind makes one feel.

One may again say "So, there IS subtle body, and etc." but it's presence is not truth only an illusory depending upon spending time/etc. on it, and when comes to Advaita one has to surpass whatever this Mind/subtle body makes one believe.

  

We/I are Atman, never ignorant, and don't attach oneself to the subtle body and consider there is Ignorance.

  

When question of Ignorance arise, find out to whom it is, find out the Atman, and automatically the feeling of Ignorance vanish in Knowledge/Realizing Atman as Oneself.

Any answer/explanation, only blocks one to realize Atman by binding one to believing "I am includes subtle body which shows me or makes me believe that I am Ignorant, and make me believe that there is a Vyavaharika Reality where the Vyavaharika is only a Dream".
