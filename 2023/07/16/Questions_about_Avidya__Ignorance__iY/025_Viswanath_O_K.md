If one is in pursuit of Knowledge or Realizing Atman, what is said in Bhashyas is not necessary.

  

Why it is is, Bhashya is a battle between objections and explaining such objections. The other schools don't really pursue Advaita but only seeks explanations/answers to fight.

  

But, when you come to Advaita Knowledge itself, it's a teaching between Guru and Disciple. A Guru won't teach these explanations.

  

Likewise, those who accept and go with Advaita, these explanations are only blockage in their pursuit. Holding to it only binds one more to believing in Ignorance in attachment to Mind,etc. as oneself.

  

You can see in Vivekachudamani,Atmabodha,etc., that those explanations are not given because those are just distractions and blocks one from Realizing Atman and bind to Intellectual stuffs and bind to Mind,Ego,Anger,Etc.
