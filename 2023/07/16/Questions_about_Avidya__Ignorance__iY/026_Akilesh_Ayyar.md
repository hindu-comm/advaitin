These “explanations” are given by a teacher not as explanations but as what are called *pointers*— something within the disciple either at that moment, or later on, can use use that statement to recognize the deeper truth. This is because it is a metaphor for something in one’s experience that is exceedingly subtle and difficult to discern.

  

As Sri Suresvaracarya put it, it’s like pointing out a star by saying “it’s at the end of that branch.” It’s not literally at the end of that branch, but it assists the seeker in seeing something. The beginninglessness of ignorance is like that. It will be recognized and understood what is meant by this when the seeker is ready.
