\> Nothing can be known without intellect!

  

There is nothing to be known here other than Atman/Self, and it can be known without Intellect.

  

\> You are mixing up everything in everything. If all you have is a hammer, everything looks like a nail. Comparing Ajatavada with DSV is a comparison of apples and oranges. Ajatavada is to be contrasted with satkAraNavAda or asatkAraNavAda, not DSV or SDV please. I understand if you do not understand what I am saying here, but if you understand ajAtavAda for what it is, you shouldn't be using the dream example at all! Mandukya uses AV to establish the fact and then DSV as sAdhana leading to such fact.

  

Okay, let me give my understanding of Ajata Vada, please correct me if things are wrong.

  

Ajata Vada speaks about "No creation, no dissolution", things present here perceived is not created but an illusory appearance like Snake in Rope, etc., Snake is not created or destroyed, not born or dead. Brahman is not cause, neither has effect, and any perception is like "Water in Mirage", etc., really not created but spoken out of ignorance. It's not like Ignorance is a Cause for creation, but all these are illusory appearances of Brahman.

  

DSV only speaks like "creation of things/Jiva/etc.", whereas there is no such creation and only illusory appearances.

  

DSV is created by a Swami named Prasanthanada in 15th Century by integrating few things in other schools like "Ishwara creator,etc.", But Traditional Advaita which Shankar and Gaudapada spoke is just AV, and there is no DSV required as Sadhana.

  

Sadhana is only for Yogis, not for Knowledge, that's very sharply said by Shankara, nd not just that even in Muktika Upanishad.

  

When it comes to Knowledge, no Sadhana is here. Only Nidhidhyasana of remaining in Self in Heart in every actions here in this dream.

  

Knowledge is Knowledge, no assistance of Practice/Sadhana is required here, and such are only limiting/making a form to stick to which is not Kaivalya Moksha. Not chanting Manthra, no action sacrifice, no Bhajan, no idealizinf and attaining by concentration, nothing here.

  

Just Negate any thought/Ignorance of "I am Body,Mind,etc. " Arises, and remain in Heart even while Body,Mind acts or not acts. This is not a practice. Practice means following a set of rules or framework, here there is no such framework but only not to fall to attachment by negating. Whatever thought arises as "I am .., etc.," to not to fall to it without any practices/aid, but just negate and realize Atman/Brahman.

  

Like Rama, like Nachiketa, no practices are said to them, but just imparted Knowledge, and keep on reflecting such knowledge and attain the Truth.

  

That's why Shankara never laid down any practice or method, and very keen in repeating that in this Knowledge, only Self-Realization and no Methods/Practices/Yogic/actions can aid it.

  

Those are developments of Post-Shankara to integrate and expand the Scope.
