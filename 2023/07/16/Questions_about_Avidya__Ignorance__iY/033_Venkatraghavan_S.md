Namaste,

Just a couple of points that jumped out which are not correct.

  
  

On Tue, 18 Jul 2023, 16:59 Viswanath O K, \<[viswana...@gmail.com]()\> wrote:  

> \> Nothing can be known without intellect!
>
>   
>
> There is nothing to be known here other than Atman/Self, and it can be
> known without Intellect.

Not so, for the Shruti says मनसैवानुद्रष्टव्यं नेह नानास्ति किञ्चन, and Shankaracharya in his bhAShya explains this asमनसैव परमार्थज्ञानसंस्कृतेन आचार्योपदेशपूर्वकं च अनुद्रष्टव्यम् ।

>   
>
>   
>
> Sadhana is only for Yogis, not for Knowledge, that's very sharply said
> by Shankara, nd not just that even in Muktika Upanishad.
>
>   
>
> When it comes to Knowledge, no Sadhana is here. Only Nidhidhyasana of
> remaining in Self in Heart in every actions here in this dream.

Again not so. There is definitely a sAdhana that is mentioned for AtmajnAna -श्रोतव्यो मन्तव्यो निदिध्यासितव्यः

>   
>
> Knowledge is Knowledge, no assistance of Practice/Sadhana is required
> here, and such are only limiting/making a form to stick to which is
> not Kaivalya Moksha. Not chanting Manthra, no action sacrifice, no
> Bhajan, no idealizinf and attaining by concentration, nothing here.

jnAna sAdhana does not mean any of the things listed above - as one will know through the samanvayasUtra's bhAShya.

>   
>
> Just Negate any thought/Ignorance of "I am Body,Mind,etc. " Arises,
> and remain in Heart even while Body,Mind acts or not acts. This is not
> a practice. Practice means following a set of rules or framework, here
> there is no such framework but only not to fall to attachment by
> negating. Whatever thought arises as "I am .., etc.," to not to fall
> to it without any practices/aid, but just negate and realize
> Atman/Brahman.

  

This is also sAdhana. What is missing in the above however is the mahAvAkya.

>   
>
> Like Rama, like Nachiketa, no practices are said to them, but just
> imparted Knowledge, and keep on reflecting such knowledge and attain
> the Truth.

  

The teaching imparted to Rama and Nachiketa is the sAdhana.

>   
>
> That's why Shankara never laid down any practice or method, and very
> keen in repeating that in this Knowledge, only Self-Realization and no
> Methods/Practices/Yogic/actions can aid it.

  

Shankaracharya clearly explains what is the sAdhana that he is talking about in his bhAShya-s. If one studies his bhAShya-s properly one will know. But if one refuses to study them because they are viewed as jalpa grantha-s, how will it be possible to state with any confidence what Shankara actually says or does not say?

  

>   
>
> Those are developments of Post-Shankara to integrate and expand the
> Scope.  

I think what you are doing in the above is to expand the scope of what Shankaracharya actually said.

  

> 

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
