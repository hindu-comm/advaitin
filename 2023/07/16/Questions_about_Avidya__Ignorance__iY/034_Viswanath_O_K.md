Namaste Venkatragavan.

Ok then, what do you mean by sadhana?

For a Sadhana to be done, a goal has to be first grasped, and after grasping that - proper methods are provided to attain such a goal grasped/thought. Here the Goal is well explained in's nature, and by methods a sadhaka reach such a Goal. This is a Sadhana.

  

But, in the case of Jnana, the Goal cannot be set by Nature. It cannot be grasped at first. A Goal and Path cannot be well set here. Methods mean repetitionof something using body,etc., here it cannot be said repeating Shravana,Manana,Nidhidhyasana because Knowledge has no relation to senses,body.

Even one can contemplate such listening while washing,bathing,etc.,

  

But, a Sadhana has strict rules on how to use a body,etc.. In the case of Bhakti, there are set of rules of how the body, etc., is to be used for Pooja for the Goal/form to be attained. In the case of Karma Yoga, you may know how to act and whole concentrate on action/body. In the case of Raja Yoga, there are rules of Body, posture, determination of Goal, focusing on that, etc.,

  

Here, there are no rules for the Body. The body can do anything, even while contemplating. This cannot be termed as a Sadhana. Maybe a new word can be given for Knowledge, but Sadhana is not the right word.
