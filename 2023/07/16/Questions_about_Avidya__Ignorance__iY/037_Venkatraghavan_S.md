Namaste,

The premise of your question is unfounded. There is no requirement for the meaning of the word sAdhana to be restricted to certainpractices of the body or the mind.

  

What you are doing is restricting the meaning of the word to something that was never stated by the bhAShyakAra and then seeking for evidence from the bhAShya for such a meaning.

  

You asked for the meaning of the sAdhana that thebhAShyakAra intended. I pointed you to the section in the bhAShya where the bhAShyakAra clarifies what the sAdhana -shravaNa manana nididhyAsana - means. It does not involve any action - whether physical or mental.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
