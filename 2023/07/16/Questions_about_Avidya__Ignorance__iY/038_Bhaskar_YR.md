praNAms Sri Vishwanath prabhuji

Hare Krishna



By going through your mails I am getting an impression that you must be highly influenced by some modern day guru’s ‘modern instant techniques’ to realize brahman without the aid of body, mind and intellect 😊 Sorry for guessing. Anyway traditional stand with regard to brahma jignAsa is not like that. Put aside bhAshya, which you have already decided that it has a clandestine agenda of just winning the arguments, But look at geeta or shruti (upanishat) they too insisting for the sAdhana in a sAmpradAyik environment.



Just couple of examples :



1.  mundaka shruti : tad vijnAnArthaM sa gurumevAbhigacchet, samitpANiH
    shrotreeyaM brahmanishTam.
2.  geeta : tadviddhi praNipAtena pariprashnena sevayaa upadekshyanti te
    jnAnaM jnAninaH tattva darshinaH.
3.  And if at all you want to give any heed to shankara bhAshya,
    bhAshyakAra says in geeta 2.21 : shAstraachAryOpadesha
    shamadamAdisaMskrutaM manaH Atmadarshane karaNaM.



And please note this sAdhana is not that you can try on your own at home while taking bath or sitting at washing room 😊 Even if you know shAstra etc. by just reading books or listening to some online discourses etc.
you should not practice (yes sAdhana emphasized here beyond any doubt) it independently and you have to have the guidance of traditional teacher ( shrotreeya brahmanishTa guru). Again bhAshyakAra insists : shAstrajnOpi svAtantryeNa brahmajnAnAnveshaNaM na kuryAt, ityetat gurumeva ityavadhAraNaphalaM. brahmajnAna anveshaNa is nothing but jnana sAdhana which needs to be done with the grace of Ishwara (ishvaraanugraha) and under the able guidance of sampradaya Acharya (AcharyOpadesha).



I don’t know what your teacher says with regard to all these traditional standards, but you must know that this is how tradition guides its aspirants in jnana mArga sAdhana.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!

bhaskar
