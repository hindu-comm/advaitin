[[Viswanath O K	2023-07-20, 16:50:45 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/iYwK-d-yYHs)]]

I don't feel sorry for not studying under a Sampradaya. I only wait happily for Right Guru, and fine to go with any bad things/scoldings because of the fate of learning this online.

  

You can see for yourself by comparing both SDV-DSV with AV.

  

Both SDV-DSV are like saying "water is created in Mirage" (one says before perception, and another says at time of perceiving - created by Mind), but AV strictly says "No water is created - it's only Belief out of Ignorance - no water here".

  

AV don't accept birth,death, and if so, then Mind creation,birth and death also not accepted. Words such as "Creation,Birth,Death,etc." has more significance here, and if you take Gaudapada Karika and read each and every word - you can understand such. Out of Ignorance, there can be Beliefs and Imaginations, but there cannot be a creation,etc.. As Gaudapada's words mostly give importance upon "No creation, Ignorance,Maya,etc.", later Swami's developed few understandings which they believed - to make a little twist in this and snap out from accusation of being in line with Mayavadhis. Maybe if Gaudapada and Shankara was alive, they wouldn't mind such accusations, and be very straight forward in what they taught as in Karika and Bhashya - but not mold these to suit with beliefs of such era.

  

Even if one takes and research about Caste system and Dharmas, Shankara were never against Manu Dharma, etc., in laws about Women,Shudra,etc..

But I see now in Sampradaya of giving up sacrifices and stop following few rules, to be in line with Modern society of Vegan,Feminism,etc.. I see this only as Survival Mechanism but not actual following of Sampradaya. To follow a Sampradaya/orthodox means, even if one faces death of body or destruction of the Math/Ashrama - never minding about survival and follow such.
