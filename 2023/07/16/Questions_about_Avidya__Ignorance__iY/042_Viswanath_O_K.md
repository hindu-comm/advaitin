\>... there was an offline question regarding the above from my first response, hence the reply to myself. The Q was as to how can I say that such reason doesn't exist? It is because no one has given any such reason. Now, if someone were to give a reason r1 for the beginning of avidyA, then that will also have to face the inquiry as to whether r1 is anAdi or sAdi? If it is anAdi, then why would anyone have a problem with avidyA itself being anAdi? So, they will have to say r1 is sAdi. If so, then, was that beginning without reason or due to another reason r2? If without reason, then anirmokShaprasanga and if with reason r3, anavasthAdoSha.  

  

Namaste.

  

Yes. Going into these r1,r2,r3, is like following the Hamunanji's tail - but that too by not understanding the seed.It is also said that "trying to know Cause for the Causal" itself is illogical.

  

Let us go to thepoint about this Ignorance. It is better to see the Ignorance in association with Entity but not individually.

  

Where is Ignorance? Sure it is not in Atman, and its beginning cannot be traced - so Ignorance can be deemed to be Beginningless. But the Mind is after Ignorance like a product of it, and as a product has Ignorance in it.

  

So, the point, between Atman and Subtle Body, what is there? It is called Causal Body - Blissful, and it is said that Ignorance has to be present there - and it is only the presence of Ignorance differentiating Prajna and Ishwara. If one is Ignorant in Deep sleep then one is called Prajna, and if not then called Ishwara and no bedha. But, this inquiry is not in association with Entity, so let's associate the Entity here.

  

If one questions the Entity for which Ignorance is there, there is only One Entity here which is Atman, and no Ignorance there. So, instead of questioning the reason for Ignorance, realize that Atman - and no Ignorance then. Simple.
