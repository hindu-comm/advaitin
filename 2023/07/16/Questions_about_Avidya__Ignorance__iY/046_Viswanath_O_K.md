For all those, I have only one point.

  

If you find out Atman with the questions "Who sees Hanumanji?", etc., it can only make you reach the Blissful Witness-Conscious, Indweller, Causal Body.

  

But, the question of "Who has Ignorance?", removes the ignorance by attaining the "true nature of Atman" - Sat-Chit-Ananda - Brahman.........

  

It's like those questions ("who sees, who wants, etc.") can show the face in the Mirror accurately, but only this one - about the Causal itself - leads to knowledge of the Real Face without any mirror.

  

It's not the debate here whether Avidya is real or unreal, but it is the Causal for these Dream states. One can know the answer to the question about anything in the tree, but about the seed is the crucial question where it has no answer but leads to reality.

  

In the case of Karma, Karma is not Causal, it's a Bandha and effects. If one attaches to any action,that attachment leads to an effect to be experienced, where both attachment with its effects is within Causal-Ignorance.
