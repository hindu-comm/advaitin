I was reminded of Arjuna's question (in Chp 3 BG) to Sri Krishna on which is better: to do work (unattached) or renunciation of works. And the Lord advises him on the efficacy of karma yoga. There is recognition that Arjuna is not a jnani and so what applies to a jnani does not apply to Arjuna given his identifications with body-mind-world. Krishna tells him to do the obligatory works, surrendering the results to Him.

  

The point is that for the jiva who has avidya/ajnana of Self, there is a corresponding path of dharma most suited to purify the mind etc. and allow for assimilation/realization of jnana/Self. So karma yoga, bhakti to ishta devata, bhajans etc. all apply at that stage. Krishna does not pretend that any talk of "works" reinforces wrong notions on Self and hence should be avoided inhis teaching; rather he doubles down on it when Arjuna directly asks him about his apparently contradictory speech.

  

III.3: ... two kinds of steadfastness ... Yoga of Knowledge for men of realization; .. Yoga of Action for the yogis.
