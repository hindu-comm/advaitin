[[Viswanath O K	2023-07-21, 08:32:40 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/iYwK-d-yYHs)]]

You want only the answer to Ignorance? Then anything you believe is the answer. If you want to believe 1, then it is. If you want to believe 2, then it is.

  

If you want to know Truth about Ignorance, then my answer is, "This Ignorance is the feel which separate Jiva from Ishwara. If you accept an answer for Ignorance, then You will strongly believe yourself as a Jiva and any Sadhana you do would be tough to remove such bedha. Instead, don't accept any answer for Ignorance/Causal/Seed, so the belief that 'I am Ignorant' be reduced and then if you contemplate on Mahavakhyas then the Duality be removed under the Guidance of Guru. This answer for Ignorance only strengthen the presence of Ignorance where such Ignorance is not really in You/Atman/Brahman."

  

These are the Answer for Ignorance and the Truth about it. Take what you want, choose wisely.

  

Let that God protect you and provide knowledge to Every living being here and there.

  

Namaste.
