Namaste.

  

In my view, until the fourfold qualities of becoming a disciple is not attained, then one has to pursue other margas.

  

Arjuna didn't have such fourfold qualities, his Mind only became averted to kill his own Guru,Grandsire,etc., but not total detachment to Life to know the Truth - seeing the sufferings in every action - like Rama had in Yoga Vasistha.

  

So, based on the questioner, the Guru like Krishna makes that person to pursue the right path suit him until he is fit for being a disciple with fourfold qualities for imparting words for Jnana.

  

And so, Bhagavat Gita didn't said anything related to "Aham Brahmasmi" as Arjuna is not fit for it, and maintaining Duality is the right thing for him to make him pursue his path.

  

If a Guru was there, and a person with fourfold qualities asks - whether to renounce or not, then the Guru says to Renounce and conducts certain rituals and express Upanishads for Shravana,Manana,Nidhidhyasana.

  

The problem in the world now is, whomever took renunciation truly didn't have fourfold qualities, and the Guru is not truly Enlightened to see that, but just announce such person as Renunced and conduct ritualsa and transfers the words of Upanishads without really Enlightened by it and also the Disciple only does Shravana and Manana but not gets truly Enlightened because of such lack of fourfold qualities, and I see why this happens is to position of becoming Head/Chief and attain respect,etc. also to increasethe fame and followers and respect of Math/Ashrama in battle between schools (like Buddhism did centuries before of renunciation which Adi Shankaracharya stood against).
