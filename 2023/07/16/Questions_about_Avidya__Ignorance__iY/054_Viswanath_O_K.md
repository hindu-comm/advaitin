To add further to Venkatji's quote, Kathashruti mentions not only intellect being needed as a means for Atmadarshana, but also what kind of intellect it should be inसुक्ष्मदर्शिभिः \*अग्र्यया सुक्ष्मया\* बुद्ध्या एषः आत्मा तु दॄश्यते॥

  

  

  

It's True about those, I have no doubt or question about it, but Intellect is not capable to show/know the Atman - only Atmn can know itself without use of Intellect or without attachment to Intellect.

  

Why the Intellect really do here is, it cannot show the Knowledge, but instead it removes all the Ignorance of what one believes one is. It removes all such and in the end removes itself/dies in Bliss, and in that Bliss - the Knowledge of Atman revealed surpassing that Bliss/Anandamaya Kosha. The Intellect can remove all your Attachment to everything and make you reach that Bliss, and then no use of such Intellect and Intellect fades away. In that Anandamaya kosha the Knowledge is revealed of Atman surpassing that Anandamayakosha.

  

I think You might be aware of Bhriguvalli in Taittiriya, where Bhrigu surpasses every sheath one by One, even the Intellect and reach Bliss, and then is such Knowledge of Atman identity with Brahman. Until reaching such Bliss, Intellect is useful to remove all "I am ..." one believed and attached to, then vanishes. Only Atman can Know itself without use of Intellect/Bliss or without any 5 sheaths.
