Sorry, it's not like that.

  

If you go through the whole chapter 13, you can see three things expressed "Ishwara,Jiva,Jagat" in the way of "Supreme One,Purusha,Prakriti".

  

Here first in Chapter 13 verse 20, Purusha and Prakriti is explained. But, when goes on, in verse 22 "Supreme Purusha present in Heart" is brought up, but the difference between "Purusha and Supreme Purusha" maintained and Duality not taken away. Till the End Triads is maintained.

  

Non-Duality is only from 108 Upanishads, it is acknowledged in Muktika Upanishads too. All other are only Smritis, and not allowed to impart such knowledge (even Upanishads only to those who has fourfold qualities not any other).
