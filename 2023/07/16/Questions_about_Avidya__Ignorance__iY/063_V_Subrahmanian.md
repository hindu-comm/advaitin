[[V Subrahmanian	2023-07-21, 13:19:41 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/iYwK-d-yYHs)]]

While the 2nd verse of the Bh.Gita 13th chapter is the Mahavakya: tat tvam asi/aham brahma asmi, taught by Bhagavan himself, the very last verse of this chapter is about the unreality/non-existence - seeming existence of the Prakriti:

  

क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोरेवमन्तरं ज्ञानचक्षुषा।  
  
भूतप्रकृतिमोक्षं च ये विदुर्यान्ति ते परम्।।13.35।।  

  

**English Translation By Swami Gambirananda**

13.35 Those who know thus through the eye of wisdom the distinction between the field and the Knower of the field, and t*he annihilation of the Matrix of beings*,-they reach the Supreme.

Thus, while the 2nd verse if jivo brahmaiva na aparah, the last verse is Jagat mithya. The complete Advaita statement is encapsulated in the chapter.
