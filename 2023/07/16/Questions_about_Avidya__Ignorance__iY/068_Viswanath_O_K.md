No. Dissolution of Purusha experiencing Prakriti, that's all it meant.

  

But, Purusha reach Supreme Purusha, and duality of "Ignorance" of being a "Purusha" still remain but not the knowledge of being Brahman.

  

Like, the Atma will reach Brahma Loka by the end of experiencing Prakriti attaining Moksha, and remain there in Abode of Brahman.
