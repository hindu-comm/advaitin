This is the Sankhya liberation and not Vedantic liberation. The realization that Prakriti does not really exist is part of the knowledge for liberation. This last verse of the Gita 13th chapter is the mirror verse of the BG.2.16:

  

नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः।  
  
उभयोरपि दृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः।।2.16।।  



  

2.16 Of the unreal there is no being; the real has no nonexistence. But the nature of both these, indeed, has been realized by the seers of Truth.
