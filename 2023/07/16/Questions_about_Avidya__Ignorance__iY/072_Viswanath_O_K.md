Nope.

  

The way it meant is,

  

"When Purusha understand difference between Purusha and Prakriti, and understand how to attain Moksha from Prakriti, will reach the Supreme Purusha".

  

The Jiva-atma when detach from Prakriti will attain moksha (End of experiencing Prakriti again), and reach the God/Paramatma/Supreme Purusha/Supreme abode of Brahman/Brahma Loka.

  

Not Anywhere in any Smriti is "Aham Brahmasmi" taught, and it should not be taught - Difference between Jiva/Purusha and Ishwara/Supreme Purusha will always be maintained in Smritis and it has to be.

  

The sole copyright is only to Upanishads. 😂

  

Arjuna never had fourfold qualities, and is not fit for such Nondual Knowledge, and even if he/others is/are fit - one has to reach such Guru and read all Upanishads, and only learn it from Upanishads. Smritis can point "Knowledge is Great", but never "what is that Knowledge" and only in Upanishads.
