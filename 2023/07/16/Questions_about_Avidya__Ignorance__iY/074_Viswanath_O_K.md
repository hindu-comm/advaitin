Let me give you a task for you.

  

What is explained here in that verse as "Sat" and "Asat", take that to the verse 13 of Chapter 13 about Brahman "anadimat parambrahma na sat na asat ..." and apply there.

  

Say, really meant is Purusha, the one embodied in temporary, and Asat is Prakriti/body which is temporary, but beyond that is Brahman.

  

Though explained all three, never Purusha/Atma/Jiva and Ishwara/Supreme-Purusha/Brahman is equated and differences negated.
