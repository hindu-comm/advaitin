[[Viswanath O K	2023-07-21, 14:00:20 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/iYwK-d-yYHs)]]

Not "Say" there but "Sat".

  

Sat - Purusha/Atma present within body as Knower of Field - Permanent

Asat - Prakriti - Temporary - the Field.

  

Brahman/Ishwara/Supreme Purusha - Beyond Purusha and Prakriti - Beyond Sat and Asat.

  

If Purusha attains Moksha from this Temporary/Asat/Prakriti, then Purusha will not experience these, but attains Brahma loka - the Supreme Abode of Brahman and no return.

  

But, the Duality of Purusha/Atman and Brahman always maintained,

and it's duality negated only in Knowledge which is only in Upanishads, never in Smriti.
