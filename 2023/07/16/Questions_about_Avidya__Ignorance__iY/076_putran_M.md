Namaskaram Viswanath-ji,

  

The question **for our list** really is whether that is what Shankara in his writings taught, as to what is meant. For instance, if he referred to Upanishads in his BG bhashya, then that is learned as the intended meaning to Krishna's words. If anywhere Shankara suggests BG is incomplete with regard to its teaching of Advaita, that also is part of the learning. Beyond that, there is no scope here to assert meaning to scripture outside of what is meant by Shankara in his writings - of course we may present our own perspectives of his teachings in BG bhashya.
