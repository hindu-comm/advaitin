In that Abhanga, It is spoken about Tri Gunas, and it's nature and about attaining liberation and Union with Brahman. I see that Union (Sangha) is spoken about "One with Brahman" - like One is with Ishwara at Deep sleep.

Other than that, what that Abhanga said is absolutely true about Gunas and liberation.
