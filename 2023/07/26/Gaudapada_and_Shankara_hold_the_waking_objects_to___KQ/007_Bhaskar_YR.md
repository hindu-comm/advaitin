- Prapancha is satya in accordance with jeeva and Ishwara satyatva.
  When jeeva’s jeevatva / jeeva bhAva goes what remains is jeeva /
  Shuddha Chaitanya call it by any name likewise when prapancha’s
  prapanchantvaM (its independent existence) goes and when jnAni
  realizes mruttiketyeva satyaM what remains is again brahman only so
  call it by any name hardly matters for the tattva nirUpaNaM. The
  bottom line is AtmaivedaM sarvaM, sarvaM khalvidaM brahma. It is
  anna, annada and shlokakarta.
