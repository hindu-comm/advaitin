Hare Krishna



\[You the jivaatmaare a field-knower limited to this body\], AND I the paramatma am ALSO the field-knower in all the fields/bodies.



- This reminds me dva-suparaNa mantra in Upanishad also, one is
  katru-bhokru another one is sAkshi who does not do anything
  (nishkriya) 😊 Yes transactionally jeevaatma is compartmentalized in
  his own BMI where as Ishwara / paramAtma resides not only in an
  individual BMI but also at macrocosm (samashti) level, so he is
  kshetrajna in sarva kshetra. But realizing this jeevatma ( individual
  knower of field) and paramAtma one and the same is samyagdarshana. We
  should not do Atma bheda like kshetrajna is different from paramAtma.
  This restriction is meaningless clarifies bhAshyakAra. Those who
  treat this difference as real will ignore the very essence of shruti
  warns bhAshyakAra. So, vijnAnaatma is paramAtma only because jna as
  Chaitanya is one and there is no second. sarvOpanishatsu hi
  vijnAnAtmanaH paramAtmanaikatvapratyayO vidheeyata ityavipratipattiH
  sarveshaam upanishat vAdinAM is the clarification given by bhAshyakAra
  in br.up. bh.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
