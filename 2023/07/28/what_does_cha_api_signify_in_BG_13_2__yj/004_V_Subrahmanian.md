The particle 'cha' has several meanings that are intended by the speaker. In this case I would interpret it as:

  

In the first/earlier verse it was stated: idam shariram kaunteya kshetram ityabhidhiyate. etad yo vetti tam praahuh kshetrajna it tadvidah

  

The knower of the filed(body) is called kshetrajna.

  

Now, in the second verse, Bhagavan continuesthat theme and says: Also 'Further (api) know Me indeed = cha (च अवधारणे cha = emphasize, indeed) to be the knower consciousness in every body. 

  

This, if we are seized by the need to give the two particles cha and api diff meanings. But in the Bh.gita itself chaapi usage is found in many places, without yielding any distinct meaning.
