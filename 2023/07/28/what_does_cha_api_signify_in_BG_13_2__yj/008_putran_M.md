Namaskaram Rishi-ji

  

I understand your point as being that bhagavan is the only existence whomanifests as creation - and hence the kshetras (the objects of cognition, the seer) and the distinct cognizer (the seer) are all only Him.

  

However, I don't see this verse as implying this advaitic tattva, at least not from the language used. He says "in all kshetra, know Me to be the kshetrajna". The "and also" is also put in there but it doesn't seem to suggest "I am both the kshetra and also the kshetrajna in all kshetras", as far as I read the translation.

  

The Lord is pointing towards advaita but from within the framework of vyavaharika standpoint and the language pertaining to it. This is fine for us but still the question of why he used "chaapi" is still worth asking given that the dvaitinsconsider it to be significantly tied to their siddhanta. But Subbu-ji's explanation makes sense to me, thatit should simply be taken as "further" to the previous verse.
