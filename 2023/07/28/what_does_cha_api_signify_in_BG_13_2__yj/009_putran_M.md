Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

Ok. But my question was specifically with regard to the usage of "cha api".

  

Here is Srila Prabhupada: "... in every body, there are two souls: the individual soul and the Supersoul... Krsna says, "I am also the knower, but I am not the individual knower of the body. I am the superknower. I am present in every body as the Paramatma."

  

I thought the "also" was interpreted in a nifty manner that aligns with their sampradaya. Then I wondered whether we have an advaitic interpretation for it or what would be our responseif they challenge us on this word. That was the basis for the question. I am fine with Subbu-ji's explanation - that essentially chaapi here is not a siddhanta implying usage for the advaitin.
