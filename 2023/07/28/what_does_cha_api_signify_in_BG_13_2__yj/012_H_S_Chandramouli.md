Namaskaram.

The following explanation for चापि (chApi ) is based on the talk on the subject by Swami Guruparananda in tamil.

The two words च (cha) and अपि (api) have been explained by different commentators in three different ways.

In version 1, the word चापि (chApi ) is understood in the sense of एव (eva) meaning \*alone\*. The anvaya would then be क्षेत्रज्ञम् माम् एव विद्धि (kShetraj~nam mAm eva viddhi ). Meaning thereby \*\* Understand Me alone as kShetraj~na \*\*. Of course सर्वक्षेत्रेषु (sarvakShetreShu ) (in all bodies or kshetrAs) should be understood. In this version, Me is understood as Chaitanya/Brahman.

In version 2, it is recalled that in Chapter 7, The Lord had stated his two aspects as parA-prkriti (Chaitanya/Brahman) and aparA-prakriti
(Creation/mAyA). In subsequent verses in this chapter also, kshetra is presented as the aparA-prakriti, the entire Creation. Hence in this verse 2, kshetra is to be understood as addressing the aparA-prakriti, the entire Creation instead of individual bodies. Then the anvaya would be

\*\* क्षेत्रम् माम् विद्धि क्षेत्रज्ञम् च अपि माम् विद्धि \*\* (kShetram
mAm viddhi kShetraj~nam cha api mAm viddhi ).

Meaning thereby \*\* Understand Me (Brahman/Chaitanya) as the Creation, and also as the kShetraj~na \*\*.

In version 3, Chaitanya/Brahman is not only to be understood as the
kShetraj~na in all other bodies, but also as the kShetraj~na in \*\*Me too\*\*, Lord Krishna referring to Himself as an individual. Thus चापि (chApi ) is applied to माम् (mAm ) itself.

All the three versions present the same meaning. There is no second tattva , other than Chaitanya/Brahman.
