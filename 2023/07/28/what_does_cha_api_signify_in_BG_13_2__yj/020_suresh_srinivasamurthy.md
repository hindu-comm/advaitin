Namaste Subbu-ji,

  

In the Taittiriya Up, there is the mantra - "ahamannam ahamannam ahamannAdOhamannAdah" where Atman is identified as anna and annAda. Similarly, in the Vishnusahasranama phalasruti sloka - "IndriyAni mano buddhih satvam tejo balam dhrutih vasudevAtmakAnyAhuh **keshetram kshetragya** eva cha - Lord vAsudeva is explicitly identified as Kshetra and Kshetragya. Also, in the nArayana upanishad, B.Gita VibhUti yoga and in the Shatarudreeya, the Lord is identified with many anAtma vastus. Atman/Brahman is also a kshetra, because the body/world rests in it.So I think , identification of Brahman as kshetra, helps to do Brahma drushti in all anAtma vastus, thereby establishing sarvAtmaikatva of Brahman.

  

Please correct me if I amwrong.

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
