Kshetrajna in other words the one who knows "kshetra" is actually indefinable since everything that can be defined would belong to be kshetra, that is, what is perceptible or conceivable. Although both could be called inseparable aspects of Being, their relationship is not reciprocal. This understanding would ruin the attempt to use, api \[also\] and cha \[and\] to postulate duality. The vision of the advaitin is that the use of api and cha, together although they apparently imply a difference, do not imply separation.
