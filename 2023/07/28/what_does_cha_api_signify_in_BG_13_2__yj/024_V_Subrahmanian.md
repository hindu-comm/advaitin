Namaste Putran ji,

  

For the Visishtadvaitin, in my understanding, the Kshetram signifies the world, jagat. This is held to be the shareeram, body in their doctrine. Hence it can be non-different from the Kshetrajna who is the atma of the kshetram. Of course for them 'resting in Brahman' of the Kshetram is not superimposed as we hold. It depends on Brahman like the body depends on the atman for everything.
