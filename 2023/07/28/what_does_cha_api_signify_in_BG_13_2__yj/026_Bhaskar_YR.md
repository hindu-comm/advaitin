Hare Krishna



One response can be this - Oh, Arjuna, Apart from being the jagatkAraNam (BhagavAn being the jagat kAraNam was mentioned in the earlier BG chapters), know that I (BhagavAn) am also the kshetrajna.



Ø Yes, it can be said this way also as brahman is the jagat kAraNa and he is antaryaami as well. He is upAdAna and antaryaami as well.
Brahma ekaM satyaM and there is no second to it which can be labelled as anAtma vastu.
