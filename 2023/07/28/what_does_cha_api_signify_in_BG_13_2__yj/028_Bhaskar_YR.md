Hare Krishna



I think I found the answer for my question. As Bhagavan/Ishwara himself identifies himself as the Atman in the Gita 10:10

(ahmAtmA gudAkesha), it should be okay from the Advaita perspective to identify sarveshwara with the Self.

The little self (jiva/dehAtma) is not external to the larger Self (Ishwara/VishwAtma). So identification of the Self as

VishwAtma essentially identifies/includes all the "infinite selves".



Ø Elsewhere in geeta itself lord says I am in everything and they are not in me 😊 so when sarveshwara is identified with tiny self it means he is everything and nothing exists apart from him (vishwarUpa darshana).



The individual jiva has no separate identification apart from Ishwara.



Ø Yes, since the Chaitanya is ONE in Advaita. Likewise this socalled jagat too does not have separate identification / existence apart from Ishwara. But it is better to keep this scenario in mind when we are identifying brahman with jeeva. It is jeeva, due to ajnAna, thinks that it is jeeva and gets the jnana and become / realizes that he is indeed brahman. We should not overturn this and say brahman acquired ajnAna and become jeeva 😊 Since vidyAvidya vyavahAra is valid only in vyAvahArika jagat.



So cha and api could be taken in the avdhAraNArtha to support the all-pervading Advaitic Self.



Ø Yes,



But still in vyavahAra, bheda should be accepted as truth is mixed with satya/anruta.

Otherwise, it may lead to "Ishwaroham" or asura bhAva.



Ø Yes no room for jeeveshwara ikya in vyavahAra ( though there is no time where we say jeeva is separate from Ishwara). In vyavahAra, jeeva, jagat and Ishwara are very much separate and operate within their own sphere. Ishwara is karmAdhyaksha, karmaphala dAta, jeeva is katru-bhOktru and jagat is the stage created by Ishwara for the sake of jeeva to experience his paapa puNya phala. Here in this empirical state jeeva cannot even think about Ishwara’s sarvashaktitva, sarvajnatva etc. Jeeva is parichinna and Ishwara is paripUrNa. And ishwara only does the srushti, jeeva does not have the capability to do the srushti kArya.
