Ok. But my question was specifically with regard to the usage of "cha api".



Ø I don’t think this ‘cha api’ grammatically problematic for the advaitic interpretation as fortunately we have the bhAshya of bhagavatpAda on this verse. ( Bit lengthy bhAshya on this verse) but really worth studying it in detail.



Here is Srila Prabhupada: "... in every body, there are two souls: the individual soul and the Supersoul... Krsna says, "I am also the knower, but I am not the individual knower of the body. I am the superknower. I am present in every body as the Paramatma."



Ø It’s been long time since I opened ‘As it is’ to understand geeta verses. Being an Advaitin it is better to avoid these translations.
But yes, shankara too talks about two entities one is shareeri and one is Ishwara, shaareera (jeeva) iti shareere bhavaH ityarthaH, nanu IshwarOpi shareere bhavati?? satyaM shareere bhavati na tu shareere eva bhavati explains bhAshyakAra and subsequently clarifies there is one Chaitanya due to upAdhi sambandha appears as many by giving the example of ghatAkAsha, mahAkAsha etc. But in vyavahAra one is active Chaitanya ( shareerastha-kartru-bhOktru jeeva) and another one passive sAkshi who is having apahatapApmaadi guNa. So I don’t think api cha is a big hindrance in understanding the ekatva of Chaitanya.
