Dear Sri Putran-ji

  

In this context, here is a verse from chapter 2 of BG.

  

अन्तवन्त इमे देहा नित्यस्योक्ताः शरीरिणः।  
अनाशिनोऽप्रमेयस्य तस्माद्युध्यस्व भारत।।2.18।।

  

These destructible bodies are said to belong to the everlasting, indestructible, indeterminable, embodied One. Therefore, O descendant of Bharata, join the battle.  

  

Here, BhagavAn brings up the multiplicity of bodies (which of course is also a matter of experience.) If there is really a multiplicity of embodied ones, this would be the right place to bring that up. But BhagavAn uses only singular and not plural for the embodied one. Hence I find it difficult to buy the dualistic interpretation. (There are other reasons too).
