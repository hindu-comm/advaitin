Dear Kalyan ji,

  

Very nice said. In the 10th Chapter too the Lord says:अहमात्मागुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः । 10.20 I am the Atma/Self residing in all beings. Shankara says:अहम् आत्मा प्रत्यगात्माI am the Self (distinct from the pancha koshas, body-mind-complex). Thus there can be only one Consciousness in each body; not two.
