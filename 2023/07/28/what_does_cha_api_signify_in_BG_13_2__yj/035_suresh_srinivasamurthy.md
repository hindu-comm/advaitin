Namaste,

  

Even if only one Atma/Kshetragya/consciousness is accepted, the world with plurality/multiplicity can continue to exist as the Kshetra/body.

  

In the antaryAmi brAhmaNa - Brahman is identified as the antaryAmi of the jiva/atma also supporting him as shareera - Atmano antaro yamAtmA na veda, yasya Atma shareeram"

  

This is also discussed in the BS 1.2.20 -शारीरश्चोभयेऽपिहि भेदेनैनमधीयतेpoint where Brahman as antaryAmi is different from jiva/shaareera.

  

Actually, accepting plurality/world as the "body" supported/sustained by Brahman/Self/consciousness, brings out the dhArmic/sustaining/supporting nature of the Advaitic Self. Seeing the same Brahman in all the many different bodies paves way for paraspara bhAvana also.

  

Personally, I see a grand union of dualistic/non-dualistic views in the Kshetra-Kshetragya relationship between the World and Brahman. Sri Shankara also mentions Brahma shareeratva while discussing the greatness of Guru-parampara in the PrashnOpanishad 6:8. It is up to the sAdhaka to realize Brahman established as the antaryAmi / inner Self of all, as SB or NB.  

  

Yes, worldly/bodily pleasures are temporal and so it is mithya. But as a Kshetra/field of dharma sAdhana perfectly in the control of the Atman, it could be considered as sanAtana/eternal also.

  

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
