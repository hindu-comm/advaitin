praNAms Sri Suresh prabhuji

Hare Krishna



- Perhaps you might be saying all this from the vishishtAdvaita
  perspective but fact is that even in Advaita also brahmaikatvaM is
  declared with this sarvAtmabhAva.



Even if only one Atma/Kshetragya/consciousness is accepted, the world with plurality/multiplicity can continue to exist as the Kshetra/body.



Ø Even though, in brahman, sva-gata bedha does not exist ( in brahman there is no visheshaNa like jagat), bedha prapancha does not exist apart from Atman. That is reason why Lord says both parApara prakruti-s are mine.



In the antaryAmi brAhmaNa - Brahman is identified as the antaryAmi of the jiva/atma also supporting him as shareera - Atmano antaro yamAtmA na veda, yasya Atma shareeram"



Ø Samashti deha is of hiraNyagarbha this provision accepted in AV also. 



This is also discussed in the BS 1.2.20 -शारीरश्चोभयेऽपिहि भेदेनैनमधीयतेpoint where Brahman as antaryAmi is different from jiva/shaareera.



Actually, accepting plurality/world as the "body" supported/sustained by Brahman/Self/consciousness, brings out the dhArmic/sustaining/supporting nature of the Advaitic Self. Seeing the same Brahman in all the many different bodies paves way for paraspara bhAvana also.



Ø This is samyakdarsha and in this darshana there is no anAtma vastu as such. Sri SSS in one of the works says that shruti does not stop just by saying there is anAtma vastu / mithyA vastu and you are seeing it as sAkshi / Atman. It also continues to declare that AtmaivedaM sarvaM, Here he observes that ( please read this in Kannada) :
anrutavAda anAtmavU kooda satyavaada Atmanige eradaneyadaagide endu avivekigaLu tappaagi bhaavisuvudakke edeyannu koduttittu. Aadare nijavu haagilla, ATMANOBBANE PARAMAARTHA SATYAVEMBA ANUBHAVAVE SAMYAK JNAANA.
ANAATMAVEMBUDU KOODA PARAMAARTHAVAAGI AATMANE AAGIRUTTADE, EDANNU NIRNAYISIRUVUDE ADHYAASA BHAASHYADA GURI.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
