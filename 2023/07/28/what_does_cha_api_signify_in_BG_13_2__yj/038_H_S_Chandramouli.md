Namaste Suresh Ji,

  

अन्तह्करणविशिष्ट चैतन्य (antahkaraNavishiShTa chaitanya) is jIva while
अन्तह्करण उपहित चैतन्य (antahkaraNa upahita chaitanya) is KshEtrajna or AntaryAmi. Chaitanya is one alone. That is Brahman.

  

Regards
