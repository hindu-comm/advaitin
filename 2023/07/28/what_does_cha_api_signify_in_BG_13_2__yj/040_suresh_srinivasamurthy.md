Namaste Sri Subbu-ji, Sri Bhaskar-ji and everyone!

  

Actually, the presence of Arjuna and Krishna in the chariot points to the existence of two chetanAs in the body. But still,I have no problem accepting Kshetragya/Atmaikatva as Ishwara chaitanya pervades everything/sarvakshetra and jiva has no separate existence.

  

But here is my thought:

  

The word Kshetra means Shareera as per Gita verse in discussion and Kshetragya is one who supports/knows/acts through it. So this establishes the body-soul relationship between jagat and Brahman. The bhAshya vAkyas I provided was to support this view.

  

The entire jagath is a prateeka/shareera of Brahman.So, is there any problem in accepting plurality in Jagath as Brahma shareera - an entity dependent on the Atman/Brahman?Instead of shirking away from Jagath as mithya should we not doBrahma drushti in jagath?

  

Please note I am not arguing for V.Advaita, as I accept Atmaikatva. But I think accepting Jagat as shareera/dependent reality on Brahman, will help to support sanAtana dharma. If the world is rejected as mithya there is the risk of making dharma also as mithya.

  

  

Namaste,  

Suresh
