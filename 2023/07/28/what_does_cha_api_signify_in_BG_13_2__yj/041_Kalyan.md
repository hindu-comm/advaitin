Dear Sureshji

  

The Gita upadesa begins withअशोच्यानन्वशोचस्त्वं..(2.11) and right in the beginning portion itself, bhagavAn says that anything that changes is unreal-

  

नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः।  
उभयोरपि दृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः।।2.16।।  

  

Arjuna is asked not to worry about transient things like happiness and sadness etc because, being transient, they are unreal!

jagat changes and therefore jagat is asatya!

  

(This theme of enduring opposites like happiness-sadness, heat-cold, praise-insult, gain-loss etc is repeated many times in the Gita and each time, 2.16 provides the implicit justification.)
