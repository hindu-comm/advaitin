Dear Suresh ji,

  

Dharma is mithya from the paramarthika viewpoint. It is required to be practiced by everyone who aims at attaining the paramarthika viewpoint. So have Gaudapada and Shankara emphatically said. Krishna too says that in the Gita in many words: Naishkarmyam the ultimate goal can be attained only through karma - chitta shuddhi - upasana - dhyana - jnana yoga. All these have Dharma and its practice as the basis. They have validity all through. Only in the Paramarthika state these are mithya.

  

regards
