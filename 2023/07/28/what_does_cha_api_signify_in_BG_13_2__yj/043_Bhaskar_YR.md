praNAms Sri Suresh prabhuji

Hare Krishna



- Since what you are talking about Advaita is in the nature of ‘all
  accommodative and all encompassing’ would like to continue to share my
  thoughts with you. Anyway, I think I am still an Advaitin not
  pracchnna vishishtAdvaitin 😊



But I think accepting Jagat as shareera/dependent reality on Brahman, will help to support sanAtana dharma. If the world is rejected as mithya there is the risk of making dharma also as mithya.



- Absolutely, if everything declared as mithyA/tuccha/atyanta abhAva
  etc. then we have to accept that we are nireeshwara vAdins or at the
  worse vijnAnavAdins. But Advaita talking about Atmaikatva darshana,
  sarvAtmabhAva and it is in this light only the brahmavAdins of Advaita
  tradition still meticulously following shAstra vidhi/dharmAcharaNa
  without any svecchhaachaara. After realization for the jnAni-s
  everything is Atman only hence when they are engaging in pooja and
  dharmaacharaNa etc. sOhaM bhAva will be there. I don’t think they
  would entertain the thoughts like, I am sAkshi and I am doing pooja to
  some mithyA entity (anAtma vastu) and whatever I am seeing is just
  mithyA and engaging myself some mithyAvyavahAra let me followers also
  follow this mithyAvyavahAra till they get the paramArtha jnana etc.
  rAmakrishna used to offer flower to devi and sometime when doing
  bhagavati ArAdhana he used to keeping the flowers on his head also
  because he realized what is there in him and bhagavati and in this
  whole world is ekaM, shankara would have not come back to his mother
  if he is of the opinion that performing antyeshti to his mother is
  mithyAvyavahAra not following the dharma is not necessary for the
  paramArtha jnAni-s, our ancient rishi muni-s treating agni,vaayu,
  Pruthvi, jala, Akaasha etc. as paramAtma svarUpa only, veda mAta too
  having the mantra-s on agni devata, vaayu devata, jala devata etc.
  conveying that these elements are in reality having the same undivided
  tattva in their svarUpa.
