Arjuna is asked not to worry about transient things like happiness and sadness etc because, being transient, they are unreal!

jagat changes and therefore jagat is asatya!

  
praNAms

Hare Krishna



IMO Krishna talking to Arjuna not to convey the jagat asatyatvam or jagat Astitva. Yes, no doubt, jagat is asat only as it is changing colors every second. But what Krishna talking here is all about Arjuna’s misconception / misperception on the jagat / battle field / about the warriors presented on the battle field. What Arjuna thinking at that time was due to his ahamkara/mamakAra as a result he was down with shOka-mOha, Krishna addressing this disease in Arjuna and talks about discharging his duty with the mind set of karma yOga. Upadesha-s like, don’t think about karma phala, don’t think that you are killing them, do the karma entrusted to you with samachitta etc. are within the scope of this Arjuna’s mind set and eradication of his misconception (aviveka) only.
