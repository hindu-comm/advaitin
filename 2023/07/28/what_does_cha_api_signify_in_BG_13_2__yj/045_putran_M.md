Namaskaram Suresh-ji,

  

I recall a story told by Sri Ramakrishna that Shuka was told by Janaka to give guru dakshina before instruction in Brahma jnana because after, he would not see distinctions such as guru vs sishya and hence not be committed to offer the dakshina. I am not sure if it is from a scripture.

  

"Shukadeva went to Janaka seeking Brahmajnana (the knowledge of Brahman). Janaka asked him first to pay his dakshina\[12\]. Shukadeva said, ‘How is the teacher’s fee due before the instruction is given?’ Janaka laughed and said, ‘When you have gained Brahmajnana, you will not be able to differentiate between the guru and the disciple. So I ask for the teacher’s fee in advance!’ "

  

As Subbu-ji pointed out, the bheda of Dharma and adharma applies in vyavaharika; it is Satya in that standpoint and mithya with reference to paramarthika. The jnani's will is merged with Ishvara's, and whatever he is seen as 'doing' is to be understood (by ajnanis) as being aligned to Dharma. In 3.22, Krishna says He has no duty (paramarthika) and still He works (vyavaharika).
