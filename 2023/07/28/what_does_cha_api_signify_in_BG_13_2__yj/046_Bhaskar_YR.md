As Subbu-ji pointed out, the bheda of Dharma and adharma applies in vyavaharika; it is Satya in that standpoint and mithya with reference to paramarthika. The jnani's will is merged with Ishvara's, and whatever he is seen as 'doing' is to be understood (by ajnanis) as being aligned to Dharma. In 3.22, Krishna says He has no duty (paramarthika) and still He works (vyavaharika).



Ø name pArthAsi kartavyaM trishu lOkeshu kiMchana says Lord to declare himself free from any duty likewise he also says na katrutvaM na karmANi lOkasya srujati prabhuH. So when the will of the jnAni’s will
merges with Ishwara’s will, he will see Ishwara srushti as Ishwara only as he is the upAdAna and nimitta kAraNaM for the jagat and Ishwara / shAstra vidhi-nishedha as pramANa for the karma / dharmAcharaNa. There is no exception from this for the jnAni and ajnAni, there is no separate set of rules to say jnAni would engage himself in mithyAcharaNa after satya darshana. Satya darshana would lead him to satyAcharaNa only not mithyAcharaNa. For the jnAni this relationship is not restricted to the ananyabhAva with nAma rUpa it also applies even to the transactions he will carry out with the presence of these nAma rUpa. This is just because, just as the effect (nAma rUpa) is only a special manifestation (vishesha darshana) of the kAraNa, the transaction (vyavahAra including dhArmika dharmAcharaNa) is only a special manifestation of the Atmashakti. That is why for the paramArtha jnAni if at all engaging himself in transaction, all the transactions and also all the names & forms are satya because they are viewed only in its svarUpa / adhishtAnam. Otherwise in paramArtha since there is no srushti, no dissolution, no mumukshu, no jnAni there is nothing from that perspective also it can not be said that there is jnAni who have satya darshana but engaging himself in mithyAcharaNa. It is untenable in either way. See what chAndOgya says in this regard : sadAtmanA satyatvAbhyupagamAt …..’sarva vyavahArANAM sarvavikArANAM cha satyatvaM because for him sarvOpyanyO vyavahAraH Atman eva vidushaH. In short when there is no anruta vastu in pAramAthika how can the jnAni engages himself anruta / mithyA vyavahAra??
