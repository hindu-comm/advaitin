Namaste Suresh ji,

All that is true, how does it prove your point though?

  

We don't dispute that the paramArtha sat vastu, Atma, is the supported of all vyavahAra. We say It does that without itself being involved in vyavahAra. For It, there is not dharma or adharma. In fact, no AchArya - whether belonging to the Advaita sampradAya or outside it - will say that Ishvara himself has any dharma / adharma.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
