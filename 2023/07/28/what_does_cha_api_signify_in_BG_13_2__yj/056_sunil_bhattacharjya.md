Dear friends,

  

Lord Krishna said that the mother is the highest guru. Fortunately Adi Shankara knew it very well and he performed the last rites for his mother, even if that needed him to ignore the convention of a sanyashi not meeting his mother. Adi Shankara even took his last breath at the feet of the divine mother Lalita. Shri prithvidhara (Hastamalaka) too gave the charge of the Shringeri math to Shri Sureshvaracharya, to be at the feet of mother Lalita in Kanchipuram.  

  

Guru has to be recognised even by the Jivanmukta, in spite of the criterion of sama-darshana.  

  

My 2 cents
