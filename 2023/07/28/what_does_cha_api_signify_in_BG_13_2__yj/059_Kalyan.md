Dear Sri Suresh

  

"If jagat is totally asatya then Krishna should have allowed arjuna totake sanyasawithoutfighting adhArmic kauravas!"  

  

Arjuna asks a similar question.

  

अर्जुन उवाच  
ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन।  
तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव।।3.1।।  

  

Arjuna said O Janardana (krsna), if it be Your opinion that wisdom is superior to action, why they do you urge me to horrible action, O Kesava?

  

Bhagavan's reply -

श्री भगवानुवाच  

लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ।  

ज्ञानयोगेन सांख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम्।।3.3।।  

  

The Blessed Lord said O unblemished one, two kinds of steadfastness in this world were spoken of by Me in the days of yore-through the Yoga of Knowledge for the men of realization; through the Yoga of Action for the yogis.  

  

Sri Shankara has elaborated on this in his bhAshya. Please check it out, specifically, bhAshya on initial few chapters.
