Ok, let me leave aside the paramarthika standpoint per se. That can be considered a differentargument. We are talking about how paramartha jnana applies inside the vyavaharika standpoint and relegates jagat mithya for a jnani.

  

Yes, the jnani sees Ishvara srishti as (appearance of) Ishvara only. However, the bheda pertaining to srishti is mithya to him because of its abheda satya being Ishvara. It is like the bheda in a movie is realized as being mithya in the context of knowledge that it is only Light projection. "Mickey mouse punched donald duck who fell on the street" is the movie narrative. It is not unreal to the child who is identified with that movie level. But the characters, their relationships and the world of internal causalitiesbetween them - though real to the child is unreal to the adult. The adult knows it all to be founded in the reality of Light, the Self.

  

But mithya is not asat. For the child, there is perfect sense, there is law, order, there is individuality and jivatvam, jagatvam, and there is the ishvaratvam of the illumining Lightthat also maintains the Order. The child wants the 'good' to win and 'bad' to lose. The adult also is free to participate and partake in the movie level; in fact he may do so to support the child in its standpoint. It matters to the child and its present stage of growth. But the fact is, the adult knows it is all only denotation/imagination on Light and its appearance. He may just as well turn off the tv and nothing whatsoever is lost to him or the movie.

  

The Self is immutable. That is the fact. It doesn't matter what the storyline is. Whether Gandhi, Bose or Tipu won the day. Brahman is unchanging Reality; all else is appearance. While we are not established in this truth, yes we will be naturally subject to the laws of srishti thatcorrespond to our identifications. We have our dharma and adharma, that is satya in the context of our wants and needs. The pot in which Space is identified as "this potspace" naturally wants to become the bigger pot and save itself from nearby pots,and eventually lose/merge its potness to the whole Container. The pot in which Space (Self) is realized as non-dual and all-pervading has nothing to do or not do; whatever happens is the play of appearance of the Space alone. But the point remains, the status of that Play is only imagination, appearance, projection - mithya with reference to that unchanging Space itself.
