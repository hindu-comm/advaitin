Namaste Suresh ji,

  

I think you are referring to Ishvara and we have no qualms with such a description. nirguNa brahma, which is the only paramArtha sat entity, is without any qualities, be they positive or negative. That is the supporter of all vyavahAra but is beyond all vyavahAra also.

  

As Shankara says in the ArambhaNAdhikaraNam -

तदेवमविद्यात्मकोपाधिपरिच्छेदापेक्षमेवेश्वरस्येश्वरत्वं सर्वज्ञत्वं सर्वशक्तित्वं च, न परमार्थतो विद्यया अपास्तसर्वोपाधिस्वरूपे आत्मनि ईशित्रीशितव्यसर्वज्ञत्वादिव्यवहार उपपद्यते ;  

*Therefore, Ishvara's omniscience, omnipotence, control of everything is premised on the delimitation by adjuncts which are products of ignorance. However, in reality, in the self in which knowledge has destroyed the reality of all adjuncts, there is no possibility of omniscience or any activities involving a controller-controlled relationship, etc.*  

  

तथा चोक्तम् — ‘ यत्र नान्यत्पश्यति नान्यच्छृणोति नान्यद्विजानाति स भूमा’ (छा. उ. ७ । २४ । १) इति ;

*Thus it has been said - "when nothing else is seen, or heard, or known, that is the absolute reality, bhUmA".*

‘यत्र त्वस्य सर्वमात्मैवाभूत्तत्केन कं पश्येत्’ (बृ. उ. ४ । ५ । १५) इत्यादि च ;

*"When the self is all, what can be seen, by what", etc.*

एवं *परमार्थावस्थायां सर्वव्यवहाराभावं वदन्ति वेदान्ताः* सर्वे ;

*In this manner, *from the standpoint of absolute reality, the absence of any possible activity is stated by all the upaniShads*.*

*  *

तथेश्वरगीतास्वपि — ‘ न कर्तृत्वं न कर्माणि लोकस्य सृजति प्रभुः । न कर्मफलसंयोगं स्वभावस्तु प्रवर्तते’ (भ. गी. ५ । १४) ॥

*The Ishvara gItA also says this - "No doership or actions or association with the results of actions attach to the Lord who is the creator of the world. His actions are natural".*

  

‘नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः । अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः’ (भ. गी. ५ । १५) इति

*"Ishvara does not have any association with pApa or puNya. Truth is obscured by ignorance which deludes all living beings".*

  

परमार्थावस्थायामीशित्रीशितव्यादिव्यवहाराभावः प्रदर्श्यते ;

*All show that from the standpoint of absolute reality, there is no activity involving the creator and the created.*

  

व्यवहारावस्थायां तूक्तः श्रुतावपीश्वरादिव्यवहारः — ‘ एष सर्वेश्वर एष भूताधिपतिरेष भूतपाल एष सेतुर्विधरण एषां लोकानामसम्भेदाय’ (बृ. उ. ४ । ४ । २२) इति ;

*However, from an empirical standpoint, activities of Ishvara have also been said in the shruti - "He is the Ishvara of all, he is the ordainer of all beings, he is the protector of all beings, he is the maintainer of order and ensures that the worlds do not simply collide with each other".*

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
