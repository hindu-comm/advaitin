Namaste Venkatraghavan-ji,

  

I fullyunderstand there is no vyavahara or dharmAdharma or vidhi-nishedha in paramArtha/NB. But vyahArika jagath/shareera is not devoid of paramArtha and derives its existence/sustenance fromthe Atman/Brahman only.

  

Even though it does not do anything, the fact that it is the sole support/source of all vyavahAra/jagath/shareera is what I would refer to as absolute positivity / nitya sattva quality.

  

Let us say that the prasiddha brahman in the form of embodied jiva is dharma and the same without body is NB. Hope we are in agreement!

IMO, as long as Vedantins are embodied, they have to accept antaryAmi jagadheeshwara as an embodiment of yagya/dharma as real.

  

  

Regards,  
Suresh
