Namaste Sri Kalyani-ji,

  

Arjuna's path and Sri ShankarAcharya's path are not different. While Arjuna defeated AdhArmic Kauravas, Sri ShankarAchArya defeated avaidhika matas. There is nivutti in pravrutti and vice versa also as long as your goal is to uphold truth and dharma.

  

Regards,
