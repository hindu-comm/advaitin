My apologies for too many postings. But just thought of sharing one more thought:

  

VarNashrama dharma which is the essence of Vedic religion / Hinduism gets justified only when we look upon the human society/nation/jagath as the body of Brahman. This is explicitly taught in the purusha suktam. Just like all the entity in jagath as well as all the different parts of the human body are performing their respective duty in a co-operative manner as a service to the indwelling Atman, every human according to their varna/ashrama are expected to engage in their respective duties as a service to the sarvAntayAmi Ishwara.
