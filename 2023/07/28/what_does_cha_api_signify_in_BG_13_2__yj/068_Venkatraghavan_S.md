> IMO, as long as Vedantins are embodied, they have to accept antaryAmi
> jagadheeshwara as an embodiment of yagya/dharma as real.

In the samanvayasUtra's bhAShya, Shankara quotes an ancient advaitin -

  

देहात्मप्रत्ययो यद्वत्प्रमाणत्वेन कल्पितः ।

लौकिकं तद्वदेवेदं प्रमाणं त्वाऽऽत्मनिश्चयात्’ इति ॥ ४ ॥

On that basis, your above statement is accepted. No AchArya / jnAni will deny Ishvara - to themselves, Ishvara is an immediately experienced truth and for others, they go by the creed of न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेत्.
