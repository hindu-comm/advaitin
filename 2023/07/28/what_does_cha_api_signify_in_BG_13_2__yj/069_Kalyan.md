Dear Sri Suresh-ji

  

"Please note I am not arguing for V.Advaita, as I accept Atmaikatva. But I think accepting Jagat as shareera/dependent reality on Brahman, will help to support sanAtana dharma. If the world is rejected as mithya there is the risk of making dharma also as mithya."

  

Please allow me to quote a small portion of Sri Ramanuja's bhAshya on Gita 2. 16.

  

देहस्य अचिद्वस्तुनोः असत्त्वम् एव स्वरूपम् आत्मनः चेतनस्य सत्त्वम् एव स्वरूपम् इति निर्णयो दृष्टः इत्यर्थः।  
विनाशस्वभावो हि असत्त्वम् अविनाशस्वभावश्च सत्त्वम्। यथा उक्तं भगवता पराशरेणतस्मान्न विज्ञानमृतेऽस्ति किञ्चित् क्वचित्कदाचिद्द्विज वस्तुजातम्। (वि0 पु0 2।12।43)

  

Non-existence (i.e., perishableness) is the real nature of the body which is in itself insentient. Existence (i.e., imperishableness) is the real nature of the self, which is sentient. \[What follows is the justification of describing the body as 'unreal' and as having 'never come into being.'\] Non-existence has, indeed, the nature of perishableness, and existence has the nature of imperishableness, as Bhagavan Parasara has said: 'O Brahmana, apart from conscious entity there does not exist any group of things anywhere and at any time. Thus have I taught you what is real existence - how conscious entity is real, and all else is unreal' (V. P., 2.12.43 - 45).  

  

The quote is from Vishnu purANa.
