Hare Krishna



apart from conscious entity there does not exist any group of things anywhere and at any time. Thus have I taught you what is real existence - how conscious entity is real, and all else is unreal' (V. P., 2.12.43 - 45).The quote is from Vishnu purANa.



Ø It is really surprising how often we ignore the prefixes and suffixes to the ‘asatya prapancha’in bhAshya vAkya-s, smruti and shruti texts !! Here it is clearly mentioned ‘***apart’ from conscious entity’*** there does not exist any group of things anywhere and at any time. How can one ignore sarvavyApakatvaM of Ishwara here?? See for the example in the 9^(th) chapter where lord says ahaM kraturahaM yajnaH, svadhAhamaushadhaM, nidhAnaM beejamavyayaM and sadasacchAhaM etc. can we imagine anything apart from brahman in this world?? Not possible at all. If jagat is non-existent as per your interpretation can we have these verses from the lord?? Don’t again say, Oh, this is said in the light of upAsya brahman but jneya brahman is something different etc. Please note in shankara bhAshya for the upAsana also krama mukti and after that jnana vihita self realization prescribed.
These things would go for a six if we try to say absolute non-existent jagat. See the shankara bhAshya, here bhAshyakAra clarifies that Lord assuring both the sat and the asat which are distinct are ‘me’only. Sat tad vipareetaM asacchaiva ahaM kAryakAraNOva sadasati. It is with this Samyak bhAva, samadrushti, sadAtma or sarvAtmabhAva the paramArtha jnAni realizes jagat is non-different from brahman.
