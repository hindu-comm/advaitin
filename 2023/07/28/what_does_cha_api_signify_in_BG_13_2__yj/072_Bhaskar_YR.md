*In this manner, *from the standpoint of absolute reality, the absence of any possible activity is stated by all the upaniShads*.*



praNAms

Hare Krishna



Yes in this light only it has been said mithyA vyavahAra is not possible in paripUrNa satya darshana. *"When the self is all, what can be seen, by what" he is anna, annada and shlokakarta so no bedha vyavahAra since here pramAtrutva itself is sublated.*
