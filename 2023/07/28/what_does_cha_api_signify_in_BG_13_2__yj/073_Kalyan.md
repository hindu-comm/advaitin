Dear Sri Bhaskar-ji

  

"See for the example in the 9^(th)chapter where lord says ahaM kraturahaM yajnaH, svadhAhamaushadhaM, nidhAnaM beejamavyayaM and sadasacchAhaM etc. can we imagine anything apart from brahman in this world?? Not possible at all. If jagat is non-existent as per your interpretation can we have these verses from the lord??"  

  

How is any of this incompatible with the jagat being asatya? If your objection is that jagat is not absolutely asat, but it is mithya, there is no disagreement between us.
