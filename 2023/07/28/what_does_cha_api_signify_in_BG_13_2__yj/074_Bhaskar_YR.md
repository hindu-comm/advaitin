How is any of this incompatible with the jagat being asatya?



- You said jagat is non-existent and some would even say this jagat is
  jeeva Kalpita due to ajnAna / avidyA. Here lord saying he is both sat
  and asat shruti also saying satyAnchAnrutaMcha satyamabhavat. So I
  was telling that which jagat in front of us is exists when it is
  ‘seen’ (in its manifested form) and it is exists when it is not seen
  also in an unmanifest form (avyAkruta /avyakta rUpa/ mUla prakruti).
  Therefore it is explained asatya in the sense that it changes from
  avyAkruta state to vyAkruta and vyAkruta to avyAkruta ( see
  taittireeya for the bhAshya explanation with regard to sat and asat)
  nevertheless it is brahman only nothing else!! How ?? simply there
  is no possibility of existing anything ‘apart from brahman /
  conscious. 



If your objection is that jagat is not absolutely asat, but it is mithya, there is no disagreement between us.



Ø Please share your explanation about asat, mithyA, bhrAnti, abhAva and English work non-existent. Especially what is mithyA?? IMO snake is rope is mithyA, seeing second moon due to cataract is mithyA when the drushti dOsha cured dviteeya Chandra does not appear, when the true knowledge of rope dawns sarpa does not exist. Do you think jagat which you seeing in front of you vanishes in thin air after Atma jnana?? If you say No, jagat does not go anywhere but our ajnAna about jagat is eliminated, if that is your observation then we say that what remains after the elimination of mithyAjnAna about jagat is brahman only and nothing but brahman. Jagat / brahman / tattva / Atman call it by any name hardly matters.
