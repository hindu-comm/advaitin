Mithya is not asat. It is a lower order of reality, subject to sublation and negation in a higher standpoint where the mithya existence is asat.  

  

So the dreamer-dream duality (Ishvara-srishti), and in particular the dream/srishti/jagat is mithya with reference to the jnana of the paramarthika standpoint of Brahman.

  

Again, mithya is *not asat* in the relevant standpoint, but is demoted (in that very standpoint) in status like imagination for those who know of the higher standpoint in which it is asat.

  

The 'movie world' where "Shankara had a mother and he fulfilled his svadharma by visiting her at the time of death" is not asat - because all that is true at the level of body identification - like "drop a ball and it will fall to the ground". But it is not sat either, because the implied identities, dualities and causalities become gibberish in the standpoint of sarvam Ishvara. The jnani has paramarthika jnana even though he is identified within vyavaharika. That is why the world that is real in vyavaharika standpoint is still mithya to him.

  

"World does not vanish, therefore is not mithya." Wrong! If it vanished for the jnani, then we would simply have termed it a product of ajnana and therefore asat for the jnani. It is verily because it does not vanish in vyavaharika (where a jnani also belongs when identified by body-mind) and still is sublated in brahmajnana that this world is known to be mithya here and now.

  

Like for the physicist, the duality of iron and wood does not vanish in the standpoint of his pratyaksha pramana and still in the light of his knowledge of substratum nonduality, he knows that iron vs wood duality to be an appearance and not absolutely real. NOTE: the duality is real in the pratyaksha standpoint and realised to be mithya by the physicist because of his nondual knowledge. We are not claiming it is asat or ajnana in the context of pratyaksha pramana itself.
