Dear Sri Bhaskarji

  

Thank you for your message.

  

I trust bhagavAn and AchArya, when they say that jagat is unreal. I tried to read the bhAshya-s of other schools, but I never got the same satisfaction that I get when I read Sri Shankara bhAshya-s. Some people may think that Ishwara's qualities are real and that is their choice, I guess. But that is not Sri Shankara's advaita, in my opinion.

  

One thing I learnt is that it is counterproductive (for myself) to raise too many questions, leading only to dry logic and hair splitting. Please note, I am speaking for myself here. I do not intend to say that people should not raise questions.

  

Therefore, please permit me to not answer your questions. I apologize. However, I will make one point. I think jagat is asatya, not just mithya. To satisfy the curiosity of intellectuals, especially from other schools, we claim jagat is mithya. But, in my opinion, for practising advaita, it is preferable to consider jagat as asatya. Of course, I can already imagine objections to my view, but that is ok.

  

Best Regards
