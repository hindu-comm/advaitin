Yes, the jiva/atman behind the body is the prasiddha brahman which nobody can deny. The aprasiddha / srutisiddha brahman is the Ishwara chaitanya behind jagath/prakriti. The oneness of these two establishes Advaita. This is the same logic that AchArya Shankara has used in his BSB. The body / jagat as upAdhi in Advaita is nothing but shareera in V.Advaita. The relationship of svatantra-paratantra between atma-shareera and the tAratamya among all the bodies/name/forms in jagat - establishes dvaita/tattva vada. As already explained, Atma-shareera relationship between Brahman/Ishwara and jagat also justifies varnasrama dharma which is common for all.

  

Perhaps this is a different kind of Advaita where we don't see any essential difference between Advaita/V.Advaita/Dvaita 🙂. They are all like different snana ghattas leading to same Ganga of Brahman. Why should we reconcile like this? To put an end to all internal conflicts, foster paraspara bhAvana and unite brahmanas and in general all Hindus. This is especially needed now because brahmin/hindu hatred is growing everywhere!

  

Please let me know if the above thought process makes sense.

  

Regards,  
Suresh
