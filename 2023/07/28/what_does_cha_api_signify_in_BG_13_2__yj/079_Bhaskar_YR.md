Hare Krishna



Yes, the jiva/atman behind the body is the prasiddha brahman which nobody can deny.



- Chaintanya satta in / behind the body nobody can deny till the ‘death’
  of that physical body. But in Advaita even prANa is also kArya /
  anAtma. And it is jeevAtma ( upAdhi paricchinna Chaitanya) who
  travels one body to another. Ishwara OTOH is nitya mukta he resides
  in shareera but not only in shareera, like AkAsha he is sarvavyApi.
  Jeeva after getting rid of his upAdhi buddhi, he is all pervading
  brahman.



The aprasiddha / srutisiddha brahman is the Ishwara chaitanya behind jagath/prakriti.



- Shruti siddha parama Chaitanya is jneya brahman, who is nirguNa,
  nirvishesha, nirupAdhika, from the jagat srushti, sthiti, vinAsha
  point of view, the SAME brahman has been depicted as upAsya brahman,
  sOpAdhika brahma or upAdhi vishesha brahman. As long as there is
  triputi jeeva, jagat and Ishwara, jeeva is conditioned who is having
  qualified knowledge, Ishwara is karmAdhyaksha and jagat is the Ishwara
  srushti for the sake of these jeeva-s.



The oneness of these two establishes Advaita. This is the same logic that AchArya Shankara has used in his BSB. The body / jagat as upAdhi in Advaita is nothing but shareera in V.Advaita.



- For the jagat brahman is abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa, brahman is
  kAraNa and jagat is his kArya and this kArya is not different fro
  kAraNa in its svarUpa. I don’t know v.advaita would agree to this.



The relationship of svatantra-paratantra between atma-shareera and the tAratamya among all the bodies/name/forms in jagat - establishes dvaita/tattva vada.



- IMO, it is better to ignore the doctrine of dvaita / tattva vAda here
  and try to look at the similarities & differences between v.advaita &
  Shuddha shankarAdvaita. I don’t think v.advaita would like to see any
  similarity with Advaita, if that is the case, v.advaita traditional
  Acharya-s like, vedAntadeshikAchAra etc. would not have written
  shata dUshANi and Sri rAmaanujAcharya would not have treated Advaita
  as mahA pUrvapaksha in his shreebhAshya. OTOH the all embracing
  Advaita give accommodation to all school of thoughts explains it in
  the light of adhikAra bedha or different view points (like vyAvahArika
  & pAramArthika).



As already explained, Atma-shareera relationship between Brahman/Ishwara and jagat also justifies varnasrama dharma which is common for all.



Perhaps this is a different kind of Advaita where we don't see any essential difference between Advaita/V.Advaita/Dvaita 🙂.



- As said above ONLY Advaita would dare to say this. Other schools of
  thought would have their own reservation in accepting the Advaita as
  ultimate dictum of shruti.



They are all like different snana ghattas leading to same Ganga of Brahman. Why should we reconcile like this? To put an end to all internal conflicts, foster paraspara bhAvana and unite brahmanas and in general all Hindus. This is especially needed now because brahmin/hindu hatred is growing everywhere!
