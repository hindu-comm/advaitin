Hare Krishna



I trust bhagavAn and AchArya, when they say that jagat is unreal. I tried to read the bhAshya-s of other schools, but I never got the same satisfaction that I get when I read Sri Shankara bhAshya-s.



- Yes, for each and every Advaitin bhagavatpAda’s words are veda vAkya.
  No doubt in that.



Some people may think that Ishwara's qualities are real and that is their choice, I guess. But that is not Sri Shankara's advaita, in my opinion.



Ø Just because shankara said IshwarOpAdhi is avidyAkruta (2-1-14 sU.bh.) Ishwara’s qualities are not real, his omnipresence, omniscience omnipotence etc. are svabhAva of brahman. But yes, when everything is realized as ‘secondless’ brahman, what is the need for vishesha IshwarOpAdhi?? But as long as we are jeeva, striving to get jnana through shAstra, Acharya, IshwarAnugraha is MUST and he-is-jnAna dAta.
And to even get Advaita vAsana in our current janma Ishwaraanugraha is must (kArika says this) and Ishwara is jnana/mOksha dAta as well. So, no advaitin would deny Ishwaraastitva & his nitya, Shuddha, buddha qualities just because as per shruti jneya nirguNa brahma is para.



Therefore, please permit me to not answer your questions. I apologize.

However, I will make one point. I think jagat is asatya, not just mithya. To satisfy the curiosity of intellectuals, especially from other schools, we claim jagat is mithya. But, in my opinion, for practising advaita, it is preferable to consider jagat as asatya. Of course, I can already imagine objections to my view, but that is ok.



Ø Nothing from my side 😊 we are welcome to have Advaita as per our suitability. And that is the beauty of Advaita vedAnta, the all-encompassing one 😊
