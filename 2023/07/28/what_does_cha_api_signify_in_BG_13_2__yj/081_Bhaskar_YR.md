praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji

Hare Krishna



NOTE: the duality is real in the pratyaksha standpoint and realised to be mithya by the physicist because of his nondual knowledge. We are not claiming it is asat or ajnana in the context of pratyaksha pramana itself.



- I think I have clarified my understanding with regard to this somany
  times in this list itself. As per my understanding, the nondual
  knowledge is ekatvajnAna, Atmaikatva jnana, absolute non-dual
  knowledge, sarvAtma darshana, sarvAtmabhAva, Samyak darshana…And in
  this ‘satya’ darshana, no room for any ‘mithyA/anAtma darshana’
  because of the simple reason for the AtmajnAni sarvaM is Atman only
  and his realization is there was/is/never will be a thing that can be
  called anAtma vastu. See the chAndOgya bhAshya which I quoted
  earlier, it says even transactions also for the jnAni sadrUpameva
  satyaM. When jnAni realizes he is anna, annaada, shlokakarta his
  knowledge is free from any katrutva, bhOktrutva because he himself is
  the eaten, eater and giver of food. There is absolutely no bedha
  ‘bhAva’ even though there is bedha ‘darshana’ in anna, annada and
  shlokakarta ( see sUtra bhAshya 2-1-13). In this sarvAtma bhAva jnAni
  would not entertain the thoughts like, see I am sAkshi, the Chaitanya
  svarUpa and these things (socalled external things) are anAtma vastu,
  existing outside me and I am having the ‘mithyA darshana’ of those
  externally existing anAtma/ mithyA vastu!! No, that is not said in
  sarvAtmabhAva. Because, in the paramArtha jnAni also the indriya-s
  necessarily would operate and go outwards as a result of prArabdha (
  vide sUtra bhAshya). As a result, there is bound to be motivation in
  his speech (vAk) mind (mana) and body (deha) despite his total
  realization. In all those times the Samyak jnAni must have the
  paripUrNa jnana, that the Jagat is abhinna from him. Only then he can
  have akatrutva, abhOktruva prajna/bhAva so that he is not related to
  the transactions of his body with the world. This is the fruit that
  jnAni would gain with the sarvAtmabhAva which is nothing but ‘mOksha’
  there is absolute absence of avidyA kAma karma. sarvAtmabhAva mOkshO
  vidyAphalaM kriyAkArakaphala shUnyaM , yatra avidyAdi kAmakarmANi na
  santi clarifies bhAshyakAra in [br.bh](http://br.bh).



- And finally, and most importantly, in this state (pAramArthika jnana
  / sarvAtma bhAva) anything even to the extent of the tip of a hair is
  seen as ‘not me’, that and this is different from me, is the state
  of avidyA only!!. Yattu sarvAtmabhAvAt arvAka vAlAgramAtramapi
  anyatvena drushyate nAmamasmeeti tadavasthA avidyA warns bhAshyakAra.
  Therefore, sarvAtmabhAva which is the knowledge of non-difference of
  anything from Atman / brahman is really the right realization, not
  that I am doing the mithyA vyavahAra with anAtma vastu. Infact
  elsewhere shruti says one who says brahmaNa, kshatriya etc. jaati is
  not Atman those jAti-s would reject him and declare that he is unfit
  to get mOksha.
