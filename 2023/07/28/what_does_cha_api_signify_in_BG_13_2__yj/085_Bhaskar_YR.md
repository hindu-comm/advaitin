Anyway, without adjudicating who is right and who is wrong here let me share my understandings too with the group.



1.  ***The characteristics of the paramArtha jnAni*** : The jnAni is
    brahman because he has the brahma jnana, brahmavit brahmaiva
    bhavati. He is krutakrutya ( there is nothing needs to be done from
    him in the post realization period) and his realization would fetch
    him the knowledge that he was akartru/abhoktru even when he was
    engaging himself in the vyavahAra. And it is quite obvious that for
    the paramArtha jnAni there is no duality whatsoever and he is sarvaM
    hence he is sarvatra samadarshi…geeta 5.18 explains this beautifully
    vidyA Vinaya sampanne brahmaNe gavi hastini shunichaivashvapaakesha
    punditAH ‘samadarshinaH’. Please note here samadarshi means not the
    absence of bedha darshana but the absence of bedha buddhi. He is
    sarvabhutAtmabhutAtma hence kurvannapi na lipyate. In short,
    sarvAtmabhAva results in samadarshana, what is this samadarshana??
    Atman is all and all indeed the Atman. The world before oneself is
    non-different from one self and the elimination of jagat with
    respect to ONLY jeeva Kalpita jagat i.e. its parichinnatva /
    apUrNatva / abrahmatvaM.



2.  ***The jagat mithyatva*** : By this time you must be knowing what
    is the mithyatva of jagat I am talking about 😊 The ajnAni usually
    see the world as an independent entity keeping himself aloof from it
    and says : in this jagat I am born, living and going to meet the
    end!! And in this perception of ajnAni he normally does not
    recognize the world ‘before him’ is as per shruti is brahman ONLY
    and it is a kArya and in its kAraNa svarUpa abhinna (non-different)
    from brahman. So with this qualified knowledge (misconception)
    aparipUrNa drushti he imagines jagat is different from him etc. and
    accordingly he perceives jagat as independent entity, this
    ‘svatantra’ jagat which jeeva sees due to his ajnAna is ‘mithyA
    jagat’ not the jagat what actually there in its kAraNa svarUpa. (
    hope I don’t have to re-clarify here that both kArya-kAraNa is one
    of the adhyArOpa-apavAda prakriya-s to drive home the point that
    what exists is brahman only and there exists nothing like anAtma
    vastu in kArya rUpa). When shruti says satyanchaanrutamcha
    satyamabhavat yadidam kimcha etc. this anruta jagat is NOT mithyA
    jagat but this asat jagat ( as this will appear in manifested form
    (vyAkruta/vyakta) and remain in brahman only in its unmanifested
    form (avyAkruta rUpa). But mithya jagat is not like brahmAbhinna
    jagat but it is very different. This mithyA jagat just believed to
    be existing quite independently, all by itself!! As per siddhAnta
    since nothing can ever exist independently of brahman such svatantra
    prapancha is pure an imagination of the conditioned mind (i.e.
    jeeva’s avidyAkruta jagat).



3.  The paramArtha jnAni’s vyavahAra : As a matter of fact, there is no
    talk possible about paramArtha jnana and paramArtha jnAni’s
    transactions etc. as it is the state where mind and speech cannot
    reach. So we are not capable of talking anything about paramArtha
    jnAni’s vyavahAra and his recognition of jagat etc. with our limited
    knowledge. Nevertheless, we have shruti and Acharya bhAshya vAkya
    with regard to paramArtha state, paramArtha jnAni etc. There are
    innumerable references are there in shruti and bhAshya how a
    paramArtha jnAni with samadarsha buddhi realizes his sarvAtmabhAva
    (that itself mOksha) and continue to do vyavahAra in the view point
    of on-lookers. For example shankara in mundaka mantra bhidyate
    hrudayagranthim chidyante sarvasamshayaaH bhAshya directly talks the
    phalaM of paramAtmajnAnam. In this fully realized state there will
    be ‘sarva vikalpa nishedha’ and sarvAtmabhAva which I have already
    explained in my previous mails and here just one for ready reference
    from geeta bhAshya 6-29 : sarveshu brahmAdisthAvarAnteshu
    vishameshu sarva bhuteshu samaM nirvishesham
    brahmaatmaikatvavishayaM darshanaM jnAnaM yasya saH sarvatra
    samadarshanaH and in this mithyA jnana kruta jagat, jeeva Ishwara
    and their respective difference is not there. 😊



So, this is how I look at paramArtha jnAni his socalled vyavahAra, the jagat for the jnAni etc. And I am not compelling any one to accept it or reject it.
