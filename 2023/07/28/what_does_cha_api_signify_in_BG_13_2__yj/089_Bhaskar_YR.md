Hare Krishna



Two pramANa-s and from these two different pramANa-s proved prameya-s should not contradict each other then only both are called ‘valid’ pramANa and whatever jnana/knowledge (pramANa janita jnana) we get from each pramANa is final and cannot be superseded by any other pramANa. In this light, jagat is pratyaksha pramANa and jnana about jagat is pratyaksha pramANa janita jnana. No other pramANa can have the capability to prove other pramANa janita jnana is wrong!! That is the reason why shankara says if the shruti says fire is cold it is not acceptable as it is saying something against lOkAnubhava i.e. pratyaksha pramANa janita ‘anubhava’ jnana. Likewise for the brahman existence and brahma jnana, pratyaksha is NOT the valid pramANa ONLY the shAstra (Shabda) is the final pramANa. And Shabda gives us the knowledge which pratyaksha cannot give and at the same time the Shabda pramANa’s scope is restricted and cannot enter into the territory of pratyaksha pramANa janita jnana to make havoc there. Both pratyaksha and Shabda have their own restricted scope and one cannot violate another. In this light, jagat is pratyaksha pramANita and there is no possibility for the Shabda pramANa to prove pratyaksha pramAnita jagat is wrong or not existent. What Shabda pramANa can do is ‘without disturbing the pramANa janita jnana of jagat ( darshana of bedha jagat) reveals the nature of this duality and removes the ‘ bedha bhAva’ behind this duality. It (shAstra) only reveals what is there in reality rather it removes our wrong perception (eradication of our aviveka / ajnAna of the jagat) about what exists in reality. And as per Advaita all pramANa, prameya vyavahAra (vaidika, shAstra, loukika etc.) sprouted from AtmAnAtma anyOnyAdhyAsa (a fine mixture of satya and anruta 😊).



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
