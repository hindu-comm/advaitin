Can a paramArtha jnAni have two different levels of jnana !!?? one is when living in his body and another one after the death of his body??
Yes, says some because since upAdhi is avidyAkruta, and the jnAni when living in his body is associated with upAdhi and he will continue to have the darshana of water in place of dry land despite the knowledge of dry land, he is having some samskAra rUpa avidyA (avidyAlesha) so whatever jnana he is having with the living body is gaUNa or not upto the mark due to this and to enjoy this jnana to the fullest extent, he has to shed his mortal coil ( physical death of the jnAni) and ONLY after the death he would enjoy absolute non-dual, transaction free state because after the death he is having no more connection with upAdhi and no more darshana of sOpAdhika prateeti. And it is explained in another manner to accommodate sarvAtma bhAva and Samyak darshana of the jnAni,
sarvaM brahmamayaM is OK, acceptable but it is just something better than normal vyavahAra, like me, you and jagat difference but it is still a vyavahAra, an elevated vyavahAra where duality still persists!!
Implying again that to get the paripUrNa jnana sans elevated or refined vyavahAra, jnAni should get rid of his upAdhi and connection with jagat!! How it is possible?? Obviously it is possible only after the physical death of the jnAni. In both these stands to get the paripUrNa jnana and resultant mOksha jnAni has to meet the physical death. Is this what shankara saying about pAramArthika jnana or mukti?? I definitely don’t think so 😊
