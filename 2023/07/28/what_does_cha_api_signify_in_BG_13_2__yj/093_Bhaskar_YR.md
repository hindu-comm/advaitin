If in spite of seeing the rope and knowing snake is not there in that place, an imagination of snake in place of rope persists due to vasanas, then perhaps we can say that snake is mithya object in his *mind standpoint* with reference to the actual object in question - it cannot be negated in the mind and yet the cognizer knows the snake attribution is asat in the world.



Ø To mitigate this confusion adhyAsOpadAna (avidyOpadAna) vAdins explain jnAnAdhyAsa and arthAdhyAsa or sOpAdhika and nirupAdhika adhyAsa, nirupAdhika adhyAsa explained taking rajju-sarpa example and sOpAdhika adhyAsa explained by taking the mirage water example. Even after knowing that there is no water only dry land the bhrAnti of existence of water (or cognition of water) would continue there due to sOpAdhika prateeti.
