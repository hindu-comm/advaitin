Well, itslike whatyou say. You know it is all water but you also have cognition of foam, wave, etc. You know it is all energy but you have cognition of iron, wood, etc. There is the pot-space level of cognition where there is difference and duality. There is knowledge of non-dual substratum.

  

But then we have to say, "The non-dual Water appears as-if dual, at the level of cognition". But then, the cognitions are continuously changing, so the Water that alone is is realized (by whom? by Water in Mr. Jnani-wave) as somehow causing/projecting a movie-world at the level of cognition. How so? By its "atmashakti" or "mayashakti"!!

  

That's simple version; it may require tweaks. I don't know if you agree to all this butI say something more which may be our place of disagreement. This standpoint of nama-rupa cognition where wave-foam duality is not lost in spite of knowledge of Water is Mithya. There is the standpoint of the Water wherein the Water-wave duality is entirely sublated. This is not a standpoint of ignorance of wave-foam; it is the final non-dual standpoint of Reality.*Having knowledge of that higher standpoint*, the knower-wave in the standpoint of cognition where he cognizes water appearing as waves and foam regards that world of cognition to be mithya.

  

BTW you probably are aware but for the general audience: what I say should not be understood as perfectly matching with tradition. Given that there are not many willing to endlessly debate Bhaskar-ji, I just imagine I should jump in every now and then with my own limited understanding and logic to defend some concepts in advaita. That's pretty much the only timesI have used theword "mithya" till now. But it should not be misconstrued that traditionalists will approve my takes.
