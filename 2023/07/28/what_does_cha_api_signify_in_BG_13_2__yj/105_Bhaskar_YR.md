Please cancel the above fanciful phrase equation. On second thought, "Brahman is the All" reminds of Saguna Brahman or a Visishtadvaita conception. Best not to have any reference to All in connection to paramarthika standpoint.



Ø Sorry prabhuji this is NOT fanciful phrase equation, it is said in shruti itself explained by bhAshyakAra with the terms like sarvAtmabhAva, Samyak darshana, sama darshana, bhUma drushti, Atmaikatva darshana etc. When the prakruti as Ishwara shakti in upAsana it is upAsya brahma but when jnAni realizes there exists nothing but brahman sarvaM khalvidaM brahma it is upAsana of the saguNa brahma it is the sarvAtmabhAva which is mOksha in the light of svarUpa nirvishesha jnana.
