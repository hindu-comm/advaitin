For how long? This "focus the mind on the witness that is the self" being only provisional, in most cases it becomes a practice for life, without the symptoms of understanding appearing.



- Shruti says sAkshi cheetah kevalo nirguNascha (shwetaashwatara). And
  mAdUkya shruti also says tureeya is avasthAteeta and sAkshi to
  avasthAtraya. So most of us as advaitins say that Atman is sarva
  sAkshi, kevala satta, nitya sAkshi (nitya sAkshitva sattaamAtreNa is
  the bhAshya vAkya) But this sAkshitva too sometimes implies that there
  is sAkshi and he witnesses the external objects as ‘anAtma’ as sAkshi
  and we failed to distinguish the ahaM pratyayi (pramAtru, jnAtru etc.)
  and sAkshi svarUpa and due to this we think (misconceive) ourselves as
  katru, bhOktru etc. (adhyAsa bhAshya says this). But as you rightly
  observed how long we have practice this ‘sAkshitva’ and keeping
  ‘everything else’ aloof from this sAkshi by labelling them as ‘anAtma’
  or sAkshi vedya?? Some one say, these external objects or
  avidyAkruta/mithya till the physical death we have to maintain this
  sAkshi bhAva treating outside world as anAtma vastu. In short, as
  long as you are living in the physical body, even though you are
  paramArtha jnAni you have to ‘just’ witness everything as mithya and
  think yourself as ‘sAkshi Chaitanya. But I am failed to understand
  how this is non-dual state when there is a sAkshi and he is witnessing
  something as mithyA?? No doubt, it is easy to detach ourselves from
  external world and sit in witnessing box but I am afraid it is not
  ekatva state. Because of the reason cited above.



Why? Because from the beginning, one agrees to the practice with the intention of destroying something that he considers separate from Being when in reality it is only different. Different, for the simple reason that it is seen as other than the Being when in reality if there is only Being alone. Jagat is the Being appearing, as if dressed with the koshas, upadis, name, form, functions, etc.



- Yes, beautifully explained, it is kAraNa-s ‘vishesha darshana’ and
  there is exists nothing but being. Seeing the different things does
  not mean it is separate or having its own individual existence
  (svatantra astitvaM). Seeing the rock as rock, sand as sand, tree as
  tree, stree as stree, purusha as purusha is not a problem but seeing
  and treating this as separate from ‘me’ (conditioned perception) is
  the problem which causes bhOktrutva, kartrutva, saMsAritva etc.

  
What would "destroy samsara" mean? If everything is Brahman as the Mandukya Upanishad verse 2 says, who could exclude samsara or jagat from it? We need then to find another meaning or implication for the term "destroy". In my understanding and based on the input of the scriptures, this can only mean the eradication of preconceptions, that we have about such samasara as something separate from Being. It is not necessary to destroy the world, or isolating ourselves, from it. It has nothing necessarily to do either with constantly mentally repeating to yourself, 'I am Brahman ... I am Brahman, or ... "The world is Brahman ... "The world is Brahman". if not simply, "see" the manifestation for what it really is, an erroneous preconception that the assimilation of the pointings of the scriptures help us to eradicate Once all this is assimilated, all phenomena should be seen as pointing to the Self instead of being a veil for the Self.



Ø Cannot agree more prabhuji. Prapancha pravilayaM should be understood in this sense and not that with the jnana I would burn the jagat into ashes.
