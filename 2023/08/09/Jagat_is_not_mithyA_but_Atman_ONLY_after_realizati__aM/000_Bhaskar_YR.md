If I remember right I have shared this information in Advaita-L list few years back when jagat mithyAtva discussion was at full swing in that group😊



In one of his write-ups (in Kannada) in shankara bhaskara magazine, Sri SSS raises a question that whether after realization this jagat would continue to appear as mithyA or satya (Atman) only for the paramArtha jnAni and answers quite tortuously at the end that there is no mithyA vastu as such for the paramArtha jnAni infact there is no time where we can say this jagat is mithyA because there exists nothing apart from brahman at any point of time. ajnAni-s donot know their svarUpa due to this jnAnAbhAva they see nAnAjeeva and respective vyavahAra jagat as separately existing from them. But when they realized their paramArtha svarUpa they will come to know shruti pratipAdita siddhAnta i.e. ‘All this is brahman ONLY’. And in this post realization period this prapancha would be Atman only hence it would become ***parama satya*** in its svarUpa. So, what would be the ultimate answer to the question whether this jagat is mithyA or satya ?? The answer is, in its paramArtha svarUpa ***jagat is Atman only, brahman only therefore it is satya only*** for that matter there is nothing that can be called mithyA/anruta/anAtma. There is no existence to the jagat which is different from brahman and this independent jagat is only in the imagination of ajnAni-s. Hence there is no need to divide and determine the jagat as satya or mithyA. You call it jagat or brahman hardly matters, what is there is the secondless brahman and that is paramArtha satya and nothing is exist apart from it. ( I don’t know these are exact word by word translation of his Kannada article but more or less the gist of that article is this only).





I think in this discussion Sri Chandramouli prabhuji also participated and said that Sri SSS wrong in his observation ( again,I don’t remember his exact words). But in this group, as a follower of Sri SSS teaching I would like to present what he said in the above article. Readers can draw their own conclusion after reading it.
