Dear Chandramouliji,

  

In the Vedic texts including the four Vedas, all the Upanishadas and even including the two great epics: the Ramayana and the Mahabharata, the wordings and sometimes even the complete sentences were not to be interpreted in very simplistic way. Jaganmithya does not mean the Jagat is not unreat. It means that everything in the Jahat is temporary or ever changing.  

  

After writing the Mahabharata, Maharshi Vedavyasa was very sad as the Mahabharata turned out to be difficult to understand by most of the people. Then Devarshi Narada advised him to write the Bhagavatam in a simplistic language so that, it would be intelligible to all people.  

  

In my childhood I used to read the Bhagavatam to my old grandmother, as her eyesight was not so good, and whenever I had problem in understanding, my grandmother used to explain that to me.
