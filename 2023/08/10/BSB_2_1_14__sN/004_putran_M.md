Namaskaram Kalyan-ji,

  

Thanks for the reference. ([https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/BS#BS_C02](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/BS#BS_C02))

  

Shankara does state in multiple places that nama-rupa is avidyakalpita but I can imagine the import will be argued on. For example, "iva" is translatable "as if", so someone can say the right meaning there is "nama-rupa is as-if \[though not really\] avidyakalpita \[but really maya-srishti\]". Likewise the swami has translated anirvachaniya as the standard definition of mithya (I think this is in accordance with tradition) but this will be denied by opponents. Anycase, given that Bhaskar-ji keeps quoting BSB, those who may disagree with him on these topics can note and analyze places in the bhashya where such references occur.
