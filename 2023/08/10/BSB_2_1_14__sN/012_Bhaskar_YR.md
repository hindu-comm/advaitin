Shankara does state in multiple places that nama-rupa is avidyakalpita



- Yes, now I wonder how mUlAvidyAvAdins here avoid tautology!!?? As per
  them, nAma-rUpa is itself avidyA/mAya which is the upAdAna kAraNa for
  the adhyAsa. But here it is clearly saying nAma rUpa is
  avidyAkalpita, so as per mUlAvidyAvAdins we have to translate this as
  : avidyA (nAma-rUpa) is avidyA Kalpita, like concept A is the cause of
  concept A, how this dvi-rukti dOsha can be avoided here as per them??
  Just curious to know.





but I can imagine the import will be argued on. For example, "iva" is translatable "as if", so someone can say the right meaning there is "nama-rupa is as-if \[though not really\] avidyakalpita \[but really maya-srishti\]".



- See those who are saying nAma-rUpa is brahman saying like that by
  keeping in mind that there is absolutely no significance in themselves
  ‘if viewed independently’. The purpose of this is to know the cause
  behind appearing nAma-rUpa. ONLY to understand the clayness (mrut
  sAmAnya) we are holding the nama-rUpa of clay ( pot, pitcher, jar
  etc.) And once we know the clay (the kAraNa) in name and shape (kArya)
  all karya-s will be known without any difficulty. Like knowing the
  basics of addition, any number of problems with regard to addition can
  be solved, this is the purpose the nAma and rUpa is served in knowing
  the tattva behind nAma rUpa. By knowing which everything will be
  known ( Sri Praveen bhat prabhuji having this tag line in all his
  mails). And it is only through them (nAma rUpa) we can understand the
  cause because cause as it is does not have any shape and name and
  through shape and name only the sAmAnya to be understood beyond the
  name and forms ( yadi hi nAmarUpe na vyakriyete tadA asyAtmanO
  nirupAdhikaM rUpaM prajnAnaghanAkyaM na pratikyAyet says bhAshyakAra
  somewhere. Hence we often say kArya-kAraNa prakriya is not there to
  advocate kAraNatvaM to brahman but to show the AtmaikatvaM of
  brahman.





Likewise the swami has translated anirvachaniya as the standard definition of mithya (I think this is in accordance with tradition) but this will be denied by opponents.



- mAya is anirvachaneeya but as per mUlAvidyAvAdins avidyA is
  anirvachaneeya. The sarpa in rope is mithyA only (adhyAropita due to
  lack of right knowledge of rope), the sarpa is not anirvachaneeya
  here, no sane person would say (after having the rope knowledge) I was
  watching some anirvachaneeya sarpa all these days I cannot say whether
  it is sarpa or rope (sadasadvilakshaNa)!! He only say it was / is /
  will ever be rope only due to my ignorance ( or lack of knowledge of
  rope) I was seeing sarpa and sarpa was not at all existent in rope at
  any point of time. Please note here before the knowledge sarpa is
  sarpa only it is not anirvachaneeya and after the knowledge of rope
  sarpa is not there. But anirvachaneeya khyAti vAdins giving some
  indescribable / undefinable existence to sarpa even after the right
  knowledge of rope, the reason they give is, the cognition of sarpa
  (prior to the dawn of right knowledge) as a result fear, trembling,
  sweating etc. if anirvachaneeya sarpa is not there how come these side
  effects?? So goes their arguments 😊
