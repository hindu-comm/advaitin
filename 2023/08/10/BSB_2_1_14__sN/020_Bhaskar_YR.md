Hare Krishna



I have been thinking about this, and another possible interpretation of तत्त्वान्यत्वाभ्यामनिर्वचनीये could be - something that cannot be determined to be either identical to tattva (brahman) or different from tattva (brahman).



I am also curious to know what Sri Bhaskar-ji's interpretation is.



Ø This has been said about ‘mAya’ and not on avidyA. Can anyone have the notion/doubt that this avidyA might be tattva (brahman) or different from brahman?? No, avidyA is like poison (see geeta bhAshya) it is ‘anishta’ a big obstacle to know our svarUpa and this avidyA is crisply and clearly explained as agrahaNa, anyathAgrahaNa and saMshaya.
So, avidyA is not anirvachaneeya it is ‘nirvachaneeya’ and it is there naturally in our lOkAnubhava. Whereas mAya when it comes to kArya-kAraNa it is brahma shakti which can not be clearly said whether it is brahman or different from brahman and it is also called beeja, shakti, avyAkruta, avyakta, mUla prakruti etc. avyaktA hi sa mAya tattvAtatvanirUpaNasya ashakyatvAt. ( 1-4-3).
