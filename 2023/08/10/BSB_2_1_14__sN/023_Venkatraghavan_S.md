What is the parama utkRShTa entity that you are talking about? If Brahman, then not sure what you mean by "jnAna does not have the capability to do anything afresh". We are not stating that avidyAkArya (this world) is a real creation, so don't understand what you mean by a fresh creation. In fact, from a paramArtha dRShTi, there is no creation.

  

As we have discussed many times before, to us, avidyA is triguNAtmikA and is the same as mAyA. Therefore, if avidyA is aniShTa, then mAyA and its kArya are aniShTa also. We have no problem with that.

> This is what main objection the MVV face in Sri SSS works. Moreover,

mUlAvidyA is not another entity apart from this beeja shakti, it is the bIjashakti itself. If you are willing to accept that the avyakta nAmarUpa can be shakti, what is so difficult in saying that we are referring to the same entity as avidyA?

  

What I see Sri SSS' camp doing is to postulate a false dichotomy between avidyA and mAyA and then say why should there be a separate entity, a special mediator called avidyA when mAyA itself is enough. The charge you are leveling (what is the need for a special mediator) is on the basis of a supposition that you made (avidyA being different from mAyA), one that we never agreed with in the first place.

  

"When it is specifically said adhyasta is Kalpita due to  

 due to aviveka is there any need to advocate ‘material’ cause for it?? "  

Every effect must have a cause. Cause in advaita is twofold - upAdAna and nimitta. The nimittakAraNa has been said as aviveka in the adhyAsabhAShya sentence. We are saying that to say that the upAdAna kAraNa for this adhyAsa (anAtma / world adhyAsa) is mithyA ajnAna, Shankara says mithyAjnAna-nimittah.

  

You tell me - what is the upAdAna kAraNa for this physical body? You will have to ultimately agree it is mAyA. That mAyA is the same as avidyA, in our view.

  

You also have to provide an alternative meaning for the phrase mithyAjnAnanimittah that is not already referred to by other words in the sentence. I haven't seen a credible alternative yet.
