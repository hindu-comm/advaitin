That is true. Brahman does have avidyA as his power.

1\) This does not mean that avidyA is capable of afflicting Brahman, because the upAdhi only affects the upahita / pratibimba and not the bimba. Just like the dirt in the mirror only appears in the reflection and not the original face.

  

2\) Secondly the ananyatva between the shaktimAn and shakti spoken of in the gItAbgAShya is the same ananyatva between kArya and kAraNa revealed in the tadananyatva adhikaraNa - it is an AdhyAsika tAdAtmya, not atyanta abheda. AvidyA / mAyA is mithyA and can have never have the same sattA as Brahman, nor is it absolutely identical with Brahman - if it was absolutely identical, because avidyA/mAyA is subject to change, Brahman would be subject to change and therefore end up being anitya.

  

3\) thirdly, on the basis of the mAyAm tu prakRtim vidyAt mAyinam tu maheshvaram shruti, we hold that Ishvara is the mAyi who wields the mAyika avidyA shakti to create this universe.

No, because lakshaNa pramANAbhyAm vastusiddhih. If what you said was true - ie that there was no need for pramANa, or lakshaNa of adhyAsa to be explained in great detail, Shankara could have simply said naisargikah ayam lokavyavahArah and stopped.

  

He doesn't - he goes on to explain what is the adhyAsa, that ignorance causes it and how it explains our daily behaviour. Why do we say that? For this, we have to underatand why Shankaracharya decides to start his bhAShya on the brahmasUtra with the adhyAsabhAShya. On the face of it, one cannot observe the connection between the first sUtra (athAto brahmajijnAsa) and the adhyAsabhAShya.

  

If you look at the first sUtra - athAto brahmajijnAsA - the sUtrakAra is indicating that brahmajnAna is the cause for moksha / bandha nivRtti. The logic is as follows:

  

1)One must commence the enquiry of Brahman (brahmajijnAsA kartavyA). Why? Because it gives rise to brahmajnAna.

  

2\) One needs brahmajnAna because it satisfies the desire of moksha for the candidate endowed with mumukshutvam (indicated by the word atha).

  

3\) What is moksha? The nature of moksha is bandha nivRtti.

  

4\) Therefore, the first sUtra implies that brahmajnAna is needed for bandha nivRtti.

  

5\) For that to be the case, it is necessary to establish that the bandha is of the nature of something that is capable of being removed by jnAna.

  

6\) For this, it is necessary to prove that bandha is AdhyAsika, and that avidyA is the cause for such an adhyAsa - because jnAna removes only avidyA.

  

7\) Even further, it is fundamentally necessary for avidyA to be a material cause for adhyAsa, because jnAna's ability to remove adhyAsa is contingent on adhyAsa having avidyA as its material content, such that upon the removal of avidyA by jnAna, the effect, adhyAsa which is made up of avidyA is also removed (kAraNa nAshAt kArya nAsha).

  

8\) It is not sufficient for avidyA to only be a nimitta kAraNa, because the destruction of a nimitta kAraNa cannot destroy the effect - the potters death does not destroy the pot; the clay's destruction destroys the pot.

  

9\) Because jnAna is capable of removing only ajnAna and nothing else, the removal of adhyAsa by jnAna is contingent on adhyAsa having ajnAna as its upAdAna kAraNa.

  

Only if all of this is proven would it be appropriate to start off with the contention - brahma jnAna is the cause for bandha nivRtti.

  

It is for this purpose that the adhyAsabhAShya was commenced by the bhAShyakAra. If this was the purpose, it is not sufficient to say that adhyAsa is naisargika - ie beginningless. It has to be established that such a naisargika thing is capable of having an end with jnAna.

  

So how does he do this? He does it by showing that the upAdAnakAraNa of the adhyAsa is avidyA, and when the kAraNa nAsha (avidyAnAsha) happens through jnAna, kArya nAsha (adhyAsanAsha) happens, justifying the start of the sUtra with brahmajijnAsA kartavyA.

  

If all that was said was naisargikoyam lokavyavahArah, what is the intent behind commencing the sUtrabhAShya with an introduction talking about an adhyAsa? How does it stand proven that brahmajnAna is necessary to remove such a naisargika / anAdi adhyAsa?
