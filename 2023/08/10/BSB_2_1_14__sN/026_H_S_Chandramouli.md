Namaste Venkat Ji,

Since no one else has answered your query on
\*\*mithyAjnAnanimittah\*\*, I will limit myself to that and just
present the views of Sri SSS as broughtout in his work \*\*SugamA\*\*.

// \*\* मिथ्याज्ञाननिमित्तः सत्यानृते मिथुनीकृत्य व्यवहारः\*\* इत्येतत्तु विस्पष्टकथनार्थम् । \*\*इतरेतराविवेकेन मिथ्याज्ञाननिमित्तः\*\* इति \*\*अविवेकोत्थं यन्मिथ्याज्ञानं तन्निमित्तः\*\* इत्यर्थः । गमकत्वात् समासः । अथवा अविवेकेनाध्यस्येति पूर्वेणैवान्वयः । अध्यासमिथ्याज्ञानयोरैक्यादुभयथापि यथोक्त एवाभिप्रायः । अविवेकोत्थं यन्मिथ्याज्ञानम् इत्युक्त्या अविवेकमिथ्याज्ञानयोरुपादानोपादेयभावो नोपेयः, किं तर्ह्यात्मैव सत्यं वस्तु नान्यतोऽस्तीति ज्ञानाभावादज्ञातात्मैवानात्मरूपेण प्रतिभासत इति निमित्तनैमित्तिकभावोऽवसेयः । //.

// \*\* mithyAj~nAnanimittaH satyAnRRite mithunIkRRitya vyavahAraH\*\*
ityetattu vispaShTakathanArtham \| \*\*itaretarAvivekena mithyAj~nAnanimittaH\*\* iti \*\*avivekotthaM yanmithyAj~nAnaM tannimittaH\*\* ityarthaH \| gamakatvAt samAsaH \| athavA avivekenAdhyasyeti pUrveNaivAnvayaH \|
adhyAsamithyAj~nAnayoraikyAdubhayathApi yathokta evAbhiprAyaH \|
avivekotthaM yanmithyAj~nAnam ityuktyA avivekamithyAj~nAnayorupAdAnopAdeyabhAvo nopeyaH, kiM tarhyAtmaiva satyaM vastu nAnyato.astIti j~nAnAbhAvAdaj~nAtAtmaivAnAtmarUpeNa pratibhAsata iti nimittanaimittikabhAvo.avaseyaH \| //.

Translation // The Bhashya part \*\* मिथ्याज्ञाननिमित्तः सत्यानृते मिथुनीकृत्य व्यवहारः\*\* is for making clear the idea.The anvaya \*\* इतरेतराविवेकेन मिथ्याज्ञाननिमित्तः\*\* means because of the wrong understanding which has arisen due to lack of discrimination. Such type of samAsa has a basis. (Note, not part of translation. VyAkaraNa shAstra admits of such a samAsa under special circumstances). Alternatively, previously mentioned word अविवेक can be related to (anvaya) the even earlier word अध्यस्य . Since both अध्यास and मिथ्याज्ञान mean the same, whichever way the anvaya is established the understanding turns out to be identical. (However here) Since it is stated that मिथ्याज्ञान arises due to nondiscrimination upAdAna-upAdEya relationship should not be understood as between अज्ञान and मिथ्याज्ञान. Then how it should be understood is
-- Due to lack of understanding of the nature \*\*Atma is the only satya vastu. There is nothing else beside that\*\*, the concealed (veiled) Atman itself appears in the form of anAtma bearing a nimitta-naimittika relationship //.

Except for the translation part, rest is exact reproduction from the text by Sri SSS. The translation (which I have added for the benefit of other readers) could be wanting in some respects. I have based it on the kannada translation of the text by Sri Chandramouli Avadhani. Any errors in translation from kannada to english is mine only.

Regards
