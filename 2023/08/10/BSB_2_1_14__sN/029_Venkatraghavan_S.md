Now I understand! Thanks for sharing this.

  

Sri SSS is doing the anvaya of mithyAjnAnanimittah with the vyavahArah. ie the "ahamidam mamedam vyavahAra" is because of mithyAjnAna.

  

This leads to the query of how avivekena can have anvaya with mithyAjnAna, following which(?) a samAsa happens with nimittah, to which a Sanskrit vyAkaraNa rule is hinted at. Perhaps someone with expertise in vyAkaraNa can comment on this.

  

The alternative is for the anvaya to be between avivekena and adhyasya, with mithyAjnAnanimittah having anvaya with vyavahArah.

  

Meaning that the adhyAsa is as a result of aviveka, and vyavahAra is as a result of that adhyAsa (mithyAjnAna).

  

In both explanations, Sri SSS is apparently drawing a distinction between the Atma anAtma adhyAsa and ahamidam mamedam vyavahAra.
