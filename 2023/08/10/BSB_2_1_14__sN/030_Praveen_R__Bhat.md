I am not even close to an expert in vyAkaraNa by any standard, but as far as I can tell, this is what is called as an ekadeshAnvaya where the anvaya of a vyasthapada is with a pUrvapada of a samAsa. It is seen at many places though not an appropriate samAsa as per strict rules, such as devadattasya guroH kulam reading as devattasya gurukulam.
