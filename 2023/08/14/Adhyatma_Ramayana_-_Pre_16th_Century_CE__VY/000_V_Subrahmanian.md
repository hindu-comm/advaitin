In the Ramayana tatparya sara sangraha stotram Sri Appayya Dikshitar cites a few verses from the Adhyatma Ramayana, by naming the text. 

  

Appayya Dikshitar's timeline is:1520–1593 CE.

  

[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adhyatma_Ramayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adhyatma_Ramayana)  

  

***Adhyatma Ramayana***([Devanāgarī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devan%C4%81gar%C4%AB "Devanāgarī"): अध्यात्म रामायण,[IAST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Alphabet_of_Sanskrit_Transliteration "International Alphabet of Sanskrit Transliteration"):*Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa*,lit. 'Spiritual Ramayana') is a 13th- to 15th-century[Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit "Sanskrit")text that allegorically interprets the story of Hindu epic*[Ramayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramayana "Ramayana")*in the[Advaita Vedanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advaita_Vedanta "Advaita Vedanta")framework.^([\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adhyatma_Ramayana#cite_note-FOOTNOTERocher1986157%E2%80%93159-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adhyatma_Ramayana#cite_note-Lamb2012p28-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adhyatma_Ramayana#cite_note-Richman2008p17-3))It is embedded in the latter portion of*[Brahmānda Purana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahm%C4%81nda_Purana "Brahmānda Purana"),*and the author is considered to be[Vyasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vyasa "Vyasa").^([\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adhyatma_Ramayana#cite_note-4))The Hindu tradition also attributes the text to the Bhakti movement saint[Ramananda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramananda "Ramananda").^([\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adhyatma_Ramayana#cite_note-FOOTNOTERocher1986158-159_with_footnotes,_'''Quote:'''_%22Among_the_texts_considered_to_be_connected_with_the_''Brahmanda'',_the_''Adhyatma-ramayana''_is_undoubtedly_the_most_important_one%22-5))
