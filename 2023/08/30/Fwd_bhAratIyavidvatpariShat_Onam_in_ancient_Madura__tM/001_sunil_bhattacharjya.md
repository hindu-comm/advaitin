Devi Meenakshi (Mother Meenakshi) was the younger sister of Lord Krishna. In her marriage, Lord Krishna Himself gave the Kanyadan in Madurai. There was a Global Flood, after one week of Lord Krishna's leaving for his heavenly abode, but the flood water came right upto the base of the Meenakshi Devii temple, on the hill-top, but no damage was done to the temple. From all these historical details, it appears to me that Maduarai could be the Southern Mathura.
