Dear Dennis ji,

  

In the scenario of this verse, there are others. The nature of realization of the Jnani is described and stated that such a realized man ought to be venerated. This is the teaching. Such veneration expressed by others will bring them a lot of good. From the Jnani'sperspective the others are not there in truth but his accepting them in vyavahara is admissible, exactly the same way Krishna taught and the Upanishadic Teachers expounded the Truth toone or more aspirants.
