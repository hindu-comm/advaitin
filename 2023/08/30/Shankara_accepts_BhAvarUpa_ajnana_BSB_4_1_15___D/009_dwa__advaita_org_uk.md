Dear Subbu-ji,



Yes – I have said much the same in my books. But this seems to deny EJV any meaning. We know that in reality there is no creation at all, whereas in empirical reality we have lots of jIva-s and a world. To talk about EJV and immediately about ‘veneration’ or whatever seems to be mixing levels of reality and is a clear contradiction of reason. As I say in ‘Confusions 2’, “It is Subjective Idealism, not Advaita”.



Also, you translate: “It is me who has projected this world out of my avidya”. Since the j~nAnI now has vidyA, one would have presumed that he no longer ‘projects the world’, in which case there is no empirical reality and no ‘others’ to venerate him.



Can you rationalize this?
