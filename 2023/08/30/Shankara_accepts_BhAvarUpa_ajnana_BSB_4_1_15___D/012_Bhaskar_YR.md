Hare Krishna



While on the subject it would be better to be noted that jnAni who realized that there is no snake in the rope (no other jeeva other than him) not playing the ‘pungi’(teaching) to appease the nonexistent snake (other jeeva) in the rope 😊 When he realized there is no snake in the rope why on the earth he plays pungi?? And bhAshyakAra very clearly says the jagat is not like nonexistent snake in the rope.



One must be careful in understanding the examples like rajju-sarpa, shukti-rajata etc. One should not imagine more than its intended meaning from an analogy. Therefore, one should not argue see in the example of rajju-sarpa, rajju stands for brahman and snake is for the world. As snake is nonexistent in the example, the world is also not existent!!
As already said, bhAshyakAra himself has cautioned about taking this analogy beyond its scope. However, these examples have been used to drive home the point that there is no causal relation like in the examples of pot-clay or survarNAbharaNa, in the examples of the two moons, silver-shell, rajju-sarpa, etc. That the one in another is only a wrong understanding (mithyAgnAna / vipareeta grahaNa), a plain illusion due to avidyA (jnAnAbhAva). In the sentence, “the rope understood as snake” the word rope represents the existing rope but the word snake represents only the impression of its existence. This is because snake is just wrong understanding, there is really no snake in it.
bhAshyakara explains this in sU.bh. by taking the example of shukti-rajata, shktikAm rajatamiti pratyeti ityatra shukti vachana eva shuktikAshabdaH. But rajatashabdastu rajata prateetilakshaNArthaH, pratyetyeva hi kevalaM rajatamiti ‘na tu tatra rajatamasti’. From this it is quite clear bhAshyakAra emphasizes only jnAnAdhyAsa not so particular about arthAdhyAsa.
