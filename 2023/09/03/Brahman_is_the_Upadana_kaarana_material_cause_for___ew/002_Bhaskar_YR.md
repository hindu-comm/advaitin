Hare Krishna



Is brahman the material cause of the world? Or is mAyA the material cause of the world?



Ø Brahman through mAya is the material cause of this world.



Both view points exist?



Ø Whenever there is talk about ‘world’ and its srushti, brahman uses his ‘power’ called maya to do srushti. mAya in this srushti prakriya ‘abhinna’ from brahman hence non-duality of brahman is not disturbed.



Further, what does the word "material cause" signify? If, say, a piece of paper is taken and investigated materially to the deepest level, will it be observed to be same as brahman? If not, in what sense is brahman the material cause of the paper? Or brahman has made an illusory transformation as the paper? But this is not exactly the same as clay-pot, is it?



Ø upAdAna and nimitta kAraNatwam to brahman is not there to dissect the material world and find out the brahman in it. It is there to teach us there exists ONLY brahman and nothing but brahman.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
