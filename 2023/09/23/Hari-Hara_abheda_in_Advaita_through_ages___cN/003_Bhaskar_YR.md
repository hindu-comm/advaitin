Hare Krishna



I had expressed my observation with regard to this earlier as well.
There is absolutely no problem in seeing the difference between hari-hara keeping their respective upAdhi bedha in mind. When we talk anything about hari we have certain ‘nAma-rUpa’ in mind to say or identify him as hari or vishNu, likewise hara-shiva also. So, in vyavahAra where nAma-rUpa holds sway there is difference between these two upAdhi-s, for that matter all remaining paNchAyatana devata-s (like gaNapati, durga, sUrya) and all other gods, goddesses in Hindu pantheon. When these deities are considered as eka Chaitanya, there is absolutely no difference among these deities and jeeva-s as well, as we are all (including hari-hara) that ‘eka chaitanyaM’ only. In transaction no one say hari’s wife is Parvati and Ganesha-subramanya are sons of mahAvishNu 😊 So both hari and hara have their own family and own legacy there is no need to see the indifference between them. But we have to always keep in mind that seeing the difference is not a problem but seeing the difference AND deciding one is superior to another or one is less powerful when compared to the other is the problem. This is what mAdhva-s actually do, tAratamya 😊



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
