In what way is parinami upAdAnakAraNatvam of prakRti different from the kAraNatvam of pradhAna of the sAMkhya-s?

  

It might be said that pradhAna is independent in nirIshwara sAMkhya. But in seshwara sAMkhya, Ishwara would control pradhAna also? Can it then be said that seshwara sAMkhya and advaita are same in vyavahAra?
