On Sun, Sep 24, 2023 at 2:31 PM Kalyan \<[kalyanchakr...@gmail.com]()\> wrote:  

> In what way is parinami upAdAnakAraNatvam of prakRti different from
> the kAraNatvam of pradhAna of the sAMkhya-s?
>
>   
>
> It might be said that pradhAna is independent in nirIshwara sAMkhya.
> But in seshwara sAMkhya, Ishwara would control pradhAna also? Can it
> then be said that seshwara sAMkhya and advaita are same in vyavahAra?

  

Yes, in Yoga (sEshwara sAnkhya) Ishwara is kevala nimitta kAraNam and Prakruti/Pradhana is upadana karana operating under His control. In Advaita, Brahman is abhinna nimitta upAdana kaaranam, with the Prakriti as Shakti.
