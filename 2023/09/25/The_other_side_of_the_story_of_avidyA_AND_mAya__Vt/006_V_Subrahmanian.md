This part of the commentary says what I have said:

  

//My own; prakrtim, Prakrti, the Maya of Visnu consisting of the three gunas, under whose; spell the whole world exists, and deluded by which one does not know one's own Self, Vasudeva;-//

  

The self of everyone is Vasudeva. But one does not know this. This shows that Vasudeva who controls his Prakriti, is the same one deluded as a jiva too.

  

We get these two very significant conclusions from Shankara's commentaries:

  

It is the One Brahman alone that appears as the Omniscient Ishwara and also as the ignorant jiva.

  

It is the One Vaishnavi Maya that doubles up as the Maya Shakti of Ishwara to control jivas and the world, and also as the avidya that keeps the jiva in its grip.

  

Both these extremely crucial tenets emerge from Shankara's commentaries.
