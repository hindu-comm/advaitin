There is no rule that there has to be a direct statement. From the various other statements made by Shankara we can conclude that Vasudeva alone is the deluded jiva:

  

Shankara establishes with logic and with a host of shruti-smriti pramanas that it is Brahman alone that is avidyAvanta, appearing as jiva: 

  

भवत्येवं नाविद्याकर्तृभ्रान्तं च ब्रह्म। किन्तु नैव अब्रह्म अविद्यकर्ता चेतनो भ्रान्तोऽन्य इष्यते —[‘नान्योऽतोऽस्ति विज्ञाता’ (बृ. उ. ३। ७। २३)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=3&id=BR_C03_S07_V23&hl=%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE)[‘नान्यदतोऽस्ति विज्ञातृ’ (बृ. उ. ३। ८। ११)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=3&id=BR_C03_S08_V11&hl=%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%83)[‘तत्त्वमसि’ (छा. उ. ६। ८। ७)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Chandogya?page=6&id=Ch_C06_S08_V07&hl=%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%BF)[‘आत्मानमेवावेत् अहं ब्रह्मास्मि’ (बृ. उ. १। ४। १०)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=1&id=BR_C01_S04_V10&hl=%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%20%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%AC%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BF)[‘अन्योऽसावन्योऽहमस्मीति, न स वेद’ (बृ. उ. १। ४। १०)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=1&id=BR_C01_S04_V10&hl=%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF,%20%E0%A4%A8%20%E0%A4%B8%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A6)इत्यादिश्रुतिभ्यः ; स्मृतिभ्यश्च —[‘समं सर्वेषु भूतेषु’ (भ. गी. १३। २७)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=13&id=BG_C13_V27&hl=%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%81%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%81)[‘अहमात्मा गुडाकेश’ (भ. गी. १०। २०)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=10&id=BG_C10_V20&hl=%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B6)[‘शुनि चैव श्वपाके च’ (भ. गी. ५। १८)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=5&id=BG_C05_V18&hl=%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%9A%E0%A5%88%E0%A4%B5%20%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%87%20%E0%A4%9A);[‘यस्तु सर्वाणि भूतानि’ (ई. उ. ६)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Isha?page=NaN&id=IS_V06&hl=%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%81%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF)[‘यस्मिन्सर्वाणि भूतानि’ (ई. उ. ७)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Isha?page=NaN&id=IS_V07&hl=%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A3%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF)इति च मन्त्रवर्णात्।

  

In this particular bhashya for[‘नान्यदतोऽस्ति विज्ञातृ’ (बृ. उ. ३। ८। ११)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=3&id=BR_C03_S08_V11&hl=%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%83)Shankara says:

  

किं च नान्यत् अतः अस्मादक्षरात् अस्ति — नास्ति किञ्चिद्द्रष्टृ दर्शनक्रियाकर्तृ ; एतदेवाक्षरं दर्शनक्रियाकर्तृ सर्वत्र। तथा नान्यदतोऽस्ति श्रोतृ ; तदेवाक्षरं श्रोतृ सर्वत्र। नान्यदतोऽस्ति मन्तृ ; तदेवाक्षरं मन्तृ सर्वत्र सर्वमनोद्वारेण। नान्यदतोऽस्ति विज्ञातृ विज्ञानक्रियाकर्तृ, तदेवाक्षरं सर्वबुद्धिद्वारेण विज्ञानक्रियाकर्तृ, नाचेतनं प्रधानम् अन्यद्वा।  

  

Apart from this Aksharam. (AntaryAmi) there is no other sentient being that is the seer, hearer, thinker, etc.

  

And that AntaryAmi, Shankara has said, in that very section at the beginning at Brihadaranyaka 3.7.3 that:

  

य ईदृगीश्वरो नारायणाख्यः, पृथिवीं पृथिवीदेवताम् , यमयति नियमयति स्वव्यापारे, अन्तरः अभ्यन्तरस्तिष्ठन् , एष त आत्मा, ते तव, मम च सर्वभूतानां च इत्युपलक्षणार्थमेतत् , अन्तर्यामी यस्त्वया पृष्टः, अमृतः सर्वसंसारधर्मवर्जित इत्येतत् ॥  

  

This Ishwara, called Narayana, is the one that impels the body-mind-organs complex of the jiva (prithivi devataa). This is the Antaryami, the Atma, of you, me and everyone.

  

So, reading these two statements of Shankara from the same Antaryami section, the conclusion is: It is that Narayana, the AntaryAmi, is the jiva who alone experiences 'I see, I hear, I think..'

  

And Shankara cites this mantra in the Aham Brahma Asmi mantra to show that Brahman alone is endowed with avidyA. He cites a host of other mantras which all say that alone: there is none other than Brahman that is the jiva. Since the Antaryami is named Narayana by Shankara and also stated that this antaryami alone is the seer, etc. it is quite evident that Vasudeva is the deluded jiva.

  

In fact in other places too in the Gita Shankara has stated that the jiva's realization is 'aham vAsudevah' as stated by Bhagavan himself.

  

Also in the specific Gita bhashya, we have the word 'yayA' to connect the Vaishnavi Maya that is in the control of Vasudeva with the power that deludes the jiva who does not know his self to be Vasudeva. That yayA in Grammar means: ekakartr. That very Vaishnavi Maya in whose control the entire world is, is the same power that also deludes the jiva. This connection is enough to prove that Vasudeva, in whose control is this Vaishnavi maya, is also the one subject to its deluding power.

  

The Vivekachudamani verse explains this very well:

  

ऐक्यं तयोर्लक्षितयोर्न वाच्ययो - र्निगद्यतेऽन्योन्यविरुद्धधर्मिणोः ।

खद्योतभान्वोरिव राजभृत्ययोः कूपाम्बुराश्योः परमाणुमेर्वोः ॥ २४४ ॥ 

  

Om Tat Sat
