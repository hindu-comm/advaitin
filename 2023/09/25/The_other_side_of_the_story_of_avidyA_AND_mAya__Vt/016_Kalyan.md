भवत्येवं नाविद्याकर्तृभ्रान्तं च ब्रह्म। किन्तु नैव अब्रह्म अविद्यकर्ता चेतनो भ्रान्तोऽन्य इष्यते

  

The first statement that is quoted above, is actually denying that brahman is deluded!

  

The second statement is only denying the existence of a second conscious entity (that can be postulated as a possible candidate for being deluded).
