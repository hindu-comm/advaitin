[[সপ্ত Rishi	2023-09-26, 16:22:34 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/Vt4RYhoZjwE)]]

Opponent: Who is it then that has this unenlightenment?

Vedantin : We say that it is you yourself who ask thus.

Opponent: Is it not stated by the Upanishad that I am God/vAsudeva?

Vedantin: If that is so, you are already an enlightened man, and so nobody has unenlightenment.

  

If Sri Shankara wanted to say God/vAsudeva/Ishwara/brahman is deluded, above would have been the best place. But he does not say so.

  

Well,sir who is this opponent,is it ishvara,obviously no.

  

The one who has question is endowed with ajnana,therefore not ishwara(sarvajna).

So then who is having this ajnana,answer:jiva.

  

What is this jiva entity ?

  

This jiva is maya+avidya upadhi chaitanya.

  

What is this chaitanya ?😊

  

It's obvious the jiva is having ajnana.

But upadhis are achetanam,and as faras I know,chaitanyam alone registers experiences.

  

The effect of upadhi is upon chaitanyam,and the result is jiva.
