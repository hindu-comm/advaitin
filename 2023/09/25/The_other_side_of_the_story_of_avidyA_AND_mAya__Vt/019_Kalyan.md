With all due respect, there is a lot of difference between "as though deluded" and "deluded". Still, apart from the Karika instance, I do not remember any other place where Sri Shankara suggests brahman to be "as though deluded" either.

  

Having said that, I agree with your point that in Gita 4.6, mAyA is treated the same as avidyA. But there are other places where they are treated differently, so I have no final opinion on this.
