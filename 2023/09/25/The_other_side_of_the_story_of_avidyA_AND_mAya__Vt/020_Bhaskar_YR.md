With all due respect, there is a lot of difference between "as though deluded" and "deluded". Still, apart from the Karika instance, I do not remember any other place where Sri Shankara suggests brahman to be "as though deluded" either.



Ø IMO both assertions i.e. ‘as though deluded’ and ‘deluded’ attributed to parabrahman / Ishwara is apasiddhAnta in AV and it is due to avidyA of jeeva, jeeva dares to say brahman is having avidyA or brahman ‘as if’ having the avidyA deluded himself by it etc.!!. In short as per AV, Ishwara is avidyA vinirmukta and he is nitya Shuddha buddha mukta svarUpa. These types of siddhAnta hAni statements you can expect only when you illogically equate the avidyA with mAya. Take the example of geeta verses 4-5 & 6 which passionately quotes by avidyA-mAya abedha vAdins, Lord Krishna himself uses the words like : ahaM punaH nityashuddhamukta svabhAvatvAt anAvaraNa jnana shaktiH etc. is he saying I am deluded by my own mAya?? OTOH he is declaring mAya shakti is under his complete control (vasheekrutya) through which he is dehavAniva bhavati, jaata eva etc. and he is also saying Arjuna you don’t know all this but I know all this and your birth is due to your karma and my socalled birth is my iccha. His anAvaraNa jnana shakti quite evidently showing us that there is no cover as such for his power of jnana and the mAya’s whose control all the jeeva-s act and suffer is under his control. So, as said above, statements like ‘as though deluded’ and deluded etc. attributed to Ishwara / brahman are just blabbering of jeeva who is deluded himself and ‘wrongly’ and mischievously pasting it on his own nitya Shuddha buddha mukta very svarUpa. Is there any limit to this jeeva’s avidyA rOdana?? I really doubt. And with respect to kArika 2.19 ‘mOhitaH svayaM’ quoted out of context mOhitaH svayaM here contextually to be understood that it is upAdhi parichinna chaitanyaM and since Chaitanya is one without second the upAdhi vishesha Chaitanya is though parameshwara in his svarUpa he looks as if he is deluded due to antaHkaraNAdi upAdhi sambandha clarifies bhAshyakAra. So strictly speaking there is no avidyA to even jeeva because as per siddhAnta he is in svarUpa parameshwara only. So when jeeva is taken as parichinna Chaitanya he looks like he is deluded but in reality he is nitya Shuddha mukta svarUpa Atman only that is what subsequently clarified by bhAshyakAra by quoting geeta 7-14 where lord addresses triguNatmika mAya as ‘mama mAya’ which is difficult to cross by jeeva-s and those who surrender unto me they will cross it and attains me. Etc. It is no way a verse which supports the equation of avidyA and mAya and no way it would help us to conclude brahman himself ‘deluded’ or as it deluded etc. 



- Further Ishwara as the creator of jagat in Advaita is omnipresent,
  omnipotent and omniscient and he is eternally pure self aware and
  liberated and in this jeeva-jagat-Ishwara triad he is greater than the
  jeeva and jeeva is NOT Ishwara. Yat sarvajnaM sarvashakti brahma
  ‘nityashuddhabuddhamukta svabhAvaM’ shAreerAt anevaM vidahaH clarifies
  bhAshyakAra. It is because of the simple fact that Ishwara as per
  Advaita siddhAnta nityanivruttAtvidyAtvAt. The magician never ever
  try to pluck the fruit by deluding himself of his own mAyA srushti.
  He knows it is under his control hence it is called brahma mAnasa
  pratyaya.



Having said that, I agree with your point that in Gita 4.6, mAyA is treated the same as avidyA. But there are other places where they are treated differently, so I have no final opinion on this.
