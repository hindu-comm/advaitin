On the topic of avidyA and mAyA, I do not have any position. But I will definitely read and re-read 4.5 and 4.6 in the Gita.



- There is Ishwara hetuka srushti which has been accepted by shankara
  and says it is vedAnta maryAda. But this srushti is not possible for
  the Ishwara without his mAyA shakti (without this shakti he is
  nishkriya says bhAshyakAra) if this shakti is itself Ishwara’s avidyA
  then even before srushti we have to say Ishwara is avidyAvanta and
  does the srushti through his avidyA…What on the earth we have to do
  with this avidyAvanta Ishwara / brahman that too he himself does not
  have any control over it and deluded himself by it?? Does it not mean
  the avidyA is more powerful than Ishwara and has the capacity to
  delude Ishwara himself?? What type of realization jeeva would get by
  realizing this avidyAvanta Ishwara or avidyA apahruta Ishwara?? Hence
  Ishwara’s avidyA or brahmAshrita avidyA which has the power to conceal
  the brahman is alien to shankarAdvaita.
