Hare Krishna



I am always hesitant to argue with full time sAdhaka-s freely due to my inferiority materialistic complex…but would like to clarify that the fact is as per Sri SSS avidyA is adhyAsa (misconception) only and for which tattvAgrahaNa (jnAnAbhAva) is the reason. BTW, this jnAnAbhAva is used in its yougikArtha and not to prove any material cause for adhyAsa because kArya-kAraNa itself within the sphere of adhyAsa as per Sri SSS. And as per SSS and shankara bhAshya there is no fourth type of avidyA which can be called mulAvidyA, bhAvarUpa, brahmAshraya, trikAla abAdhita apart from what has been already explained avidyA as jnAnAbhAva, anyathAgrahaNa and saMshaya.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
