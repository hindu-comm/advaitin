> 
>
> On the topic of avidyA and mAyA, I do not have any position. But I
> will definitely read and re-read 4.5 and 4.6 in the Gita.
>
> 
>
> - There is Ishwara hetuka srushti which has been accepted by shankara
>   and says it is vedAnta maryAda. But this srushti is not possible
>   for the Ishwara without his mAyA shakti (without this shakti he is
>   nishkriya says bhAshyakAra) if this shakti is itself Ishwara’s
>   avidyA



Jai: Jiva's avidyA is also Isvara's shakti (mAyA) and not Isvara'savidyA. Like even the Magician's shakti creates the magic which is looked upon as real by onlookers who have avidyA about the magic, but the magician does not have any avidyA about his own magic. So magic is shakti from the standpoint of the magician and avidyA from the standpoint of onlookers. This is a simple thing but Bhaskar ji refuses to understand this somehow.



> - then even before srushti we have to say Ishwara is avidyAvanta and
>   does the srushti through his avidyA…

  

Jai: Isvara being the totality must include the jIva's avidyA too. That does not make Isvara avidyAvanta like it is being made out to be. For those who understand the differences in standpoints / perspective there is no need to posit a mAyA which is different from avidyA as both are potential / causal states (upAdhi) with difference being only in standpoints.



> - What on the earth we have to do with this avidyAvanta Ishwara /
>   brahman that too he himself does not have any control over it and
>   deluded himself by it?? Does it not mean the avidyA is more
>   powerful than Ishwara and has the capacity to delude Ishwara
>   himself?? What type of realization jeeva would get by realizing
>   this avidyAvanta Ishwara or avidyA apahruta Ishwara?? Hence
>   Ishwara’s avidyA or brahmAshrita avidyA which has the power to
>   conceal the brahman is alien to shankarAdvaita.

  

Jai: Brahman is the Ashraya of everything including avidyA / mAyA. This has been made very clear by bhAshyakAra as there is no other chetana vastu which can be the Ashraya. Also the above sentences conflate brahman and Isvara which leads to all the confusion.
