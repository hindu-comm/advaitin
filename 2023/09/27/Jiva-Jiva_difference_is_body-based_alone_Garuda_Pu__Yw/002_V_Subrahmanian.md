Namaste Ram ji,

  

In non-Advaitic systems each jiva is fundamentally, by nature, a unique entity. This entity is the one that remains in the state of liberation.
The physical body is due to karma and the subtle body continues in every birth and when the jiva gets mukti jnanam, that last body, when it dies, the subtle body too gets dissolved in its prakriti. The Atma, the unique entity, then remains over in mukti. This Atma is different from other Atmas.

  

The Garuda Purana that is cited here says that the above is not the case and the distinction between one jiva and another is only due to the body, as Advaita holds.
