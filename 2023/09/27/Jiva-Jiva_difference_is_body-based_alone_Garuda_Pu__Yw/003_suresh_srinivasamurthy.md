Namaste Subbu-ji,

  

\< Shankara says: **देहभेदानुवृत्त्या बहुवचनम् , नात्मभेदाभिप्रायेण**\>  

  

I am struggling to understand this particular Shankara bhashya vakya.

  

If bahuvachana is based on bodily identification, then the words Aham, Tvam will point to the body only and that would make the Lord also asdehAtma jnAni or ajnAni which is not true.

  

Also, the bodies of jiva and Ishwara will become permanent as the Lord is saying there is no time we never existed and we will continue to exist in future - "Sarve vayam atah param".

  

If Atmaikatva and deha bahutva are accepted, then all the many bodies will end up as paratantra satya - depended reality on the Atman/Brahman. Personally, I am using this understanding, to reconcile Advaita and Dvaita as, bheda is needed to support astikya and varnashrama dharma.

  

Appreciate if you could clarify.

  

Regards,  
Suresh
