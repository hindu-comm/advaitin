praNAms Sri Suresh prabhuji

Hare Krishna



If bahuvachana is based on bodily identification, then the words Aham, Tvam will point to the body only and that would make the Lord also asdehAtma jnAni or ajnAni which is not true.



Ø Su.bh. 1-1-4 dehAtma buddhi itself adhyAsa or avidyAkruta.
Krishna says to arjuna you and me had multiple janma-s earlier you don’t know that but I know that, so here also Krishna as Chaitanya and arjuna as Chaitanya got various upAdhi-s (deha-s) in various janma-s, Krishna since he has the control over it knows it but arjuna being ajnAni and having karma janita deha does not know it. In the sense, arjuna is a separate Chaitanya in arjuna’s current body who is listening to Krishna as Chaitanya who is using his current body for the upadesha. BTW, kArika, bhAshya etc. says jeeva-jeeva bedha is just like sparks from fire, space in jar and pot etc. and this differentiation is only because of upAdhi bedha and holds water only in transactional reality. But it is a matter of fact that in vyavahAra ‘even after samyak jnAna’ jnAni’s ‘individuality’ (chaitanya’s individuality) continues and he would operate/ does action through his individual upAdhi-s due to avidyAlesha or prArabdha karma. So as soon as you bring in avidyAlesha theory and individual prArabdha karma you are forced to accept that there is an individual Chaitanya (jeeva) who realized that he is brahman BUT he, as an individual chaitanya still having the avidyA lesha or effacing the prArabdha karma phala which has already started to give its fruit. And hold back!! This individuality of Chaitanya and gradation among different Chaitanya-s (jeeva-s) continue even after physical death of samyakjnAni. And bhAshyakAra confirms some elevated already realized souls like apaantaratama-s as per the instructions from paramAtma ( super soul or parama Chaitanya) dragging the remains of their own prArabdha takes birth again to ‘fulfill’ the wishes of paramAtma. And other jeeva-s (souls) would get krama mukti and in HiraNyagarbha loka they would remain and after mahApralaya they would get final realization and yet some other individual ordinary souls ( which are not as special as apAntaratama-s) gets the final realization and do not take further births. So the scope of transactional reality can be stretched even after the realization and even after the current body’s death till we reach paramArtha satya na nirOdhO nachOtpattiH, neither mumukshu nor mOksha etc. and till that time individuality of Chaitanya-s (not only deha restricted) would continue to operate differently. 



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
