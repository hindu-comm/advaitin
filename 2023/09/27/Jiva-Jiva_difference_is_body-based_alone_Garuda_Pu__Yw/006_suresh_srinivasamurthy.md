Namaste Bhaskar-ji,

  

I understand the upAdhi kruta bheda. But in this particular verse Sri Krishna is teaching, you, me and all the many kings/jivas existed before and is going to exist in future also. The entire sloka is filled with plurality. The bhAshya vAkya says plurality is due to multiplicity / difference in shareera and not due to Atman.

  

So, the question is how to understand this continued existence of all from the Advaita perspective?
