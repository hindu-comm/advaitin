Namaste,

  

From the ajnAni's vyavahAradrsthi (empirical standpoint) all jIvas existed, are existing and will exist in future too, till one gains AtmajnAnam. The jIva is anAdi because avidyA is anAdi. As long as avidyA is there you (the embodied conscious being) will continue to take another body even when this body falls and have identification with that body. Bhagavan points that out in BG 2.22

  

वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि ।  
तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णान्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही ॥  

  

vāsāṁsi jīrṇāni yathā vihāya navāni gr̥hṇāti naro'parāṇi ।  
tathā śarīrāṇi vihāya jīrṇānyanyāni saṁyāti navāni dehī ॥  

  

Just as a person gives up old clothes and takes up new ones,so too, the indweller of the body gives up old bodies andtakes others which are new.  

  

But those who understand the mahAvAkya and know oneself as non-different from jagat-kAraNam-brahma do not have another birth. Bhagavan says that in BG 4.9

  

जन्म कर्म च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।  
त्यक्त्वा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन ॥

  

janma karma ca me divyamevaṁ yo vetti tattvataḥ ।  

tyaktvā dehaṁ punarjanma naiti māmeti so'rjuna ॥

  

The one who knows in reality My divine birth and action in this way, O Arjuna, giving up the body, that person is not born again. He attains Me.  

  

Advaita is paramArtha and dvaita is in vyavahAra. One has to be clear about what is said from which standpoint, then there will not be any confusion.

  

with love and prayers,

Jaishankar
