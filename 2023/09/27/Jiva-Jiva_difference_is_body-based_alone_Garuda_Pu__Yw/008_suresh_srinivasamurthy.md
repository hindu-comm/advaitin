Namaste Jaishankar ji

  

\<From the ajnAni's vyavahAradrsthi (empirical standpoint) all jIvas existed, are existing and will exist in future too, till one gains AtmajnAnam.\>

  

The above would make Sri Krishna also as ajnAni as he is saying **सर्वेवयमतःपरम्** -(we all going to exist in future)

  

But I think I found the answer. In the bhAshya Sri Shankara makes it clear that all jivas will exist for ever in their Atma swaroopa.

  

न तु एव जातु कदाचित् अहं नासम् , किं तु आसमेव। अतीतेषु देहोत्पत्तिविनाशेषु घटादिषु वियदिव नित्य एव अहमासमित्यभिप्रायः। तथा न त्वं न आसीः, किं तु आसीरेव। तथा न इमे जनाधिपाः न आसन् , किं तु आसन्नेव। तथा न च एव न भविष्यामः, किं तु भविष्याम एव, सर्वे वयम् अतः अस्मात् देहविनाशात् परम् उत्तरकाले अपि। **त्रिष्वपि कालेषु नित्या आत्मस्वरूपेण इत्यर्थः**। देहभेदानुवृत्त्या बहुवचनम् , नात्मभेदाभिप्रायेण ॥ १२ ॥

  

  

Namaste Subbu-ji / Bhaskar ji,

  

As Atman/Brahman is present in all the infinite bodies and remains as the ground of all, is it correct to understand that  

**Atman/Aham = sarvAtma = Vayam?**

**  **

This amounts to identifying oneself with the entire universe and ideally that alone results in total selflessness.

But still the bheda between Atman and AnAtma/Shareera is going to be real and it could exist as paratantra satya, which accommodates other vedantic schools and also helps in sustaining Varnashrama dharma 🙂

  

Look forward to your comments.

  

Thanks

Suresh
