Namaste Sri Vikram,

  

\<It seems to me that you are looking at this from a non-Advaitasampradhayam perspective (potentially Visishtadvaita).\>

  

No, the above is not true. Personally, I accept Atma-Brahmaikatva. But I also accept jagat brahmaikyatva, which according to me is the art of looking at the whole where all particulars are transcended. This may not be palatable to all but it goes well with sarvam kalvidam brahma, purusha sukta and B.Gita 11^(th)Ch.🙂

  

My main question here, was around the reconciliation of the advaitic atman with the bahuvachana taught in the B.Gita sloka - सर्वेवयमतःपरम्. Though in the bhAshya multiplicity is explained based on the body, it is also said that all jivas will continue to exist in the atma svarupa.

  

That is the reason why I equated Atman with sarvAtman which of course, as you explained is the Atman of all (bodies/jagath). But this sarvAtman/Brahmn is everywhere. That means all identifications like - you, me, this, that and so on, must culminate in the all-pervading and indwelling Brahman only, right? This in my opinion aligns with all other views.

  

I fullyunderstand, all of the above comes under vyavahAra in Advaita. Thanks very much for your detailed explanations.

  

Pranams,  

Suresh
