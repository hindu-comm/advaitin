Thank you for sharing more details. My apologies are in order then.

  

Just another subtle distinction: Brahman-Atman-ekatvam and Brahman-jagat-ekatvam, though both are accepted, are not the same in Advaita. The former is a relation of ekatve samanadhikaranyam and the latter is that of badhayam samanadhikaranyam. In the former, Brahman and Atman are both the same paramarthika satya. But in the latter, Brahman alone is paramarthika satya whereas jagat is vyavaharika satya alone.

  

Regarding plurality of jivas, hoping it is clear that it is just as in a movie screen all (equivalent of sarvam) the characters (equivalent of vayam) appear to be transitory, in their atma svarupa as light from the projector source, they are all eternal. Though the atma svarupa is one light source alone, the distinct characters (plural) on the screen do not get any higher level of reality at par with the light source. Similarly though jivas (plural - you, me, others, etc.) are nitya in their svarupa as Brahman, their plurality & distinction does not gain any higher level of reality than vyavaharika.

  

Since vyavaharika satya is purely within the realm of avidya, the acceptance & alignment of the above vyavaharika aspects is also within avidya only, and not the absolute or true moksha state.

  

Hoping this further explains the bhashya statement, as well as clarifies a potential pitfall with universal identity of Brahman/Atman/Jagat.

  

My apologies, again, for preaching to the choir.

  

with humble prostrations,

Vikram
