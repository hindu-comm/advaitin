Just another subtle distinction: Brahman-Atman-ekatvam and Brahman-jagat-ekatvam, though both are accepted, are not the same in Advaita. The former is a relation of ekatve samanadhikaranyam and the latter is that of badhayam samanadhikaranyam. In the former, Brahman and Atman are both the same paramarthika satya. But in the latter, Brahman alone is paramarthika satya whereas jagat is vyavaharika satya alone.

\>

  

Thank you for sharing the above.

  

I don't think there will be Advaita hAni, if the Tat pada refers to jagadAtma/sarvAtma brahman and tvam pada refers to the Atman. IMHO jagat as a pratIka is needed to realize sarva vyApakatva of Brahman. Please correct me if I am wrong.

  

Even to hear and know about Brahman a body is essential and soIMHO accepting plurality/jagat as brahma shareera as per the bhAshya vAkya itself, helps to support sanAtana varnashrama dharma, which is currently under threat.

  

It can be mithya later, but in order to sustain/protect dharma there is a need to accept plurality/body/jagat as paratantra sathya.
