Namaskaram Shri Suresh ji,

  
  
\<  
I don't think there will be Advaita hAni, if the Tat pada refers to jagadAtma/sarvAtma brahman and tvam pada refers to the Atman.  
\>

  
I beg to disagree here. The highest goal / objective of the scriptures is to point a person towards realization / liberation / moksha. The highest objective is not to provide any additional leverage within samsara itself. Per Advaita, all duality is only in vyavaharika; meaning, the objective of the scripture, particularly the maha-vakyas, is to point to the direct identity of Atman-Brahman that transcends all duality. There are no distinctions within Atman (such as jivatman / Paramatman) or plurality of Atman.

  

Having said so, it is accepted & started off at the surface level that 'tat' refers to collective Isvara and 'tvam' refers to individual jiva. But Advaita quickly goes deeper and points us to drop the distinctions (conditionings) to realize the underlying identity - Atman. Therefore the objective of Advaita is not to stop at the sarvatman vs individual atman level, but deeper.

  
  
\<  
IMHO jagat as a pratIka is needed to realize sarva vyApakatva of Brahman. Please correct me if I am wrong.

Even to hear and know about Brahman a body is essential and so IMHO accepting plurality/jagat as brahma shareera as per the bhAshya vAkya itself, helps to support sanAtana varnashrama dharma, which is currently under threat.  
\>

  
Indeed the jagat (collective) or sharira (individual) is needed to understand liberation. This is only because it is only through this jagat / sharira that a jiva currently misunderstands bondage! To clarify, even now, though we feel we are in samsara and seek liberation, 'I as Atman (Brahman) am ever free'. The feeling that we are bound is itself only a misunderstanding influenced by sharira. Consequently, we take sharira for granted now and believe we need to use it to attain liberation! Realizing our own true Self as Atman/Brahman doesn't technically require a sharira. Atman is ever realized.  
  
Again, having said so, agreeing with you that for day-to-day activities, particularly the political situation around sanatana dharma & varnashrama dharma, it is necessary to step up and support our culture. But not at the cost of diluting the lofty objectives of our scriptures & philosophy. There is sufficient allowance within vyavaharika to accommodate this.

  
  
\<  
It can be mithya later, but in order to sustain/protect dharma there is a need to accept plurality/body/jagat as paratantra sathya.  
\>

  
This is not accurate within Advaita. Jagat is not mithya later from paramarthika perspective. Jagat is asat (absolute non-existence) from that view. Jagat as mithya is only a vyavaharika satya. The paratantra satya itself is mithya. Understanding the mithyatva of jagat should have no bearing on the activities in vyavahara. Meaning, an ajnani believes jagat is satya whereas a jnani understands jagat is mithya, here and now. But, again, plurality / body / jagat is already considered empirically real for day-to-day activities. My only opinion is that there is no need to bring in another reconciliation within Advaita just to sustain / protect dharma.  
  
As I am sharing this, I am actually questioning how much we are deviating from each other; perhaps semantics; perhaps we are stating the same point in our own words. But I am erring on the side of clarification.  
  
with humble prostrations,  
Vikram
