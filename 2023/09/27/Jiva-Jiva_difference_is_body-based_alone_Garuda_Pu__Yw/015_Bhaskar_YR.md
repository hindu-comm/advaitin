Hare Krishna



We have discussed these issues somany times in this list itself but for one or the other reason popping up again and again 😊



What is mOksha?? The svarUpa jnana, what is svarUpa jnana or avidyA nirmUlana or realizing that I was / is / forever brahman ?? that he is none other than Atman/brahman and he is in his svarUpa nirguNa, nirAkAra, niravayava. And this svarUpa jnana is something that can be realized in some other lOka or physical death or in the complete absence of perceived jagat or some mysterious state of mind where mind is inert to external activities like in nirvikalpa samAdhi?? What exactly the difference between svarUpa jnana and sarvAtmabhAva?? Do we have to do prapancha pravilaya to realize our Atmaikatva jnana?? Do we have to literally experience the vyavahAra abhAva or do we have to sublate the vyavahAra with the samyaK jnana?? If we clearly know the answers ( atleast academic answers available in prasthAna traya bhAshya and shruti) then I believe there will be no confusion between sarvAtmabhAva and svarUpa jnana both are denoting the mOksha only.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
