Some more evidences in the Bhashya for Maya and Avidya/Ajnana synonymity:

  

BG 5.15: By AjnAna, the Jnana has been covered/enveloped. Hence jivas are deluded says Krishna. Shankara concurs.  

  

नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः।  
अज्ञानेनावृतंज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः ॥ १५ ॥

न आदत्ते न च गृह्णाति भक्तस्यापि कस्यचित् पापम्। न चैव आदत्ते सुकृतं भक्तैः प्रयुक्तं विभुः। किमर्थं तर्हि भक्तैः पूजादिलक्षणं यागदानहोमादिकं च सुकृतं प्रयुज्यते इत्याह — अज्ञानेन आवृतं ज्ञानं विवेकविज्ञानम् , तेन मुह्यन्ति ‘करोमि कारयामि भोक्ष्ये भोजयामि’ इत्येवं मोहं गच्छन्ति अविवेकिनः संसारिणो जन्तवः ॥ १५ ॥

In the very next verse Bhagavan says Jnana is the virodhi for the above stated ajnana:  

ज्ञानेनतुतदज्ञानंयेषांनाशितमात्मनः।  
तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानंप्रकाशयतितत्परम्॥ १६ ॥

*ज्ञानेनतुयेनअज्ञानेनआवृताःमुह्यन्तिजन्तवःतत्अज्ञानंयेषांजन्तूनांविवेकज्ञानेनआत्मविषयेणनाशितम्*आत्मनःभवति,तेषांजन्तूनाम्आदित्यवत्यथाआदित्यःसमस्तंरूपजातम्अवभासयतितद्वत्ज्ञानंज्ञेयंवस्तुसर्वंप्रकाशयतितत्परंपरमार्थतत्त्वम्॥ १६ ॥

In BG 9.10 the Lord says: With Me as the Presiding Consciousness, Prakriti creates the entire world:

मयाध्यक्षेणप्रकृतिःसूयतेसचराचरम्।  
हेतुनानेनकौन्तेयजगद्विपरिवर्तते॥ १० ॥  

Shankara says:मया अध्यक्षेण सर्वतो दृशिमात्रस्वरूपेण अविक्रियात्मना अध्यक्षेण मया, मम माया त्रिगुणात्मिका अविद्यालक्षणा प्रकृतिः सूयते उत्पादयति सचराचरं जगत्। Shankara says the Prakriti in the shloka is = TriguNAtmikA avidyAlakShaNA Prakriti is My Maayaa. So, Maayaa = Prakriti = AvidyA. Further down Shankara says here: किंनिमित्ता इयं सृष्टिः इत्यत्र प्रश्नप्रतिवचने अनुपपन्ने - The question and answer for 'What causes/why is this creation? is irrelevant. And goes on to cite the NAsadiya sukta of Rg Veda and also cites the Gita: दर्शितं च भगवता —[‘अज्ञानेनावृतंज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः’ (भ. गी. ५। १५)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=5&id=BG_C05_V15&hl=%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A8%20%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%83)इति ॥ १० ॥

Thus for Shankara, the AjnAna that veils the Jnana and deludes the jivas is the cause of creation, the same way the Prakriti is the cause of creation (see the first line in the bhashya above).

In the 13.2 lengthy commentary Shankara says: तथा अविद्या क्षेत्रज्ञस्य न किञ्चित् कर्तुं शक्नोति। अतश्चेदमुक्तम् —[‘क्षेत्रज्ञं चापि मां विद्धि’ (भ. गी. १३। २)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=13&id=BG_C13_V02&hl=%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%9A%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%BF),[‘अज्ञानेनावृतंज्ञानम्’ (भ. गी. ५। १५)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=5&id=BG_C05_V15&hl=%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D)इति च ॥

AvidyA can't do anything to the Kshetrajna. And he cites Bhagavan: Know Me to be the Kshetrajna. And the BG 5.15: Jnana is enveloped by ajnana.
Shankara means here: Brahman that is the jiva, is ever untouched; only the viveka jnana is enveloped by ajnana. In other words, Brahman comes to think it is a jiva only because of ajnana.

So in the above short, sample study, we have the Lord and Shankara agreeing that:

Maya=Prakriti=TriguNAtmikA=avidya=Ajnana.

Om Tat Sat
