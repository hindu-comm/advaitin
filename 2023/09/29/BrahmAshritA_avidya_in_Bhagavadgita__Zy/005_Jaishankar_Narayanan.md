Even BG 14.3 Bhashya points to the same

  

मम योनिर्महद्ब्रह्म तस्मिन्गर्भं दधाम्यहम् ।  
सम्भवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत ॥ १४.३ ॥  

  

क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञसंयोगः ईदृशः भूतकारणम् इत्याह —मम स्वभूता मदीया माया त्रिगुणात्मिका प्रकृतिः योनिः सर्वभूतानां कारणम् । सर्वकार्येभ्यो महत्त्वात् भरणाच्च स्वविकाराणां महत् ब्रह्म इति योनिरेव विशिष्यते । तस्मिन् महति ब्रह्मणि योनौ गर्भं हिरण्यगर्भस्य जन्मनः बीजं सर्वभूतजन्मकारणं बीजं दधामि निक्षिपामि क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञप्रकृतिद्वयशक्तिमान् ईश्वरः अहम् , अविद्याकामकर्मोपाधिस्वरूपानुविधायिनं क्षेत्रज्ञं क्षेत्रेण संयोजयामि इत्यर्थः । सम्भवः उत्पत्तिः सर्वभूतानां हिरण्यगर्भोत्पत्तिद्वारेण ततः तस्मात् गर्भाधानात् भवति हे भारत ॥  

  

The Lord says that association of this kind between the field and theKnower of the field is the origin of all beings: -Mama, My own Māyā, that is Prakṛti consisting of the three qualities,which belongs to Me; is the yoniḥ, womb for all the creatures. Since it(Prakṛti) is great (mahat) as compared with all its effects, and it is thesustainer (brahma) of all its own transformations, therefore the wombitself is qualified as mahat brahma. Tasmin, in that, in the womb which isthe great-sustainer; aham, I, Ishvara, possessed of the power in the form of thetwo aspects, viz. the field and the Knower of the field; dadhāmi, place,deposit; garbham, the seed—the seed of the birth of Hiraṇyagarbha, theseed which is the cause of the birth of all things—; that is, I bring the fieldinto association with the Knower of the field who conforms to the nature ofthe limiting adjuncts, viz. ignorance, desire and activity.

Tataḥ, from that, from that deposition of the seed; O scion of theBharata dynasty, bhavati, occurs; sambhavaḥ, the birth, origination; sarvabhūtānām,of all things, following the birth of Hiraṇyagarbha.

  

Further implication of this Bhashya is avidyA being the jIva-upAdhi can never be abhAva.
