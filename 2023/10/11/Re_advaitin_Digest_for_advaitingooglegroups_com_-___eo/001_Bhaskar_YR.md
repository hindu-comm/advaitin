In the रज्जुसर्प दृष्टांत there is a minor issue that has to be understood properly - The superimposition of snake on rope presupposes priorknowledge of asnake form. Thus,in the case of all examples related to विवर्त there is this implied issue. This is not a fault since this limitationis born out of the realm of the example - namelythe fact that the snake and rope belong to samerealm of knowledge. The dream or dessert mirage examples are closer to describing विवर्त but they too suffer from limitations. Examples can only take you up to a point and then they have to bedropped.



Ø The difference between two different examples i.e. mrudghata and rajju-sarpa though sometimes appearing in same sentence and same context in bhAshya-s need to be understood differently. It has been highlighted somany times earlier but some would still argue both are one and the same and conveying the same siddhAnta in Advaita. These two examples conveying two stages of real nature of brahman. First one, mrudghata example, is to establish the abedha brahman through kArya-kAraNa ananyatvaM ( non difference). But limitation of this example is one may think about brahman is itself bound to appear with attributes or nAma rUpa (like mrud itself undergoing transformation and become ghata) No need to mention this is the transactional view point of the jignAsu who is still having the vyavahAra with nAmarUpAtmaka jagat. And to deny this sort of transaction in brahman bhAshyakAra introduces the example of rajju-sarpa, shukti-rajata etc. There is sarpa for the ajnAni who is having ajnAna about real rope. ( the sarpa is illusory whereas rajju is really existing, likewise there are two jagat-s one is jeeva mAnasa pratyaya i.e. avidyA Kalpita jagat (sarpa) and another is Ishwara (brahma mAnasa pratyaya) srushti (rajju). Once the real rajju jnana revealed the illusory sarpa disappears and ONLY the real rajju seen. ( like after the dawn of paramArtha jnana the illusory independent nature of jagat would disappear the knowledge world non-different from brahman would remain). This realization would fetch the knowledge that it is the ‘rajju’ appearing like sarpa. shAstra jnana tells us that the jagat is brahman but prior to the knowledge this jagat itself appearing as independent from brahman, it is called abrahmatvaM, asarvatvam, parichinnatvaM of jagat born due to mithyAjnAna about jagat. Now the question is : is the jagat is also mithyA like sarpa in rajju-sarpa example?? No the categorical answer by bhAshyakAra in chAndOgya itself. As you have rightly observed one must be careful in understanding these analogies and its limited scope at different contexts. One should not argue like : rajju is the example stand for brahman and sarpa is for the jagat. Since the sarpa is non-existent in rajju-sarpa example, jagat too non-existent in Advaita siddhAnta. Na!!
that is really a stretch of this example. In the mrudghata example there is causal relation between mrud and ghata but in rajju-sarpa there is no causal relationship between rajju and sarpa. In the rajju-sarpa analogy there is the one thing in another is merely a wrong understanding (tappu tiluvalike, mithyAjnAna, vipareeta grahaNa) and sarpa here is a plain illusion due to abhAva of yathArtha jnana of rajju. What I am trying to say here is that these examples are there to remove the misconception about brahman by seeing the nAmarUpAtmaka jagat and not to prove or establish that nature of brahman through nAma & rUpa as in the example of mrudghata. This has been reiterated somany times but in vain.
