Coming back to the subject, I would like to say clay-pot or suvarNAbharaNa is more appropriate in understanding brahman’s abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNatvaM of jagat. One may jump on my neck and ask, what are you saying?? Don’t you know brahman as per Advaita is nirvikAri, niravayava, nirvishesha, nirguNa (totally attributeless) ??
How dare you say like this?? If the upAdhi-s of name-forms are existing in the form of jagat (world) does it not contradict the ultimate siddhAnta of shruti that brahman is ONE WITHOUT SECOND (ekamevAdviteeyaM) THERE IS NOT EVEN AN IOTA OF MULTIPLICITY IN BRAHMAN (neha nAnAsti kiMchana) etc.?? bhAshyakAra himself answers these questions in bruhadAraNyaka bhAshya. Though brahman is attributeless in itself the jagat of infinite variety has emanated ONLY from IT. Is not the clay (mrud sAmAnya) from which all the shapes (mrud pinda, ghata etc.) emanate shapeless in itself?? Similarly in the case of brahman even though all the qualities come out of it it is itself always free from all the qualities (kevalO nirguNascha). And it is to be noted that when we say from THAT brahman THIS jagat emanates or that brahman creates this jagat etc. it is only from the transactional view point but not from the transcendental view point. In the pAramArthika satya there is no distinction between that brahman and this jagat it is because of the simple fact jagat is also nothing but brahman and brahman cannot be distinguished as THAT and THIS. With this brahman and brahman alone remains call it by any name jagat, brahman, atman anything as you wish as this tattva is beyond any name and form. It is in this sense only bhAshyakAra clarifies even the shape of the effect (nAma and rUpa) it NOT different from its cause. kAryAkArOpi kAraNasya AtmabhUta ‘eva’ anAtmabhUtasya anArabhyatvAt. Ultimately what is there is brahman alone and there is no scope for attributes (nAmarUpOpAdhi) at all.
bhAshyakAra to drive home this point uses the clay-pot example and clarifies after analysing the clay from the pAramArthika drushti, one realizes that pot (nAma and rUpa) is not different from it (clay) and therefore clay alone exists. Similarly when the names and forms realized from the shAstra drushti they (names and forms) are not different from brahman, it will be realized that there is only brahman and there is nothing apart from brahman and there is absolutely no multiplicity. But when the SAME names and forms viewed from vyAvahArika parichinna drushti (limited / conditioned transactional view) then there are upAdhi-s, multiplicity as a result saMsAra, klesha, rAga-dvesha, sukha dukkha etc.



But now the question is : as per shruti siddhAnta brahman is without parts and he is like not this not this (neti neti) this brahman / purusha is formless (divyOh hyamurthaH purushaH) etc. but the jagat has the multiplicity, it has parts, how can the jagat with parts come out of brahman which is without parts?? Here bhAshyakAra uses the rajju-sarpa example and clarifies even though really we are looking at the rope sometimes (due to one or the other reason) we could see a serpent instead. In such a case what is ‘seen’ is ONLY a imagined parts of the rope ‘as’ serpent that is the imagined parts of the rope have given rise to the shape of the serpent. Similarly imagined parts in the ‘partless’ brahman could give rise to the ‘shape’ of the jagat (ch.bh.6.2.2). And subsequently bhAshyakAra uses both the famous examples i.e. rajju-sarpa, mrudghata and says : it is just like the clay taking the forms of the pot OR just as the rope produced as the imagined snake. Here pUrvapaxi holding the second example and asks : in that case is the jagat also non-existent just like the ‘imagined’ snake ?? (asadeva tarhi sarvaM yadgruhyate rajjuriva sarpAdyAkAreNa?? ) For this siddhAnti answers :
NO, it is ONLY the existent brahman appearing as many in special forms in a different way. NOTHING HERE IS NON-EXISTENT AT ANY TIME. After refuting the logicians theory bhAshyakAra clarifies : we say that there is no name or form which is different from the cause at any time. And sticking to clay pot example he insists : though the clay is called pot ‘as if’ it is different from the clay, we know that it is after all clay ONLY. And in continuation he says : similarly after examining the rope properly we conclude that though it appeared like snake it is not a snake. With this realization we only get rid of the idea of snake.
Similarly after examining and realizing the brahman we get rid of the idea of jagat and we also stop referring to it as jagat. IOW after realizing the true nature of jagat one would get rid of his own idea of jagat and realizes the brahman in accordance with shruti. From this one’s understanding that it is vyavahAraateeta will not be distracted though for the sense perception IT is seen in the form of jagat.
