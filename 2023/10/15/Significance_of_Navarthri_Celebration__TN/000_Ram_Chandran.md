***Navarathri is the celebration of the Glory of the Divine Mother, an aspect of Adi Parashakti, the supreme goddess***. Navarathri isalso associated to the prominent battle that took place between Durga and the demon Mahishasura to celebrate the victory of good over evil. We honor and worship the omnipresent Divine Devi energy during the Nine nights (Navratri). Divine Mother possesses the Divine energy that has been passed on to the entire universe including the celestial stars far away. Our ancestors discovered that during Navratri time the Divine energy can be tapped to control our mental emotions and attain peace. 

Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswati are seen as three dimensions of the Divine Devi symbolizing the Earth, the Sun and the Moon. Those who aspire for strength or power during the first 3 nights worship the Mother Earth represented by Durga. Those who seek wealth, passion, or material wealth worship Lakshmi or the sun during the next 3 nights. During the last 3 nights, those who wish for knowledge and to transcend from limitations of the mortal body worship Saraswati or the moon.
