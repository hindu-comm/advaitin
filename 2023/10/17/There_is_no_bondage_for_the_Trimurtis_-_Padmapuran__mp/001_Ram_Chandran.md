Namaste Subbuji:

From the advaitic point of view, the Brahman is Nirguna and Nirupa with no form or name or quality. We Visualize or Perceive the Brahman through the Nature. In Nature we do observe the cycles of creation, protection and destruction. We identified the functionality of Nature in the form of Trimurtis – creator for creation, protector for protection and associated a creator for creation, protector for protection and destroyer for destruction. We represented Brahma as the creator, Vishnu as the protector and Shiva as the destroyer (renewer) and called them Trimurtis. We as jiva perceived Trimurtis who can not be separated from the Brahman. In all Eras, we only perceive that Trimurtis do all the work separately for the welfare of the world. The Brahman perceived as Trimurtis certainly will not have bondage or liberation!

Warm regards,
