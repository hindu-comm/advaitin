Dear Sri Kalyan,

  

True, the prasthana traya bhashya has both the views. However, in the VSN bhashya, Shankara cites a Vishnu Purana verse to the effect that 'Janardana' takes on the three forms for creation, sustenance and destruction functions.

  

He has also cited other sources for the idea of Trimurti abheda.
