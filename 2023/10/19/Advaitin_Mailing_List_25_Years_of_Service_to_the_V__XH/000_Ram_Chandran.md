**The List used to provide an annual report every year during the month of June. After the list moved to Google Groups, we have not been doing the same. This year is special because Advaitin List has completed 25 years of service to the humanity through its scholarly postings.**

**Here is a brief account of the List from its humble beginning.**

**Advaitin Mailing List (25 Years of Service to the Vedanta Community!)**

**Histry of this Advaitin Mailing List:**

**The advaitins List started during June 1998 and was hosted at Coolist.com (no more exists). The focus of this list from the beginning on Sankara’s Advaita Vedanta. This Cyber Satsangh started with about a dozen founding members and few of them are no more with us. The major objective from the beginning was to provide an open forum to accommodate the world scholars with a diversity of opinions. The members and moderators have demonstrated the unity within the diversity of opinions. On 31st of August, the list moved to Egroups.com which later merged with the yahoo groups. The Yahoo Groups closed hosting of all mail lists during the year 2020 and Advaitin List officially got installed under Google Groups during August 2020.**

**Currently we have 1824 members and the list archive contains articles on almost all aspects of advaita philosophy. The success of this list can only be measured by how it serves its members or vice-versa, how the members contribute to the list. The list is truly blessed with scholars with both Vivekam (wisdom) and Vinayam (humility) with good communication skills.**

**The list always looks for ideas and comments to improve its service to the humanity and please feel free to send your comments to any one of the List Moderators or at the List. Your comments will be read by all moderators and they try their level best to make appropriate changes and corrections.**

**Thank you for your continued participation and support**
