Dear Aravunda Raoji,

  

Thank you for your good decision. Though I have a science background by learning and by profession, I have developed a post-retirement- interest in Advaita philosophy. Of course, there are several other ex-scientists like Sadanandaji, who are good advaitins. However, my another current
interest is in Itihaasa, which is one of compulsory pancha-lakshanas of the fifth veda, and this takes quite a good amount of my time.  

  

In fact, quite sometime ago, Subbuji sent some posts/messages on the dating of Adi Shankara in the Advaitin group and some time back, Sadanandaji also sent one post on the dating of the Mahabharata war. After that there is not much interest in Itihasa in the Advaitin group. About two or three years ago, there was an announcement by the moderator of the BVP (Bharatiya Vidvat Parishad group), in the BVP about the formation of a nine-member team for dating of the Mahabharata war, but that team also failed to find the date of the Mahabharata war, uptil now. Their total silence seems to indicate that they have abandoned the project  

  

Many people have difficulty in understanding the pre-paninian Sanskrit and that is also one of the causes of the failures in finding the date of the Mahabharata war. For example, a number of scholars have not been able to differentiate the meanings of (**i**) **prAptam**, (**ii**) **prAptam viddhi** (particularly in connection with Duryodhana, as an amsha of Kali, but born before the actual arrival of the Kali Yuga),
((**iii**) **samanuprAptam** and (**iv**) **samprAptam.**, and because of that they could not arrive at the correct date of the Mahabharata war. Secondly, most of the researchers lack good knowledge of Indian Jyotisha ((which includes both Astrology and Astronomy), because of which they could not find the actual location of the planets in the zodiac at the time of the Mahabharata war. Thirdly, most of the researchers have not read the ancient Indian history properly, as given in in the puranas, Lastly, these scholars don't know which are the right computer softwares to be actually used for dating the events of Ancient India.**  **

  

I am writing all this with the hope that a time may soon come when the Advaitin group may also encourage research on historicity of the personalities, particulary connected with the dating of Lord Buddha,and Adi Shankara. **  **
