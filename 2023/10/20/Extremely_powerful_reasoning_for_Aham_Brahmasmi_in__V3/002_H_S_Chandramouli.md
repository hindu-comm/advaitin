Namaste.

In my understanding, Brahman is NOT the \*field\* in Advaita SiddhAnta. The \*field\* is only an appearance of existence in Brahman. Not Existence itself. Brahman is the \*knower\* of the field in a figurative sense. Brahman illumines or reveals the \*field\*, but there is no change or modification accruing to Brahman due to this \*knowledge\*. \*Knower\*, as understood commonly, is associated with change, there is a change in the \*knower\* whenever fresh knowledge accrues to him. This is not the case with Brahman as \*Knower\*.
