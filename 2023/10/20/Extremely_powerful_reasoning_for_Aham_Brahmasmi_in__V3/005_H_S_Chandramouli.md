Namaste.

By \*In Brahman\* is meant that Brahman is veiled and in its stead \* the field \* appears to \*exist\*. Brahman is said to be the adhishthAna for the \* field \*. Hence the \* field \* should not be considered as Brahman itself. Brahman is not in any way affected by any of the properties or attributes of the \* field \*.

The relationship between Brahman and the \* field \* is generally explained through the rope-serpent illustration.

This is my understanding.
