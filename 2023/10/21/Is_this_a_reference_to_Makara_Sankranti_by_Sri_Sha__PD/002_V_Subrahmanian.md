Popularly the two Sankranti-s - Makara and Karkatak- are termed Uttarayana Punya kAla and Dakshinayana Punya kAla. Both these occasions are said to be apt for giving daana. While tarpana is offered during these two occasions, there are some siShTa-s who offer tarpana during all the twelve Sankranti days.
