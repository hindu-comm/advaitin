Te Sankranti is a term from the Jyotish shastra. When the Sun moves from the Tula rashi to the Makar rashi , the first day of Makar rashi is the Makar Sankranti. Likewise there are 12 Sankrantis during the Sun's movement to the 12 Solar months.
