मारयादितिः Here the मारयात् इति iti is in the mantra itself. I means: thus. In this manner 'the Jnani will cease to believe (that 'death will kill me'). The other iti after the mantra-iti is that of the Bhashya kara. It is likeप्राणं प्रयन्त्यभिसंविशन्तीति। inthe Bhruguvalli. The last word 'iti' is to show: *thus* Bhrugu realized (at that stage).
