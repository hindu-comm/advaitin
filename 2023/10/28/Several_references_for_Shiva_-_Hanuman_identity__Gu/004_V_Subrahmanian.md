Thanks Sai ji, for enabling viewing the two pages their links you provided. The mystery is now solved:

  

The whole section the Shiva Sahasra Naama that occurs s in the Mahabharata, given out by Sri Krishna himself upon Yudhishthira asking. In that occur these two verses:

  

72 बरह्मचारी लॊकचारी सर्वचारी सुचारवित्

  ईशान ईश्वरः कालॊ निशाचारी पिनाकधृक

73 नन्दीश्वरश्च नन्दी च नन्दनॊ नन्दिवर्धनः

  भगस्याक्षिनिहन्ता च कालॊ बरह्मविदां वरः

  

Verse 72 has the name Pinakadhrik. Verse 73 has the name Nandishwara.

  

  

It is this that has prompted the translator to give the Hanuman interpretation. We had recently seen the Valmiki Ramayana reporting Ravana upon seeing Hanuman recalling an old curse by Nandishwara that the former will be killed by a monkey. We had seen the Skanda Purana verses for this old story.

  

// 72.Thou art he that lives during even the night that follows the universal dissolution. Thou art the protector wielding the bow called Pinaka. Thou residest in even the Daityas that are the marks at which shootest thy arrows. Thou art the author of prosperity. 

  

73. Thou art the mighty ape Hanuman that aided Vishnu in the incarnation of Rama in his expedition against Ravana. Thou art the lord of those Ganas that are thy associates, Thou art each member of those diverse Ganas. //

  

I am yet to check the Nilakantha commentary for this verse of the SSN of the Mahabharata.

  

In any case the translator has the support of the Valmiki Ramayana and other purana for such an interpretation.
