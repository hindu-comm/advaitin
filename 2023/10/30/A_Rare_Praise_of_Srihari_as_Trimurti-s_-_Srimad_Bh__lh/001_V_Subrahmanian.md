In this hymn, it is noteworthy that in the stuti as Shiva, there is this verse:

  

नमः शिवाय रुद्राय नमः शक्तिधराय च ।  
**सर्वविद्याधिपतये भूतानां पतये नमः ॥** ३२ ॥  

  

In the Mahanarayana Upanishad of Taittiriya Aranyaka there is a Shiva Upasana section where this famous verse occurs:

  

``` ईशानः सर्वविद्यानामीश्वरः सर्वभूतानां  ब्रह्माधिपतिर्ब्रह्मणोऽधिपतिर्ब्रह्मा शिवो मे अस्तु सदाशिवोम् ॥ २॥ ```

  

The name: sarvavidyAnAm sarvabhUtAnAm adhipati in the Bhagavatam is a upbruhmanam of the above veda mantra.
