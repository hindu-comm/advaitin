Hare Krishna



I have come across people who believe in god and do pooja etc. but do not believe in traditional practices (for example formalities those need to be observed during eclipse like tarpana etc.) saying that eclipse is just a scientific thing no need to paste the divinity to it etc.
likewise ladies menstrual periods and related restrictions, argument goes like it is quite natural to the women like puberty in boys why so many restrictions etc....And someone who pose themselves as rationalists argue just do social service, serve the poor, help the needy and there is no need for doing any pooja and rituals (any vaidika karma for that matter), is this approach and thoughts OK?? No need to observe one's svadharma when he is mentally having the genuine intention to just do good to others ??



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
