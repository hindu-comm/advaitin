Namaskar:

All the five brothers have distinct qualities with diverse strength with one unified Mind! Panadavas are the greatest example to illustrate the Truth of Unity with diversity! The strength Pandavas come from the unity, discipline, highest moral values along with the grace of the Divine. Even the birth of these five brothers (six including Karna) were due to the Divine Grace. There are enough materials on each of them to write a book (Many have been written and available. It is good to know about the book on Bhima glorifying his significance in Mahabharat. None of us will ever be qualified to make a comparison of the five brothers to determine who is the greatest! We are all smart enough not to make any such attempts!

This may be a time to see the roles of the four brothers Rama, Bharata, Lakhmana and Shatrughna. Shatrughna the youngest brother of Rama has only limited amount of information in Ramayana (Valmiki, Tulsi and Kambar versions). Does this mean that he played an insignificant role in Ramayana? He is the twin brother of Lakshmana and according to Valmiki Ramayana, Shatrughna is one half component of Vishnu (Rama Avatar). It is true that Ramayana does not provide any elaborate information about Shatrugana. In Kamba Ramayana (Tamil poems) Bharata has been described in much greater details and one of the verses, the poet Kambar mentions that evan one thousand Ramas will not be equal to the qualities possessed by Bharata.

Our problem is most of us don’t think of Shatrughna as a central character in the Ramayana. We should remember the fact that he stayed in Ayodhya for 14 years, taking the burden of ruling the Kingdom unselfishly as a representative of Bharata. Bharata did not rule Ayodhya and he handed it over to Shatrughna his most trusted twin brother and stayed near the edge of the forest! It was mentioned in Ramayana that Shatrughna was prepared to kill Manthara who persuaded Kaikeyi to get the two boon to be fulfilled which sent Rama to the forest. Bharata intervened to stop him and saved manthara.

In conclusion, our epics expressed the importance of Unity in diversity! The final goal of reaching the Truth is only possible with unity of mind, body and intellect and with Divine Grace!!

Warm regards,

Ram Chandran
