> Namaste Subbu ji -- What does it mean when you say, "the released
> jiva, a Jnani"? Is this jiva now celebrated as Enlightened as a Jnani
> or is this jiva now "released" from jiva-hood? If the former, duality
> persists; if the latter, who is to see/know the world or to teach as
> there is no individuality?

  

In the Gaudapada verse, he is the one who formerly thought he was a jiva and now, after sadhana, is released. The verse says that there is no such a person since fundamentally creation itself is naught. If we accept a Jnani whom we surely admit as one who has made the journey, then inevitably we place him in the world and it is he who is spoken of in the Bh.Gita 2nd chapter elaborately.answering Arjuna's question on such a Jnani'straits. So, the world, duality, etc. all are admitted in such a scenario. If we do not want such a scenario, we should not utter the word Jnani.
