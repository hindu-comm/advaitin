panchadasi kara beautifully simplifies the topic as 1.iswara srshtyand 2. jeeva srshty. there are infinite jeeva srshtysbased on each ones set of likes and dislikes, whereas iswara srshtyis one common to all. The gnanis by transcending their raga dveshassee the iswara srhty alone and are thus objective. So even thoughthe gnani lives in the same world and sees the same objects, he is able to remove the extra mental projections by his mano nasha!

humble pranams to all!
