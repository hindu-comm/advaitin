Dear friends

  

After reading Bhikshu Gita , I felt that terms Varna and Caste are not synonyms and hence NOT to be used interchangeably. Caste is linked to birth but not our Varna.Bhagavan says I created 4 categories of Varna not Jati or castes. Humans have created many categories of jati The confusion started when we assumed that jatti unilaterally determines
the Varna whereas it has only an limited influence and that too only to a degree.It is our \[ dormant tendencies\] inner nature and will power to cultivate either Sattva or Rajas or Tamas that decides our ultimate Varna during the formative stages of our life.  

  

These are purely my assumptions humbly submitted.  

  

Shri Rammohan
