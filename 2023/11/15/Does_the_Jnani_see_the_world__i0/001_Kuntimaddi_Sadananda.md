PraNAms.

Here is my understanding.

Krishna says in Geeta

prakrutyaivacha karmaani kriyamaanaani sarvashaH\|

All actions are done by the prakruti - true for Jnaani or ajnaani. However, Jnaani understands this and realizes that even though Body, mind, and intellect (BMI) are acting, he is not the actor. He understands - akartaaham abhoktaaham.



Also in the 4th Chapter - Krishna says - karmani akarama yaH pasyet - saH buddhiman. One who realizes, that I am not a doer while doing is going on at the BMI level, is a realized soul.

This understanding is not a discrete event in the journey to realization.

I am reminded of Swami Paramarthanandaji - FIR reduction statement. As one begins to abide in the knowledge, F=frequency of perturbation, I= intensity of perturbation, and R=recovery time for perturbation decreases.

This process can take a long time depending on the purity of the mind.

The same applies to kartrutva and bhoktrutva bhaavas.

Hari Om!

Sadananda
