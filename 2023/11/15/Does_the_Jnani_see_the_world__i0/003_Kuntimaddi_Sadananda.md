PraNAms

  

Here is my understanding.

  

Jnaani and ajnaani both see the world of plurality.

  

Jnaani knows that it is not absolutely real but not unreal also - that is what mithyaa means - sat asat vilakshanam. He operates in the world as an actor in a drama - jagannatakam. Hence the karmas do not bind him
-just as an actor as a character in the drama kicking the other another character, as the script demands, even though they are friends outside the drama.



ajnaani thinks it is real due to ajnaanam.

  

Realization is a clear understanding of the absolute truth that involves - aham brahaamsi.

  

With that understanding jnaani still operates in the world as needed until the body, mind, and intellect drop out with the body merging with Virat, the mind with Hiranyagarba, and the intellect with Iswara.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
