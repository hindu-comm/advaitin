This is just what I have also stated. Rope-snake being \*\*Imagined \*\* does not involve origination or production of a vastu namely \*\*snake\*\*. Its ontological status is certainly not the same as that of Creation by Iswara. But surely \*\*production \*\* of a vastu in adhyAsa does involve vesting that vastu with a certain level of ontological status, by definition. Its ontological status is stated to be prAtibhAsika while Creation by Iswara has the ontological status of vyAvahArika.
