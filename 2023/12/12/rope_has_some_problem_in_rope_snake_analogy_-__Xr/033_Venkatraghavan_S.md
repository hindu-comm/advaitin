Namaste,

There is a typo in the sentence: "It is not like a memory where even during the recollection one is aware

  
that one is not perceiving a *memory* and one is merely recollecting what was  

seen. " Please read this to be:

  

"It is not like a memory where even during the recollection one is aware

that one is not perceiving *the object* and one is merely recollecting what was

seen."

  

Regards

Venkatraghavan
