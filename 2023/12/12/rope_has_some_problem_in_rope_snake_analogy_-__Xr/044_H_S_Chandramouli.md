Vedanta paribhasha which is almost exclusively concerned with epistemology of Advaita Siddhanta is currently considered to be the authority as far as advaita epistemology is concerned. It is much later to texts like Advaita Siddhi, Siddhanta Bindu etc wherein also advaitic epistemological issues are addressed in part. The postulates expressed in VP are considered to supercede views expressed in all earlier advaitic texts as far as advaitic epistemological issues are concerned.
