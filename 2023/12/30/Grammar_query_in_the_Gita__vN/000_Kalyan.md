I have another grammar question in the Bhagavad Gita.

  

We have -

  

यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत।  
अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदाऽऽत्मानं सृजाम्यहम्।।4.7।।  

  

Here धर्मस्य is used inषष्ठी विभक्ति to indicate decline of dharma. This is my understanding of षष्ठी विभक्ति too.

  

But consider this -

  

अश्रद्दधानाः पुरुषा धर्मस्यास्य परन्तप।  
अप्राप्य मां निवर्तन्ते मृत्युसंसारवर्त्मनि।।9.3।।  

  

How come we have धर्मस्य here, when the intended meaning is - having no faith in this dharma? Why षष्ठी विभक्ति is used when this appears to beसप्तमी विभक्ति?

  

(Swami Gambhirananda translates this as - who are regardless of this dharma...thus making it षष्ठी but many other translators translate it as - having no faith in this dharma).

  

Kindly clarify.

  

Thank you
