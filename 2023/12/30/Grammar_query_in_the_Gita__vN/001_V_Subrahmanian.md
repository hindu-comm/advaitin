Yes, Shankara in the bhashya, brings the saptami out of shashThi:अश्रद्दधानाःश्रद्धाविरहिताः *आत्मज्ञानस्य धर्मस्य अस्य स्वरूपे तत्फले च नास्तिकाः* पापकारिणः,With respect to the true nature and the fruit of Dharma, which means Atmajnana, they are bereft of faith.

  

For Shankara to take the meaning: Atmajnana for the word Dharma, we have a Kathopanishad mantra where Atma is meant by the word Dharma:सुविज्ञेयमणुरेष धर्मः। 1.1.21.  (आत्माख्यः धर्मः।). Shankara is supplying the wordsस्वरूपे तत्फले.

  

In the 9th chapter, the verse prior to the one you cite is:

  

राजविद्याराजगुह्यंपवित्रमिदमुत्तमम्।  
प्रत्यक्षावगमंधर्म्यंसुसुखंकर्तुमव्ययम्॥ २ ॥  

  

Here Atma vidya is the subject. And it is called dharmyam = that which is never bereft of Dharma. In the 3rd verse the word dharmasya means the Atma vidya of the 2nd verse. Hence Shankara takes that meaning.
