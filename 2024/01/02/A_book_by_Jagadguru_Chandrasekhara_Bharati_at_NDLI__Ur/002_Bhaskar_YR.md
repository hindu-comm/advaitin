I don’t see any problem in translation unless and until we are clear about the meaning of the word ‘samAdhi’ and its role in attaining mOksha. No doubt, VC is very clearly advocating the mandatory experience of samAdhi in a particular inert state of mind (asaMprajnAta samAdhi as per PYS or nirvikalpa samAdhi in Advaita texts). However ‘samAdhi’ the term which has been explained differently at various places in PTB and when bhAshyakAra talks particularly about samAdhi (in yOga shAstra sense) and sushupti clarifies that both states are NOT free from avidyA. jnAnAbhAva that he is brahman is quite evident in both the states says bhAshyakAra.
