Namaste.

In my view, VC is certainly not talking about nor \*\* advocating the mandatory experience of samAdhi in a particular inert state of mind (asaMprajnAta samAdhi as per PYS or nirvikalpa samAdhi in Advaita texts)\*\*. It is talking about a sAdhaka who is very actively and intensely concentrating his attention on Brahman to the exclusion of everything else. His efforts culminate in samAdhi or \*\*Realization\*\* wherein the state of the sAdhaka is one of Nirvikalpa SamAdhi; Only Brahman, no vikalpa of any sort. It is not that NS is mandatory. It is that NS and \*\*Realization\*\* are synonymous in Advaita SiddhAnta.
