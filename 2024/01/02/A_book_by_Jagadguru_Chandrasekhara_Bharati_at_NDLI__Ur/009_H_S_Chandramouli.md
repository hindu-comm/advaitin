Namaste.

Reg // he has to experience the jnana ‘practically’ in NS hence it is a ‘vidhi’ in Advaita sAdhana //,

\*\* experience the jnana ‘practically’\*\*, Yes. But through jnAna. Through SMN.

But \*\*in NS\*\*, no in my view. \*\*in NS\*\* does not convey the requirement that Realization is by way of jnAna. Hence NS is not a vidhi. NididhyAsana as a vidhi till Realization, yes. But I distinguish NS from NididhyAsana.

I am aware that Sri SSS does not agree that VC is authored by Sri Bhagavatpada. But for me the very fact that Sri Chandrashekhara Bharathi Mahaswamiji has authored a vyAkhyAna on the text is authority enough to reject any doubts about the authenticity of the author of VC as Sri Bhagavatpada.
