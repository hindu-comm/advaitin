Hare Krishna



brahmAnubhava, brahmasAkshAtkAra, brahma darshana etc. used in Advaita texts though brahman is not attainable afresh ( na Atmaa AguntakaH kasyachit svayam siddhatvAt, as he is self-established not a stranger, not an adventitious thing for any one) as it is bhUta vastu Vishaya jnana, However, shravaNa, manana nidhidhyAsana etc. recommended, Acharya upadesha, Shuddha antaHkaraNa etc. suggested to have the brahmaanubhava or brahma darshana. But it is in the sense that it is not to see brahman as a such and such object and by meditating on it you can ‘see’ him and experience him in some state ( like severe meditation by ‘keeta’ become bhramara example given in this same text VC)..it is being the inmost self shaastra by saying all these things just to remove all distinctions created by avidyA. More importantly brahma jnana is NOT the purusha tantra jnana that you can achieve through some individual efforts and individual experiences (vaiyuktika anubhava) it is vastu tantra jnana needs to be realized which is already there though sArvatrika pUrNAnubhava says Sri SSS. By sadhana you can get rid of wrong notions about it, when it is done in totality, the svayaM siddha Atma Chaitanya will blossom on its own.
