And also as you know Sri SSS does not have any qualms accepting the first five anga-s of PY (yama, niyama, aasana, pranayama, pratyahara ) as a part of adhyAtmika sAdhana and how remaining three dhAraNa, dhyAna and samadhi should be treated in vedAntic sense not as per ‘dvaita shAstra’ yOgic sense. And according to him vedAntic concept of yOga (adhyAtma yOga as enshrined in katha) has been mistaken for somany practices, occult powers, purusha taNtra saMAdhi sthiti etc. He has taken both vivaraNa and bhAmati for refutation with regard to this.
bhAmati’s interpretation about drashtavyaH ( to be seen) as Atman is to be seen by means of ‘experience’ in samAdhi or some time bound trance state of mind) and vivaraNa’s advocation that to literally experience AtmaikatvaM one should have the experience of samAdhi in particular state as in other states he would continue to see the duality due to prArabdha and avidyA lesha etc.
