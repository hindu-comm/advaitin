Hare Krishna

I am aware that Sri SSS does not agree that VC is authored by Sri Bhagavatpada. But for me the very fact that Sri Chandrashekhara Bharathi Mahaswamiji has authored a vyAkhyAna on the text is authority enough to reject any doubts about the authenticity of the author of VC as Sri Bhagavatpada.

- Some decades back I asked this same question to Sri Ashwatha Narayana
  avadhAni ( now Sri Aksharanandendra Saraswati), in Sri SSS’s
  observation on VC why this topic ( HH commentary on VC) has not been
  discussed?? For that he said, Sri SSS shared his view points about
  this work and its authorship with a ‘vimarshAtmaka drushti’ like what
  he did on various shankara biographies (vruttAnta sAra sarvasva) and
  since HH commented on this work without any analytical motive with
  regard to authorship of this work and just commented on the ‘contents’
  in appreciation and Sri SSS too appreciate the contents, essence,
  beauty and importance of this popular work, there was no need for him
  to talk anything specifically about HH magnificent commentary or Sri
  SSS might not have this work at his disposal when he was writing his
  observations on VC. And again, HH, had he analyzed and concluded
  anything about the authenticity of authorship in his commentaries with
  some justifications perhaps Sri SSS would have touched these points.
  Since HH taken it as a work of bhagavatpAda as per then existing
  traditional belief and due to non availability of anything with regard
  to authorship controversies when he was writing this commentary he
  might have ignored talking extra about the author of VC. Anyway, at
  the end of the day it is not completely out of the scope to ignore
  anything that has been said about authorship with some logical
  justification just because some mahatma written the commentary on the
  contents of it.
- And as you know how Sri SSS appreciate this work in his introduction
  before sharing his observations.
