Namaste.

Brahman is NOT experienced. Refer BUB 3-9-28 starting from

// अत्रेदंविचार्यते— आनन्दशब्दो लोके सुखवाची प्रसिद्धः ……. //

// atredaM vichAryate — Anandashabdo loke sukhavAchI prasiddhaH …… //,

Translation by Swami Madhavananda, PD F page 594 Book page 563 , starting from // Here is something to discuss. The word ‘Bliss’ is generally known to denote pleasure. …… //.

The conclusion is

// तस्मात् आनन्दं ब्रह्म विज्ञानात्मकं सत् स्वयमेव वेदयते। //.

// tasmAt AnandaM brahma vij~nAnAtmakaM sat svayameva vedayate \| //.

Translation // Hence the text, 'Knowledge, Bliss,' etc., must be interpreted as setting forth the nature of Brahınan, and not signifying that the bliss of the Self is cognized //.

Please study this Bhashya part in BUB 3-9-28 fully to appreciate the intended meaning.

Where ever terms like \*\*Brahman isexperienced, experience of Brahman, Brahman experience \*\* are stated, it must be understood as referring to experience of jnAna phala and not to Brahman per se. This applies to VC, Kanchi Maha-Swamigal’s Discourses also.
