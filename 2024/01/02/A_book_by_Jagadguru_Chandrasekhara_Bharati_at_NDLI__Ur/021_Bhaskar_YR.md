In the mangala shloka at the beginning of the vyAkhyAna, Sri HH states as below

// प्रसन्नानां गभीराणां वचसां देशिकेशितुः । भावस्त्वत्कृपया चित्ते भासतां मम शारदे \|\|
//

// prasannAnAM gabhIrANAM vachasAM deshikeshituH \| bhAvastvatkRRipayA chitte bhAsatAM mama shArade \|\| //

Translation by Sri Shankaranarayanan // O sharada ! May the import of the clear and majestic words of the best of teachers ( Sri Shankara) shine in my mind through Your Grace //.

Quite clearly, Sri HH considered this text as authored by Sri Bhagavatpada.
