> In the above BSB 4.1.2, Has Shankara used 'anubhava' to denote the
> Atman/Brahman experience,**specifically to Nirvikalpa Samadhi** ?  

  

Namaste

  

In the BSB 3.2.24 Shankara says: The direct realization of the Supreme Brahman is had 'during' a session of deep focus: It is called samraadhanam in the sutra and Shankara explains it as: practice of bhakti, dhyana, surrender to the Lord. During that time the liberating knowledge takes place:
