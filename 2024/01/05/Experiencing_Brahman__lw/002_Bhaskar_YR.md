Hare Krishna



Incidentally, if someone wants to point out that anubhava does not mean ‘experience’, why does everyone keep using that word?

- Used by bhAshyakAra himself, that is why 😊 not only shruti/shAstra
  but also anubhava etc. are valid pramANa asserts bhAshyakAra.



What word should they use instead? Words such as ‘intuition’ still require an ‘intuiter’ and an ‘intuited’ thing.



- It is intuitive experience. It is not like individual experience like
  this is my experience therefore I agree or disagree. By the way an
  individual’s samAdhi experience falls under this category. OTOH
  anubhava (intuitive experience) has been explained as cognition or
  consciousness which every one gets in an identical or same manneris
  called anubhava ( Sri SSS calls this as universal experience). For
  example drushya through drushti ( cognition of the form through eyes)
  it is everyone’s experience that one would see through eyes, no sane
  person would argue no hear sound through eyes!! In short the
  experience or intuitive experience is same to one and all (universal)
  and it is not unique. Hence it is called knowledge / jnana as well as
  Sri ChandramouLi prabhuji clarified.
