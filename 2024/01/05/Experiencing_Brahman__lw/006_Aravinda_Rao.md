Dennis ji, pranams,

You have raised an important point that theword experience implies duality. It is true.

There may be another way of understanding the word anubhava, perhaps the etymological way. The prefix 'anu' means later, 'bhava', is to be. So the total word *anubhava* will imply that the seeker, after (anu) the twin practices of the sravana trio on one side and vairagya on the other (which are needed simultaneously like two wings of a bird) becomes (bhava) Brahman. Anubhava = after that, be that. He walks the talk, as we say.

Aravinda Rao
