Namaste Sadananda Ji,

  

The Nyāya-Vaiśeṣikas also hold

that abhāva is not without any essence. It is determined by its pratiyogī (countercorrelate). In case of negation, there is an object whose non-existence is experienced

and there is a locus on which this non-existence is based. The object of non-existence

is called its pratiyogī and the locus is called anuyogī. For example, in case of ‘there is

no jar on the ground’, the jar is the pratiyogī and the ground is the anuyogī
