There is a popular text said to be of Shankaracharya by name 'Panchikaranam'. It is a short text based on the Mandukya Upanishad method of leading one to the Turiya. There is a Vartika to this text, said to be of Sureshwaracharya.

  

[https://archive.org/details/Acc.No.6045Panchikaranam1930/page/n1/mode/2up](https://archive.org/details/Acc.No.6045Panchikaranam1930/page/n1/mode/2up)  

  

In the Anubhuti Prakasha, Swami Vidyaranya says while explaining the Pranava upasana mentioned in the Mundakopanishat (with the bow and arrow analogy):

  

Mundaka 63

  

पञ्चीकृतोक्तमार्गेण प्रणवध्यानमाचरेत् \|  
ततो जगदधिष्ठानमेकमेव सदेक्ष्यते \|\|

  

The aspirant has to meditate upon Brahman with the aid of the Pranava as taught in the PanchikaraNam. He will then come to a stage when the substratum of the world, Brahman, is always within his vision.

  

From this we infer that the text predates Swami Vidyaranya.
