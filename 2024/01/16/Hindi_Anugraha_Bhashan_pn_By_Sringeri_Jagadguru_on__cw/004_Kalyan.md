Dear Sri Putran-ji, Namaskaaram

  

Temple construction and prana pratishtha, to the best of my knowledge, are subject matters of Agamas. I do not know if dharma shastra-s have anything to say on this topic. I am open to being corrected.

  

Secondly, it is not clear to me which Agama shAstra-s are being violated. Assuming that the Ayodhya temple would follow some Vaishnava Agama-s, I am aware of the existence of Vaikhanasa and Pancharatra Vaishnava Agama-s, both of which are South Indian. I do not know which Agamas are followed in Northern India for the Ayodhya temple.

  

So far as I understand, the Sringeri Acharya has not claimed that shastra violations are happening.

  

Anyway, I think in the interest of devotees, the following needs to be clarified by anyone claiming violations -

  

1\. Which Agama-s are being followed in the temple construction?

  

2\. How these Agama-s are being violated? Specifically, giving quotes.
