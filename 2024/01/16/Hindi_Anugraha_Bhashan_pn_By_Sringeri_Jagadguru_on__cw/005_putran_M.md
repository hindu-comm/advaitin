Namaskaram Kalyan-ji,

  

I don't know anything on these shastras. The article paraphrases the shankaracharya as saying: "against the dharma shastras to perform the Prana Pratishtha of a murti before the temple is complete. He also pointed out that the mahurat has not been correctly chosen revealing that, in fact, the Jyotish in Kashi was told to identify a mahurat in January rather than the most appropriate mahurat for the Prana Pratishtha." Those who know agama or dharma shastras can prove or disprove their claims; or can point to other temples that did not follow such rules and are considered legit.

  

Basically, thegovt is accused of strong-arming the process to fit into its timings and this is forcing deviation from the rules. Whereas if the kshatriyas were having theshankacharyas as their Raja gurus, then they would have been guided differently from how it is presently being done. In particular, the acharya is very clear that the shastras don't allow for the prana-pratishta to happen before the temple is complete. And the muhurtham is not chosen ideally but for the sake of a deadline.

  

I don't know if these are such absolute faults that de-legitimize thepranapratishtaor what great harm can follow,or whether they are simply inappropriate/imperfect/un-ideal. Probably the latter. Hopefully, there can be prayaschittam rituals to correct such faults.
