Hare Krishna



If I am right as per Agama (there are somany like shaivaagama, pAncharAtra, vaikhAnasa, vaidikAgama etc.) for each and every deity there is specific pratishTA kAla and lagna also prescribed. I don’t think paushyamAsa (22^(nd) Jan is Paushya masa,Shukla paksha dvaadashi ) is recommended for the pratishtApana. I heard / read somewhere for the brahma-vishNu pratishTApana kanya lagna is preferred. And before pratishtApana somany other things also to be observed like adhivAsa-s,
vAstu rAkshOghna homa, pindikAsthApana, mahAsnapana etc. And if my understanding of the current issue is right, puri Jagadguru specifically objecting PM doing the pratishtApana with his own hands and other main priests (Agamika-s) are just present there and reciting mantra-s. ‘do we have to just stand there and clapping our hands when he is doing prANa pratishTApana’?? this is what puri Jagadguru asked media. So, it is something related to traditional rituals related to prANa pratishTApana. When the Garbha gudi (sanctum sanctorum) is completed in all respects no more civil / sculptural works pending inside the garbhagudi then it is allowed to do prANa pratishtApana and strictly no more ‘outsiders’ movement allowed inside once it is done as per vaidikAgama. Further extensions outside the Garbha gudi can be done over a period of time like what we see today at somany pilgrimage places. Just few of my thoughts and I am not a big follower of current religio-political issues with regard to ayOdhyA rAma mandira 😊



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
