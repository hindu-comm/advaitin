Hare Krishna



Vaidikaagama basically accepts and recognizes the authority of veda-s and most uses the veda mantra-s for the temple pooja and pratishTA. It is shrutyOkta and not smrutyOkta, and as you said shaivaagama, vaishNavaagama, shAktAgama etc. if done in accordance with veda-s these different agama-s would be part and parcel of vaidikAgama only. And in short, in vaidikAgama prastishTA vidhi for all the rituals related to devata pratishtApana only veda mantra-s will be used. And if the rituals done with smruti and purANokta shloka / Stotra-s then that vidhi would be / can be called as avaidikAgama vidhi. For example when we are doing pratimAshuddhi by using panchagavya, normally we chant rigvedeeya agni sUkta ( agni pada sahita and agni pada rahita ) and yajurvedeeya pratimaashuddhi mantra as well. But when the same ritual is done with some purANOkta shloka related to agni and varuNa then it is called avaidikAgama vidhi.
