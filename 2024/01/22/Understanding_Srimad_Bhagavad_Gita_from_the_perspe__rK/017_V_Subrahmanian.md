Dear Vikram ji,

  

The krama mukti of Advaita involves getting the Advaita aham brahmasmi jnanam in that Brahma loka and remaining there till maha pralaya when that loka also will perish upon which the jivas there who are Jnanis, will become videha muktas as per advaita. The position of VA is that in that ultimate scenario the muktas will be in Vaikuntha, with individual identities. This is because jiva-jiva and jiva-Ishwarabheda is absolutely real in VA.
