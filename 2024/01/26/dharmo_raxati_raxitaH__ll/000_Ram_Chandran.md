Namaskar:  
Manusmriti verse 8.15 says:  
dharma eva hato hanti dharmo rakṣati rakṣitaḥ tasmād dharmo na hantavyo mā no dharmo hato'vadhī  
Translation:  
  
Those who sacrifice their virtues are themselves get destroyed. And those who preserve them are themselves are protected. We therefore, do not sacrifice our virtues considering that if destroyed, it will destroy us.!  
A similar verse is also quoted in Mahabharata in the Vana Parva (Araneya Parvadhyay) where Yudhisthira said to Yaksha. The entire Mahabharata is a symbolic narration of the importance of the Role of Dharma in our life.
