Installation of Ram in Ayodhya is done after 500 years is to register in our mind that we need to recognize the symbolism of Ram. By reinstalling Ram, we want to invoke Ram in our heart. In Tulasi Ramyan, when Sage Vishwamitra brough Ram and Laxman to the Ashram, the Rishis at the Ashram wanted to host Ram in their home! Sage Vishwamitra tells the Rishis that Ram will always stay wherever the heart is pure!
Tulasisda, the great devotee of Ram hinted to all of us that we need to clean up our mind and filled up with virtues. Significance of Sri Ram Worship is to recognize the Virtues of Ram: They include Truthfulness Love, Kindness, and Friendship Equanimity: Remember only the good deed of someone even if it is negligible; Forgive and Forget the evil acts committed even by the Devils and Live a life full of Sacrifice without selfishness By invoking and worshipping Ram we want to acquire an iota of His Character. We have through similar periods throughout the entire history of India!
