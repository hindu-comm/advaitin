Sorry for the belated reply. I must clarify here according to some :
even though certification is not required to prove that you are an engineer, whatever you learnt in engg. Course is just like instruction manual and you have to show your ‘engineering skills’ practically in engineering field. Likewise, shAstra jnana too just an instruction manual that teaches you that you are brahman but after getting this jnana through shAstra you have to literally experience that you are secondless brahman and this practical experience is what really helps you to realize that you are indeed that ekamevAdviteeyaM!! So in this scenario, shAstra jnana is secondary and practical experience of this jnana is primary. That is the reason why some vyAkhyAnakAra-s explained sharavaNa, manana as dhAraNa (as per PY terms) and nidhidhyAsana as dhyAna (again as per PY) and darshana means samAdhi (asaMprajnAtha samAdhi). Vidya svarUpa according to them is avidyA nirmUlana (vidyA prApti) AND brahma sAkshAtkAra and there is interval between these two i.e paramArtha jnana (tattvajnAna) and mOksha. So sAkshAtkAra as per this : first removes the sAkshAtkAra of prapancha (world of duality) and this sAkshAtkAra itself included in prapancha it destroys itself also resulting in saMpUrNa jnana in samAdhi or mOksha could happen only after shedding the mortal coil of the jnAni.
