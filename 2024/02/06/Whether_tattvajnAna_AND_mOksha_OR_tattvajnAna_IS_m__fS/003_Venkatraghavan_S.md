Namaste Bhaskar ji,

I can say that what you have written below is not what Swami Vidyaranya has said in Jivan Mukti Viveka.

  

Essentially he is saying

1\) tattvajnAna alone is the means to moksha.

2\) In certain cases, the rise of tattva-jnAna itself is blocked by anAtma vAsana - so for the purpose of chittashuddhi, some yogic practices are needed, for some people.

3\) After the rise of jnAna, for jnAna niShThA some of these practices are needed. jnAna niShThA is for the purpose of mental peace, not for the purpose of moksha, that has been "achieved" already with tattvajnAna.

  

Regards,

Venkatraghavan
