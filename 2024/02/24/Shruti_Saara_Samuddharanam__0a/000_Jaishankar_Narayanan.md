Shruti Saara Samuddharanam means extracting the essence of the Shruti / Veda. It is the major work of Totakaacharya who is traditionally considered as one of the four principal disciples of Aadi Shankaraachaarya. The text consists of 179 verses mostly in totaka chandas (meter), which bring out the essential meaning of the Upanishads. The teaching is presented in the form of a dialogue between a student and guru.
