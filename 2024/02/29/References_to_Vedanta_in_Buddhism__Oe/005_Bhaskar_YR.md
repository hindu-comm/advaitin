Hare Krishna



I have read somewhere at the time of shankara all vedAntins were only advaitins with variations in Advaita doctrine itself. And theory of absolute bheda between jeeva and paramAtma was conspicuous by its absence during his time. Through jnana and subsequent upAsana one can attain mOksha (not only jnana but upAsana also mandatory), through upAsana of the jnana results in ajnAna nivrutti etc. might be the beeja rUpa of later vishishtAdvaita but these schools (then existing at the time of shankara) were not absolute bhedavAdins like dualists today.
Ofcourse there is bhatruprapancha bhedAbheda doctrine which has been refuted by bhAshyakAra but I don’t think rAmAjuna praNeeta vishishtAdvaita was existing at the time of shankara. Curious to know more references.
