Namaste Subrahmanian ji,  

  

In The Brihat Samhita of Varahamihira 60.19(6th Cent) too there is a mention of Vipras who were Brahma worshippers.

  

![4tkdM.png](https://groups.google.com/group/advaitin/attach/a9a460894043/4tkdM.png?part=0.1&view=1)  

  

Just thought of mentioning  

  

The [Bhāgavatas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/bhagavata#hinduism "show Bhāgavatas definitions") are the worshippers of [Viṣṇu](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/vishnu#hinduism "show Viṣṇu definitions"); the [Magas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/maga#history "show Magas definitions") are the worshippers of the Sun; Dvijas wearing ashes are the worshippers of [Śiva](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/shiva#hinduism "show Śiva definitions"). Persons possessed of a knowledge of the [Devas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/deva#hinduism "show Devas definitions") attendant on the [Mātṛ](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/matri#hinduism "show Mātṛ definitions") Devas are the worshippers of the Mātṛ Devas. **The Brāhmaṇas (Vipras) generally are the worshippers of [Brahmā](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/brahma#hinduism "show Brahmā definitions").** The [Śākyas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/shakya#hinduism "show Śākyas definitions") are the worshipers of the God of the [Arhats](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/arhat#hinduism "show Arhats definitions"). The Bauddhas are the worshippers of [Buddha](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/buddha#hinduism "show Buddha definitions"). The ceremony of fixing the images and the like ceremonies shall be performed by the respective worshippers of the several images.  

  

[~ Brihat Samhita 60.19](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brihat-samhita/d/doc229305.html)
