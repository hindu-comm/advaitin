Namaskar:

Life is simple; It can become simpler if we just live simple; It gets complicated if we try to evaluate! The sages of Upanishads suggest us just to PLUG our EARS!

E - Excitement about our past!

A - Anxiety about our future!

R - Relax to live in the present!

S - Silence the mind passions!

These are the Six Mind Passions

1 Kama (lust),

2 Krodha (anger),

3 Lobha (greed),

4 Moha(attachment),

5 Mada(ahankara) and

6 Matsarya(jealousy)

Let us PLUG our EARS and Enjoy the Bliss of Life!!!

With my warm regards

Ram Chandran
