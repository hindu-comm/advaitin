> Manuṣyas can experience three avasthās - jāgrata, svapna and suṣupti.

It is believed, though legendarily, that devata-s do not have sleep, and therefore have no dream experience. They are said to never wink their eyelids. They are termed 'animiShaa' hence. Animals are admitted to have dream and sleep too. Even modern science has experimented on REM sleep in animals. I am not aware of the case with plants.
