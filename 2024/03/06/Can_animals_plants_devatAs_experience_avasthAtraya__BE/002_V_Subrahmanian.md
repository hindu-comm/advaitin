Regarding Devatas, that they do not have sleep/dream is scriptural.
Regarding all others - humans and animals, Shankara says:  
  
सर्वप्राणिसाधारणाएव स्वापादयोऽनुकीर्त्यन्ते विद्याप्रकरणेऽपि प्रतिपिपादयिषितवस्तुप्रतिपादनानुगुण्येन, Brahmasutra bhashya 4.2.7. Sleep, [etc.is](http://etc.is) common to all living beings...
