Namaste

  

In chhAndogya upanishad 8.3.2, there is a description of the state of deep sleep as a hidden treasure.

  

अथ ये चास्येह जीवा ये च प्रेता यच्चान्यदिच्छन्न लभते सर्वं तदत्र गत्वा विन्दतेऽत्र ह्यस्यैते सत्याः कामा अनृतापिधानास्तद्यथापि हिरण्यनिधिं निहितमक्षेत्रज्ञा उपर्युपरि सञ्चरन्तो न विन्देयुरेवमेवेमाः सर्वाः प्रजा अहरहर्गच्छन्त्य एतं ब्रह्मलोकं न विन्दन्त्यनृतेन हि प्रत्यूढाः ॥ ८.३.२ ॥  
  

Further, those of his relatives who are still alive and those who are dead, and also those things a person cannot get even if he wishes for them—all these he gets by going within his heart. All true desires of a person are in his heart, though they are hidden. It is like when there is gold hidden someplace underground and people who are ignorant of it walk over that spot again and again, knowing nothing about it. Similarly, all these beings go to Brahmaloka every day, and yet they know nothing about it because they are covered by ignorance.

  

(The word prajah in above is translated as beings/creatures)

  

Commenting on this, Acharya says -

  

अतः कष्टमिदं वर्तते जन्तूनां यत्स्वायत्तमपि ब्रह्म न लभ्यते इत्यभिप्रायः ॥  

  

Hence it is that this calamity befalls all creatures that though Brahman is quite within their reach, they do not reach it.

  

The word used is jantu, referring to creatures.

  

This implies, all creatures undergo the state of deep sleep. (All creatures are united with brahman during deep sleep.)
