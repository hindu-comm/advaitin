I know I am talking entirely different topic here 😊



This brahman in sushupti is kArya brahman (mAndUkya mantra 6) not ultimate brahman like in tureeya…some argues like this. Anyway, let that be aside. It is good that the form of avidyA in suhupti is nothing but tattvAgrahaNa (jnAnAbhAva) and this jnAnAbhAva does not come in the way of brahmaikyata in sushupti. Like not knowing the treasure underneath does not mean he has not walked on that or there is no treasure underneath, without knowing the treasure he just walked on that day in-day out. And the reason for ‘not knowing’ this ikyata in sushupti is ‘Ekata’ bhAshyakAra clarifies.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
