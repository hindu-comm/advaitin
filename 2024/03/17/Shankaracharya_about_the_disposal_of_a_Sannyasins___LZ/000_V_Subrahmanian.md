 In the Brihadaranyaka Bhashya, Bhagavatpada says:

  

[‘भस्मान्तं शरीरम्’ (ई. उ. १७)](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Isha?page=NaN&id=IS_V17&hl=%E0%A4%AD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D)इति ; न हि पारिव्राज्यपक्षे भस्मान्तता शरीरस्य स्यात्।  

  

For a Sannyasin the end (of the bodily life) is not cremation (body being burnt).

  

Does this mean that even during Shankaracharya's times the practice of not cremating the body of a Sannyasin but interring it in a samadhi/adhishthanam was in vogue?
