Dear Subbuji,

Adi Shankara cremated his mother's body, as he promised her. Usually it is the duty of the son to cremate the body of the grihasthi parents, but if the parents take sannyaAsa, they would not be cremated. One, who takes sannyAsa, has to do the ceremonial atma-shrAddha. at the time of taking sannyAsa
