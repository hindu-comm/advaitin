अज्ञानं पुरतस्तेषां भाति कक्ष्यासु कासुचित् ॥ 6.143  
  
Even if all the learned people of the world try to determine the nature of this world, they will find themselves confronted at some stage or other by ignorance.  
Nothing in the world can be determined in the manner of 'idam ittham'.
