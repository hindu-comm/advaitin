Namaskaram Shri Suresh ji,

  

It is only through shastra that the atma-nityatvais initially known. Following the guidance of shruti, then alone yukti and anubhava establish this fact,and not independent of shruti. The anatma-anityatva is very much experiential, and inference can at best establish a changeless foundation to experience the ever-changing experiences, but this experiential treatment can never help us directly realize the true nature of atman as that changeless foundation. Even this discussion of ours cannot directly & conclusively establish atman-nityatvafor ourselves unless there is aparokshanubhuti through shastra-shravana.

  

Also, it is important to note that the mityatva of shastra is only truly realized by a jivanmukta. For others, the shastra is as real as the bondage that the shastra directs us to overcome. After realization, it is perfectly acceptable for the shastra to be mithya just as the bondage it successfully guided us to overcome. In other words, the reality & utility of the shastra is closely tied to our sense of bondage and genuineness to overcome.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
