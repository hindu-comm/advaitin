Dear Suresh ji,

  

The Atma nityatva can be taught by the Shruti only first and then we can appreciate it. But without that basic teaching we can't get an idea of such nityatva. This is because the basic idea of janmantara is not an experiential reality. Only astikas believe it. There are cultures/religions who do not subscribe to that. But Atman which is not even jiva, is eternal and that is extremely logically taught by the Gita 2.16: na asato vidyate bhaavah, na abhavo vidyate satah. This truth, along with avinashi, nityah, sarvagatah, etc. are taught there itself.
Thus, without the shruti we can't know and appreciate atma nityatva.
anatma anityatva, fine, we see ourselves. That is why the shruti - yathaa iha karmachito lokah kshiyate evameva amutra...takes the example of this world where by pratyaksha we see that anything produced by karma is ephemeral.

  

Vidyaranya goes further in the Panchadashiand says:  

  

**मासाब्दयुगकल्पेषु गतागम्येष्वनेकधा**

**नोदेति नास्तमेत्येका संविदेषा स्वयंप्रभा ।७।**

**mAsAbdayugakalpeShu gatAgamyeShvanekadhA**

**nodeti nAstametyekA saMvideShA svayaMprabhA \|7\|**

*****  
This Consciousness is One only through out the different months, years, yugA-s and kalpA-s in the past or in the future. It has no birth and no death. It is self-effulgent.*

*  *

With the basic input from the shruti, when we contemplate on the above, we happily accept this truth.

  

Since, ultimately this one truth alone exists, all else like the teacher, etc. are only relatively real. This is what the Dashashloki says and is elaborated in the article.

subbu
