I am attempting this post among vidwans here since this is an issue that came up recently in some study circle I am part of. The question that was posed regarding part of the [Brhad. Up 1.4.7](https://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha#BR_C01_S04) - "यथा ह वै पदेनानुविन्देदेवं कीर्तिं श्लोकं विन्दते य एवं वेद ॥ ७ ॥" - was similar to the one above. That - how does mithya lakshana/hints/steps (pada/padena) lead to the Self? How do mithya guru-s, teachings etc lead to Sat/सत? \[This is apart from the meaning that "when the Self is attained, everything is automatically attained..."\]

  

One of the explanations offered in the above context of animal pada-s was that - mithya, in the sense of relatively-real, and *not* unreal or non-existent, can indeed point to सत/Self. Mithya just means not ultimately real, not with independent existence - therefore "real enough" for the vyavaharika world to impart guidance to the Self...

  

Not sure if that was the most convincing but I share here because of the similarity of the points raised in the write-up above...
