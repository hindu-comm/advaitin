Dear Sri Subbu-ji,

  

Long time ago, in another forum, while answering the samequestion you had mentioned that "tadpada viveka" will not arise without shAstra. I presume the tadpadarefers to the Self/ParamAtma/Ishwara that pervades and indwells in the Jagat.

  

If the above is true, then I don't see any problem as the entire universe could be looked upon as a manifestation of Ishwara/shAstra/veda/shabda, and the non-dual witnessing Self could be taken as the ultimate meaning.

  

I think the Anandagiri tIka (Gita bhAshya) is pointing to the same:

  

तत्र पूर्वषट्कात्मकं पूर्वकाण्डं त्वम्पदार्थं विषयीकरोति । मध्यमषट्करूपं मध्यमकाण्डं तत्पदार्थंगोचरयति । अन्तिमषट्कलक्षणमन्तिमं काण्डं पदार्थयोरैक्यं वाक्यार्थमधिकरोति

  

But I look forwardto your clarification.

  

Namaste,  
Suresh
