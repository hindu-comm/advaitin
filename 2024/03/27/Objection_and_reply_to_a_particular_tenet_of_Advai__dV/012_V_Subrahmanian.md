Yes, that indeed is the case. That's exactly stated by that monumental Chandogya passage: ऐतदात्म्यमिदं सर्वं तत्सत्यं स आत्मा तत्त्वमसि श्वेतकेतो which means That Sat, Brahman, is the Atman, essence of this entire creation, that is the Real/Truth, it is the (your) Atman, You are That, Shvetaketu.

  

In this one line the Upanishad is conveying that the Tatpada Brahman is the jagatkAraNam and also the essence of you, the jiva, the tvampada.
