\< Yes, the tvam, tat and aikyam are woven so nicely in the BG\>

  

This attainment of the oneness of the atman/jiva with the tadpada Brahman/Ishwara is possible only through the process of yagya/sacrifice/shAstra. The essential oneness of these in the Self proves the reality/existence ofeverything in the Self. The universal and therefore impersonal Self is actually an embodiment of yagya/shAstra/tapasya (Yagyo vai Vishnu). Probably that is why, Lord Shiva or Parameshwara is always shown to be engaged in tapasya. The witnessing Self is the fire in which everything gets sacrificed.

  

So, IMHO, mithyAtva refers to the apparent "separate existence" of these in vyavahAra. It should never be misunderstood as unreal.

  

  

Regards,  
Suresh
