Namaskaram Suresh-ji,

  
  

>   
> The essential oneness of these in the Self proves the
> reality/existence ofeverything in the Self.

  

Where Self referred to is Saguna Brahman or Ishvara. If Maya-shakti is delineated as iccha-jnana-kriya Shakti, the world that Ishvara projects/'creates' is eternal as a part of His jnana, in some potential or rudimentary form i.e. Veda.

  

The primal manifestation of this jnana is the Veda in shabda form. Then the jnana of past creation will also come into play in projecting the jivas etc. That is how I logically connect the dots.

  

  

> The universal and therefore impersonal Self

>  is actually an embodiment of yagya/shAstra/tapasya (Yagyo vai
> Vishnu).

  

Not if by impersonal Self you mean Nirguna Brahman. But yes if you mean Saguna Brahman in some higher impersonal sense. That's how I understand.
