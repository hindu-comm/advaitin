Strictly speaking there is no difference between jneya brahma ( nirguNa brahma) and upAsya brahma (sOpAdhika - saguNa brahman). If the differentiation between these two is real then ‘advaita haani’. Hence somewhere shruti says : He (parabrahman) does not have a body nor organs, no one is equal or superior to him, and he is capable of action by the strength of his natural jnana (sarvajnatva). Sarvajnatva is his intrinsic nature and through which he ‘saw’ though he ‘was’ alone at the very beginning. So, he is said to be both efficient and material cause and there is nothing else ‘apart’ from him. Hence bhAshyakAra elsewhere clarifies : sarva vishesha rahitOpi jagatO mUlaM ityavagatvAt astyeva, though it is totally attributeless it is known to be the ‘cause’ of the jagat. So, IMO, there is no need to keep two different brahman in two different compartments even if we are talking about creation coz. He is the ekamevAdviteeya kAraNam in creation module as well.
