So, IMHO, mithyAtva refers to the apparent "separate existence" of these in vyavahAra. It should never be misunderstood as unreal.





- Yes, mithya is simply misconception (wrong perception) of an object.
  It is like seeing the ‘snake’ in place of rope it is something
  different from anirvachaneeyatva existence of both bubble and water or
  foam and water. (bhAshyakAra himself gives this example). So an
  effect like sarpa that appears to be independent (separate) of the
  cause (rajju) is an illusion (bhrAnti). There is no anirvachaneeyatva
  in this to claim there exists anirvachaneeya sarpa in rajju. nAma
  rUpa when viewed separate from its kAraNa is anrutaM, but when the
  same nAma & rUpa viewed from its kAraNa drushti it is Satyameva. Due
  to jagat’s changing nature it is asatyaM ( vide tai. bhAshya) but it
  is existing and available for the transaction. Hence shruti says
  satyaMchAnrutaM cha satyamabhavat yadidaM kiMcha. Though it appears
  in a special form (vishesha rUpa) it would be realized in its kAraNa
  rUpa with the aid of Shabda brahma i.e. veda. Veda helps the jnAni to
  realize what an ajnAni sees and understands the nAma & rUpa as
  independent of brahman (kAraNa) is indeed brahman there exists nothing
  apart from brahman.
- BTW, it is not vishishtAdvaita this is absolute shuddhAdvaita where
  one see nothing apart from THAT.
