Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

Not two Brahmans in different compartments. But different standpoints, one of which is vivarta. If you use Advaita Hani language, then you have to be consistent with it. imo as you ~ wrote, "The attributeless Self is **known as** the cause of creation" is indeed the proper way to interpret such statements, and when you know it as cause of creation, you either know or refer to it as Ishvara or saguna Brahman and distinguish from the attributeless Brahman for which it is gibberish to talk simultaneously of creation, "embodiment", "everything", "yagnya, shastra, tapasya".

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
