Namaskaram Putran-ji,

  

My main point is - SB/ShAstra/Dharma is the only way for the attainment of NB - "na anya pantha ayanAya vidyate"

  

So, until the attainment of NB, ShAstra/Dharma must be considered as 100% real. It is never going to be mithya or relatively real or something likethat! Too much emphasis on the mithyAtva of jagat/Ishwara/ShAstra in vyavahAra itself would make one avaidhika/nAstika.

Opponents of sanAtana dharma are already using this mithyAtva itself as weapon against brAhmaNAs/sanAtanI Hindus.

  

I want to stress again that shAstra/Dharma is not mithya and that alone can lead one to either SB/NB.

  

Namaste,  
Suresh
