Hare Krishna



If you see a snake where there is rope, the shastra telling "This is a rope" or giving instructions "Go turn on the light, look closely, poke it with a stick ..." - all that is meaningful to you.



Ø Yes, that is true. When there is no snake at all and when everyone seeing only rope where is the question right cognition, light, poke with stick etc. !! There is something missing and to find that missing thing we need some means. As per Sri Sudhanshu prabhuji, there is something shaken, somewhere at some point of time, otherwise these questions of jeeva, avidya, bandha, mOksha etc. would have not arisen at all 😊



But if you already realize there is rope only, you don't need the shastra to tell you anything and its instructions are irrelevant to you.



- Hence veda is aveda in an enlightened state ( and deep sleep as well
  😊) All shAstra (vaidika) and loukika vyavahAra is within the realm
  of avidyA only.



They may even appear nonsensical or fanciful because they seem to imagine a problem that is nonexistent (in your awareness).



Ø Yes, it is something like dealing with the phantasm 😊 imagining there is jeeva, there is avidya, there is a shAstra to remove it, there is a shastra to realize it. But those who have already realized that there is ONLY rope not taken the problem on those who are seeing the snake, they know the gravity of the situation / plight of ‘snake’ seers and compassionately explained with all patience what exactly there.
Hence the jnAni-s even after realization did not look at scriptures are mere full of flowery imagination but as a valid pramANa to get rid of imagined snake.



Stating line 2 above as a fact does not mean line 1 is invalid or undermined for those who see snake and are suffering because of it. In the same way, however advaitins speak of NB/SB or jnana/moksha, that should not be construed as negating the relevance of shastra for the ignorant.



Ø Yes, that is what bhAshyakAra says in samanvayAdhikaraNa, all injunctions and all other means of knowledge end with the dawn of intuition ‘ahaM brahmAsmi’ pramANa-s cannot continue to hold water since after the dawn of knowledge there is neither pramAtru nor pramANa nor prameya or jnAtru, jneya, jnAna (neither knower nor objects to be known nor any means to know anything).
