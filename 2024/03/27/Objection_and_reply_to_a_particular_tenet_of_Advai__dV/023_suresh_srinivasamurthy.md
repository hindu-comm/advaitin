Namaskaram Sri Putran-ji,

  

ShAstra and ShAstra vihita anushTAna is for jagad hitArtha / dharmashthApana. Otherwise, it is impossible to explain avatAras of jnAnIs like Sri Shankara. The entire universe is Ishwara who is the vivarta of the Self - meaning mruthyu roopi Brahman. He is an embodiment of shAstra/dharma. Through every element He is teaching sanAtana dharma and we humans are supposed to be in tune with that. As long as we are in the world there is going to be shAstra and anushTAna. Even sanyasis have their duties.

  

Yes, I agree, in Kaivalya there is nothing other than the Self/Brahman. But we should not be mixing that in vyavahAra. When it comes to vyavahAra there is going to be shAstra and dharma. That is what I think Sri Shankara's intent as he explicitly teaches "Vedo nityamadheeyatam...."

  

  

Regards,  
Suresh
