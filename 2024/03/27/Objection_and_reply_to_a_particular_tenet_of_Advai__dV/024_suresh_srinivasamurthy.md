Namaskaram Vikram-ji,

  

\<This might be a verybasic question, but could you please humor me / indulge me by explaining what you mean by "attainment of NB (Nirguna Brahman)"? Where, when and how?\>

  

You are right! When it comes to NB there is only realization / svaroopa AvirbhAva. But for that also, there is need to "attain" tadpada vAchya Ishwara/Guru anugraha! which is possible only through performance of shAstra vihita Varnashrama dharma / nitya karma followed by Guru shushrUsha. Hope that clarifies.
