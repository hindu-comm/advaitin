Namaskaram Suresh-ji,

  

Sannyasis are not necessarily jivan-muktas. So I think that comment is misleading.

  

If Ishvara is the embodiment of Dharma and the entire universe is Ishvara the vivarta of Self, then there is no manifestation that is notHim (or His appearance) and no "adharmic" evolution in manifestation that deviates from Him (or His Order/Dharma).

  

When Consciousness/Ishvara identifies/projects self with the body-mind namarupa, that identification corresponds with vyavaharika duality of selves and objects and mutual causality. The duality of dharma vs adharma belongs to this standpoint. It is a relative reality, corresponding to ignorance of the non-duality of Self. Dharma leads the jiva towards knowledge of Brahman and adharma leads away; but neither changes the fact that you are Brahman always and Brahman is the All.

  

I really don't see why you are repeating that in vyavahara for us (Self identified as) human beings, shastra and dharma are real. No one says otherwise. We are also allowed to do satsang, vichara, sravana, mananam, on advaita tattva. If you are criticising that unfit people who should be focusing on pravritti marga are talking too much on nivritti and kaivalya - thus not really moving towards jnana in theirlives, well yes, that may warrant a discussion and people may entertain it so long as they don't feel the arguments forwarded undermine advaitatattva in order to make such a point.
