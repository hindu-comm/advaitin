Namaskaram Putran-ji,

  

Jeevanmuktas and nitya muktas like Sri Adi Shankara are embodiment of nivrutti dharma

which is the essence of shAstra. This is because they have completely merged themselves

with the tadpada vAchya, sarva vyApI Brahman/Ishwara. Whatever they do or not do would

be verily shAstra.

  

So IMHO, mithyAtva does not mean unreal or non-existence. It only means "separate existence"

while in reality jiva/Ishwara/shAstra exist in such a way that the Self alone is. I am sure you

definitely know that It is ahankAra and mamkAra that creates the false sense of separation and

possession.

  

MithyAchAra as "useless" can apply for kAmya karma or adhArmika/svArtha karma.

But that wont apply for nitya karma / nivrutti dharma which in essence is parArtha karma that

has jagat hita or Ishwara arpana as it's goal.

  

In the universal form of Ishwara, even adharma has it's place and that always helps

to glorify dharma. In the Vaishwadeva prayoga there is a ahuti for adharma also!

  

May be we are saying the same thing in different ways or I may be totally wrong from a

jnAnIs's point of view, but it helps ajnAnIs like me to have firm faith in the vedAdi shAstra.

  

Namaste

Suresh
