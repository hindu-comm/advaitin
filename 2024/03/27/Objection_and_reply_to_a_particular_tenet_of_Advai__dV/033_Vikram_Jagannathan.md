Namaskaram Shri Suresh ji,

  

Sashtanga pranam may be in order to Shri Purtan ji and Shri Subbu ji, but definitely not to me. All namaskarams are due to Lord Sriman Narayana.🙏🙏

  

Aligned with your thoughts and illustration.

  

Yes, just as how a pole vaulter initially supports the pole and delicately balances it on his hands (Shastra is to be delicately balanced on the intellect through shravana & manana); then uses the pole to support himself and completely depends on the pole to propel him higher (with a successful Shastra manana, with full confidence, faith and dependence in the teachings of the Shastra, guidance of the guru and grace of Isvara, the seeker ventures higher into the planes of nidhidhyasana); finally at the highest point of 180' turn, drops even the pole for a smooth effortless sail beyond the crossbar (at the peak of self-contemplation, there is a 180' turn of the mind inwards, at which point the object of the mind becomes chidabasa or conditioned Chaitanya itself, that point there is a drop of all empirical distinctions including the Shastra, guru and even Isvara, to effortless sail over the crossbar of vyavaharika into that which you already & always are).

  

Until that highest turning point, one should never simply drop the pole but hold on to it accurately and firmly. So is Shastra / guru / Isvara bhakti / upasana / dharma and karma anushtana. Premature drop and prolonged holding of the pole are both detrimental results.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
