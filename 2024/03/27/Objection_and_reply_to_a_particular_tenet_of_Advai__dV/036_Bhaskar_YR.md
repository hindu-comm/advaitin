Yes, for each and every namaskara the ultimate recipient is ‘nArAyaNa’ only. nArAyaNam namaskrutya naraM chaiva narOttamam…first bow to nArAyaNa and nara (arjuna in the context of that episode), so first and foremost namaskArams to nArAyaNa only and even if you do namaskAraMs to other deva-s, that also ultimately goes to HIM only. Sarvadeva namaskAraH keshavaM pratigacchati.
