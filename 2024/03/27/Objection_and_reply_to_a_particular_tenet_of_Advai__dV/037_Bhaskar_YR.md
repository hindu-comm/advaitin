Hare Krishna



On a second thought, I think we can defend the validity of VedAdi shAstra based on very mantra that negates everything including itself upon realization of the Truth. No other scripture has that kind of boldness to negate itself 🙂



Ø Because after realization shastra is not shAstra it is one with brahman only. The nityatva siddhi comes to shAstra because of this.
Reference vide taittireeya. When there is no bheda among pramAtru, pramANa and prameya, veda becomes aveda. It is not only in realization but in sushupti also coz. there is no duality there!!.



ShAstra is like well water and there is no need for that when there is water everywhere (as in BG 2-46)



Ø When everywhere water and only water well water also water without any without any boundaries (nAma rUpa vishesha) like well, lake, pond, river or sea water…sarvataH samplutOdakaM, apOvA edagaM sarvaM…veda mantra says this. And I don’t think there is a special mention required here about the water (aapaH) and the context of its usage😊
