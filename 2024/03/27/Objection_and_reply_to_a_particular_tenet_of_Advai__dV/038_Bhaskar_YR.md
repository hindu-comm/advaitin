Ok. What is the point? You know that advaitins distinguish mithya from non-existence, asat, unreal. They say mithya is an ontological category that is neither sat nor asat. (I mean many - not including Bhaskar-ji and co.)



Ø Even as per my understanding mithyA is something different from asat!! There is no qualms here between my company and other traditional flag bearers’ co. 😊 But contextual usage of these terms would definitely differ. When it comes to jagat / creation, yes it is asat as it is ever changing. And this asat was there in the very beginning in its mUla rUpa / prakruti. In this context of creation, asat is NOT atyanta abhAva like hare’s horn or vaNdhyA putra (son of a barren woman). It is an existing thing but changing constantly. Please refer shankara bhAshya on taitireeya (2.1.1) where shankara clarifies the meaning of both terms i.e. satya and asatya here he says : one’s svarUpa which once determined ‘will remain as such’ is satyaM and that which does not remain the same as once determined is asatyam. And this asatyaM is the product of satyaM only, satyaMchAnrutaM cha satyamabhavat. It is because of this status of asatyaM as mUla prakriti we say it is there at first, before creation : asadvA idaM agra Asit.
IF this asat in this shruti statement is something like atyanta abhAva then we will have to say from nothing, something came out 😊 As we know that is not our position. After realization rope (which is ever existent) is realized as rope only and during the bhrAnti kAla we see it as ‘snake’ only. There is no intermediatory period here for us to say : there is anirvachaneeya (sat or asat) snake was existent in the rope.
Hence I have been telling anirvachaneeyatvaM suitable only to kArya-kAraNa ananyatvaM ( i.e. mAya) not for the mithyA (bhrAnti), once it is defined as bhAranti there is no anirvachaneeyatvaM in it. The illustration used for this by bhAshyakAra is foam and water. The foam which is not quite the same as water BUT YET not a different entity either. So, in our company dictionary these terms have contextually different meanings. Though at times, elsewhere asat is used in place of mithya alternatively when it comes to the context of jagat / creation it is not mithyA but asat only. It is just like valley of difference between mAya and avidyA though avidyA is equated with mAya at some places.



If you say the Self alone is and still affirm jiva, shastra, Ishvara in distinct terms, then you are in vyavaharika standpoint that is the realm for words and thought, and where you think of Brahman at least in terms of a fundamental duality: Existence + Consciousness, Self + manifest appearance. This standpoint is mithya because thisdivide is known to be asat (inapplicable for consideration) in the paramarthika standpoint and yet it is the truth in vyavaharika.

Ø First of all we have to realize that brahma jnana is NOT vyavahAra abhAva jnana but IT IS vyavahARa badhita jnana. For vyavahAra abhAva we have to literally ‘smash’ the existing world or we have to wait till mahA pralaya. Seeing the bheda is not avidyA having the satyatva buddhi in bheda AkAra is avidyA clarifies bhAshyakAra somewhere. Bheda (changing) is quite natural to this asatya jagat but for this upAdAna and nimitta is that parabrahman only. sarvataH samplutodakasthAneeye samyagdarshane (geeta 3-19) bhAshya very relevant here I reckon. sarvaM khalvidaM brahma is the shruti vAkya. Devadatta is Devadatta only when he is folding his limbs or stretching the same. paramahamsa gives example of coiled snake and moving snake…



But, we are not debating about the condition of the ajnani. For the jnani, there is no *true* sense of separation either. That is the point.

  sarva bhUta (sarvabhUtAtmabhUtAtma) and his drushti in ekam no
  rAga-dvesha in him nor any katrutva-bhOktrutva in him. Hence other
  name for him is ‘samadarshi’. Vidya Vinaya sampanne brahmane gavi
  hastini….panditAH samadarshiNaH clarifies geetaachaarya in Geeta.



Whatever sense the ajnani thinks is real, for the jnani if at all it comes into awareness, it is sublated as mithya or only an appearance of Ishvara or Self - the separation lacks fundamental or substantial reality.



Ø Yes, hence his drushti is called paramArtha drushti consists of ONLY satyatva darshana not mithyA darshana…for him there is no anAtma vastu as he sees only Atman and nothing but Atman, just like goldsmity seeing gold only in different ornaments.
