praNAms Sri Suresh prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Jeevanmuktas and nitya muktas like Sri Adi Shankara are embodiment of nivrutti dharma

which is the essence of shAstra. This is because they have completely merged themselves

with the tadpada vAchya, sarva vyApI Brahman/Ishwara. Whatever they do or not do would

be verily shAstra.



Ø And bhAshyakAra elsewhere says though they (jnAni-s) are krutakrutya there is no shAstra vihita vidhi-nishedha rules applicable to them and they are not supposed to bound by it, they are not svecchaachaari ( they will not do acts as per their whims and fancies) they give utmost care to shAstra vihita vidhi-nishedha and never ever violate it. From this one more upasiddhAnta that arises is : veda nityatva prApti is acceptable coz. whatever dharma-karmAcharaNa is there
in that is valid and nityam for even paramArtha jnAni-s.
