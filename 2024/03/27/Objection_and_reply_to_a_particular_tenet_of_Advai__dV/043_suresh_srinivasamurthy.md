Namaskaram Vikram-ji,

  

Actually, there is jIva-jIva bheda (sajAtIya) and jIva-jada bheda (vijAtIya) and Atma/paramAtma or jIva/Ishwara bheda (swagata)in both Dvaita and V.Advaita. Only difference is that there is tAratamya among jivas in Dvaita and samAnatva in V.Advaita.

  

As you said Advaita can accommodate all the above in vyavahAra! IMHO extreme Bhakti / SarNagati lands us in Advaitic Brahman which is indescribable!

  

Actually, Brahman is accepted as the antaryAmi Atma in all the systems and Atma is both nAma-roopa Varjita and SarvanAma vAchya as it is present in all name/forms. IMHO this SarvAntaryAmi Ishwara who is the embodiment of dharma (as he sustains the body/jagat) is the connecting link for all whatever may be our world view.

  

Namaste,  
Suresh
