This talks of Abheda between Shiva and surya and speaks of the worship of Uma-maheshvara with names of Surya.

  

पद्मपुराणम्/खण्डः १ (सृष्टिखण्डम्)/अध्यायः २५

[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/ojc](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/ojc?fbclid=IwAR3xnYcE8M28ffVzxRyzW_2Ywzwhbg0tHIDqGB9uBdjz6I45kYEAYlx6zLE_aem_AaiWzeJZf-sXzkl0VreUq7RrAaiOvaki6ybjj8_5nbxqTwyuyUWEeK8xKuZEoduNQsFXldMqoB141hDTk-aCzIsX)

  
उमामहेश्वरस्यार्चामर्चयेत्सूर्यनामभिः  
सूर्यार्चां शिवलिङ्गे च प्रकुर्वन्पूजयेद्यतः ५  
उमापते रवेर्वापि न भेदो दृश्यते क्वचित्  
यस्मात्तस्मान्मुनिश्रेष्ठ गृहे शम्भुं समर्चयेत् ६  
  
~ मत्स्यपुराणम्/अध्यायः ५५  

  

Shivarchana should be done with Surya Nama-s. Suryacharana and Shivarchana should both be done. There is no difference between Shiva and Ravi. Suryacharchana should be done at home.  

  

Om
