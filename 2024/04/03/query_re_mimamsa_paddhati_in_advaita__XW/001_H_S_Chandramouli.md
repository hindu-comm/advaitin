Varnaka 2 of Vivarana Prameya Samgraha may be referred. It mainly deals with the topic covered by the Sutra \*\*अथातोब्रह्मजिज्ञासा\*\* (athAto brahmajij~nAsA) cannot be understood correctly through PUrva MImAmsA sUtrAs themselves, and needs to be understood through another set of sUtrAs, namely the Uttara MImAmsA sUtrAs.
