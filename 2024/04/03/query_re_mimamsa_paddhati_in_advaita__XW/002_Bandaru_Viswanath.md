Namaste Sakshi,

  

I am not sure I answer your question, since I am unsure of your purpose and background, but I attempt it anyway.

  

Srimad Appayya Dikshita'sParimala commentary on Brahma Sutra (Sutra-sankara bhashya-bhamati-kalpataru--parimala, in that sequence) is said to dwell significantly into Mimamsa and compare with the concepts as presented in (Advaita) Vedanta (Brahma) Sutras.

  

But I suspect you would need more understanding of Mimamsa before that, Perhaps "Artha Sangraha" or "Mimamsa Paribhasha" may be useful.  

  

May be just thorough reading of appropriate chapters of "Sarvadarshana Sangraha" would be sufficient for you.

  

Thanks

Vissu
