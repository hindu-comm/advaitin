Namaskaram,

  

This article[https://pragyata.com/catastrophic-kyotoisation-of-kashi/](https://pragyata.com/catastrophic-kyotoisation-of-kashi/) has made me sad and frustrated. A lone shankaracharya had opposed some of the destruction that was happening in the name of creating the kasi corridor. Other lay-people had complained to the PMO. But they were ignored. (Among other things, the article says the house of Mandan Mishra was destroyed in the clearing process.)

  

As I told the sender:

  

"That article unfolds like a tragedy. It is indeed sad. Yes \[the PM\] and his govt ought to be criticised and challenged for its decisions here ... When the Govt is in charge of decisions over temples instead of mathas, this is how it will turn out. The acharyas and brahmanas have to lead here and not kshatriyas and shudras. The logistics were no doubt complex and there will be compromises from both sides, but these officials should not be deciding but seeking approval and following instructions of gurus. It comes back to govt controlling temples imo.

  

I also see repeated the stark disunity among Hindus, that only one Shankaracharya was sounding the alarm and protesting as if alone."
