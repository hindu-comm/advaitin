> So, if someone is driving the mind-body, it has to be impure
> conciousness. Isn't it?

"pure consciousness" is the only reality, "one without a second". There is no second entity; rather it is pure consciousness identified as jiva and as jagat. At this "impure" level of variedidentifications of the same Reality, we can have a whole world of causality and relations. This world based on those identifications of "pure consciousness" is mithya - not absolutely unreal or absolutely real. So long as we are talking about this world, we are said to be in the vyavaharika standpoint and in this standpoint, there are two ways to refer back to pure Consciousness. Either this world is adhyasa/superimposition on That, including all divisions such as witness and witnessed; or this world is an appearance/projection of That due to Maya-shakti. Maya-identification also is anirvachaneeya/mithya, cannot be said to be sat or asat; though it is treated as the cause for the appearance, it itself has the status ofappearance on pure Consciousness, vyavaharika satya but asat in the paramarthika.
