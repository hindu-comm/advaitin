Namaskaram,

  
  

>   
> vyavaharika standpoint and in this standpoint, there are two ways to
> refer back to pure Consciousness. Either this world is
> adhyasa/superimposition on That, including all divisions such as
> witness and witnessed; or this world is an appearance/projection of
> That due to Maya-shakti.

  

  

Actually the Upanishads teach us the other common denotations of Brahman
-pure consciousness -

  

I (Aham Brahmasmi) 1st person

  

You (tat tvam asi) 2nd person

  

This (ayam atma Brahma) 3rd person

  

All (sarvam kalu idam Brahma)
