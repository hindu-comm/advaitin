praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Actually the Upanishads teach us the other common denotations of Brahman
-pure consciousness -



I (Aham Brahmasmi) 1st person



You (tat tvam asi) 2nd person



This (ayam atma Brahma) 3rd person



All (sarvam kalu idam Brahma)



Ø Yes, IT is I, you, this, that, All…if at all shruti talking about ‘something’ which is bereft of all denotations then it would have simply said I, you, This, That and All is mithya, tuccha don’t go by these wrong prefixes,but it is not saying so OTOH it is saying there exists brahman and nothing but brahman, na dviteeyOsti kaschit 😊 Yes IT is nirvishesha, niravayava, nirvikAra, nirguNa in its svarUpa there exists nothing apart from IT. karaNam, kAraNaM, karta, vikartO gahanO guhaH…nArAyaNa evedagaM sarvaM but at the same time HE is nishkalOniranjanO nirvikalpO nirAkhyAtshuddhO deva ekaH nArAyaNaH. If any one facing any problem with ‘sarvaM’ while defining ‘nirvishesha’ brahman, they should realize that brahman is NOT sarvaM but sarvAM does not exist independently apart from brahman!! 😊



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
