- I have checked it is correct!! 5^(th) Chapter 18^(th)
  verse…vidyAvinaya saMpanne brAhmaNe gavi hastini…paNditAH
  samadarshiNaH. Perhaps here also you may get the doubt like above
  about the word and usage of PaNdita…Please see shankara bhAshya for

  brAhmaNa, the cow, elephant, the dog, and the outcasted all equally

  proclaim that the world is non-existent and an illusion. These are

  jnAni. Taittireeya says ahaM annaM, annaada, shlokakarta means he is
  the food, he is the eater, he is the Ishwara. If the food, the eater,
  Ishwara, the Manu, the surya, the cow, the elephant, the outcaste
  etc. etc. are all non-existent there is no meaning for such
  sentences. For this reason too, the non-existence of the world is
  absurdly wrong and whenever shruti and smruti says jagat is bhrAnti
  Kalpita we have to understand it contextually without doing any hAni
  to shruti pramANita siddhAnta.
