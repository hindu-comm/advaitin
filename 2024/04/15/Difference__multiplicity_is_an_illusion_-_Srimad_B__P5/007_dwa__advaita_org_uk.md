Dear Bhaskar-ji,



I have re-read the section in BSB 1.1.4 from ‘atrAhuH dehAdivyatiriktasya to mithyaiva na gauNaH. It relates to the discussion as to whether the ‘I notion’ is mithyA or gauNa – false or figurative; and talks about describing a man as ‘a lion’ versus a real lion etc. I don’t actually see how this relates to whether or not the world disappears.



The link I gave is to my WordPress site. Ramesam posted a question relating to the ‘tipping point’ in Advaita and a brief discussion ensued, principally between Venkat and myself. (It is not a discussion group.) I can email this to you if you are unable to access.



You say: “In this bhAshya, bhAshyakAra is explicitly clear that jnAni’s internal organs (antaHkaraNa) functions normally even after samyak jnAna from this it is quite evident that jnAni-s would definitely continue to see the jagat…” Which bhAShya are you referring to now?



Regarding BG 5.18 – Yes! I had previously overlooked this as not relevant. I have now added the following additional reference to the section on ‘Enlightened man still sees the world’ in the second volume of my ‘Confusions in Advaita’ series:



“Finally, this śloka speaks about the enlightened man looking equally upon “*a Brāhma**ṇa endowed with learning and humility, a cow, an elephant and even a dog as well as an eater of dog’s meat.*” (Ref. 6) Clearly, the fact that there is still some discrimination here means that he still must see the world in order to know that it is Brahman!”



So thank you for that – most useful!



Best wishes,

Dennis
