In this bhAshya, bhAshyakAra is explicitly clear that jnAni’s internal organs (antaHkaraNa) functions normally even after samyak jnAna from this it is quite evident that jnAni-s would definitely continue to see the jagat and vrutti-s are definitely going to happen (please give special attention to this statement : samyakjnAnaprAptAvapi avashyaM bhaavini) ‘even after’ ultimate realization it is definitely going to happen).

Earlier today in my post to Sri Putran prabhuji in another thread:



Mundaka bhAshya 2.2.11 (sorry it is not 2.3.11 as I quoted yesterday to Sri Dennis prabhuji, it is 2.2.11) clarifies : the jagat in front of us appears as something other than brahman or separate from brahman for those who have avidyA. This appearance of jagat (separate from its substratum/adhishtAna) as fragmented / limited / independent etc. is abrahma pratyaya (not brahman perception) which is avidyAkalpita like snake on the rope. But jagat in front of us is not that. It_is_brahman alone. It is because of this reason jagat will not be lost after the knowledge of advaitam. In bruhat bhAshya (1.4.10) bhashyakAra says : the world is not lost by brahma vidyA coz. jnAna does not have the capacity to create anything new nor destroy anything existing. jnApakaM hi shAstraM na tu kArakaM. But jnAma helps us to eradicate the wrong notions about jagat conceived by the one who has avidyA.
