नमोनमः

  

Advaita Vedanta (AV henceforth) calls ब्रह्मन् as चिन्मात्र (pure consciousness) that means conciousness without object. It never makes sense to me because conciousness has no meaning if there's no object to be conscious about. So pure consciousness is like आकाशकुशेशयं (lotus in the sky) or वन्ध्यापुत्र (barren woman's child).

  

Perhaps this description of ब्रह्मन् as चिन्मात्र which is meaningless for the reason stated above, is there just to remove duality from our mind. If that's the case, can we call ब्रह्मन् as जडमात्र too, because afterall ब्रह्मन् is equally present in जड तत्त्व as it is in चेतन तत्त्व and both are अन्योन्यसिद्ध (interdependent for their existence)?
