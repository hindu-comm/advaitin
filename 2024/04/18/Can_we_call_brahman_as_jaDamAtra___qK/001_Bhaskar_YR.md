Hare Krishna



anAtma vastu has been defined to contrast it from Shuddha Chaitanya…And this Chaitanya is ultimately nirvikAri, nirvishesha and more importantly the secondless truth (ekaM-eva adviteeya satyaM) whether anAtma vastu is there or not…there is no absence for this Shuddha Chaitanya tattva. If that is not the case and if we prompt to say Shuddha Chaitanya is like vandyA putra or AkAsha kusuma then we are heading towards bauddhas’ shUnyavAda 😊 For that matter, in reality there can not be a thing that can be called as anAtma vastu coz. there exists nothing apart from THAT (Shuddha Chaitanya).



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
