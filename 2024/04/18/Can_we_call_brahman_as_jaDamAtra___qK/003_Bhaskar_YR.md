Why does the jnani see all as Ishvara the Self? Because, the jnani realizes the absolute non-duality of Space in the substratum standpoint (beyond the reach of words and mind); therefore he has realized that the division in the context of mind and perception is apparent (mithya) and a superimposition on "pure Consciousness". He therefore "sees" that Self in all things (including his own witness-self) and all in the Self (Ishvara).



Ø Yes I completely agree with this. The multifarious nature of jagat and its perception and satyatva buddhi in it are ONLY in the context of conditioned mind. The truth is not that and the truth is that there exists nothing but THAT. Mundaka bhAshya 2.2.11 (sorry it is not 2.3.11 as I quoted yesterday to Sri Dennis prabhuji, it is 2.2.11) clarifies : the jagat in front of us appears as something other than brahman or separate from brahman for those who have avidyA. This appearance of jagat (separate from its substratum/adhishtAna) as fragmented / limited / independent etc. is abrahma pratyaya (not brahman perception) which is avidyAkalpita like snake on the rope. But jagat in front of us is not that. It_is_brahman alone. It is because of this reason jagat will not be lost after the knowledge of advaitam.
In bruhat bhAshya (1.4.10) bhashyakAra says : the world is not lost by brahma vidyA coz. jnAna does not have the capacity to create anything new nor destroy anything existing. jnApakaM hi shAstraM na tu kArakaM.
But jnAma helps us to eradicate the wrong notions about jagat conceived by the one who has avidyA.
