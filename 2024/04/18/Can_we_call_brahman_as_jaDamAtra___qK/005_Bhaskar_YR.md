3\) You say that Brahman is beyond comprehension because its comprehension requires theinert. Your description was that Brahman and the inert are anyonyasiddha, interdependent. Do you mean that the existence of Brahman requires the existence of inert objects? Or do you mean the comprehension of Brahman requires the existence of inert objects?



praNAms Sri Venkat prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Perhaps it can be understood like this : since parabrahma tattva cannot be known by speech, mind and eyes or by any other sense, it has to be known through Agama pramANa only and Agama also cannot pinpoint this paramArtha tattva as such and such!! So though it is Aprameya (beyond the reach of any pramANa) it does exist and it has to be known as the cause of the world!! Clarifies bhAshyakAra in katha shruti : naiva vAcha na manasa na chakshusha anyaiH api indriyaiH prAptuM shakyate ityarthaH…tathApi ‘sarva vishesha rahitaH api jagataH mUlaM iti avagatatvAt asti eva (brahma) Kata bhAshya 2.3.12. So starting point to know the unknowable is through the world only. Therefore brahma jignAsa starts with ‘creation’ janmAdasya yatha. Somewhere else bhAshyakAra affirms that if Ishwara does not created nAma rUpa we would have not known that brahman is prajnAna ghana.
