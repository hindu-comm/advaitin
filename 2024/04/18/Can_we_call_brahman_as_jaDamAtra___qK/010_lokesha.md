\> ...is your conclusion that you do not exist in deep sleep?

  

Depends on what you identify as "you". If you identify the person देवदत्त as a collection of impressions, then देवदत्त exists during deep sleep because those impressions give rise to an almost similar person that you identify as देवदत्त on the following day. If you identify the person देवदत्त as consciousness, then देवदत्त doesn't exist during deep sleep because there's nothing to be conscious about.

  

\> Theonly claim I'm making in this email is that to know that there is "no thing", there has to be something that knows it. If you don't agree, perhaps you can explain why.

  

"To know that there is \*no thing\*" means "absence of knowing anything". Therefore, there's no requirement for a knower here. So, I do not agree that there has to be someone that knows it.
