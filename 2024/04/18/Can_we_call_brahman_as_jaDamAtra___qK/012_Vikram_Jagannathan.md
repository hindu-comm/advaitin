Namaskaram Shri Lokesh ji,  
  
Adding on to what our other erudite members have said, I would like to call out that in Advaita there is a clear distinction between pure Consciousness (pure jnana or Chaitanya) versus objective consciousness (vritti jnana or 'consciousness of').  
  

While the vritti jnana or 'chit' stands in opposition to jada or 'achit', Chaitanya is neither opposed to the vritti jnana nor jada.  
  
There are several references in our scriptures that state that both chit (vritti jnana) and achit (jada) are only aspects of one non-dual Chaitanya. This Chaitanya or Chinmatra, as the Truth, appears as both the Truth (chit / vritti jnana) and untruth (jada).  
  

In other words, there is indeed a distinction between the pramata, pramana and prameya; and furthermore agreed that there is no pramana in the absence of either prameya and pramata. But all these triputi are manifestations of the one pure Chaitanya or Chinmatra alone.  
  
It is this Chaitanya / Chinmatra, which is the Sat / Satyam, pure Chit / Jnanam, Anantam, Anandam, Brahman. Hence Brahman is truly Chinmatra, and is neither just chit (vritti jnana-matra) nor just achit (jada-matra).  
  
prostrations,  
Vikram
