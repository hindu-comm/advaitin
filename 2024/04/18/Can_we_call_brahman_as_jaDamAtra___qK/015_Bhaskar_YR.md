Since Sri Venkat prabhuji, one of the authorities of traditional Advaita in this group, handling this issue so, I don’t think I can add anything special and specific to his clarification. But would like to share my thoughts with regard to your observation. First and foremost is, in Advaita Vedanta, whenever the tattva is named as such and such a thing/entity, it should be never forgotten that the reality what Advaita / shruti talking is free from all attributes and just for pedagogical
purpose it is presented as endowed with any particular attribute, it is only intended to negate some other property commonly imputed to it by the human mind, but never implies that the particular attribute employed for the purpose of teaching and it is not meant for really characterizes Reality AS IT IS. It is just a method of adhyArOpa and apavAda. In fact, we had a discussion with regard to why the tattva (parabrahman) can never be positively described and why ‘neti, neti’ is the best possible way to define it. In that sense brahman since he is without any genus cannot be expressed by even the word like ‘being’ though we often tell brahman is secondless being (ekamevAdviteeyam). If you have faith / shraddha in shruti and Acharya’s words you can refer mAndUkya mantra 7 and its commentary.
