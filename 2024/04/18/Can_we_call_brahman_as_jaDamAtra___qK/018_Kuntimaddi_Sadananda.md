PraNAms

  

In the Tai. Up. Brahman is indicated by Satyam, jnaanam, anantam brahma. where jnaanam stands for pure consciousness - the above definition is parallel to Sat-Chit-Ananda. Hence consciousness is indicated as infinite - anantam.

  

The Mind becomes a local conscious entity by reflecting (as though) the all-pervading consciousness just like the moon becomes a local luminous entity by reflecting sunlight. (the moon is considered as presiding deity of the mind).

The object-consciousness arises when the vrutti formed in the mind when an object is perceived by the senses. The vrutti is like a disturbance in the mind and when it rises the all-pervading consciousness that illumines the vrutti gets reflected and the reflected consciousness forms the knowledge of the vrutti -These aspects are analyzed in detail in Vedanta Paribhasha. Book on the pratyaksha pramaana 'How Do I Know' that provides the critical analysis of the Vedanta Parisbhasha just got printed, thanks to the Indic Academy, and now available online on Amazon etc. under Dr. K. Sadananda, for those interested.

  

Hence all-pervading consciousness, Jnaanam, and how the local mind appears as a local conscious entity and and also how objective knowledge arises - are all discussed in that book.

  

My 2C.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
