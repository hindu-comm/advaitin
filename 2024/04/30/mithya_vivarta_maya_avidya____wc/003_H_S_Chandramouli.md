Namaste Putran Ji,

Reg // Are we to understand avidya here as an ajnani's ajnana, that is absent in both a jnani and in Ishvara? //,

Notice the translation. \*\* **That Sat \*\* ……\*\* That is the root of the universe \*\*.** What is it composed of? I am citing from BUB 4-1-4 where it is explicitly elaborated

// नैष दोषः — परस्याप्यात्मनोऽव्याकृतजगदात्मत्वेन विवक्षितत्वात्। आक्षिप्तनियन्तृकर्तृक्रियानिमित्तं हि जगदव्याकृतं व्याक्रियतेत्यवोचाम। इदंशब्दसामानाधिकरण्याच्च अव्याकृतशब्दस्य। यथेदं जगन्नियन्त्राद्यनेककारकनिमित्तादिविशेषवद्व्याकृतम् , तथा अपरित्यक्तान्यतमविशेषवदेव तदव्याकृतम्। व्याकृताव्याकृतमात्रं तु विशेषः। //

// naiSha doShaH — parasyApyAtmano.avyAkRRitajagadAtmatvena vivakShitatvAt \| AkShiptaniyantRRikartRRikriyAnimittaM hi jagadavyAkRRitaM vyAkriyatetyavochAma \| idaMshabdasAmAnAdhikaraNyAchcha avyAkRRitashabdasya \| yathedaM jaganniyantrAdyanekakArakanimittAdivisheShavadvyAkRRitam , tathA aparityaktAnyatamavisheShavadeva tadavyAkRRitam \|
vyAkRRitAvyAkRRitamAtraM tu visheShaH \| //

Translation (Swami Madhavananda) // There is nothing wrong in it, for really the Supreme Self was meant as being identical with the undifferentiated universe. We have already said that that universe was necessarily manifested with the help of the controller, the agent and the operation (of the means). This is also borne out by the fact that the word \*undifferentiated\* has been used co-ordinatively with \*this\*. Just as this differentiated universe has several distinguishing features like the controller and other factors, which serve as its causes, similarly that undifferentiated universe also must not be without a single one of these distinguishing features. The only difference between them is that the one is differentiated and the other is not //.

I am adding below some FootNotes by Sri SSS on this part of the Bhashya
which makes the above translation clearer.

// 1. ಪರಮಾತ್ಮನನ್ನು ಸೇರಿಸಿಕೊಂಡೇ ಅವ್ಯಾಕೃತವೆಂಬ ಶಬ್ದವನ್ನು ಪ್ರಯೋಗಿಸಿರುತ್ತದೆ. ಪರಮಾತ್ಮನನ್ನು ಬಿಟ್ಟಲ್ಲ ಎಂದರ್ಥ. ಇದನ್ನು ಮುಂದಿನ ವಾಕ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಸ್ಪಷ್ಟಪಡಿಸಿದೆ.

2. ಇದು ಎಂದರೆ ನಿಯಂತೃವಾದ ಈಶ್ವರ, ನಿಯಮ್ಯರಾದ ಜೀವರು, ಅಚೇತನವಾದ ನಾಮರೂಪಗಳು- ಎಲ್ಲವೂ ಸೇರಿರುವ ಜಗತ್ತು ಇದೇ ಅವ್ಯಾಕೃತವೆಂದು ಸಾಮಾನಾಧಿ ಕರಣ್ಯದಿಂದ ಹೇಳಿರುತ್ತದೆ. //

Translation (Mine) // 1. The word अव्याकृत (avyAkRRita) has been used to include ParamAtman also. Meaning thereby that ParamAtman has not been excluded. This is made clear in the next sentence.

2. \*इदम् (idam)\* (Added by me) \*This\* means Iswara the Controller, jIvAs who are the controlled, All inert (अचेतन achetana) nAmarUpAs – All inclusive Jagat. This is declared as अव्याकृत (avyAkRRita) by resorting to सामानाधिकरण्य (sAmAnAdhikaraNya) //.

Hence it would not be appropriate to // understand avidya here as an ajnani's ajnana, that is absent in both a jnani and in Ishvara //.
