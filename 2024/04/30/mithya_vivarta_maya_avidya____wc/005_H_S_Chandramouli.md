Namaste Putran Ji,

Reg // Belief in that duality being real is ajnana //,

Duality is not about // it is the same Mother in all such forms //. It is that // All such forms are nondifferent from \*I\* //. That is the result or phala of Realization. That leads to unalloyed supreme Ananda.

\*ajnAna\* is veiling of the truth that everything is \*I\*, leading to cognizing it differently and getting attached to it. Naturally this is a very brief statement. But it is the crux of Advaita SidhAnta. And I hope is sufficient for the present purpose.

Regards
