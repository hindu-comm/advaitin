Namaskaram Vikram-ji,

  

Your writings as always have both breadth and depth, and a coherent analysis. How are we to understand this "neither sat nor asat" definition of mithya, and why is jagat referred to as mithya (I think) by this definition? As you know, the word itself seems wrong for some in our group, at least some of these definitions; since you seem ok with the term, you can perhaps give your present understandings on this term and that will be food for further contemplation for members including myself.
