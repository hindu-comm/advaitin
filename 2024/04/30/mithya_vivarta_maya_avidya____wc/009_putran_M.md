A reasonable point of debate here is whether the jnani's awareness should be stated as shifting from one standpoint of knowledge to another, or whether that too is adhyasa of an ajnani only, or whether the jnani, when identified, already is in vyavaharika though having paramartha jnana.

  

What we see however is that the jnani, when identified by a BMI, knows the world of namarupas and relates accordingly, knows the condition of ajnani and teaches us on Brahman. And the standpoint (jagat) corresponding to this type of differentiating knowledge is vyavaharika, which therefore that same jnani will teach to be mithya.
