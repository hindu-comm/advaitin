You said: "Based on certain scriptural statements and verbal testimonies of masters like Bhagavan Shri Ramana Maharishi, I believe there is no more any sense of plurality whatsoever. **But then, in that sense, Jagat is no more mithya but purely asat!** In my current state, I am happy to believe and trust that a jnani perceives the mithya jagat and guides the world (including myself) as a jagadguru."

  

Later on, you mention the two types of sublation of mithya, and in one of them, the cause of misperception is also removed and hence no more misperception. Like snake on rope.

  

"8. With the dawn of true knowledge, the misunderstanding is completely removed.

9\. It is observed that sometimes the cause of mis-perception is also removed  
10. With this, there is no more perception of the object as Object-B."

  

By your earlier comment (in bold above), Object-B then would be asat and not mithya.

  

Perhaps your personal resolution for this is what you say later: "My personal concise understanding is that a mithya entity was indeed experienced as true / valid earlier; hence cannot be asat. At the same time, it has been rendered false with the current knowledge; hence cannot be sat. Consequently, as long as I continue to perceive / **only remember the earlier experience, that object is categorized as mithya."**

  

This needs clarification since we don't want mithya to be confused with asat. Or we can say the problem is open-ended and people may take either viewpoint. Maybe the mithya definition 1 is strict about this and will not admit the snake to be mithya if it vanishes after knowledge of rope - even if remembered, this definition may consider the snake to be a case of past ajnana of rope, and not mithya per se (?).
