I would defer to Subbu-ji's recent post on "Brahman is beyond name, genus etc." Words turn back from Brahman because words affirm duality. The problem is we still have to indicate the higher truth from our standpoint in the realm of words. That is a pointer; if taken literally the words will come up short or will present the truth correctly (in our standpoint) as Ishvara. That's why it is not an issue that the Upanishads (and bhashya) seem to express advaita tattva in seemingly different ways at different places: that is actually a good thing because the words are only pointers to the truth beyond words and cannot literally land us there.
