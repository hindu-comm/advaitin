Namaskaram Putran Ji,

Care needs to be exercised when understanding terms like अव्याकृत(avyAkRRita), अविद्या(avidyA) etc entirely through translation. They need to be understood both in their पारिभााषिक (pAribhAAShika) sense as well as grammar sense. Usually in translation Capital letter is used for the first alphabet when पारिभााषिक (pAribhAAShika) is meant while it is not so when grammar sense is involved. For example \*\*Undifferentiated\*\* and \*\*undifferentiated\*\* for अव्याकृत(avyAkRRita). First one is as per पारिभााषिक (pAribhAAShika) sense. The meaning is unique to Upanishadic usage. You could perhaps consider it as usage in the noun sense. But complications could arise when this is overlooked in many cases. Especially the term \*\*Ignorance\*\* and \*\*ignorance\*\*.

Use of अव्याकृत(avyAkRRita) in BUB 1-4-7 is very interesting. Bhashya observes

// तदेवंभूतं जगत् अव्याकृतं सत्नामरूपाभ्यामेव नाम्ना रूपेणैव च, व्याक्रियत ।
व्याक्रियतेति कर्मकर्तृप्रयोगात्तत्स्वयमेवात्मैव व्याक्रियत — वि आ अक्रियत — विस्पष्टं नामरूपविशेषावधारणमर्यादं व्यक्तीभावमापद्यत — सामर्थ्यादाक्षिप्तनियन्तृकर्तृसाधनक्रियानिमित्तम्। //

// tadevaMbhUtaM jagat avyAkRRitaM sat nAmarUpAbhyAmeva nAmnA rUpeNaiva cha, vyAkriyata \| vyAkriyateti karmakartRRiprayogAttatsvayamevAtmaiva vyAkriyata — vi A akriyata — vispaShTaM nAmarUpavisheShAvadhAraNamaryAdaM vyaktIbhAvamApadyata — sAmarthyAdAkShiptaniyantRRikartRRisAdhanakriyAnimittam \| //

Translation (Swami Madhavananda) // It, this sort of universe, having been undifferentiated , differentiated into name and form . The neuter-passive form of the verb indicates that it differentiated of itself, that is, manifested itself till it could be clearly perceived in terms of name and form. (Since no effect can be produced without a cause) it is implied that this manifestation took place with the help of the usual auxiliaries, viz., the controller, the agent and the operation of the means //.

अव्याकृत(avyAkRRita) and various names and forms of Jagat are akin to gold and ornaments. Just as ornaments do not lose their svarUpa of being gold, the various names and forms of Jagat do not lose their svarUpa of being अव्याकृत(avyAkRRita).
