Namaskaram Putran Ji,

Reg // If I say "It is the same Gold in ring-form, bangle-form, necklace-form", that is essentially stating "ornaments \[gold identified distinctly according to form-appearance\] do not lose their svarupa of being gold". You had however objected when I said "it is the same Mother in all such forms". I don't see the point of such scrutiny on that sentence when you are comfortable with this gold analogy //,

What I have stated is the Upanishadic concept of how the diverse Creation of inert objects, both Gross and Subtle, evolves through transformation (pariNAma) from a single material source (upAdAna kAraNa) which itself does not lose its svarUpa in the process. Other DarshanAs like nyAya, sAnkhya etc have an entirely different view. The analogy is not mine. It is that of the Upanishad.

// वाचारम्भणं विकारो नामधेयं मृत्तिकेत्येव सत्यम् //

// vAchArambhaNaM vikAro nAmadheyaM mRRittiketyeva satyam //.

If you feel offended that my observation on your statement amounts to undue scrutiny, my apologies for the same. It was not my intention.

Regards
