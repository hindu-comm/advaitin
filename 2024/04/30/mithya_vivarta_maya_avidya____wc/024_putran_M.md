**When** you talk about some Thinggoingfrom one "causal" state (undifferentiated) to another (differentiated) **and** you use words like "manifest" for that Thing, then you imply the capacity in that Thing to do that manifestation (or to cognize variously). This capacity is the shakti of that Thing (of unchanging svarupa) through which It knows and manifests/projects/appears/cognizesin such a differentiated manner.

  

Now, this Thing by above definition I call Ishvara and I understand as synonymous with the avyakkritain the BUB.

  

This Ishvara or Avyakkritais the Thing (Shiva) that has thecapacity (shakti) to appear as if dual, by projecting/cognizing namarupa, pot-spaces etc. Therefore a suitable formula is Shiva+shakti.

  

Now the second presentation is from the ChUp. There, two terms (nouns) are mentioned explicitly: Sat and avidya. Sat=Shiva is the root of the namarupa universe that avidya = Shakti superimposes (by way of namarupa) the appearance onto It.

  

This description up to here is Shiva+Shakti. There is a duality involving two Things, one of which is the root (the Screen) of the universe (movie) and the other is the projector onto that Screen.

  

But then, where is that Projector? In the movie! Sat is the root of the "Avidya" that apparently manifests "itself" as the apparent universe.

  

Which means, the only real Thing is Sat and avidya can instead be known as the limiting adjunct (maya-shakti) that has to be associated with Sat in the context of the perceived universe of "many things". We have come back to Ishvara.

  

Thus "universe" -\> Shiva+Shakti \<-\> Shiva+shakti .... Shiva.
