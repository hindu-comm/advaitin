mAyA shakti as upAdhi to the Sat is acceptable in the context of the perceived world but ‘avidyA’ as upAdhi to ‘sat’ is something weird.
Because the mAyA-shakti which is the cause to see many things /forms is indeed the nature of Sat. bhAshyakAra somewhere observes that even the nAma rUpa (kArya) are of the nature of the cause (kAraNa) because there cannot be an effect coming into existence it it is not of the nature of the cause. It is in this context of creation (srushti, sthiti, pralaya) only bhAshyakAra says like kAraNa (cause) kArya (effect) too trikAla abAdhita. If we are treating this mAyAshakti as a synonym to tuccha, neecha avidyA then we are forced to say avidyA is brahmAnanya and will always be there like brahma (sat). IOW, we are giving the nityatva (eternity) to avidyA 😊
