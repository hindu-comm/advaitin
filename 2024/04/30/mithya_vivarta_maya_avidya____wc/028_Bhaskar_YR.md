  The mother (taayi, amma in Kannada) is mAya / shakti ( I hardly care
  whether it is ‘s’ or ‘S’ and related debates) and she is in all
  forms. My praNAms to you for expressing this. yA devi sarvabhuteshu

  avyAkruta mAya (prior to creation) and the vyAkruta jagat (after
  creation) which is the source of our mithyA drushti about the existing
  mAtA mAyAshakti. The shruti warns in this regard that if anyone sees
  the world in front of his as non-Atman, then the world would reject
  and throw out such an idiot one as ineligible for mOksha /
  emancipation. mAyAshakti = prakruti=mother=nitya.
