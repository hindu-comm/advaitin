Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

> shakti is no different from shakta, the projector the screen and the
> movie etc. are all just ‘vishesha’ darshana of that ‘nirvishesha’
> shakta. The light in itself is colourless though it contains ‘all
> colours’. Though brahman is ‘nirvikAri’ in its swarUpa it is the
> source of all vikAra-s. IOW, brahman is nirvikAri because it is the
> source of ALL vikAra.



> mAyA shakti as upAdhi to the Sat is acceptable in the context of the
> perceived world but ‘avidyA’ as upAdhi to ‘sat’ is something weird.
> Because the mAyA-shakti which is the cause to see many things /forms
> is indeed the nature of Sat. bhAshyakAra somewhere observes that even
> the nAma rUpa (kArya) are of the nature of the cause (kAraNa) because
> there cannot be an effect coming into existence it it is not of the
> nature of the cause. It is in this context of creation (srushti,
> sthiti, pralaya) only bhAshyakAra says like kAraNa (cause) kArya
> (effect) too trikAla abAdhita. If we are treating this mAyAshakti as
> a synonym to tuccha, neecha avidyA then we are forced to say avidyA is
> brahmAnanya and will always be there like brahma (sat). IOW, we are
> giving the nityatva (eternity) to avidyA 😊

  

I had quoted from the Chandogya bhashya earlier:

  

Chandogya Upanishad VI.8.4

  

Shankaracharya bhashya: "That Sat on which are superimposed due to ignorance \[*avidya*\], all these transformations that have

speech only as the basis, and indeed, are unreal like the appearance ofsnake etc. on a rope -, That is the root of the universe."

  

Ch Up. VI.2.3

  

Bhashya: “ Butjust as the word and idea of a snake cease for one who has the discriminating knowledge about the rope, and as the words and ideas of pot etc. **cease** for one who has the discriminating knowledge about the earth,similarly words and ideas with regardto all other transformation cease for those people who have the discriminating knowledge about Existence.**”**



Specifically for your or the SSS school's interpretation of these passages: According to you, is the avidya being mentioned strictly that of the ajnani and the transformations being mentioned notwhat constitutes*visesha*darshana in general (that jnanis also have?) but visesha-visesha darshana in the ajnanis alone - which will *literally* cease on attaining discriminating knowledge of Existence?
