Namaskaram Shri Putran ji,

  

Apologiesfor the delayed participation in this discussion. While the reflections of the quoted bhashya from BU-1.4.7 can go deeper, perhaps in another thread, I would like to summarily say that both perspectives - 1) the continued perception of the world for a jnani after the dawn of realization, and 2) there is no more any perception of anything for the jnani after the dawn of realization - are equally valid, explainable and reconcilable in our Advaita sampradhayam.

  

The first perspective is valid from the standpointof other ajnanis alone. The erstwhile individualized BMI-V that was identified with the jnani (or as the jnani) continuesto operate under the influence of karma (or sankalpa in the case of avatara) and is perceived by the others. This perception of a functioning BMI-V is by the other ajnanis who identifywith their respective individualized functioning BMI-Vs. In this standpoint, the jnani's(as the individual) Chaitanya acts as a mere witness of the jnani's(again, the individual) functioning BMI-V. Like a burnt rope or a roasted seed there appears a continuation of the previous experiences and actions, but this is only an appearance and ineffective to the jnani Themselves. This is vyavaharika.

  

The second perspective is from the standpoint of a jnani Themselves. With the dawn of realization, the erstwhile jiva (result of adhyasa between avidya-upahita-chaitanya and antahkarana) realizes Itself to be identical and non-different from its true nature defined as Brahman. This realization of Brahman is the pure Chaitanya and not the avidya-upahita-chaitanya. Consequently, with the complete non-existence of avidya, from pure Chaitanya's perspective, there is no more any aspects of avidya such as time or karma or vasanas or memories or individuality or BMI-V or differentiation whatsoever. There is no more any concept of bondage or liberation or states prior to realization or after realization. It is just pure Brahman. This is paramarthika.

  

While a jnani revels in this paramarthika state, simultaneously from the perspective of the others in vyavaharika state the jnani's erstwhile BMI-V continues to operate and interact, under the influence of prarabdha / sankalpa, with the world as before. There is no conflict for the jnani at all. This explains the various paradoxical statements of realized masters such as BhagavanRamana Maharshi or even the interaction of Bhagavan Swami Sankaracharya with respect to the final rites of His revered mother.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
