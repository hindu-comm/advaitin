Namaskaram Vikram-ji,

  

I would defer to others to give alternate views on this; but I don't have any particular disagreement with what you are indicating. The framework in which the discussion is happening is vyavaharika, and conducted by us ajnanis who identify self with BMI and see the 'jnani' as one with the Consciousness reflected in another particular BMI. But, we recognize that the Consciousness (as reflected in the jnani) manifests a different awareness than our own, one in which there is no differentiation of self and not-self, of self and world, for all the namarupas that seem to exist independently for us as distinct selves reduces in that jnana-awareness to the unitary Self. Where we see ring and bangle, he sees Gold and Gold. The only question is whether he sees Gold (appearing) in ring shape vs bangle shape, or whether the "Gold" is the absolute Ground that precludes any sort of auxiliary shape differentiation (including seer-seeing-seen). The latter would be the pure Chaitanya perspective, the standpoint of jnana itself. Some will say this can be identified with the jnani's perspective as well, and others (ajnanis in your mail) may contend that the jnani'sperspective is of the former kind.
