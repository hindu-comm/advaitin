At the end of his gloss on Shankara's Gita bhashya, Sri Anandagiri pens a few verses:  

  

गीताशास्त्रमहार्णवोत्थममृतंवैकुण्ठकण्ठोद्भवं

श्रीकण्ठापरनामवन्मुनिकृतं निष्ठाद्वयद्योतितम् ।  

  

The Gita shastra nectar was spoken out by the Lord Vishnu/Krishna and was composed by one who is also called 'Srikantha'. It looks like Veda Vyasa is known by this name too.

  

Is there any evidence for this? Or does the second line mean something else?
