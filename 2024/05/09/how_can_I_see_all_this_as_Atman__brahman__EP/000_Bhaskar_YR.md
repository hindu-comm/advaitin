Hare Krishna



bhagavatpAda directly answers the subject question in bruhadAraNyaka (2.4.7) : The question is : kathaM punaridAneemidaM sarvam Atmaiva iti graheetum shakyate?? The answer is : chinmAtrAnugamAt sarvatra chitsvarUpamatveti gamyate.



Rough translation : this can be known by knowing / realizing the chinmAtra which is all encompassing one i.e. including ahaM pratyaya, sAkshi chaitanya as well as the perceived world (sarvatra). Sri SSS brings-in an analogy to explain this : This is like realizing the ‘svarUpa’ of waves, foam, bubble as water and water alone nothing else (sarvataH samplutOdakaM).



If you don’t realize the Atman like this then shruti warns :



//quote//



The brAhmaNa ousts or rejects or throws out the man as ineligible for mOksha who knows him to be different from the Self…like this shruti gives the examples of the gods, the beings, the worlds (lOka-s), the kshatriya etc. And it declares that the supreme self is the self of all. And there is nothing apart from the Atman. If there were, it would not be known, but there is no such thing ; the Self is everything. Since this ‘being’ the SAME in everything, after realizing this, everything would be known.



//quote//



karaNaM, kAraNaM, karta, vikartO gahanO guhaH says vishNu Sahasra nAma, ahamannaM, annAdaH, shlOkakarta says another shruti.
