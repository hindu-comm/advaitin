Global Festival of Oneness (GFO2024)  
  
Join us from May 12 to June 11 for a month-long online celebration honoring  
Jagadguru Adi Shankara Bhagavadpadacharya's teachings and legacy.  
  
His Holiness Jagadguru Shankaracharya \*Sri Sri Vidushekhara Bharati  
Sannidhanam\*, Dakshinamnaya Sri Sharada Peetham, Sringeri, will bless the  
event with his Anugraha Sandesham

[https://indica.events/event/global-festival-of-oneness-gfo2024/](https://indica.events/event/global-festival-of-oneness-gfo2024/)
