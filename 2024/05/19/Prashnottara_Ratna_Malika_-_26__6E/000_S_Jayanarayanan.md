Q143. कः शूरो?  
Who is a real hero?  
A143. यो भीतत्राता \|  
He who protects those who are afraid.  
  
  
Q144. त्राता च कः?  
Who can give real protection?  
A144. स गुरुः।  
The Preceptor.  
  
  
Q145. को हि जगद्गुरुरुक्तः  
Indeed, who is known as the universal Preceptor?  
A145. शंभुः \|  
Lord Siva, Sambhu.  
  
  
Q146. ज्ञानं कुतः?  
Where is knowledge coming from?  
A146. शिवादेव \|  
From Lord Siva.  
  
  
Q147. मुक्तिम् लभेत कस्मात्?  
How is liberation attained?  
A147. मुकुन्दभक्तेः \|  
Through devotion to Mukunda (Vishnu, Krishna).  
  
  
Q148. मुकुन्दः कः?।  
Who is Mukunda?  
A148. यस्तारयेदविद्यां \|  
He who takes us beyond ignorance.  
  
  
Q149. का चाविद्या?  
And what is ignorance?  
A149. यदात्मनोऽस्फूर्तिः \|  
That which covers our true spiritual nature.
