A compilation from the book ‘Sridakshinamurthistotram Vol.1 authored by Ved Sri D.S.Subbaramaiya, published by the Sringeri Sharada Peetham. This compilation has 14 pages.  
The schools cited here are: Sankhya, Yoga, Nyaya-Vaisheshika, Purvamimamsa, Dharmashastra, Agamas (Shaiva and Pancharatra), Shakta tantra, Vyakarana and a summing up from the Suta Samhita.
