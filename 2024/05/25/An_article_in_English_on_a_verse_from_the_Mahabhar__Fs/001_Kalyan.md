Here is the verse in BORI edition -

  

ब्रह्माणं शितिकण्ठं च याश्चान्या देवताः स्मृताः \|  
प्रबुद्धवर्याः सेवन्ते मामेवैष्यन्ति यत्परम् \|\|३२\|\|

  

Here is the verse (slightly different) in Kumbhaghonam edition -  

  

ब्रह्माणं शितिकण्ठं च याश्चान्या देवताः स्मृताः।

प्रबुद्धचर्याः सेवन्तो मामेवैष्यन्ति यत्फलम्॥ 12-350-36

  

  

Here is the translation of Sri Bibek Debroy for BORI shloka version -

  

"It is said that enlightened ones may serve Brahma, Shitikantha or any of the other gods, but they obtain me, who is beyond them."
