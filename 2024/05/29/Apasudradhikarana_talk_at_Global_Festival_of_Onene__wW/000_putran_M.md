Namaskaram,

  

Chitta-ji sent me a link to his interview in the Global Festival of Oneness (see[https://www.facebook.com/advaitaacademy/videos/299576489875260/](https://www.facebook.com/advaitaacademy/videos/299576489875260/)).

  

I also saw this next talk on Apasudradhikarana by Sri Satyan Sharma-ji.

  

Four points stood out to me on first hearing:

  

1\. Sudras (and women) are eligible for Brahma Vidya but not necessarily all other vidyas (obtained through Vedas); and even Brahma Vidya, they have adhikara to learn of it through Ithihasas and Puranas only.

  

(This does not appear new but it clears any doubt regarding Brahma Vidya being a common goal for all, including sudras and women).

  

Three points that seemed noteworthy that may be more controversial or not accepted in earlier times:

  

2\. Everyone can read Vedas/Upanishads by books etc. Here book-knowledge of Vedas (the way most of us do it) does not lead to liberation (i.e. is not right form of sravana); however such study is not barred from anyone including sudras and women.

  

3\. Everyone can even hear the Vedas. Only Vedic chanting is restricted to brahmanas.

  

(Sharma-ji also quotes contemporary acharyas from Sringeri and Jyotish mathas tosupport this assertion. This was surprising because thisviewpoint seems to contradict some highly-quoted dharma shastras which indicate sudras should not even hear the Vedas.)

  

4\. He also makes the point repeatedly that several Vedic sentences are also in Ithihasas and puranas and such are accessible for sudras and women as well (presumably for reading, chanting as well). Interestingly he refers to the Gayathri Mantra also being in these other scriptures, so perhaps implying that all can chant that mantra as well.
