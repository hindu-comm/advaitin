Hare Krishna



Just few of my practical thoughts considering the current scenario.



1.  Sudras (and women) are eligible for Brahma Vidya but not necessarily
    all other vidyas (obtained through Vedas);



- Likewise, brAhmana and kshatriya-s were not eligible ( or not supposed
  to) to have access to ‘other vidya-s’ which are exclusively meant for
  other stree and shUdra-s. What is dharma and what is karma for the
  chAturvarNa-s explained in BG.



2.  and even Brahma Vidya, they have adhikara to learn of it through
    Ithihasas and Puranas only.



Ø Yes, all varNa-s are equally eligible to get the brahma vidyA (mOksha) through one or the other means. What does it mean?? The upa siddhAnta from this, that one can infer is : for the brahma vidyA ONLY veda-s are pramANa but it_is untya pramANa for only dvija-s who have the access to the study of this ‘particular scripture’. For non-dvija-s and strees untya pramANa is veda tattva in particular enshrined in other paurusheya texts and not necessarily apaurusheya veda’s original text / Shabda itself.



(This does not appear new but it clears any doubt regarding Brahma Vidya being a common goal for all, including sudras and women).



- bhAshyakAra himself categorically clear about it in sUtra bhAshya.
  Here he clarifies ‘pUrvakruta saMskAravashAt’ all four varNa-s can get
  the final liberation. However, he also clearly denies the vedAdhikAra
  to shUdra-s. So my point is not about varNa-s and their adhikAra but
  veda-s untya pramANa and the status that we often argue : ONLY veda-s
  have the stuff (not any other paurusheya text) that can give the
  knowledge of ultimate.



Three points that seemed noteworthy that may be more controversial or not accepted in earlier times:



3.  Everyone can read Vedas/Upanishads by books etc.



- Yes, somany printed versions in various languages available both in
  soft and hard versions so everyone who is interested to learn and who
  is interested to refute can read it in their own language 😊 Now, in a
  way, readable texts of veda-s have become ‘public property’ and it is
  no more an exclusive ‘secret knowledge’ of some selected verNa-s.



Here book-knowledge of Vedas (the way most of us do it) does not lead to liberation (i.e. is not right form of sravana);



- The right form of shravaNa, manana and nidhidhyAsana in the rigid
  atmosphere of tradition has been explained by bhAshyakAra if we want
  to strictly adhere to that then we are not eligible to do brahma
  jignAsa, but still we can give big talks on it 😊



however such study is not barred from anyone including sudras and women.



- Yes, for example in BG there are some verses which are just an exact
  replica of katha shruti (mantra-s) and these smruti text is accessible
  for the shUdra-s and stree-s and when they are studying BG they are
  infact studying katha mantra-s as well in particular and vedAnta
  tattva in general.



Everyone can even hear the Vedas. Only Vedic chanting is restricted to brahmanas.



- This was not the traditional stand atleast during the ancient time.
  shUdra-s ears should be poured molten led if he hears the veda, the
  vipra’s tongue should be cut if he chants veda-s in public (or doing
  adhyayana near shUdra-s), such is the instruction in some dharma
  shAstra. Even bhAshyakAra, the sampradaya vida (who is writing his
  bhAshya within the restricted boundaries of sampradaya) did not raise
  any concerns about it, while commenting it, seems he is accepting
  this strict restrictions (if not endorsing this barbaric act of
  punishment) then existing in tradition.



(Sharma-ji also quotes contemporary acharyas from Sringeri and Jyotish mathas tosupport this assertion. This was surprising because thisviewpoint seems to contradict some highly-quoted dharma shastras which indicate sudras should not even hear the Vedas.)



- This is what I want to see from the practical perspective. Each and
  every torch bearers of dharma and dharmAcharaNa does not want to
  dilute the injunctions of dharma when it is insisted but practically
  when they are giving public discourses irrespective of varNa of
  audience they quote veda-s directly and no concerns raised when
  vipra-s chanting veda mantra-s in loud speakers publicly. Some of the
  dharma vidhi and pratishedha ONLY in books and not practical
  considering the current socio environment.



4.  He also makes the point repeatedly that several Vedic sentences are
    also in Ithihasas and puranas and such are accessible for sudras and
    women as well (presumably for reading, chanting as well).
    Interestingly he refers to the Gayathri Mantra also being in these
    other scriptures, so perhaps implying that all can chant that mantra
    as well.



- Simply plain reading of the mantra-s by everyone we cannot stop as
  said above however learning the traditional chanting of veda-s with
  svara-s is still restricted to brAhmaNa-s, though we say all dvija-s
  (including kshatriya and shUdra) it is mainly seen in traditional
  orthodox mutts only brahmaNa-s are eligible/allowed to study veda in a
  traditional style.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
