Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

I don't mind rules being understood differently in the present times in thecontext of various other changes in society which may affect what isdharma. However I think if we are still an "intellectual tradition", we have to produce tarka-based commentaries on dharma shastras that elucidate and argue out the validity of these changed perspectives. I don't mean a Satyan Sharma-ji or you and me making connections by quoting works and acharyas, but written referrable works either by acharyas or having their formal agreement, that take up positions on dharma held earlier in thetradition and argue out in a precise manner why the present day modifications are justified. We can't simply pretend that apasudradhikaranawas always acceptant of shudras hearing the vedas, by quoting an acharya of today in a speech. There is no merit in pushing things under the rug. Take up the subject directly and tell why/how that applies or applies not/differently today; or let's at least be silent about it. ([https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brahma-sutras-thibaut/d/doc63776.html](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brahma-sutras-thibaut/d/doc63776.html))

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
