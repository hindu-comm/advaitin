Just a few points:

  

The Dharmashastras guidelines on the issue is misunderstood. Though the word 'Sravana' is used, from the context it is clear that the prohibition is not to mere 'hearing' but to Adhyayana of Veda. This we know that in the next few verses there is prohibition of Veda Adhyapana by Shudras as well.

  

This shows that the prohibition is in the context of Veda Adhyayana-Adhyapana by Shudras because such means of livelihood is assigned for Brahmanas. This is not a prohibition of mere hearing of Veda mantras.
