Namaskaram Nithin-ji,

  

You have given a coherent argument. What is the actual restriction meant in dharma shastras, what type of restriction is not being addressed, and why the restriction?

  

Restriction: Shudras should not do the veda-shravana that is part of Veda adhyayana-adhyapana.

  

Not restricted: casual hearing like we listen to devotional songs, for the sake of bhakti, and not with intent to memorize and chant on our own.

  

Reason for restriction: Assigned social order that is to be strictly maintained, for the sake of harmony etc.

  

One can argue further that if there came about social practices or even secondary scriptural injunctions that were stricter in restrictions, they should be understood as non-essential and born out of practical/temporal considerations.

  

The reasons for restriction need to be understood/argued in depth, considering that varnashrama is part of Ishvara's Order and not simply a paurusheya matter.
