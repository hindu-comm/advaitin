I think there are two aspects:

  

1\. One is with respect to eligibility for Veda adhyayana & Vaidika Karmas which is limited to Dvijas.

2\. Second are the verses which speak of some punishments for 'Sravana' of the Veda. It is my understanding also reinforced by other traditional acharyas that the Shravana spoken in context of punishments does not apply to 'mere hearing', but to 'Veda Adhyayana+Adhyapana' attempts made which has the potential to rob Brahmanas of their livelihood (and hence the severity of punishment prescribed)

  

On the question of punishment and prayashchittain Dharmashastra, I believe the correct approach to understand them is to consider them as indicators revealing the magnitude of papam generated by a particular action such that people are discouraged from performing them. The actual knowledge that is to be derived is the 'Magnitude of Punya/Papa' that a particular action generates, rather than take these at face value.
