# विष्णुपुराणम्/प्रथमांशः/अध्यायः १९

[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/1srj](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/1srj)

  

ब्रह्मत्वे सृजते विश्वं स्थितौ पालयते पुनः ।  
रुद्ररूपाय कल्पान्ते नमस्तुभ्यं त्रिमूर्तये ६६ ।  

  

Prahlada, in his stuti addressed to Bhagavan, says: By assuming the Brahma role, you create the world. You sustain it and assume the Rudra role at the end of the kalpa. Obeisance to you, the Trimurti.

  

Om
