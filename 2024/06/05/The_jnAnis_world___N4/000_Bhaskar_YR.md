Hare Krishna



If the jnAni seeing the world and do vyavahAra with it is the sign of his avidyA !!?? if the answer is yes, then we have to accept that shankara who was seeing world and interacting with it, ramaNa maharshi having dialogues with people, our jagadguru-s giving discourses keeping loukika-s in mind, all are having some sort of avidyA!!?? For that matter Ishwara himself would see the forthcoming jagat (srushti) in his mind before creation must be having that avidyA because he too, projecting this jagat and perceiving the future manifestation!! this is really anishta prasanga to say the least in advaita vedAnta. By saying jnAni-s having one or the other type of avidyA ( AvaraNa or vikshepa) even after realizing he is secondless brahman is really an absurd observation and have the potential to do advaita siddhAnta hAni. So what would be the 'drushti' of paramArtha jnAni?? how does he see the jagat and himself?? shruti answers he sees the jagat with sarvAtmabhAva...he realizes that he is anna, annaada and shlokakarta as well. There is not even an iota of avidyA in this samyak realization, there is absolutely no traces of any katrutva and bhOktrutva. It is the right understanding of sarvAtmabhAva amidst the socalled transaction between eater and eaten. His realization of Atmaikatva jnAna fetch him the undiluted solid jnAna that he (Atman) is left, right, top, bottom there exists nothing apart from HIM. Rishi vAmadeva realized this truth in his mother's womb and exclaimed aham manu and aditya, ramaNa realized this truth at his uncle's house, shwetaketu realized this truth after repeatedly hearing the tattvamasi from uddaalaka, arjuna realized this after getting the jnAnOpadesha from the lord. Now the question is, is this jnAna needs to be literally experienced in a mystic state called samAdhi in a mind inert state ?? is this jnAna time-bound to say I had the realization that I am brahman from AM to PM and came back as normal avidyAvanta?? is this jnAna avasthA vishesha jnAna or temporary mystic experience ?? I dont think so. The sarvAtmabhAva is natural state of the paramArtha jnAni and even though outside objects being cognized through the indriya-s and even though body is performing natural routine activities / transactions this sarvAmabhAva completely annihilates the katrutva-bhOktrutva buddhi. Hence it is said in geeta bhAshya : bAhyAkAra bheda buddhi nivruttireva AtmasvarUpavalambanaM kAraNaM. Each and everything is HIMSELF, self realization results only with the removal of the sense of difference with the jagat/objects. It is not the denial of the outside objects perse. Hence shankara somewhere says even the daily normal routine of jnAni like bhikshAcharya appears as doing with kartrutva buddhi for the outsiders (avidyAvanta-s) but in his mind he has the firm conviction that he is neither bhOktru nor kartru and he was / is / will always be nitya, shuddha, buddha, mukta svarUpa Atman.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
