It's called "बाधित अनुवृत्ति ".



Ø Yes it is called bAdhitAnuvrutti. So what is sublated here??
When both jnAni and ajnAni seeing the ‘same’ nAma rUpa?? The ajnAni knows the world (nAma and rUpa) treats it as an independent one and ‘different’ from him, hence his drushti about the jagat is parichinna upAdhi drushti or asarvAtma drushti or anAtma drushti or abrahma drushti. This mithya Kalpita pratyaya-s would gets sublated (bAdhita) after jnAni realizes sarvAtmabhAva. The crux of this Samyak drushti is : when paramArtha jnAna dawns the existing nAma rUpa do not vanish in thin air they will remain as it is and available for contacts and communication even for the jnAni only his view point about them become different. jnAni’s erstwhile vyAvahArika limited drushti would be sublated he would get the ‘bhUma drushti. Therefore the mithyA part or avidyAkalpita jagat is abrahman, asarvaM but what is there before creation as avyAkruta and what is available now in vyAkruta rUpa is brahmAbhinna i.e. mAyAshakti which is non-different from brahman in all the three times.
