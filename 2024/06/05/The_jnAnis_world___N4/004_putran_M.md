I understand you are opposed to the position that the jnani is always in samadhi even where we perceive his BMI exhibiting iccha, kriya, viveka with regard to the world outside it. You conclude we must say the *jnani has jnana*(vidya)*of* the nama-rupa duality that however (unlike the ajnani) he realises is rooted in non-dual Sat.

  

I understand here that you identify "jnani" with Self/Consciousness reflected in a particular BMI. Thus jnani is Self seen (identified) in conjunction with a unique nama-rupa limiting adjunct. This makes the jnani a jivatma entity of the vyavaharika standpoint and discussions of what jnani experiences/knows can be corresponded to what happens to this unique BMI. What happens corresponds to the continuation of the reflected consciousness and a differentiated awareness that is however somehow also sublated in undifferentiated Self-awareness. (Similar to knowing ring vs bangle at the level of namarupas but with full awareness that both references are of gold.)
