Who is it that says that the jnani is doing vyavahara with the world? We say Ramana Maharshi does things, but is that Ramana’s own experience? Even if Ramana himself said he does things, who is it that attributes to him that saying?

  

It might be that Ramana neither does things nor does not do things, but that both are misstatements. Neti, neti...
