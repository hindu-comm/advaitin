If we can "see Ramana Maharshi" then Ramana Maharshi must be doing something right?

Similarly if we can "see a Jnani" then that Jnani must be doing something right?



praNAms

Hare Krishna



jnAni engaging himself in some activity (like bhikshAcharya etc.) or doing something for lOka saMgraha is not the sign of avidyA (avidyA lesha). The kartrutva, bhOktrutva buddhi in the activities is avidyA that is conspicuously absent in the paramArtha jnAni. The very result of paramArtha jnAna sublates the pramAtrutva clarifies bhAshyakAra in geeta.
