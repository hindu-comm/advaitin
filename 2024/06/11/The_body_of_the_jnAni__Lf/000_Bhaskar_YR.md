Hare Krishna



After reading Sri Putran prabhuji’s observation about unique identification of jnAni’s BMI I wish to share my observation and subsequent thoughts :



If one attains mere Atma jnAna that itself not enough because of the reason that the Atman the consciousness which is still identifiable in the unique body (jnAni being identified in his present birth or in embodied state) is still distinct by virtue of his association with this body. So as long as he has been identified with his present body he is a different person from us and we see him interacting with us since we too are different from him. And as long as he is imprisoned in his present body he is having the mukti in secondary sense and he would get the primary mukti ONLY and ONLY after falling of this body (videhamukti). So Jeevan mukta, while living in his body is NOT mukta in real sense as his adjuncts continue to function normally!! The linking of mukti with jnAni’s embodiedness and unembodiedness is totally unwarranted and uncalled for. Because this is IMO opposed to bhAshyakAra teaching i.e. jeevanmukti or sadyOmukti is itself primary mukti (liberation in the predominant sense) it is because of the simple fact that for the jnAni ashareeratvam is svAbhAvikaM and shareeratva (embodiedness) is mere mithyAbhimAna (just false pride due to delusion in owning or possessing the body which is different from others and signifying his innate identification with it) which will be completely eradicated after the dawn of Samyak / paripUrNa jnAna. The truth of this ashareeratvam which is the natural state of Atman can be realized in consonance with shruti, yukti and anubhava!! Again anubhava is NOT individual experience which is experienced in unique BMI of jnAni like in individual mind’s inert state in nirvikalpa samAdhi but it is intuitive universal experience which is discussed in scriptures and experienced with the logical devices utilized in the scriptures themselves. From this shrutyukta shravaNa, manana and nidhidhyAsana the jnAni attains ‘parama pada’ (ultimate realization) in this very life itself. The sAmpradAyika teaching of bhagavatpAda with regard to mukhya mukti is HERE and NOW in this life-span we can cognize, discern that the miseries or calamities of saMsAra are false (avidyAkalpita) and just unreal appearances and this mukya mukti is not posthumous or to be experienced only in a mysterious state of mind or to be achieved in some other part of celestial abode ( lOkAntara, janmAntara, vyavahAra abhAva samAdhi state).



Just I will conclude this with some observations from bhAshyakAra in samanvayAdhikaraNa :



Objection : Bodilessness can come only after the falling off of the body and not to one living.

Reply : No; for embodiedness is due to a misconception.



Here bhAshyakAra dealt and discusses this embodiedness of paramArtha jnAni and finally concludes : Therefore embodiedness being only due to a false notion, it is to be concluded that bodilessness is the nature of a wise one even while living.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
