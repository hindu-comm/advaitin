Jnani's may not have shareera abhimAna, but still their manifestation through a body cannot be ruled out right? Otherwise, how do we then explain the avataras of the Lord and Sri Shankara? Look forward to your clarification.



praNAms Sri Suresh prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Yes jnAni does not have the dehAbhimAna coz. dehAbhimAna itself is adhyAsa and in jnAni there is no adhyAsa 😊 The jnAni-s ashareeratvaM said and discussed because the ajnAni still identify jnAni with the upAdhi only and thinks that Chaitanya in particular upAdhi (jnAni-s BMI) realized that it is Atman. This is just like refrigerator having the upAdhi parichinna sOpAdhika drushti and thinking oven is something else because oven is having some special power which is not there in it. 😊 Though electricity is one and the same, for all practical purposes with different upAdhi-s the same power appearing as entirely two different things. From the electricity point of view it is always one and the same. For the jnAni the manifested jagat (vyAkruta jagat) too nothing but that brahman only. What else he could see through what when Atman is sarvaM!! Exclaims shruti. But when ajnAni who cannot go beyond the boundaries of individual BMI would ask the questions like above for him we have to say jnAni is doing that, doing this, he too like us sleeps, dreams, he too have prArabdha, he too digbhrAnta though having the knowledge etc. etc. But jnAni is krutakrutya after the dawn of jnAna he does not have to do anything coz. very pramAtrutva is sublated with that Samyak jnAna or Atmaikatva jnAna. His is NOT upAdhi parichinna drushti but Chaitanya paripUrNa bhUma drushti.
