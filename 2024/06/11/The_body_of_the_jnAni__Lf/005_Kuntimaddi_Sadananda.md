PraNAms

  

A simple explanation is jnaani plays the drama of Life - just like an actor playing his given role on the stage. The actor does not forget that he is the actor playing the role while on the sage. He may act as a beggar or a King as required by his costume of BMI and role on the stage. It knows it is Jagannatakam. Hence we have roles in Puranas like Krishna playing. Actions on the stage do not affect the actor. He may be making millions while playing like a beggar. Hence we have Vedantic teachers who are playing the role of teachers for the sincere students who come to learn from him. He eats, sleeps, gets angry, and in a cafeteria only selects those foods that he likes to eat. ajnaani is also playing the role but he takes the roles are real and forgets that he is only an actor playing the role.

  

Hari Om!

Sadananda
