Hari Om,

  

True Bhaskar Ji. The JM in itself is the ultimate moksham.

  

IMHO the issue is by connecting the 'aathyanthika moksham ' to 'ashareerattwam', it puts the moksham on the same plank of religious moksham aka going to swargam, kailasam, vaikundam etc. The problem here is that it depends upon one's faith and belief and not in jnanam. Sadly none has until now returned from that kind of moksham and confirmed it - yes. I have been to Swargam etc. We are wrongly tying up the 'aathyanthika moksham' to an event in time and space when we insist onVM as the 'aathyanthika moksham'. A simple understanding of transcending time and space, which certainly is NOT physical but cognitive, is very essential. With this the JM happens. For a perfect SCS this will be instantaneous. For others it calls for long N. We know changing HABITual errors is the HArdest BIT to get done.

  

Without doubt the ultimate moksham must be eternal and infinite and henceit must be HERE and NOW 😊. A JM recognises this by studying the 'ShashtRa' from a Guru who is 'shrotRiya and brahmanishta'.

  

If JM is not understood, doubtlessly as the ultimate, the solution is to gain clarity on the position of ultimate moksham by going back to S,M, & N.

  

The seeker may be continuing with the mistaken notion of a'Brahmaanubhava'. One just has to remember that even after confirming the mirage, the knowledge doesn't remove the mirage from the experience of seeing the same. We continue to enjoy the beautiful sunrise and sunset inspite of the heliocentric knowledge that Aryabhatta revealed centuries ago still resting in us solidly.Why,even the blue sky!

  

Regards

  

Balagopal
