Q150. कस्य न शोको?  
For whom we should not worry?  
A150. यः स्यादक्रोधः \|  
For one who is free from anger.  
  
  
Q151. किं सुखं  
What is happiness?  
A151. तुष्टिः।  
Being satisfied.  
  
  
Q152. को राजा?  
Who is a king?  
A152. रञ्जनकृत् \|  
One who makes others happy.  
  
  
Q153. कश्च श्वा?  
Who is a dog?  
A153. नीचसेवको य: स्यात् \|  
One who serves degraded masters.  
  
  
Q154. को मायी?  
Who controls Maya (the mystical energy)?  
A154. परमेशः \|  
The Supreme Lord.  
  
  
Q155. क इन्द्रजालायते?  
What is divine magic?  
A155. प्रपञ्चोऽयं ।  
The manifestation of this world.  
  
  
Q156. कः स्वप्ननिभॊ?  
What is dreaming?  
A156. जाग्रद्व्यवहारः \|  
The activities we perform in the waking state.  
  
  
Q157. सत्यमपि किं?  
And what is real?  
A157. ब्रह्म \|  
Brahman.
