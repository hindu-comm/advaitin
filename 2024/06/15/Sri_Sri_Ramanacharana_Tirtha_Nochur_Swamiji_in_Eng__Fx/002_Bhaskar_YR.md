praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji

Hare Krishna



I heard this, it is good that Swamiji without bringing in jnAni’s avidyAlesha, prArabdha karma etc. clarifies that jnAni’s body only in the view of ajnAni-s. But in reality he is ashareeri only and for others he looks like he is dehavAn. From this it is quite evident that there is no difference in jnAna in jnAni when he is physically living or when he is physically dead to others. gaUna mukti, mukhya mukti etc. is just for those who still see jnAni’s body and it belongs to that particular jnAni 😊 Some vivaraNa school vyAkhyAnakAra-s were of the opinion that jnAni’s continuation of body is an obstacle in getting the mukhya mukti hence jnAna what he attains when he is living is somewhat just parOksha jnAna.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

bhaskar
