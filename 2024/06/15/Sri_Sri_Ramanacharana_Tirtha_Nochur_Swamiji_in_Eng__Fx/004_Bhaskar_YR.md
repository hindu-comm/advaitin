praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Alongwith you I too should confess that I cannot talk anything authoritatively on jnAni and his vyavahAra, coz. I am not the jnAni nor even serious jignAsu. But I must say I am very fond of repeating whatever lord, shruti and bhAshyakAra have said on this socalled jnAni’s body and vyavahAra. Those who want to have disagreements with the stand of these three (shruti, bhAshyakAra and geetAchArya) should do so by suggesting some other alternative which should not go against core teachings of advaita vedAnta. JnAni’s vyavahAra is as said earlier for lOka saMgraha though he seems doing the karma naiva kiMchitkarOti, kurvannapi na lipyate, naiva kurvan na kArayan clarifies Lord. Tattva vida-s ( the jnAni-s) when they are seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, walking, sleeping, breathing, speaking, even attending the nature calls, blinking eye lids have the firm conviction that indriyANeendriyaartheshu and I am not the cognizer, doer, enjoyer etc.
Because he is just dehavAniva lakshyate in reality he is ashareeri because his svarUpa (Atma bhAva) is naturally ashareeratvaM only. He is NOW, then and there brahman only. From this standpoint sarva vyavahAra, sarva nAma rUpaM etc. Satyameva as he knows there exists nothing apart from THAT.
