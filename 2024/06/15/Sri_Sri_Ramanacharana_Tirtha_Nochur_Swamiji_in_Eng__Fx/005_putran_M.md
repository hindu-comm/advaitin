Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

Here is what you said earlier: "knowing that it is ONLY gold and nothing but gold does not stop him \[the jnani\] to wear the ring and bangles at their respective place, this is called bAdhitAnuvrutti where vyavahAra is not absent but sublated with Samyak jnAna."

  

I want to know what is the jnani for whom vyavahara is not absent. The vyavahara mentioned above pertains to the body. The jnani does not identify or limit self in terms of body. Yet there is awareness of duality of body and of ring and one wearing theother.

  

If the cognitions pertaining to a particular body belong to the jnani, then is the jnani Consciousness identifying self with the subtle body, whose vrittis are the immediate objects of cognition?

  

Or should we say, the jnani is Consciousness=Self identifying self with a particular body-mind projection (of Consciousness/Maya) but that mind is modified according to Jnana, so that all dualistic cognitions (including the small i associating self with some deep level of body-mind and not-self with world) are sublated with samyak jnana? This jnana-filtering happens seamlessly and cannot be altered. Hence the identification of self with that body-mind is not a case of avidya.

  

Or even this is the viewpoint of ajnanis, for the jnani sees Self in all and all in Self. There is no special self vs not-self identifications to talk about, when there is samyak jnana. In that case, why should we assert that "jnani sees the world=all ... but..."? Rather there is only the unitary awareness of Self, no further vyavahara (based on substance vs form duality either) from the standpoint of jnani. This is where, I think, there is some controversy.
