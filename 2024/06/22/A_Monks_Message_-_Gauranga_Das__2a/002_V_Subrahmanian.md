Thanks for pointing this out. I saw this now with greater attention and noticed the word 'prakriti'. He means Maya by prakriti. In any case this one-line expression is hardly representative of the general audience. Also, a little before that, he is citing the BG 16th ch. verse: Ishvaro'hamaham bhogi... and gives the meaning 'God' to the term Ishwara there. Actually there Ishwara means: a person of great ability/accomplishment/authority. In fact Madhwacharya took the meaning of God to this and misrepresented Advaita in his books to tell his audience: Advaitins consider themselves God. Even today most Madhwas believe that. A few years ago two Madhwa ladies asked this question and got a reply from Sri Mani Dravid Sastrigal. They thanked him for this important clarification.
