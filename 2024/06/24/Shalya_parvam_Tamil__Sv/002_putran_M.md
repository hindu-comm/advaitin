At 1:03:30, he states that the subtle indriyas (pulan?) of the (jiva) atma are the same ones throughout its transmigrational journey and left behind only upon attaining (videha) mukti. It reminded me of something I had read in the Sringeri acharya's Vivekachudamani commentary (pg 114-115):

  

Quote (from past email)  

  

This linga sharira ... remains identical until the state of disembodied moksha, by reason of its undergoing changes of manifestation and absorptiononly and not completely disappearing....

  

But, it may be objected: Adhyasa, in terms of kama and karma which are synonymous with avidya arise separately in several lives. How then can thesubtlebodymade of them be said to be permanent and identical throughout?

  

Though they are separate like that, the external organs like vak, the antahkarana, the intellect etc. are not different till the time of release and so the permanent identity of thesubtlebodymust be affirmed.

  

Unquote

  

This knowledge of the essentially unchanging components of the sukshma sharira must be common in both visishtadvaita and advaita traditions and known from basic scripture.
