Also note that \*\*veiling\*\* by avidyA is partial, not complete.

  

Also, reg // 1. \*\*mithyā + ajñāna\*\*:

 - \*\*mithyā\*\*: This means "false" or "illusory." It refers to something that is not real or not ultimately true but appears to have some form of existence.  

 - \*\*ajñāna\*\*: This means "ignorance" or "lack of knowledge." It denotes a lack of true knowledge about reality //,

  

This understanding is totally different from both the standard debated versions of \*\*mithyā + ajñāna\*\* and \*\*mithyā + jñāna\*\*. It makes the meaning \*\* This means "ignorance" or "lack of knowledge" which is "false" or "illusory" \*\*. That would be great indeed. \*\* false or illusory lack of knowledge \*\*.
