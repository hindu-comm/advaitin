Not sure how relevant (or true as well) is what i am writing below w.r.t the topic. But was motivated by the term Ignorance.

  

As per my view Ajah or The Unborn is True nature of Reality. But this is w.r.t an unconditioned or Ideal frame work. But i try to argue that if its truely Absolute and Non Dual in nature it must be beyond the bounds of duality of Ideal or Non Ideal Frame wor and it should have a representation in both Frame work. In ideal frame work the Ajah "appears" or takes the form as unmanifest where as in Non Ideal frame work it appears or takes the form of manifest where duality is seen. In this relam of Non Ideal frame work even though its the Pristine Ajah thats appearing as the manifested universe it gives the impression of duality when Intellect, which is an aspect of Ajah in the manisfested dual form, is operating in one aspect of duality called Ignorance. Where as its able to identify the true nature of this manifestation as the Pristine Ajah when operating in the mode of knowledge. So knowledge and Ignorance are two aspects of Intellect .

  

I could relate this to Shankars famous verse from Viveka Chudamani

  

Astiti Pratyayo Yascha Yascha Naastiti Vastuni

Budhereva Gunaveto Natu Nityasya Vastuna.
