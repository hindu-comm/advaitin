praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji

Hare Krishna



I think by non-ideal and ideal frameworks, you are referring to the vyavaharika and paramarthika standpoints of knowledge/reality. In the vyavaharika standpoint, where duality is "seen" or experienced, the duality is realized ("in the mode of knowledge") as being rooted in Sat or Brahman and hence the awareness is always of Ishvara - the non-dual Consciousness (Gold) who appears as namarupa jagat (the all, rings and bangles) including the reflected jiva-identification with a specific BMI. (Of course, this is a bit of another debated point here; but this is one take of it.)



Ø Yes and when shruti says brahman is the abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa for this charaachara jagat and when bhAshyakAra striking the indifferent between kArya-kAraNa and maintaining the kAraNatva’s nirvisheshatva, it is with the above intention only. So this view point is very much allowed and authentic within the frameworks of classical Advaita.



Now when you say knowledge and ignorance are two aspects of intellect, you are using the terms as they pertain to the jiva,who is identified at somelevel with a personalintellect and either believes that is the self or knows his trueSelf as Brahman.



Ø All jnAna and ajnAna vyavahAra pertains to jeeva only coz of the simple fact there is no vidyAvidya vyavahAra in kevalaatma which is nirguNa and nirvikAra. vidyA-avidyA, mAya etc. are mere superimposition on brahman for the sake of teaching. I did not know earlier and now I know both are vrutti-s of the mind only and both are illumined by the witnessing consciousness. Just for example ( with the permission of Sri Chadramouli prabhuji), earlier I was under the impression that he IS the follower of Sri SSS with utmost reverence and later on he helped me to clear his stand that he is NOT the follower of Sri SSS and if at all he finds anything/something that Sri SSS endorsing it is just humorous rambling and not to be taken seriously. See here both these realizations are happening in my mind only. I was ignorant about his stand earlier and now I know what he is in his stand. And both these vrutti-s are in me and I could perceive my earlier ignorant state about his stand and current knowledge of the same. For further clarification you can refer bhAshya on vedaavinAshinaM nityaM ya yenam ajaM avyayaM verse in II chapter of geeta. Here bhAshyakAra uses the examples of knowledge-ignorance taking place in dream realized as false after waking, after realization of the ultimate truth both right and wrong knowledge are avidyA vyavahAra only. There is no empirical dealing such as vidyA-avidyA vyavahAra in true knowledge.



Here, avidya belongs to the jiva (thus obstructing knowledge of Gold) and maya to Ishvara (by which power He projects the rings and bangles). When avidya is removed, 1. maya remains and the jiva sees rings and bangles while fully aware it is all only Gold.



Ø Yes this is called Atmaikatva darshana, sarvAtma bhAva, Samyak drushti or bhUma drushti.



The other position is that mayaand avidya should be reduced to a unitary Power (call it Maya or (moola) Avidya). Any attribution of causality has to be reverted back to Brahman as Ishvara (i.e. to Maya), and the jiva's avidya is part of the movie projection of Maya=Avidya. When avidya is transcended, there is neither maya nor avidya for the jnani for whom the All is entirely sublated in Brahman/Self.



Ø As said / clarified earlier when we are talking anything about avidyA it is pertaining to jeeva (antaHkaraNa dOsha) and when we are talking anything about srushti for which brahman himself is the material and efficient cause and that is about Ishwar mAya which is like Ishwara ‘eternal’, non different from brahman and trikAla abAdhitaM.
