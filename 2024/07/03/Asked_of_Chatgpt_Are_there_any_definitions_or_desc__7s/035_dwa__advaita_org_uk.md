Who knows how the AI tools reach their conclusions? Very few, I should think – closely guarded secrets by the competitors. My point was that, even if all relevant books were digitized (which I very much doubt – and many are not even in English), the sheer volume of discussions and blogs will far outweigh them. How does it determine whose views are more important, or even accurate ? Does it take into account criticisms by other philosophies, weigh all the arguments and reach a considered conclusion? I very much doubt it! When even the greatest Advaitins of the past 1000 years were unable to agree on what Ṥaṅkara understood on certain topics, are we going to put any trust at all in some glorified search engine?
