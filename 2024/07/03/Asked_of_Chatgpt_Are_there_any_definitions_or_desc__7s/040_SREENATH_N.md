First of all apologies, if i am not following some rules in responding . I am just redponding to the last message in the thread. I was trying to phrase it in a different way, i.e. narrowing down the problem to intellect of the observer who is observing the evolution of Universe . Here as i mentioned earlier, there are two modes of operation for intellect, knowledge and ignorance. One of the primary aspect of this Ignorance is the incomplete sense of I . I usually refer the Rubber Hand Illusion experiment where a Rubber hand which is not part of the biological body gets trained as a part of body or a feel gets associated to it. I call this phenomenon an aspect of correlation. So this fundemental principle of correlation which starts even before birth creates a sense of I centered around the body.

  

Here i would like to compare the verse from BG

  

Nisheka Garbha Janmani Balya Koumara Youvanam

Vayo Madhyam Jara Mrithyu ithyavastha Thanor Nava.

  

Here the nine states of body is considered which starts even before fertilization. This gives hint to the correlation based sense of I even before attaining a physical body. This i call the primary cause of ignorance based operation of the intellect. In this continous transformation of states of Body or Sharira the type of intellect could be different.

  

Now the fundamental characteristics of this ignorance mode of operation is that it sustains what i call as a resonace of duality, or a circular dependency kind of inference where a set of erroneous or dualistic aspects re affirm each other. This is what i call as the bondage due to ignorance which makes it so convincing to believe various aspects of this observation. The knowledge based mode of operation of intellect though witness this kind of correlation, it will have an inference that this circular kind of re affirmation though creates a strong impression of duality, its true nature is non dual.

  

In other words in my opinion, in a way there is no Jnani or Ajnani, but there is Jnana and Ajnana.

This i call the fundamental dual of the dualistic representation of the non dual Reality.

  

When the sense of I is regonizing the Jnana mode of Intellect that sense of I could be termed as the sense of I of a Jnani and similarly that for Ajnani. Intellect is nothing but a view or modus operandi when the ideal observer or Sakshi aspect of the nondual reality witness the Jana and Ajnana aspect of this view.

  

The level of conviction determines how strong the sense of I is recognizing the Jnana mode of Intellect.

  

Once again apologies for deviating to a different kind of phrasing. I havent undergone traditional kind of Vedantic studies and just limited the traditional aspects to Upanayanam and Sandhyavandanam. I am so attracted to the concept of Ignorance and how its related to the non dual aspect of Reality so thought of sharing my thought process.

  

Hari OM.
