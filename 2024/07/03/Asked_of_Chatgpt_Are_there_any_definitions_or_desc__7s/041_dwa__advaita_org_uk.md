But it cannot read and understand the original Sanskrit of Ṥaṅkara for itself, can it? It is making use of particular translations and the understanding of the various translators and commentators. And, as I have found, those must always be considered in the context of one’s own prior understanding and are potentially suspect. ChatGPT has zero prior (or current) ‘understanding’ of Advaita. It is not a ‘mind’ but a very sophisticated processor of data produced by humans who may or may not have that understanding. Certainly one can utilize that resource well or badly, but it should never be thought of as a ‘sage’, whose ‘teaching’ may enlighten us. I suggest that words like ‘astute’, ‘analysis’ and ‘opinion’ carry the serious danger of making us forget all of this and taking it as an authority.
