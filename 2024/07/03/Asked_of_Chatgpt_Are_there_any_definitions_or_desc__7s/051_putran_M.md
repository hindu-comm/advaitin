Also, from bhashya to mandukya karika I.16: This one, **the j:vah**, the transmigrating individual soul; that is suptah, asleep; while seeing in both the (waking and dream) states such dreams as 'This is my father', 'This is my son', 'This is my grandson', 'This is my field', 'These are my animals', 'I am their master', 'I am happy, miserable', 'I am despoiled by this one, and I have gained through this one', and so on, **under the influence of dream that is but Maya** whose activity has no beginning and **which has the two facets of non-perception of Reality or the causal state, and false perception of Reality.**
