By the way as per classical mUlAvidyAvAda :



1.  In the pure consciousness (in Atma Chaitanya) itself there exists
    this beginningless, indescribable ignorance. Since it is (avidyA)
    is dependent on anAdi chaitanyasatta (pure existence / reality of
    consciousness) it is just like pure consciousness it is too anAdi.
    ( a clear case of brahmAshrita avidyA).
2.  It is neither jnAnAbhAva, nor misconception nor doubt, it is a
    separate existing entity, which has the objective existence like a
    jada vastu and which is the material cause of adhyAsa.
3.  It is jnAna virOdhi bhAvarUpa and not mere lack of knowledge
    (jnAnAbhAva) and it is with same potency existing in all three
    avasthA-s etc.



If you are saying that this type of mUlAvidyA is existing in brahman even before jeeva bhAva and this is what these references conveying then you have to accept that what shruti talking about nirvikAri / nirvishesha brahma was endowed with this anirvachaneeya avidyA well before the jeeva bhAva.
