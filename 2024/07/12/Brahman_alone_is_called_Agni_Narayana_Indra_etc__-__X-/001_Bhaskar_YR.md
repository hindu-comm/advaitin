Hare Krishna



Yes whatever is there it is brahman ONLY and nothing but brahman, Rishi vAmadeva realized this truth in his mother's womb. brahman is not only mere agni, vayu, sUrya etc. he is even fishermen, slaves, and gamblers as well and he is having kAma, krOdha as well...It has to be noted here all these visheshaNa-s has been explained by shruti keeping transmigrating jeeva in mind though he is brahman in his svarUpa. It should not be read other way round like brahman itself is kAmamaya, krOdhamaya adharma maya etc. The bottom line is whatever you see whether it is auspicious or inauspicious there exists brahman ONLY nothing but brahman. IN this sense does avidyA also brahman ONLY??
Na!! after realizing the sarvAtmabhAVa / Atmaikatva jnAna the jnAni realizes that there was / is / never be an entity called avidyA/ajnAna in him or elsewhere 🙂



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
