Dear Bhaskar Ji

Hari Om

  

Your explanations brings greater clarity to my words, thank you.

"Realization of svarUpa jnAna to be understood as mere eradication of avidyA/ajnAna"

  

This is correct. Just as giving the last blow by the axe is the action which splits the tree is the example in the commentaries. There is no further action required after the blow. In the same way once eradicationof avidyA/ajnAna has taken place svarUpa jnAna is immediate.

  

In my statement " when second ignorance vanishes" is the term I used in place of " eradicationof avidyA/ajnAna" So both of us were in the same page.

  

**Om and With Prem**  

**Shri Rammohan**
