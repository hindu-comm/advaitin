The Rajini analogy, I want to say is not perfect, so cannot be completely equated with Ishvara bhava. The post was made in haste and I can't spend requisite time to rethink its limitations. The Rajini analogy seems good for showing satya vs mithya and the reduction of all causality to Ishvara the unitary Self projecting All; but perhaps misleading (one-sided) on the question of avidya vs vidya in Ishvara. Definitely we can agree that ishvara in jiva bhava has no avidya, in the sense of lacking knowledge. How to understand Ishvara in Ishvara bhava and whether thequestion of avidya applies there and how - are higher order questions. I don't think the Rajini analogy serves that purpose well because Rajini certain knows both the roles and is ever mentally conscious/aware of playing them.
