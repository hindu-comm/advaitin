Part3

  

Food we consume is extremely important to our transformation. 

Chandogya Upanishad chapter 6 says that of the food we consume, the subtlest part gets transformed as our mind.

Gita chapter 17 tells us what kind of food to eat: Eat Satvik food, minimize rajasic food and avoid tamasic food.

  

As a Sadhaka, I mostly ate home made food. This was inconvenient at times and it meant avoiding social gatherings to a large extent.

I gave up promotions and career growth. Not important. After retirement we are nobody any way.

  

Finally, avoid large discussions on social groups of extraneous subjects such as whether Avidya is a Bhava Rupah or Abhava Rupah.

It doesn't matter. Many erudite scholars have debated and couldn't reach a conclusion. It's a waste of your valuable time.

Swami Vidyaranya lamented in Vedanta Panchadashi 6th chapter about many scholars wasting their time discussing Avidya and not spending enough time

discussing Brahman. Don't fall into this trap.

  

Our life is short and precious. Use it wisely. Tel yourself you'll reach the goal in this life.

As Kathopanishath says, Uttishtatah Jagratah and Prapya Varaannibodhatha.

  

Best wishes my dear atma swarupins. Know your true Swarupa and get liberated.
