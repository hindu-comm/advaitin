The question is very simple BhAskar ji. In order to know whether there is abhAva of ushkalanta in the room, one has to first know what is ushkalanta.

  

So, x-abhAva-jnAna in the room requires x-jnAna.

  

This is what advaita teachings holds.

  

I was interested to know if any other Indian Philosophy branch holds contrary view.
