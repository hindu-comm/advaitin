Namaste Sudhanshu Ji,

In my understanding, in Advaita Siddhanta, prior pratiyogI-jnAna in full is not essential for its abhAva-jnAna. It is sufficient to have some features of the pratiyogI-jnAna. Partial pratiyogI-jnAna. For example in a place where there are no round bodied objects, there can be abhAva-jnAna of a pot if it is understood to have a round shape. There need not be jnAna of what a pot exactly is.
