**Namaste Chandramouli ji.**  
  
//In my understanding, in Advaita Siddhanta, prior pratiyogI-jnAna in full is not essential for its abhAva-jnAna.//  
  
So, when we have partial knowledge of pratiyogI, we say that we have knowledge of pratiyogI whose pratiyogitA is delimited by that dharma (which is known to us).  
  
//It is sufficient to have some features of the pratiyogI-jnAna. Partial pratiyogI-jnAna. For example in a place where there are no round bodied objects, there can be abhAva-jnAna of a pot if it is understood to have a round shape. There need not be jnAna of what a pot exactly is.//  
  
Yes. So, here we have ghaTa-nishTha-vartula-AkAratva-avachchhinna-pratiyogitA-nirUpita-abhAva. So, here we know pot to be an object with property round-ness. So, the condition of prior pratiyogI-jnAna is not violated.
