No, you have not understood the intent correctly.

  

~C is a mathematical operation referring to the negation of C, making C absent. That is not being meant here. What is meant is the knowledge that C is absent.

  

The whole point of this is that a hare's horn is not simply AB, the knowledge of whose absence requires the prior knowledge of A and B - it is C, something different.
