The discussion is not about whether knowing C requires the knowledge of A and B, it is whether the knowledge of the absence of C requires the prior knowledge of C or not (pratiyogi jnAna being mandatory for abhAva jnAna).

  

Even if the knowledge of the absence of C required the prior knowledge of A or B (it does), that still does not prove that it requires the prior knowledge of C.
