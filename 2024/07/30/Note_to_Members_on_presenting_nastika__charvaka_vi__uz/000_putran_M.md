Namaskaram,

  

I see views coming in our forum, including those of charvakas to "science" to "arya nagarjuna", not usually as that of purvapakshins to the tradition but as if we should accept them as being either obvious or as coherent with Vedanta or just as valid alternate philosophies. They are not considered valid here, if they are opposed to Veda sampradaya. Frankly, many members are not going to be able to discriminate the sampradaya views from such potential adulterations that are often presented with a casual conviction. Since the goal of our forum is to aid*astikas*in their study of advaita vedanta*as taught in the Vedic tradition and Shankara sampradaya,* members have to take greater care in making explicit thepurvapaksha(note, in principle, "wrong understanding" here) views as being purvapakshato the tradition (at least to the extent we know them to be so) and not as something aligned with "essential" siddhanta or as obvious, rational, up-to-date facts that Vedantins should accept going forward.

  

Say, if you wish, for example, "This imo is a purvapakshato the tradition but I find it rational ..." Then, the forum can facilitate not necessarily a debate but a discussion where astikas/scholars can attempt to present the uttarapaksha. But at the least we would be in a position where everyone knows the views that are opposed to the tradition - whether they wish to believe them or not.
