Kalyaanavrusti Mahaabhiyaana - Learning Resources  
  
Pranaams,  
  
Kalyaanavrusti Mahaabhiyaana is part of year long celebration of the fiftieth year of sanyasa of His Holines Jagadguru Shankaracharya Mahasannidhanam Sri Sri Bharati Theertha Mahaswamyji in the name of "Suvarnabharati".  
  
Given here are the resources made available for learning the three Stotras choosen by Jagadguru Sannidhanam Sri Vidhu Shekhara Bharati Mahaswami for this Mahaabhiyaana.  
  
You can find  
- Stotra Scripts of different languages in PDF format.  
- Audio files of the stotras.  
- Student-Teacher format videos for learning and praticse.
