It goes without saying that the principle of diagnosing one's inner gunas via varna and hence the right type of karma that is the antidote for that jiva's mind state is not at all an injunction for people to develop high-low feelings towards one another. Each does the svadharma appropriate for him/her and everyone in society respects each other for fulfillingtheir roles. It is simple and worked well tosustain Hindu society; now everything is topsy-turvy due to infiltration of wrong ideas.

  

For Swami-ji's comments, there is nothing wrong with his forwarding a variant understanding of varnaand dharma in general; and how things were or ought to be. What we would rather want is to allow the next person and group to follow their religion. Suppose orthodox sampradaya believes in certain smritis that RKM or Swamiji considers to be outdated or belonging to another time. Let that be. Their sampradayas then differ on this issue. Allow each to follow their religion; they can have their tarka on equal terms, like we and dvaitinshave on siddhanta. The problem always comes because of the abrahamic mentality, adopted by all these anti-varna characters, that they know what is dharma and satya **and** it is their duty to impose their "religion" on everyone and eradicate those who believe otherwise.
