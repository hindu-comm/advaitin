Swami Satyadev's Sadhan Samar is an exhaustive commentary on Devi Mahatmyam. Its Hindi version is available at [https://archive.org/details/SadhanSamarPart3Of3/Sadhan%20Samar%20Part%201%20of%203/](https://archive.org/details/SadhanSamarPart3Of3/Sadhan%20Samar%20Part%201%20of%203/)

  

English translation can be purchased from Mortilal Benarsidass or from online sellers like exoticindia.

[https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/sadhan-samar-battles-in-sacred-quest-devi-mahatmya-glory-of-goddess-spiritual-commentary-on-sri-sri-candi-or-durga-saptasati-by-brahmarsi-satyadeva-set-of-3-volumes-bag708/](https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/sadhan-samar-battles-in-sacred-quest-devi-mahatmya-glory-of-goddess-spiritual-commentary-on-sri-sri-candi-or-durga-saptasati-by-brahmarsi-satyadeva-set-of-3-volumes-bag708/)

  

VS Agrawala's Glorification of the Great Goddess, contains English translation, intro and detailed annotations.

[https://archive.org/details/devimahatmyamglo0000unse](https://archive.org/details/devimahatmyamglo0000unse)
