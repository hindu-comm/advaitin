Hare Krishna



As per our understanding both avidyA and mAya are not synonymous hence Atmika means coupling only. This has been said several times earlier also. At some places ahamkara explained as avidyA which is coupled with avyakta / avyAkruta. For example : ahaMkAraH eti avidyA saMyuktaM avyaktaM. So in our book mAya can never ever be paryAya pada for avidyA. At some places bhAshyakAra the word avyakta being identified with avidyA when applied to jeeva and same avyakta identified as ‘mAya’ when applied to hiraNyagarbha this is the glaring bifurcation between two different aspects. Is bhAshyakAra making the fault of such extreme self contradiction in the same sentence?? One is applied as mAya and another is applied as avidyA with respect to jeeva and hiraNyagarbha respectively?? Somewhere I had read these two words avidyA and prakruti have entirely different root and cannot be equated at any stretch of imagination. According to shankara vedAnta Ishwara’s mAya and jeeva’s avidyA is settled issue. Jagat is effect of mAya (hence mAya is also called jagadbeeja) which is same for any sane person whereas adhyAsa is effect of avidyA (jnAnAbhAva) which exists only in ajnAni in his antaHkaraNa. mAya is food and avidyA is poison. Without knowing this we are talking about vishAnna as visha only. There is absolutely no worthy purpose served by equating mAya with avidyA in shankaraadvaita.





Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
