Hare Krishna



At one place (in sUtra bhAshya or bruhadAraNyaka I don’t remember) bhAshyakAra observes avyAkruta itself is brahman. Here the question was asked : at one place it has been said that avyAkruta (naama rUpa beeja) became vyAkruta (nAma rUpa) and at another place it has been said that paramAtma made avyAkruta into vyAkruta. How can this be acceptable??
For this bhAshyakAra clarifies paramAtma himself in the form of avyAkruta jagat so there is no dOsha in asserting like this. If this avyAkruta is mUlAvidyA then paramAtma svarUpa itself is mUlAvidyA and we have to load the avidyA to even Ishwara tattva as Lord says mama svarUpa madeeya mAya. And mAyin parameshwara becomes avidyAvanta. Oh, parameshwara where are we going!!??



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

bhaskar



praNAms

Hare Krishna



As per our understanding both avidyA and mAya are not synonymous hence Atmika means coupling only. This has been said several times earlier also. At some places ahamkara explained as avidyA which is coupled with avyakta / avyAkruta. For example : ahaMkAraH eti avidyA saMyuktaM avyaktaM. So in our book mAya can never ever be paryAya pada for avidyA. At some places bhAshyakAra the word avyakta being identified with avidyA when applied to jeeva and same avyakta identified as ‘mAya’ when applied to hiraNyagarbha this is the glaring bifurcation between two different aspects. Is bhAshyakAra making the fault of such extreme self contradiction in the same sentence?? One is applied as mAya and another is applied as avidyA with respect to jeeva and hiraNyagarbha respectively?? Somewhere I had read these two words avidyA and prakruti have entirely different root and cannot be equated at any stretch of imagination. According to shankara vedAnta Ishwara’s mAya and jeeva’s avidyA is settled issue. Jagat is effect of mAya (hence mAya is also called jagadbeeja) which is same for any sane person whereas adhyAsa is effect of avidyA (jnAnAbhAva) which exists only in ajnAni in his antaHkaraNa. mAya is food and avidyA is



poison. Without knowing this we are talking about vishAnna as visha only. There is absolutely no worthy purpose served by equating mAya with avidyA in shankaraadvaita.





Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!

bhaskar
