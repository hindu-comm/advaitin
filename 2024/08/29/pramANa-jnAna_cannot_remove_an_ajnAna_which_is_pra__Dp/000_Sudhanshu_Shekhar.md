Hari Om respected members,  
  
the famous anumAna presented in MANDUkya kArikA Anandagiri TIkA 1.2 and also in Advaita Siddhi to prove bhAvarUpatva of ajnAna is as under:  
  
देवदत्तप्रमा, तन्निष्ठप्रमाप्रागभावातिरिक्ता‍नादिप्रध्वंसिनी, प्रमात्वाद्, यज्ञदत्तप्रमावत्। \[As per Anandagiri TIkA, MK 1.2\]

  
While the anumAna is crystal clear, a slight modification in the anumAna can also be done which will prove that ajnAna cannot be pramAtri-nishTha.  
  
देवदत्तप्रमा,देवदत्तनिष्ठ-अज्ञान-अतिरिक्त-अनादिप्रध्वंसिनी, प्रमात्वाद्, यज्ञदत्तप्रमावत्। \[Modified anumAna\]

  

This anumAna proves that Devadatta-pramA, just like Yagyadatta-pramA cannot remove an ajnAna which is located in Devadatta. Therefore, the ajnAna which is removed by Devadatta-pramA has to be located at some place other than Devadatta.

  

This is well in accordance with the siddhAnta that ajnAna is located in Brahman. And ghaTa-ajnAna is located in ghaTa-avachchhinna-chaitanya and not in Devadatta.

  

However, it is only true in respect of abhAna-ApAdaka-AvaraNa, which is accepted to cover vishaya-avachchhinna-chaitanya. In case of asattva-ApAdaka-AvaraNa, which is removed by parOksha-pramA as well, the AvaraNa is accepted to be pramAtri-nishTha. Thus, there would seem to be a *prima facie*contradiction with the following anumAna:

  

देवदत्तपरोक्षप्रमा, देवदत्तनिष्ठ-असत्त्वापादकावरण-अतिरिक्त-अनादिप्रध्वंसिनी, परोक्षप्रमात्वाद्, यज्ञदत्तपरोक्षप्रमावत्। \[Modified anumAna\]

  

Learned members may kindly share their views as to how can the apparent contradiction be resolved.

  

I would also like to mention the SIddhAnta Bindu commentary, page 115
-[https://archive.org/details/SiddhantaBinduShriMadhusudanaSarasvati/page/n139/mode/2up?view=theater](https://archive.org/details/SiddhantaBinduShriMadhusudanaSarasvati/page/n139/mode/2up?view=theater)

  

Here, NyAyaratnAvalIkAra says -अत्रेदं विभावनीयम्। असत्त्वापादकाज्ञानस्यापि विषयावच्छिन्नचिदाश्रयताकत्वम्।  

  

So, one way the contradiction can be easily resolved is by accepting that asattva-ApAdaka-ajnAna is also vishaya-avachchhinna-chaitanya-nishTha like abhAna-ApAdaka-AvaraNa. Is there any other way possible?

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
