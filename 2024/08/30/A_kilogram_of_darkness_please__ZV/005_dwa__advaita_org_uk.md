Dear Sudhanshu-ji,



Apologies, but as soon as I see the word ‘pratiyogi’, I switch off. And there is no way that I can spend hours trying to translate all of your Sanskrit and logic into terms that I can understand. I speak neither Sanskrit nor symbolic logic. To my mind, this is a topic that can be discussed at a level that everyone can follow, because it relates to everyday experience and the language that we all use. (I bet you read and admire writers such as Vimuktātman and Maṇḍana Miśra!) You must concede that, although such an approach often puts a stop to arguments, it does not necessarily win them.



Best wishes,

Dennis
