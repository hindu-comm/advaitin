it is not about winning or losing but about learning what the advaita siddhAnta holds.

  

I will try presenting my own understanding as to how I got convinced in the heart of hearts that darkness is triguNatmaka. Let us refer to drishTi-srishTi-vAda which posits absolute identity between waking and dream. Aitareya Upanishads says - trayah swapnAh. I am sure you would be well aware of this.

  

Now, if we analyse the dream-darkness, is it logical to say that it is dream-prakAsha-abhAva? No. Whatever is seen in a dream, be it light or darkness or pot or pot-abhAva or whatever -- is all avidyA-kArya and hence triguNAtmaka.

  

Dream-light cannot be placed at a higher pedestal than dream-darkness. No one is contingent on another. Both are seen, hence both are illusory and hence both are at par being triguNAtmaka avidyA-kArya.

  

Waking being identical to dream impleswaking-darkness to be also triguNAtmaka avidyA-kArya.

  

SrishTi-drishTi-vAda also holds the same but uses complex logic as I presented. I agree that it is difficult to even understand. But we need to put in requisite effort if we need to have clarity.
