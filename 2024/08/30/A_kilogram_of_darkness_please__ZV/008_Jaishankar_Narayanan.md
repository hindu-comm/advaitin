Namaste,

  

I think one needs to first have clarity with respect to how we interpret Veda as a means of knowledge. We all know that Shankara has said that even 100 Shruti vakyas cannot contradict pratyaksha anubhava that fire is hot and even if Shruti says 'Fire is cold' it has to reinterpreted in a way that it does not contradict our experience.

  

Now Shruti clearly says Darkness is an existent entity. Shankara and Sureshvara agree and interpret it as an existent entity. Should we accept this as it is or sugarcoat it as figurative / metaphorical / metaphysical etc.?

  

The current scientific consensus is that darkness is the absence of photons (or any radiation). But is it enough to reinterpret the Shruti? Hundred years back if we went by Scientific consensus we would have reinterpreted shrutis revealing that space is created. Shankara in Taittiriya Bhashya also says time also is created along with space. Space-time was considered to be absolute by classical physics . Even our Naiyayikas consider space, time etc as eternal. Even today I saw an argument by a Vishitadvaiti that atma cannot be bothnitya and sukhasvarupa, as all sukha experienced in the world is anityam and space and time are nityam (according to nyAya) but not sukhasvarupa. Should we override the Shruti based on such logic?

  

In this case I will still go with the Shruti and our acharyas as I think Science has not yet matured enough to understand darkness and avidya. Even Scientists talk about Dark matter and Dark energy. They sayDark matter is entirely invisible, emitting no light or energy, making it undetectable by conventional sensors and detectors.Its existence is inferred because, without it, the behavior of stars, planets, and galaxies would be inexplicable. Further they say Dark matter constitutes over 80% of all matter in the universe. So darkness may very well be the dark matter which is an existent entity and light may just be something which is opposed to that or which neutralises it temporarily.

  

So if you accept Veda as a pramana then just accept that darkness is an existent entity.

  

with love and prayers,

Jaishankar
