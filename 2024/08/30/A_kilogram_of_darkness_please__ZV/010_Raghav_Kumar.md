Namaste Sudhanshu ji

  

Kindly check the following.  

To assert abhAva-pratiyogIjnAna-ajanya-pratyaxa-viShayatvaM for darkness may not seem obvious since we have knowledge of both light and darkness since we can recollect.

  

But there is a clear difference in our experience of any abhAva like pot-abhAva etc. vis-a-vis darkness. For example, when I assert pot-abhAva on the adhikaraNa like the ground, the thought of pot also necessarily comes - but not so in the case of darkness. That is our anubhava of pratyaxa whereby we can assert abhAva-pratiyogIjnAna-ajanya-pratyaxa-viShayatvaM for darkness because we don't have to think of light when we experience darkness.
