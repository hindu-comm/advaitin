Namaste Raghav ji.



> But there is a clear difference in our experience of any abhAva like
> pot-abhAva etc. vis-a-vis darkness. For example, when I assert
> pot-abhAva on the adhikaraNa like the ground, the thought of pot also
> necessarily comes - but not so in the case of darkness. That is our
> anubhava of pratyaxa whereby we can assert
> abhAva-pratiyogIjnAna-ajanya-pratyaxa-viShayatvaM for darkness
> because we don't have to think of light when we experience darkness.

  

True. abhAva-jnAna is always pratiyogI-jnAna-janya. Since darkness-jnAna is not pratiyogI-jnAna-janya because we can directly perceive it, it shows darkness is pratiyogI-jnAna-ajanya. Hence, darkness is not abhAva.
