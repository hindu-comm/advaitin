> I believe that the original (scriptural) prakriyā-s and Śaṅkara’s
> bhāṣya-s use everyday (at the time) understanding of what can be seen
> and reasoned upon to bring about this realization. **They do not
> require complex logic** or modern scientific understanding to cut
> through the examples that they use.

  

This is simply untrue. Anyone reading the bhashya to understand its nuances would require a great deal of logical sophistication — at the time they were composed, and now too.

  

This does not mean that all of these complexities are actually necessary for most seekers; they most certainly are not. They may well be obstacles for many. Clearly a lot of it was written for essentially academic and ideological purposes, to provide grounding in a theological war against Advaita’s opponents.
