Namaste Dennis ji,

  

I don't have any disagreement with most of what you say, but I think you missed the point of Advaita if you think Advaita accepts any absence other than unholy compounds like vandhyAputra. All specific absence points to only the presence of something, leading finally to the sad-AtmA.

  

What wesay is that darkness is not merely the absence of light (that too visible light if you go by human vision) but always a presence. I only talked about dark matter because even if you remove all the detectable matter including light, there can be the presence of dark matter. So when shruti talks about tamas it is talking about the presence of a covering ( as opposed to empty space, which is what you are implying). I don't think science has had the last word on darkness and you seem to somehow be very certain about your ideas of darkness which also explains your use of sarcasm here. I don't think there is anything called empty space if we go by the definition of bhashyakAra in taittiriya Upanishad Bhashya that akAsha is अवकाशप्रदात्राकाशम्
avakAsha-pradAtr-AkAsham (that which accommodates). So all space should have something which it accommodates, whether you are able to detect it or not.

  

with love and prayers,
