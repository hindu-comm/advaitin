Respected Dennis ji.

  

You have presented a common sense understanding of absence. That is refuted in advaita. That is precisely what BhAshyakAra did. His anumAna is actually very simply worded.

  

Let me modify BhAshyakAra's anumAna a little bit and word it in English.

  

1\. "mutual non-existence of pot" is composed of three guNAs, being cloth.  
  
2. "absolute-non-existence-of-pot, prior-non-existence-of-pot and non-existence-due-to-destruction-of-pot" , are composed of three guNAs . On account of having transactability through pot. Like mutual-non-existence-of-pot.  
  
3. Similarly, "all non-existences with a counter-positive" are composed of three guNAs.

*  *

This is as simple as it can be worded. If you can refute it, please go ahead. If you still cannot, I give up as I cannot simplify it further.
