One passage is the key

"Again these are words of BhAshyakAra - घटेन व्यपदिश्यमानात्वात्. One uses pot-prAk-abhAva, pot-atyanya-abhAva, pot-dhvansa-abhAva in terms of pot."

  

This key word (vyapadishyamAnatvAt) implies, even "horns of a hare" (atyantAbhAva) implies a notion, no doubt, but uses the terms of 'horns' and a 'hare' and so is bhAvAtmaka - this is what bhAShya is saying. We are not saying bhAva but bhAvarUpa or bhAvAtmaka.
