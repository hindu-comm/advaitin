Namaste Raghav ji.

  

BhAshya is silent about nirvishesha-abhAva, which are without pratiyogI, like horns of hare, which are nihswarUpa and vastu-shUnya.

  

BhAshya is talking about vishesha-abhAva, which has a pratiyogI, like pot-abhAva. BhAshya proves their bhAvAtmakatA, like cloth.

  

So, nirvishesha-abhAva is beyond the purview of ghaTa-bhAshya.

  

Regards.
