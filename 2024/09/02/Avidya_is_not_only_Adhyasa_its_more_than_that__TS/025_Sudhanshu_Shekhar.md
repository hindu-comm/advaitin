Namaste Raghav ji.

  

ajnAna-adhyAsa, which is kAraNa-adhyAsa, is anAdi and it ceases on Brahma-jnAna. It is present in deep sleep.

  

All kArya-adhyAsa, starting from aham-adhyAsa, are sAdi and they cease in deep sleep.

  

Regards.
