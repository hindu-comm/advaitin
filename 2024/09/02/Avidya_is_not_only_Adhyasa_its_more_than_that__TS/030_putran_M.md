Namaskaram Raghav-ji,

  

>   
> kAraNa-adhyAsa is anAdi, not kArya-adhyAsa which is sAdi.
>
>   

  

I assume this does not contradict the satkaryavada post of Venkataraghavan-ji. My understanding: The karana is anadi and thekarya is 'known' in thekarana prior to its (nimitta) manifestation. This karya-jnana is anadi in the karana that is the upadana for that jnana and the karya-adhyasa (being referred) is specifically the nimitta-karya of the karana where the jnana is uncovered or brought to cognition.
