Namaskaram,

  

When we identify the pots (pot-spaces), the "samanya" (using Subbu-ji's reference) is Clay and the visesha is the pot.

  

Clay can denote Ishvara who imagines/projects/appears as the pot-world.

  

Clay can denote Maya-shakti and Space+Clay is Ishvara.

  

Clay can denote Avidya that superimposes the pot-world onto Space.

  

Perhaps we can look at Space as the samanya-adhishtana - that is true in all standpoints, the non-dual Reality, whereas Clay is the visesha-adhishtana that is coeval to the standpoint of the pot-world - and this pot-world and its samanya Clay are mithya.
