Namaskar Ram Chandranji,

  

I have had this 'feeling' for the longest time. The feeling is that when we read about other people's experiences on 'the path' toward self-realization, we can only understand this as a particular person's journey. This is the same no matter if the person is SHankaracharya or any of the realized people that we take guidance from.  

  

From there was born this idea that there is no 'wrong' way to progress to the goal. Each person's goal is their own - and everyone that gives us a means of proceeding toward that goal can only provide us with POINTERS. That's it. Everything we have is a pointer. Thus, just like in software we can have multiple pointers (potentially infinite) to the same item (whatever it might be), similarly we have pointers from great masters. But each one of us HAS TO FIND OUR OWN WAY. Everything else is merely noise.Some of the noise could be signal - others could be pure noise. It is for each one of us to act as a sieve, and determine which is which.

  

Humbly,

Sai
