[[Akilesh Ayyar	2024-09-11, 10:28:14 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/m1U9_ngNkNg)]]

The “full elephant” is not an object. It cannot be perceived and it cannot be imagined or conceived, and so it can hardly be said to be the exclusive possession of any one religion or doctrine.

  

Doctrines can be disputed, but doctrines are only pointers, and should never be confused with that to which they point.

  

The ultimate doctrines are after all the ones which dismantle themselves.

  

Thus ekam sad vipra bahudha vadanti and “The Tao that can be told is not the eternal Tao.  
The name that can be named is not the eternal name.”

  

Many of our great sages have warned seekers about falling into the pits of futile philosophical disputation.

  

Akilesh
