The “full elephant” is not an object. It cannot be perceived and it cannot be imagined or conceived, and so it can hardly be said to be the exclusive possession of any one religion or doctrine.



praNAms Sri Akhilesh prabhuji

Hare Krishna



- The other name for our sanAtana vaidika dharma is ‘paripUrNa
  darshana’. In all seriousness we claim that ours is ‘paripUrNa’
  philosophy to deduce the indescribable. For others it may sound like
  a bigoted statement, for us it is a fact 😊
