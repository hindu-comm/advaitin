[[V Subrahmanian	2024-09-13, 13:34:58 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/rexsXdsvGNo)]]

For Shankara a Vaishnava is the one who is an aspirant of the Brahman that is vyaapana sheela, an etymology he has given for the word Vishnu in the Kathopanishad for the mantra Tad Vishnoh paramam padam. The Vishnavas of the post Shankara systems are the devotees of saguna deity Vishnu.
