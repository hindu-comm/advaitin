Namaste,



> Let us say I have a major surgery next week. I am not a doctor and I
> do not know the details of how the surgery is performed.  

  

God forbid! You could have taken a better example. Anyway, let us proceed.



> So, here, my ignorance is jnAna-abhAva.

  

No. Your ignorance is not jnAna-abhAva. An abhAva cannot be known unless you know the pratiyogI. If I ask you, do you have a tuoli in your home? You cannot answer it. Because you don'tknow what tuoli is. That means, to aver tuoli-abhAva, you need to have tuoli-jnAna.

  

So, in order to have surgery-jnAna-abhAva, you need to have surgery-jnAna-jnAna which will imply you already possess surgery-jnAna. That is self-contradiction.

  

So, surgery-ignorance does not mean surgery-jnAna-abhAva.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
