Namaste Sri Sudhanshu Swamy,

  

Let us say there is absolutely nothing in my home. It is fully empty. Then, can't I say that there is no tuoli in my home, without knowing what a tuoli is?

  

Or, let us say I don't know what a car is. And I don't have any vehicles in my house. You ask me if there is a car in my house. I say - please tell me what is a car. You give a basic description - that a car is a machine that can help me move from one place to another place quickly. Now, this is not complete car-jnAna, because this description also fits a bike, bus etc. So, without possessing complete car-jnAna, can't I say, there is no car in my house?
