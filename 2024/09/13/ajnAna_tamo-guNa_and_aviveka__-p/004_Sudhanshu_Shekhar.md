Namaste sir.

  

> Let us say there is absolutely nothing in my home. It is fully empty.
> Then, can't I say that there is no tuoli in my home, without knowing
> what a tuoli is?  

  

No sir. Because despite having nothing, you room has space, walls, air, bacteria, hydrogen, oxygen. Tuoli can be any of these.

> 

  

> Or, let us say I don't know what a car is. And I don't have any
> vehicles in my house. You ask me if there is a car in my house. I
> say - please tell me what is a car. You give a basic description -
> that a car is a machine that can help me move from one place to
> another place quickly. Now, this is not complete car-jnAna, because
> this description also fits a bike, bus etc. So, without possessing
> complete car-jnAna, can't I say, there is no car in my house?

  

To the extent you know what car is, to that extent you know its abhAva. The example of tuoli clinches the issue. Rest you can analyse yourself.

  

Sir, it is a fundamental concept of NyAya. I suggest perusal of texts of NyAya, if deemed fit by your good self.
