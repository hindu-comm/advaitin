When avidyA and avidyA-kArya are said to be sat-asat-vilakshaNa, sat refers to Brahman and asat refers to tuchchha i.e. horns of hare.

  

When Brahman is said to be sat-asat-vilakshaNa, sat refers to bhAvAtmaka-avidyA-kArya such as pot and asat refers to abhAvAtmaka-avidyA-kArya such as pot-abhAva.

  

Do you think that "horns of hare" is covered within the ambit of word "asat" when Brahman is said to be sat-asat-vilakshaNa?

  

I think it is covered -- because horns of hare is also nAsti-buddhi-anugata-pratyaya-vishaya.
