If it helps, I would consider this usage of anirvachaniya for Brahman as non-different from saying "Ishvara is anirvachaniya/mithya", since Ishvara is Brahman seen in conjunction with the limiting adjunct Maya (that, when identified as if distinctly, we know to be anirvachaniya).
