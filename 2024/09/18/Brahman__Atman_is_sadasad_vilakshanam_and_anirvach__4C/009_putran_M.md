**"...after it is posted one can ask him to provide the English meaning for many members to understand and participate in the discussions."**

  

Thereforethat will be the guideline going forward. If you write in Sanskrit exclusively, please provide the English translation as well. Here also, I would request Lokesh-ji to give the translation for the benefit of other members.
