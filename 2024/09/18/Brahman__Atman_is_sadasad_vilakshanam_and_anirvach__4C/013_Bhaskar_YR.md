Hare Krishna



Can we have name called ring and its shape without ‘gold’?? nAma rUpa when seen with gold drushti it is not different from gold. When seen independently apart from gold ‘this nAma rUpa’ do not exist. nAma rUpa jagat is asat (changing its colour each and every second) but it does not have any independent existence apart from its kAraNa. Hence shruti says satyanchAnrutancha satyamabhavat yadidaM kiNcha. Hence bhAshyakAra says : sarvaM cha nAmarUpAdi sadAtmanaiva satyaM vikArajAtaM svatastu anrutam eva. If the gold in the ring, say, destroyed, where is the question of its existence as effect : ring?? But ring with the ever existence of gold in it can change (or can be made) its naama rUpa as bangle. So with the existence of gold there is nAma rUpa like ring bangle etc. So nAma rUpa depends on gold but gold is not depend on nAma rUpa and gold IS abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa for the nAma rUpa. Though we speak a lot about non-difference of gold and ring we are talking this within the scope of the effect is only the form of cause and NOT cause is NOT a form of effect. If we understand this correctly then nAma rUpa which is Ishwara srushti cannot be treated as separate entity called mithya but vyAvahArika satya which has its roots in kUtastha nitya satya brahman.
