Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

That is my loose translation. I will state my general understanding.

  

Yes, namarupa is not a *separate* entity; but if we feel the need to talk about them, then we are identifying Brahman through the lens of namarupa duality. This is pertaining to the vyavaharika standpoint wherein Brahman is seen/known/conditioned as Ishvara. This duality/division based identification is adhyasa on nirguna, nirvisesha Brahman. In the context of this knowledge of reality, namarupa is not asat because we experience it in the plane of cognition and not sat either because again it is akin to adhyasa on Sat. asti-bhaati-priyam (sat-chit-anandam/satyam-jnanam-anantam) are the constant pointers to nirguna Brahman, that shine in our awareness even as we see Brahman divided as Ishvara and His namarupa srushti.
