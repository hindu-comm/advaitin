Namaskaram,

  

Nama-rupa subsists only and always in the realm of distinctions (sajatiya / vijatiya / svagata bheda) & limitations (desha / kala / vishaya). Where distinctions / limitations are perceived, there nama-rupa-viseshana will & does exist. This is dvaita & vyavaharika.

In a completely distinction-lessentity / state (or whatever / however we label it) there is no more possibility of any nama-rupa whatsoever. This is paramarthika.

  

As a crude example, imagine being submerged in the middle of a deep ocean with no other sea creatures / entities / water currents / or even light penetrating through. What nama-rupa can be attributed to the object of our perception? However, the only distinction that exists is the nomenclature of 'I' and 'this'. Hence this example too is only vyavaharika.

  

The realm of distinctions or the perception of distinctions is *only* vyavaharika and *is not* paramarthika. Whether one sees a gold ring as fundamentally gold or as a ring, as long as that piece of gold is perceived in terms of distinctions or limitations, it is vyavahara drishti alone of a jnani & ajnani respectively. It is not paramarthika. When this world is perceived as fundamentally non-different from Atman-Brahman, this is still only a vyavahara drishti of a jnani. The realization of distinction-less Atman-Brahman alone is paramartha drishti.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
