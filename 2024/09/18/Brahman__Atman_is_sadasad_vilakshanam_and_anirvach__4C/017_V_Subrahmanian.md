अद्वैतमनिर्वाच्यंब्रह्म । Tripadvibhuti Upanishad. It says: Brahman is Advaitam, anirvaachyam.

  

[https://www.tititudorancea.com/z/tripadvibhutimahanarayana_upanishad_sanskrit_devanagari_with.htm](https://www.tititudorancea.com/z/tripadvibhutimahanarayana_upanishad_sanskrit_devanagari_with.htm)

  

This upanishad also says Brahman is sadasadvilakshanam.  
  
अनिर्वचनीयज्योतिः सर्वव्यापकंनिरतिशयानन्दलक्षणं परमाकाशम् - says Brahman is anirvachaneeyam: Mandala Brahmana upanishad.  

  

The above are listed in the 108 Upanishads enumerated in the Muktikopanishad. All these 108 have been commented upon by Sri Upanishad Brahma Yogin, a contemporary of Sri Sadasiva Brahmendra.
