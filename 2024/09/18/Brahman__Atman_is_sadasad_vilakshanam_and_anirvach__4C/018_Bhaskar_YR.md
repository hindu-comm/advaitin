Hare Krishna



Yes, namarupa is not a *separate* entity; but if we feel the need to talk about them, then we are identifying Brahman through the lens of namarupa duality.



- Yes and nAma rUpa is something like negotiator who conveys or helps us
  to realize brahman is pragnAna ghana. The brahman is the
  imperceivable cause of the world, janmAdyasya yataH, it is THAT
  through which the creation and the dissolution of jagat happens.
  Realizing the nonduality which is the real existing tattva behind all
  nAma rUpa duality is called Atmaikatva darshana or sarvAtma bhAva.
  And this is what explained in geeta as well “ PunditAH
  samadarshinaH”.



This is pertaining to the vyavaharika standpoint wherein Brahman is seen/known/conditioned as Ishvara.



- The brahmaikatva jnAna is not vyAvahAra abhAva jnAna but vyavahAra
  bAdhita jnAna and again it is not avasthAteeta lOkAntara jnAna it is
  to be realized here in this very life. Again it is not some
  mysterious experience in mind inert state called samAdhi to make it
  happen in one particular state of mind. It is pratyabhigna or bhUta
  vastu Vishaya jnAna ( the knowledge of what already existing). So,
  IMO here in this realization brahman is NOT seen as conditioned but
  brahman is everything i.e, jeeva-jagat-Ishwara. When one realizes
  this ultimate truth (samyag darshana) there is avidyA nivrutti and
  there is no punarjanma says bhAshyakAra : samyagdarshanaphalaM
  avidyAdisaMsArabeeja nivrutti dvAreNa janmAbhAvaH.



This duality/division based identification is adhyasa on nirguna, nirvisesha Brahman.



- Yes, this is adhyAsa but in the Atmaikatva darshana this adhyAsa
  drushti will not be there. sarvAtma drushti fetches us the knowledge
  that anna, annaada shlOkakarta everything one and the same.
  bhOktru-bhOgya (enjoyer – things being enjoyed) both are different one
  can become another coz. for both brahman is the svarUpa and even
  though in vyavahAra both are different and one cannot become another,
  IT IS NOT DIFFERENT from brahman. These two statements I am not
  saying but bhAshyakAra himself saying this in sUtra bhAshya.



In the context of this knowledge of reality, namarupa is not asat because we experience it in the plane of cognition and not sat either because again it is akin to adhyasa on Sat. asti-bhaati-priyam (sat-chit-anandam/satyam-jnanam-anantam) are the constant pointers to nirguna Brahman



- Yes, satyaM, jnAnam and anantaM are the constant pointer to
  pAramArthika satya brahman which is equally applicable to jagat as
  well as per your previous definition. And now the question is : how
  the jagat is also asti-bhAti-priyaM like brahman?? How can we
  determine this jagat is also satyaM, jnAnam and anantaM without the
  help of nAma rUpa which is the very essence of manifested jagat?? To
  answer this question bhAshyakAra says : yadi hi nAmarUpe na
  vyAkreeyate tadA asyAtmanO nirupAdhikaM rUpaM pragnAnaghanAkhyaM na
  pratikyAyeta…if only nAma rUpa were not distinguished then the
  adjunctless (upAdhirahita) nature of brahman would have not been
  known. Having said this we are not too eager to prove the satyatva of
  nAma rUpa but we are just saying its content is nothing but brahman.
  In short, as I said earlier, the nAma rUpa viewed independently by
  themselves have no content. The content is only their cause. Which
  is kUtastha nitya. Let there is thousands of pots, jars, pitchers our
  concern is all about their content and realization of that unchanging
  content. vAchAraMbhaNam vikArO nAmadheyaM mruttiketyeva satyam.



, that shine in our awareness even as we see Brahman divided as Ishvara and His namarupa srushti.



Ø Yes, the shine in our awareness is called Atmaikatva darshanaM, samyAk darshana, sarvAtma bhAva.
