In my understanding, the paramarthika standpoint is not a sleep/samadhi state where duality is simply not cognized though existing in some sense or other.



- Yes both sleep / samAdhi states are not free from avidyA. avidyA
  (jnAnAbhAva) in sleep and vipareeta darshana deliberately and
  forcefully suppressed in mind inert state of samAdhi. However,
  scriptures and bhAshyakAra say the sushupti is the only state which
  stands for the universal experience to prove brahmaikatvaM. The jeeva
  remains here as brahman. Though jeeva is always identical with
  brahman, due to his association and disassociation with upAdhi-s he
  appears to be different from Atman/brahman. As these upAdhis’
  disassociation happens in sushupti it is said that the jeeva / soul
  merges in brahman during sleep state. Anyway that jnAna is ‘absent’
  in that state and adhyAsa yet to go in other states, it is prescribed
  the sAdhana…otherwise shruti-bhAshya would have suggested : have some
  sleeping pills and get realization 😊 



Namarupa is asat in paramarthika; guna and vyavahara are abhava/asat/impossible/unavailable for consideration in Nirguna Brahman.



- Yes, shruti also says neha nAnAsti kiMchana (there is no duality
  whatsoever in nirvishesha brahman) but duality cannot exist apart from
  brahman is also equally true. Hence the statement when nAma rUpa seen
  independently it is asat but when it is seen in its svarUpa
  (adhishtAnaM / substance) it is satyaM eva. And without the help of
  nAma rUpa jagat we cannot determine the svarUpa of brahman. Shruti-s
  like yatOvA emAni bhUtAni jAyante, yena jAtAni jeevanti,
  ….tadvijijnAsasva, tadbrahmeti (taittireeya upanishat) and sUtra-s
  like janmAdyasya yataH (very second sUtra of brahma sUtra) etc. saying
  and emphasizing same thing.



This jnanam of Nirguna Brahman if projected within the vyavaharika standpoint will have to be vyavahara-badhita and not vyavahara-abhava - because the jnana itself must be conditioned to this standpoint where vyavahara (i.e. duality in some sense) is not asat. What this means is, the vyavahara-badhita jnana of our standpoint is itself realized as a pointer to vyavahara-abhava of nirguna brahman.



Ø Yes exactly. But now the question is whether literally vyavahAra abhAva jnAna of nirguNa brahman happens only after attaining some peculiar state or after the physical death of jnAni or when living in his physical body itself it is the realization of svarUpa jnAna ?? IMO bhAshyakAra / shruti talks about this Jeevan mukti / or sadyO mukti.
Hence there is no difference between svarUpa nirvishesha jnAna and Atmaikatva Samyak darshana.



Then, the followup question is: Do we consider the vyavaharika standpoint (and its satya, involving a duality of namarupa-denotations and Ishvara the All as adhishtanam and cause) and the paramarthika standpoint (and its satya, that is nirguna, turiya Brahman) as distinct but equally real: two sides of the same coin, one notinterfering and diminishing the knowledge of other in any fundamental manner?



Ø Yes, that is what I understand. It is not even two faces of the same coin it is ekam eva adviteeya jnAnam. There is absolutely no difference between Samyak darsha and svarUpa nirvishesha jnAna. Hence somewhere bhAshyakAra says : kshetrajna (jeeva) and paramAtma are not two different entities but it is ONE and ONLY One. The fruit of Samyak darshana is sarvAtmatva. samyakdarshanakAlameva tat phalaM savAtmatvaM darshayati. And this sarvAtmatva is nothing but Atmaikatva and this Atmaikatva is what is called nirvishesha Atma svarUpa jnAna.
bhAshyakAra elsewhere gives the example of folded limbs – Devadatta, stretched limbs -Devadatta…both are that same Devadatta only and one should not thing that there are two different Devadatta-s😊And the main aim of shruti and Acharya is advocating this Atmaikatva vidyA and that is what bhAshyakAra declares at the very beginning of sUtra bhAshya rather in adhyAsa bhAshya : AtmaikatvavidyApratipattaye sarve vedAntA Arabhyante.



Or is turiya the satya and namarupa-associated Ishvara the mithya?



- Prabhuji, shruti somewhere says : the brahman itself presented as
  object of word and thought such as ‘this’, and this object of word is
  not non-Atman (anAtma) as for this object of word brahman is the ONLY
  abhinna nimittopadaana kAraNa. Hence shruti warns : if anybody sees
  the world in fron as non-Atman then the world would reject him and
  throw out him as he is ineligible for mOksha. Hence I am afraid to
  say Ishwara srushti is just like snake on the rope i.e. mithya. It is
  satya and good enough to do vyavahAra, good enough to do our sAdhana,
  good enough to realize it is not asarvam, anatman but sarvam and
  brahman only.





Does knowledge of nirguna Brahman demote the vyavaharika standpoint to the status of imagination, adhyasa



- Yes brahmaikatva jnAna eradicates the Kalpita bhrAnti jnAna i.e.
  adhyAsa / misconception.



whose cause Maya is anirvachaniya?



- For adhyAsa mAya is not the cause but provisionally / metamorphically
  jnAnAbhAva is the cause. mAya is the stage. We are not saying for
  seeing the snake in place rope, rope is the reason but rope is the
  Asare for seeing the snake.



The bAdhitam should be such that it does not leave any room to add a clause "not abhava"; if you are tempted to do the latter, then the right clause is "mithya".



Ø Not able to understand linking jnAnAbhAva to bAdhitAnuvrutti of jnAni. Yes for the jnAni there is no mithyA jnAna but his jnAna is satyasya satya jnAna.



I accept this latteranswer as the siddhanta. Not sure how much it affects the practical side of things.



- The practical side of paramArtha jnAni is well explained by

I would guess most mumukshus do mananam focused on or channeled through Ishvara-tattva, that is natural to the vyavaharika standpoint. Even MS was said to be krishna bhakta, though he was the explicator for mithya.
