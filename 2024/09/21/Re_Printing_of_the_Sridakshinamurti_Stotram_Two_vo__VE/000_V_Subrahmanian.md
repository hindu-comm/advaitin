> Printing of the 'Sridakshinamurti Stotram' (Two volumes) in English  
>   
> The above book, a repository of a vast range of topics of Advaita
> Vedanta, was authored by Ve.mu.Br.Sri.D.S.Subbaramaiya and published
> by the Sringeri Sharada Peetham in two volumes in 1988. The book has
> seen two prints after that and owing to popular demand, the third
> print is now taken up by the Peetham.
>
>   
>
> The SridakshinamurtiStotram, a Vedantic hymn consisting of ten
> verses, is a composition of Shankara Bhagavatpada and has the
> commentaries: Manasollasaof Sureshwaracharya and Tattvasudha of
> Swayamprakasha Yati thereupon. The English book is based on these
> commentaries and supplements the exposition with a very huge number of
> citations from the Shruti, Smriti and also many rare and well known
> Advaitic texts. The original Sanskrit quotations are provided with
> lucid English translations too. The two volumes together span over
> 1300 pages.
