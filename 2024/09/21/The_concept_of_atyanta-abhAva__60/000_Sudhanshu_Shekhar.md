Hari Om,

  

advaita vedAnta does not prefer the four-fold classification of abhAva and accepts atyanta-abhAva alone. At places, such as in anupalabdhi parichchheda of VedAnta ParibhAshA andin निषेधप्रतियोगित्वानुपपत्त्याऽनिर्वचनीयत्वसमर्थनम् of Advaita Siddhi, the four-fold division is accepted. However, the preferred view is that of singular atyanta-abhAva as explained in ajnAna-pratyaksha-vichArah of Advaita Siddhi.

  

Further, this atyanta-abhAva is manadatorily adhikaraNa-swarUpa in case the atyanta-abhAva is not contradictory to the pratiyOgI.  
  
In case atyanta-abhAva is contradictory to the pratiyOgI, it can be either adhikaraNa-swarUpa or different therefrom, depending on prakriyA.  
  
In case atyanta-abhAva-contradictory-to-pratiyOgI is accepted as adhikaraNa-swarUpa, anupalabdhi pramANa is either not accepted, or accepted to enable knowing the adhikaraNa "abhAvatva-prakAraka-tvena".
