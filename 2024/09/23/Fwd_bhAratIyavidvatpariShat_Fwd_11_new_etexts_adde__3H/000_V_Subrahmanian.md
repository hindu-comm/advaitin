Dear list members,

  

11 new tantric etexts have been added to the Muktabodha searchable etext library. The cumulative zipfile download has also been updated to include these. All searchable etexts in the library can be viewed in either transliteration or devanagari. There are now over 500 searchable etexts in the library.

  

The link to the library is: [https://muktalib7.com/DL_CATALOG_ROOT/digital_library.htm](https://muktalib7.com/DL_CATALOG_ROOT/digital_library.htm)

  

The etexts are:

1\. **kaumāra-saṁhitā** from Shri Raghunath Temple MSS Library manuscript 4078  
  
2. **mātṛkābhedatantram** from Calcutta Sanskrit Series vol. 7  
  
3. **mṛgendra-vṛtti** by bhaṭṭanārāyaṇakaṇṭha from Shri Raghunath Temple MSS Library manuscript 4387  
  
4. **paramahaṁsa-paddhati** from Shri Raghunath Temple MSS Library manuscript 1065gh  
  
5.**prāsādamaṇḍana** by sūtradhāramaṇḍana from The Kashmir Series of texts and studies vol. 72  
  
6 **ramārcana-candrikā** by ānandavanaḥ mukundavana-śiṣyaḥ from Shri Raghunath Temple MSS Library manuscript 4740  
  
7. **śivarahasya** part 1 from the 1913 edition by Ganesh Shastri and Sundaresvara Shastri.  
  
8.**śivasūtravṛtti** from Shri Raghunath Temple MSS Library manuscript 1672 kh  
  
9. **svātantrya-dīpikā** from Shri Raghunath Temple MSS Library manuscript 1127 k  
  
10.**tripurāsārasamuccaya** written by nāgabhaṭṭa, commentator Govindācāryya, editor and publisher Jīvanānda Vidyāsāgara Bhaṭṭācāryya
1897.  
  
11. **navārṇacaṁḍīpaṁcāṁgam** from Shri Raghunath Temple MSS Library manuscript 4818 gh  
  
All Shri Raghunath Temple MSS Library manuscripts were copied from [archive.org](http://archive.org) from photographic copies generously provided by eGangotri.
