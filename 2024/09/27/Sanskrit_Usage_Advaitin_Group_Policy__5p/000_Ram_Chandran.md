Dear all:  

English language is the communication medium for all list discussions, but members do enjoy the privilege to use Sanskrit texts when necessary to enhance our understanding of the original scriptural materials. Since the goal of the list is to spread the message of Sankara's Advaita, members who use Sanskrit Texts are OBLIGATED to provide appropriate English Translation with adequate explanations.



This cyberspace list discussion is conducted interactively to enable members to raise questions which include doubts on a specific Sanskrit word or phrase. Senior members and moderators of the list have been very responsive to such requests. They will be happy to provide meanings and explanations to Sanskrit words and texts if and when members post such requests.

Though most of the participants are fully aware on the importance of providing English translations, sometimes, some may omit to provide meanings and explanations. In those situations, members who don't understand should feel free to ask for English translations. Please don't hesitate to use this privilege whenever you need help.

We hope that this clarification is helpful,
