Namaskaram,

  

I just noticed that :) Ram Chandran-ji made the post as senior moderator for the purpose of maintaining a cordial atmosphere in our forum, so best we take the spirit of it and not get into nitpicking each quote.

  

My understanding, on thispoint: "Brahma satyam", "aham brahmasmi" are (point to) facts/truths, not interpretations. We know facts from the appropriate pramanas. It may be said that different people have different ideas on what is fact and what is fiction; but our aptas and pramanas are not taking the position that therefore everything is interpretation and no one can be said to be right. That is perhaps modern philosophy's ambivalence on truth and the possibilityof knowing it. We reject this position. The goal of seeking knowledge (including all the tarka that we do) is to arrive at truth.
