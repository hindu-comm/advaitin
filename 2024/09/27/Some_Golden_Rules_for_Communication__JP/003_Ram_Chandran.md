Here are some more details about the quote : "There are no facts, only interpretations"

“There are no facts, only interpretations” isa quote by German philosopher Friedrich Nietzsche(1844–1900).Nietzsche's statement is a critique of positivism, which holds that there are only facts.Nietzsche believed that truth is impossible, and that there are only interpretations and perspectives driven by a person's interests.He called this doctrine "perspectivism"

The question that you have raised - "Are there no facts," is also subject to interpretation. The message of that quotation is very subtle. I am a retired professional Statistician with a doctoral degree and my major job was to look at the facts (Data collected) and interpret them using statistical inference. One thing that I learnt as a statistician is the fact that we can't escape from committing an 'error' and our goal has to minimize such errors!

This is the fact of life!!
