All the differing schools accept certain entities as facts: Advaita - Brahman ( a name for that Truth) as the only ultimate fact. All others are admitted as relative facts. Dvaita and Vishishtadvaita accept Brahman and the jiva, world, Ishwara, his gunas, leelas, etc. as non-negotiable facts. All other dualistic schools like sankhya, etc. have accepted certain facts. As to what that fact means to each school is an interpretation of the shruti. That does not deny the very existence of a fact.
