Dear Sri Dennis-ji

  

Mechanical engineers use Newton's laws everyday for design and analysis. These Laws are treated as excellent approximations to facts. They are not treated as interpretations.

  

Same goes with Einstein's equations. It is a better approximation, not an interpretation. Same goes with electromagnetism, quantum mechanics etc. Many of the Technological devices that we use are proof that these laws are not just interpretations. We don't need to understand about dark matter or dark energy in order to build MRI or CT Scan machines or similar working devices, for which, our approximations of the laws of Physics etc. are sufficient.

  

Now, in Quantum Mechanics, there is the Mathematical theory and then there are interpretations of QM. Whatever interpretation of QM one may choose, the mathematical theory and the results of experiments don't change. QM is a fact or at least an excellent approximation to a fact.

  

As for advaitic facts, I think I already answered your question. We ought to accept the basic premise - brahma satyam jagan mithyam, with which you also agree. Now whether somebody accepts mUlAvidyA etc or not can be discussed and debated.
