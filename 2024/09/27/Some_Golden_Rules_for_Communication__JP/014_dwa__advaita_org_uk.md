Dear Kalyan-ji,



I don’t disagree with any of this. My point was that physical ‘laws’ are not facts. Science provides us with working hypotheses that explain the things that we are presently aware of. When something new comes along, we often have to modify those explanations or come up with new ones. Advaita is not really comparable. I certainly only accept teaching that does not contradict reason or experience (as Śaṅkara advises) but most of it is not exactly ‘testable’ by experiment. I don’t see that you can deny that there are many interpretations even within Advaita, let alone the different explanations of Ramanuja and others.
