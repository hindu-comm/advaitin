Dear Dennis ji,

  

These definitions are about the fact. The fact has to be taught and words are used towards this purpose. Real is the name for that fact.
In fact there are many names for that Fact:  
  

ऋतमात्मापरं ब्रह्म सत्यमित्यादिका बुधैः ।  
कल्पिता व्यवहारार्थं तस्य संज्ञा महात्मनः ॥4.54॥  
  
\[In order to facilitate parlance, the wise employ words such as Rtam, AtmA, Param, Brahma, Satyam, etc., to designate that Supreme Self.\]  

  
That Fact, the Self, cannot be denied. Words are employed only to enable the aspirant to realize it.

  

Advaitins say Brahman/Self is that Fact. Buddhists say void is that fact.
