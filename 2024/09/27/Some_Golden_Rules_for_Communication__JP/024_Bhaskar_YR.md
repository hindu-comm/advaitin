Presumably you cannot deny that Ramanuja, Madhva and others claim the Vedas as the source of their philosophy and yet their teaching differs from that of Advaita.



- Yes, infact this is a fact (objectively verifiable 😊) that both
  rAmAnuja and mAdhva after seeing shankara bhAshya (commentaries by
  Advaita school of thought) on vedOpanishads decided that ‘there are
  severe ‘flaws’ in the interpretation of Advaita school and decided to
  refute it by their own interpretation of the same texts and advocated
  their own doctrine. Just for example, tattvamasi is mahAvAkya for us,
  the advaitins, which carries the meaning of ‘ekatvam’ (nonduality).
  But, for the dualists, the same mahAvAkya is somewhat categorically
  declaring duality by interpreting the same vAkya as ‘atatvamasi’.
  There is hell a lot of talks and debates between dvaitAdvaita school
  Acharya-s with regard to the interpretation of this vAkya as per their
  own perspective.



- And unfortunately, within Advaita school, we do have lot of internal
  different interpretations of the same text (bhAshya) that suits
  respective vyAkhyAnakAra-s taste and tendency ( am including here Sri
  SSS way of taking the shankara bhAshya as an ultimate authority as
  well) and in accordance with that they attacked other vyAkhyAnakAra-s
  as well. See these are all as the result of ‘interpretation’ of some
  undisputed facts. For us shankara’s interpretation of vedAnta vAkya-s
  are facts, same way for the dualists (dvaitins and vishishtAdvaitins)
  their respective sampradaya Acharya-s commentaries / interpretations
  are ultimate authority to understand the true imports of SAME shruti
  vAkya-s and we both are ready to debate and prove that our
  interpretation of the shruti siddhAnta is the ONLY correct
  interpretation 😊 In that way, yes, Advaita too is just an
  interpretation / commentary on some source text, like other schools of
  thought. But in Advaita, we go forward and claim that the truths /
  facts embedded in the scriptures and our understanding of the same can
  be realized here in this very life and very much verifiable as fact by
  doing the requisite sAdhana and achieving the resultant phala
  (fruit). So, for us, unlike dualists (who are mainly have faith in
  lOkAntara sthiti) we are more close to the shruti interpretation as we
  stake the claim that our interpretation is demonstrable as fact 😊
  Again, no need to mention this just another statement / interpretation
  without any ‘stuff’ as per dualists 😊



Therefore, Advaita MUST be an interpretation. Although this is the case, I am nevertheless convinced that Advaita’s interpretation is the truth. I don’t believe there is any contradiction here. Also, that I believe it to be the truth does not make it a ‘fact’, as I have explained. For it to be a fact, it has to be objectively verifiable.



Ø But Advaita satya is not objectively verifiable as what Advaita teaches is not something about object, it is subjective realization that I am not an object (Vishaya) 😊 That does not mean it is not a fact (atleast as per our siddhAnta) once we realize this truth, the doubts like whether this is fact or fiction etc. do not arise at all.
Chidyante sarvasaMshayAH declares shruti.
