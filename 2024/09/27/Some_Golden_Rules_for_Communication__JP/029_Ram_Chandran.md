Namaskar:  
  
Dennisji is not disputing the ultimate reality at the paramarthika level. Simply speaking, Paramarthika Reality is our ultimate spiritual experience which unifies us with the Brahman. But at the vyavaharika level, we only make empirical experience of the objective world. Advaita declares all our experiences are not real but just an illusion. All our discussions are just interpretations of what we believe about the ultimate reality.  
  
Dennisji has nicely summarized with this profound statement: "Presumably you cannot deny that Ramanuja, Madhva and others claim the Vedas as the source of their philosophy and yet their teaching differs from that of Advaita. Therefore, Advaita MUST be an interpretation. Although this is the case, I am nevertheless convinced that Advaita’s interpretation is the truth. I don’t believe there is any contradiction here. Also, that I believe it to be the truth does not make it a ‘fact’, as I have explained. For it to be a fact, it has to be objectively verifiable."  
  
We do need to recognize that what we state during all our discussions are just speculations because we are still at the Vyavaharika level and all we define and assume are just our own creations of "notions!"  
  
We need to detach ourselves from body, mind and intellect to realize the ultimate Brahman and that is bottom line of the Truth as stated by Sankara Bhagavadpada. Sankaracharya, Madhwacharya and Ramanujacharya have no dispute with the fact that "Vedas represent the Ultimate Truth." But this did not prevent them to provide their own interpretations and came up with three different philosophies.
