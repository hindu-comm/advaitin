 "Presumably you cannot deny that Ramanuja, Madhva and others claim the Vedas as the source of their philosophy and yet their teaching differs from that of Advaita. Therefore, Advaita MUST be an interpretation. Although this is the case, I am nevertheless convinced that Advaita’s interpretation is the truth. I don’t believe there is any contradiction here. Also, that I believe it to be the truth does not make it a ‘fact’, as I have explained. For it to be a fact, it has to be objectively verifiable."

  

Dennis Ji has excellently and comprehensively summarized the advaitin's stand and explained the need to acknowledge the others too.

Aravinda Rao
