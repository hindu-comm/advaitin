This can be easily seen from the struggle the other schools have experienced in explaining, rather **interpreting**, the shruti passages like Tattvamasi, Aham brahmasmi, neha nAnAsti kinchana, udaram antaram kurute, etc. and many such ones, all of which deny difference emphatically. On the other hand for Advaita the apparently difference-teaching shrutis like dvA suparNaa, sutras like bhedavyapadeshaat cha, etc. are not problematic but only alluding to vyaharic bheda in samsara/bandha.
