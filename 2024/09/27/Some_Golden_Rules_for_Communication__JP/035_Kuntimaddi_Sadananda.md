Ram - just some clarification.

  

Even Ramanuja says 'ignorace' is the fundamental problem. But he defines the 'ignorance' of Jeeva is not knowing that he depends on the Iswara and his role is to serve him as organs in the total body. Sharagati or surrendering completely at His feet is the only way to overcome this maayaa which is of divine origin - daiveem eshaa gunamayi, mama mayaa duratyayaa, maam evaye prapadyante maayaam etam tarantite - this sloka become very important for Vishistaadvaita along with the charama sloka - sarva dhramaan ...

  

Just my 2C.

  

Hari Om!

Sada
