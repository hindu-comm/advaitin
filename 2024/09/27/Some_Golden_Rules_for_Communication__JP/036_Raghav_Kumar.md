Is an "electron" a fact or merely an interpretation of sensory data?

  

Scientists agree that the only facts are sensory observations of color, sound etc. Everything else is an interpretation which has a certain convenience. Electrons, quarks, mass, energy etc are all just useful mathematical interpretations.

Of course one needs exposure to the shabda pramANam to get this.
