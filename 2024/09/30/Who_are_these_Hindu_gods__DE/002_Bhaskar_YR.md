I have a few questions. How to answer these in the best way:



Ø The best way possible that you have already done it !!. One of the funny ways of dealing these questions would be as follows :



\- Do you think hindu gods are always moral?



Ø Yes they are always moral but don’t think they have same constitution and penal codes that we have to adjudge which is moral or immoral. vAli, shambuka, karNa killings by rAma-krishna may have different background and justification than what we normally think as moral and ethics.



\- Are they real or metaphorical?



Ø No!! definitely not!! They do have their own family and family problems but at the same time they have some special powers to address the agonies of some lower order mortals.



\- If they're real, why do we need 1 million gods?



Ø Since we are already more than billion and our problems and sufferings also more than a million, we seek solutions from different gods at different times. If there is only one god then he may not be able to address the innumerable problems of innumerable beings at the same time. So, HE will send different gods to different parts of the universe to address the problems of his devotees.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
