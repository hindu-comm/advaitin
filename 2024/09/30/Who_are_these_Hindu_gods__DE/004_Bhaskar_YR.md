It is quite logical that a person who has lived the life of an ascetic, meditating on the Supreme will attain tapo loka or Brahma-loka (not Supreme Brahman). Someone who has been a good ruler will enjoy a high status in Yama loka. The subtle body creates its own loka and dwells in it. The description there is quite interesting. We can explain the lokas and gods in this way.



praNAms

Hare Krishna



Yes, for this we have the support of bhAshyakAra as well. upAsya devata-s must be in accordance with scriptures and upAsana of respective devata would yield results also accordingly. He clarifies. For all these ateendriya jnAna / loka shAstra is the only valid pramANa he further clarifies. Same Chaitanya would appear differently with different upAdhi-s to the upAsaka-s. ekaM sat viprA badudA vadanti. We look sun and worship it as Sun god, we look at fire and worship it as agnideva, we look at water and worship it as varuNa deva, we see the earth and worship it as bhU devi etc. Though Chaitanya behind all these different deva-devata-s are one and the same, due to upAdhi bedha the results of doing the upAsana of different upAsya devata also would be different clarifies bhAshyakAra. So in this sense only all karma samruddhi upAsana, krama mukti upAsana ( attaining the abode of Ishta devata like vaikunta, kailaasa, brahma lOka hiraNyagarbha lOka, pitru lOka etc.) etc. holds good. Pratima, prateeka upAsana definitely would give the (different) results depending on the upAsaka-s bhakti, shraddha and sAdhana. Tam yathA yathOpAsate tadeva bhavatee, yo yo yAM yAM tanum bhaktaH shraddhayaarchitu micchati…Krishna clarifies in geeta. So multiple gods-goddesses very much allowed based on upAdhi vishesha and various results also can be expected from them. But ultimately Advaita says there is ONLY god and there is nothing apart from THAT.
