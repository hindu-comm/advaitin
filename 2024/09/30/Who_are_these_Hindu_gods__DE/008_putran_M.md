I know I had read this idea (that even gods are jivas) somewhere but don't know if this is the position of shankara sampradaya. My idea is that once their tenure as gods is over, they as jivas must eventually return to human birth in orderto acquire atma-jnana. The jivas experience heaven and godhood as karmaphala but their upadhis are such that they cannot stray from their dharma and cannot create new karma-impressions. And if we do see heavenly beings like Narada seeking jnana, it must be interpreted as part of the leela enacted to serve humans.

  

I recall reading that even Brahma's position is temporary for the jiva occupying it presently. So I assume that would be the case for all the subsidiarygods. Logically that would extend evento Shiva and Vishnu, even though we traditionally give them a special eternal status. Can we make a distinction among gods (Shiva, Vishnu, Narada) who are self-realized and eternal and those (Brahma, Indra, Agni, Varuna) who are jivas?
