द्वौ हुडाविव युध्येते पुरुषार्थौ समासमौ ।  
प्राक्तनश्चैहिकश्चैव शाम्यत्यत्राल्पवीर्यवान् ॥ ५ ॥

*dvau huḍāviva yudhyete puruṣārthau samāsamau*\|  
*prāktanaścaihikaścaiva śāmyatyatrālpavīryavān*\|\| 5 \|

  

The two 'free wills' (ie one being the free will bring exerted now and the second being prArabdha which is the second 'free will' since it too was created by our free will exerted in the past or past lives) contend with each other like two head-butting rams of unequal strength, where in the mightier ram overcomes the other. (Paraphrased)  

  

It's noteworthy that even prArabdha or the karma phala which has already begun manifesting is called puruShArtha (of an alternative kind). Because all so-called destiny is also a product of our past exercise of free will alone.

  

  

This verse from the yoga vAshiShTha (2.5.5) was often quoted by Sri Yellamraju Srinivas Rao garu, a stalwart Vedantic acharya who discoursed on shAnkara vedAnta in Telugu.
