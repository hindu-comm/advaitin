There is an incident that marks the composition of this Evarani song by Thyagaraja, as reported in that blog:

  

[https://thyagaraja-vaibhavam.blogspot.com/2007/07/thyagaraja-kriti-evarani-nirnayinchiri.html](https://thyagaraja-vaibhavam.blogspot.com/2007/07/thyagaraja-kriti-evarani-nirnayinchiri.html)  
  
//It is stated that Gopalakrishna Bharati (Tamil composer) – a contemporary of ThyAgarAja visited the latter and sang a song (sabhApathikku) eulogising Lord Siva – that there is no God equal to Lord Siva. It is stated that then and there, SrI ThyAgarAja extempore composed this song. If this is true, then ‘nara varulu evarani’ would be the correct rendering.  //
