This is a nice initiative. I am reminded of a practice in vogue:  
  
During the 5-day Shankara Bhashya Parayanam conducted by Vedantha Bharati under the Yedathore Mutt, every year soon after the Shankara Jayanti, in the evening, in front of Shankaracharya shrine, three persons will present any topic of their choice from the Prasthana traya Bhashya for about 3 - 5 minutes in simple Sanskrit.

  

This gives a very wide opportunity to delve into infinitenumber of topics from the Bhashya corpus. More and more people will be inspired to search and discover topics.
