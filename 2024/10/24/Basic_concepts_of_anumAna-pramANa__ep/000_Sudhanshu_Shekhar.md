Hari Om respected members,

  

anumAna-pramANa holds a pivotalplace in the advaita epistemology. A clear conception thereof is required. The view of VedAnta as distinguished from that of NyAyA should also be clearly understood. I have prepared a write-up which is primarilybased on VedAnta ParibhAshA commentary by Panchanan Bhattacharya ji and Swami Vidyananda Giri ji. All concepts of VedAnta ParibhAshA present in anumAna-parichchhedahave been covered in the write-up. Relevant section from VivaraNa regarding the siddhAnta position of anumiti-karaNa is also covered.

  

Some other useful keywords frequently used in advaita discussions have also been covered in the write-up at the fag end.

  

I would request learned members to peruse the write-up and share any infirmity therein for correction.
