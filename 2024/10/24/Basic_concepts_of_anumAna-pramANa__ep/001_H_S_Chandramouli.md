I am not sure if there could be so much divergence between different commentators on the vedantic view of anumAna pramANa. For whateverit is worth, I would like to bring to the attention of members on the understanding stated by Sri Mani Dravid Shastri Ji on this topic in his tamil talk covering PanchapAdikA, link copied below. His view directly contradicts what is attributed to Sri Panchanan Bhattacharya ji and Swami Vidyananda Giri ji.

  

//[https://www.mediafire.com/file/6rjaj4g7prw6tut/PP_12-24.10.13.WAV/file](https://www.mediafire.com/file/6rjaj4g7prw6tut/PP_12-24.10.13.WAV/file) //.

  

Around minute 15 onwards. Reference PanchapAdikA statement

  

//दृश्यते हिलिङ्गज्ञानसंस्कारयोःसम्भूय लिङ्गिज्ञानोत्पादनं, प्रत्यभिज्ञानोत्पादनञ्च अक्षसंस्कारयोः। उभयत्रापि स्मृतिगर्भमेकमेव प्रमाणज्ञानम् //

  

//dRRishyate hi li~Ngaj~nAnasaMskArayoH sambhUya li~Ngij~nAnotpAdanaM, pratyabhij~nAnotpAdana~ncha akShasaMskArayoH \| ubhayatrApi smRRitigarbhamekameva pramANaj~nAnam //.

  

Sri MDS makes an explicit statement that **this forms the basis for the vedantic view of anumAna pramANa as elaborated in Vedanta ParibhAsha.** Since VivaraNa is a further elaboration of PanchapAdikA, I presume this applies to VivaraNa as well.

  

There are many members here in these Forums who refer to Sri MDS for clarifications. It would be appropriate, in my view, for some of these members to draw attention of Sri MDS to these divergent views and seek clarificaion.
