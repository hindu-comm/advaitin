In fact DSV/EJV is the default final position of Shankaracharya's Prasthana traya Bhashya. It's all over there and one can't afford missing that. The very popular statement: Brahman is Satyam, Jagat is mithyA can become true only, and only when the aspirant positions himself as the only sentient entity experiencing the world just like a dream is experienced. He is also part of the dream but he is witnessing the dream that includes himself and the whole world. It reduces to just that one entity.

  

Gaudapadacharya and Bhagavatpada have brought this out unmistakably.
