Dear Sudhanshu Ji

  

\> That has been rejected in detail in advaita vedAnta. There is no complication.

  

I don't think so. See for example गीताभाष्य 2.16

  

न असतः अविद्यमानस्य शीतोष्णादेः सकारणस्य न विद्यते नास्ति भावो भवनम् अस्तिता।। न हि शीतोष्णादि सकारणं प्रमाणैर्निरूप्यमाणं वस्तु सम्भवति।

  

The word असत् has been directly defined as अविद्यमान which means not existent. Even an example is given of feeling of cold, heat, etc, and these certainly are felt unlike hare's horn.
