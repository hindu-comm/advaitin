Dear Sudhanshu Ji

I'm confused as to what you are trying to say. For clarity, kindly clear my following doubts, before I can make my point -

\> क्वचिदप्युपाधौ सत्त्वेन प्रतीत्यनर्हत्वम् अत्यन्तासत्त्वम्

This definition that you gave defines अत्यन्तासत्त्वम् while you claim it defines असत्त्वम् I'm confused

\> So, the reference of word asat in GItA 2.16 is अत्यन्ताभावत्व-अवच्छिन्न-असत्-शब्द-प्रयोगः

If the reference of 2.16 is that of अत्यन्ताभाव which I suppose you define as unavailable in all three times, then how come, Adi Shankara gave the example of feeling of heat, cold etc if he meant अत्यन्ताभाव ?

\> Asat is not defined as non-existence in advaita.

What is the word of non-existent in Advaita according to you? If I want to say something is non-existent irrespective of whether it appears or not, what is the word to convey that in Advaita according to you?
