Namaste Sudhanshu ji

One of the five definitions of mithyA is

  

सदसदनधिकरणत्वं मिथ्यात्वम्

(That which has no locus in either sat or asat)  

  

In other words, mithyA dies not have it's locus in either sat-brahma nor in asat-horns-of-a-hare

  

In the above the word asat is used synonymously with tucchaM

  

  

Again, asat is used for mithyA itself in the  
Bhagavad Gita bhAShya for the shloka नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः where the sad-buddhi and sad-buddhi are both present in each cognition.
