> In the above the word asat is used synonymously with tucchaM

  

True. That is the primary meaning of asat. As is evident from NAsadIya SUktam also.

  

> Again, asat is used for mithyA itself in the Bhagavad Gita bhAShya
> for the shloka नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः where the sad-buddhi
> and sad-buddhi are both present in each cognition.

  

MithyA shares the feature of non-existence with asat. So, delimited by अविद्यमानत्व, there is no incongruity if asat word is used to denote mithyA. However, asat delimited by asat-tva cannot be used for mithyA. That is my point.

  

If asat delimited by asat-tva is stated to be identical to mithyA, as is being argued, it would violate NAsadIya SUktam, bhAshya, smriti etc.
