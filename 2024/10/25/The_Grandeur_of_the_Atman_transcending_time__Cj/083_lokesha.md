Sudhanshu Ji

  
  
\> Define existence and non-existence. That is, define sattva and asattva.

  
To be is to exist. To not be is to not exist. It cannot be simplified more than this.  
  
सत् comes from the root word असँ which means भुवि which means "to be" अस्ति / भवति / विद्यते all these are equivalent.  
  
A healthy human has 2 eyes through which he sees. The eyesexist. This is existence.  
  
A healthy human has no horn on his head. The horn does not exist. This is non-existence.

  

\> So, I don't know what you are arguing about.  
  

I'm saying that asat simply means non-existent. Both hare's horn and snake in rope are asat (non-existent) ultimately.
