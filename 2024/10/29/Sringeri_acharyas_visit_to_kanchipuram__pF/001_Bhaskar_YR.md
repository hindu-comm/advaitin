Yes, though I am unaware of any traditional rule, normally they don’t meet for that matter very rarely they visit other’s mutt. It seems there is clandestinely developed aversion between these two mutts / authorities / followers. I don’t recall any events where previous Jagadguru-s of dakshiNaamnaya Jagadguru-s visited kanchimutt and their traditional acharya-s. Comparatively, unlike previous Jagadguru-s and current jyeshTa sannidhAnam, HH Sri Vidhushekhara bhArati mahAswaminaH is more accessible and without any such reservations he freely visits ‘all’ places. By the way, Kanchi is not considered as authentic Amnaya mutt that has been established by Adi shankara as per shringeri tradition followers.
