Hari Om Respected Members,

  

I have made an effort to summarise the arthApatti-parichchheda of vedAnta ParibhAshA in a rather practicalmanner by noting the similarity thereof with vyatirekI-anumAna. How the words upapAdya, upapAdaka are correlated with sAdhana and sAdhya and how the vyApti works - has been emphasised as they sometimes prima facie appear to be a bit confusing.

  

The note is small but very crucial in understanding some of the key concepts of VedAnta. It is based on commentary by Swami Vidyananda Giri ji and by Panchanan Bhattacharya ji.

  

The note in PDF may be accessed from[https://sudhanshushekhar.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/a-practical-guide-of-arthapatti-pramana.pdf](https://sudhanshushekhar.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/a-practical-guide-of-arthapatti-pramana.pdf).
