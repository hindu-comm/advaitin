The Bhagavatam is replete with statements for:

  

1\. Brahman itself appearing as the Jiva (Chit) and the Jagat (Achit)

  

2\. That the jiva is unreal and so is the Jagat - Chit and Achit are both unreal, leaving Brahman alone to be Real.
