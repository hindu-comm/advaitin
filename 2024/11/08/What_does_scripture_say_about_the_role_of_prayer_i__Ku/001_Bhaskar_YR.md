praNAms Sri Akhilesh prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Does anyone have scriptural references on the effectiveness or non-effectiveness of *prayer*— as opposed to mantras or rituals per se — in achieving material (not purely spiritual) ends?





- Prayer is NOT the separate sAdhana like karma yOga sAdhana or jnAna
  yOga sAdhana, prayer is part and parcel of bhakti mArga. And for that
  matter after each and every kAmya karma there is a prayer 😊 And
  prayer is really effective when it is done with Shuddha antaHkaraNa.
  When it is non-effective?? the answer is quite obvious, when it is
  done with ashuddhAntaHkaraNa.



When are prayers answered, when are they not answered,



- I think this is nothing but asking about asking about prayer’s
  effectiveness and ineffectiveness.



what determines whether they are answered or not, and so on?



Ø When we get the desired result (material gain) we will come to know whether our prayers are answered or not. Samashti prayers (loKAH samasthA sukhinO bhavantu) and vyashti prayers ( mama durita kshaya dvAra) both are need to be done with purified antaHkaraNa then only our prayers will be answered by the Almighty.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
