I’m not interested in prayer as sadhana. I want to understand when it is scripturally effective, regardless of that.

  

Clearly it cannot be the case that a pure mind is required for its effectiveness, since per the Puranas, highly rajasic demons and rakshasas too pray and get their (foolish, self-destructive) wishes granted.
