Hare Krishna



I’m not interested in prayer as sadhana. I want to understand when it is scripturally effective, regardless of that.



Ø Prayer or prArthana is the way we express our admiration to our ishta / arAdhya devata though it is not strictly termed as sAdhana it is a sacred practice by someone who is suffering or who needs some material gain. I am not able to understand ‘when it is scripturally effective’?? what exactly do you want to know here?? Do you need scriptural reference for doing prArthana?? Or scriptural reference with regard to materialization of prayers and its time duration to get the fruits (prArthana phala)??



Clearly it cannot be the case that a pure mind is required for its effectiveness, since per the Puranas, highly rajasic demons and rakshasas too pray and get their (foolish, self-destructive) wishes granted.



Ø I am sorry I beg to differ from this. IMO, in order to get the blessings and favours or to fulfill our material / spiritual desires, we must express our love and devotion towars HIM with shuddhaantaHkaraNa.
When we sincerely call out to Him, he would definitely answer and rescue us. ananyAschintayantOmAM ye janAH paryupAsate….yOgakshemam vahAmyahaM is the assurance of Lord in geeta. I don’t think this can be achievable without shraddha and bhakti. Yes demons did pray and did get the results though they were tAmasic but I don’t think at the time of their Tapasya / prArthana they would have wayward focus and despite that got the desired results 😊 It is just like batsman with one pointed concentration hitting the century and after task completed engaging himself in all sorts of activities.



HarI Hari Hari Bol!!!
