Namaste Akilesh ji.

  

1\. In Dvaita vedAnta and in srishTi-drishTi-vAda, the fructification of your prArthanA related to material desires is dependent on whether the Almighty God is pleased with your prArthanA. You cannot fathom as to how exactly and when exactly God will be pleased with you. But as evident from several mentions such as that of Draupadi and Grah, it requires one-pointed complete surrender to the Almighty. If that is done, it is likely that He will be pleased. But there is no guarantee.

  

However, there are instances where despite not such intense surrender, God is pleased and desired material object is gained.

  

So, no condition is either necessary or sufficient.

  

God's pleasure, which is independent, is both necessary as well as sufficient condition for grant of material wishes.

  

2\. You are not interested for Atma-jnAna, but there is specific mention regarding that -- a knower of Atman gets all his material desires fulfilled on account of sarvAtma-bhAva. य एवमुक्तलक्षणं सर्वात्मानमात्मत्वेन प्रतिपन्नस्तस्य सर्वात्मत्वादेव सर्वावाप्तिलक्षणं फलमाह — यं यं लोकं पित्रादिलक्षणं मनसा संविभाति सङ्कल्पयति मह्यमन्यस्मै वा भवेदिति, विशुद्धसत्त्वः क्षीणक्लेशः आत्मविन्निर्मलान्तःकरणः कामयते यांश्च कामान् प्रार्थयते भोगान् , तं तं लोकं जयते प्राप्नोति तांश्च कामान्सङ्कल्पितान्भोगान् । (Mundaka 3.1.10)

  

3\. In case of non-Atmavit and in drishTi-srishTi-vAda, there is no causation. Causation is illusory on account of being seen.

  

Just as prayers appear to be fulfilled/not-fulfilled in dream without any causal relationship with the prayer done, similarly in waking also they may seem to be fulfilled/non-fulfilled without any causal relationship with your prayer.

  

So, there is no conditionalities possible in DSV.

  

And in SDV and Dvaita, God's pleasure/kripA is necessary and sufficient condition which is sAdhana-nirapeksha, being independent. All your efforts namely sincerity/intensity/surrender are for enabling the Bhagvan's kripA. But there is no guarantee.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
