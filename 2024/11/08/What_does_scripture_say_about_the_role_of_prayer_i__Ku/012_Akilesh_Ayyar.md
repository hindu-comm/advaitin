Thank you. Yes, these are the standpoints from which I am interested in the question.

  

But are we to understand thatBhasmasura and others *pleased* God? It’s also always said in these cases they performed*penance*— which I suppose is some sort of pain to the body and mind to show one’s dedication… standing on one toe for a thousand years, that sort of thing... so this seems to be a further condition for extraordinary wishes?

  

Is this penance considered sacrifice, I wonder, along the lines of BG 3:10:

  

*Having created mankind along with sacrifice,*

*Prajapat:i, (the Lord of Creatures) anciently said,*

*"By this (i.e. sacrifice), may you bring forth;*

*May this be your wishfulfilling cow."*

  

Perhaps prayer requires sacrifice to be effective?

  

But that cannot be quite right… Draupadi prays for Krishna’s help without any particular sacrifice, and she gets it.
