Draupadi's instance or GrAh's instance shows the importance of one-pointed surrender to God alone for desired material object. However, the instance of AjAmila shows the non-necessity thereof.

  

It is BhagvAn's kripA which is both necessary and sufficient. And, He is independent. He may be pleased with your efforts. But there is no guarantee. There is something called ahaitukI-kripA also.

  

So, in a nut-shell, in the prayer one has to ask for Bhagvan's kripA, mercy, love, attention to oneself for desired material object.
