Ah, that is interesting. In every case of a demon getting their wish, then, it is always through mantra and ritual, and not merely intense prayer? And if so, I wonder then: is this broadly true of *all*wishes that are rajasic or tamasic in nature?

  

For example, what are we to make of Amba’s penance in the forest and devotion to Shiva for the purpose of revenge against Bhishma? Would that be considered ritualistic as well? Is there specific mention of those components anywhere in the Mahabharata?
