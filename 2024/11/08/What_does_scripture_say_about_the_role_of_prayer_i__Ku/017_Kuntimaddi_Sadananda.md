[[Kuntimaddi Sadananda	2024-11-08, 13:56:56 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/KuQMQl3QegQ)]]

Krishna says:

  

samoham sarva bhuuteshu name dweshti na priyaH\|

ye bhagantitu mam bhaktyaa mayi te teshu ca apyaham\|

  

I am equal to all beings - neither hate anybody nor love anybody. However, those who pray to Me, they are with Me or in tune with Me, and I am with them.

  

In essence, prayer by itself will not help but those who are pray (properly) they can tap My infinite resources.

  

Prayer helps in keeping the mind calm and serene; and hence becomes fully available for the course of action that is needed to solve the problem at hand. In that way prayer helps.

  

In Kenopanishad, there are five slokas of interest in terms of seeking the Lord or where exactly the Lord resides.

  

yan manasaa na manute, yenaahur manomatam\|

tat eva brahma tvam viddhi; nedam yadidam upaasate\|

  

That which mind cannot think off, but because of which the mind can think off; that alone is Brahman and not this that you worship here.

  

ya kshakhusaa na pasyati; yena kshakuumsi pasyati\|

tat eva brahma tvam viddhi; nedam yadidam upaasate\|  

  

That which eyes cannot see; but because of which eyes can see!' that alone is Brahman and this that you worship here.

  

..........

  

The last line is repeated five times, with scripture emphasizing the importance of that. In essence dismissing the objectification of the Lord and recognizing His very presence in one's heart - Iswara sarva bhuutanaam hrudyeshu arjuna tiShTati.

  

With that understanding, if one prays, prayer becomes useful; scripturally sanctioned.

  

However, as Krishna says - there are four types of Bhaktas - artho, arthaarthi, etc. Of the four, Jnaani is the supreme bhakta, says Krishna.

  

Hari Om!
