Namaste Subbu ji

It's particularly noteworthy that the Buddhists who opposed Sri Shankara, were all answered and they consequently worshipped/revered Sri Shankara.

शास्त्रेषु सर्वष्वपि दत्तवन्तं प्रत्युत्तरं तं समपूजयन्स्ते \|

In other words the respect accorded to Sri Shankara was on account of his being able to answer/reply to all questions. It ended on a positive note for everyone.

  

The fate of the Jain monks in Tamil Nadu after losing a debate/wager to Shaivism, was quite different.
