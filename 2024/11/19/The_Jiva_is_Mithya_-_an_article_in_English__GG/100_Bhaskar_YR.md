Hare Krishna



If the subject/adhishtanam of all cognition is Brahman but the vyavaharika content of cognition involves mithya-upadhis (namarupa, guna etc. as well as agency, Ishvaratvam, seer vs seen division) superimposed on Brahman, then what truth or indication of the bimba shines continually in awareness in all pratibimba cognitions? (Since we are saying the pratibimba is non-different from bimba except that it is "seen with two horns" due to Maya/Avidya).



Ø The first and foremost thing that we need to understand from this bimba-pratibimba illustration is bimba is Chaitanya (brahman) and pratibimba is jeeva (avidyA pratibimbita- relection of brahma/Chaitanya due to avidyA). So, as per this theory since both bimba and pratibimba are one and the same in svarUpa / material, even pratibimba also like bimba a chaitanya, a sentient entity. So this pratibimba (which is Chaitanya) should realize that it is not pratibimba but bimba itself 😊
whereas bimba being pure Chaitanya does not have headaches like this.
To prove that pratibimba is not insentient and to strike the absolute identity between bimba and pratibimba there are theories like pratibimba is not the effect of mirror / water etc. and they go the extent that even in vyavahAra also they are one and the same !!?? And every sane person would know for all the practical purposes what holds good and what not. And from this practical point of view the thing what is reflected is an inert thing which does not have any practical utility, and how the jeeva (pratibimba) can realize that he / she is bimba (or bimba rUpa brahma!!??) pratibimba achetana but bimba is Chetana but despite this glaring difference the logicians keep on arguing that both are one and the same in that case pratibimba has to get the Chaitanya in it and realize that I am ( pratibimba) nothing but bimba 😊 In other words, this pratibimba has to realize what is standing outside (outside the mirror) as bimba is I am.



- And in this bimba-pratibimba analogy, even if we say bimba is jeeva
  and he is seeing his pratibimba due to avidyA (pratibimba bhrama to
  bimga rUpa jeeva) and we may have to conclude that this jeevatva
  bhrama along with pratibimba brahma etc. happens to ONLY brahman as he
  is the ONLY Chaitanya. And if the bimba is brahman itself than this
  brahman is not aware that he himself that pratibimbita jeeva then he
  is not sarvajna and even if he knows bimba as me different from
  pratibimbita jeeva in that case also Advaita hAni and brahman becomes
  dvaita darshi (cognizer and experiencer of duality) and again in this
  analogy bimba is an object should have the media to reflect himself /
  itself as pratibimba and later should realize that this pratibimba is
  nothing but bimba. If the bimba is jeeva what exactly it is seeing in
  the pratibimba?? I don’t know how these problems clarified in
  bimba-pratibimba vAda.



This bimba-knowledge/truth indicated in the pratibimba cognition, I am thinking, would be the asti-bhaati-priyam/sat-chit-ananda/satyam-jnanam-anantam “svarupa lakshana” (?) of Brahman.



Ø As I said above for this to happen pratibimba should become Chaitanya as bimba and this pratibimba should realize that I am bimba not pratibimba 😊



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
