Hare Krishna



It is vital only to refute your attempts to establish absence, darkness and mithya jnana as positive substances otherwise I see no purpose.

I am attaching Swamiiji's 35 minute talk today ridiculing darkness as an entity opposed to light as violatingreason, common sense and scripture taken from SSSS's Mulavidya Nirasa.



Ø I really appreciate your efforts to convince the logicians about the darkness which is mere prakAsha abhAva. No matter whatever they say with all flowery languages about positive existence of darkness like table and chair in the room, it simply goes against our lOkAnubhAva and anubhava sammata yukti. So I really don’t think we have to give special heed to these type of illogical assertions. It is simply quite incongruous. As you know Sri SSS takes this stand of vivaraNakAra and refutes it in his work paNchapAdika prasthAna. The absence of light is called as darkness, I am not a science student but those who know the definition of darkness would agree to this. It is not an existing thing or a positive thing like chair / bench or other some solid objectives.
I was quite surprised to hear the answer : Yes when I asked whether darkness too solid things like chair and table in the dark room!! That which is dark for us (the human beings) will not be so for a few animals like owl, cat, tiger etc. but for them also objects like table, chair etc. are objects only and cannot see through it. This general lOkAnubhava shows that the eyesight of these animals is able to see things even in that least available light. From this anubhava one can easily understand that darkness is simply an absence of light and not something objectively existing. Any length of logical explanation would not help us to this simple lOkAnubhava and anubhava sammata yukti.
