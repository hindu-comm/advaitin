The point remains that pot-abhAva, of either of the four kinds, is proved to be bhAvarUpa i.e. avidyA-kArya i.e. triguNAtmaka. It is not nihswarUpa like nirvishesha-abhAva such as horns of hare.

  

Pot-abhAva has two concepts - either identical with adhikaraNa or different from adhikaraNa. In either case, it is triguNAtmaka avidyA-kArya.
