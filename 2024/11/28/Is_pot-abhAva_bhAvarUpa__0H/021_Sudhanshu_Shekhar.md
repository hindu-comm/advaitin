Namaste Akilesh ji.

  

> However — doesn’t the answer to the below question depend on what
> precisely is the character of the pot-abhava?

  

True.

  

> For example, a cloth is pot-abhava and is bhavarupa.

  

True.

  

> But are not the horns of a hare also pot-abhava — and abhavarupa?

  

No.

  

Pot-abhAva is not nihswarUpa. Horns of hare are nihswarUpa.

  

Pot-abhAva is avidyA-kArya. Horns of hare are not avidyA-kArya.

  

Pot-abhAva is prameya and known through anupalabdhi-pramANa. Horns of hare are not known through any pramANa as they are not prameya.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
