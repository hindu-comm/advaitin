True. But all of them are triguNAtmaka bhAvarUpa (as proved in bhAshyaतथैव भावात्मकताभावानाम् ).

  

Let us take a straight wooden stick. You can burn it and cook food. You can also use it to make a pot. The causality of the stick vis-a-vis cooking is delimited by wood-ness whereas the causality of the stick vis-a-vis pot is delimited by stick-ness.

  

Similarly, the causality of pot-abhAva vis-a-vis wiping things is delimited by cloth-ness whereas air-ness-vishishTa-pot-abhAva does not have this capability. For e.g. a golden stick can cause a pot, but cannot cause cooking.

  

What it means is the following - while pot-abhAva can have distinct adjectives namely cloth-ness, air-ness etc, the bottom line remains the same -- that pot-abhAva is a triguNAtmaka bhAvarUpa vastu.

  

So, pot-abhAva-tva-vishishTa-pot-abhAva is triguNAtmaka vastu. That is the claim. This is present in all visheshya-pot-abhAva no matter what the visheshaNa is.

  

Now, the moment you include horns-of-hare within the ambit of pot-abhAva, a problem would come.

  

How?

  

You agree that horns-of-hare is non-triguNAtmaka. Now, I will present an anumAna -शशविषाणम् त्रिगुणात्मकम्, घटाभावात्, पटवत्.

  

If you accept horns-of-hare as pot-abhAva, you cannot refute this anumAna and it will lead to you accepting shasha-vishANa as triguNAtmaka, which will be contrary to your position.

  

So, while it is true that pot-abhAva can refer to many "things" such as air, cloth etc having diverse properties, it cannot refer to horns-of-hare, as it will lead to horns-of-hare becoming triguNAtmaka through the application of anumAna.

  

> The vikalpa-vritti is not horns of hare, but it nevertheless tells us

Sir, it is not that horns-of-hare is something which has a property nihswarUpatva. When we say horns-of-hare is nihwarUpa, all we mean is, "hares have no horns" or "there exists no horn which has shashIyatva (belonging-ness-to-hare).

  

A vritti arises namely vikalpa-vritti with zero object corresponding to it.

  

It does not mean that "characteristics" of horns-of-hare are being spoken about through vikalpa-vritti. एवं च शशशृङ्गं नास्तीत्युल्लिखन्त्या अपि बुद्धेः शशे शृङ्गाभाव एव विषयः । गवि शशशृङ्गं नास्तीत्यस्या अपि गवाधिकरणकशृङ्गे शशीयत्वाभावो विषयः, अनन्यगतिकत्वात् ।

> That only proves my point. Of course it is not a pot; thus it is

> No, you’ve assumed without proof that pot-abhava is an object of

Pot-abhAva is known by pot-anupalabdhi. Right? It is gives rise to abhAva-pramA which is a vritti. Pot-abhAva is vishaya of that vritti. And that vritti is not produced by shabda. So, pot-abhAva has shabda-ajanya-vritti-vishayatva. Therefore, pot-abhAva has drishyatva i.e. it is an object of perception.

  

That pot-abhAva has drishyatva i.e. shabda-ajanya-vritti-vishayatva is clearly accepted in VedAnta and is logically proved also. Where is the scope of debate?

  

Now, once it is understood that pot-abhAva has drishyatva, it automatically implies that it cannot be horns-of-hare as shabda-ajanya-vritti-vishayatva is not present in horns-of-hare.

  

Hence proved that horns-of-hare is distinct from pot-abhAva.

😀 Nice. The point remains - horns-of-hare is a non-vastu. It does not and cannot have a sambandha with anything. I mean, there can be a pot-abhAva in this room. Can there be a horns-of-hare in this room?

  

See, horns-of-hare is a nirvishesha-nishpratiyOgika-abhAva. It does not have a pratiyOgI or anuyOgI. When you say pot-abhAva, there is a vishesha here. Pot. There is a pratiyOgI and anuyOgI.

  

To equate pot-abhAva with horns-of-hare is downright incorrect, therefore, on following counts:

1.  It turns horns-of-hare into triguNAtmaka-vastu through the anumAna -
    शशविषाणम् त्रिगुणात्मकम्, घटाभावात्, पटवत्. 2.  Horns-of-hare have no drishyatva on account of absence of
    shabda-ajanya-vritti-vishayatva whereas pot-abhAva possesses
    shabda-ajanya-vritti-vishayatva. 3.  Horns-of-hare is nishpratiyOgika-abhAva without any pratiyOgI or
    anuyOgI. Whereas pot-abhAva has definite pratiyOgI pot, with
    pot-ness as pratiyOgitA-avachchhedaka and definite anuyOgI such as
    room etc.
      

> So how is it known, then, if not by pramana? “We know… we know….” You
> keep saying this. On what basis is this knowledge if not pramana?  

  

Sir, we say -- we know "horns of hare is nihswarUpa", "horns of hare does not exist" etc. It does not mean that there exists something called horns-of-hare having property nihswarUpatva and that is known by pramANa.

  

All it means -- is that -- there exists no horns with shashIyatva -- or
-- there exists no hare with horns. एवं च शशशृङ्गं नास्तीत्युल्लिखन्त्या अपि बुद्धेः शशे शृङ्गाभाव एव विषयः । गवि शशशृङ्गं नास्तीत्यस्या अपि गवाधिकरणकशृङ्गे शशीयत्वाभावो विषयः, अनन्यगतिकत्वात् ।

  

So, all it tells is about the horn-abhAva in hare, or about shashIyatva-abhAva in horn. It does not imply that horns-of-hare is being known by pramANa.
