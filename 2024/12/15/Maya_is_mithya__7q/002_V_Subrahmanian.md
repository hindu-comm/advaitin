Dear Putran ji,

Nice thoughts. The accepted position in Advaita is that Maya is an adhyaropa, a deliberate superimposition by the Shaastra with a view to explain the imagined problem of samsara. Samsara requires avidya, jiva, Ishwara and jagat. Maya framework explains all this. Once the plan is known, understood beyond doubt, Maya is withdrawn leaving Nirguna Brahman as the Adviteeya Tattva of all periods of time.
