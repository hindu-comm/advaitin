Namaskaram Subbu-ji,

  

I don’t deny the adhyaropa by shastra position, but the way I look at it is that the shastra is completing (revealing fully) the circle already implicit in the samsara-adhyasa as imagined in the jiva. There may be equivalent ways (Maya, Avidya, Ishvara) to present this complete picture of the samsara adhyasa, but the complete-knowledge is not an optional, unnecessary or additional/new adhyaropa but a statement of fact regarding the full framework already underlying the samsara adhyasa.

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
