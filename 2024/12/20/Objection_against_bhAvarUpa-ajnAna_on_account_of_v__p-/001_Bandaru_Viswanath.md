Hi Sudhanshu,

  

Just a nit-pick.

  

> When we say दण्डी पुरुषः, here purusha is visheshya and danDa is
> visheshaNa. This दण्डी पुरुषः is vishishTa-vaishishTya-avagAhi-jnAna.

  

This is not a vishishTa-vaishishTya-avagAhi-jnAna, but just vishishTa-jnana.

  

An example of a vishishTa-vaishishTya-avagAhi-jnAna would beगोपिका घटविषिष्टमक्षिकावती.
