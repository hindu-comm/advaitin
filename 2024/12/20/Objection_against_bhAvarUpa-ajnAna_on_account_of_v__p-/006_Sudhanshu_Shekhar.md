"दण्डी पुरुषः" is qualified by danDatva, just as ajnAna in "अहम् अज्ञः" is qualified by savishayakatva and jnAna-virOdhitva, the text did not elaborately write दण्डत्व-विशिष्ट-दण्ड-वान् पुरुष as an example of vishishTa-vaishTya-avagAhi-jnAna (VVAJ). However, I completely agree with you that for better clarity, it should have been written. Otherwise there would appear no differencebetween jnAna wherein only visheshya-visheshaNa are there without any visheshaNAntara AND the jnAna where visheshaNantara is present.
