The recollection post-waking is impossible without anubhava in sushupti. Recollection is always preceded by anubhava. Recollection implies awakening of samskAra which is formed due to jnAna-nAsha. The recollection shows ignorance in sushupti was qualified by all vishaya. na-knichid-avedisham. Hence, bhAvarUpa-ajnAna was experienced by sAkshI during sushupti, throughajnAna-vritti. Such jnAna-nAsha leads to samskAra which in turn leads to smriti after waking.
