That is why Bhagavatpada said: what is appearing is only falsely appearing so as it is non-existent. That's why he gives the mirage water example. Even after knowing it is a mirage, the water appearance phenomenon continues to appear. But the person will not attach reality to it. Nobody claimed that the world will disappear into thin air.
Shankara has himself clarified in the BSB that: who indeed can deny a Jnani'sexperience that he is Brahman and that he also continues to enliven the body? As long as the body is there the world also will be perceived, but with the understanding that it is false.
