See, world is changeable, active. It cannot be Brahman, because that would make Brahman vikArI. This is pretty simple. Isn't it?



Clay-pot is given keeping pariNAma-vAda in mind, which is the first level. Then vivarta-vAda where rope-snake is taught. Then ajAtivAda, where there is no perception, no creation.



Ø In short kArya does not affect the kAraNa in any manner. But at the same time kArya on its own cannot have any sort of existence apart from its kAraNa.

- The shape of the pot, pitcher, jug etc. which appeared in the clay
  prompts us to think that clay (mrutsAmAnya) is vikAri But as we know
  this mrutsAmAnya continues to be clay whether this shape exists or
  does not exist. This means that the shape does not affect the clay in
  any way. We are not saying this ghost is post. We are simply saying
  what exists is only exists call it with the name you want.
  Mruttiketyeva satyaM. And when one has this mrutsAmAnya jnAna he
  would realize just as brahman the cause never deviates from the
  existence in all the three periods of time, so also the effect, the
  world, never deviates from existence in all the three periods (of
  srushti, sthiti, laya). And existence again is ONLY ONE. So the
  effect is none other than the cause. If one allergic to the name
  jagat they can call it as brahman by dropping the name ‘jagat’ we have
  no qualms with that 😊
