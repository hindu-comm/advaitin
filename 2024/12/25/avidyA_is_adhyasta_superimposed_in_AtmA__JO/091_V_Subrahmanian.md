Even in the [Br.Up.Bh](http://Br.Up.Bh). 1.4.10 Aham Brahmasmi, Shankara discusses at length and concludes: We have to attribute avidya to Brahman alone because: Avidya can be for a sentient entity alone, and there is no other sentient entity than Brahman.

Nevertheless, such avidya is mithyA and the attribution is also an as if. Only then the pAramArthika Adviteeya status of Brahman stands unchallenged. So, the system of Advaita is very strongly built by Shankara himself, on the basis of the shruti.
