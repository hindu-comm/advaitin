[[Akilesh Ayyar	2025-01-09, 08:00:26 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/JOytEkyPKJE)]]

If X = not X, any statement can be said to follow.

  

Is the locus of (X = not X) in the statements derived from it?

  

Or is its locus in the universe of non-contradictory logical truths?

  

—

  

From the standpoint of avidya, there are two standpoints: the standpoint of avidya, and the standpoint of Brahman.

  

From the standpoint of Brahman, however, there cannot be said to be any such thing as standpoints at all.

  

Which is right?

  

Akilesh
