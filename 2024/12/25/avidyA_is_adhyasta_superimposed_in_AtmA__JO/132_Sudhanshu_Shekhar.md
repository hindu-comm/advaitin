Namaste Akilesh ji.

  

> If X = not X, any statement can be said to follow.

  

But such a statement is downright wrong.

  

> Is the locus of (X = not X) in the statements derived from it?

  

Since (x = not x) is self-contradictory, the question of locus is not relevant.

  

> Or is its locus in the universe of non-contradictory logical truths?

  

Not applicable as pointed above.

  

> From the standpoint of avidya, there are two standpoints: the
> standpoint of avidya, and the standpoint of Brahman.

  

This is true.

  

> From the standpoint of Brahman, however, there cannot be said to be
> any such thing as standpoints at all.

  

Very true.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
