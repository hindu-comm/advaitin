That's a good example. Parental absence creates a suitable context and mischievous acts occur. But i have a friend in whose absence, mischievous acts by his children do not occur. The cause of the acts is not parental absence but the mischievous saMskAras present in the children. That was the idea being conveyed. So there has to be some bhAvarUpa entity (children's mischievous saMskAras) for a viparIta-grahaNa (mischievous actions) to occur.



- Again this is over stretching the example. My absence (abhAva) in
  house is not the material cause (upAdAna kAraNa) for my kids
  mischievous acts (adhyAsa) The moment of my arrival from office they
  become silent. coming. In this example, the empty house (or my absence
  in the house) is the ajnAni’s intellect. The my arrival is the vidyA
  of realizing oneself as Brahman. My absence or absence of this vidyA
  is the emptiness of the house (jnAna abhAva in antaHkaraNa). Due to
  this abhAva of the jnAna tAmasic, rAjasic and sAtvik thoughts may
  enter into my empty house or antaHkaraNa in which this jnAna is
  absent. Ofcourse as you said your friend’s kids are having saMskAra
  and might be silent, my kids due to their saMskAra, are mischievous.
  These existent thoughts are born out of the mAya of the three guNa-s
  according to their respective / corresponding vidyA, karma and
  pUrvaprajna. And as you know mAya and avidyA are not synonymous in my
  books. You should understand from all these is what is being conveyed
  is jnAna abhAva cannot be the upAdAna but there is a possibility of
  adhyAsa due to this jnAnAbhAva. If you understand the purported
  intention of these examples, you won’t stretch these examples beyond
  its scope.
