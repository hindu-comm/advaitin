Namaste,

  

Bhashyakara by saying mulAvidya is tAmasa has clearly pointed out that it is upAdAna as the triguna - sattva, rajas and tamas are dravya as per Sankhya terminology. Many people are confused because Guna is generally translated as quality in keeping with Nyaya-Vaisheshika but when triguna is translated as qualities of prakriti it is wrong. Triguna are actually constituents of prakriti and are dravya.

  

With love and prayers,

Jaishankar
