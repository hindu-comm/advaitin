Another interesting thing I observed.

  

To develop Shiva bhakti, Shaiva puranas tend to denigrate Vishnu.

To develop Vishnu bhakti, Vaishnava puranas tend to denigrate Shiva.

  

I think this is explained as some "nahi ninda nyaaya".

  

But we never see Rama being denigrated for developing Krishna-bhakti or Krishna being denigrated for developing Rama-bhakti or both of them being denigrated for developing Narasimha-bhakti. Even if mild denigration happens, we just shrug it off.
