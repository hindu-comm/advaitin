Both SDV and DSV agree on illusoriness of seen. Bhagvan's body transacting with Arjuna is within the domain of seen. Both unreal. So, it is like two charactersof movie. One is acting as Bhagvan and the other is shishya. The transaction, i.e. fetching water, is within mirage.
