A couple of months ago, I concluded my participation in the discussions on this topic with the promise that I would post a summary of my thoughts to my website. In fact, what I did was to ‘discuss’ the topic with ChatGPT. I had to begin in this way so that I could translate and understand Sudhanshu-ji’s Devanagari quotation on the topic.



I still have significant concerns over ChatGPT’s ability to track down and translate scriptural quotations. I have requested these quite a number of times now and received definite citations that were clearly incorrect. However, there is no arguing with the fact that it can make for an interesting discussion and, in particular, it is always very polite, eager to help, and sometime even funny! As long as you bear these provisos in mind, I can heartily recommend it.



The discussion begins at [https://www.advaita-vision.org/ignorance-or-absence-of-knowledge/](https://www.advaita-vision.org/ignorance-or-absence-of-knowledge/) and there are 7 parts. It is unlikely that I will discuss this further but feel free to post any useful observations to the site.
