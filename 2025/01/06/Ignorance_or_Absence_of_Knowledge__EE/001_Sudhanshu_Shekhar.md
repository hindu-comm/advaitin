There is no confusion. What to say of Shankara, the Shruti itself speaks of AvaraNa and vikshepa. GItA says -- ajnAnena Avritam jnAnam. And Shankra keeps repeating - avidyA-krita. So, AvaraNa and vikshepa of avidyA are Shrut-Smriti-bhAsya-siddha.

  
//I also still believe that understanding avidyā as simply ‘lack of knowledge’ is adequate for explanations in Advaita and less likely to lead to confusion or misunderstanding.//

  

You hold so because you did not delve deep into the issue. You did not inquire into the nature of abhAva so as to arrive at the correct understandingthat there can be no pratyaksha of abhAva whereas there is pratyaksha of ajnAna. This itself shows that ajnAna cannot be abhAva. Of course, the anuyOgI-pratiyOgIissue has already been described which refutes any possibility of ajnAna being abhAva.

  

The reason you are unable to appreciate this distinction between ajnAna and jnAna-abhAva is becauseof their co-appearance. When pot-ajnAna is present, pot-jnAna-abhAva is also present. However, as discussed earlier, pot-jnAna-abhAva is known by pramANa whereas pot-ajnAna is sAkshi-bhAsya. I wish you applied mindon this subtle distinction which will at once show the beauty of post-Shankara AchAryA's works.

-krita by an abhAva makes no sense either. Please! even to make sense, one needs bhAvarUpa ajnAna, let alone serving useful purpose.

  

//However, I do accept that it is meaningful to differentiate ‘degrees’ of the ‘lack of knowledge’. On the other hand, not knowing that it is a rope on the path in front of me can lead to experienced fear that it is snake.//

  

It does not demonstrate degrees in jnAna-abhAva. It merely shows absence of supporting factors. Again, ignorance of Mandarin is not merely Mandarin-jnAna-abhAva but ajnAna-of-shuddha-chaitanya with Mandarin as avachchhedaka. Similarly, rope-ajnAna is ajnAna-of-shuddha-chaitanya-with-rope as the avachchhedaka.

  

Please apply mind - anAtmA is never the vishaya of ignorance. It is only the shuddha chaitanya which is the vishaya of ajnAna. The objects, superimposed in shuddha chaitanya, act as avachchhedaka of shuddha chaitanya becauseof which they erroneously appear to have ajnAna-vishayatA.

avidyA is upAdAna kAraNa of adhyAsa, as it is proved by anvaya-vyatireka.

//When it comes to Advaita, our lack of knowledge that we are Brahman and reality is non-dual may well be thought to lead to all sorts of unpleasant consequences. So, ajnAna is always about Self and never about non-self.

  

It is immaterial what majority of people think. It does not matter what others are preaching/saying when I see something as clearly as a gooseberry kept on my palm.
