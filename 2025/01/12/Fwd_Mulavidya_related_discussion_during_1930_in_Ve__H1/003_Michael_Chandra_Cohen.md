Namaste Sudhanshuji,

"abhAva cannot be known without the prior knowledge of pratiyOgI. This is the golden rule. And that is why avidyA cannot be jnAna-abhAva."

  

The notion of absence as also the notion of presence are themselves superimpositions of Conscious Awareness which Itself is wholly free of presence and/or absence. So how does pratiyogi even apply? Sruti and Bhasyakara's teaching is that Deep Sleep is not merely the absence of manifestation but the unalloyed Presence of Self. It is absenceofobjects from the waking state perspective only but in truth, from its own perspective, there is only nondual Self. Eskimo is an exampleonly and not to be logically wrangled into existing secretly somewhere Karnataka :)

  

Regards, Michael
