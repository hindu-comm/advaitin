> I understand jnAnAbhAva is not pratyaxa-gamya. But the above seems to
> say there are cases where it is pratyaxa-gamya?

  

NaiyAyikAs hold that abhAva is pratyaksha-gamya. MImAmsakAs hold that abhAva is anupalabdhi-pramANa-gamya.

  

In response to their position, it is said by VPS that in both cases dharmI-pratiyOgi-jnAna requirement will lead to vyAghAta in the case of jnAna-abhAva.

  

Our position of jnAna-abhAva being known through vyatirekI-anumAna/arthApatti or (arthApatti+anupalabdhi) ensures that this pratiyOgI-jnAna requirement is not present. So, for us, ajnAna is the sAdhana/upapAdya in vyatirekI-anumAna/arthApatti and jnAna-abhAva is sAdhya/upapAdaka.
