Hari Om,

  

in Brahma-vidyA, we have both vidhi-vAkya as well as nishedha-vAkya.

  

***Example of vidhi-vAkya:***

  

1\. The four mahAvAkya

2\. सत्यं ज्ञानमनन्तं ब्रह्म (तै. उ. २ । १ । १) etc.

*  *

***Example of nishedha-vAkya:***  
  
1. अस्थूलमनण्वह्रस्वमदीर्घम् (बृ. उ. ३ । ८ । ८)  
2. नेति नेति (बृ. उ. २ । ३ । ६) etc.***  ***

  

Here, what is the relationship between vidhi-vAkya and nishedha-vAkya?

  

\(a\) Both are same. OR;

  

\(b\) vidhi-vAkya give rise to tattva-jnAna and nishedha-vAkya are anuvAda. OR;

  

\(c\) nishedha-vAkya are upakArI to vidhi-vAkya, and it is the vidhi-vAkya which have the primacy. OR;

  

\(d\) nishedha-vAkya have the primacy over vidhi-vAkya. OR;

  

Learned members may share their views.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
