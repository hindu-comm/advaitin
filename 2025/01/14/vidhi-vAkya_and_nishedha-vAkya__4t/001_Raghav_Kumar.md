Namaste Sudhanshu ji

I once heard Pujya Swami Dayananda Saraswati ji say that while satyam jnAnam anant is no doubt unfolding the svarUpa laxaNa and is a vidhi vAkya, it is not sufficient to generate the mahAvAkya pramA by itself. Instead, only when read with "tasmAd vA etasmAt Atmana Aakasha saMbhUtaH", the sAmAnAdhikaraNyam between tasmAt (brahmaNaH) and etasmAt (AtmanaH) generates aikya pramA (tattva jnAnam).

  

So, even within vidhi vAkyas, some are only unfolding jagan-mithyAtvaM but not jIvEshvara aikyaM the way a mahAvAkya does. Even "sarvaM khalvidaM brahma" is a vidhi vAkya but it cannot produce tattva jnAnam.

  

Om

Raghav
