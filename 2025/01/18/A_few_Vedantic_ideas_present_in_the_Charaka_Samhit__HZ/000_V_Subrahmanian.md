The Charaka Samhita is a very ancient Ayurvedic text. On perusing portions of the work, we realize that it contains considerable Advaitic tenets. This reveals that the author of the Charaka Samhita was a Vedantin himself; otherwise his thoughts would not be expressed in such a manner.  
  
In this article, several portions of the text and the commentary of Sri Chakrapani (of the 10th or 11th century), have been culled out to indicate the references to Vedantic tenets.
