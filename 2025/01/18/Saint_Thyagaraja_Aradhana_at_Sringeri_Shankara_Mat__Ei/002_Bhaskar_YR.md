**227. All this universe which through ignorance appears as of diverse forms, is nothing else but Brahman which is absolutely free from all the limitations of human thought.**

****

**Ø** **Yes, the ‘perceived’ world with avidyA is mithya but what exactly is there is nothing but brahman. The same shAstra which talks a lot about nirvishesha, nirvikAri, niravayava brahman talks a lot about brahman as abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa of this jagat. We cannot selectively apply the ‘ardha kukkuti’ nyAya to say brahman is ONLY this or that.**







I recall in Gospel of Sri Ramakrishna, him saying ~ how he becomes spiritually awakened or ecstatic when seeing large gathering of people. I couldn’t find it but this is similar:



\[Once someone asked Sri Ramakrishna, 'Why can't we see God?' He immediately covered his face with a towel and asked: 'Can you see my face? No. Because this towel is the barrier; yet I am seated in front of you. Similarly, remove the veil of ignorance, which is between you and God and you will see Him everywhere."\]



Ø bhagavatpAda in geeta bhAshya says : vAsudevaM sarvasya AtmAnaM sarvatra sarva bhuteshu sarvaM cha brahmAdibhUtajAtaM mayi sarvAtmani pashyati (geeta shloka 6-30). sarvAtma bhAva, Atmaikatva darshana, bhUma drushti is nothing but this and it is called mOksha sthiti.
