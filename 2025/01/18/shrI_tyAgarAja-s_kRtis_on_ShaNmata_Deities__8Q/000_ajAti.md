**Nārāyaṇa 🙏**  
🌸 On the occasion of *Tyāgarāja Ārādhana*, I would like to share some kṛtis of Tyāgarāja composed in praise of Śiva, Devī, Gaṇeśa, and Murugan. Although Tyāgarāja is more famous for his kṛtis dedicated to Śrī Rāma, his compositions on other deities hold a special place as well.

Being a Smārta Brahmin, Tyāgarāja saw no sectarian boundaries and found bliss in the names of Śiva and Devī. 🌸
