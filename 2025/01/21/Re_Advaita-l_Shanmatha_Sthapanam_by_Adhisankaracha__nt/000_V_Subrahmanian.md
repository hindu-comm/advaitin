True, he has not named these two schools but implies. According to the Madhva school, all the 21 pre-Madhva Sutra bhashya authors (there is a list of the 21 given by others, which includes Ramanuja, Shankara, Yadavaprakasha, etc.) and their followers are daityas and illicit-born and are destined to eternal hell. A contemporary Madhva scholar in his book has stated this unique tenet of the Madhva system: He says if one has to remain in the Madhva system and strive for purusharthas he should determine all non-Madhva Darshanikas and their followers to be daityas and illicit born and hold Madhva alone to be divine. He goes on to say: such a requirement is not there in other schools. Hence the uniqueness of the Madhva system. He even cites the Mantralayam Raghavendra Tirtha's Gita vivruti as pramana for saying 'asuras acquire the bodies that are illicit born, etc. (But this part is not there in the Gita Vivruti is another matter.)
