In Srimad Bhagavatam there is a stuti of Brahma by Hiranyakashipu, which has a lot of Vedantic content. Is Hiranyakashipu a vedantin?

श्रीहिरण्यकशिपुरुवाच।  
कल्पान्ते कालसृष्टेन योऽन्धेन तमसावृतम्।  
अभिव्यनग्जगदिदं स्वयञ्ज्योतिः स्वरोचिषा २६।  
आत्मना त्रिवृता चेदं सृजत्यवति लुम्पति।  
रजःसत्त्वतमोधाम्ने पराय महते नमः २७।  
नम आद्याय बीजाय ज्ञानविज्ञानमूर्तये।  
प्राणेन्द्रि यमनोबुद्धि विकारैर्व्यक्तिमीयुषे २८।  
त्वमीशिषे जगतस्तस्थुषश्च प्राणेन मुख्येन पतिः प्रजानाम्।  
चित्तस्य चित्तैर्मनैन्द्रि याणां पतिर्महान्भूतगुणाशयेशः २९।  
त्वं सप्ततन्तून्वितनोषि तन्वा त्रय्या चतुर्होत्रकविद्यया च।  
त्वमेक आत्मात्मवतामनादिरनन्तपारः कविरन्तरात्मा ३०।  
त्वमेव कालोऽनिमिषो जनानामायुर्लवाद्यवयवैः क्षिणोषि।  
कूटस्थ आत्मा परमेष्ठ्यजो महांस्त्वं जीवलोकस्य च जीव आत्मा ३१।  
त्वत्तः परं नापरमप्यनेजदेजच्च किञ्चिद्व्यतिरिक्तमस्ति।  
विद्याः कलास्ते तनवश्च सर्वा हिरण्यगर्भोऽसि बृहत्त्रिपृष्ठः ३२।  
व्यक्तं विभो स्थूलमिदं शरीरं येनेन्द्रि यप्राणमनोगुणांस्त्वम्।  
भुङ्क्षे स्थितो धामनि पारमेष्ठ्ये अव्यक्त आत्मा पुरुषः पुराणः ३३।  
अनन्ताव्यक्तरूपेण येनेदमखिलं ततम्।  
चिदचिच्छक्तियुक्ताय तस्मै भगवते नमः ३४।

*Hiraṇyakaśipu said*:

26-27.^([\[3\]](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-bhagavata-purana/d/doc1128372.html#note-e-190351 "Bhāgavata Candrikā notes that due to the tāmasa nature of Hiraṇyakaśipu, he wrongly thinks god Brahmā to be the Almighty Lord. Padaratnāvalī also points out that the attributes used here are inapplicable to the fourfaced god Brahmā but are proper for Hari who, as an antaryāmin, dwells in ..."))“Bow to the all-pervading Supreme Lord (Brahma), the support of the*[guṇas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/guna#purana "show guṇas definitions")*—*[sattva](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/sattva#hinduism "show sattva definitions")*,*[rajas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/rajas#hinduism "show rajas definitions")*and*[tamas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/tamas#purana "show tamas definitions")*, who, being self- luminous, manifested by his own splendour (power), this universe which was enveloped in intense*tamas*(not darkness), produced by Time, at the time of the periodic destruction of the universe (at the end of each*Kalpa*), and who, assuming the three*guṇas*of*[Prakṛti](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/prakriti#purana "show Prakṛti definitions")*creates, protects and destroys this universe.

28.^([\[4\]](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-bhagavata-purana/d/doc1128372.html#note-e-190352 "Padaratnāvalī shows that the attributes mentioned herein are applicable both to the Supreme Lord and to god Brahmā."))Salutations to the Primary Cause (the instrumental cause of the universe who is knowledge and wisdom*incarnate*, and who (as the material cause) becomes manifest through the modifications, viz., vital airs (*[prāṇa](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/prana#purana "show prāṇa definitions")*), senses (*[indriya](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/indriya#purana "show indriya definitions")*), mind and intellect.

29\. You govern the mobiles and immobiles through the chief*Prāṇa*(chief of the five vital airs) and hence, you are the protector of all the creatures, the controller of the mind, and life principles, of the heart and the sense-organs. You are the*[mahat](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/mahat#purana "show mahat definitions")*(and) as such the ruler of the elements (*[bhūtas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/bhutas#purana "show bhūtas definitions")*), their*guṇas*(objects such as sound, touch etc.) as well as their*[vāsanās](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/vasana#purana "show vāsanās definitions")*(sense-impressions).

30.Through your body consisting of the three[Vedas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/vedas#hinduism "show Vedas definitions"), and the sacrificial lore, requiring four officiating priests (viz.[Hotṛ](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/hotri#purana "show Hotṛ definitions"),[Adhvaryu](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/adhvaryu#purana "show Adhvaryu definitions"),[Udgātṛ](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/udgatri#purana "show Udgātṛ definitions")and Brahmā), you perform the seven types of sacrifices (such as*[Agniṣṭoma](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/agnishtoma#purana "show Agniṣṭoma definitions")*,*[Atyagniṣṭoma](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/atyagnishtoma#hinduism "show Atyagniṣṭoma definitions")*,*[Uktha](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/uktha#purana "show Uktha definitions")*,*[Ṣoḍaśī](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/shodashi#purana "show Ṣoḍaśī definitions")*,*[Atirātra](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/atiratra#purana "show Atirātra definitions")*,*[Āptoryāma](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/aptoryama#purana "show Āptoryāma definitions")*and*[Vājapeya](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/vajapeya#purana "show Vājapeya definitions")*). You are the Soul of all living beings. You are indivisible, without a beginning and end, and unlimited by time and place, the Omniscient Indwelling Soul and Controller.

31.You alone are the unwinking Time. You reduce the duration of the life of men by units of time called*[lava](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/lava#purana "show lava definitions")*,*[nimeṣa](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/nimesha#purana "show nimeṣa definitions")*and others. You are the immutable Soul, the occupant of the most exalted position, the birthless, all-pervading principle, the supporter and controller of all living beings.

32.^([\[5\]](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-bhagavata-purana/d/doc1128372.html#note-e-190353 "Padaratnāvalī points out that the attributes in this verse are applicable to the Supreme Lord and this verse is a eulogy of the Inner Controller (antaryāmin).“No mobiles and immobiles of the past, present and future can stand outside and unsupported by you. Both the parā and aparā vidyās abi..."))Neither cause nor effect, or neither mobiles nor immobiles are apart from you. All the[branches](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/branch#hinduism "show branches definitions")of knowledge (viz. Vedas and[upa-Vedas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/upaveda#hinduism "show upa-Vedas definitions")) and arts (auxiliaries of Vedic studies e.g. phonetics, grammar, prosody etc.) it, this shining are your forms. You are the great Brahmā containing within egg of the universe. You are beyond (transcendental to) Prakṛti consisting of three*guṇas*.

33.^([\[6\]](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-bhagavata-purana/d/doc1128372.html#note-e-190354 "This verse sums up both the gross and subtle forms of Brahmā.—Bhāgavata CandrikāPadaratnāvalī.—The words hiraṇya-garbha etc. show that the universe is Hari’s gross form, in which he enjoys supreme bliss and not misery, or pain like Devadatta (an ordinary man).As usual Padaratnāvalī gi..."))Oh all-pervading Lord! This gross manifested universe is your body through which you enjoy the objects of senses, life-breath (vital energy) and the mind, remaining all the while established in your original most exalted state. You are the unmanifest (subtle)*[ātman](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/atman#purana "show ātman definitions")*and the most ancient Supreme Person.

34\. Salutation to the glorious Lord who has pervaded the whole of the universe by His infinite, unmanifest form and who is possessed of powers called*cit*or*[vidyā](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/vidya#purana "show vidyā definitions")*and*[acit](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/acit#hinduism "show acit definitions")*or*[māyā](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/maya#purana "show māyā definitions")*
