If you have a dental pain (samsara) and went to a doctor(Guru) for relief and the medicine(sastra) givento you have to be taken with faith and trust (sradha and bhakti). After that who will know and verify that you have been freed (mukti) of the pain? Not even the doctor (Guru) can verify.

Similar is the case.
