Charamouliji and others who responded to my inquiry - PraNAms

  

Those familiar with Bhagavan Ramana Maharshi's lifeknow that he was meditating like a statue unaware of the external world. The compositions, particularly 'Upadesha Saara' and 'Sat Darshanam', and his answers to the questions came later. Obviously, he was answering using his mind. The question that arises is whether that state of - unawareness of the universe - is a prerequisite for Self-Realization.
I am also reminded of the sloka - 'vidhyaa vinaya sampanne ... samadarshinaH' -and 'samatvam yoga uchyate'.

  

Q. Is akhandaakaara vritti - same as Nirvikalpaka Samadhi?

  

Hari Om!

Sada
