Hare Krishna



In the Bhashya, it is stated that destruction of avidyA and origination of jnAna are not related as cause and effect. On the otherhand the two terms mean the same.



- Perhaps you may find the relevance here in 4.4.6 bruhad bhAshya,
  tasmAdavidyA nivruttimAtre mOksha vyavahAra eti chAvOchAma, yathA
  rajjvAdau sarpadhyajnAnanivruttau sarpAdi nivruttiH. Or in sUtra
  bhAshya phalatva prasiddhiH api mOkshasya bandhanivruttimAtrApekshA,
  nApUrvOpajananApekshA.
