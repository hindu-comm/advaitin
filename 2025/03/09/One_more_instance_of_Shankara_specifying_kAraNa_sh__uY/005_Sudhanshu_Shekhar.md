Namaste Subbu ji, Bhaskar ji.

  

One line caught my attention (IshAvAsyOpanishad BhAshya, Mantra 8):

  
अकायम् अशरीरं ***लिङ्गशरीर***वर्जित इत्यर्थः । अव्रणमस्नाविरमित्येताभ्यां ***स्थूलशरीर***प्रतिषेधः । शुद्धं निर्मलमविद्यामलरहितमिति ***कारणशरीर***प्रतिषेधः ।

  

Clearly AchArya accepts three sharIra; namely sthUla, sUkshma and kAraNa sharIra. And all three are being denied for Atman.

  

Let us see how AchArya denies kAraNa sharIra - by negating avidyA.

  

Clearly avidyA is accepted by AchArya as kAraNa sharIra.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
