Clearly avidyA is accepted by AchArya as kAraNa sharIra.



- Yes, kAraNa shareera is avidyA and Atman is deha trayAteetaH. Since
  avidyA is destroyable by knowledge, it is appropriate to call it a
  shareera/ body. In this sense avidyA is called kAraNa shareera in
  this ishAvAsya .Bh.8. For identifying oneself with stUla and sUkshma
  shareera the kAraNa is avidyA and that avidyA can be here contextually
  called as shareera since ignorance about himself is the basic cause
  for janana-maraNa chakra.
