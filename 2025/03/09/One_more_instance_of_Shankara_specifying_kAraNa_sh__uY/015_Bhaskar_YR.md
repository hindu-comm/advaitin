- Kindly pardon me prabhuji, I beg to differ here. The contention
  observation that mAyA is NOT avidyA is quite clear here, as you
  explained mAya belongs to Ishwara and it is not in jeeva and avidyA is
  in jeeva whereas Ishwara is nitya buddha mukta svarUpa. tAnyahaM veda
  sarvANi na tvaM vetta paraNtapa, clarifies Lord to arjuna. For this
  Jagat brahman is the abhinna nimittopadAna kAraNa, and mAya is Ishwara
  shakti hence jagat is the effect of this Ishwara shakti mAyA which is
  transactionally the same for everyone. If your goodself and myself
  stand on the same stage every sane person would identify both of us as
  male bodies. That is the mAyA / Ishwara srushti. Whereas you one is
  vedAnta Acharya and another one is loukika can be known only by one’s
  own mind it cannot be known by others. Hence avidyA and resultant
  adhyAsa exists only in one’s mind that is, it cannot be known by
  others. If we see the strangers in male and female bodies we can say
  there is Man and woman whereas their personal relationship (like
  husband and wife, brother-sister, mother-son, father-daughter etc.) is
  known only to themselves. In this sence mAya is divine. It is showing
  us the brahman in the form of jagat. OTOH avidyA is tuccha, neecha
  and anartha hetu. It causes grief through the samsAra, jeeva suffers
  due to avidyA. mAya as effect not different from its cause (trishu
  kAleshu) it would carry the status of pariNAmi nityatva like veda
  carries the pravAha nityatvaM. whereas complete annihilating of
  avidyA is possible with the dawn of paramArtha jnAna. With all this
  it is quite evident that there is a world of difference between avidyA
  and mAya. somehow, there is reason unknown, scholars and
  sampUrNAvadhi vedAntins like your goodself carrying and entertaining a
  wrong impression that they are synonymous just because it is said so
  in traditional later vyAkhyAnakAras’ works.
