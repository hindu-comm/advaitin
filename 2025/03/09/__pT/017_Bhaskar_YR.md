> The main siddhAnta of ShAnkara BhAshya is ajAti-vAda. Rest all are
> intermediate explanations. Wherever BhAshya equates waking and dream,
> that is DSV. Wherever it distinguishes waking and dream, that is SDV.
> I am sure you would have noticed these two mutually exclusive
> propositions in bhAshya. Generally the majority of people experience
> difficulty in accepting DSV. And hence Shruti and Smriti primarily
> deal with SDV. That is the reason for the predominance of SDV related
> material everywhere.
