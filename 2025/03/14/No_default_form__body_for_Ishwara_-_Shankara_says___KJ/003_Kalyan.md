Namaste Bhaskarji

  

मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना। Gita 9.4.1

  

This whole world is prevaded by Me in My "unmanifest form".

  

There is no specific given form even for Bhagavan Krishna or Vishnu.
