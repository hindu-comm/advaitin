Dear Michaelji,

  

Permit me to elaborate what Lord Krishna said. Some great event took place, say one century ago and that particular event with the particular spectators / audience, under particular situation, would not be repeated today. Take another case of an event taking place today with great audiences in a particular environment and that event would not exactly be repeated one century later in future. Things and events come and go, and they are lost for ever, and they will not exactly occur again. That way every occurrence, whatever it is, is impermanent and mithya, it will never be repeated exactly. Brahman alone is the creator and the knower of everything, and is real. To us, everything else is 'Mithya'
or as good as non-existent.  

  

Hope you will agree.
