Namaste Sudhanshu Ji!

  

It seems to be only aimed at sannyAsa Ashrama as Guru ji BhAshyakAra himself obviously was.But from what I have read so far, it should be possible to incorporate householders i.e. karmayogins.

  

As Sri Ramakrishna said:

"Whenever you have leisure, go into solitude for a day or two. At that time don't have any relations with the outside world and don't hold any conversation with worldly people on worldly affairs. You must live either in solitude or in the company of holy men."

Source: [https://www.ramakrishnavivekananda.info/gospel/volume_1/15_last_visit.htm](https://www.ramakrishnavivekananda.info/gospel/volume_1/15_last_visit.htm)
