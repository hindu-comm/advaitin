Does the name/form/sound of Hitler, Aurangzeb, Pol Pot, Duryodhana, Ravana also refer to Brahman?

- Yes!! It is because of the simple reason There exists nothing apart
  from brahman. Brahma dAshA brahma dAsA brahmaiveme kitavAH..he is
  taskara, he is gambler what not?? 😊guNA guNEshu
  vartante…svabhAvastu pravartante…

\>

  

IMHO, we have to be careful here. All such name/forms primarily refer to the indwelling antaryAmi Brahman/Ishwara. There is a need to eliminate the jada/jiva/shareera associated with those particular name/forms. 🙂

  

Those Rajasic/Tamasic name/forms cannot be treated the same as the Lord Sri Krishna, Sri Rama, Sri Shankara and so on. 🙂
