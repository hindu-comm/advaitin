- Yes!! It is because of the simple reason There exists nothing apart
  from brahman. Brahma dAshA brahma dAsA brahmaiveme kitavAH..he is
  taskara, he is gambler what not?? 😊guNA guNEshu
  vartante…svabhAvastu pravartante…

IMHO, we have to be careful here. All such name/forms primarily refer to the indwelling antaryAmi Brahman/Ishwara. There is a need to eliminate the jada/jiva/shareera associated with those particular name/forms. 🙂



- To realize the antaryAmi brahman either we have to reject all upAdhi-s
  (made up of of sAtvika, rAjasika or tAmasika) or accept that all
  kArya-s are in its kAraNa rUpa brahman only. We cannot selectively
  say one body is good and another body is bad. From the pAramArthika
  drushti whatever is there that is brahman only, hence it is said
  brahma jnAni is samadarshi : vidyA Vinaya saMpanne brAhmaNe gavi
  hastini….puNditAH samadarshinaH. When the different nAma rUpa are
  realized from the pAramArthika drushti that they are not different
  from brahman, it will be realized that there is only one Brahman and
  absolutely no multiplicity like deva-dAnava 😊



Those Rajasic/Tamasic name/forms cannot be treated the same as the Lord Sri Krishna, Sri Rama, Sri Shankara and so on. 🙂
