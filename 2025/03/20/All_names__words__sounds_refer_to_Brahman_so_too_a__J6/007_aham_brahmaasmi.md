To a brahmanishtha, yes, ALL names/forms/sounds will be only brahman.

  

But, I presume the context here is from a sadhaka's perspective. To invoke brahman, we need to have divine forms, one associated withsattva and beyond. The brahman/ brahmananda, although all pervasive, reflects only in a sattvic antahkarana. It is encouraged for sadhakas to associate with mahatmas keeping in mind the keeta-bhramara-nyaya. Encouraged to avoid dussanga. The mental vritti takes the form of what it associates with.

Therefore, as a sadhak, I would stay away from associating these names with brahman. However, in the same breath, there is no reason to hate them. Of course, if one cannot avoid interacting with Hitler, Aurangzeb and the likes, the sincere sadhaka will attempt using thoseinteractions for vishvarupa-darshanam.

hari: Om !

  

sAkshI
