praNAms Sri Kalyan prabhuji

Hare Krishna



Brahman is full of luster, lacks shine, kAmamaya -akAmamayaH krodhamayaH akrOdhamayaH explains shruti. So edamittham type of description is not possible and we cannot subjectively choose who is good and who is bad!!. He, the brahman, sarva kAraNa-karta can be noble like mahAvishu or filthy worm in vraNa…It may be true that at the vyAvahArika level we have to differentiate between Hitler and Hari but this differentiation will not hold good and is not true at the transcendental level. The reason is very simple, we say that THAT Hari who creates THIS Hitler is sarvashakta and sarvajna. But the descriptions “THIS Hitler, THAT Hari / brahman is possible only from the transactional view, but not from the transcendental view. It is because the Hitler is also brahman from the transcendental view as there exists nothing but brahman. Brahman cannot therefore be distinguished as ‘THAT’ and ‘THIS’. When we keep red rose behind Shuddha spatika it appears like red rose and same spatika (crystal) appears as black rose when it is associated with it, upAdhi bedha plays a big role here but Chaitanya behind every upAdhi is ekaM.
Hence we cannot categorically conclude Hitler is not brahman only noble souls like Vivekananda or paramahamsa is brahman though it is true that we have to maintain distance from evil spirits ( dushta sahavAsa) in this vyAvahArika world. OTOH, if we give some special status to disgraceful personalities aloof from ekamevAdviteeya kAraNam brahman then subsequent query will be : ‘who are they’??



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
