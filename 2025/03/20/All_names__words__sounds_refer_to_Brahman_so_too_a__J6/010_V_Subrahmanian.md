Yes, this is perfectly stated. Swami Paramarthananda said theevolution of the aspirant ideally is: From Eka rUpa Ishwara Bhakti to VishwarUpa Ishwara bhakti to arUpa Ishwara Bhakti.

  

At the VishwarUpa Ishwara bhakti there is no excluding of anything (Hitler, etc.) from Ishwara, for that would make Ishwara a-pUrNa. That would not be the correct understanding. So, everything in creation is viewed upon as Ishwra. One example, from the Gita itself is: the Jnani would view the Brahman even in a chanDAla, a dog, etc. The arUpa stage is where all these forms are within creation and Brahman is beyond creation. That is the final Advaita vision.

  

Note:....... for that would make Ishwara a-pUrNa: This is called vastu pariccheda doSha which is denied in Brahman. If A is not B, then both A and B limit each other as objects: there is abhAva of A in B and vice versa. If Brahman is not x y z, then Brahman's absence in these and the absence of these in Brahman. Hence alone the entire creation consisting of the good, the bad and the ugly is to be viewed as not separate from Brahman but as truly Brahman.
