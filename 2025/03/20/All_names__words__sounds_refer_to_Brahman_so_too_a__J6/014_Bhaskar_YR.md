Hare Krishna



I remember read this somewhere, most probably in bruhadAraNyaka only, it is full of dharma, it is full of adharma, angry, angerless, full of desires, desireless etc. Whether one is an short tempered person or an angerless person, whether one is dharmAnushtAnavaNta or adharmi both are brahman only. But brahman in Itself is neither dharmi nor adharmi as you said it is the ‘not this, not this’ brahman (nirupAdhika, nirguNa, nirvishesha brahman). But in the background of an angry mind the brahman appears to be angry, likewise in the background of an adharmAcharita mind It appears to be adharmi. I reckon brahma dAshA, brahma dAsA etc. said in this context only. Would search for the exact reference anyway.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
