Hare Krishna



"At the VishwarUpa Ishwara bhakti there is no excluding of anything (Hitler, etc.) from Ishwara, for that would make Ishwara a-pUrNa."



Yes, someone like Hitler would be included in the Vishwaroopa, but Hitler would not be included as a form of Bhagavan. How will such people be included then?



Ø I am really surprised that Advaitin like you thinking like dvaitin !! If we subjectively segregate some forms / upAdhi-s as brahman / bhagavaan and some are different from bhagavaan, then it is simply duality not Advaita. Because we are differentiating the Chaitanya behind different upAdhi-s. It is shAnta mUrthy devi only drank the blood of asura-s, chew the flesh of daityagaNa. It is the Lord only the kAla (jagadbhakshaka) arjuna is nimitta mAtra, likewise Hitler is nimitta mAtra even if there was never the person called Hitler, the devastation was bound to happen through some other way and for that kAraNa is again Lord himself. Can we think about anything apart from that avichinna Chaitanya?? Yes, as said earlier characters like Hitler etc. in the transactional world entirely different from sajjana and we need to leave the dushta sahavAsa and should have satsanga, but whatever we see in this world are not different from brahman.



Gita itself gives an answer-



अर्जुन उवाच  
  
स्थाने हृषीकेश तव प्रकीर्त्या  
जगत् प्रहृष्यत्यनुरज्यते च।  
रक्षांसि भीतानि दिशो द्रवन्ति  
सर्वे नमस्यन्ति च सिद्धसङ्घाः।।11.36।।



11.36 Arjuna said It is proper, O Hrsikesa, that the world becomes delighted and attracted by Your praise; that the Raksasas, stricken with fear, run in all directions; and that all the groups of the Siddhas bow down (toYou).



Hitler, Aurangazeb would be like the Rakshasas fleeing with fear. They will not be forms of Bhagawan.



Ø They are indeed nothing apart from bhahman but when there is Ishwara / bhagavaan, jeeva and jagat bedha, then definitely they are different from sajjana, a vyAvahAric / practical satya which we have to accept and follow as we are still jeeva and thinking ourselves someway superior to these demons, someway inferior to some mahAtma-s 😊
