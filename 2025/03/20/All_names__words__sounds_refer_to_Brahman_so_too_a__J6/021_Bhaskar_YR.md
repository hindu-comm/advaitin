praNAms Sri Kalyan prabhuji

Hare Krishna



- Thanks sudhAnshu prabhuji for giving the reference from upanishad.



The reference does not say that brahman in unrighteous.



Ø Yes, likewise we should not say brahman is righteous (full of dharma), full of desires (kAmamayaH) etc. And when we say he is mere sarva kalyANa guNa saMpanna and there is some other Chaitanya is having durguNa / dushchintana etc. then it is Advaita hAni or dvaita. That which Chaitanya having the upAdhi of rAma (nAma rUpa), same secondless chaitanya having or associated with the upAdhi of rAvaNa as well. Both are different in this vyAvahArika jagat where rAma ‘killed’ rAvaNa 😊



The bhAshya makes things clear. This is about the transmigrating self and it lays down the theory of karma.



Ø Yes, jeeva is getting the upAdhi, he will have to enjoy / suffer his karma phala and Ishwara is the karma phala dAta. But that should not lead us to think in such a way brahman is associated ONLY with some selected upAdhi-s. As we the advaitain upholders of ekamevAdviteeya Chaitanya.



The transmigrating self is of course brahman from the paramarthika viewpoint.



Ø And in vyavahAra the same brahman appears as jeeva, jagat and Ishwara. And when it is said jeeva and jagat, the same brahman as sakala jeeva rAshi and sarva jagat.



There is no indication here that brahman is adharma-maya.



Ø As said above, on the same line, he is neither dharma maya, he is always nirguNa, nirupAdhika, nirvishesha kevala Chaitanya svarUpa and selective attribution or qualified characters to it is dvaita.
