Hare Krishna



Yes, this is what the ultimate realization of the jnAni, sarvatra samadarshiNaH, Atmaikatva darshana, this is what Lord explains in vidyA Vinaya saMpanne brahmaNe gavi hastini…the paramArtha jnAni perceives same truth/brahman in brAhmaNa who is endowed with knowledge and good culture, a cow, an elephant, a dog and a dog-eating outcaste also. And the point to be noted here is even though he is having the sama darshana it does not stop him to address, cow, dog, elephant, braHmaNa, an out-casted one etc. differently maintaining their respective social status.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
