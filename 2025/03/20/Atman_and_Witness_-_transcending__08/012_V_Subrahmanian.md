I would like to add that in the 13 chapter of the Geeta Bhagwan says that the witness of the body is kshetratrajna. Immediately He says that that witness in every body is Me that is bramhan. If this second sloka were not to be there, then the defect of the witness being a entity which is available in many bodies as many entities will go uncorrected.
Such a defect is correctef by Bhagawan by making the second statement.
