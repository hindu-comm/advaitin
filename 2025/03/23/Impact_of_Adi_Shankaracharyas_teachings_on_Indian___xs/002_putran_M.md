I heard part of this interview of this swami. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4wD6SzjSJY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4wD6SzjSJY)

  

See 36:57 to 44:20, where he speaks on his coming back to India (from US) and till his guru left (as a wanderingmonk) the ashram permanently. The anecdotes are again stirring examples of this "sannyasa-type" of vairagya, where one's jnana and/or shraddha is such that when the inner call of Ishvara comes, whether as a choice or beyond our choice, we can detach from the past bondage, people, possessions, at once and with full certitude, and move in the path He (the selfless Self) directs.

  

That apart, the swami had a rather unusual early life. He joined the ashram at age 6 and then was sent by his guru to England and US at age 11 (!) with the mandate of spreading yoga there (presumably working under otherseniorswamis). He says that outside of short visits to India, he spent the next 12 years in US until suddenly at age 23 was called back to take over the main ashram in Bihar.
