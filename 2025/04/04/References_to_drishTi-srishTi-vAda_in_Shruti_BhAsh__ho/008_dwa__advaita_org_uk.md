Dear Michael-ji and Sudhanshu-ji,



When I wrote ‘A-U-M – Awakening to Reality’, which is an analysis and commentary on the Māṇḍūkya and Kārikās, I studied every English translation and commentary then available. (There have been a couple more published since then.) The only kārikā that contained an inkling of DSV was 2.16, and it was in connection with that that I made the only reference in the book to DSV and EJV (in an Appendix!). Gauḍapāda was really only interested in going straight for AJV, not on making diverting detours along the way!
