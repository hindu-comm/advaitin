Few thoughts -

  

1\. I am associated with an Organization that conducts annualVeda exams across my state. It's a multi day event, and many small talks and other events are organized at the same time. On oneoccasion, some 15 years ago, there was a round table conference where many scholars were invited to talk on how to increase the number of students taking up vedic education. Many people spoke, talked about how importantthe vedic education is, how beneficialit is for everyone and to the students etc. and also suggested ideas. In the end, the chair of that event asked a question - How many of these scholars had their kids join the Vedic education ? The answer was zero.

  

2\. At NSU Tirupati, there are eager students to study advanced Nyaya Texts, either as a BA/MA student enrolled in that Shastra, or students of other shastras also interested in advanced navya-nyaya because of the cross-connect. The monthly vakyarthas are lively. So I don't think there is a dearth of people trying to become experts in these shastras. (the numbers are relative anyways). But the real concern is how would these future scholars earn their livelihood.

  

Thanks
