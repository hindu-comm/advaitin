Dear Sri Viswanath,

  

What you say is generally true with regard to the scholar fathers not inspiring their sons/daughters to come into shaastraic learning.
Despite this general apathy, we do see quite a number of scholars making up their minds to put their children in Vedic/Shaastric education. I myself know of quite a number of scholars from Sringeri and elsewhere who have committed their children to such education. There are rare cases where fathers who are engineers, chartered accountants, etc. put their children in the Veda/shaastra system.

  

Regarding the future job/earning prospects, we need not worry as the system itself will generate jobs at variouslevels. Just as in any other vocation, it is the skill sets theindividual has that gets him plum jobs.
