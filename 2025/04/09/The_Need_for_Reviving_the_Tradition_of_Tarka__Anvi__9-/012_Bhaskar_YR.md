*Now, for the question you actually asked, at the individual level, I think we should become more deliberate in our reasoning and decision-making process, that it is pramana-based and how so. So, we have to first of all teach ourselves what the pramanas are and how they are used. Then we have to do reasoning within the bounds of the accepted pramana-framework. And we have to dissuade ourselves from going off in speculative tangents independently of the pramanas.*

I am happy that you have agreed to Sri Putran prabhuji’s observation that : reasoning should be within the boundaries of the accepted pramANa. The tarka (the reasoning) mananaM should be done within the frame work of pramANa. For the brahma jignAsa ( I think this jignAsa is most important in the jnAna mArga) the reasoning should be within the framework of Shabda pramANa (shrutyanugraheeta tarka) and tarka should not be mere intellectual tarka using dry logic without any backdrop of Shabda and anubhava pramANa. The logic which is mere intellectua can never analyze completely any complicated system especially our svarUpa jignAsa. The reason is as such an analysis needs the information of all its related parts. Since brahma jignAsa is itself a razor path and huge it is impossible for anyone to have it on hand without the aid of shuti and anubhAva.

It is true that logical analysis will lead to some clarity about the darshana but it has its own limitation which cannot be denied. And when the mere logic is not self-sufficient and when the new information from other logician comes the already established logical conclusions will stand on the shaky ground and own logic which created by the earlier logician will have some new problems also. In order to analyze these things further, the logicians are forced to either refine their own logic, extend it, or do both to cope with newly found logical conclusions. Due to all these topsy-turvy (utterly confusing) logical conclusions may become more knotty. For example when someone says :
there is mithyA vastu darshana in sushupti because it is so in other two states, so it has to be logically there in sushupti also!! This is utter confusing logical conclusion and no need to say plainly goes against accepted pramANa shruti and goes against our anubhava as well.
The dry logical conclusions like this and refining and extending the own created logic making the jignAsa increasingly more complicated. In this way the final solution can never be found and obviously any conclusions arrived through mere dry logic does not have the support of valid pramANa and it is subject to change time and again. IMO and with all due respects to those great logicians, it is the biggest limitations inherent in mere dry logical conclusions. And as a matter of fact, the shushka tarka and its limitations briefed by bhAshyakAra and he also explains how shrutyanugraheeta pUrNa tarka based on sArvatrika pUrNAnubhava is going to help to do the mananam in the sAmpradAyik way in sUtra bhAshya 2-1-11.
