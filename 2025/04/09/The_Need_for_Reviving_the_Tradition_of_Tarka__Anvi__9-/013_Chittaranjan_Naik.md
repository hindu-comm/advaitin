Dear Sri Bhaskar Prabhuji,

  

*I am happy that you have agreed to Sri Putran prabhuji’s observation that : reasoning should be within the boundaries of the accepted pramANa. The tarka (the reasoning) mananaM should be done within the frame work of pramANa.*

  

Yes, we are in agreement here.

  

  

*For the brahma jignAsa ( I think this jignAsa is most important in the jnAna mArga) the reasoning should be within the framework of Shabda pramANa (shrutyanugraheeta tarka) and tarka should not be mere intellectual tarka using dry logic without any backdrop of Shabda and anubhava pramANa.*

  

In this thread I am restricting myself to the field of pravritti dharma and am not attempting to foray into the domain of brahma-jnana. That is the reason why I am emphasizing the need for reviving Nyaya shastra and the tradition of tarka. Of course, it goes without saying (at least to members on this forum) that Vedants is the ultimate knowledge that we must aspire to but at the same time it is important to lay stress of those aspects of knowledge that would help steer us towards obtaining chitta-shuddi by which alone we would obtain the fitness to effectively pursue the path of brahma-jnana.

  

  

Warm regards,

Chittaranjan
