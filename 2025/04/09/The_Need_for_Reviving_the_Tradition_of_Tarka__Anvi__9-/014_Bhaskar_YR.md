Just wanted to say even in the pravrutti mArga (karma pradhAna mArga) the shAstra is the ONLY pramANa as shAstra vihita karma ends in adrushta phala. And for both the pravrutti and nivrutti mArga-s the ultimate phala is ‘niHshreyasaH. And in pravrutti mArga to determine which is ‘dharma’ and which is ‘adharma’ we need to take the shelter in shAstra only. To get the ‘dharma shuddhi’ one has to clearly understand pratyaksha, anumAna and shAstra and one has to align the tarka (yukti) without going against veda-shAstra. And he is the dharma veda who does this and there is no other way clarifies shankara. So, IMHO when we are constructing the tarka that helps us to handle the pravrutti mArga we have to invariably have the aid of shAstra. Anyway, this is just what shAstra / sampradaya says.
