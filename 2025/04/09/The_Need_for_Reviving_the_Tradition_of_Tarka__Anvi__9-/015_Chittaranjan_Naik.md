Dear Sri Bhaskar Prabhu-ji,

  

You are right, whether it is nivritti marga or pravritti marga, the pramana for dharma is shastra only. But here we are not trying to derive the principles of dharma from logic. we are trying to dismantle the theories and arguments of the purva-pakshins and the instrument to do so is tarka. The primary aim of such tarka is to remove those doubts about our own (Vedic) dharma that have been implanted into our minds by the theories and arguments of the purva-pakshins. This is what Nyaya shastra is meant for.

  

Warm regards,

Chittaranjan
