Namaskaram,

  

Wonderful initiative; knowledgeable and pleasing to hear. Typically we see this type of scholarly discussion done in Sanskrit; but here it is a fresh approach with young erudite vidwans discussing in the Englishlanguage. Looking into their page, I recall seeing episode 1 of the earlier series on Sanatana Dharma (which now has othervideos on important topics). Ideally time-stamps can be added.

  

The explanations on the importance of karma and the nature of advaita bhakti are presented clearly and convincingly. I have read people claim that adi shankara distorted Gita's meaning in order to push through his own siddhanta. These scholars not only took that claim head on but simply inverted it on its head - that it is shankara who took great care to convey the actual meanings without the least distortion.

  

I was not sure aboutthestatement that Veda Vyasa wrote theGita based on his knowledge of the Vedas. Is it not typically understood that Vyasa wrote the Gita that Krishna spoke to Arjuna? We think of the verses as if that is what Krishna actually uttered, and not as a paraphrasing into poetry by Vyasa - right?

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
