Namaste Bhaskar Ji,

Reg // mAya is the shakti through which brahman become creator, without which brahman is incapable of doing anything. Hence it would be better to go by siddhAnta that shakti or shakta is Shuddha brahman only //,

I am not sure I understood this correctly. In the first sentence you are saying mAyA is the shakti. In the second sentence you are saying
\*\* shakti or shakta is Shuddha brahman only \*\*. The two appear to me to be contradictory. In my understanding, in the first sentence, mAyA should be stated as the \*\*entity\*\* and not \*\*shakti\*\*. That it is a \*\*shakti\*\* is adhyAsa only. I am not sure if you have meant the same.
