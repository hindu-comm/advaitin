Namaskaram,

  

I heard the first 30 min and some parts later. I thought the topics overlap with theinterests of Chitta-ji; perhaps he is a good person for Chitta-ji to communicate with. So I thought and think. However, while I was appreciating Pratosh-ji's knowledge, and contemplations about the greatness of our shastras and nyaya-logical ("scientific") thinking, I could also not help but note that he likely has a more favorable consideration for modern science and AI than Chitta-ji seems to. For example, Pratosh-ji thinks our logical and epistemological methods are parallel with (or essentially equivalent to) those of science whereas Chitta-ji thinks the latter (as perpetuated and practiced) is fundamentally confused due to faultylogic and dogmatism. Likewise Pratosh-ji suggests at one point that AI replicates or even supercedes human intelligence; Chitta-ji would probably deny that and say AI operates according to a certain logic that is limited in scope and cannot cover the range of human intelligence (based on other type of logic).
