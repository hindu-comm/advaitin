Namaskar:  
  
Thanks for sharing a very useful reference. In Vedanta, any elaboration to comprehend our understanding of the scriptures is always welcome and useful. We always want to use our intellect to develop our thinking for greater understanding. This is one of the primary reasons that Acharyas introduce notions (definitions, assumptions) etc., to explain and to make us understand a difficult concept. The sages of the Upanishads also told us that ultimately the notions that helped us to understand needed to be discarded. The Neti-Neti is a process of Self-inquiry where we repeatedly negate, or say "not this, not that,"to arrive at a more authentic understanding of the self. The final destination of Self-realization requires that we throw all notions! Swami Chinmayananda used to give the Pole-vault example to illustrate this point. The pole-vault jumper uses the pole to cross over the pole with the help of another pole but to clear the pole, the jumper discards the pole!
Swamiji used to say that just like the Pole-vault jumper, we also need the intellect to jump over our intellect in order to go beyond the intellect!!
