I don't know whether Bhashyakara directly quotes this text anywhere. But many analogies which he uses are already present in this text.

  

This text is also referenced in Vidyaranya's Jeevanmukti Viveka, where it is called Aaryaa-panchaasheeti (85 verses in Aarya chandas / metre). Abhinava Gupta who is a great acharya of Kashmiri Shaiva Darshana adapted this text, and expanded it, while retaining the original title, to form a manual of the Pratyabhijna Shaiva Darshana.

  

In fact Abhinava Gupta refers to this text as Shruti in one place in his Gita Bhashya. So scholars consider this text as one of the two early authoritative vedanta texts along with Gaudapada Karika. Some even say Gaudapada has used the ideas present in this text in his Karikas and it is older than Gaudapada Karika.
