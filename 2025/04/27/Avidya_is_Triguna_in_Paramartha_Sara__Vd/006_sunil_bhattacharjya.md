Dear Jaishankara Narayananji,  

  

Yes, Avidya cannot touch Atma, yet the Jiva does not begin with the realization that the world is unreal. Till the realization comes the world is not unreal for the Jiva. During the Mahajnani Tathagata Buddha's time (in the 19th century BCE) some Vedic Brahmin scholars were confused and they approached Lord Buddha and requested him to explain how an ajnani becomes a Jnanl. Then Bhagavan Buddha coined a term "Tathagata-garbha", or the Buddha-embryo. The born being begins with the tathagata-garbha stage, shadowed by the Physical and mental defilements of everyday life. Eventually through the grace of the guru, the tathgata-garbha develops into Tathagata. That is how Lord Buddha started his Mahayana teachings, to suit the scholars of his time. Lord Buddha said that what he was teaching was nothing but the Sanatana Dharma.   

  

Any comment?

  

Regards
