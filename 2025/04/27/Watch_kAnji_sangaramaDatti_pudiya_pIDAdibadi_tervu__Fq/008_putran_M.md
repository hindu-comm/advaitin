> Does the measurement and way of draping the saree really matters

Yes, that is how I have been taught. For Brahmins, married man must wear panchakaccham style and married women 9-yard madisar. At least they would follow that when visiting matha, or change to such within matha before going for darshan.

  

> She is just wearing the traditional attire, is that not enough?? Do we
> have to be too fussy here!!??

That's what the original question was. It did not look traditional according to orthodox requirements. Subbu-ji suggests it was not. In that case, yes there is room to be fussy here. Its a picky group, tamil Brahmins vouching allegiance to kanchi matha and treating paramacharya as God! Hey, then don't bring common excuses now! Don't worry, they know it imo.

  

> I am just asking this out of curiosity as I really don’t know what
> parents should wear when their son taking the Ashrama sannyasa. By
> the way, what would be the sampradaya dress of North Indian SanyAsi
> paretns when their son taking sannyasa, especially when to-be Swamiji
> and his parents born and brought up in North India culture !!?? If I
> am right there even dresses like salwar kameez, jubba pyjama etc.
> considered as a traditional dress is it not?

According to their sampradaya and *orthodox* traditions of their matha etc. In Tamil kanchi matha culture, these things are equated to visesha svadharma. I don't know about north. But, I am not asking that the telugu brahmin dress like the tamil, just whether they are according to their own at least.
