Over a year back the Sringeri Peetham put in place a dress code for devotees visiting Sringeri temples and going for darshan of the Acharyas. This is strictly implemented. During my recent visit it was a pleasant sight seeing non-brahmin families too bringing their even very small boys clad in dhoties and angavastrams over a shirt. Of course, the traditional style sari/pancha kaccham is followed by many devotees over generations voluntarily, both in Sringeri and Kanchi.

  

In the North, the situation is somewhat different. There is traditional sari, covering the head, in many states, Maharashtra (there is a kacchi very much resembling the Madhwa women's attire), UP, Bengal, Bihar, etc. even for Brahmin women. In Punjab even though salwar kameez is widely prevalent, covering the head is part of the tradition. So is in Rajasthan. One reason for the salwar kameez/pyjama even for Brahmin men/priests is the cold weather. But many vaidikas wear the pancha kacchamthere, they sport shikhai too.
