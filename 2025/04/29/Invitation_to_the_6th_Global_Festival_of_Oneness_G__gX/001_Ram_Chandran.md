Namaskar:

I am happy to report that I have registered both for the morning and evening sessions of the 6th Global Festival of Oneness. The program starts at the exact time and ends exactly in one hour. Those who want to get inspiration and motivation towards the learning of Shankara Bhagavad Pada's Advaita Vedanta can gain a lot by listening. Some of the lectures at a higher level and needed a good background on the subject matter. Also the presenters use Sanskrit (though a limited extent) but they do provide English meanings appropriately. The speakers are great scholars and we all need to thank them for spending their valuable time to propagate advaita Vedanta and related scriptures such as Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads. Brahmasutra, and other works of Shankara Bhagavad pada. The presentations are in lecture format with out using audio/visual items such as the Power-point. Since the time is limited to one hour, there is not really any problem during listening.
With a large number of scholars, no one should expect that all of them will be very clear in communicating everything that they wanted to convey. The organizers hopefully will be able to provide one page summary of each topic which will be useful for those who couldn't attend these lectures due to their constraint to time. This forum will certainly entertain anyone who wants to post materials from the Global Festival of Oneness.

I wish the Global Festival all success and thanks to all the volunteers who made this possible.

  

With my warm regards,

Ram Chandran
