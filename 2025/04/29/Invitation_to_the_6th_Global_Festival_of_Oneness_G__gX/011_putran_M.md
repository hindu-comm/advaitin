Namaskaram,

  

On the last day, Nagaraj-ji was saying that they are looking for new ways to reach out to the public. Based on my online yoga class experience, I think a good method will be to try and bring about ways to make the sessions interactive with the audience, where they are not simply passive listeners. It can but it doesn'thave to be two-waywhere their privacy is lost; just instructor does something which they are to follow in live-time or instructor asks them to do something and gives time for them to finish. A class setting where they have to do things on their side, like maybe read the sloka out loud or search out a certain online reference for particular information. "Ok, everyone opento page273 of BSB, sutra \#4. Lets read it aloud, now read the bhashya. You see that compound word, I will give you 30 sec, can you guess the sandhi? Ok. this is how Shankaracharyainterprets this type of usage." It will be less quantity per session but perhaps more quality that will attract people to commit to the process of learning.

  

Since this is knowledge transmission, we have to be innovative about how to bring in kriya that will keep the audience actively engaged and participating while at the same time gaining in knowledge of the material. At least there canbe some classes structured in this approach.
