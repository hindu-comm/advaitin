A similar theme. Amazing "Sankalpam", a new organization started by Smt Latha *Rajinikanth* (yes, that's the husband).

  

Sri U Ve Venkatesh-ji gives a captivating speech in Tamil/Sanskrit:

  

[https://youtu.be/p55t9VBPYKs?si=r1woZRt29-ExV0Lg](https://youtu.be/p55t9VBPYKs?si=r1woZRt29-ExV0Lg)  

  

Their sankalpas:

  

1\. Veda Samrakshanam (protection/preservation of Veda) by supporting Veda patashalas and the children studying in them.

  

2\. Support the functioning of temples (that used to be centerof city/society) by providing for the needs of the archakas.

  

3\. GO samrakshanam (cow protection). By registering different go-shalas under one umbrella organization thatcan coordinate and operate in a unified manner. (What we wonder whether can be done for temples themselves, they are aiming to do at least for go-shalas.)

  

4\. To preserve and spread the knowledge and awareness of our heritage inthe society, thereby enhancingcivilizationalpride and positivity. Create a Center of Excellence for this purpose.

  

5\. To bring unity among all Hindus, sects, groups - the leaves and branches of the umbrella of sanatana dharma - by connecting with our common roots that are the Vedas.

  

Don't miss his naming the 64 kalas (min 11).
