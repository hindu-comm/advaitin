Namaskar dear Ramchndranji,

  

Lord Buddha was born in the early 19th century BCE, i.e., more than thirteen centuries before Adi Shankara. Lord Buddha has been considered to be the 9th avatar of Lord Vishnu. No hindu, including advaitin has opposed the teachings of Lord Buddha. Adi Shankara's paramguru Shri Gaudapadacharya has been said to be an admirer of Lord Buddha's teachings.  

  

On the other hand, Shri Parshvanath, who was living in the 8th century BCE, is considered to be the first historical guru of Jainism. Shri Mahavira was the second and the last guru of Jainism. In Adi Shankara's time there were scholars of the Sanatana dharma, who did contest the teachings of Jainism.  

  

Any comment?

  

Regards

Sunil K.B.
