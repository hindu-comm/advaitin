Dear Chittaranjanji,

  

Congratulations to you for the good work in the area, our thinkers became aware of, a century Ago. Our ancient seers knew that the universe has a beginning and end, like the scientists today too know around when the universe appeared, and possibly when it will end. In the very beginning of the Original Bhagavad Gita, Lord Krishna draws the attention of Arjun to the fact that the world has a beginning and end, i.e., the world is impermanent and unreal. Lord Krishna tells Uddhava the same thing in the chapter on Jnana, towards the end of the Uddhava Gita.  

  

We also find that Lord Krishna told us in the Bhagavad Gita that he knows about his past appearances. Lord Buddha too told us that he could remember his past births. It is thus believed that one, whose spiritual eyes are opened, can see through his / her past births. The "Logical Positivism" seems to be unable to enter this area of investigation. Any comment?  

  

Regards,

Sunil KB
