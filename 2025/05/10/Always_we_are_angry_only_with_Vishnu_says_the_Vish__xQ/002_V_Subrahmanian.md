Dear Kalyan ji,

  

Undoubtedly, this is a tough sadhana. It involves practice of transcending likes and dislikes. It is karma yoga, bhakti yoga and jnana yoga all rolled into one. Can't be achieved easily. Analogies given are: same sightedness with regard to gold and clay, praise and censure, gain and loss, etc.
