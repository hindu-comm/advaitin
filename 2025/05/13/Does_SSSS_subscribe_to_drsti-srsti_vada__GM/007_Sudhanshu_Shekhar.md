Equating waking, dream and sushupti is drishTi-srishTi-vAda.

  

This is present in Shruti, Smriti and BhAshya. त्रयः स्वप्ना: of Aitareya Shruti decimates all objections against DSV. Those who are keen to give some special reality to waking vis-a-vis dream are taught by AchArya - स्वप्नवत् असत्-वस्तु-दर्शनात् च.

  

To aver that DSV is post-Shankara phenomena displays lack of understanding of the writer.
