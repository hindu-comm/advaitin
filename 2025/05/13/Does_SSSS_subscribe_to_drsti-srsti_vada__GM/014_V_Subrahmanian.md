Here is a line from the Sankshepa shariraka of Sarvajnatman, a PSA:

  

कृपणधीः परिणाममुदीक्षते क्षयितकल्मषधीस्तु विवर्तताम्

  

\[‘The unprepared aspirant understands only the ‘creation, transformation’ scheme whereas the one who has purified his mind of all dross is able to appreciate the ‘transfiguration’ vivarta, of Atman/Brahman as appearing as the world and jIva-s.’\]

  

Note that the requirement of a purified mind to appreciate vivarta and by extension DSV. So the Acharyas have taken enough care to avoid pitfalls in Vedanta sadhana.
