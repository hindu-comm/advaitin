//Upahita does not mean conditioned. Take from example face. When you put mirror in front of it, it becomes bimba. Nothing changes in face. But it becomes eligible to be called a bimba. This is what is upahita-chaitanya. It is shuddha chaitanya. But called as sAkshI/Ishwara from the frame of reference of avidyA/mirror.//

  

Yes, I get sakshi/ishwara is namarupa only or a sastrika adhyaropa but I don't get how SSSS's correctives are different from PSA doctrine withMUC. Your bimba/maya must have a positive status SSSS doesn't like.

  

//Does the idea of buddhi as reflecting medium being the upAdhi of asanga chaitanya make sense to you? It has to. There are explicit statements in bhAshya for this.//

  

makes sense as adhyaropa for the sake of deconstructing the notionof individual pramata/pramana/prameya. It's not buddhi reflecting but Chaitanya appearing as buddhi along with the entire prapanca rather than maya appearing as buddhi and prapanca. Is that the difference? .

  

//And if that makes sense to you, then buddhi being avidyA-krita, avidyA-pratibimbita-chaitanya should also make sense. Nothing illogical or impractical.//

Makes sense I suppose unless you are positing causation with avidya krita and/or APC. What then is the buddhi's substratum? Can't be maya as it is illusion and if it's asanga chaitanya then namarupa buddhi must be chaitanya perceived as other than chaitanya. Removing avidya means no pramata, pramana, prameya

  

//MUlAvidyA is central to Advaita. Whenever Advaita says avidyA, it is mUlAvidyA.
