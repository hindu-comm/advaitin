Hare Krishna



Malavidya is central to Advaita. Whenever Advaita says avidyA, it is mUlAvidyA.



- As per Sri SSS mUlAvidyA as explained by vyAkhyAnakAra-s and its
  implications and its influence over the Shuddha Chaitanya is just a
  dangerous tumor in Shuddha shankara vedAnta, not at all found an inch
  of space in shankara’s PTB, hence it should have been nipped in bud.
  But unfortunately now this theory of mUlAvidyA become a big tree and
  we should atleast now try to understand shankara bhAshya without the
  taint of bhAvarUpa mUlAvidyA. And as per Sri SSS ( and as per adhyAsa
  bhAshya of shankara) whenever shankara’s bhAshya talks about avidyA
  primarily it talks about adhyAsa not vyAkhyAnakAras’ adhyAsOpadAna
  kAraNa, bhAvarUpa avidyA!!!



The sum and substance is this -- whatever is seen, is illusory.



- The sum and substance of whatever is seen is brahman not illusory…that
  is what shruti saying. satyaMchAnrutaMcha satyamabhavat, yadidaM
  kiMcha, yatOvA emAni bhUtAni jAyante yena jAtAni jeevanti,
  yatprayantyabhisaMvishanti, tad vijignAsasva “tadbrahmeti”. So at
  the best your above statement holds water only in bhrAnti vAda or
  shUnya vAda not definitely vedAnta’s brahma kAraNa vAda.





The seer is non-illusory. Even I is illusory. The entity intended by the word I is non-illusory.



- And why I should not treat your above statement itself is illusory and
  just born out of dry logic without any support from shruti and
  anubhava!!??
