Hare Krishna



Commenting on this Shankara says: It means that those who are given to enquire into the ultimate reality, do not hold creation to be of any purport. ...A section of those stated above hold that creation is akin to......



- First of all it should be noted that those who are upholding the
  shruti siddhAnta i.e. Ishwara hetuka srushti, doing so coz. whenever
  there is talk of creation it is invariably from Ishwara / srushtikarta
  and understanding this shruti siddhAnta would help us avoid fancy
  theories of jeeva’s bhrAnti vAda. That is the reason why bhAshyakAra
  accepting Ishwara srushti is VEDANTA MARYAADA. So for all practical
  purposes Ishwara is the srushtikarta not this tiny jeeva. All the
  vedAntic statements teach about the creation as that which has Ishwara
  as (the rationally valid and acceptable) kAraNa (hetu) the cause of
  srushti. That Ishwara is the kAraNa means, He creates the jagat

according to jeevas’ own Karma.



Shankara can be seen here to hold the latter group also as those who are not really after the ultimate upanishadic Truth. Shankaracharya concludes by saying: Therefore aspirants after liberation are concerned with the Turiya alone and not with the creation that is of no use, consequence. See the last part of the commentary.



- Na nirOdhO nachOtpattiH…when the ultimate knowledge dawns the jnAni
  would realizes that sarvaM brahma mayaM…in that ekatva drushti there
  is neither sAdhaka, nor sAdhana nor mOksha nor bandha….It does not
  mean one can promote the erroneous theory that jagat is jeeva’s
  bhrAnti in conditioned state.



This is not to deny the invaluable benefit of imbibing aasthikya, bhakti, etc. that can be had from looking upon Brahman as the creator.



- I don’t think those who are denying the sarvajnatva and sarvashaktitva
  of brahman / Ishwara can claim this!! At the best it is just a
  hypocritical statement. Because as per DSV even sarvajna / sarva
  shakta/nitya Shuddha, buddha mukta Ishwara too just a mental creation
  of avidyA jeeva!! A complete blasphemous conclusion by nAstika-s. In
  Kannada for the above type of statements we say : kevala kaNNoraso
  mAtu. (just an eye-wash statement).
