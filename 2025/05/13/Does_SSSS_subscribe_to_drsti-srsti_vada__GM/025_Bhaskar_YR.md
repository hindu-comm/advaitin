Hare Krishna



The very first Brahma Sutra enjoins the prepared mind to embark upon the Vedanta enquiry. All bhakti sadhana is included in the chitta shuddhi required.



Ø Yes and karma, karma yOga, nishkAma karma too advised for the chitta Shuddhi.



The path is: Karma \> Bhakti / Upasana \> Jnana. All Upanishads, Gaudapada and Shankara have this in built carefully crafted path.



- And the result of that ultimate jnAna is samyakjnAna, Atmaikatva
  jnAna, advaya jnAna where in the paramArtha jnAni realizes that
  nothing is subject to janana and maraNa including himself and also
  nothing is different from HIM. This experience of the world being not
  different from oneself is called sarvAtmabhAva.
