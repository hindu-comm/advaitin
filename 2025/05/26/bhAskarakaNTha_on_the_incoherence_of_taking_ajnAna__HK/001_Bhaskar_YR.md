Hare Krishna



Whether it is called absence of knowledge of non-duality (jnAnAbhAva), or doubtful knowledge (saMshaya) about it, or opposite knowledge (vipareeta / anyathAgrahaNa), whatever be the names, all these disappear with jnAnaM, the knowledge, clarifies bhAshyakAra in bruhad bhAshya. So as per this author bhAshyakAra is one among those fools 😊 OTOH if one asserts that there exists jnAna virOdhi ajnAna which is having the ashraya in brahman, ascribing positivity to it etc. simply going against the bhAshya but definitely under the erroneous impression that they are representing shuddhaadvaita 😊



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
