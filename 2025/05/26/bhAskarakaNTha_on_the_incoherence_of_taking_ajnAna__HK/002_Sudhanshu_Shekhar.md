Namaste Lokesh ji.

  

While it is true that ajnAna is not jnAna-abhAva and that these two are different like chalk and cheese, it needs to be appreciated that ajnAna implies jnAna-abhAva.

  
So, if I have x-ajnAna, then it implies x-jnAna-abhAva. So, ajnAna is like smoke and jnAna-abhAva is like fire. Wherever there is smoke (ajnAna), there is jnAna-abhAva (fire). ajnAna is vyApya and jnAna-abhAva is vyApaka. \[ भावरूप-अज्ञानं ज्ञानाभाव-व्याप्यम्-इत्युक्तमेव । भावारूप-अज्ञानेन ज्ञानाभाव-अनुमानप्रयोगं दर्शयति “पूर्वकाले प्रातरित्यर्थः, प्रातः कालीनोऽहम्, गजज्ञानाभाव-वान्, गज-अज्ञान-वत्त्वात्”। ज्ञानाभाव-व्याप्य-अज्ञानवत्त्वादित्यर्थः ।\]

  

Further, while there is sAkshi-pratyaksha of ajnAna, the jnAna-abhAva is known through vyatireki anumAna (arthApatti) or anupalabdhi-pramANa.

  

I mention this because from the write-up of learnedभास्करकण्ठ, it appeared to me as if jnAna-abhAva is being denied. So, I just put forth the considered view of VedAnta regarding jnAna-abhAva.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
