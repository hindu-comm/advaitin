Hare Krishna



I myself seen 9 days fasting only on water during navaraatri. It is a maarvaadi woman who does this every year. I go there during navaraatri for chandi paaraayana, 10^(th) day after durga homa and poorNAhuti and Ashirvachana she will take the theertha (paNchAmruta) and prasaada (gudAnna) to break the fasting. I don’t think normal people would survive 45 days nirjala upavAsa. So I think, teertha here must be something other than water. paNchAgni tapa and 45 days upavAsa without food and water, as prAyaschitta for inadvertently touching the married girl’s hair !! Frankly hard to believe. Is this type of prAyaschitta prescribed in any of our dharma shAstra-s to protect celibacy !!?? Not sure. But don’t know we have the allegories which say our ancient Rishi-muni-s used to survive thousands of years just by air !!



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
