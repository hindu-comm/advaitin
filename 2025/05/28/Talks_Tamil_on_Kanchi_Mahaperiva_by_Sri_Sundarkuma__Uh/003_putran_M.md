Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

I don't know what exactly "no biksha and teertha" means. I just assumed he has no other food either, which as you said is hard to believe. Perhaps some other forms of water or food intake, or the number "45" is a hyperbole - I am ok with this as part of common lore but I don't feel ok if upanyasakarsstandardize such in their talks for the sake of that extra "feel-good" feel to highlight their larger point. The acharya has enough examples of the extraordinary but if we jump over to the "impossible" (for which we only have puranic counterparts), then people's shraddha will get challenged unnecessarily.

  

Again, that is my modern mind talking apachara. We may be clueless about these things. When PM says he fasts 9 days, my logic and background cannot support it; I have to take it by shraddha in his vakya alone. By knowing him in other respects, one finds easierto have that shraddha in this statement as well. But when you say you have seen someone else, or othertrustworthy sources confirm the practice, there is even greater assurance and doubt is practically cleared completely.

  

With 45 days, the story is so unique that without corroboration from shastra, science, or other eye-witness testimony, it has to be just accepted in the sense of and for the sake of bhakti. The problem is that almost definitely the general story is true, but it has come down to us with the questionable 45; and the upanyasakarhas to present it as he has learned it from the tradition. It is upto the devotee to take the whole in the right spirit and discrimination.

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
