The reference is Ch Up 6.7.1-2. However, Chitta-ji in a private email expressed that this (overcoming hunger and thirst for 45 days) need not be difficult to believe (if we knew our vidyas) and gave Yoga sutra reference:

  

Quote

  

See Yoga Sutra III.30 (in some versions it appears as III.31):

  

**"By samyama on the pit of the throat, hunger and thirst are overcome."**

  

By***samyama***is meant the performance of*dharana*(the focussed streaming out of the mind towards an object),*dhyana*(the unwavering meditation or holding of the mind on the object) and*samadhi*(the absorption of the mind in that object) - all done on a single object.

...

Chandogya 6.7.2 is speaking about sustenance of life in the ordinary course of our lives. Yoga III.30 is speaking about an extra-ordinary power - a yogic siddhi - that can be obtained by doing samyama.  

  

Unquote
