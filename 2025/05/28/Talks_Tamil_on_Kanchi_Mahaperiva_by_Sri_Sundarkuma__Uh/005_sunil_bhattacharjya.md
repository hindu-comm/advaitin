Dear Putranji,

  

We have a custom of fasting on Purnima and Amavashya as well as on Janmashtami and Shivaratri. Some of us fast on Ekadashi also. We should surely abstain from grains on those occasions. We should also generally keep in mind what Lord Krishna told us : to take neither more nor less food, and to sleep neither less nor more.  

  

Sunil KB
