In the Valmiki Ramayana, Vishwamitra initiates Rama and Lakmana into a **bala-ati bala** mantra which is said to make the practitioner transcend hunger and thirst.  
  
From the internet:  

  

Bala and Atibala aremantras taught by Vishwamitra to Rama and Lakshmana in the Valmiki Ramayana.These mantras, also known as "Bala Atibala Vidya", are said to grant the ability to overcome hunger, thirst, and fatigue."Bala" specifically conquers sleep, while "Atibala" conquers hunger.

Vishwamitra taught these mantras to Rama and Lakshmana to help them endure the rigors of their journey and to protect them from physical weakness.[According to a Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/sagarworldmultimedia/posts/everyone-knows-vishwamitra-as-the-teacher-of-lord-rama-and-lakshmana-who-gave-th/2484111831647981/), Vishwamitra also initiated them into the knowledge of celestial weaponry and guided them to defeat demons like Tadaka.

For a more detailed explanation, you can refer to the[Valmiki Ramayana](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/baala/sarga22/balaitrans22.htm)(Bala Kanda - Sarga 22) where these events are described.
