praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji

Hare Krishna



And this is also conducive to bhakti because for an Advaita sadhaka, it is the same Ishwara who appears in all names and forms.



- Very beautifully said prabhuji. I always enjoy the siddhAnta vAkya-s
  like this keeping the ‘paraabhakti’ of the mumukshu. The nAstika-s do
  not have any opportunity to taste the nectar of this wisdom..They only
  resort to dry logic to understand the brahma tattva which they do not
  realize that it is invariably connected to IshwarAnugraha as well.
  Ishwara is the bestower of both karma phala and jnAna phala as well.
  madAdeenAm karmiNAM karma phalaM jnAninAmcha jnAna phalaM clarifies
  bhAshyakAra with regard to the Ishwarecche.



And the duality of name and form is a Kalpita Jnanam of Ishwara rather than Ishwara Swaroopam, which means the differentiations of this plane are not truly significant.



- Yes there is no duality whatsoever when Ishwara is considered as both
  nimitta (efficient) and upAdAna (material) kAraNaM (cause) of the
  jagat. HE is secondless ekaM in his svarUpa.



Even the differentiation between myself and the Lord, that is ultimately to be

surrendered in the consciousness that He alone is.

And that is the basis for Advaita bhakti.



- ahamkara tyAga, saMpUrNa sharaNagati is very much there in parAjnAna
  nishTa (paramArtha jnAni) as well. The one who sees vAsudeva in
  everything inside and outside is a mahatma who is very rare and
  available easily as it is an elevated state of the paramArtha jnAni.
  vAsudevaH sarvamiti sa mahatma sudurlabhaH said in geeta because This
  is because he does not recognize even himself as a separate entity
  from vAsudeva..IOW, he will have merged in the Atman/svarUpa of
  vAsudeva i.e. the consciousness. Hence Lord says in geeta : jnAni
  tvAtmaiva me mataH.
