Namaskaram,

  

I inserted the below blue sentence in an update. I felt my initial talk could give the impression that "right guidance in samsara" matters only in VA and D, and not in advaita. So have tried to do a patch up.

  

Quote

  

In Advaita there is a stark difference.

  

It is not real to real relationship. It is actually unreal to real or mithya to satya, relatively real to absolute real. It is only one appearing as the other and the other is actually an appearance or an imagination of the one.

  

So that was Advaita. Therefore you free yourself from the world of imagination. It doesn't matter what happens there, from the final standpoint. Only from the standpoint of ignorance of reality, there is the process of religion to wake up from the dream. But ultimately the dream is a dream. It didn't really matter at the level of the Self that you are.

  

In Visishtadvaita and Dvaita it matters what happens there. It is the right guidance of the samsara that has to free you from the problems and the suffering by realizing the ultimate reality of God, whether it is the total being of whom you are a part of or the being in which you are completely dependent on and rooted in. So one way or the other you free yourself.

  

In all three of them what is the point? The point is turn your attention from the duality to the substratum or ground reality that is God or Brahman and by knowing that you free yourself from this world. The world of the mind, the sense world and so on and so forth. Om Namah Shivaya.

  

Unquote
