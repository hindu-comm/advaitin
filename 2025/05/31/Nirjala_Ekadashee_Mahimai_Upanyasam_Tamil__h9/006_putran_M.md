Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

I presently (~last 1-2 years) follow in a partial manner - the initial motivation was more for health than religion but I like using such body-consciousness to align to religious injunctions. I fast from the last food of the previous 'night' to 6 PM on ekadasi day (water allowed if thirsty). Then 6-10, fruits nuts milk etc allowed; after 10 pm, break the fast with formal heavier food. Not proud of these ad hoc self-made limits (my body condition certainly can allow for a full day fast) but itsbetter than before.

  

I just heard briefly from Subbu-ji's video where the upanyasakar mentions about how people as recently as 2018 have got nobel prizes for 'discoveries' on the health benefits of fasting. Just yesterday, some youtube advertisement video mentioned how 'intermittent fasting' is good for producing 'ketones' which accelerate fat loss naturally - like 12 hours fasting between meals.

  

My yoga instructor also did a month challenge last december that we have at most three eating sessions a day up to 40 min each. No snacks or coffee outside. (For vedantins, "food" can expand to other kinds of desire fulfillments as well, so ideally we can try putting them also into these time-brackets). He gave some insulin based reasoning as well:[https://www.youtube.com/live/r4Q8qPfC0fo](https://www.youtube.com/live/r4Q8qPfC0fo) min 25+ (plug in for general group: [https://habit.yoga](https://habit.yoga))

  

It became tougher as thedays wore on and after that month, Ihave more or less forgotten it. Want to get back to it at least for some days a week.
