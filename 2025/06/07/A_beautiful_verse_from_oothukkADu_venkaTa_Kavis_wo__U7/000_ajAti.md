A poetic echo of the "neti neti" principle. Addressing Lord Murugan, he admits that though he has heard of many philosophical truths, none can define the Divine as "not this, not that":

**pala poruḷ kēṭṭunai adu idu enādu** I’ve heard about many truths, but when it comes to you, I cannot say “it is this” or “it is that.” **paṭṭenṛu oru poruḷ kēṭṭiḍuvēn - anda** So I ask you: please grant me just one essential Truth,

That final Truth beyond name and form.
