Hari Om,

  

In Upadesha SAhasrI 17.26 and 17.27, AchArya clearly states sushupti to be another name, a synonym, of ajnAna. He says:

  

***सुषुप्त्याख्यं तमोऽज्ञानं*** ***बीजं*** स्वप्नप्रबोधयोः।

स्वात्मबोधप्रदग्धं स्याद्बीजं दग्धं यथाऽभवम्।।

  

He states ajnAna, darkness and sushupti as identical. Further, this ajnAna is posited as the bIja of waking and dream.

  

In the very next verse, AchArya substitutes the usage of bIja to MAyA to preclude any misinterpretation that MAyA is different from ajnAna. He says:

  

तदेवैकं त्रिधा ज्ञेयं ***मायाबीजं*** पुनः क्रमात्।

मायाव्यात्माऽविकारोऽपि बहुधैको जलार्कवत्।।

  

The confusion of equating sushupti with turIya arises because SSSS ji confused the drishTAnta as dArshTAntika. AchArya clearly says in Brihadaaranyak Upanishad that sushupti is drishTAnta for mOksha. This basic error by SSSS ji led to fundamentally erroneous theory of his which is contradictory to bhAshya at every step.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
