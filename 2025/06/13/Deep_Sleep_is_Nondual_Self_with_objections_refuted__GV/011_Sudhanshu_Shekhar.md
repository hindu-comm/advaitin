Namaste Michael ji.

  

BhAshya says - तामबीजावस्थां तस्यैव प्राज्ञशब्दवाच्यस्य तुरीयत्वेन देहादिसम्बन्धजाग्रदादिरहितां पारमार्थिकीं पृथग्वक्ष्यति ।

  

Here, तामबीजावस्थां does not mean sushupti. It means turIya. Prashant Neti ji is understanding incorrectly. It is ताम् + अबीजावस्थाम्.

  

That seed-less state, which is devoid of sambandha-of-body and devoid of waking/dream/sleep, and which is paramArtha, will be spoken separately. That seed-less state is of that very entity which is spoken by the word prAjna but not as prAjna but as turIya.

  

Further, to say that sushupti is stated as bIja from the frame of reference of ignorance but as turIya from the frame of reference of truth -- is misleading.

  

It is true that sushupti is stated as bIja from the frame of reference of ignorance. However, from the frame of reference of truth, there is no sushupti at all. To selectively aver sushupti as turIya from the frame of reference of truth is erroneous.

  

Waking/dream/sushupti are all from the frame of reference of ignorance. From the frame of reference of truth, there is only truth. No selective treatment of sushupti can be made from the frame of reference of truth. This is obvious.

  

Thus, Prashant Neti Ji's point is erroneous.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
