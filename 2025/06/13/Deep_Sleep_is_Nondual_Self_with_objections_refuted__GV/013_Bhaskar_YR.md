Ø Hare Krishna



I am sympathetic to the view that there is no avidya in deep sleep.



- And it is not just a whimsical thinking of some tiny jeeva but it is
  the observation by both shruti and bhAshyakAra. Hence sushupti
  avasthA is used as drushtAnta to explain the state of tureeya. Now
  the question is what is the difference and similarities between
  tureeya and prAjna?? What is the need of introduction of tureeya when
  prAjna himself is the parabrahman in sushupti ?? These queries need
  to be addressed jeeva who is having the upAdhi saMbandha. It is that
  jeeva with upAdhi who will see the duality in waking and dream. It is
  that jeeva only who embraces the paraM in sushupti by getting detached
  himself from upAdhi. So seeing no duality is SAME in both prAjnA and
  tureeya says kArikAkAra and at the same breath highlights difference
  between prAjnA and tureeya. Here he clarifies the Non-cognition of
  duality is common to both prAjnA and tureeya. Nidra (beeja nidra) in
  the form of cause is associated with prAjnA but it is not found in
  tureeyAtman at all (kArikA 1.13).and the knowing that he is non-dual
  (advaya) in his nature and he is sarvadruk is tureeya and whereas even
  though he is brahman ‘not-knowing’ the same is prAjnA. Hence avidyA
  in agrahaNa rUpa (jnAna abhAva) accepted in prAjnA as the jeeva enters
  the sushupti state without getting this knowledge of ekatvaM. So, for
  all practical purposes it has been said though sushupti is Atman’s
  abode we are not going to get our svarUpa jnAna just by going to
  sleep. If that is the case, we can simply throw the sAdhana part out
  of window and achieve the ultimate by just going to sleep 😊 So as
  long as we consider the Vishwa ( the waker) is having the jnAna abhAva
  and resultant adhyAsa, we have to accept that the jeeva associated
  with Vishwa who enters the sushupti as prAjnA is having the jnAna
  abhAva type of avidyA though he is free from ‘seeing the duality’ and
  ekatvam with brahman. Here again prAjnA is having the ignorance that
  is absence of right knowledge. And it has to be eradicated through
  the rise of right knowledge it has to take place only in the extravert
  or Vishwa in his intellect. Hence as long as this Vishwa has not
  acquired the right knowledge the shAstra accepts the jnAna abhAva
  (agrahaNa) type of avidyA in prAjnA. But when the sushupti viewed
  from its own view point bhAshyakAra very clearly clarifies : anyatvena
  kAmya mAnAH yathA jAgrat svapnayOH tasya Atmaiva,
  anyatvapratyupasthApakahetOH avidyAyAH abhAvAt (bruhad bhAshya). So
  we have every reason to declare that sushupti is avidyA rahita state
  when it is viewed from its own view point with the help of shAstra and
  bhAshya without giving the special and biased heed to waking state.



But then, what causes the sleeping person to wake up the same as before?



- With the above backdrop now we can understand how and why bhAskara who
  goes to sushupti comes back as bhAskara (same individual) only 😊 The
  problem can be resolved when we first define clearly the jeeva in
  relation to an upAdhi, then only the question how come same jeeva
  wakes up from sleep, would be meaningful. Who is jeeva here? Is not
  shAstra saying brahman itself is figuratively called as jeeva in
  relation to upAdhis?? So bhAshyakAra clarifies as long as the
  relation with this upAdhi continues, we refer to him as one particular
  jeeva, like with upAdhi specially meant for me I am bhAskara etc. . In
  relation to another upAdhi that you have you are called with some
  other name and will be treated as a different jeeva. With this jeeva
  upAdhi drushti Ishwara would take the responsibility to link the
  respective avidyA kAma karma to individual jeeva after waking even
  though he is ONE in sushupti. And it may be noted that avasthA-s is
  for jeeva and jeeva svarUpa is avasthAteeta, nirvishesha brahman.
