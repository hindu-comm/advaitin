Translation is not my expertise but Chatgpt & Nikhilananda suggests anotherinterpretation equating prajna and turiya. .  

//**"तामबीजावस्थां तस्यैव प्राज्ञशब्दवाच्यस्य तुरीयत्वेन देहादिसम्बन्धजाग्रदादिरहितां पारमार्थिकीं पृथग्वक्ष्यति ।"**

### **Translation:**

*"He (the Bhāṣyakāra / commentator) will separately (पृथक्) explain that **tam abīja-avasthām** (that seedless state), which belongs to the very one denoted by the word 'Prājña' (i.e., the deep sleeper), as **Turīya**, which is the absolute (pāramārthikī), free from connection with body etc., and devoid of waking and other states."//  
  
//*Further, to say that sushupti is stated as bIja from the frame of reference of ignorance but as turIya from the frame of reference of truth -- is misleading.

It is true that sushupti is stated as bIja from the frame of reference of ignorance. However, from the frame of reference of truth, there is no sushupti at all. To selectively aver sushupti as turIya from the frame of reference of truth is erroneous.//

Agreed but we are only trying to convey sastra's view for which there is no avidya in deep sleep.
