Michael ji.

  

Let us not confuse. It is a very simple thing.

  

You hold that avidyA is present in deep sleep from the frame of reference of avidyA. avidyA is absent in sushupti from the frame of reference of paramArtha.

  

It is a correct statement. There is no error.

  

However, you must add. Should you not? --

  

avidyA is present in waking and dream also from the frame of reference of avidyA. From the frame of reference of paramArtha, avidyA is absent even in waking and dream also.

  

So, basically -- avidyA is stated to be appearing in waking/dream/sushupti from the frame of reference of avidyA. From the frame of reference of truth, there is no avidyA. Neither in waking, nor in dream, nor in sushupti.
