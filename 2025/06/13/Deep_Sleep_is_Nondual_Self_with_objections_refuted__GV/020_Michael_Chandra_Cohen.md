Vyvaharikadrsti - is avidya - inherent to all embodied beings and varied as per empirical interpretation, i.e., scientific materialism, countless philosophic, spiritual and mundane speculations from time immemorial - all based on atma/anatma adhyasa. Paramartika is Absolute and free of all views, as you point out. But, Sastrika drsti is singular as corrective of adhyasa culminating in Paramartika. Perhaps you would prefer sastikadrsti as an enlightening drsti of vyavaharika drsti -- that's Ok too but the distinction between views is obvious
