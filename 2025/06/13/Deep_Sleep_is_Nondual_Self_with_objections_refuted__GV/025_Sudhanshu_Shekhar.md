Namaste Michael ji.

  

//Please, I know you recognize the vision of sastra. Why deny its distinct status?//

  

I am not denying distinctive status of shAstra. I am saying that the teaching of shAstra can be either from the frame of reference of avidyA OR from the frame of reference of non-avidyA. There can be no third category.

  

//What is gained by insisting it is within avidya?//

  

That is a fact. And wherever it is from the frame of reference of non-avidyA, shAstra can at best say - not this, not this.

  

//The miracle of sruti is that it directs us out of the confusion - that renders it a singular drsti. Categorizing is language only//

  

My point is only this -- you cannot have a shAstrika-drishTi in addition to avidyA-drishTi and non-avidyA-drishTi.

  

And from avidyA-drishTi, you have admitted sushupti to have avidyA AND from non-avidyA-drishTi, there is no sushupti at all.

  

So, in no circumstance can there arise a occasion to say "sushupti has no avidyA".

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
