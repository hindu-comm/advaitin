Ø Since you are familiar with Sri SSS’s works you know how he accepts agrahaNa in sushupti. IOW, jnAna abhAva is not an objectively existing one like upAdhi to say jnAna abhAva is an upAdhi. It is just absence of right knowledge. Sri SSS in one of his Kannada works says : sushuptiyalli yaava upAdhiyu ellavembudu ellara anubhavadalliyu eruttade. Addarinda Aga adviteeya brahma svarUpavanne jeevanu padediruttaanendu heluvudakke yaava addiyu eruvudilla. Now with regard to the question whether this avidyA (jnAna abhAva) is the cause for the anyathA grahana (qualified knowledge)?? Sri SSS quotes taittereeya bhAshya 2-8. Hope you are aware of that bhAshya portion. So in upAdhi upashanta sthiti agrahaNa has been accepted to exist in sushupti. This existence is not in the sense of mUlAvidyAvAda who declaring that the agrahaNa is here in sushupti is mUlAvidyA which is an existing one in bhAva rUpa which is upAdAna kAraNa, which is brahmAshrita etc. He (Sri SSS) further clarifies about the word ‘beeja’ and the common meaning of kAraNa and it should be taken in the farfetched / distorted meaning like upAdAna kAraNa. upAdhi means denendriyAntaHkaraNa which will be merged in brahman in sushupti or will be in dormant state sushupti. He further clarifies from the paramArthika drushti for the jagat and nAma rUpa brahman is the upAdhi hence tureeya is called sarvadruk / sarvaM so it is sensible to say in sushupti it will be merged in its svarUpa. Where as if we give any objective existence to avidyA also (unlike jnAna abhAva) then for the ekatvaM we have to merge this avidyA with brahman, by that it (avidyA) will be the svarUpa of brahman and what purushArtha you are going to get by realizing this type of brahman who is having this avidyA as its svarUpa 😊 ??
