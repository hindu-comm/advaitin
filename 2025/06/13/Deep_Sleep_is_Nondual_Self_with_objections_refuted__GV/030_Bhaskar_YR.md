No not at all, it is you (mulAvidyAvAdins) who say agrahaNa which is accepted as jnAna abhAva is just another offspring of ‘main mother’ mUlAvidyA which is upAdAna for the subsequent qualified (vishesha / dvaita ) knowledge, so according to you even jnAna abhAva is also a product of bhAvarUpa avidyA. You (MAV) declaring that this agrahaNa in sushupti signifies here in the context of mUlAvidyA. For us, agrahaNa is simply jnAna abhAva NOT definitely objectively existing mUlAvidyA like chair, table in a room to say in sushupti also there is mithyA vastu darshana 😊
