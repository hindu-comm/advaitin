I was just interested in correct depiction of mulAvidyA. I proceed to do that as under.



MUlAvidyA is different from agrahaNa, mithyA-jnAna and the samskAra thereof. That is to say - avidyA is different from agrahaNa, samshaya and viparIta-grahaNa. It is like an AvaraNa, which results in agrahaNa and anyathA-grahaNa.



Ø Yes, it is the well known definition of mUlAvidyA existing since ages in the books of vyAkhyAna. But as per shankara there are only three types which is natural for human mind. And when the knowledge dawns all these three types of avidyA go…ignorance whether it is jnAnAbhAva or vipareeta pratyaya or saMshaya tat jnAnenaiva nirvartyate…So for us, the shankara followers, the alien concept like mulAvidyA is not required and there in bhAshya as well.



AchArya says in GItA bhAshya - तामसे च आवरणात्मके तिमिरादिदोषे सति अग्रहणादेः अविद्यात्रयस्य उपलब्धेः। (When there is AvaraNa, there is the perception of the three avidyAs namely agrahaNa, viparIta-grahaNa and samshaya). Here, please note, AvaraNa is different from avidyA-traya. That is what is mUlAvidyA which is AvaraNa.

It is just tAmasa pratyaya pertaining to the intellect or antaHkaraNa alone. It only reveals what is there already. The correct knowledge of rajju neither destroys existing snake nor creates rope afresh.
