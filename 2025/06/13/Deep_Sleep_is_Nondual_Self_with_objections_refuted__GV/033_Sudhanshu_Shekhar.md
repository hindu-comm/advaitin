BhAshyakAra clearly says:

  

When there is AvaraNa, there is upalabdhi of avidyA-traya.

  

It is logical, patent, manifest and clear that AvaraNa is different from avidyA-traya.

  

This AvaraNa is what is referred by mUlAvidyA.
