Thank you. Same old objections. But because I respect Sudhanshu ji please let me answer to him again:

//// From standpoint of truth, there is no sushupti. There is only truth. There is only turiya. ////

I agree. See, when I said “from standpoint of truth sushupti itself is said to be the truth”, I said so because the objection from other side is that, ‘sushupti is never turiya’ - What I mean is ‘snake is not snake but rope alone’. Similarly, sushupti is not sushupti (as vishwa fancies) but turiya alone. If someone wants to use that and also say waking is also not waking but Turiya alone. I am more happy to accept. All I am saying is, that enquiry pivots over correction about sushupti.

//// prAjna-tva of prAjna and taijasa-tva of taijasa are Avidyā-krita ////

Let whoever considers themselves to be vishwa and prAjna have that Avidyā (Avidyā kRtam) also! Why care? Sastra is teaching prAjna is after all an idea of vishwa. And Sastra is also teaching, you are NOT vishwa. Let the focus be there. Therefore, let us not (ab)use sastra by point to Avidyā as a positive fact of life. But please, let us use sastra only to sublating Avidyā by paying attention to sarvatrika anubhava.

What PSV followers are doing is exactly stopping happening of that sublation. To them, the most favorite statement from sastra is ‘you have Avidyā’. But to me most purposeful statement is, ‘you are not what you think you are!’. Therefore, based on this difference it is clear where Post-Sankara Vedānta leads to - Avidyā-sthapana and nothing else.

Whenever Post-Sankara Vedānta followers again and again deny atma’s asangatvam in Sushupti, there is no need to say that in Post-Sankara Vedānta, ātma’s asangatvam is a lip service and mere dogma to be believed in - not a nature of vastu.

As I have responded elsewhere, purpose of avasthatraya vichara is not reaffirm my status quo that, ‘there are avasthas’. But the true purpose is to teach me that ātma is ever asanga even when it appears to be having avasthas as it were, because in ātma, there is really no sanga with anything (including sanga with an avastha).

Please, to me that alone is Vedānta which sublates my status quo i.e. sublates my idea that there are avasthas. And that is not Vedānta which gives me a satisfying justification to status quo by explaining the role of Avidyā as the caretaker of my status quo (saying vishwa-tva is Avidyā-krita). I sat down to listen to Vedānta to understand how “I am Brahman”, not sat down to know that “I am a Jiva” - I already know that I am a Jiva and that is why I came to Vedānta with a jignasa. Let me transcend Jivatvam, please leave me from that Vedanta which reinforces jivatvam. I take leave here. Praṇām.
