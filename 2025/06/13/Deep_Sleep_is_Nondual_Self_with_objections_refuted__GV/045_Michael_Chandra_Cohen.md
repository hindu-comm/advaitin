Namaste Jaishankarji,

A simple and I hope, quick response.

  

//First is abhAva as a bija / kArana.//

  
--There is no cause/effect in the Absolute nor time/space. Cause/effect is imagined only. Even in DSV, C/E is imagined. Not recognizing the ever-evident Self is abhava avidya. Abhava avidya is not something but a logical account for what appears. It is not kala krama but the presupposition to the adhyasa that must be recognized by the question itself - pratipatti krama. Not acknowledging this distinction clouds your interpretationof avidya and maya as cause and effect - a notion of Post Sankara Advaita only.

  

//But they propose a jnAna-abhAva which is the bijA even in sushupti.//

When sushupti is spoken of from the perspective of the waking state, sushupti supporting an avyakta anirvacaniya bija, may be considered however, so many other texts speak of sushupti as "the Self alone liberated from jivatvam" or "jiva joins its own svarupa". I can paste a dozen or more collected quotes that I'm sure you've seen and somehow discounted. Bhasyakara's intendedteaching is clear, imho.

  

//Whenever we point to clear bhAshya statements or GaudapAda kArikA they giveumpteen quotations to show sushupti and turiya are the same. Obviously there will be similaritiesbetween a drshtAnta and dArshtAntika and we have no dispute with all these quotations. But based on these similaritiesto claim that the drshtAnta and dArshtAntika are the same is really not common sense.//

  
--Umpteen quotations should be convincing but apparently we need to determine Bhasyakara intention in these quotations.

I ask you, what is the argument they are routinely/always drshtAnta and what argument to take them literally?  
  
//Thirdly they accept mAyA as anirvachineeya but it is an effect of jnAna-abhAva. //

Again, the notion cause and effect when all is adhyasa only.
Anirvacaniya is interpreted differently by Post Sankara Advaita and by Prasthanatraya Bhasya. Kindly consult SSSS, Hacker, Alston, Potter and that short paper I've referred more than once that summarizes and confirms several of the difficulties SSSS discovered. IMHO, these emit studies are critical to an impartial interpretation of some very technical issues. The link again,

[https://drive.google.com/file/d/16cebqYXnguJqyYVTdTJNB6UJurHSszq7/view?usp=sharing](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16cebqYXnguJqyYVTdTJNB6UJurHSszq7/view?usp=sharing)

  

//Further they have an allergy for any mithyA-vastu which is considered as bhAvarupa. Even after being explained that bhAvarupa is to be understood like smritirupa i.e. 'as though existent', they say how can some mithyA vastu which is solid/concrete be negated by jnAna. //

This is a fair point but when you say mithyA-vastu is bhAvarupa - you give it a positive identity and that morphs into 'relative reality' or as something that can't be known as real or unreal but a bhavarupa. Though Padmapada defines a material bhavarupa avidya, I recognize later authors somehow shifted that into a quasi-ontological entity. But, only an error can or need be falsified - the rope exists, the snake is an error only - a mistaken conception that hallucinates with fear etc.
But, a bhavarupa appearance of snake must remain appearing even when it is determined to be unreal. The outcome of that thinking is Videhamukti, gauna mukti - a kind of upasana.

  

//Fourth is what is this jnAna-abhAva, which is the cause of samsAra? It cannot be svarUpa-jnAna-abhAva as it is nitya and svayamprakAsha. So the jnAna-abhAva is vritti-jnAna-abhAva which will be there even after the so-called jnAnam takes place as vritti is only momentary. So jnAna-abhAva will continue and so moksha is not possible (anirmoksha-prasanga). /

  

--Sir please, this is not the argument of SSSS. I've already disclaimed your view on causation. Any vritti is adhyasa only - quite clear in SSSS and Bhasya. Please do educate yourself better on SSSS and not rely on second hand sources.

  
Respectfully, Michael Chandra
