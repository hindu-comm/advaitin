The **Vivaraṇa school** of Advaita Vedānta (founded on **Padmapāda’s** *Pañcapādikā* and elaborated in the *Vivaraṇa* sub-commentaries) diverges significantly from **Swami Satchidānandendra Saraswati’s** (SSSS) interpretation of Śaṅkara, particularly in its understanding of **cause and effect (kārya-kāraṇa-bhāva)**. Here is a **detailed comparative analysis** between the **Vivaraṇa view** and the **SSSS/Śuddha-Śaṅkara view**, with **precise doctrinal contrasts** and **citations** from authoritative texts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🟡 I. Core Differences in the Understanding of Causality

| Aspect | **Vivaraṇa School** | **SSSS (Śuddha-Śaṅkara View)** |
|----|----|----|
| **Brahman’s Role** | **Brahman associated with Māyā** is the **material and efficient cause** (*upādāna* + *nimitta*) of the world. | Brahman is **not truly a cause**; causality is **adhyāsa**, imposed through **avidyā** alone. |
| **Mūlāvidyā (root ignorance)** | **Affirmed as a positive beginningless entity** that is **indescribable** (*anirvacanīya*), **located in Brahman**, and **causes** the projection of the world. | **Rejected entirely** — SSSS insists *mūlāvidyā* is a **non-Śaṅkaran interpolation** postdating Śaṅkara. |
| **Cause–Effect Status** | **Empirical (vyāvahārika)** causality is accepted as **necessary for explaining** the world’s manifestation. | Cause-effect relation is **notional and only due to ignorance**, not even empirically valid ultimately. |
| **Explanation of Creation** | The world is a **projection (vivarta)** by **avidyā (mūlāvidyā)** associated with Brahman. | The world is **superimposition** due to **mutual adhyāsa**; **no real or even empirically valid creation exists**. |
| **Māyā as Upādhi** | Māyā is a **positive, beginningless, indefinable power** that **limits Brahman** and **produces** names/forms. | Māyā is **not a power**; it is **a conceptual error (bhrānti)** born of superimposition, not a real upādhi. |

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🟢 II. Authoritative Citations

### 📘 **Vivaraṇa School Citations**

1.  **Padmapāda, Pañcapādikā (on Brahmasūtra 1.1.1)**:

    > *māyā hi nāma anādi-brahmāśrita-anirvacanīya-śaktiḥ… saguṇasya
    > brahmaṇaḥ kārya-janakatve nimitta-upādāna-rūpeṇa vyavasthitā.*

    **Translation**: Māyā is a beginningless, indescribable power,
    dependent on Brahman, which functions as both the efficient and
    material cause of the world.

2.  **Prakāśātman’s Vivaraṇa**:

    > *mūlāvidyāyāṁ satyāṁ sarva-vyavahāraḥ sambhavati.*

    **Translation**: All empirical activity becomes possible only if
    mūlāvidyā exists.

3.  **Brahmānandī, in Kalpataru (on BS 2.1.14)**:

    > *mūlāvidyā saguṇa-brahmaṇi vartate... tayā jagat pariṇatam.*

    **Translation**: Mūlāvidyā exists in saguṇa Brahman and from it the
    world has evolved.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 📕 **SSSS Rebuttals**

1.  **Mūlāvidyānirāsa**, p. 13:

    > “na kvāpi Śaṅkarabhāṣye mūlāvidyā-śabdaḥ prayuktaḥ... idam sarvaṁ
    > prakalpitaṁ vivartam, na tu vastu-pariṇāmaḥ.”

    **Translation**: Nowhere in Śaṅkara’s bhāṣya is the term *mūlāvidyā*
    used. All this is imagined appearance (vivarta), not real
    transformation.

2.  **Vedānta Prakriyā Pratyabhijñā (VPP)**, p. 93:

    > “kāraṇa-kārya-bhāvaḥ adhyāsitaḥ — na tu satyaḥ; yāvat avidyā,
    > tāvat vyavahāraḥ.”

    **Translation**: The relation of cause and effect is superimposed,
    not real. As long as ignorance persists, transactional life
    continues.

3.  **Adhyāsa Bhāṣya Commentary (by SSSS)**:

    > “adhyāsa eva sarva-vyavahāra-bījam... prapañcasya mūla-kāraṇaṁ iti
    > yad ucyate, tad avidyā-dṛṣṭyā matam.”

    **Translation**: Superimposition alone is the seed of all worldly
    activity. When it is said that Brahman is the cause of the world, it
    is so only from the standpoint of ignorance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🔴 III. Philosophical Consequences

| Consequence | **Vivaraṇa** | **SSSS** |
|----|----|----|
| Creation of World | Requires **mūlāvidyā** as causal basis | Creation is **unreal**, a mistaken **appearance (vivarta)** |
| Brahman’s Involvement | Brahman with **māyā-śakti** creates world | Brahman **remains untouched** by the world |
| Status of Empirical World | **Mithyā**, but has **structured causal dependence** | **Mithyā**, but with **no real dependence**, only adhyāsa |
| Path to Liberation | Removal of **mūlāvidyā** through **aparokṣa-jñāna** | Removal of **adhyāsa** through clear discrimination (viveka) |

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ✅ Conclusion

| Summary | **Vivaraṇa** | **SSSS** |
|----|----|----|
| Affirms **mūlāvidyā** | Yes – as the cause of appearance of jagat and jīva | No – considers it a post-Śaṅkara error |
| Brahman as cause | Saguṇa Brahman + Māyā = cause of the universe | Brahman is never the cause; cause-effect is projected through **adhyāsa** |
| Causal explanation of the world | Essential, based on *anirvacanīya-khyāti* and mūlāvidyā | Entirely **mithyā**, not even a valid empirical relationship |
| Śaṅkara’s authentic view (SSSS) | Vivaraṇa view **distorts** Śaṅkara by inserting causal models absent in bhāṣya | Restores Śaṅkara’s **pure epistemological non-dualism** based on **adhyāsa** and *vivarta-vāda* |
