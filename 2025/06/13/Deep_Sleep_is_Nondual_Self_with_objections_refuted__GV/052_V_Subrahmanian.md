1.  **Mūlāvidyānirāsa**, p. 13:

    > “na kvāpi Śaṅkarabhāṣye mūlāvidyā-śabdaḥ prayuktaḥ... idam sarvaṁ
    > prakalpitaṁ vivartam, na tu vastu-pariṇāmaḥ.”

1.  **Translation**: Nowhere in Śaṅkara’s bhāṣya is the
    term*mūlāvidyā*used. All this is imagined appearance (vivarta),
    not real transformation. //

A counter to that, would be, to reproduce from the rival schools of Advaita: Nowhere in the Principal Upanishads, the Bh.Gita and the Brahma sutras are the terms 'adhyāsa, adhyāropa-apavāda, anirvachaniya, etc.' are used.
