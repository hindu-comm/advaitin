1. **Mūlāvidyānirāsa**, p. 13:

> “na kvāpi Śaṅkarabhāṣye mūlāvidyā-śabdaḥ prayuktaḥ... idam sarvaṁ

**Translation**: Nowhere in Śaṅkara’s bhāṣya is the term*mūlāvidyā*used. All this is imagined appearance (vivarta), not real transformation. //
