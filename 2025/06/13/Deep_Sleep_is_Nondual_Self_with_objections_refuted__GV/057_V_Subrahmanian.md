It is not there in any Upanishad. Gaudapadacharya has 'cited' this in a work attributed to him: uttara gita bhashya. Shankara cites the maxim as the words of 'sampradaya vits'. So with the term mithya in the sense Shankara/Gaudapada used it: not in any PT texts. I am not doubting but a justified counter by other schools.
