And how this avidyA lesha, saMskAra in ‘paramArtha jnAni’ can fit in DSV, which is as per some superior to SDV. Here bhAshyakAra (as per you) not talking about some ordinary avidyAvanta-s but clarifying that this traces of avidyA can be found in ***paramArtha jnAni***!!?? what does it mean?? Is it not proving that this paramArtha jnAni is some one different from other jeeva-s who are full fledged avidyAvanta-s?? Is it not implying that even after paramArtha jnAna there is an individuality to some one and who is still identifying himself with his own BMI (or traces of avidyA) and he is also having the darshana of avidyA lesha with himself and complete avidyA in some other jeeva-s ?? Please note you have already denied these things are not just from bystander’s perspective but it is indeed the status of paramArtha jnAni. So, even after paramArtha jnAna the socalled SDV continues, jnAni will be having prArabdha karma phala, he is having avidyA lesha, he will be getting mOksha after eradication of this remnants of avidyA…here avidyAlesha something related to continuation of BMI of jnAni as the result of his prArabdha karma phala which already started giving its results, that means to get that avidyA lesha rahita jnAna he has to shed his mortal coil that means you are still giving undue importance to deha, which is not at all there as per ajAta vAda. Don’t you think by mentioning saMskAra you are indirectly accepting, janma, karma, karma phala etc. to even this paramArtha jnAni who is already realized that the jagat, Ishwara, other jeeva-s, IshwarAnu graha, Ishwara kAruNya etc. etc. just fancy stories in shAstra and bhAshya and shAstra and bhAshya too are just his own imagination in bhrAnti lOka ?? I am not exaggerating anything here, according to some logicians who want to deduce shruti siddhAnta with mere logic, it is just some fancy story told by shAstra and bhAshyakAra not at all real in some other module i.e. DSV. To say the least treating shAstra and bhAshyavachana like this is just a plain blasphemy. Poor Ishwara does not find even a little space in this scheme of things. A nAstika vAda.
