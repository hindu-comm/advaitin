'Avidyalesha' is not mūlāvidya. The latter is dispelled by aparoksha jnana. By this much the Jnani does not die. The body mind complex will live out its destined tenure. Avidya lesha is that power that sustains the post-Jnana life. That's what Shankara calls samskara. Shankara says it is on the basis of this post-Jnana life that it is called **sthita prajna lakshana** in the shruti/smritis. The BG shloka 'nava dvArE purE dEhi naiva kurvan na kArayan' is the statement of jivan mukti. So, your charge // means you are still giving undue importance to deha// is directed at Shankara and the Shaastra. 

  

In DSV there is no avidya lesha. Bondage and moksha are within the dream. Jivanmukti is also within the dream. There is none bound and none released.
