- OK, noted. avidyAlesha is something other than mUlAvidyA. Am I
  right??

- Here you are saying that the latter (mUlAvidyA) will be effaced /
  eradicated by aparOksha jnAna. Am I right??



By this much the Jnani does not die. The body mind complex will live out its destined tenure.



- That means after the eradication or complete annihilation of mUlAvidyA
  jnAni does not die and he would continue with his BMI as destined by
  his prArabdha. Am I right??

- In saMskAra rUpa you are trying to say this right?? Obviously this
  saMskAra rUpa avidyA should have locus (ashraya) in paramArtha jnAni

  avidyA lesha ?? Is it not his antaHkaraNa?? If yes, what is that

  elaborate.



That's what Shankara calls samskara. Shankara says it is on the basis of this post-Jnana life that it is called **sthita prajna lakshana** in the shruti/smritis. The BG shloka 'nava dvArE purE dEhi naiva kurvan na kArayan' is the statement of jivan mukti. So, your charge // means you are still giving undue importance to deha// is directed at Shankara and the Shaastra.
