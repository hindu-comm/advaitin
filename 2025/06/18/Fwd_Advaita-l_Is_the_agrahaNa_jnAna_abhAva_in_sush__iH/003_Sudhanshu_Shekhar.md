Thanks for the reference. Yes. The agrahNa is of two types. One is tattva-agrahaNa and the other is, bheda-agrahaNa i.e. dvaita-agrahaNa i.e. dvitIya-agrahaNa. First one is common to all states of jAgrat, swapna and sushupti. The latter one, is however, only in sushupti.

  

One needs to read bhAshya carefully. Otherwise, they will confuse dvaita-agrahaNa for tattva-agrahaNa and infer incorrect deductions.
