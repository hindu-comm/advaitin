jagat as vishesha-rUpa of nirvishesha-Brahman requires some activity. That is impermissible in nishkriya Brahman.  
  
WIthout some activity, clay cannot appear as a pot, which is a vishesha-rUpa.  
  
So, inactive singular Brahman cannot have vishesha-rUpa.
