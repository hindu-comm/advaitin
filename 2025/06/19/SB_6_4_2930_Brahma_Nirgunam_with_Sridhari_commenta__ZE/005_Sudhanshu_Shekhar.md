Namaste Bhaskar ji.

  

> I was trying to write something about your new inventions
> tattvAgrahaNa and dvaitAgrahaNa but this another gem comes from your
> desk 😊

  

It is not my invention Bhaskar ji. It is well-established in siddhAnta. The agrahaNa in sushupti is bheda-agrahaNa or dvitIya-agrahaNa. It is not tattva-agrahaNa. Anyway, more on this after your reply.

  

> Please note that which (shruti) says brahman is nishkriya, nirvayava,
> nirvishesha etc. also says it is the efficient and material cause of
> this universe.

  

Yes prabhuji. But that is explained by the superimposition of features of ajnAna. Is it not?



> You are not supposed to chose one part of shruti and ignore another
> part of shruti by applying ‘ardha kukkuti’ nyAya 😊

  

I am not ignoring any part of Shruti. I am saying that it is plain impossible for a singular inactive entity to have vishesha-rUpa. It is not great logic. A class 5 student will also say this. In the name of Shruti, one cannot propagate plain illogical stuff, which can be rejected by even a class 5 student.

  

The visheah-rUpa is explained through superimposition of features of ajnAna. The kAraNa of the world is ajnAna. The feature kAraNa-tva belongs to ajnAna. Due to superimposition, this feature of ajnAna is imputed to singularinactive Brahman, and shAstra says - Brahman is kAraNa.

  



>  Therefore you have to accept shruti siddhAnta as such, ofcourse this
> call is applicable to those who are vedAntins not mere
> logicians.Logicians are very happy to accept one statement of the
> shruti and reject another if it is not matching with their logical
> conclusions 😊

  

Logical vedAntIs will never do this.



> The shruti tells that sarvajna sarvashakta brahman is the
> ekamevAdviteeya kAraNam for this jagat. karaNam, kAraNam, karta,
> vikarta gahano guhaH, HE is one and the same in its svarUpa (annaM,
> annada, shlokakarta).

  

SwarUpa to vishesha-rUpa requires ajnAna. Without ajnAna, vishesha-rUpa is impossible.



> Therefore being a vedAntin stick to vedAnta and refrain yourself from
> seeing everything from the prism of mere logic and stop putting
> limitations on brahman on the basis of ordinary creatures activities
> and its limitation. In the vedAnta siddhAnta this is not at all
> acceptable.

  

It is incorrect to hold that VedAnta contradicts logic. They are not mutually exclusive. That is why BhAshyakAra says in MANDUkya -अद्वैतं किमागममात्रेण प्रतिपत्तव्यम् , आहोस्वित्तर्केणापीत्यत आह — **शक्यते तर्केणापि ज्ञातुम् ; तत्कथमित्यद्वैतप्रकरणमारभ्यते** । This is the emphatic statement of BhagvAn BhAshyakAra.
