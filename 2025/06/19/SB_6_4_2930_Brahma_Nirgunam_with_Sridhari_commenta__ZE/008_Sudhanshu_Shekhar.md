Hare Krishna Bhaskar prabhuji.

> Ø Features of saguNatvaM / sarvajnatvaM / sarva shaktitvaM of
> brahman is not ajnAna, it is for the sAdhakAnugrahArthaM…

But sAdhakatva is also avidyA-krita only. Brahman in nirgUNa. Its saguNatva can only be avidyA-krita.

  

GuNa belongs to the realm of avidyA. They are superimposed on Brahman leading to saguNatva of Brahman. That is what is called adhyArOpa.

  

So, sAdhaka-anugrahatva does not imply that saguNatva and sarva-shaktimattva is not avidyA-krita.

  

Sarvajnatva needs a little different analysis.

> If you want to reject Acharya upadesha and IshwarAnugraha you are not
> vedAntin but a shushka tArkika atleast IMO.

I don't deny AchArya's upadesha. The Ishwara-tva of Ishwara is not swarUpa. The swarUpa is neti, neti. The Ishwara-tva is avidyA-krita. It is not shushka tarka. This is what Shruti teaches.

> Even to assert that the vedAntic upAsya brahman is ajnAni you need
> that same IshwarAnugraha, he is the buddhidAta, karma phala dAta…we
> are just exhibiting our pUrva janma saMskAra. 

The upAsya Brahman is upahita-chaitanya and not vishishTa-chaitanya. Without adhyArOpa of avidyA, shuddha nishkriya chaitanya cannot be karma-fala-dAtA etc. This is basic.

  

> 
>
> You are not supposed to say devadutta with folded limbs is someone
> different from same devadutta who stretched his limbs. Ishwara hetuka
> srushti to prove that he is beyond srushti is vedAnta maryAda.

Bhaskar prabhu ji. Please tell this to a 5th class student and convince that singular inactive Brahman is folding and unfolding his limbs.

  

It is the ajnAna-upahita-chaitanya who can be Ishwara and can be stated to be folding and unfolding the limbs. Shuddha nirhuNa nishkriya chaitanya cannot be stretching. This is obvious. Without ajnAna, creation cannot be stated.

> vedAnta siddhAnta, Acharya upadesha, Ishwara anugraha, IshrAstitva
> etc. is just for neophytes, let it be I am always happy to be infant
> in that.

You are just putting words in mouth. The basic idea is simple. Without adhyArOpa of ajnAna (whose apavAda is done subsequently), shuddha nishkriya chaitanya cannot appear as Ishwara.

> Atleast I have the satisfaction that I am not the follower of mere dry
> logic and approach towards vedAnta is theistic.

You are entitled to your own emotional approach.

  

> Vishesha rUpa which has been created and appeared is there to
> ultimately drive home the point that brahman is prajnAna ghana. If
> you need to know more about brahma kAraNatva of this jagat please go
> to second adhyAya of sUtra where all these mundane logical objections
> clarified / refuted by bhAshyakAra himself.

To say vishesha-rUpa is created implies activity. And that is impermissible for shuddha nishkriya chaitanya. This is so simple a fact that it cannot be stressed more.

  

> The kAraNa of the world is ajnAna.
>
> Another gem from dry logician desk 😊 No my dear tArkika prabhuji,
> brahman is the abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa, if at all there is
> srushti prakriya, we cannot create even a tinge of gross through our
> avidyA.

Sir, without activity, nothing can be created. And that activity is prohibited for nishkriya Brahman. Your srishTi-prakriyA is completely avidyA-kalpita. It does not exist.

> dhAtA yathA purvamakalpayatu. It is brahman who desired to be many,
> not your pet ajnAna which thought on its own to become many 😊

Bravo Bhaskar ji. Nishkriya Brahman is desiring!!! Do you really understand what you are saying.

  

Only ajnAna-upahita-chaitanya can be desiring. Shuddha chaitanya cannot desire. Because desiring is an activity and that is impermissible for nishkriya chaitanya.

> So what is the purpose of this creation??

Sir, creation hua hi nahi hai. To explain mithyAtva of creation, and through that the advitIyatva of Brahman, this story of creation is told by Shruti.

  

See Aitareya - अस्तु तर्हि सर्वमेवेदमनुपपन्नम् । न, अत्रात्माववोधमात्रस्य ***विवक्षितत्वात्सर्वोऽयमर्थवाद*** इत्यदोषः । मायाविवद्वा ; महामायावी देवः सर्वज्ञः सर्वशक्तिः सर्वमेतच्चकार सुखावबोधप्रतिपत्त्यर्थं ***लोकवदाख्यायिकादिप्रपञ्च*** इति युक्ततरः पक्षः ।

  

This creation Shruti is either arthavAda OR a plain story. Take whatever you want. Both are agreeable to BhAshyakAra.

Sir. You have no idea what adhyArOpa-apavAda is. Without doing the adhyAropa of ajnAna on shuddha chaitanya, your theistic approach cannot work. That you disregard. I wonder how your shuddha nishkriya chaitanya is creating stuff. You are an example of वदतो व्याघातः।

> Again nimitta and upAdAna kAraNa for this jagat is brahman and Ishwara
> hetuka srushti and acceptance of it is vedAnta maryAda.

Jargons will not help you. You have not understood the definition of Ishwara.

> The shruti tells that sarvajna sarvashakta brahman is the
> ekamevAdviteeya kAraNam for this jagat. karaNam, kAraNam, karta,
> vikarta gahano guhaH, HE is one and the same in its svarUpa (annaM,
> annada, shlokakarta).

You need activity for that one to appear as kAraNa, kartA, vikArta whatever. That is impermissible for nishkriya chaitanya. So, you need to perforce do the adhyAropa ajnAna. Otherwise, you are self-contradicting.

> ajnAneva satyaM 😊

Sir, is activity required for clay to appear as pot? If yes, then matter ends.

  

When Shruti talks about Sat as kAraNa - it is by accepting ajnAna. That has been stated in MANDUkya 1.2. Please go through that.

  

Chaitanya aspect of sat is satyam. ajnAna aspect is mithyA.
