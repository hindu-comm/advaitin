Namaste Chandramouli ji.

  

I will give a summary of my previous write-ups.

  

Tattva-agrahaNa is present in jAgrat, swapna and sushupti.

  

Bheda-agrahaNa, (dvitIya-agrahaNa, dvaya-agrahaNa) is present ONLY in sushupti.

  

The impugned TaittirIya bhAshya, which mentions agrahaNa in sushupti to be swAbhAvika and not avidyA-krita, refers to bheda-agrahaNa and NOT tattva-agrahaNa.

  

The MANDUkya bhAshya 1.5 too is harmonious to what is said above.
