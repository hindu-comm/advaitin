Namaste Jaishankar ji.

  

So, basically, in MANDUkya 1.13, AchArya is clearly and unambiguously making a distinction between dvaita-agrahaNa and tattva-agrahaNa.

  

In 1.11, he says - तत्त्वाग्रहणान्यथाग्रहणाभ्यां बीजफलभावाभ्यां तौ यथोक्तौ विश्वतैजसौ बद्धौ सङ्गृहीतौ इष्येते । प्राज्ञस्तु बीजभावेनैव बद्धः । तत्त्वाप्रतिबोधमात्रमेव हि बीजं प्राज्ञत्वे निमित्तम् ।

  

Thus, tattva-agrahaNa = bIja.

anyathA-grahaNa = fala.

  

In prAjna and turIya, dvaita-agrahaNa is common. But what is not common is tattva-agrahaNa.

  

So, prAjna has tattva-agrahaNa and dvaita-agrahaNa.

  

TurIya has dvaita-agrahaNa but NO tattva-agrahaNa.

  

Vishwa and taijasa has tattva-agrahaNa but NO dvaita-agrahaNa.

  

Vishwa and taijasa = tattva-agrahaNa AND ~ bheda-agrahaNa.

  

PrAjna = tattva-agrahaNa AND bheda-agrahaNa.

  

TurIya = ~tattva-agrahaNA AND bheda-agrahaNa.

  

In TaittirIya, when AchArya says agrahaNa to be not avidyA-krita, he refers to bheda-agrahaNa.
