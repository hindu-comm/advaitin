So, basically, in MANDUkya 1.13, AchArya is clearly and unambiguously making a distinction between dvaita-agrahaNa and tattva-agrahaNa.



Ø It is also clear from sUtra bhAshya / bruhad bhAshya / chAndOgya bhAshya that for dvaita agrahaNa in sushupti is ekatvaM and there is no lOpa to the seer even though where there is nothing that can be ‘seen’.
Here beejanidraayutaH prAjnaH. Beeja nidraa here is tattvAgrahaNa which is the cause for the anyathaagrahaNa. The kAraNa is what?? The ignorance about himself. Vishwa and taijasa bound by both ignorance and wrong knowledge (mithya jnAna). But prAjna is bound only by ignorance.
And it has been already said umpteen times that as long as there is wrong knowledge in the extrovert knower it must be accepted that prAjnA also has ignorance in the form of tattvAgrahaNa and this ignorance is the absence of right knowledge and knowledge is an antidote of this absence. So beejanidra has another beeja in the form of mUlAvidyA is not fit into this context. And this what emphasized (only two aspects of avidyA i.e. avidyA in the form of jnAnaabhAva and avidyA in the form of adhyAsa (vipareeta grahaNa) in 1.11 and clarified neither has any relation to tureeya. And please note in the very next verse tureeya explained as sarva druk (turyaM tatsarvadruksadA). No where in the bhAshya or in the kArika you can prove the existence of mUlAvidyA.
