Problem here is this Bhaskar ji. tattva-agrahaNa, which is basically tattva-grahaNa-abhAva, cannot really obstruct the self-effulgent Brahman. Something which is swaprakAsha cannot remain hidden unless there is AvaraNa. Now, in sushupti also, we accept that there is tattva-agrahaNa. So, some AvaraNa must be accepted. That is why AchArya says in GitA that only when there is AvaraNa, that there is upalabdhi (perception) of avidyA-traya. This is an extremely extremely important statement.

  

So, for tattva-agrahaNa also, you need an AvaraNa. Otherwise, how can the grahaNa of self-effulgent tattva be absent?

You have virtually ignored the AvaraNa portion specified clearly in bhAshya.

Yes turIya is sarva-drika i.e. sAkshI. But that is from the frame of reference of avidyA. Not from the frame of reference of turiya. Please see VArtika -अज्ञानमात्रोपाधित्वादविद्यामुषितात्मभिः ।। कौटस्थ्यान्निर्द्वयोऽप्यात्मा साक्षीत्यध्यस्यते जडैः ।। ३७२ ।।

  

> Ø BTW, where your earlier statement that in sushupti there is
> mithyA vastu darshana fits here!!??

  

As I discussed above, AvaraNa is a pre-requisite for tattva-agrahaNa. This AvaraNa is what is called avidyA or ajnAna. This is mithyA.

  

This ajnAna is sAkshi-bhAsya. It is not pramANa-gamya. pramANa removes avidyA. So, even in jAgrat also, avidyA is sAkshi-bhAsya.

  

Tattva-agrahaNa is present in waking, dream, sushupti. So, AvaraNa i.e. avidyA is also present in waking, dream and sushupti.

  

sAkshI is also always present.

  

So, avidyA is always seen. In jAgrat, swapna and sushupti.

  

That is why it is said, there is mithyA-vastu-darshana in sushupti, AND hence, sushupti is also swapna.

  

Shruti says that in sushupti, it does not see other as there is no other. There is no dvaita. This is true because avidyA-kArya, dvaita is absent. dvaita-agrahaNa, i.e. dvaita-grahaNa-abhAva, being adhikaraNa-swarUpa, is verily Brahman.

  

dvaita is avidyA-kArya. dvaita-abhAva is Brahman. dvaita-grahaNa-abhAva is equally Brahman.
