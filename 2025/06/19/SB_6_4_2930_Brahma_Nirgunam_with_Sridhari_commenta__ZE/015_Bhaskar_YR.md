I thought the following citation from MandUkya Bhashya on mantra 5 would settle the issue hopefully. Atleast from factual point of view. Sri Bhagavatpada admits both the understandings (bheda-agrahaNa or dvitIya-agrahaNa as well as tattva-agrahaNa ) for agrahaNa as far as sushupti is concerned. I have not given the English translation of the kannada version by Sri SSS. However I confirm that it also endorses the above view ( though there could be some differences on some other

Hare Krishna

I am afraid here some confusion!! See what sudhAnshu prabhuji is saying there are two types of avidyA in sushupti one is tattvAgrahaNa and another one of dvaita agrahaNa. And for the jnAnaabhAva (tattvAgrahaNa) there is bhAvarUpa avidyA which is the cause. For this I said for the dvaita agrahaNa the cause (hetu) is ekatvaM and jnAna abhAva is svAbhAvika (taittireeya 2.8) which is anyway not an hindrance to consider sushupti sthAnaM is Atman. Sri Devarao Kulakarni in saurabha gives an example : kaNNu kattikondu kaadinalli odaadida haage, bhutalada nidhiya mele dinavu tiliyadi odaadida haage etc. (roaming in forest blind folded, walking on the treasure daily underneath the floor. We cannot deny the fact that he used to walk on the treasure without knowing it. Jeeva enters sushupti as avidyAvanta, comes back as avidyAvanta (same jeeva wakes up from sushupti) just because we have to accept the fact that the jeeva has yet to eradicate avidyA. But when the sushupti being viewed from the shAstra drushti, without any partiality to waking state and its experience, Sri SSS quotes sUtra bhAshya and clarifies : brahman is ‘always’ unfailingly the abode of deep sleep of the jeeva. It this truth is realized the benefits of determining the truth that jeeva is verily brahman (jeevo brahmaiva na aparaH) accrues as also the additional benefit of determining the truth that in HIM ( the sushuptAtma) the vyAvahArik transactions of jAgrat and Svapna do not exist / accrues.

Now coming back to taittireeya 2.8 where bhAshyakAra clearly negates
the kAraNa for the not knowing (dvaita agrahaNa) as avidyA and clarifies it is just svAbhAvika, it is to be noted here for dvaita agrahaNa the kAraNa is ekatvaM and for the tattva agrahaNa in sushupti is just from empirical drushti where jeeva still wants the vidyA to eradicate avidyA or for the singular reason of not intuitively knowing the tattva/reality. And this not knowing / absence of right knowledge though he established himself in that very knowledge is the cause for being prAjna. Here Sri SSS quotes both kArika 1-11 and taittireeya 2.8 and clarifies both the above two sentences from the bhAshya ‘agrahaNa’ accepted to exist in sushupti. And agrahaNa here means ‘not cognizing’ and this is the common meaning adduced to it. He further clarifies in the bhAshya it is accepted that in sushupti ajnAna of the type : not cognizing / agrahaNa exists and it is also clarified that for this sarvaikatva (everything becoming one and the same ) is the cause. So in sushupti also the cause for the dvaitAgrahaNa is ekatvaM and ajnAna hetum in sushupti is being ONE and ONE ALONE.

Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
