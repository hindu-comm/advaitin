Bhaskar prabhuji.

  

> See what sudhAnshu prabhuji is saying there are two types of avidyA in
> sushupti one is tattvAgrahaNa and another one of dvaita agrahaNa.

  

Please read properly what I have said. In sushupti, there is AvaraNa and tattva-agrahaNa. AvaraNa is mUla-avidyA. Tattva-agrahaNa is one of the effect thereof.

  

dvaita-agrahaNa is Brahman. It is dvaita-grahaNa-abhAva. This is adhikaraNa-swarUpa and hence Brahman. It is not some type of avidyA.

  

Please don't distort my words by saying that as per me, dvaita-agrahaNa is one type of avidyA.

> And for the jnAnaabhAva (tattvAgrahaNa) there is bhAvarUpa avidyA
> which is the cause.

This is true.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
