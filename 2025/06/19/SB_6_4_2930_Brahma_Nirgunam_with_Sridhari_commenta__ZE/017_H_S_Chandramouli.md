Namaste Bhaskar Ji,

I just wanted to present some relevant part of the Bhashya having a bearing on the topic under consideration which could be made use of in the course of the ongoing discussions. I really did not want to join the discussion nyself per se. My problem is my views are different in some aspects from yours, and in some other aspects from those of Sudhanshu Ji’s. Unnecessarily complicates the discussions. However I will draw your attention, as well as Sudhanshu Ji’s, to the following aspect which first needs to be resolved and which, in my opinion, is fundamental to the disagreement between the \*traditional\* and \* Sri SSS \* approach (terminology under asterix marks for want of better terms ).

Sudhanshu Ji states // Problem here is this Bhaskar ji. tattva-agrahaNa, which is basically tattva-grahaNa-abhAva, cannot really obstruct the self-effulgent Brahman. Something which is swaprakAsha cannot remain hidden unless there is AvaraNa. Now, in sushupti also, we accept that there is tattva-agrahaNa. So, some AvaraNa must be accepted. That is why AchArya says in GitA that only when there is AvaraNa, that there is upalabdhi (perception) of avidyA-traya. This is an extremely extremely important statement //.

This is the \* traditional \* view. Sri SSS, in my understanding, disagrees. According to him ( Sri SSS ) this may be so in respect of entities which are \* sAdi \*, which have a bginning. Not necessarily so for entities, like jIva for example, which are \* anAdi \* , which are beginningless. Sri SSS draws support for this view from vArtika as well. Therefore AvaraNa need not be accepted in respect of the jIva or to be present in sushupti.

This is a fundamental diference between the two systems, leading to other collateral differences. Unless this is resolved, there is no hope of ending the dispute. This is my understanding. I had hoped that some time or the other this would have been presented by some of the followers of Sri SSS, and followup on the same through the discussions. If you analyze your own views on sushupti, I am sure you will notice that they stand on this particular viewpoint.

I do not wish to get into other differences. You , as well as Sudhanshu Ji, may like to consider the above while progressing the discussion. I think it would be greatly beneficial.

Regards
