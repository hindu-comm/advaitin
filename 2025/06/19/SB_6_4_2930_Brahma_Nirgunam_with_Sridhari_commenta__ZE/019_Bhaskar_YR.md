This is the \* traditional \* view. Sri SSS, in my understanding, disagrees. According to him ( Sri SSS ) this may be so in respect of entities which are \* sAdi \*, which have a bginning. Not necessarily so for entities, like jIva for example, which are \* anAdi \* , which are beginningless. Sri SSS draws support for this view from vArtika as well. Therefore AvaraNa need not be accepted in respect of the jIva or to be present in sushupti.

As you are well aware, Sri SSS taken this socalled traditional view i.e. avaraNa is mother of other three types avidyA for refutation in his mUlAvidyAnirAsa, sugama and geeta bhAshya itself. The avaraNa just like mask (a concealing type / musuku) is of three types clarifies Sri SSS and also clears the wrong notions like the anirvachaneeya mUlAvidyA is the kAraNa and kArya is avidyA traya etc. Why avidyA is antaHkaraNa dOsha not the problem of kshetrajna bhAshyakAra himself clarifies in the same geeta bhAshya (13.2) by sighting the example of cataract. No need for fourth type of avidyA or mother avidyA since bhAshyakAra himself clarifies the types of avidyA are three and all the three would vanish after the jnAna. (jnAna nivartya avidyA) yadi jnAnAbhAvO yadi saMshayajnAnam yadi vipareeta jnAnaM va uchyate ‘ajnAnaM’ eti sarvaM hi tat jnAneneva nivartyate.
