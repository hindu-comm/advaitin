Sir, think simply. Dvaita is avidyA-krita. Dvaita-abhAva is not avidyA-krita. Dvaita-abhAva is Brahman.

  

Tattva is Brahman. Tattva is not avidyA-krita. Tattva-agrahaNa i.e. tattva-grahaNa-abhAva is avidyA-krita.

  

In sushupti, there is dvaita-agrahaNa as well as tattva-agrahaNa. While the former is Brahman, the latter is avidyA-krita.
