Namaskar:  
  
It is with great sadness that I would like to let everyone know of the passing of our beloved Prof. V. Krishnamurthy, one of the moderators of advaitin since 1998. We are sorrowed with the news with the recognition that we lost a spiritual giant with a great legacy. He attained Videha Mukti at 11:58 PM Pacific Time on June 20, 2025 in Bangalore. He was 97 years old. Last year I met him and he was healthy and very cordial to us.  
  
It is indeed a great loss for humanity and for the spiritual community around the world. He has written more books in his life and he always remembered to send a copy to me. He completed writing his last book late last year. Here is a list of a few books that he had written: [https://notionpress.com/author/V_Krishnamurthysrsltid=AfmBOoqi52VfQ14sDmi6cu8QRB1ZJNX4ox9c5VFWzQi8gPo3G2uaHLoI](https://notionpress.com/author/V_Krishnamurthysrsltid=AfmBOoqi52VfQ14sDmi6cu8QRB1ZJNX4ox9c5VFWzQi8gPo3G2uaHLoI)  
  
I used call him Mama (uncle) and he was quite active on web forums on the subject of Advaita and has also many postings on YouTube. Here is a link to one of his conversations on YouTube.
[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wzf7K8uD6A4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wzf7K8uD6A4)
