Namaskaram,

  

My thanks to Prof Vk-ji for his kindness, his spritely enthusiasm (which is also evident when we see his videos) and his stately presence in our forum during my early years here. He must have been 79 when I joined. Two particulars that I recall were his approach to several advaita topics using his knowledge of mathematics and his devotion to the kanchi paramacharya (which left us a lasting English translation of theacharya's talks on advaita sadhana). He was respectful to all and to his elder Shastri-ji at the time, and then as he became our senior-most member I saw that he eventually withdrewfrom participation in our forum. I wondered if this means ailing health etc. but was relieved eventually seeing he wasactive elsewhere. (Only negative news in this regard came in 2019 when Ram Chandran-ji reported his wife's passing.) Then suddenly some new book or talk by him will pop up (!) and I would feel "ah!!". The most recent was when his name was listed as one of the final speakers at the Global Festival of Oneness for yet another book; searching google I was again happy that he was still well and active into sucha ripe age. But, portentously the name was removed from the list as the festival was coming to a close - which I mentioned to Ram Chandran-ji and Subbu-ji.

  

[https://www.profvk.com/home](https://www.profvk.com/home)  

  

But this is a full and great life by human standards. He was immersed in advaita and sanatana dharma to the end; and (in my mind) happy and at peace with himself, this life, the journey here and ahead, and ultimately the immutable Truth He is, because of grounding in knowledge. Ishvara will ensure as He has assured that the jivatma, His bhakta, will move forward in the spiritual path in future births; so while there is sadness thatsomeone we knew has left us, there is also a sense of peace and self-surrender to the Divine will which is perfect and that perfection we can relate to better when we see the life of noble people like Prof Vk-ji.

  

thollmleukaalkizhu
