Namaste,

I am sad to hear the news of the passing away of Prof V K ji. He was not only an erudite scholar but also a great devotee and a wonderful human being - always kind, gentle and of noble bearing. He had contacted me in the year 2018 to inform me that he had taken excerpts from a few of my postings to the Advaitin list for his book ‘*Thoughts of Spiritual Wisdom’* and we had exchanged a few mails at that time. 

One of the things that left a lasting impression on me was the series of postings of Prof V K ji on the *Saundaryalahari* of Sri Shankaracharya. (I think it was during the years 2005-2006.) I had entreated Prof V K ji on more than one occasion to compile those postings and bring them out in a book form. The last time I had done so was after the old Advaitin Yahoogroup was closed down and the archives of the group were available no more. I felt sad to think that such precious postings would be lost.

This morning, after I read Sri Ram-ji’s message, I was reminded of those Saundaryalahari postings. I casually did an internet search on Google with the keywords “Prof V Krishnamurthy Saundaryalahari” and discovered, to my surprise, that Prof V K ji had indeed compiled all those postings and published it, in the year 2023, as a book titled ‘Paramacharya on Saundaryalahari’ !

I promptly purchased the Kindle version of the book this morning. And when I opened the pages, I found a picture of Prof V K ji’s family deity – **Devi Bhuvaneshwari**. Was it a coincidence that I had just listened to the Bhuvaneshwari Suprabhatam just a while ago this morning? Perhaps. Or perhaps Devi Tripurasaundari was giving me what I had requested from Prof V K ji all along - just at the point of his departure from this bleak earth to Her Divine Radiant Sphere. May Devi Bhuvaneshwari lead his atma to its final destination – the abode of Brahman. 

Regards,

Chittaranjan
