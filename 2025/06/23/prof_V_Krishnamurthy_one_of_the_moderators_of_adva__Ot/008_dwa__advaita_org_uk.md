Sad to hear of ProfVK’s passing. All long-term members of the group will acknowledge his erudition and willingness to help. As well as within the group, I liaised with him through my own website and administratively through the Advaita Academy site, while that existed. Just as an indication of his knowledge and attention to detail, have a look at his ‘definition’ of ‘Brahman’ at [https://www.advaita.org.uk/discourses/definitions/brahman.htm](https://www.advaita.org.uk/discourses/definitions/brahman.htm) back in 2007. My comment on this was as follows:



“Special thanks to Sri ProfVK for a most comprehensive definition of brahman, complete with extensive notes of sources and glossary – quite amazing! I would wish to point out to all other potential definers of terms, however, that we were not expecting such a depth of detail and I would not want them to think that they had to spend so much time and effort, too. If they thought this, we might not get any volunteers!”



Best wishes and condolences to his family,

Dennis
