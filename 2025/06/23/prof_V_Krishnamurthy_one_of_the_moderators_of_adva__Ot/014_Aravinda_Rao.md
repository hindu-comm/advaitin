Namaskars to all,

Thedeparture of Prof. V. Krishna Murthy garu is very saddening. He was a jivan muktawho hasnow attained videha mukti. I was amazed to see his energy and perseveranceabout two years ago when we met. I was fortunate to have his affection and kindness. He gave me the opportunity to write a foreword to a couple of his books. He was a great combination of modernity harmonized with tradition. His biography gives good glimpses of social history in a turbulent period. My humble pranams to him.

Pranams,

Aravinda Rao
