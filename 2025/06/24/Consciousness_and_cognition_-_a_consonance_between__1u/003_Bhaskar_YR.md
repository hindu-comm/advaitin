Rope does have something to do with them, in that it is their true Self or adhishtanam; and the underlying reality is indicated at least in our knowledge of their astitvam (that corresponds to Sat/Satyam).



- I agree with this point. Yes, there should be something really
  existing there to see that as something else. The rope has to be
  there to misconceive it as snake. The nacre has to be there to ‘see’
  the silver. And some sAdrushyaM (similarities) also should be there
  between vastu that is existing there and vastu that we are seeing
  wrongly. If adhishtAnaM is not there one can imagine anything
  anywhere!! like silver in place of rope or snake in place of nacre
  without any sAdrusyaM. However fact remains that when we are seeing
  snake, before seeing snake or after realizing that it is rope, rope
  was / is / will ever be rope only. Our gymnastics do not bring not
  even an iota of change in the existing rope. Hence we say rope has
  nothing to do with ‘our’ problems 😊
