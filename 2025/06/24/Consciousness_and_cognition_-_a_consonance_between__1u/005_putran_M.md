Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

Thanks for the explanations. Your second point on the limitations of the rope-snake analogy when addressing the question of bhaatior jnanam is very interesting. I did not expect that, rather thought someone will tell me how to understand in that analogy itself. In both seer and seen in the plane of duality, Sat or Existence is indicated as reality - this knowledge seems somewhat direct knowledge or self-evident and not as much requiring of shastra; but interestingly Jnanam/Consciousness/bhaati is indicated only in the seer and not the seen; and yet that too is pointer to Brahman. It is hidden in the seen; so to consider Consciousness as the indicator of Brahman will require shastra pramana more. Because our experience within the realm of duality shows the mixture of bhaati and jada, whereas all objects we can consider more easily as rooted in Existence.
