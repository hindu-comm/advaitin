> Can you also explain why sometimes jnanam is said to be a pointer to
> Brahman whereas other times it is almost equated to Brahman?

  

Whenever jnAnam is used as a pointer, it is the vAchya-artha of jnAnam. When the lakshya-artha of jnAnam is meant, it is identical to Brahman.

  

So, vAchya-artha of jnAnam is not identical to Brahman. Lakshya-artha of jnAnam is identical to Brahman.

  

Similarly, for Ananda, sat etc also. Their vAchya-artha is not identical with Brahman. But their lakshya-artha is identical to Brahman.

  

Advaita Siddhi says - ज्ञानानन्दयोरभेदेऽपि कल्पितजातिभेदनिबन्धनप्रवृत्तिकतया पदद्वयप्रयोग्यस्य व्यावृत्तिभेदेन साफल्यात् ।

  

Laghuchandrika explains - तथा च ज्ञानत्व-आनन्दत्वयोः (वाच्यार्थयोः) न अभेदः, किन्तु तदुपलक्षितव्यक्त्योः इति भावः.

  

Thus, basically vAchya-artha is vishishTa-chit and lakshya-artha is vishishTa-tAdAtmya-upalakshita-chit. The former is different but latter is identical to Brahman. The former is pointer but latter is identical to Brahman.

> As I said to Bhaskar-ji, I can understand Existence because in my mind
> that is practically universally associated with all objects of
> cognition; but when it comes to bhaati or Consciousness, or
> Ananda/Priyam, these are not easily identifiable as universal and yet
> they are given the priority of being pointer to Brahman. Is shastra
> the only pramana for this, or how can tarka justify this preference
> for them?

  

Actually, sat and chit are accepted in siddhAnta to be not covered by mUla-avidyA. Only Ananda is accepted to be covered. That is why we can have an idea about is-ness. Similarly, we have an idea about consciousness, because all objects are illuminated by our consciousness. So, I don't see any difficulty for sat and chit.

  

I understand that there is difficulty regarding Ananda because unbroken, singular, homogeneous Ananda cannot be comprehended. It is covered by ajnAna. However, should we say that we are completely dependent on shAstra to use Ananda as a pointer to Brahman?

  

I don't think so. Because if we analyse the mechanism of vishesha-Ananda, we see that it is nothing but a reflection of shuddha-chaitanya in a particular kind of vritti. From there we can infer that shuddha Brahman can be pointed through vishesha-Ananda.

  

Now, one more thing -- what exactly is this vastu called being pointed? That is answered in Laghuchandrika:

  

वस्तुतस्तु निरुपाधीष्ट्त्वादिनोपलक्षितत्वं तत्प्रकारकमनोवृत्त्युत्तरं निर्विकल्पकमनोवृत्त्या विषयीकृतत्वम्;

  

So, when it is said that take lakshya-artha of satya, jnAna, anata, Ananda etc -- all we are asked is -- arrive at nirvikalpaka vritti. That is be without anAtma-vritti. That is -- situate as Brahman.
