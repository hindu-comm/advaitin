Namaskaram Bhaskar-ji,

  

Your understanding is the one I recall holding onto though without fully understanding why. That our experiential meanings given to sat and chit are different and hence they must be seen as pointers to Brahman. But it is a question whether we can look at root meanings with the knowledge given by shastra and come to the conclusion that Brahman is Satyam, is Jnanam, is Anantam, is Anandam etc. where they are not different but equivalent. Is this possible? I have to think on Anandam yet more carefully, but reading and replying to Sudhanshu-ji and Venkataraghavan-ji gives me a greater intuition to this perspective. (Yes, imu, Venkataraghavan-ji presents a different or more direct-meaning interpretation than the pointer-one.) You can read my replies to them.
