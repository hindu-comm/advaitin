What you say is true. One is never going to experience the all pervasive nature of consciousness because our minds being finite, we cannot experience all pervasiveness as the nature of our consciousness. Despite that, it is possible to recognise that our consciousness is infinite.

  

We can never see our own face apart from in a mirror. What we see is a reflection, not our face. However we do identify that reflection with our face, and even while looking at the reflection, declare its identity with the original. Similarly, even while experiencing only the chidAbhAsa, we can equate that with the original chaitanya.

Yes, it is not a matter of experience, because the experience of consciousnessrequires a medium and that medium of experience (the mind) is finite. It (the mind) is present here and not in the rock, so the notion "I" emerges here and not there. Because it (the notion "I") emerges here, we identify the "I" with the mind and declare "I am here", when the original "I" is all pervading consciousness. It is like a person looking at his reflection in the mirror, ignoring his own face, and thinking that the face exists only in the mirror, because he sees the reflection only there.
