Happiness, sorrow etc are kshetra. They are vikAra. They are non-existent. They just appear as if they exist. Like the mirage-water.

  

Being mirage-water like, they are mere appearance.

  

Cause-effect-relationship is equally an appearance.

  

Therefore, happiness/sorrow don't have a cause. They just appear to have a cause, while they don't.
