Namaste

  

The statement of Swami Vidyaranya is in the realm of vairagya = doṣa dr̥ṣṭi. The other elements of sādhana chatuṣṭaya are also in the vyavaharika. In the Jivanmukti viveka he says: first in order to overcome the ashuddha vāsana one should cultivate shuddha vāsana and finally transcend even that to be in nirvāsana.
