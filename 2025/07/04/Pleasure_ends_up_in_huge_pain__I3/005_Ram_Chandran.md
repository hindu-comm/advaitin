Namaste:

As rightly pointed out by Subbuji,, when Jiva identifes with the body, mind and intellect (anatma), they seek pleasure which is transient and eventually the pleasure disappears and sorrow appears!! Identification, attachment and ownership happens in Vyavaharika which makes the Jiva to experience pleasures, sorrow and pain which never exist when Jiva gives up attachment and ownership. When we came to this world, we came with 'nothing' and when we leaves this world, we take 'nothing.' The world where the possession of materials that are responsible to provide pleasure and pain again fall inot "Maya!' To keep Happiness for ever, we should prepare to "give up" everything! This is the subtle message of Swami Vidhyaranya!! The question is, "pleasure or pain to whom - Atma or Anatma!!  

  

with may warm regards,
