Hare Krishna Bhaskar prabhu ji.

  

> Ø So you mean to say we just like that laugh or just like that
> cry without any substantial cause / kAraNa!! Do I have to say what
> this person who without any reason cries/laughs being called !!??
> when you go against lOkAnubhava you will end up like this 😊

  

Seen-1, seen-2 and the causal-connection between them ---- all these three are illusory. Your laughter at moment 2, your listening to moment 1, and the connection between joke and laughter -- all these three are illusory.

  

When I disregard causality as illusory, I disregard the so-called cause and effect as well. Please do not miss that.

  

LokAnubhava is akin to dream-anubhava. Just as the dream-joke and dream-laughter were not causally connected but were two disjoint mental fluctuations, similarly in waking.

  

> Ø So seeing the sarpa is not a cause of fear, it is just appear to
> have a cause…and it is just like that happen….Thanks for accepting
> that for the adhyAsa there is no substantial cause, it is just
> happens like that.

  

The fear is illusory, the seeing-of-snake-in-rope is illusory and their causal connection is also illusory.

  

Just as seen is illusory, the chirantanava चिरन्तनत्व, pUrva-pashcAt-bhAvitva पूर्व-पश्चात्-भावित्व and pasapara-kArya-kAraNatA परस्पर-कार्य-कारणता are also illusory.

  

I highlight more on illusoriness of causation as to me. To me, it seems much more effective in realizing the illusoriness of entire seen.



> And this is what bhAshyakAra too say adhyAsa is just svAbhAvika/
> naisargika lOka vyavahAra no need to prove it with pramANa nor it is
> an effect of something else.

  

Causation itself is illusory. So, to say that ajnAna is cause of adhyAsa, is within the framework of illusion only. It is akin to saying clay is cause of pot. ajnAna as cause of adhyAsa has only as much validity as saying clay is cause of pot.

  

The truth is - there is neither any clay, nor any pot, nor any causal-connection between them. That is how we have to understand ajnAna-adhyAsa also.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
