Hare Krishna Bhaskar prabhu ji.

  

This is what Upanishads also declare when they hold trayah swapnAh. BhAshyakAra would go on to augment this by saying that jAgrat, swapna and sushupti are all swapna. That just as you see non-existent objects in dream, similarly in waking (and sushupti).

  

So, this is what is acceptable to Upanishads also.

  

By the way, it is immaterial as to what anyone says. Truth stands on its own feet.

  

Regards.
