[[Akilesh Ayyar	2025-07-16, 03:15:01 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/I36APnDTFfM)]]

If one *really*believes everything is bhranti, the mind stops, and no further questions arise. To the extent that further questions arise, one does really believe that everything is bhranti. To the one in that position, these various vadas (including the idea that everything is bhranti) are introduced.

  

The idea that everything is bhranti is not itself to be taken as perfect truth, but is a kind of counter-statement to the common sense idea that things are to be taken at face value. When the "matter" of "common sense" and the "anti-matter" of the radical doctrines of Vedanta meet, they annihilate each other.

  

What then is left?



Akilesh Ayyar
