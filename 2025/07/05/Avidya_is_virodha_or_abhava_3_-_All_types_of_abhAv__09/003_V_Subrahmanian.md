Could you bring out the instances where Shankaracharya in his commentaries accepts a positive avidya that is at the root of samsara?

It generated several Sanskrit quotes as that of the Bhashya, etc. but none of them turned out to be genuine on verification. I had had such an experience earlier too, with respect to the Bhashyam and even the Valmiki Ramayana😄 Please note that I am not insulting anyone but only wanted to show how Chat gpt can be a kind of 'chintāmaṇi' or 'kalpataru', a wish fulfillingtree in a humoroussense..
