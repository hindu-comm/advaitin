Namaste Sri Subbuji

  

There is no inconsistency between what is stated in the Mahabharata Ashwamedhika parvan and what is stated by Sri Shankara in Brahmasutra Bhashya.

  

In paramArtha, all forms are due to mAyA, and this includes the VishwarUpa.

  

However, in vyavahAra, the VishwarUpa is ShAshwata. It is eternal. This is because the Mahabharata verses that I quoted themselves say this.
