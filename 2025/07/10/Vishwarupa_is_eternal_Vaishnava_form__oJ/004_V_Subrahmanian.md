Dear Kalyan ji

  

In the Gita itself Arjuna, not bearing to behold the viswarupa, requests for the withdrawal thereof and assume Krishna's benign sowmya form and Krishna obliged. So, it can't be shashwata in the literal sense.

  

There is also another dimension to it. The Vishwa that is shown in the Gita contains destruction and that's what Arjuna couldn't bear. That's not the default form of Ishwara. For instance in laya state there's no manifest world which can be seen by anyone. Hence also the viswarupa cannot be shashwata, default form of Ishwara.

  

Regards

subbu
