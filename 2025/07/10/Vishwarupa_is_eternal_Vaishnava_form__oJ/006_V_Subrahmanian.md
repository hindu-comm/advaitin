शाश्वत

वि\* - शश्वद् भव: अण्

"नित्य, सनातन, **चिरस्थायी**"

  

That Bhagavan himself withdrew the show proves that it's not eternal. In the Bhagavadgita itself we have another instance:

  

प्राप्य पुण्यकृतां लोकानुषित्वाशाश्वतीः समाः ।

शुचीनां श्रीमतां गेहे योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते ॥ ४१ ॥

  

The yoga bhrashtha lives in the heavenly regions for 'eternal' years and comes back to this world. The adjective eternal does not mean literally. It has an end in vyavahara itself. So did the viswarupa. In fact such a rupa can neither be shown nor seen in pralaya as the term rupa itself defies such a sense. It's vyakta rupa. In avyakta there can't be any rupa.

  

There are many such usages:

  

“शाश्वतं देवपूजादि विप्रदानञ्च शाश्वतम् ।शाश्वतं सगुणा विद्याः सुहृन्मित्रञ्च शाश्वतम् ॥

”इति गारुडे नीतिसागरे १०९ अध्यायः ॥

  

It's clear that deva puja, friends, gifts to brahmanas, etc are not permanent even though they have been termed shaashvata.
