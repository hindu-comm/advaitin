Namaskaram Kalyan ji,

  

\<\< In paramArtha, all forms are due to mAyA, and this includes the VishwarUpa. \>\>

  

A quick note - in paramartha there is no maya and its effects. Maya and its effects are in vyavahara alone. A jnani with vyavahara drishti recognizes the play of maya in and through every thing including Bhagavan's vishwarupa, whereas an ajnani fails to recognize the play of the all-powerful maya.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
