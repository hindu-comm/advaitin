Namaskaram Kalyan ji,

  

\<\< The Vishwarupa form is eternal because its conception exists at all times. There is no comparison with the usage as mentioned in the Bhagavad Gita verse, because the fruits of actions are finite. Whereas, the VishwarUpa is not finite in vyavahAra. At the time of laya, only prakRti becomes avyakta \>\>  
  

Per Mandukya Upanishad Bhashya, Virat is Brahman-Atman manifesting through all gross bodies. The same manifestation (through all gross bodies of the universe) is rendered perceivable by Bhagavan to shri Arjuna in the context of SBG, and hence accurately called "Virat-rupa". Consequently, the manifestation of Virat-rupa exists as long as the gross universe is manifested; and Virat-rupa becomes unmanifest at pralaya.

  
  
\<\< Bhagavan does not become avyakta. It is stated several times in Mahabharata, that after pralaya, Bhagavan Vishnu lies resting on a serpent, so this too shows that Bhagavan exists during pralaya. \>\>  
  

Furthermore, during pralaya, Bhagavan exists as manifesting through the causal body and is called the "avyakrita".  

  

prostrations,

Vikram
