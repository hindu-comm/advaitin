Namaskaram Kalyan ji,

  

Yes, and a similar description is available in Srimad Bhagavamas well. The intent isMaharshi Markandeya'sattempt to understand the unimaginable power of Maya. If one were to say that during the state of pralaya there is the existence of a distinct 'Maharshi Markandeya' with a name-form, then this is not the absolute pralaya, but only an approximation or illustration of pralaya by Bhagavan through His grace. It is clear through several scriptures and bhashyas that in the state of pralaya there is no distinct manifestation of any name-form whatsoever.

  

If you believe that name-form distinction exists in pralaya, please provide reference based on prasthanatrayabhashya.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
