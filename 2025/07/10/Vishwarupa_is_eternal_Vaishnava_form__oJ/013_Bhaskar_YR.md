I will not read any intent into a straightforward text. The text provides for an appropriate description of Pralaya, which needs no secondary explanations. In the text, Markandeya is describing the greatness of Narayana, not the greatness of mAyA.

And Markandeya maharshi is one of the ‘chiranjeevi’s’ like hanumanta 😊 So whether he will be there after pralaya or not with his specified nAma rUpa is quite mute here. And after pralaya Yes each and every yugAntya, the Jagat undergoes mahApraLaya but it retains its power potentialities and there cannot be creation without the (causal power-

potentialities) in the cause. In beeja rUpa (avyAkruta rUpa) this mAyA will always be there with Ishwara and Ishwara is nitya like his parApara prakruti in srushti-sthiti-pralaya prakriya.
