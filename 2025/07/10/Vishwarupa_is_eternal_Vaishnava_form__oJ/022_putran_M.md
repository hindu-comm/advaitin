Namaskaram Kalyan-ji,

  

I am reading a consistent differentiation of sage Markandeya from Narayana. That is dvaita. The teaching "aham brahmasmi" or "tattvamasi" are not there in this passage. That doesn't mean the dualistic description of Narayana from the standpoint of the jiva Markandeya is necessarily wrong; but it is within the category of "jagan mithya" because the presumed duality is not real but adhyasa on the non-dual Self. Similarly, associating Brahman with eternal vishwarupa is apriori a dvaita-knowledge; from advaita standpoint this is all adhyasa on non-dual Self.

  

But yes, we are smarthas because we accept that the smritis can be clarified and provided a fuller (vyavaharika vs paramarthika) context so that they are all consistent with advaita and their dvaita-descriptions of Brahman have proper place and relevance within the plane of ajnana/imagination/maya.
