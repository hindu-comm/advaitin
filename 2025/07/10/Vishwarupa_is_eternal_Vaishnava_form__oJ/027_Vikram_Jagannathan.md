Namaskaram Kalyan ji,

  

\<\<  
There is \*\*absolutely nothing\*\* in the link that is non-advaitic.  
There is \*\*nothing that is inconsistent\*\* with "brahma satyam, jagan mithyam".  
However, some things may appear non-advaitic only to those who restrict advaita too much.  
\>\>

  

As Putran ji had clarified, it is the interpretation that seems non-Advaitic. The verses are clear that Maharshi Markandeya had an amazing darshana of the Great Lord and there is definitely no conflict with Advaita siddhanta, when interpreted appropriately. However your interpretation, particularly the 'absolutely eternal' (not 'relatively eternal', which is accepted in Advaita) nature of Bhagavan's 'Vishwarupa' (or more accurately Viratrupa) seems non-Advaitic.

  
Per Advaita siddhanta, would you agree that:  
1. Any and all perceptions of distinctions are mithya and anitya.  
2. Any and all perceptions of change are mithya and anitya.  
3. The entire jagat is mithya and anitya.  
4. Consequently, Bhagavan's Viratrupa, which is His manifestation through the entire gross jagat is mithya and anitya.  
5. Also, all distinctions perceived by Maharshi Markandeya within Lord Narayana's body, in the state of pralaya, is mithya and anitya.  
6. The continuous cycle of srishti-stithi-pralaya change is mithya and anitya.  
7. Furthermore, the concept of maya is purely vyavaharika and not paramarthika; maya is mithya and anitya.  
8. Even the 6-fold qualities of 'Bhagavan', as well as the powers of jagat srishti-stithi-pralaya is purely vyavaharika and not paramarthika; 'Bhagavan-hood' is mithya and anitya.  
  
Please do share if you disagree with any of these points.  

  

prostrations,

Vikram
