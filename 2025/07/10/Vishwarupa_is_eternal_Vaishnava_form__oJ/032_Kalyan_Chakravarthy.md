I had written this in an earlier message, which was probably missed.

  

"In paramArtha, all forms are due to mAyA, and this includes the VishwarUpa."

  

"However, in vyavahAra, the VishwarUpa is ShAshwata. It is eternal."

  

I hope these two statements answer all your questions.

  

I will add to this - The Vishwarupa of Bhagavan Vishnu survives pralaya, because that is what is depicted in the Mbh text.
