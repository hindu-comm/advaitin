Namaste Kalyan ji.

  

> "In paramArtha, all forms are due to mAyA, and this includes the
> VishwarUpa."

  

In paramArtha, there is neither any form not is there any mAyA. So, this statement is erroneous.

  

माया नाम वस्तु तर्हि ; नैवम् , सा च माया न विद्यते । मायेत्यविद्यमानस्याख्येत्यभिप्रायः ॥ (Mandukya Upanishad bhAshya)

  

MAyA is not any vastu. The word mAyA is used to indicate something which is not existent.

  

This is paramArtha.

  

In vyavahAra, there are are two views. In one view, mAyA is real, and in another view, it is mithyA. They are respectively called स्वाज्ञ दृष्टि and स्वज्ञ दृष्टि. Please refer to Upanishad Brahm Yogi commentaries.

  

Thus, there are three descriptions of mAyA in shAstra, वास्तवी, अनिर्वचनीया and तुच्छा.

  

In paramArtha, mAyA is tuchchhA i.e. horns of hare. In vyavahAra, depending on drishTi, shAstra says mAyA to be either real or mithyA.

  

There is no mOksha until mAyA is realized as tuchchhA.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
