> Which upanishads from UpanishadBrahmaYogi'scommentaries should I
> referto for more understanding?

  

This is the common theme running through all of his commentaries. For the instant issue, please refer to the commentary on Tejobindu Upanishad, chapter 5, verse 75.

  

इदं प्रपञ्चं यत्किञ्चिद्यद्यज्जगति विद्यते ।

दृश्यरूपं च दृग्रूपं सर्वं शशविषाणवत् ॥

  

(This world and whatever (appears to be) present in this world, be it in the nature of seer, or in the nature of seen -- they are all like horns of hare).

  

You can download the commentary from archive. It comes under the section "The yoga Upanishads" - [https://archive.org/details/wg1249](https://archive.org/details/wg1249).

  

The book is edited by Alladi Mahadeva Shastri ji.
