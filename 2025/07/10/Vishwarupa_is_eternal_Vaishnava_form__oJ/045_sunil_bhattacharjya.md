Dear friends,

  

There has been a prolonged discussion on Vishwaroopa, after I stated that Lord Krishna told Arjuna that during the first description of the Bhagavad Gita, Lord Krishna was the param Brahma and he could also show even the Vishwaoopa to Arjuna.  

  

Call him the 'Param Brahma' or the 'Brahman', He is the same. Lord Krishna was in a Yoga-state and at that state He was no different from the Param Brahma, There is no distinction as to whether there was Vaishnavism or Shaivism etc. The True Bramajnani can be one with the Brahman when he is in the yogic state. At that stage there is only Brahman and nothing else.  

  

In the beginning of the Original Bhagavad Gita, as well as in the end of the Uddhava Gita (in the chapter on Jnana),  Lord Krishna clearly told that In the beginning there was nothing and at the end there would be nothing, everything else we see is basically unreal. There is no point in differentiating in terms of Vaishnavism or Shaivism etc too. . There is only Brahman. The same advice was also given by Brahmarshi Vashishtha to Lord Ram, as the 'Yogavashishtha' tells us.  

  

Adi Shankara wanted to write his commentary on the Original Bhagavad Gita, but his paramguru Shri Gaudapadacharya did not permit him to do so, even though Vedavyasa clearly said that all the upanishads are cows, Lord Krishna was the milk-man and the milk was the Bhagavad Gita. There is nothing above the Bhagavad Gita.  

  

Best wishes,

Sunil KB
