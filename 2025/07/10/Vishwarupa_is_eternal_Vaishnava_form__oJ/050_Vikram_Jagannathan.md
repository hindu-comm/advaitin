Namaskaram Suresh ji,

  

\<\< By selflessness I meant the elimination of "dehAtma" bhAva. \>\>

  

If by 'self', you mean the ignorant deha-atman bhava, you are correct, full elimination of deha-atman bhava (absolute selflessness) results in Brahman-Atman realization alone.

  

\<\< IMHO all the yagya/daana/tapasya prescribed by the shAstra is to overcome or transcend this dehAtma bhAva to attain true Atmatva/Brahmatva. \>\>

  

Agreed. However, this deha-atman bhava is adhyasa and can only be actually overcome through jnana (akhandakara vritti jnana). Karmas like Yagya/dana/tapasya are auxiliaries in the genesis of this jnana. When this adhyasa is removed, all that remains is Brahman/Atman alone - AS-IS.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
