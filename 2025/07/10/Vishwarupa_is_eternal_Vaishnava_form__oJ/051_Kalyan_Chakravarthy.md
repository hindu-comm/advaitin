The Mahabharata episode shows that BhagavAn can make certain exceptions for a few pralaya cycles, in order to give us the knowledge of His own nature and the nature of pralaya.

  

Thus, there is no contradiction between what is mentioned by Sri Shankara (a general rule) and what is mentioned in the Mahabharata (specific exceptions to the general rule).
