Namaste Michael ji.

  

> You objected to vyavaharika/lokyia drsti and sastrika drsti but say
> the same with catchier terms: स्वाज्ञदृष्टिः & स्वज्ञदृष्टिः.

  

Whether it is स्वाज्ञदृष्टि or स्वज्ञदृष्टि, it is from the frame of reference of ignorance only. From the frame of reference of Brahman, i.e. paramArtha-drishTi, there is nothing to be seen.

  

When there is appearance of seen, i.e. when the frame of reference is that of ignorance, one can either treat the seen as real or treat the seen as illusory. The former is swAjna whereas latter is swajna.

  

But both of these are from the frame of reference of ignorance.

  

From this frame of reference, mAyA is not tuchchhA. This is vivarta-vAda. And that is why it is not the ultimate.

  

The ultimate is ajAti, wherein even the vivarta-buddhi is denied.
