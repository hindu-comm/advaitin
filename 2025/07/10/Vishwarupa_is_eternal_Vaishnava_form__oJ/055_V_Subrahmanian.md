On Sun, 13 Jul 2025, 11:57 pm Kalyan Chakravarthy, \<[kalyanchakr...@gmail.com]()\> wrote:  

> The Mahabharata episode shows that BhagavAn can make certain
> exceptions for a few pralaya cycles, in order to give us the knowledge
> of His own nature and the nature of pralaya.

  

But there is absolutely no use to the seeker because that is not the way pralaya is. Nowhere the Vedanta speaks of pralaya that way. What is described in that episode is no different from the waking. Waking is characterized by experiencing things through sense organs. That is completely absent in sleep and pralaya. If someone is asked what is pralaya, one cannot give the Markandeya episode as an answer. Even as an exception it's inadmissible.

  

As I said before it's akin to the description of Vaikuntha in many texts. Since such a concept is impossible it's given a place of arthavada.
