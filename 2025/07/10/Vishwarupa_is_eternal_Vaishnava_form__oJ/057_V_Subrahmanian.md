Even if Markandeya survives many pralayas, yet during pralaya he would remain as purely sukshma sharira and not the person with body mind complex as we see in the episode. In pralaya jivas would not have the gross bodies. They will acquire the gross body in the next creation depending upon the karma that has fructified during pralaya. But contrary to this we find Markandeya, himself with a gross body cognizing gross bodies and gross objects.

  

There is a Brahma Sutra where the question: since in pralaya all

Jivas go into avyakta mode, how can one be sure the same jiva emerges in the next creation. The reply given is: each jiva is differentiated by its avidya and karma and hence there will not be the danger of any mix-up. This clinches that pralaya is a state of all particulars having merged with there being no way of perceiving them distinctly.

  

These lakshanas are absent in the episode. As Nilakatha points out the episode is conveying that Brahman is the cause underlying all differentiations. That way alone it is helpful in Advaita siddhi.
