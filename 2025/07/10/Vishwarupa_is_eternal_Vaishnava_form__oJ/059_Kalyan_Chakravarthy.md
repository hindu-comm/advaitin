> \>If pralaya is the way it's described then it's not different from
> sthithi or waking. If such were the case there was no need for the
> Shankara Bhashyam to specify that in pralaya there's no possibility of
> knower knowing and known triputee.

We have already discussed this. All jIvas, with the exception of Markandeya have no possibility of knowing anything.

  

Markandeya is the exception to the general rule.

  

\>Even if Markandeya survives many pralayas, yet during pralaya he would remain as purely sukshma sharira and not the person with body mind complex as we see in the episode. In pralaya jivas would not have the gross bodies. They will acquire the gross body in the next creation depending upon the karma that has fructified during pralaya. But contrary to this we find Markandeya, himself with a gross body cognizing gross bodies and gross objects.

  

It's true that jIvas have no gross body in pralaya. Whether Markandeya had a gross body or subtle body is immaterial. Markandeya is the exception to jIvas not having particular consciousness in pralaya.

  

I reiterate, if Markandeya is not exception, then it would render Yudhisthira as a liar.
