There is no exception to such niyamas. If there is, it will be seen as a kind of a tostaged one meant to convey a message using that as a medium. This is exactly the idea behind the various panchatantra type stories where animals, birds, rivers, mountains etc converse, etc. These are not the norm, not natural, but the author devices this to convey his message. The Markandeya episode is of this category. Hence there is no contingency of Yudhishthira or Veda Vyasa being charged with lying.

  

The shruti too does the same. So says Gaudapada:

  

The various creation shrutis are only a means to know the Truth that is shown as the one behind the creation, while no creation ever has taken place. By that much the Veda does not become a liar.
