This does not contradict my understanding. In the higher four lOkAs, depending upon his upAsanAs or sAdhanAs, Markandeya could well come across any higher entities in the Brahma LOka. It is useful to remember that the same word is used in the Shrutis and PurANAs for jIvAs at different levels. For example there are thousands of rudrAs who are certainly different from THE Rudra spoken of in the purANAs themseves. Or multiple sUryAs etc not all of whom are jnAnIs. Contextually they need to be understood. However I do admit that this contextuality itself could be different as between advaities/dvaities/vishishtAdvaitiesetc. My understanding is from the perspective of an advaitin.
