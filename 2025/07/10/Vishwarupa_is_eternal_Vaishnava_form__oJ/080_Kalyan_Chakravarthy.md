Notwithstanding my better judgment, I am writing this reply.  

  

Agreement of a majority of members is no yardstick for truth, so I will not even go in that direction. You can be happy and contented that you are part of majority. This doesn't work for me.

  

Instead, I go by the real discussion points -

  

1\. Its true that jIvas have no particular consciousness in pralaya. But this can be easily answered by saying that a chiranjeevi like Markandeya is an exception.

  

2\. Nasadiya Suktam - This suktam itself wonders in the end on what was there before creation. It says - only the Lord knows or may be He doesn't. So the Suktam does not rule out anything.

  

3\. Nasadiya Suktam - It explicitly mentions the waters of creation, which are waters of pralaya. In fact, Vishnu is called Narayana because naara refers to waters and the waters are his first residence.

  

4\. Even if the NS is stating nothing moved, it does state there was a Being that existed.

  

5\. The NS is stating a general rule. Markandeya is an exception.

  

6\. Sri Chandramouli-ji mentions about the Mbh episode representing lesser pralaya rather than greater pralaya. This is already addressed by showing specific BORI verses that talk of pralaya after 1000 yugas.

  

7\. If the incident is not real, if Markandeya did not survive that great pralaya, it would portray Yudhisthira as a liar - this is not possible.

  

8\. Finally, in my opinion, the episode does not contradict advaita at all. I know you disagree, but that's fine.
