So when scholars and "majority" who participated, are disagreeing with a conclusion, then that does have weightage here over the loneopinion going against them.

See, "surviving" the great pralaya and remaining a chiranjeevi (because Ishvara manifests that jiva again in a "same" way in next Creation) is different from stating that this jiva was somehow cognitively and individually active during mahapralaya when all else is withdrawn. NS absolutely negates such possibility.

  

The resolution has to be that the MB episode is araadhanaof Ishvara within which we are also being taught higher truths. So, there is arthavada, adhyaropa by the bhaktha-jivas, to describe Him in that primal state, "as if" the sage too was present to witness Him separately or to worhshipBrahma on lotus flower etc. All this should not be taken literally.



> 
>
> 8\. Finally, in my opinion, the episode does not contradict advaita at
> all. I know you disagree, but that's fine.

  

Advaita does not become true or not true because of pralaya/deep-sleep vs creation/waking. The Rope does not become snake whether you see snake or don't see anything.

  

The MB episode of course contradicts advaita. It is talking about the snake. At least NSindicates the non-duality of Ishvara and suggest that duality is not real but His imagination or maya. It allows us to see the parallel of pralaya with the Brahman-standpoint. The MB episode (if taken literally) insists on retaining duality in Self (Markandeya vs Brahma vs Narayana) even in pralaya, so takes out the possibility of affirming Unity in advaitic sense there as well.
