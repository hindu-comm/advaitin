Dear Kalyanji,

Now you have put it properly. The whole creation is Vishnu's creation. One can call it as Vishnu's Maya, or some may even put it like that it is Vishnu's sister Yogamaya's Maya. Mahamaya Durga devi is also called as Vishnu's sister. Essentially they are all one.  

  

As regards the 'Amaras' or the 'Undiables', it appears that they may be able to live till the end of the cycle of creation and destruction, if they so wish.  

  

Let us see how our other friends in the advaitin group look at this issue.

> > > >
> > > > Translation by Swami Gambhirananda:  
> > > >   
> > > >   
> > > > Opponent: **You cannot certainly admit any connection of the
> > > > soul with the intellect during sleep and dissolution,** for the
> > > > Upanisad says, "O amiable one, he then becomes identified with
> > > > Existence (Brahman), he becomes absorbed in his own Self" (Ch.
> > > > VI. viii. 1). **This also follows from the admission of the
> > > > (final) dissolution of the entire creation.** So how can there
> > > > be the persistence of the contact with the intellect as long as
> > > > the transmigratory state of the soul endures?
> > > >
> > > >   
> > > > Vedantin: The answer is thus:  
> > > >   
> > > > We see in the world that manhood etc. though existing all the
> > > > time in **a latent state,** are not perceived during boyhood
> > > > etc. and are thus treated as though non-existent, but they
> > > > become manifest in youth etc.; **and it is not a fact that they
> > > > evolve out of nothing,** for in that case even a eunuch should
> > > > grow those (moustaches etc.). **Similarly, too, the contact with
> > > > the intellect etc. remains in a state of latency during sleep
> > > > and dissolution and emerges again during waking and creation.
> > > > For thus alone it becomes logical.** Nothing can possibly be
> > > > born capriciously, for that would lead to unwarranted
> > > > possibilities (of effects being produced without causes). The
> > > > Upanisad also shows that this waking from sleep is possible
> > > > because of the existence of ignorance in a seed form (remaining
> > > > dormant in sleep): "Though unified with Existence (Brahman) in
> > > > sleep, they do not understand, 'We have merged in Existence.'
> > > > They return here as a tiger or a lion" (just as they had been
> > > > here before) (Ch. VI. ix. 3) etc. Hence it is proved that the
> > > > contact with the intellect etc. persists as long as the
> > > > individuality of the soul lasts.
> > > >
> > > >   
> > > >
> > > > तस्मात् तत्प्रकृतित्वेऽपि******सुषुप्तप्रलय****वत् बीजभावावशेषैव** एषा
> > > > सत्सम्पत्तिरिति ॥ ८ ॥// Brahma sutra bhashya 4.2.8
> > > >
> > > >   
> > > >
> > > > In sleep and pralaya the jiva is one with Sat, by retaining its
> > > > body mind complex and the world in **seed form.**
> > > >

> > > >

> > > >
> > > > ………— ‘नाह खल्वयमेवं सम्प्रत्यात्मानं जानात्ययमहमस्मीति नो एवेमानि भूतानि’
> > > > इति सुषुप्तावस्थायांविशेषविज्ञानाभावमेव दर्शयति, न विज्ञातारं प्रतिषेधति ।
> > > > यत्तु तत्र ‘विनाशमेवापीतो भवति’ इति, तदपिविशेषविज्ञानविनाशाभिप्रायमेव,
> > > > न विज्ञातृविनाशाभिप्रायम्; ‘न हि विज्ञातुर्विज्ञातेर्विपरिलोपो
> > > > विद्यतेऽविनाशित्वात्’ (बृ. उ. ४…
> > > >
> > > > In sleep (by extension, in pralaya too) there is no
> > > > perception/experience of any **particularized knowledge.**
> > > >
> > > > सुषुप्तावस्थायां परं ज्योतिराकाशशब्दितं परं ब्रह्मोपसम्पद्य,विशेषविज्ञानवत्त्वं च
> > > > परित्यज्य, स्वेन रूपेणाभिनिष्पद्यते
> > > >
> > > > In the deep sleep the jiva exists in its native form (Brahman)
> > > > by giving up particularized knowledge.
> > > >
> > > >   
> > > >
> > > > Thus, from the above sample passages it is clear that in the
> > > > state of dissolution, pralaya, the body-mind-organs complex is
> > > > merged and no particularized knowledge is possible, and is in a
> > > > latent state in Brahman and hence there is no possibility of
> > > > anyone to make any conversation with any other entity or have
> > > > any vision.
