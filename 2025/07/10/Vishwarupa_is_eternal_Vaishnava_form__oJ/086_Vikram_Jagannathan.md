Namaskaram Kalyan ji,

  

Notwithstanding your last response to me, you are, of course, free to offer your own reading of the scriptures, but please recognize that what you have presented does*not*align with Advaita Siddhanta. If you wish to share it as a personal opinion, that is fine - just avoid attributing it to Swami Sankaracharya's Advaita sampradaya.  
  
In Advaita, Sruti carries greater authority than smrti, puranas, itihasas or vedangas; any apparent conflict with the former must be resolved by re-interpreting the latter. To qualify as Advaita Siddhanta, an interpretation must not contradict the Prasthana-traya bhasya or the accepted teachings of later acharyas.  
  
As I have requested several times, please cite explicit passages from the Prasthana-traya bhasyas or other prakarana-granthas that describe — even as an exception — a distinct manifestation during maha-pralaya. Until such references are produced, your position remains a personal view rather than an Advaitic doctrine, irrespective of how strongly you feel this interpretation does not contradict Advaita at all.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
