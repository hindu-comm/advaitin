You have written a long post, but I will be succinict in my response.

  

The story about prANa in upanishads is kalpita, does not mean that the Mbh is kalpita.

  

Mbh is itihAsa. We treat the pandavas, kauravas as real (in vyavahAra).

  

The pandavas were really (vyavahAra) sent to the forest and there they really met Markandeya.

  

The Gita was really (vyavahArika satya) taught by Krishna.

  

Nowhere does AchArya say that the Gita episode is kalpita or arthavAda. It literally happened.

  

The Kuruksetra war really happened.

  

Similarly, Pandavas literally met Markandeya in forest.

  

Yudhishthira, who speaks satya, literally asked him questions.

  

Markandeya literally answered about pralaya.

  

From a paramArthika perspective, there is no pralaya.

  

From a vyavahArika perspective, there is.

  

Mbh is itihAsa, it literally happened.

  

We cannot selectively pick what is real (vyavahArika) and what is not.
