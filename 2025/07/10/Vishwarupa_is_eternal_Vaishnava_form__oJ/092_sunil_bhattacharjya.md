Dear friends,

  

\(A\) Not for nothing, the Mahabharata is called the 'Pancham Veda'. Calling the Mahabharata as itihasa alone is not necessarily, a complete statement. Seeing that people had difficulty in understanding the Mahabharata, which is also called the "Pancham Veda", Shri Vedavyasa was very sad and it is then he was advised by the sage Narada to write the Bhagavatam, which would be intelligible to all, (of course, barring the scholars like Shri Madhvacharya, who claimed that people would need his help to understand the Bhagavatam).  

  

\(B\) Not for nothing, Shri Vedavyasa cited the 'simili' or 'analogy', that the Original Bhagavad Gita of 745 verses (as was stated in the Mhabharata) as the essence (the milk) of the Upanishads (the cows), extracted by none other than Lord Krishna.  

  

\(C\) Not for nothing, Lord Krishna said in the beginning of the original Bhagavad Gita that the creation has a beginning and an end, and everything in the creation is impermanent.  

  

Best wishes,

Sunil KB
