What I meant is that the Markandeya episode is devised, kalpita, by Veda Vyasa to convey some message which Nilakantha has stated: the teaching of Advaita Brahman. By extension, the Mahabharata is a story to convey several values. So with the Ramayana.

  

In the prasthana traya bhashya Shankara says with regard to the story, for example, of Nachiketa as a Vidya stuti, a euology of the Brahma Vidya taught there. In other words, the story is the medium through which the vedantic teaching is conveyed, similar to the Prana-organs conversation which is devised to convey the supremacy of Prana.

  

आख्यायिका विद्यास्तुतये, Taittiriya

  

ऋषिप्रश्नप्रतिवचनाख्यायिका तु विद्यास्तुतये । Prashnopanishat

  

सैषा आख्यायिका आचारार्थसूचिता विद्यास्तुतये चेह ॥२६ Brihadaranyaka

  

In the Bh.Gita he says: With Arjuna as a nimitta, incidental, Bhagavan gives out the teaching. Finally, the Krishna-Arjuna samvaada is devised by Veda Vyasa.

  

That's how Advaita sees the Veda, Upanishads, the Itihaas, Purana, Smritis, etc.

  

In all cases the story is only a medium for the Teaching. Hence the medium is not real; only incidental.
