Namaste Kalyan ji,

From what I understand, the pralaya referred to in Nasadiya Sukta is greater than the pralaya which occurs at the end of each kalpa (day of Brahma). This is the prakrtika pralaya where even Brahma's lifetime comes to an end and a new Brahma is created in the next creation. Yudhishtira mentions that Markandeya Maharshi is older than everyone except Brahma. Had Markandeya Maharshi lived through Prakrtika Pralaya (which is impossible), he would be older than the current Brahma. Also, Yudhishtira mentions about sleeping and waking of Brahma, indicating that it is a naimittika pralaya (end of kalpa) and not the end of Brahma's lifetime.

  

Best Wishes,

Sridhar
