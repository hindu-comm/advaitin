This is the prakrtika pralaya where even Brahma's lifetime comes to an end and a new Brahma is created in the next creation. Yudhishtira mentions that Markandeya Maharshi is older than everyone except Brahma.



praNAms

Hare Krishna



Let us for the time being keep aside who is older to who etc. Please tell me more about the creation of new brahma, is this not jeeva?? Who gets brahma padavi?? If he as a jeeva getting this new position, on what merit he gets this position?? And what happens to that ‘old’ brahman?? If old brahman after retirement merges in supreme brahman then the new brahman at the beginning must be another jeeva, must have done some meritorious deeds in the previous kalpa to get this brahma padavi, is it not??



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
