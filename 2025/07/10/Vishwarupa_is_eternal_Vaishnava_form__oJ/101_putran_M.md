I just want to note after reading multiple others (Vikram-ji, Chandramouli-ji, Sridhar-ji) mention of lower vs higher pralayas, that would be my ideal way to resolve this contradiction. Where MB is referring to a lower type of pralaya that admits some survival of dualistic cognition and NS is referring to Mahapralaya. But if we insist on both referring to Mahapralaya, then something like above (or Subbu-ji, Sudhanshu-ji, in the highersense) has to be the samanvaya. NS pralaya negates MB type exceptions.
