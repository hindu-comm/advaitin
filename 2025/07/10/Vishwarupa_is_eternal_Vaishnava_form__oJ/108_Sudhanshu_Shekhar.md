Namaste Vikram ji.

  

Upanishad Brahm YogI explains this concept of स्वाज्ञदृष्टि, स्वज्ञदृष्टि and , परमार्थदृष्टि in alAtashAnti prakaraNa of MANDUkya also, verse 87 and 88. Please check.

  

Basically,

  

Vastu + perception = स्वाज्ञदृष्टि

  

~vastu + perception = स्वज्ञदृष्टि

  

~vastu + ~perception = परमार्थदृष्टि

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
