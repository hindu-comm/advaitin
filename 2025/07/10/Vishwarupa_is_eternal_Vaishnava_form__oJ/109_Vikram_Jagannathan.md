Thanks a lot! This is very interesting.

  

Bhagavan Bhashyakara interprets these three states (empirical-dual, empirical-pure, and extra-empirical) as the waking (vastu+perception), dream (~vastu+perception) and deep sleep (~vastu+~perception) respectively.

  

Need some help in understanding Swami UpanishadBrahmaYogi'scommentary. Could you please clarify how these three states are equated to svaajna, svajnaandparamatha drishti respectively?

  

My understanding of Swami UpanishadBrahmaYogi'scommentary is that verse 87 explains the nature of perception from both svaajnadrishti (ajnani drishti) and svajnadrishti (jnani drishti). From / for both these drishtis, there is the duality (waking state) and purity (dream state) depending on the vastu and non-vastu respectively. But he starts verse 88 by correlating the paramartha drishti with non-vastu non-perception deep sleep. How is this done? Paramartha drishti is turya, whichis not the same as deep sleep. But, further, I understand his interpretation that Knowledge (absolute, paramarthika) alone appears as the tri-fold division of empirical-dual, empirical-pure, and extra-empirical.
