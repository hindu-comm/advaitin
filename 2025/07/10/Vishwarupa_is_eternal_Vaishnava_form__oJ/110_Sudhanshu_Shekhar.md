Namaste Vikram ji.

  

Reference Verse 3.87 and 3.88 of MANDUkya kArikA

  

> Bhagavan Bhashyakara interprets these three states (empirical-dual,
> empirical-pure, and extra-empirical) as the waking (vastu+perception),
> dream (~vastu+perception) and deep sleep (~vastu+~perception)
> respectively.

  

True.

  

> Need some help in understanding Swami
> UpanishadBrahmaYogi'scommentary. Could you please clarify how these
> three states are equated to svaajna, svajnaandparamatha drishti
> respectively?

  

I feel these states are utilized as an illustration for these three drishTis. Everyone accepts that in jAgrat, there is vastu as well as perception; in dream, there is no vastu, but perception and that in deep sleep, there is neither vastu nor perception.

  

In swAjna, swajna and paramArtha drishTi, precisely these three are the features. It may not be in the manner the same is present in these states. For e.g. in sushupti, there is neither kArya nor perception of kArya, but kAraNa-avidyA and sAkshi-perception thereof is present. But in so far as kArya is concerned, the illustration of sushupti for paramArtha-drishTi is valid.

  

Similarly, for swapna, everyone agrees that there is no vastu but perception. This is what swajna-drishTi holds. There are two types of swajna-drishTi, one corresponding to SDV (some seen is vyAvahArika, and some seen is prAtibhAsika. All seen is mithyA) and second corresponding to DSV (All seen is prAtibhAsika. All seen is mithyA).

  

Let us see:

  

***यथा*** जागरणपरिदृश्यमानकलनाजातं वस्तुत्वेनोपलब्धिविषयवत् भाति ***तथा*** ***असल्लौकिकादिषड्दृष्टेः*** संप्रति वस्तुना सह वर्तत इति स्वातिरिक्ताविद्यापदतत्कार्यजातं सवस्तु - through this UBY is basically describing how swAjna-drishTi has jAgrat as an illustration. I feel असल्लौकिकादिषड्दृष्टेः refers to swAjna-drishTi.

  

Further, ***यथा*** स्वाप्निकप्रपञ्चोऽवस्त्वपि उपलब्धिविषयो भवति **तथा****असल्लौकिकादिषड्दृष्ट्यतिरिक्त**-***दृष्टिद्वयस्य*** स्वातिरिक्तमवस्त्वपि व्यवहारमात्रोपयोगि व्यावहारिकत्वेन प्रतीतिकालमात्रस्थायिप्रातिभासिकत्वेन चोपलब्धिविषयो भवति । Here, basically swajna-drishTi is being explained as distinct from swAjna-drishTi, which is asat-laukika-Adi-shad-drishTi. So, swajna-drishTi holds the seen to be mithyA. But this mithyA seen can be either vyAvahArika & prAtibhAsika (SDV) or prAtibhAsika (DSV). But both are mithyA seen. However, this drishTi-dvaya is different from swAjna-drishTi. And this drishTi-dvaya has swapna as an illustration because despite there being no vastu, there is perception, just as it happens in dream.

  

Now in order to give illustration for pAramArtika-drishTi, UBY refers to sushupti - पारमार्थिकदृष्टिः ***कीदृशी*** ? इत्यत आह-अवस्त्विति । परमार्थदृष्टेः ***यथा*** स्वापे ग्राह्यप्राहककलनावैरल्यात् तत्प्रपञ्चजातस्यावस्तुत्वेनानुपलब्धि वेषयत्वं ***तथा*** 'स्वातिरिक्तं शशविषाणवदवस्तुत्वेन प्रतीत्यभावात् अनुपलंभं च दृष्टं तत्पारमार्थिकदर्शनम् ।

  

So, please note the recurring usage of words yathA and tathA. This makes clear that jAgrat, swapna and sushupti are illustrations for swAjna, swajna and paramArtha-drishTi. This also explains as to how sushupti is used as illustration for mOksha.

True. But sushupti is used in texts as an illustration for mOksha/paramArtha-drishTi. It is not turIya per se, but an illustration thereof.

  

> But, further, I understand his interpretation that Knowledge
> (absolute, paramarthika) alone appears as the tri-fold division of
> empirical-dual, empirical-pure, and extra-empirical.

  

I checked Anandagiri TIkA - it says -ज्ञानमत्र मनोवृत्तिरूपं विवक्षितम् अवस्थात्रयातिरिक्तमपि परीक्षकपरिकल्पितं ज्ञेयं सम्भवतीत्याशङ्क्याऽऽह – सर्वेति ।

UBY says - एवं लौकिकशुद्धलौकिक लोकोत्तरभेदेन यत् ज्ञायते तत् ज्ञानं शास्त्रजन्यमित्यर्थः ।

  

So, basically, jnAna here refers to shAstra-janya manO-vritti-rUpa-jnAna. Basically, the point is that jneya is only these three. As AchArya says in bhAshya -एवं स्मृतं सोपायं परमार्थतत्त्वं लौकिकं शुद्धलौकिकं लोकोत्तरं च क्रमेण येन ज्ञानेन ज्ञायते, तत् ज्ञानं ज्ञेयम् एतान्येव त्रीणि, एतद्व्यतिरेकेण ज्ञेयानुपपत्तेः.

  

But I don't see this jnAna-jneya as much of importance in swAjna-swajna-paramArtha connection. Please share your view.
