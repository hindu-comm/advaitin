Namaskaram Kalyan ji,

  

Advaita siddhanta includesvarious prakriyas on the concept of creation as part of the student's knowledge development. Parinama vada, Vivarta vada (including SDV & DSV), Ajati vada presents a sequence of understanding. They are all explained through the adhyaropa-apavada method.

  

The absolute reality of the creation is denied in Advaita; the mithyatvamof creation in vyavaharika is not denied.

  

prostrations,

Vikram
