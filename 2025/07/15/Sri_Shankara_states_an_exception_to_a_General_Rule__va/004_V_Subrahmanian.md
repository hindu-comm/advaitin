Namaste

  

The case of Apantaratamas is not one of an exception but still following the rule that the jiva involved will continue to take births till the prarabdha karma is exhausted, despite sparoksha jnana. That sutra is to establish this niyama of Aadhikarika purusha.

  

The case of Markandeya is where not just he is with a gross body but even the other jivas in pralaya are with bodies. This is not the pralaya taught in the Upanishads. While the state of pralaya is technically called avyakta, not identifiable with names and forms (see Eighth chapter of the Gita), the Markandeya case is fully vyakta, not for others, but for Markandeya. Jivas would have exhausted their bodily existence in the previous kalpa and are in a state of laya, without the bodies, and will get a body only when the next creation starts, when their karma has fructified to give them an appropriate body. All this is absent in that episode. And hence alone is not the pralaya of the Vedanta.
