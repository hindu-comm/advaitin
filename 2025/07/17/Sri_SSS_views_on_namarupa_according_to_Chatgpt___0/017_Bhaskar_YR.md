So, you can have two different pramanas simultaneously telling two different knowledge about the same reality; but both must be simultaneously known correctly - lest there is both ignorance and knowledge together?



- So basically your question boils down to : is it possible that
  different pramANa-s can convey opposite features like one-ness and
  multiplicity of the same object / jagat?? I don’t think it is an
  impossible scenario. For that matter why different pramANa-s? even a
  single valid pramANa would suffice to give this different knowledge.
  Just for the example, from perception alone multiplicity of ornaments
  is recognized but when we look at them from the point of view of their
  cause their one-ness is also recognized. Don’t you think it is
  possible ?? Similarly, pratyaksha pramANa will give rise to the
  cognition of multiplicity in the world which is again valid prama
  within its own domain, while the shAstra or shruti pramANa says it is
  all only One Brahman and nothing exists apart from HIM which is again
  another valid prama from another pramANa. I don’t think there is any
  contradiction between the two.
