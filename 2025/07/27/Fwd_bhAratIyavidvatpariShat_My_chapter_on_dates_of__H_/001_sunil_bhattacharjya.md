Dear Subbuji,  

  

Thank you for sending the 'U-tube program' as well as the article on the "Historical Timelines of the Mahabharata and the Ramayana" by Dr. Jayasree Saranathanji

  

Firstly, I am surprised that Jayasreeji is not aware of the convention that 3101 BCE is actually (-)3100 CE or AD. Lord Krishna passed away in 3102 BCE, which is equal to (-)3101 CE or AD. This shows the date of the Mahabharata war found by her is not correct. Further, the eclipse pair which appeared just before the Mahabharata war reappeared 36 years and 2 months later just before the passing away of Lord Krishna. So Jayasreeji will have to rework her dating of the Mahabharata war.  

  

Now coming to the 'U-tube program', it is clear that Jayasreeji is not aware that the Mahabharata told us that a great flood occurred immediately after Lord Krishna's departure from the Earth in 3102 BCE \[i.e.,in (-)3101 AD\]. Mahabharata described that the rivers in India started flowing in the reverse discretion, which was possible only if a Mega Tsunami took place. There is also proof that the Saraswati river submerged to a great depth below the surface of the earth. This was surely due to a global flood. To my knowledge the Noah's global flood also took place around the same time.  

  

Now coming to the discussion on the yugas, one cycle of yuga is equal to 12,000 Solar years. Man uninformed scholars wrongly imagine that one yuga cycle is equal to 12,000 divine years and one divine year is equal to 360 human years.

  

Jayasreeji gave a list of 10 scholars who gave 10 different dates for the Mahabharata war. She was not aware that one Mr. Rao, was the first scholar to claim 3139 BCE to be the year of the Mahabharata war.  

  

Kindly permit me to stop here.

  

Regards

Sunil
