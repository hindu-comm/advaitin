One should definitely know that all material ingredients, activities, time and modes, and the living entities who are meant to enjoy them all, are non different from Vāsudeva, Brahman. They have no existence apart from Brahman, their material and intelligent cause.



praNAms

Hare Krishna



This is what Upanishads too crying at the top of its voice 😊 there exists ‘nothing’ apart from brahman. brahmaivedaM amrutaM purastAt, brahma pashchAt, brahma dashiNataH uttareNa, he is top, bottom, brahmaivedaM vishwamidaM varishTaM…sarvaM khalvidaM brahma, AtmaivedaM sarvaM etc. And in geeta and geeta bhAshya also bhagavatpAda endorses this sarvAtma bhAva or Atmaikatva drushti.
