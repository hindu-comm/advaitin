By singing the glories of Lord Hari, forgetting the body and the senses,  
Becoming one with Him — “I am verily He” — that alone is enough. O Lord Rāma! Rāma! What bliss can be greater than this?



praNAms

Hare Krishna



This is what Upanishad Rishi vAmadeva too realized and exclaimed in his mother’s womb : ahamanurAdityaH, tattvamAdhyAtmikaM drushtvA tattvaM drushtvA tu bAhyataH.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
