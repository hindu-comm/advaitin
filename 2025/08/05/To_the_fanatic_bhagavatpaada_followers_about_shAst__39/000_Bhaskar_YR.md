This is what he says about shAstra pramANa as against anumAna in sUtra bhAshya :



The intuition of brahman is indeed achieved through the ascertainment of the import of vedic ctext and NOT BY OTHER EMPIRICAL means of knowledge like inference (anumAna). (1-1-2)



For this entity is no object of perception because it has no colour and other properties. Nor is it the object of inference and other means for it has no indicatory mark etc. (necessary for those means to function).
This entity can be KNOWN THROUGH AAGAMA ALONE like dharma. ( 2-1-6)



As for shAstra, it is the FINAL PRAMAANA it derives its validity only by pointing out and removing what is not the property of Atman, but not by directly making known what is not known through OTHER MEANS. (geeta 2-18)



Sri SSS observes that : it is for the purpose of teaching that brahman is known only through Agama (shAstra / veda / Upanishad) that the revered bAdarAyaNa has written the aphorism shAstrayOnitvAt (for it has shAstra for its means). And the author of the bhAshya has explained it thus : the meaning is that brahman the cause of the origin etc. of the universe, is knowable through the shAstra pramANa alone.



However ultimately what we have to realize is that all usage of the means and object of knowledge whether ordinary or vedic, proceeds on the presupposition of the adhyAsa of the self and the not-self known by the name of avidyA, so also do the shAstra purporting to lay down injuctions or to prohibit certain acts or to teach release. Atra veda aveda…and till the realization of this jnAna, for the brahma jignaasa shAstra is the ONLY pramANa not the anumaana (not the dry logic in particular).
