Dear All,  
  
It gives me great pleasure to inform you that Guruji is presently teaching  
\*Chitsukhi\* (\*Tattvapradipika\*), one of the most advanced and profound  
texts of Advaita Vedanta. Despite the highly complex nature of this work,  
Guruji is explaining it in a remarkably simple and accessible manner,  
carefully breaking down every technical term.  
  
Those who wish to study \*Chitsukhi\* are warmly encouraged to follow the  
link below, where you will find Guruji’s lectures on \*Tattvapradipika\*. The  
classes are conducted live on YouTube every evening from 6:30 PM to 7:30 PM  
(IST), in Hindi. Viewers proficient in Hindi may benefit greatly, You may  
also address your queries to Guruji in Hindi or Sanskrit during the live  
sessions through the comments section whenever you seek clarification.  
  
\*About Guruji:\*  
Dr. Ramkishor Tripathi is a distinguished scholar, honored with numerous  
titles and awards such as \*Vidyavachaspati, Vedanta Shastra Shiromani,  
Vedanta Martanda, Kashi Vidvat Gaurava, Vidvat Bhushana,\* and the  
prestigious \*Shankara-Vishishta-Vishesh Vidwan Puraskar\* and \*Hariharananda  
Saraswati Puraskar,\* among many others. He has also presided over  
International Vakyartha Sadas in Varanasi.He has also presided over the  
Nāgakūpa Shastrath Sabha in Varanasi, as well as several other Shastrath  
Sabhas.  
  
At present, Guruji serves as the Vice President of the Shree \*Kashi Vidvat  
Parishad\*, and last year he was felicitated by the \*Sringeri Sharada  
Peetham's Jagadguru Swami Vidhusekhar Bharati during Varanasi Shastrath  
Sabha. \*
