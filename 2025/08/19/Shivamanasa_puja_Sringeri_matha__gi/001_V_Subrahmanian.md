[[V Subrahmanian	2025-08-19, 22:21:23 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/git7B4w2_Qc)]]

There is this statement on record: HH Sri Chandrashekhara Bharati Swamin, the 34th Jagadguru once addressed the devotees saying: The engaging in devotional ritualistic worship is not required for us. We do that so that those who come here may not think - when they themselves are not engaging in such worship, why should we?. (He implied that others require that and the Jagadguru shows the way for them to emulate and get elevated.)

  

On another occasion, a devotee, watching the same Acharya doing the Srichakra puja, thought: He is a sannyasin and should be contemplating on the Impersonal Brahman but here he is doing this Devi Aaradhana.
After the Puja, HH of his own accord told this devotee: there is this nāma of the Devi - mithyā-jagat-adhiśṭhāna. Is this not Vedanta?

  

The 35 Jagadguru HH Sri Abhinava Vidyatirtha has said, citing the Bhagavatam, 'even those who are in committed contemplation of the impersonal Brahman, are devoted to Sri Hari with no longing for anything in return (from such devotion), for the Lord is of such an irresistible repository of auspicious qualities.

  

This Acharya has been stated to be so ardent in performing the daily Chandramauli puja (every night) that he would not like to miss it even when not doing well healthwise. So has said his disciple the 36th Jagadguru.

  

There are many many laymen devotees who have taken to formal worship of the deities after having witnessed the Jagadgurus doing the same. They would come back with a newly procured Shiva Linga or Saligrama or Srichakra or any deity image and seek the blessings of the Jagadguru before they commence the worship at home.
