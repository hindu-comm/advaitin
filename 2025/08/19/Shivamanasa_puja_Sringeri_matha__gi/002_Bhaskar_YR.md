Hare Krishna



For whateverreason, my mind started wondering whether shankaracharyas are allowed to or consider it ok to express/have such devotion to deities or Ishvara.



- Why this doubt?? Ishwara anugraha, Acharya upadesha is MUST in
  advaita sampradaya too. Unfortunately these things suffer ONLY in the
  hands of dry logicians who give scant respect to shruti, Acharya and
  Ishwara and they prefer their own intellectual offspring logic over
  this!!



Or whether it is simply symbolic "show" for the sake of public, and whether the acharyas within themselves are negating these Dvaita aspects as mithya.



- No sane/noble person who already realized there is NO water in mirage
  would guide the innocent to fetch the water from mirage. If the
  Ishwara / acharya etc. are just fancy ideas in an ignorant mind and it
  is mithya like snake in rope, why the souls who already realized that
  there is ONLY rope would say : beware of snake!!??



Not sure now as well, but the sthothras and the acharyas themselves doing manasa puja and simultaneously realising Advaita tattva suggest a deep integrated approach that both engages fully in vyavaharika and yet is fully rooted in the knowledge of Brahman.



Ø Yes, this is how it should be. That is the reason why sampradaya insists we need the IshwarAnugraha, shrOtreeya-brahmanishTa guru. This (brahma) jnAna that which we should not try to realize on our own with the aid of mundane dry logic nor by studies of shAstra-s on our own. bhAshyakAra warns us : shAstrajnOpi svAtantryeNa brahmajnAnAnveshaNaM NA KURYAAT… ityetat ‘gurumeva’ ityavadhAraNaphalaM. Yasya deve parA bhaktiH yathA deve tathA gurau, tasmaite kathithA hyarthAH prakAshante mahAtmaNaH shruti further endorses this traditional path which needs to be mandatorily guided by Ishwara and Acharya. Yes these guru, Ishwara, shAstra, shishya, sAdhana etc. etc. are just vikalpa-s in nitya nirvikalpa paramArtha brahman but it does not mean that it is just a fancy imagination of the conditioned soul. It is there in a system it is there in a specific order and it would definitely serve the purpose in the mOksha / jnAna mArga. And in short, AcharyOpadesha and IshwarAnugraha sin qua non in the Advaita jnAna mArga. And it is not only in the pre-jnAna period but in post jnAna period as well 😊 For the paramArtha jnAni these vikalpa-s are nothing but that parabrahman since he does realizes the nirvikalpa behing all these vikalpa-s. IshwarO gururAtmeti mUrtibhedAd vibhAgine, vyOmavad vyApta dehAya dakshiNamurthaye namaH…says famous guru dakshiNamurthy stOtra.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
