Hare Krishna.

  

We know that cloth-A, made of thread-A, is proved to be absent in thread-A. This is done through anumAna as shown in VedAnta ParibhAshA. The anumAna is presented as under:

  

अयं पट एतत्-तन्तुनिष्ठ-अत्यंत-अभाव-प्रतियोगी, पटत्वात्, पटान्तरवत्।

  

Let "अयं पटः" = cloth-A, "एतत्-तन्तु = thread-A".

  

The anumAna uses a vyApti by using all clothes other than cloth-A, which is the paksha.

  

Let us take any cloth (cloth-B) other than cloth-A. That cloth-B is certainly absent in thread-A. So, naturally all clothes (other than cloth-A) are pratiyOgI of atyanta-abhAva in thread-A. So, the vyApti is, यत्र यत्र पटत्वम् (हेतु), तत्र तत्र thread-A- निष्ठ-अत्यन्त-अभाव-प्रतियोगित्वम्(साध्य).

  

Now, cloth-A is paksha. It has hetu i.e. paTatva. So, by the force of anumAna, we are forced to admit that even cloth-A will also be pratiyOgI of thread-A-nishTha-atyanta-abhAva.

  

No matter how ming boggling the conclusion is, the anumAna works harmlessly and forcefully. And is in tune with the vedAnta siddhAnta which holds paTa as mithyA. And therefore, paTa has to be absent in whatever is accepted to be its Ashraya, such as cloth-A.

  

The same anumAna can work effortlessly to show that a ghaTa kept on bhUtala is absent in that very bhUtala. The anumAna will be as under:

  

अयं घटः एतद्भूतलनिष्ठ-अत्यंत-अभाव-प्रतियोगी, घटत्वात्, घटान्तरवत्।

  

The explanation will be as before. When we say एतद्भूतल, we mean that very space where pot-A is kept. No other pot can occupy same space where pot-A is kept.

  

Here, we need to note the following. One can argue as to how both ghaTa and ghaTa-atyanta-abhAva can be located in the very same bhUtala. After all, atyanta-abhAva is virOdhI of pratiyOgI. If pratiyOgI is present, atyanta-abhAva cannot be present and vice versa.

  

It is submitted that atyanta-abhAva is of two types. One, AA-1 which is not contradictory to pratiyOgI and second, AA-2 which is contradictory to pratiyOgI. The atyanta-abhAva which is part of the mithyAtva definition and mithyAtva anumAna is AA-1. Hence, there is no problem. (For more details, please see pages 5-8 of the PDF [https://sudhanshushekhar.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/the-concept-of-atyanta-abhava.pdf](https://sudhanshushekhar.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/the-concept-of-atyanta-abhava.pdf))

  

This clarification on AA-1 and AA-2 can be found in ब्रह्मणः स्वप्रकाशत्वविचारः in Advaita Siddhi.
