A delightful read Subbu ji. Many thanks for the write-up.

  

Especially drawing attention was the following:

  

त्वं तु राजन् मरिष्येति पशुबुद्धिमिमां जहि ।

न जात: प्रागभूतोऽद्य देहवत्त्वं न नङ्‌क्ष्यसि ॥

  

"I will die" truly is the pashu-buddhi. So obviously occuring, but so completely wrong.

  

We start our enquiry based on certain beliefs and certain desires. I believe that I am a finite individual. That I am born. That I will die. That I should make full use of this human birth. Let me attain the timeless. Let me not waste time. Let me attain the eternal bliss. Let there be Atyantaka-nivritti of dukha and prApti of niratishaya-sukha. Etc etc.

  

With these beliefs and desires, I start my enquiry. How to attain mOksha! Why I am in bondage!

  

And enquiry reveals the seen to be illusory. And it shows my own self to be the eternal seer.

  

And then the seen is rendered immaterial. It is seen with the same eye with which one sees the mirage knowing it to be a mirage. One discards it being illusory. One just sees it. One does not fret over it.

  

One sees the sukha with the same eye as one sees the dukha. That both are momentary appearances. No need to find why/how of it. Just see it. There is nothing more to it.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
