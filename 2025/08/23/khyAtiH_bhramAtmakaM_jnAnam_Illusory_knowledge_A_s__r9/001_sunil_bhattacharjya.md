You must be aware that Lord Krishna says in the beginning of the Original Bhagavad Gita, that in the beginning, the world was not there and this world will also not be there at the end. Thus the world is not real, i.e., It is illusory.

  

In the Rudra Yamala, we have the शिव षडक्षर स्तोत्रम् where it says as follows:  

वाहनं वृषभो यस्य वासुकिः कंठभूषणम् ।  
वामे शक्तिधरं देवं वकाराय नमो नमः ॥५॥

  

This refers to Shakti, who is at the left of Lord Shiva and the Shakti is who creates

the world in the beginning and she can give Mukti to her worshippers. Lord Buddha, who said "Esha Dharma Sanatanah", recommended worship of Shakti in the form of "Goddess Tara" for "liberation or Mukti" and the Tibetan Budddhists follow this custom. Adi Shankara worshipped goddess "Lalita or Kamakshi" for the last few years of his life at Kanchipuram and he attained Mukti. This is in short the way to the end of the illusory world.
