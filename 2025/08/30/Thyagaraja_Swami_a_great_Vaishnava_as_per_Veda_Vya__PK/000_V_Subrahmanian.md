In the composition karuNa jUDavammaon Devi of Thiruvārur Sri Thyagaraja says in a charanam:  
  
[https://www.karnatik.com/c2397.shtml](https://www.karnatik.com/c2397.shtml)

  

purANa puruSaDugu rAmuniki yA purArikini  
nIku rAjadhari maccarAna vErEnca rAdu

  

He shows unity between Rama, Shiva and Devi, by telling her that there is no envy and rivalry among themselves.

  

The above was stated by Brahma Sri Balaji Bhagavatar in his discourse on Thyagaraja Vaibhavam.

  

It would be interesting to note that the Padma Puranam, in the Bhagavata 12th Canto Mahātmyam says:

  

भेदं न कुरुतेयस्तु दुर्गाविष्णुशिवादिषु ।

ब्रह्मत्वेनैव जानाति वैष्णवः स परो मतः ॥ ११

He is admitted to be a great Vaiṣṇava who does not differentiate between Durgā, Viṣṇu, Śiva, etc. and looks upon all to be Brahman Itself.
