Dear Subbuji,

  

It is very nice to see that Prof. Srinivasa Varakhedi, VC. CSU, told us in the UTUBE, about the Launching of the ‘Setubandh Vidwan Yojana’.  

  

However, I think it is more important that the dating of the time of Lord Ram is more important than further research on the Setubandh, as nobody has been able to do the dating of Lord Rama's time uptill now.  

  

Secondly, more than four years ago, the Bharatiya Vidvat Parishad (BVP). owned jointly by Prof. Srinivasa Varakhediji, announced a seven member team for the dating of the Mahabharata war, and it will be nice to know the progress of that project. If for any reason, that project has been abandoned, it should be announced propmptly, so that others can also try to date the Mahabharata war.
