> rAga, there is ignorance
>

In Jivanmukti-Viveka also, this issue is taken up. The issue is as under:

  

As per Naiskarmya Siddhi 4.67, rAga indicates absence of tattva-jnAna. "रागो लिङ्गम् अबोधस्य"

  

As per BrihadAraNyaka BhAshya VArtika 1.4.1539, the presence of rAga etc can do no harm (to the jnAnI). "रागादयः सन्तु कामं न तद्भावोऽपराध्यति ।"

  

The apparent contradiction is resolved by postulating the former to refer to sthita-prajna i.e. jIvanmukta, whereas the latter to jnAni who is not a jIvanmukta.

न च अत्र परस्परव्याहति:, स्थितप्रज्ञे ज्ञानिमात्रे च वचनद्वयस्य व्यवस्थापन-उपयुक्तत्वात्।  
  
Then a question arises that if jnAni-mAtra (jnAnI who is not a jIvanmukta) is accepted to be subject to rAga-dvesha etc, then there will be occasion of rebirth for him (which is not possible for jnAni-mAtra).

  

ननु ज्ञानिनो रागादि-अभ्युपगमे धर्म-अधर्म-द्वारेण जन्मान्तर-प्रसङ्ग इति चेत्,

  

The answer is given that the rAga etc for jnAni-mAtra are not mukhya-rAga as they are not preceded (coupled) with avidyA. They are merely rAga-AbhAsa and hence cannot result in rebirth.

  

मैवम्, अदग्धबीजवत् अविद्या-पूर्वक-कामादेः एव मुख्य-रागादि-त्वेन पुनर्जन्महेतुत्वात्। ज्ञानिनः तु दग्धबीजवत् आभासमात्रा एव रागादयः। 

  

This discussion is in second chapter of JMV.

  

This so harmoniously reconciles the concepts.

  

So, with shravaNa-manana-nididhyAsana, tattva-jnAna occurs. And one attains mukti (videha-mukti as per JMV with deha meaning future body).

  

Subsequently with manO-nAsha and vAsanA-kshaya, jIvanmukti happens.

  

rAga-dvesha are completely exterminated post jIvanmukti. Prior to jIvanmukti and post-jnAna, rAga-AbhAsa continue.
