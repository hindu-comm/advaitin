Namaskaram,

  

I am quoting a reply sent by Sunil-ji. This history topic is not for further discussion here (as per an earlier moderators' decision) but decided his objection and counter-position should be allowed this time since the fb post forwards a particular history that Sunil-ji regards controversial.

  

Quote

  

Dear Subbuji,

  

Thank you the mail. Yes the Kudali Sringeri math was started by Adi Shankara. Kudali was known as Bhadratunga from the Mahabharata times and it was a great tirtha.

  

But I can't agree with the following statement:

"In Śriṅgeri, Śrī Śaṅkara Bhagavatpāda established the Dakṣiṇāmnāya Pīṭha and appointed Śrī Sureśwarācārya as its first head. Starting from Śrī Sureśwarācārya, a total of fourty-seven Pithacharyas presided over Śriṅgeri Jagadguru Pīṭha". (Source: Śrī Kūdali Maṭhada Saṅkṣipta Itihāsa: Author Viṣṇu Tīrtha)"

  

The first mathadhipati installed at Kurli by Adi Shankara was Sri Prithvidharacharya, He was also called Hastamalakacharya by Adi Shankara himself. After Staying in Kudali for twelve years, he wished to go to Kachi kamakoti math to worship Mother Lalita and requested Shri Sureswaracharya to relieve him. That is how Sri Sureshvarachrya was the second mathadhipati of Kudali. After a short while in Kudali Sri Sureshwaracharya appointed a new Acharya in Kudali and he went back to Manchikamaoti math.

  

Hope the readers will appreciate these corrections.

Regards

Sunil KB
