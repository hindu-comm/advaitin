Hare Krishna.

  

MithyAtva is variously defined in advaita vedAnta. In this write-up, we will see the definitionof mithyAtva whichis stated as siddhAnta-rahasya-bhUta-lakshaNa. This definitionreveals mithyAtva as per the core siddhAnta of advaita directly. It is discussedin VedAnta-tattva-Viveka of Shri NrisimhAshram.  
  

1.In siddhAnta, the entire world/duality is stated to be absent in Brahman. This is stated in Shruti as नेह नाना अस्ति किञ्चन. This means - there is atyanta-abhAva of world in Brahman.

  

So take any vastu x which is non-Brahman; let us take chair, table, pot. By siddhAnta, there is atyanta-abhAva of this vastu in Brahman. So, for any vastu x, there is atyanta-abhAva of x in Brahman.

  

2\. The atyanta-abhAva of a mithyA-vastu is never different from the adhishThAna. When we say, shuktirUpya-atyanta-abhAva, then it is identical with shukti. Pot-abhAva may not be identical with bhUtala, as pot and bhUtala have bhinna-sattA; but, superimposed vastu does not have bhinna-sattA than adhikaraNa. So, its atyanta-abhAva is identical with adhikaraNa.

  

3\. Since in siddhAnta, world is accepted as mithyA; means it is superimposed in Brahman; means world-atyanta-abhAva has to be identical with Brahman. Now, everything is located in Brahman but Brahman is not located anywhere. Brahman is nirAshraya. Therefore, world-atyanta-abhAva is nirAshraya. It is sarvatra-avritti (not located anywhere).

  

Thus, if there is a vastu x which is mithyA =\> then =\> it will be the pratiyOgI of Brahman because x-atyanta-abhava is Brahman. This further means that x is pratiyOgI of an atyanta-abhAva which is sarvatra-avritti. Thus, x is pratiyOgI of an atyanta-abhAva which is not only not-located in x's adhikaraNa but also in x's non-adhikaraNa.

  

Now, it is a tautology to say that x-atyanta-abhAva is not-located in x's adhikaraNa.

  

So, mithyAtva of x will be established by saying that x is the pratiyOgI of an atyanta-abhAva which is not-located in non-adhikaraNa of x.

  

Thus, siddhAnta-rahasya-bhUta-lakshaNa of mithyAtva is as follows
-***स्व-अनधिकरण-अनधिकरण-अत्यन्ताभाव-प्रतियोगित्वं मिथ्यात्वम्***

**  **

This is essentially saying that mithyAtvam is Brahman-pratiyOgitvam.
