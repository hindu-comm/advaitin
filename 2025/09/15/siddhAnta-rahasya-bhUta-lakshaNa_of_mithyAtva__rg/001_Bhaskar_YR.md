Hare Krishna



MithyAtva is variously defined in advaita vedAnta. In this write-up, we will see the definitionof mithyAtva whichis stated as siddhAnta-rahasya-bhUta-lakshaNa. This definitionreveals mithyAtva as per the core siddhAnta of advaita directly. It is discussedin VedAnta-tattva-Viveka of Shri NrisimhAshram.

- With all due respects to this work, it seems to me this mithyatva
  definition is nothing but pracchanna shUnyavAda.



1.In siddhAnta, the entire world/duality is stated to be absent in Brahman.



- Yes, but brahman is the abhinna nimittopadAna kAraNa for this
  perceived jagat says shruti and bhAshya.



This is stated in Shruti as नेह नाना अस्ति किञ्चन. This means - there is atyanta-abhAva of world in Brahman.



Ø satyanchaanrutaMcha satyamabhavat, yadidaM kiMcha too shruti vAkya.



So take any vastu x which is non-Brahman; let us take chair, table, pot.



- There is nothing that can be called anAtma vastu (non-brahman) when
  one realized sarvaM khalvidaM brahma.



By siddhAnta, there is atyanta-abhAva of this vastu in Brahman.



- By siddhAnta the effect the jagat not different from its kAraNa
  (brahman), with its paraNAmi nityatya, like kAraNa it is ( the jagat,
  the effect) too trikAla abhAditaM.



So, for any vastu x, there is atyanta-abhAva of x in Brahman.



Ø Jagat in its vyAkrutAvyakruta rUpa not different from brahman.
Nowhere it is stated jagat is atyanta abhAva like hare’s horn or son of a barren woman.





2\. The atyanta-abhAva of a mithyA-vastu is never different from the adhishThAna.



- So as per this brahman is also not different from mithyA vastu which
  is also atyanta abhAva !!??



When we say, shuktirUpya-atyanta-abhAva, then it is identical with shukti.



- Jagat in its svarUpa not different from brahman. When one realizes
  the truth behind nAma rUpa (vishesha) pot, pitcher etc. is mrut
  sAmAnya he will come to know that mruttiketyeva satyaM.



Pot-abhAva may not be identical with bhUtala, as pot and bhUtala have bhinna-sattA; but, superimposed vastu does not have bhinna-sattA than adhikaraNa. So, its atyanta-abhAva is identical with adhikaraNa.



Ø It is awkward logic to say the least.



3\. Since in siddhAnta, world is accepted as mithyA; means it is superimposed in Brahman; means world-atyanta-abhAva has to be identical with Brahman.



- First of all note that the jagat is not atyanta abhAva, it is only
  shUnyavAdins say that. In shruti siddhAnta, the jagat is brahman and
  there exists nothing apart from brahman. And to know that brahman is
  prajnAna ghana, we need the Ishwara srushti i.e. nAma rUpa. I am not
  saying this bhAshya itself emphasizing this.



Now, everything is located in Brahman but Brahman is not located anywhere.



- When you are already declared jagat is atyanta abhAva, where did you
  bring ‘everything’?? what is that ‘everything’ when you have already
  concluded jagat is atyanta abhAva like shasha-vishANa!!??



Brahman is nirAshraya. Therefore, world-atyanta-abhAva is nirAshraya. It is sarvatra-avritti (not located anywhere).



Ø Yes brahman is nirguNa, nirvishesha, niravayava but HE IS THE JAGADEKA KAARANAM….says bhAshya.

Ø Your rest of the observation rather logical equations is based on the fundamental error i.e. jagat is atyanta abhAva, so not worthy for any comments.



By the way I am really surprised to see the grand silence by ‘the clapping crowd’ behind you even after reading your logical deduction to prove the shUnyavAda in Advaita vedAnta 😊
