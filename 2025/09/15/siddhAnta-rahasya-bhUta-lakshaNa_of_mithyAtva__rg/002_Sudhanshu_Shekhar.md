Just a quick question -

  

Do you appreciate that:

  

1\. Brahman is jagat-kAraNa====\> is an adhyArOpa. (जन्मादि अस्य यतः)

  

2\. Jagat is absent in Brahman =====\> is apavAda. (नेह नाना अस्ति किञ्चन)

  

Is 1 negated by 2? Do you agree that adhyArOpa is negated by apavAda?

  

Or, do you hold both these statements to be on equal footing having equal force?
