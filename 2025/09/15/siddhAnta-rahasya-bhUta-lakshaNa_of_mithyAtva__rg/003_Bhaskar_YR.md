Do you appreciate that:



1.  Brahman is jagat-kAraNa====\> is an adhyArOpa. (जन्मादि अस्य यतः)



- Before saying anything, you must know that there is a place for
  creation, sustenance, and destruction in shAstra so at the outset you
  must realize that there is a place for jagat, there is a specific
  purpose behind this perceived nAma rUpa of the jagat, one cannot
  easily or with the help of mere dry logic push aside this when these
  things are drenched with wet logic of vedAmruta ( the nectar of
  shruti). Yes we the ignorants, first wrongly think that the jagat is
  independent and brahman is its creator. Starting from this view point
  the shAstra too treats the jagat as upAdhi to brahman and shAstra
  superimposes (adhyArOpita) all transactions of creation, sustenance,
  destruction on Brahman.



2.  Jagat is absent in Brahman =====\> is apavAda. (नेह नाना अस्ति किञ्चन)



- You cannot subjectively choose one shruti rides over another one. The
  same shruti is categorically saying brahman which is nirvishesha is
  also jagadeka kAraNaM. See the methodology of teaching by shruti. At
  the later stage after superimposing the srushti, sthithi and laya to
  brahman, the same shAstra (shruti) a analyses the jagat on the basis
  of the Jagat-Brahman non-difference relation and educates the jignAsu
  that jagat cannot be treated as an upAdhi to brahman because it is not
  different from it at any time, whether the past, the present or in the
  future. Equipped with this ananyatvaM knowledge, looking beyond the
  nAma rUpa the jignAsu will focus his mind only on the unchanging
  brahman (nirvikAri brahman). Through this shAstrOkta analysis,
  mananaM, all the transactions, attributes, and features which were
  superimposed on it due to avidyA (adhyAsa i.e. jagat is different from
  brahman and brahman’s upAdhi is jagat), will drop off. Then, in spite
  of one witnessing the changing world with the Indriyas, one will not
  lose the sight of the antarAtma brahman which is without attributes.
  In this way, dropping of the superimposition made in the beginning
  that is apavAda.



- If you see the definition of Samyak jnAna, Atmaikatwa darshana, Samyak
  drushti, sarvAtma bhAva which are available aplenty in bhAshya you
  will come to know what is adhyArOpa and what is apavAda without
  resorting to shUnyavAda.
