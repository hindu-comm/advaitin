Bhaskar ji Namaste,

  

It's evident from your usage of the word " Sunyavada " so nonchalantly that you lack a fundamental understanding of " Sunyavada " of Madhyamaka (prachina) & seem to confuse it with Asat-Karana vada of the Sarvastivadins & some schools of Vijñānavādins.

To say Sunya of Nagarjuna is Nirguna of Post Śhankarā Advaitins is actually a fun statement because Madhyamaka Sunyavada was not an Asat-Karana Vada Darsana; they accepted Bhava of Paramarthananda which they tagged as Buddha.

  

So mixing Asat-Karana Vada with Nirguna Brahman (alleging PSA to be like AKB) & having in mind Sunyavada of Nagarjuna is seriously problematic.

  

I'd recommend you to first learn what this sunyavada is & how many types of Sunyavadas are there before using these terms so loosely.

  

Regards,

Manuj
