[[Yasoda Jivan dasa	2025-09-21, 10:41:48 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/Id5yQqNFbLw)]]

**Respected Vidvaj-janas,**

**Namo Namah,**

I have heard that **Śrīdhara Svāmī**, the author of *Bhāvārtha Dīpikā*, the well-known commentary on the *Bhāgavata Purāṇa*, is considered by some to be coming from the **Advaita Sampradāya**. However, according to others, he is said to have been in the line of the **Viṣṇu-svāmī Sampradāya**.

I humbly request the learned scholars to kindly share references supporting both perspectives—those which indicate his connection to the Advaita Sampradāya, as well as those suggesting his affiliation with the Viṣṇu-svāmī tradition.
