"Relative reality" is misleading and oxymoronic. Reality cannot be partial or not real - the law of excluded middle - either somethingexists or does not exist. Snake is not a relative reality - it is ropemisperceived. The fear of snakeis a reaction to the misperception of rope but not a reaction caused by the 'relative reality' of an imagined snake. To impose reality upon error or misperception whether 'borrowed' or 'relative' or 'temporary' etc. is a misguided teaching. In that way we need to view vyavaharika. It is only a perception taken to be real.
