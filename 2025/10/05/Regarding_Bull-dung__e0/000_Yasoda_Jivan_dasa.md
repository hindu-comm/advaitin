Respected Vidvaj Janas,

  

I have come across references indicating that cow dung (gomaya) is used in Panchagavya preparations and other purification rituals. I am keen to understand whether there are specific injunctions especially, in the Dharma Shastras regarding the use of bull dung, or if there exist any traditional practices that prescribe its use.

  

Although the term gomaya is often used to refer to both cow dung and bull dung, I have noticed that the scriptures do not seem to provide detailed instructions distinguishing the two. I would be grateful if learned scholars could kindly provide clarity on this matter and guide me regarding scriptural references or traditional practices concerning the use of bull dung in ritual contexts.
