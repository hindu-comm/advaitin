An article titled 'The Vision of the Supreme Being' appeared in the October 2025 issue of "Adhyatma Prakash" monthly magazine.

Author: Sri Sri Sachidanandendra Saraswati Swami, Adhyatma Prakash Karyalaya, Holenarasipur / Bangalore.

  

Is it possible to see the Supreme Lord in His true form? It is described in the Puranas that devotees saw Him with their own eyes in the form of their favorite deity and received His grace. But that form was a temporary form that the Lord assumed to bless the devotees. Similarly, when unrighteousness increased and religion declined, it is written in the histories that the Lord incarnated in forms like Ramakrishna for the upliftment of religion; that too was a temporary form that the Lord assumed from Maya for the benefit of the world. Is it possible to see the Supreme Lord in the form that He has always existed? The Upanishads answer this question as follows:

  

I have seen the house of the Lord, and I have heard the words

M. 3-1-8

  

The eternal form of the Supreme Being has no shape or color. Therefore, He cannot be seen with the eyes. He does not have any special features such as caste, substance, qualities, action, relationship; therefore, it is not possible to express Him by any word. Since there is no sound, touch, form, taste, or smell, He cannot be known by the senses of touch, skin, eyes, tongue, or smell; for this reason, He cannot be contemplated by the mind. There is a scripture that even the heavens can be attained by the actions mentioned in the Vedas, such as the Agnihotra. But

The Supreme Being's supreme form cannot be attained by any action. Because He is not something separate from the aspirant and far away to be attained. It is well-known in the scriptures that many great fruits are attained through penance. But the Supreme Being, who is always ready, cannot be attained through penance. It is not that the sacred actions of sacrifices and penances have no fruit; it is just that it is never possible to attain the Supreme Being directly through them.

  

So what is the means of seeing the Supreme Soul that is different from all these? Someone may ask. The Supreme Soul is the true All-Soul that is deep within all of us. He is different from all the false souls that we mistakenly think of as 'I', 'me', and because He pervades all of them, He is called 'the Supreme Soul'. It is through the experience of that Supreme Soul, which is the form of consciousness, that we come to know the mind and the senses. It is through His influence that the mind and the senses illuminate and show their respective things.

  

Our mind has become polluted by the desires and hatred of external things; that is why, although it has the power to understand the things related to it, it does not have the power to understand even the Supreme Being who is very close. Just as if a mirror is cleaned, or if a still body of water is brought to a standstill, the reflection of the rising sun falls on it, similarly, if the mind is withdrawn from external things and directed inward, and if one concentrates and makes an effort to see the Self, the image of that Supreme Being falls on the mind. Then the mind gives up its own mind and becomes immersed in the Supreme Being and becomes the Supreme Being. Through this meditation, the Supreme Being can be found.

  

The vision of the Supreme Soul is not to know Him by objectifying Him through any object; how, and by whom, can the all-pervading, immaterial, omnipresent, omniscient, be known? When the mind is concentrated and meditated upon, it takes on the form of the Supreme Soul; the unique experience that is then attained is the vision of the Self.
