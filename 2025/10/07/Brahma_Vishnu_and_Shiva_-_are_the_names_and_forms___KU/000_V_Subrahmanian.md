In this verse of this chapter of the Vishnu Puranam, Sage Parashara says that the creation, sustenance and destruction of the universe is performed by Hari through his own forms with those respective names:

# विष्णुपुराणम्/पञ्चमांशः/अध्यायः ३०[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/1ssa](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/1ssa)

  

सर्गस्थितिविनाशानां कर्ता कर्तृपतिर्भवान् ।  
ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवाख्याभिरात्ममूर्तिभिरीश्वर ॥ ५,३०.१० ॥

  

Sridhara Swamin says:

  
ब्रह्मादिसंज्ञाभिः**स्वमूर्त्तिभिः** स्वयमेवसृष्ट्यादीनांकर्त्ता । Brahma, etc. are the names of His own forms.

  

Elsewhere too the Vishnu Purana has said this. Also the Bhagavatam gives the same message: the Trimurtis are the very forms of Vishnu. See a detailed post giving several such references on the Trimurti atyanta abheda and the Turiya Vishnu and Turiya Shiva concepts here:
