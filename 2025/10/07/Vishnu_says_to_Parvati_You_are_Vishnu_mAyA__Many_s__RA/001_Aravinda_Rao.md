A lot of useful information, as usual. I keep reading puranas now and then but I find that the identity of Vishnu maya is very difficult to establish by the study of Puranas. I read puranas from a sociological perspective, to find things which are useful for explaining the present day criticisms on issues such as the status of women, the caste conflicts and many others..
