भारोऽविवेकिनः शास्त्रं भारो ज्ञानं च रागिणः।  
अशान्तस्य मनो भारो भारोऽनात्मविदो वपुः॥  
  
योगवासिष्ठ:

  

Scriptural knowledge is a burden for one who lacks discrimination, spiritual wisdom is a load for a passionate person, the mind is a burden for one who is restless and lacks control and the body itself is a burden to one devoid of knowledge of the Self.  

  

Yogavāsiṣṭha
