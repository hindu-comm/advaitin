I gave a talk on the topic of 'Bheda Khandanam - Refutation of distinctness in khaṇḍana-khaṇda-khādya' in the online symposium conducted by Indica Moksha on Sunday, 24th Aug 2025 at 10.00 AM IST.

  

You can listen to the recording of this talk in YouTube

  

[https://youtu.be/pEBmqhqcstk?si=maFp2CN1M3ZtbgmS](https://youtu.be/pEBmqhqcstk?si=maFp2CN1M3ZtbgmS)

  

In this talk bheda which is distinctness or difference is shown as negated by the Shruti vakyas which reveal advaita. Sense Perception cannot negate the shruti as their validity is only in vyavahAra (empirical transactions) and their range of revealation is limited to finite objects while the Shruti negates bheda in its entirety in all periods of time. It is shown that Perception (pratyaksha), inference (anumāna) and presumption (arthāpatti) cannot reveal bheda as ultimately real. Further it is shown that Self-awareness and Object-awareness cannot reveal bheda implicitly. Further the four definitions of bheda are taken up and shown how they lead to defects like contradiction, mutual dependence, self dependence, circularity and infinite regress. In this manner bheda-khaṇḍanam is done in khaṇḍana-khaṇda-khādya.
