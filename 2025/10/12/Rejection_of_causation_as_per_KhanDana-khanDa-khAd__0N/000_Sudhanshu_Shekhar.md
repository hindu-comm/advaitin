Hari Om,

  

kAraNa-tva is proved to be anirvachanIya in KhanDana-khanDa-khAdya by refuting eight descriptions posed by NyAya. The anirvachanIyatA of kAraNa-tva implies the mithyAtva thereof. And hence all belief in reality of cause-hood/causal character/causation is liable to be ignored being illusory. And one situates as one's own Self.
