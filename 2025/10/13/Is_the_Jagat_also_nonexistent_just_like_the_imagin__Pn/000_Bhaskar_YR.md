Hare Krishna



Is this jagat just like imagined snake in the rope!!?? No!! is the categorical answer from bhAshyakAra in the chAdOgya (6.2.3) because of the simple shruti siddhAnta that for the jagat, parabrahman is the abhinna nimittopadAna kAraNa and hence No. It is only the existent Brahman (the ‘isness’ (the existence) is common between brahman and jagat clarifies bhAshyakAra. So it is existent brahman only appearing as many in Vishesha rUpa in a different way. ***Nothing here is non-existent***

***at any time***. Here only bhAshyakAra talks about the tArkikA-s who imagine a thing different from its kAraNa and speak of it as non-existent before its appearance and after its disappearance. And here bhAshyakAra further clarifies that there is no nAma or rUpa which is different from the cause at any time (vide in sUtra bhAshya also this clarification is there). Though the mrut sAmAnya is called ghata as if it is different from the mrut, we know that it is after all clay only.



And the rope-snake analogy is also not going to prove jagat is not just like imagined snake in rope, rather it proves for seeing the snake (independent jagat) in place of existing rope, rope is the nimitta (rope here is Ishwara srushti and snake is jeeva’s avidyAkalpita srushti-adhyAsa). So, when the rope is examined properly, we would realize that though it appeared like a snake and it was/is/will not be a snake. With this realization we only get rid of the idea of a snake ( the snake which we perceived independently due to adhyAsa). Similarly after examining Brahman we get rid of the idea of Jagat and we also stop referring to it as Jagat and for the paramArtha jnAni it is parabrahman only and for HIM there exists NOTHING apart from brahman. After realization ONLY asarvatvam, abrahmatvam i.e. wrong perception of the jagat goes.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
