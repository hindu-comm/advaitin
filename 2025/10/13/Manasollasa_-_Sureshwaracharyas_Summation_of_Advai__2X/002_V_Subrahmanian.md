Namaste

  

Yes, we hold Veda to havepravāha nityatvam. This too is in vyavahara. In Paramarthika, Brahman alone is there. In fact the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad itself says 'vedā avedāh' - the Veda is no-Veda in deep sleep and by extension in Mukti. So, even as per the Upanishad, the Veda is having only a transactional reality and not absolute reality.
