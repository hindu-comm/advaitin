Even if he himself indicates that he is svayaM vishNu / Ishwara, what is the use!!?? Because, rAma, as a normal human being was eccentric at the loss of his dear ones (the explanation of his abnormal suffering you already read 😊). He was having very fickle mind, even after knowing the chastity and purity of his wife through agnideva, he took a laundry man’s words seriously and sent her to forest again, instead of teaching and educating the dharma of his varNa to Shambuka, he straightaway killed him, killed vAli also hiding himself …Yes there are somany justification / clarification for all these questionable acts. But as you said if we read these things within its context, we are forced to decide rAma is not Ishwara / vishNu he was an ordinary human being suffered like any other normal human being and he is not even a jnAni / sthita pragna as he has not shown any signs of sthitaprajna when something lost which is closed to him. In these incidents rAma hardly showed any lakshaNa of sthita prajna like duHkeshu anudvigna manaH sukheshu vigata spruhaH etc. So there is no valid reason for us to worship him as Ishwara / bhagavaan / vishNu. He was just an ordinary King, suffered a lot all through his life and was not able to withstand it, so he wept / cried / rolled his body on the floor in utter grief and agony. I would always like to read these episodes with pinch of salt and mainly without the help of socalled LOGIC lest rAma and Krishna would lose their sanctity in the sight of logicians.
