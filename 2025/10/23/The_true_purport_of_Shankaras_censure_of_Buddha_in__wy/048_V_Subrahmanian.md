The VSN bhashya of Shankara is quite popular as his work. There are some who say it's not his. One reason they give is: Shankara has cited Hari Hara Trimurti abheda verses from Harivamsha, etc. there and Shankara would not accept anyone other than Vishnu as the saguna Brahman. This is the stand of those non-Advaitins who want to somehow portray Shankara as a Vaishnava. When I ask them 'Did Ramanuja and Madhwa agree to this?', they draw a blank.
