In RamayanaRama did not recognise himself as an avatara and had to be reminded by Brahma ji and Devatas about his divinity in the Yuddhakanda after Sita does Agnipariksha.He says he only considers himself as Rama, the son of Dasharatha and asks Brahma to tell him who he is. There is also the Vedanta Mahavakya uttered here by Brahma ji to Rama. So how do we take all of Rama's utterances before he heard this Mahavakya? How traditionalcommentators have considered this? The learned members of this group can fill in here.

  

Check the below slokas

  
अन्ते चादौ च लोकानाम् दृश्यसे त्वं परन्तप ।  
उपेक्षसे च वैदेहीम् मानुषः पाकृतो यथा ॥ 6.117.10  
You are the only one to be seen before the worlds come into being and after they cease to exist, O scorcher of the foe! Why do you ignore Vaidēhee, like an ordinary man!  
  
इत्युक्तो लोकपालैस्तैः स्वामी लोकस्य राघवः ।  
अब्रवीत्रिदशश्रेष्ठान् रामो धर्मभृतां वरः ॥ 6.117.11  
Thus addressed by the Guardians of the world, Rāghava, the lord of the world and foremost of the patrons of Dharma said to those foremost of the Dēvas:  
  
आत्मानं मानुषं मन्ये रामं दशरथात्मजम् ।  
योऽहं यस्य यतश्चाहम् भगवांस्तद् ब्रवीतु मे ॥ 6.117.12  
I think of himself as Rāma, son of Daṡaratha, a human. Be pleased, O Bhagawan, to reveal to me who I truly am, who I belong to and where I came from!  
  
इति ब्रुवन्तं काकुत्स्थम् ब्रह्मा ब्रह्मविदां वरः ।  
अब्रवीच्छृणु मे राम सत्यं सत्यपराक्रम ॥ 6.117.13  
With Kākutstha imploring thus, Brahma, the foremost of the knowers of Brahman, said, 'Listen O Rāma of true prowess, here is the Truth!'  
  
भवान्नारायणो देवः श्रीमांश्चक्रायुधो विभुः ।  
एकशृङ्गो वराहस्त्वम् भूतभव्यसपत्नजित् ॥ 6.117.14  
You are the blessed lord Nārāyaṇa, the Dēva who wields a discuss as a weapon. You are the primordial Boar with single tusk. You are the vanquisher of foes, past and future.  
  
अक्षरं ब्रह्म सत्यं च मध्ये चान्ते च राघव ।  
लोकानां त्वं परो धर्मो विष्वक्सेनश्चतुर्भुजः ॥ 6.117.15  

You, O Rāghava, are the true Brahman that does not perish in the Middle or at the End. You are the ultimate Dharma of all the worlds. You, of four arms, are the chief of defense forces everywhere.

  

Interestingly Totakacharya also uses this episode of Rama in Shruti Saara Samuddharanam to answer the poorvapaksha - how can one already be brahman and still require the teaching 'You are that Brahman'

  

द्वयमप्यविरोधि शरीरभृतो वचनीयमिदं रघुनन्दनवत् ।  
उपदेशमपेक्ष्य सदाऽऽत्ममतिः परमात्मसतत्त्वकता च सदा ॥ ८५ ॥

  

dvayamapyavirodhi śarīrabhṛto vacanīyamidaṃ raghunandanavat ।  
upadeśamapekṣya sadā''tmamatiḥ paramātmasatattvakatā ca sadā ॥ 85 ॥  
  
It has to be said that with regard to the embodied self even both these do not present a contradiction, namely that the knowledge of the oneself as Brahman is a matter requiring instruction, and the fact that one is always identical to the supreme self. It is analogous to the situation of Raghunandana (Sri Rama)
