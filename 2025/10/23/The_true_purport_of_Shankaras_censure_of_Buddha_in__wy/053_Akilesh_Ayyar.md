Or perhaps we might consider that the external actions of someone and their outward reactionscannot possibly indicate their inner status as jnani or not.

  

Just as a jnani'sso-called body may seem from an outside perspective to get cancer -- caused by vasanas -- the jnani's so-called mind may also suffer from seeming bouts of anger, lust, greed, or seeming to care about the world's approval/disapproval, etc. These too would be caused by vasanas.

  

Neither of these say anything about the jnani's true status.

  

Akilesh Ayyar
