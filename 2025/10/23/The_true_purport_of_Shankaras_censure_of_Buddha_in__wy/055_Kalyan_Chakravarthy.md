Namaste Sri Sudhanshu ji

  

If someone claims that Sri Shankara accepts Buddha as an avatAra of Vishnu - since this is a positive claim and since both Buddha and Vishnu are topics in Shankara bhAshyas - the burden of proof is on the claimant to establish his or her claim.

  

With that background -

  

\>Non-mention of x by Shankara in his bhAshya" = p,

\>"Mention of x by Shankara in his bhAshya" = ~p,

\>"Non-acceptance of x by Shankara" = q,

\>"Acceptance of x by Shankara" = ~q.

\>I asked you a question as to how can a person with common sense hold that p =\> q.

  

Please note, p implies q is not my position.

  

Please note that not q implies not p is also not my position.

  

I only say that not p is an evidence for not q.

  

In case of p, the other party cannot claim not q.

  

Hope this helps.

  

Best Regards

Kalyan
