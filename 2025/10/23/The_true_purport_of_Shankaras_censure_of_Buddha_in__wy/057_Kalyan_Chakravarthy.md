I probably might end this discussion here with this message.

  

\>That has been demonstrated through acceptance of Shankara of PurANAs as pramANa, which in turn accept BuddhAvatAra. It is your insistence that acceptance of BuddhAvatAra by Shankara requires mention thereof in the bhashya.

  

1\) Sri Shankara rarely quoted purANas.

2\) Several purANas openly contradict each other on many topics.

3\) It is not known whether the purANas existed in the same form at the time of Sri Shankara, that they exist today.

4\) It is not known whether Sri Shankara accepted each and every statement in all the purANas that existed during his time.

5\) Sri Shankara's focus is more on prasthAna traya, not purANas.

  

Due to all these uncertainties, no conclusion can be made that Sri Shankara accepted buddhAvatAra.

  

If you go by probabilistic reasoning, chances are, he did not accept Buddha as an avatAra of Vishnu.

  

Please feel free to believe that my position is illogical or I lack common sense.

  

I am perfectly fine with it and I try not to take it personally.

  

It was nice discussing this topic with you. Sri KrishnArpaNam.
