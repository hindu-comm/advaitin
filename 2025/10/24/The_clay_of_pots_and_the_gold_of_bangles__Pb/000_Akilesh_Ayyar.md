[[Akilesh Ayyar	2025-10-24, 12:25:52 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/PbF0ue-f0c4)]]

Pots are made of clay, but are also made of space that is not clay.  
  

Bangles are made of gold, but are also made of space that is not gold.

  

If space were also made of clay or of gold, where then would pots and bangles be?

  

Akilesh Ayyar
