Bali in Indonesia is a Hindu country and has much of our culture

This month oct 2025 there is a writers and Readers festival to be held at Ubud which is an art city in Bali.The theme for the festival is Aham Brahmasmi.

  

Aham Brahmasmi" is the theme for the 2025[Ubud Writers & Readers Festival](https://ubudwritersfestival.com/news/festival-unveils-the-2025-theme-aham-brahmasmi-i-am-the-universe), which translates from Sanskrit to "I am the Universe".The festival will explore themes of the connection between humanity and the cosmos, the coexistence of wisdom and innovation, and other global issues through a series of talks, discussions, and performances from October 29 to November 2, 2025, in Ubud, Bali.
