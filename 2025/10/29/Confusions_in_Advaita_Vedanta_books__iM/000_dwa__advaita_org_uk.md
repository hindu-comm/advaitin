This is just to let members know that the Indian publisher of this series has kindly agreed to allow me to publish the books on Kindle. The first (of three) volume – Knowledge, Experience and Enlightenment – is now available and can be purchased from any of the Amazon stores. The Indian link is [https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0FXVCQ5PT](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0FXVCQ5PT) and the price is ₹ 449.



The publishing blurb is as follows:



\<\<\<

Many modern presentations of Advaita Vedānta blur the clear distinctions carefully drawn by Śaṅkara and the tradition that follows him. Confusions in Advaita Vedānta examines the most persistent misunderstandings about knowledge (jñāna), experience (anubhava), and enlightenment (mokṣa), showing how they arise and how the scriptures actually resolve them.  
  
Drawing on the prasthāna traya—the Upaniṣads, Bhagavad Gītā, and Brahma Sūtra—and the commentaries of Śaṅkara, Dennis Waite clarifies subtle points that often lead sincere seekers astray. He shows that self-knowledge, not mystical experience or mental quietude, is the true means to liberation, and that traditional Advaita is a coherent and rigorous teaching method rather than a set of experiences or insights.  
  
Written in plain language but grounded in śruti and śaṅkarabhāṣya, this book is intended for readers who already have some familiarity with Advaita and wish to deepen their understanding by resolving doubts and misconceptions.
