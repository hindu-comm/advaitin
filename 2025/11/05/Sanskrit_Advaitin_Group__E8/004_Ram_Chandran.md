It is good to start a group in Sanskrit for advaita Philosophy. In the beginning, it may be worthwhile to provide English translation (and/or English summary) which will motivate more to learn Sanskrit. By keeping a group without translation, you are taking multiple challenges - (1) fewer participants and (2) fewer messages because posters are quite shy to expose their weakness. By providing translations, you are motivating more to learn the Divine Sanskrit langluage.

Good Luck and wish you the very best,
