*Namo Vaḥ*

  

Here is an introduction to the must-read book “The Essential Sanātana Dharma” (Bengaluru: Prekshaa Pratishtana, 2023) by two erudite scholars:*Śatāvadhānī* Dr R. Ganesh and Hari Ravikumar. The book is a much-needed, concise introduction to Sanātana Dharma, or Hinduism, based on primary sources. This is one of the best English books on Hinduism that I have come across. The authors have accomplished the admirable feat of distilling the knowledge and wisdom of thousands of years and presentingits essence in a small book, which will take a few days to read but many years to absorb.
