Dear Putranji,

  

Jesus Christ belonged to the 'ESSENE' sect of the jews, and the people of this sect were concerned about knowing the relation, which they had with GOD. At the age of 12 years Jesus visited India, as an Indian text says so and an Indian emperor met Jesus in the Himalayas. Not being a brahman himself, probably Jesus could not approach a brahmin guru, and instead he could have been taught by the Buddhist teachers of his time. That could have convinced him that God has created all the human beings and God is the father of all human beings, including him. After 19 years of stay in India, he went back to his homeland and he taught this great truth to his people. However, some priests of his time did not approve his teachings and they wanted to have Jesus crucified. However, by the grace of God, Jesus was saved, and only a look-alike person was crucified.  

  

Best wishes,
