Kindly let me clarify - my observation was not directed at the Vedanta texts themselves, but at the inferences, interpretations, and conclusions that have been drawn from them through various refined pedagogical traditions over the past few centuries.

My comments are directed toward the historical interpretations, including those found in the Smritis and Sutras that has shaped this disconnect.
