Namaste:

Your assertion that the majority practice of**Sanatana Dharma (Hinduism) does injustice to the teachings of Advaita Vedanta**
reflects the viewpoint held by some critics based mostly on misunderstanding. The argument that rituals and idol worship in modern practice diverges from Advaita Vedanta has no validity. These practices are not necessarily contradictory to the path of Advaita Vedanta. If we consider the Truth of Advaita Vedanta is at the top of the mountain, then there can be many paths to reach the top and ultimately those who pursue will likely reach the non-dualistic truth of Advaita. This entire topic is quite complex and will require very careful of all scriptures preferably through a Guru. We need to be quit cautious before making such assertions based on what little we know. I am glad that you have started asking the right questions and get clarifications.

In this group, we want to explore the diversity of beliefs and try to find the unity within the diversity. I hope this clarification is helpful,

Warm regards,
