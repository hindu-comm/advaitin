Namaste,

  

There are two small corrections in my previous post, in the para which reads:

  

Yes, the way Sanathana Dharma is**practiced today by the majority**does gross injustice to (not only to the teachings of Advaita Vedanta) but to the teachings of the entirety of Sanathana Dharma itself. This is because the**majority practices have deviated from the shastras**and not because the inferences, interpretations and conclusions of the traditional pedagogical based on them have deviated from (the practices that lead to the goal of) Advaita Vedanta.

  

\(i\) The paratheses in the first sentence may be ignored, and (ii) the word 'pedagogical' in the last sentence may be read as 'pedagogies'.

  

The corrected para is as follows:

  

Yes, the way Sanathana Dharma is**practiced today by the majority**does gross injustice to not only to the teachings of Advaita Vedanta but to the teachings of the entirety of Sanathana Dharma itself. This is because the**majority practices have deviated from the shastras**and not because the inferences, interpretations and conclusions of the traditional pedagogies based on them have deviated from (the practices that lead to the goal of) Advaita Vedanta.
