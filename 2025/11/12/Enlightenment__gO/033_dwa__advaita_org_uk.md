A very clear explanation as usual, but I would like to make a slight amendment to what you have written to avoid potential confusion.



You said: “The teaching of Advaita consists of two things: (i) The articulation of the Truth of Advaita, and (ii) the sadhana required to attain knowledge of that Truth as an '**ImmediatelyExperienced Truth*',***referred to as**aparoksha-jnana**.” I would like to reword and simplify this slightly: “The teaching of Advaita consists of two things: (i) The articulation of the Truth of Advaita, and (ii) the sadhana required to attain certain knowledge of that Truth”.



Two reasons for this. 1) The reference to aparokSha j~nAna is an unnecessary complication. 2) Enlightenment has nothing to do with ‘experience’. Certainty of the truth of the mahAvAkya-s that you mention is what is meant by ‘Enlightenment’.
