Now my doubt is : per tradition, to become brahmanishTa, shrotreeyatva is indispensable is it not?? Coz. for the brahma jnAna vedAnta vAkya shravaNa, mananaM, nidhidhyAsanaM are direct means as shruti is the untya pramANa. So if we want to identify someone as brahmanishTa he has to be shrOtreeya in his current janma,
if not, in his previous janma/janma-s. So brahmanishTa without the aid of shAstra adhyayana / vedAnta vAkya shravaNAdi sAdhana is not acceptable atleast within the tradition. Hence, to maintain this rigid stand of tradition, we argue that brahmaNishTa-s like ramaNa etc. might have completed shAstra sAdhana in their previous life/lives and that sAdhana fructified in this janma etc. So, can we say each and every brahma jnAni must be shrotreeya as well either in his current janma or previous janma-s. Or can we say there might be some brahmanishTa-s without any backdrop of traditional shrOtreeyatvaM?? Answer for this question perhaps ‘yes’ as for the vedAnta adhyayana / shravaNa traditionally only dvija-s are allowed but for the brahma jnAna all are allowed through ‘other’ means!!
