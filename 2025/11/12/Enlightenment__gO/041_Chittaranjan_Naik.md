*So if we want to identify someone as brahmanishTa he has to be shrOtreeya in his current janma, if not, in his previous janma/janma-s.*

  

Yes, since it is said that knowledge of Brahman can be obtained only from the Upanishads, this seems to be the only way we can reconcile what we see in the world with what is said in the scriptures.

  

*  *

*Hence, to maintain this rigid stand of tradition, we argue that brahmaNishTa-s like ramaNa etc. might have completed shAstra sAdhana in their previous life/lives and that sAdhana fructified in this janma etc. So, can we say each and every brahma jnAni must be shrotreeya as well either in his current janma or previous janma-s. Or can we say there might be some brahmanishTa-s without any backdrop of traditional shrOtreeyatvaM?? Answer for this question perhaps ‘yes’ as for the vedAnta adhyayana / shravaNa traditionally only dvija-s are allowed but for the brahma jnAna all are allowed through ‘other’ means!! Please share your observations.*

  

I'm afraid my view will be based on a kind of speculation. I think that as far as jivanmuktas who attain mukti in this very life are concerned, we can conclude that they must have been shrotriyas in some previous janma but with regard to other brahmanishtas, I tend to think that they are on the krama-mukti route and that if they haven't studied the sruti so far, they will be led to study it in either a future birth or in another loka after shedding this body through the Grace of Ishvara before they attain final mukti. Just my thoughts on the topic - I am of course open to correction.
