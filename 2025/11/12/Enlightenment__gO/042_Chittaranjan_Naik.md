*I forgot to add here that how the schools like bhAmati would argue that the knowledge of scripture should be validated by ‘experience’ based on shankara’s bhAshya wherein bhAshyakAra says : brahmajignAsAyAm kim tu shrutyaadaya, ‘anubhavaavasAnatvaat, bhUtavastuvishayatvAccha brahma jnAnasya, unlike in dharma jignAsa wherein ONLY shAstra is the pramANa. As per this bhAshya vAkya, they argue that : the knowledge of brahman i.e. ultimate reality has to be culminated in “one’s own experience”. And no need to add here that in later post shankara works, interpretations like this, given way to advocate the importance of practical ‘samAdhi’ experience to validate what is learnt in shAstra.*

  

I do not think the Bhamati Chatuhsutri is arguing that knowledge of scripture should be validated by 'experience'. It is arguing that knowledge of Shruti Mahavakhya obtains only when there is immediate cognition of the identity of 'thou' and 'That' on hearing the Mahavakhya. And it goes on to argue that if it does not result in such an immediate cognition, or it results only in a mediate cognition, then further contemplation on the Mahavalkya is needed until a particular kind of vrittiof the internal organ(I suppose the reference is to akhandakaravritti) is generated having Brahman for its content. And the author argues furthermore that what this vritti brings about is illumination of Brahman Itself through Sabda or Verbal Testimony. I know that this is a point of contention between the Bhamati school and the Vivarana school (and the SSS school which seems to be aligned with the Vivarana school on this point), but I don't see so much a problem here.I remember having discussed this point with Sri Sastri-ji a long time ago on this list when it was a Yahoogroup.**
