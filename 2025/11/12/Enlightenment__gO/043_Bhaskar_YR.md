I do not think the Bhamati Chatuhsutri is arguing that knowledge of scripture should be validated by 'experience'.



- But in the hierarchical order of Advaita sAdhana of SMN, as per
  bhAmati, shravana and mananaM means dhAraNa nidhidhyAsana means
  dhyAna and darshana/bramasAkshAtkAra/AtmasAkshAtkAra/akhandAkAra
  vrutti (materialization or actualization of the ultimate reality) is
  the samAdhi where there is NO sAkshAtkAra of prapancha. I reckon it
  shows how bhAmatikAra tried to bring-in patanjali’s ashtanga yOga and
  asaMprajnAta samAdhi within the frame of Advaita sAdhana. 



It is arguing that knowledge of Shruti Mahavakhya obtains only when there is immediate cognition of the identity of 'thou' and 'That' on hearing the Mahavakhya. And it goes on to argue that if it does not result in such an immediate cognition, or it results only in a mediate cognition, then further contemplation on the Mahavalkya is needed until a particular kind of vrittiof the internal organ(I suppose the reference is to akhandakaravritti) is generated having Brahman for its content. And the author argues furthermore that what this vritti brings about is illumination of Brahman Itself through Sabda or Verbal Testimony.



- Yes that is true even as per bhAmati avidyA is destroyed by vidyA.
  But when defining the svarUpa of vidyA which removes the avidyA, they
  say that vidyA is a particular antaHkaraNa vrutti in the form of
  adviteeya brahma sAkshAtkAra, it not only removes avidyA BUT also
  shows up the brahmasvarUpa..and whenever there is brahmasAkshAtkAra
  there is an absence of sAkshAtkAra of prapancha, hence special
  insistence to samAdhi experience. And this vidyA cannot remove the
  prArabdha karma although it removeds anArabdha karma (saNchita/unbegun
  karma). And once the prArabdha karma is completely exhausted then
  ONLY mukti accurues. mOksha according to this school to attain brahma
  svarUpa alone which is Paramanandaghana. And this attainment and
  establishing in that truth needs the absence of sAkshAtkAra of jagat.



I know that this is a point of contention between the Bhamati school and the Vivarana school (and the SSS school which seems to be aligned with the Vivarana school on this point),



- And vivaraNa too if I am not wrong argues that the vidyA is brahman
  which is nishprapancha is itself I am…is itself vidyA happens through
  vedAnta vAkya shravaNa..but jnAna needs to be practiced over and over
  again till the aparOksha jnAna. And for seeing the world, they say
  this jnAna though removes the avidyA, the avidyA remains in the form
  of saMskAra therefore duality also may be seen and on certain
  occasions in asaMprajnAta samAdhi / nirvikalpa samAdhi there accrues
  cognition of Atmaikatva which is testimony to verify the shAstra
  vAkya janita Atmaikatva.
