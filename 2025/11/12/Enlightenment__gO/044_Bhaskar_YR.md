I'm afraid my view will be based on a kind of speculation. I think that as far as jivanmuktas who attain mukti in this very life are concerned, we can conclude that they must have been shrotriyas in some previous janma but with regard to other brahmanishtas, I tend to think that they are on the krama-mukti route and that if they haven't studied the sruti so far, they will be led to study it in either a future birth or in another loka after shedding this body through the Grace of Ishvara before they attain final mukti. Just my thoughts on the topic - I am of course open to correction. 



Ø With regard to your second observation, may I ask little elaboration, brahmanishTa-s who are not Jeevan mukta-s (paramArtha jnAni through the sAdhana of shAstra SMN) but on the route of krama mukti. In the krama mukti like sAlOkya, sAmeepya, sArUpya and sAyujya is there any scope for ‘further sAdhana’ in hiraNya Garbha lOka?? or after crossing all these stages the Ishwara grants them the paramArtha jnAna after mahApraLaya by default?? Krama mukti is the result of upAsana if I am right, so, can we say brahmanishTa type of jnAni-s (not Jeevan mukta-s) are jnAni-s different from jnAni-s who realized through shAstra adyayana??
