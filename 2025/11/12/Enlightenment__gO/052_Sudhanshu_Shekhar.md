> > One can know this world and still realize it as nothing but (mithya)
> > appearance in (adhyasa on) "all-pervading" nondual Light (Sun).
>
>   
>
> This does not contradict Sarvam Khalu Idam Brahma.

  

Not only does it not contradict, rather, as per Advaita Vedanta, this indeed is the meaning of "sarvam khalvidam Brahma". This is what is called bAdhAyAm-sAmAnAdhikaraNya. To say that "sarvam is Brahman" means "sarvam is not, only Brahman is". It is like saying "snake is rope" which means "snake is not, only rope is".

  

This is what BhAshyakAra also tells - ‘सर्वं ब्रह्म’ इति तु सामानाधिकरण्यं प्रपञ्चप्रविलापनार्थम् , न अनेकरसताप्रतिपादनार्थम् (BSB 1.3.1).
