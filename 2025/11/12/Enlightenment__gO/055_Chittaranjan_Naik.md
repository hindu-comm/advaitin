*With regard to your second observation, may I ask little elaboration, brahmanishTa-s who are not Jeevan mukta-s (paramArtha jnAni through the sAdhana of shAstra SMN) but on the route of krama mukti. In the krama mukti like sAlOkya, sAmeepya, sArUpya and sAyujya is there any scope for ‘further sAdhana’ in hiraNya Garbha lOka?? or after crossing all these stages the Ishwara grants them the paramArtha jnAna after mahApraLaya by default?? *

  

Does not Shankara himself say in the Sutra-bhashya that those who attain mukti through the krama-mukti path attain absorption with Brahman at the end of the Mahakalpa? So, I think it is fair to conclude that after fall of the body, if there is anything further to be done by these jnanis to attain the final and ultimate Truth, then Ishwara Himself will lead their way through His Grace. And I believe there are passages in the bhashya which say that sadhana in other lokas is possible. I may be corrected if I am wrong.

  

*  *

*Krama mukti is the result of upAsana if I am right, so, can we say brahmanishTa type of jnAni-s (not Jeevan mukta-s) are jnAni-s different from jnAni-s who realized through shAstra adyayana?? Can we say upAsana phala ( the fruits of upAsana) is also brahmanishTata??*

  

I am inclined to think that those sadhakas who cannot get rid of the sense of agency would naturally fall into the path of upasana. The realization of the knowledge of Advaita (which is not attained by performance of karma or through mental effort like meditation) requires the sadhaka to realize his atman as being free of agency, and such realization is not possible for all. I believe those who get excluded in this way fall into the path of upasana and consequently into the path of krama-mukti. But such a path is also shastra adyayana since it is also the subject matter of the Aranyaka part of the Vedas. Would I be too generous if I said that some people who were born in other religions in their past births and are now born into Vedic dharma, or those who have studied Vedic shastra in their past births and are now born into other religions, should also be included among those who are treading the path of mukti? :-)
