praNAms Sri Putran prabhuji

Hare Krishna



One can know this world and still realize it as nothing but (mithya) appearance in (adhyasa on) "all-pervading" nondual Light (Sun).



This does not contradict Sarvam Khalu Idam Brahma. Connecting to recent topic on vachyartha vs lakshyartha raised by Sudhanshu-ji, one can be aware of vachyartha (duality) at the level of appearance and still have realization of Brahman as the nondual lakshyartha.



- To derive either vAchyArtha or lakshyArtha we have to have something
  on hand and as per the theistic approach of Advaita that something is
  Ishwara srushti for which brahman is the abhinna nimittOpadAna
  kAraNam. Yes, when we say, see there is a pot fetch me water from
  it, it is seeing the pot and utilizing it for the transactional
  purpose exercising its vAkyArtha but keeping its lakshyArtha that it
  is always clay ONLY though we are calling it as ‘pot’. This
  lakshyArtha (mruttketyeva satyaM) does not come in my way as obstacle
  to do the vyavahAra or using the vAchyArtha or calling the clay (mrut
  sAmAnya/lakshyArtha) with nAma rUpa pot (mrut vishesha / vAchyArtha)
  . After all it is Ishwara srushti and it is there for some definite
  purpose. By the way, bhAshyakAra himself says to know/realize that
  brahman is nirvishesha we have to have the nAma rUpa vishesha
  otherwise it would have not been possible to realize that truth.



- But having said that we are not telling brahman is multi designed
  product as we have been saying kArya is kAraNa but kAraNa is not kArya
  ( bangle is gold but gold cannot be described as bangle). So IOW,
  this should not be argued that brahman is associated with the
  vagaries of the world of effects and that therefore one has to
  understand oneself also as such. Though there is a sense of
  non-difference in this paramArtha jnAni’s sarvAtma bhAva of “all this
  is me”, notice that ‘all this/sarvam’ implies difference and ‘is me’
  implies non-difference. This non-difference is sometimes described as
  non-difference with tolerance of difference. (bedha Sahishnu abedha).
  But point to be noted here that brahman does not tolerate any
  difference as there is absolutely no trace of difference in the
  svarUpa of brahman. Shruti asserts ‘this was all one sat only without
  a second, sadevasoumya edam agra aasit ekaM eva adviteeyaM/neha
  nAnAsti kiMchana etc. It is in this sense bhAshyakAra says : sarvaM
  brahma eti samanAdhikAraNyaM prapancha pravilApanArthaM etc. Again
  effect is not different from cause but cause is always free from
  effect. The shruti tells this without any ambiguity he who
  understands himself as brahman will become all this!! Ya evaM veda
  ahaM brahmAsmeeti sa edam sarvaM bhavati. It is not saying sarvaM
  needs to be eradicated completely to know / realize he is brahman but
  it is saying he becomes ‘all’ (like knowing pot, jug, pitcher etc. all
  are ONLY clay and there exists nothing but clay).



- Prapancha pravilayaM needs to be understood in this context without
  disturbing the ‘Astika drushti’ of Advaita that is Ishwara and Ishwara
  srushti/jagat for which he himself is material and efficient cause.
  And the world which is deemed

different from the brahman/Atman is non-existent like rabbits horns or like snake in the rope. But the

- world in front of us is not atyanta abhAva like that. But it is also
  true that not paramArtha satya like the nirvishesha brahman either.
  brahman is the transcendental truth and the jagat in front is
  transactional/ empirical truth (vyAvahArika ‘satya’). Since there is
  no transaction at all in the brahman, this is to be sublimated for
  nirvishesha svarUpa jnAna. But sublimation of Jagat does not mean its
  whole-sale destruction nor to argue it is atyanta abhAva like
  shasha-vishANa, so as to make it inaccessible to jnAni-s during post
  realization. bhAshyakAra makes some interesting comments on ‘What is
  sublimation of Jagat?. He asks, is it like melting the solidified
  ghee by contact with fire? No; because such a sublimation can
  effectively be done by the parameshwara at the time of ‘mahApralaya’
  only. It cannot be done by any mortal. Seeing even in prapancha
  pravilayam / sublimation of world, how bhAshyakAra maintains the
  divine approach of shruti/Advaita without ignoring the importance of
  srushti / sthiti / layakarta Ishwara, and clarifying that such a
  sublimation cannot be advised to mortals!! Not only that; if such a
  sublimation has been done by one who has attained jnAna already by
  now, then the world should not be existing for us to explore but
  alas!! We are still seeing and experiencing it that means despite
  there are jnAni-s and their realization the Ishwara srusuti/jagat is
  still existing!!



- Bottom line is sarvaM khalvidaM brahma shruti vAkya driving us to the
  Ishwaraastitva Advaita Vedanta where sarvaM is brahman but brahman in
  its svarUpa is nirvishesha. Brahma vidya neither create nor destroy
  an object, it only gives us the knowledge that whatever is ‘existing’
  and that existence is nothing but that ekameva adviteeya satyam.



- Only nireeshwara / naastika vAdins would argue that Ishwara his
  srushti etc. is just an illusion in your mind by using shruti bAhira
  shushka tarka.
