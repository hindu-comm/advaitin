Dear Puran-ji,



I never suggested that there is ‘lack of knowledge in the Self’. Indeed, ‘knowledge’ does not really apply to the Self at all, since there is nothing else to know! The ‘lack of knowledge’ belongs to the intellect of the jIva. The wrong ideas and confusion have to be resolved by a qualified teacher so that the intellect realizes that ‘I am Brahman’. We call these wrong ideas and confusion ‘ignorance’ for the sake of talking about them. That the input of the ‘right ideas’ brings enlightenment shows that they were initially lacking.



Best wishes,

Dennis
