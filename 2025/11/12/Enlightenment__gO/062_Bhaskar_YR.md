- This again comes to the notorious topic : locus of avidyA 😊 shankara
  very clearly says it is antaHkaraNa dOsha and it is through
  antaHkaraNa only we have to get rid of this avidyA / adhyAsa. But
  some smart people who want to find the avidyA in brahman (brahmAshrita
  avidyA) would ask : what is this antaHkaraNa!!?? It is mUlAvidyA
  which is the mother of tUlAvidyA (an individual specific avidyA).



The wrong ideas and confusion have to be resolved by a qualified teacher so that the intellect realizes that ‘I am Brahman’. We call these wrong ideas and confusion ‘ignorance’ for the sake of talking about them. That the input of the ‘right ideas’ brings enlightenment shows that they were initially lacking.



Ø Yes there is no vidyA-avidyA transaction in pure brahman, it is at the jeeva level these things entertained and suggested for the teaching purpose. When this jeeva realizes that he is verily Ishwara / brahman then to NO ONE there avidyA / ajnAna whatsoever. jnAna abhAva (lack of knowledge) anyathAgrahaNa ( adhyAsa / misconception) saMshaya (doubt) are just antaHkaraNa dOsha as per bhAshyakAra.
