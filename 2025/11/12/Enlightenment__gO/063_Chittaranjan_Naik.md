Dear Sri Prabhu-ji,

  

*But my question is after attaining the krama mukti at any stage the upAsaka has to once again do the sAdhana for the jnAna mArga or by the grace of Ishwara (upAsya devata) by default he would get ultimate jnAna after pralaya?? That is not clear to me and I have not come across anything like this in bhAshya.*

  

By sadhana I did not necessarily mean something to be pursued actively, it can also mean allowing the fruit of past karma to run out. Maybe the word 'sadhana' was used a bit loosely. It is said in the Sutra-bhashya that the jnani who has attained 'release' on the path of krama-mukti may, after the fall of the earthly body, still have a (subtle) body; and that he still retains some desires, some of which he can fulfil merely through his will, such as manifesting multiple bodies, contacting his forefathers, etc. Since such jnanis still have a sense of agency, it means that they have not attained complete identity with Brahman. In that sense, we may consider that they still have some sadhana to do for attaining that identiity. In any case, such jnanis do not return to this world again - the bhashya to the last sutra (IV.iv.22) says "On the final dissolution of the world of the conditioned Brahman, they attain along with the Lord of that world what is higher than that conditioned Brahman (IV.iii.10)" which I understand to be 'final release' obtained through the immediate realization of the complete identity of the individual-atman and Brahman.
