Sir, please read Naishkarmya Siddhi with Klesha-apahAriNI TIkA, page 266 and 267, where SSSS ji has accepted that AtmA is both the Ashraya and vishaya of ajnAna. He has followed what SureshwarAchArya said. I am giving the link of SSSS ji's words -

  

[https://adhyatmaprakasha.org/php/bookreader/templates/book.php?type=sanskrit&book_id=017&pagenum=0000bx#page/336/mode/1up](https://adhyatmaprakasha.org/php/bookreader/templates/book.php?type=sanskrit&book_id=017&pagenum=0000bx#page/336/mode/1up)

  

SSSS ji says - आत्मन एवाज्ञानित्वम्, अज्ञानविषयत्वं च... \[It is only to AtmA alone that ajnAna belongs (Ashraya of ajnAna), and it is only to AtmA that vishaya-tva of ajnAna belongs (vishaya of ajnAna).  
  
He further says while proving as to how AtmA is the Ashraya of ajnAna
-आत्मनोऽज्ञानाश्रयत्वोपपत्तिः .....न केवलं गत्यन्तराभावात् परिशिष्ट आत्मैवाश्रयत्वेन विषयत्वेन चाश्रीयते किं तूपष्टम्भकहेतुसद्भावाच्चेत्येतद्दर्शयितुमारभते तस्येत्यादिना ।

  

Now, it is up to you to sit down and reconcile as to how SSSS ji does not contradict himself if he elsewhere holds that AtmA is not the Ashraya and vishaya of ajnAna.
