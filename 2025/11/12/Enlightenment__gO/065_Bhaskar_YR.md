Anyway, it is not so difficult to understand in which manner Sri SSS implies Atman is the Vishaya and Ashraya of avidyA especially when bhAshyakAra himself categorically stated avidyA is in day to day transaction is karaNa dOsha. Anyway let me try to explain this with an example : If I say Sri Sudhanshu prabhuji is master in logic and very intelligent logician and no one can beat him logically…what does it mean?? I am pointing my finger to the person called sudhAnshu (particularly his physical body and knowing his academics) and explaining he is intelligent. Does it mean you intelligence have ashraya and Vishaya in yourself or in your intellect?? Sudhanshu as Chaitanya is always one and the same when he is toddler or what he is now..So your karaNa trained in a specialized way resulting in gaining some special intelligence and forcing us to call you as a scholar, is it not?? Like jewelry filled box called as jewelry itself or visha mishrita anna as visha. So these Atma Ashrita avidyA and subject matter of avidyA etc. need to be understood in this way. If you don’t do that then you are striving to realize Atman who himself giving the shelter to avidyA who himself the subject matter of that same avidyA…this is simply ridiculous conclusion considering Atman was / is / always will be nirlipta and Nirmala. So you can be rest assured whenever it is said Atman it is said in this sense that sOpAdhika Atma in jeeva svarUpa having these problem in the karaNa / upAdhi.



Sri SSS elsewhere observes that to whom is avidyA about which matter or things is there avidyA etc. do not arise in the Advaita Vedanta. For the queries to whom, about which matter have to arise only in dvaita where duality having the sway and the whole gamut of dvaita is within the sphere of adhyAsa and adhyasta (adhyArOpita or misconceived). If someone asked to whom is avidyA about which thing there is avidyA definitely he/she not understood the profound truth of this ekatva siddhAnta. And these people are under the impression and assumed that due to avidyA dvaita / duality has come into existence by means of vidyA we have to eliminate this avidyA then only Advaita gets established etc. And for these “avivekins” bhAshyakAra given the answers like : avidyA to YOU who is asking this question, avidyA is karaNa dOsha and it is not the problem of Chaitanya, and jeeva as the Chaitanya is the custodian of avidyA due to his association with upAdhi etc.



Hope I don’t have to give the sutra bhAshya, geeta bhAshya, taitireeya bhAshya quotes to prove this. In short all these issues like avidyA the Vishaya of avidyA the adhishtAnaM of avidyA etc. are in truth conjured up or protected by avidyA itself holds water only when jeeva is encompassed by upAdhi-s and in the absolute truth there is no Ashraya whatsoever for avidyA, for the purpose of carrying out the AtmAnAtmaviveka bhAshyakAra has expressed his opinion that : since avidyA is a pratyaya it is just antaHkaraNa dharma. ( see geeta bhAshya 13-2 where bhAshyakAra gives the example of cataract).
