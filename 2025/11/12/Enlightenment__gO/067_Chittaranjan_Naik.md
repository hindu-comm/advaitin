Dear Sri Dennis-ji,

  

*In my understanding, there is something 'to be done'. For Brahman gets to be tasted through the akhandakaravritti. As to what is to be done to bring it about, if the intellectual understanding is correct, it is nidhidhyasana.*

  

Just to prevent any misunderstanding, let me clarify that the immediate cognition of Brahman follows from the hearing of the shruti-vakhya, and manana and nidhidhyasana are required only to remove internal obstructions - the knots of the heart, so to speak - in the event that the hearing of the shruti vakhyas does not result in immediate cogntion of the Truth. 

  

Warm regards,

Chittaranjan
