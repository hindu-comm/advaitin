Not only AtmA is stated to be Ashraya and vishaya of ajnAna, it has also been proved that anAtmA is not and cannot be either the Ashraya or vishaya of ajnAna \[**anAtmA is not ajnAna-Ashraya (page 264 of Kaleshha-apahAriNI), anAtmA is not ajnAna-vishaya (page 266)**.\]. So, holding antah-karaNa as Ashraya of ajnAna is ruled out by SSSS ji as antah-karaNa is anAtmA.
