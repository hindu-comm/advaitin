Namaste Sri Dennis-ji,

  

*But nidhidhyasana is strictly not some thingto be donefor it is not a kind of meditation or contemplation; it is more a kind of renunciation in which one abides as Brahman and renounces everything else so that everything extraneous to It drops off just as water falls off the petals of a lotus flower.*

  

I would say Nidhidhyasana is the essence of Sannyasa. And Shankara in the Gita Bhashya (V.6) has quoted the Taittiriya Upanishad to equate Nyasa with Brahman Itself.

  

"What is called Nyasa is Brahman, and Brahman is verily the Great."
(Tai. Up. 4-78)

  

Warm regards,

Chittaranjan
