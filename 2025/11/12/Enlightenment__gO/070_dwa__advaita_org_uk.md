Dear Chitta-ji,



Obviously I am aware of these sort of arguments, and I concede that it is the view probably held by most seekers. However, no one has ever explained what is meant by being ‘in immediate cognition of Brahman’, as opposed to ‘appreciating what it means intellectually’. Since the reality is Brahman and is non-dual, reason tells me that we can NEVER cognize Brahman. As jIva-s, the best we can hope for is the intellectual conviction. As Brahman, there is no need (or possibility) to do anything. Śaṅkara’s commentaries make it clear (to me) that knowledge alone is all that is required; there is no need for any subsequent ‘action’. The akhaṇḍākāra vṛtti takes place with the final realization of the truth of the teaching.



The format/practice/purpose of nididhyAsana is another topic.
