Dear Sri Dennis-ji,

  

*Since the reality is Brahman and is non-dual, reason tells me that we can NEVER cognize Brahman.*

  

Brahman can never be cognized as an object of cognition but it is always self-cognized because the Self is Sva-Prakasha, Self-Illuminating. But this is not evident due to the presence of an obstruction, in the form of an underlying sleep known as mula-avidya or anadi-bija-nidra, which conceals the self-effulgence. Now, to discuss this topic further would take us straight into a discussion of bhava-rupa-avidya / mula-avidya which I think both of want to avoid at this stage. So I think it is best to stop here. Thank you, this has been a good and stimulating discussion.

  

Warm regards,
