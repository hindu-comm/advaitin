Namaskaramseveryone. Thank you for the engaging and insightful exchange.  
  
I captured some notes for myself from the discussion so far; Have intentionally left out the Sanskrit words and jargon, and have also omitted your individual names, kindly bear with that.  

- There was a distinction made between intellectual grasp and the direct
  cognition in the premises of the assertion “I am *Brahman*” as the
  ultimate truth.
- The discussion also questioned whether this realization is purely
  intellectual or requires something beyond reasoning.
- The goal is to transcend “I am *Brahman*” - not as a concept but as
  the sole reality.
- Ignorance is best understood as lack of correct knowledge, and not a
  substantive entity.
- Knowledge does not arise anew; it only removes misconceptions and
  reveals the ever-present Self.
- The ONLY process that works is SMN, and the realization is non-dual
  which is beyond intellectual constructs.
- The world is NOT negated but understood as *mithya* - an appearance in
  nondual Brahman.
- Intellectual clarity and direct cognition are complementary stages,
  not mutually exclusive.
- *Brahman* can never be cognized or understood.

Hari Om,  
Harish
