Namaskaram Dennis-ji,

  

> I never suggested that there is ‘lack of knowledge in the Self’.
> Indeed, ‘knowledge’ **does not really apply** to the Self at all,
> since there is nothing else to know!



(imu) It does not apply to the paramarthika/turiya standpoint.

  

In the vyavaharika standpoint (when there is any reference back to duality, world, "knowledge"), the Self is realized as the Ground/adhishtanam, Source/Cause, Knower/Dreamer of all knowledge. The Self is Existence-Consciousness, Ishvara, in this case.

  

Knowledge of all kinds apply to the Self only because it is the sole Existence, Reality, and their adhishtanam. The Self is the Knower and the Self is the known. The only caveat for advaita is that any particular objective identification, call it 'knowledge' or 'world', applies as an adhyasa or imagination of the Self. And if the question of "what is cause?" is raised, there is no ambivalence: the Self alone visualizes into manifestation/cognition Its Knowledge, by recourse to Its intrinsic shakti.

  

One jiva and its avidya and another jiva and its vidya are all limited cognitions of His knowledge in His dream, are manifestations of His Maya. And ishvara as karmaphaladaata is another part of the dream only. Ishvara (capital I) is the Reality so long as they are referred to; and when their reference is dropped, it is the Turiya beyond all duality.

  

> The ‘lack of knowledge’ belongs to the intellect of the jIva.

  

As I said above, "jiva" and "its avidya" are part of the dream-adhyasa of Self by Self. It all reduces to His/My knowledge, cognition. I am the nondual Self, the Knower of all knowledge.
