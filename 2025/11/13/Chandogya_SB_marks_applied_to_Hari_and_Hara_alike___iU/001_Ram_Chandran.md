Namaskar:  
Appaya Dikshitar has composed this Harihara Stuti. Harihara is the divine form combining Shiva and Vishnu as two halves in the same body.
The symbolism of this form is to convey the idea of Abheda (non-difference) between both the deities, stressing that they both are forms of Para Brahman, The Absolute
