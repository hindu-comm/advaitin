I’ll treat “post-Śaṅkara Advaita” here mainly as the Vivaraṇa–Bhāmatī complex and its living sampradāya descendants (incl. the “mūlāvidyā” mainstream), and contrast that with ****SSSS’s reconstruction of “pure Śaṅkara”**** from the ****prasthāna-traya bhāṣyas + Upadeśa Sāhasrī + Gauḍapāda Kārikās****.

Below is a ****structured list of the main axes of difference****. “Exhaustive” in the sense of themes; within each theme there’s a lot of literature, but these are the big joints.

  

## 1. What Advaita is fundamentally about

****Post-Śaṅkara mainstream****

- Systematizes Advaita as a ****metaphysical doctrine****: Brahman plus
  an ontological “ignorance/māyā” principle explaining world-appearance
  and bondage.([Hindupedia, the Hindu
  Encyclopedia](https://hindupedia.com/en/Post-%C5%9Aa%E1%B9%85kara_advaita?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Post-Śaṅkara advaita"))
- Often presents Advaita as a ****cosmological theory of one reality and
  its inexplicable projection**** (Brahman + māyā = Īśvara; Īśvara
  creates the world, etc.).

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Reads Śaṅkara as giving ****primarily an epistemological /
  adhyāsa-based analysis****: bondage is mistaken knowing (adhyāsa), not
  a real metaphysical “thing” called
  avidyā.([](https://www.advaita.org.uk/discourses/chittaranjan/summary_chittaranjan.htm?utm_source=chatgpt.com "A Realist view of Advaita - Summmary")[advaita.org.uk](https://advaita.org.uk))
- Advaita is essentially the ****negation of superimposition**** by
  right knowledge, not a theory about how an ontological power produces
  the world.

  

## 2. Nature of avidyā / ajñāna

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Avidyā (or māyā) is typically described as a ****bhāvarūpa,
  beginningless, positive but anirvācya entity****—a “power” or śakti
  that veils and projects (āvaraṇa–vikṣepa).([Hindupedia, the Hindu
  Encyclopedia](https://hindupedia.com/en/Post-%C5%9Aa%E1%B9%85kara_advaita?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Post-Śaṅkara advaita"))
- It is the ****material cause**** (upādāna-kāraṇa) of nāma-rūpa /
  jagat; synonyms: māyā, avyakta, avyākṛta, prakṛti, śakti,
  etc.([Hindupedia, the Hindu
  Encyclopedia](https://hindupedia.com/en/Post-%C5%9Aa%E1%B9%85kara_advaita?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Post-Śaṅkara advaita"))

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Rejects any “avidyā” as independent ****ontological stuff****; insists
  it is ****only epistemic error****—a privation/failure of right
  discrimination (viveka) plus the superimposition it
  licenses.([Academia](https://www.academia.edu/74345102/The_M%C5%ABl%C4%81vidy%C4%81_Controversy_Among_Advaita_Ved%C4%81ntins_was_%C5%9Aan_kara_Himself_Responsible?utm_source=chatgpt.com "(PDF) The Mūlāvidyā Controversy Among Advaita Vedāntins"))
- Equates “avidyā = adhyāsa” as one phenomenon described in two ways,
  not cause and effect. Any causal talk (avidyā as “cause” of adhyāsa)
  is taken as ****not Śaṅkara’s**** but
  post-Śaṅkara.([](https://adhyatmaprakasha.org/php/circulars/reportmar17.php?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Adhyatma Prakash Karyalaya")[adhyatmaprakasha.org](https://adhyatmaprakasha.org))

  

## 3. Mūlāvidyā vs “no root ignorance”

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Posits a ****mūlāvidyā**** (root ignorance) distinct from individual
  misperceptions; universal, beginningless, sometimes distinguished from
  tūlā-vidyā (individual
  ignorance).([](https://www.advaita-vedanta.org/archives/advaita-l/2011-November/029800.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com "[Advaita-l] Fwd: On avidyA being anirvachanIya etc")[advaita-vedanta.org](https://advaita-vedanta.org))
- Often says mūlāvidyā may ****persist in some sense**** as long as
  vyavahāra continues, even for the jñānī (with many internal nuances).

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Denies any mūlāvidyā as “thing-like” at all; calls it a
  ****post-Śaṅkara construction**** foreign to Śaṅkara’s
  texts.([Academia](https://www.academia.edu/74345102/The_M%C5%ABl%C4%81vidy%C4%81_Controversy_Among_Advaita_Ved%C4%81ntins_was_%C5%9Aan_kara_Himself_Responsible?utm_source=chatgpt.com "(PDF) The Mūlāvidyā Controversy Among Advaita Vedāntins"))
- For Śaṅkara (as SSSS reads him), ****all “ignorance” is just the fact
  of adhyāsa****; when adhyāsa is sublated there is no residue called
  mūlāvidyā.

  

## 4. Locus and content of avidyā

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- ****Bhāmatī****: jīva is locus of avidyā; Brahman is content (object)
  of avidyā.([Sanskrit
  Documents](https://sanskritdocuments.org/sites/snsastri/bhamati-vivarana-diff.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "points of difference between Bhamati and Vivarana schools"))
- ****Vivaraṇa****: Brahman is both ****locus and content**** of
  avidyā.([Sanskrit
  Documents](https://sanskritdocuments.org/sites/snsastri/bhamati-vivarana-diff.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "points of difference between Bhamati and Vivarana schools"))
- There is extensive debate about whether avidyā is one or many, how it
  relates to each jīva, etc.([Hinduism Stack
  Exchange](https://hinduism.stackexchange.com/questions/31692/what-are-the-philosophical-differences-between-the-vivarana-and-bhamati-schools?utm_source=chatgpt.com "What are the philosophical differences between ..."))

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Treats this entire ****“locus/content of avidyā” debate as
  unnecessary**** and non-Śaṅkara.
- For Śaṅkara, on SSSS’s view, the only sensible “locus”-talk is:
- - adhyāsa appears in the ****buddhi/antaḥkaraṇa**** of the empirical
    individual,
  - but is ****ultimately not real**** and ****not an ontological
    predicate of Brahman****.
- So there is no separate “entity” needing locus/content; only
  ****mistaken cognition**** that has Brahman as its ultimately sublated
  adhiṣṭhāna.

  

## 5. Two powers: āvaraṇa / vikṣepa

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Avidyā/māyā is described as having ****two functional powers****:
- - ****āvaraṇa-śakti****: veils Brahman from the jīva.
  - ****vikṣepa-śakti****: projects the manifold
    universe.([](https://www.advaita-vedanta.org/archives/advaita-l/2011-November/029800.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com "[Advaita-l] Fwd: On avidyA being anirvachanIya etc")[advaita-vedanta.org](https://advaita-vedanta.org))

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Rejects these as ****later schematizations****; Śaṅkara, he argues,
  ****never**** builds a two-śakti ontological theory of ignorance.
- For him, “veiling” and “projection” are just ****two descriptive
  aspects of one mistake**** (adhyāsa): failure to discriminate and
  consequent mis-seeing, not two powers of a substance-like avidyā.

  

## 6. Māyā as ontological principle vs adhyāsa-language

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Māyā is a ****cosmic principle****—Īśvara’s upādhi or śakti; often
  equated with mūlāvidyā, prakṛti, etc.([Hindupedia, the Hindu
  Encyclopedia](https://hindupedia.com/en/Post-%C5%9Aa%E1%B9%85kara_advaita?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Post-Śaṅkara advaita"))
- Used heavily in cosmological explanation: how Īśvara “creates”,
  sustains, and resolves the universe.

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Sharply distinguishes Śaṅkara’s ****sparingly used**** “māyā”
  metaphors from post-Śaṅkara’s systematized ****Māyā-tattva****.
- Emphasizes that Śaṅkara’s primary technical term is ****adhyāsa****,
  while “māyā” is ****largely figurative or upacāra**** – a way of
  speaking about inexplicable appearance, not a quasi-thing.

  

## 7. Khyāti (theory of error) and anirvācya-status

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Leans on a full-fledged ****anirvachanīya-khyāti**** theory: the
  erroneous object (e.g. snake in the rope) is anirvācya (neither sat
  nor asat), thus “ontologically” supported by
  avidyā.([](https://www.sivanandaonline.org/?cmd=displaysection&section_id=804&utm_source=chatgpt.com "The Six Khyatis : Theories of Error in Indian Philosophy")[sivanandaonline.org](https://sivanandaonline.org))
- This gets woven into a ****three-level reality**** hierarchy
  (pāramārthika / vyāvahārika / prātibhāsika) with more explicit
  technicality than Śaṅkara.

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Accepts that Śaṅkara treats the erroneous object as ****sublated
  appearance****, but resists building a ****heavy ontological
  machinery**** on top of this.
- Adhyāsa-bhāṣya is read as giving a ****simple misidentification****
  model (one thing taken as another) rather than a detailed metaphysical
  doctrine of anirvācya entities.

  

## 8. Brahma-jñāna: vṛtti or not?

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Develops the doctrine of ****akhaṇḍākāra-vṛtti****:
- - Śravaṇa etc. generate a special mental modification whose ākāra is
    undifferentiated Brahman,
  - That vṛtti “destroys ignorance” and ****illumines
    Brahman****.([WHO](https://who.rocq.inria.fr/Ramakrishna.Upadrasta/Advaita/PHIL_PAPERS/Arun_Murthi_Mulavidya_Controversy.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The Mu¯la¯vidya¯ Controversy Among Advaita Veda¯ntins"))
- Brahma-jñāna is thus often treated as a ****particular mental
  event**** (though different from ordinary cognition).

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Insists Śaṅkara ****never**** teaches any special “Brahman-shaped
  vṛtti”.
- For him:
- - The ****buddhi**** is the locus where the ****removal of error****
    occurs,
  - But ****Brahman/Ātman is self-revealing (svayaṃ-prakāśa)**** and
    ****never an object**** constituted by a vṛtti.
- “Knowledge” is simply ****the cessation of adhyāsa**** through
  understanding the mahāvākyas, not the arising of some positive special
  vṛtti.

  

## 9. Role of śravaṇa–manana–nididhyāsana

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Tends to treat ****śravaṇa, manana, nididhyāsana as three distinct,
  sometimes sequential sādhanas****, often giving nididhyāsana a
  quasi-yogic, contemplative flavor.([Hinduism Stack
  Exchange](https://hinduism.stackexchange.com/questions/31692/what-are-the-philosophical-differences-between-the-vivarana-and-bhamati-schools?utm_source=chatgpt.com "What are the philosophical differences between ..."))
- Bhāmatī often emphasizes ****nididhyāsana****; Vivaraṇa more
  ****śravaṇa****—yet both maintain a multi-step process beyond bare
  hearing.

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Common SSSS reading:
- - ****Śravaṇa**** of the mahāvākya from guru is ****the direct
    means**** to knowledge;
  - ****Manana/nididhyāsana**** are ****auxiliary clarifications /
    stabilizations****, not separate independent means producing a new
    kind of knowledge.
- Strong resistance to making nididhyāsana into a ****separate
  meditative path**** like Pātañjala Yoga; it remains
  ****Vedānta-vichāra-based assimilation****, not an independent
  pramāṇa.

  

## 10. Status of the world and perception after knowledge

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Usually says:
- - The jñānī continues to ****perceive the world**** and transact, but
    with the firm cognition that it is ****mithyā****.
  - Vyavahāra continues by ****prārabdha-karman****; mūlāvidyā is said
    to be gone, but upādhi and prārabdha account for continued
    experience.

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara (as you’ve been emphasizing)****

- Reads Śaṅkara as making duality ****entirely dependent on
  adhyāsa/avidyā**** – once that is removed, ****strictly speaking there
  is no duality left at all****.
- Bādhita-anuvṛtti (continued appearance of the bādhyas) is handled
  ****very carefully****:
- - SSSS tends to minimize any suggestion that ****real perception of a
    real world**** persists after total avidyā-nāśa,
  - Instead stresses that from the ****standpoint of truth****, there is
    only Brahman; any talk of continued appearance belongs to the
    ****teaching level**** (adhyāropa).

  

## 11. Jīvanmukti, prārabdha, and “continuing body”

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Develops a detailed theory:
- - ****Jīvanmukti****: ignorance destroyed, but prārabdha keeps the
    body going;
  - The jñānī still has ****mind, senses, and experiences****, but is
    internally free.
- A lot of explanatory machinery around ****how prārabdha survives
  brahma-jñāna****.

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Accepts the ****language**** of prārabdha-karman and jīvanmukti as
  found in Śaṅkara,
- But interprets it as largely ****pedagogical / concessive****: a way
  to talk at the vyavahāra level while insisting that ****from the
  realized standpoint there was never bondage at all****.
- Strongly guards against any hint that a ****real bondage–real
  prārabdha–real persistence**** of ignorance remains.

  

## 12. Cosmology: sṛṣṭi-dṛṣṭi vs dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi etc.

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Gives more systematic cosmology: pañcīkaraṇa, hiraṇyagarbha,
  īśvara-sṛṣṭi vs jīva-sṛṣṭi, etc., frequently leaning toward
  ****sṛṣṭi-dṛṣṭi-vāda**** (first creation, then perception) with later
  developments of ****eka-jīva-vāda, dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi-vāda**** in some
  sub-traditions.([Internet Encyclopedia of
  Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/advaita-vedanta/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Advaita Vedanta"))

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Reads Śaṅkara’s cosmology as ****purely provisional (adhyāropa)****—a
  teaching device that collapses entirely with apavāda.
- Emphasizes that ****Śaṅkara’s core is not cosmology but
  adhyāsa-analysis and its sublation****; any commitment to “how the
  world really comes out of Brahman” is sidelined.

  

## 13. Levels of reality

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- ****Formally articulates**** three levels:
- - Pāramārthika (absolute)
  - Vyāvahārika (empirical)
  - Prātibhāsika (illusory)
- Builds extensive doctrinal work on this triad.

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Does not deny that Śaṅkara distinguishes ****truth vs appearance****
  and sometimes gradations,
- But insists Śaṅkara doesn’t ****systematize a three-level ontology****
  in the later technical way; that’s a post-Śaṅkara elaboration.
- For SSSS it’s basically ****two standpoints****:
- - adhyāsa-ridden (vyavahāra),
  - adhyāsa-free (paramārtha).

  

## 14. Hermeneutic method: adhyāropa–apavāda

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Accepts adhyāropa–apavāda in principle, but often ****mixes
  “provisional” (adhyāropa) layers into the final doctrine**** (e.g.
  keeping a robust māyā-tattva even after apavāda).

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Makes ****adhyāropa–apavāda the master key**** to reading Śaṅkara:
- - Anything that seems to give ontological status to māyā, avidyā,
    sṛṣṭi, etc. is treated as ****adhyāropa****.
  - The ****final apavāda**** leaves only:
  - - Brahman as the sole reality,
    - and bondage as nothing but wrongly superimposed individuality
      which is ****entirely sublated**** by knowledge.

  

## 15. Use of Nyāya / dialectics

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Sub-commentators significantly ****expand dialectical
  argumentation****: sophisticated anvaya-vyatireka, detailed
  refutations of rival schools, etc.
- Sometimes ****harmonize Śaṅkara with Maṇḍana Miśra**** and others,
  smoothing real
  tensions.([](https://www.advaita-vedanta.org/avhp/bhavir.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Bhamati and Vivarana Schools")[advaita-vedanta.org](https://advaita-vedanta.org))

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Uses logic, but primarily to highlight ****where, in his view,
  sub-commentators have overridden Śaṅkara’s explicit
  statements****—especially around avidyā and
  adhyāsa.([Sankaracharya](https://www.sankaracharya.org/library/WhenceAdhyasa.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Whence Adhyasa?"))
- Insists that ****Śruti as taught in Śaṅkara’s own bhāṣya**** has
  priority over any later scholastic harmonizations.

  

## 16. Upāsanā, yoga, and other sādhanas

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Often gives ****greater independent weight to upāsanā, dhyāna, and
  yogic practices**** as ****separate means**** (with their own phala:
  citta-śuddhi, upāsaka-loka, etc.).
- The interface with Pātañjala Yoga can become quite tight, with some
  seeing ****nirvikalpa-samādhi**** as crucial.

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Takes Śaṅkara’s ****karma-yoga, upāsanā, and meditation**** as
  ****purely preparatory**** for ****śravaṇa-based brahma-jñāna****.
- Rejects making any ****non-śruti-vichāra samādhi**** an independent
  pramāṇa for brahma-jñāna; that is, no samādhi-centric “yoga-vedānta”
  synthesis as doctrinally necessary.

  

## 17. Reading of key Śaṅkara works

This is more meta, but important:

****Post-Śaṅkara****

- Treats Śaṅkara’s bhāṣyas largely ****through the lens**** of the
  sub-commentators; their interpretations become almost
  canonical.([WHO](https://who.rocq.inria.fr/Ramakrishna.Upadrasta/Advaita/PHIL_PAPERS/Arun_Murthi_Mulavidya_Controversy.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The Mu¯la¯vidya¯ Controversy Among Advaita Veda¯ntins"))
- For example, passages that could be read as purely epistemic about
  avidyā are read as implying a ****root ontological ignorance****.

****SSSS / strict-Śaṅkara****

- Makes ****Adhyāsa-bhāṣya**** the interpretive spine of all the
  prasthāna-traya.
- Reads ****Upadeśa Sāhasrī and Gauḍapāda Kārikās**** strictly in
  harmony with that adhyāsa-model, and uses them to ****correct****
  post-Śaṅkara readings wherever they posit an extra “mūlāvidyā–tattva”.
