Dear Subbuji,

  

Two and a half millenia ago, Adi Shankara never heard the term 'Prasthana-traya', as that term came to use only two millenia after the times of Adi Shankara. Don't you think so?

  

Even in about one and a quarter millennia ago, the term 'Prasthana- traya' was not used in the times of Abhinava Shankara.  

  

I am sure, the Advaitin scholars, who are concerned with truths, must be baffled by the use of term 'Prasthana-traya', in connection with Adi Shankara and Abhinava Shankara, as these two past Advaitin-stalwarts were themselves not familiar that term 'prasthana-trata'.

  

Any comment or advice to the Advaitins in general?

  

Regards.

SKB
