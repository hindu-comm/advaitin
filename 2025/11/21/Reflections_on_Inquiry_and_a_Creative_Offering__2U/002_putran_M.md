Namaskaram Shweta-ji,

  

Just now looked into your lyrics from the description. Posting it below. It is a nice introspective thought-provoking composition. Yes, we see ourselves as the jiva, human, father, mother etc., identified with nama-rupa, lost in thegames of their samsara. But that is not the real Self. Wrt them, I am their sakshi, the witnessing Consciousness, the One who projects, supports, and knows the world of darkness/ignorance. The world is never apart from Consciousness. Then, the higher true Self (I) is envisioned as calling upon itself (i) lost in upadhi-identified imaginations, of restlessness and suffering, to realize its Reality - that I will thus remove the mask and dispel the darkness.
