What a lovely interpretation! You have nailed my original thought process behind the making of this song. Indeed, in the song, the Self is calling upon the masked identity of name, form, attributes in the mirror of Self..."Who you see is not you....I am the one who sees you" As in the excerpt from Kena Upanishad, when the sishya asks the Guru what is the principle because of which this inert body, senses, and mind/intellect behave as though they are sentient. The Guru so as not to confuse the student into searching for some experience of Brahman refrains from using Atman or Brahman and instead says, it is "the eye of the eye," "ear of the ear, "nose of the nose", "mind of the mind", "speech of the speech", "prana of the prana" emphasizing that consciousness is distinct from each sense organ yet pervades and empowers them.

  

This was just my humble attempt to convey this to the world in simple layman terms.

Thanks for listening.
