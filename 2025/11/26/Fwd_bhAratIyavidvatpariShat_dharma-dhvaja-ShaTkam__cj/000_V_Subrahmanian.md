**धर्म-ध्वज-षट्कम्**

रचयिता - म.जयरामः

composed by Prof M Jayaraman

**  **

**जीवतैव हि देहेऽस्मिन् दृष्टं द्रष्टव्यदर्शनम् ।**

**ध्वजं रघूतमस्येह समारूढं समुन्नतम् ॥१॥**

While living in this very body, I have seen the Sight that was to be seen—the flag of the best of the Raghus (Śrī Rāma) raised here, standing high.

**  **

**अहो भाग्यमहो पुण्यं नैव चिन्तितमेव यत्।**

**राममन्दिरनिर्माणपूरणं मम जन्मनि ॥२॥**

Oh, what good fortune, what great merit—something never even imagined— that in my own lifetime the construction of the Rāma temple has been completed.

  

**समुच्छ्रितं ध्वजं यद्वत् सिन्दूरारुणसुन्दरम्।**

**तद्वत् समुच्छ्रितो भूयाद् धर्मोऽस्माकं सनातनः ॥३॥**

Just as the flag, beautiful with its vermilion-red hue, is raised high—so may our Sanātana Dharma also stand uplifted and exalted.

**  **

**करसेवक-साधूनां पपात रुधिरं भुवि।**

**समारूढध्वजेनाद्य केसरेण स्मराम हि ॥४॥**

The blood of the noble kar-sevaks fell upon the earth. Let us remember that today, as the saffron flag has been raised.

**  **

**धर्मकेतुः सतां सेतुः येन पेतुर्दुराग्रहाः।**

**मातुस्त्वयोध्यानाम्न्यां तु शोभते शिखरस्थितः ॥५॥**

The flag of Dharma, the bridge of the virtuous, by which stubborn evils were overcome.It now shines upon the temple’s summit in Ayodhyā, the city named after our Mother.

  

**कोविदारेण सूर्येण प्रणवेन सलाञ्छनः ।**

**हिन्दुधर्मध्वजोऽस्माकं सादरं प्रणिपत्यते॥६॥**

Marked by the Kovidāra tree, the sun, and the Pranava (ॐ),our flag of Hindu Dharma is respectfully saluted.
