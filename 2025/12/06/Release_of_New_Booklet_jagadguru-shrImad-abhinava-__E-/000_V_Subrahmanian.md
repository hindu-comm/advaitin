**Release of New Booklet: Jagadguru-śrīmad-abhinava-vidyātīrtha-mahāsvāmi- sādhana-jīvanmuktyāpti-līlā-śatakam for Free Download**

His Holiness *Jagadguru* Śrī Abhinava Vidyātīrtha *Mahāsvāmin*, a sage par excellence with unbounded compassion, adorned the Dakshinamnaya Sringeri Sri Sharada Peetham as its 35th Jagadguru Shankaracharya for 35 years.

From the day of His *saṁnyāsa*, His Holiness deeply engaged in spiritual practices. These culminated in His attaining the direct realization of the Supreme *Brahman* on 12 December 1935 and His thereafter remaining steadfastly established in the Supreme as a glorious *jīvanmukta* (one liberated even while alive).

A few months ago, a disciple composed a century of verses on the divine *Guru’s* sport of spiritual practices and attainment of perfection. He submitted the verses to His Holiness *Jagadguru* Śrī Bhāratī-tīrtha *Mahāsvāmin*. His Holiness not only perused the verses, He refined some of them, directed that they be published in the form of a booklet and even kindly conferred His personally penned and signed *śrīmukha* for the same. Accordingly, this booklet titled “Jagadguru-śrīmad-abhinava-vidyātīrtha-mahāsvāmi- sādhana-jīvanmuktyāpti-līlā-śatakam” – Century of Verses on H.H. Jagadguru Śrī Abhinava Vidyātīrtha Mahāsvāmin’s Divine Sport of Engaging in Spiritual Practices and Attaining Jīvanmukti – is being brought out by the Centre for Brahmavidya on 6 December 2025, on the holy occasion of the *Sākṣātkāra* (Enlightenment) Day of His Holiness *Jagadguru* Śrī Abhinava Vidyātīrtha *Mahāsvāmin*.
