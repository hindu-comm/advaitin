**Release of New Youtube Video: Guru-dhyanam**

**  **

His Holiness *Jagadguru* Śrī Bhāratī-tīrtha *Mahāsvāmin* composed two devotional, philosophical, captivating, and inspiring stotras on His *Guru*, His Holiness *Jagadguru* *Śrī* Abhinava Vidyātīrtha *Mahāsvāmin*, during His *Guru’s* lifetime. The ‘Gurudhyānam’, one of these *stotras*, spontaneously occurred to Him in March 1982.

In the five verses of this *stotra*, He describes His *Guru* as abiding effortlessly in *nirvikalpa-samādhi* on the Supreme *Brahman* of the nature of absolute existence, pure consciousness and infinite bliss – and as the Supreme *Brahman* and *Īśvara.*

The Centre for Brahmavidya is privileged to release this audio-visual presentation of the verses of the ‘Gurudhyānam’ on 6 December 2025, on the holy occasion of the *Sākṣātkāra* (Enlightenment) Day of His Holiness *Jagadguru* Śrī Abhinava Vidyātīrtha *Mahāsvāmin.*
