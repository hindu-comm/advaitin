**Release of New Youtube Video: Jagadguru *Śrī* Candraśekhara Bhāratī Mahāsvāmin’s Verses About His Disciple Jagadguru *Śrī* Abhinava Vidyātīrtha Mahāsvāmin**

**  **

The relationship between His Holiness *Jagadguru* *Śrī* Candraśekhara Bhāratī *Mahāsvāmin* and His Disciple *Jagadguru* *Śrī* Abhinava Vidyātīrtha *Mahāsvāmin* was truly unique. The Disciple served His *Guru* with unwavering devotion and flawless dedication for over two decades.

His Holiness *Jagadguru* *Śrī* Candraśekhara Bhāratī *Mahāsvāmin’s* love and regard for His Disciple were equally profound. In 1936, the *Guru* referred to the Disciple’s extraordinary vision of Lord Śiva, and His enlightenment and establishment in *Brahman*, and told Him, “I know that you are God Himself and ever perfect. Your engaging in *sādhanā* and apparently attaining enlightenment were only a part of your divine sport.”

He even composed eight verses about His Disciple. The first of these was composed by the *Guru* in Bengaluru on 25 July 1937. He wrote the other seven verses on His Disciple as a set, in 1943. When giving them to His Disciple, He said about the first verse – this is the first verse presented in this audio-visual presentation – ‘I have written only what I definitely know to be true about you. There is no exaggerated praise here.’

The Centre for Brahmavidya is privileged to publish this Audio-Visual presentation of a set of five verses of His Holiness *Jagadguru* *Śrī* Candraśekhara Bhāratī *Mahāsvāmin* on 6 December 2025, on the holy occasion of the *Sākṣātkāra* (Enlightenment) Day of His Holiness *Jagadguru* *Śrī* Abhinava Vidyātīrtha *Mahāsvāmin*.
