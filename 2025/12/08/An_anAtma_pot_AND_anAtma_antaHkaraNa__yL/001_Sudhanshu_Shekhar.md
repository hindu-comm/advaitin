Your take is that when Naishkarmya Siddhi states AtmA as Ashraya and vishaya of ajnAna, then it is jIva-AtmA which is meant. This is incorrect.

  

When NS explains as to how anAtmA cannot be the Ashraya of ajnAna owing to it being ajnAna-prasUta, it says subsequently that owing to very same reason, anAtmA cannot be the vishaya of ajnAna. एतेभ्य एव हेतुभ्यः, *नाऽनात्मविषयमज्ञानं संभवतीति ग्राह्यम्* । *एवं तावत् नाऽनात्मनोऽज्ञानित्वम्, नापि तद्विषयमज्ञानम्*।.... किंविषयं पुनस्तदात्मनोऽज्ञानम् ? *आत्मविषयमिति ब्रूमः* ।

  

*It is clear that that very AtmA is stated to be the vishaya which has just been proved to be the Ashraya.*

  

Now, it is clear in the whole of advaita vedAnta that only shuddha-nirupAdhika-chaitanya-AtmA is ajnAna-vishaya and not the jIva-AtmA. How? Because it is only by the jnAna of shuddha-AtmA that ajnAna is removed. Hence Brahman/shuddha-chaitanya/nirupAdhika-AtmA is ajnAna-vishaya. That is the accepted position. (Please clarify if SSSS ji holds otherwise).

  

Now since same AtmA is stated as Ashraya and vishaya, it is clear that shuddha-nirupAdhika-AtmA alone is Ashraya also. To posit that jIvAtmA is Ashraya but shuddha-AtmA is vishaya, in the very same passage of NS, is contradictory. Had it been so, it would have been mentioned by NS-kAra or the commentator, SSSS ji. Neither have done so. So, whatever AtmA is ajnAna-vishaya, that very same AtmA is ajnAna-Ashraya also. Since ajnAna-vishaya is unquestionably shuddha-AtmA, ajnAna-Ashraya has to be shuddha-AtmA alone.

  

Moreover, the NS starts 3rd chapter like this - "सर्वोऽयं प्रमितिप्रमाणप्रमेयप्रमातृलक्षणः, आब्रह्मस्तम्बपर्यन्तोमिथ्याध्यास एव-इति बहुश उपपत्तिभिरतिष्ठिपम् । आत्मा च जन्मादिषड्भावविकारवर्जितः कूटस्थबोध एवेति स्फुटीकृतम्। तयोश्च मिथ्याध्यासकूटस्थात्मनोर्नान्तरेणाज्ञानं संबन्धः"

  

Here, pramANa-prameya-pramiti-pramAtA have been stated as mithyA-adhyAsa (anAtmA). AtmA is stated as kUTastha-chaitanya.

  

The connection between such mithyA-adhyAsa (anAtmA) and AtmA is stated to be imopossible through any means other than ajnAna. An analysis of this shows the following:

  

\(1\) PramAtA i.e. jIva-AtmA is included in mithyA-adhyAsa/anAtmA (jIva being AbhAsa is mithyA in VArtika). So, your claim of jIva-AtmA as AtmA in NS is rejected by the very first line.

  

\(2\) mithyA-adhyAsa and ajnAna are distinguished.

  

\(3\) AtmA is stated to be nitya-kUTastha which is shuddha-chaitanya. And hence, the possibility of jIva-AtmA being the meaning of AtmA is rejected.
