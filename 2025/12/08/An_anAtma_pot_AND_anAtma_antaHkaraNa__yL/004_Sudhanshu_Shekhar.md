That is the objection of opponent. SSSS ji explains later on page 269 - अथ समाधत्ते - घटत एव।

  

See, there are two viewpoints. From the ajnAna-drishTi and from Atma-drishTi.

  

From ajnAna-drishTi, AtmA alone can be Ashraya and vishaya of ajnAna. anAtmA cannot be either the Ashraya or the vishaya as has been explained in NS on account of anAtmA being ajnAna-prasUta.

  

Now, from Atma-drishTi, there is neither existence of ajnAna nor its appearance. So, there is no question of its Ashraya or vishaya either. So, these ajnAna-Ashrayatva or ajnAna-vishayatva are merely mithyA dharma.

  

So, it is clear that SSSS ji has no qualms in accepting here that shuddha-AtmA is Ashraya and vishaya of ajnAna. Elsewhere what he says is not a matter of discussion. JIva-AtmA being the meaning of AtmA here, as suggested by Bhaskar ji, is completely out-of-place and is incorrect understanding of even what SSSS ji is saying.
