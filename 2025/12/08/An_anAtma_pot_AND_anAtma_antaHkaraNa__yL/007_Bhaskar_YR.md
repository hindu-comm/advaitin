  avidyA is Sri SSS’s clarification, it is pUrvapaxi expressing his
  views / justification how kUtastha nitya Atman can not give Ashraya to
  avidyA as Atman itself is jnAna svarUpa. For this NS-kAra says : yes
  it is indeed possible (ghatata eva)..for that again pUrvapaxi asks :
  how can it be possible (kathaM??) for that clarification is
  ajnAnamAtra nimittatvAt…Further Sri SSS clarifies if Atman is
  practically / really the ashraya then only your objections holds good
  but when we say AjnAna Ashrayatva / vishayatva, separation of
  AtmAnAtma etc. are within the domain of ajnAna it does not holds its
  ground. So IMO what Sri Sudhanshu prabhuji saying is correct. It is
  just pUrvapaxi’s objection about brahmAshrita avidyA and Sri SSS
  agrees / accepts these objections are valid ONLY this stand of
  brahmAshrita avidyA is vAstavika. But that is NOT the case is his
  subsequent clarification. Perhaps it would be clear for you when you
  read the Kannada version of kleshApahAriNi.
