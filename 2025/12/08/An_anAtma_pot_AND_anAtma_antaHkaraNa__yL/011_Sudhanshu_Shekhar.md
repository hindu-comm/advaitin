Hare Krishna Chandramouli ji.

  

So, as per you, what is SSSS Ji's view regarding Ashraya of ajnAna as per the impugned portion of Naishkarmya Siddhi?

  

1\. Shuddha-AtmA is Ashraya of ajnAna.

  

2\. anAtmA is Ashraya of ajnAna.

  

3\. JIva-AtmA is Ashraya of ajnAna.

  

4\. Shuddha-AtmA is Ashraya of ajnAna but this avidyA-Ashrayatva is mithyA dharma (ajnAna-krita) and not pAramArthika dharma.

  

5\. Anything else. (Please write).

  

As per my understanding, the Advaita siddhAnta is 4. And that is explained in NS and that is accepted by SSSS ji here. What SSSS ji says elsewhere is not a matter of discussion.
