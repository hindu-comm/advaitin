And with regard to this Sri SSS’s observation, it is to be understood that when the Atma is being mentioned it is said so without any prefixes to indicate it is only pratyagAtma and it is not kUtastha, nirvikAri, nirvishesha and nishkriya paramAtma. And basic thing that needs to be kept in mind that there is NO AtmAnAtma vyavahAra in ekamevAdviteeya Chaitanya. This is very clear in adhyAsa bhAshya itself that all vyavahAra (AtmAnAtma vibhAga) takes place only on the basis of adhyAsa/avidyA. avidyA of what on whom? AtmanAtmanO etaretarAdhyAsaH of the Atman on the anAtman and conversely. Here it is very clear that the Atman is kshetrajna (pratyagAtma) and the anAtma is kshetra (Vishaya). Somewhere in bhAshya itself bhAshyakAra clarifies, like the word cow etc, the word Atman without any prefix refers only to pratyagAtman. In discussions like Atma-anAtma viveka etc the same rule applies. So when NS talks about Atma and anAtma and attributes Vishaya & Ashraya etc. to Atma only it is quite clear it is not talking about parishuddha Chaitanya but it is about upAdhi parichinna Chaitanya only, hence nArada example given and later elaborately discussing about tat and tvaM padArtha etc. With all these, I reckon I have very valid and logical reason to infer that the literal meaning of Atman without a prefix cannot refer to the aparichinna chaitanyam (nirvikAri chaitanyaM) because of the simple fact that being sarvAtman there is nothing that can be labelled as anatman (again, for this also bhAshya vAkya support is there!!). If you don’t have any problem with Sri Devarao KulkarNi’s works, for more details you can refer to his Sri Shankara Vedanta Saurabha in Kannada.
