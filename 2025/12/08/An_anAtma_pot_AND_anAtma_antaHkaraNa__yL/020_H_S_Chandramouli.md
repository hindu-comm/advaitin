Namaste Sudhanshu Ji, Bhaskar Ji,

There has been no response to my post to Michael Ji. Perhaps he is busy being Christmas time. I will present my understanding of Sri SSS on the topic for whatever it is worth. If there is any error, hopefully it will be pointed out. If correct, perhaps further discussions could be based on that.

Shudha Chaitanya is unattached. Chaitanya AbhAsas (look alike Chaitanya entities) make their appearance. Countless numbers. They are the jIvA ChaitanyAs. Jagat ,inert entities, AntahkaraNa downwards, all entities, also appears. Both jIva ChaitanyAs as well as Jagat are unassociated with Shudha Chaitanya. Much like Rope and Rope-serpent illustration. Both are anAdi ( beginningless ). JIva ChaitanyAs have no knowledge about Shudha Chaitanya nor do they have have any knowledge about any relationship between Jagat and Shudha Chaitanya. JIva ChaitanyAs identify themselves with AntahkaraNa,one each, and such an association is anAdi ( Beginningless ). This is adhyAsa. Being anAdi, any kAraNa ( cause ) for such an adhyAsa is not required.

Since there is no association with Shudha Chaitanya of any of the other entities, the question of mUla ajnAna , veiling ( AvaraNa ) of the Shudha Chaitanya does not arise. There is no AvaraNa postulated. Hence the terms Ashrayatva and Vishayatva for mUla ajnAna is not relevant or valid.

I have purposely kept this very brief so as to highlight only the issue under discussion. Please ask for any clarification on the postulate itself before arriving at any conclusion.
