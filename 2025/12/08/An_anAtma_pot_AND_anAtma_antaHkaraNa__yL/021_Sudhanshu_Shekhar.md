There are two drishTi in VedAnta. One, Brahma-drishTi, second, avidyA-drishTi.

  

\[नाविद्याऽस्येत्यविद्यायामेवाऽऽसित्वा प्रकल्प्यते ।।

ब्रह्मटृष्ट्या त्वविद्येयं न कथंचन युज्यते ।। १७६ ।।\] (Sambandha BhAshya VArtika - 176)

  

From Brahma-drishTi, there is neither any avidyA nor any AvaraNa nor any avidyA-kArya. Hence, discussion of Ashrayatva and vishayatva is meaningless.

  

It is only from avidyA-drishTi that AvaraNa, avidyA, Ashraya and vishaya thereof are discussed.



> There is no AvaraNa postulated. Hence the terms Ashrayatva and
