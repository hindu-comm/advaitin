Reg // 1.This statement of yours "There is no AvaraNa postulated. Hence the terms Ashrayatva and Vishayatva for mUla ajnAna is not relevant or valid." is from Brahma-drishTi or avidyA-drishTi? //,

All statements are made staying within avidyA only.

A brief straying away from current specific issue.Even when it is claimed to be from Brahma drishti, it is our understanding , staying within avidyA only, of the declaration by Shruti as Brahma drishti. Such declarations by Shruti is simply accepted as from Brahma drishti. However as such understanding of the Shruti is by staying within avidyA only, such understandings could be and do vary. As could be seen from so many different interpretations of the same Shruti by different AchAryAs of eminence.

Yes. Point No. 1, statement is made from avidyA drishti only. Position is same even where AvaraNa is postulated.

Point No 2 also is from avidyA drishti only. However a conception of Atma and anAtma can be postulated and issue discussed .
