Namaste Sudhanshu Ji,

Reg // AvaraNa is postulated. Then AvaraNa is negated.

avidyA-Ashrayatva and vishayatva to shuddha-AtmA are logically proved. And then negated //,

It is so in the VivaraNa School only. Not in the SSS School.

Reg // If it is an irrelevant or invalid issue, why would NS take pains to explain that Ashrayatva and vishayatva are to shuddha-AtmA and not to anAtmA //,

Please refer to my earlier posts in this thread. The above is your understanding of NS which in my understanding is faulty. In fact Sri SSS has presented his arguments here in support of his contention that AvaraNa cannot be postulated as it can neither have Atma ( Shuddha Chaitanya ) nor anAtma for its Ashraya and/or vishaya. He further contends that his view is supported by Swami Sureswaracharya in this part of NS.
