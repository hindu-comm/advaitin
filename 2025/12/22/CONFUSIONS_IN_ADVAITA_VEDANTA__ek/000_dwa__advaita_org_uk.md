Just to inform existing or potential readers of my books ‘**CONFUSIONS IN ADVAITA VEDANTA’:**

****

The Kindle versions of Vols. 1 and 2 that I have already issued were facsimiles of the print versions. This means that the full contents list and index of each book are included, since the page contents are identical. Thus, those versions are ideal for ‘academic’ study on laptops or PCs. Unfortunately, reading on a tablet or phone is much less convenient, since text may be too small at full-screen to be comfortable.



Accordingly, I have revamped the content of Vol. 2, ‘Ignorance and Its Removal’, removing pagination and changing the Devanagari font so that pages can be resized as desired and will flow naturally on any device. The Kindle publishing process also automatically generates a hyperlinked contents list of the main headings. Because page numbering is lost, however, the index is no longer useful and has been removed. The previously noted [problems](https://www.advaita-vision.org/problems-with-confusions-2/), regarding words withoutspaces between them, have also been fixed in this edition.



This Kindle ‘reflowable’ version is now available for Vol. 2 and I will now be working on converting Vol. 1 similarly. More information is available at [https://www.advaita-vision.org/more-confusions/](https://www.advaita-vision.org/more-confusions/).



The paperback and hardback versions of Vol. 2 will be available from [Indica Books](https://www.indicabooks.com/) early in the new year.
