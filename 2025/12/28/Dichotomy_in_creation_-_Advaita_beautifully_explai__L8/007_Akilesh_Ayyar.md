[[Akilesh Ayyar	2026-01-01, 02:44:09 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/L8RKMHasKME)]]

There's a beautiful passage in theYoga Vasistha where Rama, after learning the truth of the Self from Vasistha, sits in samadhi, intending to relax there forever.

  

Vasistha enters Rama's sushumna nadi and wakes him and says -- Rama, you can't be sitting and meditating. Go out and do all your kingly things. Go live out your adventures.

  

And Rama says something fascinating. He says: Though I have NO duties or obligations WHATSOEVER, it is STILL my duty to follow my guru's instructions!

  

And off he goes.

  

Akilesh Ayyar
