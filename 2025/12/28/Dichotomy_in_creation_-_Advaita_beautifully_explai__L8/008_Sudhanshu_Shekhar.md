Namaste Akilesh ji.

> 

  

> Though I have NO duties or obligations WHATSOEVER, it is STILL my duty
> to follow my guru's instructions!

  

This statement is self-contradictory. "I have no duties whatsoever" and "It is my duty to follow my guru" are mutually contradictory.

  

Hence, the statement has to be taken to be arthavAda for eulogy of Guru and Brahma-vidyA rather than interpreting it to imply the persistence of a specific duty towards Guru.
