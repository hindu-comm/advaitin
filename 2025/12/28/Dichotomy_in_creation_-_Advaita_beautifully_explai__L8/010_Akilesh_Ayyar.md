Namaste Sudhanshu ji,

  

The contradiction is quite open and obviously intentional, and is a pointer to the Truth. The “I” who has no duty and the “I” for whom there is a duty are both the same and not the same I.

  

As the Gita says, the way of action is subtle indeed, and even the wise are baffled by it.

  

I unfortunately don’t know the exact reference sloka. It’s on the last page of the Concise Yoga Vasistha.

  

Akilesh Ayyar
