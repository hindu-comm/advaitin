Namaste Subbu ji, Akilesh ji,

  

"For me, there remains no vidhi and nishedha" -- The "I" can refer to both vAchya-artha and lakshya-artha. For lakshya-artha, it is obvious. For vAchya-artha also, there can remain no vidhi-nishedha as jnAnI is solely driven by the prArabdha.

  

"For me, despite jnAna, there remains the kartavya of obeying guru's directions" -- the sentence is self-contradictory. Hence, it has to be interpreted to be arthavAda for eulogy of guru and Atma-jnAna. There appears to be no other way. I checked Ananda Bodhendra commentary. Nothing on this aspect is explained.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
