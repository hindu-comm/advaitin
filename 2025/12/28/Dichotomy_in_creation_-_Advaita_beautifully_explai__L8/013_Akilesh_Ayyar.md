Namaste Sudhanshu ji,

  

The manifest contradiction is pointing to something beyond the action/inaction dichotomy, beyond the duty/non-duty dichotomy... beyond the splits of thought, and specifically the split ofthought on what it means to act or not act, to have a duty or not to have it.

  

Compare BG 3.22, where the Lord says:

*O son of Pṛthā, there is no work prescribed for Me within all the three planetary systems. Nor am I in want of anything, nor have I a need to obtain anything – and yet I am engaged in prescribed duties.*

Akilesh Ayyar
