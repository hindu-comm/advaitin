Namaste Krishna ji.

  

Indeed it is true in srishTi-drishTi-vAda that prArabdha is accepted and a jnAnI acts purely in accordance with his prArabdha. LOka-sangraha falling within the domain of prArabdha thus becomes the reason for karmas (appearing to be) undertaken by jnAnI.

  

This is how the shlOka in YV or GItA should be understood.

  

However, we should clearly understand that those karmAs are not driven by kartavya (vidhi-nishedha), but by prArabdha. Like a leaf floats around in wind, a jnAnI (appear(s) to) act(s) in the world.

  

However, in drishTi-srishTi-vAda, this explanationis no longer valid.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
