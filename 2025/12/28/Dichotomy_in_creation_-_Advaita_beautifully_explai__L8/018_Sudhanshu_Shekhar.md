ShAstra deals with adhikArIs differently. Some are manda-adhikArI and some are uttama-adhikArI. The distinction between waking and dream is for manda-adhikArI. Their identity is for uttama-adhikArI. \[More refined statement would be - different prakriyAs are valid for same adhikArI at different level of his adhikAritvam\]. This is the siddhAnta explained in advaita vedAnta.

  

You may like to check MANDUkya-rahasya-vivriti 2.4 where SSSS ji writes explaining **स्वप्रजागरयोर्वैधर्म्याभावः** \[absence of distinction between waking and dream\] - नन्वन्तःस्थानादिभिर्हेतुभिस्त्वयैव जागरितवैलक्षण्यं स्वप्रस्य प्रदर्शितम् \| बाध्यमानत्वं चाशक्यापह्नवं स्वप्रस्य । तत्कथमिदानीमात्थ सर्वथा साम्येन सममेवोभयोर्वैतथ्यमिति ? अत्रोच्यते \| ***सत्यमुक्तं वैलक्षण्यम् \| तत्तु जागरितदृष्ट्या मन्दबुद्धीनां परमार्थप्रतिपत्त्युपायत्वेन, न वस्तुदृष्ट्या \| वस्तुतस्तु नायमपि विशेषो विद्यते ।*** यतोऽयमेव श्लोकः स्वप्रजागरितयोः सर्वसमताप्रतिपादनपरत्वेनापि योजयितुं शक्यते । \[True that difference between waking and dream has been stated. However, that is only through waking-view-point for people with dull intellect as a tool to accomplish the absolute and not from the view point of truth. In truth, this distinction between waking and dream is not present.\]

  

You can check the same at[https://adhyatmaprakasha.org/php/bookreader/templates/book.php?type=sanskrit&book_id=006&pagenum=1b0176#page/321/mode/1up](https://adhyatmaprakasha.org/php/bookreader/templates/book.php?type=sanskrit&book_id=006&pagenum=1b0176#page/321/mode/1up)

  

It is a well-understood siddhAnta that BhAshya mentions both - distinction-between-waking-and-dream AND identity-of-waking-and-dream. While the former is a salient feature of SDV, the latter is a salient feature of DSV. They apply to same adhikArI at different stages of his adhiKAritvam. abhAva-adhikaraNa, nAnA-jIva-vAda, prArabdha, jIvanmukti etc are all acceptable within the domain of SDV for manda-adhiKArI.
