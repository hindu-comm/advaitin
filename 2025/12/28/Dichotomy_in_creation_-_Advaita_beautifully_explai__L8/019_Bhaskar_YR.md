Hare Krishna



ShAstra deals with adhikArIs differently. Some are manda-adhikArI and some are uttama-adhikArI. The distinction between waking and dream is for manda-adhikArI. Their identity is for uttama-adhikArI. \[More refined statement would be - different prakriyAs are valid for same adhikArI at different level of his adhikAritvam\]. This is the siddhAnta explained in advaita vedAnta.



Ø Though for the argument’s sake we say both are different prakriya-s serving the same purpose, no way we can judge that one is superior to another when shruti and bhAshya loaded with Ishwara hetuka srushti. IMO there is no categorical ranking of SDV & DSV in PTB. If I am right texts like yogavAshishTa strongly endorses the views like there is drushti so there is srushti. In kArikA to waking state is equaled with that of dream state but entirely for different purpose not to give the rankings. Uttama and Madhyama adhikAri gradations too for some other purpose as per shankara bhAshya ( upAsya brahma – jneya brahma) not to deny the srushti prakriya in siddhAnta though bhAshyakAra confirms that no purushArtha is achieved by discussing these things in length. So in that sense it is wrong / deluded notion to assert that anyone prakriya is superior to the other and meant for some type of adhikAri-s in particular. And as I said, bhAshyakAra employs this anubhava sammata model in his bhAshya-s for the majority of seekers, as it very closely aligns with day to day experience and more importantly and parallelly protects the validity of vedic dharma, vidhi-nishedha ritual, morality, and the mukti mArga through IshwarAnugraha and guru kAruNya within the Ishwara kruta srushti.

And I also think, in your present mind set you would not be in a position to understand Sri SSS’s observation based on shankara bhAshya on 4-61-67 in line with Mundaka tadetat satyaM avitathaM.



It is a well-understood siddhAnta that BhAshya mentions both - distinction-between-waking-and-dream AND identity-of-waking-and-dream. While the former is a salient feature of SDV, the latter is a salient feature of DSV. They apply to same adhikArI at different stages of his adhiKAritvam. abhAva-adhikaraNa, nAnA-jIva-vAda, prArabdha, jIvanmukti etc are all acceptable within the domain of SDV for manda-adhiKArI.

Anyway, prArabdha and equating this with avidyAlesha, mUlAvidyA and granting the objective existence to it, it’s locus and subjectg etc. is the grand contributions from some which is obviously outside the domain of Shuddha shankaraadvaita.
