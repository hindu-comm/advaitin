Sir, the vAchya-artha of jIva and Ishwara are different. There is identity in lakshya-artha. The abheda-Shruti is taking lakshya-artha. The distinction in vAchya-artha of jIva and Ishwara is avidyA-krita and due to upAdhi.

  

This is the method of reconciliation.

  

Bheda-shruti and abheda-shruti are reconciled. If one perpetuates the avidyA-krita-difference between jIva and Ishwara by harping upon creation and upAsanA, then it is not advaita.

  

I am not interested in showing whether SSSS ji contradicts himself or not. I cited one statement. It is for you to accept it or reject it or do whatever you want with it. I have seen ISKCON followers defend the following, so nothing amazes me.
