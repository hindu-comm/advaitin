**Terms and Definitions**

****

Twenty years ago, or thereabouts, when I was Chief Moderator of the group for the year, I inaugurated a program in which senior members of the group posted explanations of key terms used in Advaita. I thought that this would be very helpful to those members who were not so familiar with the Sanskrit words and who were consequently unable to participate in the discussions. (The results of this exercise can be seen in the section [https://www.advaita.org.uk/discourses/definitions/definitions.htm](https://www.advaita.org.uk/discourses/definitions/definitions.htm) at my old website.)



I have no idea what proportion of members today would class themselves as ‘beginner’ or ‘intermediate’ students of Advaita. Are there any? Cynically, I would imagine that most have left long ago. It is clear that the majority of posts and discussions are pretty advanced and only really accessible to long-term or very knowledgeable seekers. It is understandable that ‘advanced’ students are mainly interested in further advancing their knowledge or in discussions with similarly advanced seekers. But, recalling the aims of this group when it was first established, it was clearly intended to appeal and cater to those seekers who found the Advaita-L group far too advanced. I have never looked at the other group but, if that is still more advanced…



Anyway, I have just begun a series of posts to my website to explain key terms and, realizing that not all members of this group will visit that with any regularity (if at all), I decided to post them here also. If there are any non-advanced seekers left, they should find these useful. (Maybe even some of the advanced ones might find them interesting.)



It is not intended that these posts should stimulate discussion. Certainly, I do not intend myself to participate in any prolonged exchanges. But obviously if anyone disagrees with what I say and can present reasoned and Śaṅkara-supported arguments as to why they do not, then I would like to hear from them. (I await with trepidation the response to a post on ‘avidyā’, for example, which should be in 4 – 5 weeks’ time!)



I have not written the posts from ‘scratch’ but they all stem entirely from my own writing over the past 25 years. I have uploaded all of my books, website material, blogs and posts and use AI (NotebookLM) to generate the descriptions only from that material.



I will begin these immediately following this, with the term ‘adhyāropa-apavāda’ and aim to post one every week or so from now. So that they will be recognizable to readers, each description will be preceded by ‘T&D – ‘.



Dennis
