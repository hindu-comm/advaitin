Namaskars,

Sri Dennis's contribution and clarity is phenomenal. I used to study his notes and the notes of Sri Sadananda when I was preparing for my Ph. D. Now it is also true that AI is giving surprisingly detailed notes on even comparative philosophical concepts. A few days ago I wanted to know the similarities between the idea of the principle of interdependence of things (upakarya-upakaraka bhava mentioned in the Br. Up) and the idea of pratitya-samutpada of Buddhism. The AI gave a detailed note on the similarities and differences between the two and it also gave references to some articles. It is true that AI is emerging as an immediate check point for any question.

Aravinda Rao
