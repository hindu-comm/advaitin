Veda recitation classes will be offered around the Silicon Valley/San Francisco Bay Area, California, USA.  
The classes may be held at an apartment home in Santa Clara, or any other convenient location.  
  
  
Here are the requirements for being a student of the Veda Class:  
  
1) Must have undergone upanayanam, with sacred thread (i.e. Yajnopavitam or Poonal or Janoi).  
2) Must perform Sandhya Vandanam regularly - or at least Gayatri Mantra Japam.  
3) Must be able to pronounce "Mahapranas" like KHA, THA, DHA, etc.  
4) Must be able to chant the Gayatri Mantra with correct SVARAS.  
5) Must bring a "Veshti" or "Dhoti" (White) to the Veda class - can wear it over shorts or pants.
