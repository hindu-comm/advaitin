The logic is NS is pretty simple. Whateveris anAtmA cannot be the Ashraya of avidyA. Why? Because anAtmA is ajnAna-prasUtah. अनात्मनश्चाज्ञानप्रसूतत्त्वात् .. एवं तावन्नानात्मनोऽज्ञानित्वं नापि तद्विषयमज्ञानम् ।

  

There are only two entities, AtmA and anAtmA.इह च पदार्थद्वयं निर्द्धारितमात्मानात्मा च । \[ajnAna is also anAtmA\].

  

The very swarUpa of anAtmA is ajnAna. तत्रानात्मनस्तावन्नाज्ञानेनाभिसंबन्धः । ***तस्य हि स्वरूपमेवाज्ञानं*** न हि स्वतोऽज्ञानस्याज्ञानं घटते ।

  

So, it naturally means that AtmA-without-a-tinge-of-anAtmA is the Ashraya and vishaya of ajnAna. It is so clear. This is what is shuddha-chaitanya, which is also stated as nirvibhAga-chit.

  

I don't understand how can anything else be read in plain and simple words of NS.
