Hare Krishna Bhaskar prabhu ji.  
  

It is true that from the frame of reference of Shuddha-AtmA, there is neither any avidyA nor anAtmA nor Atma-anAtma-bheda. That is completely accepted.  
  
It is only from the frame of reference of avidyA that Ashraya or vishaya of avidyA is discussed. And certainly Shuddha Brahman can be discussed from the frame of reference of avidyA. We do that, Shruti does that.

**  **

**First reason**

  

If you hold that upAdhi-parichchhinna-AtmA is the Ashraya of avidyA, then issue would arise because upAdhi is avidyA-kArya and anAtmA. So, the Ashraya of avidyA will be an entity which necessarily needs avidyA-kArya. That is logically impossible. Isn't it?

  

So, upAdhi-parichchhinna-AtmA or jIva cannot be Ashraya of avidyA.

  

Only that entity which has no requirement of avidyA can be Ashraya-of-avidyA. Otherwise, there will be AtmAshraya-dOsha. It will be like standingon one's own shoulders.

**  **

**Second reason  
  **

If you see the discussion in NS, AtmA is stated as Ashraya and vishaya of avidyA. And same logic is used. Suppose someone holds that upAdhi-parichchhinna-AtmA is the Ashraya of avidyA, then he will also have to hold that upAdhi-parichichhinna-AtmA is the vishaya of avidyA. NS says -किंविषयंपुनस्तदात्मनोऽज्ञानम् । आत्मविषयमिति ब्रूमः ।

  

But this is unacceptable. UpAdhi-parichchhinna-AtmA is not and cannot be the vishaya of ajnAna. Ignorance-about-x can be removed by knowledge-about-x. It is well known that knowledge about shuddha-Brahman alone removes ajnAna. So, ajnAna necessarily has to be about Shuddha Brahman.

  

So, the coherent reading of NS demonstrates that only shuddha-AtmA can be Ashraya and vishaya of ajnAna.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
