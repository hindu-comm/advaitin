*The siddhAntin - Ok, so let Brahman not be the creator of ignorance or be deluded. *But it is also taught that there is no other conscious entity other than Brahman who can be the cause of ignorance or be deluded*. See texts like "there is no other knower but Him", "there is no other knower but This", "you are That", "it knew *only* itself, as I am 'Brahman' ", "He who thinks 'He and I are two different entities', he is ignorant" so say the shrutis.*



So, Shankara asks - who else can possess ignorance, when there is only one conscious entity. You tell me, who is that?z

I think to the best of my knowledge I have shared my understanding about this shruti /bhAshya vAkya. First of all, we approach shAstra and guru thinking that we are having avidyA about ourselves, hence suffering and to get rid of it to gain / realize our svarUpa jnAna. No one would would ask the question whether this avidyA is mine or pertains to someone else. So IMO no one would get the silly doubt like : is it my avidyA or parabrahman’s avidyA?? When Ishwara himself is nitya,Shuddha, buddha, mukta and devoid of any avidyA (Ishwarasya nityanivrutyavidvatvAt…Ishwara is forever free from avidyA) how can we say nirguNa-nirvishesha-nishkriya, parishuddha, kUtasTha brahman is having the avidyA !!?? And what purushArtha we are going to achieve by realizing the brahman who himself is the Ashraya for that avidyA!!??
So, in the language of shAstra jeeva is nothing but brahman ( jeevO brahmaiva na aparaH) and when it comes to vidyA-avidyA, jnAtru-jneya-jnAna triputi vyavahAra it is ‘that’ jeeva only supposed to have avidyA like jnAnAbhAva, anyathAgrahaNa and saMshaya. Can we say since brahman is having the avidyA it is having jnAnAbhAva, it is mistaking itself as another (where is the question of another BTW!!) and it is also doubting about itself…these things well suit to explain ONLY jeeva who is associated with his antaHkaraNa though he is brahman ultimately. Hence without tainting brahma with avidyA my reading would be : the jeeva (Chetana in the bhAshya) who has created avidyA (avidyAkartA) for himself and confused is not different from brahman.
Here jeeva thinks about himself as something else and suffers due to lack of his svarUpa. And later on, approaches guru-shAstra, do the shravaNAdi sAdhana and realizes his svarUpa and become/realizes that he is indeed that secondless brahman. Here as I clarified these things are meant for shAreeri and not Ishwara.



In this way jeeva realizes that he is brahman by getting rid of his avidyA or become brahman by getting the brahma jnAna through guru kAruNya and shravaNadi pratyaksha sAdhana. And we cannot reverse this order and say brahman even before jeeva bhAva was having the avidyA and due to which become jeeva or brahman by acquiring avidyA become jeeva!!



And this is what in the story of prince brought up by a hunter realized that he is indeed the prince belong to royal family and not to the hunter family and became the king. (bruhadAraNyaka is having this story I think). In this way the hunter (jeeva) realizes that he is king by giving up his ignorance. Though he was / is / will always a king (brahman) due to his wrong identification (adhyAsa) he was thinking that he was a hunter. Similarly by giving up avidyA (adhyAsa) the jeeva realizes that he is brahman and he was brahman all through.
