Hare Krishna



How do you know this prabhu ji?



Ø Intuition, sva-hrudaya pratyaya 😊 no one should dare to question it 😊 how do you know that your guru is brahmA, vishNu and maheshwara??
It is the shraddhA, bhakti in one’s guru.



Did SSSS ji tell this? Or is it your imagination? Or is it your fanatic belief?



Ø None of the above, it is guru bhakti, treating guru as sarvaM..IshwarO gururAtmeti mUthybhedAt vibhAgine…yasya deve parA bhaktiH yathA deve tathA gurau…says shruti. But all these things mere fanactic beliefs for the nAstika-s, we cannot help it.
