Namaste all,

  

Is it acceptable for some sadhaka's to change over to another math/peetham in the same-sampradaya?

  

My 'custom-has-shifted' to another math over the years, gradually so, but got to a critical-juncture last year. This is due to 'operational and corporate-governance-reasons' as my clouded-intellect sees it. The collective-reasons have now been beyondmy intellect's ability to deal with constructively. 'All-stakeholder-safety and welfare,
Common-sense' are additional-terms involved in my decision(s). Although, I recognise the sheer-splendour of my Guru-Parampara, yet, this move seems inevitable.

  

If my interpretationsare correct, my 'choice' of the 'new-math' has met with The One approval that any sadhaka seeks, from their Guru, and that too, Absolutely-WholeHeartedly.

  

In my antah-karana, the 'New-math' visit has been taken up very well, but, yet, the decision of ***'leaving-behind-my-earlier- math' is the extremely-painful-one'***.

  

I have heard of some spiritual giants who have crossed over, for ex. Pujya Mataji Krishnabai, was born in, and had contact with many towering giants of Saints in her own birth-obtained Math. However, she ended up surrendering only at the feet of Pujya Swami Ramdas (Papaji). However, I am cautious in that Pujya Mataji, even before her Brahma-Sakshatkara, was a sadhika of the highest-calibre, as opposed to the manda-adhikari (? no-adhikari- actually) me.

  

For a long-time I have tried meditating on this, but each time the decision to 'cross-over' seems to get stronger.

  

And in the 'new' Math, do I need to report / relay anything? For me, as always, just the Sannidhya and the 'katakshaya-vyapara' i.e the 'mere'-presence and the 'sideways-glance' of the Guru have been more than enough in all these decades. However, I don't want to be flouting any protocols, which is why I ask.

  

I (try to ) continue my meditation and listening to online-lectures of my Deeksha-guru, although at this stage, truly-speaking, even that is painful.

  

Is this one form of 'attachment' and ' ahankara/mamakara' ? And why/why not?

Clearly, ths BMSC is a manda-adhikari / non-adhikari. So, is attachment to Math and Guru not an essential requisite on the pathway to 'liberation'? In theory, I can probably quote 'there is only brahman' 'even moksha is only from the viewpoint of bandha' however, one-little event in life is enough to drown the anadhikari me into samsara again.

  

Any insights will be gratefully appreciated,

with regards,

Sakshi
