Dear friend (aham brahmaasmi)  

  

In the ancient times, before initiating a new disciple, the guru used to look at (or check) the possible astrological compatibility with the new shishya. In some cases the guru decided to change the name of the disciple to bring in the compatibility between the guru and the shishya.  

  

Thus, it appears that, if you are comfortable with a new guru in another math, please do not hesitate to meet the new guru in a new math, and you may even share your doubts on the present issue, with the new guru.  

  

Best wishes,
