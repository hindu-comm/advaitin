Pranams to lotus feet of Gurus,  
Shri Ramo Jayatu.  
This is a vedapatashala-like full-time gurukulam initiative for kanya  
girls starting at 6-8 yrs of age\*. This gurukulam is run under the guidance  
of the Paramapujya Sringeri Shankaracharya Jagadgurus.  
  
Core curriculum: Shastriya samskritam preparatory for learning shastra.  
Samskritam is the medium of instruction.  
  
Focus areas in the initial years - shabda-dhatu patanam, amarakosha,  
ashtadhyayi, sahityam/kavyam, tarka sangraha, shaankara stotrams, Srimad  
Ramayanam and Srimad Bhagavatam.  
  
\*Other areas of focus\* \*samskriti\*- nithya pooja paddhati, go paalanam,  
nithya anushtanam/japam, kolam, cooking, e.t.c \*kala - choice of dedicated  
adhyayanam of vocal music, veenai, fine arts, chess\* kriya -weekly silambam  
sessions and play time. yoga - daily yoga sessions with focus on yoga  
shastram  
  
\*Modern subjects\* - English, Math, science via agile shaala, Bangalore (  
[https://agileshaala.com](https://agileshaala.com)) \*X, XII through NIOS exam system\*.  
  
WHO IS ELIGIBLE: girls aged \*6-8 yrs\*. older girls may be considered after  
screening. Parents and children should have a strong intent to pursue  
shastriya samskritam and samskriti.  
  
FEE: This is a dharmic institution. \*No fee\* is charged. Voluntary  
donations are accepted as \*guru dhakshina\*.  
  
ADMISSIONS: ONLY AFTER a screening interview of the child and parents.  
Academic year begins in June 2026. Recommend parents to visit and witness  
the operations of the gurukulam \*before April 22, 2026\* to get the  
experience.
