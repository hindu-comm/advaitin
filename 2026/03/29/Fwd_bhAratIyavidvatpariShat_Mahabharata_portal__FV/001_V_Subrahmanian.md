Namaste,

  

The launch of the Mahabharata, along with Nilakantha's commentary, on the Internet on April 5th 2026, was a momentous event, for me personally. It is available at

[sanatana.in/mahabharata](http://sanatana.in/mahabharata)

Any work of this magnitude requires enormous support and Sanatana Sampatti Foundation ([sanatanasampatti.in](http://sanatanasampatti.in)) was blessed to receive a generous support from the Hindu Fund. There is a continuous need for funds to support this kind of work and we would be grateful for help in this direction from members of BVP - [https://sanatanasampatti.in/Donate](https://sanatanasampatti.in/Donate).

In these troubled times for the world, it is appropriate to recall one of the lines from the mangalacharana shlokas of Nilakantha Chaturdhara in the beginning of his Bharatabhavadeepa:

एषा महाभारततीर्थगङ्गा लोकत्रयीजाड्यमलं धुनोतु ॥

  

Regards. . . .yoga
