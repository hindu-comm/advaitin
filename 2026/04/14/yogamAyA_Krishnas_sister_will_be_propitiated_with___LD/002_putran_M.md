Namaskaram,

  

We can interpret that Devi reveals Herself to us in manner suitable to our upadhis, knowledge and dharma, and accepts the worship that we consider best, if we are sincere.

  

But ahimsa is a samanya dharma. There is virtue in pursuing it in general unless one's visesha dharma contradicts it. So if we have knowledge that much and that killing an animal constitutes himsa, then best to assume Devi should not be worshipped by us in such a manner. Doing so likely indicates ulterior desires and motivations that are not conducive to bhakti.

  

thollmelukaalkizhu
