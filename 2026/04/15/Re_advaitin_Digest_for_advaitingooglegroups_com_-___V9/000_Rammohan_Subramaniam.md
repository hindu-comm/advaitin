Hari Om Putran Ji

Namaskrams!  
Quote:  
We can interpret that Devi reveals Herself to us in manner suitable to our  
upadhis, knowledge and dharma, and accepts the worship that we consider  
best, if we are sincere.  
  
But ahimsa is a samanya dharma. There is virtue in pursuing it in general  
unless one's visesha dharma contradicts it. So if we have knowledge that  
much and that killing an animal constitutes himsa, then best to assume Devi  
should not be worshipped by us in such a manner. Doing so likely indicates  
ulterior desires and motivations that are not conducive to bhakti.

Unquote

You have put forward a very important observation. It is the right approach to Bhakti

and specially if one is aiming for Ananaya Bhakti, which is gained when the mind becomes extremely

Sattvic. In that state 'Ahimsa' becomes automatic. This 'Ahimsa' is not an acquired trait but a natural

expression of infinite compassion towards all Jiva that emerges in one's heart.

  

Thank you for sharing

  

**Om and with Prem**

**Rammohan**
