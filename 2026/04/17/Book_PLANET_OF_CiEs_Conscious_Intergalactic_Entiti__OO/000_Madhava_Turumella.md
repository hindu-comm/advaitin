Dear Members,

I have been following the discussions in this group with great interest. The depth of engagement with Advaita Vedānta here is rare, and it is precisely this level of inquiry that encouraged me to reach out.

I would like to introduce my recent work, *PLANET OF CiEs (Conscious Intergalactic Entities)*. The book attempts to explore reincarnation, karma, and the non-dual nature of existence through a structured framework that bridges traditional Advaitic insights with a more contemporary, analytical lens.

At its core, the work proposes that what we perceive as individual life forms are manifestations of a deeper, unified field of consciousness, aligning with the Advaitic view of a single underlying reality. The framework examines how karma operates within this field and how repeated entanglements may be interpreted as cycles of reincarnation.

To support this exploration, I have included a range of embedded references and video materials that examine documented cases and phenomena often associated with reincarnation. My intention is not to impose conclusions, but to present patterns and invite critical examination.

I would genuinely value the perspective of this group. If you are open to it, I would be grateful if some of you could read the book and share your thoughts, critiques, or disagreements. A rigorous Advaitic lens would be especially valuable in testing the strength of the ideas presented.
