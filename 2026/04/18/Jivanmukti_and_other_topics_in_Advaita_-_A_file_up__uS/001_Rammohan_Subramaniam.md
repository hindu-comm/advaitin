Well crafted & presented will be useful as a reference for all. Just adding a supplementary note .  

  

One way to describe Jivan Mukti is to consider a situation of a Waker entering & remaining in a dream, with the *complete understanding* that he is Waker enclosed in the "thought" of a dreamer (identity) and likewise many other "thoughts" about the dream world, linked to this identity-thought. Seeing them all as mere thought projections even when dream is in progress is Jivan Mukti state for the Dreamer.  
  
This may help our contemplation.  
  
Shri Rammohan
