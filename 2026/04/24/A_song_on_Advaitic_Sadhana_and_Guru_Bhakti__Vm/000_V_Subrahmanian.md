Here is a clear English translation, with each verse kept separate as requested:  
  
---  
  
At the Guru’s feet, the mind has not merged; birth has not ceased.  
That steady bliss of liberation has not been attained; the sinner does not understand.  
  
---  
  
Do not create a difference between the individual soul and Śiva—do not see it so.  
Do not dwell in actions with such notions; do not wander about.  
  
---  
  
The delusion of duality has not gone; birth has not ended.  
Service at the Guru’s feet has not reached its culmination; one has not become Brahman.  
  
---  
  
What if one forgets oneself? What if one purifies the body? What if one smears ash?  
What if one practices various forms of meditation? What if one studies the Vedas?  
  
---  
  
Once name and form are given up, there is no anger, no merit or sin.  
In that Supreme Brahman there is no suffering; all superimposition is gone.  
  
---  
  
After realizing the Truth, there is no code of conduct, no caste or lineage.  
When the unreal has vanished, there is no prescribed way, no fear of the world.  
  
---  
  
One must know “Who am I?”; distinctions must dissolve.  
The sense of “I-ness” must end; birth must come to an end.  
  
---  
  
Without knowledge, what use is mere silence? What use are japa and meditation?  
After becoming one with oneself, what need is there for honor or for bathing in the Ganges?  
  
---  
  
When there is no marking (of distinction), there is no worry; there is no other peace.  
When delusion has gone, there is no path; everything is of one’s own nature.  
  
---  
  
After becoming one with the body (as the Self), there is no break, no association with sin.  
When the body itself is transcended, there is no Ranga, no Narasiṃha (no separate deities).  
  
---  
  
When doubt has dissolved, there is no body-consciousness, no awareness of the world.  
Though one appears in the world, one is not bound by it.  
  
---  
  
If one places one’s head at the feet of Guru Śaṅkarārya,  
one will not be born again on this earth—  
one will merge in the Supreme, merge in the Supreme.
