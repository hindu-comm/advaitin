> explicitly accepted the Sharabha (Śarabha) avatar of Bhagavān Śiva?  

  

This selection from a chapter of the Prapanchasara, widely attributed to Shankaracharya, includes a Shiva stuti. In one of the verses Shankara alludes to the episode in the Kaalikapurana that details Shiva taking the Sharabha form to contain a Varaha form of Vishnu under certain compulsions. The article is available here for download:

  

  

[https://adbhutam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/shankara-sharabhadocx.pdf](https://adbhutam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/shankara-sharabhadocx.pdf)

  

Also, there is a Sharabha Upanishad, which is part of the 108 listed in the Muktikopanishad. An Advaitin, a contemporary of Sri Muthuswamy Dikshitar, Thyagaraja and Shyama Sastrigal, **Sri Upanishad Brahmayogin** has commented on all the 108. You can find the link, on the right panel here,
[https://www.vyasaonline.com/sharabha-upanishad/](https://www.vyasaonline.com/sharabha-upanishad/)
