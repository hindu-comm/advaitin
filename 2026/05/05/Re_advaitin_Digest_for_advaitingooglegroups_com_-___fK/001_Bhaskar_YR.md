Further it would be good to recognize 'Shudra' may be referring to "characteristic of the Mind based on its texture and the activities one engages oneself with" rather than the caste one is born into.



- Though we can agree and argue that varNa ( or caste in lose terms) is
  based on guNa-karma, it is quite evident in our smruti and
  mythological texts insist more on birth based caste. karNa (daasi
  putra), vAlmeeki (hunter), vyAsa (fisherman), shambuka & guha in
  rAmAyaNa though they were spiritually evolved souls we do recognize
  them based on their birth not by guNa nor by karma.
