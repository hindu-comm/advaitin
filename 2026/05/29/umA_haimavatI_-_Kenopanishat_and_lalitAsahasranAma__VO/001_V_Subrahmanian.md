> > Umā Haimavatī is the expression in the Kenopanishad 3.2.  
> >   
> > शोभमानामुमां हैमवतीं ...  
> >   
> > Shankara explains the term Haimavati as follows: 1. One who is
> > adorned with  
> > golden ornaments and 2. as the daughter of Himavān and the consort
> > of the  
> > Omniscient Iśwara.  
> >   
> > In the Lalitāsahasranāma (LS), it is noteworthy, these two distinct
> > names  

> > appear in the \*same sequence \*as in the Kenopanishad:

> >   
> >   
> > उमा शैलेन्द्रतनया - Umā, Śailendratanayā. This latter name means "the  
> > daughter of the Lord of mountains, Himavān."  
> >   
> > In the Upanishad the name Haimavatī is an adjective to Umā while in
> > the LS,  
> > the two are distinct names.
