(Around the beginning of the month, a nugget of Wisdom from the Jagadguru may be posted  
on the Chaturamnaya list : [http://lists.advaita-vedanta.org/cgi-bin/listinfo/chaturamnaya](http://lists.advaita-vedanta.org/cgi-bin/listinfo/chaturamnaya) )  
  
  
It would be a tremendous shortcoming if man, no matter how  
learned or authoritative, does not have the right company. Satsanga  
(association with the noble) is extremely essential to lead a  
meaningful life.  
  
A person in satsanga will utilise his education, authority and  
wealth in a righteous manner and attain sreyas (higher good).  
Subsequent generations will glorify him and progress in their lives  
by emulating his virtues.  
  
Association with the wicked will only ruin man's wealth and  
knowledge. Such associations will not generate any righteous  
thoughts in man's mind and, harbouring only unwanted thoughts,  
he will squander away his wealth and knowledge through wrong  
path. That is why our forefathers have instructed us in varied ways  
to ever remain in satsanga,  
  
असज्जनेन सम्पर्कादनयं यान्ति साधवः ।  
मधुरं शीतलं तोयं पावकं प्राप्य तप्यते \|  
  
It means that through association with the wicked, even a noble  
one may turn unrighteous, in the same way as cold water, though  
sweet in taste, turns unpleasant and hot when exposed to heat.  
  
Our blessings to everyone to understand this well and be in  
satsanga always.
