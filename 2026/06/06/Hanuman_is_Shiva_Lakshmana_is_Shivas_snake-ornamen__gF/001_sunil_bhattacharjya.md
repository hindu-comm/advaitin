Dear Subbuji,

  

I read it in a somewhat different way. Lord Brahma came out of Lord Vishnu and he created the universe. After doing that LordBrahma requested Lord Shiva also to create and Lord Shiva created the eleven Rudras (i.e., the EkadashaRudras), who were all immortals. Then, Lord Brahma requested Lord Shiva not to create any more immortals. Hanumanji was the eleventh Rudra.

  

Ravana worshipped the Ten Rudras and ignored Hanuman. Lord Ram took the helpof Hanuman, during the Rama-Ravana war.

  

Regards,

SunilKB
