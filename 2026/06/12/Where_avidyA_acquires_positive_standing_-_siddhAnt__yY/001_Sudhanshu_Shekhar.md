Namaste Michael ji,

  

Please note:

  

1\. avidyā delimited by avidyā-tva is sākṣi-bhāsya, anubhavaika-gamya etc.

  

2\. avidyā delimited by bhāvarūpatva, anāditva, jñāna-nivartyatva is pramāṇa-gamya. The pramāṇa-vyāpāra is not for establishing viśeṣya-avidyā, which is sākṣi-bhāsya. The pramāṇa-vyāpāra is for viśeṣaṇa of avidyā i.e. bhāvarūpatva etc.

  

This is as clear a presentation as it can get. This is what SB and Vivaraṇa say.

  

> *avidyā na pramāṇagamyā, kintu anubhavaikagamyā* (Sugamā). "Ignorance
> is not accessible to any pramāṇa, but is grasped by experience alone."

SSSS ji did not appreciate the difference between avidyā-delimited-by-avidyātva vis-a-vis avidyā-delimited-by-bhāvarūpatva.

  

> §55 makes a different kind of move:

  

It is not different kind of move. It is for emphasising the different delimiting factor of avidyā which requires pramāṇa.

  

> And this is not of the nature of mere absence (*abhāva-rūpa*)."

This requires pramāṇa.

> So across this seam: §52–54 leave avidyā unnamed — on SSS's reading,
> the mere *anubhava-siddha* confusion; §55 introduces it as a
> postulated cause (*kalpanīyam*) carrying pramāṇa-registered
> (*dharmi-grāhaka-māna*), witness-attested (*sākṣi-pratīti*),
> non-negative (*na abhāva-rūpa*) standing.

SSSS ji omitted the crucial distinction of diffedent delimiting factors of avidyā. And hence incorrect conclusions by him were derived.

> My question, held loosely: is that positive standing already required
> by the §52–54 diagnosis, so §55 only makes it explicit — or is the
> *kalpanīyam* of §55 the genuine addition, the point at which avidyā
> passes from the *anubhava-siddha* name for the confusion to a posited
> positive cause the argument now needs? On SSS's reading of the
> *Adhyāsa-bhāṣya*, *adhyāsa* simply *is* what the learned call avidyā,
> and the closing characterization needs no further posited principle..

  

Hope it is clear.
