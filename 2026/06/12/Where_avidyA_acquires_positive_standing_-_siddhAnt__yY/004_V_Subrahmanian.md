Dear Sudhanshu ji,

  

Today, in the Siddhāntabindu class, at the Jnankura event of Aham Brahmasmi at Bangalore, the renowned Vidwan Sri Mani Dravid Sastri ji read out the statement regarding tamas (darkness) as a bhāvarupa entity. A short discussion ensued: We all experience that darkness 'sets in' upon sunset, and it's only gradually that we reach pitch darkness. Sastriji immediately said, 'The very use of adjectives like \*mandha-andhakāra\* and \*gāḍha-andhakāra\* makes it a \*bhāva padārtha\*, as we cannot have adjectives for anything that is fundamentally \*abhāva\*.
