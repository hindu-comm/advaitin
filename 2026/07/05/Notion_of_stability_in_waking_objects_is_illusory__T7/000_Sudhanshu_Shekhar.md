Hari om,

  

In dreams, even though the objects are momentary, there appears a notion of permanency/stability/ancient-ness (cirantanatva) in them. When we see a mountain in dream, we perceive them to be there from immemorial past. We do not qeustion that. We don't treat them as momentary.

  

***Similar is the case for objects in this so called waking state. This is the cornerstone of Advaita Vedānta.***

  

Sri Ramana Maharshi says:

  

Permanency and constancy in the world is seen on account of wrong ideas. When someone says that he took a bath in the same river twice he is wrong, because when he bathed for the second time the river is not the same as it was when he bathed for the first time. On looking twice at the brightness of a flame a man says that he sees the same flame, but this flame is changing every moment. The waking state is like this. The stationary appearance is an error of perception.

  

\[Be As You Are\]

  

Vicāra Sāgara says in page 218:

  

स्वप्ने तत्क्षणोत्पन्नेषु पदार्थेषु इमे गिरिनदीसमुद्रादयो मम जन्मनः प्रागेवोत्पन्ना वर्षसहस्रकालादारभ्य वर्तन्ते इति प्रतीतिर्भवति केषांचित् । तत्र तत्क्षणोत्पन्ने स्वाप्नपदार्थे या चिरन्तनत्वप्रतीतिः सा भ्रान्तिरेव । तस्माद्ययाऽविद्यया स्वप्ने मिथ्याभूताः गिरिनदीसमुद्रादयः पदार्था उत्पादिता: तथैवाविद्यया तत्तत्पदार्थानां चिरन्तनत्वं तादृश चिरन्तनत्व-विषयकज्ञानं चानिर्वचनीयतयोत्पाद्यते । एवमेव जाग्रत्पदार्थेषु वस्तुतश्चिरन्तनत्वाभावेऽपि अविद्यावशादेव तत्तत्पदार्थैः सहैव मिथ्याभूतं स्थिरत्वमप्यनिर्वचनीतयोत्पद्यते । अतश्च जाग्रत्पदार्थानामपि स्थिरत्वप्रतीतिर्भ्रान्तिरेव ।

  

For some people, when objects are instantaneously created in a dream, a perception arises that "these mountains, rivers, oceans, etc., were created even before my birth and have existed for thousands of years." In that situation, the perception of long-standing existence (ancientness) in a dream object that was actually created at that very moment is nothing but an illusion.

  

Therefore, just as the false objects like mountains, rivers, and oceans are produced in a dream by ignorance (Avidya), in the exact same way, the long-standing nature of those respective objects, as well as the knowledge regarding such long-standing nature, is produced in an indefinable (Anirvacaniya) manner by ignorance.

  

In this very manner, even though there is actually no real long-standing existence in waking-state objects, a false stability/permanence is produced along with those respective objects in an indefinable manner, purely due to the power of ignorance. Consequently, the perception of stability/permanence in waking-state objects is also nothing but an illusion.
