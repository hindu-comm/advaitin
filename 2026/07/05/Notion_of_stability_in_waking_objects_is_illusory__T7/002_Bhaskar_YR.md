Hare Krishna



Accepting the Ishwara srushti in Advaita vedAnta as per bhagavatpAda is ‘vedAnta maryAda’, I repeat it_is_vedAnta maryAda. Yes the similarity between waking and dreaming states reiterated at various places in PTB but it does not anyway mean that Ishwara srushti is just like snake on the rope, it only (the similarity between jAgrat and Svapna) conveys the fact that our svarUpa is avasthAteeta and it pervades all avasthA-s in ekarUpa. The similarity between waking and dream states does not deny the creation of **Ishvara** (the Creator) at any stretch of our imagination in bhagavatpAda’s theistic Advaita vedanta. That is the reason why bhagavatpAda states what we see in the waking world is a projection of Ishvara’s mind (brahma mAnasa pratyaya), where as Svapna is an individual mind’s projection (jeeva maanasa pratyaya). One is vyAvahArika another is prAtibhAsika. Shankara states elsewhere that there is not even an iota of pAramArthika in individual’s dream world whereas for this vyAvahArika jagat brahman is the ONLY abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa. Shankara asks us : do I have to tell you again & again that Ishwara who is nitya Shuddha 

possesses nitya jnAna about the srushti-sthiti & laya??



bhAshyakAra, in sUtra bhAshya, also particularly emphasizes that the tiny jeeva who is not srushtikarta Ishwara is not at all capable of creating the many and varied names and forms of

mountains, rivers, oceans and the like. For that matter even the siddha-s who have acquired the siddhis (supernatural powers) like
aNimAdi ashta siddhi-s etc., cannot effect the manifestation of the creation and manage its affairs!! Creation if at all there is any talk about creation is an exclusive power of Ishwara.



To KISS : Comparing jAgrat prapancha to a Svapna prapancha doesn't say Ishwara’s creation is fake like rope on the snake (vide chAndOgya), but rather points out that it isn't absolutely real and nirvikAri brahman.
But at the same time vyAkruta prapancha which was/is/will always be one with brahman in avyAkruta rUpa is NOT different from brahman. Though waking world changing each and every minute it is in it’s svarUpa brahman only and nothing but brahman.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
