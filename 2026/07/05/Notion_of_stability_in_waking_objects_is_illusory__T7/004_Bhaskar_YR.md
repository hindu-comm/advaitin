praNAms Sri Subbu prabhuji

Hare Krishna



What you say about Ishwara's unique power is not denied and Shankara's great regard for that power.



- Kindly elaborate in what sense Ishwara’s unique power which is NOT
  possessed by any normal jeeva or even siddha-s, when there is
  absolutely no difference between Ishwara’s mAyA srushti and jeeva’s
  avidyA parikalpita drushti or srushti!!??



However, Shankara does not differentiate the Ishwara-created world from the jiva-created dream:



Ø I have already said creation by Ishwara and accepting the Ishwara hetukatva is vedAnta maryAda. Yes as far as the ‘creation’ as a common word is concerned there is no difference between Ishwara’s and that of jeeva’s. But Ishwara’s creation is at macro level (samashti) it is meant for the jeeva-s. And for this creation Ishwara himself is the abhinna nimittOpadAna kAraNa. It is governed, managed and maintained by omniscient and omnipotent Ishwara himself for the sake of jeeva-s and this is the stage on which Jeevas will perform actions and experience the results of their respective karma.

**Nor, indeed, does even the creation beginning with space possess absolute reality**.

- Yes we have already seen this we are not saying creation is absolute
  reality. But this vyAvahArika jagat is NOT independent from that
  pAramArthika reality. But by this we cannot deny the role of srushti
  by Ishwara. In the saNdhyAdhikaraNa which you have quoted, itself
  bhAshyakAra clearly differentiate the Ishwara srushti jAgrat prapanch
  and jeeva’s srushti Svapna prapancha and clarifies that the srushti of
  Ishwara is universally perceived in the waking state such as AkAsha
  etc. and it is objective whereas the dream creation is not objective
  and it is not publicly on the common platform transactionable. And in
  that sence the Svapna prapancha, the dream world is indeed unreal.
  There is not even the smell of reality in it.



For it has already been established in the aphorism **'tad-ananyatvam ārambhaṇa-śabdādibhyaḥ'** (Brahma Sūtra 2.1.14) **that the entire phenomenal universe is nothing but māyā.**

- And that mAya (shakti) is not different from Ishwara (shakta).

However, prior to the realization that Brahman is the Self, the empirical universe beginning with space appears as a well-established and orderly reality. The dream-world, on the other hand, is sublated every single day upon waking. Therefore, it is only in this special sense that the dream has been declared to be merely an appearance produced by māyā.

- Yes, that is the reason why shankara’s Advaita reiterates that there
  is sarvashakta and sarvajna Ishwara who is having the exclusive power
  of creating this phenomenal world. And he does so for the sake of
  jeeva-s karma bhOga and apavarga. This is the justification given for
  the Ishwara’s vishama srushti.

And Shankara says in the BGB 2.16:

**(Verse 16 Commentary)**

The intention is this: You too, Arjuna, **adopting the standpoint of the knowers of Truth (tattva-darśins),** should abandon grief and delusion.

Ø Can be noted here it is all about Arjuna’s rAga-dvesha-bhrAnti etc. It is merely problem of ours seeing the snake (jeeva srushti) in place of rope (Ishwara srushti) and deluded. For jeeva’s avidyA srushti (seeing the snake, resultant suffering like fear etc.) is due to mAya srushti, the rope which is having its svarUpa as brahman. Tattva drushti is sama drushti…vidyAvinaya sampanne brAhmaNe gavihastini etc. seeing the brahman in everything is sama drushti / Samyak jnAna seeing the srushti independently from brahman is avidyA drushti or mithyA drushti.
