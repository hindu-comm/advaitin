Dear Bhaskar ji,

  

I wish to state the following points:  
  
1. In the Sandhyādhikaranam itself, Shankara says, 'The entire creation, including the dream and waking states, is māyāmātram. Note that the sutra used this term only for the svapna.

  

2\. In 2.16
नासतोविद्यतेभावोनाभावोविद्यतेसतः।उभयोरपिदृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः॥ १६ ॥ bhashya Shankara does not differentiate between jiva srushti and Ishwara srishti. He says: all \*vikāras\* found or experienced in creation are \*mithyā\*.
नहिशीतोष्णादिसकारणंप्रमाणैर्निरूप्यमाणंवस्तुसद्भवति।विकारोहिसः,विकारश्चव्यभिचरति।यथाघटादिसंस्थानंचक्षुषानिरूप्यमाणंमृद्व्यतिरेकेणानुपलब्धेरसत्,तथासर्वोविकारःकारणव्यतिरेकेणानुपलब्धेरसन्।
Cold, heat, etc, are not jiva's creation; they are in the prakriti. He uses the example of a pot and clay.
तस्माद्देहादेःद्वन्द्वस्यचसकारणस्यअसतोनविद्यतेभावइति।तथासतश्चआत्मनःअभावःअविद्यमानतानविद्यते,सर्वत्रअव्यभिचारात्इतिअवोचाम॥
The body, etc. duality is asat, mithya and Brahman, Sat, alone is satyam, he says.

  

3\. In the G.Karika we have: मृल्लोहविस्फुलिङ्गाद्यैः सृष्टिर्या चोदितान्यथा।उपायः सोऽवताराय नास्ति भेदः कथञ्चन ॥ १५ ॥

अन्यार्थत्वादुत्पत्तिश्रुतीनाम्।He says the srishti vakyas have a different purport/purpose. The utpatti shrutis (giving examples like clay or metal) have a different purport. For instance, the Chandogya passage, 'tadaikshata bahu syaam...' indicates Ishwara's sankalpa is also present. But Shankara says:यदि हि वादः परमार्थ एवाभूत् , एकरूप एव संवादः सर्वशाखास्वश्रोष्यत, विरुद्धानेकप्रकारेण नाश्रोष्यत ; श्रूयते तु ; तस्मान्न तादर्थ्यं संवादश्रुतीनाम्। तथोत्पत्तिवाक्यानि प्रत्येतव्यानि। He gives the example of prana samvada that is told in different ways in different Upanishads, and says: **If srishti was real, the Upanishads would have stated it in the same way everywhere;** but it is not so, they talk of srishti, method, in different ways. **So, it's not with a view to teach us that 'srishti happened,' but rather to teach us Advaita.** Thus, even based on the Ishwara srishti vakyas, with sankalpa, sarvajnatva, sarvashaktitva, karma phala daatrutva, etc., **Shankara/Gaudapada do not accept that creation ever occurred.** For them it's ajātivāda, no-creation. The talk about srishti in shrutis is a thesis.
