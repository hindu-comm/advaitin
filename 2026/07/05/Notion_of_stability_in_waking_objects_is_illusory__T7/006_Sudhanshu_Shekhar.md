हरे कृष्ण भास्कर प्रभु जी, सुब्बु जी।

  

It is the considered position of sampradāyavit ācāryās that distinction of waking as Īśvara-sṛṣṭi and swapna as jīva-sṛṣṭi is upheld in bhāṣya within the purview of SDV. However, the identity of waking and dream is the hallmark of DSV.

  

Let us see some of the bhāṣya-vākya from Māṇḍūkyà wherein waking is equated completely to dream:

  

स्वप्नदृश्यवज्जागरितदृश्यानामप्यसत्त्वमिति …. अतो मन्यामहे तेषामप्यसत्त्वं स्वप्नदृश्यवदनाशङ्कनीयमिति । \[2.7\]

  

Just as dream-seen-objects are non-existent (asat), similarly waking-seen-objects are non-existent….. Therefore, we hold that one should not doubt that (how can) waking-seen-objects are non-existent like dream-seen-objects.

  

अतः कल्पिता एव जाग्रद्भावा अपि स्वप्नभाववदिति सिद्धम् ॥ \[2.15\]

  

Therefore, it is established that objects-in-waking-world are imaginary just as the objects-in-dream-world.

  

बाह्यं पृथिव्यादि तत्त्वमाध्यात्मिकं च देहादिलक्षणं रज्जुसर्पादिवत्स्वप्नमायादिवच्च असत् । \[2.38\]

  

The external elements like prithivī etc and internal elements like body etc are non-existent like illusory-snake-in-the-rope, dream and Māyā.

  

न हि स्वप्ने हस्ती हस्तिचित्तं वा विद्यते ; तथा इहापि विवेकिनामित्यभिप्रायः। \[4.67\]

  

In a dream, there exists neither the elephant nor the citta-of-elephant. Similarly, here also is the case for people with discrimination. This is the purport

  

स्वप्नमायानिर्मितका अण्डजादयो जीवा यथा जायन्ते म्रियन्ते च, तथा मनुष्यादिलक्षणा अविद्यमाना एव चित्तविकल्पनामात्रा इत्यर्थः ॥ \[4.70\]

  

Māyāmayaḥ (of 4.69) means that which is created by a magician. Nirmitakaḥ (of 4.70) means that which is created by mantra and medicine. Such jīvās created by dream, magic, medicine or charms, born of eggs etc take birth and die. Similarly, these jīvās such as humans etc are non-existent and are merely the vibrations of mind.

  

One has to reconcile such statements with statements such as वैधर्म्यात् च न स्वप्नादिवत्।

  

The way of reconciliation in sampradāya is through explicit postulation of different prakriyā namely SDV and DSV for a sādhaka at different stages of his spiritual evolution. I find it to be completely convincing.
