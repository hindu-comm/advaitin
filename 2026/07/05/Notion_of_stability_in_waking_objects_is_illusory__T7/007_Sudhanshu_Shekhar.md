Hari Om,

  

If we see the following at page 189 from Māṇḍūkya Rahasya Vivṛti of SSSS ji, it is clear that he is also saying the same.

  

***स्वप्नजागरयोर्वैधर्म्याभावः*** --

  

नन्वन्तःस्थानादिभिर्हेतुभिस्त्वयैव जागरितवैलक्षण्यं स्वप्रस्य प्रदर्शितम् । बाध्यमानत्वं चाशक्यापह्नवं स्वप्रस्य । *तत्कथमिदानीमात्थ सर्वथा साम्येन सममेवोभयोर्वैतथ्यमिति* ? अत्रोच्यते \| *सत्यमुक्तं वैलक्षण्यम्* । ***तत्तु जागरितदृष्ट्या मन्दबुद्धीनां परमार्थप्रतिपत्त्युपायत्वेन, न वस्तुदृष्ट्या*** \| *वस्तुतस्तु नायमपि विशेषो विद्यते* । *यतोऽयमेव श्लोकः स्वप्नजागरितयोः सर्वसमताप्रतिपादनपरत्वेनापि योजयितुं शक्यते ।*

*  *

Translation by Gemini (Checked and concurred by me)

***  ***

***The Absence of Heterogeneity (Difference) Between the Dream and Waking States***:

  

(Objection): By citing reasons such as "being confined to the internal space," you yourself have previously demonstrated that the dream state is different from the waking state. Furthermore, the fact that dreams are sublated (contradicted upon waking) cannot be denied. So, how can you now claim that both states are entirely identical and that their unreality is exactly the same?

  

(Reply): To this, it is said in response: What you say about their difference is true. However, that difference was stated from the viewpoint of the waking state for the benefit of less intelligent minds (dull-witted persons) as a means to help them understand the ultimate reality—not from the standpoint of truth.

  

In reality, even this distinction does not exist. Because this very verse can also be applied to establish the complete equality (identity) between the dream and waking states.

==================================

  

Here, SSSS ji is accepting that the distinction of waking and dream is stated in shāstra for manda-buddhi people for their evolution. So, for followers of SSSS ji, this should be a point of consideration to revisit their penchant with Īśvara-sṛṣṭi etc.

  

Regarding usage by SSSS ji of vastu-dṛṣṭyā, it should be understood that in Brahman, there is neither waking nor dream. So, the question of their identity or dissimilarity does not arise.

  

So, basically, it is an advanced knowledge. Both DSV and SDV are teachings to the very same jīva. In one, he is given the concession and his belief in the distinction of waking and dream is allowed. In DSV, his this belief is shattered and he remains alone - in his own glory - and as Buddha said -- walks alone like a rhino. एको चरे खग्गविसाणकप्पो।
