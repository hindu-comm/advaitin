Hare Krishna



Yes, paramArtha jnAni is Bhagavan (god) himself, when he has the realization that he is himself that secondless brahman why not he is God!!?? Interestingly, we address Sri Ramana maharshi as bhagavAn Shri Ramana maharshi whereas while addressing Sri Shankarachaarya we say “ ‘bhagavatpAda’ shankarachaarya. Let it be anything, the someone’s view point is that since we are addressing the ‘jnAni’ by pointing our fingers to some individual, that individual jnAni must be in ‘his’ individual BMI, as a result he is not brahman but jnAni mAtra and still carrying the avidyAlesha and under the influence of his own prArabdha karma phala. Note, as per them, it is not just in the eyes of bystanders but it is the real fate of that jnAni.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
