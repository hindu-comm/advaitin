Dear Putran ji,

  

Yes, Swamiji alludes to the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad and the Bhashyam, which discuss Chaitanyam and how it can't be pointed out, like saying, 'this is a cow.' The only method, therefore, is 'neti neti'—excluding everything that is not Chaitanyam.  
  
Then he dwells upon how Shankara incorporated this method alone in many of his other texts like Dashashloki and Ekashloki.

  

Swamiji says: If science can arrive at the existence of Chaitanyam through computers and AI, the great benefit would be also that all who consider science the leading domain would walk the path of righteousness. He bases this on the Taittiriya statement: "asan eva sa bhavati, asad brahmeti veda chet." He who denies God/Ishwara/Chaitanyam, himself becomes non-existent, in the sense that he does not follow the path of purushartha. Conversely, if one accepts Chaitanyam, they attain the supreme.  
  
Swamiji is very positive and open to the role of Science and AI in arriving at the existence of Consciousness.

  

Warm regards

subbu
