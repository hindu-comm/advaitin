Hare Krishna,

  

Explanation by SSSS ji in Alātaśānti prakaraṇa, 4.63-66 may be perused which is reproduced below.

  

He has plain accepted, following bhāṣya, that whatever is seen during waking state is nothing but mind, just like in dream.

  

Bhāṣya says - जाग्रतो दृश्या जीवाः तच्चित्ताव्यतिरिक्ताः, चित्तेक्षणीयत्वात् , स्वप्नदृक्चित्तेक्षणीयजीववत् । तच्च जीवेक्षणात्मकं चित्तं द्रष्टुरव्यतिरिक्तं द्रष्टृदृश्यत्वात् स्वप्नचित्तवत् । उक्तार्थमन्यत् ॥

  

No one should confuse that citta here should refer to ātmā, because citta is being stated in bhāṣya as dṛśya-of-draṣṭā. So, it is nothing but mind. Just as the dream-world and dream-jīvās are nothing but mind, similarly in waking. This is the clear articulation through anumāna by ācārya. जाग्रतो दृश्या जीवाः तच्चित्ताव्यतिरिक्ताः, चित्तेक्षणीयत्वात् , स्वप्नदृक्चित्तेक्षणीयजीववत् .

  

  
![](https://groups.google.com/group/advaitin/attach/15356f720c180/1784003351959.jpg?part=0.1&view=1)  
  

SSSS ji accepts it plainly as follows:

  
![](https://groups.google.com/group/advaitin/attach/15356f720c180/1000477384.jpg?part=0.2&view=1)  
  

Thus, it is clear that SSSS ji has no qualms accepting that this world and the jīvās-seen-in-world are nothing but mind alone.

  

If he holds elsewhere that world is not mind, then he is self-contradicting. His followers may take pains to reconcile.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
