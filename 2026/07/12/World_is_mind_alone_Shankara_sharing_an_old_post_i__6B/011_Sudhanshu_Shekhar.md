Hari Om Michael ji/Bhaskar ji/Subbu ji/Raghav ji,

  

There are two limbs.

  

One -- this world/jīvās is product of mind.

  

Second -- this mind is non-existent.

  

By logic, as Śankara proves in kārikā bhāṣya - जाग्रतो दृश्या जीवाः तच्चित्ताव्यतिरिक्ताः, चित्तेक्षणीयत्वात् , स्वप्नदृक्चित्तेक्षणीयजीववत् । - it is clear whatever is seen, be it table, chair or other jīvās, are all nothing but citta.

  

This proves the first limb. The basic logic is -- being seen by citta.

  

For second limb, citta is stated to be "seen of seer" -- and hence citta is non-separate from seer. This means, both jīva and citta are seen by each other. And just as in a dream, there exists neither the elephant nor the elephant-citta, similarly in waking. Jīva and citta, being seen of each other, neither of the two exist.

  

This is the sum and substance. Most certainly, one does not stop by saying that world is product of mind/ignorance. One immediately adds -- sā ca māyā na vidyate. This mind/ignorance/māyā doesn't exist.

  

This is what is mithyātva. Appears but exists not.

  

This is actually very straightforward, like gooseberry on palm. This much literature is not needed.. it is plain and clear.. just take dream example and in a flash one understands.

  

See how simply Śankara says -- न हि स्वप्ने हस्ती हस्तिचित्तं वा विद्यते ; तथा इहापि विवेकिनामित्यभिप्रायः । Just as neither elephant nor elephant-citta existed in dream, similarly even now in waking, neither elephant (jīva) nor elephant-citta exist.

  

There can remain no doubt after such clear statements unless one has take a vow to not understand. That come what may, I will not understand. 🙂

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
