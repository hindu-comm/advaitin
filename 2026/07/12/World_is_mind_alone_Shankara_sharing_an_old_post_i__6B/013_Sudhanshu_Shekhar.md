You agree that neither elephant nor elephant-citta existed in dream. You agree that neither elephant nor elephant-citta exists in waking.

  

Now, you saw Michael and Michael-citta in dream. Like elephant and elephant-citta, they did not exist in dream. Similarly, neither Michael nor Michael-citta exist in waking.

  

> GK 4.67's own conclusion is not "citta is unreal absolutely"; it is
> *citta-dṛśyavac cittam api naivāsti paramārthataḥ* — non-existent
> *like its object*, i.e. at the object's level, the level at which
> elephant and elephant-citta stood as a seen pair. What survives is one

Nothing survived sir except the ultimate reality which is present in both dream and waking. That is not one level up. That is the only reality on which elephant and elephant-citta of both waking and dream are non-existent appearances.

> *draṣṭur ekatvam* — the oneness of the seer — which is precisely why
> the chapter's own summary word is *draṣṭā*, not *cittam*, and why
> 4.67's close is *jāgrat-svapnayor ubhayor api dṛṣṭr-aikatvaṃ tu
> vaitathye pratipāditam* — sameness of the seer *established through*
> vaitathya, the negation being the route, not the terminus.

Sir, whether it is waking-citta or dream-citta, it is held to be on the same footing as elephant-citta, all non-existent appearances. This is being shown on the force of anumāna, where the hetu is "seen by draṣṭā".

  

First, one needs to arrive at this. If one is fine with that, obviously it transpires that the draṣṭā is the sole reality which sees, always sees. Merely sees. Whether it is waking-citta or dream-citta or whatever is seen, they are all non-existent.

> That is the whole of my point, restated once more since it seems not

> without being — I have granted this from the first reply in this

Other followers of SSSS ji like Bhaskar ji etc don't agree. Both of you can sit down and decide what is it that SSSS ji holds. Both of you claim to be disciple of SSSS ji with no coherence. He would reject your claim that citta is mithyā i.e. appearance without being.

> What I have not granted, because the texts you are citing do not say

Sir, ācārya says - whatever is seen by citta are not at all different from citta. What does this mean? Please read 4.64 to 4.66. What do you mean? The dream-world with elephants and tables and elephant-citta and rivers and moutanins is not mind? Then what is it? An interaction with a young child of eight year may help one in this regard.

> The rope-snake needs no rope-substance called "mind" to be silver
> from; it needs a rope. Here the rope is the draṣṭā throughout, and
> citta — like the silver — is what is *negated*, not what does the
> *appearing*.

These are all useless stuff. Sitting in rope, you don't perceive rope-snake. Only while being in non-rope, you perceive rope-snake. Sitting in dṛśya, reality appears as draṣṭā. You need illusory-dṛśya to call the reality as draṣṭā. And the moment you have accepted illusion, you ipso facto need a mind/ignorance. The illusory rope-snake thus has to be nothing but mind/ignorance.

  

This means that draṣṭṛtva too is illusory. The reality i.e. rope is not draṣṭā but "draṣṭā without draṣṭṛtva".
