A very good initiative indeed. However could you think of using AI for this? Just now we have seen someone has made a voice output of the Mahabharata etc. if such a thing can be made for reading out the Prasthana Traya Bhashyam that would make the work much easier. But a lot of checking and verification will have to be done before it is put in public domain.

  

Several years back late Sri Sundar Hattangadi made his own voice recording of the Bhagavad Gita bhashya and put it on [archives.org](http://archives.org).  

  

About a year back a friend commenced recording in a studio his own reading of the Upanishad bhashya taking the blessings of the Sringeri Jagadguru. But it is not progressing. They encountered challenges in recording. Mistakes occur while reading out the words, splitting them etc. A tough job indeed for such a huge project. That is why I think AI could be a better option to achieve this.
