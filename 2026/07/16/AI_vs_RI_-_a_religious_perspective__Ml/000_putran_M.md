AI vs RI - a religious perspective.

  

AI is the simulation of intelligence in material objects by human engineering.

  

Sothere should be no confusion that the attribution of intelligence to a machine is indeed "artificial". Not real.

  

When do we call it "real intelligence"? When the reality of unchanging Consciousness-Self is affirmed as the adhishtanam of that Intelligence. Not the body-mind but the Self identified through that embodiment. Then we say: "ah, it is that (Self) that knows, understands, desires, determines and manifests anew".

  

Thus jiva is intelligent not because of but in spiteof the body-mind. Action and emotion are identified at the gross and subtle levels. At the causal level is Maya, the primal Knowledge-Intelligence, the shakti of Shiva. The jiva therefore is intelligent because its antaryami (inner reality) is Ishvara (Self+ Intelligence (Maya)). This fundamental Intelligence of Ishvara is manifest in the jiva as its jnana-iccha-kriya shakti.

  

When the jiva says "I am running", the running-action pertains to the body. When it says "I think", thinkingpertains to the mind. These attributions on Self are superimposition and are equivalent to imagination even from the causal standpoint, but they make sense with reference to the Self identified with body-mind upadhis at the gross-subtle level. "I am running" is valid vyavaharika usage so long as Self remains affirmed as the final adhistanam in the midst of upadhi-adhyasa. Because all cognition/imagination (running, thinking) is ultimately manifest-Maya, the projection of His Intelligence.

  

On the flip side, is it ok to say "the body runs", "the mind thinks"? Yes, if by "body", we realize that (at the causal level) we are referring to the Self that manifests as body and "its running". So "body runs" means "the atma that projects/identifies (or is identified) as body manifests/cognizes/enlightens the changes in the superimposed gross plane that constitute running". The atma that is manifest as mind manifests/enlightens the changes that constitute thinking.

  

Devoid of this affirmation of Self, the body-mind become nama-rupa jada identifications thathave zero potential/coherence to maintain identity or to manifest change and continuity. It is like watching the movie sun and saying it is shining light on the rest of the movie world. The causal "Intelligence" is at the Projector level. Saying I will not look beyond the movie objects (gross plane) will lead to ignorant or nonsensical conclusions.

  

Now let us cometo the question whether machines can be made really intelligent. First of all, as we said above, things exist because the adhishtanam is Real, and they as the identifications of the adhishtanam-Self are the projections/identifications by Him of Him through His Maya. One who knows this 'sees' Ishvara in (or appearing as) the jiva and the machine also, so such a person can say the machine is intelligent because he sees the Intelligence of Ishvara at play. However the ordinary person does not realize the causal adhishtanam level when he sees the machine; his knowledge is restricted according to the limited extent revealed at the gross-subtle levels. The knowledge revealed at this level is only "jada" and not chaitanya, the immediate reason being that the upadhis of a machine do not reflect the knowledge “I am”, which is the fundamental mark of Consciousness/Self and also the manifest-criterion for assigning it Intelligence.

  

Thus, though Self is evident and Intelligence is evident, we can't quite pinpoint them in the material object which resists the realization of a unitary adhishtanam and instead appears a composite of more jada parts.

  

This is not so in the jiva, because in the jiva, Chaitanyam is realized in spite of upadhi-adhyasa; the seen-playof intelligence is linked back to the Self being identified as that jiva. The jiva's upadhis allow for the holistic jnana-iccha-kriya manifestation of Intelligence, in particular the "I am" knowledge-reflection of Self shines in the background as the basis for uniting and centering all other cognitions in the Self.

  

Note this is a corollary of Ishvara's manifestation:

  

The projection of jiva includes the projection of its jnana of self and not-self, and the consequent associated projections of iccha and kriya alongside the changes in its upadhis. In the context of identification with jiva-upadhis, we say "mind is upadana for this, body does that" and so on. In the context of its conditioned self-knowledge, the jiva exhibits a fuller intelligence based in knowledge, and based in knowledge, it desires, acts, says "I think", "I run", and so on. (At the causal level, all duality reduces to simply Ishvara-Maya/Intelligence from which standpoint the manifestation of jiva-jagat is akin to imagination. And at the adhishtanam pure Consciousness-Self level, there is no duality to consider.)

  

The projection of jagat\* however is not accompanied with the key jnana-manifestations of Intelligence found in the jiva. The jagat upadhis preclude them\*\*; that is Ishvara srishti. Silicon cannot be moulded into the subtle mind. Therefore the machine can never be made intelligent in the same way a jiva is. We can simulate in machine an action-response mechanism that appears as-if equivalent or superior to human intelligence. But it will remain AI.
