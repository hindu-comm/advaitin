Namaskaram,

  

In this talk, min 6, Swami Saravapriyanandasays "If you are able to make a subtle body, you will get a reflected consciousness."

  

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=\_OlUk6eo84o](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_OlUk6eo84o)  

  

I see two groups.

  

1\. One says you can't "make a subtle body" through the material means and theories of science, so a jiva or a robot that reflects consciousness like a jiva cannot be created in the lab.

  

2\. The other think it may or may not be doable, basically keep open the possibility that a subtle body as understood in our shastras may be makeable through the future methods of science.

  

I am thinking, Group 1 has its stance based on shabda pramana and possibly aided by the other pramanas thereafter. And Group 2 does not quite agree to such usage of pramanas or uses them in a different way that justifies thinking scientific methods can access the subtle plane.

  

To be clear, we are thinking scienceproper, like biochemical or material engineering, and not something that extends to esoteric religious practices (like yoga, mantras, yagnyasetc). So presumably Group 2 (including Swami Sarvapriyananda here and the Brahmachari in the RKM seminar) think it is an open question whether such science can produce the mind (out of silicon etc) - basically create a living being from scratch through engineering.

  

Is there further nuance to this? And does shankara sampradaya of orthodox mathas have a position on this topic? If so, we have to base it on accepted pramanas and stand by them without ambivalence.
