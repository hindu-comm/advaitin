the primary reason being that Ishvara does not manifest the reflective self-knowledge, the self-awareness of "I am" alongside the upadhis of the machine, which is the fundamental mark of Consciousness and also the manifest criterion for assigning an Entity intelligence.

  

Thus, though Self is evident in "*This* machine" and Intelligence also evident in "This that projects, acts, perpetuates *thus*", yet in the absence of self-knowledge the machine can just as well be deemed a self-less composite of other jada parts operating under laws of nature external to it. The knowledge of Ishvara as its antaryami is largely obstructed in the gross-subtle planes.



This is not so in the jiva ...
