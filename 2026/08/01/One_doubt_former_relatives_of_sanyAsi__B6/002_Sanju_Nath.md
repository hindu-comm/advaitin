### **Conclusion**

The mainstream traditional position is therefore:

- **The marital relationship ends at the time of valid saṃnyāsa**, not
  at physical death.
- **The former wife is no longer ritually the wife of a gṛhastha.**
- **The former son ordinarily does not perform the saṃnyāsin’s funeral
  rites or annual śrāddha.**
- **Those rites belong to the saṃnyāsa order, usually through fellow
  monks or disciples.**
- **The son continues his own ancestral (pitṛ) rites through the family
  lineage but does not generally include the saṃnyāsin as an ordinary
  pitṛ.**

These conclusions are consistent with the Dharmaśāstra treatment of **pravrajita** as one who has exited the gṛhastha order and with the long-standing practice of traditional Advaita monastic institutions.
