### **“Buddhism, Advaita and Dvaita”**

Sri Madhvacharya, in his treatise *Tattvodyota*, quotes eight verses from Buddhist literature and expresses the view that the doctrine presented in those verses is not different from Advaita.

A critique of these eight verses was written in English, in six parts, about 15 years ago. They have now been compiled into a single article and presented here. This critique shows that the ideas expressed in all eight of these verses are in fact also taught in the Upanishads, the Bhagavad Gita, and similar scriptures.
