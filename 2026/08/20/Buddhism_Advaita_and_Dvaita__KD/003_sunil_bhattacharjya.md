Dear Putranji and Subbuji,

  

Lord Krishna told us the ultimate reality to be as follows:     
         

By direct perception, logical deduction, scriptural testimony and personal

realization, one should know that this world has a beginning and an end and

so is not the ultimate reality. Thus one should live in this world without

attachment                              
                                   
                                   
          This means that the world ends is Brahman alone and there is no second

thing. This means advaita is the fundamental principle . Lord Buddha also

taught Advaita in his Mahayana teachings. He toldtheMahayanis not to

spread the Mahayana teachings for five hundred years. That is why the

the 'First Assembly of Mahayana' was held in the time of Kanishka.

  

Regards

Sunil KB
