Hari Om,

  

> How do members here understand the proper role of the Aṣṭāvakra
> Saṃhitā within a traditional Advaita curriculum?

  

Aṣṭāvakra Gītā is the zenith of traditional teachings, which lays out sṛṣṭi-ḍṛṣṭi-vāda, dṛṣṭi-sṛṣṭi-vāda and ajātivāda in a sequential manner.

  

Aṣṭāvakra Gītā is within the framework of DSV and ajātivāda.

  

> In particular, would it be appropriate to relate its teaching to
> nididhyāsana after śravaṇa and manana, or is that comparison itself
> misleading?

  

Whether it is SDV or DSV, one has to take the teaching to nididhyāsana. Aṣṭävakra Gītā is no exception. It propounds DSV clearly, simply and beautifully. Its beauty and directness without any of and but make nididhyāsana so much simpler.
