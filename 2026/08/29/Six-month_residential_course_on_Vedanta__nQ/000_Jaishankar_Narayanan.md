Om Shri Gurubhyo Namah, On the occasion of Guru Purnima, the Acharyas of Arsha Vidya Varshini Gurukulam are happy to announce a six-month residential course in Advaita Vedanta & Sanskrit. The medium of instruction of the courses will be in the English language. The six-month residential course will start on Jan, 15th 2027. The course will be a pre-requisite to the Three-year Residential Course which will be announced later. The course will present a syllabus on Advaita Vedanta including Introduction to Bhagavadgita, Upanishads and some Prakarana Granthas. Further it will have classes on the Sanskrit language and an introduction to Paninian Grammar, traditional shloka/stotra chanting and aligned subjects. The residential course will involve a minimum of four hours of instruction per day, and it may come to 7 hours per day towards the end of the course. It is likely to be a hectic course. All classes are compulsory. Seva timings will be announced at the time of the start of the course. We invite applications from those who already have previous exposure to Advaita Vedanta and Sanskrit, preferably from any teacher of Arsha Vidya Parampara. Prerequisites for the applicants: 1. Men or women of age between 21 and 65 years, 2. Should be fluent in the English Language, 3. Preferably should be a graduate from any of the University courses, or at least completed High School, 4. Preferably should have worked in a job in any field. As per the tradition, the teaching is free. There are no tuition fees. The students can make a contribution towards the infrastructure and maintenance of the Gurukulam as per their means. The final admissions will be decided by the Acharyas of the Gurukulam.
