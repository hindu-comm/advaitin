Namaste Michael Ji,

  

Does the survey admit or reject the contention that adhyAsa as defined by Sri Bhagavatpada in AdhyAsa BhAshya includes adhyAsika tAdAtmya between the adhishthAna and adhyastha. My understanding of \*nondiscrimination\* would be \*AdhyAsika tAdAtmya\*.

  

My reference would be BUB 3-5-1 //शोकं मोहम्—शोक इति कामः;इष्टं वस्तु उद्दिश्य चिन्तयतो यत् अरमणम्,तत् तृष्णाभिभूतस्य कामबीजम्;तेन हि कामो दीप्यते;मोहस्तु विपरीतप्रत्ययप्रभवोऽविवेकः भ्रमः;स च अविद्या सर्वस्यानर्थस्य प्रसवबीजम्;//

//shokaM moham — shoka iti kAmaH ; iShTaM vastu uddishya chintayato yat aramaNam , tat tRRiShNAbhibhUtasya kAmabIjam ; tena hi kAmo dIpyate ; mohastu viparItapratyayaprabhavo.avivekaH bhramaH ; sa cha avidyA sarvasyAnarthasya prasavabIjam ;//
