The word \*positive\* in this context refers to the Reality/existential status of avidyA, as defined above, in relation to the Self itself. If the Self is considered as \*positive\*, then avidyA also is \*positive\* though the level or order of that status could be different. If the \*rope\* in the rope-serpent illustration is considered \*real/existent/positive\*, then the rope-serpent is also of the same status, though to a lesser level/order. It differentiates from what the rope-serpent is called as \*negative\* concept or \*nonexistent\* order or just \*imaginary\*.
