Amusing comment, nicely put, but I think it misses the distinction we are examining.

A pot or mountain is simply one object within the empirical order. *Ajñāna*, by contrast, is invoked as the explanatory ground of that entire order—capable of concealing, projecting, persisting causally, and being sublated by knowledge. So the question is not whether something “positive” can appear without defeating Advaita; obviously it can.

The question is what additional explanatory status is being assigned to *ajñāna* itself, and whether that status is already present in Sureśvara or belongs to a later development of Advaita theory. That is precisely the historical issue of the paper.🙏🙏🙏
