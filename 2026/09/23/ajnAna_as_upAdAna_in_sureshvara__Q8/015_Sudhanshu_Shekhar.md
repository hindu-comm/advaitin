Namaste Michael ji and others,

  

taking cue from what Venkatraghavan ji said, I am putting forth the following:

  

I am not interested in positive-avidya or negative-avidya discussion. I am not interested in discussing whether avidya is cause of bondage or not.

  

I am interested in discussing causation.

  

My understanding of advaita vedanta shows me this -- causation itself is illusory. Even if avidya is accepted as cause of bondage, world, misery - it hardly matters - because even this cause-effect-relationship is illusory. It merely appears but it does not exist. Conducting affairs presuming causation is actually foolishness. Like taking dream-insulin to cure dream-diabetes under the strong belief that dream-insulin is the cause of removal of dream-diabetes.

  

Such is our life. Strongly based on reality of causation, it falls apart when causation is questioned.

  

And when causation is given up, we at once situate as the seer, which we really are. The penchant for acquisition of advaita-jnana too falls apart as our penchant for advaita-jnana is under the belief in reality of causation - "advaita-jnana is cause of removal of ignorance."

  

So, causation itself drops off and our self-shining glory, even manifesting, shines forth.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
