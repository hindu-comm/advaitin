Namaste Venkat ji.

  

Nice excerpt and equally beautiful English translation.

  

One thing I would like to highlight, which puts everything in absolutely incredible perspective (for me).

  

That Brahman which is taught as the entity to be known is not some far-off entity. It is saakshaat aparoksha. Even as we discuss, it is shining. That shining, everything else shines. It is the most immediate entity. It is my very being, my very self. It is not the other, sitting there, to be known then... It is right now, right here. All I have to do is to give up all identification with everything which is not-my-being. To know Brahman is to just be it. And to be it, nothing needs to be done. Giving up is not doing something. Giving up is not-doing. To be it, I have to do nothing. Doing nothing whatsoever is being my self.

  

There is no penchant for removal of some ignorance then. I am not bothered about removal of a mirage. I am what I am. Why would I care for non-me. Ignorance is seen, or not seen, it does not matter.

  

So, whether the akhanda-ananda-swarupata of myself is shining forth or not - I am not concerned. Seen-mirage is being seen, and I have no interest therein. I am not analysing. I am not evaluating, I am not comparing knowing fully when that this time itself is illusory and seen.

  

So, denying causation, time and seen-world, I am in this moment. I don't care whether I know some Brahman or not. I don't care whether some positive or negative ignorance is being seen or not being seen. I am in this moment without any concern whatsoever for what appears to have passed, or what may seem to happen in future. I don't care whethere akhanda-ananda-swarupata is shining or not.

  

I am in this moment. And that is the end of all pursuits.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
