Hare Krishna Bhaskar prabhu ji.

  

Nothing which is seen is me. The thoughts, the mind - nothing is me. The "I" also is not me.

  

My real being does not have an iota of I-ness. "I" and "you" are both illusory appearances.

  

The same underlying reality is present beneath "Bhaskar" and "Sudhanshu" and "Venkat" and "Brahma" and "Vishnu". The Bhaskar-ness, Sudhanshu-ness, Vishnu-ness are all illusory mirages.

  

That is the real being.

  
यस्य बोधोदये तावत्स्वप्नवद्भवति भ्रमः। तस्मै सुखैकरूपाय नमः शान्ताय तेजसे॥ \[Ashtavakra Gita\]  

Salutations to that peaceful Effulgence (pure consciousness) which is the embodiment of pure joy—upon whose awakening, all illusion disappears like a dream.

  

समं सर्वेषु भूतेषु तिष्ठन्तं परमेश्वरम् । विनश्यत्स्वविनश्यन्तं य: पश्यति स पश्यति ॥\[Bhagvad-Gita\]

One who sees the Supreme Lord dwelling equally in all beings, imperishable within the perishable—he alone sees.

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
