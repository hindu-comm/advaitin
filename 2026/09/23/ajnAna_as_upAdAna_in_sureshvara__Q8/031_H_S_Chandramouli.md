BSB 2-3-48 // देहादिरयं सङ्घातोऽहमेव — इत्यात्मनि विपरीतप्रत्ययोत्पत्तिः । दृष्टा च सा सर्वप्राणिनाम् — अहं गच्छामि अहमागच्छामि, अहमन्धः अहमनन्धः, अहं मूढः अहममूढः इत्येवमात्मिका । न हि अस्याः सम्यग्दर्शनादन्यन्निवारकमस्ति । प्राक्तु सम्यग्दर्शनात्प्रततैषा भ्रान्तिः सर्वजन्तुषु । तदेवमविद्यानिमित्तदेहाद्युपाधिसम्बन्धकृताद्विशेषादैकात्म्याभ्युपगमेऽप्यनुज्ञापरिहाराववकल्पेते ।//

BSB 2-3-48 // dehAdirayaM sa~NghAto.ahameva — ityAtmani viparItapratyayotpattiH \| dRRiShTA cha sA sarvaprANinAm — ahaM gachChAmi ahamAgachChAmi, ahamandhaH ahamanandhaH, ahaM mUDhaH ahamamUDhaH ityevamAtmikA \| na hi asyAH samyagdarshanAdanyannivArakamasti \| prAktu samyagdarshanAtpratataiShA bhrAntiH sarvajantuShu \|
tadevamavidyAnimittadehAdyupAdhisambandhakRRitAdvisheShAdaikAtmyAbhyupagame.apyanuj~nAparihArAvavakalpete \| //

Tanslation (Swami Gambhiranda ) // It consists in a rise of a perverse idea to the effect , ‘This aggregate of body etc. is but myself. This is seen to exist in all creatures and to take such forms as ; ‘I go”, ‘I come’, ‘I am blind’, ;I am not blind’, ‘I am dull’, and so on.There is nothing else apart from full enlightenment that can eradicate this. Before the dawn of that enlightenment, this error permeates all creatures. In this way , even though the Self is admitted to be one, still injunction and prohibition can find scope owing to the distinction created through the association with the limiting adjuncts like the body etc., conjured up by ignorance //.

Same sentiment is expressed in the verse from Ashtavakra Gita cited by sudhanshu Ji, copied below for ready reference

Ashtavakra Gita // यस्य बोधोदये तावत्स्वप्नवद्भवति भ्रमः। तस्मै सुखैकरूपाय नमः शान्ताय तेजसे॥ \[Ashtavakra Gita\]

// Salutations to that peaceful Effulgence (pure consciousness) which is the embodiment of pure joy—upon whose awakening, all illusion disappears like a dream //.

Notice the statement \*यस्य बोधोदये ताव\* (upon whose awakening) \*.

Both BSB and Ashtavakra Gita cited above confirm the need for aparOkshajnAna for the type of intuitive understanding stated by Sudhanshu Ji quoted in the beginning of this post and which alone (aparOkshajnAna) can lead to Liberation/Realization/mOksha. . In the absence of aparOkshajnAna, they are at best the result of parOkshajnAna which itself is achieved through extraordinary sAdhanas. And by itself they do not lead to Liberation or mOksha. In fact if the sAdhaka stops continuing with his sAdhanAs at this stage and takes interest in enjoying the phala, he is led astray and misses out on the desired phala of mOksha.
