**About the Talk**

  

Title: Swami Dayananda Saraswati: My Favourite Intellectual Kshatriya

  

In this Memorial Lecture, Shri Rajiv Malhotra reminisces about his association with Pujya Swami Dayananda Saraswati and reflects on why he regards him as his “favourite intellectual Kshatriya.” Drawing on their conversations and shared intellectual concerns, he recalls Pujya Swamiji’s clarity of thought, courage in engaging difficult questions, and commitment to representing Dharma on its own terms. The talk offers a personal window into Pujya Swamiji as a teacher and thinker, while exploring the qualities that made his voice distinctive and consequential in contemporary discourse on Dharma and Indian civilisation.
