Hari Om,

  

The goal of Advaita Vedanta is moksha. It literally refers to freedom.

  

Now if freedom is the goal, this means that one is not having it already. That means, one is presently not free. That is -- he is dependent, a slave, in prison.

  

This state of being a slave, dependent, being in a prison is called bondage.

  

Question is - what is this bondage from which Vedas teach moksha to be the goal.

  

What is the form of this bondage. How do we experience this bondage in day-to-day life? And do we appreciate that we are in bondage?

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
