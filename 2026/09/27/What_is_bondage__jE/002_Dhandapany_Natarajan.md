Hari Om Sudhanshu ji,

I would define bondage as:

1) inability to do what one wants to do and

2\) inability to avoid what one does not want to do.

Chandogya calls the latter repeatedly as अकामचारत्वम्

  

As regards the expressions of bondage, we can

present them as an A to Z list of mental states.

While the list below is subjective, it is fairly representative and non overlapping, I think.

  

Anger, Boredom, Cynicism, Desperation, Enmity, Fear, Guilt, Hatred, Indulgence, Jealousy, Killer Instinct, Loneliness, Mortality, Nervousness, Ostentation, Pretense, Quarrelsomeness, Regret, Sorrow, Turpitude, Uncertainty, Violence, Wretchedness, Xenophobia, Yearning and Zealotry.

  

Xenophobia is the instinctive, fearful rejection of the unfamiliar or foreign,

reminiscent of ब्रह्म तं परादात् ... series

  

Zealotry is the fanatical, rigid adherence to

mental constructs, views, and ideologies.

  

Comments welcomed.

  

Regards,

  

Natarajan.
