Very well summarized Natarajan ji.

  

If we slightly drill down all these mental states, it pretty well manifests that it is "desire-to-be-in-state-x whereas the present state is ~x" is the bondage. It gives rise to fear, it gives rise to anger, it gives rise to all sorts of issues.

  

Once I understand that it is this "desire-to-be-in-state-x whereas present state is ~x" is the bondage --- the next question is -- am I really feeling the weight of this bondage? Am I feeling suffocated? Am I feeling that I am in a prison? That I am a prisoner?

  

If not, then again, it becomes a mere intellectual exercise and there is no real quest for freedom.

  

It is only when I feel the real weight of bondage -- real suffocation of my slavery -- that I can truly enquire - "how to get rid of this bondage".
