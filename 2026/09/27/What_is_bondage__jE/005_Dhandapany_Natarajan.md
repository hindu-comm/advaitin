[[Dhandapany Natarajan	2026-10-04, 02:08:45 [Source](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/jEE27TKTuRo)]]

1\. Bondage isn't felt when everyone around has identical limitations. A frog in a well has a world view that starts and ends with circular walls topped by a disc of sky. External input (via satsanga) is essential to know about X before I can appreciate that my current state is ~X.

2\. What X is, depends on what that external input is. If karmakāņḍa introduces svarga as a field of experience devoid of hunger, thirst, old age and untimely death - all of which seemed natural and acceptable till I received karmakanda's inputs - then, my desired state X becomes just svarga. Dvaitins seem quite comfortable with relocation to vaikuntha as a flawless end state to aspire and work for. One desires what one is taught in one's sampradāya.

3\. It takes mahat puņyam to get exposure to the existence of nityamukti and to get channelled into a programme whereby such nityamukti can be claimed as one's own eternal nature. Thereafter also, I will feel like my head is on fire in my state of ~nityamukti only when I am sufficiently sensitive or refined mentally. As Vidyaranya says, aneka-janma-bhajanāt svavicāram cikīrṣati. vicāreņa vinașṭāyām māyāyām śiṣyate svayam. As long as the perceived delta between X and ~X isn't great and as long as my self-belief in my ability to bridge the gap is great, vedanta will not become my top priority in life.
