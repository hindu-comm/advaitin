Namaste Natarajan ji.

  

We are never satisfied with our present state, whatever that state might be. There is partial satisfaction when a particular desire is fulfilled. However, always there being hundreds of desires pending, we are always dissatisfied.

  

This dissatisfaction or desire is basically this. I want to be in state x. And present state is ~x. I know my present state. I might not be knowing what x is, or I might be basing my understanding of x on the basis of hearsay - but one thing I am pretty sure - I don't want to be in present state.

  

This is the state of affairs. If we drill down all our mental states, analyse the entirety of our life - it boils down to this - I want to be in some other state than this present state.

  

I might be wanting to be in a state where my senses are being indulged, where I am being treated like a king or whatever - I am not satisfied with present state.

  

And when I deeply analyse the state of affairs -- we "want" to be in a state where there is no bondage, no fear, no desire.

  

Please see that even then bondage remains because the desire to be in a state which is not the present state, remains.

  

However, if feeling suffocated by desires and fears, I analyse and understand that desire is a mirage, that the bondage is a mirage - it at once loses its binding effect... Then even the continued appearance of desire becomes ineffective.

  

So, I would no more desire an end of desires, an end of bondage.

  

Desire appears - fine. Desire does not appear - fine. Fear appears - fine. Fear does not appear - fine. Bondage appears - fine. Bondage does not appear - fine.

  

Everything is fine. The present state is fine.

  

न शान्तिं लभते मूढो यतः शमितुम्*इच्छति* ।

धीरस्तत्त्वं विनिश्चित्य सर्वदा शान्तमानसः ॥

  

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
