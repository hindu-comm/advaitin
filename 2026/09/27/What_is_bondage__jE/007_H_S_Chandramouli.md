Namaste.

Bondage is the आध्यासिकतादात्म्य (AdhyAsika tAdAtmya) (mithyA mutual identification) between the अज्ञात आत्मन् (aj~nAta Atman) (veiled Atman) and the सूक्ष्मशरीर (sUkShmasharIra) (Subtle Body) in microform forming
the anAdi transmigrating jIva.

BSB 3-1-1 // तत्र कथं सामान्येन प्रतिज्ञायते — सर्वैरेव भूतसूक्ष्मैः सम्परिष्वक्तो रंहतीति ? अत उत्तरं पठति //

BSB 3-1-1 // tatra kathaM sAmAnyena pratij~nAyate — sarvaireva bhUtasUkShmaiH sampariShvakto raMhatIti ? ata uttaraM paThati //

Translation (Swami Gambhirananda) // Now it has to be considered whether the transmigrating soul moves out from the body without being surrounded by the ( mixed ) subtle element which are the seed of the next body, or it goes surrounded by them //.

This Bondage is what is addressed as हृदयग्रन्थि (hRRidayagranthi) in the well known Mundaka Up mantra

MU 2-2-9 // भिद्यते हृदयग्रन्थिश्छिद्यन्ते सर्वसंशयाः ।

क्षीयन्ते चास्य कर्माणि तस्मिन्दृष्टे परावरे ॥ ९ ॥//

// bhidyate hRRidayagranthishChidyante sarvasaMshayAH \|

kShIyante chAsya karmANi tasmindRRiShTe parAvare \|\| 9 \|\| //

Liberation is freedom from bondage / transmigration. This is achieved by destruction of the आध्यासिकतादात्म्य (AdhyAsika tAdAtmya) stated above.

The numerous items listed in previous posts are really the effects (results) of Bondage.
