Namaste Chandramouli ji,

  

What you have said is absolutely correct. Indeed, it is ādhyāsika-tādātmya. It is illusory-identification of my-real-being with illusory-seen.

  

And what a great thing -- this identification is unreal... It is ādhyāsika. It is not real...

  

So, my fears, my desires, which enslave me -- they are unreal, illusory... Like the desires I had in dream, like the fears I had in dream -- and I suffered..

  

So, treating all desires, all fears as absolutely identical to those in dream -- one sits back ... And sees the play..

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
