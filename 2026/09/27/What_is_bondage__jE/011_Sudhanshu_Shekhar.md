Namaste Chandramouli ji.

  

> Firstly what I have stated is AdhyAsika tAdAtmya between veiled Atman
> and सूक्ष्मशरीर (sUkShmasharIra) (Subtle Body) in microform forming the
> anAdi transmigrating jIva.

The ādhyāsika-tādātmya starts between ajñāna and shuddha-ātmā. Then between ajñāna-adhyāsa-viśiṣṭa-ātmā (ajñāta-ātmā) and ahaṁkāra (antah-karaṇa). Subsequent ādhyāsa are with ahamkāra-adhyāsa-viśiṣṭa-ātmā (sometimes dharmī-adhyāsa  
and sometimes dharma-adhyāsa).

  

ahaṁkāra-adhyāsa-viśiṣṭa-ātmā is superimposed in ajñāna-adhyāsa-viśiṣṭa-ātmā etc.

  

You mentioned one part of it. The fact is, there is ādhyāsika-tādātmya between śuddha-ātmä and entire-seen.

  

> How does it lead to the conclusion you have arrived at, dream and
> waking states to be identical etc.

Waking is seen, dream is seen, deep sleep is seen. All seen are illusory. All these are identical.

  

> Secondly, even assuming that it does, how and whom does it help if \*
> one sits back ... And sees the play \*.

It is not a question of helping anyone. It is natural concomitant of knowledge. I have incorrectly assumed myself to be sahyādṛi mountain out of delusion. Knowledge and automatic dissociation from being-sahyādṛi-mountain appear together.

  

> May be it can be done with the current body. And be enjoyable. But
> what is the position with respect to my later bodies?. Back to square
> one.

These are all assumptions, future bodies etc.

  

> Also, why should the \*one\* referred to above be the Real \*One\*.
> Can it also be not unreal?.

Sitting back is being-the-witness. The witness-hood is unreal but being-of-witness is real.
