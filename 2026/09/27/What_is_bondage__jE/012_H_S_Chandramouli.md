Namaste Sudhanshu Ji,

Reg // You mentioned one part of it. The fact is, there is ādhyāsika-tādātmya between śuddha-ātmä and entire-seen //,

Yes. I only mentioned the part that was immediately relevant to the context. The ādhyāsika-tādātmya between śuddha-ātmä and entire-seen takes the form \* (This) is \* and not \* \*(I) am \*.

Reg // Waking is seen, dream is seen, deep sleep is seen. All seen are illusory. All these are identical. //,

These are your conclusions outside of what I had mentioned in my post.

Reg // I have incorrectly assumed myself to be sahyādṛi mountain out of delusion. Knowledge and automatic dissociation from being-sahyādṛi-mountain appear together //,

I wonder. No one assumes himself to be \* sahyādṛi mountain out of delusion \* , even in dream, let alone waking state. The question of
\*from being-sahyādṛi-mountain \* does not arise at all.

Reg // These are all assumptions, future bodies etc //,

I am getting a serious doubt if we are talking about the same Advaita SiddhAnta. I have not come across this type of statement, as well as the ones like \* I have incorrectly assumed myself to be sahyādṛi mountain out of delusion \* in the SiddhAnta as advanced by Sri Bhagavatpada. Please confirm that these are consistent with the SiddhAnta as advanced by Sri Bhagavatpada. I will continue if need be after your confirmation.
