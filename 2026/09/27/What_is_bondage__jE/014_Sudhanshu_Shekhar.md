Incredible description by Niralamba Upanishad. Pure beauty.

  
  
केवलमोक्षापेक्षासङ्कल्पो बन्धः।

  

The very notionthat "I am bound" is enough to perpetuate bondage. Both I and bondage are illusory mirages. Whether they appear or not is of no consequence. My-real-being, the Self, the AtmA is untouched, pure, ever untainted.

  

Regards.

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
