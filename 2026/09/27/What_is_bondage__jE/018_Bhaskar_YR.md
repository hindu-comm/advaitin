Yes from the pinnacle of highest realization it is true that identifying that I am BMI is as ridiculous as saying I am ‘chair’ or ‘computer’.
But I am surprised without understanding the vyAvahAric importance of identifying ourselves with BMI (considering the close proximity of it with pratyagAtma) you still arguing that both are same…I have seen you are making this mistake in understanding the NS 3.1 introduction, without understanding the tat and tvam you argued that avidyA is brahmAshrita even as per NSkAra. Don’t you agree though from certain POV both represent forms of mistaken identity, they operate at very different levels in our day to day business!!?? That is what Sri Chandramouli prabhuji trying to say you. If some one don’t accept that thinking that "I am a mount everest" is a clear case of mental disorder or delusion then that mind needs a serious professional psychological councelling 😊 There is hell a lot of difference between the mount Everest is an inert and external object (anAtma in introduction to NS 3.1) and you are the Chaitanya the perceiver using his senses etc. (vishayi) and the mount everest is the perceived (vishaya). You can climb the mountain, see a mountain with all divinity etc. If you claim to be the mount Everest itself than you obviously ignoring the living awareness ( the Chaitanya that operates through BMI) which is currently looking at, touching, and thinking about the mount Everest !! Hope without arguing further you understand the context in which it is said : no one thinks that he is ‘mount sahyAdri’ 😊



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
