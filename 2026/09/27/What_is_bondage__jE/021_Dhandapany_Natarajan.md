As regards the mountain seen as oneself...

  

1\. The classical as well as the common view is as stated by Sri Bhagavatpada in his Taittirīya Upaniṣad Bhāṣya 2.8.1 :

  

"न हि स्वापे पर्वतादि-अचेतन-भावं गतः 'पर्वतोऽहम्' इति ... आत्मानं वेत्ति।"

  

One does not see the dream objects as the dream self.

  

2\. That said, there \*are\* rare but documented instances of such queer experiences!

A. In transpersonal psychology, particularly in the pioneer research of Dr. Stanislav Grof (Realms of the Human Unconscious and The Stormy Search for Self), there are extensive clinical documentations of "identification with inorganic nature."

  

Grof documented numerous case studies of patients under high-dose LSD or holotropic breathwork experiencing total ego-dissolution coupled with solid material identification:

  

Case Account (Identification with a Mountain / Rock):

  

Subjects frequently reported losing awareness of their human limbs and feeling their consciousness expand into the physical mass of a mountain or granite cliff.

  

"I was no longer observing the mountain; I became the mountain itself. I felt the immense weight, the absolute immobility, and the silent stillness of being millions of tons of solid rock enduring through centuries. There was no 'me' looking at it—my entire sense of 'I' was the granite mass."

  

B. Out-of-Body & Hypnagogic (Sleep-Onset) Anecdotes

  

In modern dream research and lucid dreaming forums, a known phenomenon called "Inorganic Shape-Shifting" or "Environmental Identification" occurs during sleep paralysis or hypnagogic states:

  

Hypnagogic Micropsia/Macropsia (Alice in Wonderland Syndrome):

  

As the brain transitions into dream sleep, body schema perception can fail dramatically. Individuals report feeling as though their body has expanded to fill an entire valley or that their body has become a massive, heavy mountain range pressing down on the earth, where they perceive their breathing as wind moving across cliffs. \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
