Hari Om Natarajan ji,

  

My point was not to examine the specific illustration of identifying with Sahyadri mountain. I just wanted to highlight the insanity behind the assumption "I am body/mind". As insane an assumption "I am Sahyadri mountain" appears to be, of equal insanity is the assumption "I am body/mind". This is the teaching of primary Vedanta, and this is what I wanted to highlight.

  

Atma-anAtma-viveka is primary teaching. AtmA and anAtmA are like chalk and cheese. They are like light and darkness. And yet, look at the insanity, there appears an identification of AtmA and anAtmA. And bondage appears accordingly.

  

PAramArthika realisation is not Atma-anAtma-viveka. It is the initial thing. PAramArthika realisation is --- anAtmA is not, only AtmA is.

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
