With profound sorrow, I learned of the passing of Mahamahopadhyaya Mullai Vasal Brahmasri Krishnamoorthy Sastrigal, the revered father of my previous supervisor, Prof. K. Ramasubramanian of IIT Bombay.

  

Although I never had the fortune of meeting him personally, I had heard much about him through my association with Prof. Ramasubramanian. I also had the opportunity to listen to Sri Krishnamoorthy Sastrigal’s lectures online, which gave me a glimpse of his profound learning, clarity, and deep grounding in Vedānta and the śāstric tradition.

  

He belonged to that rare generation of traditional scholars for whom śāstra was not merely an academic subject, but a way of understanding and living life. His scholarship and dedication to Vedānta are deeply inspiring, particularly for those of us who are trying to engage seriously with the traditional Indian knowledge systems.

.

My heartfelt condolences to Prof. Ram and his family during this difficult time.

  

May his journey be towards the highest state spoken of by the Vedānta.
