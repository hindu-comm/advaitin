Hare Krishna



My conclusion: Both schools have a coherent way to reconcile the verse with their theology. But unless we locate a traditional commentary specifically addressing Bhima's words, we should not claim that either Madhva or Ramanuja explicitly interpreted this particular verse in that way.



- IMO, even in advaita too without compromising the theology part of it
  we can interpret this ‘nirguNa’ brahman coz. we are accepting that
  this same nirguNa brahman for ‘sAdhakAnugrahArthaM’ assumes somany
  divine forms. Though mruttikA (mrut sAmAnya) in its own svarUpa is
  nirvikAra (formless), it can appear as pot, pitcher, jug etc. that is
  the reason why bhAshyakAra says somewhere that brahman who is bereft
  of all vishesha-s (particulars) is still the source of the world!! I
  think this is the way we can finely blend the divinity / theology with
  jnAna pradhAna means to realize the nirguNa brahman in advaita.



- Ofcourse this episode is to teach the Bheemasena a lesson by lord
  Krishna through his elder brother hanuman to realize his limitations.
  Divinity / theology quite the base of this episode. And Bheema was
  not the realized soul when he asserted the above words.
