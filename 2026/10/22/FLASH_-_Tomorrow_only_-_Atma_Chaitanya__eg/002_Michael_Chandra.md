Namaste Bhaskarji,Ira spent a couple of years studying with Śrī Swami Satchidanandendra Saraswati (SSS) before Swamiji's passing and was, to my knowledge, his only Western disciple. I believe his formal meetings with Swamiji were only once a week, but Ira can best tell that story himself.

Prior to that, Ira had spent a couple of years studying and traveling with Śrī Swami Dayanandaji, possibly as his first Western student.

Later, Ira met Śrī Swami Atmanandendraji, with whom he studied for more than fifteen years. Atmanandendraji was an extraordinary tapasvin who spent some thirty-five years living in a small hut in Uttarkashi and traversed India barefoot more than once. I once asked him which of his many austerities he had found the most difficult. His answer was simple: walking barefoot in the snow.  
  
It is through Ira that I have come to appreciate SSS and Bhasyakara'a teachings dimensionally more clearly.  
  
Regards, Michael
