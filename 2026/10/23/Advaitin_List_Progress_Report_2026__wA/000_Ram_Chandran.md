**Advaitin Mailing List (28 Years of Service to the Universal Vedanta Community!)**

**History of this Advaitin Mailing List:**

**The advaitin List was started during June 1998 and was hosted at Cool List (no more exists). The focus of this list from the beginning was on Sankara’s Advaita Vedanta. This Cyber Satsangh started with about a dozen founding members and few of them are no more with us. The major objective is to provide an open forum to enable the world Vedantic scholars to express their diverse viewpoints. On 31st of August 1998, the list moved to Egroups.com which later merged with the yahoo groups.
The Yahoo Groups closed hosting mailing lists during 2020 and Advaitin List officially moved to Google Groups during August 2020. Most of the founding members were originally part of Advaita-L list which was started by Dr. Vidyasankar Sundaresan and Ravi Mayavaram.**

**Currently we have 1796 members and the list archive contains articles on various topics covering advaita philosophy. The success of this list can only be measured by how it serves its members or vice-versa, how the members contribute to the list. The list is truly blessed with scholars with good communication skills possessing both Vivekam (wisdom) and Vinayam (humility). Currently Vedanta discussions happen through Cyber Satsangh like ours and also in person at Vedantic Institutions such as Chinmaya Mission, Ramakrishna Mission, Sankara Mutts, etc.**

**This list always looks for ideas and comments from members which can help us to improve our service to the humanity. Please feel free to send your comments to any one of the List Moderators or at the List. Your comments will be read by all moderators and they try their level best to make appropriate changes and corrections if and when needed.**
