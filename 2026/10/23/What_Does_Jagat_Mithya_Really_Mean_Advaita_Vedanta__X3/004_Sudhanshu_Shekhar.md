In Mandukya Rahasya Vivriti, 2.4, SSSS ji writes the following:

  

**स्वप्रजागरयोर्वैधर्म्याभावः--**  
नन्वन्तःस्थानादिभिर्हेतुभिस्त्वयैव जागरितवैलक्षण्यं स्वप्रस्य प्रदर्शितम् । बाध्यमानत्वं चाशक्यापह्नवं स्वप्रस्य । तत्कथमिदानीमात्थ सर्वथा साम्येन सममेवोभयोर्वैतथ्यमिति ? अत्रोच्यते \| सत्यमुक्तं वैलक्षण्यम् \| तत्तु जागरितदृष्ट्या मन्दबुद्धीनां परमार्थप्रतिपत्त्युपायत्वेन, न वस्तुदृष्ट्या \| वस्तुतस्तु नायमपि विशेषो विद्यते । यतोऽयमेव श्लोकः स्वप्रजागरितयोः सर्वसमताप्रतिपादनपरत्वेनापि योजयितुं शक्यते ।

  

Translation:

### Non-difference between Dream and Waking States:

**Objection:** Now, by reasons such as internal placement, you yourself have demonstrated the distinctness (difference) of the dream state from the waking state. Moreover, the sublatability (being contradicted/falsified upon waking) of a dream is undeniable. Then how can you say now that, with complete equality, both are equally unreal (*vaitathya*)?

**Reply:** It is said here: What has been stated as the difference is indeed true; but that is from the standpoint of the waking state, as a means for the dull-witted to attain the ultimate reality (*paramartha*), not from the standpoint of ultimate reality. In reality, even this distinction does not exist. For this very verse can also be interpreted as establishing the absolute equality of both the dream and waking states.

===================================================

In dream, you imagine that there is some Ishwara who created the world, the mountains, the rivers, stars etc. However, those creations were not creation of some Ishwara, but creation of your own mind. The Ishwara in which you believed in the dream was conjured by your ignorance.

Since there is absolute equality between dream and waking, your belief in an Ishwara who has created these rivers, mountains etc is rooted in ignorance.

The absolute identity of waking and dream is a key concept in "advanced" Vedanta. That is what SSSS ji says by "यतोऽयमेव श्लोकः स्वप्रजागरितयोः सर्वसमताप्रतिपादनपरत्वेनापि योजयितुं शक्यते ।"

If you hold difference in waking and dream, then you are entitled to have an Ishwara-srishti for waking-world and a jiva-srishti for dream-world. But that distinction in waking and dream is stated for manda-buddhi, as a tool for achieving the ultimate.सत्यमुक्तं वैलक्षण्यम् \|
