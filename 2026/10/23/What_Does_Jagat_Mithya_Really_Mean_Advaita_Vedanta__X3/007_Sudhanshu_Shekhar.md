Shri Madhusudan Saraswati says in Siddhanta Bindu
-जीवेश्वरविभागादिकल्पनास्तु पुरुषबुद्धिप्रभवाअपि शास्त्रेणानूद्यन्ते ।तत्त्वज्ञानोपयोगित्वात् 'फलवत्सन्निधावफलं तदङ्गम्' इति न्यायात् भ्रमसिद्धस्यापिश्रुत्यनुवादसम्भवात्।

  

The main point of the scriptural teaching is the Reality of the Self, without a second, because that is pruposeful and not known (otherwise). Imaginations regarding the distinctions between Ishwara, Jiva etc., are born of man's mind. Their anuvada is done by shastra because they are useful in understanding the essence or truth. That is in accordance with the (established) principle, "In the presence of the purposeful, the unpurposeful is (to' be accepted as) helping it", it is possible for the scriptures to repeat information regarding even what is proved only within illusion. 

  

When SSSS ji accepts waking-dream-identity in MK 2.4, he has accepted that the Ishwara assumed for waking-world is no different from the Ishwara we assume in our dream-world as the creator thereof.

Sir, Bhashyakara tells the vision of a realized person -त्वमपि तत्त्वदर्शिनां दृष्टिमाश्रित्य शोकं मोहं च हित्वा शीतोष्णादीनि नियतानियतरूपाणि द्वन्द्वानि **‘विकारोऽयमसन्नेव मरीचिजलवन्मिथ्यावभासते’** इति मनसि निश्चित्य तितिक्षस्व इत्यभिप्रायः ॥ He asks an "advanced" student - Arjuna - to follow it -- that this seen is illusory like mirage-water.

  

Waking/dream/deep-sleep are mirages. Whatever is seen, is a mirage. This is being the saakshi. He may see a seen. But he knows that the seen is mirage-water. It is he himself which is appearing as this mirage. He is the adhishthana. The seen is just not existent. It only appears to exist. This is for "advanced" students. This is what SSSS ji holds in MK 2.4. If he holds otherwise elsewhere, it is for him to explain.

Sir, truth is our being. We don't need any Shruti for being what we are. Shruti helps in shedding what we are not. The anuvadas done by Shruti are for helping us to shed non-me. It is pointless to perpetuate the non-me in the name of shraddha to Shruti. See what Shruti says -

  

ग्रन्थमभ्यस्य मेधावी ज्ञानविज्ञानतत्त्वतः। पलालमिव धान्यार्थी त्यजेद्ग्रन्थमशेषतः ॥

  

ज्ञानी मनुष्य को चाहिए कि ग्रन्थ का अभ्यास करने के पश्चात् उसमें निहित ज्ञान-विज्ञान को (मूलतत्त्व को ) ग्रहण कर ले, तदनन्तर ग्रन्थ को त्याग देना चाहिए। ठीक उसी भाँति, जैसे कि धान्य (अन्न) को प्राप्त करने वाला व्यक्ति अन्न तो प्राप्त कर लेता है और पुआल को खलिहान में ही छोड़ देता है॥

  

If one keep holding on to non-me, it is useless.
