Dear friends,

  

From time totime, there are discussions on **Prasthanabheda** in the Advaita forums, a subject originally started 0nly in the 16th century CE, by Madhusudana Saraswati, an Advaita scholar from Bengal.
Additionally, around that time the Visishtadvata and the Dvaita schools also came into prominence, as a sort of rivals of theAdvaitins.

  

Moreover, Madhusudana Saraswati did differ to some extent from the views Adi Sankara. Moreover the teachings of the Upanishads do not differ fromthe teachings of the Bhagavad Gita. Thus, in my opinion, there is no point in having prasthana trayas, as was proposed by Madhusudana Saraswati.

Regards,

Sunil
