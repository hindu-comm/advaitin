Hare Krishna



I was unable to open this link in my official laptop, so opened this in my mobile where Sri Subbu prabhuji shared this link in a WhatsApp group. After reading the index I thought it would have been better to give title to this write-up with a suffix ‘AS PER VYAKHYAANA’ 😊 because it is full of quotes from later vyAkhyAna or from the works of post Shankara era (pro pancha pAdika vivara era). Interestingly there is a section DSV as per PTB I curiously opened it to learn more about DSV as per prasthAna traya bhAshya of shankara but to my disappointment, in that section also there is extensive references from later vyAkhyAna on bhAshya completely ignoring the context and intent of mUlabhAshyakAra.
kArika references given as if it is undeniable source to prove waking world too just imaginary like dream world and strongly advocating DSV etc. but sadly those references also fail to recognize/reconcile the intentions of both kArikAkAra and bhAshya on kArikA coz. this write up completely ignores where kArikAkAra himself says jeeva too imagination of Atmadeva before anything else!! I don’t know under these circumstances how can kArikA endorse the eka jeeva vAda / DSV. kArika references, prashnOpanishad, sUtra, bruhad, chAndOgya etc. selectively/ subjectively chosen without considering the mUla bhAshyOddesha. Anyway, the good thing is that the author at the very beginning praying the Almighty Narasimha as satchidaananda brahman without considering the lord Narasimha too just a figment of his own avidyA imagination 😊 But I don’t know for the people who have the firm conviction that Ishwara is just product of dark and dense avidyA, can bow down to divine forms like Narasimha when these divine forms are phantoms in the conditioned mind of the ONLY existing jeeva 😊 hypocrisy or traditional obligation, difficult to make it out!!??



Anyway a good resourceful write-up/material for those who want to learn DSV from the perspective of later authors of Advaita.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
