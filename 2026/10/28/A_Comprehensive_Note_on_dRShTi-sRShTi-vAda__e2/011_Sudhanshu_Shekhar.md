1\. Are dream and waking identical in all aspects?

  

2\. Why SSSS ji holds that MK 2.4 propounds complete identity (sarva-samataa) between waking and dream? And that waking-dream distinction is for manda-buddhi? He says the following:

*//Objection:Isn't it the case that by you yourself, the distinction of the dream state from the waking state has been demonstrated through reasons such as the internal locus (of experiences), etc.And the sublatability (the fact that it gets negated upon waking) of the dream state is impossible to deny.Then how is it that you now assert, with complete equality, that the unreality (illusory nature) of both is exactly the same.*

*In response to this, it is said.*

*It is true that the distinction has been stated.However, that is from the perspective of the waking state, as a means for dull-witted people to attain the ultimate reality, not from the perspective of absolute truth.*

*As a matter of fact,however, even this difference does not exist. This is becausethis very verse can also be interpreted as conveying the total equality of both the dream and waking states.//*

Be precise. I am not interested in discussing other explanations of SSSS ji.

  

He has stated something in Mandukya. Please explain how it does not contradict his elsewhere admitted position that waking and dream are distinct.

  

One cannot get away by saying x at some place and ~x at some other place. Reconcile.
