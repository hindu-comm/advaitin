Hare Krishna



We, the loukika-s/paamara-s/gruhastha-s not supposed to make any counter sarcastic comments to ‘sAdhus’ words to avoid accrue any ‘sAdhu aparAdha. We have to accept these comments from the desk of monk,
feeling that intension of comments /sarcasm like this stem purely out of compassion. If it is due to some other reasons, which again we the normal householders would not be in a position to adjudge, should only feel that sarva karma sanyasi-s engaging in sarcasm sometimes would indicate that their mind is still reacting to external stimuli with irritation, superiority, or cynicism. I sincerely wish that is not the case here.



My humble praNAms to all those noble sAdhu-saNta-s.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
