I read till about 24 pages of Sudhanshu-ji's document. He points out that both SDV and DSV propound vivarta-vada, but that SDV differentiates vyavaharika and pratibhasika and DSV reduces all duality to pratibhasika. This is a bit confusing to me because if creation/duality is vivarta in SDV also, does that not imply the equivalence of vyavaharika and pratibhasika in the standpoint of the knowledge that all of creation is vivarta? Likewise when we say all beings are Brahman in svaroopa, what is meant? Does Brahman have a duality-embedded svaroopa in a real sense? That is probably held by VA. For Advaita, is it not the case that the rupa constituting the contingent beings is not really "**sva**-roopa" of Brahman but rather a vivarta appearance or maayic kalpitham of Brahman? If we claim vivarta, then sagunatvam or svaroopam should be in a lower standpoint of ignorance?
