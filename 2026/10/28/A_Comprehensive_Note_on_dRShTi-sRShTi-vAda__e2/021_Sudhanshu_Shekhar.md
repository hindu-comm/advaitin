> I read till about 24 pages of Sudhanshu-ji's document. He points out
> that both SDV and DSV propound vivarta-vada, but that SDV

This is very true.

  

> This is a bit confusing to me because if creation/duality is vivarta
> in SDV also, does that not imply the equivalence of vyavaharika and
> pratibhasika in the standpoint of the knowledge that all of creation
> is vivarta?

  

Vyāvahārika and prātibhāsika in SDV have equivalence in so far as both are mithyā. However, they are different in SDV in so far as prātibhāsika can be sublated by non-Brahma-jñāna whereas vyāvahārika can be sublated only by Brahma-jñāna.

  

In DSV, there is no such distinction.

  

  

> Likewise when we say all beings are Brahman in svaroopa, what is
> meant?

  

It means that caitanya aspect of all beings is identical reality which is the substratum of entire world. So, a being has two parts, one Brahman which is ātmā whereas the other part is anātmā. The anātmā is mithyā and hence non-existent. The reality of living being is thus ātmā which is taught in Vedānta to be the substratum of entire world i.e. Brahman. This is Brahma-ātmā-aikya.

  

> Does Brahman have a duality-embedded svaroopa in a real sense?

  

No.

  

> That is probably held by VA.

  

True. Rather, Viśiṣṭa-advaita has Brahman coupled with cit and acit. So, total three entities are there. However, cit and acit can never exist separate from Brahman. So, Brahman coupled with cit and acit is an organic whole.

  

> For Advaita, is it not the case that the rupa constituting the
> contingent beings is not really "**sva**-roopa" of Brahman but rather
> a vivarta appearance or maayic kalpitham of Brahman?

  

It is true to say that nāma and rūpa are avidyā-kārya and are not Brahman. They are non-existent. However, during avidyā, when they are categorised as mithyā and not tuccha, their swarūpa i.e. adhiṣṭhāna is Brahman like rope is adhiṣṭhāna of snake.

  

  

> If we claim vivarta, then sagunatvam or svaroopam should be in a lower
> standpoint of ignorance?

  

Saguṇatvam of Brahman is only through avdiyā-dṛṣṭi.
