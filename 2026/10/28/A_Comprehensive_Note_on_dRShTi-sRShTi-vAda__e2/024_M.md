Philosophical nihilists are different from Śūnyavādins. You can refer to Prof Garfield's & Dzongsar Jamyang Rinpoche's lectures to know the difference.

And no, it's not a silly statement because DSv especially the Prakasanandian variant, is quite close to philosophical nihilism with the catch of 'adhisthana chaitanya'. For him, everything except the Adhisthana Chaitanya ; (drsya prapancha) is a phantom entity. The seer (Ajnana Upahita chaitanya) illuminates the phantom world which has phantom objects & phantom beings (jeeva abhasas).

  

That's essentially philosophical nihilism with the catch of the 'seer'.

Hence, I tag it as phantom-Samkhya too, occasionally.

  

That's all !
