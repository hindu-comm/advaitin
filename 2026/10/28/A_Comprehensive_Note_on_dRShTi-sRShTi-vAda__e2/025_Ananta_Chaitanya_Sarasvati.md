> For him, everything except the Adhisthana Chaitanya ; (drsya
> prapancha) is a phantom entity. The seer (Ajnana Upahita chaitanya)
> illuminates the phantom world which has phantom objects & phantom
> beings (jeeva abhasas).

That is not only DSV, it is tattva in SDV also that is clear in Vedanta Paribhasha. The difference is in DSV which doesn't shift itself from apavAda sthAna to adhyAropa stage of SDV.



> That's essentially philosophical nihilism with the catch of the

If it is not shUnyavAda and still nihilism with the catch of "philosophical" adjective, and with a difference of adhiShTAna chaitanya, it is not even close. There is make or break difference.  


> Hence, I tag it as phantom-Samkhya too, occasionally.
