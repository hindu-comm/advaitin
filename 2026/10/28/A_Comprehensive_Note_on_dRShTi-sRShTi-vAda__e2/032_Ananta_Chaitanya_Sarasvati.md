The Arrogant "Pūrva Pakṣa" Accusation

They condescendingly claim that Vedantins only understand Buddhism through *pūrva pakṣa* (the traditional method of stating an opponent's view before refuting it), calling them "secondhand texts."

- This is historically illiterate. Masters like Adi Shankara, Kumarila
  Bhatta, and Udayanacharya did not read "secondhand summary booklets."
  They debated living, breathing Buddhist pandits at universities like
  Nalanda and Vikramashila for centuries.
- The Vedic critique of Buddhism wasn't born out of misunderstanding; it
  was born out of a perfect understanding that Buddhism rejects the
  *Atman* (Eternal Soul). If you reject the Eternal Soul, the Vedic
  rishis state you have rejected the absolute foundation of *Sat*.
