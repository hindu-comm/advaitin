Hare Krishna



Yes, as per shankara’s AV, for all practical purposes vyAvahAra yOgya jagat is brahma mAnasa pratyaya / Ishwara Srushti for the sake of jeeva-s, no hands in it by individual conditioned jeeva who is in full force to see his own Srushti in dream (jeeva mAnasa pratyaya). One is vyashti another one is samashti (macro and micro). Though this vyAvahArika jagat is not paramArtha as it has pariNAmi nityatva (like veda-s have pravAha nityatva) it never ever deviates from its cause.
And this cause is sarva shakta and sarvajna brahman/Ishwara clarifies bhAshyakAra in janmAdasya adhikaraNa and concludes that Ishwara, whose creation is not even conceivable by the individual mind. So it is quite evident the socalled atheistic theory of origination of Srushti from avidyA laden individual jeeva’s mind quite conspicuous by its absence in Shankara bhAshya reckons Sri SSS. ON the other hand he quotes Shankara from 2-1-16 as follows : Just as brahman the cause never deviates from existence in all the three periods of time, so also the effect the world, never deviates from existence in all the three periods. And existence again is only one,. So for this reason also the effect is none other than the cause. Case is shut on the face of the jeeva mAnasa Srushti vAdins or individual single jeeva’s avidyA hetuka Srushti.
2-2-28 to 30 sUtra bhAshya should be understood properly before jumping to conclude absolute non-difference of waking and dream world in kArika / kArikAbhAshya.
