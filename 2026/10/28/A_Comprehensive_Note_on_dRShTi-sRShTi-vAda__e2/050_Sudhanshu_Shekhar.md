> 1.  Is pot a pariNAma of clay?
>
> 
>
> Is pot different from clay??

  

Pot can be used to store water. Clay cannot be used to store water.

Pot breaks. Clay does not break.

  

Pot is different from clay.

  

Pot cannot exist separate from clay. It is not different from clay in the manner it is different from cloth. Pot and cloth can exist separately. But pot and clay cannot exist separately.

  

So, pot is non-different from clay.

  

So, pot is both different as well as non-different from clay.

  

This is called pariNAma. Pot is a pariNAma of clay.
