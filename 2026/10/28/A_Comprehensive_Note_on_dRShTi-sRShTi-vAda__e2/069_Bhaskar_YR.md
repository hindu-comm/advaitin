Most scholars argue that in the PTB, Shankara primarily operates from Srishti-Drishti-Vada (**SDD**) as a pragmatic tool (*vyavahara*) to teach morality, devotion, and structural cosmic creation. From this viewpoint,

Ø You are absolutely right prabhuji, Shankara while advocating traditional teaching of AV heavily depends upon shruti siddhAnta where brahman is the abhinnanimittOpadAna kAraNa for the jagat and emphasizes the fact that accepting the Ishwara hetuka Srushti is VEDANTA MARYAADA.
Hence he called jagat perceived in jAgrat as brahma mAnasa pratyaya argued against dream world when countering the vijnAnavAdins (2-2-28 to 2-2-30 sUtra bhAshya). The irony of the EJV / DSVAdins is that they unhesitatingly give ontological status to avidyA and argue that it is external, it has its own power like AvaraNa vikshepa, it is a solid thing etc. and at the same time completely deny the existence of Ishwara Srushti for which brahman itself material and efficient cause 😊 They nonchalantly argue that whatever you see external is just imagination of your own mind or one jeeva who is existing with avidyA projects his own whims and fancy world, no role whatsoever to Ishwara in waking and dream worlds…it’s all bhrAnti due to that EJ avidyA !! This is plainly goes against shruti / bhAshya / yukti and anubhava as well for the following reasons :

These are all not my own imagination to say that I am just doing soliloquies within my own mind without any external stimulation.

DSV is a higher-level pedagogical stepping stone meant to dissolve the reality of the external world entirely, prepping the seeker for absolute non-duality (*Ajata-vada*). Several proponents of DSV argue that the seeds of this doctrine are directly embedded across dozens of places in Shankara's commentaries.

Ø Prabhuji in my sincere opinion if any doctrine that advocates IshwarAnugraha rahita jnAna and argues that Ishwaratva itself a unwanted product of dark and dense avidyA of the individual mind, then that is NOT vaidika sampradaya anugraheeta jnAna mArga, it is simply blasphemous nAstika vAda though they are taking the shelter under sampradAya/veda.
