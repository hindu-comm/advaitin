Namaste Ram Chandran ji, Dennis ji, Hishi ji

> At some threshold point it is necessary for the intellect to bow down
> to faith and intuition.

  

I am not sure as to what does it mean for intellect to bow down to faith. I am not sure of the meaning of the word intuition also. May be the intended Sanskrit word be used.



> It is intellectually arrogant for anyone to believe that through
> arguments,we cancome to a conclusive position and there will be no
> disagreement whatsoever. None of us can ever claim that we have
> completely understood our scriptures and the philosophical and
> religious dogmas that have been postulated in the past. If anyone
> dares to make such claims, no one will ever believe! 

  

I do not agree to this view. There is no inherent infirmity in debate. The infirmity lies with the debater. What exactly is he looking for?

  

My view is this - one has to be interested in truth. If this condition is satisfied, then debate, shravana, reading - whatever - is a good enough tool for arriving at truth.

  

If one is not interested in truththen I am afraid nothing is going to help. Not even Brahmaji is going to help.

  

One has to be interested in truth - and then even an eight-year old boy can be of help. Facebook and Instagram can help. YouTube can help.

  

So, let us analyse -- what is it that I am interested in!!

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
