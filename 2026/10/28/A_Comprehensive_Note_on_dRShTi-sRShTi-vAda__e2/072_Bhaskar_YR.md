> praNAms Hare Krishna



I am not sure as to what does it mean for intellect to bow down to faith. I am not sure of the meaning of the word intuition also. May be the intended Sanskrit word be used.



Ø It is something called shraddha in sampradaya / shAstra vAkya /gurUpadesha after realizing the boundaries of mere logic.
manOvAchAmagOchara Atma tattva can be realized only through shAstra pramANa..So our conditioned intellect in which we are completely proud of should bow down to this antya pramANa shAstra vAkya and which has been explained by sampradaya knower shrotreeya brahmanishta tattva jnAni. It has to be like that just because we cannot logically deduce or construct a mental map to capture the infinite Absolute; logic or our limited intellect can only be used certain extent and definitely not beyond that brahma jignAsa in shAstra vidhi starts where human intellect realizes its limitations. Naisha tarkena matiH Apaneya, yato vAcho nivartante aprApya manasa saha is the Upanishad verdict. 





So, let us analyse -- what is it that I am interested in!!



Ø True it is satya but how to realize that satya svarUpa of ours??
Is it possible through mere dry logic or shruti, shruti sammata tarka and sarvatrika purNAnubhava?? sampradaya teaches us it is later not former 😊
