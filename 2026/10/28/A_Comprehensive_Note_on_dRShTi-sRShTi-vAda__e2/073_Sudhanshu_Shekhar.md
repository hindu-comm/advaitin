Namaste Bhaskar ji,

  

Truth stands on its own legs. It is swaprakāśa.

  

Tarka should be shruti-sammata. That does not mean that one cannot arrive at truththrough tarka. One can arrive at truth in any manner. Just that one has to arrive at truth.

  

When we say shruti-sammata-tarka, it just means that tarka which leads to same conclusion as mentioned in Shruti is capable of leading one to truth.

  

Tarka not in conformity to Shruti cannot lead to truth. Agreed. But that is not because of violation of tarka vis-a-vis Shruti-delimited-by-Shruti-tva. But because of violation of tarka vis-a-vis Shruti-delimited-by-satya-tva. Please appreciate this difference.

  

FollowingShruti-delimited-by-Shruti-tva is faith. FollowingShruti-delimited-by-satya-tva is sanity.

  

Shruti-sammata-tarka ("SST") and Shruti are valid because they are truth. And not because they are SST or Shruti.

  

You reach attruth in any manner - anumana, arthāpatti, Shruti, whatever
-- and mukti is ensured.  

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
