Namaste Ram Chandran-ji,

  

The intellect is the very instrument through which “ahaṃ brahmāsmi” is understood.

It is understood as mithyā, not transcended as an instrument.

Both Swami Dayananda and Swami Paramarthananda do not accept “going beyond the intellect” in this sense.

The pole does not stand for the intellect; it stands for the means that remove the obstructions to firm knowledge, jñāna-niṣṭhā.

  

Warm regards.
