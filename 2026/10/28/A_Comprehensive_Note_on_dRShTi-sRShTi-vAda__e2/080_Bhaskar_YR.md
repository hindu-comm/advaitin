Do you agree that Swami Chinmayananda refers to going beyondintellectual concepts and not the intellect as an instrument ?



praNAms

Hare Krishna



Perhaps what Sri Ramachandran prabhuji intended to say is : intellect as an instrument is just an object (Vishaya) to vishayi and vishaya itself cannot know vishayi. The intellect can only know objects (things outside itself). Since the vishayi / sAkshi / paramArtha Chaitanya / brahman is the ultimate Subject—he / it is the Knower of the intellect itself—the intellect cannot look back and "know" Brahman as an object.
Ultimately it has to be realized that IT is the karaNam, kAraNaM and karta as well. I am sure Sri Ramachandra prabhuji would respond to you in a clearer and more eloquent way.
