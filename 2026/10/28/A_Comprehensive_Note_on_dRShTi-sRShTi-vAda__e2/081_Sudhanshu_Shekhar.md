Yes, that avidyA-AvaraNa-nAsha is required. That is achievedthrough akhaNDAkAra-vritti. That vritti can be generated by Shruti or by Shruti-sammata-tarka (anumAna, arthApatti, Shabda).

  

Please note the specific statement from MANDUkya BhAshya:अद्वैतं किमागममात्रेण प्रतिपत्तव्यम् , आहोस्वित्तर्केणापीत्यत आह — ***शक्यते तर्केणापि ज्ञातुम्*** ; तत्कथमित्यद्वैतप्रकरणमारभ्यते ।

  

Now, please note another by AchArya in BSB -श्रुत्यवगाह्यमेवेदमतिगम्भीरं ब्रह्म न तर्कावगाह्यम् ।

  

Prima facie, one would feel a contradiction - in MANDUkya, AchArya says that advaita is not known by Shabda alone, but it is also possible to know it through tarka. How? For that, Advaita prakaraNa was started.

  

But in BSB, He says that Brahman is known by Shruti **alone**, and it is not possible to know it by tarka.

  

None is authorized to place one sentence over the other. The reconciliation is this -- Shruti-anugriheeta-tarka can lead to truth.

  

What exactly is Shruti-anugriheeta-tarka. It is that tarka which is blessed by Shruti. Which has anugraha of Shruti. That means - that tarka which leads to same truth which Shruti teaches.

  

So, truth is the sole determinant. Whatever conforms to truth is acceptable and is capable.
