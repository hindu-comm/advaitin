Hare Krishna



My humble praNAms for this clarification Sri Ananta Chaitanya prabhuji.
It seems Sri Sudhanshu prabhuji is of the opinion that tarka needs to be first and this tarka should help us to arrive at the truth of shruti or IOW we have to interpret/deduce the siddhAnta of shAstra in a logically acceptable way. So as per him tarka occupies the first place and shAstra is something at the second place. Hence he is insisting truth can be realized in any manner and shAstra is not the ONLY means but just one of the means among many. But as we all know in shAstra yOnitvAt adhikaraNa how bhAshyakAra establishes shAstra (shAstrameva) is the pramANa to realize brahma. yathOktaM RigvedAdi shAstram yOniH kAraNaM pramANam asya brahmaNaH yathAvat svarUpAdhigame…shAstraadeva pramANAt jagatO jnamAdikAraNam brahma adhigamyate ityabhiprAyaH. Elsewhere he emphasizes na hi edam ati gambheeraM bhAvayAthAtyaM muktinibandhanaM AgamantareNa utprekshitumap shakyaM.



It is good that you have cleared to him all other pramANA-s are sahakAri not direct pramANa.



Hari Hari Hari Bol!!!
