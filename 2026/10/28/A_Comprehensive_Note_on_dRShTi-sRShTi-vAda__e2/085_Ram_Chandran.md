**Namaskar:**

The pole vault analogy often by Swami Chinmayananda was to explain how a seeker must eventually transcend the mind, intellect, and even the "desire for liberation" to achieve Self-realization. The purpose of this analogy was to illustrate that the Pole vaulter needs to leave the pole if he/she wants to cross over the pole. Analogies in Vedanta are used to illustrate an important idea (concept) to nail a point. Please do not ANALYSE the Analogy or Swami Chinmayanandaji.

I have personally listened to Swami Dayanandaji, Swami Paramarthanandaji and Chinmayanandaji and we are fortunate they came to educate and PURIFY our inner minds. If our mind is not purified, we can never transcend from Vyavaharika to Paramarthika. Please note that one of the important point of Swami Dayananda Saraswati was “Anthakarana Shuddhi,.” Please note that **Antahkarana Shuddhi** is the purification and pacification of the inner psychological instrument in Advaita Vedanta.

This continuation of discussion indicates that we need to do much more
Anthakaarna Shuddhi!. Let us focus on mind purification before continuing this discussion!
