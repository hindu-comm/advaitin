I will respond in detail later. Just couple of clarifications:

  
  
\> The question then is how will anyone using only tarka know that they have landed on the truth? If you say one will know when one knows, then it is no different from intuition-pakSha. (Swami Paramarthananda used to joke that AtmA is not known by intuition, but in tuition, learning from the Guru in sampradAya). Yogis would claim this intuition independently. Else you have to accept some dependency. The advaita tattva revealed by the shruti as the conclusion has to be already known in shravaNa with pramANanishchaya and that is what the tarka must conclude with prameyanishchaya for it to be called as shruti-anugRhIta tarka. Else tArkikas would also call their tarka concluding in nAnA AtmA as also shruti-anugRhIta.  
  
  

If we hold on to this position, then it means even if one arrives at truth, he needs something else to confirm that he has arrived at truth. Isn't it? I don't know how much valid it is. I arrive at truth, that should be enough, unless of course there are pratibandhaka. But then it is not the fault of the tool, it is due to the pratibandhaka. That way, even Shruti cannot lead to truth until there are pratibandhaka.

  

So, if there are no pratibandhaka, how do tarka-leading-to-truth and Shruti-lead-to-truth differ.  
  
Also, let us analyse the Advaita PrakaraNa sentence of bhAshya carefully - if we still need Agama along with tarka, then will it be justified to say - अद्वैतं किमागममात्रेण प्रतिपत्तव्यम्.. शक्यते तर्केणापि ज्ञातुम्. If despite using tarka, I necessarily need Shruti, then the indispensibility of Shruti remains unquestioned.  
  
So, tarka can never lead one to truth - without Shruti.  
  
If this be the case, the statement अद्वैतं किमागममात्रेण प्रतिपत्तव्यम्.. शक्यते तर्केणापि ज्ञातुम् becomes unjustified.  
  
Also, in DSV, it is clearly held that Shruti is pramANa not because of being pramA-mUlaka, but because of beingabAdhita-vishayaka. \[शास्त्रस्य प्रमामूलकत्वाभावप्रसङ्गादिति-चेन्न; ***प्रमामूलकत्वाभावेऽप्यबाधितविषयत्वेन*** शास्त्रप्रामाण्योपपत्तेरन्त्यपक्षाभ्युपगमात् ।\]  
  
So, if tarka (anumAna, arthApatii) also has abAdhita-vishaya then I don't understand how Shruti can have a higher place. Is it our claim that an anumAna cannot have abAdhita-vishaya (Brahman). Of course, not positively, but negatively.

  

My point is this -  
  
1. Tarka-delimited-by-satya-tva and Shruti-delimited-by-satya-tva are on identical footing. Both have abAdhita-vishaya.  
  
2. Tarka-delimited-by-tarka-tva is inferior to Shruti-delimited-to-Shruti-tva. In fact, it is not only inferior, it just stands nowhere.  
  
3. Mumukshus are interested in Tarka-delimited-by-satya-tva and Shruti-delimited-by-satya-tva.
