I would direct you to the following janmAdyadhikaraNa bhAShya passage:



वाक्यार्थविचारणाध्यवसाननिर्वृत्ता हि ब्रह्मावगतिः, नानुमानादिप्रमाणान्तरनिर्वृत्ता ।

The direct cognition of Brahman (which you as a mumukshu seek) is only possible through an enquiry into the meaning of vedAntic sentences (which is only possible if we understand the tAtparya of vedAntic sentences), it is not possible for it to arise through reasoning etc.



praNAms

Hare Krishna



The above is more explicitly expressed by bhAshyakAra in the second adhyAya ‘vilakshaNatvAdhikaraNa’. tulyaM atrApi parama gambheerasya jagatkAraNasya ‘tarka anavagAhyatvaM’ ‘tarkasya cha apratishTitatvaM, anyathA anumAne api avimOkshaH Agama virOdhashcha…etyevaMjaateeyaM nirAkaraNaM. Neither pratyaksha nor anumAna supported by pratyaksha would help us to know / realize the truth coz. rupAdyabhAvAt hi nAyamarthaH pratyakshagOcharaH, lingAdyabhAvAccha nAnumAnAdeenAM, so what is that which talks about truth i.e. AgamamaNtareNa utprekshitumapi shakyaM clarifies Shankara. If one does not realize the role and importance of Shabda pramANa in vaidika mArga then they are not vaidika-s they are mere tArkikA-s. If at all one to be identified himself as vaidika and NOT tArkika, he has to rely totally on the supremacy of the shruti as pramANa rather most authentic pramANa that has been originated from the paramAtma himself.
