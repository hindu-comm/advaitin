My thought:

  

Truth is ever shining and its knowledge is intrinsic to the Self. However the ajnani's predicament is this inherent knowledge of Self (I am satyam, unchanging...) competing against the opposing knowledge (adhyasa) of not-self (i am limited, mortal...). And the ajnani cannot discern/establish in Self-knowledge through non-shruti pramanas, and hence tarka which has to be based in knowledge will for the ajnani be delimited by his bipolar confusion.

  

The only pramana that decisively affirms Self-knowledge as Truth is the shruti, and hence the ajnani who is also an astika is alone capable of transitioning from tarka-delimited-by-ajnana to tarka-delimited-by-satyatva via the necessary steps of Sravana, astikyam, tarka-delimited-by-shruti...
