In DSV, where Shruti also is accepted to be prätibhāsika, I fail to see any inherent superiority of Shruti except that its content is true.

So, vimatam mithyā, driśyatvaat, swapnavat --- is complete truth. It negates all seen. And makes one situate as the draṣṭā. The draṣṭṛtva also being driśya, is also negated.

Before proceeding there, please share your view on admissibility of Śruti as supreme pramāṇa in DSV. I am aware of supremacy of Śruti in SDV and am in agreement with you and Venkat ji on this. Due to apaurusheyatva, there is doṣa-abhāva in Śruti. And prāmāṇya being swtah and aprämāṇya being paratah, there is no possibility of aprāmāṇya of Śruti.

  

So, I agree with standard traditional view of supremacy of Śruti.

  

However, my understanding is that it is all valid in SDV and not in DSV.

  

In DSV, Śruti is kalpita by jiva who is anishchita-advaita. Svapna-anunāna is good enough there. Śruti vākya in DSV is also good enough. But then it cannot have exclusive domain over truth. It also is subject to drishti-srishti and has pratibhāsa-mātra-śarīra. What exclusivity it can have when it itself is having jnAtaika-sattA and motion of its continuity being illusion.

  

Please note that in DSV, it is accepted that none has ever attained liberation. Liberation of Shuka etc are arthavāda. We may add Shankara, Ramana etc in the list. So, these are unsettling derivations.

  

Regarding BSB and Upanishads also, we must note that they operate primarily in SDV. Seldom they go to DSV. And our discussion is mainly in the prakriya of DSV.
