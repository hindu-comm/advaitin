Hari Om,

  

In this connection, page 174 and 175 of Vedānta Siddhānta Muktāvalī may be referred to (Hindi translation). I am giving here the relevant excerpts.

  

किञ्च, ब्रह्मातिरिक्तं शास्त्रसत्वमस्तीति ये मन्यन्ते तान् प्रति भवतु शास्त्राप्रामाण्यप्रसङ्गो दोपः, तस्य प्रमाणैकस्वभावत्वात् । ये तु मन्यन्ते सदा अद्वैतमेव अस्ति द्वैतवार्त्तानभिज्ञाः तान् प्रति कथमयं दोपः स्यात्? तैः शास्त्रस्य वा तत्प्रामाण्यस्य वा ब्रह्मातिरिक्तस्य अनभ्युपगमात् ।

  

अन्यथा ताभ्यामेव अद्वैतक्षतिः स्यात् ।

  

न च वेदप्रामाण्यानभ्युपगमे पाषण्ड-त्वप्रसङ्गः, तदप्रामाण्यस्यापि अनभ्युपगमात् ।

  

न च वेदप्रामाण्यानभ्युपगमे अद्वैतस्य अप्रामाणिकत्वप्रसङ्गो दोष:, द्वैतदर्शिनं प्रति प्रमाणाधीनसिद्धिके आत्मनि च तस्य दोषत्वात् ।

  

====================

  

In DSV, the prāmāṇya of Śruti is also prātibhāsika and mithyā. It has jñātaika-sattā. Śruti itself being aniścita-advaita-kalpakena-kalpita, cannot be said to possess the pre-eminent position it enjoys in SDV. If it enjoys that position, let us have a proper discussion as to how can it enjoy.

  

It needs to be logically discussed as to how the Śruti seen in Swapna, though believed to be pre-eminent, is actually superior to a tarka in Swapna - which leads to same goal.

  

So, as per my unt, in DSV, the non-prāmāṇya of Śruti is not admitted. But prāmāṇya of Śruti too is mithyā. Otherwise, by that prāmāṇya itself, there will be loss of Advaita.

  

Further, prāmāṇya of Śruti in DSV is both mithyā as well as prātibhāsika. A tarka which negates all seen, is itn equal footing with Śruti which teaches neti, neti.

  

One can have belief in prāmāṇya of Śruti in DSV also. One can believe that it enjoys pre-eminent position. But that is a belief. Like in a dream. When examined critically, one would have to admit that Śruti seen in dream does not enjoy superior position to tarka in the dream.

  

This is my humble view. I am open for correction in this regard.
