Let us examine that. My view is that - Shruti reveals the same truth which the swapna-anumAna leads to. Accordingly, there is no reason to aver that one is superior to the other.

  

> It isn't. pakShAMshe AtmAntare nAnAtmani vA hetvAbhAvaH. Does anya
> AtmA or nAnA AtmA have the drshyatva hetu? How will devadatta landing
> on his AtmA using your anumAna know there is no other
> yajnadattAdi-AtmA? This is the issue with Sankhya and Yoga too. Pls
> give exact tarka you will use that will be flawless.

  

pakshAmsha cannot be admitted. AtmAntara itself is not yet established.

  

The objection is based on prior acceptance of jIva-bheda. But that itself is prAtibhAsika. There is one jIva (AtmA), and entire seen is negated by him. Take for e.g. swapna. There might be appearance of Yajnadatta. But that is devoid of jIva.

  

So, jIva-bheda being drishya, is mithyA and prAtibhAsika. So, another jIva is not established. So, pakshAmsha or AtmAntara is not established. This is the precise answer.

  

So, whatever is seen is illusory -- leaves one to negate everything and situate as what one is. Arriving at the same conclusion as that in Neti, Neti.



> To assert once more, DSV also has Shruti as the only pramANa. Tarka is
> to ascertain the Shruti nirNaya with 1 out 6 lingas in SDV and DSV
> both, and later only for prameya, nothing more.

  

Why should Shruti be the only pramANa in DSV? I can understand in SDV, wherein we hold apaurusheyatva as the reason for Shruti being devoid of dOsha. And on account of dOsha-abhAva, there is aprAmANya-abhAva. No other pramANa can reject the possibility of it being defective, on account of being paurusheya. So, we hold Shruti to be the only pramANa for Brahman, as others cannot proclaim the same with conviction.

  

But when we come to DSV, this very premise of apaurusheyatva stands questioned. And the entire structure which gives pre-eminence to Shruti is questioned. Tell me one reason - why should Shruti enjoy a pre-eminent position in DSV?
