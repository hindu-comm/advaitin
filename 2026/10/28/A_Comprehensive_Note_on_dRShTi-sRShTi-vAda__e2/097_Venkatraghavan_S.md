Namaste Sudhanshu ji,

"But when we come to DSV, this very premise of apaurusheyatva stands questioned."  
  

Why and from what standpoint are you questioning? If you can accept svapna shruti, you must have no qualms accepting svapna apauruSheyatva also.

  

For a dreamer who is convinced of the reality of the dream, it follows that with respect with to the dreamer the svApna shAstrayonitva of Brahman is valid. Its only upon waking up that we realise it is svApna. But when in that frame of reference, there is internal capacity of shruti to reveal BrahmAtma aikya independently and for svApna tarka to depend on svApna shruti for the same.

  

Outside of svapna (ie waking up to the absolute reality) , there is no pramANa prameya vyavahAra anyway. So there is no scope to question apauruSheyatva of a non-existent shruti. Inside svapna for the eka jIva, that apauruSheyatva is equally unquestionable.

  

  

Regards

Venkatraghavan
