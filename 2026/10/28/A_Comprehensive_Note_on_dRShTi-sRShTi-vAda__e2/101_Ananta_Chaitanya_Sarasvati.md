If you're using tarka, you cannot have both ways, meaning you will refute nor can you say that you will use only tarka suitable to you. You must refute every possible tarka opposite to that. eg,I can have more tarka for you to ponder on:

\*yajnadattAtmA satyam, adRshyatvAt, svapnadraShTRdevadattAtmavat\*

  

Well, Devadatta in utthAnAvasthA after samAdhi would say that he is not ahaMkArAdi. His friend Yajnadatta claims wiith the same anubhava that he is also different from ahaMkArAdi, 2nd AtmA... how will either know that they are one? They won't, without shruti. Nor would you with your tarka. Can Devadatta claim that he didn't have Yajnadatta during his samAdhi, so there is no 2nd AtmA?

  
This is something no tArkika can ever do without shruti. VSM gets away with it precisely because it starts with shruti's advaita conclusion as an axiom and then shifts the onus of proving the dvitIyAtmA on the pUrvapakShI. You are sadly mistaken in trying to use that on siddhAnta itself that gave the axiom for you to use. It is thanklessness at best and arrogance at worst in going against entire sampradAya and thinking that you can do something that no one else has ever been able to do.

  

Finally, you can't have two pramAnas for anything. This understanding is fundamental.
