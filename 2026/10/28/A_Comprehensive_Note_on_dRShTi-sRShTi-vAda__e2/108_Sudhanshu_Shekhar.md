I feel I have never made such a claim. Of course there is prAtibhAsika-apaurusheyatva of Shruti in DSV. Like the प्रातिभासिक-स्थायित्व, प्रातिभासिक-चिरन्तनत्व, प्रातिभासिक-पूर्वपश्चाद्भावित्व in the seen-world, there is absolutely no qualms in accepting प्रातिभासिक-अपौरुषेयत्व of prAtibhAsika-Shruti.

  

However, please note that the wordsप्रातिभासिक and स्थायित्व, प्रातिभासिक and चिरन्तनत्व, प्रातिभासिक and पूर्वपश्चाद्भावित्व, प्रातिभासिक and अपौरुषेयत्व are mutually contradictory.

  

When one understands that seen is prAtibhAsika, through a prAtibhAsika-tarka, he cannot carry on with pre-eminence to Shruti by highlighting its apaurusheyatva.

  

The prAtibhAsika-tva of tarka and Shruti will bring them on equal footing. That was my point. The only reliability of such prAtibhAsika-tarka and prAtibhAsika-Shruti is not some prAtibhAsika-feature-imagined-in-them but their revealing the same truth.

  

The prAtibhAsika-Shruti will be relied upon by a person, who knows that entire seen is prAtibhAsika, only because Shruti says the same truth - and not because there is prAtibhAsika-apaurusheyatva therein.
