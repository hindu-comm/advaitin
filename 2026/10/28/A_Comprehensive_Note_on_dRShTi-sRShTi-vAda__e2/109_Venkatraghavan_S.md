> However, please note that the wordsप्रातिभासिक and स्थायित्व, प्रातिभासिक
> and चिरन्तनत्व, प्रातिभासिक and पूर्वपश्चाद्भावित्व, प्रातिभासिक and अपौरुषेयत्व
> are mutually contradictory.

  

I do not agree with the above - what is apauruSheyatva? Essentially that there was no author of the Veda. Why? Because there is no memory of there being an author of the Veda.

  

There being no author to a Veda and the reason for it, there being no memory of an author, are equally true in DSV too.

  

Just like the AnupUrvI (the exact varNa sequence) of the Veda is preserved from one sRShTi to another in SDV, the AnupUrvI of the Veda is preserved from one sRShTi to another in DSV. That the sRShTi lasts only one one instant in DSV, does not preclude the AnupUrvI being the same.

  

Therefore, the unchanging Veda in every sRShTi in DSV is apauruSheya, and therefore does not suffer from the flaws of normal shabda which happen to be pauruSheya.

  

That being the case, that the Vedas are uniquely capable of revealing Brahman and that tarka is dependent on such a Veda to reveal Brahman can equally be true in DSV.
