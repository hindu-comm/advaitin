Objection: Is there a pramANa for AtmA or not?

1.  If not, it would result in Its non-existence, for we do not see the
    existence of a human horn, which lacks any pramANa.
2.  In the first case (if there is a pramANa),
    - is it worldly (i.e., pratyakSha, anumAna, etc.)?
    - or the Veda (shruti)?

Reply: Not the first (laukika/worldly), because AtmAis devoid of attributes (hence not an object of pratyakSha), and because the senses only have external objects possessing form, etc. as their sphere, there can be no ascertainment of a hetu pervaded by AtmA; therefore, It is not an object of anumAna.

**\*****Objection: कथं तर्हि औपनिषदत्वम् आत्मनः** सर्वप्रमाणविषयस्य **उपनिषन्मात्रगम्यत्वाभावादिति चेत्**\*,

How then can AtmAbe called 'aupaniShada' (known exclusively through the Upaniṣads), since, being the viShaya of all pramANas, It is not knowable solely through the Upanishads?

  

**\*Reply:**न, परिपूर्णसच्चिदानन्दप्रत्यग्रूपेण **मानान्तराविषयत्वादौपनिषदत्वमिति** वदामः\*

No! We declare that Its status as ' aupaniShada' is because, in Its true specific nature as the all-pervasive, limitlesssacchidAnanda innermost Self, It is completely beyond the reach of any other pramANa!
