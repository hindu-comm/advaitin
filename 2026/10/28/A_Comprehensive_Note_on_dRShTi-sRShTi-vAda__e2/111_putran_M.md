Namaskaram,

  

My thought on what I followed in the discussion.

  

The discussion on dream (seen) falls back to seer-seeing-seen. Either we have the dvaita vyavaharika at dream level, or the dsv eka-jiva or singular Ishvara at Dreamer level. In the latter, the seen is the seeing of the Seer. The seeing denotes Maya-shakti in action, but this seeing is of the Jnana inherent to the Seer. This Jnana of Seer-Self in its rudimentary seed manifest level is the apaurusheya veda. Understood this way, shruti has special place in DSV as well. (The mistake (if we accept the traditional position on Shruti) is when we try to point out the dream and leave out the Dreamer from the equation - they come and go together and the Veda always is special in the fuller framework.)

  

But then, is not the seer-seeing-seen duality also adhyasa? Yes we can run into a circular conundrum therefore imo or we can understand in terms of the two levels of dream analysis, and point out that both are mithya only - without making ambivalent the place of Veda.
