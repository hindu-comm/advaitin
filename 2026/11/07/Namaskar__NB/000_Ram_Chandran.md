Happy Deepavali to Everyone!

*The Vedic Prayer, Asato ma sad gamaya, Tamaso ma jyotir gamaya* is an ancient Sanskrit prayer from the [*Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavamana_Mantra) of the Yajur Veda.

*The English translation is: Lead us from the unreal to real (from falsehood to the Truth). Also move us from* the darkness of ignorance to the light of truth and immortality..

Deepavali (Diwali) connects through the deep inner symbolism of moving **from darkness to light**, where external lamps and myths represent the awakening of true self-knowledge (*Atman* and *Brahman*).
