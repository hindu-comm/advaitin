I want to understand the distinction between Brahman and Isvara as intended by Adi Shankara in the Prasthanatrayi Bhasya. I could understand the following things:

  

1\. Isvara is the controller of Maya.

2\. Brahman itself when viewed with upadhi and as samasti appears as Isvara.

3\. Maya = upadhi = ajnana

  

Is this correct? If it is correct then how could Isvara be the controller of maya given that the very existence of isvara depends on it?

  

I hope the doubt is clear. Let me explain more clearly - it is said that isvara has subdued living beings through maya. And at the same time it is being said that the concept of isvara itself is not true and only appears out of maya. So, how can Isvara be the controller of Maya in the first place given that its own existence depends on it.

  

Therefore we must accept that either isvara doesn't control maya.In that case who's the real controller of maya?

  

Is isvara and maya on the same level of reality, meaning if maya is false, then isvara has to be false or is isvara standing at a higher plane of reality than maya? Is maya controlling the isvara or isvara controlling the maya because to me it looks like maya is controlling isvara by granting it existance.
