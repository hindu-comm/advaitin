Namaste Lokesh ji,

  

Maya is mirage, Brahman is desert.

  

Ishwara refers to the desert from the frame of reference of mirage.

  

Brahman refers to the desert from the frame of reference of non-mirage.

  

Now, all understandings can be made accordingly as to who is controller etc and how can it be controlled.

  

Controller-controlled etc are talked from the frame of reference of Maya. Maya seeks a controller, and that can be non-Maya. And that is Brahman. From the frame of reference of Maya, Brahman is Ishwara. So, Ishwara is called controller.

  

Ishwara's existence does not depend upon Maya. The name "Ishwara" is used due to Maya.

  

So, Ishwara-tva of Ishwara is Maya-kalpita. Ishwara is Brahman.

  

Please let me know if there remains any issue.

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
