I have a few doubts -

  

\>*From the standpoint of logic, maya cannot be described as*either real or unreal.

From the standpoint of ultimate reality, I assume both world and maya are unreal. If that's the case what so special about that we call the world unreal but maya as neither real nor unreal? Or is it that even the world from the standpoint of logic is neither real nor unreal?

  

\> So, Ishwara-tva of Ishwara is Maya-kalpita. Ishwara is Brahman.

I understand that maya and world are assumed as entities because they appear true in transactional reality. However what's the utility of assuming the controller of maya ie Isvara.Isvara appear to us neither in ultimate reality nor in transaction reality.
