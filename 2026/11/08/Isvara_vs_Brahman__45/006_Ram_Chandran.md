Namaskar:



Your explanation, “Maya is mirage, Brahman is desert,” is a brilliant as per the framework of Advaita Vedanta Philosophy. The illusory appearance (mirage) of water has no independent existence of its own. It entirely depends on the desert (the Brahman) to be seen. Also Mirage doesn’t alter the true nature of the desert (the Brahman).



Ishwara or the Saguna Brahman is the ultimate reality viewed *through* the lens of the illusion (MAYA). From the perspective of someone standing in the presence of mirage, the desert is the creator, sustainer, and governor of that environment. Ishwara, the Saguna Brahman is the God with attributes (cosmic intelligence).

****

The Brahman or Nirguna Brahman is the ultimate reality as it truly is, independent of any projection. As per Paramarthika Satya (the Absolute Truth), there never was a mirage.

There was no water, and there was no traveler being fooled. There is only the Brahman. 



This is a good analogy and thank you very much!
