Dear Sudhanshu Ji

  

I'm still not convinced as to why do we need mayavi (a controller of maya when maya ultimately is unreal). You said

  

\>Māyā being an illusion needs a substratum.

  

Are you saying that needing a substratum amounts to needing a controller? How? A person may not know what a gyroscope is, however it doesn't imply that someone has induced that ignorance upon him. The ignorance is natural in this case and is anadi.

  

Also, coming to think of the substratum of illusion, ain't I the substratum of the illusion given that it is my illusion? I am the one in ignorance, not Īśvara. Why is Isvara required to account for my illusion?
