Namaskar:

In your message, you made a profound statement, “Ok, let's take a break like you suggested. This will be my last reply.” Taking a break implies let us all be silent for some time. In Vedanta, silence (*mauna*) is not merely the absence of sound or a passive state of quiet. Instead, it is the full, dynamic presence of the eternal Self (*Atman*) and the ultimate reality (*Brahman*). The Sanskrit Proverb, **"मौनं** **सर्वार्थ** **साधनम्" (Maunaṁ sarvārtha sādhanam)** translates to **"Silence is the means to achieve all goals."** More correctly, **Silence is an instrument that accomplishes all ends."**

Silence does not necessarily means Emptiness, It is in fact, Fullness (Poorna JustlLike the vast sky contains the stars and clouds but without being touched by them. True silence holds the entire universe of thoughts and forms while remaining untouched. The lotus grown on the pond is not touched by the water! The Upanishads state that words and the mind turn back from Brahman, having failed to grasp it. Language creates duality (speaker and spoken to, subject and object); silence is the nondual experience of reality.

Silence in Advaita Vedanta:

In Advaita Vedanta, silent transmission is considered the most potent form of instruction. Adi Shankara’s *Dakshinamurti Stotram* famously honors the teacher who reveals truth through *mauna*, and sages like Ramana Maharshi taught that silence expresses the whole truth more effectively than any lecture. In the Bhagavad Gita, Chapter 10, Verse 38, Lord Krishna declares: *"Among secrets I am silence."* Swami Vivekananda noted that choosing silence over forced, argumentative debates protects spiritual energy and prevents the mind from becoming disturbed.

The true goal of Vedantic practice is to become an impartial Witness (Sakshi), where thoughts are observed neutrally. By using reflective analysis (*neti, neti*—"not this, not that"), the seeker separates the silent, unchanging Atman from the transient body and mind.

In short, Silence is a virtue when it is silently without any debate!
