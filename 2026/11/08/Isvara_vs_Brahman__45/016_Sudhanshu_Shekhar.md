> Could you explain how is the illusion non-existent in itself? When
> Iwake up from the dream or get to know that it is not a snake but
> arope, I don't say that the illusion I saw was non-existent.  

  

We did this because we believe that some seen are not-illusion. So, we saw an illusory snake, realized that it was an illusion, we said that snake-was-non-existent but illusion happened. So, we give illusion vyAvahArika-reality, like vyAvahArika-table.

  

However, when entire seen is treated as illusion -- then illusion also being a seen is discarded as illusion. So, illusion itself becomes non-existent like illusory-snake.

> You say that the illusion-hood of illusion is also an illusion.
> Howso? Don't we all know that the water seen in a mirage is not real,
> yetwe do not deny this illusion?  

  

All seen is illusion. Illusion-hood of illusion is also a seen. So, illusion-hood is also an illusion. This may sound simple. But it is profound.

  

The illusion-hood being illusory does not turn illusion into non-illusion. This is discussed in detail in[https://sudhanshushekhar.wordpress.com/2023/05/16/6750/](https://sudhanshushekhar.wordpress.com/2023/05/16/6750/)
