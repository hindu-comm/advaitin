कैवल्योपनिषत् - KAIVALYOPANISHAD

  

Aśvalayanopanisad

  

A Handbook for the English Reader

  

Bindo

  

  

\*न कर्मणा न प्रजया धनेन त्यागेनैके अमृतत्वमानशुः\*

  

Not by action, not by offspring, not by wealth-by renunciation alone have some attained immortality.

  

ABOUT THIS BOOK

  

For the reader unfamiliar with Sanskrit, the Upaniṣads can feel like a locked door. This handbook on the Kaivalya Upanişad the Atharvavedic text also known as the Āśvalāyanopanişad turns the key.

  

Twenty-six verses are opened word by word, compound by compound, through a disciplined sixfold method - padaccheda, padaparicaya, anvaya, samāsavicāra, darśanika-sara-akalanam and sandhanam so the reader sees not only what the text teaches, but how its meaning is drawn out.

  

At its heart lies a single recognition: that the Self is ever distinct from body, mind and the fleeting states of experience - and that this recognition alone is kaivalya, freedom lived here and now.
