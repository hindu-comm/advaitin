Sending a pointer to my essay titled:[“On the Nature of Consciousness"](https://advaitadrishti.org/2026/11/08/on-the-nature-of-consciousness/).

  

Abstract: The fundamental questions concerning Consciousness have given numerous deep thinkers sleepless nights. Science, too, has tried to explain the emergence of Consciousness. However, an understanding of Consciousness continues to elude science as well. And for good reasons, as discussed in this essay. Advaita Vedanta is an ancient yet timeless teaching tradition that involves an inquiry into the questions related to Consciousness guided by the ancient texts of Vedanta. This essay presents insights into the Nature of Consciousness gathered from Advaita Vedanta.

  

There is also a section titled “Are AIs Conscious?” :-) for anyone interested in that topic.
