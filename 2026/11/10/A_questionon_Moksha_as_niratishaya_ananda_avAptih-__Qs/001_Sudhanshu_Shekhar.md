NamasteSubbu ji.

  

Actually, "niratishaya ananda" needs to be properly explained. It is nothing but Sat. "Niratishaya ananda" should not be confused with some extremely unsurpassable pleasurable ecstasy. "Niratishaya ananda" is AtmA. That can be understood logically also.

  

We desire pleasurable things. Not for their sake, but for the sake of Ananda. So, I desire money - money has इच्छा-विषयत्व, but it is dependent on the ultimate desire of Ananda. So, money has अन्य-इच्छा-अधीन-इच्छा-विषयत्व. This is not so for Ananda. We desire Ananda not for anything else, but for its own sake. So, Ananda has अन्य-इच्छा-अनधीन-इच्छा-विषयत्व.

  

Qualified-Ananda will not have अन्य-इच्छा-अनधीन-इच्छा-विषयत्व. The Ananda (visheshya) will have अन्य-इच्छा-अनधीन-इच्छा-विषयत्व.

  

Now, interestingly, the same lakshaNa also satisfies in the case of one's Self. It is for the sake of oneself that one does everything. Son is dear because he gives Ananda to oneself. Everything is dear because it is anukula to oneself. So, Self i.e.AtmA also possesses
अन्य-इच्छा-अनधीन-इच्छा-विषयत्व which in other words is nothing but परमप्रेमास्पदत्व.

  

So, what we refer to as niratishaya-Ananda is nothing but AtmA. And its avApti is nothing but to situte as AtmA.

  

So, situating as AtmA (and not as anAtmA) is indeed mukti. And that is being sat-chit-Ananda. And there is no notion of time here. At this moment, I situate as sat-chit-Ananda and not as anAtmA. And that is the end of matter. There can be no evaluation, no comparison that for so long I was situating as anAtmA. That is an error. Time is an error.

  

I have this moment. And here in this moment, I situate not as "I", not as mind, not as body but as that which is none of these. I situate as not-anAtmA. I don't enquire what it is. That is not an useful pursuit. I situate as non-anAtmA, in this moment. And that is end of pursuit.

  

Regards,

Sudhanshu Shekhar.
