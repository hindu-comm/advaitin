**Namaste Ananta Chaitanya ji,**

  

In the above the ' pashyatyachakShuH' is from the standpoint of samsara where Brahman/Ishwara is admitted to be endowed with the power of cognizing all sensual things without the need for sense organs:  
  
 ब्रह्मसूत्रभाष्यम् · द्वितीयोऽध्यायः

  

…सामर्थ्यं दृष्टं तथान्यस्यापि सामर्थ्येन भवितव्यमिति नियमोऽस्तीति । **प्रतिषिद्धसर्वविशेषस्यापि ब्रह्मणः सर्वशक्तियोगः सम्भवतीत्येतदप्यविद्याकल्पितरूपभेदोपन्यासेनोक्तमेव ।** तथा च शास्त्रम् — ‘अपाणिपादो जवनो ग्रहीता पश्यत्यचक्षुः स शृणोत्यकर्णः’ इत्यकरणस्यापि ब्रह्मणः सर्वसामर्थ्ययोगं दर्शयति ॥ ३१ ॥

In the case of the videha mukta who has become one with Akhanda Advaiteeya prapanchopashamam, \*na nirodho... na baddho na muktaḥ\*... Brahman, there is no way we can admit     
'anubhavatyanantakaraNaH' as the antahkarana dharma is also admitted for Brahman, as per the Bhashya above, in the prapancha level alone.

I think there is a problem here. The Bhashya explains that 'Sat' is the absence of \*mithyātva\*, 'Chit' is the absence of \*jaḍatva\*, and 'Ananda' is the absence of \*duḥkha\*. In the prapanchopashamam Turiya scenario the lakshanas will have no application. However, with reference to Sat, Brahman must be admitted as Sat, since a scenario where nothing exists (śūnya) cannot occur. And the Sat mentioned in BG 2.16 is of this nature. Hence, it was explained there that Brahman/Atman is \*acchedya\*, \*adāhya\*, etc.
