**Namaskar:**

When a presenter’s ego feels threatened by a disagreement or a challenge to his beliefs, he or she often treats the intellectual debate as an existential attack. Consequently the person becomes defensive, arrogant, or rigid rather than being objective. Persons with high intelligence do not automatically prevent an ego-driven mindset, and such persons think that they are always right! Persons who are able to subdue their Ego are able to recognize that their view points are limited and they could go wrong! This will enable them to separate the accuracy of their personal ideas from the accuracy of other ideas which will help them remain open to new evidence and other perspectives.

The discussants need to understand the following:

· Have Intellectual Humility and Accept that no one other than the Brahman knows everything and those blind spots do exist in any reasoning.

· Agree to correct any helpful opinions (feedback) of others instead of attacking their intelligence.

· Actively seek counter arguments and agree to change when presented with stronger evidence.

· Respect others and assume that other people may hold more valuable insights, even when you strongly disagree with their conclusions.

**I recommend everyone to revisit Adi Shankara’s Bhajagovindam to get more insights. The very first verse starts as follows:**

**Bhajagovindam Bhajagovindam**  
**govindam bhajamuudhamate**  
**sampraapte sannihite kaale**  
**nahi nahi rakshati dukrijnkarane**  
  
Here Shankara suggests that Worshipping [***Lord Govinda***](http://www.hinduismabout.com/2015/06/lord-vishnu.html) can help one for salvation. Probing the grammar rules will not help anyone to focus their mind. In a subtler way, Shankara Bhagavadpada suggests that intellectual scrutiny such as correctness of grammar will not help anyone to find the path of liberation. Here Shankara provides a logical reason for worshipping Govinda which may be a reasonable approach to curtail one’s Ego. In other words, he insists our Ego will take us farther away from liberation.
