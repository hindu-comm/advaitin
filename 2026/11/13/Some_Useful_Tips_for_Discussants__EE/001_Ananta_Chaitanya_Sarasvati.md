On Fri, Nov 13, 2026 at 2:14 AM Ram Chandran \<[ramvch...@gmail.com]()\> wrote:

> Probing the grammar rules will not help anyone to focus their mind. In
> a subtler way, Shankara Bhagavadpada suggests that intellectual
> scrutiny such as correctness of grammar will not help anyone to find
> the path of liberation.

Taking this literally that the correctness of grammar will not help in liberation can't be right; else, Bhagavan Bhashyakara wouldn't discuss so many things in bhAShya based on grammar. If one takes the story of the old man trying to learn dhAtupATha by rote that led to Bhaja Govindam as true also, even then it only means not to spend time learning grammar by rote in that old age and instead spend time in worship.  
  
However, there appears to be a hidden meaning as to why Bhagavatpadacharya picked an 8th gaNa dukR~n karaNe dhAtu instead of 1st gaNa's 1st parasmaipadI bhU sattAyAM or AtmanepadI edha vRddhau. He wanted to state that karma cannot lead to mukti.

  

mokSha is only due to shAstra, vAkyajnAna, which is possible only when we understand the words and sentences, which in turn is impossible without vyAkaraNa. Hence the statement in sampradAya: pANinIyaM kAnaDaM cha sarvashAstropakArakam; grammar and tarka are helpful for \[study of\] every shAstra.
