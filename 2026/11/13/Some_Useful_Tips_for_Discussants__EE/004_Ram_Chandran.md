Faith is a starting point for inquiry to find the Truth is a central concept in classical philosophy, theology, and religious traditions including Advaita Philosophy. We need to have faith in “Shastra Pramana,” to start our enquiry to find the Truth! [Swami Chinmayananda](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F01rymp&q=Swami+Chinmayananda) occasionally referenced Western spiritual thinkers, mystics, and Christian saints.In one of his books, he explains the importance of universal principles of devotion, faith, the nature of the mind, and the pursuit of God, by quoting St. Augustine: “Faith is to believe what you don’t see; and the reward is to see what you believe.”
